So, a corpse. I want to start with the corpse, because it is the only piece of this that was ever, in the proper sense, found.

In 2163, a batarian survey team was working the Dis system and found something lying in a crater on a world called Jartar. Hot, silicates, a trace of krypton and xenon in the air, a rock that nobody would give a second look. At the bottom of the crater was a body. A gigantic body, and the first guess was that it had been a starship, a genetically engineered one, organic, unlike anything in known space, and the dating put it at nearly a billion years old. A billion. I have spent my life on fifty thousand and been called ambitious. They had almost no time to look at it, because shortly afterward a batarian dreadnought arrived in the system, and shortly after that the crater was empty. And then the Hegemony did the thing governments do, which is deny it with a loud voice, and the louder, the more the salarians put documentation on the table. The salarian researchers had made their records before it vanished. I wish I knew who they were, by the way, and I have a feeling that one day I will, because I have not stopped looking. The Hegemony said there had never been anything there, and the more they were shown, the more vigorously they said it. That is not a denial. That is a flinch. I know the sound.

What happened to it afterward, I have in two pieces, and I will tell you which is which. It is one Leviathan, speaking inside your head. That is testimony, Shepard. It is not a stratum.
