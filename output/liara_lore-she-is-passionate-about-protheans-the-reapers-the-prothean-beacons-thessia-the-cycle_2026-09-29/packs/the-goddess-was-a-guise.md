- `summary:asari` [summary] (Athame Doctrine Protheans guise Janiri Lucen) — The asari, native to the planet Thessia, were often considered the most influential and respected sentient species in the galaxy, known for elegance, diplomacy, and biotic aptitude. They were among the earliest races to achieve interstellar flight after the Protheans and the first to discover and settle the Citadel, and they were instrumental in proposing and founding the Citadel Council.

A mono-gendered race with a feminine appearance and a roughly thousand-year lifespan, asari could reproduce with a partner of any gender or species through a process of parthenogenesis. During "melding" (the joining or union), an asari attuned her nervous system to her partner's to randomize her offspring's second gene set; offspring were always asari regardless of the partner's species. Melding could also be used purely to share thoughts, as when Liara T'Soni melded with Commander Shepard to interpret the Prothean beacon visions and Shiala uploaded the Cipher into Shepard's mind. The Ardat-Yakshi were asari with a rare genetic defect that destroyed a partner's nervous system during joining, appearing almost exclusively among "purebloods" born of two asari parents, a status considered a grave insult. Asari passed through three life stages: Maiden (from puberty, marked by exploration), Matron (around age 350, a desire to settle and raise children), and Matriarch (around age 700, acting as community sages and councilors).

Asari civilization on Thessia was secretly accelerated by the Protheans, who posed as the goddess Athame with guides Janiri and Lucen, genetically enhanced asari biotics, and left a beacon containing the VI Vendetta in what became the Temple of Athame; this was a closely held state secret. The asari found the Citadel in 580 BCE with the aid of the keepers, and with the salarians founded the Council in 500 BCE. During the 2186 Reaper invasion the asari initially withheld support, but after the Reapers overwhelmed their defenses their government revealed the Prothean beacon to Shepard; Thessia fell, and the asari finally committed forces to the Allied war effort.

Asari culture favored the "long view," centrism, consensus, and cultural domination over conflict. Their government, the Asari Republics, functioned as an inclusive electronic democracy deferring to Matriarchs. Their mainstream religion was siari ("All is one"), alongside worship of Athame. The asari military resembled tribal warrior bands of full-time professionals, favoring commando huntresses skilled in ambush, infiltration, and assassination, and fielding one of the galaxy's most powerful navies, including the dreadnought Destiny Ascension. - `summary:prothean` [summary] (Athame Doctrine Protheans guise Janiri Lucen) — The Protheans were an ancient race that vanished roughly 50,000 years ago after building a galaxy-wide empire. They were long credited with creating the Citadel and the mass relays, though in truth they only inherited that Reaper technology. Anthropoidal in form, they had two pairs of dual-pupiled eyes, three pairs of nostrils, a layered head carapace, three-fingered hands, and a flanging voice like turians. A unique trait was an experiential exchange system: by touch, a Prothean could absorb another's memories, skills, or language. They had quad-strand DNA, some resistance to low radiation, and at least some members wielded potent biotics.

After achieving spaceflight, the Protheans discovered ruins of the earlier inusannon and learned mass effect physics, gaining a centuries-long secret advantage. Facing a hostile machine intelligence, they decided all organic life must unite under their rule; resisting species were conquered and assimilated, coming to call themselves "Prothean." United, they held off the machines in the Metacon War. They observed and cultivated primitive species including asari, hanar, humans, quarians, salarians and turians, intending eventually to offer them the "choice" of joining the empire.

The Reapers arrived around 48,000 BCE, entering through the Citadel, decapitating the government, disrupting the relay network, and using Citadel census data to hunt every Prothean. Their unified empire and single military doctrine became fatal weaknesses. Indoctrinated Protheans acted as sleeper agents. Subject races like the densorin and the AI zha'til were destroyed; the Protheans themselves annihilated the zha'til by sending a star supernova. Some Protheans built a Crucible from designs left by prior civilizations, but indoctrinated builders sabotaged it. After centuries the Reapers killed or enslaved every Prothean and withdrew into dark space.

Survivors persisted in stasis. On Ilos, the Conduit research team under Ksad Ishan went dark; the VI Vigil cut power to non-essential pods until only about a dozen scientists remained, who later used the Conduit to reach the Citadel and alter the keepers' signal to trap the Reapers. On Eden Prime, nearly a million warriors and their commander Javik were stored; traitors betrayed the site, most pods were destroyed, and the VI Victory kept only Javik's pod alive. Shepard could revive Javik in 2186. Meanwhile the Reapers repurposed captured Protheans into the Collectors, directed by Harbinger to abduct humans for a Human-Reaper.

The Protheans held evolution, the "Cosmic Imperative," as the universe's driving force. Their influence shaped the asari (uplifted as a possible failsafe), humans (via the Mars archive) and the hanar, who worship them as the "Enkindlers." Notable Protheans included Javik, avatar of vengeance, and the guides Janiri and Lucen of asari myth. Prothean tech included mind-to-mind beacons, memory shards, indoctrination detection, kinetic barrier grids, the Particle Rifle, the flagship Penumbra Apex, and the Conduit.
