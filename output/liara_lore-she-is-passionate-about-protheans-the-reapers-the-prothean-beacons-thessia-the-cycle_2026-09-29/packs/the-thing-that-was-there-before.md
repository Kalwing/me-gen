- (leviathan-of-dis-discovery) In 2163 a batarian survey team exploring the Dis system found a gigantic corpse lying in a crater on the planet Jartar. Initially dated at nearly a billion years old, it was believed to be the remains of a genetically engineered starship - an organic vessel unlike anything in known space. There was almost no time to study it: shortly after the discovery a batarian dreadnought arrived in the Dis system, and soon afterward the corpse vanished from the crater. The Batarian Hegemony thereafter strenuously denied the Leviathan of Dis had ever existed, growing more vocal in its denials when confronted with documentation compiled by salarian researchers before the body disappeared. In truth the Hegemony had secretly transported the corpse to the batarian homeworld Khar'shan. As revealed in Mass Effect 3 by the batarian terrorist Balak (if he survived the 2183 attack on Terra Nova), the "Leviathan of Dis" was in fact the body of a dead Reaper. After the Terra Nova attack failed, the Hegemony accelerated its research on the Reaper corpse. Prolonged exposure indoctrinated numerous batarian scientists and government and military officials. When the Reapers invaded in 2186, those indoctrinated officials sabotaged the Hegemony's defenses, allowing the Reapers to overrun batarian space almost immediately and shatter its scattered navy. Decades later, Admiral Hackett's Task Force Aurora, led by Dr. Garret Bryson, studied records of the Leviathan of Dis while hunting the entity that had killed it - the being Bryson identified as the true Leviathan, an ancient race predating the Protheans that had the power to destroy Reapers.

The attacking Reapers abruptly withdraw. Shepard continues to Namakli, where Dr. Ann Bryson's team is pinned down by Reaper forces. Shepard extracts Ann but finds that most of her team—Boyles, Hopkins, and Kirkwood—have been killed in the assault. On Namakli, Shepard discovers Leviathan artifacts: ancient alien technology capable of projecting visions and inducing quantum entanglement states to stimulate neural activity, similar to rachni communication. Ann theorizes that Leviathan was using the artifacts to induce a quantum entanglement state and may have been preparing the rachni to fight the Reapers. However, when Marauders activate one of the artifacts, it possesses Ann, forcing Shepard's squad to destroy the device to free her. After the battle, Shepard informs Ann of her father's death, deeply affecting the scientist. Back on the Citadel, Ann collaborates with EDI to understand the artifacts' function: they act primarily as receivers but become transmitters while possessing a victim, and their signals could be traced to pinpoint Leviathan's location. Despite Shepard's objections, Ann volunteers to expose herself to the artifact again, with James Vega standing by to intervene. If allowed to continue, Ann emerges relatively unharmed and warns that Leviathan is angry and intends to kill Shepard. The quantum entanglement analysis identifies Leviathan's location: 2181 Despoina, a celestial body in deep space. Shepard travels to Despoina and confronts Reaper forces defending the location. After a fierce battle, Shepard makes contact with Leviathan itself—revealed not as a single being but as one of several surviving members of an ancient organic race that predates the Protheans and, remarkably, gave rise to the Reapers. Leviathan explains that the Reapers were created as synthetic servants to preserve organic life in a endless cycle, but the cycle became twisted into harvesting civilizations. Leviathan and its kind have survived for millions of years, going into hibernation during Reaper cycles. Impressed by Shepard's determination and the human effort to understand ancient history, Leviathan agrees to aid the modern galactic races in fighting the Reapers. The ancient organic demonstrates its power by disabling an attacking Reaper capital ship, proving that the Leviathans represent a force capable of challenging the Reapers themselves. This discovery offers humanity and its allies a potential strategic advantage: the possibility of ancient allies with millions of years of experience fighting the Reapers.

Set during the events of Mass Effect 3, the pack has Shepard hunt a mysterious being rumored to be powerful enough to destroy a Reaper, beginning with Dr. Garret Bryson's lab on the Citadel and following the trail through his colleague Garneau and his daughter Dr. Ann Bryson to the ocean world 2181 Despoina, uncovering more about the origins of the Reaper race. Content included ten missions (Citadel: Dr. Bryson, Citadel: Leviathan I-III, Leviathan: Find Garneau, Mahavid: Leviathan, Leviathan: Find Ann Bryson, Namakli: Leviathan, Leviathan: Scan Locations, Despoina: Leviathan), ten locations across several clusters, two weapons (AT-12 Raider shotgun, M-55 Argus assault rifle), five weapon mods, eleven new War Assets (including Dr. Ann Bryson, the Leviathan Enthrallment Team, and the Husk Neural Map), the Dominate bonus power, a cabin item (Bryson's Husk Head Experiment), and new rapid-transport terminals on the Citadel. It cost 800 BioWare Points on PC and $9.99 USD on consoles and is included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. BioWare promoted it with Twitter and blog stories about an anti-conspiracy activist.

- `summary:mahavid-leviathan` [summary] (Leviathan fragments erasing evidence) — Mahavid: Leviathan was a mission in the Mass Effect 3: Leviathan DLC, following Leviathan: Find Garneau. Shepard, Cortez, and squad traveled to the asteroid mining facility on Mahavid, which was under Reaper attack, searching for the missing Dr. Alex Garneau and evidence gathered from Dr. Bryson's lab. The facility was operated by T-GES Mineral Works, a front whose personnel behaved strangely and sealed themselves away as Shepard advanced. Shepard fought through Reaper ground forces (Marauders, Brutes, Husks, Ravagers, Banshees, Cannibals, Swarmers), repaired an inoperable elevator using a repair drone, and collected datapads, weapon mods, and about 12,500 credits from seven datapads.

Shepard tracked Garneau by finding an undelivered message containing a security-terminal password, a personnel ID, and a patient number for the medical bay. There Shepard met a man claiming to be Garneau who was in fact a thrall controlled by the Leviathan; he fled outside, pursued by Husks that chased him rather than Shepard. After a second, harder drone-escort section under fire, Shepard reached the mine, where the thrall detonated the Leviathan artifact, killing himself and the surrounding Husks. Shepard's squadmate then discovered the real Dr. Garneau's body; the pursued man had been an impostor. The freed facility personnel had no memory of Garneau, of Reapers, or of anything but being somewhere dark and cold. An asari miner stated the year was 2176, revealing the Leviathan had held the facility's staff as thralls for about ten years. The Reapers attacking the base suddenly withdrew. The mission led into Citadel: Leviathan II.
- `summary:citadel-embassies` [summary] (Leviathan fragments erasing evidence) — The Citadel Embassies was a location on the Citadel in Mass Effect 3, distinct from the earlier Embassies seen in Mass Effect. It contained Councilor Donnel Udina's Earth Councilor's office, Commander Bailey's C-Sec office, and a Spectre outpost. After Priority: The Citadel II the Earth Councilor's office became inaccessible, except when meeting the asari councilor there during Priority: The Citadel III.

The Spectre Office held a Spectre Terminal for tracking Citadel political and security situations, a Procurement Terminal for buying specialized equipment and missed side-mission items, and a shooting range with a weapon-modification workbench and three targets with health, shield, and armor bars that could be moved to varying distances, plus nearby ammunition and grenade refills. Miranda Lawson could be encountered there as a hologram. Spectre Requisitions operated as a shop.

The Embassy Offices contained an Avina terminal and Ambassador Dominic Osoba. James Vega, Samara, and Javik could be found there at various times looking over the Presidium, and Khalisah al-Jilani appeared after Shepard first spoke with Bailey. The Spectre Jondum Bau was present, along with a volus diplomat offering volus support to the Alliance and the Turian Hierarchy. Other petitioners appeared according to story progress: an old human woman asking after her son and, after the attack on the Citadel, an Alliance marine, an asari war strategist seeking Reaper code fragments, and a turian C-Sec officer seeking Cerberus ciphers; an elcor diplomat sought help evacuating Dekuuna after the Battle of Rannoch. The Office Suite, accessible only during a specific mission, held the hanar Diplomat and volus Ambassador Din Korlack's recordings.

Side missions staged from the area included Aria: Eclipse, Benning: Evidence, Citadel: Batarian Codes, Citadel: Cerberus Ciphers, Citadel: Hanar Diplomat, Citadel: Reaper Code Fragments, Citadel: Volus Ambassador, Dekuuna: Elcor Extraction, and Shrike Abyssal: Prothean Obelisk.
