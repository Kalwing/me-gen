- `summary:prothean` [summary] (hanar Enkindlers Protheans religion) — The Protheans were an ancient race that vanished roughly 50,000 years ago after building a galaxy-wide empire. They were long credited with creating the Citadel and the mass relays, though in truth they only inherited that Reaper technology. Anthropoidal in form, they had two pairs of dual-pupiled eyes, three pairs of nostrils, a layered head carapace, three-fingered hands, and a flanging voice like turians. A unique trait was an experiential exchange system: by touch, a Prothean could absorb another's memories, skills, or language. They had quad-strand DNA, some resistance to low radiation, and at least some members wielded potent biotics.

After achieving spaceflight, the Protheans discovered ruins of the earlier inusannon and learned mass effect physics, gaining a centuries-long secret advantage. Facing a hostile machine intelligence, they decided all organic life must unite under their rule; resisting species were conquered and assimilated, coming to call themselves "Prothean." United, they held off the machines in the Metacon War. They observed and cultivated primitive species including asari, hanar, humans, quarians, salarians and turians, intending eventually to offer them the "choice" of joining the empire.

The Reapers arrived around 48,000 BCE, entering through the Citadel, decapitating the government, disrupting the relay network, and using Citadel census data to hunt every Prothean. Their unified empire and single military doctrine became fatal weaknesses. Indoctrinated Protheans acted as sleeper agents. Subject races like the densorin and the AI zha'til were destroyed; the Protheans themselves annihilated the zha'til by sending a star supernova. Some Protheans built a Crucible from designs left by prior civilizations, but indoctrinated builders sabotaged it. After centuries the Reapers killed or enslaved every Prothean and withdrew into dark space.

Survivors persisted in stasis. On Ilos, the Conduit research team under Ksad Ishan went dark; the VI Vigil cut power to non-essential pods until only about a dozen scientists remained, who later used the Conduit to reach the Citadel and alter the keepers' signal to trap the Reapers. On Eden Prime, nearly a million warriors and their commander Javik were stored; traitors betrayed the site, most pods were destroyed, and the VI Victory kept only Javik's pod alive. Shepard could revive Javik in 2186. Meanwhile the Reapers repurposed captured Protheans into the Collectors, directed by Harbinger to abduct humans for a Human-Reaper.

The Protheans held evolution, the "Cosmic Imperative," as the universe's driving force. Their influence shaped the asari (uplifted as a possible failsafe), humans (via the Mars archive) and the hanar, who worship them as the "Enkindlers." Notable Protheans included Javik, avatar of vengeance, and the guides Janiri and Lucen of asari myth. Prothean tech included mind-to-mind beacons, memory shards, indoctrination detection, kinetic barrier grids, the Particle Rifle, the flagship Penumbra Apex, and the Conduit.

The media fixated on the questions of who the aliens were, whether they were friendly and whether they were still out there.

While the discovery fractured most religions, it unified politics into an "us versus them" stance. In early 2149, political leaders across the system set aside their differences, and along with representatives from Mars and Luna, Earth's eighteen largest nations drafted and ratified the Systems Alliance Charter to establish a representative body to expand and defend human territory in space. The founding nations were the Argentine Republic, the Chinese People's Federation, the Commonwealth of Australia, the Federal Republic of Germany, the Federal Republic of Nigeria, the Federative Republic of Brazil, the French Republic, the Indian Union, Japan, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, the Republic of Korea, the Republic of Turkey, the Republic of Singapore, the Republic of South Africa, the Russian Federation, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom of Great Britain, and the United North American States; the Republic of Ireland joined a year later. These nations pooled their scientific and technological might to form the Systems Alliance military.

Relativity threatened to make expansion slow and disconnected, since primitive FTL drives caused time dilation and even advanced drives could not run indefinitely because of an eezo fuel side effect. The answer came from the translated Mars data cache, which in 2148 revealed that Charon, the largest moon of the planetoid Pluto, was actually a colossal piece of Prothean technology encased in ice, dormant for 50,000 years. A human science team near Pluto confirmed the finding and, under orders from Earth, began excavation to reactivate it. Activation proved surprisingly simple, but exploratory probes sent through the Charon Relay instantly lost contact, so people had to be sent. Because many high-ranking Alliance officers came from Earth's militaries, the decorated Rear Admiral John Grissom was chosen to lead. Around mid-2149, Grissom and several volunteers boarded an experimental jump ship and became the first humans to transit the Charon Relay, arriving instantly at a second mass relay in the Arcturus system, 36 light-years away, where they learned the relays formed a galaxy-spanning network. On their return, Grissom and his crew were hailed as heroes, and the sullen, retiring Grissom became the face of the Systems Alliance as humanity entered a new age of expansion.
