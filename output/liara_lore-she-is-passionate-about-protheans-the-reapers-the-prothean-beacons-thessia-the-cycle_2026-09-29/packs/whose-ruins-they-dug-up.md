## Form
**tunnel** — One enthusiasm followed all the way down. The narrator opens on a subject they love and know cold — how a mass relay actually throws a ship, the grammar of their homeworld's oldest language, the breeding lines behind a varren pit — and talks it out well past where a listener would have stopped them, then follows a seam into the next subject and the next. An infodump, but a personal one: every fact is here because the narrator went and got it, and why they cared runs under the telling — the childhood that started it, the person who taught them, the love and the plain curiosity of it. Sections are subjects, each opening where the last one's tangent left off.

- `summary:biotics` [summary] (Eingana thoi'han) — Biotics was the ability of some lifeforms to create mass effect fields using element zero nodules embedded in body tissues, accessed and augmented through bio-amps. Biotic individuals could knock enemies over at range, lift them into the air, generate gravitational vortices to tear apart obstacles or enemies, or create protective barriers. Abilities fell into three categories: telekinesis (mass-lowering fields), kinetic fields (mass-raising fields), and spatial distortion (shifting fields that rip apart objects). Powers were triggered by "physical mnemonics," physical gestures that fired neurons in sequence.

All asari were naturally biotic from birth and required no implants or special training. Biotics of other species were individuals exposed to dust-form element zero in utero who developed eezo nodules throughout their nervous systems. Exposure rarely produced biotics; most exposed fetuses were unaffected, and others developed brain tumors or other complications. In humans, only about one in ten eezo-exposed infants developed usable talent, and in rare cases talents emerged in young adulthood through additional exposure. Identified biotics were typically fitted with a surgically implanted amplifier around puberty and then underwent long biofeedback training.

The first human biotics were born after starship accidents in the 2150s dispersed element zero over populated areas; the first known was female, dubbed "Subject White." Conatix Industries was founded to track exposed individuals and develop implants, and in 2160 established the BAaT programme on Jump Zero, which was shut down after the turian instructor Commander Vyrnnus was killed by a student. Human biotics were classed by implant model: L1, the notorious L2 (introduced 2167, powerful but often unstable and medically damaging), L3 (2170, stable but weaker), retrofit and experimental variants, L4 used in the Ascension Project, and L5x and L5n for Adepts and Vanguards by 2185. Dr. Ellen Ryder created the first human biotic implants. Human biotics faced widespread prejudice, while the Alliance military eagerly recruited them, granting them a 4,500-kilocalorie daily ration.

Among aliens, biotics varied in rarity: common and powerful in asari, rare and prized in salarians, uncommon and distrusted in turians (relegated to Cabals), few but extremely powerful in krogan (Battlemasters such as Skarr and Wrex), and very rare in quarians. Batarians, drell (Thane Krios), Collectors, Protheans (Javik), volus, and vorcha also produced biotics. Non-sapient biotic creatures existed on eezo-rich worlds like Thessia and Eingana, including Jack's biotic varren Eezo. Biotics were exceedingly rare or absent in the Andromeda galaxy.

- `summary:mass-relay` [summary] (mass relays Prothean origin Reaper) — Mass Relays were mass transit devices scattered across the Milky Way, forming a galaxy-wide network that enabled near-instantaneous interstellar travel between paired or linked relays, turning journeys of years or centuries into days or hours. Each relay consisted of two fifteen-kilometer curved metal arms around a set of revolving gyroscopic rings five kilometers across, containing a blue-glowing core of element zero. They were built from the same unknown, extremely resilient material as the Citadel and protected by a quantum shield that locked their structure at the subatomic level, letting them survive even a supernova's wake. They emitted no heat or radiation, making displaced relays hard to locate. Relays worked by creating a nearly mass-free corridor of space-time; a pilot supplied the transit mass before being moved into the approach corridor, and ships emerged with positional "drift" of thousands of kilometers, making relays impractical to mine or trap. Primary relays could propel a ship thousands of light years but linked only to a single partner; secondary relays linked to any relay over shorter distances of a few hundred light years. After the Rachni Wars, Council Space species refused to open a primary relay without knowing its destination; human pioneers ignorant of this prohibition opened relays at random while exploring, contributing to the First Contact War with the turians. Many relays were dormant but easily reactivated; a Prothean data cache on Mars led humanity to the Charon Relay, encased in ice and orbiting Pluto, previously thought to be the moon Charon.

Despite common belief, the relays were created not by the Protheans but by the Reapers. Sovereign revealed that the Protheans were merely one of many species to find the relays and the Citadel; using them, galactic civilizations evolve along paths the Reapers desire and advance faster, shortening the time between harvests and streamlining galactic extinction. The Protheans nonetheless cracked the relays' operating principle and used it to build the Conduit. Commander Shepard discovered that the Citadel itself is an enormous inactive mass relay leading to dark space and the control center for the entire network, letting the Reapers sever cluster-to-cluster travel on their return. Dr. Amanda Kenson's team calculated that a large enough mass striking a relay could destroy it, producing a supernova-scale explosion; steering an asteroid into the Bahak system's Alpha Relay annihilated the system and killed more than 300,000 people. During the battle for Earth, activating the Crucible transmitted its energy through the relay network from the Citadel, overloading the relays' element zero cores; the relays were rebuilt afterward by survivors (or by the Reapers under the control option), with more extensive damage and harder rebuilding if Shepard rallied insufficient forces. The Relay Monument on the Presidium is Prothean artwork interpreted as either vanity or a symbol of galactic unity. In cut content the relays were called "phase gates."
