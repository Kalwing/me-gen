## Form
**motivational** — A speech meant to move someone to act — the narrator talking Shepard (or whoever is listening) up off the floor and back into the fight. Sections are the rising beats of an address: the stakes named, the doubt named plainly, the evidence from a life lived that it can be done, the call. The narrator's own history is fuel for the exhortation, not the subject of it — but so is the listener's: the narrator holds up the other person's record as often as their own, since it's their record the speech is meant to land on.
