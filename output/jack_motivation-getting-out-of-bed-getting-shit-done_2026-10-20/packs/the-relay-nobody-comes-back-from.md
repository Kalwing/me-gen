## Form
**motivational** — A speech meant to move someone to act — the narrator talking Shepard (or whoever is listening) up off the floor and back into the fight. Sections are the rising beats of an address: the stakes named, the doubt named plainly, the evidence from a life lived that it can be done, the call. The narrator's own history is fuel for the exhortation, not the subject of it — but so is the listener's: the narrator holds up the other person's record as often as their own, since it's their record the speech is meant to land on.

- (derelict-reaper-iff-mission) The Derelict Reaper was a two-kilometre Reaper found orbiting the failed star Mnemosyne in the Thorne system of the Hawking Eta cluster. Roughly 37 million years earlier an unknown spacefaring race had fired a mass accelerator round of extraordinary power that struck and disabled the Reaper; the round continued on and hit the planet Klendagon, carving its Great Rift Valley - likely that race's last act before their extinction. Despite massive hull damage the Reaper kept a weak mass effect field so it would not fall into the star, and its indoctrination field remained active. Cerberus discovered it and sent a research team under Dr. Chandana; the team was indoctrinated and turned into husks. The Illusive Man sent Commander Shepard to investigate the loss of contact and to recover the Reaper's Identify Friend/Foe (IFF) unit, which was needed to survive transit of the Omega 4 Relay. When Shepard's squad boarded, the ship's kinetic barriers engaged and trapped them, so they had to destroy the ship's mass effect core to escape. Fighting through husks, the squad retrieved the IFF and reached the core, where they found a single disabled geth that had been overwhelmed by husks. Shepard could recover the geth or leave it. Shepard destroyed the core and escaped by shuttle just before the Reaper fell into Mnemosyne's atmosphere and was destroyed. Back on the Normandy, EDI installed the IFF, which carried a Reaper signal that let her assume full control of the ship. Shortly afterward Joker sent a distress call; how long Shepard delayed before returning to the Normandy (per `iff_delay`) determined whether the non-combat crew, including Dr. Chakwas, were carried off by the Collectors or rescued.

The Illusive Man claimed this offered a rare opportunity to study Collector technology and understand the threat posed by the species to human colonies. Upon boarding the massive, derelict vessel, Shepard's team moved through a linear interior filled with dead bodies and advanced Collector technology. Inside the ship's control terminals, EDI began analyzing the Collectors' genetic samples and discovered evidence of comprehensive genetic comparisons between Collector and human biology. The analysis revealed a shocking truth: the Collectors were revealed to be descendants of the long-extinct Prothean civilization, transformed and enslaved by the Reapers. Back on the Normandy, EDI's full analysis revealed that the Collectors possessed an advanced Identify Friend/Foe (IFF) device allowing them to navigate the Omega 4 Relay safely. The Illusive Man then directed Shepard to locate a derelict Reaper in the Thorne system, from which a compatible IFF could be recovered to access the Omega 4 Relay—warning that the passage would likely be one-way. Key revelations from this mission included the Collectors' true Prothean origin, evidence of human-targeted genocide, the Illusive Man's willingness to sacrifice assets for intelligence, and the necessity of acquiring an IFF to proceed against the Collector threat.

- `summary:omega-4-relay` [summary] (Omega 4 relay) — The Omega 4 Relay was a mass relay in the Sahrabarik system of the Omega Nebula, surrounded by hazard beacons and automated warnings. Over the previous thousand years many ships had attempted to pass through it and none had returned; the only ships to travel freely back and forth were the mysterious Collectors. Theories about its far end ranged from a black hole to an earthly paradise, but most assumed the Collectors captured or destroyed those who passed.

In Mass Effect 2 it was revealed that the relay led to the galactic core, where the Collector Base sat on the edge of a black hole. Standard relay transit produced a drift of several thousand kilometers, which was fatal in the galactic core, so the area was choked with debris from ships that had used the relay without a Reaper IFF. The Reaper IFF triggered advanced safety protocols in the Omega 4 Relay that ensured a ship survived close approach to the black hole. It was the only known mass relay to glow red rather than blue, and if Shepard survived the suicide mission, Alliance officials were reported to be extremely concerned about the relay's recent use.

Most of its explorable environs were the territory of criminal elements. - `summary:derelict-reaper` [summary] (Reaper IFF derelict Reaper) — The Derelict Reaper was a two-kilometer-long Reaper found orbiting the failed star Mnemosyne in the Thorne system of the Hawking Eta cluster in Mass Effect 2, accessible after the Collector Ship mission. It gave off only faint, localized power signatures far weaker than its size implied, maintained a mass effect field that kept it from falling into the failed star, and had massive unrepaired holes blasted and melted through its hull, indicating it had "died" or been reduced to minimal function long ago.

Approximately 37 million years earlier, an unknown spacefaring race fired a mass accelerator round of extraordinary speed and power that struck and disabled the Reaper; the round continued on and impacted the planet Klendagon, creating its Great Rift Valley. This was likely the unknown race's final act of defiance before their extinction. Despite the damage, the Reaper's indoctrination mechanism remained active. Cerberus discovered the Reaper and sent a research team led by Dr. Chandana to study it; the team was indoctrinated and converted into husks.

The Illusive Man sent Commander Shepard to investigate the loss of contact with Chandana's team and to recover the Reaper's Identify Friend/Foe (IFF) tag, needed to safely use the Omega 4 Relay (the Reaper IFF mission). When Shepard's squad boarded, the ship's kinetic barriers engaged and trapped them; disabling them required destroying the ship's core, which also released the mass effect field. Fighting through husks, Shepard retrieved the IFF and reached the core, where the squad found a lone geth that was overwhelmed by husks. Shepard destroyed the core and escaped with the inactive geth just before the Reaper fell into Mnemosyne's atmosphere and was destroyed. A Cerberus log described the Reaper as "a dead god who still dreams."

- `summary:hawking-eta` [summary] (Reaper IFF derelict Reaper) — Hawking Eta was a star cluster lying uncomfortably close to the 5 kiloparsec ring around the galactic core, a region containing a large fraction of the Milky Way's hydrogen and most of its star formation activity, and largely too dangerous to travel safely. In Mass Effect it connected by mass relay to the Horse Head Nebula and Pangaea Expanse; in Mass Effect 2 its connections were the Caleston Rift and Omega Nebula. Access required completing the Collector Ship mission or Project Firewalker: Rosalie Lost.

The cluster bore the scars of multiple cataclysms, at least one involving the Reapers. About 37 million years ago a powerful mass accelerator round struck and disabled a Reaper, leaving it inert in the Thorne system; the round continued through space until it impacted Klendagon in the Century system, carving the Great Rift Valley. Between 20 and 40 million years ago a spacefaring civilization on Schwarzschild was wiped out by orbital bombardment from dreadnought-class weapons.

Its systems included Century (containing Klendagon, Tamahera, Canctra, and Tharopto), Chandrasekhar, Schwarzschild, Thorne (where Mnemosyne held the Derelict Reaper), and Verr. Chandrasekhar served as the Mass Effect 2 gateway system, though several planet descriptions place the cluster's mass relay in Century. The Derelict Reaper on Mnemosyne was the site of the Reaper IFF mission. Mass Effect assignments in the cluster included UNC: Locate Signs of Battle, UNC: Major Kyle, UNC: Turian Insignias, and UNC: Valuable Minerals; Mass Effect 2 added Project Firewalker: Survey Sites Located. The cluster is likely named for astrophysicist Stephen Hawking, with several systems named for other black hole physicists. With the Firewalker Pack installed, completing Rosalie Lost unlocked only Chandrasekhar, Century, Schwarzschild, and Verr; unlocking Thorne still required the Collector Ship mission.
