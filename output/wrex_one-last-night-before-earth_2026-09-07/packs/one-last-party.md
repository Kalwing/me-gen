- `summary:tuchanka` [summary] (tuchanka harshest planet) — Tuchanka was the krogan homeworld, the fourth planet of the Aralakh System in the Krogan DMZ. Scarred by bombardment craters, radioactive rubble, choking ash, salt flats, and alkaline seas, it could barely support life. Thousands of years earlier, life had grown in fierce abundance under the F-class star Aralakh (a Raik clan word meaning "Eye of Wrath"), with tree-analogues in thick jungles rooted in shallow silty seas. The world died in nuclear firestorms around 1900 BCE after the krogan split the atom, and a "little ice age" of nuclear winter killed off most remaining plant life, collapsing krogan society into warring clans and a dark age that lasted roughly 2000 years.

First contact with the salarians in 80 CE, during the Rachni Wars, made a krogan resurgence possible: krogan taken to less hostile worlds bred exponentially and returned to reconquer their home. They built vast underground bunkers against surface radiation, which proved prescient during the Krogan Rebellions when many sealed themselves in to avoid the genophage, convinced they could outbreed it; instead the genophage was transmitted into more than 90 percent of the sealed bunkers. During the salarian uplift the salarians also built the Shroud facility on Tuchanka to shield the planet from harmful solar radiation. By the time of the games, the genophage kept the population sharply limited, with few replacements despite krogan longevity.

In recent centuries many krogan returned; the reduced albedo raised global temperatures, so a vast shroud was assembled at the L1 Lagrange point, maintained by the Council Demilitarization Enforcement Mission (CDEM) from orbiting battlestations. CDEM advisories warned that visitors landed at their own risk and would not be extracted from clan warfare, and that nearly every native species, even the carnivorous vegetation, was predatory. Tuchanka was the first alien homeworld Shepard could visit. Missions set here included Grunt: Rite of Passage and Mordin: Old Blood in Mass Effect 2, and Priority: Tuchanka, Tuchanka: Bomb, and Tuchanka: Turian Platoon in Mass Effect 3.

The name meant "eye of wrath" in the krogan language and was also given to a place on Tuchanka and to an elite krogan warrior unit. Access to the system required prior completion of "Mordin: Old Blood" or "Grunt: Rite of Passage" in Mass Effect 2, and of "Priority: Sur'Kesh" in Mass Effect 3.

All of Aralakh's terrestrial worlds were inhospitable; even the krogan homeworld Tuchanka was barely livable by other species' standards. The Krogan Rebellions had stripped most of the system's resources for the war effort, though some deposits remained. The Council Demilitarization Enforcement Mission (CDEM) kept a careful watch on krogan hotspots to prevent another uprising like the Rebellions, and part of its role was regulating helium-3 fuel sales skimmed from the system's gas giants.

The planets orbiting the star Aralakh were Durak (first), Kanin (second), Kruban (third), Tuchanka (fourth), Ruam (fifth), and Vaul (sixth); the system also contained a mass relay and a fuel depot. Tuchanka was the focus of extensive gameplay content: in Mass Effect 2 it hosted the missions "Grunt: Rite of Passage" and "Mordin: Old Blood" and several assignments, and in Mass Effect 3 it hosted "Priority: Tuchanka," "Tuchanka: Bomb," "Tuchanka: Turian Platoon," "N7: Cerberus Attack," and Citadel-related missions.
