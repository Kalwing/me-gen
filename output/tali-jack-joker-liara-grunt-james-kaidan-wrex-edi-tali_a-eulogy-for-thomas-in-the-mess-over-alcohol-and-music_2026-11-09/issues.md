Scope, set by the controller: the user hand-edited and approved the first nine turns at the voice check. ## Grounding
- **G1 `thomas-answer-me`**: "set against my people, who put flowers on their dead and drift them out, who keep everything, who keep every body". No quarian funeral custom appears anywhere in the pack. The line also misreads the death text (see S1). **Fixed.**
- **G2 `thomas-answer-me`** (Akuze): "There's a monument now, fifty names … Neither of them is on it", "Admiral Hackett wanted a ceremony out there", "Thomas said he'd go if he could sit at the back". The pack says the Akuze monument has "a section dedicated to Shepard", so "neither of them is on it" contradicts it. Thomas's answer about the ceremony is invented. **Fixed** (cut; see R1).
- **G3 `thomas-answer-me`**: "I didn't know that part until the Commander told me". Nothing in the notes has Shepard telling Tali about Cerberus and Akuze. **Fixed.**
- **G4 `thomas-answer-me`**: "Not the marines', Akuze, none of them". The death text never mentions Akuze. **Fixed.**
- **G5 `the-help-desk-is-closed`**: "And he never gave one. Not one." (his party speeches). This contradicts the notes: at his thirtieth, in Rouen, he gave a birthday speech, and Joker read it. **Fixed** with a one-word scope: "never gave one aboard".
- **G6 `hardware-and-software`**: `sources.json` cites `tali-and-the-geth_003`, which is not in this section's pack. The geth soul-or-bullet argument it was meant to support traces to the subject notes. **Fixed** (removed from sources).
- Accepted as lore-consistent framing, so not defects: Tali's "drive-core panel I'd scratched it on" (`thomas-answer-me`); Joker's toast folder; EDI's clover "picked on the Presidium"; Tali's bad Pilgrimage meal (kept by an earlier decision, C9).

## Subject
- **S1 `thomas-answer-me`**: the death text is told as his wishes: "He wants a pine box. Plain. And the ashes, the authorized ones, the Alliance form, nothing more … a man who wanted the smallest possible thing." The notes have it the other way round. The text attacks administered death (the pine box as "the only choice left", the controlled speech, the authorized ashes, the bills) and sides with peoples who keep their dead for months under flowers. It was also called "the last one, that he wrote before"; the notes date it to 2180, after his mother died. **Fixed.**
- **S2 `thomas-answer-me`**: "the Commander gave him a reason to get up. After." This puts it straight after Akuze. The notes put it in 2183, on Omega, after six years of drifting apart. **Fixed.**
- **S3 `where-is-the-soft-one`**: the translator gag is the reverse of the notes. The notes have him fixing Wreav's swearing so it stopped coming out polite; the section has his insults coming out as a poem. This was a read-through decision by the user ("reworked as a prank on Wreav"). Left.
