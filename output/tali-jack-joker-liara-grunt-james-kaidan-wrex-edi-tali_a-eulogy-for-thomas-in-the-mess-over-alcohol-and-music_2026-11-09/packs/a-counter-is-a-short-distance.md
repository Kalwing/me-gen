- `summary:prothean` [summary] (Prothean empire cycle Reapers) — The Protheans were an ancient race that vanished roughly 50,000 years ago after building a galaxy-wide empire. After achieving spaceflight, the Protheans discovered ruins of the earlier inusannon and learned mass effect physics, gaining a centuries-long secret advantage. Facing a hostile machine intelligence, they decided all organic life must unite under their rule; resisting species were conquered and assimilated, coming to call themselves "Prothean." United, they held off the machines in the Metacon War. They observed and cultivated primitive species including asari, hanar, humans, quarians, salarians and turians, intending eventually to offer them the "choice" of joining the empire.

The Reapers arrived around 48,000 BCE, entering through the Citadel, decapitating the government, disrupting the relay network, and using Citadel census data to hunt every Prothean. Some Protheans built a Crucible from designs left by prior civilizations, but indoctrinated builders sabotaged it. Survivors persisted in stasis. The Protheans held evolution, the "Cosmic Imperative," as the universe's driving force. Notable Protheans included Javik, avatar of vengeance, and the guides Janiri and Lucen of asari myth.
