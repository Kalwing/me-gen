They reproduced via spores that could lie dormant for millennia and survive deep space and atmospheric re-entry, so maw spores appeared on many worlds, spread by earlier generations of space travelers, possibly predating the asari.

They lived alone in large underground nests, growing over 30 meters above ground with a body nearly twice that size beneath the surface; an adult's body never fully left the ground, with only the head and tentacles erupting to attack. They fed through an unusual photosynthesis using absorbed solar radiation plus minerals and ores consumed from the ground, favoring bodies with little or no atmosphere.
