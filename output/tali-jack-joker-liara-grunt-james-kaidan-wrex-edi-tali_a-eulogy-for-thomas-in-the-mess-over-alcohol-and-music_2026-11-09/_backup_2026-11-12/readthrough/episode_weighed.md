## Pondered

- Realism: mostly good. She admits she never knew where Shepard found him, and she forgets the "something so stupid". She announced her own running order three times (C1); fixed.
- Emotions: found: amusement, spite, anger (the bench, Raan), tenderness, grief she swallows, and a flash of protectiveness toward Joker. Missing but plausible: nothing major. Believable and varied.
- Lore vs. brief: drive-core alerts serve the brief ("handyman first", which she owns). The Flotilla-as-neighborhood passage serves, because his question is in it. Pilgrimage kids serve, and they pay off with Raan. Raan, the shelf, the corner, the trial, the locker and the bench all serve. None is cut.
