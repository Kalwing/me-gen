I am going to make mine shorter anyway, and not to be clever. Shorter is simply what the arithmetic gives me, Tali, and you of all people should respect an honest number over a flattering one. You get to keep a list the length of a life you are living at the speed everyone around you is living it — your Fleet, your father, your own hands on a Pilgrimage gift that turned out to matter. I get to keep a list the length of a life that is going to keep going, on its own, long after the people in it have stopped. Asari do not so much remember as accumulate, and accumulation is a colder verb than I would like it to be tonight. You asked for stranger. You are getting it.

Here is the first thing, then. Somewhere in a war I have already told you too much about, I put my mind against Shepard's — properly, the way my mother would have called an act of trust, not a diagnostic — to make sense of a vision that would otherwise have unmade him from the inside. What is left of that is not memory the way your memory works. It does not fade evenly; it sits in me the way strata sit in a dig site, out of order, surfacing when something above it is disturbed, and I have learned not to go looking for it on purpose. Our people use the same joining as a farewell, when there is time for one. There was not, with him. I have thought, more than once, that I got it backward — the goodbye arrived before I understood it would need to serve as one.

The second thing is smaller and costs more to say. I am going to watch you go, Tali. Not tonight, I hope not for a very long time, but I have done the sums the way I do every other kind, and I already know the shape of the years I will have left when yours are finished — Garrus's, Wrex's, everyone currently making that racket in my memory of a mess hall I have not eaten in for a decade. Matriarchs are supposed to carry a community's memory forward, sages dispensing the wisdom of centuries, and I used to think that sounded almost dignified. Nobody mentions that the dignity is optional and the carrying is not. I will be old by any measure your people or mine recognize, telling some very young asari about a rock on Rannoch and a quarian who made me sit through the load-tolerance joke for the eighth consecutive year, and she will have no way of knowing you, only my version of you, worn as smooth by then as everything else I keep. That is the honest answer under the strange one. Not a list of good things. Custody, held past the point where holding stops being memory and starts being something closer to a religion I never signed up to practice.

And Shepard. Yes. You are not getting a longer version of that one, and you do not need it — you already know what I am leaving folded shut, the same way I know the shape of what you are not going to ask. He is the reason I know how to sit still at all. I told you earlier tonight that I did not know how, and it was not quite true — I have been sitting still with the two of you for hours now, the first time in longer than I want to admit that I have managed it without a report running in the back of my skull. That is his, whatever else of him is or is not mine to claim.

The sky has gone from the heat-sink orange you warned me about to something nearer the color under a struck match, and then, while neither of us was watching for it, to nothing much at all — the grey-blue Tikkun leaves behind instead of stars, since the dust past the Veil never quite lets a sky finish going dark. Somewhere below us the geth are almost certainly repairing something that did not need repairing, and somewhere further off the fleet that stayed up is doing whatever a fleet does at rest, which I have never found interesting enough to watch through anyone's satellite. Legion's name is on that wall too, smaller weld, you told me, and I am glad, and I will not make either of us do that arithmetic again tonight. The peace held. That will have to be the whole sentence.

We should get up eventually. Neither of us is going to. You still have not told me whether the cold up here actually bothers you or whether you are simply enjoying watching me pretend it does not bother me, and I find I do not especially want to know yet, or move, or do anything at all except this — sitting on your rock, on your planet, with nothing left on either list that needs saying out loud before the day is properly finished.
