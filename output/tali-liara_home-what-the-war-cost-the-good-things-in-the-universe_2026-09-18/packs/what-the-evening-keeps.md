## Form
**reminiscing** — Two old friends going back over the campaigns over beers. Warm, unhurried, no case to make — just "remember when." Sections are adventures recalled, and the narration keeps losing the thread onto opinions about lore, culture and how things work, then circling back. Runs everything against the Shepard it remembers — this persona's choices, temper and habits are the yardstick the stories and the digressions get measured by.

This run's note: Two old friends over drinks, except the beer is on a cliff on Rannoch and the war is the Migrant Fleet's and the Council races' shared one. Tali and Liara alternate turns strictly, 6 each; no voice holds two in a row, since this is exactly the kind of unhurried back-and-forth the form wants. Each turn's first promise is its reply to the one before — Liara picks up Tali's line about the air, Tali corrects Liara's secondhand version of the trial, and so on — and both keep losing the thread onto lore (geth cognition, Thessia, admiralty politics, asari mourning custom) before drifting back to the cliff. The two women never knew Shepard chose Synthesis, and the section list is built so neither has to name it: they talk about what changed after the Crucible fired, not why.

It orbited an older orange star about 90% the mass of Sol and half as luminous, but because it formed closer to its star and had slightly less ocean coverage it was arid by Earth standards, with photosynthetic life concentrated around rivers and oceans and large deserts between. The importance of plant life and shade in ancient quarian culture is reflected in the planet's name, which translates as "walled garden." Reaching Rannoch in Mass Effect 3 required the mission Priority: Perseus Veil.

Almost three hundred years before the games, the quarians were driven from Rannoch by the geth, synthetic servants who gained sapience and rebelled against their creators. Rannoch became largely uninhabited, though the geth acted as caretakers, repairing the planet's ecology, restoring ancient structures, and cultivating farmland. Thousands of geth space stations watched over the planet, and the geth armada lurked among them awaiting a chance to counterattack. Rannoch had no insect life, so its pollinating plants evolved to rely on animals for propagation; this flora-fauna symbiosis is blamed for the quarians' weakened immune systems, which made colonizing other worlds extremely difficult after their exile, making reclamation of Rannoch a cultural and physiological necessity.

Native lifeforms included the qorach, a carnivorous bighorn-sheep analogue whose belly was always coated in blue-green fern pollen and seed casings, whose blind newborn was called a ke'sed; the lemek worm, a planarian analogue covered in metallic rose-colored scales that could regrow from pieces; and the fa'yin, an edible nut dating to the era of the first proto-quarian beasts. Rannoch was the setting for the missions Priority: Rannoch, Rannoch: Admiral Koris, and Rannoch: Geth Fighter Squadrons.

- `summary:asari` [summary] (asari commando and matriarch lifespan) — The asari, native to the planet Thessia, were often considered the most influential and respected sentient species in the galaxy, known for elegance, diplomacy, and biotic aptitude. They were among the earliest races to achieve interstellar flight after the Protheans and the first to discover and settle the Citadel, and they were instrumental in proposing and founding the Citadel Council.

A mono-gendered race with a feminine appearance and a roughly thousand-year lifespan, asari could reproduce with a partner of any gender or species through a process of parthenogenesis. During "melding" (the joining or union), an asari attuned her nervous system to her partner's to randomize her offspring's second gene set; offspring were always asari regardless of the partner's species. Melding could also be used purely to share thoughts, as when Liara T'Soni melded with Commander Shepard to interpret the Prothean beacon visions and Shiala uploaded the Cipher into Shepard's mind. The Ardat-Yakshi were asari with a rare genetic defect that destroyed a partner's nervous system during joining, appearing almost exclusively among "purebloods" born of two asari parents, a status considered a grave insult. Asari passed through three life stages: Maiden (from puberty, marked by exploration), Matron (around age 350, a desire to settle and raise children), and Matriarch (around age 700, acting as community sages and councilors).

Asari civilization on Thessia was secretly accelerated by the Protheans, who posed as the goddess Athame with guides Janiri and Lucen, genetically enhanced asari biotics, and left a beacon containing the VI Vendetta in what became the Temple of Athame; this was a closely held state secret. The asari found the Citadel in 580 BCE with the aid of the keepers, and with the salarians founded the Council in 500 BCE. During the 2186 Reaper invasion the asari initially withheld support, but after the Reapers overwhelmed their defenses their government revealed the Prothean beacon to Shepard; Thessia fell, and the asari finally committed forces to the Allied war effort.

Asari culture favored the "long view," centrism, consensus, and cultural domination over conflict. Their government, the Asari Republics, functioned as an inclusive electronic democracy deferring to Matriarchs. Their mainstream religion was siari ("All is one"), alongside worship of Athame. The asari military resembled tribal warrior bands of full-time professionals, favoring commando huntresses skilled in ambush, infiltration, and assassination, and fielding one of the galaxy's most powerful navies, including the dreadnought Destiny Ascension. Some asari became justicars, adherents of an ancient code.
