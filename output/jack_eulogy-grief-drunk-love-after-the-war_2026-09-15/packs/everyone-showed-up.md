After being chased through earlier identity theft attempts (including an ambush at Ryuusei's Sushi Bar and infiltration of the Silver Coast Casino), Shepard pursues the clone into the Citadel Archives. Brooks, an ex-Cerberus agent who had previously assembled the dossiers for the Suicide Mission, has activated the clone as a loose end after becoming disillusioned with Cerberus and the Illusive Man's descent into madness. The clone views itself as the true Shepard—a lone wolf unconstrained by the emotional attachments that define the original's leadership. Shepard's team fights through waves of CAT6 operatives inside the Archives. The clone uses the captured Normandy codes to contact Samantha Traynor, ordering the ship prepped for emergency launch under false authority. However, before escaping, the clone seals Shepard and the squad inside iridium vaults—a trap that appears to succeed as the clone heads toward the docking bays. Shepard's AI assistant Glyph discovers the vault system and frees them, allowing the pursuit to continue into the Normandy Docks.

- `summary:citadel-archives-escape` [summary] (the clone Shepard dying by letting go) — Citadel Archives: Escape was a mission in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, following Citadel: Identity Theft II. Commander Shepard learned that the culprit behind the identity theft was a clone of Shepard, and pursued the clone into the Citadel Archives before it could escape. Shepard, accompanied by chosen squadmates plus Brooks, rode skycars to the archives, where James breached a wall with a bomb; if Wrex was present he rushed in first. The team fought CAT6 mercenaries, and the clone appeared on a catwalk holding Brooks hostage and ordered Shepard's group to surrender.

The clone revealed it had been grown by Cerberus during the Lazarus Project as a source of spare parts should the real Shepard need them, but the Illusive Man abandoned the project after recovering the original Shepard. The clone remained comatose until roughly six months earlier, around the time Shepard was arrested, then learned to behave human through neural implants. It intended to impersonate Shepard by flying the Normandy, contacted Traynor using Shepard's own codes to prepare an emergency departure, and ordered its men to execute Shepard and the squad.

Shepard's crew fought through successive CAT6 units, including Specialists, Snipers, Heavies, and an Atlas mech, aided by covering fire from the unselected squadmates and from Cortez and Brooks. Rewards included 15,000 credits, the M-7 Lancer assault rifle, and several weapon mods.

Jack arrives aboard the Normandy broken: psychologically tortured, conditioned to enjoy killing, then used and discarded by everyone she met after escaping her captors, so she lives on edge waiting for betrayal. She refuses to move on from her past. Her tattoos mark prisons, memorable kills, personal reasons, and some "just because"; her haircut is from the cult she ran with. She wears little clothing deliberately, believing nearly everyone wants to use her for sex, and uses it to read intentions. Kelly Chambers notes she pushes people away yet approaches sex casually and warns Shepard to "think twice" if he wants her respect. Given her childhood, Jack is entirely desensitized to death and crime; Cerberus built a Pavlovian link between narcotic euphoria and killing, so she feels bliss where others feel regret, kills without hesitation ("Every time someone dies and it's not me, my chances of survival go up"), and would kill a hostage as the simpler option. Her "no regrets" stance borders on narcissism and a refusal of accountability; asked whether she would do anything differently, she answers a flat "No."
