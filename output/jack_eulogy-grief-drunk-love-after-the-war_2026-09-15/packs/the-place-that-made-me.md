## Required facts
- (subject-zero-cerberus-teltin-experiments) Jack, birth name Jennifer and later self-styled "Jack," was born in 2161 with unusual biotic potential caused by accidental in-utero element zero exposure. In 2165 Cerberus operatives abducted her as an infant, reportedly from Eden Prime, and told her mother the child had died. She was taken to the Teltin facility on Pragia, in the Dakka system of the Nubian Expanse, where a Cerberus research program sought to maximize human biotic potential regardless of cost. Designated Subject Zero, Jack became the sole focus of the research. Other abducted biotic children were treated as expendable: lethal or risky enhancements were tested on them first, and only beneficial results were applied to her. Scientists tortured her physically and psychologically to study how pain and stress affected biotic ability, kept her behind a soundproofed one-way mirror where she could see and hear the other children but not the reverse, and forced her into gladiatorial fights against them. She was shocked for hesitating and injected with narcotics for killing without hesitation, conditioning her to associate killing with euphoria. The other children came to hate her as the source of their suffering. The abuse made her one of the most powerful human biotics alive while still very young. Eventually the subjects rioted during a transfer, damaging the facility and freeing Jack, who flew into a blind fury and killed nearly everyone present, including a distraught doctor, before stealing a shuttle and escaping into the Dakka system. The Illusive Man later claimed the site had "gone rogue" and ordered it shut down; clean-up crews found the guards and most subjects dead, gave the surviving children amnesic treatments, and delivered them to the Alliance as "slaver attack" survivors. A passing ship that picked up Jack used her and sold her into slavery, beginning a decade of piracy, gang membership, and imprisonment that ended aboard the Blue Suns prison ship Purgatory. The Teltin facility's records and Jack's own memories are uncovered in 2185 during her loyalty mission, Jack: Subject Zero.

Referred to as Subject Zero, Jack was the core of the project, with every successful biotic enhancement applied to her. She was tortured frequently, both psychologically and physically, in various experiments to test how pain and distress affected her biotic abilities. As she grew in both age and hatred for her captors, her powers eventually advanced to such a level that she became a serious threat to the staff, easily killing one by blasting him into a wall. - `summary:jack` [summary] (Jack blowing up the facility with Shepard) — Jack, also known as Subject Zero and Jacqueline Nought (birth name Jennifer), was a notorious human criminal and possibly the most powerful human biotic alive. Born in 2161, she was abducted by Cerberus in 2165, reportedly from Eden Prime, after accidental in-utero element zero exposure gave her biotic potential; her mother was told she had died. She became the central subject of Cerberus' experiment at the Teltin facility on Pragia, which sought to maximize human biotic potential regardless of cost. Every successful enhancement was applied to her; other biotic children were used as expendable test subjects and frequently killed. She was tortured physically and psychologically, kept behind a soundproofed one-way mirror, and forced into gladiatorial fights against the other children, being shocked for hesitating and injected with narcotics for killing, conditioning her to associate killing with euphoria. The other children came to hate her as the source of their suffering.

During a transfer, the subjects rioted, freeing Jack, who flew into a fury and killed nearly everyone in the facility before stealing a shuttle and escaping into the Dakka system. The ship that picked her up used her and sold her as a slave. The Illusive Man shut Teltin down shortly after, later claiming it had "gone rogue." Jack took the name Jack, reacting violently to "Subject Zero," and lived a violent life of piracy, gang membership, vandalism (crashing a space station into a hanar moon), and a stint in a cult (from which she kept her shaved head). In Mass Effect: Foundation she assaulted a Cerberus facility, killed Doctor Angersol, defied the Illusive Man, tried to free biotic student trainees, fought Kai Leng and Rasa, and was knocked out and captured by Blue Suns.

Jack was eventually imprisoned on the Blue Suns prison ship Purgatory, where she was assaulted and raped by guards and prisoners and then murdered every attacker; the Warden kept her in cryostasis until Shepard's team bought her freedom in 2185. Rendered a borderline psychopath but still haunted by her past, she joined Shepard in exchange for her Teltin files, then asked Shepard to help her destroy the abandoned facility with a bomb planted in her old cell (the mission Jack: Subject Zero). There the team encountered Aresh, a fellow test subject trying to restore Teltin; Shepard could stop Jack from killing him. If Miranda's loyalty mission was done, Shepard had to mediate a fight between Jack and Miranda over Cerberus' blame; siding with Miranda cost Jack's loyalty and ended any romance.

Jack was a romance option for a male Shepard. Casual sex ended communication; a slower approach revealed her past, including the partner Murtock who died to save her, leaving her survivor's guilt, and culminated in a tender, emotional love scene. Mordin Solus offered Shepard biotics safety advice. Rejecting her as a "lost cause" made her hostile.

By 2186 (if she survived the Suicide Mission), Jack had grown her hair into a ponytail with shaved sides, matured, and taken a teaching post at Grissom Academy training combat biotics, considering her students the family she never had. During Cerberus' raid to kidnap the students (Grissom Academy: Emergency Evacuation), Jack defended them alongside Shepard and Kahlee Sanders, punching Shepard on reunion for reasons depending on prior choices. She disciplined Ensign Rodriguez and Jason Prangley and led the students in support of Shepard's squad. If Shepard did not arrive in time, Jack appeared as an indoctrinated Phantom at Cronos Station and had to be killed. If romanced, she could rekindle or end the relationship at Purgatory Bar. In the final battle she could be contacted by comm; in a surviving-galaxy ending she is seen gazing at the stars, visiting her students' graves, or alive with her students, depending on choices. If killed, her name appears on the Normandy memorial wall as Jacqueline Nought. In the Citadel DLC she fights in the Armax Arena, introduces her biotic varren Eezo from Thessia, and can tattoo a romanced Shepard so his body can be identified. Jack was voiced by Courtenay Taylor and written primarily by Brian Kindregan.
