She became the central subject of Cerberus' experiment at the Teltin facility on Pragia, which sought to maximize human biotic potential regardless of cost. - `summary:normandy-sr2-04` [summary] (Jack's hideout on the engineering deck of the Normandy SR-2) — Part 4 of a breakdown of the Normandy SR-2, covering the Alliance refit. By 2186 Shepard had defected from Cerberus and turned the SR-2 over to the Systems Alliance, which considered the ship its property since it was based on the SSV Normandy SR-1 design. The Alliance studied and modified her, adding new parts, fixing faults in Cerberus's construction, redesigning areas, and swapping Cerberus equipment for Alliance equipment, then recommissioned her in Alliance colors. The refit was only partially complete when the Reaper invasion interrupted it. Most notably, Deck 2 was rearranged to add a war room, meeting room, and QEC, and Deck 5 became the new armory.

Deck 1 was the captain's cabin, with an aquarium, armor locker, private terminal, model ship display case, and bathroom. Deck 2 held the bridge, galaxy map, and a conference room; the armory was moved to Deck 5 per Alliance regulations, and the laboratory became the conference room to make space for the war room, where war assets and galactic readiness could be examined and which contained a quantum entanglement communications room for contacting individuals aiding the war effort. Deck 3, the crew deck, held the medical bay, crew quarters, kitchen, observation deck, AI Core, life support, Liara T'Soni's office (in the former Miranda space, now holding the Shadow Broker terminal), and the main battery. Just outside the elevator was a memorial wall recording crew casualties, including those lost on the derelict Reaper mission, the Suicide Mission, and the 20 crew lost when the SR-1 was destroyed. Deck 4, engineering, held the drive core; the starboard cargo room doubled as Diana Allers's quarters and broadcast studio, the port cargo room served as Javik's quarters, and Engineer Adams worked here, joined by Kenneth Donnelly and Gabriella Daniels if pardons were issued. Deck 5, the shuttle bay, held the armory, cargo hold, two Kodiak shuttles, a procurement terminal, weapon and armor benches, and a small gym; James Vega and Lieutenant Steve Cortez spent their time here.

Built by Cerberus to be larger and more powerful than the original, the SR-2 ended up nearly double the SR-1's size and mass, more heavily armed and armored, with an even larger Tantalus core housed in a sphere holding element zero. With her speed, maneuverability, Javelin torpedoes, GARDIAN lasers, Thanix cannon, kinetic barriers, deflective armor, stronger shielding, and EDI coordinating combat functions, the Normandy was still touted as the fastest, most advanced, and most iconic vessel of the Alliance fleet. She led the charge of the united Citadel races in the battle for Earth at the close of the Reaper War; her whereabouts afterward remain unknown.
