## Required facts
- (suicide-mission) After acquiring a Reaper IFF and repelling a Collector boarding action that carried off the Normandy's non-combat crew, Commander Shepard took the Normandy SR-2 through the Omega 4 Relay to the Collector Base, a station on the accretion disk of a black hole in the galactic core. The ship survived the Collectors' Oculi and security systems, destroyed an intercepting Collector Cruiser, and crash-landed on the base. The assault ran in three segments. In "Infiltration," Shepard sent a tech specialist (ideally a loyal Legion, Tali, or Kasumi) through a thermal vent while a second fire-team leader (ideally Garrus, Miranda, or Jacob) ran a diversion; a non-ideal or non-loyal choice for either role got the vent specialist killed. In "The Long Walk," the squad reached the pods holding the abducted crew and colonists: whoever occupied the first opened pod was liquefied (Lilith of Horizon if Shepard rushed the mission, Yeoman Kelly Chambers if Shepard had delayed). The whole crew, half, or only Dr. Chakwas survived depending on how many missions Shepard ran after the abduction (`iff_delay`); assigning a squadmate to escort the survivors back to the ship saved the crew but pulled that squadmate from the finale, while sending no escort doomed the crew. The squad then advanced under a biotic barrier held by a biotic specialist (ideally a loyal Samara, Jack, or Morinth); a weak choice meant the rear-guard squadmate was carried off by Seeker Swarms in a fixed death order. A non-ideal second fire-team leader was shot dead on reaching the door. In "Final Battle," Shepard picked a squad and left the rest to hold the line; if that holding force's collective defense value was too low, members died in a fixed order starting with the non-loyal (Mordin first if present). Shepard destroyed the four feeding tubes of a Human-Reaper larva built from thousands of liquefied humans, dropping it into the chasm. The Illusive Man then asked Shepard to preserve the base with a radiation pulse instead of destroying it (`collector_base`): destroying it is the Paragon option, preserving it Renegade. The reactivated Human-Reaper was destroyed a second time, and the Normandy outran the base's end. If Shepard alone survived, Joker failed to pull the Commander aboard and Shepard fell to their death. Afterward Joker handed Shepard Reaper schematics as a fleet of Reapers was shown advancing on the Milky Way.

- (suicide-mission) Per `collector_base`: the base was destroyed (radiation-and-explosion, Paragon) or preserved intact via a radiation pulse handed to Cerberus (Renegade). Destroying it left the Illusive Man furious; preserving it left him pleased.
- (suicide-mission) In Mass Effect 3 the choice yields a War Asset from the salvaged Human-Reaper: a Reaper Brain (Military Strength 110, favors Control) if preserved, or a Reaper Heart (Military Strength 100, favors Destroy) if destroyed; Cerberus built the remains into Cronos Station regardless.

- `summary:collector-base` [summary] (the suicide mission against the Collector base) — The Collector Base was a massive space station on the accretion disk of a black hole in the galactic core, reachable only through the Omega 4 Relay and, Commander Shepard speculated, shielded by Reaper technology. It was surrounded by the Tartarus Debris Field, a graveyard of ships destroyed attempting the relay, patrolled by hidden Oculi, and kept at least one Collector Cruiser docked. It housed countless Collectors, Seeker Swarms, husks, Abominations, and Scions, along with the Collector General, who was directly controlled by the Reaper Harbinger. After infiltrating and escaping a Collector Cruiser, Shepard pinpointed the base near the Milky Way's core and obtained a Reaper IFF to cross the relay safely. During the suicide mission the Normandy SR-2 survived the Collectors' security systems, destroyed an intercepting cruiser, and crash-landed on the base. Shepard split the squad into teams to fight through Collector troops, husks, and Seeker Swarms, found the kidnapped Normandy crew stored in pods among thousands of humans, and learned the Collectors were breaking victims down into an organic paste piped to another part of the base. At the core Shepard found a Human-Reaper larva built from thousands of abducted humans and seemingly destroyed it by shattering its feeding tubes.

Shepard then opened a terminal to overload the base's reactor, but the Illusive Man asked instead for a radiation pulse that would kill the Collectors while leaving the technology intact for Cerberus to use against the Reapers. Depending on Shepard's response, Garrus, Legion, Zaeed, Grunt, and Mordin argued to preserve the base, while Tali, Kasumi, Thane, Jacob, and Jack objected to keeping it, and Samara and Miranda were neutral. The Human-Reaper climbed back out and was destroyed again before Shepard escaped as the countdown expired, with Harbinger vowing to continue its plans. If Shepard destroyed the base the star behind the Illusive Man appeared pure blue; if the base was preserved, deep red. In Mass Effect 3, Cerberus sent teams through the Omega-4 Relay to salvage Reaper technology from the base whether it had been destroyed or preserved, and incorporated the recovered Human-Reaper into Cronos Station, using it as the station's brain if the base was preserved or its heart if it was destroyed. After the Alliance captured Cronos Station, the Human-Reaper's remains became Crucible War Assets. In an unmodified, unimported Mass Effect 3 game the base had been destroyed by Shepard.

- `summary:collector-base-final-battle` [summary] (the suicide mission against the Collector base) — Collector Base: Final Battle was the climactic mission of Mass Effect 2, triggered after Collector Base: The Long Walk as part of Stop the Collectors: Defeat the Collectors, with no manual saving allowed and autosaves at each stage. Beforehand, Commander Shepard selected a squad to continue on and assigned the remaining team members to hold the line against the Collectors; the game calculated a collective defensive value for that group, and if it was too low, specific squadmates were designated to die in a fixed order beginning with any non-loyal members, with Mordin taking priority if present. As Shepard opened the platform's core to overload the reactor, the Illusive Man interrupted and asked Shepard to instead trigger a radiation pulse that would kill the Collectors but preserve the facility against the Reapers; destroying it was a Paragon action and preserving it a Renegade action. After Shepard's choice, the Human-Reaper reactivated and attacked, and was destroyed by fire on its eyes, chest, and mouth while Collector reinforcements flew in, one possessed by Harbinger. The Normandy then raced to outrun the base's end: a massive explosion if destroyed, or a blue radiation blast that killed all organic matter but left the base intact if preserved. Closing scenes varied with who survived the suicide mission, with surviving companions pulling Shepard aboard or, if Shepard was alone, Joker failing to save the Commander, who fell to his or her death.
