I said the most embarrassing true thing I've ever said at Thane's service. Everyone else was dignified about it. Garrus wouldn't let anyone call him an assassin: they covered each other in firefights, so partners, and that was the end of it. Bailey stood at the back and said nothing at all, which from Bailey is practically a eulogy. And then I got up and said Thane could remember every moment of his life down to the smallest detail, all his mistakes included, and that I'd have been a nervous wreck with a gift like that. Keelah. In front of his son.

But it's true. Drell memory isn't a trick. It's survival hardware. Rakhana was dry rock, and if you forgot where the water was, you died, so they evolved to forget nothing. And then a smell or a sound sets a memory off by itself, and it's so vivid some of them think they're back in it. Kolyat wrote you that he was scared he'd have one halfway through his speech and remember something inappropriate in front of everyone. I thought that was the most drell thing I'd ever heard.

Imagine me with that. Every weld I botched on the Rayya. Haestrom, second by second. The trial. I'd never get out of bed. Thane carried every kill he ever made, and he still spent the end of his life on the Citadel keeping his son from becoming what he'd been. I can't make that add up. I've tried.

And the hanar pulled three hundred and seventy-five thousand drell off a dying world and put them down on an ocean, and the ocean is what killed him. Kepral's. The lungs just give up in the damp. Rescued into the wrong air. Quarians know how that goes.

He was already dying when he went after Kai Leng. Dropped on him to save the salarian councilor and took the blade straight through the middle, and the bosh'tet got off the Citadel anyway. You were at the hospital for the end. Kolyat read the prayer to Kalahira out of a book, because he'd been spending his time with the priests, and the prayer wasn't even for Thane. He'd already asked forgiveness for himself. *Wash the sins from this one*, and he meant you. And at Cronos you ran Leng through and told him exactly who that was for. Good.

After Thessia you found me at the wall. Garrus and I had just been arguing on the comm about which of us should go to Liara, and I'd just got my homeworld back and she'd just lost hers, so it couldn't be me. I didn't go there to be found, I just... it's right outside the crew-deck elevator, I walk past it forty times a day. It starts with Jenkins, from Eden Prime. First name, first day. Pressly, who didn't like aliens, and then wrote in his datapad that he'd die for any of us, whatever world. You brought that back from Alchera with the other names from the first ship. Ashley. I knew her a few months. She didn't trust aliens, and she read old Earth poetry, which I only understood later, watching you read yours. She stayed with the bomb on Virmire so you could get to Kaidan. He walks past her name every day. I've seen him not look.

Mordin I only heard about. You rang for a thresher maw — you, of all people, after Akuze — and Kalros dragged a Reaper into the ground. The Shroud, though. The salarians built it, the turians used it to spread the genophage, and then STG sabotaged it so nobody could ever spread a cure, and set the whole thing in a shrine to the biggest maw alive so nobody would come looking. A lockout you can only clear by hand, at the top, while the tower comes apart? Whoever designed that — ugh. He went up anyway. *Had to be me. Someone else might have gotten it wrong.*

And Thane, newest. Garrus stood in front of the wall after the coup until you came to stand with him.

Here's the thing, Shepard. You stand at that wall like it belongs to you. Akuze taught you every name is yours to carry on your own. It isn't. On the Fleet the dead don't belong to the captain, they belong to the ship, to everyone breathing the air they kept clean. Nobody's meant to carry them alone, that's the whole arrangement. So I'm taking half. Jenkins to Pressly are mine. Mordin's mine, he'd have had opinions about my filters. And I'm not drell, I'll forget things, so you have to come back and hold the other half.

Jenkins. Pressly. Ashley. Mordin. Thane. Mm. I've got them.
