There's a monument there now with a whole section for you, and Hackett was going to hold a ceremony as soon as they'd cleared the maws. Cleared them. As if you can.

And then you told me yourself, in that voice. The report voice. "Thresher maws, bad luck, I got out." And then a joke, straight after, so nobody gets time to look at you. I know what the joke is for, Shepard. It goes over the thing like a hull patch goes over a breach. It holds, mostly. It doesn't mean there's no breach.

I'm not going to lecture you. I'm just saying I can see it. Some part of you thinks Akuze made a mistake, and you've been charging in first ever since to give it another chance to fix it. That's all. I've said it. Now I'll talk about worms, because that's easier.

Keelah, the worms. I looked them up too. The spores can lie dormant for ages and survive vacuum and re-entry, so there's one on half the worlds nobody's charted, like somebody seeded the galaxy and forgot. Toombs, though. You told me about Toombs on the SR-1, and I remember because it was the first time I saw you not joke. The other one who came out of Akuze. That's what I want you to hear. You didn't steal your life from those fifty. You gave one back.
