## Required facts
- (tali-treason-trial-alarei) In 2185, Commander Shepard travels to the quarian Migrant Fleet to investigate charges of treason leveled against Tali'Zorah vas Neema. Tali is accused of sending operational geth components to her father, Admiral Rael'Zorah, which were allegedly used to breach a quarian laboratory ship. However, the truth is more complicated: Tali sent only non-operational geth parts at her father's request, believing he was experimenting with weapons. Her father did not inform her of his true intentions—to discover a viable way to hack geth and potentially retake Rannoch. Despite Tali's innocence, her father's secret research project spiraled out of control. The quarian Admiralty Board treats the trial as a "trial of arms": Tali and Shepard must successfully retake the laboratory ship Alarei or face execution. Even if they fail, the Admirals promise Tali will be pardoned posthumously. The situation becomes more politically charged because the quarian government is deeply divided over whether to pursue open war with the geth for Rannoch or to search for new colony worlds. Tali's trial becomes a proxy for this larger debate, though the personal cost to Tali is largely ignored by the leadership. During the assault on the Alarei, Shepard and Tali discover the horrifying truth: Rael'Zorah has been killed, apparently by the geth he was experimenting on. Upon retaking the ship, Tali faces a critical choice: she can ask Shepard to reveal the evidence of her father's research to clear his name, or she can request Shepard withhold the truth to protect his legacy. If the evidence is revealed, Tali is pardoned but becomes angry at Shepard, learning that the Flotilla is now torn between those demanding immediate war with the geth and those seeking peace. If the evidence is hidden, Tali becomes deeply grateful, though the Fleet remains unaware of Rael'Zorah's true work until it is eventually discovered through geth data recovered by Xen during the Reaper War.
