- `summary:normandy-sr-2` [summary] (captain's cabin Normandy SR-2) — The Normandy SR-2 was the starship serving as Commander Shepard's base of operations after the SSV Normandy SR-1 was destroyed. It entered service in 2185, built by Cerberus at great and secret expense in the Voyager Cluster on the orders of the Illusive Man for the revived Shepard's team to stop the Collector abductions of human colonists; it served as headquarters of the Lazarus Cell. Based closely on the SR-1's layout, it was almost twice the mass of the original, added civilian-grade comforts (leather chairs, a kitchen), retained the SR-1's stealth characteristics, but could not land on every planet, requiring shore parties to use a Kodiak shuttle.

The ship had five decks: Deck 1, the Captain's Cabin ("the loft"), with a fish tank, model ship case, space hamster, and souvenir displays; Deck 2, the Combat Information Center, with bridge, galaxy map, armory, research/tech labs, and a communications room with a quantum entanglement array for real-time holographic contact with the Illusive Man; Deck 3, the crew's quarters, with the XO's office, mess hall run by Mess Sergeant Gardner, medical bay run by Dr. Karin Chakwas, the AI core, life support, main battery, and observation lounges; Deck 4, engineering, housing the oversized Tantalus drive core (three times the SR-1's, vertically oriented); and Deck 5, the hangar for the Kodiak shuttle and M-44 Hammerhead.

EDI (Enhanced Defense Intelligence) was the ship's AI, functioning as electronic-warfare defense with behavioral blocks preventing direct interface with ship systems, and serving as the Illusive Man's eyes and ears. She was designed in part from Sovereign's remains, making her partly Reaper-based. Armament included Javelin disruptor torpedoes and an upgradeable Turian-designed Thanix cannon (a miniature of Sovereign's weapon); defenses included kinetic barriers, cyclonic barrier technology multicore shielding, Silaris ablative armor, and GARDIAN lasers. Weapon, armor, and shield upgrades, plus the Reaper IFF recovered from the Derelict Reaper at Mnemosyne, determined crew survival in the final mission; the IFF also let the Collectors locate and board the ship.

By 2186 Shepard had defected from Cerberus and turned the ship over to the Systems Alliance, which refitted it, stripped Cerberus equipment, and recommissioned it as the SSV Normandy SR-2 in Alliance colors. Intended as Admiral Anderson's command center, it instead rescued Shepard from the Reaper invasion of Earth and became the mobile base for the war effort and the Crucible mission. In the final battle for Earth the Normandy deployed Shepard's team, then was caught in the Crucible's energy wave mid-jump and crash-landed on an unknown world; the severity of the crash and crew survival depended on galactic readiness.

- `summary:homeward-sol` [summary] (Sol system combined fleets gathering) — Homeward Sol was a human-centric grassroots movement opposed to human expansion beyond the Sol system. Its members were xenophobes and Earth-centrists, likened to the Tenth Street Reds but without the illegal drug trade. - `summary:sol` [summary] (Sol system combined fleets gathering) — Sol is the home star system of humanity and the Systems Alliance. In Mass Effect it is accessible after reaching level 20. In Mass Effect 2 most of its planets can be mined by the Normandy SR-2, the exceptions being Earth and Mars, though none of the bodies rate better than "Good" for minerals.

The system was previously cut off from the galactic mass relay network because the Charon Relay orbiting Pluto had been encased in ice; its discovery and activation allowed humanity's expansion into the galaxy. During their height 50,000 years ago, the Prothean Empire maintained an outpost on Mars and studied the primitive inhabitants of Earth.

Sol contains nine planets recognized for game purposes: Mercury (first), Venus (second), Earth (third), Mars (fourth), Jupiter (fifth), Saturn (sixth), Uranus (seventh), Neptune (eighth), and Pluto (ninth), along with Luna, a moon of Earth. Associated missions and assignments include Prologue: Earth and Priority: Earth, UNC: Rogue VI on Luna, and Priority: Mars. In Mass Effect and Mass Effect 2, Sol is one of the largest systems in the game, with nine planets where most systems have four or five. Only the Asteroid Belt is depicted in-game, not the Kuiper Belt. The in-game positions of the planets in Mass Effect 2 correspond to a date of 26 January 2185, 175 years after the game's North American release date.
- `summary:tamayo-point` [summary] (Sol system combined fleets gathering) — Tamayo Point was a space station in the Sol system designated as a tourist jump-off point to the wider galaxy, with extensive commercial amenities catering to human and alien visitors. It possibly took its name from the Cuban cosmonaut Arnaldo Tamayo Méndez.

In Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation, Tamayo Point was Cora Harper's first waystation on returning to Sol after four years of military service with the asari. She was almost immediately targeted by xenophobes following an unflattering ambush interview by Khalisah al-Jilani. Her friend Ygara Menoris was also on the station, ostensibly bound for Illium, but turned out to have ulterior motives. After Ygara's betrayal, Cora returned to Tamayo Point and used the station's direct line to the system comm buoy to relay her report to Alec Ryder; because the line was not fully secure, Ryder spoke in hypotheticals whenever the subject touched on illegal topics such as artificial intelligence.

The station had medical facilities on its higher levels equipped to treat damage from metabolic overdrive. Cora was brought there again after overexertion on Quiet Eddy harmed her body, spending weeks in a coma while Homeward Sol protesters who recognized her from al-Jilani's interview gathered at the hospital, requiring armed guards at her door. When she was discharged, Tamayo Point authorities cleared protesters around her shuttle after her implant AI SAM-E issued a possible bomb threat.
