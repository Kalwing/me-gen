## Born Wrong, Then Stolen

First one starts before I was born. They always do. Somebody makes a call in a boardroom and it lands on a kid twenty years later, and by then nobody in the fucking room remembers signing it.

You want to know how the galaxy makes a biotic. Not the recruitment-vid version, the real one. It's element zero. Dust-form eezo, breathed in by somebody carrying a baby, and if the dust settles right it grows little nodules all through the kid's nervous system, and those nodules push a mass effect field when the brain lights them up. That's it. That's the whole magic trick. And it barely ever fucking works — maybe one exposed kid in ten comes out able to do anything with it. The other nine get tumors. Get born wrong. Get born not breathing. People on the Citadel think we read minds, Shepard. We don't read shit. We got poisoned in the womb and lived.

Yandoa's the one everybody knows now. 2170. Cerberus wanted more biotic children to study, and the cheapest fucking way to grow a crop is to poison a colony and wait nine months. You never have to meet the kids. You just read the numbers later.

Now me. Born 2161. Nobody aimed a freighter at my mother — mine was just an accident, bad luck and eezo where it had no business being. I came out with something nobody had a word for yet. ## How the Galaxy Makes a Biotic

## Going Back to Blow It Up

You shifted. I felt it. You're not sleeping for shit, so you can listen instead.
