Kaidan tells it the way it happened to him, so I'm not guessing. Bunch of guys in suits show up at your door after school. Next thing you know you're on Jump Zero. The outfit doing the knocking was Conatix Industries — implant shop, Alliance money underneath it. They started out chasing accidents, kids whose mothers stood downwind of the wrong crash. Then they ran short of accidents and got suspiciously fucking quick at finding new ones around '63. Yandoa was the loud version — you got that one from me already. There were quieter ones.

They called the program BAaT. The kids called it Brain Camp. Stood it up in 2160 on Gagarin Station, out past Pluto, far enough out to pull it off the extranet, a few hundred teenagers gone dark. They cut the kids from their families on purpose. Told the parents their own children were dangerous, so there'd be nobody left to write home to. Then the bastards opened their skulls.

L2 implants. First generation, wetware socketed into the base of a growing kid's neck by people who did not understand the hardware they were installing. Wiring before understanding. Kids got migraines that put them flat on the deck. Some got worse. Some snapped. Some fucking died. Kaidan got the headaches, and he still gets them — you've watched him go grey and sit down mid-sentence, a twenty-year-old injury he's hauling, guilt and all, into your war. The L3 came in 2170: safer, weaker. The L2 kids kept their damage and got to go beg a Citadel subcommittee for reparations. You still hear people call dropping the L2 a mistake — all that raw ceiling, thrown away. Easy to say when it's not your skull the shit's wired into. Some of the left-behind kids went militant; some drifted into communes that curdled into cults. I ran with one. You know where the haircut's from.

The man they put in charge of the room was a turian. Conatix wouldn't ask the Citadel for help — didn't want Earth looking weak — so they hired mercenaries off the books, First Contact War veterans. Commander Vyrnnus introduced himself to one boy by telling him he'd been at the helm of the dreadnought that killed the kid's father. He used hunger and thirst as teaching aids, the sadistic fuck. In 2168 a girl named Rahna got tired and reached for a glass of water with her hand instead of her mind, and he broke her arm for it. Kaidan loved that girl. He hit Vyrnnus with everything he had — the turian already had a combat knife out — and it landed harder than he meant it to and snapped the bastard's neck.

The Alliance didn't apologize. They shut BAaT down overnight and sealed the goddamn records so nobody could point a finger back at them. Scattered the kids, most of them already wrecked. Rahna came out more afraid of Kaidan than she'd ever been of Vyrnnus, and never spoke to him again. Kaidan went back to the Alliance five years later on his own terms and swore off biotic instruction for good. Conatix folded. The men who signed the checks retired somewhere warm.

Gagarin they kept. Jump Zero — the name's off the failed faster-than-light jumps they ran out there before the Mars ruins turned the station into a monument to wasted fucking money. After the kids, it became an AI lab — the Alliance's first real AI, Eliza, woke up out there. Nine thousand people live there now. In 2184 a crew called Totenkopf hit it with a prion weapon — EHE, brain-rot — and the Alliance redacted the body count down to nothing. Never confirmed where the agent came from — an asteroid called Israfil, or a lab on site. Jump Zero's always been where they run the shit they don't want witnessed.

Then they built the clean version. Grissom Academy, commissioned 2176, up over Elysium — the Ascension Project, they called the biotic wing. L3s only, families welcome, ethics classes.
