You want a story, Shepard? This one's older than the genophage, older than the Rebellions. The salarians tell it like they did us a favor. Every clan on Tuchanka tells it different, and none of us were there for it. You don't need to have been somewhere to know the shape of it. You just need to know salarians.

Before any of this, Tuchanka wasn't the dustbowl you walked through with me. It was worse. We'd fought a war on our own world, krogan on krogan, nukes back and forth until the sky went the color of a bad bruise and stayed that way. Nuclear winter. Radiation you could taste. That's what the salarians found when they came looking for muscle — a species that had already burned its own house down and was still snarling at anything that moved.

They needed us bad. This was during the Rachni Wars — some salarian survey team woke a relay they had no business waking, and on the other side was the rachni. A swarm with a mind at the bottom of it, queens buried in worlds that'd kill anything else just to breathe there. For the better part of a century — I don't remember the number — the Council races got pushed back and pushed back, because you can't out-talk something that doesn't negotiate.

So the salarians came and found us. Handed out gifts. Built the Shroud, meant to scrub the sky clean enough to breathe. Gave us medicine, ships, technology, moved whole krogan populations onto a world that didn't try to kill you every hour of the day. Here's what they leave out of the vids: they did it because a krogan breeds like nothing else in this galaxy and can stand on ground that'd cook anything else down to the bone. We weren't uplifted. We were bought.

We drove the rachni back planet by planet, hit the queens where they lived because nobody else could survive long enough to try. Cornered the last of them, burned their cities, dropped the nests into sinkholes deep enough to lose a ship in. Three hundred years after that relay woke, the Council declared them extinct and banned anybody from waking a dormant relay without—

Funny thing about the rachni, though. Not as extinct as the vids say. There was a live queen in a lab on Noveria and I told you to put her down. Said it flat. Said it again when you didn't move. You let her go anyway. I've had three years to chew on that and I'll get to what I decided, but not while I'm still on the Rebellions.

Here's the other joke, and it's not a good one — we came home good at exactly one thing, and it wasn't peace. A species that breeds like we do, trained for a century to win, doesn't just go back to farming rocks. Different story, for when I'm less tired — the one where that same gratitude ends up needing a genophage to fix what it started.

The salarians taught us to kill the rachni because they needed us. Then we spent generations being called monsters for doing exactly what they asked. You see the pattern yet, Shepard? Salarians make a problem. Krogan solve it. Then everybody gets surprised when the krogan are still standing afterward.
