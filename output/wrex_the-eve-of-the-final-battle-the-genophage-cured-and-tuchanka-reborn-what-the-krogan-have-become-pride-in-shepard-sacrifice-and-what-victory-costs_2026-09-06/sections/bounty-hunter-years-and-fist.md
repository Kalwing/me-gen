Hnh. Merc years — you asked, or somebody did.

You want to know what a krogan does when there's no war left to fight and no clan worth bleeding for? He gets hired. That's the whole trade. A thousand years is a long time to fill with somebody else's grudges, and I filled most of mine on contract. Half the corpses I made after the Rebellions, I couldn't tell you the client's name if you put a pistol on my head. Credits moved, somebody died, I moved on. There's no glory in it, whatever the vids tell you. Some jobs paid. Some jobs the client got cute and decided a dead krogan doesn't need his fee, and found out different.

Port Hanshan, on Noveria — I did a lot of freelance work there. Cold as death, snowing every time I set foot on the place, but good money, because on Noveria the fastest way up the ladder is putting the guy above you in the ground, and somebody always wants that arranged.

A batarian broker paid me half up front for a job on — hn, doesn't matter. Never finished it. Somebody finished it for me.

An asari, Aleena. Commando, then merc, same as half of us end up. Somebody paid me to put her down over a salarian station out past the frontier, and we chased each other through it for days, and the whole place went up when its core cracked, and I figured that was that. Wasn't. Turned up years later just to tell me "better luck next time," grinning the whole time. Good hunter, that one.

Turian by the name of Tonn Actus — pirate, collector, liked trophies from the Rebellions. Had krogan armor hanging on his wall like it was game he'd shot himself. Had my family's armor, generations old, and it took me a long time to find where he'd hidden it and take it back. That one I did for free.

Fist had a good thing going at Chora's Den, on the Citadel, until he got greedy — took Saren's credits over the Shadow Broker's, the kind of arithmetic that gets a man killed twice. Broker hires the best krogan he can find to make the point, and that was me. C-Sec hauled me in before I got through the door, accused me of threatening the man, which, fine, I had. Standoff with a few officers who wanted no part of what came next if they pushed it, and they backed off, same as they always do. And that's when this human walks up. New Spectre, I find out — first one of his kind, which meant nothing to me yet, and a lot since. Told him what I was there for. He told me Saren's the reason he's on the Citadel too. Same target, different reasons, close enough to call it the same fight.

I didn't know it yet, but that was the whole rest of my life starting right there in a C-Sec hallway. Just a krogan with a contract and a human with a hunch, both after the same man.

Hnh — that's Fist, and that's Noveria and the rest of the merc years, near enough.
