Older than my father's stump of a story, and I wasn't standing in this one either. Got it from him, from scarred old bastards who all told it different, and from Council histories, which tell it clean because they're written by whoever won. Where mine and theirs don't match, believe mine.

Starts with a gift, same as the rachni story does, because that's how it always starts with us. Rachni worlds, plus a few clean ones besides, handed over for burning out queens nest by nest. Nobody on that Council worked out what a species that breeds the way we do does with good ground and no rachni left to eat its children. Four hundred years and we'd filled every world they gave us and started taking the neighbors'. Didn't ask first. Moved in, and if somebody objected we told them to try and stop us. That's on us, I'll say it straight. We took worlds because nobody had told us no in a long time, and it felt good.

Somebody finally did. Asari colony, Lusia, good soil, exactly what we go looking for. Council told our man there, Overlord Kredak, to pack it up. Kredak stood in the Council chamber and told them to come take it back themselves. They did. They'd quietly trained salarian operators and asari huntresses into something they called Spectres, and the first thing those Spectres did was burn our infrastructure down without declaring a war first — networks gone, refineries gone, command posts rammed by freighters nobody was flying. We shrugged it off, and for a while it looked like they'd started a fight they couldn't finish that way.

Turians next.

So they found somebody new. Turians. Barely past their first handshake with the Council, and here's an invitation to fight a species three centuries deep into eating relay space. They said yes fast, built a command post on some rock, and came at us hard enough that we threw rocks. Three turian colonies, we put asteroids into. Nothing left standing, nothing left br—

Hn. Genophage. Salarians had it sitting in a drawer since before the shooting started, built and never touched. Turians asked, salarians handed it over. Every krogan pregnancy in the galaxy started turning into a corpse instead of a child, and none of us knew it yet.

There was a female warlord near the end, Shiagur. Every male worth breeding wants to be standing near a strong fertile female, so when word got out she was still fighting, she drew half of what was left of our army into one band. Turian peacekeepers cornered her at Canrum and killed her there. Last real push we had.

I'm awake. Don't start.

What was left of her people hunted down the turians who did it, one at a time, took years, didn't win the war back. Just made somebody pay for it personally. Can't win the war, still collect on the debt.

After Canrum we surrendered. Some kept fighting anyway, because peace looked like extinction too. Rest scattered — pirates, guns for hire, dying somewhere nobody counted, same road my father walked and handed straight to me. And the Council put a chair up at its own table for winning that war, gave it to the turians. Third full seat, forever, for cleaning up a mess the Council itself built by handing us worlds we didn't earn either. Still laugh at that part. Everyone keeps the receipt for what we did to their colonies. Nobody keeps the one for what they built us into first.
