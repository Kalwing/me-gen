Who cooks now?

You said ask, Liara, so I asked. Everybody here talks around it. His recipes are still taped inside that cabinet door with his stains on them, and after the next jump somebody opens that door and stands there. Not me. I tried a pan once and the pan lost.

He let me in the kitchen. I guarded it like a clan holding. Nobody got the bread, nobody got the pie, and Wrex, you tried twice at the Commander's place, and you remember how both ended. Thomas thought that was the best thing he'd ever seen. He gave me a knife and told me to chop in a straight line, which is harder than killing, and when I did it he said it was good like it mattered. Okeer put the knowing in me. Thomas gave my hands something to do that wasn't a fight, then asked what I wanted. Nobody had asked. I came out of a tank as a weapon, and he talked to me like a person until I was one.

I still chop in a straight line. Meat too. I did it this morning and didn't notice until the board was done.

He talked a lot. He wanted the Rite told three times. I always told it to the maw. He asked which part I liked best. I said Uvenk, the one who called me factory-made, and what I did to him. Hah. He laughed so hard he sat on the floor.

Then I asked him a thing I never asked anyone. Was I real, or just Okeer's idea of me. Every word in my head, put there. He didn't joke. He said he'd asked himself the same thing. That he'd built himself out of what people around him needed, piece by piece, since he was small, and some nights he couldn't find what was under it. I said: Okeer made me. You made you, for everyone else. Same tank. He didn't joke for a whole minute. I counted. It was the longest he ever went.

He was vegan. I told him a meal with no body in it is a rumor of a meal. Still true. But he made things only for me, thick and salted and heavy, and never said they were for me. I ate all of it. Nobody tell the clan.

Strong. Here is my rating. He was not a fighter. He could not have killed a varren pup. And he stood in front of Wrex, told him he was wrong, and did not move. I thought, this one dies today. He didn't. Wrex listened. That is the strongest thing I have seen a soft thing do. When I told him so, he started talking about dessert.

His memory was bad. Everybody knows. He forgot my decanting day. I didn't know I had one. Three weeks late a crate came to Tuchanka, to Aralakh. Inside, a small plastic lizard from Earth. A dinosaur, he wrote. Big teeth. Little arms. A note: "Late. Sorry. It looked like you." My warriors laughed until I hit one. It is on my armor rack. I didn't know I had a decanting day until he forgot it. Now I have one, and he isn't here for it.

Utukku, after. Our scouts dead in the tunnels, me walking out alone and broken. Thomas came to Huerta with food I wasn't allowed. Bad week for him too, I could smell it. He came anyway.

The tank put strength in me, and hunger, and anger. He put in the rest: dancing badly, laughing loud, being bad at something with people around.

I gave him one thing. A word. On the Citadel, by the door, at the end of the party, I said it to him. Brother. Krogan don't give it out. He cried. Humans leak. Then he said thank you. Only that, and he kept it. He didn't give it back or make a joke. He kept it. Of all the things anyone gave him, I think that one he kept.

Krogan found him at the Conduit a day later. Suicides, we leave for the scavengers. He wasn't that. He didn't run. A body that didn't run, you carry, so they carried him. If I were shaman, he'd get the pyre.

He showed me a way. He fought with me and for me. He drank with me. Brother. That is the word. I said it once to his face. Now I say it twice.

So. Who cooks? Vega, you eat the most. You decide.
