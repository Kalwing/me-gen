- `summary:biotics` [summary] (how biotic amps and L2 implants work) — Biotics was the ability of some lifeforms to create mass effect fields using element zero nodules embedded in body tissues, accessed and augmented through bio-amps. Biotic individuals could knock enemies over at range, lift them into the air, generate gravitational vortices to tear apart obstacles or enemies, or create protective barriers. Abilities fell into three categories: telekinesis (mass-lowering fields), kinetic fields (mass-raising fields), and spatial distortion (shifting fields that rip apart objects). Powers were triggered by "physical mnemonics," physical gestures that fired neurons in sequence.

All asari were naturally biotic from birth and required no implants or special training. Biotics of other species were individuals exposed to dust-form element zero in utero who developed eezo nodules throughout their nervous systems. Exposure rarely produced biotics; most exposed fetuses were unaffected, and others developed brain tumors or other complications. In humans, only about one in ten eezo-exposed infants developed usable talent, and in rare cases talents emerged in young adulthood through additional exposure. Identified biotics were typically fitted with a surgically implanted amplifier around puberty and then underwent long biofeedback training.

The first human biotics were born after starship accidents in the 2150s dispersed element zero over populated areas; the first known was female, dubbed "Subject White." Conatix Industries was founded to track exposed individuals and develop implants, and in 2160 established the BAaT programme on Jump Zero, which was shut down after the turian instructor Commander Vyrnnus was killed by a student. Human biotics were classed by implant model: L1, the notorious L2 (introduced 2167, powerful but often unstable and medically damaging), L3 (2170, stable but weaker), retrofit and experimental variants, L4 used in the Ascension Project, and L5x and L5n for Adepts and Vanguards by 2185. Dr. Ellen Ryder created the first human biotic implants. Human biotics faced widespread prejudice, while the Alliance military eagerly recruited them, granting them a 4,500-kilocalorie daily ration.

Among aliens, biotics varied in rarity: common and powerful in asari, rare and prized in salarians, uncommon and distrusted in turians (relegated to Cabals), few but extremely powerful in krogan (Battlemasters such as Skarr and Wrex), and very rare in quarians. Batarians, drell (Thane Krios), Collectors, Protheans (Javik), volus, and vorcha also produced biotics. Non-sapient biotic creatures existed on eezo-rich worlds like Thessia and Eingana, including Jack's biotic varren Eezo. Biotics were exceedingly rare or absent in the Andromeda galaxy.

- `biotics_002` [page] (element zero exposure and biotic training) — All asari are naturally biotic from birth, though not all choose to develop their abilities. Biotics of other species are individuals who were exposed to dust\-form element zero, or colloquially, "eezo", in utero and subsequently developed eezo nodules throughout their nervous systems. These nodules can generate mass effect fields when energized by electrical impulses from the brain. Eezo exposure is by no means guaranteed to result in biotic ability. On the contrary, most fetuses that are exposed are not affected at all. Others will develop brain tumors or other horrific physical complications. In humans, only about one in ten eezo\-exposed infants will develop biotic talents strong and stable enough to merit training, and these abilities are not always permanent. In extremely rare cases, humans who were exposed in utero but did not manifest biotic talents as children can develop them during young adulthood through additional exposure. Once identified as having biotic ability, a person might be outfitted with a surgically implanted amplifier around the age of puberty. This amplifier is designed to make their talents strong enough to be useful, and some races have experimented with biotic drugs, such as red sand, to stimulate power instead. Once the implant is installed, its user must then develop conscious control over their nervous system, which is a long, slow, difficult ordeal (except for the asari, who naturally possess a degree of voluntary control). Biofeedback therapy is often used to assist the progression of this process, and once they fulfil their training, a biotic can control their emission of dark energy to generate mass effect fields that move objects, generate protective barriers, or restrain enemies. Biotic abilities are activated using a technique called "physical mnemonics", in which the biotic uses a physical gesture to cause neurons to fire in a certain sequence, sending an electrical charge through their eezo nodules and creating the desired effect. A biotic may enhance certain aspects of his or her biotic abilities by installing implant upgrades called bio\-amps. Biotic abilities fall into three general categories: Telekinesis, the use of mass\-lowering fields to lift or hurl objects; Kinetic Fields, mass\-raising fields to immobilize objects (in combat, usually enemy projectiles or enemies themselves); and Spatial Distortion, the creation of shifting mass\-effect fields which rip apart any objects caught within them.

In manufacturing, low-mass fields permit evenly-blended alloys, while high-mass compaction creates dense, sturdy construction materials. Many biotics can generate and control mass effect fields biologically, which requires intensive training and cybernetic implants but can produce powerful offensive or defensive effects. Some biotics are not strong enough for offensive use, but all biotics are sensitive to the presence of mass effect fields.

Using mass effect fields creates a static electrical charge. In starship drive cores this charge must be grounded at regular intervals, either by touching a planet's surface or interacting with its geomagnetic field, to prevent the electricity discharging into the hull and causing catastrophic damage. For biotics, the charge manifests as an occasional static shock when they touch metal or other people.
