- `summary:steven-hackett` [summary] (Hackett's pre-battle address) — Admiral Steven Hackett was a top-ranking Systems Alliance Navy officer and commanding officer of the Fifth Fleet, based at Arcturus Station. Born in Buenos Aires in 2134, he was placed in the Advanced Training Academy for Juveniles after his mother's death in 2146, enlisted in 2152, was commissioned as a second lieutenant in 2156, and fought in the First Contact War; his rise from enlisted man to admiral became an Alliance legend.

In Mass Effect, Hackett was one of the three officers, alongside Ambassador Donnel Udina and Captain David Anderson, who recommended Commander Shepard as the first human Spectre. He frequently contacted Shepard with assignments vital to Alliance security and was generally more supportive of Shepard's decisions than Udina. During the Battle of the Citadel he commanded the Alliance reinforcements and, on Shepard's judgment, either defended or abandoned the Destiny Ascension; afterward he was promoted to head of the Alliance military. In Mass Effect: Paragon Lost he commended Lieutenant James Vega's Delta Squad on Fehl Prime and later decorated and promoted Vega.

In Mass Effect 2 Hackett helped plan a memorial for the presumed-dead Shepard, transmitted the location of the Normandy SR-1 crash site so Shepard could place a monument and recover crew dog tags, and in the Arrival DLC asked Shepard to rescue his friend Dr. Amanda Kenson from batarian custody, later debriefing Shepard after the destruction of the Alpha Relay and warning of consequences to face on Earth.

By Mass Effect 3 Hackett had become Fleet Admiral. Facing the Reaper invasion he sacrificed the Second Fleet to save the Third and Fifth, presided over "the worst military defeat in human history," and directed Shepard to Mars. He oversaw construction of Project Crucible at an undisclosed location, ordered the Illusive Man killed, mobilized the Fifth Fleet against Cerberus at Cronos Station, and delivered a pre-battle address to the assembled fleets before the assault on Earth. He commanded Shield Fleet escorting the Crucible to the Citadel; after its activation he ordered the fleets to withdraw and, in surviving endings, narrated the aftermath and the galaxy's rebuilding.

- `summary:alliance-navy` [summary] (Hackett's pre-battle address) — The Alliance Navy was the space combat branch of the Systems Alliance military, with the Alliance Marines a specialized ground branch within it. Charged with protecting human colonies, stations, and mass relays, it concentrated its strength in fleets stationed at relay nexuses so it could respond quickly to attacks. It held over 200 vessels and several dreadnoughts at the time of the First Contact War in 2157, and by the Reaper War in 2186 it fielded nine dreadnoughts across at least eight fleets, each subdivided into flotillas of a cruiser and four to six frigates. Fighter carriers, dreadnought-sized ships armed mainly with fighters, were a human innovation. Its size and skill drew Citadel Council interest and helped make humanity a candidate Council species. After the Battle of the Citadel in 2183, Admiral Steven Hackett was made head of the Navy.

The First Fleet, largest in the Navy and based at Arcturus Station, guarded the Charon Relay; during the Reaper invasion it was cut in half, and Admiral Ines Lindholm sacrificed a tenth of the survivors to let the rest escape. The Second Fleet, under Admiral Kastanie Drescher, liberated Shanxi in 2157 and was later sacrificed by Hackett to let the Third and Fifth Fleets flee Arcturus. The Third Fleet was led by Admiral Nitesh Singh aboard the SSV Logan. The Fourth Fleet, stationed at Earth, was wiped out. The Fifth (Arcturus) Fleet, Hackett's command, frequently tasked Commander Shepard with missions and received the Palladium Star after the Battle of the Citadel; its 63rd Scout Flotilla under Rear Admiral Mikhailovich was to have received the Normandy. The Sixth and Seventh Fleets, near Terra Nova and Eden Prime, were bypassed entirely by the Reapers; the Sixth later rallied after Shepard's visit to Horizon. The Eighth Fleet was ambushed near Ontarom.

Ship naming conventions assigned dreadnoughts mountain names, cruisers city names, frigates battle names, and carriers names of great human figures; warships carried the "SSV" prefix. Dreadnought classes in service were the Everest and Kilimanjaro. The 2nd Frontier Division garrisoned Eden Prime and was nearly destroyed by Saren Arterius's geth attack in 2183. During the Fall of Earth the Navy suffered its worst defeat, losing Arcturus Station and 45,000 people. The Alliance Naval Exploration Flotilla, damaged by a 2185 mining-fraud scandal, later aided Crucible construction on Tyr.

- `summary:ssv-normandy` [summary] (the Normandy captain's cabin) — The SSV Normandy SR-1 (Systems Alliance Space Vehicle Normandy Stealth Reconnaissance-1) was a prototype "deep scout" frigate, first of the Normandy class, co-developed by the Systems Alliance and the Turian Hierarchy with Citadel Council sponsorship. Commissioned in 2183 and named for the 1944 Battle of Normandy, she was optimized for solo reconnaissance deep in unstable regions. She was initially commanded by Alliance officer Elli Zander, who was removed after clashing with turian chief engineer Octavio Tatum over the drive core's limits; David Anderson then took command, and shortly after handed the ship to Commander Shepard when Shepard became the first human Spectre.

The Normandy was built on three decks: a Command Deck with bridge, airlock, CIC and comm room; a habitation deck with quarters, mess, medical bay and captain's cabin; and an engineering and storage level holding the M35 Mako and the drive core. Her defining feature was the IES (internal emission sink) stealth system, which stored waste heat in lithium heat sinks, allowing silent running for hours; the system did not work at FTL and could be defeated by visual scan. It was powered by the experimental Tantalus Drive Core, roughly twice the size of a normal core and costing about 120 billion credits. Like all frigates she carried GARDIAN lasers, kinetic barriers, a spinal mass accelerator, and could mount Javelin disruptor torpedoes. Key crew included Joker (helmsman), Navigator Charles Pressly, Dr. Karin Chakwas, Engineer Greg Adams, Kaidan Alenko, Ashley Williams, and later Tali'Zorah nar Rayya, Garrus Vakarian and Urdnot Wrex.

During her shakedown the Normandy went to Eden Prime to recover a Prothean Beacon, which was destroyed during a geth attack. Rear Admiral Mikhailovich later inspected and criticized her as over-designed. When the Council refused to pursue Saren Arterius, Udina locked the ship down; with Anderson's help Shepard stole her and flew to Ilos. At the Battle of the Citadel the Normandy led the assault that destroyed Sovereign. In 2183 an unidentified starship (later revealed as a Collector cruiser) ambushed and destroyed the Normandy near Alchera, killing over twenty crew including Pressly; Shepard died saving Joker. Cerberus later built the larger Normandy SR-2 from the SR-1's design.
