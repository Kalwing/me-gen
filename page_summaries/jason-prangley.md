Ensign Jason Prangley was a biotic student at Jon Grissom Academy. He arrived at Grissom at age six, a shy child who barely spoke, and was known for constantly reading and studying battles, with some peers calling him a brownnoser. He proved capable of leadership when circumstances demanded it.

During the 2186 Reaper invasion, Cerberus attacked Grissom Academy to forcibly conscript its gifted students. If Jack survived the Suicide Mission, Prangley was among the students she protected; he was nearly killed by Atlas fire before his teacher intervened. When Commander Shepard arrived, Jason aided the firefight, providing biotic support strikes so that he and his teacher could retreat to higher ground. Afterward Jack derided his biotic fields as "weak," and he noticeably improved in later battles.

If Jack did not survive the Suicide Mission, Prangley interacted with Shepard in her place during the evacuation. Shepard found him stepping up to save Ensign Rodriguez from Atlas fire. Jason reported that their commanding officer had been killed, leaving him assuming command, and spoke on the students' behalf that they needed time to recharge after the strenuous exertion of biotics. In this scenario, Prangley later died protecting Rodriguez again from Cerberus troops just short of the hijacked escape shuttles. The survivors eulogized him aboard the shuttles, and Shepard invoked his memory to motivate the other students, either channeling their anger at his loss or holding up his sacrifice as an example to follow. Datapads left by Cerberus troops around the Academy indicate Prangley was specifically targeted as a prized acquisition.
