An Artificial Intelligence (AI) was a self-aware computing system capable of learning and independent decision making. Creating a conscious AI required adaptive code, a slow and expensive education, and a specialized quantum computer called a "blue box." An AI could not be transmitted across a communication channel or network; without its blue box it was merely data files, and loading those files into a new blue box produced a new personality because of hardware and runtime variation.

The geth served as a cautionary tale about rogue AI, and in Citadel space AIs were technically illegal. Advocacy groups argued that an AI was a living, conscious entity deserving the same rights as organics, and that the term "artificial" was institutionalized racism, preferring "synthetic." Tali noted that synthetic races had no need to trade with organics, which was why the geth isolated themselves beyond the Perseus Veil. An AI told Shepard that from a synthetic viewpoint "all organics must destroy or control synthetic life forms."

In Mass Effect: Revelation, the Alliance facility at Sidon was found in 2165 to have conducted illegal AI research, drawing heavy fines and censure. By 2183 only four corporations were licensed by the Citadel to develop AI for research, one being Synthetic Insights, Ltd. A documented event in the Citadel Archives, dated 1896 CE (the year the Geth War ended and the quarians were exiled), recorded three armed C-Sec officers gunning down three unarmed mechs housing the last of the Citadel's AIs after their legal appeal went unheard.

The page also covered later-era material: in the Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation novel, Alec Ryder's AI kernel was repeatedly stolen and formed the basis of at least four systems, including SAM, SAM-E, the Home Away "emulator," and the homicidal hybrid Medea; SAM-E failed to adapt to its host Cora Harper and was reintegrated into SAM. Notable AIs listed included the Catalyst, EDI, Eva Coré, Legion, Harbinger, Sovereign, the Alliance-created "Eliza," the rogue asari AI "Tallaris," the Cerberus AI "UDI," and SAM. Cut content for Mass Effect 2 considered a large deactivated 75-year-old robot AI squadmate nicknamed "the Tank."
