The Final Hours of Mass Effect 3 is a documentary work by journalist Geoff Keighley, published in March 2012 around the release of Mass Effect 3. Built from exclusive interviews and anecdotes with BioWare, it traces the studio's origins and the conception of the first Mass Effect through to the end of Mass Effect 3's development.

It was originally sold as an interactive document with embedded links, music and video, features that were later deprecated; the original version was pulled from sale, leaving only a truncated Kindle edition. After the release of Mass Effect Legendary Edition, Electronic Arts made the original version available as a free downloadable bonus requiring Adobe Air, though some functionality remains broken.

This page serves as the table of contents. The work is divided into a Welcome section and twelve chapters: Chapter 1: The Beginning of the End, Chapter 2: The Little Tinkerer, Chapter 3: The Doctors, Chapter 4: Codename SFX, Chapter 5: The Possibility Space, Chapter 6: That Which Is True, Chapter 7: The Two-Front Team Assault, Chapter 8: Devastatingly Impactful Choices, Chapter 9: The Illusive Date, Chapter 10: The Darkest Hours, Chapter 11: The Perfect Illusion, and Chapter 12: The Letter on the Door. It also links to the official website and the EA downloadable extras.
