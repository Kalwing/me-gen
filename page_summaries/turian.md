The turians were a militaristic, disciplined species from the planet Palaven in the Trebia system, and the third race to join the Citadel Council. Their civilization spanned fifteen thousand years and was known to the Protheans, who considered them primitive. The first turian ship to use a relay was the THV Ravuna. Around the time the asari and salarians formed the Council, the turians were fighting the Unification War among their far-flung colonies, run by local chieftains who had drifted from the Hierarchy; colonists adopted facial markings to distinguish themselves, a practice that survived into the modern era. The Hierarchy stayed neutral until fewer than a dozen factions remained, then intervened and forced reunification.

During the Krogan Rebellions the Council made first contact with the turians and enlisted their war machine against the krogan. The turians suffered devastating counterattacks, losing three worlds to asteroid bombardment and fighting the bloodiest battle in their history at Digeris, before deploying the salarian-developed genophage, subduing most krogan by 800 CE. In gratitude they received a full Council seat by 900 CE and took over the peacekeeping role left by the krogan. In 2157 a turian fleet fired on human explorers activating an uncharted relay, triggering the Relay 314 Incident (First Contact War); turians besieged and briefly occupied Shanxi before the Council imposed peace and ordered reparations, leaving lasting mistrust. During the 2186 Reaper War the Reapers destroyed Taetrus and assaulted Palaven and Menae; a joint turian-krogan counterattack achieved the "Miracle at Palaven" by smuggling warp bombs aboard Reaper ships, before Primarch Victus withdrew the fleet to join the assault on Earth.

Biologically, turians stood over six feet tall with avian, raptor-like features, taloned two-fingered hands, mandibles, and a reflective metallic carapace containing thulium that shielded them from Palaven's high solar radiation. They were viviparous, had dark blue blood, distinctively flanging voices, and were built on dextro-amino acids, sharing that trait only with quarians; levo food was at best non-nutritious and at worst fatally allergenic. Females lacked the swept-back cranial crests of males. Their lifespan was comparable to a human's.
