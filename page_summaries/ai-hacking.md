This page documented AI Hacking, a tech power in Mass Effect 2 that temporarily turned a synthetic enemy against its former allies, optionally granting the hacked target a temporary shield and, at rank 4, evolving into Improved AI Hacking (stronger, longer shield) or Area AI Hacking (affecting multiple synthetics in a radius). A single point in the power could hack any hackable synthetic, including Geth Primes, but only worked once the target's defenses had been stripped. It was available to the Engineer and Infiltrator classes and to squadmates Tali'Zorah vas Neema and Legion. The page is otherwise composed of power-rank statistics and gameplay mechanics notes rather than narrative lore.
