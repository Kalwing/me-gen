Citadel: Expose Saren began when Shepard first arrived at the Citadel after Prologue: Find the Beacon. Ambassador Udina was arguing with the Citadel Council, demanding action against the Spectre Saren Arterius; the turian councilor deferred everything to a hearing. Udina directed Shepard and Anderson to the Citadel Tower. Optional exploration let Shepard meet the elcor Xeltan (starting Citadel: Xeltan's Complaint if General Septimus Oraka had been met during Sha'ira's Asari Consort assignment), Executor Pallin at C-Sec, Private Fredricks, and Nelyna at the Consort's Chambers. Outside the Council Chambers, Shepard met C-Sec officer Garrus Vakarian, who was investigating Saren.

At the hearing, Udina and Anderson raised the geth attack on Eden Prime, the eyewitness Powell, and Shepard's beacon vision. Saren, who called the dead Nihlus a friend, dismissed the vision as dreams, and the asari, salarian, and turian councilors ruled there was insufficient evidence, leaving Saren a Spectre. Udina said bringing Anderson had been a mistake, hinting at history between the two men and Saren, and pointed Shepard toward C-Sec informant Harkin, while Anderson suggested Barla Von. This granted the Citadel: Garrus mission, plus Citadel: Shadow Broker if Barla Von was named.

Investigation revealed Dr. Chloe Michel had referred a quarian carrying evidence against Saren to Fist, a Shadow Broker agent who had defected to Saren; Garrus was protecting Michel, and Urdnot Wrex had been hired to kill Fist. Shepard fought through Fist's mercenaries at Chora's Den, including a Krogan Bouncer, Thugs, Warehouse Workers, and turrets, and confronted Fist, who admitted he had sent the quarian to meet assassins rather than the Shadow Broker. Fist could be killed or spared, though Wrex killed him regardless. Shepard then had four minutes to reach the back alley and kill the assassins, saving the quarian, Tali'Zorah nar Rayya.

In Udina's office, Tali played an audio recording of Saren admitting to the Eden Prime attack, with a second unidentified voice referencing the Reapers, which the geth revered and which had wiped out the Protheans 50,000 years earlier. Tali could join the squad. The Council reviewed the evidence, stripped Saren of his Spectre status, and, after Udina pressed for a Citadel Fleet, instead named Shepard the first human Spectre, unlocking Spectre Training and higher Charm and Intimidate. Anderson was relieved of command of the SSV Normandy, which passed to Shepard. On the way out Shepard could pick up assignments from Admiral Kahoku and Garoth and the Citadel: Homecoming lead from Samesh Bhatia. Consequences carried forward: a spared Fist reappeared on Omega, and failing to recruit Garrus or Wrex altered their later fates, with Wrex potentially dying and Urdnot Wreav seizing Clan Urdnot.
