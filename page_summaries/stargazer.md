The Stargazer was a humanoid entity of indeterminate species and background who narrated the deeds of "The Shepard" to a child companion in the closing scene of Mass Effect 3. Which of two Stargazers appeared depended on Commander Shepard's final choice in the battle for Earth.

If the Crucible was activated, a male Stargazer finished telling the tale, saying it happened long ago and that many details had been lost to time. If the Crucible was not activated and the Reapers continued their cycle, a female Stargazer of an unknown species finished the tale, explaining that her people knew of Shepard's actions and the war through archives left behind by Liara T'Soni; that information had saved the contemporary generation from the threat and was why they lived in peace. In either version the child asked to hear more, and the Stargazer replied that there was time for one more story.

According to trivia, the scene evolved from an early storyboarded Synthesis ending presented by a "Reaper Queen" featuring an asari mother and daughter. The male Stargazer was voiced by former astronaut Buzz Aldrin.
