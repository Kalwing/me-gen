Ethan Jeong was an ExoGeni Corporation representative on Feros and, after the geth attack, the only surviving ExoGeni representative on the planet, desperately trying to protect the company's local interests. He hid with several ExoGeni employees at the Skyway Weigh Station and was the only survivor there who knew about the Thorian beneath Zhu's Hope. Aware of the danger of spore infection, he deliberately kept the survivors from returning to the port, told them Fai Dan's group was probably dead, and discouraged any investigation of Zhu's Hope, only to realize the group was trapped between the geth and the Thorian's thralls, leaving him to wait for an ExoGeni rescue team.

If Commander Shepard found the hiding place before reaching ExoGeni headquarters, Jeong panicked over the secret being exposed and, failing to dissuade Shepard, demanded the Commander not damage corporate property while secretly hoping the geth would kill Shepard. On Shepard's return, after the Commander learned of the Thorian and ExoGeni's role in the Zhu's Hope infection, Jeong was found threatening Juliana Baynham with a pistol; communications had been restored and ExoGeni planned to purge the colony. Shepard could defuse the situation with sufficient persuasion or was forced to shoot Jeong in self-defense. If killed, two guards backed down when Shepard's teammates drew weapons. If Jeong lived, he took leadership of the colony, and if Shepard had raised business opportunities he said ExoGeni would like the proposal.

After the Thorian's destruction, Jeong and the other weigh station survivors appeared at Zhu's Hope, claiming all traces of the Thorian would be erased and congratulating Shepard on surviving. If the colony survived, he promised to honor the deal, and Juliana praised him for negotiating well on the colonists' behalf, with Jeong crediting Shepard for giving Feros a second chance. In the game's internal data he is often called "Hea Jeong."
