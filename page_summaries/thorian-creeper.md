Thorian Creepers were creatures produced by the Thorian and encountered on Feros, appearing as corpse-like humanoids with long claws and no visible eyes. They hibernated by sitting and curling up, becoming invulnerable to attack, until approached or until the Thorian was threatened, then rose at its command to strike with melee attacks or vomit green acid that inflicted heavy toxic damage and ignored shields. Most were found in the Thorian's lair, with a few above ground at Zhu's Hope guarding the wrecked freighter Borealis.

Creepers were also shipped offworld. Commander Shepard could encounter them on Nodacrux, where ExoGeni scientists said they had found a way to turn the creatures into docile labor until the Thorian's death sent them berserk, and Cerberus obtained some for its facility on Binthu. Enemies using the Creeper model also appeared aboard the MSV Fedele and, as "Test Subjects," on the MSV Fedele-related content, though those were not vulnerable to anti-Thorian gas grenades.

The bulk of the page is combat tactics for the original game: Creepers were highly resistant to gunfire but wore no armor, making them extremely vulnerable to melee knockdowns and to biotic forces. An anti-Thorian gas grenade killed one instantly, and destroying a nearby Thorian neural node killed most of the creepers guarding it. They were nearly immune to toxic damage, so toxic ammo was ineffective, while Lift, Throw and Singularity could ragdoll them, crush them against walls, or knock them off ledges to their deaths. Their acid attack, which bypassed shields, was best countered with damage-protection armor upgrades, Immunity, Medical Exoskeletons and medi-gel, and by engaging at mid to long range since the vomit could one-shot on higher difficulties.
