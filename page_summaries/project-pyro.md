Project Pyros were enemies encountered during the Mass Effect 2 assignment Arrival, attacking Commander Shepard throughout Project Base. They first appeared during the Object Rho sequence in up to five waves alongside Project Guards, Elites and Engineers, and later accompanied larger Project forces.

They closed on Shepard's position wielding the M-451 Firestorm flamethrower, whose flames staggered the Commander backward and could be disastrous on higher difficulties. They had no shields on normal difficulty but moderate shielding, slightly stronger than a Project Guard's, on harder ones. Like Blood Pack Pyros and Geth Destroyers they were lethal up close and best kept at range. Shooting the large canister on their backs (once shields were removed) or hitting it with Overload, Incinerate or the Arc Projector caused them to panic and explode; luring them near other enemies before detonation spread splash damage and fire.
