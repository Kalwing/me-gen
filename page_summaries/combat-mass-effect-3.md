Combat (Mass Effect 3) was a game-mechanics overview page for Mass Effect 3's combat and the first Mass Effect game with a multiplayer mode. It covered the faster, more agile movement with unlimited sprinting and combat rolls, expanded cover navigation and swat turns, the return of grenades as a limited power, Power Combos, the Power Wheel and HUD, all classes being able to use any weapon subject to a weight capacity that modifies power recharge speed, two-slot weapon mods, thermal clips with a few self-cooling weapons, headshots and headshot modifiers, expanded melee including heavy melee, per-class melee damage optimization, Grab kills and enemy sync kills and how to survive them, health/armor/shields/barriers, armored plating, the enemy shield gate and the player shield/health gate with attacks that bypass them (including Kai Leng's stab), cover damage reduction, squad selection, the Narrative through Insanity difficulty settings, game options, bugs, and Legendary Edition changes.
