Weyrloc Clanguards were krogan warriors in the service of Clan Weyrloc on Tuchanka, encountered by Commander Shepard during the mission Mordin: Old Blood (Mordin's loyalty mission). They were armed with shotguns that could carry incendiary ammunition and could use the Carnage power. Like all krogan they had high armor and health, charged in melee, and regenerated health rapidly.

In combat Clanguards advanced steadily on Shepard's squad, firing periodically and occasionally charging nearby squadmates but otherwise moving slowly. The recommended tactics were to strip their armor quickly with powers such as Incinerate, Warp or Incendiary Ammo, then use Incinerate, Warp or Reave to suppress their regeneration, or Pull to knock them helpless once unprotected.
