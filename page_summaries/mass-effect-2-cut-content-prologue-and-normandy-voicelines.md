This page collects dialogue cut from Mass Effect 2 related to the prologue missions (Prologue: Awakening, Freedom's Progress) and the Normandy SR-2, recovered from files where the audio remains but the lines were disabled; none of it is canon. Contents include an Illusive Man story-introduction monologue similar to a trailer narration; an alternate tech-powers tutorial and Cerberus reveal during Awakening; an unused cutscene for Miranda's introduction in which she shoots Wilson; alternate Illusive Man lines at Freedom's Progress where he offers a variable number of dossiers and comments on Kaidan/Ashley, Wrex, Tali, Garrus, and Liara; full-dialogue versions of EDI's Normandy room tours (tech lab, observation deck, gunnery control); an Illusive Man briefing introducing Zaeed and Kasumi; a cut Grunt post-loyalty exchange; a cut Mordin-Grunt confrontation with multiple resolutions; and the follow-up Normandy conversations for siding with either.

Representative lines:

Illusive Man, introductory monologue: "Cerberus is the only one willing or capable to protect humanity. But we can't do it alone. We need a leader. Someone who'll stop at nothing... Someone who's faced the Reapers... and won."

Miranda, after shooting Wilson: "My job. Wilson betrayed us all."

Illusive Man, on Liara: "She's on Illium. My sources say that she's working for the Shadow Broker. If so, she can't be trusted."

EDI, on the observation deck windows: "In combat, the observation rooms are sealed and depressurized. Sheets of ablative armor are extended to cover the windows."

EDI, on gunnery control: "The CIC decides who to shoot. Gunnery Control decides how to shoot."

Grunt, confronting Mordin: "Come on, fight back! You'll think the krogan to death but won't raise a gun to my face?"

Mordin, to Grunt: "Shooting near bulkhead unwise. Limited to questioning your female parentage in a manner known to insult differentiated species. Factually inaccurate in your case."

Grunt, on being overruled about Uvenk: "You waver like this, maybe I start thinking someone else should lead."
