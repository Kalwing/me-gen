The Info Drone was a VI aboard the Shadow Broker Base that served as the Shadow Broker's personal assistant, later renamed Glyph. It was a shell for a specialized data-processing VI custom-built to the Shadow Broker's specifications, effectively a secretary. In Mass Effect 2, during Lair of the Shadow Broker, it followed Commander Shepard around the Intel Center, programmed to recognize anyone in the room as the Broker, and delivered reports such as selling the Broker's copper stock before a market crash, acquiring the soul name of the hanar Enkindlers leader, purchases by the turian Councilor's mistress, and video of the duel between General Partinax and Kihilix Tanus on Taetrus.

When Cerberus tracked Liara T'Soni's ship down on Hagalaz, the drone was among the items Liara took with her. Over time she began calling it "Glyph" (95% of the time, by the drone's own account). After the Normandy SR-2's retrofit, Liara moved into Miranda Lawson's old Deck 3 quarters and brought Glyph to coordinate her networks as the new Shadow Broker during the Reaper invasion. It reminded Shepard of intel and research gathered on missions, and assisted Liara constantly: running safe-mode diagnostics during EDI's Mars mech glitches, reporting the self-destruction of the colony Tyvor, tracking agents Detrace, Calamis and others, extending amnesty to ex-Sonax scientists for the Crucible Project, warning krogan away from the dormant Reaper on Tuchanka, and advising caution over placing an agent among the Council's bodyguards after the Cerberus coup. Liara incorporated Glyph into time capsules seeded on multiple planets so a future cycle would learn about the Reapers and the Crucible; if Shepard refuses to activate the Crucible, Glyph is seen delivering Liara's recorded message.

In Mass Effect 3: Citadel, Glyph accompanied Liara through the identity-theft plot, buying casino tickets, reporting Archives activity, getting confused by Shepard's clone, lowering enemy morale by reciting survival statistics, and helping free the trapped squad from the iridium vaults. It later helped Shepard organize the aftermath party, donning a holographic bow-tie and taking a group photograph.
