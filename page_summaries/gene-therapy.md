Gene therapy was the insertion of genes into an individual's cells and tissues to alter DNA, used to treat genetic disorders by replacing a defective mutant allele with a functional one via a vector, most commonly genetically altered viruses carrying normal human DNA. Before first contact, human genetic research was advanced enough to "uplift" animal species near the borders of sapience. After humanity joined the Citadel, concerns that such modifications might erode Earth's biodiversity led the Alliance Parliament to pass the Sudham-Wolcott Genetic Heritage Act in 2161, imposing strict controls: modifying natural abilities (such as increasing muscle mass) remained legal, but acquiring new ones (such as digesting cellulose) did not.

By the time of the games, basic genetic flaws could be corrected in utero or shortly after birth; Ashley Williams received gene therapy for a maternal disposition to nearsightedness. Most human governments offered free assessment and correction therapy, nearly eliminating genetic diseases from the human gene pool, and all Alliance soldiers received gene therapy from MarsGene on enrollment, though most therapies took years to reach full effect. Citadel regulations permitted creating life for scientific or medical reasons but forbade new sapient species; a few products slipped past, notably Sirta Foundation's medi-gel. Commander Shepard could discuss Binary Helix's military enhancement packages with Rafael Vargas while posing as a buyer, and could debate gene therapies with an arguing couple on the Presidium.
