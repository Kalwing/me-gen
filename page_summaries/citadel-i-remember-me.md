Citadel: I Remember Me was available only to a Colonist-background Shepard after completing Find Liara T'Soni, Noveria: Matriarch Benezia, or Feros: The Thorian. It began with a call from Lieutenant Girard as Shepard left the Dock 422 elevator. At the docking bay, Girard explained that a traumatized, armed woman had barricaded herself behind shipping containers near the SSV Normandy's berth. She was Talitha, a survivor of the Mindoir colony raid, the same batarian slaver raid that killed Shepard's family, who had escaped captivity. Girard wanted Shepard to talk her into taking a sedative so she could be moved to a psychiatric facility, keeping a sniper on standby only as a last resort.

Shepard conversed with Talitha at a distance across four topics: the batarian raid, her liberation, her parents' fate, and Shepard's own childhood. Progress required stepping forward one at a time; approaching too fast or giving an accusatory or tactless response caused her to shoot herself. Each topic had one valid response that allowed a step forward ("I was lucky," "You were a kid," "You were afraid," and "Deal with it"), and at least one Paragon or Renegade response was needed to calm her enough to be sedated. After three steps, Shepard could persuade or force her to take the sedative, then report back to Girard. Shepard could instead order Girard to have her shot, for Renegade points and less experience. Leaving the Citadel before finishing auto-completed the assignment with no rewards and a note that Girard had handled it himself.

If Talitha was sedated, she later sent Shepard a message titled "Thanks" in Mass Effect 2, saying her doctors, one connected to Shepard's employer, were helping her, that she had been placed in a special school, and asking Shepard to help others being taken as she was. The assignment was written by Chris L'Etoile.
