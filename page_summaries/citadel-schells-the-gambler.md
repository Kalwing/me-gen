Citadel: Schells the Gambler was a Mass Effect assignment acquired after Citadel: Rita's Sister. On entering Flux, Shepard saw a salarian named Schells thrown out by the bouncer for cheating at the Quasar gambling machines. Schells claimed he was not cheating but gathering data to perfect a system for predicting Quasar outcomes, using a device that emitted a signal to tamper with the machines and raise his odds of winning so he could collect more winning results. Flux's owner Doran had accused him of cheating. Schells asked Shepard to collect the data and waited at the entrance until the player agreed.

With the device, the player had three options. Recording the data meant playing Quasar until winning five times, after which the device signaled it had enough; returning it to Schells gave credits and experience but no morality reward. If the player kept playing greedily and reached seven wins, Doran confiscated the device and all winnings and ejected Shepard from the bar, ending the assignment with no reward. Alternatively, the player could give the device to Doran, even after using it, for an experience reward and credits offered to play a few rounds of Quasar on the house, which the player could simply keep. The experience and credit amounts were identical whether the device went to Schells or Doran.

Afterward, Doran could be seen dancing on the club floor, and Shepard could join him; a love interest present in the party would dance with Shepard. The assignment was internally named "Gamblatronomatic." In 2186, an undercover security officer in the Silver Coast Casino referenced Schells' device regardless of the assignment's outcome. Unused voicelines would have made clear Schells was caught cheating, and the assignment resembled a cut sub-plot, Citadel: Cheaters Never Prosper, in which the role was played by someone named Beck.
