Charr was a krogan transport technician on Illium who, unusually for his species, was gentle and romantic rather than a brute, described as a "lover-boy" type. In 2185 he was in a strained relationship with Ereba, an asari merchant at the Illium transport hub who was hesitant to settle down permanently and wondered whether Charr was interested in her only in order to reproduce. To win back her affection, Charr stood near her reciting love poetry. If Commander Shepard convinced Ereba to rekindle the relationship, Charr later brought her to Tuchanka; put off by the ramshackle state of the planet and the xenophobic attitudes of other krogan, Ereba wanted to leave, but Charr persuaded her to stay by saying that Tuchanka's dilapidation "builds character" and that she was under his protection. By 2186 Charr had joined a group of krogan scouts deployed to Utukku to investigate rumored rachni activity. His entire unit was wiped out by the creatures and his body ended up deep within the caves. If Shepard followed up on the missing scouts, Charr's corpse could be found behind webbing amid clusters of mutated rachni eggs, along with a passionate final voice recording he had left for Ereba. Shepard could retrieve the message and deliver it to Ereba on the Citadel; if Charr and Ereba had reconciled in 2185, the recording also contained a poetic reference to their unborn child.
