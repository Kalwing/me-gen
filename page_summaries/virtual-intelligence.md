A Virtual Intelligence (VI) was a sophisticated program designed to make computer systems easier to use. VIs were distinct from artificial intelligences such as the geth: they only assisted users and processed data, and although they appeared intelligent, they were not self-aware, merely the product of clever programming. This greatly reduced, though did not eliminate, the risk of a VI exceeding its intended function or rebelling against its creators. Some VIs carried "personality imprints," basing their behavior, speech, and appearance on specific individuals, though it was illegal to model a VI on a living person. The quarians had been attempting to make their "ancestor VIs" truly intelligent to achieve a kind of virtual immortality when the geth rebellion halted most synthetic-intelligence research.

VIs varied widely by deployment. They handled extranet search queries, served as tour guides like the Citadel VI Avina, and managed lab and database work like Mira on Noveria. Humans and elcor both used VIs extensively in military operations to process status reports and react faster than organics, with elcor VIs instantly selecting among millions of pre-designed combat gambits. Many armor upgrades used VI enhancements to optimize combat performance or dispense medi-gel, and the L4 biotic implant used VI technology to monitor the user's brain waves and adapt in real time.

A VI that deviated from its programming due to software errors or malicious tampering was called a "rogue VI." Such a VI was not self-aware; going rogue was akin to a software malfunction that caused the VI to fail its function or interpret its duties dangerously. Notable incidents included a Systems Alliance VI at a Luna training facility that went rogue after scientists tried to convert it into a controllable AI and triggered the facility's weapons; the VI on Jarrahe Station that engaged a lockdown killing all scientists aboard; and a Cerberus project on Aite where an experimental hybrid VI became a virus that seized a research facility and its living geth test subjects, killing nearly all personnel present.

Notable VIs listed included Avina (Citadel and Nexus information), the ExoGeni VI on Feros, the geth-created Geth VI studying the Reapers, the rogue Alliance VI Hannibal on Luna, the Shadow Broker's Info Drone "Glyph," the Andromeda ark VI "K" (Keelah Si'yah), the last surviving Ancestor VI Liat'Nir, Mira at Peak 15 on Noveria, the Cerberus Overlord VI on Aite, the Shepard VI, the Prothean VI Vendetta on Thessia, the Prothean VI Victory on Eden Prime, and the Prothean VI Vigil on Ilos.
