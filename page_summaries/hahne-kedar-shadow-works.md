The Hahne-Kedar Shadow Works (HKSW) was a subsidiary of the Earth-based manufacturer Hahne-Kedar, drawn entirely from cut content and not considered canon. Founded a decade before the events of Mass Effect, it was described as a place for younger employees to "think outside the box." HKSW supplied weapons and armor to Alliance special forces units, and in keeping with its parent company's philosophy its products were rugged, reliable and required less maintenance than competitors' gear.

It was a "hidden" manufacturer whose equipment was fully functional in the game files but only accessible through the PC console or third-party software. Its weapon lines were the Diamond Back assault rifles, Viper shotguns, Cobra pistols and Python sniper rifles, all obtainable only through console commands and all with higher stats than standard Hahne-Kedar equivalents. Its armor lines were the Light Spectre Armor (for humans, turians and quarians), the Light Janissary Armor (humans and turians), and medium and heavy Spectre/Janissary variants (adding krogan). The quarian version of the Spectre Armor had no model and took on the appearance of the last worn armor. All of this gear could only be acquired through console commands.
