In Mass Effect 2, Commander Shepard's armor could be customized by swapping pieces of the default N7 Armor for parts purchased from merchants or supplied through downloadable content, using the Armor Locker aboard the Normandy SR-2. Besides changing physical appearance, each piece affected Shepard's combat effectiveness, and the player could further alter color, reflectiveness, or pattern. Because money was limited, the page recommended buying selectively and always using vendor discounts; purchasing every armor piece with all discounts cost 54,996 credits, or 66,000 without. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition, formerly DLC-exclusive equipment had to be bought with credits and resources or researched in the Normandy Tech Lab rather than being granted automatically.

Customizable slots were head, chest, shoulders, arms, and legs, each with an N7 default piece and several alternatives. Head options included the N7 Helmet and Breather Helmet, Kuwashii Visor, Death Mask, Umbra Visor, Recon Hood, Sentry Interface, Archon Visor, Capacitor Helmet, and Kestrel Helmet, with effects ranging from bonus health and headshot damage to reduced power recharge time. Chest, shoulder, arm, and leg pieces came from manufacturers such as Kassa Fabrication, Armax Arsenal, Rosenkov Materials, Elanus Risk Control Services, Aldrin Labs, and the Sirta Foundation, granting bonuses to health, shields, power damage, weapon damage, melee damage, or ammo capacity. The Kestrel set pieces, from the Aegis Pack, provided combined multi-stat bonuses.

Full armor sets could also be equipped; these could not have individual pieces swapped but offered unique bonus combinations. Shepard could additionally choose casual outfits worn aboard the Normandy and on discreet missions, including outfits obtained through Kasumi - Stolen Memory. Documented bugs included the Archon Visor conferring no benefit in the original release, fixed in Legendary Edition.
