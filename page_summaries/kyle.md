Major Kyle was a former Alliance officer who served at the brutal battle of Torfan in 2178. If Shepard had the Ruthless background, Kyle had been Shepard's commanding officer during the Torfan raid. Though an exemplary officer, he could not cope with the loss of so many men under his command and was given an honourable discharge after a psychiatric evaluation found him unfit for duty.

Kyle later resurfaced as leader of a biotic commune on Presrop, in the Century system of the Hawking Eta cluster. The commune was effectively a cult whose members called him "Father Kyle." According to Admiral Hackett, Kyle, who was never a biotic himself, was probably seen as a protector, and through "protecting" the biotics he assuaged his guilt over Torfan. The commune broadcast extranet transmissions, one intercepted by C-Sec, inviting biotics to join them. Kyle grew more militant, preaching that the Alliance was responsible for all biotics' problems, and the Alliance became concerned.

Two Alliance negotiators sent to convince Kyle to accept treatment for post-traumatic stress disorder were judged a threat and killed by the cultists. Hackett then sent Commander Shepard to resolve the situation by force or persuasion. Shepard could negotiate an audience or break into the compound; a Ruthless Shepard was received willingly and addressed as "The Butcher of Torfan." Face to face, Shepard could open fire or convince Kyle to surrender peacefully, in which case he was taken for treatment and his cult disbanded. In Mass Effect 2, if Martin Burns was not saved in 2183, a Citadel news report stated that Kyle was again trying to form an all-biotic community, since reparations were never given to L2 biotics and they had grown further alienated from galactic society.
