Dalatrass Linron was the leader of the Salarian Union in 2186, described as the "queen bee" of Sur'Kesh's wealthiest bloodline, having narrowly retained leadership of the Linron bloodline in a recent election. During a war summit between salarians, krogan and turians, she was openly hostile to the Clan Urdnot leader (Wrex or Wreav), who demanded a genophage cure in exchange for krogan help holding the Reapers off Palaven. Linron protested that a cured krogan would again wage war on the galaxy. When the Urdnot leader produced video evidence of fertile female krogan from Maelon's experiments held on Sur'Kesh, Linron questioned its authenticity and lied about their location, but was forced under pressure from Primarch Victus and Shepard to reveal it.

Before Tuchanka, Linron tried to cut a secret deal with Shepard: she claimed the Shroud facility needed to disperse any cure had been sabotaged years earlier by salarian operatives, and offered the salarian First Fleet and top scientists for the Crucible in exchange for Shepard keeping the sabotage secret. If Shepard cured the genophage, she withheld salarian support and sent an angry email ("the blood of my people will be on your hands"), though STG strike teams aided the Alliance anyway. If Shepard faked the cure, she sent a pleased email and pledged formal Salarian Union support, with Colonel Vaykom relaying her thanks in London. An Alliance News report implied "Linron" was a bloodline name rather than a personal one. She was voiced by Grey DeLisle.
