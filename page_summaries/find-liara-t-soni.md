Find Liara T'Soni was a Mass Effect main-story mission acquired after Shepard gained Spectre status and command of the Normandy from Anderson. Liara T'Soni, an asari archaeologist renowned for her Prothean expertise and daughter of Matriarch Benezia, was being sought by Saren; her last known location was the Artemis Tau cluster. Anderson advised searching for the world with Prothean ruins, leading to Therum in the Knossos system. The mission was written by Chris L'Etoile.

Therum was a volcanically active world where the Mako could not survive contact with lava. Shepard fought through Geth Armatures dropped by a dropship, optional defense turrets around a refinery outpost, and a heavier push including Geth Destroyers and a Juggernaut, then faced a Geth Colossus on the road to the ruins. Outside the Prothean ruin, a dropship deposited snipers, shock troopers, Geth Hoppers, and an Armature; the entrance stayed locked until nearly all enemies were cleared.

Inside, Shepard descended by elevators and damaged platforms and found Liara floating trapped inside a Prothean security barrier she had accidentally activated while hiding from the geth. She directed Shepard to use a nearby mining laser: entering a code fired the laser to bore a tunnel past the barrier curtains, and a central console raised an elevator to her level. Freeing her triggered a tremor attributed to the mining laser causing a seismic event, and Shepard called the Normandy to the ruins. On the top level, Shepard fought a stand-off against geth and a Krogan Battlemaster before escaping the collapsing ruins as the Normandy hovered above.

Back aboard, Liara relayed her research, including a theory of recurrent galactic extinctions with the Protheans the latest to vanish, connected by Shepard to the Reapers and the Eden Prime vision. She joined the crew, considering the Normandy a safe haven from Saren, and offered her Prothean knowledge for interpreting the vision. If the mission was delayed until after Virmire, Liara instead believed Shepard was a hallucination until physically freed, cringed from the Battlemaster, and reacted angrily during the debrief as the squad's discoveries stripped away her decades of research. Recruiting Liara early unlocked unique interactions on Noveria and romance opportunities.
