In Mass Effect 3, a Turret was a portable weapon emplacement carried and deployed on the battlefield by Cerberus Combat Engineers. Once set down it was stationary but could lock onto any target entering its range, firing a rapid, sustained volley of bullets capable of stripping even strong shields and health in moments. It was protected by heavy shields and armor, and a nearby Combat Engineer could repair armor damage.

Turrets could only fire at one target at a time, rotated slowly toward new targets, suffered a reload cooldown, and always exploded when destroyed, causing heavy area damage and staggering nearby infantry (including allies). They could be distracted by Combat Drones, Sentry Turrets and Decoys, turned against their owners with Sabotage, destroyed during placement by killing or staggering the Engineer, or worn down through cover with piercing mods. Powers such as Overload, Energy Drain and Incinerate stripped their defenses quickly for weapon or Power Combo follow-ups. They had a limited lifespan and eventually self-destructed, and in multiplayer a deployed turret stayed active between waves, so a "wave clear" announcement did not mean the area was safe.
