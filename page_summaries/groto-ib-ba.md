Groto Ib-ba was a batarian mercenary and a member of the Blue Suns in 2165, as depicted in Mass Effect: Revelation. That year the Blue Suns were contracted by Edan Had'dah, a wealthy batarian, to attack and destroy the human research base at Sidon. Groto was not directly involved in the attack but shared equally in the contract fee, since the Blue Suns split payment regardless of participation. His knowledge of Blue Suns activity attracted the attention of Saren Arterius, the Spectre investigating Sidon. When Groto went to a high-class brothel called the Sanctuary intending to hire a human consort and take out his hatred of humans on her, Saren struck. The Spectre brutally tortured Groto until he revealed that a female human had survived the Sidon attack and that Skarr had been sent to find and kill her. Once Saren had the information, he killed Groto by breaking his neck.
