Stasis was a biotic ability appearing in Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3 that trapped an enemy in a mass effect field, freezing the target in place (including in mid-air) and preventing it from acting. This page documented its gameplay behavior across the trilogy; it is distinct from Cryogenic Stasis.

In the first Mass Effect, Stasis was an unlockable talent for the Adept, Sentinel, Asari Scientist and Krogan Battlemaster, available to Shepard through advanced training and to Liara T'Soni. Frozen enemies were normally immune to damage unless the caster had the Bastion specialization's Stasis Specialization. Powerful enemies such as Saren or asari commandos resisted it strongly, and squadmates would only use it on direct order. Talent ranks raised duration from 12.5 to 21 seconds while lowering recharge time.

In Mass Effect 2, Stasis (available to Shepard as advanced training and to Liara) bypassed all defenses but built temporary, fading resistance with repeated use. Rank 4 evolved into Deep Stasis (longer duration) or Enhanced Stasis (slower resistance buildup). A known bug caused a roughly 4x damage bonus to enemies as Stasis wore off, letting players one-shot many foes.

In Mass Effect 3, Stasis had a 12-second recharge and 6-second duration, no effect on armored targets, and broke when the target took major health damage. Rank 6 offered a Bubble evolution that trapped enemies walking into a field, or a Vulnerability evolution increasing damage taken by 50%. Unlike ME2, resistance and immunity became permanent. In multiplayer it was available to the Asari Adept, Asari Vanguard and Volus Adept. In the mobile game Mass Effect Galaxy, Jacob Taylor could use a variant.
