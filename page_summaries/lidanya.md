Matriarch Lidanya was the commanding officer of the Destiny Ascension, the asari dreadnought and flagship of the Citadel Fleet in 2183. During peacetime she engaged with tourists visiting her ship. During Saren Arterius' attack on the Citadel, Lidanya ordered the evacuation of the Council when she realized the station was no longer responding to commands, including the order to close the arms to protect civilians. The evacuation placed the Council aboard the Destiny Ascension, which then sustained heavy damage from the geth fleet and the Reaper Sovereign. If the Alliance Navy reinforcements did not assist the Ascension, it was destroyed, killing Lidanya and everyone aboard. If the Council was sacrificed, Lidanya's dialogue included the line, "This is the Ascension. We are taking heavy damage. GARDIAN defenses are overwhelmed, kinetic barriers are off-line."
