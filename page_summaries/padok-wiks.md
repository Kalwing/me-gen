Padok Wiks was the salarian STG base commander on Sur'Kesh and a scientist researching a cure for the genophage. If Mordin Solus did not survive the Collector attacks, Wiks assumed Mordin's role in the Sur'Kesh and Tuchanka missions of Mass Effect 3.

Wiks's earlier career had involved uplifting lesser species, work he deeply regretted after becoming fascinated by evolution's role in the galaxy. He came to believe every species was valuable and that the salarians had been arrogant to interfere with the destinies of the krogan or others, which motivated him to cure the genophage. If he was Wrex's STG mole, his summit footage came from security cameras rather than Mordin's handheld recording.

When Shepard arrived at the STG base, Wiks defused a standoff after STG sharpshooters trained weapons on the krogan leader following a violent landing, then guided Shepard toward the krogan females. If Mordin was absent, Wiks accompanied Shepard to the last surviving female, and when Cerberus attacked, he forced a subordinate at gunpoint to release the "specimen" despite lockdown protocol and escorted her out. Afterward he remained aboard the Normandy SR-2 to help cure the genophage and secure a krogan-turian alliance, christened the female krogan "Eve", proposed using the Shroud, and developed a friendship with Eve. He described his faith in a higher power he called the Originator and his opposition to species uplift. He once worked with Mordin and had punched him during a drunken argument over evolution, leaving Mordin a black eye and Wiks broken fingers.

During the cure mission Wiks monitored Eve's vital signs and, when the Thresher Maw distracted the Reaper Destroyer, finished the cure in the Shroud, giving his life to spread it. If Shepard instead took Dalatrass Linron's offer to sabotage the cure, Shepard had to shoot Wiks, unless Eve was dead and Wreav led Clan Urdnot, in which case he stood down. A surviving Wiks later emailed Shepard that he had retired as a farmer on a remote colony, intending to take the truth of the sabotaged cure to his grave. BioWare writer John Dombrow named him after colleague Patrick Weekes, who retaliated by naming Jondum Bau after Dombrow.
