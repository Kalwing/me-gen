Maji was the first planet of the Vamshi System in the Armstrong Nebula of the Milky Way, explored in Mass Effect. It orbited the Vamshi binary giant stars: Vamshi-A, a blue A4III star burning at about 1.5 times Sol's temperature, and Vamshi-B, an aging red giant of class M5III over 220 times Sol's size. Maji had a thin atmosphere of methane and carbon monoxide, and the temperature difference between its sun-facing and deep-space-facing hemispheres drove constant wind that stirred the silica and sodium dust of the surface. Its soil supported grasslike lifeforms adapted to the methane/carbon monoxide environment. Unsavory characters from the Terminus Systems occasionally used Maji for cruel sport, dumping slaves, hostages, quarreling shipmates, or vicious animals on the surface, where one had to kill the other before rescue from the giant stars' lethal radiation.

Maji was the site of the assignment UNC: Geth Incursions, centered on a geth outpost defended by heavy turrets and hosting Geth Colossi, Rocket Troopers, and Snipers. Other points of interest included a crashed probe requiring Electronics skill to salvage, ancient debris marked with the Edessan Colony insignia for the UNC: Turian Insignias collection, and a massive unidentifiable skull scored by mass accelerator shots, apparently brought from an unknown world. Mineral deposits included magnesium, beryllium, and thorium. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition the terrain gained 3D grass rendering in place of plain dirt textures.
