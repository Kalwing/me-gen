"N7: MSV Strontium Mule" was an optional Mass Effect 2 assignment in the Arinlarkan system of the Omega Nebula, unlocked after "N7: Archeological Dig Site" via a Cerberus e-mail tracing Lieutenant Locke's rendezvous with Captain Vorhess. The derelict freighter MSV Strontium Mule had been captured by Blue Suns mercenaries led by Captain Vorhess and Sergeant Boortis, and Cerberus wanted a particular sealed cargo canister aboard, including sensitive intel. The ship's main log, left by Captain Jarrett Barnes, revealed the crew had answered a distress signal two weeks earlier that turned out to be a Blue Suns trap; they escaped with heavy damage but were later run down and killed, and Barnes encrypted the canister's authorization codes into the log.

Shepard and squad entered through an underbelly hatch into the cargo hold and fought upward through the claustrophobic ship: waves of Blue Suns Troopers, Heavies, and Legionnaires in the hold, more in the central hub, a Senior Engineer in the drive core, and finally Vorhess with a heavily protected Command Bodyguard on the bridge. Decrypting the command codes at the bridge console triggered a FENRIS Mech ambush from the rear, after which Boortis and his men attacked. Recovered Blue Suns transmitters showed Boortis was planning to let the intruders kill Vorhess so he could seize the canister for himself. Consoles noted the FTL drive had been damaged and repaired with substandard parts.

Clearing all Blue Suns opened the upper cargo hold, where Shepard recovered the canister, 2,000 iridium, and a researchable Heavy Skin Weave damage-reduction upgrade. The mission recovered the intel for Cerberus upload and prevented the looting of the freighter. Rewards were 7,500 credits, 2,000 iridium, and the Heavy Skin Weave upgrade.
