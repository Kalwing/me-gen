Collector Assassins were the long-range marksmen of the Collector forces in Mass Effect 2. They were armed with the Collector Particle Beam, which fired at an extremely high rate, tore through shields and barriers with ease, and could not be overheated by powers such as Overload or Flashbang Grenade; like other Collector troops they had wings used to drop into combat and a light biotic barrier that grew stronger on higher difficulties. Assassins almost always fought from cover at long range, positioned behind other Collector troops, and used their particle beams as sniper rifles in long bursts. They were especially dangerous alongside Harbinger, pinning a target down while Harbinger closed in to use his cover-breaking attack, which is why prioritizing Assassins was advised. Recommended counters included counter-sniping, Pull-Throw or Pull-Warp combos, and Incendiary Ammo, along with Reave, which stripped the barrier, flushed an unprotected Assassin from cover, and could briefly halt its fire. Once the barrier was removed, an Assassin was a prime target for Dominate because of its high damage output and rear position, which forced other Collectors to turn away from Shepard's squad. A Vanguard could Charge in for bonus barrier damage and a stagger, though Assassins rarely appeared alone. Like all Collector troops, Assassins could be possessed by Harbinger.
