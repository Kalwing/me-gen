Elanus Risk Control Services (ERCS) was a private security corporation offering services ranging from simple event security to full mercenary companies and anti-piracy starships. It began as a privately owned turian security firm and expanded into an interstellar conglomerate after opening to foreign investment. Its affordable, reliable armor, weapons and omni-tools were popular with security personnel and mercenaries across species.

ERCS operated a weapons facility on Noveria producing lethal and non-lethal arms, and provided the station's security personnel, mostly human and turian, overseen by Captain Maeko Matsuo and Sergeant Kaira Stirling; Noveria's executive board received a discount. An armory license could be bought from Morlan in the Citadel Wards or Commander Rentola on Virmire for 100 credits.

In the first Mass Effect, ERCS weapons used scram dissipation, reducing performance relative to competitors, and produced the Banshee assault rifles, Hurricane shotguns, Striker pistols and Hammer sniper rifles. Its armor lines included Duelist Armor and Guardian Armor (for humans, krogan and turians, with Duelist modifiable for quarians) and the krogan-exclusive Warlord Armor; ERCS armor suffered reduced damage protection due to its use of kinetic plastite. In Mass Effect 2 and 3, ERCS manufactured the M-3 Predator heavy pistol, the M-9 Tempest submachine gun (produced for Eclipse), the M-15 Vindicator battle rifle (produced for the Blue Suns) and the M-100 Grenade Launcher (favored by Blood Pack vorcha), plus the Amplifier Plates for N7 Armor. In Mass Effect: Revelation, Striker pistols were instead attributed to the Ahial Syndicate.
