Flashbang Grenade was a power in Mass Effect 2 that threw a disorienting concussive charge inflicting minor damage and incapacitating all nearby targets, temporarily disabling both electronics and enemy nervous systems to cause weapon overheats, omni-tool jams, and difficulty using biotic powers. Its three base ranks increased impact radius, damage, and incapacitate duration. Rank 4 evolved it into either Frag Grenade, packed with metal fragments for extra damage (120 points), or Improved Flashbang Grenade, with a 9-metre radius and 6-second incapacitation.

It was Kasumi Goto's fourth power, unlocked after her loyalty mission; her version aimed at an enemy and impacted instantly and reliably. When used by Shepard as a bonus power obtained through Advanced Training, it arced like a thrown grenade and only detonated after coming to rest on a horizontal surface, behaving inconsistently on uneven ground and able to briefly stagger or blind Shepard and squadmates at very close range. At all ranks it could overheat the standard weapons of most enemies for several seconds, though many tougher types, shotgun-wielders, YMIR Mechs, and heavy-weapon users were immune. Its force component caused instant kills against unprotected Husks and could knock down even lightly protected enemies if the damage penetrated their remaining protection. Its ability-suppression effect disabled enemy biotic and tech powers for upwards of 40 seconds regardless of protection, suppressing Harbinger, preventing Collector Guardians from deploying hex shields, and denying vorcha their armor regeneration. It had no secondary effect on Gunships, Thresher Maws, Scions, Praetorians, Geth Primes, the Haestrom Colossus, YMIR Mechs, or final-boss enemies.

Besides Shepard and Kasumi, the power was used by Eclipse Troopers on Bekenstein, Shadow Broker Agents, Prison Guards, and Project Guards.
