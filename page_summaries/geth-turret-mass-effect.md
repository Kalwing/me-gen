Geth Turrets, also called Geth Heavy Turrets, were stationary emplacements the geth deployed to reinforce positions and defend key areas during the events of Mass Effect. They performed a role comparable to other turrets but had a distinct geth appearance. Shepard and squad fought through several of them on Noveria while advancing from Port Hanshan to Peak 15, encountered more during a geth incursion into the Armstrong Nebula while destroying geth bases across the cluster, and faced them again, protected by multiple geth barriers, during the ascent up the Citadel exhaust plain.

Geth Turrets had very high health and six bars of regenerating shields, making them extremely durable. Fighting them from the Mako was straightforward since they were large stationary targets; a few main gun rounds plus machine gun fire destroyed them, and their slow-moving missiles could be dodged, though killing them from the Mako yielded less experience than an on-foot kill. On foot a single hit could kill Shepard, so constant movement and approaching one turret at a time were advised, with the common tactic being to weaken a turret from the Mako then finish it on foot for the experience bonus. Sabotage overheated a turret for about ten seconds, and Master Hacking could turn one against the others for full on-foot experience. Because the turrets were immobile, kinetic biotics were useless against them and only powers such as Warp were effective.
