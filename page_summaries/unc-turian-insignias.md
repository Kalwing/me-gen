UNC: Turian Insignias was a collection assignment in Mass Effect. Several turian outposts and colonies had been destroyed during the Unification War, and each colony's insignia had disappeared. The assignment appeared in the journal after Commander Shepard discovered the first insignia. There were seventeen insignias in total, but only thirteen had to be collected to complete it, which then chained into UNC: Collection Complete. The lost turian colony names referenced nations conquered or fought by the Roman Empire, on which the turians are modeled, such as Parthia, Gothis, and Macedyn.
