The M-99 Saber was a heavy-duty semi-automatic assault rifle appearing in Mass Effect 3. Favored by only the most elite marksmen, it was jokingly called "The Big Iron" for its stopping power. Each Saber was custom-built for its owner, making it one of the Alliance's more expensive weapons. In single-player it was found during the mission Priority: Horizon, obtained by activating a "Pod - manual override" option and then opening the pod containing the weapon; it could not be purchased if missed. In multiplayer it was randomly awarded from item packs and categorized as ultra-rare, requiring no expansion packs.

The Saber carried more damage per shot than any other assault rifle, capable of killing infantry in one or two well-placed shots with appropriate modifiers, and functioned essentially as a designated marksman rifle with high damage and low rate of fire, comparable to the M-97 Viper but heavier. It had no default scope, suffered no hip-fire damage penalty, but had only average unzoomed accuracy and a delay before its crosshairs tightened after zoomed fire. Its high-damage shots made it effective against armored targets but left it more vulnerable to enemy shield gates on higher difficulties, mitigated by Disruptor or Warp ammo or by augmented headshot damage. It had no innate penetration, so a piercing mod or piercing ammo was recommended against terrain and armor plating. It compared unfavorably to the N7 Valiant, which weighed less and fired faster, though the Saber's larger clip capacity synergized well with the Marksman power. Squadmates such as James Vega and Kaidan Alenko could use it as a substitute sniper. CAT6 Specialists were seen carrying the Saber during the cutscene of the assault on Ryuusei's Sushi Bar. A saber is a curved cavalry sword of 17th-century European origin.
