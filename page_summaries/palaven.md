Palaven was the turian homeworld, the third planet of the Trebia System in the Apien Crest cluster of the Milky Way. Alliance hero Jon Grissom, among the first humans to see it after the First Contact War, described its people as "made of steel". An asari diplomat had earlier called Palaven "a silver world of fortresses and fire". The planet's weak magnetic field left it poorly shielded from its sun, so most animal life developed metallic carapaces against solar radiation and photosynthetic life shut down vulnerable processes during daylight, repairing cellular damage at night. An Alliance travel advisory warned human visitors to wear enviro-suits or radiation protection.

The turians' martial attitude permeated every aspect of Palaven society, from architecture to art to politics, and its visibly fortified cities reflected this; after the turians joined the galactic community, internal conflicts became honor-bound affairs with few civilian casualties. Palaven was never occupied by an invading force until the Reapers, who brought overwhelming force, bombarded every city that resisted, and left the dust and smoke of pulverized cities as a breathing hazard across much of the planet, with water and power supplies nearly gone. The fight there nonetheless cost the Reapers dearly.

Shortly after the First Contact War, the mercenaries Jack Harper, Eva Coré, and the heavily modified Ben Hislop became the first three humans to set foot on Palaven, paraded through the streets by Desolas Arterius to persuade the populace to use the Arca Monolith. Garrus Vakarian grew up on Palaven and compared parts of it to Virmire. A Citadel business in Zakera Ward was named after the planet, and a regular shuttle route connected Palaven to Illium as of 2185. The Mass Effect 3 multiplayer map Firebase Condor was set on one of Palaven's moons.
