Patavig was the third planet of the Macedon System in the Artemis Tau cluster of the Milky Way, described as the second of the system's giant terrestrial planets and by far the more interesting. Most of its surface was covered by a vast sea of liquid ammonia in which a unique aquatic, ammonia-based biosphere had developed; its frozen continents were largely lifeless, but the chilly, toxic seas held a rich variety of complex organisms, many larger than a human.

Though dreadfully inhospitable to humans, Patavig was suitable for colonization by the volus, and negotiations between the Systems Alliance and the volus' patrons, the Turian Hierarchy, made good progress. If the asari-led Council died in Mass Effect, Patavig was granted to the Vol Protectorate in 2183 in exchange for volus support in a key human-led Council vote, and the Alliance's Fifth Fleet visited the planet in 2185 during its cruise of the Attican Traverse. In 2186 the anti-conspiracy activist Bernard Plim claimed to have exposed "the Alliance funding of the Volus Mafia" on Patavig. The wiki noted that the planet's listed surface temperature of -130 °C, high atmospheric pressure, and negligible stellar radiation made a genuine ammonia ocean physically implausible.
