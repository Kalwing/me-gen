Yaroslev Tartakovsky was a human researcher for Binary Helix at Peak 15 on Noveria, working in the hot labs on one of the company's classified projects. One of his journal entries, found in the dining hall if the Asari Diplomacy assignment had not been completed, expressed concern about a mercenary group present at the facility.

When Commander Shepard entered the hot labs, Tartakovsky was sitting in a chair, weakened by an unidentified condition; how he survived the rachni outbreak was left unexplained. He told Shepard that Binary Helix had recovered a rachni egg from a drifting rachni ship and had planned to clone the rachni and mass-produce an army. The egg proved to be a queen, and the researchers chose to separate her offspring from her to make them more obedient; instead the isolated rachni were driven insane by her absence.

Tartakovsky held the codes that the station VI Mira could use to activate a neutron purge. As he rose to hand them over, he was impaled through the chest by a Rachni Soldier. Shepard killed the rachni and took the codes from his body. Shortly before dying he remarked, "mother always said I would come to a bad end." The purge code is 875-020-079, a number used as coordinates in the Star Trek episode "The Mark of Gideon." Cut dialogue would have had him survive long enough to speak the purge instructions aloud and mention being born in "Arhangelsk."
