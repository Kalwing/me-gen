Korlus was the second planet of the Imir System in the Eagle Nebula, described by one Citadel Council member at the turn of the century as "a garbage scow with a climate." Efforts by the Korlus Tourist Bureau to rebrand it, including as "the recycling center of the galaxy," failed against corruption scandals and a staggering murder rate; it ranked second in the Terminus Systems for murder per capita and first for offworlder murder. Its biggest business was recycling decommissioned and junked spacecraft into component parts, a dirty process that choked its sky with smog even after the invention of omni-gel, supported by a shady hospitality industry and a scavenger underclass.

Per the codex, Korlus was known as the starcraft cemetery and served as the regional toxic junkyard for centuries, with ships at astronautical "near-death" dumped planetward to clear shipping lanes. Ancient volcanism had greenhoused the planet, leaving it too hot and CO2-rich for a biosphere despite abundant lakes; it later cooled enough for protected habitation and hosted krogan outposts, who seeded it with hardy varren kept as war hounds. By Mass Effect 2 it hosted merc factions such as the Blue Suns, rumored to test advanced munitions on downed ship hulks, and was defended by massive gun batteries. It was the location of the mission Dossier: The Warlord and could not be mined. Its concept was based on a ship-breaking area in India; cut content shows it was once meant to be the site of Zaeed Massani's recruitment.
