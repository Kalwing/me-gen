Mordin: Old Blood was Mordin Solus's loyalty mission in Mass Effect 2, unlocked after Horizon when Mordin learned that Blood Pack mercenaries had captured his former protege Maelon on Tuchanka. Maelon had helped Mordin modify the genophage, so his capture raised security concerns. Shepard travelled to Clan Urdnot's base, where the clan leader was Wrex or Wreav depending on the Virmire outcome. Wrex was seen dismissing the rival emissary Gatatog Uvenk. The Chief Scout revealed that Clan Weyrloc held Maelon in a hospital converted into a fortress and asked Shepard to look for a missing scout, Urdnot Darg.

Shepard's squad fought through Blood Pack Boom-Squads, Pyros, Troopers, klixen and varren outside and inside the hospital. Along the way conversations with dead bodies prompted Mordin to reflect on his role in the genophage, his hatred of live experimentation, and religion; a female krogan corpse who had volunteered for fertility experiments and died of tumors deeply affected him. A Weyrloc Clanspeaker monologued about curing the genophage, enslaving salarians and sending the Citadel "into the sun," and could be killed by a Renegade interrupt igniting a gas pipe. Darg could be persuaded to abandon the krogan and return to Urdnot as a warrior. After defeating Chief Weyrloc Guld, Shepard found Maelon unharmed and working willingly out of guilt, believing curing the genophage was worth any cost. Mordin drew a gun on Maelon; a Paragon interrupt spared him. Shepard then directed Mordin to destroy Maelon's research data or preserve it (to help the krogan or "just in case").

Consequences: Urdnot grew stronger, adopting Weyrloc survivors under Wrex or raiding their stockpiles under Wreav. In Mass Effect 3, saved data let Mordin stabilize Eve so she survived Priority: Tuchanka; destroyed data caused Eve's death. With Wrex dead and data unsaved, Mordin could still be talked down; otherwise he sacrificed himself dispersing the cure or was killed by Shepard. Rewards included the Neural Shock power, Krogan Vitality and Heavy Weapon Ammo upgrades. A grateful Darg later emailed Shepard from the female camp.
