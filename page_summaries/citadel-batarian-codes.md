Citadel: Batarian Codes was a Mass Effect 3 mission acquired after Priority: The Citadel II by speaking to C-Sec Officer Jordan Noles, who stood just outside the C-Sec Outpost on the Presidium Commons and asked for help tracking illegal use of batarian diplomatic codes. Shepard went to the Spectre area in the Citadel Embassies, read the message about the codes, and clicked "Enable," which added console locations to the Citadel map. Shepard then disabled the code at consoles in the right-side lab of Lab Services at Huerta Memorial Hospital, at the Passenger Lounge of Normandy Dock Bay D24, and at the end of Bay E28 in the Docks Holding Area. Two versions existed for how the codes had been used: to secure extra provisions for batarians, or for sabotage targeting humans and Alliance military assets on the Citadel.

After the final code was disabled, someone put a pistol to the Commander's back. It was Ka'hairal Balak, if he had been allowed to escape in X57: Bring Down the Sky; he appeared even if that DLC was never played. Blaming Shepard for the destruction of Bahak and the batarians' plight, Balak could be killed or talked down with a Reputation check. If spared, he grudgingly committed the batarian fleet to the war; if killed, the Alliance Third Fleet War Asset lost value. If Balak had instead been killed or handed to authorities earlier, Shepard met Noles in Cargo Hold C near the Batarian State Arms kiosk, where she held an unnamed batarian who, if Arrival had been completed, acknowledged Shepard's role in it; that batarian could not be killed and was either convinced or hauled off. Convincing Balak or the batarian added the batarian fleet to War Assets, plus 210 experience and morality points. The Kestrel Helmet became available at Batarian State Arms only if the fleet was secured.
