The Shaman was a krogan living at the Urdnot Camp on Tuchanka who was in charge of Clan Urdnot's rites. Despite being responsible for keeping krogan traditions alive, he was irritated by the ostensibly traditionalist Gatatog Uvenk's objections to the tank-bred Grunt, and allowed Grunt to perform the Rite of Passage over Uvenk's protests.

If Commander Shepard taunted Uvenk for using politics instead of fighting, the Shaman praised the Commander for undermining Uvenk with words, Uvenk's "natural weapon"; if Shepard headbutted Uvenk in krogan fashion, the Shaman was particularly amused, judging that the Commander understood krogan ideals better than "those who whine like pups." Afterward the Shaman told Shepard he had gone through many rites to become shaman, including giving up his name, that those rites were so physically and mentally torturous they made him wish he were dead, and that further rites had to be performed every dawn and dusk. Grunt, if present, called the job terrible, and the Shaman agreed. Shepard could also return to tell him stories of past battles against worthy foes, including stopping Saren and a Reaper at the Citadel; the Shaman knew of and respected Spectre combat prowess.

In Mass Effect 3, if Eve died during the mission to cure the genophage, the Shaman delivered the eulogy at her funeral. A separate multiplayer class, the Krogan Shaman Adept, is unrelated to this character.
