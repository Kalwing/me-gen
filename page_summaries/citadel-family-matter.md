Citadel: Family Matter became available after Shepard completed any of Therum, Noveria, or Feros and returned to the Citadel, where Rebekah Petrovsky and Michael Petrovsky stood near the Rapid Transit stop outside Barla Von's office. Rebekah's husband, Jacob Petrovsky, had recently died of a rare heart condition, and Rebekah was carrying his child, which risked inheriting the condition. Michael, Jacob's brother, wanted the risk corrected with in-utero gene therapy; Rebekah, citing extranet reports that the therapy was dangerous, preferred less effective alternatives.

Shepard could argue for the therapy, against it, or remain neutral, using Charm or Intimidate. Persuading the couple toward therapy awarded +8 Paragon, and persuading them against it awarded +9 Renegade. Hearing the couple out granted a codex entry if it had not already been obtained from Rafael Vargas during Noveria: Smuggling. Opting out after hearing them gave the codex entry and a journal resolution; opting out beforehand gave only the resolution notice. The journal entry was recorded only once the matter was settled one way or another.

In Mass Effect 2, the couple were found on the 26th floor of Zakera Market with their one-year-old son Jake, named for his father's nickname, still arguing over his upbringing and Jacob's legacy, with no option to intervene; their attitudes varied with Shepard's earlier choice. In Mass Effect 3, after Priority: Palaven, they appeared on the Presidium Commons arguing about placing Jake in daycare during the Reaper War; supporting them granted 2 Reputation, and Shepard referenced the earlier meeting if the assignment had been completed in Mass Effect. The assignment was written by Trick Weekes and was known internally as "More Human than Human."
