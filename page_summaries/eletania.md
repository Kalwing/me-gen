Eletania was the third planet of the Hercules System in Attican Beta, carrying a Level 1 Toxic Hazard rating. It appeared ideal for colonization, covered in mosses, algae and lichen with a thick oxygenated atmosphere, but its animal kingdom consisted of microscopic symbiotic creatures impossible to filter from the air and necessary for native life, which caused anaphylactic shock when inhaled by non-native life. Settlement therefore required fully sealed environment suits or wholesale replacement of the ecosystem; some proposed limited colonization at high altitudes above the symbiotes' range. The planet had a visible ring system and a moon.

In Mass Effect the planet hosted the assignment UNC: Lost Module, involving a downed recon probe and a mineshaft, plus mineral surveys for palladium and gold, and geth and pyjak lifeforms. Its main point of interest was a Prothean ruin containing a Prothean sphere artifact. Using the trinket obtained from the Citadel: Asari Consort assignment on the artifact triggered a text-delivered vision: Shepard experienced the memories of a Cro-Magnon hunter who was observed and implanted by a hovering silver Prothean craft, the recording captured by an implanted Prothean data recorder. On waking, Shepard realized the Protheans had studied primitive humans, observing and analyzing the results at their base on Mars, and concluded it was a mystery that would likely never be solved. Unlocking the sphere was flagged when importing a save into Mass Effect 2, though it had no effect.
