Cartagena Station was a space station in the Nemean Abyss and the closest thing the Abyss had to a beacon of civilisation. It was frequently assaulted by pirates, who took hostages in order to blackmail the local security force, the Cartagena Patrol or "C-Pat", a clear analogue of the Citadel's C-Sec.

A notable bar on the station was the Fringe, a two-storey venue of similar size to the Citadel nightclub Flux, with its own bar. During the mission Into the Abyss in Mass Effect Galaxy, Jacob Taylor went to the Fringe on Derek Izunami's suggestion to meet his contact, Miranda Lawson. When Jacob arrived, he found the bar under attack by the pirate Clint "Black Eye" Darragh and his men. After talking his way past the C-Pat officers guarding the entrance, Jacob dealt with Black Eye's men and then Black Eye himself, either in a firefight or by convincing the pirate to surrender. Miranda, who had watched the confrontation from the bar, then greeted him.

The station's name referenced Cartagena, a city on the northern coast of Colombia that was repeatedly raided by English pirates such as Francis Drake and John Hawkins during Spanish colonial rule, and also Cartagena in southeastern Spain, a walled naval town with a fort-defended harbour that was a major Spanish seaport under Philip II.
