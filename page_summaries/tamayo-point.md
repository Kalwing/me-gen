Tamayo Point was a space station in the Sol system designated as a tourist jump-off point to the wider galaxy, with extensive commercial amenities catering to human and alien visitors. It possibly took its name from the Cuban cosmonaut Arnaldo Tamayo Méndez.

In Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation, Tamayo Point was Cora Harper's first waystation on returning to Sol after four years of military service with the asari. She was almost immediately targeted by xenophobes following an unflattering ambush interview by Khalisah al-Jilani. Her friend Ygara Menoris was also on the station, ostensibly bound for Illium, but turned out to have ulterior motives. After Ygara's betrayal, Cora returned to Tamayo Point and used the station's direct line to the system comm buoy to relay her report to Alec Ryder; because the line was not fully secure, Ryder spoke in hypotheticals whenever the subject touched on illegal topics such as artificial intelligence.

The station had medical facilities on its higher levels equipped to treat damage from metabolic overdrive. Cora was brought there again after overexertion on Quiet Eddy harmed her body, spending weeks in a coma while Homeward Sol protesters who recognized her from al-Jilani's interview gathered at the hospital, requiring armed guards at her door. When she was discharged, Tamayo Point authorities cleared protesters around her shuttle after her implant AI SAM-E issued a possible bomb threat.
