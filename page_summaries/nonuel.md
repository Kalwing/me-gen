Nonuel was the fourth planet of the Plutus System in the Hades Gamma cluster of the Milky Way, carrying a Level 1 Heat Hazard warning. Although one of the oldest entries in the star charts, it had never been fully mapped. It was the largest body in the asteroid belt of the blue star Plutus, large enough to hold a spherical shape and massive enough to retain the noxious carbon and sulphur dioxides venting from its many volcanoes as an atmosphere. Nonuel was rabidly volcanic; the source of its great heat was also the source of its inordinate mass, since it had coalesced around a large chunk of element zero ejected by a supernova billions of years ago, making it a "secondary source" of eezo. Surface conditions were extremely hazardous, with a thin crust, numerous magma flows, and landscapes coated in slippery ash and cinders.

Commander Shepard could visit after reaching 75% Morality in Mass Effect, for the assignment UNC: The Negotiation. A warlord's outpost belonging to Lord Darius and his retainers stood on the surface, guarded also by thresher maws. A mercenary outpost held two mercenaries who had shot each other to death over a crate, which contained a carefully wrapped handwritten copy of one of Matriarch Dilinaga's treatises (for the UNC: Asari Writings collection) and a League of One medallion. Mineral deposits included light titanium and rare samarium. Special Ops Team Zeta could be found here during Search and Rescue. Streams of lava could incinerate even the Mako, and in Mass Effect Legendary Edition the lava lake still caused an instant Game Over if driven into. A galactic news report in Mass Effect 2 noted that if Lord Darius was killed, the Alliance secured his mining facility on Nonuel over competing factions.
