Mass Effect: Foundation 8, tagged "Death From The Shadows!," was published February 26, 2014, written by Mac Walters with art by Tony Parker. After Kai Leng and the agent Rasa failed to retrieve Subject Zero (Jack), the Illusive Man refused their apologies. At Minuteman Station, Miranda Lawson gave each new orders; Leng threatened Rasa against ever pairing with him again.

At the labs, Miranda briefed Rasa on the Lazarus Project, showing her Shepard's backup clone, Shepard's reconstruction, and Cerberus's full archive on Shepard. Rasa's intelligence had been invaluable to the project, but the Illusive Man still wanted the real Shepard restored. Rasa's assignment was to steal Shepard's classified files from the Spectre offices on the Citadel. Miranda gave her a disc of outdated Cerberus secrets to trade to the Spectre Tela Vasir for sanctuary in the Spectre offices, where she could download the files. Posing as a Cerberus defector with lightened skin and a blonde wig, Rasa approached Vasir, who took the disc and left her at an office. Vasir suspected treachery, returned with armed guards, undid Rasa's disguise, and took her prints and DNA. Vasir blackmailed Rasa into delivering the Shepard files to the Illusive Man carrying a virus meant to force him to meet Vasir's demands, offering a choice between prison and betraying Cerberus.

Afterward, at a bar, Rasa was slipped a spiked drink. Stumbling into the Presidium, she was pulled from a C-Sec officer by a stranger who claimed her and set her down in an alley: the assassin Thane Krios, knife drawn. Rasa blurted that she did not want to die because she had never lived; Thane decided to believe her, said he had been misinformed about her, apologized, and carried her off praying to Kalahira. Days later Rasa reached Minuteman Station on a gurney and warned Miranda about the virus; Miranda extracted the data and countered it. The Illusive Man, pleased, agreed Rasa's field days were over but said he still had a purpose for her: drawing up the dossiers for Shepard's prospective team.
