The Ahn'Kedar Orbital Platform was a space station orbiting an unknown planet, featured in Mass Effect Galaxy. The high number of batarians in the area suggested that both the planet and the station were under batarian control. The batarian ambassador Jath'Amon claimed the station was a research facility. Acting on information from his informant Miranda Lawson that several scientists were being held hostage there, Jacob Taylor investigated.

On arrival, Jacob found that the research agenda was a front. He joined the krogan Nax, who was searching for his captive scientist friend Batha, and fought through the platform, discovering Dr. Hendricks, one of the hostages. Hendricks claimed the batarians had been struck by a terrible disease and had brought the scientists in to develop a cure. In truth, the batarians had engineered the disease themselves as a weapon to assassinate the Citadel Council. With Nax's help, Jacob rescued Batha and the other scientists and left the station with Batha's knowledge of the cure. These events occurred during the mission A Formula for Hope.
