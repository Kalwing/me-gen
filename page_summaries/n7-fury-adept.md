The N7 Fury Adept was a Mass Effect 3 multiplayer character class representing the "go-anywhere, fight-anything" special forces of the Alliance, trained at the Interplanetary Combatives Academy in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Candidates had to survive combat situations "in an admirable and effective fashion" to earn the N7 rank, and by the time of the Reaper War many N7s were training other species in anti-Reaper asymmetric warfare.

Fury operatives used implants to fuel their biotics and extreme movement speed, weaving an unpredictable path through the battlefield, darting in and out of combat before unleashing sweeping biotic attacks on unsuspecting targets. The class had a 500 barrier and 500 health, and its powers were Throw, Annihilation Field, Dark Channel and Fitness. Its heavy melee, Biotic Strike, teleported the Fury forward and released a biotic burst at the cost of barrier; its grab dragged an enemy over cover for a charged biotic punch; and its dodge, Acrobatics, was a short-range teleport flip that could pass through walls and move between levels. In play, a low-cooldown Fury chained Biotic Explosions by priming enemies with Annihilation Field and detonating with Throw, or with the Dark Channel–Throw combination, moving from enemy to enemy and staggering foes before they could respond.
