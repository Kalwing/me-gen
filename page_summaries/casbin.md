Casbin was the second planet of the Hong System in the Armstrong Nebula, in the Milky Way. It was a classic "pre-garden" terrestrial world with conditions resembling Earth millions of years ago: a hot, humid atmosphere composed mainly of nitrogen and carbon dioxide, with a growing cover of simple lichen and algae expected to shift the atmosphere toward an Earth-like nitrogen-oxygen mix over the following millennia. Because of its potential for future habitability and sapient life, the Citadel Council designated Casbin a Sanctuary World; landing was prohibited by law, and disturbing the fragile ecosystem carried harsh fines and imprisonment. At the time it was passing through the debris trail of a long-period comet.

Despite the landing ban, Commander Shepard could visit Casbin during the assignment UNC: Geth Incursions, clearing a geth outpost and a geth encampment guarded by three geth heavy turrets. The encampment's array was transmitting regular encrypted data bursts over the Alliance's own FTL comm buoys to an unknown destination. Other points of interest included a salvageable crashed probe and a beacon for UNC: Locate Signs of Battle, beneath which was buried an identification tag belonging to Captain Thein, with no indication of who had hidden it or why. Enemies present included Geth Armatures, a Geth Dropship, and geth rocket, shock and sniper troopers. Surveyable minerals included gold, samarium and uranium, most of the sites lying in rough mountainous terrain, with the samarium deposit atop a sheer wall east of the Mako's landing zone.

The planet may have been named after a provincial capital of Iran, and its codex text may reference Earth's Proterozoic oxygenation. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition the lingering "Anomalous Signal" at the second point of interest could finally be switched off at a console, and the terrain gained 3D grass instead of plain green textures.
