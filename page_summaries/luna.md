Luna was Earth's moon, located in the Sol System of the Local Cluster. Once an early source of helium-3, by the time of Mass Effect it was mined chiefly for materials used in space-habitat construction; two dozen major stations at Earth's L4 and L5 Lagrange points had been built entirely from lunar resources.

In gameplay, Luna became accessible at Level 20 and was the site of the assignment UNC: Rogue VI, in which Shepard shut down a malfunctioning Alliance training VI. Points of interest on the surface included a training ground defended by three dual turrets, the wreck of the Soviet probe CCCP Luna 23 (salvageable with average Electronics), and two unmarked clusters of four Alliance heavy turrets that remained active after the assignment. The rachni song could also be heard at one location. The presence of the Luna 23 probe placed the operational zone in the southern part of Mare Crisium.

The novel Mass Effect: Revelation described Luna as having been "terraformed"; developers clarified that several craters had been "domed over" for localized surface terraforming, since the low-mass moon could not retain an Earth-like atmosphere. A prevalent wind sound played despite the negligible atmosphere, and the original game showed a reversed image of North America when Earth was viewed from the surface, corrected in the Legendary Edition. Luna was referenced again in Mass Effect 2, when EDI was introduced and Shepard could order her shut down, and in Mass Effect 3, in video-logs examined at Cronos Station during Priority: Cerberus Headquarters.
