The Human Vanguard was a cooperative multiplayer class in Mass Effect 3 (distinct from the Project Phoenix Vanguard and the Andromeda class of the same name). Its lore blurb repeated that humans joined the galactic community after discovering a Prothean data cache on Mars in 2148. Vanguards were feared frontline fighters who charged across the battlefield to surprise and destroy enemies in close combat.

The class had 500 barrier and 500 health and used the Alliance Training passive with the powers Biotic Charge, Shockwave, Nova and Fitness. Melee options included a punch sequence, a biotically-charged Biotic Strike heavy melee, and a Biotic Punch grab execution; it could combat-roll to dodge. It was the only class with Nova. Its signature tactic was the Charge-Nova cycle: Biotic Charge restored barriers and granted invulnerability during the animation, allowing a follow-up Nova, repeated continuously. Nova consumed barriers rather than triggering a cooldown; the Half Blast evolution halved its cost and damage for better survivability, and its Power Recharge evolution stacked to a 300% cooldown bonus. Shockwave was the only power usable safely at range to knock down enemies and detonate combos, and the only Vanguard priming power via the Lifting Shockwave evolution. The page details faction tactics: Charge-Nova was near-unstoppable against Cerberus (except Guardians and Atlases), highly effective against Collectors (but Seeker Swarms and Abominations were deadly), risky against Geth (Hunters, Primes and Rocket Troopers could stun), and effective against most Reaper troops, with extreme caution urged around Brutes, Ravagers and especially Banshees, whose glowing/teleport state must never be charged.
