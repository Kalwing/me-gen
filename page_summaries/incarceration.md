Incarceration was the bonus mission of Mass Effect: Infiltrator. Its protagonist was not Randall Ezno but the turian target Randall had captured in the opening mission, Ice Giant, now held prisoner by Cerberus aboard the Barn space station and strapped to an operating table for experimentation. During Randall's escape and disruption of the facility, an alarm sounded, the turian's restraints released, his guard was killed, and the actions of Randall and his volus ally freed all the test subjects, throwing the station into chaos. The turian grabbed a weapon and fought toward escape.

The mission ran across six checkpoints through corridors, ruined experiment rooms, and a large hall turned into a warzone where Cerberus troops fought escaped prisoners. Enemies included Orcus Mechs, Grad Turrets, Troopers, Riot Troopers, Centurions, Snipers, Engineers, Asari Prisoners, Krogan Prisoners, and, at the final checkpoint, an Ogre Mech backed by an endless supply of Orcus Mechs. The turian had a limited, low-powered arsenal with no upgrades, no biotics, no store access during the mission, and a weapon that overheated quickly, making the "veteran" checkpoint ranks much harder to earn than in the main campaign; Cloak had to be used sparingly for repositioning and assassinations. The game's morality system had no effect on this mission.

After defeating the Ogre Mech, the turian reached a large empty room and approached the far wall, where the Director and Cerberus personnel watched through a window. The Director laughed as a turret deployed in the coverless room and the screen faded to black; there was no escape and the mission ended. Credits earned across the checkpoints could be spent in Randall's store, and earning a veteran medal on all six checkpoints added 40 points to the Cerberus Escapees war asset in Mass Effect 3.
