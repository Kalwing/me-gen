Miranda Lawson was a human Cerberus officer, born in 2150. She had no mother; her influential, wealthy, egomaniacal father genetically engineered her from a modified copy of his own genome to be a specimen of human perfection, with enhanced intelligence, strength, reflexes, biotics, looks, faster healing and an extended lifespan. She was the first "daughter" he kept. Denied friends and affection and pressured to always be better, she fled home, and her father pursued her across the galaxy until she joined Cerberus in exchange for protection, at which point he severed his ties to the group. She rose to answer directly to the Illusive Man.

In Mass Effect Galaxy she worked as an informant for Jacob Taylor, helping him foil a batarian plot to assassinate the Citadel Council with a bioweapon. In the Mass Effect: Foundation comics she recruited Jacob to Cerberus while searching Terminus space for the fallen Shepard's body, ran the Lazarus Project at Minuteman/Lazarus Station, used the agent Rasa to steal Shepard's Spectre files, and later pursued Rasa when she defected with Shepard's backup clone. In Mass Effect: Redemption she guided Liara and Feron toward recovering Shepard's body from the Shadow Broker's agents and had it transferred to Lazarus Research Station.

The Illusive Man placed Miranda in charge of the Lazarus Project, which spent two years and vast Cerberus resources reviving Shepard. She became Shepard's second-in-command aboard the Normandy SR-2 and a proxy for the Illusive Man, having wanted (but been denied) to implant Shepard with a control chip. Her loyalty mission, "Miranda: The Prodigal," concerned protecting her twin sister Oriana from their father. During the Suicide Mission she could lead the second fireteam, and if present when Shepard destroyed the Collector Base she resigned from Cerberus. She was a romance option for a male Shepard.

In Mass Effect 3, if she survived, Miranda spent the war investigating Oriana's disappearance. Her father Henry Lawson, allied with the Illusive Man, had abducted Oriana and taken her to the Sanctuary facility on Horizon, whose true purpose was processing refugees into Reaper husks. Miranda was gravely wounded by Kai Leng there. Whether she survived the Horizon confrontation depended on securing her loyalty, warning her about Kai Leng, and granting her Alliance resources; if she lived she killed Henry, planted a tracker on Kai Leng leading to Cronos Station, joined the war effort striking Cerberus targets, and reunited with Oriana. She could appear in Mass Effect 3: Citadel, including at Shepard's party where she sparred with Jack.
