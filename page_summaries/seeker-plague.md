The Seeker Plague was a Collector unit encountered in Mass Effect 3, an enhanced variant of the Seeker Swarm. Possessed Collector Captains deployed Seeker Plagues to paralyze opponents, and a Possessed Captain could only have one swarm active at a time.

Once launched, a Seeker Plague pursued players in order to explode near them. Its detonation radius was much larger than that of a normal Seeker Swarm and penetrated terrain. A player caught in the blast took significant damage, had cooldown-based powers disabled for a short period, and suffered greatly reduced weapon accuracy. NPC squadmates were immune to the disabling effects. Powers without cooldowns, such as Nova, Phase Disruptor, and grenade-type powers, were unaffected, as were readied "charge" powers such as Biotic Orbs, Siege Pulse, and the Krogan Warlord Sentinel's hammer powers. The Marksman power, while active, made its user immune to the disabling effect.

Seeker Plagues were individually weak but carried a barrier for added protection. Their small size and erratic, fast movement made them hard to hit, and they could climb short drops and traverse ladders. They could not be frozen or placed in Stasis. Effective counters included area-of-effect and rapid-fire weapons, barrier-damaging powers such as Warp, Concussive Shot, and Overload, and Singularity, which locked down groups of seekers since they never dodged it. Pull could occasionally kill an unprotected seeker instantly. As a swarm prepared to explode it briefly stopped and vibrated more frantically; moving away during that moment could cause it to dissipate, though the larger blast radius made this far harder to escape than with a standard swarm. As a summoned unit, Seeker Plague kills did not count as enemy kills for certain multiplayer purposes, and eliminating all active swarms was not required to complete a wave. Swarms lasted until killed or detonated.
