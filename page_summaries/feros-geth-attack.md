Feros: Geth Attack was a Mass Effect main-story mission acquired after Shepard received Spectre status and command of the Normandy, when Udina relayed reports of geth sightings on the young ExoGeni colony world of Feros. The primary objective was to reach the Zhu's Hope colony and learn what Saren wanted there. It was written by Lukas Kristjanson.

On arrival, no one answered hails. Shepard was met by David Al Talaqani, who directed the team to colony leader Fai Dan before being shot in the back by geth. Fighting through Geth Troopers, Shock Troopers, Snipers, Hoppers, and Destroyers, Shepard reached Zhu's Hope, built into the downed freighter Borealis, and met Fai Dan and Arcelia Silva Martinez. After clearing a geth assault on the colony's defense tower, Fai Dan explained the colony's dire straits and pointed Shepard to colonists needing help, generating the assignments Water Restoration (Macha Doyle), Varren Meat (Davin Reynolds), Power Cells (May O'Connell), and Geth in the Tunnels. In the tunnels beneath the colony Shepard could meet the strangely behaving Ian Newstead.

An elevator led to a garage opening onto the Prothean skyway to ExoGeni Headquarters, traversed in the Mako against Geth Armatures, Rocket Troopers, and Juggernauts, with fires as level 3 hazards and lootable passages under the road. At the Skyway Weigh Station, Shepard met refugees led by Juliana Baynham, whose daughter Lizbeth might still be alive at ExoGeni HQ, and the uncooperative ExoGeni manager Ethan Jeong; Gavin Hossle offered the assignment to recover corporate data. Reaching ExoGeni HQ, Shepard encountered Lizbeth Baynham, who explained the geth were likely after the Thorian, then fought roughly seven varren and a Krogan Commander. An ExoGeni VI, mistaking Shepard for Lizbeth via her ID badge, revealed the Thorian was a plant-like creature that had taken over the colony, and that ExoGeni had allowed it to happen in order to study the results. A Geth Dropship clamped to the building jammed comms, preventing Shepard from reporting to Joker. The mission was then renamed Feros: The Thorian.

Reaching ExoGeni HQ cut Shepard off from the Normandy, locking squadmate choices for the rest of the Feros arc. Completing the colony assignments and choices made here fed into the later formula determining whether Zhu's Hope survived.
