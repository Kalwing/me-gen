Mass Effect 3: Datapad was a free iOS app released March 12, 2012 that connected to Mass Effect 3. It let players check the game's galaxy-map status, receive in-fiction text messages from Mass Effect 3 characters as missions were completed (stored locally, re-receivable after deletion), browse Alliance News feeds, view screenshots and videos, and read a portion of the Mass Effect 3 Codex. It was not compatible with the Wii U Special Edition.

Its Galaxy at War section was a mini-game tied to the game's Galactic Readiness Rating. Players deployed up to five fleets to fifteen locations across five theaters of operation on short (1 hour), medium (3 hour), or long (6 hour) missions, earning credits (75, 600, or 1,125) and readiness bonuses for the associated theater, with a chance that a fleet became crippled and unavailable afterward. Credits bought upgrades in Speed, Fleets, Shielding, and Bonus Readiness Rating (ten levels each, three for Fleets), and, once those were maxed, the expensive Galactic Command Coordination upgrade (three levels at 10,000, 25,000, and 40,000 credits). On June 6, 2013 the app was removed from the App Store and replaced by a simplified browser-based version on BioWare's N7 HQ, which dropped the credit and fleet-evolution mechanics but retained the Codex and Galaxy at War functions.
