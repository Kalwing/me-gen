The M-22 Eviscerator was a shotgun in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. Properly the Lieberschaft 2180, nicknamed "Eviscerator," it was of human civilian design with a unique ammunition generator: rather than shaving chips or pellets from an ammo block, it shaved serrated metal wedges shaped to fly aerodynamically. This dramatically improved armor penetration and kept a tight grouping that extended lethality beyond standard shotgun range. Because the design violated several intergalactic weapons treaties, the M-22 was not issued to militaries. The M-11 Wraith was a variant of it, and an unused text string called it the Cerberus M-22a "Thornpatch."

In Mass Effect 2 it came with the Cerberus Weapon and Armor DLC (or, in Legendary Edition, via research in the Normandy Tech Lab for 2,500 iridium), and could be used by Grunt, Jack, Jacob, and Tali. It used default weapon range values instead of shotgun-specific ones, giving it an assault rifle's damage falloff (though not better spread), which rendered the M-23 Katana obsolete; it dealt less than the M-300 Claymore but had a larger magazine and more range.

In Mass Effect 3 it was found during Grissom Academy: Emergency Evacuation, with a possible second upgrade in David Archer's security room, and was purchasable from Batarian State Arms; multiplayer rated it uncommon. It out-damaged the Katana and M-27 Scimitar per shot but had a slower fire rate and smaller clip, was light for a shotgun, and despite its description had no special anti-armor ability in that game. Mass Effect: Infiltrator featured a heat-based model used by Randall Ezno. "Lieberschaft" roughly translates as "dear shaft."
