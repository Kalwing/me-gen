Citadel: Crime in Progress was a Mass Effect 2 assignment. On Level 26 of the Citadel, Commander Shepard came upon a marketplace disturbance in which the volus Kor Tun demanded that the human C-Sec officer Tammert arrest the quarian Lia'Vael nar Ulnay, accusing her of stealing his lost credit chit after she bumped into him while he was leaving the Sirta Foundation. If Tali was in the squad she took Lia'Vael's side against the racial prejudice of the volus and the officer, and Shepard's second companion might react to Tali's vehemence.

Shepard offered to look into the matter and spoke with the salarian clerk Marab at Saronis Applications on the same level, who explained that Kor Tun had made purchases and left his credit chit behind before Marab could return it. Reporting back to Kor Tun completed the assignment for 5 Paragon points, with Shepard telling him the chit was waiting at Saronis Applications. The unrepentant volus insisted the quarian still could have stolen it, and Tammert warned Lia'Vael to get a permanent residence or be run in for vagrancy. Shepard could use a Paragon interrupt to stand up for her for 5 additional Paragon points, and Tali wished her luck. The mission gave 40 experience (50 on a second playthrough) and 1,000 credits. Its working title during development was "You Know What They're Like."
