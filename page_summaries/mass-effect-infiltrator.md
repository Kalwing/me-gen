Mass Effect: Infiltrator was a third-person shooter developed by IronMonkey Studios for mobile devices (iOS, Android, Windows Phone 8, BlackBerry 10), first released for iOS on March 6, 2012 and later on other platforms through 2013. It was published by Electronic Arts (Nokia on Windows Phone 8), cost around $6.99, was removed from the App Store in September 2015, and became unavailable everywhere by April 2018. Combat used touchscreen controls and a scoring system called Combat Analysis rating Style, Time, and Health, awarding Rookie, Soldier, or Veteran medals.

Set in 2186 during the events of Mass Effect 3, the game cast the player as veteran Cerberus operative Randall Ezno, who procured aliens for illicit experiments. Returning from an ice-planet mission to the Barn, a Cerberus research station, Randall found turian soldiers attacking and discovered his advisor Inali Renata subjected to horrific experiments ordered by the station's Director. Vowing revenge, he fought Cerberus troops, escaped test subjects, mechs, and a mutant krogan called X1. A voice claiming to be a volus working with the Alliance guided him to the comm tower, from which he signaled the Alliance and took a shuttle to a desert planet for pickup. There Inali appeared, heavily augmented and under the Director's control, and attacked him; Randall either spared or killed her based on the player's morality, then vowed to kill the Director. A bonus mission added in patch 1.0.39 let players control a turian experiment victim escaping the medical bay; unlike Randall, that character had no dialogue and ended trapped with no escape as the Director watched.

The game featured a Paragon/Renegade morality system affecting Randall's scars and biotic colors, a Store for upgrades, and no conversation wheel. Completing it unlocked the Cerberus Escapees War Asset for Mass Effect 3, upgradeable by earning Veteran medals throughout; collected Intel Points could be uploaded to the Galaxy at War system to raise Galactic Readiness.
