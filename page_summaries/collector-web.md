The Collector Web was a stationary Collector unit in Mass Effect 3, created by Possessed Trooper enemies, that had moderate barriers and no offensive capability; walking into one drastically decreased player movement speed and prevented evasion maneuvers, while enemy movement was unhindered. Webs could be taken down quickly with barrier-damaging powers such as Warp, Concussive Shot, and Overload, or with weapons like the Acolyte, Reegar Carbine, or Adas Anti-Synthetic Rifle, could otherwise be ignored unless blocking a path, and persisted after the Trooper that created them was killed, even lasting past the end of multiplayer rounds.
