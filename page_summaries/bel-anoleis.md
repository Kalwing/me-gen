Administrator Bel Anoleis, full name Rannadril Ghan Swa Fulsoom Karaten Narr Eadi Bel Anoleis, was the salarian in charge of Noveria's Port Hanshan. He represented the executive board and oversaw the local corporations' rents and day-to-day business. His secretary, Gianna Parasini, directed Commander Shepard to him to request a pass to leave Port Hanshan and pursue the investigation into Matriarch Benezia's presence on the planet.

Anoleis was an officious bureaucrat who refused to cooperate despite Shepard's Spectre status, prioritizing investor interests and stonewalling questions about Benezia, citing bad weather and the private status of Peak 15. If Shepard supplied him proof of a Port Hanshan smuggling operation, he rewarded the Commander with a garage pass and a warning. Otherwise Parasini pointed Shepard to the turian Lorik Qui'in, who revealed Anoleis had seized direct control of lab rents, taken bribes, and paid off members of Hanshan's Elanus Risk Control security to ransack Qui'in's offices at Synthetic Insights, where evidence of Anoleis' corruption was stored.

Shepard could help Qui'in recover the evidence and hand it to different parties, affecting Anoleis' fate. Given to Qui'in, Anoleis was eventually pressured by the Board to resign. Reported to Anoleis, he dismissed it as fabrication and offered access to Benezia plus credits. If Qui'in testified, it aided Parasini, an undercover Noveria Internal Affairs agent, and Anoleis was hauled away protesting; he had left no records of his deeds other than Qui'in's testimony and files. If Shepard exposed Parasini instead, Anoleis summoned her and the two killed each other in a shootout, drawing a response led by Maeko Matsuo. If Anoleis remained administrator after Benezia's death, he offered Shepard up to 400 credits for information about what happened at Peak 15, interested chiefly in potential profit. In Mass Effect 2, a surviving Parasini revealed on Illium that Anoleis was either serving time in a white-collar prison or had been fired over an incident with an asari tax inspector.
