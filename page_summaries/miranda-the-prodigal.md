"Miranda: The Prodigal" was Miranda Lawson's loyalty mission in Mass Effect 2, available after the Horizon mission and requiring Miranda in the squad. Miranda's estranged father was moving to abduct her sister Oriana, whom Miranda had taken as a baby and hidden on Illium to give her a normal life. On Illium, Miranda's asari contact Lanteia at the Eternity bar revealed that Miranda's trusted childhood friend Niket was arranging the transfer. Traveling to the Illium cargo terminal, the squad was ambushed by Eclipse mercenaries led by Captain Enyala, hired by Miranda's father, and fought through the cargo docks with an optional Renegade interrupt to kill the lead Eclipse Engineer.

At Dock 94, Miranda confronted Niket, who admitted he had sold her out because he believed Oriana deserved to know the truth. A Paragon interrupt could stop Miranda from shooting him; either way Enyala shot Niket dead, and Miranda biotically flung Enyala across the room, beginning a difficult final battle against Enyala, an asari Vanguard, a salarian Eclipse Operative and waves of mercenaries. Afterward Miranda watched Oriana with her adoptive family from a distance and could be persuaded to introduce herself or leave unseen. The mission secured Miranda's loyalty and her trust in Shepard, rewarded the Slam power, credits and element zero, and could trigger the confrontation with Jack aboard the Normandy if Jack's loyalty mission was already complete. If left uncompleted, Kai Leng mortally wounds Miranda on Horizon in Mass Effect 3. Completing it and convincing Miranda to speak to Oriana yields an email from Oriana titled "Guardian angels."
