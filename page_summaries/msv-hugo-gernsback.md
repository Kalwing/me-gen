The MSV Hugo Gernsback (registry BW46-10034-087) was a privately held frigate commanded by Captain Harris Fairchild with first officer Ronald Taylor. Built off-world, it was not rated for suborbital descent. In 2175 (the Aeia codex says 2173) it was sent to survey the newly discovered planet 2175 Aeia. On approach it was struck by an unspecified impactor that damaged its sublight drive, leaving it unable to control its descent, and it crash-landed on Aeia. Some crew, including Captain Fairchild, were killed, and Ronald Taylor became acting captain. Known crew included the officer Anders, an unnamed female doctor, and Leslie.

Ten years later, Ronald Taylor's son Jacob obtained the crash location through Cerberus channels and could pass it to Commander Shepard to investigate, forming the basis of Jacob's loyalty mission. The page notes several inconsistencies: the loss year (2173 vs 2175), the prefix (MSV in the distress call vs SSV on the hull, despite it being a civilian ship), and the named captain (Fairchild on the VI beacon vs Taylor in the codex). The name likely honored publisher Hugo Gernsback, and the registry number encoded BioWare's initials and Edmonton phone number reversed.
