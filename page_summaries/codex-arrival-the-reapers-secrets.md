This Mass Effect 2 "Arrival" DLC Codex covered three subjects tied to Dr. Amanda Kenson's research. Object Rho was a large artifact rooted into the core of the asteroid in the 157-Golgotha system. Kenson's team catalogued three findings: it was shielded by a stasis-like barrier far stronger than a biotic field, resisting even laser drilling; its interior held a quantum stasis field rivaling Prothean mass relay technology that activated in response to threat and consumed enormous power apparently drawn from dark matter; and it broadcast on many spectra, including a quantum-entanglement-like pulse reaching into Reaper territory and an infrasound signal at frequencies linked to Reaper indoctrination. Kenson's lab monitored these signals hoping for a clue to defeating the Reapers.

Aratoht was a batarian colony in the Skyllian Verge, once claimed by both human and batarian governments until the Alliance withdrew after learning of its low atmospheric pressure and oxygen; batarians saw this as cowardice and colonized it with large numbers of laborers, many of them indentured servants or slaves. Cyanobacteria dumping had raised atmospheric oxygen by a fraction of a percent, which Hegemony propaganda trumpeted as eventual victory. Short-term profits came mainly from mining its metal-rich crust. The planet was rumored to hold military bases, with information tightly restricted by the Hegemony's Ministry of Information Control.

The Alpha Relay was dated using Dr. Kenson's revived method of analyzing dust trapped in relay gravity wells, which dated relays back millions of years, some possibly predating the Protheans. Kenson concluded the oldest known relay lay near the star Bahak in batarian space. Nicknamed "Alpha," it normally functioned as a standard secondary relay but, with certain controls adjusted, could draw immense dark energy to send cargo to sixteen other relays and even to the Citadel. Hegemony authorities kept this secret, fearing Council species would read a sudden route into their space as hostile intent.
