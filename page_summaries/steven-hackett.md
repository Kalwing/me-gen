Admiral Steven Hackett was a top-ranking Systems Alliance Navy officer and commanding officer of the Fifth Fleet, based at Arcturus Station. Born in Buenos Aires in 2134, he was placed in the Advanced Training Academy for Juveniles after his mother's death in 2146, enlisted in 2152, was commissioned as a second lieutenant in 2156, and fought in the First Contact War; his rise from enlisted man to admiral became an Alliance legend.

In Mass Effect, Hackett was one of the three officers, alongside Ambassador Donnel Udina and Captain David Anderson, who recommended Commander Shepard as the first human Spectre. He frequently contacted Shepard with assignments vital to Alliance security and was generally more supportive of Shepard's decisions than Udina. During the Battle of the Citadel he commanded the Alliance reinforcements and, on Shepard's judgment, either defended or abandoned the Destiny Ascension; afterward he was promoted to head of the Alliance military. In Mass Effect: Paragon Lost he commended Lieutenant James Vega's Delta Squad on Fehl Prime and later decorated and promoted Vega.

In Mass Effect 2 Hackett helped plan a memorial for the presumed-dead Shepard, transmitted the location of the Normandy SR-1 crash site so Shepard could place a monument and recover crew dog tags, and in the Arrival DLC asked Shepard to rescue his friend Dr. Amanda Kenson from batarian custody, later debriefing Shepard after the destruction of the Alpha Relay and warning of consequences to face on Earth.

By Mass Effect 3 Hackett had become Fleet Admiral. Facing the Reaper invasion he sacrificed the Second Fleet to save the Third and Fifth, presided over "the worst military defeat in human history," and directed Shepard to Mars. He oversaw construction of Project Crucible at an undisclosed location, ordered the Illusive Man killed, mobilized the Fifth Fleet against Cerberus at Cronos Station, and delivered a pre-battle address to the assembled fleets before the assault on Earth. He commanded Shield Fleet escorting the Crucible to the Citadel; after its activation he ordered the fleets to withdraw and, in surviving endings, narrated the aftermath and the galaxy's rebuilding.
