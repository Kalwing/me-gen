Mass Effect was a science-fiction action role-playing game developed by BioWare, released for Xbox 360 on November 20, 2007 in North America and later ported to PC (2008, with Demiurge Studios) and PlayStation 3 (2012, via the Mass Effect Trilogy boxed set). A Limited Collector's Edition included an art book and the Galactic Codex: Essentials Edition 2183 booklet. In 2021 the game was remastered as part of Mass Effect Legendary Edition, which consolidated the trilogy with most of its DLC and major graphical and gameplay overhauls. The PC version replaced the Power Wheel with a tactical HUD, overhauled the inventory, and changed the hacking minigame; it originally shipped with SecuROM DRM including a 10-day revalidation, which was removed after criticism in May 2008.

The game is set in 2183 CE, 35 years after humans discovered Prothean ruins on Mars and learned mass effect physics, element zero, FTL travel, and the mass relay network. The player controls Commander Shepard aboard the SSV Normandy. A mission to recover a Prothean beacon on Eden Prime goes wrong when geth forces led by the rogue turian Spectre Saren Arterius attack. The beacon imparts visions of synthetics slaughtering organics. Shepard becomes the first human Spectre and hunts Saren, who seeks the Conduit to bring about the return of the Reapers, the machine race believed to have destroyed the Protheans. Recruitable squad members are Kaidan Alenko, Tali'Zorah nar Rayya, Liara T'Soni, Garrus Vakarian, Ashley Williams, and Urdnot Wrex. Locations include the Citadel, Eden Prime, Feros, Ilos, Luna, Noveria, Virmire, Therum, Asteroid X571, and (with DLC) Pinnacle Station.

Combat was a third-person shooter shaped by six classes (Soldier, Infiltrator, Vanguard, Adept, Engineer, Sentinel). Advancement used experience points and talent points with a level cap of 50, raised to 60 after completion. Dialogue used a paraphrase-based conversation wheel, and morality tracked Paragon and Renegade on separate scales. Travel used the Galaxy Map, with uncharted worlds explored in the M35 Mako. Save files could be transferred into later games. The Jack Wall and Sam Hulick soundtrack blended 1980s electronic music with orchestral scoring. Two DLC packs were released: Bring Down the Sky and Pinnacle Station. The game was developed under working titles including SFX, and the "Mass Effect" name was suggested by BioWare co-founder Greg Zeschuk.
