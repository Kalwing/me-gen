Mass Effect Team Assault was a planned first-person shooter set in the Mass Effect universe that was eventually scrapped, its ideas forming the basis for Mass Effect 3's cooperative multiplayer mode. It was intended as a downloadable competitive multiplayer game and was developed for four months in 2010 following the release of Mass Effect 2. Its existence was revealed through the behind-the-scenes documentary The Final Hours of Mass Effect 3. According to that documentary, its gameplay drew on Unreal Tournament and Battlefield 1943, with more open levels than any in the trilogy, suited to vehicle travel, prefab capture points, and a radar or mini-map. Footage showed three player-controlled vehicles (the M35 Mako, M-44 Hammerhead, and A-61 Mantis), the M-8 Avenger and ML-77 Missile Launcher, and third-person co-op split-screen play as Grunt and Thane fighting a krogan mercenary in the environment of the N7: Captured Mining Facility assignment. Concept art indicated four playable classes: Sniper (human male in N7-striped light gear), Bruiser (krogan), Recon (resembling default Mass Effect 2 female Commander Shepard), and Biotic (drell).
