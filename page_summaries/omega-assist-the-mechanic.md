"Omega: Assist the Mechanic" was an optional side mission in the Omega DLC for Mass Effect 3. After entering Aria's Bunker on the reclaimed side of Omega, Commander Shepard could speak with a batarian mechanic whose power inverters kept shorting out. He asked Shepard to retrieve a replacement Kehri power inverter from the field.

The inverter was located at the Talon Outpost, found after the cutscene explaining the Adjutants. It sat behind a group of boxes by the left wall near the entrance, beside a hiding salarian, near a group of turians, a batarian, and a stripe-faced krogan. Shepard needed to grab it before or after talking to Nyreen and before fighting Cerberus at the landing pad. Bringing it back to the bunker completed the task.

The mechanic and the hacker in Aria's bunker were among the only NPCs in the game who reacted to Shepard walking away mid-conversation; leaving too quickly could prevent the journal from updating with their offered missions. Even without finding the inverter, the mechanic would thank Shepard, but the mission would not register as complete, awarding no experience or Reputation and remaining in the journal permanently, even after the DLC ended.
