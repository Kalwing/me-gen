Blue Suns Legionnaires were highly skilled infantry hired and deployed by the Blue Suns mercenary organization in Mass Effect 2, ranking as high-level troops well above standard Troopers. Usually a couple were present at any Blue Suns base, always accompanied by Troopers and other Blue Suns types. They carried M-15 Vindicator battle rifles that could be armed with Disruptor Ammo, were highly accurate, rarely missed a burst, and could quickly strip away a player's defenses. Defensively they had shielding that increased in durability with the difficulty setting and could regenerate once downed, plus Tech Armor.

As with the Centurion, the Legionnaire's biggest weakness was the Vindicator's burst-fire delay, which kept it from suppressing a squad as effectively as a Heavy or Trooper and let the player coordinate concentrated firepower and powers against it. Sustained-fire weapons suppressed and damaged Legionnaires easily; on harder difficulties, picking off weaker enemies first while using powers on the Legionnaires was advised, and in close quarters they were dangerous while shielded and best handled with combined powers and gunfire. The page noted that legionnaires were the standard infantry of both the Roman Republic and Empire.
