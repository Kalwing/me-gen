Nax was a krogan mercenary encountered by Jacob Taylor on the Ahn'Kedar Orbital Platform in Mass Effect Galaxy. Jacob found him gunning down batarians inside the facility. Nax gave Jacob until a count of five to justify his life; the standoff ended when Jacob explained he was there to rescue captive scientists. The two realized their goals aligned, as Nax had come to rescue Batha, an asari scientist and an old friend of his, and they agreed to split up to cover more ground.

Later, after Jacob learned from a scientist that the formula for the cure to the batarians' bio-weapon was held by an asari in a separate facility and seemingly out of reach, a nearby door was blown open and Nax appeared, fleeing a group of batarians with Batha over his shoulder. Nax then headed to the cargo hold to help Batha manufacture the cure, insisting he would not wear an apron. Nax counting to five before attacking was noted as a likely reference to Calo Nord from Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic, and a vidscreen in the security room during Mass Effect 2's "Kasumi: Stealing Memory" shows Nax firing his assault rifle from his first cutscene.
