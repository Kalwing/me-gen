The M-920 Cain was a heavy weapon in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, nicknamed the "Nuke Launcher" or "nuke gun" because its detonation produced an archetypal mushroom cloud, though it created no fallout. In Mass Effect 2 it was described as a Normandy-prototyped high-explosive round applied to a 25-gram slug accelerated to 5 km/s. In Mass Effect 3 it was reframed as a portable particle accelerator surrounding dust-form element zero chambers that projected shearing mass effect fields, using graphite rods as neutron moderators that the omni-tool could refabricate from heavy weapon ammunition. It inflicted very high damage over a large area, effective against armor, shields and barriers.

In Mass Effect 2 the Cain was unlocked at the Normandy research station after researching three Heavy Weapon Ammo upgrades, costing 25,000 iridium. It had to be charged for about four seconds, could be interrupted by enemy hits, and on Normal difficulty killed almost anything in one shot within a huge radius. It damaged Shepard and the squad, and along with the M-490 Blackstorm was one of only two Mass Effect 2 weapons that could damage enemies through cover and terrain. Its explosion caused a brief time-dilation effect.

In Mass Effect 3 two could be found near the crashed shuttle during Priority: Earth, one used to destroy the Hades Cannon and the spare usable against Reaper forces. It was named not for the Biblical Cain but for Fallout designer Tim Cain, and bore a radiation trefoil despite not being nuclear.
