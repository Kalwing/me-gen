Priority: Eden Prime was a Mass Effect 3 mission added by the Mass Effect 3: From Ashes DLC (included by default in Legendary Edition), available after Priority: The Citadel I and completable any time before Priority: Cerberus Headquarters. An Alliance message reported that Cerberus had attacked and occupied the Eden Prime colony to secure a major Prothean artifact, and tasked Shepard with infiltrating the colony to recover it. Liara T'Soni was a mandatory squadmate, and the opposition was heavily shielded Cerberus infantry, turrets, and an Atlas mech.

On landing, the team learned that Cerberus had excavated a living Prothean held in stasis, whose lifepod had been damaged during the dig, leaving his life signs unstable. Opening the pod without killing him required both a command signal to end stasis mode and a safe physical method of opening it, each being researched at a separate Cerberus laboratory. Shepard fought through the colony to both labs. At the terminals, Shepard's eyes glowed green while viewing footage the Protheans had recorded: scenes of Prothean soldiers and the VI "Victory" during the Reaper invasion 50,000 years earlier, including the sealing of bunker bulkheads, the destruction of some 300,000 lifepods, and the broadcast of the stasis readiness signal even as refugees still tried to reach shelter. Liara noted she saw only static, and deduced that Shepard's exposure to the Prothean Cipher on Feros (or the Eden Prime beacon in a non-imported game) allowed the Commander to interpret the images.

After obtaining both the opening instructions and the command signal, Shepard returned to the pod and transmitted the signal, then defended the site against waves of Cerberus troops and an Atlas that could optionally be hijacked. Opening the pod revealed the Prothean, who introduced himself as Javik and joined the squad as a new squadmate, found afterward in the port cargo hold. He remained unavailable until Priority: Palaven was completed, stating he was still recovering from stasis. Rewards included the Particle Rifle and Cerberus intel feeding the Eden Prime: Resistance Movement side mission.
