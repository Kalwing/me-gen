Pel was a Cerberus operative who worked for the Illusive Man for many years. A large man, standing a few inches over six feet, with olive skin, a close-cropped afro and a narrow beard, he began his career as a mercenary and kept a casual disregard for human life that let him do difficult "wetwork." He cared less for Cerberus' mission to guide humanity than for profit or revenge against aliens, and usually operated in the Terminus Systems.

One of Pel's notable early jobs was posing as a bodyguard for Claude Menneau. Working alongside his unsuspecting partner Keo and with Paul Grayson, he cut Menneau's throat while the man slept aboard his starship. Pel taunted Grayson, who had dealt with Keo, by calling him "Killer," a nickname he kept.

In 2183 the Illusive Man sent Pel to Omega to meet a new contact, Golo, and obtain Migrant Fleet transmission codes Cerberus could use to monitor quarian vessels. Before leaving, Pel visited Grayson's apartment on Earth to deliver supplies for further experimentation on Gillian Grayson at the Ascension Project. Golo arranged an ambush: when he lured Feda'Gazu vas Idenna to Shelba, Pel shot Feda through a sniper scope and helped capture the pilot Hilo'Jaa vas Idenna, the only quarian taken alive. Hilo's injuries left him too sick to interrogate. Golo then relayed a Collector offer to pay well for healthy human biotics, and Pel, knowing Grayson was bringing Gillian to Omega to hide her, decided he could earn more from the Collectors. He captured Grayson's group and held them in his Omega warehouse. Lemm'Shal nar Tesleya then attacked the warehouse, forcing Pel to scramble his team. Trying to take Gillian and Hendel Mitra alive, Pel attempted to destroy their rover with a rocket launcher, not realizing Paul Grayson was behind him; Grayson shot Pel's teammate and then killed Pel.
