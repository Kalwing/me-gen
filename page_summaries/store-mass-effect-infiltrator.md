Brief reference page: the Store in the mobile game Mass Effect: Infiltrator was a menu, accessible from the main menu, pause screen or mission-failure screen, where the player spent credits earned in missions (or real money via microtransactions) on permanent upgrades for protagonist Randall Ezno. It offered weapons (M-96 Mattock Auto, M-22 Eviscerator, M-29 Incisor, M-333 Particle Beam), biotic powers (Pull, Leash, Storm, Salvo), abilities (Sprint, Slide, Cloak), melee upgrades (Punch, Push, Charge), and helmets and armor sets (Cerberus Assault, Aegis Defensive, Stealth, Infiltrator) with stat bonuses. Intel Points could be traded for credits or Mass Effect 3 Galactic Readiness. The Store was unavailable to the protagonist of the Bonus Missions, and all purchases were lost if the game was uninstalled or reset.
