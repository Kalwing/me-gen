Krogan Battlemasters were a unique enemy class in the first Mass Effect game, distinguished by biotic talent and seldom encountered due to their rarity. Commander Shepard fought a Warlord Armor-clad Battlemaster on Therum, sent by Saren Arterius with a squad of geth to capture Liara T'Soni; after finding Liara, the squad ran into the Battlemaster at the elevator to the surface. A second Battlemaster, wearing sealed Battlemaster Armor, could be fought on the Citadel in the elevator maintenance shaft beside the exterior defense grid.

The Battlemaster was one of the toughest organic enemies in the game. On top of heavy armor he carried a biotic Barrier and a regeneration ability that could refill his entire health bar, though he lacked Immunity. His melee attacks were an effective instant-kill, dealt area damage capable of downing an entire team, and could knock Shepard prone. Recommended tactics included incapacitation powers (Lift, Singularity at Master rank, Stasis, Neural Shock, Throw at Advanced rank), breaking line of sight around columns to stop his charge, Damping to block his biotics, damage-over-time ammo and Warp to deny regeneration, and Overload to strip shields. The Therum Battlemaster grew more aggressive as his geth escorts were killed, so leaving one geth alive discouraged him from charging. Bringing Kaidan on Therum was advised for access to the relevant biotic powers.
