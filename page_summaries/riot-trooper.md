Riot Troopers were advanced Cerberus soldiers equipped with M-22 Eviscerator shotguns and large bullet-resistant riot shields. They were fought by Randall Ezno on the Barn space station during his escape (Mass Effect: Foundation / Cerberus material). Depleting a Riot Trooper's armor bar broke its shield, and the shield and armor were also lost if the trooper was lifted by a biotic Pull or hit by Storm; they could still be knocked down with Slide. While carrying their shields they did not take cover, but after losing them they would take cover and stop pursuing the player. Like other humanoid enemies they took bonus damage from headshots, and the biotic power Leash always killed one outright due to their low health. They wore the Stealth armor and helmet also available to Randall Ezno, and served as the counterpart of the Guardian from Mass Effect 3.
