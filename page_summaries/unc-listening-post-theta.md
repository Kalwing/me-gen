UNC: Listening Post Theta was an assignment in Mass Effect, part of the Cerberus rachni experiment chain (prerequisite: Noveria: Matriarch Benezia, which opened access to the Styx Theta gateway cluster). An Alliance base in the Acheron system was believed to be in danger, and Commander Shepard was sent to find out what was happening. Unusually, the journal entry for the assignment was received already marked complete, from a terminal at the far end of the facility; if Shepard had already finished UNC: Listening Post Alpha, Lieutenant Durand would have mentioned this post going offline three days earlier.

The base was on Altahe, at the south center of the map, defended by four nests of three Rachni Soldiers each. After clearing them one at a time, Shepard entered the underground base on the north side. The interior was mostly clear at first, with two hardened weapon lockers behind a defense barrier and a corpse in the back of the first room. The main room held eight Rachni Workers and two Rachni Soldiers; the fast-moving Workers had to be killed before they could explode, and explosive cryo containment cells were available. A sleeping-quarters room to the southeast held a wetware kit, storage lockers, charred corpses, and strange triangular eggs that could only be destroyed. The comm room to the southwest held the objectives: accessing the Emergency FTL Comm played a distress call from Lt. Durand and granted the UNC: Listening Post Alpha assignment, or, if that was already done, the UNC: Depot Sigma-23 assignment.

The assignment was written by Chris L'Etoile and is referred to internally as "Rachni Rebirth: Listening Post Theta." A cut Investigate option was removed from the game.
