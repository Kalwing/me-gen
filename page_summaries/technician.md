Short page. The Technician was a minor enemy encountered aboard the Purgatory during the mission Dossier: The Convict in Mass Effect 2, found at the end of the hall in the room just before Jack is released. After Warden Kuril springs his trap, the Technician frantically calls for aid and is quickly killed by Shepard's squad. He is armed only with a pistol, has no armor, and carries only health on lower difficulties or light shielding on higher ones, making him very weak.
