"Omega: The Professor: Missing Assistant" was an assignment in Mass Effect 2, acquired during Dossier: The Professor. In his clinic in the slums of Omega, Mordin Solus mentioned that his assistant Daniel had gone missing in vorcha territory and asked Shepard to find him while clearing the plague from the area.

After leaving the clinic, a large fight against krogan and vorcha, and a hackable bank terminal, Shepard found Daniel behind a door on the way to environmental controls, held at gunpoint by shady batarians. Using Charm or Intimidate convinced the batarians that Daniel was trying to help cure the plague; they released him, and Shepard could let them leave or express distrust, in which case they turned hostile and were killed offscreen while Daniel covered his eyes. Refusing the persuasion options, or signaling the squad to attack, caused the batarians to shoot Daniel dead before turning hostile.

The assignment completed once Daniel left or was killed. If Daniel lived, he returned to the clinic, later emailing Shepard about improvements there, and saving him granted experience (40/50) not awarded if he died. If he died, his corpse could be examined for squadmate comments. The batarian enemies had very low shields and only 1 hitpoint of health.
