This war-asset list collected the forces contributed by the asari, coordinated by Asari High Command. Completing Priority: The Citadel II unlocked the core assets: the Asari Science Team (90), whose long-lived scholars worked on the Crucible; the Second Fleet (90), a nimble frigate-and-fighter force, boosted by 25 if Shepard encouraged Liara T'Soni to speak with Matriarch Aethyta; and the Sixth Fleet (90), the dreadnought-heavy fleet with a fearsome reputation dating to the Rachni Wars and Krogan Rebellions, boosted 40 by recovering the Library of Asha.

The Destiny Ascension (70), flagship of the Citadel Fleet under Matriarch Lidanya, joined only if it survived the 2183 Battle of the Citadel; the asari repaired and upgraded its shielding and firepower. Asari Commandos (20) came from the Kallini: Ardat-Yakshi Monastery mission, and the justicar Samara (25) joined if she survived Mass Effect 2 and was stopped from suicide with a Paragon interrupt.

Numerous planet scans in the Silean, Athena and Nimbus regions added smaller units and ships: Dr. Jelize (25), whose nanocircuitry theories guided the Crucible's circuit boards; the Armali Sniper Unit (30), commandos with strength-boosting implants firing the M-98 Widow; the Serrice Guard (30), five commandos who forced a Blood Pack slaver force to surrender after nine days; the cruisers Nefrane (30) and Cybaen (30, with dreadnought-grade barriers from an oversized drive core); Asari Research Ships (35); and Asari Engineers (30) who stress-tested the Crucible's structure. The Leviathan DLC added the Husk Neural Map (30), a study of Reaper-creature brain structures useful for civilians evading Reapers on occupied worlds. A Citadel Reaper code fragment passed to Asari High Command added 8.
