Armando-Owen Bailey was a human C-Sec officer stationed in the Zakera Ward of the Citadel in 2185 and early 2186. He began as a regular C-Sec officer, neglected in the largely turian-dominated force until the geth assault on the Citadel forced C-Sec to hire and promote more humans, including Bailey. A no-nonsense officer more interested in results than rulebooks, he had previously served with the Alliance Navy and joined C-Sec to remain in one place and spend more time with his family, though his long absences strained that relationship. He was comfortable beating suspects and accepting bribes, preferred field work, was wary of politicians, and missed Earth food, especially sockeye salmon.

In Mass Effect 2, then-Captain Bailey corrected the identity record errors that listed Shepard as dead, sparing the Commander bureaucratic delays. He aided Garrus's hunt for Sidonis by pointing out the criminal Fade, and gave Shepard and Thane Krios a lead while they searched for Thane's son. In the comic Mass Effect: Inquisition, early in 2186 Councilor Udina ordered Bailey to investigate Executor Pallin; Bailey tracked Pallin to an office building, found two dead C-Sec officers, and killed Pallin after Pallin attacked him, taking a shoulder wound. Udina then promoted Bailey to Commander and postponed his planned vacation to visit his child.

In Mass Effect 3, Commander Bailey ran C-Sec operations for the Citadel Embassies, functioning largely as an administrative assistant to the Council and unhappy with the role. He had family left on Earth during the Reaper invasion and had lost contact with his ex-wife, son, and daughter. Shepard asked him to release Eclipse leader Jona Sederis, whom Aria T'Loak had leaned on the Council to free; Bailey stalled, fearing the consequences. During the Cerberus coup, Bailey was wounded retaking C-Sec Headquarters, was saved by Shepard, set up a backup network, and helped Shepard beat Kai Leng to the Council after learning Udina was the traitor, forcing Leng to flee. If the genophage cure was sabotaged with Wrex as krogan leader, Bailey arrived with a security detail to help Shepard kill Wrex in the Normandy docking bay. In the Citadel DLC he attended Thane's memorial in silence.
