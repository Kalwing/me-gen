Arcturus Station was the de facto capital of the Systems Alliance, a Stanford torus–style space habitat built in the Arcturus system, the first interstellar system humanity reached through the Charon Relay. Arcturus lay 36 light-years from Sol; its older type-K star was orbited only by lifeless gas giants and an asteroid belt remnant, offering no colonizable worlds, but the local relay was a nexus linking countless other systems, making the system a defensible hub for Alliance expansion. Lacking a habitable planet, the Alliance built a station instead, sited at the trailing L5 Lagrangian point of the gas giant Themis.

Construction began in 2151, using the system's metal-rich asteroids for material. The toroidal core habitat was completed first, letting officers and crews move in; the outer arms, docks, and shipyards were expanded over the next eleven years. The station was officially inaugurated in 2156, though construction did not finish until around 2162, and its permanent population reached about 45,000. Codex descriptions call it a 5-kilometer-diameter Stanford torus, though sources differ on whether 5 km is the full length or the torus diameter. Its central superstructure resembled the Citadel Tower in function.

Arcturus Station housed the Alliance Parliament and Fleet Command and served as the nerve center of the Alliance Navy, guarding the mass relay leading to Earth and functioning as a shipyard where the dreadnought SSV Aconcagua was built. Its tactical importance during the Relay 314 Incident (First Contact War) was decisive: without it the Alliance could not have fielded the task force that liberated Shanxi and forced the Turian Hierarchy to negotiate. Afterward General Desolas Arterius came there to exchange prisoners with General Williams, with Jack Harper and Eva Coré traded for turian soldiers (Mass Effect: Evolution). In 2183 the station hosted the first test flight of the SSV Normandy, jointly designed with the Turian Hierarchy; Arcturus-born pilot Jeff "Joker" Moreau, watched by Captains Anderson and Dillard and General Invectus, trapped the ship's original pilot on the station, locked himself in the cockpit, evaded scrambled patrol fighters, and returned the Normandy undamaged, after which Invectus pressured the Alliance to make him the Normandy's pilot (Mass Effect: He Who Laughs Best). Fleet Command handled station defense, for a time under Admiral Steven Hackett; developer intent had the First Fleet stationed there with Hackett and the Fifth Fleet at the Citadel, though the games muddled this.

In 2186 Arcturus Station was the site of a battle between the Alliance's Second, Third, and Fifth Fleets and a Reaper force bound for Sol. The Alliance held against about a dozen Reaper capital ships, but the bulk of the Reaper fleet bypassed the station and surged through the relay toward Earth. Unable to win, Hackett abandoned the station and sacrificed the Second Fleet so the Third and Fifth could escape. Arcturus Station was then utterly destroyed; per Donnel Udina, the entire Alliance Parliament and Prime Minister Amul Shastri were aboard and killed, leaving a grave of more than 45,000 people. Intelligence from the battle could later be salvaged from the wreckage.
