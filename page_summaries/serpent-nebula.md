The Serpent Nebula was a dense nebula surrounding the ancient Citadel and one of the station's most effective defenses. Its thick gas and dust made it impossible for enemy ships to launch an organized attack or to locate the Citadel without coordinates. Commander Shepard could get a good view of it from the Upper Wards.

The composition and origin of the Serpent Nebula were subjects of debate; some believed the Citadel itself had created the nebula over millennia by ejecting particles and debris from its self-repair processes into space.

The nebula was home to dozens of mass relays, and several races' home clusters were connected directly to the Citadel in a single jump: the Annos Basin (salarians), the Apien Crest (turians), the Athena Nebula (asari), the Krogan DMZ (krogan), and the Silean Nebula (elcor). Its systems included Boltzmann, Wheeler, and Widow, the last containing the Citadel itself, and, in later games, Aria's Fleet.

Routes to and from the Serpent Nebula changed across the games. In Mass Effect it connected directly to the Horse Head Nebula and the Exodus Cluster, with Local Cluster travel routed through the Exodus Cluster first. In Mass Effect 2 the Local Cluster and the Ismar Frontier gained direct lanes to it. In Mass Effect 3 travel from the Horse Head Nebula or Exodus Cluster was routed through the Annos Basin, and travel from the Ismar Frontier through the Minos Wasteland. The nebula was the hub for numerous Citadel missions and assignments across all three games. Unused text in the first game implies the cluster was called "Citadel Cluster" at some point in development.
