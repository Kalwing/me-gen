Lieutenant Steve Cortez was an Alliance pilot aboard the Normandy SR-2 in 2186, flying the UT-47A Kodiak that carried Commander Shepard and squad to missions, and a potential romance option for a male Shepard. An only child who lost both parents years earlier, Cortez had a husband, Robert, who was killed when the Collectors abducted the colony of Ferris Fields in 2185; Robert phoned Steve during the attack to tell him to escape and let him go. Steve kept a recording of the call and grieved heavily. He had served in the Alliance since around 2176, first in the First Fleet aboard the SSV Hawking flying F-61 Tridents, and five months before the Reaper invasion joined the Normandy SR-2 retrofit team, moving the armory to the shuttle bay.

Aboard the Normandy, Cortez maintained the armory alongside Lt. James Vega, an old friend from Fehl Prime, and preferred to keep busy to avoid thinking about Robert. Shepard could persuade him to take shore leave on the Citadel; he later visited the refugees' Memorial Wall to say goodbye to Robert and began to move on, reconnecting with old pilot friends and forwarding their information to Admiral Hackett to give the Alliance more pilots. A meeting at Purgatory could begin a relationship with a male Shepard or reinforce their friendship.

During the final assault on Earth, Cortez's shuttle was shot down by a Harvester after dropping off the squad. If he had visited the Memorial Wall he survived the crash and could be contacted by hologram; otherwise he died and Vega confirmed it, and his name appeared on the Normandy's memorial wall as "Steven Cortez." In the Leviathan DLC he crash-landed the shuttle and devised the plan for Shepard to reach Leviathan using a Triton mech. In the Citadel DLC he provided suppressive fire during the Spectre Archives infiltration, flew a skycar with Joker to block the hijacked Normandy from jumping to FTL, delivered a restrained Maya Brooks, and could take Shepard on a joyride in the Kodiak with the inertial dampeners disabled. If the Crucible was activated and the Reapers were controlled or Synthesis was chosen, Cortez placed Shepard's name on the memorial wall.
