The Mass Effect Original Soundtrack was released on November 20, 2007. It was composed by Jack Wall and Sam Hulick with additional music by Richard Jacques and David Kates. The final-credits song, "M4 Part II," was written by Faunts, an Edmonton band from the city where BioWare's main studio developed the game. The press release described the score as inspired by classic science fiction films such as Blade Runner and Dune, blending 1980s electronic music with modern orchestral scoring; project director Casey Hudson described it as a synthesis of originality and familiarity. The album ran 37 tracks, including "Mass Effect Theme," "The Normandy," "Battle at Eden Prime," "Sovereign's Theme," "Vigil," and "M4 Part II." Several in-game compositions were omitted from the album. In May 2021, BioWare released the soundtrack as a free bonus content download (available until July 31, 2021 or until the download limit was reached), omitting "M4 Part II" and adding as track 1 a new song, "Resynthesis" by Sam Hulick, originally made for the cancelled Cerberus Brainchild DLC. The Limited Collector's Edition bonus disc included an exclusive track called "Strangers" and used alternate names for several tracks, and the Australian iTunes store mistitled the album "Mass Effects" with further errors. "Uncharted Worlds," the galaxy map theme, appeared in the 2025 documentary What Exactly Is Inside Jupiter?
