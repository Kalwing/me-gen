Marksman was a combat power in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes, distinct from the Marksman unlockable ability in the first Mass Effect. When activated it boosted weapon accuracy and firing rate for a short time, with a base recharge of 6 seconds and a 6-second duration. Higher ranks improved recharge speed, duration, accuracy, firing rate, and headshot damage, and the power carried an innate 25% headshot damage bonus. As of patch 1.04 it automatically reloaded the current weapon in multiplayer, though self-cooling weapons such as the Particle Rifle and M-7 Lancer instead began recharging their magazines instantly.

The power did not reduce recoil and could make muzzle climb harder to control, but its accuracy bonuses could make some weapons fully accurate in cover or aiming down sights, and could negate screen shake on weapons like the N7 Typhoon. It worked well on inaccurate fully automatic weapons, large-magazine weapons, and slow-firing high-damage weapons such as the M-12 Locust, M-99 Saber, and M-97 Viper, and produced tight groupings with shotguns and the M-358 Talon. Grenade powers and the Vanguard's Nova could still be used while it was active, and it rendered the user immune to Seeker Swarm and Seeker Plague disabling effects. In single-player it could be dovetailed with Tactical Cloak for near-constant uptime.

Ashley Williams's version was bugged in many releases: activating it caused her omni-tool to endlessly activate and sometimes froze her in place, with no official fix planned until Legendary Edition corrected it. The power was available in single-player to Ashley Williams and to Shepard as a bonus power, and in multiplayer to the Turian Soldier and Quarian Marksman Soldier.
