The eighth chapter of Geoff Keighley's documentary profiles lead designer Preston Watamaniuk, who has completed each Mass Effect roughly 60 or 70 times and can speed-run the first game in one hour and 38 minutes, three minutes off the world record. His broad knowledge of play styles feeds team meetings.

It recounts that during the final two weeks on Mass Effect 2 Watamaniuk realized there was no payoff for players faithful to a first-game romance, and with no time to record dialogue the team added a simple scene of Shepard looking at a photo of the love interest. He also pushed for a "massive consequence" in Mass Effect 3 for the more than 70 percent of players who, per statistics revealed in the book, pursued a side romance in Mass Effect 2.

The chapter describes the team's goal of making Mass Effect 3's choices genuinely agonizing, unlike earlier games where killing Wrex mainly swapped in Wreav. Two plot points were identified as highest-impact: resolving the quarian–geth war, and whether to deploy the salarians' new genophage cure. Walters describes wanting players to be unsure they made the right choice, and a video segment explains the design of the Interrupt allowing Shepard to shoot Mordin or Padok Wiks in the back as they walk toward the Shroud controls, with time slowed. The chapter also notes that Mass Effect 3 introduced same-sex relationships for both male and female Shepard after checking that the voice actors were comfortable, and that in March 2011 the team had to confront that the scope exceeded the schedule.

An interactive "Consequence of Choice" feature walks through the Citadel showdown with Ashley (or Kaidan), showing how a hidden Trust value built from choices across all three games, romancing Ashley, cheating, saving the Council, kindness on Mars, hospital visits, whether Thane or Kirrahe survived, and asking the crew to lower weapons, produces at least four distinct resolutions, ranging from Ashley siding with Shepard to shooting Udina herself to refusing and, at negative Trust, telling Shepard "I hope the Reapers send you to hell."
