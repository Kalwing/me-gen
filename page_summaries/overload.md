Overload was a tech power appearing in the single-player and multiplayer modes of Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. It fired an electromagnetic pulse that travelled instantly in a straight line from user to target, inflicting heavy damage against shields and synthetic enemies while being far less effective against organic health. The corresponding talent in the first Mass Effect was Electronics, and the Andromeda equivalent was the Overload skill.

In Mass Effect 2, Overload dealt double its listed damage to shields and could, at higher ranks, stun unprotected synthetics and overheat enemy infantry weapons for several seconds. Its rank 4 evolutions were Heavy Overload, which made synthetics explode on death, and Area Overload, which widened the impact radius to three meters for hitting multiple targets. It could detonate flamethrower-wielding enemies and bypass Collector Guardian hex shields. Users included the Engineer, Sentinel, Dr. Amanda Kenson, Garrus Vakarian, Kasumi Goto, Miranda Lawson, and Wilson.

In Mass Effect 3, Overload lost its impact area and weapon-overheat effect but gained the ability to damage organic health at a penalty, deal triple damage to all barriers and shields, and briefly stagger most infantry regardless of protection. Its evolutions included Chain Overload (arcing to additional targets), Neural Shock (incapacitating weaker organics), and Shield Damage (further multiplying anti-barrier damage). It could both prime a Tech Burst and, with chain evolutions, detonate multiple Power Combos. Single-player users included the Engineer, Sentinel, EDI, Garrus Vakarian, Kaidan Alenko, Nyreen Kandros, Kasumi Goto, and Miranda Lawson.
