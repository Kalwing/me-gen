This page collects a promotional social-media broadcast BioWare ran to advertise the Mass Effect 3: Omega DLC. In the fiction, during 2186 Cerberus invaded and occupied the station Omega under General Oleg Petrovsky, having lured Aria T'Loak off-station, and a rebellion led by the Talon mercenary gang rose against them. A communication war followed across three comm channels: ANN (@AllianceNewsNet) reporting events and rumors, the Talons (@FightForOmega) broadcasting on low-tech microwave emissions in "Talon Cryptolect," and Cerberus (@Omega_Cerberus), Petrovsky's official citizen feed of directives and propaganda. The Talons hacked ANN to recruit "Codebreakers" to decrypt Cerberus's coded messages, which used a simple substitution cipher. The messages track skirmishes through the Fumi and Zeta Districts, with Nyreen Kandros drawing Cerberus forces away so extraction teams could evacuate civilians.

Representative lines:

Nyreen Kandros / the Talons, opening broadcast: "Cerberus may have taken over Omega, but that doesn't mean they've won. This is our territory. Our home. ... The Talons have not abandoned you. We shall not rest until Cerberus is out of Omega and your homes are returned to you."

Cerberus, propaganda feed: "As we secure Omega against Reaper incursions, please cooperate with all Cerberus directives. Your safety is our top priority."

Cerberus, recruiting: "Cerberus is recruiting! No experience? No problem! Our orientation provides combat training and gene therapy packages. Change your life now!"

General Petrovsky, quoted on the Cerberus feed: "The civilized being prizes freedom above all. Yet civilization itself is only possible through unyielding discipline."

Nyreen Kandros, spotted in Fumi District: "Base leaders, codebreakers are reporting I've been spotted by Cerberus forces. Looks like I'm the decoy."

Cerberus, decoded cipher message: "Locking down Fumi District due to persistent Talon activity. Full purge authorized--get every Talon. Petrovsky's orders."

Nyreen Kandros, closing: "Cerberus may have the upper hand, but they can't keep us down. We're all in this together, Omega civilians and friends alike."
