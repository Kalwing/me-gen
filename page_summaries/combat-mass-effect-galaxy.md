Combat (Mass Effect Galaxy) was a game-mechanics overview page for the iOS game Mass Effect Galaxy, a top-down shooter in which the player controlled former Alliance soldier Jacob Taylor through single-screen maze-like combat zones cleared by killing every enemy. It covered the touch-screen controls with accelerometer tilt movement that auto-recalibrated each zone and after each pause, the pause-enabling power wheel and the HUD's green and red lock-on reticles, Jacob's single M-8 Avenger assault rifle that never overheats and has automatic aim-lock with no weapon switching or melee, the abilities selected from the powers wheel with their blue/orange/grey states, the absence of a true cover system beyond hiding behind walls, health and auto-recharging shields with floor pick-ups for both the player and enemies, and the shield overload tech power, noting that unlike Mass Effect 2 and 3 biotic powers still worked against shielded targets.
