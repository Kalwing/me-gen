Protect the Council was the final mission of Mass Effect Galaxy, following A Batarian Army. After completing three investigation missions, a cutscene and a conversation with the ailing Illo Nazario took place aboard Jacob Taylor's ship. In exchange for medical care, Nazario revealed that the true target of the batarian extremists was the Citadel Council itself, not Ambassador Jath'Amon as previously believed. In fact, Jath'Amon was the leader of the batarian extremists, and he intended to infect the Council with a bioweapon during his peace talks. Jacob and Miranda Lawson set course for the Citadel to end the threat.

On arrival, Miranda told Jacob that Batha's vaccine would protect him from the bioweapon. Jacob headed for the Council tower, crossing an area guarded by two friendly turrets. He was stopped by a C-Sec officer who was exposed as a batarian, revealing that the terrorists had infiltrated Citadel Security. Fighting through combat zones held by Terrorists, Terrorist Elites and a Terrorist Leader, Jacob entered the Council chambers during the ambassador's speech. The asari Council representative questioned his intrusion; Jacob presented his evidence, Jath'Amon denied it, and the representative refused to act until certain of the claims.

The furious ambassador then revealed his plan, released the virus into the Citadel tower, and fled by air. Jacob warned everyone to escape and fought through five further combat zones crowded with batarian terrorists and turrets, reaching a final zone where he confronted Jath'Amon, two bodyguards and two turrets. After Jacob defeated him, Jath'Amon swore revenge on Jacob and the Council as Citadel Security led him away. Miranda thanked Shepard before the game's concluding cinematic. The mission featured nine combat zones.
