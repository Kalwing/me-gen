The Armax Arsenal Arena was a combat simulator complex on the Citadel's Silversun Strip, introduced in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC. It featured a large rectangular room capable of projecting fully immersive holographic environments and scenarios for public entertainment. Spectators watched from upper areas outside the room and bought refreshments at a nearby food kiosk. Combat participants redeemed prizes at a company store, used procurement and weapon upgrade terminals, and could be treated in an adjacent infirmary. Kiosks were staffed by VIs in the form of female turians. Below the spectators' level was an athletes-only area with a scenario-setting terminal and a ready room containing an armor selection computer, a weapon bench, a rules datapad, and the elevator to the simulator. The venue started nearly empty and grew more crowded with spectators as more matches were fought.

Commander Shepard battled holographic enemies to score points and earn prizes, receiving a complimentary pass from the arena staff after retaking the Normandy. Initially only Cerberus and geth enemy sets were available at the Foot Soldier level; additional maps, enemy factions (Reapers, Collectors, and Mirror Match), ally licenses, and score modifiers were unlocked as prizes. Enemies could be fought at Foot Soldier, Elite, and Super Elite challenge levels. Up to two squad members could provide support, including former squadmates Urdnot Wrex, Miranda Lawson, Jack, Jacob Taylor, Samara, Grunt, Zaeed Massani, and Kasumi Goto, provided they were alive and their loyalty issues resolved and their ally licenses purchased from the Bronze prize pool. Maps included Wingman, Blasteroid, Spin Zone, Prime Evil, The Armax Classic, Pain Train, Brimstone, and Fatal Error. Six score modifiers, such as No Medi-Gel and One-Shot Player Shields, adjusted scoring bonuses.
