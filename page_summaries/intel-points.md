Intel Points were a mechanism introduced in Mass Effect: Infiltrator to improve Galactic Readiness for Mass Effect 3: Galaxy at War. Randall Ezno and the Turian from the Bonus Missions could acquire them during their missions. They were randomly looted from fallen enemies, even those killed by something other than the player, and once looted remained visible on the battlefield until a new game was loaded. Because the Combat Analysis screen often appeared before the player could reach them, collecting them sometimes required launching the next checkpoint.

Intel Points could be traded to raise the Galactic Readiness Rating in Mass Effect 3, with the bonus per point varying; an internet connection was required to link the game account with Mass Effect: Infiltrator. Alternatively, they could be exchanged within Mass Effect: Infiltrator itself for credits, at a rate of 25 credits per point.
