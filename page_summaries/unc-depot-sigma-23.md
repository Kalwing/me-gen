"UNC: Depot Sigma-23" was a Mass Effect assignment and the culmination of the Cerberus rachni experiment chain, requiring completion of both "UNC: Listening Post Alpha" and "UNC: Listening Post Theta." Data from Listening Post Theta indicated that a supply ship had come from Depot Sigma-23 in the Gorgon system of the Argos Rho cluster just before the attacks on the two listening posts, marking it as the likely origin of the rachni infestation. The assignment was acquired from a terminal on Altahe or from Lt. Durand on Nepmos.

Shepard boarded the Unidentified Space Facility and fought through numerous Rachni Workers and Rachni Soldiers in the cluttered freighter interior. In the left module of the forward section, a terminal held data logs by Major Elena Flores: the station was a Cerberus munitions and barracks depot built to house two reinforced platoons of Cerberus Commandos; a special shipment of rachni had arrived, meant to develop in proximity to a "master control unit," with Flores planning to test them on local pirates; the rachni then escaped, seized the freighters and set them to random destinations. Flores blamed Cerberus for treating the creatures "like animals" rather than POWs and urged "the General" to destroy the station. Shepard armed demolition charges next to the terminal, triggering a roughly 70-second countdown that did not stop, and had to fight past a fresh wave of five Rachni Workers and a Rachni Soldier blocking the route back to the Normandy before escaping to complete the assignment. The cargo hold shared its layout with the MSV Cornucopia from "UNC: Derelict Freighter."
