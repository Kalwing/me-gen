This page covers the Sniper enemy in the mobile game Mass Effect: Infiltrator, distinct from the Cerberus Sniper of the first game and the Nemesis of Mass Effect 3. Snipers were Cerberus troops equipped with Incisor sniper rifles and specialised for long-range engagements. They were fought by the player character Randall Ezno aboard the Barn space station.

In combat, Snipers had low health protected by a weak, regenerating layer of shielding, a fragility offset by very high damage output. They typically stayed behind cover, emerging only to fire, and could be pulled into the open with the Pull power. Like other humanoid Infiltrator enemies they took bonus damage from headshots. Because the game did not display the player's health, a single sniper shot could kill Ezno without warning. Every Sniper shot was preceded by a red targeting beam; on seeing one, the player had to dive into cover or use Slide to avoid an otherwise near-certain one-hit kill. Snipers wore the Stealth armor and helmet that were also available to Randall Ezno.
