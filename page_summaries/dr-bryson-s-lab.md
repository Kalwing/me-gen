Dr. Bryson's Lab was a location on the Citadel's Presidium that served as both the private residence of Dr. Garret Bryson and the operational center for the Systems Alliance's Task Force Aurora, a research team he headed. Admiral Hackett ordered Commander Shepard to visit the lab to assist Bryson's investigation into the Leviathan, a mandate to pursue any and all leads on the entity. Shepard's involvement with the lab spanned the missions Citadel: Dr. Bryson, Citadel: Leviathan I, II and III, and Leviathan: Find Ann Bryson.

The lab was cluttered with research data. A galaxy map could be accessed to track locations with potential Leviathan leads and cross-reference scattered data points. A transparent display case held a Prothean artifact and a mysterious spherical artifact, and nearby terminals contained audio logs and analyses of the sphere. A worktable held bones, a microscope, and a severed husk head that screamed when interacted with and eventually exploded. Other stations displayed data on crimes whose perpetrators had no memory of committing them, storage lockers of offworld exploration gear, and equipment for studying geological samples containing element zero.

An adjacent studio held more clutter, including a hanging plesiosaur fossil, desks with data on ancient rachni movements and comparisons between the Thorian and Leviathan, one of Ann Bryson's childhood drawings, and a wall of creature sightings that revealed extra data points under a different light spectrum. The passage out contained books, a couch, an encrypted project terminal, and a Reaper fragment kept as a souvenir from Sovereign's invasion of the Citadel.

Upstairs, the bedroom held audio logs proving the Batarian Hegemony's complicity in the disappearance of the Leviathan of Dis, a video log of Ann Bryson, and sentimental personal items including another of Ann's drawings made when she was nine. The bathroom held nothing of significance beyond a stone painting echoing artwork from the Pylos Nebula.
