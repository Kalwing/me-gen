Marab was a friendly-natured salarian customer service representative employed at Saronis Applications, encountered in Mass Effect 2. A great admirer of Commander Shepard, he was one of the few individuals to recognize the Commander without prompting. Manipulating him into offering a discount was markedly less hostile than similar exchanges with other vendors, since he could be deceived rather than confronted or coerced. Marab also held the volus Kor Tun's credit chit, which helped resolve a dispute between the volus and a quarian bystander.
