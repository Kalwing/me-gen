Kal'Reegar was a quarian marine of the Migrant Fleet Marines. In Mass Effect 2, Commander Shepard met him on the former quarian colony of Haestrom in the Far Rim, where he was part of a squad assigned to protect Tali'Zorah vas Neema during her research on the system's dying sun. Geth attacked and killed most of the expedition, leaving only Reegar and Tali. Shepard found him pinned at a bridge, firing a missile launcher at a geth platoon including a Geth Colossus that was trying to reach Tali's observatory. His suit was punctured and he was injured, but he was reluctant to stand down; Shepard could order him to hold back or accept his help against the Colossus. If Shepard took too long in the fight without benching him, Reegar was killed; if he survived, he recovered with only a minor infection.

If he survived Haestrom, Shepard could meet him again during Tali's loyalty mission on the flotilla, where he was one of few quarians who publicly held Tali in high regard during her treason trial and could testify to her character. He argued that a war to reclaim the homeworld from the geth would be futile and harmful to the Fleet, though he would never shirk his military duty. If Shepard rallied the crowd, Reegar declared Tali had done more for the Fleet than any Admiral and said that if she were exiled, he should be too. Admirals Raan and Gerrel sought his opinion and accepted his recommendation to let Tali retake the Alarei. He was afterward assigned to escort quarian techs investigating the dark energy buildup.

In Mass Effect 3, if the quarians survived their war with the geth and Reegar had survived Haestrom, Shepard received an email that Reegar and his squad had died on Palaven repairing and protecting a communications tower relaying critical information to the Turian Hierarchy. The Reegar Carbine was named after his family of marines; cut content gave his full name as Kal'Reegar vas Lomas.
