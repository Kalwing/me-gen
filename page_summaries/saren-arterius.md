Saren Arterius, born 2139, was the longest-serving turian Spectre, an agent of the Council for 24 years and a zealous defender of stability in the Skyllian Verge. He entered the turian military at 15, was promoted to active service after a year, and in 2159 became the youngest turian ever accepted into the Spectres. Intelligent, cunning, and ruthless, he got results despite persistent rumors about his brutal methods. He recruited both Avitus Rix and Nihlus Kryik. He became an outspoken opponent of human expansion; Anita Goyle told David Anderson that Saren lost a brother in the First Contact War and had hated humanity since. His principles were "Never kill anyone without a good reason" and "You can always find a reason to kill someone."

In Mass Effect: Evolution (2157), Saren escorted the human prisoners Jack Harper and Eva Coré and aided his brother Desolas, who planned to use a Prothean Monolith to evolve the turians into dominant "meta-turians." Recognizing the extent of Desolas's madness after a second monolith was uncovered beneath Temple Palaven, Saren destroyed the temple with Desolas inside and vowed to avenge him. In Mass Effect: Revelation (2165), while investigating arms dealing and the Blue Suns, Saren tortured the batarian Groto Ib-ba, saved Kahlee Sanders, Anderson, and Jon Grissom from the krogan Skarr, then abandoned Anderson during a raid, destroyed an element zero refinery, killed Dr. Shu Qian and Edan Had'dah, and took their research on an artifact he came to believe was a weapon to control the geth and humble humanity. He gave a damning report that cost Anderson a Spectre candidacy.

Pursuing the artifact, Saren encountered Sovereign and learned the fate of past civilizations. Rather than wield the Reaper as a weapon, he resolved to save organics by serving the Reapers and proving their worth, not realizing Sovereign was steadily indoctrinating him. He became Sovereign's most visible agent, rallying the geth as their "prophet," with Matriarch Benezia—herself indoctrinated—as his ally. He led the geth attack on Eden Prime to use the Prothean Beacon, murdered Nihlus, and evaded a Council hearing until Tali'Zorah's audio evidence cost him his Spectre status. He sought the Conduit via the Mu Relay, using the Rachni Queen, the Thorian's Cipher, and a genophage cure. Confronted on Virmire, he escaped a nuclear blast; Shepard's accusation of indoctrination bred doubt, prompting Sovereign to give him further implants. On Ilos he used the Conduit to reach the Citadel. Shepard could convince him to shoot himself—"Goodbye, Shepard. Thank you."—or kill him in battle, after which Sovereign reanimated his body for a final fight whose destruction crippled the Reaper.
