The Firepower Pack was a downloadable content pack for Mass Effect 2, released on August 3, 2010. It added three new weapons to the game: the M-5 Phalanx heavy pistol, the M-96 Mattock heavy rifle, and the Geth Plasma Shotgun. The Phalanx was described as a hard-hitting hand cannon fitted with an integral laser sight. The Mattock was a semi-automatic hybrid of sniper and assault rifle, usable by any class with an assault rifle skill. The Geth Plasma Shotgun was a three-barreled weapon that fired superconductors to create plasma on impact, could be charged for greater damage, and functioned at longer range than standard shotguns.

Each weapon arrived aboard the Normandy with an accompanying email from the Illusive Man. He explained that Jacob had acquired M5 Phalanx pistols from unnamed contacts, and that he was sending more Phalanxes along with spare laser-sight parts and repair manuals. For the Mattock, he credited EDI with suggesting that proven older technologies were being overlooked in favour of state-of-the-art gear; the delivered rifles were the reliable weapons long used by colonist militias, modified to accept thermal clips. For the Geth Plasma Shotgun, he said a Cerberus cell had acquired the weapon from the geth, and that its specialized ammunition was not difficult to manufacture or modify.

The pack cost 160 BioWare Points on PC and $1.99 USD on consoles. According to developer commentary, each weapon was designed with a full understanding of Mass Effect 2 and intended to disrupt rather than fit into the existing weapon ecosystem, giving experienced players a fresh experience.
