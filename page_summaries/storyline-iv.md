This page is a chronological plot synopsis of the Mass Effect: Andromeda-era fiction.

In Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation, Cora Harper, working for Alec Ryder and his prototype AI SAM-E, recovered a stolen Andromeda Initiative AI codebase, surviving a betrayal by mercenary Ygara Menoris and a massacre of cyborg "augments" run by the rogue AI Medea at the Alliance black site Quiet Eddy; SAM-E was later folded back into SAM after a bombing aboard the Hyperion, and Cora committed to the Initiative. In Mass Effect: Discovery, turian Tiran Kandros infiltrated the Initiative under the alias "Sanjin Vinnick" with biotic officer Luna Shanks, chased the quarian scientist Shio'Leth vas Novarra and the powerful biotic Agent Zeta (with help from Nyreen Kandros and Aria T'Loak on Omega), recovered corrected astro-cartographic data on Andromeda's golden worlds, and chose to join the Initiative after being dishonorably discharged.

In Mass Effect Andromeda: Annihilation, aboard the ark Keelah Si'yah, Sleepwalkers led by Senna'Nir, including Anax Therion, Borbala Ferank, the elcor doctor Yorrik and the hanar Ysses, fought the Fortinbras Plague and a computer worm; the grandmother VI Liat'Nir was sacrificed into the ship's systems, and Captain Qetsi'Olam was revealed as the plague's designer, aided by Malak'Rafa. Cured by walking the ship as a living retrovirus, Qetsi died. In Mass Effect Andromeda: Nexus Uprising, security director Sloane Kelly, Jarun Tann, Foster Addison, Nakmor Kesh and engineer Calix Corvannis dealt with the Scourge-crippled Nexus; Tann waking Nakmor Morda's krogan to crush Calix's rebellion led Sloane and the exiles to leave the station.

The Mass Effect: Andromeda recap covered Ryder awakening at Habitat 7, Alec Ryder's death and the transfer of the Pathfinder role and SAM to a Ryder twin, arrival at the damaged Nexus, the settling of Eos, first contact with the angara on Aya (Jaal Ama Darav, Evfra de Tershaav, the Moshae Sjefa), the rescue of the Moshae and discovery of kett "exaltation," the pursuit of the Archon, the Kadara exiles (Sloane Kelly, Reyes Vidal), the liberation of Ark Paarchero and salarian Pathfinder Zevin Raeka, the journey to Khi Tasira and Meridian, and the Battle of Meridian ending the Archon and terraforming the Heleus Cluster.
