Pitne For was a male volus merchant who did business between Illium and the Terminus Systems. His business partner, Dakni Kur, was murdered in a district of Nos Astra on Illium by Eclipse mercenaries. Fearing for his life despite two turian bodyguards, and afraid the justicar Samara present at the crime scene would "go crazy and start shooting," Pitne was eager to leave the planet, but Detective Anaya would not let him go while investigating the murder, in which he was a suspect.

When Commander Shepard arrived at the spaceport to recruit Samara, they could question Pitne about Samara's influence on the district and about the killing. Pitne claimed to run only legitimate business and said he had no idea why the Eclipse would target him and his partner, adding that Dakni Kur had been killed with a modded shotgun of the type Eclipse used. Later, at Samara's prompting, Shepard confronted him and he admitted that he and Dakni Kur had swindled the Eclipse by selling them a biotic-enhancing drug without mentioning that lethality was a possible side effect. He then handed Shepard his pass card to the Eclipse base.

Inside the base, Shepard could learn that Elnora had killed Dakni Kur with a close-range shotgun blast; his body was recovered by police the next day and identified by Pitne. A shipping manifest in the base revealed Pitne had smuggled red sand onto Illium. Giving that evidence to Detective Anaya led to Pitne's arrest and a small reward for Shepard; giving it instead to Pitne earned a larger reward.
