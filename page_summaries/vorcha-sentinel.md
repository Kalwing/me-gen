The Vorcha Sentinel was a playable class in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer. Its background noted that vorcha were considered vermin by other Citadel species but that their unique physiology and innate aggressiveness made them shockingly durable combatants, their malleable DNA allowing them to adapt to any situation and their lightning-quick pounce a product of societally ingrained bloodlust.

The class had 250 shield and 750 health under Vorcha Resilience, with the powers Bloodlust, Flamer, Cluster Grenade, and Fitness. Its regular melee was a continuous omni-tool claw slash; its heavy melee was a pounce and outward slash that decapitated humanoid enemies on a fatal blow; its grab dragged an enemy over cover and executed them with four alternating omni-claw slashes. Unlike other volus and some classes, the Vorcha Sentinel could take cover and roll between cover, and dodged by leaping away on all fours.

Bloodlust could be triggered by almost any enemy kill or assist, excluding minor summoned enemies, with each stack lasting fifteen seconds, forcing constant kills to maintain the maximum bonuses; unlike krogan Rage it did not require melee kills or killing blows. Flamer could be swung across multiple enemies to set them alight, and Cluster Grenades detonated Fire Explosions for potential killstreaks. Choosing Cluster Grenade over Flamer enabled a no-cooldown build that let the class carry the heaviest weapons as long as Bloodlust stayed active. The Reegar Carbine paired well with the class's regenerative Bloodlust to make it a durable close-quarters tank. Heavy melee was fast and closed distance like a krogan charge but risked death against strong enemies with instant-kill animations. The vorcha multiplayer classes had unique callouts, referring to Phantoms as "Ghosts!" and Brutes as "Big monster!"
