Calyn was the elcor ambassador to the Citadel, sharing an embassy office with Din Korlack, the volus ambassador. Calyn was more accepting of humans than his volus counterpart and would freely share information about elcor culture and history with Commander Shepard. Like most elcor, he was even-tempered, and he often chastised Din Korlack for his rudeness.

When Shepard first met Calyn, he was trying to help Xeltan, a fellow elcor, with Xeltan's complaint against the consort Sha'ira. Shepard's conduct in assisting Xeltan as part of an assignment earned Calyn's approval, and Calyn made a positive comment to Din Korlack about how humans behaved.

In Mass Effect: Retribution, Calyn worked alongside David Anderson during trade negotiations involving the Vol Protectorate and the Turian Hierarchy. Calyn expressed exasperation with the tempestuous volus ambassador. Anderson privately harbored frustration with Calyn but kept quiet, because the Systems Alliance needed the support of the elcor government. When Anderson jokingly suggested smacking the volus ambassador the next time he became annoying, Calyn expressed shock and horror, saying humans had a disturbing sense of humor.
