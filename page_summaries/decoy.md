Decoy was a tech power in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes. When activated, it created a stationary hologram roughly three to five meters from the user that mimicked the user's appearance and movements but did not move or attack, drawing enemy fire away from the user. At rank 1 it had an eight-second recharge, a fifteen-second duration, and 1000 decoy shields. Higher ranks improved recharge speed, duration, and shield strength, and offered branching evolutions: a Shock effect that damaged and staggered nearby enemies, and an Exploding Decoy that detonated for 300 damage across four meters when destroyed or when its timer expired.

The power was most effective against ranged attackers, whom it could halt and expose, such as Geth Hunters, Geth Pyros, Geth Primes, and their summoned turrets and drones, and it was useful for drawing sniper fire and grenades from Cerberus and CAT6 forces, including during the Kai Leng fight. Many melee-focused Reaper enemies ignored Decoy entirely, including Husks, Abominations, Brutes, Scions, Praetorians, Ravagers, Atlases, and others, as did the final bosses of the Citadel DLC. Enemies that watched the Decoy spawn often ignored it, though less intelligent enemies such as Cannibals were more reliably fooled, and Cerberus troops could be heard saying "drone deployed." Kai Leng could be fooled by it. Unlike other summoned "pet" powers, the Decoy appeared at a fixed distance in front of the user, defaulting to directly on top of the user if space was lacking. Enemies firing hitscan weapons were highly inaccurate against it, while area-effect attacks like grenades and rocket fire destroyed it easily. If EDI was downed while her Decoy was active, the hologram also dropped and stopped drawing fire, standing back up if she was revived.

In single-player, Decoy was available to EDI, Kasumi Goto, and Commander Shepard as a bonus power; in multiplayer it was used by the Salarian Engineer and the Volus Mercenary Sentinel.
