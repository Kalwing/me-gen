The page compiled Codex entries on humanity and its interstellar government. Donnel Udina was described as the lone human on the Citadel Council; depending on Shepard's choice, David Anderson held the seat first and resigned in frustration with Council politics (with Udina replacing him), or Udina held it from the start. Earth was entering a new golden age fueled by colonial wealth, though still divided into nation-states under the Systems Alliance and marked by a widening rich-poor gap and past environmental damage; a Mass Effect 3 version described its devastation by the Reapers, who treated its cities as stores of human DNA. The First Contact War began in 2157 when a turian patrol attacked a human fleet activating a dormant relay, occupied the colony Shanxi (the only human world ever conquered), and was driven off by Admiral Kastanie Drescher's Second Fleet before the Council forced a truce. The Systems Alliance grew from national space programs into humanity's supranational government, gaining credibility after Shanxi and forming its own Parliament. "Rise of the Alliance" entries gave Paragon and Renegade accounts of humanity gaining associate Citadel membership in 2165 and either full membership or dominance in 2183, at the cost of eight named cruisers and 2,400 crew, and noted the resulting galactic resentment.

Secondary entries covered the privately owned Alliance News Network and its embedded reporter Diana Allers; genetic engineering law under the 2161 Sudham-Wolcott Genetic Heritage Act, which permitted screening, therapy, and enhancement but banned adding new abilities or creating sapient life; Gagarin Station ("Jump Zero"), built beyond Pluto to test FTL drives and later an Alliance biotic-training and AI-research site where the AI "Eliza" achieved sapience in 2172; human diplomatic relations with the major species; the Alliance Geological Service's survey bounties; Alliance military doctrine of bypassing strongpoints and striking rear areas; military jargon and the rank structure; the Military Vocational Code and the N-designation system; Interplanetary Combatives Training, whose graduates earned the N7 designation; the xenophobic Terra Firma Party; and a timeline running from the 2069 founding of Armstrong Outpost through Shepard's 2154 birth, the 2157 First Contact War, the 2183 attack on the Citadel, the 2185 Collector abductions, and updates to 2186.
