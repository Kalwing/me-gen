This page collects cut and non-functional dialogue from Prologue: Find the Beacon in the first Mass Effect, set aboard the SSV Normandy and on Eden Prime, reconstructed from leftover files. It is explicitly non-canon. Much of it belongs to a longer planned version of the Eden Prime mission with disabled Makos, aerocultures, crop harvesters, a geth sniper, and more obstacles. A fully choreographed Normandy cantina scene had Kaidan biotically hurl a cup at Jenkins; Dr. Chakwas later references a similar incident in Mass Effect 2.

Jenkins, in the cantina pestering Kaidan for a biotics demonstration: "Ah come on, man! The whole crew's talking about it, let me see! Do something easy, one of the chairs or something." After Kaidan throws the cup: "Erm, no... No, I'm good, Lieutenant, I... better head down below."

Chakwas, afterward: "That was a little extreme. Are you feeling alright, Lieutenant?" Kaidan: "Just a slight headache. Between Jenkins and Joker, there's always someone yapping in my ear." Chakwas: "It's not their fault and you know it. Those damned biotic implants..."

Jenkins, on Eden Prime's gas bags: "Gas bags. Don't worry - they're harmless." Later: "Oh, yeah. Don't stand next to them when the shooting starts."

Ashley, on the hexa-cows: "They're hexa-cows. Local herd species. High milk yields. Meat's a bit tough, though." Kaidan: "They make steaks out of those things? I'm glad I'm a vegetarian."

Kaidan, spotting destroyed geth: "I bet it was Nihlus."

Ashley, at Sovereign's landing imprint: "I've never seen a ship big enough to leave a landing imprint like that. Not in our fleet." Kaidan: "Or anyone else's."
