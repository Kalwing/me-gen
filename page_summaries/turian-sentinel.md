The Turian Sentinel was a playable class in Mass Effect 3's cooperative multiplayer. Its lore text restated the turian ethos: a strict moral code that "the needs of the group outweigh that of the individual," discipline as the foundation of society, and lines that never collapse but fall back in order setting ambushes, so "you will only see a turian's back when they're dead." Turians received the best military training in the galaxy, their weapon proficiency, focus and determination offsetting a lack of speed and agility.

Mechanically the class had 750 shields and 500 health, the Turian Veteran passive, and the powers Warp, Overload and Tech Armor, plus Fitness. It had no dodge but could roll between cover, and used omni-blade melee attacks. It was a durable, well-armored fire-support character effective against every enemy type, using Overload to strip shields and barriers, Warp to weaken armor and set up Tech Bursts, and Tech Armor for damage reduction and a staggering detonation.
