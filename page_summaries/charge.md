Charge, also called Biotic Charge, was a biotic power available in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, in both single-player and multiplayer. It enveloped the user in a mass effect field that launched them almost instantaneously across the battlefield toward a locked enemy target, ending in a collision that damaged and knocked back most unprotected foes. It required a clear line of sight and a HUD target lock, could not be used to teleport to empty ground, and did not trigger against destructible objects such as explosive canisters. It allowed the user to phase through solid obstacles en route and could multiply physics damage against enemies already levitated by other biotics.

In Mass Effect 2 the power had three ranks before a rank 4 evolution into Heavy Charge, which added force, full shield restoration, and an 80% game-speed slow for 1.15 seconds, or Area Charge, which burst on impact with a 3-meter radius to hit multiple enemies. Recharge time was 6 seconds and range 40 meters at all ranks. From rank 2 onward Charge restored and temporarily boosted the user's shields, the maximum-shield bonus calculated from a base value of 250 and behaving like the Tech Armor bonus. It dealt only token damage, 25/35/45/45 base by rank and 60 for Heavy Charge. It was intentionally unaffected by the Biotic Cooldown upgrade, though Mass Effect Legendary Edition changed this. In Mass Effect 2 it was available to the Vanguard class and to Tela Vasir.

In Mass Effect 3 Charge had six ranks with branching evolutions at ranks 4, 5, and 6: Force & Damage or Radius; Weapon Synergy or Power Synergy; and Bonus Power or Barrier. Base recharge was 10 seconds, 14 for krogan, and the user was invulnerable while the power was in effect. It no longer raised maximum shields; instead a successful Charge granted a 50% damage-reduction buff for 4 seconds and restored 50% of barriers, or 100% with the rank 6 Barrier evolution. Charge dealt 1.5x damage to barriers, completed even if its target died en route, and set up Biotic Explosions when combined with lasting biotics such as Warp or Pull. Alongside Nova it was a Vanguard signature move. It was available in single-player to the Vanguard and in multiplayer to eight Vanguard variants, including the Asari, Krogan Battlemaster, Volus Protector, and N7 Slayer. Trivia noted that the Cabal Vanguard used Poison Strike instead of Charge.
