Mass Effect 3: Rebellion Pack was a free multiplayer DLC pack for Mass Effect 3, released May 29, 2012 for Xbox 360 and PC worldwide and in North America for PlayStation 3, May 30, 2012 for PlayStation 3 in Europe, and included on-disc with Mass Effect 3: Special Edition for Wii U. It added two maps (Firebase Jade and Firebase Goddess); six multiplayer characters (Vorcha Soldier, Vorcha Sentinel, Quarian Male Engineer, Quarian Male Infiltrator, Project Phoenix Vanguard, Project Phoenix Adept); four weapons (Cerberus Harrier, Reegar Carbine, Krysae Sniper Rifle, Prothean Particle Rifle); the Retrieval match objective, in which teams carry high-priority packages to an extraction zone during waves 3, 6 and 10; and the persistent Gear equipment slot for bonuses that do not expire at match end.
