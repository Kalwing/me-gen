Falere was the youngest of Samara's three daughters and an Ardat-Yakshi. At a young age she chose to live out her days in the monastery on Lesuss with her sister Rila, while their sister Morinth escaped. In Mass Effect 3, Commander Shepard met Falere on Lesuss while investigating a distress signal, and she was saved from a Cannibal by Shepard's squad or by Samara if present. Falere explained that the Reapers were turning the Ardat-Yakshi into Banshees and begged Shepard to save Rila from the monastery's Great Hall. When told about the bomb meant to destroy the monastery, she grew angry, accused the Commander of not trying to help people, and rushed ahead alone to save her sister.

In the Great Hall, Falere was attacked by Rila, who had begun to suffer indoctrination. Rila fought off the Reapers' control, found the bomb's detonator, and insisted Falere leave with Shepard while she stayed behind. Falere struggled but was dragged to an elevator by Shepard. Rila, mortally wounded by a Banshee, declared that the Ardat-Yakshi were not the Reapers' slaves and triggered the detonator. Outside, Falere was distraught with Shepard for letting Rila die. If Samara was present, she attempted suicide rather than break the Justicar code requiring her to kill her last living daughter; if stopped, Falere resolved to stay in the monastery and Samara joined the war effort. If Samara did not survive, Shepard could let Falere remain on the planet, where she promised never to be taken alive by the Reapers, or kill her as a risk.
