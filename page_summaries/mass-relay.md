Mass Relays were mass transit devices scattered across the Milky Way, forming a galaxy-wide network that enabled near-instantaneous interstellar travel between paired or linked relays, turning journeys of years or centuries into days or hours. Each relay consisted of two fifteen-kilometer curved metal arms around a set of revolving gyroscopic rings five kilometers across, containing a blue-glowing core of element zero. They were built from the same unknown, extremely resilient material as the Citadel and protected by a quantum shield that locked their structure at the subatomic level, letting them survive even a supernova's wake. They emitted no heat or radiation, making displaced relays hard to locate. Relays worked by creating a nearly mass-free corridor of space-time; a pilot supplied the transit mass before being moved into the approach corridor, and ships emerged with positional "drift" of thousands of kilometers, making relays impractical to mine or trap. Primary relays could propel a ship thousands of light years but linked only to a single partner; secondary relays linked to any relay over shorter distances of a few hundred light years. After the Rachni Wars, Council Space species refused to open a primary relay without knowing its destination; human pioneers ignorant of this prohibition opened relays at random while exploring, contributing to the First Contact War with the turians. Many relays were dormant but easily reactivated; a Prothean data cache on Mars led humanity to the Charon Relay, encased in ice and orbiting Pluto, previously thought to be the moon Charon.

Despite common belief, the relays were created not by the Protheans but by the Reapers. Sovereign revealed that the Protheans were merely one of many species to find the relays and the Citadel; using them, galactic civilizations evolve along paths the Reapers desire and advance faster, shortening the time between harvests and streamlining galactic extinction. The Protheans nonetheless cracked the relays' operating principle and used it to build the Conduit. Commander Shepard discovered that the Citadel itself is an enormous inactive mass relay leading to dark space and the control center for the entire network, letting the Reapers sever cluster-to-cluster travel on their return. Dr. Amanda Kenson's team calculated that a large enough mass striking a relay could destroy it, producing a supernova-scale explosion; steering an asteroid into the Bahak system's Alpha Relay annihilated the system and killed more than 300,000 people. During the battle for Earth, activating the Crucible transmitted its energy through the relay network from the Citadel, overloading the relays' element zero cores; the relays were rebuilt afterward by survivors (or by the Reapers under the control option), with more extensive damage and harder rebuilding if Shepard rallied insufficient forces. The Relay Monument on the Presidium is Prothean artwork interpreted as either vanity or a symbol of galactic unity. In cut content the relays were called "phase gates."
