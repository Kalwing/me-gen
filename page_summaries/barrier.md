Barrier was a biotic ability appearing in Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, and Mass Effect 3, distinct from the Andromeda skill of the same name. The page explained that biotic barriers used mass effect fields to protect the user much like a kinetic barrier, but with unique characteristics.

In the first Mass Effect, Barrier was a biotic talent that surrounded the user with a high-gravity mass effect field, effectively strengthening the player's shields for a set period or until the extra points were depleted by damage. It did not protect against physical damage such as melee attacks, explosive ordnance, flying debris, or environmental hazards. It scaled from Barrier (400 points absorbed, 10 seconds) through Advanced Barrier and Master Barrier (1000 points, 23 seconds), and unlocked Stasis at higher ranks. Bastions and Vanguard Shock Troopers gained Barrier Specialization at level 9, which increased strength and duration and let the shield regenerate while taking damage. Classes with access were the Adept, Sentinel, unlockable Vanguard, Asari Scientist, and Krogan Battlemaster. Changing body armor cancelled an active Barrier.

In Mass Effect 2, Barrier created a shield that soaked large amounts of damage, functionally equivalent to Fortification and Geth Shield Boost. It added a percentage of maximum shields (up to +100% with the Heavy Barrier evolution) and could evolve into Heavy Barrier or the longer-lasting Improved Barrier. It uniquely had no activation animation, making Shepard less likely to be interrupted under fire, and could be used while storming. It was available to Shepard via Advanced Training and as Jacob Taylor's loyalty power.

In Mass Effect 3, Barrier reinforced armor with a biotic field that reduced all damage taken while slowing power use, and could be detonated to lift nearby targets in an omnidirectional blast that dealt 1.5x damage to barriers and hit up to three targets. Its rank evolutions included Recharge, Blast Effect, Barrier Strength, Shield Recharge, Power Synergy, and Power Recharge. It was available to Kaidan Alenko, Urdnot Wrex, Jacob Taylor, and Shepard as a bonus power in single-player, and to the Krogan Battlemaster Vanguard and Krogan Shaman Adept in multiplayer. The trivia noted that in Mass Effect: Ascension, Hendel Mitra used a Barrier more like a stationary geth barrier than an in-game biotic Barrier.
