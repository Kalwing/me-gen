Zhu's Hope was the small settlement and port where Commander Shepard docked upon arriving at Feros. Built into one of Feros's ancient crumbling skyscrapers, it made use of ancient Prothean water conduits and abandoned hydroponic bays, and colonists sometimes hunted varren for food. It served as ExoGeni Corporation's main port, while the larger colony lived up the Skyway near ExoGeni headquarters. Conversations with Fai Dan and Arcelia indicated that before the geth assault the main colony held about a thousand inhabitants and the outpost about 200. Zhu's Hope was centered on a crashed freighter called the Borealis, which the salarian merchant Ledra had brought intending to profit by supplying the fledgling colony but which the colonists now used mainly for shelter.

During Mass Effect, ExoGeni secretly used Zhu's Hope as an experimental control group after discovering the Thorian beneath the colony. The ExoGeni VI's last report before the geth attack stated that 85% of colonists were infected with mind-controlling Thorian spores, which made them act strangely and turn violent when Shepard returned from ExoGeni headquarters. Shepard's actions determined the settlement's fate: shooting Ethan Jeong caused ExoGeni to pull funding but left Zhu's Hope free to grow independently, while persuading Jeong to spin the incident as good publicity brought ExoGeni investment to rebuild it, contingent on how many colonists survived.

In Mass Effect 2, if Shepard killed more colonists than were saved with the anti-Thorian gas, the Feros colony was shut down and survivors relocated to Earth; otherwise Zhu's Hope persisted. Either way ExoGeni's stock rose. Surviving colonists suffered lingering side-effects from Thorian exposure and signed a one-sided contract with the medical firm Baria Frontiers; Shiala, if alive, appointed herself their intermediary and traveled to Illium to renegotiate it.

In Mass Effect 3, if Shiala and the colony survived and their medical issues were addressed, Zhu's Hope held out well against a Reaper attack: the lingering Thorian hive-mind let colonists coordinate and rapidly learn combat skills, mainly from Shiala, buying time to fully evacuate. After Tuchanka and the genophage cure, Shepard received an email from Shiala describing the situation and providing additional War Assets, along with an ANN report on the colony's actions.
