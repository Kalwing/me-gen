This archive collected Cerberus Daily News reports from May 2185, dominated by the aftermath of the Vallum Blast and the start of the war on Taetrus. Minister of Agriculture Idus Valen became Taetrian Primarch by succession; galactic sympathy poured in, with Thessia playing the turian anthem, the quarian Migrant Fleet sending supply cruisers, and the Salarian Union donating heavy equipment. Investigators narrowed the culprits to the separatist group Facinus, which sought to split the island continent of Eluria from the Turian Hierarchy; a Facinus video showed leader Kihilix Tanus praising pilot Vamire Squaron, who had used an improvised FTL plotter, and threatening weekly attacks unless Eluria was recognized as the independent state of Parthenix. Final tallies put recovered bodies at 4,278, estimated dead at 138,000, and repair costs at 338 billion credits; a nine-year-old child known as "Octa" was pulled alive from a collapsed museum, and four turians were arrested for harvesting organs from corpses at an overwhelmed hospital. The Carifinum emergency agency was criticized for a delayed response. On 13 May, Primarch Valen declared war on Facinus with Hierarchy support; over 60,000 Hierarchy troops under General Partinax arrived in orbit, met by Valen and General Timus Aurelos, while Diluvian provincial ruler Pavan Sahira insisted Facinus had already left. The air campaign, dubbed the Constant Lightning Offensive, began on 25 May with press barred from the battle zone, flying over 2,100 sorties a day and establishing air supremacy; an HCC News poll found 93 percent supported the war and 54 percent would accept weapons of mass destruction. Related items covered a government reorganization ceremony swearing in minister Anela Saneraxis, clawball legend Neno Raxirian volunteering for service, the MSV Atlanta crew arrested for spying, and reporters chartering the MSV Zothera to test the blast scenario. Elsewhere, corporatists on Garvug intercepted a weapons shipment blamed on Clan Urdnot (dismissed by Urdnot Jatt), the TR-15 Letus probe team was accused of falsifying neutron-star data by Dr. Aurana T'meles with team lead Dr. Akil Carinii apologizing for "sloppy record-keeping," and a "Cerberus witch hunt" saw over 300 human media workers fired after the steganography discovery, with Emily Wong among those surviving a mass firing.
