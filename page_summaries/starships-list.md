This reference page cataloged every identified starship in the Mass Effect universe, giving each ship's class, affiliation, a brief description and its source work. Entries spanned Alliance, turian, asari, salarian, volus, geth, Collector, Reaper, Cerberus, batarian, Migrant Fleet, Andromeda Initiative and independent vessels, drawn from the games, novels, comics, Cerberus Daily News and DLC.

Notable Reaper entries included Sovereign, Harbinger, the Derelict Reaper above Mnemosyne, and the billion-year-old Leviathan of Dis. Council-race capital ships included the asari dreadnought Destiny Ascension (flagship of the Citadel Fleet, commanded by Matriarch Lidanya), the Geth Dreadnought destroyed by Commander Shepard in 2186, the Prothean flagship Penumbra Apex, and the Vol Protectorate's sole dreadnought Kwunu. The Alliance section listed the Everest- and Kilimanjaro-class dreadnoughts (SSV Everest, Kilimanjaro, Orizaba, Logan and others), numerous Fifth Fleet cruisers that fought at the Battle of the Citadel, and both Normandy frigates. Cerberus fielded the cruiser Elbrus under Oleg Petrovsky; the Blue Suns ran the prison ship Purgatory; Liara T'Soni inherited the Shadow Broker Base.

The Migrant Fleet accounted for many entries, including the liveships Rayya, Chayym and Shellen, Admiralty crew ships such as Neema, Tonbay, Moreh and Qwib-Qwib, and the lab ship Alarei. Dozens of independent MSV freighters (many Kowloon- or Athabasca-class) were logged, most lost to pirates, geth, mechanical failure or crew breakdown. The Andromeda Initiative section covered the arks Hyperion, Nexus, Natanus, Leusinia, Paarchero and Keelah Si'yah, the Pathfinder survey ship Tempest, and the ND1 Nomad; the kett fielded the Archon's flagship Verakan and the Decimation.
