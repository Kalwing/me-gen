Access Corridor was a level in the mobile game Mass Effect: Infiltrator. Having contacted the Systems Alliance and arranged a rendezvous, Randall Ezno had to fight his way through the access tunnels of the Cerberus facility known as the Barn back to the Hangar Bay to steal a shuttle. The level contained six checkpoints.

When Randall left the comm relay's elevator, the station alarm sounded and emergency red lighting activated, putting the facility's synthetic defensive forces on alert. Across the first four checkpoints he pushed through corridors and rooms defended by Grad Turrets, squads of Orcus Mechs, Geth Troopers, a Geth Juggernaut, Cerberus Troopers, Assault Troopers, Riot Troopers, Centurions, snipers, and engineers, with Krogan Prisoners and geth sometimes fighting the Cerberus forces. Throughout the fighting the Director taunted Randall over comms and explained his plan for the human race, which Randall ignored, while a volus hacker ally relayed warnings, including that geth deployed from the Comm Relay had spread across the station.

The final two checkpoints took place in the Hangar Bay. On the upper platform Randall fought patrolling Troopers, a distant sniper reachable only with the M-29 Incisor sniper rifle, jetpack-equipped Assault Troopers, and Riot Troopers led by a Centurion. At the last checkpoint he faced Troopers backed by a powerful Ogre Mech, more Riot Troopers and a Centurion, and then a Geth Dropship that deployed Geth Troopers and Geth Juggernauts alongside further Cerberus reinforcements. After clearing all forces, Randall boarded a shuttle bound for LV426, his rendezvous point with the Alliance, and his volus ally wished him luck before cutting communication. The level connected the missions Comm Relay and Desert Planet.
