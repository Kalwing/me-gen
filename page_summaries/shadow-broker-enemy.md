This page covered fighting the Shadow Broker as a boss in the Mass Effect 2 DLC Lair of the Shadow Broker. The Shadow Broker was the individual at the head of an information-trading organization that always sold to the highest bidder. After Shepard and Liara T'Soni tracked and infiltrated the Broker's ship, they broke into his intel center and confronted him in person, where he was revealed to be a member of a pre-spaceflight race called the yahg. His brute strength knocked out one of Shepard's squadmates, forcing Shepard and Liara to fight him alone.

In combat the Broker continuously fired a Revenant while closing distance, then charged, an attack that could shatter the cover pylons in the room and damage through them, and could melee with his shield at close range. He carried shields, armor and health and replenished shields and armor once stripped; in the fight's later stages he also carried an impenetrable omni-shield, and while his shields and armor were down he was invincible to everything except melee attacks.

Recommended tactics were to strip shields with rapid-fire Disruptor Ammo and Overload, then armor with Incendiary Ammo, Incinerate or Warp, then run in and melee him, repeating the strip-and-melee cycle three times. In later stages powers had to be aimed carefully around his omni-shield, targeting his left leg or arm to stagger him. Liara's Singularity or Stasis briefly stunned him. The page also noted class-specific approaches, exploits using the room's pillars and staircases to make him charge into obstacles or stand idle, and that heavy weapons like the M-920 Cain were impractical.
