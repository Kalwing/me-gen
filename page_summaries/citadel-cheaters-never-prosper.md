Citadel: Cheaters Never Prosper was an assignment cut from Mass Effect during development, set on the Citadel. Commander Shepard would have dealt with a cheater known as "Beck" who needed some "data," possibly described as "unencoded"; after acquiring it, Shepard would choose to hand it to Beck or to help a hanar by giving him the data or letting him collect Beck's reward, then select the reward type (weapon or armor) to complete the assignment. Beck also appeared in the cut mission Citadel: Oculon Syndicate, where he may have gone against Harkin, and the plot resembles the shipped mission Citadel: Schells the Gambler.
