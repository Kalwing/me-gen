Mass Effect: Foundation 13, tagged "This Aimless Dance," was published July 23, 2014, the final issue of the series, written by Mac Walters and Jeremy Barlow with art by Tony Parker. In 2184, the Cerberus agent Rasa moved to steal Commander Shepard's backup clone from the Lazarus Research Station. Interspersed flashbacks to 2160 showed her childhood as a pickpocket raised by an abusive master, informing her refusal to be owned by anyone, Cerberus included.

Accompanied by a LOKI Mech, Rasa seized the clone's tank; the theft turned bloody when a staffer recognized her and she killed all other agents present. Miranda Lawson found the clone lab gone dark, its camera feeds hijacked to display images of Blasto, and the station's mainframe crippled by a virus Rasa had planted. Rasa suited up beside an airlock amid active fire suppression. When Miranda's security team cornered her, the LOKI and YMIR Mechs Miranda had brought switched to Rasa's side and massacred her human troops.

As Rasa neared an escape Kodiak with the clone, a sniper round from a recovered Miranda hit her mech. Rasa's LOKI transferred the tank harness to Rasa's own body and put itself in Miranda's path to stall her. While the LOKI crept behind Miranda during a conversation about how much each woman owed Cerberus, Rasa loaded the clone aboard the shuttle and escaped. At the postmortem debrief, the Illusive Man ordered Miranda to scrub any retrieval team, deeming Rasa and the clone irrelevant next to the coming of the Reapers and the real Shepard. At an undisclosed location, Rasa monologued to the dormant clone about trust and betrayal and vowed to make it superior to the original.
