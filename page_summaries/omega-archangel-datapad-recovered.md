"Omega: Archangel: Datapad Recovered" was a short Mass Effect 2 assignment acquired during "Dossier: Archangel" by reading one of the datapads near the Eclipse leader Jaroth. The datapad was a message from Jaroth to Tarak revealing that Garm and the Blood Pack had joined the operation and that none of the three mercenary organizations (Blue Suns, Eclipse, Blood Pack) could move against Aria T'Loak without the other two.

After the Archangel recruitment mission, Shepard delivered the datapad to Aria at the Afterlife Club. Aria glanced at it, flung it in Garka's face, and demanded to know how the information had slipped the net; Garka said he would look into it. Aria thanked Shepard and, if "Omega: The Patriarch" was already complete, forwarded coordinates to an Eclipse depot she had no use for. Accepting the reward or saying "Just like that?" gave +2 Renegade points; refusing it gave +2 Paragon points, and Aria sent the coordinates regardless. The experience reward was 40 (50).
