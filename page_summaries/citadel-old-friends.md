Citadel: Old Friends was a Mass Effect assignment available only to a Commander Shepard with the Earthborn background, after one of the core missions had been completed: finding Liara T'Soni, Noveria: Matriarch Benezia, or Feros: The Thorian. Outside Chora's Den in the Wards, a human named Finch addressed Shepard in passing. Finch was a member of the Tenth Street Reds, a gang on Earth to which Shepard had once belonged, and said another member, Curt Weisman, had been arrested by the turians for a "minor offence" committed while in turian space. Finch wanted Shepard to talk to the turian guard in Chora's Den to secure Weisman's release, and threatened to reveal Shepard's shady past with the Reds otherwise.

From the guard, Shepard learned that the Reds had become a violent anti-alien group and that Weisman had been responsible for a hate crime, poisoning medical cargo meant for a turian colony that could have killed millions. Shepard could negotiate Weisman's release, inform the guard that Finch was likely to try to free the prisoner, or change course after hearing about the crime. Tipping the guard off gave Paragon points, while using Intimidate or Charm to free the prisoner gave Renegade points.

If Shepard did not free Weisman, Finch appeared and declared he would tell the galaxy that Shepard had once been affiliated with the Reds and their anti-alien agenda. Shepard could talk him down or simply shoot him; killing Finch impressed the turian guard, whereas letting him go made the guard think less of the first human Spectre. In Mass Effect 2, Shepard received a message from Finch titled "Hello from the Reds" encouraging Shepard's fight against the Collectors. The assignment was written by Trick Weekes and was named "Memories of Green" in the game's internal data.
