This page documents romance across the original Mass Effect trilogy for both male and female Commander Shepard. In Mass Effect, Shepard could pursue a heterosexual human romance (male Shepard with Ashley Williams, female Shepard with Kaidan Alenko), a xenophilic romance for either gender with Liara T'Soni, or a one-time sexual encounter with the asari Consort Sha'ira. Romance progressed through conversation stages (Attraction, Courting, the Liara complication, an optional love-triangle confrontation, the Virmire Sacrifice, Lockdown, and a Conclusion scene en route to Ilos), and completing it unlocked the Paramour achievement. Because of a programming workaround, all human romance lines were voiced for both Shepards, allowing same-sex romances via save edits. The game's sex scene drew media controversy and a temporary ban in Singapore.

In Mass Effect 2, an imported love interest carried over (a picture appears in Shepard's cabin) though they could not join the squad; Kaidan or Ashley sent a letter, and Liara's romance could continue in Lair of the Shadow Broker. Primary, mutually exclusive romances were: for male Shepard, Miranda Lawson, Tali'Zorah, or Jack; for female Shepard, Jacob Taylor, Garrus Vakarian, or Thane Krios. Secondary romances not granting Paramour were Kelly Chambers, Samara (Paragon only, unconsummated), and Morinth (whose romance scene killed Shepard, a critical mission failure). Romances were built by repeated favorable conversation, "locked in" at a specific dialogue point, and could be challenged by a competing love interest, forcing Shepard to dump the locked-in partner permanently. The page details the exact lock-in lines for each squadmate and the full multi-step requirements for romancing Kelly, including having dinner before the Collectors abduct the crew and entering the Omega 4 relay immediately to save her.

In Mass Effect 3, most prior romances could continue, some could begin anew, and some required an imported rekindled save. New interests were Diana Allers, Samantha Traynor, and Steve Cortez; Ashley/Kaidan became exclusive heterosexual options and Cortez/Traynor exclusive same-sex options. Mechanics were simpler, with a final lock-in before the assault on the Illusive Man's base and an intimate scene at Cronos Station. The Extended Cut added a Conduit-run farewell if the partner is in the squad (they die to Harbinger if Effective Military Strength is too low) and a memorial-wall or hopeful-smile beat depending on the ending. The Citadel DLC allowed extra romances including Javik and James Vega for female Shepard. Legendary Edition reworked the Paramour achievements to require relationships established across one, two, and three games.
