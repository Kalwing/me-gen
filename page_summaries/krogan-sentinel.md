The Krogan Sentinel was a playable class in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer, with 1000 shields and 750 health, and the powers Incinerate, Lift Grenade, Tech Armor and Rage. Its melee set was the Krogan Headbutt, the Krogan Charge heavy melee, and a physical-punch over-cover grab; it had no dodge and could not roll between cover.

It functioned much like the Krogan Soldier, with Incinerate replacing Carnage, Lift Grenade replacing Inferno Grenade, and Tech Armor replacing Fortification, making it slightly less bulky but more versatile. A common build ignored Incinerate to fully evolve the other four skills, letting it carry heavy weapons and Tech Armor without cooldown concerns since Lift Grenade had none. Tech Armor plus Rage damage-reduction evolutions gave strong protection, and the class could also be built for pure melee damage without needing to destroy its armor. Player notes covered faction threats: Phantoms and Nemeses for Cerberus, Praetorians for the Collectors, Hunters and Primes for the geth, and Banshees for the Reapers, the last to be avoided entirely since a slow krogan could be grabbed and instant-killed.
