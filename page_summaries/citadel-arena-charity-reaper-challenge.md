Citadel Arena: Charity Reaper Challenge was a side mission in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, available after Citadel Docks: Retake the Normandy, once the Armax Arsenal Arena opened with the rest of Silversun Strip. After Shepard completed some arena matches, an asari named Manava T'Khanna emailed the Combat Sim Controls. She wrote as a parent rather than a fundraiser: her daughter Lati had been diagnosed with a cerebral tumor a year earlier, had the bad tissue removed by microsurgery machines, and faced a slow recovery with only partial use of her limbs. Lati had wanted to be a huntress and wished to see Commander Shepard compete live; a small charity called Help a Dream had brought her to the Citadel. Manava asked Shepard to fight the toughest Reapers in the simulator and dedicate the match to Help a Dream.

To complete the challenge, Shepard set the simulator to Reapers at Super Elite difficulty, both of which had to be purchased with prior winnings as the silver-level Reaper Combat Set and the gold-level Super Elite enemy set, and completed the match. Any squadmates and modifiers were allowed, so other arena challenges such as the Prothean Challenge or Medi-Gel Challenge could be finished simultaneously. Afterward Manava sent a grateful second email, calling Shepard a hero rather than just a soldier. Receiving and reading it granted +15 Reputation.
