Donnel Udina was humanity's ambassador to the Citadel Council, succeeding Anita Goyle, and a career diplomat determined to advance human interests while avoiding actions that might endanger humanity's political standing. He was apprehensive of Commander Shepard, particularly after Shepard's induction as the first human Spectre, and repeatedly demanded irrefutable proof before acting.

In Mass Effect, Udina pushed for a human Spectre as a step toward a Council seat. He blamed Shepard for the Eden Prime mission, regretted bringing Captain Anderson into the hearing against Saren Arterius (which cleared Saren), and directed Shepard to Harkin for leads. He constantly criticized Shepard's handling of Feros, Noveria, and Virmire, and reported the Council's doubts about Shepard's Reaper warnings. When Shepard tried to warn of the Reaper invasion, Udina locked down the Normandy; Anderson countermanded the order, knocking Udina unconscious to use his computer. Udina survived Sovereign's attack on the Citadel. If the Council survived, humanity was offered a Council seat, and Shepard could nominate either Udina or Anderson.

In Mass Effect 2, if nominated, Udina became the first human Councilor with Anderson as his aide; if Anderson was Councilor, Udina served as his adviser. In Mass Effect: Retribution (2186) Udina was the human Councilor with Admiral Anderson as advisor; after Anderson's turian-assisted strikes on Cerberus, Udina threatened an investigation and Anderson resigned. In Mass Effect: Inquisition, Udina tasked Bailey with investigating Executor Pallin, promoted Bailey to Commander, and dismissed Bailey's doubts about Pallin's guilt.

In Mass Effect 3, following the destruction of Arcturus Station and the deaths of the Alliance Parliament and Prime Minister Amul Shastri, Udina became the Alliance's most powerful political figure. He supported the Crucible, instituted a colonial draft, pressed allies for resources, appointed the Virmire survivor as the second human Spectre, and converted Presidium docks into refugee housing. Desperate after the Council refused to aid Earth, Udina conspired with Cerberus in a coup to arrest the Council and seize emergency powers. The Salarian Councilor exposed him; the coup, worsened by Kai Leng's conflicting goals, became a massacre. Shepard cornered Udina, who tried to frame Shepard and open the door for Cerberus; when he shoved and threatened the Asari Councilor with a pistol, he was shot dead by Shepard or the Virmire survivor. Anderson and Shepard later speculated he might have been indoctrinated.
