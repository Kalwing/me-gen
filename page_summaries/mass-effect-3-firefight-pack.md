Mass Effect 3: Firefight Pack was a DLC pack for Mass Effect 3 adding seven weapons to the single-player campaign, released on all platforms on August 7, 2012. The weapons were the M-90 Indra and Krysae sniper rifles, the Reegar Carbine, the Cerberus Harrier battle rifle, the Geth Plasma SMG, and the newly created Blood Pack Punisher SMG and Adas Anti-Synthetic Rifle. Four of the guns had previously been available only in multiplayer. It cost 160 BioWare Points on PC and $1.99 on PS3 and Xbox 360, and was on-disc in the Wii U version.
