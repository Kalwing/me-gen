The M-92 Mantis was a single-shot sniper rifle in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, manufactured by Devlon Industries and primarily used by police and planetary militia groups. It was incredibly accurate at long range and able to take out most targets in one shot, offset by a slow rate of fire and low ammunition reserve. It was effective against armor but weaker against shields and biotic barriers, and the prevalence of kinetic barriers had made it less popular with militaries than in the Terminus Systems.

In Mass Effect 2 it was granted automatically at the start of Freedom's Progress to a Soldier or Infiltrator, and usable by Garrus, Legion, Thane and Zaeed. Infiltrators and Soldiers could line up devastating headshots using Adrenaline Rush, Tactical Cloak and time-dilation effects. Against protected enemies on higher difficulties it lost its one-shot-kill potential, requiring the correct ammo power for each protection layer; the M-98 Widow was a direct upgrade.

In Mass Effect 3 it was given automatically to Infiltrators at the start of Priority: Mars, otherwise found on dead Alliance soldiers there, and was one of five multiplayer starter weapons, categorized as common. It was easily the most powerful weapon early in the game, one-shotting unprotected light targets even on higher difficulties, but suffered from shield gates, high weight, and few spare clips. It was an effective sniping option for Ashley or Garrus until weapons like the M-98 Widow, Black Widow or Javelin became available.
