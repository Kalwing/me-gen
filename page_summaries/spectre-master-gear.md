Spectre - Master Gear items are prototype equipment in the first Mass Effect, not sold by regular vendors and standing as the game's ultimate weapons, superior to anything else available. Only two merchants sell the gear: the Alliance Requisition Officer aboard the SSV Normandy and the Requisition Officer in the C-Sec Academy. Completing Admiral Ahern's wager on Pinnacle Station also randomly awards Spectre gear (tier VII to X) at the player's apartment terminal there for 220,000 credits.

Access is gated by achievements: unlocking "Rich" by amassing one million credits enables the tier VII gear, and reaching level fifty in addition unlocks the tier X gear. Tier VII becomes available to all new careers once unlocked, but each character must individually reach level fifty for tier X. In Legendary Edition, level X Spectre - Master Gear first becomes available after reaching the Citadel Lockdown portion of the story, once all four main story worlds are complete.

There are eight Spectre - Master Gear weapons in total, one tier VII and one tier X of each of the four weapon types. Each name begins with "HMW" followed by a letter code for its type: A for assault rifle, P for pistol, SG for shotgun, SR for sniper rifle. All share a distinctive matte-black finish unique among manufacturers, with a strip of slanted light-emitting slits whose color changes depending on whether the weapon is holstered. All Spectre weapons incorporate adaptive fire control technology, making them the most powerful and accurate weapons in the game. Only the VII and X sophistication levels can be obtained in-game; other levels, and the full Basic and Advanced lines plus matching HMBA biotic amps and HMOT omni-tools, are accessible only through PC console commands. In Legendary Edition the Master Pistol is the only fully-automatic pistol naturally obtainable. The Master Gear emblem, an arch enclosing three stars, resembles the Systems Alliance emblem.
