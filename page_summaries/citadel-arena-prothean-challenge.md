Citadel Arena: Prothean Challenge was a side mission in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, requiring the From Ashes DLC (or Mass Effect Legendary Edition) and completion of Citadel Arena: Reaper Solo Challenge. After Shepard finished the previously requested Reaper match, the "Hour 20" news team emailed the Combat Sim Controls through executive producer Lara J. Delante of "Hour 20 With Azad Carter" on the Alliance News Network. The team had learned Shepard had a living Prothean aboard and wanted footage of a Prothean fighting Reapers in the Armax Arena as a symbol of galactic resistance and a morale boost, asking Shepard to bring the Prothean and sign likeness-release forms for use in Council space. Delante added that Javik could have an interview once he stopped calling their audience "stupid primitives."

To complete the challenge, Shepard completed a combat simulation against Reapers with Javik in the squad; a second squadmate and other settings were permitted, allowing simultaneous completion of other non-conflicting arena challenges such as the Thermal Clip Challenge or Charity Reaper Challenge. Afterward Delante sent a second email saying the Prothean was making waves and giving people hope, with a finder's fee attached. Receiving and reading the second email granted 5,000 credits and +2 Reputation.
