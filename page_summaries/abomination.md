Abominations were a type of husk created by the Collectors, used as suicide troops that charged their targets and exploded at close range. They glowed red rather than the blue of standard human husks. In Mass Effect 2, set in 2185, Abominations accompanied groups of Husks in attempts to overwhelm Commander Shepard, and were encountered at Collector sites and near Reaper artifacts.

An Abomination charged like a regular husk while glowing and emitting flames, then self-destructed once close enough, dealing heavy damage in a small area with a lingering damage-over-time burning effect. Once it began glowing it would always explode regardless of how it was killed, and a glowing one could be detonated by knocking it to the ground or into terrain, though any armor it had generally needed to be destroyed first. It had moderate health and gained light armor on Hardcore and Insanity difficulty. Recommended tactics included using knockback powers such as Throw, Concussive Shot, Shockwave, or Slam rather than Pull, which risked drawing the Abomination close before it exploded. Unlike regular Husks, Abominations could be controlled with Dominate, would not shatter if frozen, and could be removed without exploding by the M-490 Blackstorm vortex. Their legs were a weak point.

Abominations returned in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer with the Retaliation Pack DLC and appeared in the Armax Arsenal Arena combat simulator added by the Citadel DLC. There they ran up and grabbed the player, dealing damage over time and exploding unless the player broke free by rapidly meleeing; they also exploded on death, harming friend or foe, and in later waves could be possessed to gain weak barriers and a stronger blast. They had low health and lacked shields or barriers, making them vulnerable to powers, but like Husks were immune to electrocution stuns. Their post-mortem explosion could be prevented by decapitating them, such as with a fatal headshot, a broken Grab, or certain heavy melee attacks by batarians, vorcha, the N7 Shadow, the N7 Slayer, and the Cabal Vanguard.
