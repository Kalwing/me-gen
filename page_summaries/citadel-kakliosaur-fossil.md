Citadel: Kakliosaur Fossil was a Mass Effect 3 mission available after Priority: The Citadel II and Priority: Geth Dreadnought. It began by overhearing a salarian War Strategist standing outside Apartment 90 in the Presidium Commons Apartments, who discussed how the krogan would benefit from the extinct kakliosaurs as war mounts able to help them fight on toxic worlds. An alternative journal entry framed the task simply as finding anyone on the Citadel who could make use of a kakliosaur preserved in amber. If the comm conversation was never overheard, the objective was instead obtained during a Search and Rescue operation in the Argos Rho cluster, which became available only after the geth dreadnought's destruction over Rannoch.

Commander Shepard scanned the planet Intai'sei in the Phoenix system of Argos Rho and recovered a kakliosaur skull preserved in amber. Returning it to the salarian War Strategist, who still stood outside the apartment, Shepard heard him remark that kakliosaur DNA was remarkably pliable and that the cloning facilities on Sur'Kesh could quickly produce battle-ready units. Completing the mission awarded 5 Reputation, 15,000 credits, and updated the Krogan Clans War Asset. Further eavesdropping on the salarian revealed that the krogan had already begun riding the kakliosaurs into battle and did not mind how the creatures marked their territory.
