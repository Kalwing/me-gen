Spectres (Special Tactics and Reconnaissance, abbreviated ST&R) are agents entrusted with extraordinary authority by the Citadel Council, including power of life and death over the galaxy's inhabitants. They form an elite group drawn from several species whose primary duty is to preserve galactic stability by any means necessary. Though effectively above the law and free to choose their own methods, a Spectre's status can be revoked by the Council for gross misconduct. They work alone or in small groups as the task and their preference dictate.

The Special Tactics and Reconnaissance branch was founded in 693 CE, shortly before the Krogan Rebellions, when the Council grew uneasy about unchecked krogan expansion. The first Spectres were chosen from the finest salarian STG operatives and asari huntresses, meant to observe but also to serve as the Council's first line of defense; they used guerrilla tactics, computer viruses, and sabotage to slow the krogan until the turians joined the war. Their existence was a Council secret until after the Rebellions. All Spectre records are sealed; Alliance intelligence estimates fewer than a hundred exist. Each is hand-picked after proving exceptional ability and self-reliance, in practice usually from the Council races. Spectres have no command structure and answer only to the Council; candidates typically have years of military or law enforcement service, undergo background checks and psychological evaluation, and train under an experienced mentor. Rogue Spectres are rare, and the only remedy is to revoke status and send another Spectre after them.

Ambassadors Anita Goyle and Donnel Udina both pushed to get a human into the Spectres as a step toward a Council seat. The first candidate, David Anderson, was sabotaged by his observer Saren Arterius, who falsified his report. The second, Commander Shepard, was inducted in 2183 after exposing Saren as a renegade. In the last thousand years only two people, both asari matriarchs, had declined an offer to join; Shepard can become the third by declining reinstatement after resurrection in 2185. Spectres lose their status when declared legally deceased and must be reinstated with proof of life. Known Spectres include Saren Arterius (status revoked 2183, killed at the Battle of the Citadel), Nihlus Kryik (killed on Eden Prime), Jondum Bau, Beelo Gurji (the first Spectre, appointed 693 CE), Tela Vasir (a Shadow Broker agent), Avitus Rix, and, if they survived Virmire, Kaidan Alenko or Ashley Williams as the second human Spectre by 2186.
