The United North American States (UNAS) was a coalition on Earth at the time of the Mass Effect trilogy, formed in the late 21st century when Canada, Mexico, and the United States of America merged. The union, established around 2096 CE, was strongly opposed by secessionist factions including the extremist group Freedoms First. On the morning of November 1, 2096, the New York chapter of Freedoms First attacked Liberty Island and destroyed the Statue of Liberty, and the resulting outrage triggered the Second American Civil War between secessionists and UNAS forces.

In 2149 CE, following the discovery of a cache of Prothean technology on Mars, the UNAS and seventeen other nations ratified the Systems Alliance charter, and the UNAS contributed military assets to the new Alliance military. When the garden world Watson was discovered in 2165 CE, the UNAS, the Chinese People's Federation, and the European Union all claimed it; the Alliance brokered the Reykjavik Compromise allowing each to settle its own colonies there.

In 2176 CE, UNAS President Enrique Aguilar was assassinated during a photo opportunity with Chinese People's Federation premier Ying Xiong. The killer, patent clerk and dissident Michael Moser Lang of Virginia, concealed a Kassa Fabrication M-12 Locust submachine gun inside a shoulder-mounted video camera. His first burst broke through the stage's kinetic barriers; Xiong tried to shield Aguilar, but Lang's next shots passed through Xiong and killed both men. During his term, President Christopher Huerta suffered a stroke that left him legally dead for 90 minutes before his brain functions were transferred to a computer. In 2185 CE, Speaker of the House Lisa Ford brought the case Ford v. Huerta, arguing his time in office afterward was illegitimate and that she should have been Vice President; the Systems Alliance heard the case and ruled 5-4 that Huerta's continued term was legitimate, prompting protests in several UNAS cities.

The UNAS was one of many Earth nations invaded by the Reapers in 2186 CE. Commander Shepard was in Vancouver as the first Reaper forces landed and escaped aboard the Normandy SR-2, while reporter Emily Wong witnessed the invasion at Los Angeles and transmitted her account via a Quantum Entanglement Communicator. The Reapers bombarded military bases, missile silos, and industrial centers and annihilated cities of low millions, including Fort Worth; the communications blackout meant little else was known about the UNAS during the occupation. Mentioned cities included New York City, Vancouver, Los Angeles, Mexico City, Toronto, Ottawa, Washington D.C., and the Texan Megapolis of Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston, and San Antonio.
