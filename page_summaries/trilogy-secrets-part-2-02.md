A hand-corrected YouTube-transcript lore video collecting five more easily missed trilogy details.

Sixth, if Shepard romances neither Garrus Vakarian nor Tali'Zorah, the two can be caught together near the end of Mass Effect 3, flustered and improvising excuses. Tali plays it down — "This is just a fling, Vakarian. I'm using you for your body." — "You're so mean." — "And I'm okay with that." Garrus notes it helps to have something to come back to.

Seventh, if Shepard romanced Kelly Chambers in Mass Effect 2, she can be met again in the Citadel refugee camp in Mass Effect 3, unable to rejoin the Normandy after the trauma the Collectors inflicted. Shepard can convince her to change her identity so Cerberus cannot find her; if so, she survives the Cerberus attack on the Citadel and is grateful, and if not, her death is reported afterward.

Eighth, Conrad Verner recurs across all three games: a harmless fan seeking Shepard's validation in Mass Effect 1, a troublemaker in fake N7 armor on Illium in Mass Effect 2, and still dim in Mass Effect 3. Engaging with him in every game pays off at the end; missing a single encounter means he never returns. Nearly every Conrad line contains a fourth-wall break poking fun at Mass Effect's mechanics or RPG conventions, making him the series' equivalent of M'aiq the Liar. Asked whether he has combat training: "I'm saving the galaxy, Shepard. I don't have time for training."

Ninth, the pigeons on the Citadel are explained by the VI Avina: the current decoration budget was approved by Earth's human ambassador, and each species with ambassadorial rights can apply for a slot on the Citadel's decoration schedule to change the promenade and parks, the only condition being that introduced environmental features not be toxic to residents. Represented species can bring native flora and fauna from their homeworlds, which is how a pest species like the pigeon reached the station.

Tenth, the "refund guy" recurs across all three games trying to get a refund — 15 credits fought over for three years. In Mass Effect 3 Shepard can finally intervene, either helping him get the refund or talking him out of it.
