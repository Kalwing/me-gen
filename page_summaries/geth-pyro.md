The Geth Pyro was a geth enemy introduced in Mass Effect 3, functionally similar to the Geth Destroyer of Mass Effect 2. It wielded a flamethrower and was protected by both armor and shields. Its only weapon was an M-451 Firestorm, which had short range but stripped enemy shields very quickly and did considerable health damage, so players were advised to engage Pyros at long range. The Pyro moved very slowly, making it an easy target, but could absorb a great deal of damage and could still dodge attacks by hopping aside like other geth. It was immune to the physics-based effects of Pull, Throw, Concussive Shot, Singularity and Stasis, and, being armored, could not be grabbed by players (this was enforced after Patch 4). It could be stunned by the electrical shocks of Overload and Energy Drain once its shields were down, and by Disruptor Ammo even while shielded.

Like other pyro-type enemies, the Pyro carried a canister on its back; enough fire on the exposed tank once shields were down would ignite it and cause an explosion that killed the Pyro and damaged nearby units, and a killing blow from tech powers such as Arc Grenade, Overload, Energy Drain, Tech Burst, Hex Shield or Electric Slash could trigger the same result. In multiplayer a single piercing shot from a weapon like the Javelin, lining up the head and fuel tank, could kill one outright. The flamethrower's damage range extended beyond its visible animation and heavily obscured the player's vision. Overload and an SMG, assault rifle or shotgun countered its shields while Warp and a sniper rifle or heavy pistol worked on its armor; Reave damaged it over time, but most other biotics were ineffective. Sabotage was strongly recommended since Pyros operated deep within geth formations. Unlike almost all other enemies, Pyros emitted no voice chatter, making their frequent flanking attacks unusually stealthy.
