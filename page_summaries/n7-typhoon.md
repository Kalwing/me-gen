The N7 Typhoon was an assault rifle in Mass Effect 3. It was a distinctive light machine gun fitted with a face shield to protect the shooter from headshots, and its power and recoil were so notorious that it included a high-tech kinetic reducer to fight muzzle climb. Because the reducer tried to limit all weapon motion, marksmen did not engage it while moving and instead used it only from cover.

It was the heaviest assault rifle in the game apart from the multiplayer Geth Spitfire, making it impractical for power-dependent classes, though firing it did not slow the user. It was likened to an improved M-76 Revenant and was the only assault rifle with native shot penetration, letting it ignore Guardian shields, armor plating, and thin cover; in single-player the penetration distance was a large 100 cm, matched only by the Javelin. Like the Geth Plasma SMG it gained a damage bonus after about 0.75 seconds of sustained fire (2x in single-player, 1.5x in multiplayer), the same time needed to reach maximum rate of fire, at which point ammo consumption also doubled; the Marksman power granted this spin-up bonus instantly. It also dealt 50% bonus damage against any protected target, giving it sustained DPS exceeding even the Cerberus Harrier, though it was less effective in short bursts or against unprotected infantry and was not accurate enough by default for consistent headshots. In single-player its recoil worsened as it leveled up, unlike in multiplayer.

It was acquired via the Groundside Resistance Pack (or Spectre Requisitions for 150,000 credits in the Legendary Edition) and was an ultra-rare multiplayer weapon requiring Mass Effect 3: Earth. It was named after the Hawker Typhoon, a British World War II fighter-bomber.
