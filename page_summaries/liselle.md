Liselle was a daughter of Aria T'Loak and the lover of Paul Grayson. When Grayson began working for Aria under the alias Paul Johnson, Aria, unable to find any information on his past, ordered Liselle to kill him; instead Liselle pleaded for his life, arguing he was useful and loyal, and later became his lover. She was part of the team Aria sent to secure a shipment of red sand from the Talons, a turian mercenary gang, and did not object when Grayson had the sand stashed in his apartment. Visiting Grayson's apartment after his call, the two were attacked by Grayson's former employers, Cerberus. Liselle was hit with a tranquilizer dart and knocked unconscious, and Kai Leng then killed her by slitting her throat with a kitchen knife, leaving her to bleed out on Grayson's bed, where Aria later found her. The small dart wound was overlooked against the neck injury, leading Aria to suspect Grayson had killed her, and her grief and fury drove her to seek revenge. These events occurred in Mass Effect: Retribution.
