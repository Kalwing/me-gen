Short page. Eclipse Engineers were the tech specialists of the Eclipse mercenary organization, exclusively human and salarian, encountered in nearly every engagement with Eclipse in Mass Effect 2. They carried the M-6 Carnifex hand cannon and used tech abilities such as Incinerate and Combat Drone to strip defenses and create extra threats, preferring to hang back and let Troopers and Vanguards advance. They were protected by shields, which they could fully regenerate with Tech Armor like a Sentinel. The rest of the page is enemy tactics.
