Garrus: Eye for an Eye was Garrus Vakarian's loyalty mission in Mass Effect 2, acquired after Horizon once Garrus received a message that "put some fire in his eyes." Garrus had learned that Sidonis, the mole who betrayed his Omega squad and caused their deaths, was on the Citadel with the help of Fade, a forger who helped people disappear. Talking to Garrus at the Normandy's weapons batteries began the mission.

Garrus arranged a meeting with Fade at a warehouse near the Neon Markets in Zakera Ward. There Shepard and Garrus confronted a volus flanked by two krogan bodyguards; the volus revealed Fade was actually former C-Sec officer Harkin, hiding in an old prefab factory in the Factory District under Blue Suns protection. At the factory, Harkin fled and Shepard's team fought through waves of Blue Suns Troopers, Legionnaires, Commanders, Heavies, LOKI Mechs, and three YMIR Mechs, two of them simultaneously in the final exterior battle. Cornering Harkin, Garrus slammed him against a wall and pinned him with a foot to the throat until Harkin contacted Sidonis and arranged a midday meeting outside the Orbital Lounge. A Paragon interrupt could stop Garrus from shooting Harkin in the leg, replacing it with a headbutt.

At the Orbital Lounge, Garrus set up in a sniper perch and told Shepard to draw Sidonis into the open for the shot. Shepard could stay silent and let Garrus kill Sidonis, or warn Sidonis, who explained he had sold out the team only to save his own life and had been haunted by guilt ever since. With enough Paragon points, Shepard could convince Garrus to spare him. If Garrus killed Sidonis, C-Sec investigated the death with no leads and Garrus quipped "what a shame." If spared, Harkin was taken into custody and Sidonis confessed to C-Sec to the murder of ten people on Omega, though jurisdiction issues left C-Sec unsure how to proceed. Either outcome earned Garrus's loyalty and unlocked his Armor Piercing Ammo. Rewards included 750 experience, 28,800 credits, a Sniper Rifle Damage upgrade, and 2,000 iridium. A female Shepard who let Garrus shoot Harkin's leg could tell Harkin, "You're lucky. I wouldn't have shot you in the leg."
