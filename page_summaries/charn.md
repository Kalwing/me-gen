Charn was a batarian who served as Balak's second-in-command during the batarian attack on Asteroid X57, depicted in the Mass Effect: Bring Down the Sky content. Charn and his underlings had originally signed on for a fast, efficient slave raid, but Balak's arrival changed the operation into a plan to destroy the human colony below by sending the asteroid on a collision course with the world. Charn harbored rebellious and resentful feelings about the change but stayed on the asteroid because he feared Balak would "skin him alive out of spite" if he left. He was posted to guard the last of the fusion torches as a final line of defense, where he confronted Commander Shepard and the squad. Rather than fighting at once, Charn tried to negotiate, telling Shepard "this doesn't have to end in bloodshed" and insisting that he and his men had enlisted only for slaving, not for wiping out a colony. Shepard could persuade Charn to abandon the asteroid peacefully in exchange for a promise that Balak would be killed and Charn would then be left in charge; Charn accepted immediately, handed over a pass to the main facility, and warned that Balak was a "mean bastard." Alternatively, Shepard could attack Charn, either on sighting the squad or after hearing him out, judging Balak's slaving raid reason enough to fight; Shepard could then take the facility pass from his corpse. Charn was voiced by Jason Singer. Cut content indicates that in earlier development Charn could have been persuaded or bribed to free the terrorists' hostages, back when those hostages were written as human slaves rather than engineers.
