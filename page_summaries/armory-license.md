An armory license in Mass Effect automatically granted the shipboard Alliance Requisition Officer aboard the Normandy access to supplies made by the company or manufacturer that owned the license's rights. Licenses were bought with credits from vendors across the galaxy. Although the Requisitions Officer's stock was random, owning more licenses widened the variety of available items, with each license allocated its own pool of additional stock so that new licenses did not reduce the chance of another manufacturer's goods appearing.

There were fourteen licenses in total, all but two purchasable from merchants on the Citadel. Five were available from the start of a new game, five more unlocked at level 18, and the final four at level 36. The Normandy Requisitions Officer already had access to Hahne-Kedar goods for free from the outset. Unlocking the "Rich" achievement gave access to Spectre - Master Gear at quality VII on later playthroughs, upgrading to quality X at level 50. In Legendary Edition, which has no Rich achievement, that gear became available after completing all main worlds and Virmire.

Entry-level licenses covered Aldrin Labs, Elanus Risk Control, Elkoss Combine, Hahne-Kedar, and Sirta Foundation, each costing 100 credits (Hahne-Kedar free). Advanced gear licenses at level 18 cost 6,250 credits each and covered Ariake Technologies, Armali Council, Devlon Industries, Haliat Armory, and Rosenkov Materials. Elite and prototype licenses at level 36 cost 30,000 credits each and covered Armax Arsenal, Geth Armory, Kassa Fabrication, and Serrice Council. Some licenses required rescuing Captain Kirrahe from Virmire to become available from Commander Rentola. The Armali Council license was meant to be obtainable through conversation choices with Nassana Dantius at the end of UNC: Asari Diplomacy, but a bug prevented its acquisition. Purchased licenses did not carry over to new characters and had to be repurchased each playthrough.
