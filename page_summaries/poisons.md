Poisons were substances administered or designed primarily to harm one or more species. Areas contaminated by them were called toxic hazards. The page catalogued numerous examples from across the series. Butanediamine (putrescine) and pentamethylenediamine (cadaverine) were byproducts of organic decomposition. The Cerberus turian poison was a neurotoxin tailored to turians and used during Cerberus's failed takeover of the Citadel; General Korilius was stabbed with a poisoned blade while defending civilians and left comatose at Huerta Memorial Hospital, doctors unable to identify the toxin. Schematics recovered from an ex-Cerberus facility on Gellix, or comparable data on an anti-Palaven toxin bought from a former Alliance researcher at Spectre Requisitions, could be used to synthesize an antidote.

Drell subcutaneous oils were toxic secretions that other species found intoxicating and hallucinogenic, the reason drell generally avoided environmental suits. Element zero exposure either granted biotic potential or promoted cancerous tumors, with overexposure favoring the latter. Hanar secreted natural toxins as a defense mechanism. Hydrogen chloride was a corrosive compound that cut through kinetic barriers and was lethal to most organics. Polonium's radioactivity was used to coat anti-organic ammunition. Ruthenium dichloride, used in commercial canning and found in horosk bottles, was toxic to asari, humans, and salarians. Fusion containment cells emitted poison gas clouds when destroyed. Thoros-B was a multispecies bioweapon developed by Binary Helix.

Several batarian poisons available on Khar'shan were described in gruesome detail: Final Jest turned blood to lighter fluid and set victims aflame from within; Mother's Milk dissolved the victim's throat mid-swallow; Pauper's Pleasure caused purple hives before the victim's saliva boiled and they braised in their own fluids. Kvastyr ("last light" in Shelesh) was a lethal fungus on Kadara, cultivated by the Collective at its headquarters. Sulfur compounds pervaded Kadara's water supply, requiring filtration before it was potable.
