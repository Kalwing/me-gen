The Journal was the mission log in Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, and Mass Effect 3, allowing the player to review completed, ongoing, and newly available missions. It could be accessed at any time from the main in-game menu.

In Mass Effect and Mass Effect 2, the Journal was divided into two tabs: Missions, for objectives considered part of the main storyline, and Assignments, for side missions. On each tab, uncompleted missions appeared on top with the most recently acquired at the top, while completed missions were shown below in grayed-out text and organized by type (such as "Citadel:", "Illium:", "UNC:", "N7:") rather than strictly by completion order.

In Mass Effect 3, the Journal was merged with the Codex and reorganized into three tabs: Mission Summary, listing all missions in the game; Codex, for primary Codex entries; and Codex (Secondary), for secondary Codex entries. The Mission Summary tab no longer separated side missions into Assignments, classifying all objectives as Missions and listing them in a similar manner to the earlier games.
