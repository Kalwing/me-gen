2175 Aeia was the first planet of the Alpha Draconis System, in the Rosetta Nebula of the Milky Way. It was named after an asari scientist and lay on the list of forbidden mass relay destinations that led to uncharted space. Early information came from a single distant probe flyby, which reported two planets orbiting a white dwarf star. Closer scans by Commander Shepard's crew showed the planet sat within the star's habitable zone, held oceans of liquid water, and carried a thin nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere consistent with carbon-based plant life, raising the possibility that it was an unexplored garden world.

According to the Codex, humans first detected Aeia as an Earth-type world through telemetry in 2165. Probe surveys indicated lush vegetation, ample fresh water, and breathable air, prompting the Systems Alliance to upgrade the planet to a garden-world colonization priority. In 2173 the Alliance survey vessel Hugo Gernsback, under Captain Ronald Taylor, made planetfall on the jungle world. Ship transmissions stopped soon afterward. The precise fate of the command and crew was unknown, and they were presumed killed in action with their vessel destroyed.

The planet was the setting of the Mass Effect 2 loyalty mission Jacob: The Gift of Greatness, unlocked after Jacob Taylor was recruited. Its mineral deposits scanned as rich, with medium palladium, high platinum, medium iridium, and low element zero. By the time of Shepard's visit the planet showed four visible moons. The wiki noted a continuity conflict: the Codex named Ronald Taylor as the Gernsback's captain, but the mission established that he was second in command and became acting captain only after the actual captain, Harris Fairchild, died in the crash. The name may derive from Aiea near Pearl City, Honolulu, or from the Ancient Greek "aei," meaning "always" or "ever," a possible reference to the long delay between the crash and the activation of the distress beacon.
