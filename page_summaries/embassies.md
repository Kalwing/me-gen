Many races maintained an embassy on the Citadel's Presidium. The volus held the first; the human Systems Alliance held the most recent. Being granted an embassy signified that a species had made a significant contribution to Citadel space, and required signing the Treaty of Farixen first. Some embassies were closed due to their species' actions: the krogan lost theirs during the Krogan Rebellions, the quarians were asked to leave after the geth rebelled, and the batarians closed theirs in protest at the Council's perceived preferential treatment of humans.

In the first Mass Effect, the only embassies Commander Shepard could visit were those of the Alliance, the elcor, and the volus, located near the Wards Access corridor across the bridge from the Consort's Chambers. Ambassador Udina and Captain Anderson were usually in the Alliance embassy, where Shepard could report between missions. Shepard could also speak with the embassy receptionist and use the nearby Avina terminal for a guided tour, though the VI would not offer political opinions.

The Embassy Lounge was an expensive bar frequented by diplomats, across the lobby from the Alliance embassy. Shepard could talk with the bartender for background on Flux, Chora's Den, and the asari Consort Sha'ira. Nassana Dantius worked at a terminal near the bar, and Clerk Bosker appeared there later. Shepard could also overhear Private Fredricks boasting to friends about visiting the Consort's Chambers.
