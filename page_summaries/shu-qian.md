Dr. Shu Qian was an Alliance scientist researching artificial intelligence and the project leader at the Sidon facility in 2165. According to Kahlee Sanders, Qian was studying a newly discovered alien artifact believed to possibly pre-date the Protheans. The research changed him: he grew increasingly irrational and obsessed with the work. Kahlee became suspicious, copied several of his files, and left Sidon just before the Blue Suns attacked it. Qian disappeared after the attack and his whereabouts were listed as unknown.

In fact, Qian was the traitor at Sidon who gave the Blue Suns access to the facility. He was secretly working for the batarian Edan Had'dah, who had employed him to uncover the artifact's secrets, but the artifact affected Qian's mind until studying it was all he cared about, and Had'dah began losing patience. It was Qian's idea to capture Kahlee Sanders, since with his team dead he had lost the help he needed to continue the research. Qian joined Had'dah at the eezo refinery on Camala after Kahlee's capture. When David Anderson and Saren Arterius attacked the refinery, Qian copied all his files to a flash drive, still desperate to protect his data even when confronted by Saren. Saren asked Had'dah whether Qian was still necessary; when Had'dah called him a liability who should be replaced, Saren immediately shot Qian in the head.
