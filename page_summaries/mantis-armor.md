Mantis Armor was a hardsuit set in the first Mass Effect, manufactured by Hahne-Kedar. It provided light to moderate protection, and like other Hahne-Kedar armor its field destabilizer gave heightened shield efficiency compared to competitors. It was produced in light, medium, and heavy classes for humans, and was also available to turians (light and medium) and krogan (medium and heavy). Across classes it shared stat lines with the manufacturer's Predator, Scorpion, and Ursa armor sets. Heavier variants traded tech and biotic protection for greater damage protection and shield capacity; for example, human light armor ran from 12 damage protection and 84 shields at level I to 30 and 300 at level X, while heavy armor ran from 40 damage protection and 144 shields to 58 and 360, with tech/biotic protection dropping to as low as 0. The name is shared with the unrelated M-92 Mantis sniper rifle and A-61 Mantis Gunship.
