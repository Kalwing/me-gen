This page described the Aegis Pack, a Mass Effect 2 DLC pack released on July 6, 2010 that added the M-29 Incisor burst-fire sniper rifle (effective against shields) and the five-piece Kestrel armor set to Shepard's armory. The items were delivered in-game via emails from the Illusive Man, who noted that Miranda had requested additional long-range anti-shield firepower and that the Kestrel suit's shield algorithms were best exploited by mixing its modular parts with other armors. It sold for 160 BioWare Points on PC and $1.99 on consoles. The page is a product listing with little narrative prose.
