Inner Council Space was the central region of the space controlled by the Citadel Council in the Milky Way galaxy. It was bordered by Outer Council Space, Earth Systems Alliance Space, and the Attican Traverse.

Besides its centrality, the region held great importance to the Council. It contained the Citadel, the seat of galactic government, and the homeworlds of several sapient races: the salarians (Sur'Kesh) and turians (Palaven), both Council races, as well as the hanar (Kahje) and volus (Irune), both with Citadel representation. Tuchanka, homeworld of the krogan, a former Citadel race, also lay within the region.

Locations in Inner Council Space included the clusters Aethon Cluster, Annos Basin, Apien Crest and the Krogan DMZ; the homeworlds Irune, Kahje, Palaven, Sur'Kesh and Tuchanka; the colonies Digeris, Gembat, Oma Ker, Pheiros and Vard; and Pildea Station. There was a noted contradiction over the Citadel's placement: the Galaxy at War map put it in Inner Council Space, while the Normandy's navigation map placed it in Outer Council Space near the border, which also affected the colony Bekenstein in the same cluster.
