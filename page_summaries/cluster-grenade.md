Game-mechanics reference for the Adept biotic grenade power Cluster Grenade in Mass Effect 3 single-player and multiplayer, detailing its six power ranks, damage, force and radius values, its split into multiple tracking and scattering projectiles, its role as a universal Power Combo detonator, and usage strategies with powers such as Warp and Reave.
