Mass Effect 3: Groundside Resistance Pack was a DLC pack for Mass Effect 3 adding seven weapons to the single-player campaign, released on all platforms on October 16, 2012. It included the new Venom Shotgun and Executioner Pistol, plus the Kishock Harpoon Gun, Striker Assault Rifle, N7 Typhoon, N7 Piranha, and Acolyte, five of which had previously been available only in multiplayer. It cost 160 BioWare Points on PC and $1.99 on Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3.
