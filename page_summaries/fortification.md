Fortification was a defensive power in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes.

In Mass Effect 2 it gave a brief but massive boost to armor by reinforcing it with a non-Newtonian fluid that hardened when struck. Its three base ranks raised the armor bonus to 25, 50, or 75 percent of maximum shields over a 60-second duration with a 12-second recharge. Rank 4 evolved it into Improved Fortification, extending the duration to 180 seconds, or Heavy Fortification, raising the bonus to 100 percent. When used by Shepard as a bonus power from Advanced Training it boosted maximum shields and restored them to the new maximum, with no effect on health. It was functionally the same as Barrier and Geth Shield Boost but counted as a "combat" power that received no biotic or tech upgrades, making it an inferior choice for Shepard when Barrier was available. It was also Grunt's loyalty power, unlocked after Grunt: Rite of Passage, though he tended to reactivate it constantly in battle.

In Mass Effect 3 Fortification reinforced armor with protective Foucault currents that could be purged to charge the user's gauntlets for increased melee damage, while slowing power use by 50 percent. Rank 1 gave 25 percent damage reduction, a 50 percent melee damage bonus for 20 seconds, and a 5-second recharge. Higher ranks improved recharge speed, melee damage, durability, and at rank 5 offered a choice between faster shield recharge and Power Synergy, which increased all power damage and force by 30 percent while active. The damage protection applied to both health and shields, persisted when shields broke, and could stack with Tech Armor and Reave. It was available to David Anderson, James Vega, Grunt, and Shepard as a bonus power in single-player, and to the Krogan Soldier and Geth Trooper Soldier in multiplayer.
