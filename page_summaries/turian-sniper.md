Short enemy page. Turian Snipers were turian troops equipped with sniper rifles for long-range engagements in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, first met by Randall Ezno during the mission to recover a turian target and later part of the Turian Hierarchy's raid on the Cerberus Barn space station. They had low health and a weak regenerating shield offset by high damage, sat behind cover popping out to shoot (Pull drew them into the open), took bonus headshot damage, and telegraphed each shot with a red targeting beam that could be dodged with Slide or by diving into cover.
