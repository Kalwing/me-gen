Mass Effect: Inquisition was an eight-page mini-comic written by Mac Walters with art by Jean Diaz, first released on the USA Today website and later collected with Mass Effect: Evolution and paired with Mass Effect: Incursion on Dark Horse Digital. Walters described it as a character study of C-Sec officer Captain Bailey, offering a view of the universe outside Shepard's perspective.

Set on the Citadel in 2186, some time after Mass Effect 2, the story opens with Bailey speaking with his child and hoping to return to Earth for the salmon run. Councilor Donnel Udina summoned him and claimed C-Sec had been compromised by factions aiding enemies of the Council, with Executor Pallin himself involved. Udina asked Bailey to investigate an office in Bailey's district that Pallin had been visiting, and to report only to Udina. At the office Bailey found two dead C-Sec agents, one holding a datapad displaying Pallin's picture. Pallin emerged, demanded the datapad, and warned that Udina could not be trusted; Bailey noted Udina had said the same of Pallin. Pallin opened fire, grazing Bailey's shoulder armor; Bailey returned fire, hitting Pallin in the chest, and a final struggle ended with Bailey killing the Executor.

Bailey wondered why Pallin had not killed him when he had the chance and why the Executor had acted so uncharacteristically sloppily. He returned to Udina, saying his instincts told him Pallin was innocent and had been genuinely shocked by the accusations. Udina dismissed the concern as a matter for investigators, said Bailey's planned Earth trip might not happen given new responsibilities, and promoted him to Commander, telling him the Council expected a full report. Bailey was left looking over the Presidium, regretting he would not see his child.
