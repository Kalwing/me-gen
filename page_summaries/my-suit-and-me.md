"My Suit and Me" was an old quarian lullaby. Tech Specialist Oliver Barthes was hired to install a recording of the song in the quarian cryopods of the ark Keelah Si'yah, to be played once a century during the roughly 600-year journey to the Andromeda galaxy. The lyrics dwell on a quarian child's bond with their environment suit, love of family, ship and homeworld, and the dream of one day reaching Rannoch, ending "My self and my suit / My suit and I."

In Mass Effect: Annihilation, the song was a pretext to plant a computer worm in the Keelah Si'yah's systems. Shortly after installing it, Oliver was murdered on the walkways of Hephaestus Station by a figure who sang part of the lullaby. Centuries later, during the Fortinbras Plague crisis worsened by the malfunctioning worm, drell detective Anax Therion, who had witnessed the killing, recognized the singing voice as that of the ark's captain, Qetsi'Olam vas Keelah Si'yah. After explaining herself to Anax and her former lover Senna'Nir, Qetsi tried to atone by walking without her suit through all six of the ship's environments to spread the Fortinbras cure, singing the lullaby until she died in the volus section.
