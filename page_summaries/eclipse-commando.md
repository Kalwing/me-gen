Short page. Eclipse Commandos were the elite soldiers of the Eclipse mercenary organization, asari commandos whose training, experience and biotic ability made them among the finest individual warriors in the galaxy and among the toughest organic enemies in Mass Effect 2. They buffed nearby allies and could be heard issuing orders; leaders such as Enyala and Wasea were themselves asari commandos. In combat they carried the M-300 Claymore heavy shotgun, used Warp frequently, and were protected by strong, rechargeable barriers plus armor and health. The rest of the page is enemy tactics.
