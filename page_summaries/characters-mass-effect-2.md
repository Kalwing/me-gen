This page was a roster index for Mass Effect 2, listing links to its characters grouped as protagonist, squad members, temporary squad members, allies, and adversaries. It held roughly thirty entries, including Commander Shepard, squadmates such as Garrus Vakarian, Grunt, Jack, Jacob Taylor, Kasumi Goto, Legion, Miranda Lawson, Mordin Solus, Morinth, Samara, Tali'Zorah, Thane Krios and Zaeed Massani, allies such as Aria T'Loak, EDI and The Illusive Man, and adversaries such as The Collector General, Harbinger, The Shadow Broker and Tela Vasir. It contained no original prose beyond the link tables.
