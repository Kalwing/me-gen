Short enemy page (under ~150 words of real prose). Human Looters were a pair of thieves Shepard could encounter during the mission to recruit Mordin Solus, found in an apartment around the corner from Mordin's clinic on Omega. Caught in the act, they argued that they had arrived first. Shepard could simply question them about Mordin, convince them to stop looting, or kill them. They were armed with M-3 Predator heavy pistols and had minimal health with no shields, armor or barriers, so they put up little fight and sought cover when engaged.
