Brief gameplay page with minimal lore prose: Biotic Protector was a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's single-player mode, used by the squad member Nyreen Kandros in the Omega DLC. It deployed a shield that protected against all damage while the user could not move, shoot, or use other powers, with higher ranks adding a deploying blast, faster recharge, longer duration, and post-shield weapon-damage or damage-reduction buffs.
