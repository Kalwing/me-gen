This Shadow Broker dossier, unlocked after the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, covered Jack, called "Subject Zero." The Broker noted her abnormal biotic power compared favorably with the Pragia (Teltin) facility's expected results and that she was easily manipulated through emotional impairment caused by childhood abuse and confinement.

An extranet log shows she avoided the Normandy's connection and used a private omni-tool line, searching Citadel Newsnet archives for Cerberus, Pragia, Teltin, the Illusive Man and Miranda Lawson, watching footage of the geth attacks on Eden Prime and the Citadel (and closing Shepard's Spectre inauguration after twelve seconds), studying escape-pod procedures, mass relay maps and no-flight zones, and being banned from a news forum for swearing and from Galactic Champion Poker 2100 after an altercation. She then wiped her history with a commercial VI.

An intercepted message is a rejection from Galactic Poetry Monthly addressed to "Jacqueline Nought," reproducing her submitted poem, which ends: "I mark myself / Black and jagged / To cover the scars / That make me a monster / A warning / This is not a place of honor / No esteemed dead are buried here."

A surveillance transcript from the Helios Medical Facility on Eden Prime, dated 2165 and tapped for possible Cerberus involvement, records Doctor Castor telling a mother that her infant daughter died of seizures from prenatal element zero exposure, one of several such deaths the facility had seen, and blaming government inaction on treatments that might "interfere with their biotic recruitments." Castor persuades the grieving woman to donate the body "for tests," then opens a secure channel: "I've got a candidate... highest potential I've seen in years... No, the family has been dealt with. Send a team from Pragia ASAP." The page's trivia notes the inconsistency between this 2165 "baby" and the Cerberus dossier's listed 2161 birth year for Subject Zero.
