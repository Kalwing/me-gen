Citadel Arena: Thermal Clip Challenge was a side mission in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, available after Citadel Docks: Retake the Normandy, once the Armax Arsenal Arena opened with the rest of Silversun Strip. After Shepard completed some arena matches, Hoshichiri Saburo, a miner working the asteroid belts to supply raw materials for the war effort, emailed the Combat Sim Controls. Saburo wanted Shepard to settle a bet: a friend claimed thermal clips were the worst thing to happen to the military in fifty years because of reload time and effective ammo shortages, while Saburo argued that conserving shots and using biotics or omni-tool weapons made shortages irrelevant. He asked Shepard to complete a match, ideally against Collectors, in which spare thermal clips did not spawn, offering half the bet's winnings.

To complete the challenge, Shepard selected the "No Thermal Clips" modifier, a silver-level prize purchased with prior winnings, and finished a match; Collectors were suggested but not required, and other settings allowed simultaneous completion of non-conflicting challenges such as the Prothean Challenge or Spin Zone Elite Challenge. The M-7 Lancer or Particle Rifle made the match easier because both had unlimited reserve ammo. Afterward Saburo sent a triumphant follow-up email, and completing the mission granted 5,000 credits and +2 Reputation.
