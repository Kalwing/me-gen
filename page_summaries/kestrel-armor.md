The Kestrel Armor system was a customizable armor set in Mass Effect 2, released as downloadable content on July 6, 2010 as part of the Aegis Pack; in Legendary Edition the full set could be purchased at Serrice Technology on Illium. Its pieces could be mixed and matched with N7 Armor and other armor components at the Normandy SR-2's armor locker. The suit emphasized a high shield boost along with generalized bonuses to weapon damage, melee damage, and headshot damage, making it a strong choice for Vanguards, Sentinels, and close-range Soldiers, and available from the start of the game.

The set comprised the Kestrel Helmet (reinforced faceplate with internal HUD and microcameras; +5% headshot damage, +3% weapon damage, +3% shields), the Kestrel Torso Sheath (spinal-mount processor synchronizing artificial torso muscle fibers; +10% melee, +5% weapon damage, +3% shields), the Kestrel Shoulder Pieces (backup shield capacitors and spider-silk muscle fibers; +8% shields, +10% melee), the Kestrel Arm Sheathing (artificial muscle fibers steadying aim, ulnar-side capacitors; +3% weapon damage, +3% shields, +10% melee), and the Kestrel Power Pack (hip-mounted emergency energy; +8% shields, +5% heavy weapon ammo capacity). The Illusive Man emailed Shepard that Cerberus technicians debated the suit and felt its shield algorithms were best exploited piecemeal in custom arrangements, shipping a modular Kestrel suit to the Normandy. In Mass Effect 3, similar-looking Rosenkov Materials armor pieces existed with different attributes, though the helmet was still called the "Kestrel" helmet.
