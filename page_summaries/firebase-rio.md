Multiplayer map page. Firebase Rio is a Mass Effect 3 multiplayer map included in the Mass Effect 3: Earth DLC, set on the outskirts of Rio de Janeiro; the Cristo Redentor statue and city are visible to the south. Its areas are the Central Tower (elevated command room and starting location), West Bridge and East Bridge (parallel multi-level mezzanine bridges crossing water channels), Armored Core (overlooking anti-aircraft batteries), Helipad (extraction), Relay Station (a communications array), and Loading Dock (a cargo bay). It has the most ammo dumps of any multiplayer map, favoring grenade-based characters; the Central Tower makes a strong sniper nest, and the Loading Dock offers a container "panic room" safe from most instant-kill attacks.
