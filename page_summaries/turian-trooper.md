Short enemy page. Turian Troopers were standard turian troops armed with the M-96 Mattock Auto in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, encountered by Randall Ezno as part of a turian squad, presumably sent by the Turian Hierarchy, raiding the Cerberus Barn space station. They had low health with a moderate regenerating shield layer, tended to stay in cover unless they had line of sight (Pull flushed them out), and took bonus damage from headshots.
