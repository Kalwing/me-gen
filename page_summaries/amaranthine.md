Amaranthine was the third planet of the Fortuna System in the Horse Head Nebula, in Mass Effect. It became accessible after the player destroyed the two pirate bases on Mavigon and Klensal as part of the assignment UNC: Hostile Takeover. It was a chilly rock world with an atmosphere of carbon dioxide and nitrogen; its frozen surface was largely light titanium and aluminum oxides, with thorium and other heavy metals deep in the crust. It was named by the human poet Sofia Cabral during her tour of duty aboard the Alliance surveyor ship Kupe. Orbiting the dim red dwarf Fortuna, its surface appeared in twilight blues and purples even at midday, which also made for very poor visibility, and one moon was visible from the landing site.

The planet hosted the conclusion of UNC: Hostile Takeover at an engineering outpost, a confrontation with Helena Blake, and enemies including mercenaries and mercenary snipers. Other points of interest were turian wreckage containing an old beaded necklace marked with the Galatana Colony insignia (a collection item) and a crashed probe requiring average Electronics to salvage. Mineral deposits included iridium, uranium, and thorium. Many points of interest lay on or below highlands, requiring Mako traversal over mountainous terrain. The word "amaranthine" means unfading or everlasting and also denotes a deep purplish-red color, likely the source of the name; BioWare reused the name for a region in the Dragon Age: Origins expansion Awakening.
