Powell was a human spaceport dockworker on Eden Prime. He ran a small smuggling ring, paying a man named Cole to store his goods. Powell had been sleeping behind some crates during his shift when the geth attacked the colony in 2183, and this negligence saved his life. When Commander Shepard's squad arrived on Eden Prime, they found Powell near the corpse of the Spectre Nihlus.

Powell told the Commander that he had witnessed Saren Arterius murder Nihlus, his fellow Spectre, and he directed Shepard toward Saren along the cargo train route. Shepard could confront Powell about his smuggling operation and persuade him to hand over useful equipment, and with further pressure some prototype technology.

Powell was later contacted by the Alliance to give an official testimony supporting the accusation that Saren had turned traitor. The salarian Councilor dismissed his account, arguing that the word of "one traumatised dockworker" was insufficient evidence of Saren's alleged treachery.
