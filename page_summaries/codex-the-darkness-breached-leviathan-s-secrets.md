This Codex entry recorded what Task Force Aurora inferred about the aquatic species dubbed "Leviathans" after Commander Shepard's report; essentially nothing had been known of them before. No record of their true name existed, no ruins were attributed to their civilization, and no influence on other races had been recorded, so the task force could only conjecture from Shepard's limited information. Their current world was judged unlikely to be their homeworld, so it was surmised that the Leviathans had mastered technology and spaceflight despite their immense size and aquatic nature, and that their homeworld had included a land-based thrall species whose civilization served their purposes and which they communicated with and mentally dominated. Their bodies were presumed able to withstand both the extreme pressure of the depths and the lesser pressure of coastal areas where they met such thralls. Transporting a creature of Leviathan size into space was considered a significant engineering challenge, likely solved using eezo for ease of travel as the hanar did, together with cybernetic filtration allowing them to breathe both air and oxygenated water. The task force thought it probable that the Leviathans had modified themselves with the equivalent of a biotic amplifier, and noted that the faster-than-light communication of their pulses would require precise control previously unknown to modern science.
