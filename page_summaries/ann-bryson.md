Dr. Anneliese "Ann" Bryson was a Systems Alliance scientist and the daughter of Dr. Garret Bryson. Born in 2158, she was a member of Task Force Aurora, a group formed to investigate ancient legends about the Reapers. As a child she drew crayon pictures, including a hanar and a crude portrait of her father; that drawing, together with Garret's references to keeping things "close to his heart," gave Commander Shepard the decryption key to a file of Reaper activity data used to track Dr. Garneau.

Ann led a division of Task Force Aurora called Project Scarab, which traveled to the planet Namakli in the Pylos Nebula in search of clues to the fabled Leviathan. After the mission to find Dr. Garneau, Shepard investigated Dr. Bryson's Lab, determined Ann's location, and traveled to Namakli, where she was pinned down by Reaper forces. Shepard extracted her, though the rest of her team was killed. Ann showed Shepard a Leviathan artifact, but a trio of Marauders activated it and Ann was possessed by Leviathan, forcing the squad to destroy the artifact.

While leaving Namakli, Shepard told Ann of her father's death. Grief-stricken, she returned to his lab on the Citadel, where Shepard found her crying and offered sympathy. She recalled that her last conversation with her father had been a trivial argument, reminisced about him, and vowed to find the monster that murdered him. She researched the artifacts, theorizing that Leviathan used them to induce a quantum entanglement state to stimulate neural activity, similar to rachni communication, and speculated that Leviathan might have been preparing the rachni to fight the Reapers. Discussing quantum entanglement with EDI, she concluded the artifact acted mainly as a receiver and became a transmitter while possessing a victim, which could be traced. Against Shepard's wishes, Ann insisted on exposing herself to the artifact so EDI could narrow down Leviathan's cluster, with James Vega standing by to intervene. If Shepard let the process continue, Ann emerged relatively unharmed and warned that Leviathan was angry and wanted to kill Shepard; if Shepard stopped Vega twice, Ann was incapacitated and taken for medical treatment. If she survived unscathed, she later assisted Admiral Hackett, using her paleotechnology research to aid the Crucible project.
