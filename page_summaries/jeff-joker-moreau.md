Flight Lieutenant Jeff "Joker" Moreau was the pilot of the SSV Normandy and later the Normandy SR-2, an exceptional helmsman confident to the point of arrogance and a constant wisecracker. Born in 2155, he had a moderate-to-severe case of Vrolik syndrome (brittle bone disease), was born with severe leg fractures, and relied on crutches and leg braces. He grew up on Arcturus Station where his mother was a civilian contractor, joined the Alliance Navy, and earned the nickname "Joker" from a flight instructor because he rarely smiled, being too focused on work; he graduated top of his class.

Per Mass Effect: He Who Laughs Best, Joker was rejected as the Normandy's pilot in 2183 because of his disease. Goaded by the turian General Invectus, he covertly boarded and hijacked the ship, completing the test course flawlessly while evading a fighter patrol. Facing court-martial, he was instead made the Normandy's official pilot after Invectus, impressed, personally insisted on it and Anderson agreed.

In Mass Effect, Joker rarely left the bridge and served as Shepard's eyes and ears. He famously dropped the Mako with only twenty meters of open ground. During the battle of the Citadel he rallied the Fifth Fleet against Sovereign and led the Alliance charge with barely a scratch. In Mass Effect 2, the Collectors destroyed the original Normandy; Shepard died getting Joker to an escape pod. Joker joined Cerberus to pilot the Normandy SR-2 and initially feuded with the AI EDI, but during the Collector attack he handed EDI full control of the ship to escape, gradually developing respect and affection for her, and began referring to her as "she." He piloted the SR-2 through the Omega 4 relay and, if enough squadmates survived, gave covering fire with an M-8 Avenger during the escape.

In Mass Effect 3, Joker and EDI had secretly arranged for EDI to pose as a VI so the Alliance would not dismantle her. Freed at the start of the Reaper invasion, he resumed piloting and kept in touch with Anderson, who trusted him to watch over Shepard. He carried lingering guilt over Shepard's earlier death. Shepard could encourage a romance between Joker and EDI, taking her on shore leave as his "mobility assistance mech." When the Reapers hit the colony Tiptree, home to his father and his fifteen-year-old sister Hilary ("Gunny"), Joker clung to hope she was among the child refugees. Before the final assault he stood to salute Shepard. If Shepard activated the Crucible, Joker refused to leave the blast range until a companion convinced him, then crash-landed the Normandy on a tropical planet; if enough forces were rallied, he emerged alive, with EDI if their relationship was pursued and she was not destroyed by the Destroy ending. An unused first-game soundbank contains many cut cockpit voicelines in English and Polish, plus flirty remarks to a female Shepard. He was written by Drew Karpyshyn, Lukas Kristjanson, and Patrick Weekes, and voiced by Seth Green.
