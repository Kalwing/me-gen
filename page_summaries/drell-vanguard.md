Short page. A Mass Effect 3 multiplayer character kit presenting the drell as a Vanguard class, almost entirely gameplay stats and tactics. Lore content is limited to the standard drell description: dense muscle tissue giving wiry strength, unique hyoid throat bones allowing high-pitched vocalization and resistance to strangulation, and a fast but fragile physique. The kit used Biotic Charge, Pull, Cluster Grenade and Fitness, and had among the lowest endurance of any class, offset by high movement speed.
