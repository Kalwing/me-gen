General Williams was the Systems Alliance commander of the garrison on Shanxi when the First Contact War broke out in 2157. After humans were caught activating Relay 314, a turian patrol fleet destroyed an Alliance expeditionary fleet and broke through to Shanxi, the nearest human colony, beginning a siege with orbital bombardment. Cut off from reinforcements, Williams commanded a desperate guerrilla campaign from the surface, but turian tactics were overwhelming, with entire city blocks destroyed to eliminate small marine teams. Believing the only way to save Shanxi's population was surrender, Williams did so, becoming the only human commander to surrender to an alien force. Shanxi was soon liberated by the Alliance Second Fleet, and the Citadel Council brokered a truce before full-scale war.

Williams fell into disgrace, was brought to Earth in handcuffs, and though never formally charged, was demoted, assigned to desk jobs, and retired a year after Shanxi's liberation, spending his remaining days working construction in the colonies. His disgrace stained his family: his son never rose above serviceman third class, and his granddaughter Ashley Williams was kept from fleet postings and relegated to colony garrison duty. Alliance historians' attempts to exonerate him failed.

In Mass Effect: Evolution, Williams armed and trained a group of mercenaries, including Jack Harper, to fight the turians on Shanxi. Convinced the turians sought something on the planet beyond humans, he gave Harper's team a Mako and a captured turian prisoner before ordering a retreat to Arcturus Station. He drank on the job under the stress. Weeks later he presided over a prisoner exchange at Arcturus, where Harper's prisoner proved to be turian General Desolas Arterius, who had captured Harper and Eva Coré; when Desolas tried to take Harper back, Williams ordered him off Arcturus. Williams offered condolences over the death of Harper's teammate Ben Hislop, disagreed with the Alliance's new policy of peaceful coexistence, planned to resign his command, and gave his awarded personal shuttle to Harper and Coré to aid their investigation. His story is told during Ashley's conversations with Commander Shepard.
