The N7 Defender Armor was a full armor set in Mass Effect 3, released as part of the N7 Warfare Gear downloadable content pack, a retail pre-order bonus also included in the Wii U Special Edition. In Legendary Edition it was available from the Spectre Requisitions terminal in the Citadel Embassies for a base 80,000 credits, and came with a confirmation email from Admiral Hackett, who said he had arranged its delivery to the Normandy before supply lines worsened.

In-universe, the Defender Armor was a variation on the N7 Special Forces' combat gear, built for long engagements with few reinforcements. When the wearer fired a weapon, the suit's computers diverted energy from the main power cell to the gun's kinetic coils for extra punch; storage compartments held spare thermal clips, capacitors fed extra power to shields at critical moments, and an injection system with neural-linked bio-monitors regulated the wearer's breathing and adrenaline. Mechanically it increased health, shields, weapon damage and ammo capacity, making it well suited to the Soldier class or a shotgun Infiltrator.

The armor is visually identical, apart from color, to James Vega's default outfit, and the same model is used by Human Soldiers and Vanguards in multiplayer with the helmet replaced by the Death Mask. The N7 Valkyrie assault rifle was included alongside it in the N7 Warfare Gear pack.
