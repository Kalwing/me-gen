The Citadel was a colossal deep-space station in the Widow system of the Serpent Nebula, serving as the capital of the Citadel Council and the political and cultural heart of Citadel space. Roughly pentagram-shaped, it consisted of a central ring about 7.2 km in diameter from which five arms, the Wards, extended, each about 43.6 km long; open, the station measured 44.7 km in length, housed about 13.2 million people plus the keepers, and could close its arms into an armored cylinder about 45 km long in emergencies. Gravity was simulated through rotation. The asari discovered the station in 580 BCE, soon joined by the salarians and then the volus; other races gained embassies as they were contacted, and the Council eventually comprised asari, salarians, and turians. The station's builders, its core and master control unit, its construction material, and the nature of its keeper caretakers all remained mysteries. It was long believed to have been built by the Protheans, but they were merely the last race to use it before the asari arrived. In truth, the Citadel and the mass relays were creations of the Reapers, built to facilitate their cycle of galactic genocide: the station was an inactive mass relay leading to dark space, and the keepers were placed to keep its occupants from discovering its key functions, allowing the Reapers to wipe out galactic leadership in a single strike. According to the VI Vigil, the last Protheans used the Conduit to reach the Citadel and disrupt the cycle, forcing the Reapers to travel to the Milky Way at normal FTL speeds, though those Protheans likely starved to death on the station. The Presidium ring held government offices, embassies, shops, and an artificial 20-hour day-night cycle, monitored by the VI Avina; the Citadel Tower housed the Council Chambers and station traffic control. The Wards were the densely populated residential and commercial arms, policed by roughly 200,000 C-Sec officers and home to the club Flux, the Silversun Strip, and the Citadel Archives; Barla Von called them the cultural heart of Citadel space. Defenses included the hard-to-navigate Serpent Nebula, the Citadel Fleet led by the asari dreadnought Destiny Ascension, rapid mass relay reinforcement, the closing hull, and anti-fighter turrets. Unique inhabitants included the duct rats, homeless children living in the ventilation system, among them Mouse, an informant for Thane Krios, and the bio-engineered insectoid keepers. In 2186 the Illusive Man fled to the Citadel and alerted the Reapers, who seized it and moved it into Earth's orbit; the Catalyst needed to complete the Crucible was revealed to be part of the station. During the battle for Earth, Commander Shepard boarded via a transport beam, opened the Wards for the Crucible to attach, and met the Catalyst, the Intelligence controlling the Reapers, then chose to destroy the Reapers, control them, or merge organic and synthetic life; the Citadel amplified the Crucible's energy and transmitted it to the Charon Relay and the wider relay network. Depending on the choice, the Citadel and relays were severely damaged and later rebuilt, or the Citadel remained intact while the relays were damaged. Proposed development names for the station included "Optigon" and "Oculon," and "C-Sec" was at one point "Oc-Sec."
