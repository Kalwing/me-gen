Lieutenant Locke was a human Blue Suns mercenary in charge of the raid on the dig site on Joab, apparently taking orders directly from Commander Santiago. He was killed during a firefight with Commander Shepard and the squad. In gameplay he was equipped with an M-15 Vindicator (adding Disruptor Ammo if left alone), had high health, strong shields, and Tech Armor, and was supported by other Blue Suns including two heavies. He functioned essentially as a stronger Blue Suns Centurion, tending to take cover behind a crate while his heavies engaged.
