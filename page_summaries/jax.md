Jax was a krogan arms dealer on the Citadel, notorious for selling illegal weapon mods that gave criminals an advantage over C-Sec officers. C-Sec detective Chellick asked Commander Shepard to pick up some mods from Jax to aid an investigation, a request tied to Shepard pressing Chellick, on Rita's behalf, to pull Rita's sister Jenna out of her undercover informant role at Chora's Den. Chellick agreed to extract Jenna but insisted on completing his investigation.

Chellick directed Shepard to meet Jax in the Lower Markets and warned against tipping him off. At the meeting Shepard could either hand over the payment as instructed or attempt to arrest Jax, which triggered a firefight ending in Jax's death. If paid, Jax suspected nothing and left quickly, unaware that Chellick could still track his movements and use him to identify other buyers. In gameplay Jax had higher health than his humanoid bodyguards, three regenerating shield bars, and would revive once after being killed unless his body was destroyed; he fought mainly with a shotgun, switching to an assault rifle at range while closing distance with Berserker Charge.
