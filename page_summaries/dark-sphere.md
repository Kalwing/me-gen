Dark Sphere was a biotic power exclusive to Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode, available only to the Awakened Collector Adept. It launched a slow-moving sphere of dark energy that traveled in a straight line toward the crosshairs and stuck to any solid surface or cover it hit, including a Geth Juggernaut Soldier's Hex Shield. The sphere dealt damage over time to any target it passed over and could be detonated at any time for massive area damage; the power only incurred a cooldown when detonated, and it dissipated on its own after a few seconds if not detonated. Its six ranks offered evolutions for recharge speed, detonation damage, damage over time, detonation radius, and a rank 6 choice between Dark Matter (100% more damage but 50% smaller radius) and Unstable Dark Sphere (larger radius and greater damage over time). Base values included 1000 detonation damage, a 5-meter radius, 250 damage over time over 5 seconds, and a 24-second detonation recharge. It primed enemies for Biotic Explosions before detonation and could clear large groups. Certain "Duration" evolutions were noted to reduce damage due to a suspected bug.
