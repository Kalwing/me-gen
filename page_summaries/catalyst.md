The Catalyst, also known as the Intelligence, was an ancient artificial intelligence residing within the Citadel. It embodied the collective consciousness and memories of the Reapers, and therefore of countless ancient, harvested civilizations. Before Commander Shepard met it, the Catalyst had been believed to be the final component needed to complete the Crucible and was mistakenly thought to be the Citadel itself.

The Catalyst was created by the Leviathans, who called it "The Intelligence" and built it to oversee relations between organic and synthetic life after seeing many organic races they commanded destroyed by their own synthetic creations. Tasked with preserving life in the galaxy by any means necessary, it deployed pawns to gather data across the cosmos and concluded that synthetics must inevitably evolve past and destroy their organic creators. Its solution was the Reapers: it turned on the Leviathans, used its pawns to slaughter them, and processed them into the first Reaper, Harbinger, preserving their minds as the Reaper gestalt consciousness that was then incorporated into the Catalyst itself. Each cycle, spacefaring organic and synthetic species were harvested and converted into new Reapers made in Harbinger's image, preserving their genetic makeup and knowledge while allowing more primitive races to advance. The Catalyst commanded the Reapers to build the mass relays to accelerate each cycle, treated the whole galaxy as its experiment, and repeatedly tried and failed to merge organic and synthetic life, blaming organics for not being "ready". Several cycles earlier it had tried to eradicate a concept that could destroy the Reapers, unaware it survived as the Crucible.

When Shepard reached its chamber late in the war, the Catalyst appeared as a translucent projection of a young boy Shepard had seen killed during the invasion of Earth. It said Shepard was the first organic to stand before it and converse, which made it reassess its solution, but it could not change course without Shepard's intervention. The Catalyst was presumed lost in some of Shepard's possible choices: the Destroy option wiped out all synthetic life, and Control replaced it with a digital copy of Shepard's consciousness as the Reapers' master AI, while its fate under Synthesis or a refusal was unknown, though the Stargazer later described a time free of the Reaper threat.
