The Freelance Bomb Tech was a mercenary with specialist bomb training who appeared during the Omega gangs' assault on Archangel's base in Mass Effect 2. He was deployed to set up an explosive near the living room of the hideout but was killed when Commander Shepard's team arrived to save the besieged vigilante. He was armed with an M-9 Tempest submachine gun and had only health on lower difficulties, gaining light shielding on higher ones.

Functionally he was identical to an ordinary freelancer, sharing the same behavior, strengths, and weaknesses, so standard tactics against freelancers applied. Because he began the encounter crouching with his weapon holstered, the element of surprise favored Shepard: shooting the bomb beside him as the other freelancers reached his position would detonate it and wipe out the group, or at least leave them too disoriented to retaliate.
