The Mu Relay was a mass relay lost roughly four thousand years before the events of Mass Effect. It was the only relay linking to the Pangaea Expanse and the former Prothean colony of Ilos. A nearby supernova pushed the relay out of its orbit without damaging it, then swathed it in hot gas and dust, making it undetectable because scanners cannot pick up "cold" objects in interstellar space.

The enveloping nebula lay in rachni-controlled space. Matriarch Benezia explained to Commander Shepard that the fiercely territorial rachni, seeking to close all routes into their space, eventually located the relay through sheer numbers of spacefaring ships. When the krogan exterminated the rachni, the coordinates were thought lost forever, but the Rachni Queen resurrected by Binary Helix carried them in genetic memory. Benezia passed the coordinates to Shepard, though Saren Arterius had already obtained them. Because shifting borders now placed the Mu Relay in the Terminus Systems, the Council refused to send the Citadel Fleet through it to Ilos for fear of starting a war, leaving the stealth-equipped SSV Normandy as the only ship able to reach the relay secretly. The relay was likely named after the fabled lost continent of Mu.
