Rila was an asari Ardat-Yakshi and a daughter of Samara. Because of her genetic condition she had been required to retire to the Ardat-Yakshi monastery on Lesuss, where she lived with one of her two sisters, Falere. She was encountered in Mass Effect 3 when Commander Shepard investigated a distress signal from Lesuss during the Reaper invasion.

Rila was first mentioned by Falere, who told Shepard's squad that Reaper forces had dragged Rila into the monastery's Great Hall to be turned into a Banshee and begged the Commander to save her. When Shepard explained that a bomb was meant to destroy the monastery, Falere became angry, accused Shepard of not trying to help people, and set off alone to find her sister. Fighting their way to the Great Hall, Shepard's team found Falere trying to wake Rila, who appeared to have fainted. Rila regained consciousness but, already suffering the effects of indoctrination, attacked Falere before being knocked out again.

After Shepard held off Reaper attackers trying to sabotage the bomb, Rila briefly fought off the indoctrination, found the detonator, and insisted that Falere leave with Shepard while she stayed behind. Falere struggled but was dragged by Shepard to an elevator. Rila was seized by several Banshees, one of which mortally wounded her. In a final act of defiance she declared that the Ardat-Yakshi were not the Reapers' slaves and triggered the detonator, destroying the monastery.
