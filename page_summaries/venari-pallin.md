Executor Venari Pallin was the turian head of Citadel Security and the liaison between C-Sec and the Citadel Council, with an office on the Presidium near the embassies. A thirty-year C-Sec veteran, he was extremely by-the-book, unlike Garrus Vakarian. After the attack on Eden Prime he assigned Garrus to investigate Saren Arterius, then pulled him off the case when Garrus found nothing conclusive and demanded more time; Commander Shepard witnessed their argument in the Citadel Tower. Pallin openly disliked both Spectres, whom he distrusted because he believed no one should act above the law and claimed he had never broken the law to do his job, and humans, whom he thought were rising too quickly and were becoming the Council's "pet." He conceded the galaxy sometimes needed Spectres but insisted they be held to a higher standard, or the Council would produce more agents like Saren.

In Mass Effect: Retribution (2186), Pallin remained head of C-Sec and created an all-turian task force to assist Ambassador Orinia, David Anderson, and Kahlee Sanders against Cerberus, agreeing readily because he had served under Orinia in the military. In Mass Effect: Inquisition, Donnel Udina suspected Pallin of aiding the Council's enemies and had Captain Bailey investigate; Pallin denied the accusations, resisted arrest, opened fire on Bailey, was wounded in return, and was fatally shot while trying to tackle him, a killing that led to Bailey's promotion to Commander of C-Sec. The Mass Effect 3 codex instead states Pallin was alive during the Cerberus coup, having caught wind of the plot with the salarian councilor, and was killed by Kai Leng. Cut content variously had Pallin survive the Battle of the Citadel or be killed by geth and replaced by Chellick.
