Atlas Station was the central facility in a series of Cerberus research bases on the planet Aite dedicated to Project Overlord, featured in the Mass Effect 2 DLC Overlord. It housed the main laboratory where all Project Overlord experiments were conducted and where the project's rogue VI resided.

After overriding the lockdowns at Prometheus Station and Vulcan Station, Commander Shepard gained access to Atlas Station. Dr. Gavin Archer informed Shepard that the VI was attempting to upload itself off the station. Shepard and the squad stopped that upload, but the VI infected Shepard's omni-tool and cybernetic implants, forcing the Commander into a virtual reality environment. Shepard fought through to the VI, which then tried to upload itself to the Normandy. Shepard destroyed it and discovered the truth behind Project Overlord: David Archer, Gavin's autistic brother, had been forcibly fused with the VI, which overwhelmed him, and he simply wanted the ordeal to stop. Shepard then made a moral choice about the future of the project and of David before leaving Aite. Following the naming scheme of the Aite stations after Greco-Roman mythological figures, Atlas Station was likely named for the Titan Atlas, who was forced to hold up the sky.
