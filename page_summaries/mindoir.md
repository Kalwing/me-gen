Mindoir was a human farming colony in the Attican Traverse. In 2170 it was raided by batarian slavers, who slaughtered most of the colonists and fitted survivors with horrific cranial control implants. The Alliance sent Marines to drive the batarians out, but their defenses were too strong and the Marines were pinned down, forced to watch the colonists suffer without being able to reach them. The attack may have been batarian retaliation for humanity's aggressive colonization of the Skyllian Verge.

The colony features in Commander Shepard's possible backgrounds. A Colonist Shepard was born to a farming family on Mindoir and was sixteen during the raid; Shepard's family fought back but an artillery strike killed everyone but Shepard, who survived buried under rubble and was rescued by an Alliance patrol, enlisting two years later. Shepard can meet another survivor, a traumatized girl named Talitha who suffers Stockholm Syndrome after Marines killed her batarian "masters." For a Spacer Shepard, Hannah Shepard and Lieutenant Ernesto Zabaleta served aboard the carrier SSV Einstein, which responded to the raid; witnessing the atrocities left Zabaleta with PTSD that Shepard can later help treat. For a War Hero Shepard, Officer Eddie Lang watched the broadcast of Shepard's Star of Terra ceremony on Mindoir, indicating the settlements were rebuilt.

By Mass Effect 2, Mindoir had a shuttle route to Nos Astra on Illium, suspended after attacks on human colonies, and a Colonist Shepard's likeness was adopted on the colonial seal. In Mass Effect 3, on Eden Prime, a Colonist Shepard could remark of rebuilding: "They rebuilt Mindoir. It wasn't the same."
