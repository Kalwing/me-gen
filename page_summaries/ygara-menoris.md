Ygara Menoris was a former subcaptain of the asari commando unit Talein's Daughters and one of the few close acquaintances Cora Harper made during her time with the asari. Taller and more muscular than the average asari and biotically stronger than some matriarchs despite being near the end of her maiden years, she had served as a commando for about 300 years before amicably leaving her unit, partly because she disliked missions with shady dealings. She bought and refurbished a surplus asari destroyer she named Audacity and planned to start her own mercenary group in the Kepler Verge, considering Council space too tame. She had once spent 20 years on Palaven in a relationship that ended, leaving her with lasting culture shock.

In Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation, Ygara ran into Cora at Tamayo Point station in Sol and helped her fend off harassers, then later lent her mercenary crew to a software-retrieval job. Posing as Council eezo-converter inspectors, they infiltrated the Home Away station so Cora could retrieve a code package while Ygara's crew distracted administrators, though the station's facial recognition flagged Cora after an unexpected Khalisah al-Jilani interview aired. The group escaped without casualties.

Aboard Audacity below Mars orbit, Ygara's betrayal was revealed: moneyed parties had paid her to steal the code package, and the Tamayo Point "chance encounter" had been an engineered ruse. She had briefed her mercenaries, grouped them at Home Away while Cora was exposed, and tipped off al-Jilani hoping station security would eliminate Cora. She had spared Cora during the escape only because she had just saved her from a drone blast and did not want to risk the shuttle. Once her crew was equipped, Ygara threw a shockwave at Cora, claiming she would only be sold to batarian slavers; Cora escaped through the bay airlock before Audacity jumped to FTL. On Illium, Ygara housed her crew at the Bulwark mercenary hotel in Nos Astra, intending to betray her original client and sell the code to the highest bidder, but was assassinated first: stabbed in the back with an omni-blade, her room's temperature lowered to obscure the time of death, and the kernel copied and deleted from Audacity. Cora found her body after infiltrating the Bulwark with credentials from another double-crossed associate, the volus broker Eppo Wen.

Her mercenary crew included asari ex-commandos Tella Namir, Leri T'Eln and the sapper Bannyn T'Dahn; the human-sized krogan pilot and heavy-weapons specialist Jorgal Kih; the turian disinformation specialist Octavia Suran; and the twin quarian brothers Hanon'Milah and Shilu'Milah nar Qwib-Qwib, on their Pilgrimage, who forged documents and hacked networks.
