This short Mass Effect 3 mission was acquired automatically after liberating Rannoch from the Reapers. The asari Councilor contacted Shepard on the vid-com claiming vital information that had to be shared in person. Returning to the Citadel, Shepard went to the late Donnel Udina's office in the Embassies, where the Councilor reported that the Asari Republics were losing badly and revealed that a Prothean artifact had been hidden for millennia in the Temple of Athame on the asari homeworld, Thessia. The conversation ended the mission and unlocked Priority: Thessia.

If Shepard had sabotaged the genophage cure and Urdnot Wrex led the krogan, Wrex was waiting at Normandy Dock: Bay D24 with a recording of Shepard's conversation with Dalatrass Linron. Enraged by the betrayal, he attacked Shepard and could not be reasoned with. Two Renegade interrupts let Shepard shoot him; otherwise Bailey and C-Sec gunned him down. Killing Wrex cost the Clan Urdnot and krogan mercenary War Assets, and the incident was afterward covered up, evidenced by an ANN mail. If Urdnot Wreav led the krogan, he never discovered the deception and remained oblivious through the end of the game. The page also documents a sequence-break exploit using the DLC rapid-transport terminals that allowed players to sabotage the cure, gain salarian support, and keep Wrex alive until the final battle in London.
