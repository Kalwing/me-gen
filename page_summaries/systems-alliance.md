The Systems Alliance was the supranational body representing Earth and all human colonies in Citadel space, backed by Earth's most powerful nations and serving as humanity's military, exploratory and economic spearhead. It was governed by a parliament at Arcturus Station, which was also its capital, and headed by a Prime Minister (Amul Shastri as of 2186). Member nations retained sovereignty on Earth, and other Citadel species held no right of oversight into Alliance affairs.

The Alliance was founded after the 2148 discovery of Prothean ruins on Mars proved humanity was not alone. The discovery fractured religions but unified politics into an "us vs. them" outlook, and within a year Earth's eighteen largest nations drafted and ratified the Systems Alliance charter; they then pooled their militaries into the Systems Alliance Military. In 2149 the Alliance discovered the Charon Relay orbiting Pluto, which propelled humanity into rapid interstellar expansion. In 2157, activating a dormant relay led a human scout fleet to encounter a turian patrol, triggering the First Contact War: the turians invaded Shanxi, the Alliance took control of the human response, and the roughly three-month conflict cost 623 human lives before the Citadel Council brokered peace. Humanity then joined the galactic stage, gaining a Citadel embassy in 2165 with Anita Goyle as first ambassador.

In the 2160s, Goyle's attempt to make Lieutenant David Anderson humanity's first Spectre was sabotaged by his observer, Saren Arterius. The Alliance co-developed the stealth frigate SSV Normandy with the turians, commissioned in 2183 under Anderson. After Shepard exposed Saren as a rogue agent and Sovereign attacked the Citadel, Shepard became humanity's first Spectre; depending on whether the Council was saved, humanity gained a Council seat or unexpected military superiority. After the Normandy's destruction and Shepard's presumed death, the Alliance suppressed talk of the Reapers and was caught unaware by the Collector abductions, initially blaming Cerberus. In 2186 the Reapers destroyed Arcturus Station, wiped out the Alliance leadership and conquered Earth within hours; the Alliance regrouped with a multi-species coalition fleet to deploy the Crucible.

The Alliance military was respected for novel tactics and technology, emphasising fire support, flexibility, speed, mobility and individual initiative, bypassing enemy strongpoints to strike supply lines while leaving only token scouting garrisons on colonies and massing powerful fleets at relay nexuses. Only about 3% of humans volunteered for service, earning the Alliance a "sleeping giant" reputation. As a Treaty of Farixen signatory it was limited in dreadnoughts and compensated with treaty-exempt carriers. Key suppliers included Hahne-Kedar and Aldrin Labs; soldiers received gene therapy, and biotics were trained via BAaT and equipped with L3 implants. Facilities included Arcturus Station, Gagarin Station ("Jump Zero"), Jon Grissom Academy, Fort Charles Upham on Titan, and Luna Base. Notable dissenters included the isolationist party Terra Firma and the Illusive Man's Cerberus.
