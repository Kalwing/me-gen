After Shepard stopped the Cerberus coup on the Citadel during Priority: The Citadel II, Primarch Adrien Victus sent an email asking Shepard, in a Spectre capacity, to investigate volus ambassador Din Korlack as a possible Cerberus mole. Cerberus had ambushed a turian cruiser carrying high-ranking Hierarchy officials whose top-secret position was known only to Korlack, and Victus had heard rumors of a bounty on Korlack's head, but could not level the accusation himself because the turian and volus economies were tightly intertwined. Shepard used the Spectre Terminal in the Citadel Embassies to obtain Korlack's location, then gained access to an office suite from a turian guard. A terminal there held three audio recordings proving Korlack's collusion with Cerberus and his later severance of ties; if Zaeed Massani had been imported alive from Mass Effect 2, his voice was among them. An earpiece linked to Korlack led to a scene in the Presidium Commons where Korlack, held captive, slyly named "Selkish Arms" within his captors' hearing so Shepard could find him among apartments at the far ends of a hall, triggering a brief timed sequence. Inside the correct apartment, Korlack's captors were killed: by a loyal Zaeed if he had survived the suicide mission and been made loyal on Zaeed: The Price of Revenge; a disloyal Zaeed died on the floor after killing the mercs; otherwise a human woman and a krogan man were dispatched by Shepard. Shepard then chose between saving the colony Cerberus was targeting or gaining the Volus Bombing Fleet as a War Asset, with a loyal Zaeed enabling a Paragon or Renegade option that secured both. Completion granted 210 experience, 5,000 credits, and morality points, and a surviving Zaeed could be recruited from the refugee camp as a War Asset. Choosing the bombers over Korlack's intel produced a later news report about the unrevealed Cerberus target.
