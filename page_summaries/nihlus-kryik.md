Nihlus Kryik was one of the Citadel Council's most decorated Spectre agents, a turian. Born in a small mercenary outpost outside Hierarchy space, he learned to fight for what he wanted. His father died when he was sixteen and his mother forced him into the turian military, where his outsider status meant that despite topping his class he was never accepted. His skills as a soldier were unquestionable, but his attitude got him in trouble; he repeatedly disobeyed direct orders to do what he thought best, and even single-handedly routing an enemy patrol earned him rebukes for recklessness. After being reassigned to a new squad for the third time, he met Saren Arterius, who was impressed, befriended and mentored him, and within a year Nihlus was invited to join the Spectres. Free of military procedure he excelled, stepping out of Saren's shadow to become one of the Council's top agents. Cool under pressure, he found and exploited enemy weaknesses; his methods were less brutal than Saren's, and unlike Saren he did not hold a grudge against humanity, instead seeing its potential.

In Mass Effect, Nihlus accompanied the Normandy on its shakedown mission, treated with suspicion by the crew who doubted a Spectre was sent merely to check stealth systems. He was there to recover the Prothean Beacon on Eden Prime and to assess Commander Shepard, whom he had personally nominated for Spectre candidacy; his stated reasons varied with Shepard's psychological profile. He scouted ahead on Eden Prime and encountered Saren; surprised but grateful for help against the geth, he dropped his guard, and Saren shot him in the back of the head. A hiding dockworker named Powell witnessed the killing and later told Shepard. Saren, as Nihlus's mentor, received his personal files upon his death, potentially gaining insights that helped him evade capture.

Mass Effect 2 added that Nihlus was once drawn into a two-week cat-and-mouse pursuit in the wilderness with the asari justicar Samara, who tried to kill him after witnessing him kill an unarmed civilian. Nihlus used her code against her, forcing her to choose between saving an innocent and capturing him; she chose the former, letting him escape, though she came to admire his adaptability. Alastair Duncan voiced both Nihlus and the turian Councilor Sparatus.
