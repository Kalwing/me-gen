The CAT6 Heavy was a CAT6 mercenary unit encountered in the Mass Effect 3 Citadel DLC. It was armed with an M-76 Revenant light machine gun for sustained fire at medium range, and in melee it swiped with its shield or weapon or performed a combo in which it slammed its shield down to create a stunning shockwave before a lethal rifle barrage. Deprived of its shield, a Heavy fought with a stun baton and, unlike the CAT6 Specialist, could be staggered by light stun effects during that animation.

CAT6 Heavies had heavy armor and could generate large ballistic shields similar to those of Cerberus Guardians. Unlike a Guardian's shield, the Heavy's was at least partly energy-based: it could be destroyed by Overload, Energy Drain and Sabotage, which also caused feedback that electrocuted and stunned the Heavy, and it could be yanked away entirely with Pull, though Lash did not work on it. A destroyed shield regenerated if the Heavy was left alone. While the shield was up the Heavy was largely immune to powers, debuffs and gunfire from the front, but remained vulnerable to flanking fire and to Reave, the only biotic power that could hit it or prime it for a biotic combo through the shield. The shield had a "mail slot", but because Heavies were armored even beneath it, headshots through the slot were less effective than against Guardians.

Even without a shield, Heavies could not be frozen, lifted, knocked down by force-based powers or affected by Stasis, though Charge and Nova could inflict heavy staggers that displaced the shield. They took 40 percent extra damage from headshots and could be staggered by gunfire or powers. Weapons that pierced by default, such as the M-98 Widow, Black Widow, Javelin, N7 Crusader, N7 Typhoon and Executioner Pistol, along with the Chakram Launcher, Geth Plasma Shotgun and Kishock Harpoon Gun, could hit them through the shield without the usual piercing penalty. Heavies consistently closed to short range, where their shield ground-slam could flush Shepard from cover, and unlike Guardians they could climb ladders by briefly deactivating their shields.
