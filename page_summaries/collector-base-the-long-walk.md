Collector Base: The Long Walk was the second segment of the Mass Effect 2 suicide mission, triggered after Collector Base: Infiltration. Entering a room holding the colonists and the Normandy crew, Commander Shepard wiped condensation from a pod and found either Lilith from Horizon, if Shepard had gone to the base immediately after the crew's abduction, or Kelly Chambers, if Shepard had done one or more missions first; the occupant stirred, screamed, and was liquefied. Shepard ordered the remaining pods opened, saving the whole crew, half of it, or only Doctor Chakwas depending on how quickly the Commander had departed. Chakwas explained that the Collectors were processing humans into a gray paste of genetic material routed to another part of the station. EDI reported that the only route ahead held too many Seeker Swarms for Mordin's countermeasures, so a biotic had to hold a protective field over a small squad while a second team created a distraction. Shepard chose the biotic specialist, ideally a loyal Samara, Jack, or Morinth; a second fire-team leader, ideally Miranda or a loyal Garrus or Jacob; and optionally a squad member to escort the crew back to the Normandy, since Joker said they had to go backwards. Assigning a non-loyal escort saved the crew but killed that squadmate, while assigning no escort kept all squadmates for the final battle but doomed the crew.

The chosen squad then advanced through the swarm-filled area inside the biotic bubble, where leaving the field meant death, fighting Collectors, Harbinger, husks, Abominations, and a Scion across several stops. During the final run to the security door, if the biotic specialist was non-loyal or non-ideal, the biotic collapsed from exhaustion and whichever squadmate held the rearguard was carried off by the swarm, following a fixed death order; a loyal, ideal specialist pushed the barrier back, sending the swarms and Collectors flying, and everyone reached safety. As the second fire-team leader entered they were shot, shrugging it off if ideal or dying in Shepard's arms if non-ideal, though with only three squad members left any leader survived. Joker then reported on whoever was escorting the crew and warned that the Collectors were massing outside the doors, and Miranda said the rest of the team would stay back to cover Shepard and a small squad. Cut content included unused Mordin voicelines volunteering as the biotic specialist despite his lack of any biotic powers.
