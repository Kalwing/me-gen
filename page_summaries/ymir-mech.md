The Battle YMIR Mech, or Model 34-A, commonly called simply a Heavy Mech, was a massive anti-infantry combat walker manufactured by Hahne-Kedar and introduced in Mass Effect 2. Clad in bone-white armor with red lighting, YMIRs were used as infantry support by Eclipse and Blue Suns mercenaries and as security in some colonies, frequently deployed alongside LOKI Mechs. Like geth Armatures and Colossi, they "unpacked" when deployed, and they occasionally spoke in a low robotic voice reporting their combat status.

YMIR Mechs carried twin automatic mass accelerator cannons in the right arm and a rocket launcher in the left, both concealed until the arm opened to fire. They could engage at long range but relentlessly advanced on enemies, moving very slowly and loudly and unable to climb steep elevation changes. They possessed high shielding and armor; while either layer held, they were virtually immune to force-based attacks and could not be stunned or overheated. Once stripped of protection they became vulnerable to freezing, stunning and bonus weapon damage.

A YMIR always exploded when destroyed. A headshot kill triggered a critical overload producing a delayed, much larger blast comparable to an M-920 Cain projectile, capable of instantly killing a fully healthy character and penetrating cover; luring a YMIR among other enemies before killing it could wipe them out. Recommended tactics included using Disruptor Ammo, Overload or Energy Drain against shields; Warp, Reave, Incinerate or piercing ammo against armor; and headshots as the primary damage tool, best lined up during the mech's switch between machine gun and rocket launcher. The M-622 Avalanche stripped and froze a YMIR, the M-920 Cain one-shot it on all difficulties, and AI Hacking could turn an unprotected YMIR into a temporary ally. In Mass Effect: Infiltrator, Cerberus fielded apparently upgraded YMIR variants incorporating salvaged Collector Base technology.
