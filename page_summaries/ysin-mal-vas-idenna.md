Captain Ysin'Mal vas Idenna, usually called Captain Mal, was the quarian captain of the Idenna, a vessel of the Migrant Fleet. Unlike most conservative quarians, he was an aggressive proponent of change, notably advocating long-term missions for ships to leave the Flotilla and search for new homeworlds.

In Mass Effect: Ascension, Captain Mal welcomed Kahlee Sanders, Hendel Mitra and Gillian Grayson aboard the Idenna but privately warned Kahlee that the Migrant Fleet was less safe than it appeared. During a meeting with Admiralty Board and Conclave representatives he grew excited at mention of the Reapers, suggesting the quarians could find and reactivate one to control the geth and reclaim their homeworlds. He later confided to Kahlee and Hendel that the Flotilla was slowly dying, its ships breaking down faster than they could be replaced. Cerberus then attacked the Idenna; Captain Mal rallied and fought alongside his people but was being overwhelmed until Gillian Grayson intervened and Paul Grayson switched sides, warning them he had planted explosives to cripple the ship. Mal and Hendel disabled the explosives and saved the vessel.

Shaken, the Admiralty Board finally approved Mal's proposal, dispatching several cruisers including the Idenna on long-term missions to seek quarian survival through a Reaper or habitable planets. Mal hand-picked a crew of fifty, including Gillian, Hendel and the young quarian Lemm, judged Paul Grayson leniently by making him Kahlee's prisoner, and prepared to lead a five-year mission through a newly activated mass relay into uncharted space. In Mass Effect 2, Captain Ysin'Mal sent Commander Shepard a message of personal thanks for the Normandy SR-2's rescue of Lieutenant Forzan vas Idenna, the sole survivor of the crash of the Cyniad on Gei Hinnom.
