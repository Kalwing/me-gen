This page collects the situational lines Kaidan Alenko speaks when in Commander Shepard's squad across Mass Effect and Mass Effect 3, including reactions at specific plot points, ambient "talk to squadmate" location remarks, and spontaneous comments. It shows his dry humor, by-the-book instincts, and distrust of AI, with dialogue changing based on the other squadmate present and on Shepard's gender or class.

Representative lines:

Kaidan, on the Citadel Wards after Eden Prime, replying to a female Shepard about whether the Council could like humanity: "When you put it that way, there's no reason they wouldn't like you. I mean us. Humans. Ma'am."

Kaidan, during Citadel: Signal Tracking when Shepard asks whether the matter can be resolved peacefully: "What are you, crazy? It's an AI, like the Geth, it would kill us all if it could!"

Kaidan, at the Council meeting before Ilos when Udina moves to detain Shepard: "You bastard! You're selling us out!"

Kaidan, on Feros when Gavin Hossle describes the danger of recovering his data: "Well, that's very comforting. Certain death for some, fine for us."

Kaidan, on approach to Saren's base on Virmire: "Sun, surf, sand... and geth. I bet they don't put that in the brochure."

Kaidan, on Ilos: "Feel like I'm walking over a grave."

Kaidan, during UNC: Lost Module on Eletania if a space monkey is killed: "Remind me to stay on your good side, Commander."

Kaidan, on the shuttle after Tuchanka: Bomb, of Ashley: "It's been a long time, but it's still..."

Kaidan, during the Kai Leng fight on Thessia when the assassin taunts Shepard over Kirrahe/Thane's death: "Come closer! I didn't catch that, you bastard."

Kaidan, seeing London during Priority: Earth: "That can't be Earth..."

Kaidan, during Citadel Archives: Escape while climbing a ladder under fire: "Never a crate to hide behind when you need one!"
