Jack's loyalty mission in Mass Effect 2, available after Horizon and written by Brian Kindregan. After Shepard gives Jack access to Cerberus files aboard the Normandy, Kelly Chambers relays that Jack wants to see Shepard in her "hidey hole" on the Engineering Deck. Jack asks Shepard to help her destroy the decommissioned Teltin facility on Pragia, in the Dakka system of the Nubian Expanse, where Cerberus experimented on her as a child. She wants to plant "a big fucking bomb" in her old cell and watch it detonate from orbit.

On arrival EDI detects thermal signatures inside the complex but none near the landing zone. Moving through the facility, Jack recognizes rooms and revisits memories: a skylit atrium where she first saw sunlight, a bloodstained arena where she was pitted against the other children and drugged as a reward for killing and shocked for hesitating, a cell block, and a two-way mirror outside her own cell where she used to bang on the glass while the other children ignored her. Security consoles reveal the staff had "gone rogue" from the Illusive Man's orders, that all subjects were expendable except Jack, that children were exposed to chemicals and freezing temperatures, and that the project planned to infiltrate the Alliance's Ascension Project; a final log shows Jack killing the distraught doctor during the riot. The team fights varren, Blood Pack vorcha and krogan mercenaries (including a Pyro and the krogan Kureck), hired by Aresh.

In Jack's cell, Shepard meets Aresh, a former test subject who recognized Jack and is trying to restart the facility so the experiments "must have been for something." Jack draws a pistol; Shepard can use Charm to spare him, Intimidate to have Jack kill him, or plain dialogue options with corresponding Paragon or Renegade interrupts. Shepard can then leave or stay while Jack walks the cell recounting memories of the window, bed, desk, and a bloodstain. On the shuttle back, Jack fumbles with the detonator, then presses it when Shepard nods, and the bomb destroys the facility.

Back on the Normandy, if Miranda's loyalty mission is complete, Jack confronts Miranda in her office, throwing a chair, furious that Miranda will not admit Cerberus was wrong. Without sufficient Paragon or Renegade standing, Shepard must alienate one crewmate, losing that person's loyalty and any romance (recoverable later). Cerberus Intel emails that the Illusive Man had ordered the project shut down before the riot, that arriving personnel found all guards and most subjects dead, and that surviving children were given amnesic treatments and delivered to the Alliance as "slaver attack" survivors. Rewards include 750 experience, 30,000 credits, 500 element zero, a Biotic Damage (Hyper-amp) upgrade, and Warp Ammo for Jack. If Aresh is spared, a Mass Effect 3 news alert reports he died saving a shuttle of children evacuating a Reaper-occupied colony; his full name is given as Aresh Aghdashloo.
