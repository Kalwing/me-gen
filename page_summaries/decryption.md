Decryption was a tech talent in the first Mass Effect, available to Engineers, Infiltrators, Sentinels, the Turian Agent, and the Quarian Machinist as a starting talent. It allowed characters to unlock sealed doors and containers and granted access to the Sabotage ability. Progressing through its twelve talent levels expanded the difficulty of objects that could be decrypted (easy at level 1, average at level 5, hard at level 9) and steadily increased tech mine and tech explosion damage from Sabotage, Damping, and EMP, rising from a 10% bonus at level 2 to 30% at level 12. Certain levels unlocked additional talents: Electronics for the Sentinel and Damping for the Turian Agent at level 4, and First Aid for the Infiltrator and Hacking for the Engineer and Quarian Machinist at level 7.

Sabotage, one of the core tech talents, worked by overheating an enemy's weapon, stopping them from firing. It was especially valuable against gun-carrying enemies such as Geth Colossi and Geth Armatures (to stop their Siege Pulse), rocket turrets, and snipers. Because it overheated equipment, it could also be used on containment cells and fuel tanks to trigger explosions and chain reactions, so care was needed when using it near explosives. It did not disable organic attacks such as those of the Rachni or Thorian Creepers, and it deployed a tech proximity mine on use. Being Sabotaged, whether the player or an enemy, prevented use of any special weapon abilities (such as Overkill or Assassination) for the effect's duration, even with a weapon that could still fire, unless the ability had been activated beforehand. Higher tech hardening reduced Sabotage duration for both the player and enemies. Enemies such as Geth Hoppers could Sabotage the player's weapon. The three tiers were Sabotage (50 damage, 6m radius, 15 sec), Advanced Sabotage (100 damage, 8m, 20 sec), and Master Sabotage (150 damage, 10m, 25 sec), all with a 60% accuracy cost.
