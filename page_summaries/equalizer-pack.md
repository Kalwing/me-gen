The Equalizer Pack was a downloadable content pack for Mass Effect 2, released on May 4, 2010, that added new gear to Shepard's armor locker: the Capacitor Helmet, the Archon Visor, and the Inferno Armor. The Capacitor Helmet stored power for kinetic barriers to recharge shields faster; the Archon Visor managed omni-tools and biotic amps to reduce power recharge time; and the powered Inferno Armor sped Shepard's movement, increased tech and biotic power damage, and included stress-analysis software that aided negotiations outside combat.

Each item arrived with an email from the Illusive Man describing the hand-off; the Inferno Armor email noted Miranda Lawson had recommended sending the suit, which had been well received by Cerberus squad leaders in the Verge. The pack cost 160 Microsoft Points on Xbox 360, 160 BioWare Points on PC, and $1.99 USD on PlayStation 3. The Inferno Armor had previously been offered as a pre-order bonus at certain retailers, using the same email.
