This page catalogs diseases known in the Milky Way and Andromeda galaxies, spanning natural, genetic, and bio-engineered conditions across many species. Element zero exposure underlies several: AEND, a terminal human neurodegenerative disorder (Ellen Ryder was a victim); cancer, a common result of prenatal eezo exposure; and grayboxes, originally developed to combat Alzheimer's.

Asari conditions included Ardat-Yakshi, a rare genetic nervous-system condition causing sterility and lethal hemorrhaging in a partner's brain during mating, which could not be detected until maturity (Morinth was affected), plus Ahok Fasciitis and the blood-borne Asari Cyanophage. Drell suffered Kepral's Syndrome, an incurable respiratory disease caused by long-term exposure to the humid climate of Kahje after evacuation from Rakhana (Thane Krios was affected). Turians faced Corpalis Syndrome (severe neurological degeneration, under study at the Helos Medical Institute), Titan's Tears, and Titan-like hemorrhagic fevers. Humans included Vrolik Syndrome or "brittle bone disease," which afflicted Joker, and migraines suffered by Kaidan Alenko from his L2 implant damage.

Bio-engineered weapons featured prominently: the genophage, a salarian-engineered krogan population-control weapon deployed by the turians to end the Krogan Rebellions; the Blood Plague, created by batarian extremists under Jath'Amon to assassinate the Citadel Council, with a 100% fatality rate; the Omega Plague, a Collector-made airborne disease lethal to many species but harmless to humans and vorcha, ultimately cured by Mordin Solus; and Ayalon B, a salarian proto-syphilis whose samples were destroyed as too volatile.

Andromeda-era entries included TH-314, a lethal three-stage human viral disease carried by Andromeda Initiative member Ruth Bekker aboard Ark Hyperion, which threatened a Heleus Cluster pandemic until dealt with by Pathfinder Ryder; the Fortinbras Plague, an artificial chimera virus aboard ark Keelah Si'yah based on the volus disease Yoqtan, stopped by a cure spread through the ship captain's sacrifice; Neural Decay, caused by eating from the ecosystem of 2175 Aeia or prolonged cryostasis, with a holding serum developed by Dr. Lexi T'Perro; and Angaran Photoreceptivity Degeneration, terminal to angara in the Mithrava commune on Havarl. Other listed conditions included various species-jumping fevers, elcor pandemics, raloi H7N7 Flu, varren-borne Scale-Itch, and common human ailments such as chickenpox, shingles, Ebola, and Marburg virus.
