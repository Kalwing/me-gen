The klixen were an insectoid species resembling crabs or beetles, encountered as enemies in Mass Effect 2. They bore a resemblance to the rachni but were clearly a distinct species, and were likely not sapient, as they used no weapons. Klixen were usually, if not always, found alongside larger flying creatures called harvesters, and appeared on the krogan homeworld Tuchanka and at a few exploration sites.

Klixen moved slowly but were dangerous at melee range: they had a fire-breathing attack that could stagger a target, and their corpses exploded violently on death, causing splash damage. A klixen killed while frozen would thaw before exploding. Like husks, they tried to overwhelm prey with numbers. They lacked armor on lower difficulties but had high health and heavy resistance to fire from all sources, did not panic from burning effects, and took no extra damage from headshots. Recommended tactics included stripping their armor on high difficulties, disabling them from range with concussive, cryogenic, or biotic attacks (Singularity, Stasis, Reave, Flashbang Grenade), staying mobile, and avoiding melee. Warp, Incinerate, Shredder Ammo, Warp Ammo, and Armor Piercing Ammo killed them quickly; the M-622 Avalanche served as emergency heavy support. The Arc Projector was ineffective, as klixen were immune to its stopping and stunning effects. Their fire breath left a purely visual burning aftereffect with no damage over time.
