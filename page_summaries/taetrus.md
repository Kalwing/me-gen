Taetrus was a turian colony in the Mactare system. Its capital city was Vallum, and other major cities included Iratiana, Spaedar and Madra, located in the marsh-like Diluvian Wildlands on the island continent of Eluria. Decades before the events of Mass Effect 3, a civil war on Taetrus fortified many cities and strategic sites, such as the Spaedar Spaceport and Kasatum Fortress, to military specifications.

In 2185, a turian separatist group called Facinus reprogrammed the FTL plotter of a starship and rammed it at near-FTL speed into the heart of Vallum, destroying the city center and killing tens of thousands of people in an attack known as the Vallum Blast. In response, the Turian Hierarchy and Taetrian colonial forces invaded Facinus strongholds and systematically eliminated the separatists in a short, decisive conflict called the War on Taetrus. Facinus had drawn support from Wildlands civilians sympathetic to its cause, and benefited from the earlier civil-war fortifications. After months of fighting, civilians returned home from safe camps while Hierarchy forces worked to reconstruct the colony and reunite families. Captain Guard Vidinos led a special operatives team that distinguished itself in the war by tracking down separatist leaders.

During the Reaper invasion in 2186, Taetrus was one of the first worlds attacked after the fall of Khar'shan and Earth. The Turian Hierarchy made two failed attempts to enter the Mactare system by mass relay to liberate it, and as the Reapers poured into the Trebia system to assault Palaven they broadcast images of their victory at Taetrus across the turian comm buoy network.
