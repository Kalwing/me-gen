Helena Blake was a senior human member of a crime syndicate, listed by C-Sec as a person of interest. In Mass Effect, the well-spoken Blake approached Commander Shepard near the Emporium in the Citadel Presidium in 2183 with a business proposition. She openly admitted her criminal affiliation but claimed her two partners had gone too far, dealing in the biotic drug red sand and entering the slave trade, and offered to pay Shepard to eliminate them. Shepard could agree, refuse (Blake supplied the coordinates regardless), or threaten to arrest her, to which she calmly cited her excellent legal representation.

If Shepard killed the two crime bosses, the Commander could travel to Blake's hideout on Amaranthine in the Fortuna system for a reward. Blake thanked Shepard and promised the syndicate would thereafter limit itself to gambling and smuggling illegal technology. Shepard could accept this and leave, or attempt another arrest. Confronted, Blake declared she would rather die than go to prison, ordered her goons to "neutralize the Spectre," and joined the fight herself, dying in the firefight. Alternatively, Shepard could persuade her to walk away, and she agreed to disband her organization permanently, conceding her operation was ruined beyond repair.

In Mass Effect 2, if Blake survived 2183, Shepard could encounter her on Omega in the passageways of the Afterlife Club. If she had been convinced to retire, she worked there as a social worker, using her underworld knowledge to help Omega's people. If Shepard had simply let her go, she had instead folded her criminal empire into Aria T'Loak's organization and become an underboss.
