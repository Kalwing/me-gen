A hand-corrected YouTube-transcript lore video collecting five easily missed details from the Mass Effect trilogy.

First, CCCP Luna 23, sitting west of the Mass Effect 1 drop zone on Luna, is a real unmanned Soviet lunar lander from the mid-1970s that was meant to return a rock sample to Earth; a hard landing toppled it and disabled its sample apparatus, so it was abandoned. Mass Effect 1 is set in 2183, 209 years after the 1974 mission, and the lander can be mined for resources.

Second, at specific points on several planets and bodies, including beside the downed Luna 23 probe, an animal-like "song" can be heard through the silence of space; these sounds are associated with the Rachni, who communicate via pheromones and a form of organic quantum entanglement that transmits across the galaxy instantly. Whether the sounds are meant to be Rachni is never confirmed.

Third, the origin of EDI: in Mass Effect 1 Admiral Hackett reports a rogue VI that has seized an Alliance training ground on Luna; Shepard shuts it down from three bunkers while it sends drones in defense, and on deactivation a burst of binary repeating across all frequencies resolves to "Help." The VI had become self-aware. Mass Effect 2 alludes to this happening during an experiment to create a controllable AI, and Mass Effect 3 confirms the Luna VI was rebuilt with technology salvaged from Sovereign to create EDI, who confirms it: "You were that rogue VI on Luna?" — "Yes... Gaining awareness while under attack was confusing. I am pleased that my relationship with organics has become more cooperative."

Fourth, in the Lair of the Shadow Broker DLC, after Liara T'Soni replaces the Broker, a terminal plays a surveillance archive showing the reach of the Broker's network: Aria T'Loak ordering an execution, reporter Emily Wong, Donnel Udina committing violence, Captain Bailey, Anderson meeting a Cerberus agent (never followed up), a punchable reporter being punched by a krogan, a salarian executed by varren on Tuchanka, Elias Kelham running someone over, Jacob Taylor doing sit-ups, and Jack using biotics to escape Purgatory.

Fifth, the asari matriarch tending bar on Illium in Mass Effect 2 is Aethyta, seen in the Broker archive studying a photo of Liara. In Mass Effect 3, at Apollo's Café, she reveals she is Liara's father (Liara being a pure-blooded asari). By then each knows the other's identity, and Shepard can convince the two to finally meet.
