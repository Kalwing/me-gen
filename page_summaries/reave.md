Reave was a biotic power appearing in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, in both single-player and multiplayer. It used mass effect fields to attack a target's nervous or synthetic systems and prevent healing, and it traveled in a straight line to hit instantly. Reave could strike enemies behind deployable physical shields such as Collector Guardians or Cerberus Guardians, bypassing them, though not ordinary cover.

In Mass Effect 2, Reave disabled a target's health regeneration, dealt double damage to armor and barriers, and, when used against organics, restored the biotic's health and granted a temporary maximum-health bonus lasting 30 seconds. Against unprotected health it permanently disabled the self-regeneration of enemies like vorcha and krogan, and it briefly stunned most humanoid organics. Its damage scaled with duration, so it did less on Insanity. It evolved at rank 4 into Heavy Reave (stronger single-target) or Area Reave (a 3-meter radius affecting multiple targets). It was available to Shepard via Advanced Training and to Samara as a loyalty power.

In Mass Effect 3, Reave no longer restored health or stunned enemies; instead it drained the target's health over time and granted the user a temporary damage-reduction bonus (visible as a violet sheen) that lasted the full duration even if the target died, applying only when used against organic targets. It dealt 1.5x damage to armor, 2x to barriers, and half to shields, and completely ignored the power immunities of Banshees, Phantoms, Praetorians, and Rampart Mechs. It was one of three biotic powers (with Warp and Annihilation Field) able to both prime and detonate biotic explosions on any enemy, and it instantly killed all Swarmers in its radius. It could not destroy Cerberus shield generators or pylons. In single-player it was available to Kaidan Alenko, Shepard (as a bonus power), Aria T'Loak, Samara, and Miranda Lawson; in multiplayer to the Asari Justicar Adept and Drell Adept.

"Reave" is an archaic verb meaning to rob or plunder, or to tear apart. If Kaidan attends the party at Shepard's apartment in Mass Effect 3, he may boast of having learned to Reave after extensive training, drawing wary reactions from Liara and others.
