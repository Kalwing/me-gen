This Shadow Broker dossier, accessible after the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, profiled Cerberus, described as a rogue human-supremacist agency founded in 2157 and active in both Citadel and Terminus space, classified as a terrorist threat by the Systems Alliance and the Citadel Council. The Broker noted that credits lost to the Illusive Man's interference in recovering Commander Shepard's body were offset by data from the Lazarus Project, and advised withholding retaliation until Shepard's long-term functionality was confirmed one year after resurrection.

An "Activity Highlights" timeline listed decades of covert operations: the 2157 manifesto; construction of the Pragia facility in 2160; the birth of Subject Zero in 2161 alongside the purchase of shell companies; infiltration of human extrasolar media (Constant Times, Scott Examiner, the Galactic Broadcasting Corporation) from 2162; Project DOORWAY forging Council medicards and identitags in 2163; the discrediting of prime-minister front-runner Vera Safin in 2164; a failed 2165 antimatter theft from the SSV Geneva whose sole survivor named his sponsor "Cerberus"; arms manufacturing through Haribon Military Industries; a 2168 massacre of 22 salarian syndicate members on Trident to seize the narcotics trade; and Project TRAPDOOR (2169), which tested omega-enkaphalin on asari captives to suppress biotic powers. In 2170 nanosurgery made Subject Zero resistant to omega-enkaphalin, after which the drug was used against Matriarch Tilia Eraza in 2181. Other entries covered the assassination of Pope Clement XVI (replaced by the militant Pope Leo XIV), manipulation of the Terra Firma party, the 2177 acquisition of Light Shadow Pictures' RealityPlus video-forgery technology, military outposts established on Binthu in 2181, and the 2182 sabotage of the MSV Anixara during FTL, which killed turian Hierarchy hawk Raherix Ursivus. The timeline ended with Admiral Kahoku beginning his investigation of Cerberus.

A satirical "Illusive Man Profile" logged his day: four cigarettes, seven drinks, a fifth consecutive perfect skyball game (160/0), a plum Giuli Vorn linen suit with built-in weapons protection, sexual liaisons with celebrities and Matriarch Trellani, and messages sent to the Citadel, Illium, Omega, Earth, Horizon, Eden Prime, Thessia, Palaven, Tuchanka and the Migrant Fleet.
