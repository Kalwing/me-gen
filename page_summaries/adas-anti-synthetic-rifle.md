The Adas Anti-Synthetic Rifle was an assault rifle in Mass Effect 3, named in memory of the quarians killed in the Morning War on the planet Adas. Its electrical attack was optimized for medium- to long-range firefights. Alliance marines objected to calling it a "rifle" because it technically had no rifling in its barrel, which the quarians dismissed as a translation issue.

In single-player the weapon became available with the Mass Effect 3: Firefight Pack, and in Legendary Edition it could be purchased from the Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies terminal in the Presidium Commons for a base 10,000 credits. In multiplayer it was a rare weapon requiring the Mass Effect 3: Reckoning DLC and was awarded randomly through item packs.

Despite its name, the Adas dealt equal damage to organic and synthetic targets, though it had a 2x modifier against shields and barriers. It fired area-damage rounds that stunned and knocked back most enemies, including whole squads and large foes such as Brutes, making it strong for crowd control. The rounds had slight target-tracking that helped aiming over longer distances but could not lock onto cloaked or smoke-obscured targets, and the weapon tended to miss small moving targets at close range. Its explosive munitions meant penetration mods and weak-point damage were useless, but it suffered no penalty against armor. It was one of the heaviest assault rifles and consumed thermal clips quickly in single-player due to its high fire rate. It was devastating against shielded enemies and the geth, could knock out Ravager Swarmers as they spawned, and its area effect made it excellent for applying ammo effects such as Incendiary or Cryo Ammo to prime power combos. Activating Tactical Cloak while firing did not break the cloak. It was noted as extremely effective against Kai Leng and the final bosses of the Mass Effect 3: Citadel mini-campaign.
