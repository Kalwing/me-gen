Peak 15 was one of seventeen remote research facilities on the planet Noveria, a large station set in the Skadi Mountains and reached through the winding Aleutsk Valley. Port Hanshan normally ran shuttles there, though a Mako or M29 Grizzly could be used in bad weather. Its labs were rented by Binary Helix, and the turian Lilihierax noted the site had a bad reputation. The facility was governed by a safety system called Code Omega, triggered by a critical containment failure: power was cut so the cold would kill escaped experiments, and the VI Mira, which ran the station, was purged from the system. The Port Hanshan executive board would then vote whether to destroy the facility from orbit with an antimatter warhead from one of Noveria's battle stations.

Peak 15 was overrun by rachni after Binary Helix efforts to breed and weaponize them. Commander Shepard entered the facility and resolved the crisis, one way or another. Afterward, survivors of the incident were reassigned to postings offworld, and some people at Port Hanshan believed nothing had really happened there, treating it as a distraction from the "real" projects.

The station was divided into sections. The main garage handled cargo ground vehicles and led through a security checkpoint whose turrets faced inward. Central Station held Mira's core, roof access, and the helium-3 reactor core, plus a tram station with a plasma-jet decontamination chamber linking to Rift Station. Rift Station was the main scientific area, with staff barracks, canteen, maintenance, small research labs, and a medical bay. The Hot Labs, built into a stable glacier and reached by elevator or tram, could have their exterior heated to sink the whole facility into the ice in the event of a breach, and were equipped with a neutron purge system that flooded the area with lethal radiation.

The name is a play on "Area 51," and was also the temporary designation for Mt. Everest before its height was measured. Cut content indicates the layout was once more complex, that evacuation routes locked down until the neutron purge was activated, and that the Executive Board did in fact nuke Peak 15 in the aftermath.
