Jormangund Technology was a manufacturer described in cut content from Mass Effect and is not considered canon. It was presented as a rising star of post-contact human industry that returned consistent profits through aggressive integration of alien technologies. It was mainly known for advanced optical computers and AI "blueboxes," and had recently developed a "micro gravitic" personal weapon that effectively fired small disruptor torpedoes.

In-game, Jormangund was a hidden manufacturer: its equipment was fully functional in the game files but only accessible via the PC console or third-party software. Its armor lines included Light, Medium, and Heavy Crisis and Hazard Armor for various species. If any squad member wore Crisis or Hazard Armor, it negated the timer in environments with Level 1 or 2 environmental hazards, an effect stronger than that of Devlon Industries armor. Jormangund armors had the highest shields of all armors in the game, ranked third in damage protection after Cerberus Skunkworks and Colossus Armor, and third in tech/biotic protection after Predator L/M/H and Cerberus Skunkworks armors. Its weapon lines, all acquirable only through console commands, were the Torrent assault rifles, Savage shotguns, Pinnacle pistols, and Helix sniper rifles.

The name refers to Jormungandr, the giant sea serpent and child of Loki in Norse mythology, and the company's logo was a sea serpent. Jormangund Technology was the subject or starting point of an investigation in a cancelled Mass Effect DLC known only as "DLC_EvilCorp," whose only remaining trace is the achievement "Lockdown Survivor."
