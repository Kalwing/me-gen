Lair of the Shadow Broker was a DLC pack for Mass Effect 2, announced on July 22, 2010 and released September 7 for Xbox 360 and PC, with technical issues preventing its sale in parts of Central and Eastern Europe. It concerned Liara T'Soni and her history with the Shadow Broker, and associate producer Michael Gamble described it as the "biggest DLC yet, period." It was the first piece of DLC to begin expanding the story in preparation for Mass Effect 3, and though playable during or after the suicide mission, its dialogue assumed the player had already completed that mission.

In the story Liara tracked down the mysterious Shadow Broker to recover a man he had kidnapped, chasing clues from Illium to the Broker's secret lair while his agents tried to stop her. The pack added one mission (Lair of the Shadow Broker), one location (the Shadow Broker Base in the Sowilo system of the Hourglass Nebula), the bonus power Stasis, six weapon and defense upgrades, the Shadow Broker intel center with new research, five achievements, two cabin items (Shepard's old dog tags and a Shadow Broker Base ship model), and the chance to continue a relationship with Liara.

It cost $9.99 USD on Xbox 360 or 800 BioWare Points on PC, was included on the disc of the PlayStation 3 version of Mass Effect 2, and was bundled into Mass Effect Legendary Edition. Pre-development notes mention cut plans including a player upgrade slowing time when injured, two tech upgrades for a "popular henchman," biotic and tech cooldown upgrades, and an intended runtime of about 2.5 hours.
