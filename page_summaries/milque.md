Milque was a human sniper and member of Delta Squad, an Alliance special-forces unit deployed to the colony of Fehl Prime, as depicted in the animated film Mass Effect: Paragon Lost. A cautious, cynical man with a dry sense of humor, he was often taken for a coward but obeyed orders and reliably covered his teammates, usually in exchange for free drinks. Armed with an M-92 Mantis, he was the squad's marksman.

Delta Squad first arrived on Fehl Prime around 2183 to repel a Blood Pack mercenary attack. Milque's unit was shot down during a strafing run from a Kodiak shuttle; Lieutenant James Vega took command after Captain Toni was incapacitated. Milque surrendered his power cells to Vega, covered his charge against the krogan Battlemaster Archuk, was grazed across the collarbone by the mercenary Brood, and recovered in time to kill Archuk and save Vega. The Blood Pack were defeated and Milque was commended.

Two years later, with Fehl Prime rebuilt and fortified with an anti-ship cannon, Delta Squad investigated a jamming signal and found an alien artifact, which Vega ordered destroyed. The colony was then attacked by the Collectors, who harvested colonists aboard a ship. The squad's counterattacks failed; the defense cannon could not penetrate the ship's barriers, and a Praetorian containing Kamille's torso killed Mason. The survivors escaped in the imprisoned Brood's ship in exchange for freeing him. Vega spiked the group's drink flask with a cure for the seeker plague. Brood was shot dead by the Cerberus informant Messner while attacking the Collector ship, and the group was captured. Milque, Essex and Vega synthesized more antidote through the Collectors' systems and revived the colonists. Milque, who had washed out of pilot training, was made to fly a captured freighter, using it to rescue Vega at the ship's core. Forced to choose, Vega saved Treeya and her intel rather than the colonists, and the Collector ship crashed with everyone aboard killed; Milque judged it the wrong choice. Vega was later commended, promoted and invited into the N7 program. Milque joined Vega and Treeya at the Fehl Prime Memorial Monument to honor the dead.
