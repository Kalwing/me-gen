A short Mass Effect 3 side mission acquired after Priority: Palaven. In the Presidium Commons, near the Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies kiosk by the bank, a volus known as the Angry Accountant (or "Angry Volus") laments that his people lost their way by clinging to capitalist habits during the war. He believes recovering the Book of Plenix from the volus homeworld, Irune, would help the volus remember their values and inspire them. If Shepard never overheard him, the book and its journal entry (alternatively labeled "Citadel: Book of Plenix") can instead be picked up during a Search and Rescue operation.

Shepard acquires the book by scanning Irune in the Aru system of the Aethon Cluster, which only appears on the Galaxy Map after Priority: Tuchanka. Once the Citadel is secure, Shepard returns to the Presidium Commons and gives the book to the volus. Completing the mission grants 40 points of military strength toward the Citadel Defense Force War Assets, 5 Reputation points, and 15,000 credits. Retrieving the book early can be delayed by intervening events at the Citadel.
