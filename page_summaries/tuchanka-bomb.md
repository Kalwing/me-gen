"Tuchanka: Bomb" was a timed Mass Effect 3 mission acquired automatically after Commander Shepard met the stranded turian 9th Platoon in "Tuchanka: Turian Platoon." It became permanently failed if Shepard completed any three other missions before undertaking it. The turian ship that crashed on Tuchanka had been on a secret mission to prevent Cerberus from detonating a massive bomb in the Kelphic Valley, a weapon planted long before that would kill a large portion of the krogan population.

Landing under heavy artillery bombardment, Shepard's squad fought through Cerberus Assault Troopers, Centurions, Combat Engineers, Guardians, an Atlas mech and turrets to reach the bomb site alongside Lieutenant Tarquin Victus, son of Primarch Adrien Victus. In the final stage Shepard had to defend Tarquin Victus from waves of shuttle-borne Cerberus infantry while he disabled the device; his health could not be allowed to reach zero. A closing cutscene showed Tarquin Victus sacrificing himself to stop the bomb from detonating, ending the mission.

Successful completion granted a +75 turian War Asset and preserved the fragile krogan-turian alliance. Failure caused a 300-point reduction of the krogan War Asset, killing a significant portion of the krogan population and Eve (if she had survived Priority: Tuchanka). Admiral Hackett debriefed Shepard afterward, ordering that peace be kept between the turian Primarch and krogan leader. If the mission was done before Priority: Tuchanka, the Urdnot leader asked Garrus over the intercom whether he had known about the bomb. Depending on outcome and the krogan leader's identity, Shepard received follow-up emails: furious threats of slaughter from Urdnot Wreav, despondent messages from Urdnot Wrex about the many krogan dead, or, on success, a grateful letter from Primarch Victus thanking Shepard for saving the krogan homeworld and the alliance, and for letting his son complete his mission and die with the respect of his men.
