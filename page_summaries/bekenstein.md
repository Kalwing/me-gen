Bekenstein was the second planet of the Boltzmann System in the Serpent Nebula. It received the first wave of Earth's colonization efforts, with founders who planned a less agrarian colony than Eden Prime, intending it to become Earth's off-planet manufacturing base and to integrate humanity into galactic culture by producing needed goods. After mixed early success, Bekenstein leapfrogged its competition by producing high-quality luxury goods sold directly to nearby markets via the Citadel, becoming known as "the humans' Illium," a place where new-money tycoons flaunted wealth.

The codex entry described it as more glittering than diamonds and more expensive than surgery. The Systems Alliance chose Bekenstein to be its trading arm after the First Contact War. Early human products sold on novelty alone before demand collapsed and hurt the economy; only the second generation of colonists found a sustainable niche in high-quality entertainment and luxury goods, after which aliens flocked to its spaceports. The planet boasted more millionaires and billionaires per capita than any other human colony. Its crime was mostly white-collar and nonviolent, but its suicide rate and inflation were extremely high, unemployment was artificially low, and the cost of living drove out the jobless within months.

The mission Kasumi: Stealing Memory took place on Bekenstein and required the Dossier: The Master Thief from Mass Effect 2. Its mineral deposits scanned as depleted. Late in the war with the Reapers, Diana Allers mentioned that Bekenstein was attacked and destroyed by Reaper forces because of its industrial infrastructure, even though its factories only produced commercial items such as binoculars; rather than landing ground forces to harvest its population, the Reapers bombarded it from orbit. The planet was possibly named after theoretical physicist Jacob Bekenstein.
