Maelon Heplorn was a salarian geneticist, former Special Tasks Group operative, and former student of Mordin Solus. Alongside Mordin he took part in modifying the genophage, the krogan sterility bioweapon whose effects had been wearing off. In Mass Effect: Foundation, Maelon accompanied Mordin and an STG team to Tuchanka to test the modified strain, tasked with hauling the heavy dispersal unit to the deployment zone. Despite Mordin belittling him, he proved stronger and more capable than he looked, killing a hammer-wielding krogan and activating the device under fire. After an explosive diversion buried the team and killed a krogan female who had confronted Mordin, Maelon bandaged Mordin's wounds and questioned whether their work was right; Mordin insisted the risk of freeing the krogan was too high, while Maelon countered that risk was necessary in science.

In 2185 (Mass Effect 2), Maelon was reported captured by Clan Weyrloc krogan seeking to reverse the genophage. Mordin asked Commander Shepard to help retrieve him, but Maelon was found working willingly for the krogan, wracked with guilt over what he saw as cultural genocide. He argued the genophage had not made the galaxy less chaotic and that a larger krogan population could have helped stop Saren at Eden Prime; Mordin dismissed this as speculation. Mordin and Shepard shut down his experiments, and an enraged Mordin tried to shoot him. Shepard could intervene or let Maelon die; if spared, Maelon was advised to open a clinic on Omega. Shepard also chose whether to keep or destroy Maelon's research data.

In Mass Effect 3, it was revealed that some female krogan survived Maelon's experiments and were held by STG on Sur'Kesh; if Mordin survived the Suicide Mission he leaked this to Clan Urdnot, forcing their release, and Maelon's preserved research proved essential to finalizing a cure. Eve noted Maelon's methods had grown barbaric despite good intentions. If spared, Maelon opened his Omega clinic but went into hiding after Clan Weyrloc placed a bounty on him, and sent Shepard a grateful message if the genophage was cured. If Mordin died in the Suicide Mission with his loyalty mission incomplete, Maelon completed the cure himself, but gaps in his research left problems that Padok Wiks could not fully account for.
