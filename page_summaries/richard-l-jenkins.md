Corporal Richard L. Jenkins was an Alliance Marine serving under Captain Anderson aboard the SSV Normandy. He was born in a rural district of Eden Prime and had dreamed of visiting more urban areas before enlisting, saying "even paradise gets boring." He wanted to prove himself as a notable soldier and test himself in combat, envied Commander Shepard's record, and admired the Spectres, being especially impressed by Nihlus Kryik after hearing that Nihlus once defeated an entire platoon single-handed, though his views were shaped more by vids than experience.

In Mass Effect, Jenkins was assigned to Shepard's ground team for the mission to Eden Prime and was killed by geth drones shortly after landing. His death was a blow to the morale of the Normandy's crew, who remembered him for his bold exuberance. Two years later, in Mass Effect 2, Doctor Chakwas recalled him during a drinking session with Shepard, telling how Jenkins was thrown back by a biotic display from Kaidan and, after Chakwas feared he had broken his back, jumped up shouting "That was awesome!" Chakwas cited Jenkins as one of the reasons she considered the Alliance great compared to Cerberus.

In 2186, the Normandy SR-2 received a memorial wall bearing the names of fallen crew from both the SR-2 and the SR-1, and Jenkins' name was included. If Shepard revisited Eden Prime, the Commander and some squadmates might briefly recall him and how he died on his birth world. Cut content from Mass Effect included a mess-hall scene, closely mirroring Chakwas' later story, in which Jenkins insists on Kaidan demonstrating his biotics and is tossed a cup before leaving disheartened for the cargo bay.
