This page collects dialogue cut from Mass Effect 2's mission Dossier: The Professor, recovered from the game's dialogue files where the audio remains present but the lines were made non-functional. Almost all of it consists of squadmate reactions for characters who cannot yet be recruited at that point in the released game, plus unused voicelines for human refugees at Mordin's clinic. The lines are grouped by scene: entering the quarantine zone, the burning bodies, the dying batarian, the squad falling sick, a clawed-at corpse, the looters, the merc battle, meeting Mordin, Daniel's death, and activating the air-processing fans.

Representative lines:

Garrus, preparing to enter the plague quarantine: "A quarantine zone for a plague that kills turians. Why don't we ever go anywhere nice?"

Tali, on being asked to come into the plague zone: "My suit filters out every known germ, toxin, parasite, and allergen. I shouldn't have trouble unless it's breached."

Garrus, seeing the burning corpses: "Over the years I've grown used to the smell of burning bodies. That's probably a bad sign."

Grunt, at the burning bodies: "Anyone else hungry? No?"

Garrus, over the dying batarian: "Poor bastard. Never thought I'd say that about a batarian."

Grunt, as the squad falls ill: "I don't get sick!"

Kasumi, on Daniel the dead assistant: "And the moral of the story: help nobody."

Mordin, meeting a squad of aliens he has just cured: "Turian physiology resilient with simple immunobooster. Should be fine now... Drell. Very rare. Cure untested. Will not hurt, at least."

Refugee at Mordin's clinic: "All the aliens are blaming us for this plague, but we're victims just like everyone else."
