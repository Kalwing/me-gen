The Citadel Tower stood at the centre of the Citadel, rising parallel to the Wards, and was the station's most distinctive monument and the seat of the Citadel Council, from which all Council decisions were made. It was reached by an elevator on the Presidium near the Relay Monument or from any Citadel Rapid Transit point. The Tower housed the Council Chambers, filled with trees and fountains, with anteroom staircases leading up to the Council's audience chamber; in Mass Effect: Revelation this area was called the Petitioner's Stage, beneath which lay a garden protected by glass. Politicians and petitioners such as Rear Admiral Kahoku gathered there, and Commander Shepard could overhear a salarian and a turian discussing rumours of Saren Arterius' involvement in Eden Prime and the volus bid for a Council seat, which the salarian rated as next to none. According to the VI Avina, the keepers frequently passed through the Tower and Council Chambers on their way elsewhere. The Tower was also used to manually open the Citadel's relay leading to dark space; when Sovereign attacked the station, it settled on the Tower and extruded cables into it. The Council Chambers were the site of the final confrontation between Shepard and Saren: pursuing him to the station's master control unit in the audience chamber, Shepard saw Saren die and fall through the glass into the garden below, after which his transformation into Sovereign's avatar collapsed the stage and the squad destroyed the creature in the garden. When Sovereign was destroyed by the Alliance and the remaining Citadel Fleet, a large piece of debris crashed into the Tower, nearly burying and crushing Shepard's team before a rescue party led by Captain Anderson found them.
