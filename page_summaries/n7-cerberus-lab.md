An optional N7 assignment in Mass Effect 3, given by Specialist Traynor after Priority: The Citadel I and Priority: Palaven, prompted by an email from Admiral Hackett requesting Shepard for a series of special ops. The Alliance had located a new Cerberus laboratory on the planet Sanctum, and Shepard landed to investigate it and deal with any threat. The mission uses the multiplayer map Firebase Glacier, a claustrophobic indoor environment.

Shepard's squad fought basic Cerberus troops, Assault Troopers, Centurions, Combat Engineers, Guardians and Turrets, to reach and retrieve two contained artifacts, deactivating each artifact's containment and carrying it back to the shuttle. On the map were a failed medi-gel experiment and journal entries. During the second artifact's extraction, Cerberus drove Cortez away from the landing pad, forcing Shepard to hold out against an unlimited stream of Cerberus troops until Cortez could return. Once the shuttle arrived, reaching it ended the mission in victory.

Two logs by "C. Talavi" bracketed Cerberus indoctrination-style conditioning: before "integration," the recruit wrote "the troops here are really intense. No one smiles. Ever. And suicide on capture orders? Seriously? ... I start being 'integrated' tomorrow. Can't wait." Afterward: "Integration successful. Suicide on capture orders confirmed. Cerberus is my friend. Obey. Protect humanity. At any cost." A Private Talavi later appears on the Citadel seeking a transfer to fight Reapers after her brother joined Cerberus. Completion gave the Cerberus Research Data war asset, the Alien Medi-Gel Formula, 20,000 credits, a Sniper Rifle Enhanced Scope and +5 Reputation.
