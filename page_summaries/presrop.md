Presrop was the moon of Klendagon, in the Century System of the Hawking Eta cluster in the Milky Way. It was a frigid, barren world with an extremely thin atmosphere of carbon dioxide and ethane, and its crust held plentiful deposits of heavy metals. The Alliance had opened bidding on the moon's mineral rights, but exploitation was complicated by the system's proximity to the "Five Kiloparsec Ring" around the galactic core, an area of intense star formation too dangerous to travel safely.

Presrop's surface was a nightmare of jagged, overlapping ridges and geological shock zones left by some ancient disaster. This had not deterred a generation of illegal "wildcat" miners from trying to exploit its mineral wealth, and many had died in the attempt; a wrecked mining rover with dead occupants could be found on its surface, along with an abandoned wildcat camp and a crashed probe.

In the original Mass Effect the moon was the setting of several assignments. A biotic commune led by the unstable Major Kyle had built a compound there (UNC: Major Kyle). Other points of interest included ancient freighter debris marked with the Macedyn Outpost insignia (UNC: Turian Insignias) and surveyable deposits of gold and uranium (UNC: Valuable Minerals). A thresher maw and a band of mercenaries, including Mercenary Adepts and Anti-Tank troops, also roamed the moon. Its terrain required extensive climbing through sheer cliffs to reach objectives, though the cliff tops were generally stable enough for near-smooth Mako driving.
