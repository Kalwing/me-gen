"Omega: Assist Harrot" was a side mission in the Mass Effect 3: Omega DLC. On entering Aria's Bunker after gaining the Talons' aid, the elcor merchant Harrot appeared next to a Harrot's Emporium terminal and asked Commander Shepard to recover Aria's old couch, which General Oleg Petrovsky had thrown out of Afterlife after the Cerberus conquest, in order to gain Aria's favor. If Shepard refused the mission, Shepard could not later alert Harrot upon finding the couch.

During "The Assault on Afterlife" segment of "Omega: Aria T'Loak," after defeating the Cerberus forces in the water treatment facility and raising the dam to cross the drained channel, Shepard could climb down an access ladder on the catwalk into the channel and find the couch's remains in a pile beneath the destroyed bridge. Interacting with it prompted Aria to ask, "Is that my couch?", completing the mission.
