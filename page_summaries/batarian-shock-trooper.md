This page contained under ~150 words of real prose. Batarian Shock Troopers were batarian troopers with Vanguard-like abilities, armed with pistols and shotguns and able to use biotic powers. During the mission to stop batarians from crashing an asteroid into the human colony of Terra Nova, they were deployed at the fusion-torch stations and the main facility in smaller numbers than other troopers. In combat they closed to shotgun range, used cover, and favored Throw and Warp to knock back and weaken enemies before finishing them at close quarters, moving cover to cover after each biotic attack.
