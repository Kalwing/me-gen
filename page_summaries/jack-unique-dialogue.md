A catalogue of dialogue unique to Jack when she is in Shepard's squad in Mass Effect 2, organized into location-triggered lines, "talk to squad mate" conversations, and spontaneous combat and ambient barks. Notable in the collection: on the Citadel, Jack tells a story in the bar bathroom of being sexually assaulted in the Purgatory prison showers and then hunting down and murdering every one of her attackers. Representative verbatim lines:

- Jack, on the Collector Ship when the trap springs: "I knew Cerberus would screw me again!"
- Jack, meeting Legion at the end of the Reaper IFF mission, on whether to take the geth: she favors taking it "so it can be carved into a lamp."
- Jack, on Legion's loyalty mission, objecting to rewriting the heretics: "If you screw with my head, made me nod and smile at everything... I'd rather you blew my head off. Let me die as me."
- Jack, dying if chosen to lead the second diversion squad in the Suicide Mission: "Too many... tore me up. How'd you talk me into this? Heh... I wasn't supposed to care." (Romanced male Shepard: "Too many of them... I knew I'd get hit on this job. I was too happy... too happy with you.")
- Jack, when Miranda is suggested to lead the second squad: "No one wants to take orders from you."
- Jack, on Horizon after the first Collector encounter: "Peaceful. Usually takes a lot of chemicals for me to get this kind of quiet."
- Jack, landing on Korlus and hearing Jedore's PA orders: "I already want to kill this person." Later: "Can we shut that bitch up, already?"
- Jack, starting an encounter: "Hello, dead people!"
- Jack, if knocked unconscious: "If I die Shepard, I'm haunting you!"
- Jack, using Pull: "Fly, bitch!" / "I'll throw you like a toy!" Using Shockwave: "I will destroy you!" / "I will kill you all!"

Other tagged lines include her approving of Shepard throwing a merc through a window during Thane's recruitment ("I could take notes from you, Shepard"), betting 15 credits that Niftu Cal will die, and calling the Shadow Broker's remark that her corpse is valuable during Lair of the Shadow Broker.
