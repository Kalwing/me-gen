Clan Urdnot was a krogan tribe on Tuchanka. Urdnot Wrex, a mercenary from the clan, crossed paths with Commander Shepard during the hunt for Saren Arterius and could join the squad. By 2185 Shepard visited a clan base camp while helping the tank-bred krogan Grunt. If Wrex led the clan, Urdnot was a reformist group holding neutral ground open to all krogan, requiring each clan to punish its own criminals, while clans expelled from the neutral ground were slaughtered by Wrex's allies; Wrex had also stopped weapon manufacture and ordered his chief researcher Fortack to study crop genetics and medicine. If Wreav led, it was a powerful but traditional clan. An ambassador from Clan Nakmor was present in the camp for alliance negotiations. Completing Mordin Solus's loyalty mission strengthened the clan; under Wrex the survivors of Clan Weyrloc were absorbed, while under Wreav their weapon stocks were plundered. By 2186 Clan Urdnot had become the uncontested representative of krogan power to the galaxy, having absorbed or destroyed Clans Nakmor, Jurdon, Quash, and the ferocious Clan Raik. During the Reaper invasion the clan leader met Dalatrass Linron and Primarch Adrien Victus at a war summit aboard the Normandy SR-2, where turian aid depended on krogan help, krogan help on a genophage cure, and the salarians refused the cure; Shepard secured access to a STG base on Sur'Kesh holding a fertile female krogan called Eve. After Eve was rescued during a Cerberus raid, Mordin Solus or Padok Wiks synthesised a cure from her genetic data, drawn from Maelon Heplorn's earlier experiments. During the mission to deploy the cure through the Shroud on Tuchanka, the krogan leaders met at the Hollows, where Eve urged them to unite against the Reapers. Depending on Shepard's choices a real or fake cure was dispersed, and the clan leader pledged to aid the turians on Palaven. If Wrex led, he intended to rebuild krogan civilisation and restrain clans seeking revenge; if Wreav led, he pursued an aggressive path, having commissioned more weapons of mass destruction than any krogan in known history. Known members included Wrex, Wreav, Eve, Fortack, Grunt, Ratch, and the Shaman.
