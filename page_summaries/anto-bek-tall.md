Anto Bek'tall was a batarian bodyguard who served Aria T'Loak on Omega. He was one of her most trusted henchmen and acted as her second-in-command.

In Mass Effect: Redemption, set in 2183 after the destruction of the SSV Normandy, Anto blocked Liara T'Soni and Feron from seeing Aria until reminded that Aria might not appreciate him selling Feron information about her meetings, at which point he brought them up. Aria revealed she already knew of his dealings, since nothing on Omega was secret from her, and that he was lucky she was fond of him.

In Mass Effect 2, in 2185, Anto was stationed near the right staircase to Aria's booth in the Afterlife Club when Commander Shepard came to speak with Aria. One of the packages Ish asked Shepard to retrieve contained a message revealing that Anto, while working for Aria, also sought outside work, which could be exploited to infiltrate Aria's organization. Shepard could alert Anto to Ish's activities; Anto thanked Shepard and later eliminated Ish.

In Mass Effect: Invasion, in 2186, Anto led Omega's defenders protecting Afterlife when the Adjutants attacked and was nearly killed before Aria arrived to save him. While Aria boarded the Cerberus warship Elbrus with General Oleg Petrovsky, Anto stayed in Omega Control and was among the first to learn the Elbrus had crossed the Omega 4 Relay and left Omega. Assuming Aria dead or transformed, he began planning to take over as Omega's leader, but Colonel Raymond Ashe interrupted, slaughtering Omega Control's operators and preparing the station for a Cerberus occupation. In Aria's absence Anto tried and failed to unite Omega's factions against Cerberus. When Aria returned, Anto resumed his role as her assistant. During the battle for the station, he and Aria fought toward the Afterlife Club to confront the Illusive Man, but Cerberus sprang a trap: an Adjutant that Ashe had smuggled in under stasis was awakened and killed Anto. His last name was originally given as "Bek'tall," though in Mass Effect: Invasion he called himself "Anto Korragan."
