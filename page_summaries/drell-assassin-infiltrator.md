Short page. A Mass Effect 3 multiplayer character kit presenting the drell as an Infiltrator class, almost entirely gameplay stats and tactics. Lore content is limited to the standard drell description: dense muscle tissue giving wiry strength, unique hyoid throat bones allowing high-pitched vocalization and resistance to strangulation, and a lightweight, fast, accurate but fragile body. The kit used Tactical Cloak, Homing Grenade, Recon Mine and Fitness, and was among the most agile Infiltrators in multiplayer alongside the N7 Shadow.
