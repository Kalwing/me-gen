Serviceman Nirali Bhatia was an Alliance marine serving on Eden Prime and the wife of Samesh Bhatia. She was a member of the 212, Ashley Williams' unit. Nirali had joined the military under the "Deferred Education Plan," intending to open a restaurant after leaving the service, and according to Ashley's dossier she thought highly of the gunnery chief's leadership. Ashley could tell Samesh that Nirali missed him and listened to recordings of his voice every night.

Nirali was killed by the geth during the Eden Prime assault. Because she sustained unusual injuries, the Alliance retained her corpse to study new defenses against the geth, distressing her husband. During the events of Mass Effect, Shepard could arrange for her body to be returned to Samesh for burial on Earth. A Citadel elevator news report featured a tribute to her in an Alliance "Profiles in Courage" series.

In Mass Effect 2, if Shepard had ensured her body was returned in 2183, Samesh sent an e-mail in 2185 thanking Shepard and revealing that he had fulfilled his wife's dream by opening a restaurant on Earth. A galactic news report noted that Alliance recruiting quotas had fallen due to public doubt about countering geth technology. Trivia observes that although called "serviceman" in game, a marine of her role would more likely hold the rank of private, unless she was a corpsman using the naval ranking system.
