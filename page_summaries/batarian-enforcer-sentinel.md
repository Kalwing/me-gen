The Batarian Enforcer Sentinel was a playable class and enemy type in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer. Its description noted that little was known about the batarian homeworld of Khar'shan or its inhabitants, because the Batarian Hegemony allowed only certain citizens to travel beyond its borders: organized-crime enforcers, pirate boarding parties, slaver gangs, and gladiatorial combatants, by whom the race was judged. Batarians were characterized as large brawlers who used nets and spiked weapons to capture targets or bleed them dry, and who bludgeoned close opponents with spiked armor and enforcement gauntlets.

The Enforcer had 750 shields and 750 health and used the powers Blade Armor, Shockwave, Submission Net and Fitness. In melee it delivered a punch sequence of two one-handed blows and a two-handed finisher; a heavy Enforcement Gauntlets strike wound up an omni-tool punch that could send an unprotected soldier flying and destroy its head on a fatal hit; and an Enforcement Punch grab dragged an enemy over cover and executed them. It could take cover but could not roll between cover positions and had no dodge maneuver, which left it vulnerable to instant-kill moves and to staggering attacks such as a Geth Prime's pulse cannon. It was quicker than krogan classes but less durable.

Player notes weighed Submission Net's damage-over-time and freezing effect against Shockwave's biotic-combo utility and crowd-clearing knockback, described the Rank 6 area-of-effect net evolution for crowd control, and recommended pairing the net with the Graal Spike Thrower or Kishock Harpoon Gun. Faction-specific advice covered trapping Phantoms and Guardians, juggling Cerberus Troopers off map edges, using Blade Armor to blunt Collector melee and stagger Phantoms, netting cloaking Geth Hunters, and relying mostly on guns against heavily armored Reaper units like Brutes, Ravagers and Banshees. It also noted that batarian heavy melee carried one of the highest damage-reduction bonuses of any heavy melee.
