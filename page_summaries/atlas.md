The Atlas was a large piloted Cerberus mech encountered in Mass Effect 3 and also fielded by the CAT6 mercenaries in Mass Effect 3: Citadel. Cerberus's research arm built it by combining the armor and firepower of a YMIR mech with the tactical judgment of a trained human pilot seated inside. An element zero core allowed it to be air-dropped onto a battlefield with minimal impact damage. Its thick armor included a transparent canopy of a proprietary polycrystalline composite that Alliance engineers hypothesized was a synthetic sapphire with crack- and heat-resistant interlayers. Though unwieldy in the field, its antipersonnel machine guns and heavy durability made it a serious threat, and unprepared encounters were expected to cause heavy casualties.

Offensively the Atlas carried a rocket launcher that dealt area damage plus burn-over-time, and a high-powered mass accelerator cannon fired in bursts (four shots in single-player, three in multiplayer). It launched guided missiles that were very hard to evade, and at close range it could swing or slam its claw arm or grab and crush a target for an instant kill, though only after a normal melee attack first. Destroyed Atlas mechs exploded violently, dealing severe damage through cover and terrain even after death. Defensively it had durable shields and armor, destructible armor plating on the shoulders, knees, and crotch, smoke grenades, and weak points at the canopy and rear exhaust port. It took 24% less damage from all Power Combos.

Recommended tactics included staying in cover, using armor-debuffing powers such as Expose-evolved Warp, Cryo Blast, and Proximity Mine, repeated Warp-Throw biotic explosions, Sabotage (to which Atlases built immunity over time), and the Cobra Missile Launcher in multiplayer for instant kills. In single-player, once its shields were down, sustained fire could shatter the canopy and leave the pilot, who had no more health than an Assault Trooper, vulnerable; killing the pilot could let Shepard hijack the Atlas and potentially earn an Achievement. Empty Atlas units could be found intact during Grissom Academy: Emergency Evacuation and Priority: Cerberus Headquarters. Per The Art of the Mass Effect Universe, it was first conceived as a heavier YMIR variant with a soldier inside before a protective canopy was added and the frame enlarged. An aquatic variant, the Triton ADS, appeared in Mass Effect 3: Leviathan.
