Dr. Alestia Iallis was an asari molecular geneticist educated at the University of Auräis and later stationed at Peak 15 on Noveria. She specialized in biotic-enhanced allele-specific hybridization, tracking inherited variations in genetic sequences. Records indicated she had been transferred to Peak 15 about a month earlier at the behest of an investor, Saren Arterius.

Commander Shepard first met Alestia with the science team at Rift Station. She was oddly calm about the crisis and pointedly rude, accusing Shepard of interrupting her meditations. Asked about Matriarch Benezia, she claimed to know her no better than Shepard knew Earth's President Huerta, and suggested Shepard ask Liara instead if she was present.

If Shepard agreed to help Dr. Zev Cohen develop a cure for scientists sickened by a biotoxin, one of Captain Ventralis's guards let the squad into the quarantined lab and locked them in until they could be scanned clean. Once the cure was made, Alestia ambushed the squad with a group of geth, revealing herself as an asari commando under orders from Benezia to kill Shepard at the first opportunity and expressing delight that the squad was trapped for quiet disposal. Alestia and her team were killed in the ensuing fight. Afterward the squad found the murdered guard outside the lab and confronted Dr. Cohen, who insisted he had been unaware that Alestia was a commando. If a firefight broke out with Rift Station's guards before Alestia revealed herself, she simply disappeared from her post. Alestia was voiced by Shanelle Workman.
