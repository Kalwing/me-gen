Petozi was an elcor at Rift Station on Noveria's Peak 15 facility. He ran a small kiosk selling Commander Shepard basic equipment and supplies, though most of his stock had been commandeered by Captain Ventralis. He was one of two merchants who sold the Kassa Fabrication License.

Nervous about the crisis at the station, Petozi remarked that he was increasingly worried about the guards, who had not slept in days. When Shepard questioned his opportunism in trading under the circumstances, Petozi said he had nothing better to do and pointed out that he gave freely to Ventralis and his men, and that Shepard's team were not being paid to protect civilians. If pressed about Matriarch Benezia, he revealed only that an asari Matriarch had recently visited the facility, citing rumors she had gone down to the hot labs. If fighting broke out between Shepard's squad and the guards, Petozi and the other civilians refused further contact with Shepard, and in some outcomes he vanished from the station entirely; otherwise he kept his head down and asked Shepard to take the shooting elsewhere. Either way he became unavailable once Benezia had been dealt with and access to Rift Station was cut off.
