Project Elites were enemies encountered during the Mass Effect 2 assignment Arrival, attacking Commander Shepard throughout Project Base. They first appeared during the Object Rho sequence, coming in up to five waves alongside Project Guards, Pyros and Engineers, and later accompanied larger Project forces.

They were armed with the M-15 Vindicator assault rifle and carried Disruptor Ammo on all difficulties, dealing extreme damage but tending to hang back and snipe from cover. They had good shielding, augmented on higher difficulties, and used Tech Armor to restore depleted shields. Because they could recharge their shields when left alone, the recommended approach was to hit them hard and fast, use powers such as Combat Drone, Inferno Grenade, Flashbang Grenade or Shockwave to flush them from cover, eliminate more aggressive enemies first, and detonate a nearby Project Pyro to catch them in the blast.
