"Dossier: The Convict" was a Mass Effect 2 recruitment mission acquired after Freedom's Progress. The dossier described Jack (no last name known) as rumored to be the most powerful human biotic ever encountered, with a history of violence, held in cryostasis aboard the turian prison ship Purgatory, whose operators Cerberus had paid for the prisoner's release. On arrival, the turian station commander Warden Kuril welcomed Shepard, gave a tour of Cellblock Two, and left to confirm the credit transfer. At Outprocessing, Kuril betrayed Shepard over the intercom, declaring the Commander too valuable to leave without a price tag, and activated the prison's defenses.

Fighting through Blue Suns mercenaries, FENRIS and YMIR mechs, and rioting prisoners, Shepard reached a security console and released Jack from cryostasis. Jack broke her restraints, destroyed the four YMIR Mechs guarding her cell with a massive biotic blast, and smashed a hole through the wall, triggering a chase through decompressing cellblocks. Kuril, using an M-76 Revenant and protected by a shield powered by three generators, made a final stand; Shepard destroyed the generators and killed him, ending the mission.

Jack reached the airlock, killed the guards, and was enraged on seeing the Cerberus logo on the Normandy. A batarian guard nearly ambushed her before Shepard shot him. Jack agreed to join in exchange for access to Cerberus files aboard the ship; Miranda and Jacob objected. Back on the Normandy, Jack demanded the files and immediately developed a dislike for Miranda, whom she called "the cheerleader." She took up residence in the sub-deck below main engineering. Because all prisoners had to be released to free Jack, Shepard received a threatening email from an escaped psychopath named Billy. Rewards included element zero, credits, and Ablative VI (Damage Protection) and Synchronized Pulsar (Shotgun Damage) upgrades. The mission was written by Brian Kindregan.
