Thin page: a Mass Effect 2 enemy-unit stub with mostly gameplay tactics. The Blood Pack Boom-Squad were vorcha trained for an anti-personnel role within the Blood Pack, each squad led by a squad commander answerable to a krogan overseer. They wore light armor, relied on vorcha regeneration, and fought from cover with missile launchers, firing a rocket or two before ducking back. In 2185 Commander Shepard's team fought boom squads in and around the Gozu District's main ventilation control room on Omega, where a boom squad leader disclosed the origin of the district plague and the vorcha's plans to seize the district. Unlike Blood Pack Troopers, Boom-Squad members could not regenerate their armor bars.
