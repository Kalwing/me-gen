Dark energy was the name given to the universal "fifth force" identified by astronomers in Earth's late 20th century. Like gravity, it influenced all matter on both cosmological and subatomic scales, and its repulsive effects were the primary reason the expansion of the universe was gradually accelerating. Dark energy was manipulated using the rare material element zero: when subjected to powerful electrical currents, element zero released dark energy that could be harnessed to create mass effect fields, raising or lowering the relative mass of objects within the field. Biotics, with eezo nodules and natural electrical impulses in their nervous systems, could generate and wield dark energy biologically, though the effort was physically demanding. Its most significant application was enabling FTL travel.

In the years after the Battle of the Citadel, interest in dark energy itself grew. Quarian studies on Haestrom revealed that its sun, Dholen, was aging prematurely as a result of dark energy influence, and Gianna Parasini of Noveria Internal Affairs mentioned her superiors' concern about clients' increased dark energy interests. In the Arrival mission, the Object Rho in the Bahak system used vast amounts of dark energy to sustain itself and its protective barriers, and adjusting the nearby Alpha Relay's controls let it tap unprecedented dark energy reserves, extending its range to sixteen other relays and the Citadel. In Mass Effect: Andromeda, the Scourge, a cluster-wide artificial phenomenon in the Heleus Cluster, was posited to be composed of dark energy used in ways unknown to conventional science.
