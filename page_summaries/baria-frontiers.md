Baria Frontiers was a corporation in Mass Effect 2 that specialized in selling star charts. A Baria Frontiers kiosk stood outside the police station on Illium, where Shepard could purchase star charts that added new star clusters to the Galaxy Map aboard the Normandy SR-2. The corporation also maintained an office in the Dracon Trade Center. This page concerned the corporation itself, as opposed to the separate Illium shop.

A Baria Frontiers representative named Erinya was stationed near the store kiosk and was involved in a contract dispute with the colonists on Feros. Erinya held a contract that exploited the colonists and initially refused to amend it, citing her dislike of aliens. Her hostility stemmed from her bondmate dying on the quarian homeworld during the geth uprising, and from both her daughters, one who worked in the Citadel embassy and one who worked as a greeter for the Consort, being killed during the Battle of the Citadel. Depending on Shepard's actions, Erinya could back down and issue new, fairer contracts, saying there was already enough suffering in the galaxy without her adding to it.

The Baria Frontiers office featured in Lair of the Shadow Broker. After Shepard gave Liara T'Soni intel about the Shadow Broker courtesy of Cerberus Information Processing, Liara asked to meet later at her apartment while she checked some things. Following her near-assassination, Shepard and Spectre agent Tela Vasir discovered Liara had gone to the Baria Frontiers office in the Dracon Trade Center to obtain the Shadow Broker's location. On arrival a massive explosion tore through the office; Liara had signed in only minutes earlier. Shepard and Vasir fought through the office against the Shadow Broker's private army. In the room where Liara had been meeting her contact Sekat, Shepard found Sekat dead and Vasir standing over him, claiming she had arrived seconds too late. Liara then emerged and pointed an M-4 Shuriken at Vasir, revealing that Vasir had been the one who tried to kill her. As Shepard's squad drew weapons, Vasir shattered the office windows, Liara shielded the squad with a barrier, and Vasir flew out the third-story window with Shepard in pursuit.
