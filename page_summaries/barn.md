The Barn was a Cerberus space station in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, located near the planet LV426. The station was used for experiments carried out on humans, aliens, and Reaper technology in order to enhance the performance of weapons and biotics. Its personnel answered to an individual referred to only as the Director. The Barn was the home base of Randall Ezno, and the workplace of his handler, Inali Renata. The majority of the station's test subjects were krogan and asari.

The facility included a hangar bay for docking ships, a med bay, a comm relay for external communication, and a station artificial intelligence called UDI (Ultra Defence Intelligence). It also housed an X1 Wing dedicated to producing an ultimate soldier designated X1, which was stored in the Colosseum. The base was heavily fortified and garrisoned with Assault Troopers, Assault Captains, Centurions, Riot Troopers, Snipers, Engineers, Grad Turrets, and Orcus and Ogre Mechs.

Sometime in 2186, Randall Ezno returned to the Barn after capturing a valuable turian target. On his arrival the station was attacked by a group of turian soldiers, presumably sent by the Turian Hierarchy. After defeating the initial invaders, Randall went rogue upon learning that Inali Renata had been conscripted as a test subject for experimentation. With the help of an unnamed volus ally, he attempted to contact the Alliance. To create a diversion, the volus hacked UDI and unlocked strategic cells, allowing many asari and krogan test subjects to escape. Geth and additional turian forces also attacked the station, turning the Barn into a war zone.
