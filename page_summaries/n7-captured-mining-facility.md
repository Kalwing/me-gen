An optional N7 assignment in Mass Effect 2, available after the mission Horizon. Scans of the planet Helyme, second planet of the Zelene system in the Crescent Nebula, detected Eclipse mercenary activity inside a mining facility registered to the Eldfell-Ashland Energy Corporation. The site's distress beacon had been powered down and sensors recorded multiple ships launching from the surface.

Shepard's squad landed and fought through roughly seven waves of Eclipse mercenaries, including Eclipse Troopers, Vanguards, Heavies and their leader Captain Vorleon. Logs recovered on site explained the attack: the cargo ship Ter Alan had docked and taken on a large shipment of resources bound for a facility in the Dranek system, requesting an escort because of pirate activity. Eclipse attacked the facility; workers were ordered to escort civilians to the crew quarters and then defend the main computer at all costs. Shepard hacked the facility's main computer and launched a decryption of encrypted data giving the Ter Alan's last reported location, which triggered three further mercenary waves led by Vorleon. The decryption came back incomplete, and the partial data was uploaded to Cerberus for further analysis.

Rewards included experience, 15,000 credits and 4,000 palladium. News reports on Illium and the Citadel later stated that the wreckage of the Ter Alan had been found on a planet in the Dranek system with no survivors. The assignment's environments were reused as a testing area for split-screen co-op in an early prototype of Mass Effect Team Assault, featuring squadmates Grunt and Thane fighting a krogan mercenary.
