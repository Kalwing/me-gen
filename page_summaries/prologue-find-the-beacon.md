Prologue: Find the Beacon is the opening mission of Mass Effect, acquired after reporting to the Normandy comm room. The SSV Normandy jumps through the Arcturus Prime relay toward Eden Prime, an Alliance colony under attack. Joker runs a system check while the turian Spectre Nihlus observes; Joker dislikes having a Spectre aboard. In the comm room, Nihlus and Captain Anderson reveal the mission is more than a shakedown run: a science team on Eden Prime discovered a Prothean beacon that must be secured and brought to the Citadel, and Nihlus is evaluating Shepard's Spectre potential. A garbled transmission from marines on Eden Prime shows heavy fighting and an immense alien ship. Anderson orders the team down; Nihlus scouts ahead alone.

On the surface, Shepard's squad advances toward the dig site. Corporal Jenkins is killed by Geth Recon Drones. The squad fights through more drones and encounters husks impaled colonists on dragon's teeth spikes. They rescue Gunnery Chief Ashley Williams of the 212, who joins the squad. The beacon has been moved from the dig site to the spaceport. In a cutscene, Nihlus is ambushed and shot dead by fellow turian Saren Arterius. The squad meets farmers led by Cole (who can name a smuggling contact, "Powell") and dockworker Powell near Nihlus's body. Fighting through geth troopers, shock troopers, and a Geth Destroyer, Shepard boards a tram to Spaceport Platform Two, where Saren orders the colony destroyed and is lifted by the beacon. Shepard disarms four explosive charges under a five-minute timer.

Kaidan and Ashley investigate the beacon; it activates and pulls one squadmate toward it. Shepard throws them clear and is lifted instead, receiving chaotic visions before the beacon explodes, knocking Shepard unconscious. Aboard the enemy ship, an asari in black informs Saren that Anderson's team foiled the colony's destruction and that a human may have used the beacon; Saren flies into a rage and orders the human eliminated. Shepard wakes in the Normandy med bay under Dr. Chakwas's care, briefs Anderson, and is ordered to take the ship to the Citadel. Enemies include Geth Destroyers, Recon Drones, Shock Troopers, Snipers, Troopers, and Husks.
