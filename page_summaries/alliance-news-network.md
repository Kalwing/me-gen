The Alliance News Network (ANN) was a galactic news broadcasting network operating in the Milky Way. Founded during the early decades of human space expansion, it began as a low-profile organization known for a series of exposes on the First Contact War, then opened bureaus on planets across known space and built a multi-species audience while winning awards for excellence. Despite its name, ANN was never funded by the Systems Alliance, a fact that caused regular public confusion until a high-profile sale to a media conglomerate clarified its ownership. The new management adopted a more sensational approach, prompting some veteran reporters to retire or leave; its more lurid programming drew wide attention and improved ratings, which management treated as validation. ANN nonetheless maintained that integrity and credibility remained newsroom standards.

Battlespace was among ANN's highest-rated programs, known for titillating reporting, and featured Diana Allers's front-line dispatches, which the network called gritty and realistic and critics called excessively focused on violence, though even critics conceded her reported facts were accurate. Known ANN reporters included Diana Allers, Iris Dunnigan, Shirin Kazemi, Amita Qasid, Pietro Pagano, Chisilix Palanurus, Ruxius Rilius, and Emily Wong (with FCC News).

In 2186, during the Reaper invasion, ANN and its correspondents reported the opening events of the war and the days leading to it through several archives: ANN Reports and Information Partners; Emily Wong's SolComms broadcasts during the initial Reaper attack on Earth; and two ANN Blogs concerning N7 Special Operations. Anti-conspiracy activist Bernard Plim hacked ANN to publicize alleged claims of a Systems Alliance conspiracy involving Task Force Aurora, forming the Conspiracy Accountability League. During the Cerberus invasion of Omega, Talon forces hacked the ANN comm channel to report events from the station in the Fight for Omega! feed. A special edition of the ANN show Profiles in Courage followed a squad of four N7 agents on shore leave during the war.
