Schells was a salarian inventor who gambled occasionally at Flux on the Citadel. Doran, Flux's owner, caught him using a scanner on the quasar machines that slightly improved his odds and ejected him for cheating. Schells then set about gathering data for a more sophisticated cheating system he had been developing for five years, which he intended to sell for a fortune, and asked Commander Shepard to help him collect the last few quasar results needed to complete his algorithms.

If Shepard accepted, the Commander could gather the data while evading the suspicious Doran and return the scanner to Schells for a reward. Alternatively, Shepard could hand the scanner to Doran, leaving Schells dismayed that five years of work were wasted and asking, "What am I going to do now?"; the squad's suggestions for better uses of his talents left him unimpressed. In Mass Effect 3, Schells held scores at the Castle Arcade on the Citadel's Silversun Strip.
