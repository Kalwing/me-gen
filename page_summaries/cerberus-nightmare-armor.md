The Cerberus Nightmare Armor was a full-body armor set in Mass Effect 3, added by the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC and obtained by scoring over 9,999 points in the Armax Arsenal Arena. In fiction it was worn by Cerberus Phantoms expecting long-range conflicts, using powerful capacitors to deliver extra energy to their weapons, circulating coolant in the gloves to allow more shots before a thermal clip overheated, and residual power for shield strength and regeneration. Reaching the score triggered a congratulatory in-game e-mail, "Thanks for breaking the scoreboard," from Armax Arsenal Arena principal systems administrator Virginia Ruojian, who wrote that a team of krogan battlemasters had never reached 10,000 points and offered Commander Shepard a Cerberus Phantom's armor captured during the attack on the Citadel. The armor then appeared beside the arena's bronze, silver and gold token machines, alongside the Cerberus Shade Armor and Cerberus Spirit Armor; only one of the three could be chosen, and the choice carried across later playthroughs with the same character. Nightmare increased shields, weapon damage, headshot damage, shield regeneration speed and ammo capacity, making it arguably the best weapons-focused set for Soldiers, Infiltrators and Vanguards when first available and rendering the N7 Defender Armor and Cerberus Assault Armor largely obsolete. Together with the Cerberus Ajax, Shade and Spirit sets its bonuses contributed to an 80% cumulative total, above the 60% of most full-body armors. Setting combat difficulty to Narrative was the fastest way to reach the required score. The male version was identical to Kai Leng's outfit without a helmet and was reused for the multiplayer N7 Slayer Vanguard, while the female version matched the N7 Shadow Infiltrator; the set was visually identical to the Shade and Spirit armors, with the Legendary Edition giving all three dark face shields instead of gold.
