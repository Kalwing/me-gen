Menae was one of two moons orbiting the turian homeworld Palaven in the Trebia System of the Apien Crest, its sister moon being Nanus. During the Krogan Rebellions the Turian Hierarchy classified nearly all data on both moons, fearing the krogan could smash them into Palaven's surface as weapons, and the classification persisted. The military took control of Menae soon after the first turian landing, halting civilian research and exploration. A few active naval bases and infantry installations for extreme survival training dotted the moonscape, supported by subterranean tunnels with powerful mass effect field generators that retained heat and atmosphere over patches of surface, allowing personnel to walk without enviro-suits. The mystery of Menae and speculation about its resources became a recurring subject in turian vids, novels and poetry.

During the Reaper invasion of 2186, Menae became the site of the mission Priority: Palaven. The Reapers' attempts to bombard the bases were met by fierce resistance from the turian fleet and the moon's anti-aerospace defenses, so they deployed ground units to take the bases one at a time; each captured base denied the turian fleet a place to repair and refuel. In Mass Effect: Andromeda, the turian Vetra Nyx was suggested to have been involved in an attempt to steal sensitive military information from a base on Menae.
