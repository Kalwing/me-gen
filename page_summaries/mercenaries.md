Mercenaries were individuals who operated wherever there was profit, usually hired by criminal organizations needing "heavy muscle" or by individuals wanting protection or assassination services. Often calling themselves "private security organisations", they commonly raided remote outposts or unprotected starships when unemployed. They came from all races but were most often turians, humans, batarians or krogan, and tended to use superior weapons and armor sourced from many manufacturers rather than a single company. They were typically found at bases on remote worlds or guarding an employer's operations.

As an enemy type in the first Mass Effect, mercenary forces included the standard Mercenary (assault rifle or shotgun), Mercenary Sniper, Mercenary Anti-Tank (rocket-firing rifles focused on the Mako), the rare Mercenary Adept (biotic), and Mercenary Turrets defending bases. Notable individuals included the asari commando Aleena, the turian BAaT instructor Commander Vyrnnus, the Terminus pirate Elanos Haliat, the Talons leader Nyreen Kandros, the krogan Battlemaster Skarr, the salarian bounty hunter Tazzik, Urdnot Wrex, Blue Suns co-founder Vido Santiago, Ygara Menoris and Zaeed Massani. Notable organizations included the Blood Pack, Blue Suns, CAT6, Eclipse, Grim Angels, Grim Skulls (mostly wiped out by Saren Arterius in 2165), Talons, Three Sabers, and Tiburón Rojo of Mexico City.
