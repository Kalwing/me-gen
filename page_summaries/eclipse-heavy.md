Short page. Eclipse Heavies were the heavy weapon fire support of the Eclipse mercenary organization, an exclusively female role. They stayed at maximum range behind cover, letting allies pin the enemy while they fired the ML-77 Missile Launcher, whose missiles could kill in two or three quick hits and gained homing capability on harder difficulties. They had no defenses on Normal or below but gained shields on Hardcore and Insanity. The rest of the page is enemy tactics.
