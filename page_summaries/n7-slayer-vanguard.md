The N7 Slayer Vanguard was a Mass Effect 3 multiplayer character kit representing the Alliance's N7 special forces. N7 soldiers were described as the go-anywhere, fight-anything special forces trained at the Interplanetary Combatives Academy in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, who had to survive combat situations "in an admirable and effective fashion" to earn the N7 rank; by the time of the Reaper War many N7s trained other species in anti-Reaper asymmetric warfare.

The Slayer used implants to dramatically improve mobility, fighting with a monomolecular sword whose sweeping attacks could hit multiple opponents, and could "slip fire" to become hard to pin down. Its dodge (Acrobatics) and heavy melee both triggered a short-range teleport that cost a portion of barrier and let the player phase through objects, walls, and ladders. Its powers were Phase Disruptor (a long-range barrier-fueled energy burst functioning like a no-cooldown Concussive Shot, effective for detonating power combos and stunning infantry), Biotic Charge, Biotic Slash (a long-reaching straight-line attack that damaged all protection types and could be fired through cover and walls), and Fitness. Its regular melee was a fast three-strike sword sequence, its heavy melee a teleporting slash that decapitated on a fatal hit and could appear behind enemies in cover, and its grab dragged an enemy over cover for a charged biotic punch.

The page is largely tactical guidance: "Charge + Melee" cycling was among the strongest melee tactics in the game, the kit excelled against Geth and struggled against Cerberus Guardians, and care was needed against enemies capable of sync-kills such as Phantoms, Atlases, Brutes, Banshees, Scions, and Praetorians.
