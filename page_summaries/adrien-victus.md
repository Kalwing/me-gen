Adrien Victus was a turian general who fought in the First Contact War and served most of his life in the turian military. He was regarded as an exceptionally strong leader, popular with his men, but the wider turian military disapproved of the ethics of his clever, unorthodox tactics. During the war on Taetrus in 2185 he held his forces back to let a salarian spy ring and turian separatists wear each other down before attacking, claiming victory with no casualties. His son, Lieutenant Tarquin Victus, commanded the turian 9th Platoon.

In 2186 Victus fought on Menae, a moon of Palaven, holding off Reaper forces. When Primarch Fedorian was killed after his shuttle was shot down, Hierarchy succession rules made Victus the new Primarch of Palaven, though he was initially unaware. Directed to him by General Corinthus, Commander Shepard convinced a reluctant Victus to leave Menae and represent the turians at a war summit. As Primarch, Victus agreed to help Earth only after Shepard secured krogan support to relieve Palaven, and he acknowledged Dalatrass Linron's concerns about curing the genophage while preferring a grateful ally to an angry enemy.

After the krogan female was recovered from Sur'Kesh, Victus asked Shepard to aid a top-secret turian team whose ship had crashed on Tuchanka, led by his son Tarquin. Their mission was to stop Cerberus from detonating a bomb in the heavily populated Kelphic Valley, a relic of the Krogan Rebellions planted by the turians and kept secret. Tarquin sacrificed himself to defuse the bomb; Victus mourned his son but expressed pride that the sacrifice would be remembered in the 9th Platoon's histories.

After the genophage was cured, Victus tasked Garrus Vakarian with coordinating turian efforts on the Crucible. Following the Cerberus attack on the Citadel, he emailed Shepard asking them to investigate the volus ambassador Din Korlack, suspected of collaborating with Cerberus. If he survived, Victus could be spoken to before the final push on Earth, pledging that the turians would stand with the humans as the humans had stood with Palaven.
