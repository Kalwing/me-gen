Lorik Qui'in was the turian manager of the Synthetic Insights branch on Noveria, based at the Port Hanshan mezzanine. A polished, refined businessman fond of discussing current events, he was noted for mangling human idioms. During the events of Mass Effect, Commander Shepard needed a garage pass to leave Port Hanshan and pursue Matriarch Benezia. Administrator Anoleis, who controlled such passes, was corrupt and taking bribes; Qui'in had gathered evidence of that corruption, but Anoleis discovered this, accused Qui'in of being the corrupt party, closed his office, and illegally paid Noveria security to ransack it looking for the incriminating data.

Found day-drinking at the mezzanine, Qui'in offered Shepard a garage pass in exchange for recovering the evidence from his office computer, warning that violence might be required. After the data was retrieved, Gianna Parasini revealed herself as an undercover Noveria Internal Affairs agent working for the Executive Board and asked Shepard to convince Qui'in to testify against Anoleis. Qui'in resented the presumption but could be persuaded.

Outcomes varied: if the evidence was given to Anoleis, Qui'in announced he would resign before his company was tainted and drowned his sorrows, calling one drink his "only friend." If Anoleis and Parasini shot each other dead, he decided he no longer needed the evidence. If the evidence went directly to Qui'in, he took Anoleis' place as Administrator of Port Hanshan. If he was convinced to testify, he was placed in protective custody pending the trial. In Mass Effect 2, should Shepard have handed him the evidence directly, Qui'in sent an email as Administrator of Port Hanshan offering to "clear a path" for Shepard should the Commander return to Noveria, signing off with more garbled idioms. His voice actor, Peter Jessop, also voiced Sovereign.
