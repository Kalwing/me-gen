Citadel: Party was a Mass Effect 3 mission in the Citadel DLC in which Commander Shepard hosted a gathering at the Personal Apartment during shore leave. Joker suggested the party immediately after the attempted hijacking of the Normandy SR-2 in Citadel Docks: Retake the Normandy. To prepare, Shepard bought Party Provisions for 100 credits, or 90 with both Intel Terminal discounts, from a kiosk at the Silver Coast Casino bar, then sent invitations from the apartment's private terminal. The party had to be held before Priority: Cerberus Headquarters, after which the DLC content closed.

Joker, EDI, Liara, James Vega, Steve Cortez, and Samantha Traynor were auto-invited as guaranteed survivors. All other guests were optional and required surviving all three games with their Mass Effect 3 arcs complete: Garrus, Grunt, Jacob, Jack, Javik, Kasumi, Samara, Zaeed, Wrex, Ashley or Kaidan, Tali, and Miranda Lawson, each with specific prerequisites. Squadmates fated to die in their arcs, Legion and Thane Krios, did not appear; Mordin Solus would not break cover even if he survived; Morinth was absent. Other Normandy crew such as Karin Chakwas could not attend, with Traynor stating Chakwas was handling a medical emergency on the Citadel.

Glyph asked Shepard to pick a Quiet or Energetic theme, which set dialogue but not group arrangements. The party ran in phases: an opening, a second part where Shepard could keep or switch the theme and settle guest disagreements, and a final phase whose arrangements followed the second theme, with Shepard choosing which group Glyph joined. Kasumi repeatedly uncloaked to interject. Conversations covered the two Normandys, biotics versus muscle, Joker's refusal to keep a gun in the cockpit, and Miranda and Jack's feuding. The phase ended with a Group Photo, later hung near the downstairs bar, in which romantic couples faced each other.

Shepard woke in the aftermath, potentially beside a love interest; an unromanced female Shepard could wake with James Vega or Javik. If Mordin had survived through the genophage arc, a datapad of his recordings lay by the bed. The guests dispersed around the apartment before Shepard signaled Rapid Transit to return everyone to Docking Bay D24. The Normandy crew gathered at the observation window; if Shepard had romanced Thane, his spirit appeared to speak to his siha before Shepard boarded last, closing the DLC. The Silversun Strip remained accessible afterward. Mark Meer later said Shepard's final franchise line, recorded for this mission, was "The best."
