Arrival was a downloadable content pack for Mass Effect 2. Its existence was accidentally revealed on February 21, 2011, when a patch for the PlayStation 3 version of Mass Effect 2 added three new trophies that BioWare had assumed would be invisible. BioWare released the first screenshot on March 14, 2011, officially confirming the pack, which launched on March 29, 2011. Like most original trilogy DLC, it was included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. Admiral Hackett returned in a prominent role.

The pack's premise was that a deep-cover agent had gone missing in Batarian space after reporting evidence of an imminent Reaper invasion; Shepard had to travel to the edge of the galaxy, rescue the agent, and discover the truth behind the Arrival. Its content consisted of a single assignment (Arrival), one location (the Bahak system in the Viper Nebula), three upgrades (Heavy Weapon Ammo, Medi-Gel Capacity, and Heavy Skin Weave), and three achievements. It was priced at 560 BioWare Points on PC and $6.99 on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360. To mark its release, BioWare published a promotional Cerberus Network broadcast describing Dr. Kenson's discovery concerning the origin of the Mass Relays. Pre-development notes for cut content indicate the DLC was planned to run about 2.5 hours and to include additional biotic, tech, and health upgrades.
