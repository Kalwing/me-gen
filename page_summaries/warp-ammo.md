Warp Ammo was an ammo power in Mass Effect 2 and the single-player mode of Mass Effect 3, described as transforming absorbed biotic energy into heavier weapon damage against health, armor, and biotic barriers. It was distinct from the Warp Rounds ammo bonus in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer.

In Mass Effect 2, a biotic mass effect field keyed into the ammo increased its damage against health, armor, and barriers and did extra health damage to targets affected by biotics. Ranks 1-3 raised the bonus from 15% to 35% of weapon damage; rank 4 evolved it into Heavy Warp Ammo (50% of weapon damage, likened to a miniature disruptor torpedo) or Squad Warp Ammo (35%, shared with the whole squad). The bonus damage was doubled against enemies lifted by Pull, Singularity, or Slam, but gave no extra damage against non-physics powers such as Reave or Dominate or against targets merely knocked down. It was the only Mass Effect 2 ammo power that dealt bonus damage to barriers, making it the best choice for sniping barrier-protected enemies. It was available to Shepard via Advanced Training and to Jack as a loyalty power.

In Mass Effect 3, Warp Ammo could confer up to three benefits: boosted damage against health, armor, and barriers; a large bonus against enemies primed for Biotic Explosions; and armor weakening that reduced armored enemies' damage mitigation by 25% (50% with the rank 6 Enhanced Warp evolution). Rank 1 gave +15% health and armor damage, +30% barrier damage, -25% armor weakening, and +50% damage to lifted targets. Rank 2 was Biotic Combo, rank 3 Damage, rank 4 Damage or Squad Bonus (squadmates at 50% effectiveness), rank 5 Ammo Capacity or Headshots, and rank 6 Damage or Enhanced Warp. Warp Ammo dealt 2x damage to barriers inherently. Since Patch 1.04, the lifted-target bonus applied to all enemies primed for biotic combos, including those under Warp, Reave, Dark Channel, or Stasis, but not to shields. The armor-weakening debuff was party-friendly and lasted about two seconds. It was the only ammo power effective against both barriers and armor, making it efficient against Banshees, Adjutants, and Praetorians. Single-player users were Liara T'Soni, Jack, and Shepard as a bonus power; it did not exist in multiplayer.
