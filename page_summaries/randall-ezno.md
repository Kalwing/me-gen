Randall Ezno was a veteran Cerberus operative and Infiltrator, tasked with capturing aliens for study during the Reaper War around 2186. He received more extensive Reaper-grade neuronal implants than any other human test subject at his main station, upgrading his biotic and tech abilities. It was implied his biotics resulted from these implants rather than being natural. The implants were intended to indoctrinate him, but the process failed, and he adapted to them faster than most Cerberus operatives. He wore Cerberus armor and bore implant markings on his face similar to Commander Shepard's after the Lazarus Project; his implants glowed red for Renegade choices and blue for Paragon choices.

In Mass Effect: Infiltrator, while hunting a turian for Cerberus on an Ice Giant, Randall learned of his own extensive modification. He returned to a Cerberus station called "the Barn," which was then attacked by Turian Hierarchy soldiers. Summoned for a debriefing by his handler, Inali Renata, he lost contact with her along the way. Guided by a voice claiming to be a volus hacker on his radio, he found Inali, who had "volunteered" for implants and was being brutally treated. Randall rebelled, fighting waves of Cerberus troops, mechs, turrets, and escaped alien test subjects, including a mutated cybernetic krogan named X1. Following the volus's advice, he freed the station's other test subjects, turning the Barn into a warzone, and reached the communications tower to contact the Systems Alliance, arranging pickup on a Desert Planet.

There he confronted a heavily modified Inali, controlled by the operative in charge of the Barn, known only as "The Director." After defeating her, Randall could spare or kill Inali depending on his psychological inclination, and vowed revenge on the Director. He left the planet to fulfill that promise and provided the Systems Alliance with valuable intelligence on Cerberus operations. His weapons included Cerberus-modified versions of common guns such as the M-96 Mattock Auto, M-22 Eviscerator, M-29 Incisor, and M-333 Particle Beam, and he used powers including Pull, Leash, Storm, Salvo, Charge, and Tactical Cloak.
