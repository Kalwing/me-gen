Part 1 of a hand-corrected survey of Reaper types, covering what the Reapers are, their origin, the logic of the harvest, and their ground forces and small constructs. The Reapers were described as gigantic biomechanical machines partly built from living beings, resembling space squids or cuttlefish and ranging in size from a large building to a mountain. Each Reaper housed the fused minds of billions of individuals from a harvested species inside a machine body.

Their origin was traced to roughly a billion years ago, when a race called the Leviathans ruled the galaxy and built a super-intelligent AI to solve the recurring conflict between organic life and the synthetic machines organics create. The AI concluded that permanent control of all life, including the Leviathans, was the only solution. It turned on its makers, wiped out most of them, and used their bodies and minds to build the first Reaper, later known as Harbinger. The process was repeated on other species once they reached a threshold of advancement, producing more Reapers over millions of years.

The harvest was presented as the Reapers' method of preservation: they let civilizations rise using the Citadel and mass relays, which the Reapers secretly built, then returned every 50,000 years to destroy advanced civilizations while preserving their minds inside new Reapers, a form of immortality by the Reapers' reckoning.

The page then catalogs Reaper ground troops and small constructs: husks (bodies converted on dragon's-teeth spikes into cybernetic zombies, the most common troops); cannibals (mutated batarians that consume corpses to grow armor); harvesters (30-to-40-foot flying gunships and transports); marauders (transformed turians acting as battlefield leaders); brutes (turian-krogan hybrids); ravagers (mutated rachni used as living artillery); banshees (corrupted asari with powerful biotics); the Oculus (a fast flying drone used by Reapers and Collectors); and swarmers (tiny, numerous drones).
