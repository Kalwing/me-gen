This page collects the squad barks, conversation lines, and spontaneous remarks Urdnot Wrex delivers when he is in Commander Shepard's squad, drawn from Mass Effect and Mass Effect 3 (including the Citadel DLC). The lines showcase his bluntness, love of violence, contempt for turians and salarians, and pride in fallen krogan glory. Representative quotes:

Wrex, when Shepard spares the warehouse workers during Citadel: Expose Saren: "It would've been quicker to just to kill them."

Wrex, to Executor Pallin claiming he never bends the law: "Don't piss in my ear and tell me it's raining, I've known plenty of dirty C-Sec agents."

Wrex, meeting the Thorian on Feros: "And they call *me* ugly..."

Wrex, opening negotiations with the Rachni Queen at Peak 15: "This is going to be a fun conversation."

Wrex, to the Mira VI when it insists neutron radiation is lethal to all known life: "I won't have some virtual wench questioning my fitness!"

Wrex, if brought without Garrus on Garrus: Find Dr. Saleon: "If you're too weak to hunt, you don't deserve the kill."

Wrex, at the Krogan Monument on the Presidium: "This statue represents what the krogan use to be: a proud and fierce nation. Now we're just brutes for hire to the highest bidder. We've forgotten our roots."

Wrex, to Tali at the Krogan Monument: "At least you still live. In another two hundred years, this statue may be the only evidence my people ever existed."

Wrex, on Therum's surface: "Fifty degrees hotter and I might start to feel uncomfortable. How are you mammals doing, Shepard?"

Wrex, on Ilos: "There's something in the air here; death is on the wind. It's a good place for killing."

Wrex, distracting a guard during Silver Coast Casino: Infiltration: "I need to look at your kitchen. I'm a food inspector."

Wrex, after the squad guns down Shepard's pursuers in Citadel Archives: Escape: "That's why I love hanging out with you guys! Why shoot something once when you can shoot it 46 more times?"

Wrex, trapped in the vault with Shepard: "Refresh my memory. Didn't we used to win these things back in the old days?"

Wrex, in combat during Mass Effect 3: "Uncle Urdnot has a present for you!"

Wrex, if Shepard talks Brooks into surrender during Retake the Normandy: "In the old days, we would have just shot her. Good times."

The page also notes recurring bits: Wrex and Garrus argue in Citadel elevators about the genophage and about whether Shepard or Saren would win a fight, and Wrex repeatedly disdains bars that want to hire krogan bouncers "as a status symbol."
