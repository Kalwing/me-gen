War Assets in Mass Effect 3 were the people, weapons, armies, fleets, items and technologies that Commander Shepard accumulated across the game to fight the Reapers. Each asset carried a point value contributing to Total Military Strength. That total, multiplied by a Readiness Rating percentage, produced Effective Military Strength (EMS), which determined the outcome of the final battle. Assets came from missions, between-mission conversations, scanning planets in Reaper-controlled systems, and off-screen galactic events. They were inspected at the War Terminal in the War Room on the Normandy's second deck, and were grouped into categories: Alien, Alliance, Asari, Crucible, Ex-Cerberus, Geth, Krogan, Quarian, Salarian and Turian.

The Readiness Rating was raised through the Galaxy at War system: Mass Effect 3 Multiplayer, Mass Effect: Infiltrator, Mass Effect 3: Datapad, and N7 HQ. Playing single-player alone capped EMS at roughly 50% of collected assets. Without DLC there were 8,370 total assets available, though not all could be gained in one playthrough because some were tied to mutually exclusive choices (saving Kaidan or Ashley, saving or sacrificing the Council, curing or sabotaging the genophage); the practical maximum was about 7,500, or about 3,750 EMS without multiplayer, below the roughly 4,000 EMS needed to see all endings. Extended Cut lowered that threshold to about 3,100. Leviathan added roughly 620 assets, Omega about 405, and Citadel added 70 points to the Normandy SR-2. With all DLC the ceiling rose to around 8,500. Multiplayer matches added readiness to specific theaters of war, averaged across all theaters.

In Mass Effect Legendary Edition, Galactic Readiness and EMS were removed with multiplayer; only Total Military Strength was shown, with adjusted ending thresholds (for example, at least 7,400 for the "Destroy" ending in which Shepard breathes). When Priority: Earth began, all assets combined into three groups codenamed Sword, Shield and Hammer. Sword and Shield comprised allied fleets and starships; Hammer comprised ground forces. Sword attacked the Reaper fleet above Earth as a distraction so Hammer could land and support Anderson's resistance, while Shield escorted the Crucible to dock with the Citadel. Higher EMS meant lighter losses, a faster Normandy extraction of squadmates, and the Crucible docking with little damage.
