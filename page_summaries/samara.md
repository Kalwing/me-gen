Samara was a nearly thousand-year-old asari justicar, a member of an ancient monastic order bound by a strict, absolute honor code: "The Code of the Justicar is black and white." She was a potential love interest for a male or female Commander Shepard. As a Maiden she ran with a mercenary gang and found a talent for combat; that career ended when she discovered a transport she led was carrying slaves bound for the Collectors, forcing her to kill her own team. She later settled with an asari mate and had three daughters, all of whom proved to be Ardat-Yakshi, a genetic condition that killed mates during melding. Samara was a pureblood and believed purebloods more often bore Ardat-Yakshi daughters. Falere and Rila accepted seclusion; Morinth, whom Samara called the smartest and bravest, fled and became a serial killer, and Samara gave up her possessions, swore the Code, and hunted her for centuries. She once fought Nihlus Kryik for days after witnessing him kill a civilian, and was impressed when he turned the Code against her.

By 2185 Samara had tracked Morinth to Illium, where Shepard recruited her. After Shepard obtained the name of Morinth's ship, the AML Demeter, Samara swore the Third Oath of Subsumation: "By the Code, I will serve you, Shepard. Your choices are my choices. Your morals are my morals. Your wishes are my code." She warned that if Shepard's actions violated the Code she would kill the Commander after the mission. On her loyalty mission Shepard acted as bait to lure Morinth on Omega; the confrontation ended with either mother or daughter dead.

In Mass Effect 3, provided she survived the Collector base, Samara reunited with Shepard on Lesuss, where Reapers were converting Ardat-Yakshi into Banshees. She rescued Falere while Rila detonated a bomb to destroy the Banshees. The Code demanded Samara kill Falere, who could not live outside the monastery, and Samara attempted suicide; Shepard could restrain her or let her die. If stopped, Falere agreed to rebuild the monastery and Samara joined the war. On the Citadel she told Shepard, "Only your actions will be remembered. May you choose them well." If both survived, they were shown at peace in an asari cityscape after the Crucible's use. In the Citadel DLC she could rekindle romance, spoke at Thane's memorial, and attended Shepard's party.
