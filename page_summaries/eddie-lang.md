Officer Eddie Lang was a C-Sec officer on the Citadel who stood guard near a skycar parking lot in the Wards. He greeted Commander Shepard enthusiastically, having heard of the Commander's achievements, prompting the squad to tease that Shepard had a fan. Lang answered Shepard's questions about the Citadel and C-Sec, explaining that he joined C-Sec partly out of family tradition since his grandfather had been a police officer on Earth. He had lived on the Citadel for about a year but claimed not to have seen even a tenth of the station, said he liked the Wards, and suggested Shepard visit Flux. He also told Shepard that although vids portray Spectres as super-agents, many C-Sec officers dislike them, and grew uneasy discussing Harkin, offering only that "They suspended him, so some of it must be true."
