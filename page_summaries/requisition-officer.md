The Requisition Officer was a turian merchant found at the C-Sec Academy on the Citadel Wards, to the left of the docking bay elevator, in Mass Effect. He could only sell to authorized buyers and had already run a background check on Commander Shepard. After Shepard became a Spectre, Captain Anderson suggested speaking to him to acquire new equipment. When Shepard approached, the officer initially assumed his system was malfunctioning because it told him to offer Shepard his select stock, then realized Shepard was the new Spectre he had heard about and apologized. He was a key source for Spectre - Master Gear. In the original Mass Effect's Rich Achievement text and the Prima guide he was called the C-Sec Requisitions Officer.
