This page transcribes the fifth part and acknowledgements of The Art of Mass Effect (2007), with commentary by Casey Hudson. The section covers the game's landscapes and architecture, tracing how early combat-visualization paintings set an overall style before specific locations were developed. It discusses Eden Prime (shifted from green farmland to a red, ember-filled palette to darken the story's opening, keeping a cargo train), its train station, the Therum mining complex and mines, the Prothean tower where Liara is found, the Thorian's lair, and Feros (a human colony amid Prothean ruins, built largely from reusable parts). It also covers Ilos (changed from lush jungle to an ominous dead world) and its Archive of suspended Protheans, Noveria (a spaceport inspired by the Swiss Alps, made colder and more isolated), the ring-shaped Citadel Presidium and its grungier wards, docking bays and exterior, and Virmire (inspired by the islands of Palau, the site of Saren's oceanfront fortress). The acknowledgements credit the concept artists: Derek Watts, Matthew Rhodes, Sung Kim, Adrien Cho, Fran Gaulin, Mike Spalding, Mike Trottier, Michael Jeffrey, Sasha Beliaev and Mike Higgins.
