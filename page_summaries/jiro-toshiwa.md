Dr. Jiro Toshiwa was a human scientist working at the Ascension Project. Of American and Asian descent, he had a reputation as a charming, easy-going young man and was in a non-serious relationship with Kahlee Sanders despite an age difference of nearly twenty years. Hendel Mitra was suspicious of him, but Jiro was one of the few people Gillian Grayson trusted.

This was a facade. Jiro was actually a Cerberus operative, planted to monitor the Ascension Project and pass information to the Illusive Man. His relationship with Kahlee was a lie used to extract information. When Cerberus wanted to advance its experiments on Gillian, Jiro was to secretly administer a dose of neurostimulant. The first dose caused Gillian to unleash a biotic storm in the cafeteria; Jiro, absent at the time, later questioned Kahlee about it in bed and convinced her not to let Gillian be removed from the Project. He made a critical mistake by mentioning Kahlee's father, since the fact that Kahlee was Jon Grissom's daughter was known only in classified Alliance documents filed by David Anderson.

By the time Kahlee realized this the next morning, Jiro had already smuggled Gillian out of the Academy's hospital to a secluded spot to administer the second dose. This time he injected it into Gillian's spine, causing a seizure. Jiro was caught when Hendel Mitra arrived and ordered him away; while Hendel was distracted by Gillian's condition, Jiro used a standard-issue stunner to knock him unconscious. Fleeing, Jiro ran into Kahlee and was subdued after a brief struggle.

After his capture, the Alliance interrogated Jiro. He held out under harsh questioning but eventually admitted membership in Cerberus without revealing his mission. His ultimate fate is unknown; Cerberus sometimes rescued captured operatives, but Jiro would likely have been considered expendable.
