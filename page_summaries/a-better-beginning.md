A Better Beginning was the Mass Effect: Andromeda Priority Ops mission in which the newly appointed human Pathfinder Ryder settled the Andromeda Initiative's first viable colony. Acquired after Nexus Reunion, it tasked Ryder with making the radiation-blasted planet Eos habitable after two previous colonization attempts had failed. Director Tann committed a ship, the Tempest, and a support crew for the effort.

Aboard the Hyperion, Ryder learned at the SAM Node about the modifications Alec Ryder had made to the AI SAM to enhance his own abilities, which started the Ryder Family Secrets thread. Ryder then boarded the Tempest, met crew including Cora Harper, Vetra Nyx, and pilot Kallo Jath, and traveled to Eos in the Pytheas system. On the surface at Site 1: Promise, Ryder restored power to an abandoned outpost, negotiated with the salvager Clancy Arquist to bring a relay antenna online, and located a Remnant signal. Following the signal, Ryder activated a Remnant monolith and was ambushed by the asari Remnant researcher Peebee, who tackled Ryder, then shared information and gave navpoints. Ryder activated two further monoliths, fighting kett forces including Chosen, Anointed, and Wraiths, and met the old krogan Nakmor Drack, who was hunting kett.

With all three monoliths active, their beams converged on a hidden Remnant vault beneath a lake. Ryder and Peebee entered, fought Remnant Assemblers and Observers, repaired conduits, activated gravity wells, and finally triggered a purification field they had to outrun to escape. The vault's activation terraformed Eos, clearing its skies. Peebee then joined Ryder's team, as did Drack after helping fight a kett platoon near the outpost site.

Ryder established the colony, choosing a scientific or military focus that set the standard for all future Initiative outposts and pleased or upset different Nexus leaders; a military choice would later send a Prodromos squad to aid at Meridian. Colonists were shuttled down and August Bradley was made mayor of the settlement, named Prodromos. Foster Addison discussed the situation with Ryder before returning to the Nexus. At the new Pathfinder HQ, Tann, Tiran Kandros, and Nakmor Kesh met Ryder and directed the next lead, starting A Trail of Hope I. Eos's remaining lethal radiation cleared after Ryder's first visit to Aya. The journalist Keri T'Vessa became available outside the Pathfinder HQ.
