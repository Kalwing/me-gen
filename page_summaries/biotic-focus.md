Brief gameplay page with minimal lore prose: Biotic Focus was a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode, available to the Cabal Vanguard, that focused biotic energy and atavistic muscle structure to reduce damage taken and increase melee damage and movement speed for a short time, with higher ranks adding recharge speed, duration, a Biotic Shield barrier-regeneration option, and a Heightened Focus state.
