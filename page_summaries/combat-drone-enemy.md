Combat Drone (enemy) referred to the hostile drones deployed by Geth Primes and enemy engineers in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, distinct from the player power of the same name and from the Disruption Drone created by CAT6 Specialists.

In Mass Effect 2, Geth Primes and enemy engineers deployed Combat Drones that had only a weak close-range shock attack, which stunned the player and knocked them out of cover if it connected. They had minimal health, moved quickly, making them hard to track, and dissipated after successfully landing a single attack. AI Hacking could make them stop attacking but would not turn them against other enemies; Overload, Energy Drain, or a melee attack destroyed them instantly.

In Mass Effect 3, Geth Primes deployed Combat Drones purely as battlefield distractions. These had a short-range shock attack and a long-range rocket attack, both dealing very low damage but able to stun a target for volleys from the summoning Prime. Unlike in Mass Effect 2 they had only shields, which were very weak, plus a timed lifespan, and they dissipated quickly if the Geth Prime that summoned them was destroyed. Overload destroyed one instantly, Tactical Scan killed them outright, Sabotage could hack them though they rarely survived long, and Stasis had no effect on them despite their lack of armor.
