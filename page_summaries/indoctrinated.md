Indoctrinated were beings who had fallen under the control of Reaper indoctrination, appearing as enemies in Mass Effect. They were encountered at Saren Arterius's base on Virmire, where he ran experiments on indoctrination and its effects on organic beings. Captain Kirrahe's STG regiment had been assigned by the Citadel Council to investigate Saren, which led them to the Virmire base, where Kirrahe lost half his men. Some of the missing were not killed but imprisoned and experimented on by Saren, and could be found in various rooms and detainment cells.

Indoctrinated salarians were armed only with pistols and occasionally had weak shields, though some had no defenses at all. Because indoctrination impairs the ability to think, they did not take cover and were easy to kill, mostly just charging and firing while keeping a minimum distance from their targets.
