Geth Destroyers were one of the larger and more dangerous forms of geth infantry, encountered wherever geth were concentrated in force. In the original Mass Effect they stood roughly eleven feet tall, wore dark grey armor with a yellow chest stripe and a single bright blue "flashlight" eye, carried a Geth Pulse Shotgun, and possessed strong health and four to six bars of regenerating shielding. They advanced slowly while firing, occasionally launched a damaging Carnage, then charged for a powerful melee attack. Because they carried no secondary weapon, Sabotage left them defenseless until their shotgun cooled; Overload stripped their shields, Lift disabled a charging Destroyer, and Neural Shock was useless against them as synthetics. Advanced or Master AI Hacking could turn one to Shepard's side. In the Legendary Edition they were given the leaner redesign shared by other large geth platforms, with three small eyes and no left shoulderpad, but unchanged behavior.

In Mass Effect 2 Destroyers were rare but far deadlier at close range, adopting a red and black color scheme similar to the earlier Geth Juggernauts and arming themselves with the M-451 Firestorm flamethrower, which forced them to close distance. Their very strong shields were their main defense since they did not use cover. A shot to the silver fuel pack on their upper back, once shields were down, detonated them instantly; the Arc Projector could set off the tank from any angle, and Incinerate also detonated them, though the Firestorm itself and Flashbang effects did not, and a ruptured Destroyer would keep charging rather than panic. In the Firewalker Pack they instead carried ML-77 Missile Launchers for range. A separate Mass Effect 3 flamethrower geth was named the Geth Pyro.
