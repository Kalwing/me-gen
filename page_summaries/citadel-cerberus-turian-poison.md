Citadel: Cerberus Turian Poison was a Mass Effect 3 mission with prerequisites Priority: The Citadel II and Arrae: Ex-Cerberus Scientists. A salarian doctor at Lab Services in Huerta Memorial Hospital was looking for a Cerberus poison tailored to turians. Shepard could acquire the mission by talking to him, by overhearing him speak to someone else, or by finding the toxin directly. An alternative journal entry framed it as a turian-tailored toxin recovered from a Cerberus base needing someone on the Citadel who could use it.

The poison was on a microscope at the control center of the Gellix facility staffed by ex-Cerberus personnel, to the left of the stairs leading to the main room, near where Brynn Cole and Dr. Fredricks held their initial conversation. Shepard delivered it to the doctor in the Examination Rooms section of Huerta Memorial, where he and his colleagues used it to develop an antidote. Completion gave 5 Reputation, 30 experience, 1,000 credits, and an update to the Turian Sixth Fleet War Asset. If the poison was not found during the mission, it could be bought at the Spectre Requisitions store for 1,000 credits. After completion, the turian patient, identified as General Korilius, was reported awake and responsive the next time Shepard passed within hearing range of the salarian doctor.
