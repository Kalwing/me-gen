Warp was a biotic skill in Mass Effect: Andromeda multiplayer, distinct from the Warp power of the original trilogy. It shredded an enemy with rapidly shifting mass effect fields, weakened enemy armor, and was less effective against shields. Warp detonated combo primers, and enemies it affected were themselves primed for combo detonations.

At rank 1 it dealt 260 initial damage plus 32 damage per second, applied a 20% armor defense debuff, carried a 50% damage penalty against shields, lasted 10 seconds, and had a 10-second recharge time. Rank 2 added recharge speed, and rank 3 added 35% all damage. Rank 4 branched into Recharge Speed (+40%) or Anti-Armor (+60% additional Warp damage versus armor). Rank 5 branched into Duration (+50%) or Expose (affected targets took 15% more damage from all sources). Rank 6 branched into Escalation (ongoing damage increased as the target's health fell, up to a 500% bonus) or Double Warp (launched two projectiles that sought separate targets). The Expose evolution was noted as non-functional due to a bug. The skill was available in multiplayer only, to the Human Commando and Human Guardian kits, with no single-player availability.
