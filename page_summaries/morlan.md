Morlan was a salarian merchant who ran a "famous shop" in the Lower Markets of the Citadel Wards, selling basic supplies and non-human armor; his most notable product was the Geth Armory license. In Mass Effect, when Dr. Michel was being blackmailed, Commander Shepard could deliver medical supplies to Morlan to expose the blackmailer. Morlan grew flustered that Michel had not come herself, until the blackmailer, a krogan, arrived and said "I told Banes you'd screw this up." After the krogan was dealt with, Morlan claimed to know nothing about Armistan Banes, saying his only contact had been the krogan.

In 2185, Shepard received a spam message from Morlan titled "YOUR 2 SMALL!" advertising his extranet site selling hormone augmentation, cybernetic enhancement and genebiotic xenografting, with disclaimers including no shipping to Omega and no krogan reproductive organs available.
