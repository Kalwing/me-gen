The unnamed ExoGeni VI on Feros was installed by ExoGeni Corporation when it took over one of the Prothean towers as its headquarters. Appearing as a red-tinted human male, the VI let employees access the mainframe to retrieve and share information between departments according to clearance level, authenticating staff by reading an ID card each employee carried. It mentioned having an extensive sensory network and may, like Mira, have coordinated research and development in the ExoGeni labs.

When Commander Shepard's squad reached the ExoGeni headquarters after encountering Lizbeth Baynham, they found the VI being interrogated by the Krogan Commander, who was trying to extract information. When the VI politely refused to release confidential data without sufficient security clearance, the krogan became enraged and threatened to blast its "virtual ass into actual dust"; the VI responded that other users were queuing for the console, that queue actually being Shepard's squad approaching from behind. After the squad killed the krogan, the VI addressed Shepard as Lizbeth Baynham, since Lizbeth had earlier given the Commander her ID card, which carried high-level clearance despite her being on probation. The VI explained that the krogan had wanted information on Species 37, the Thorian, and told Shepard the truth behind the colonists' behavior at Zhu's Hope: ExoGeni had deliberately allowed them to be infected with mind-controlling alien spores as an experiment. It also noted that Lizbeth's probation stemmed from her vehement opposition to the experimentation, but could give no useful information on the geth activity in the building because the geth had blocked its sensors.
