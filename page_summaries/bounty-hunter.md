The Bounty Hunter was an unnamed krogan encountered on Illium in Mass Effect 2. During the mission to recruit Thane Krios, he descended a lift with two Eclipse Engineers and was killed in the resulting firefight. He was equipped with an M-6 Carnifex that could be armed with Incendiary Ammo to boost its damage, could use Carnage at longer ranges, and possessed strong shielding and armor along with the standard krogan charge and regeneration. He was notable as one of only two krogan in Mass Effect 2, the other being Kalusk, to have a blue shield bar; all other krogan used armor, biotic barriers, or some combination of the two. His armor suggested that he was normally a member of the Blood Pack, indicating that freelancing with Eclipse was not a problem for him. The page directed readers to Dossier: The Assassin for related tactics.
