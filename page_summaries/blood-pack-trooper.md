Thin page: a Mass Effect 2 enemy-unit stub, mostly gameplay tactics. Blood Pack Troopers were vorcha forced into service by the Blood Pack and literally beaten into soldiers by their krogan commanders. They were the most commonly encountered Blood Pack soldiers and were regarded as cannon fodder by their krogan overseers. They carried M-3 Predator pistols, M-8 Avenger assault rifles, and M-23 Katana shotguns, and fought in a supporting role, suppressing enemies while a krogan warrior advanced. They typically appeared in large numbers and could enter a "berserker rage" when a nearby Trooper was killed, dealing more damage and sometimes charging recklessly. They had no shields or barriers, but on higher difficulties gained instantly regenerating armor, and constantly regenerated health on all difficulties.
