Short gameplay page: the Asari Justicar Adept is a Mass Effect 3 multiplayer class with under ~150 words of real lore prose (a generic asari description followed by power lists, melee/movement notes, and faction-by-faction multiplayer strategy). The only lore stated is that the asari are the most influential species in the galaxy, native to Thessia, the first to discover the Citadel, long-lived galactic mediators, and powerful natural biotics who favor hit-and-run tactics. The class has a unique character model distinct from other asari multiplayer classes.
