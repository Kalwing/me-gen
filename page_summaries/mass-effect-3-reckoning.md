Mass Effect 3: Reckoning was a free DLC pack for Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode, announced February 21, 2013 and released February 26, 2013 worldwide for Xbox 360 and PC and for PlayStation 3 (February 27 in Europe). It was the last multiplayer DLC for Mass Effect 3. It added six multiplayer characters (Geth Juggernaut Soldier, Cabal Vanguard, Talon Mercenary Engineer, Krogan Warlord Sentinel, Alliance Infiltration Unit Infiltrator, Awakened Collector Adept); seven weapons (M-7 Lancer, M-11 Suppressor, Adas Anti-Synthetic Rifle, Blood Pack Punisher, Executioner Pistol, Geth Spitfire, Venom Shotgun); five weapon mods including Assault Rifle Ultralight Materials and pistol/SMG Power Magnifier; and two Gear upgrades, the Batarian Gauntlet and the Geth Scanner. The Krogan Warlord wielded a Biotic Hammer.
