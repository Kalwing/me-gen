This short enemy-unit article, distinct from the page on the batarian race, described a trio of batarians encountered in the slums district of Omega in Mass Effect 2. During Mordin Solus's request to locate his missing assistant Daniel, Shepard found the three batarians threatening Daniel because they were convinced he was spreading the plague. The confrontation could be resolved peacefully or with gunfire. All three were armed with basic M-3 Predator pistols, carried only low shielding and a single point of health, and could be killed with a few shots each. The remainder of the page consisted of brief capability and tactics notes rather than lore.
