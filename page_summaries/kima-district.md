Kima District was a neighborhood on Omega, sharing a border with Gozu District, best known as the location of Archangel's base. Its station ID was 776, and a large sign on the facility read "Welcome - District B." The district's name may derive from a species of spider, fitting given that the base became a trap for Archangel's enemies. The district was inaccessible to the player after the mission Dossier: Archangel was completed.

Archangel's Base was a three-level building accessible via a bridge into the mid-level or tunnels into the lower level. The mid-level held a main room and kitchen; the basement held a utility room and a garage with several parked shuttles, its three entry passages fitted with emergency shutter controls. The facility's history before Archangel repurposed it was unknown. Starting in mid-2185, he used it as headquarters for his band of vigilantes. Its single exposed-bridge approach was easily covered by a skilled sniper, but this proved a double-edged sword: after Blue Suns, Eclipse, and Blood Pack mercenaries joined forces and annihilated his band, Archangel was trapped inside as all three groups laid siege. Nine of the eleven vigilantes were laid out under burial shrouds within the facility; Sidonis escaped the massacre, so the eleventh body's location was unknown.
