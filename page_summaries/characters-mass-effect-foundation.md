This page was a roster index for the thirteen-issue comic series Mass Effect: Foundation, listing links to the characters appearing in each issue. It held roughly forty entries across the thirteen issues, with Maya Brooks, Kai Leng, Miranda Lawson and Jacob Taylor recurring across multiple issues, alongside figures such as Ashley Williams, Kaidan Alenko, Urdnot Wrex, The Illusive Man, Mordin Solus, Thane Krios, Kolyat Krios, Kasumi Goto, Donovan Hock, Zaeed Massani and The Shadow Broker. It contained no original prose beyond the per-issue link tables.
