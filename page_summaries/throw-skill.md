This page is a game-mechanics reference for Throw, a Biotic skill in Mass Effect: Andromeda (distinct from the Throw power of the original trilogy). Throw hurled a gravity-field projectile that flung unshielded and unarmored targets away and detonated combo primers. It could be combined with the Pull power to hurl held targets, which then took on Throw's properties, arcing and homing toward enemies; a primed enemy that was pull-thrown triggered a combo detonation on impact.

The page lists single-player and multiplayer skill ranks. Base single-player values were 850 Force, 225 damage on world collision, and a 10-second recharge; multiplayer base values were 850 Force, 240 damage, and an 8-second recharge. Higher ranks improved recharge speed, added all-damage and all-force bonuses, gave Throw a 2.5-meter area of effect (Radius), extended float and combo-prime duration (Duration, +400%), and at rank 6 offered either Double Throw (two projectiles seeking separate targets) or Swift Detonation (increased combo detonation damage and recharge speed).

In single-player, Throw was available to Pathfinder Ryder as a starting Biotic skill or through Biotic Training. In multiplayer it was available to the Asari Adept, Asari Huntress, Asari Sentinel, Human Sentinel, and Human Kineticist.
