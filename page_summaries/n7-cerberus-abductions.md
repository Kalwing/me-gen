An optional N7 assignment in Mass Effect 3, unlocked after completing either Attican Traverse: Krogan Team or Tuchanka: Turian Platoon. Cerberus was attacking and abducting civilians on the human colony of Benning, in the Euler system of the Arcturus Stream. Shepard landed to protect civilians during their evacuation. The mission plays out on the multiplayer map Firebase Ghost, a tight urban environment of numbered prefab buildings.

Shepard's squad fought Cerberus light infantry, Assault Troopers, Combat Engineers, Centurions and Turrets, through the upper streets, then reached a group of civilians and defended them as they moved toward a landing pad for extraction. The civilians were invulnerable and sprinted forward in stages toward the shuttle while Cerberus focused fire on Shepard. Comm logs from Svetlana Alexandrov and other residents warned "don't trust Cerberus. They're taking people against their will," and urged people to run. On the map Shepard could recover the dog tag of Alliance Soldier Bilal Osoba, needed for the side mission Benning: Evidence.

After Shepard declared an all-clear and the civilians reached the landing zone, the mission ended when Shepard selected the shuttle. At debriefing, Admiral Hackett said a rogue Cerberus group might be behind the continued civilian attacks; the Alliance regained a foothold on Benning and retook the planet, and Shepard received the Arcturus First Division war asset (+5 Reputation, 20,000 credits, the Capacitor Helmet). A saved civilian, Jessica Frohm, later emailed Shepard thanking her and saying she found hope that her lost friends' memory lived on in her.
