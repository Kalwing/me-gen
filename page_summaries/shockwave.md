Shockwave was a biotic power appearing in Mass Effect 2 and in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes. It sent out a cascading series of explosive biotic impacts that toppled enemies in the caster's path.

In Mass Effect 2 the power launched a string of concussive mass effect fields in a narrow line in front of the user, ignoring obstacles and able to travel along the ground or strike airborne targets, sometimes flinging enemies off the map for environmental kills. It instantly killed unprotected Husks and glowing Abominations, stunned protected targets while stripping a little of their protection, and dealt 50% more damage to barriers than to shields or armor. Rank 4 evolved it into Heavy Shockwave (a long, forceful "biotic freight train" dealing the most damage to protections) or Improved Shockwave (a wider 3.5-meter impact radius). It was available to the Adept, Vanguard, Jack, the Scion enemy, and Tela Vasir. Harbinger and intact YMIR Mechs could not be stunned even by Heavy Shockwave.

In Mass Effect 3 the power no longer traveled along the ground but fired straight outward in the direction faced, letting it hit targets above or below the caster. Its range and area were smaller but more consistent, and it was guaranteed to deal damage on top of its force. It could still pass through cover, retained the 1.5x barrier modifier, hit up to two targets per cast, and staggered or knocked down infantry. Shockwave was a detonator for all Power Combo types, and the rank 6 Lifting Shockwave evolution let it also prime unprotected infantry for Biotic Explosions. Other evolutions boosted recharge speed, force and damage, radius, cascade reach, and detonation strength. It was available single-player to the Adept, Vanguard, and Jack, and in multiplayer to the Human Adept, Human Vanguard, Batarian Enforcer Sentinel, and Krogan Shaman Adept. On PC the rank 4 Radius evolution was bugged so that impacts stopped affecting enemies after several casts.
