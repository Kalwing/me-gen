The Cerberus Ajax Armor was a set of armor in Mass Effect 3 included with the Alternate Appearance Pack 1 downloadable content. It increased shields, weapon damage, power damage and ammo capacity. In-fiction, Ajax armor was favored by Cerberus soldiers brave or unlucky enough to lead charges that broke enemy formations, and its role was to keep the wearer standing and shooting as long as possible. It gave some benefit to omni-tool and biotic-amp systems but emphasized boosting and recharging its kinetic barrier and bleeding weapon heat off through coolant circulation in its gloves. Because of the high-risk tactics associated with it, the Alliance had captured very few intact suits.

The armor came from the Alternate Appearance Pack 1 DLC, and in Mass Effect Legendary Edition it could be equipped once Commander Shepard returned to the Normandy SR-2 after Priority: The Citadel I. It suited players wanting to combine extra shield resilience with boosts to ammo capacity, weapon damage and power damage, and it rendered the N7 Defender Armor mostly obsolete, since it matched or beat that set on ammo capacity, weapon damage and shields while also granting power damage and shield regen speed, which the N7 Defender lacked. Weapon-dependent classes might instead prefer the Cerberus Nightmare Armor, and power-dependent classes the Cerberus Spirit or Cerberus Shade sets. The Ajax, Nightmare, Shade and Spirit sets shared a bonus structure whose combined total was 80 percent, compared with the 60 percent of most full-body armors and the 65 percent maximum of individual armor pieces.

The male version, without Cerberus insignia and colors, was identical to an alternate outfit for Kaidan Alenko and James Vega, while the female version was used in multiplayer by the N7 Demolisher Engineer. All of the set except the helmet resembled that of a Cerberus Combat Engineer.
