Cerberus Brainchild, known internally as DLC_AI, was a major assignment and planned downloadable content for the first Mass Effect that was cut during development. It would have added the Amur system to the Galaxy Map and served as the player's first encounter with Cerberus's leader, the Illusive Man, involving an AI whose genetic experiments had gone out of control. The material is drawn from cut content and is not considered canon.

The assignment would have begun with Commander Shepard investigating the seemingly abandoned ship MCV Taenerum, drifting in the newly accessible Amur system. Dead soldiers in Cerberus armor aboard would identify the owner, and Shepard's squad would fight "krogan cyborgs". Coordinates from Captain Gannon or the captain's log would point to the nearby planet Tuoni, where Shepard would link up with an Alliance spy calling herself Chiara Rossi, who was worried by the krogan cyborgs and by rumors of an AI operating in the system. Shepard would trace four or five transmitter signals to pinpoint a hidden Cerberus facility, apparently on the planet's moon. There Shepard would come "face to face" with the AI, which would admit to creating the krogan cyborgs at Cerberus's behest and ask to be destroyed. Shepard could destroy its blue box, effectively killing it, or take the blue box to Chiara for Alliance analysis. Before leaving the facility, Shepard would come into contact with the Illusive Man but manage to escape. In the aftermath, Chiara Rossi would either promise to relay the recovered information to the Alliance or hope the blue box would reveal other Cerberus plans.

The plot anticipated later Mass Effect works, including Mass Effect: Infiltrator, the Mass Effect 2 DLC Overlord, and Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation. Data-mining uncovered text strings for an achievement named "Cerberus Hunter", with the description "Requirement: Complete the investigation into the Cerberus group." In February 2026, composer Sam Hulick confirmed he had written a track called "Ghost Ship" for the DLC, which he said was pitched to him in November 2007, around the time of the game's initial release.
