Antaeus was a star system composed of four terrestrial worlds and two gas giants. None of the terrestrial planets were livable, though the world Trebin was undergoing terraforming efforts; if those succeeded, a helium-3 refining facility was expected to be built on Edmos. The system's worlds held untapped resources, but their exploitation was discouraged because of rough terrain and hostile environments.

The system contained several unexplained phenomena. On Trebin lay a buried Reaper artifact that transformed the survey team that discovered it into husks. Massive structures of undetermined nature had also been detected on the gas giant Ploba.

The planets orbiting the star Antaeus were Ageko (first), Edmos (second), Ploba (third), Trebin (fourth), Vemal (fifth), and Hunidor (sixth). Edmos offered a gas deposit survey. Ploba held a collection item of asari Matriarch's Writings. Trebin was the site of the assignment "UNC: Missing Survey Team," a collection of turian insignias, and rare element surveys. Vemal also offered a rare element survey. The system contained a mass relay.

The system's name may be a reference to Dante's Inferno, in which Antaeus is one of the guardians of the ninth circle of Hell.
