Power Combos were a combat mechanic introduced in Mass Effect 3, expanding on the Warp detonations of Mass Effect 2. Attacking an enemy with two powers in sequence combined their effects into an explosion dealing heavy area damage to up to four targets, ignoring shield-gate protection. There were four types: fire (also called Flame Combos), cryo, tech (also called Electric Combos or Tech Bursts), and biotic; fire, cryo, and tech were sometimes grouped as "tech combos."

Every combo required two different powers: a source or "primer," which determined the combo type, and a detonator, which triggered the explosion. Primed enemies displayed a colored effect (violet for biotic, red flame for fire, blue sparks for tech, white frost for cryo), though not all enemies showed it and multiple primer types could be layered invisibly. Biotic Explosions could only be detonated by other biotic powers; the other three types could be detonated by many powers regardless of damage type. Certain "universal detonators" such as Warp, Throw, Shockwave, and Biotic Charge could set off all four types.

Priming durations varied by power, from very brief windows (Overload) to extended periods (Warp). Many powers could serve as both source and detonator, but a combo always required a different source and detonator. Layered primers detonated in reverse chronological order, the most recent first. Advanced players chained explosions by alternating powers that were both source and detonator of different types, potentially producing one fewer explosion than the number of powers used. A datapad at Jon Grissom Academy contained an in-universe analysis of creating a Biotic Explosion.

Combo base damage ranged from 100 to 250 depending on combo level (the summed ranks of the source and detonator), then scaled sharply with difficulty, making combos increasingly central to power-based builds at higher difficulties. Biotic Explosions dealt double damage to barriers and armor, Cryo Explosions froze unprotected enemies and could chain, Tech Bursts dealt double damage to shields and could stun, and Fire Explosions dealt double damage to armor and could panic organic infantry.
