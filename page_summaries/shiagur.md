Shiagur was a krogan warlord and one of the few fertile krogan females remaining after the widespread deployment of the genophage, circa 700 CE. She used her fertility to amass male followers, who competed among themselves for the right to have children with her. Despite her legendary reputation, her band was not particularly powerful, and she was defeated and killed at Canrum by turian forces during the last stages of the Krogan Rebellions. Vengeful krogan admirers retaliated against her killers, ultimately causing thousands of casualties.

Shiagur's bloodline remained a status symbol among later krogan, with her direct descendants bearing clan brands proving their lineage; Nakmor Drack and his granddaughter Nakmor Kesh were among them, and the tank-bred Grunt carried some of her genetic legacy through Okeer's selection of "worthy" krogan. Being compared to Shiagur was considered a mark of honor: the Urdnot Shaman might bestow the compliment on a female Shepard who understood the krogan better than some of their own kind, and her name was invoked in a song Mordin Solus could sing for Eve.
