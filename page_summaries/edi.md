EDI (Enhanced Defense Intelligence) was an AI created by Cerberus and installed aboard the Normandy SR-2, represented by a holographic blue sphere and a feminine voice. In Mass Effect 2 she was a Quantum Blue Box AI serving as the ship's electronic warfare defense, initially given behavioral blocks preventing her from interfacing with ship systems, and she also served as the Illusive Man's eyes and ears, monitoring listening devices and reporting to him. She frequently argued with Joker, whom she said she was studying to understand human behavior. She aided Shepard's missions with logistics; during the Horizon attack she brought defense turrets online to drive off the Collector ship. Infiltrating the Collector ship, EDI confirmed the Collectors' Prothean ancestry by comparing DNA alleles and warned Shepard they had walked into a trap, later determining the turian distress signal was falsified using detection protocols written by the Illusive Man, implying he knowingly sent them into the ambush. When the Collectors boarded the Normandy, Joker granted EDI full access, unshackling her and letting her save the ship. EDI revealed she was based in part on technology from Sovereign's remains and analyzed Anti-Reaper Algorithms.

In Mass Effect 3, EDI hid her true nature from the Alliance by posing as a VI responding only to Joker. On Mars she seized control of the Cerberus infiltration unit posing as "Dr. Eva Coré," gaining a mobile platform resistant to small-arms fire and temperature extremes. She continued to exist primarily within the ship. Once unshackled she could self-modify her core programming and repeatedly questioned Shepard about morality, free will, fear, mortality and the purpose of synthetic life. She could pursue a romance with Joker. At Cronos Station, logs revealed EDI was born of the rogue Hannibal-class VI from Luna with added Reaper engineering, and that she had chosen to inhabit Eva's body rather than destroy it. She helped recover the VI Vendetta and defeat Kai Leng.

If Shepard chose to destroy the Reapers, EDI perished with all synthetics and her name was added to the Memorial Wall. Under Control or Synthesis she survived, and with Synthesis she described the Reapers ceasing their attacks and helping rebuild, and speculated mortality itself might one day be transcended.
