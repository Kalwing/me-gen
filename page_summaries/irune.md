Irune was the volus homeworld, the second planet of the Aru System in the Aethon Cluster, accessible in Mass Effect 3 after Priority: Tuchanka. It was a high-pressure, high-gravity planet supporting an ammonia-based ecology, an environment so rare that the volus had been slow to colonize elsewhere; most preferred to remain on Irune and work remotely over the extranet, while the more adventurous donned pressure suits to seek their fortunes in Citadel space. Its ammonia atmosphere was toxic to non-volus life, requiring visitors to wear pressure suits outside controlled areas.

Irune was remarkable for having abolished warfare as an institution of the state, volus culture lacking the romantic view of war held by more aggressive species; disputes were settled by social castigation, bargaining, or economic sanctions. Its cities were built along fast trade routes rather than defensible positions, leaving them vulnerable to husk attack once the Reapers overwhelmed the turian fleets and began a ground war on the planet. Its flora included the kympna lobes, a pungent silver carnivorous plant with teeth. Irune featured in the mission Irune: Book of Plenix and as a Search and Rescue site for the Book of Plenix artifact.
