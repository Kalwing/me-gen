Xeltan was an elcor diplomat on the Citadel, usually found near Ambassador Calyn in the Embassies. During the assignment concerning the asari Consort Sha'ira, Xeltan initially refused to talk to Commander Shepard, saying only that he was there on business to complain about Sha'ira, who he claimed had compromised his authority, though he would not give details.

After Shepard spoke to the turian Septimus on Sha'ira's behalf, it emerged that Sha'ira was innocent: Septimus had revealed one of Xeltan's secrets and blamed the Consort for the leak. Shepard brought Xeltan evidence proving Sha'ira was not involved. Xeltan was shocked, insisting Septimus could not have discovered the secret, but accepted the irrefutable evidence and became worried that if Septimus could find out, anyone could. Shepard could either reassure him that the secret would be extremely hard for anyone else to uncover or leave him anxious, after which Xeltan went to speak with Sha'ira.
