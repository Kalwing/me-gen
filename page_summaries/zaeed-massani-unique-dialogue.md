This page collects dialogue unique to Zaeed Massani when he is in Commander Shepard's squad during Mass Effect 2, plus the anecdotes he tells aboard the Normandy and spontaneous combat lines. On missions he is consistently cynical and violence-hardened: he tells a poisoned Shepard to "walk it off," dismisses Warden Kuril's complaint about bounty hunters by saying Kuril "is not hiring the right ones," recognizes Blue Suns leader Tarak on Omega, and mocks Okeer's krogan ("We're not here to help you, jackass. We're here about the Collectors."). He distrusts letting enemies like Rana Thanoptis go, offers to kneecap the drugged volus Niftu Cal, and on the Citadel voices conspiracy suspicions about the Council hiding the Reapers behind the geth.

The shipboard anecdotes flesh out his mercenary past. He recalls his assault rifle "Jessie," an M-8 Avenger he retired around 2180 and never had to re-sight, boasting "more men have been killed by that gun than died in the Skyllian Blitz." His first "suicide mission" was leading five men to destroy the turian frigate Verrikan from the inside; he alone survived. In 2168 he pried a Blood Pack helmet off the krogan Gesark after a one-on-one duel in which he took out Gesark's legs. He has killed over 50 Cerberus operatives and marvels that the Illusive Man "must be big on forgiveness." He describes a botched job on Vatar where mercenaries strapped grenades to a kidnapped local girl, a 2175 drop into the Krogan DMZ, an eight-year-old abductee from the Skepsis system who outfought her kidnappers, an asari lover who sold him to the Blood Pack, and a skilled salarian tech specialist (whom Mordin resembles) he eventually had to kill.

Representative quotes:

- Zaeed, if chosen to lead the second diversion squad in the Suicide Mission, as he dies: "Too many of them. Shields couldn't hold up. Figured it might end something like this."
- Zaeed, questioning Avina about the Reapers on the Citadel: "So, the Council's lying -- there's a shock. Why spook people with the damn Reapers when you can blame everything on the geth?"
- Zaeed, on Legion's loyalty mission: "The geth says to kill the geth. That's all I need to hear."
- Zaeed, on the Collectors' torture-like experiments during Jack's recruitment: reflecting that "nobody walks away from torture unchanged," and that he "never found torture worth the price."
- Zaeed, seeing the Teltin experiments on Pragia: he admits doing "a lot of bad stuff" but "draws the line at harming children."
- Zaeed, at the female camp on 2175 Aeia: "Any CO who'd do this to his own men is asking for a knife in his spine."
- Zaeed, on Tali's recruitment mission when the Geth Colossus appears: "Son of a bitch! Move!"
- Zaeed, on Joker after the crew's abduction: he did well "for a kid with glass bones," and should have come down to Zaeed's room "to get a real weapon."
