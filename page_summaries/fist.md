Fist was the human owner of Chora's Den and a known crime figure on the Citadel. The reporter Emily Wong investigated his activities but could not gather enough evidence because the krogan bouncer at the Den kept her out.

In the comic Mass Effect: Foundation, Fist was caught between the Shadow Broker, who wanted him dead, and Cerberus, who wanted the secrets he held about the Broker. The arranged deal was that Fist would defect to Cerberus for asylum, but the Broker learned of it and sent Urdnot Wrex to kill him. A Cerberus agent named Rasa confronted Fist in his apartment and found that he had instead sold out to the Spectre Saren Arterius, who had made a better offer. Seeing Wrex approaching, Fist proposed a third option and somehow convinced Rasa to let him go; Rasa reported to the Illusive Man that she expected the krogan to deal with him.

In Mass Effect, Fist, a former Shadow Broker agent, switched allegiance to Saren, prompting the Broker to hire Wrex to assassinate him. Shepard learned that Fist had contact with a quarian holding evidence of Saren's treachery. Shepard raided Chora's Den and confronted Fist in his turret-defended office; Fist surrendered and revealed he had sent the quarian to a nearby alley to meet Saren's associates while believing she was meeting the Broker. Shepard could execute or release him, but if Wrex was in the squad he killed Fist regardless to fulfill his contract. An OSD of Fist's private files could be recovered and given to Emily Wong.

In Mass Effect 2, if Fist survived, he had started over on Omega as a dockworker and could be found on the lower floor of the Afterlife Club with two asari, bitterly complaining that Shepard had ruined his life and telling Shepard to leave him alone.
