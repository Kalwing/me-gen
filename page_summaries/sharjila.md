Sharjila was the first planet of the Macedon System in the Artemis Tau cluster, carrying a Level 1 Pressure Hazard warning. It had a very dense ammonia-and-oxygen atmosphere, a temperate surface of alumina with sulfur deposits, and an extensive silicon-based, oxygen-breathing ecology; populated areas were covered in fine silica dust, the respiratory by-product of the world's higher animals. High-speed surface winds carried abrasive silica that made footing treacherous, so EVAs were discouraged. The planet's soil supported grasslike lifeforms adapted to high-pressure ammonia/oxygen conditions, and comm buoys had recently logged unregistered vessels operating nearby.

In Mass Effect, Sharjila was the setting for the assignment UNC: Asari Diplomacy, which involved a stronghold held by pirates and pirate snipers, with Dahlia Dantius present. Points of interest included a crashed probe to salvage and an abandoned camp with an asari capsule containing one of Matriarch Dilinaga's writings, collected for UNC: Asari Writings, plus an unmarked spot where the rachni "song" could be heard. The planet also held mineral deposits of thorium, uranium and magnesium for the UNC: Valuable Minerals survey. Its points of interest lay mostly along cliffs and inclines near the edge of the operational area, with easy driving between them.
