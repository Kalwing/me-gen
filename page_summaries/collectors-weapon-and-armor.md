The Collectors' Weapon and Armor was a Mass Effect 2 downloadable content pack, exclusive to buyers of the Limited Collectors' Edition and available to PlayStation 3 owners through the Recon Operations Pack, that added one armor set, the chitinous Collector Armor (with regeneration amplifiers boosting movement speed, damage resistance, and health recovery), and one weapon, the Collector Assault Rifle (built to strip enemy shields).
