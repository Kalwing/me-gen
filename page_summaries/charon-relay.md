The Charon Relay was a mass relay in the Sol system of the Local Cluster, in orbit of Pluto. For thousands of years it lay encased in an ice shell hundreds of kilometers thick and was mistaken for one of Pluto's moons. In 2149 a human science team working near Pluto determined that "Charon" was not a moon but an enormous piece of Prothean technology; the finding was not wholly unexpected, since Prothean ruins uncovered on Mars in 2148 had referred to such a device. Although the underlying science was still beyond human understanding, researchers managed to reactivate the dormant relay. Probes sent through immediately lost contact, so a crewed expedition led by Jon Grissom traveled through and found that it connected to Arcturus and three further mass relays. The team's safe return made them heroes and made Grissom the public face of the Systems Alliance as humanity entered a period of rapid exploration and expansion. Operating the relay gradually circularized Pluto's previously elliptical orbit. The Charon Relay went on to serve as the point of access between Earth, the economic and cultural heart of human civilization, and the rest of the galaxy. During the battle for Earth in 2186, if Commander Shepard chose to activate the Crucible, the Crucible fired its energy into the Charon Relay to transmit it across the galaxy; the Charon Relay and the rest of the mass relay network were severely damaged, but were eventually rebuilt, with or without Reaper assistance depending on Shepard's final choice. In Greek mythology, Charon was the ferryman who carried the dead across the river Styx into the Underworld.
