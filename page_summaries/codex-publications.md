Index page collecting Codex entries on in-universe human publications: Drew Karpyshyn's military-historical novels Ascension (Cerberus's secret biotics program and the fate of operative Paul Grayson and his biotic-prodigy daughter Gillian Grayson, pursued by scientist Kahlee Sanders) and Revelation (Lt. David Anderson's 2165 investigation of Kahlee Sanders after a military research station massacre, which forced him into alliance with turian Spectre Saren Arterius), and Fornax, a five-sensory xenophilia magazine launched in 2167.
