Citadel: Oculon Syndicate was an assignment cut during development of Mass Effect; the information is drawn from cut content and is not canon. Set on the Citadel, it would have let Commander Shepard delve into the station's criminal underworld and consisted of several interlinked sub-plots, some mutually exclusive depending on the manner and order in which they were handled.

In the general plot, Shepard would come "recommended" to Harkin and could then choose to work for the Oculon Syndicate or against it, potentially resulting in its destruction, peacefully or violently. Another contact, Beck, would offer several jobs, and Beck and Harkin could eventually confront one another; Shepard could also work with Fist directly, and some warehouse guards could be killed or made to leave peacefully. In the sub-plot "Shakedown Dak", the character Dak could be killed, persuaded into a partnership, forced to pay, or paid for by Shepard, with an "ambush meeting" involving people called "dwellers". "Missing Workers" had Shepard encounter a group of turians who could be killed or let go, choose whether to accept a bribe during a confrontation with a foreman, and possibly help a turian escape the Citadel, with a warehouse section where a tool and data could be acquired and where a female Shepard could flirt.

"Dwellers" was a short job about defeating the dwellers. In "Eliminate Harkin", Shepard would find and deal with Harkin, sparing or killing him, then contact Fist and either reveal Harkin or keep the secret. "Help Dak" and "Help Workers" were versions that worked against the Syndicate. In "Retrieve Data" and "Elimination Round", Harkin and Fist both featured, with Fist giving a "speech" in an elevator and possibly being killed; some of the data concerned Shepard. Trivia noted parallels with Citadel: Expose Saren, where Harkin and Fist also appear, and that Beck also featured in the cut assignment Citadel: Cheaters Never Prosper.
