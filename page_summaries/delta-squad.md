Delta Squad was a Systems Alliance special forces unit featured in the animated film Mass Effect: Paragon Lost, stationed at the human mining colony of Fehl Prime. In 2183 it was deployed there after Blood Pack forces attacked the colony, its first real engagement. Commanding Officer Captain Toni delivered the briefing. During the assault, the krogan warrior Brood shot down Delta Squad's Kodiak shuttle, which pilot Mason crash-landed; about half aboard died. Toni broke his leg, so Lieutenant James Vega took command, defeating the krogan leader Archuk's forces in single combat while biotic Essex, assault specialist Kamille, tech specialist Nicky, and sharpshooter Milque executed his plan. Vega was commended by Anderson and Admiral Hackett, and Delta Squad was assigned to guard the colony.

Two years later, in 2185, the squad was still on guard duty as the Alliance installed military-grade kinetic barriers and defense cannons, amid rumors of colonies vanishing in the Terminus Systems. Toni sent the squad to investigate a signal disrupting communications; after they destroyed it, a Collector ship appeared and began harvesting colonists. Vega led a plan to use the defense cannon against the ship, but it fired at half power, was deflected, and was destroyed. Kamille was captured and incorporated into a Praetorian, which incinerated her boyfriend Mason. The squad freed Brood in exchange for his ship. Nicky was fatally stabbed by the Praetorian before Essex and Vega destroyed it. The civilian Messner was exposed as a Cerberus informant who identified the attackers as Collectors. The survivors flew into the Collector ship, but Messner shot Brood, crashing them, and they were taken prisoner. The remnants used the Collector processing systems to disperse a cure to harvested civilians. Milque prepared an escape freighter while Vega and Essex fought the Collectors; Essex and Messner were killed, and the asari archaeologist Treeya was vented into space. Vega faced a final choice between recovering Treeya's intel or rescuing awakened colonists. Only Vega and Milque survived; a psychologically scarred Vega later returned to Fehl Prime with Milque to honor the dead.
