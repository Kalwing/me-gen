The Javelin was a sniper rifle in Mass Effect 3. Named by Alliance marines, it was a geth weapon that held a reservoir of ferrofluid, magnetically drawn into the firing chamber and expelled at lethal speed. Like a high-pressure water-jet, the ferrofluid cut through nearly anything it struck, generating so much heat that the shot resembled a beam of light and caused terrible wounds.

In single-player, the Javelin was found during the mission Rannoch: Admiral Koris, on the path past a crashed escape pod, and could otherwise be purchased afterward from Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies; it became permanently unavailable if Priority: Rannoch was completed before rescuing Koris. In multiplayer it was an ultra-rare random reward from item packs, requiring no expansion.

The Javelin was among the heaviest weapons in the game, surpassed in weight only by the Geth Spitfire in multiplayer. It offered the highest undivided damage-per-shot of any weapon, including the M-98 Widow, along with a very high innate 100cm penetration that ignored damage penalties even with armor-piercing mods, allowing multi-kills on lined-up enemies. Its built-in thermal scope let the user see through as much cover as the weapon could pierce, highlighting all foes, including cloaked ones, in a purple outline, making it especially effective against Cerberus and geth. Drawbacks included a short delay between trigger pull and firing that made moving targets harder to hit, a slow reload that could be nearly halved by reload cancelling, and a smaller ammunition reserve than the Widow. In Legendary Edition, because weapons were not based on the latest patch, the Javelin dealt less damage than the Widow at the same weight, leaving its thermal scope and penetration as its main advantages. It shares its name with the Alliance-developed Javelin missiles.
