"Dossier: The Veteran" was a short Mass Effect 2 recruitment mission requiring the Zaeed - The Price of Revenge DLC (included in the base game in Legendary Edition), acquired automatically after Freedom's Progress. The dossier described Zaeed Massani as the galaxy's most feared mercenary and bounty hunter, retained by Cerberus at extremely high cost and awaiting pickup on Omega. Shepard found Zaeed in an alley near Afterlife Club, brutalizing a batarian prisoner. Approaching triggered a conversation in which Zaeed mentioned an unresolved job: Vido Santiago, head of the Blue Suns, had seized an Eldfell-Ashland Energy facility on Zorya, and EAE had hired Zaeed to retake it; Zaeed wanted Shepard's help with it. Zaeed shot the fleeing prisoner in the leg, excused himself to turn in the bounty, and was immediately available as a squadmate, found in the Starboard Cargo Hold on the Engineering Deck. Completing the mission unlocked the loyalty mission Zaeed: The Price of Revenge and the Faia system. Cut content indicates Zaeed was originally to be recruited on Korlus for a one-million-credit fee involving an attack on the local mafia and a Mako city chase.
