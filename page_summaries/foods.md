This page catalogues foods referenced across the Mass Effect series, noting that while most creatures simply consume what nature provides, sapient species turn raw foods into complex dishes ranging from nutritious to chemically incompatible. Levo- and dextro-amino-acid physiologies require separate variants of many items, and eating the wrong chirality can be harmful or lethal.

Listed dishes and ingredients include batarian burgat ("the Other Blue Meat"), served on sticks; Blast-Oh's cereal, made in both dextro and levo brands; angaran fire-roasted spider-beetle, served alive; and haggis, which Kenneth Donnelly claimed Mess Sergeant Gardner could not prepare well. Many entries are Andromeda plant species with tentative classifications, such as amanita jatanum on Elaaden (mildly psychoactive, medicinally useful for enhancing medi-gel), cardacha cthonis on Voeld, keleven celery bred in quarian liveships, and ossilbir, an Alliance military staple vine whose hallucinogenic creeper-mold strain fuelled a black market. Aya fruits like elmohk, paripo, and quilloa are given water and cellulose percentages.

Other items include nutrient paste, manufactured by the Citadel's recycling systems in the Foundations and heavily consumed by quarians and on Aya; Rupert Gardner's calamari gumbo, based on an asari recipe; snacks such as graxen (a dextro snack Vetra favours), popcorn, and gingerbread cookies misinterpreted by the krogan Jair Ulchee; and meats including varren (advertised as skewers and steaks by the Fishdog Food Shack, and made into jerky by krogan), pyjak (favoured by varren and by Omega's population), and fiend steaks that Drack wants on Elaaden. The Protheans allegedly ate the primitive races of their era, boiling or frying hanar and treating salarian body parts as delicacies. The page also covers spices differentiated by chirality, canned products GX5, TH34T, and GX-marked goods, and cut content such as Eden Prime hexa-cows and Wrex ordering buffalo wings on Caleston.
