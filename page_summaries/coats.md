Major Coats was a British Systems Alliance soldier stationed on Earth during the Reaper invasion, serving under the command of Admiral Anderson. Before Hammer's final assault, Coats spent three days holed up inside the Big Ben clock tower in London, firing at anything that moved.

During the final battle on Earth, Coats provided covering fire while Commander Shepard was extracted by a UT-47 Kodiak Drop Shuttle. Admiral Anderson later ordered him to coordinate the artillery strikes that supported Shepard's team as it advanced toward the Citadel beam. After Shepard was hit by Harbinger during the run on the beam, Coats was heard over the radio ordering all surviving soldiers to fall back from the attack.

The aftermath depended on Shepard's choices. If Shepard activated the Crucible, Coats was seen helping rebuild London alongside Gabriella Daniels and Kenneth Donnelly, provided the engineering pair had also survived the war. If Shepard had not brought most of the galaxy's forces into the final battle, Coats was instead shown smoking a cigarette amid London's ruins.

Coats first appeared inside Big Ben in the debut trailer for Mass Effect 3 and again in the "Take Earth Back" trailer, and he referenced his time in the tower in dialogue before Hammer's attack. He was voiced by Nick Boulton, who also voiced Reyes Vidal in Mass Effect: Andromeda and the male Hawke in Dragon Age.
