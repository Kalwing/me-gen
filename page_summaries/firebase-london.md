Multiplayer map page. Firebase London is a Mass Effect 3 multiplayer map included in the Mass Effect 3: Earth DLC, set among ruined buildings in London. Its areas are the Ruined Buildings (starting location), Parking Structure, Pedway (an elevated damaged bridge), Intersection (a central cross-street with parked M-080s), and Shopping Arcade (extraction). The map has several long, narrow sections and overlooks offering strong sniping opportunities, though long-range enemies such as Nemeses and Geth Rocket Troopers benefit equally; the extraction point is relatively easy to hold.
