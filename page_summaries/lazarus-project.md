The Lazarus Project was a Cerberus project whose sole purpose was to bring Commander Shepard back from the dead. After Shepard was killed in a Collector attack that destroyed the SSV Normandy, Cerberus recovered Shepard's corpse with the help of Liara T'Soni. The Illusive Man created and funded the project, pouring in vast resources in the belief that only Shepard could stop the Reaper threat while the Council did nothing.

The project took two years and over 4 billion credits, undertaken by the Lazarus Cell headed by Miranda Lawson with her assistant Wilson. It involved cybernetic implants to reconstruct Shepard's skeleton, rebuilding of skin, and fluids to restart blood flow and organs, along with facial reconstruction; Shepard partially lost prior abilities and talents. The Illusive Man refused to implant a control chip in Shepard's brain, fearing it would alter Shepard's personality. The project succeeded in 2185 when Shepard regained full neurological activity.

Cerberus also created a clone of Shepard, kept secret and intended as a source of spare organs during the revival, though it was never used. In 2186 the disillusioned ex-Cerberus operative Maya Brooks awakened the clone and convinced it to kill and replace the original Shepard to pursue a pro-human agenda; Shepard stopped the clone from escaping the Citadel aboard the Normandy SR-2, and the clone was killed while Brooks was captured or killed. During the assault on Cronos Station near the end of the Reaper War, recovered Lazarus Project records confirmed Lawson's claim that Shepard had been reconstructed without genetic or neural tampering. The project's name references Lazarus of Bethany, raised from the dead in the Bible.
