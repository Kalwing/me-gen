The Citadel Council was the governing body of the Citadel and the ultimate authority in Citadel space, convening in the Citadel Tower to judge violations of Council law, settle disputes between governments, and maintain order, often through its covert intelligence service, the Spectres. It was an executive committee with one representative per member species. It held no official power over independent governments, but its decisions carried great galactic weight, and no single Council race was strong enough to defy the others. The asari were regarded as diplomats, the salarians as intelligence-gatherers, and the turians as the military and peacekeeping force. Species granted a Citadel embassy were associate members bound by the Citadel Conventions and could raise issues but not affect final decisions.

The asari and salarians founded the Council in 500 BCE after independently discovering the Citadel, beginning the Galactic Standard calendar (year 0 GS). Over the next 500 years it absorbed the volus (granted an embassy in 200 BCE for the Unified Banking Act), batarians, elcor, hanar, and quarians. The accidental discovery of the rachni around 1 CE triggered the Rachni Wars; the salarians uplifted the krogan in 80 CE to fight them, and the rachni were declared extinct by 300 CE. Krogan overexpansion later led the Council to order the new Special Tactics and Reconnaissance division to strike krogan infrastructure, starting the century-long Krogan Rebellions, which ended after first contact with the turians, who deployed the salarian-developed genophage; the turians gained full Council membership by 900 CE. Subsequent events included the Geth War of 1895 CE, which cost the quarians their embassy; the 2125 CE slaughter of a Council delegation by the yahg on Parnack; and the 2157 CE Relay 314 Incident with humanity, after which humans received an embassy in 2165 CE.

In Mass Effect, the Council made Shepard the first human Spectre and tasked the Commander with hunting Saren Arterius, while repeatedly dismissing the Reaper threat as a manipulation. During the Battle of the Citadel, Shepard's choice of how to deploy the Alliance fleet determined whether the Council died aboard the Destiny Ascension, replaced by Irissa, Esheel, and Quentius, or survived as Tevos, Valern, and Sparatus; Anderson or Udina became the first human Councilor. By 2186, with the Reaper war underway, Udina represented humanity regardless of prior circumstances; he secretly aided a Cerberus coup, drew a gun on the asari Councilor, and was killed by Shepard or Kaidan Alenko or Ashley Williams. The Council afterward backed construction of the Crucible. Its enforcement arms included the Citadel Fleet, whose flagship was the Destiny Ascension, the Council Demilitarization Enforcement Mission patrolling the Krogan DMZ, the salarian Special Tasks Group, and the Spectres.
