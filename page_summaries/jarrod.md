Jarrod was a krogan warlord who lived during and after the Krogan Rebellions and the subsequent turian counterattack, and the father of Urdnot Wrex. One of the few warlords to survive the war, he carried a lasting grudge against the Citadel races over the devastation of the genophage. His anger blinded him to the demographic consequences of the krogan's drastic decline, and he and the tribal leaders who followed him intended to keep fighting regardless.

Other krogan leaders, including his son Wrex, refused Jarrod's call to war and wanted to focus on breeding to recover the losses. Insulted, Jarrod arranged a Crush, a meeting on neutral ground, with Wrex and the other peace-seeking leaders, setting it at the ancestral burial grounds of the Hollows. There he first tried to sway Wrex to his cause, but Wrex insisted the species' survival depended on abandoning revenge and focusing on repopulation for at least one generation. When it became clear Wrex and his supporters would not be persuaded, Jarrod gave a signal, and his men, hidden in the graves, ambushed Wrex's party. Most of Wrex's allies were quickly slaughtered; Wrex himself fought Jarrod and killed him by driving a dagger into his chest before escaping.

The betrayal by his own father, and Jarrod's willingness to commit a forbidden act of violence at the Hollows to pursue his vendetta, opened Wrex's eyes to the fatalistic mindset that had gripped his species after the genophage. This prompted Wrex to leave his home system and become one of countless krogan drifting the galaxy as mercenaries and bounty hunters.
