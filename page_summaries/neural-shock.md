Neural Shock was a tech power in Mass Effect 2 that paralyzed a single organic enemy with pain, briefly incapacitating it. It fired in a straight line with no projectile, instantly hitting the target under the crosshairs, similar to Overload. The power worked only on unprotected (red bar) organic targets and had no effect on synthetics such as mechs or geth. Bipedal targets spasmed upright for a second or two before falling over, and they took double weapon damage while ragdolled. Ranks 1 through 3 increased the incapacitation duration from five to nine seconds and, from Rank 2, permanently impaired the target's accuracy. It also permanently stopped the health regeneration of enemies like krogan and vorcha.

At Rank 4 the power evolved into either Heavy Neural Shock, which allowed multiple shocks to be chained on a single target for up to twelve seconds of denial, or Neural Shockwave, which gained a three-meter area of effect to disable multiple targets at nine seconds duration. Recharge time was three seconds for Shepard and nine seconds for Mordin.

Neural Shock instantly killed unarmored Husks and stunned Abominations like other organics. As a hitscan power it could penetrate the circular shields deployed by Collector Guardians, though a Guardian's personal barrier had to be removed first. It dealt no health damage but a small amount to protections, and could not destroy or detonate objects. Repeatedly hitting a knocked-out target did not refresh the duration, and enemies recovering from other disables were immune. It was valued as an instant "panic" disable with a fast cooldown, and made a useful one-point bonus power for classes lacking an immediate knockdown, with Neural Shockwave suited to the Reaper IFF mission. It was available to Shepard via Advanced Training and to Mordin Solus as his loyalty power.
