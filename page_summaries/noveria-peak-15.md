"Noveria: Peak 15" was a Mass Effect mission in which Commander Shepard investigated the loss of contact with Peak 15, the remote Binary Helix research facility on Noveria to which Matriarch Benezia had travelled. It followed the prerequisite "Noveria: Leave Port Hanshan" and could be acquired several ways: by talking to a businesswoman and salarian at Port Hanshan Plaza, by asking Lilihierax about rumors at Peak 15, by receiving a warning from Gianna Parasini after she revealed her true allegiance, or, at the latest, by switching on the facility's backup power core in person.

The mission's sole objective was to bring the Peak 15 VI back online, which occurred naturally in the course of the larger "Noveria: Matriarch Benezia" mission. After activating the power core, Shepard took an elevator down to repair the VI, either by spending 100 omni-gel or by manually solving a Tower of Hanoi box puzzle whose rules forbade placing a box beneath a blue box; the wiki provided direct button and column solution sequences. Once repaired, Shepard was raised back up and the VI came online, requesting credentials before granting Secure Access (higher Privileged Access being reserved for Binary Helix executives). The VI explained that two tasks were needed to restore the station: "Noveria: Reconnect Landlines," restoring Mira's full functionality via the roof landlines, and "Noveria: Reactor Repair," bringing the reactor core online by fixing the helium-3 fuel lines. Shepard could tackle either first, with the landlines elevator behind the VI and the reactor down and to the left of the main room.

The mission was written by Chris L'Etoile and was referred to internally as "(Noveria:) Reconstruct Mira."
