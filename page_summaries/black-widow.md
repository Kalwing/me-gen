The Black Widow was a heavy sniper rifle appearing in Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Andromeda. It was developed by the Systems Alliance as a variant of the M-98 Widow, intended to cut that weapon's reload time without sacrificing stopping power. The solution was to increase how many shots the rifle could fire before needing a fresh thermal clip, giving it three rounds per clip and a larger ammunition reserve. It remained heavy for a sniper rifle, but its firepower was said to more than compensate for the encumbrance.

In Mass Effect 3 single-player, the Black Widow could be bought from the Spectre Requisitions terminal in the Citadel Embassies for a base 250,000 credits, halved to 125,000 in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. In multiplayer it was an ultra-rare drop from item packs requiring no expansion. It dealt roughly 25 percent less damage per shot than the standard Widow at the same weight, but fired fast enough to loose three shots in the time a Widow fired and reloaded once, and each shot still roughly matched the M-92 Mantis, enough to kill unprotected infantry outright on any difficulty. It had a slow reload, shallow innate 25cm penetration that pierced Guardian shields and thin cover, and was effective against armor without anti-armor mods, though it was hampered by enemy shield gates on higher difficulties. Disruptor ammunition let it ignore shield gates. Squadmates, especially Garrus, used it well, and it paired with Tactical Cloak, Adrenaline Rush, and turian stability bonuses.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda it was described as a smaller, higher-capacity Widow variant firing three powerful shots per thermal clip, with an Andromeda Initiative scope tuned for varied atmospheres. It was built through the research and development system using omni-gel, beryllium, lithium, and element zero, and had ten upgrade ranks in both single-player and multiplayer. Trivia noted the black widow is a venomous North American spider, and that ME3 multiplayer item cards mistakenly showed the M-98 Widow's image.
