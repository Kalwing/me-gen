Thin page (under ~150 words of real prose). Captain Vorleon was a salarian Eclipse captain in charge of the men who seized an Eldfell-Ashland Energy refinery on the planet Helyme. He was killed in the firefight when Commander Shepard and the squad arrived to liberate the refinery. In combat he used an M-9 Tempest along with tech attacks such as Combat Drone and Incinerate, and had strong shields and armor, moderate health, and Tech Armor to regenerate his shields.
