Priority: Tuchanka followed Priority: Sur'Kesh and required completion of either Tuchanka: Turian Platoon or Attican Traverse: Krogan Team. Eve was the key to curing the genophage, but the cure had to be synthesized and dispersed across Tuchanka from the Shroud, a salarian-built atmospheric tower later used by the turians to spread the original genophage. The salarian STG scientist (Mordin Solus, or Padok Wiks) would travel to the Shroud with Eve.

At Tuchanka the Reapers were using the Shroud to poison the atmosphere, and a Reaper Destroyer guarded the site. The krogan attacked from the ground with turian air support. Dalatrass Linron privately told Shepard that the Shroud had been sabotaged to prevent a cure, offering salarian war support if Shepard let the krogan believe a fake cure had been deployed. Shepard could reveal or conceal this deal at several points.

Shepard's team defended Eve against husks at the sacred Hollows, where Eve rallied the assembled clans; if Wrex led, tension rose with his disloyal brother Wreav. The krogan convoy set out for the Shroud but was halted by a damaged road, forcing Shepard's squad through ancient catacombs of a krogan city abandoned for millennia, where artifacts depicted Kalros, the mother of all thresher maws, and a rachni-like corpse hinted at events at Utukku. After fights against Cannibals, Ravagers, Brutes and Marauders, Wreav's (or Jorgal Thurak's) truck was dragged under by Kalros. Eve proposed using the ancient Maw Hammers to summon Kalros to distract the Reaper. Shepard activated both hammers while dodging the Reaper Destroyer's laser and legs and fighting continuously spawning Brutes; Kalros then dragged the Reaper beneath the surface.

At the damaged Shroud, if Maelon's data had not been saved Eve had died. Mordin (or Wiks) prepared to disperse the cure. Shepard could let him complete it—deploying a counter-sabotage and the real cure, killing Mordin in the explosion—or sabotage it per the Dalatrass. If Wrex and Eve were both dead and the deal unrevealed, Mordin could be persuaded to fake his death and live; otherwise curing the genophage cost him his life. The turians and krogan joined as war assets; sabotage instead granted the Salarian First Fleet. If the cure was faked and Wrex led, he later discovered the deception and died confronting Shepard.
