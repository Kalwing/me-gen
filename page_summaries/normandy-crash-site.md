Normandy Crash Site was a DLC pack for Mass Effect 2, released alongside the game on January 26, 2010, and later included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. It required download or purchase of the Cerberus Network. The pack let Commander Shepard explore the crash site of the SSV Normandy after Alliance Command asked Shepard, as the vessel's last commanding officer, to be first to walk the site, recover the remains of missing crew members and place a monument there. It added one assignment (Normandy Crash Site), one location (the Amada system in the Omega Nebula cluster), and one cabin item (Shepard's N7 helmet).
