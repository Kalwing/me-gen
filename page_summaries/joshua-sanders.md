Joshua Sanders was James Vega's father. After the death of his wife, Josh's relationship with his son became strained and he began using red sand. He lived in a beach house in Solana Beach, California.

In 2176, on the day James enlisted with the Systems Alliance Marines, Josh sent him to San Diego to pick up a package. When James returned home after evading police pursuit, Josh revealed the package contained a supply of red sand and promptly took some. When James said he would no longer help and was leaving, Josh revealed he had known about the enlistment all along and threatened to tell the military about James's actions in San Diego that night so they would reject him. James's uncle Emilio Vega later pointed out that Josh would never admit to the military that he had made a drug deal, and James successfully joined the Alliance.

During the Reaper invasion of 2186, James arrived at the Citadel and sought news of his father and uncle, who were still on Earth. Citadel authorities were unable to contact them.
