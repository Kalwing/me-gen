"Silean Nebula: Rings of Alune" was a fetch mission in Mass Effect 3. An asari consultant at Huerta Memorial Hospital on the Citadel needed the Rings of Alune, an asari artifact, recovered from the Silean Nebula. An alternative journal entry framed it as the artifact already recovered and needing to be matched with a user on the Citadel.

The mission required Priority: The Citadel II and Priority: Rannoch. It could be acquired by overhearing the asari consultant's comm conversation with a salarian near the hospital elevator, or from a Search and Rescue operation in the Silean Nebula, in which case it was labeled "Citadel: Rings of Alune." The artifact was on the planet Nevos in the Teyolia system, which only became available after Priority: Rannoch, leaving a gap of at least four Priority missions between acquisition and completion. Shepard scanned Nevos and returned the artifact to the asari, who needed it to declare a "cultural crisis" and secure more asari support for the hospitals. Completion granted 5 Reputation, 15,000 credits, and an update to the Citadel Defense Force War Asset.
