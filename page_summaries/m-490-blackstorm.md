The M-490 Blackstorm Singularity Projector was a heavy weapon in Mass Effect 2, colloquially called the "black hole gun." It encased particles of matter in a high-powered mass-increasing field, elevating them to near-infinite mass to create a gravitational singularity that drew nearby enemies and objects inward and tore them apart, before the field destabilized and returned to normal mass with explosive results. It was included in the Terminus Weapon and Armor DLC and, in the Legendary Edition, could be purchased on Omega at Harrot's Emporium for 5,000 credits.

The weapon fired an orange Singularity-like projectile that traveled in the aimed direction, disrupting and staggering enemies until it detonated about five seconds after firing, dealing massive damage and flinging targets across a large area. Only unprotected enemies were lifted into the air, and unlike the biotic Singularity power its vortex duration was fixed, making it useful for tying up tough foes like Scions. The trigger had to be held to charge for three seconds. Its detonation caused a brief time-dilation effect, and both its vortex and blast could damage enemies through cover and walls, making it one of only two Mass Effect 2 weapons (with the M-920 Cain) able to do so. It caused no friendly fire and was especially effective against clustered Husks, including during the final battle of Collector Base: The Long Walk. Enemies killed by it disintegrated, leaving no corpse.
