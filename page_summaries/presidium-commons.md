The Presidium Commons was a location in the Presidium on the Citadel featured in Mass Effect 3. It contained shops, living quarters, a bank, Apollo's Cafe and the Meridian Place Market, with a C-Sec outpost overseeing security and one of the overhead support struts housing Huerta Memorial Hospital on the same side as Apollo's Cafe. The living-quarters hallway carried an advertisement for the new Blasto movie, Blasto 6: Partners in Crime.

The area served as a social hub where Shepard could meet numerous characters. Before the Cerberus attack on the Citadel, Barla Von could be found at the bank, Liara T'Soni and Matriarch Aethyta at Apollo's Cafe, EDI and Joker at Meridian Place Market, and Rupe Elkoss and General Septimus Oraka in the courtyard. Miranda Lawson could be found in the apartments after her third correspondence; the volus ambassador Din Korlack and Zaeed Massani appeared in an apartment during the investigation of a Cerberus mole. Narl (one of Aria's men), the black-market dealer Kannik and the asari merchant Ereba tied into Aria's mercenary-recruitment missions. Tali'Zorah appeared in the courtyard after the quarians' arrival, arguing with a turian diplomat about medical supplies or, if she survived Rannoch, coordinating fleet actions. Kaidan Alenko or Ashley Williams, if alive, could be met at Apollo's Cafe.

The Commons incurred battle damage during the Cerberus attack on the Citadel, leaving smoke, bullet holes and debris, with increased C-Sec presence and ongoing cleanup afterward. Shops included Kanala Exports, Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies, Aegohr Munitions, Cipritine Armory, Kassa Fabrication and Nos Astra Sporting Goods. With Leviathan or Citadel DLC installed, a rapid-transit panel allowed direct travel to the Normandy, Purgatory Bar or Silversun Strip. The location anchored many side missions, including Aria's Blood Pack, Blue Suns and Eclipse recruitment tasks, Citadel: Barla Von, Citadel: Volus Ambassador and numerous scanning-reward errands.
