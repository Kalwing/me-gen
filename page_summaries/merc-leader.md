The Merc Leader was an Eclipse mercenary encountered during Miranda Lawson's loyalty mission in Mass Effect 2, taking orders from Captain Enyala. After Miranda met her contact on Illium and rode with Commander Shepard into the cargo areas, A-61 Mantis Gunships led by the Merc Leader dropped Eclipse troops who opened fire on the car. Once he confirmed Miranda was aboard, he ordered his men to hold fire and confronted her, explaining he had been ordered to give her one chance to walk away. Shepard could snap his neck while Miranda shot an overhead fuel container to kill his mercs, or let him keep talking, in which case Miranda killed him and a firefight followed. Functionally an Eclipse Vanguard, he carried an M-15 Vindicator, used biotic powers, and had Tech Armor with moderate barriers and health.
