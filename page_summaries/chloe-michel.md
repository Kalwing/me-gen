Dr. Chloe Michel ran the Med Clinic in the Upper Wards of the Citadel. The daughter of two Alliance military medics, she kept the family's medical tradition but chose civilian practice; before opening her own clinic she was fired from another practice for giving out free medical supplies. In Mass Effect: Homeworlds, a badly wounded Tali'Zorah nar Rayya collapsed in her clinic and, while being treated, told Michel how she had obtained data linking Saren Arterius to the geth and been pursued to the Citadel by Saren's agents. A volus who overheard revealed he was an information broker with a line to the Shadow Broker, and Michel encouraged Tali to trust him. In Mass Effect, Commander Shepard came to the clinic seeking Garrus Vakarian and found Michel threatened by Fist's thugs; Garrus and the squad drove them off. Michel explained she had treated a quarian with evidence against Saren over the Eden Prime attack and had put the quarian in touch with Fist, and was surprised to learn Fist had betrayed the Shadow Broker to work for Saren. Later Shepard found Michel receiving a blackmail demand for medical supplies from someone exploiting her past firing; exposure could cost her her licence and close the clinic. Shepard could deliver the supplies to a merchant named Morlan, where a krogan collected them for a man called Banes. Michel identified him as Armistan Banes, a former colleague last known to be on an Alliance military contract; Captain Anderson revealed Banes was dead and that the crew who recovered his body had gone missing. Grateful, Michel offered discounts and the best resale prices of any Citadel merchant, though her stock was limited and did not restock. In Mass Effect 2, if the blackmail had been resolved, she emailed Shepard expressing relief at the Commander's survival and asking after Garrus. By 2186 she was the human medical lead at Huerta Memorial Hospital and oversaw treatment of the Virmire survivor, Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko, after Mars. Shepard could recruit her as the Normandy's doctor, though if Dr. Chakwas had survived the suicide mission Shepard first had to deny Chakwas's request and then convince Michel. Aboard the Normandy she noted Shepard's Cerberus cybernetics, brokered a medical-supply trade with the refugee leader Tactus after the Cerberus attack on the Citadel, and frequently talked with Engineer Greg Adams, including about her history with Banes. Like Chakwas, she argued that synthetics were not a valid form of life. If Garrus survived the suicide mission, she brought him turian chocolates, recalling that he had saved her life in the Wards in 2183. If Shepard never interacted with her, she disappeared from Huerta after the Cerberus attack. Her parents were in Geneva on Earth when the Reapers arrived, and she had a brother on the Citadel. During this period Mordin Solus or Padok Wiks treated Eve in the med-bay.
