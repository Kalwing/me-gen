The Personal Apartment was a location on the Citadel, in Tiberius Towers on the Silversun Strip, introduced in the Citadel DLC for Mass Effect 3. It originally belonged to Admiral David Anderson, who planned to retire there with Kahlee Sanders. While the Normandy SR-2 was docked at the Citadel for repairs, Anderson gave the apartment to Commander Shepard as a place to relax during shore leave. It included a weapon bench, armor locker, private terminal, and a furnishings catalog for items bought from Home Spun on the Strip. Scattered through the apartment were audio recordings of parts of Anderson's biography, including an unfinished interview with reporter Khalisah al-Jilani.

The apartment had two floors: a ground floor with a large seating area, fireplace, bar, piano, kitchen with a Normandy SR-2 model, a bedroom, and a smaller living room holding the Private Terminal and Catalog; and a first floor with an art gallery, a second seating area, Shepard's large bedroom with the armor locker and weapon bench, an en-suite bathroom with a hot tub, and a third bedroom. Squadmates and other characters could be summoned to the apartment or invited out onto the Strip after messages arrived at the private terminal; some characters had one interaction on the Strip and a second in the apartment, in fixed order.

Accessories accumulated through play: a plush volus doll and stereo music tracks won from the Claw Game at Castle Arcade, a trophy left by Samantha Traynor after a visit, a photo of Thane Krios left on the piano after Kolyat's visit, and Glyph playing Liara's song on the piano. The Anderson biography recordings covered his London childhood (born June 8, 2137), his enlistment, service in the First Contact War, N7 training, his first meeting with a salarian Spectre, cross-species training on Palaven under Bartus Aurix, his failed marriage, the design of the Normandy SR-1, and his decision to release the Normandy to Shepard before the Battle of the Citadel. Associated missions included Citadel: Shore Leave, Citadel: Identity Theft, and Citadel: Party.
