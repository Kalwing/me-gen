Citadel: Cerberus Automated Turret Schematics was a Mass Effect 3 mission with prerequisites Priority: The Citadel II and Tuchanka: Bomb. After the Cerberus attack on the Citadel, a C-Sec officer standing beside a volus on a Presidium Commons balcony, just outside the vid-wall hallway, needed a way to detect or deactivate Cerberus automated turrets that were threatening civilians. Eavesdropping on him activated the mission. It could also be triggered by acquiring the Turret Control Schematics during the bomb-defusal mission on Tuchanka, and an alternative journal entry framed the schematics as recovered from Tuchanka and needing someone on the Citadel who could use them.

The schematics were in crates in an area where two Assault Troopers fled instead of fighting and a Combat Engineer set up a turret, past the large open area where Shepard's squad first saw the turian bomb. They had to be taken before meeting Lieutenant Victus, since the next area was a point of no return. If not retrieved there, they could be purchased at the Spectre Requisitions terminal in the Citadel Embassies for 1,000 credits. Handing the schematics to the officer gave 5 Reputation, 30 experience, 1,000 credits, and an update to the Citadel Defense Force War Asset.
