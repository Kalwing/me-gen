The Collector SMG was a submachine gun in Mass Effect 3, a hybrid of mechanical and organic parts described as in places more advanced than the Collector assault rifle. It vented heat automatically rather than requiring thermal clips and, when wielded by a Collector, extended a neural link to the skin at the wrist. Its ammunition was effective against armor, and its low recoil made it good for sustained suppression. It was exclusive to multiplayer, randomly rewarded from item packs as an ultra-rare weapon, and required the Mass Effect 3: Retaliation DLC.

Its self-cooling system resembled that of the Particle Rifle, M-7 Lancer, and Collector Sniper Rifle, but it began its cooldown the instant firing stopped, at a slower rate, regenerating 30% of its maximum clip size per second. Base magazine size ran from 30 at level I to 40 at level X, and the SMG Magazine Upgrade was strongly recommended to offset the small magazine. The weapon received a 50% damage bonus against armored targets, letting it barely surpass the damage reduction such targets received on Gold and Platinum difficulty, especially when paired with the High-Velocity Barrel mod or Armor-Piercing Rounds. It suited power-focused classes because its magazine recharged during ability use, and it was commonly used as the lightest effective anti-armor weapon for such builds. It was one of the three heaviest SMGs alongside the N7 Hurricane and Blood Pack Punisher, its regenerating clip being its main advantage over both.
