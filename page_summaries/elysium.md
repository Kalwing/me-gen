Elysium was the second planet of the Vetus System in the Petra Nebula, a human colony often called an "alpine paradise" for its low gravity, tolerable atmospheric pressure, and mild climate. Founded in 2160, it was humanity's oldest colony in the Skyllian Verge, only a few hours from Sidon, and its capital city was Illyria. Positioned at the nexus of several primary and secondary mass relays, it quickly became a major hub for travel and commerce, growing to several million inhabitants by 2165, nearly half of them non-human, which necessitated tight security and extensive screening.

Alliance hero Jon Grissom retired to Elysium in 2160, later becoming involved in an investigation into an attack on an Alliance research station. The Jon Grissom Academy space station was commissioned in his honor in 2176, though he refused all invitations and never visited it. He remained on Elysium until his death in 2186 at age 75.

In 2176 the colony was attacked by a large force of pirates and mercenaries under Elanos Haliat in the Skyllian Blitz. The Alliance repulsed the assault, prompting increased patrols throughout the Terminus Systems. The frigate SSV Agincourt was among the ships present; Navigator Pressly earned his commission after the Blitz. Haliat could be encountered by Shepard in 2183. If Shepard had the War Hero background, the Commander was on shore leave during the Blitz and rallied a resistance force that held off the enemy ground assault until reinforcements arrived; a memorial plaza was later built, becoming a popular wedding venue in 2185 with proceeds directed by Admiral Hackett to Alliance veterans' funds.

The Collectors did not attack Elysium in 2185, but a security advisory about the Collector threat canceled all traffic and trade from Illium, hampering the colony's economy. Elysium's airspace was defended by mobile planetary defense cannons, its starship complement being light. Materiel and volunteers from Elysium formed part of the Alliance Second Fleet, stationed near Arcturus before it was destroyed by the Reapers.
