Most weapons in the Mass Effect universe were micro-scaled mass accelerators that used mass-reducing fields and magnetic force to propel miniature slugs to lethal speeds, laden with features from targeting auto-assists to projectile shavers that generated thousands of rounds from a small internal metal block. This article tracked how personal weaponry and its systems changed across the series.

In Mass Effect, a weapon shaved a sand-grain-sized projectile from a dense metal block and launched it at supersonic velocity by reducing its mass in a mass effect field, so ammunition was never a concern but heat management was, forcing operators to pause when a weapon overheated. Weapons fell into four categories, assault rifles, pistols, shotguns, and sniper rifles, and had attributes including Damage, Shots before Overheat, Accuracy Rating, and a Level (I-X) denoting quality. Weapons of level I-VI accepted one weapon mod and one ammunition mod, and level VII and above accepted two weapon mods; Geth Armory weapons accepted none. Manufacturers included Ariake Technologies, Armax Arsenal, Devlon Industries, Elanus Risk Control Services, Elkoss Combine, Geth Armory, Hahne-Kedar, Haliat Armory, Kassa Fabrication, Rosenkov Materials, and Spectre - Master Gear. Legendary Edition added headshot bonus damage and manufacturer-specific firing modes (fully automatic, burst, semi-automatic, bolt action).

In Mass Effect 2, detachable heat sinks called thermal clips, adopted first by the geth, replaced heat dispersal; a weapon without a clip could not fire, so clips had to be scavenged from battlefields. Weapon types expanded to six, adding submachine guns, heavy pistols, and heavy weapons (Shepard only, powered by power cells). Class determined which weapons could be carried. Upgrades were bought through research and applied across a whole weapon type. Ammo powers replaced fixed ammo upgrades: Armor-Piercing, Cryo, Disruptor, Incendiary, Shredder, and Warp Ammo, each with a rank 4 squad-sharing evolution. Squadmates with their own ammo powers were Garrus Vakarian, Grunt, Jack, Jacob Taylor, Thane Krios, and Zaeed Massani.

In Mass Effect 3, any class could carry up to five weapons, one per type, subject to a weight limit that affected power recharge speed. Weapon levels returned but were upgraded with credits at the Shuttle Bay terminal, up to V on a first playthrough and X in New Game Plus. Weapon mods returned with two slots per weapon. Ammo powers (Armor-Piercing, Cryo, Disruptor, Incendiary, Warp) no longer had recharge times. Squadmates with ammo powers were Ashley Williams, James Vega, Garrus Vakarian, and Liara T'Soni. Many weapons had special attributes: bonus damage to specific protections, innate piercing (Javelin, N7 Typhoon, M-98 Widow, Black Widow, Executioner Pistol, N7 Crusader), chargeable shots, explosive rounds, and self-cooling (Particle Rifle, M-7 Lancer). Reload cancelling could shorten thermal clip reloads. Mass Effect: Andromeda used assault rifles, shotguns, sniper rifles, pistols (including SMGs), and melee weapons, customized with Mods and crafted-in Augmentations. Concept art books noted guns were given twin barrels chiefly for a distinctive look.
