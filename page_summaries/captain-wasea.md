Captain Wasea was an asari commando and the leader of an Eclipse cadre on Illium in Mass Effect 2. Commander Shepard encountered her in an Eclipse hideout while helping the justicar Samara track down the name of a ship the gang had used to smuggle Samara's target off world, a person Wasea called a "filthy creature." Annoyed at the intrusion, Wasea set down her drink and ranted about the disruption Shepard and the justicar had caused to her operations, then relished the chance to vent her frustration in a fight. She could cryptically warn Shepard that helping the justicar meant involving themselves in something terrifying. She was killed in the following firefight. Under her leadership the Eclipse had experimented on the volus Niftu Cal, injecting him with so many drugs that he began exhibiting biotic powers; if Shepard encouraged Niftu to fight her, his biotic attack fizzled out in her face and her much stronger counter sent him flying before she faced Shepard's squad.

In combat Wasea was armed with an M-300 Claymore heavy shotgun and used Warp, and she flung crates of the biotic-enhancing toxin Minagen around the room to obscure visibility and control the battlefield. She had strong armor and biotic barriers with moderate health and could completely restore her barriers once, but only after her armor bar was also depleted. Despite her rank she was no harder to fight than an average Eclipse Commando, and killing her quickly stopped further Eclipse reinforcements from spawning and kept the battle from moving into the back room; a Flashbang Grenade could disable her biotics for about 40 seconds. Captain Wasea was voiced by Gwendoline Yeo, and unlike other Eclipse members her armor bore no Eclipse logos and was colored dark blue and red instead of yellow and black.
