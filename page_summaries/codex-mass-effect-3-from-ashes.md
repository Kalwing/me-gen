This Mass Effect 3 "From Ashes" DLC Codex covered Eden Prime and the Prothean beacon. Settlers reached Eden Prime through the new Charon relay in 2151; its stable climate and compatible biology made the colony almost immediately viable, and over decades several million humans immigrated, building a primarily agrarian society that became self-sufficient and then exported goods, chief among them rare plants grown from Earth's heritage seed libraries. In 2183 the rogue Spectre Saren Arterius and his geth attacked Eden Prime, and soon afterward the Citadel was attacked and the neighboring world Terra Nova was threatened with destruction. Rather than evacuate, Eden Prime's colonists voted against building evacuation ships and voted to ease restrictions on Terra Nova refugees, swelling the colony's population and cementing its status as a symbol of humanity's future.

The Prothean beacon uncovered on Eden Prime in 2183 was triggered when Commander Shepard's squad came near it while repelling the geth; it sent Shepard jumbled sounds and images warning of the Reaper invasion, then overloaded and violently exploded. The Council dismissed the vision as subjective, but its Committee on Paleotechnology analyzed the fragments and found the mechanism was not telepathic. The beacon used a mass effect field to hold its subject immobile, scanned the structure of the target's optic and auditory nerves and memory-related brain regions, then stimulated them with pinpoint electromagnetic and other irradiative energy to recreate images and sound, similar in principle to a "simstim" but vastly more comprehensive and directed at memory rather than the senses. The Normandy's medical officer noted it delivered more information in seconds than a simstim could in hours, to a species whose physiology was unknown to its designers.
