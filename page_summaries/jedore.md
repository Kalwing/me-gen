Jedore was a Blue Suns commander and a financial contributor to Okeer's krogan cloning project. She was tasked with evaluating the cloned krogan's combat effectiveness and determining whether they were suitable as shock troops. She was disappointed by the results, finding the clones crazed and uncontrollable and near useless as frontline troops. Okeer dismissed her complaints, attributing her lack of control over the krogan to her own inability to command.

Concluding that Okeer and his krogan were not worth the time and money invested, Jedore attempted to destroy both him and his creations. She was killed by Commander Shepard, who intervened on Okeer's dying wish to save the still-unawakened tank-bred krogan later named Grunt. Jedore was described as unstable, with a massive superiority complex and a hair-trigger temper; her ranting over the comm during Shepard's infiltration was severe enough that her own subordinates demanded she stop clogging the channel. In combat she carried an ML-77 Missile Launcher, had very strong shields, armor, and health, and used Tech Armor like other Blue Suns commanders. During the fight she activated four dormant Krogan Berserkers from their tanks one at a time and was supported by a YMIR Mech, and was highly susceptible to Stasis.
