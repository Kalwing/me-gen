"Dossier: The Warlord" was a Mass Effect 2 recruitment mission, available after Freedom's Progress as part of the Illusive Man's first set of dossiers. Dr. Okeer was described as a brilliant, brutal krogan warlord who had fought in the Krogan Rebellions, become obsessed with saving the krogan from the genophage, and was believed to have contacted the Collectors for technology to that end. He was operating from a Blue Suns camp on Korlus commanded by Jedore.

Fighting through Blue Suns forces (Troopers, Legionnaires, Centurions, and rocket-firing Heavies), Shepard's squad encountered a "Tank-Grown Krogan," only seven days old, one of Okeer's many rejected creations. He opened a path into the research base but would not accompany the squad. Inside, if Shepard had spared Rana Thanoptis on Virmire, she appeared and fled, saying she knew how Shepard worked. Okeer revealed he had been mass-producing and flash-indoctrinating tank-bred krogan in search of the "perfect" specimen, supplying his rejects as an army for Jedore, who could not command them. He wanted to bargain his knowledge of the Collectors for escape. Jedore then announced over the PA that Okeer had released the krogan without authorization and began pumping krogan-killing gas into the facility. Okeer chose to stay behind to protect his legacy, the perfect krogan in his tank.

Shepard fought Jedore, who wielded a rocket launcher and a YMIR Mech while tank-bred krogan broke loose around her, and killed her. Returning to the lab, Shepard found Okeer already dead from the toxins; his final recorded words asked Shepard to protect his legacy and admitted he had not known what the Collectors planned for the human colonists. The tank was recovered to the Normandy. Opening it (in the separate mission "Dr. Okeer's Legacy") released Grunt, who slammed Shepard against a wall before being persuaded or forced to back down; impressed by Shepard's strength, he joined the crew. Rewards included platinum and Sniper Rifle Damage and Krogan Vitality upgrades. If Grunt was never released he would not appear in Mass Effect 3; if released and surviving the suicide mission, he later led a krogan commando unit investigating a resurgent rachni. The mission was written by Lukas Kristjanson after a first pass by Brian Kindregan.
