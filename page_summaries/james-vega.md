Lieutenant James Vega was a human Systems Alliance Marine and a member of Commander Shepard's squad in 2186. An experienced soldier but unfamiliar with galactic politics and the criminal underworld, he was written primarily by Mac Walters and intended to reflect a new player's perspective. He was originally named James Sanders before the name was changed.

Per Mass Effect: Homeworlds, Vega joined the Marines at Camp Pendleton in 2176 with the encouragement of his uncle Emilio Vega, despite his father Joshua "Josh" Sanders trying to trap him into a life as an errand boy through a coerced drug run in San Diego. Emilio convinced James that only he could choose how to live.

In Mass Effect: Paragon Lost, Vega served as second-in-command of Delta Squad on the colony Fehl Prime under Captain Toni. After defeating a Blood Pack siege in single combat with the krogan Archuk, Delta Squad was assigned as colony security. Two years later the Collectors attacked; with Toni incapacitated, Vega took command. His decision to fire the colony defense cannons at half power failed, alerting the Collector ship. Betrayed by the undercover Cerberus/Collector agent Messner, and unable to save both the asari anthropologist Treeya (who carried vital intel) and the colonists, Vega chose to save Treeya and the intel, sacrificing the colony. He was commended and promoted but felt he was no hero. He later learned Shepard had destroyed the Collectors without the intel, meaning the colony died for nothing. This guilt drove him to reckless behavior. In Mass Effect: Conviction, Anderson recruited him from Omega to guard the Alliance brig holding the surrendered Shepard.

In Mass Effect 3, Vega escorted Shepard to the defense committee hearing interrupted by the Reaper attack on Earth, then helped extract Shepard aboard the Normandy. On Mars he crashed his shuttle into Dr. Eva Coré's to stop her escaping with the Prothean data. He served as squadmate throughout the war, based in the Shuttle Bay bantering with his friend Steve Cortez, doing pull-ups, and sparring with Shepard, to whom he confided the Fehl Prime story. He nicknamed people ("Loco" for male Shepard, "Lola" for female, "Scars" for Garrus, "Sparks" for Tali, "Esteban" for Cortez). He noted his father's surname was Sanders but that he was unrelated to Kahlee Sanders. Offered N7 training and doubtful he deserved it, he ultimately accepted, celebrating with a back tattoo. A female Shepard with no prior commitments could romance him. If Shepard could not rally enough forces, Vega could be killed by Harbinger during the final push to the conduit; otherwise he survived and honored Shepard at the Memorial Wall. He was voiced by Freddie Prinze, Jr.
