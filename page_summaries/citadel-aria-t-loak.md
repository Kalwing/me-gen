Citadel: Aria T'Loak was the preamble mission for the Mass Effect 3: Omega DLC, requiring that DLC and completion of Priority: Palaven. Aria T'Loak sent Shepard two emails: the first, received after the primarch's retrieval from Menae, indicated the DLC was installed and invited Shepard to meet her at the Purgatory bar on the Citadel; the second, sent after Shepard had spoken with her at Purgatory at least once, told Shepard to come to Dock 42, where a batarian named Bray would be waiting. Reading the second email activated the mission in the Journal.

At Dock 42 Shepard met Bray, one of Aria's batarian guards, and boarded a skycar with Aria for a joyride around the Presidium, kept private because of the sensitive nature of the plan. Aria explained her intention to retake Omega from Cerberus: she had assembled a fleet to break through the Cerberus ships guarding the station, but expected the real fighting to happen on foot through the station. She named the leader of the occupation as Oleg Petrovsky, admitted she had little intelligence on enemy ground forces, but claimed to know exploitable secrets about Omega. She offered Shepard troops and supplies for the Reaper war in exchange for help retaking Omega, on the condition that Shepard come alone, without squadmates, while her forces engaged the bulk of the enemy elsewhere. No morality points were awarded. The skycar dropped Shepard and Bray at a docking bay; Aria left her fleet's coordinates with Bray, urged secrecy while on the Citadel, and Shepard was automatically returned to the Normandy SR-2. The mission was written by Neil Pollner.
