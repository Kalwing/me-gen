Clan Weyrloc was a large krogan tribe on Tuchanka, tied to the Blood Pack because the Blood Pack's founder had been a chief of the clan. In 2185 the clan was based in a hospital, awaiting results from a project to cure the genophage. Clan Weyrloc planned to expand its numbers to overcome Clan Urdnot and then launch a war against the rest of the galaxy. Commander Shepard's team fought through clan members, killing Chief Weyrloc Guld, while making their way to rescue Maelon, Mordin Solus's student. Maelon was in fact helping Weyrloc willingly, believing the clan was willing to do whatever was necessary to cure the genophage, even experimenting on humans and on their own kinsmen. After Maelon's research was stopped, Clan Urdnot largely absorbed the remnants of Clan Weyrloc if Urdnot Wrex led it, or wiped them out if Urdnot Wreav led it. Despite the death of Weyrloc Guld and most of his lieutenants and the further losses to Clan Urdnot, the clan survived as a shadow of its former glory. If Maelon had not been killed by Mordin, the remaining clan members blamed him for their downfall and put a bounty on his head, forcing him into hiding; after the genophage was cured, Maelon messaged Shepard hoping the clan would now leave him alone. Known members included Chief Weyrloc Guld, the Weyrloc Clanguard, and the Weyrloc Clanspeaker. A cut quest, Tuchanka: Female Quest, would have tied the clan to raids that killed Clan Urdnot females, prompting Clan Urdnot retaliation.
