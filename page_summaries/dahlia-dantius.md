Dahlia Dantius was the sister of the asari diplomat Nassana Dantius and the black sheep of a prominent family. She was a slaver and the leader of a band of pirates in the Artemis Tau cluster whose members included humans, turians, salarians, and krogan, operating from a stronghold on Sharjila. Though not an asari commando, she was a formidable biotic and a well-trained combatant. Knowing her sister Nassana, a diplomatic emissary on the Citadel, would be heavily monitored or demoted if Dahlia's activities were exposed, Dahlia blackmailed Nassana and extracted a large sum of credits. Nassana retaliated by contacting Commander Shepard, falsely claiming Dahlia was an innocent victim kidnapped by mercenaries and directing the Commander to Dahlia's base. Dahlia rallied her men to defend the stronghold but was killed in the assault. Because her identity was unknown during the fight, she was labeled "Asari Slaver." Alternatively, Shepard could learn the truth about both sisters only afterward, with Nassana still willing to reward the Commander. In combat she carried a pistol and a rarely used assault rifle, tended to descend from an upper level accompanied by a human sniper, and was vulnerable to crowd-control powers.
