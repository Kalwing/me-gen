The Presidium was the gleaming central ring of the Citadel, roughly 10km wide, housing the government offices of the Citadel Council and the embassies of every species represented on the station. While the Wards held most of the Citadel's population and were livelier, the Presidium was the province of Citadel space's most elite and powerful individuals. Its interior was landscaped to resemble lush parkland on a 'G'-type planet under the Systems Alliance's Morgan-Keenan classification, complete with a large freshwater lake, fields, trees, clouds, artificial sunlight and gentle breezes. It ran a brief day-night cycle with six hours of darkness. The lakes served as reservoirs independent of the Wards' water tanks, and the water was purified to prevent bacteria from one species fatally infecting another due to differing amino acid structures.

Buildings were built along the curving walls of the ring and meant to blend unobtrusively into the surroundings. At the heart stood the immense Citadel Tower, where the Council met and received ambassadors. Key locations included the human, elcor and volus Embassies, the Embassy Bar, Executor Pallin's office, the Consort's Chambers, the Financial District (with Barla Von's office and the Emporium), docking bays including Bay D24, Huerta Memorial Hospital, Dr. Bryson's Lab and Presidium Commons. Avina information terminals were placed at key points, and a Citadel Rapid Transit terminal sat by the Wards Access corridor alleyway.

Points of interest included the Relay Monument, a scale reconstruction of a mass relay believed to be Prothean art, near the Citadel Tower entrance, and the Krogan Monument near the Emporium, a huge statue commissioned to commemorate the krogan's efforts in the Rachni Wars. Delan, a hanar merchant at the Emporium, offered insight into his people. The name derives from the Latin praesidium, meaning fort or garrison; the design was inspired by a 1976 artist's conception of a Stanford Torus space habitat.
