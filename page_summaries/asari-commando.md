Asari Commandos, also called asari huntresses, were powerful asari biotics forming the elite of the asari military. They were usually drawn from the maiden stage of asari life and typically fought in small groups using guerrilla tactics. Because the asari were regarded as the most powerful species in the contemporary Milky Way, most commando operations involved fighting other asari: terrorists, cults, rogue Ardat-Yakshi, and Citadel secessionists.

Some asari commandos were among the first individuals chosen as Spectres. Commandos also served as bodyguards to prominent asari, such as Matriarch Benezia, and undertook mercenary work; Nassana Dantius kept Eclipse commandos stationed at her towers, and Urdnot Wrex recalled fighting a commando named Aleena. Commandos wore distinctive brown uniforms and were usually armed with shotguns or assault rifles. They were considered among the finest individual warriors in the galaxy, comparable to krogan Battlemasters. Huntress activities were typically sealed for five millennia, though a commando could choose to share her experiences at her own discretion.

Like many military units, commandos practiced set combat stances. One classic shooting stance was the Karita, which Cora Harper said was not suited to everyone; she remarked that a krogan attempting it would look like a whale trying to dance ballet, while Nakmor Drack countered that it seemed better than a salarian tied to a ceiling fan. Notable commandos included Aleena, Vederia Damali, Captain Enyala, Cora Harper (a human who trained and served with commando units), Alestia Iallis, Shiala, Sarissa Theris, Nisira T'Kosh (commanding officer of Talein's Daughters), Aria T'Loak, Captain Wasea, and the asari who served as translator for the Rachni Queen.
