The Crucible was an ancient, highly complex superweapon device intended to stop the Reapers. Its design was contributed to by countless species across millions of years and many extinction cycles, but no species deployed it before being wiped out. The Reapers believed all traces of the design were destroyed, yet the schematics were continuously preserved and passed between cycles. The Protheans were the latest species to build it, but before they could deploy it, infighting broke out between those who wanted to use it to destroy the Reapers and a faction, later found to be indoctrinated, who wanted to control them. The schematics survived in a Prothean archive on Mars for 50,000 years.

In 2186, after recovering encryption keys from a Prothean shrine on Kahje, Liara T'Soni traveled to the Mars Archive and recognized the schematics' significance. The Illusive Man also sought them and sent the infiltration unit Dr. Eva Coré to steal the data, wiping it from the archives. After a chase involving Shepard and Liara, James Vega rammed Eva's shuttle; Shepard disabled her with the plans intact, though Shepard's Virmire survivor squadmate was gravely injured. The schematics were handed to the Systems Alliance, which began a massive galaxy-wide construction effort on Admiral Hackett's authority after the fall of Earth.

Scientists determined the device exploited mass relay technology but could not fully explain its function. It required a final component, the Catalyst, revealed by the Prothean VI Vendetta to be the Citadel itself, which could massively amplify the Crucible's energy. The Illusive Man fled to the Citadel and alerted the Reapers, who seized it and moved it into orbit around Earth. Hackett committed the galaxy's combined fleets to Sol, with the Crucible entering the battle behind a token defense force.

Shepard boarded the Citadel, opened it for docking with the Crucible, and met the Catalyst, which manifested as the boy Shepard saw die during the escape from Earth. The Catalyst described the Crucible as a crude but effective, adaptive power source, and offered Shepard choices: destroy the Reapers and all synthetics (red energy); control the Reapers at the cost of Shepard's physical form (blue energy); or synthesize organic and synthetic life (green energy). Shepard could also refuse, leaving the cycle to continue. Each firing option transmitted energy through the Charon Relay and the mass relay network, damaging the relays and, in most cases, the Citadel.
