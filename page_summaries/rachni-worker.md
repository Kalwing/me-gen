Rachni Workers were tiny green rachni, likened to aphids, that usually formed a first wave ahead of Rachni Soldiers. Acting in swarms, they rushed hostiles and detonated in suicide attacks that dealt heavy toxic damage and ignored shields. Their name suggests they filled the worker niche common to hive species, tending the queen's needs. Combat guidance on the page noted they were fragile and could be made to explode at a distance with a single shot or a tech proximity mine, and that killing them at close range spread toxic damage to nearby allies.

The page connects Workers to later events. If the Rachni Queen was spared on Noveria, Shepard could meet an asari on Illium whose crashed ship had been repaired by Workers, reportedly running better than before. In Mass Effect 3, Swarmers, miniature Reaper units, were suspected to be derived from Workers, spawned either by the original Noveria queen or an artificial Breeder. If Shepard rescued the queen or Breeder from Reaper captivity, she would use her Workers to help build the Crucible; however, the Breeder proved treacherous and directed her spawn to sabotage the work, causing numerous casualties before the situation was contained.
