This page collects the lines Harbinger speaks through a possessed Collector during combat in Mass Effect 2, grouped into general taunts, remarks about Shepard, assessments of Shepard's squadmates by species, self-declarations, pain lines, lines about the dead, "destiny" lines, and lines spoken while taking or releasing control of a Collector body. The quotes emphasize Harbinger's contempt for organic life, its claim to immortality, and the Reapers' stated purpose of "perfecting" and harvesting the galaxy.

Representative lines:

Harbinger, on taking over a Collector: "Assuming direct control."

Harbinger, taunting Shepard in combat: "I am the Harbinger of your perfection."

Harbinger, dismissing organic resistance: "You are bacteria."

Harbinger, on the Collector body it possesses: "This body does not matter. The flesh is a machine."

Harbinger, ordering Collectors to spare Shepard: "Preserve Shepard's body if possible."

Harbinger, appraising a krogan squadmate: "Krogan; sterilised race, potential wasted."

Harbinger, appraising a human squadmate: "Human; viable possibility, impressive genetic malleability."

Harbinger, when its possessed Collector is killed: "Releasing this form. We do not die. I will find you again."

Harbinger, when defeated: "This changes nothing, Shepard. I am limitless. You are bacteria."
