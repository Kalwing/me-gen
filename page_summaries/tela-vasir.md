Tela Vasir was an asari Spectre and powerful biotic, publicly known for investigating the 2185 theft of confidential galactic economic data from the Citadel Council's Ministry of Finance and for breaking up a slave-trading ring in the city of Aegohr on the salarian colony of Nasurn. Secretly, she was an agent of the Shadow Broker, justifying the arrangement on the grounds that the Broker's information had ensured stability in Citadel space.

In Mass Effect: Foundation, Vasir received a tip from a Cerberus turncoat offering the organization's secrets. Meeting the informant at the Citadel, she took a data disc, then doubled back with reinforcements and caught the informant downloading Commander Shepard's classified Spectre files. Stripping away a blonde wig and lightened skin revealed the Cerberus agent Rasa. Vasir gave Rasa a choice of prison or working against Cerberus, and supplied her with a virus payload meant to force the Illusive Man to confer with her. Days later Miranda Lawson took the disc from Rasa and counteracted the virus, thwarting Vasir's plan but alerting her to Cerberus's interest in Shepard.

In Lair of the Shadow Broker, Vasir met Shepard at Liara T'Soni's ransacked Illium apartment, dismissing local police with her Spectre authority and letting Shepard investigate. She accompanied Shepard to the Dracon Trade Center after a recording pointed to Liara's contact Sekat. A bomb detonated as they arrived; Vasir split off, claiming she would cover the building from her skycar. Shepard found Sekat dead and Vasir standing over a mercenary. Liara then emerged with a gun drawn, revealing that Vasir had tried to kill her and had murdered Sekat to seize his intel on the Shadow Broker's location. Vasir shattered a window with biotics, hurled the glass at the squad, and fell out with Shepard. She escaped by skycar, triggering a chase through Illium's skyways that ended when she collided with another car and crash-landed on Hotel Azure. Badly wounded, she fought Shepard's squad after taking a hostage, and finally collapsed and died. Before dying she insisted she was nothing like Saren Arterius and argued Shepard's partnership with Cerberus was no better than hers with the Broker, citing Cerberus assassinations of Alliance admirals, biotic "death camps," and Akuze.
