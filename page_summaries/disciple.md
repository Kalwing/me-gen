The Disciple was a shotgun appearing in Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Andromeda. It was originally handcrafted for the exclusive use of asari justicars, and its schematics were released to asari commandos only after centuries of negotiation. The weapon fired shells packed with microscale submunitions, dealing heavy damage and stunning even shielded enemies with the force of the blast.

In Mass Effect 3 single-player, the Disciple could be found during the mission Kallini: Ardat-Yakshi Monastery in a small room shortly after the first conversation with Falere, and was otherwise purchasable afterward from Nos Astra Sporting Goods. In multiplayer it was a rare reward from item packs requiring no expansion. It was one of the more accurate standard shotguns and the lightest, benefiting power-intensive classes, though it had far less stopping power than heavier shotguns and a fairly slow reload. Its description overstated its stun, which actually only raised the standard stun chance, but it reliably staggered most infantry and some armored, shielded, or barriered targets, and knocked bodies backward on kills, which made it unable to prevent Abomination explosions since it did not destroy heads.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda the Disciple was described as a relatively lightweight, semi-automatic shotgun built in the Milky Way for asari justicars and commandos, of which the Andromeda Initiative had only a small number due to asari reluctance to share the design. It had ten ranks with rising damage and falling weight, a six-round clip, and used iron, fluorite, and element zero to craft.
