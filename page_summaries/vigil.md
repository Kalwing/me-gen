Vigil was a Prothean Virtual Intelligence located in the underground archives on Ilos, an advanced non-organic analysis system carrying personality imprints of Ksad Ishan, the Prothean Chief Overseer of the Ilos facility. During Commander Shepard's operation on Ilos in 2183, Vigil used force fields to halt the Mako and guided Shepard's team into the Watcher's Chamber. Its holographic interface had badly degraded, but its memory core remained intact, and it had monitored the squad's communications on the surface so it could translate its output for them. Vigil expressed relief that none of the squad were artificial intelligences or indoctrinated, which it took as proof the Protheans' contingency plan had worked.

Vigil explained that the Reapers returned in a cycle spanning millions of years and had begun exterminating organic civilizations roughly fifty thousand years earlier. The Prothean scientists on Ilos survived because records of the planet were destroyed in the Reapers' first wave, leaving the underground facility undiscovered. The staff were placed in cryogenic suspension with Vigil watching over them, intended to be revived once the Reapers left. Because the extermination took centuries, Vigil was forced to conserve power by shutting down cryogenic pods one at a time, killing the occupants. Only the twelve top scientists were revived after the Reapers departed, too few to save the species. Those survivors used the Conduit to reach the Citadel and interfered with the signal to the keepers that would have let the Reapers activate the Citadel relay; their ultimate fate was unknown because the Conduit was a one-way passage.

Shepard's squad was horrified that Vigil had killed those it was meant to protect, though Vigil stated the outcome was foreseen in contingency planning. Vigil gave Shepard a data file granting temporary direct control of the Citadel's functions and urged the Commander to break the Reapers' cycle of genocide. It could not explain why the Reapers fostered and then destroyed organic life, saying the motivations of synthetic life were beyond Prothean comprehension. Its programming limited it to information directly related to stopping the Reapers, disappointing Liara T'Soni, who wanted to learn more about the Protheans.

By the time of Mass Effect 2, groups that visited Ilos found Vigil had shut down, possibly from total power loss, which helped the Council continue believing Sovereign was merely a geth ship. The musical theme "Vigil," also Mass Effect's main menu theme, played over scenes with the VI.
