Elanos Haliat was a turian pirate and prominent figure in the Terminus Systems. He was once the most powerful leader of the Terminus pirate bands and, with batarian funding, rallied the inhabitants of the Terminus Systems into a large assault on the human colony Elysium, an attack later known as the Skyllian Blitz, intended to boost his prestige. The Alliance repelled the Blitz and destroyed the pirate ships from orbit. The Terminus pirates blamed Haliat for the failure, stripped him of leadership, and he retreated to a remote world in disgrace.

Seeking to regain his prestige by killing the first human Spectre, Haliat recovered an Alliance espionage probe left over from the First Contact War, carrying a heavy nuclear payload. He activated the probe's homing beacon and hid it in a mine on Agebinium, expecting the Alliance to send someone to retrieve it. When Commander Shepard arrived, Haliat waited until the team was inside, then detonated a seismic charge to trap them and activated the probe's nuclear payload with a ten-second countdown, taunting Shepard over a hologram (or blaming Shepard for his disgrace if the Commander was a War Hero). He stole Shepard's Mako and brought it to his camp, but Shepard's team had deactivated the nuke and escaped. Haliat and his followers attacked, and all the pirates, including Haliat, died in the firefight.

In gameplay Haliat had three bars of regenerating shields and health, could use Immunity, took cover and flanked, and switched between an assault rifle and a shotgun. He was originally intended as a turian but given a human model in the original game, with his turian identity restored in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. He was voiced by Gary Anthony Williams.
