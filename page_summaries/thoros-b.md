Short page (under ~150 words of prose). Thoros-B was a biotoxin developed by Dr. Zev Cohen on Noveria, based on an exotic frontier lifeform. Binary Helix wanted something to kill that creature, but since a weapon affecting one rare species had no profit, Cohen was ordered to broaden it to affect more species. A quarantine accident infected several of his team, and he could ask Commander Shepard to fetch a cure from their lab, made by mixing four chemicals in precise amounts.
