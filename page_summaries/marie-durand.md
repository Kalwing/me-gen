First Lieutenant Marie Durand was the acting commander of the 14th Regiment, 3rd Brigade, Alliance 10th Frontier Division on Nepmos. When she and her men came under sustained attack by hostile lifeforms, she sent a mayday requesting assistance. After landing on Nepmos, Commander Shepard met Durand at the listening post. She was tough-talking but exhausted from resisting repeated rachni attacks, and to her it did not matter that the "animals" were actually a sentient species, since they remained extremely dangerous. She coordinated her remaining forces and held the line with the help of Shepard's squad.

After fighting off several waves of attackers, Durand learned that the main rachni nest was deep underground, and was reluctant to send Shepard to destroy it alone. If Shepard had not already investigated it, Durand also asked the Commander to check the other listening post in the Acheron system, which had dropped out of contact. Shepard could also tell Durand about Depot Sigma-23 if it had already been investigated. Durand was voiced by Grey DeLisle, who also voiced Nassana Dantius, Kahlee Sanders, and Dalatrass Linron.
