Comm Relay was a mission in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, following the Colosseum mission and preceding Access Corridor, comprising six checkpoints. Having defeated X1, Randall Ezno, guided over comms by his volus hacker ally, took an elevator to the exterior of the Cerberus facility ("the Barn") to reach a communications tower and send a message to the Systems Alliance.

Across the first five checkpoints Randall fought waves of Cerberus forces on exposed exterior platforms and bridges: Troopers, jetpack-equipped Assault Troopers, Riot Troopers, Centurions, snipers with Assassination skills, and the game's only Assault Captain. Cover was often destructible or absent, and the walkthrough recommended biotic Pull, tactical Cloak, the M-29 Incisor, and varied weapons and powers to earn "veteran" style rankings and gold medals. At checkpoint five the volus confirmed the relay was ahead; after clearing the defenders Randall activated the console (two side buttons then the central button) and transmitted the message to the Alliance.

In the final checkpoint the tower's defenses activated and a Cerberus Ogre Mech deployed, while a Geth Dropship arrived and landed Geth Troopers and Geth Juggernauts that attacked both Randall and the mech. After destroying all forces, Randall was tasked by the volus to steal a shuttle from the facility's Hangar Bay and head for an Alliance rendezvous on the desert planet LV426, taking the Ogre Mech's elevator into the station toward the Access Corridor.
