Ilos was the second planet of the Refuge System in the Pangaea Expanse, reachable in Mass Effect after completing all missions on Therum, Feros, Noveria, and Virmire. In the golden age of the Protheans it was a verdant world covered with the spires and arches of magnificent cities. By the time of the games its entire surface had been devastated by unknown means and turned rust-colored, with heightened atmospheric oxygen and wildfires burning on the dark side, indicating that most or all respirating animal life had died off. Surface gravity was 1.17 G.

The Codex compared Ilos to Troy: a world known only through second-hand references found at other Prothean ruins. It lay in a remote area of the Terminus Systems accessible only via the legendary Mu Relay, which had been knocked out of position by a supernova roughly four thousand years earlier, leaving Ilos and its cluster inaccessible. University expeditions to chart a conventional FTL route never progressed past planning due to the distance and danger.

The quarian Migrant Fleet had expended effort searching for Ilos, once considering it a settlement prospect, according to Tali'Zorah. If the Citadel Council did not survive the Battle of the Citadel, a 2185 news report describes the Alliance's Fifth Fleet stopping at Ilos while salarian researchers protest its use of dreadnoughts; if the Council survived, the researchers instead applaud Alliance protection against batarian demands for the relics. Liara T'Soni owned a painting of Ilos, displayed in her Illium apartment. The Prothean Javik, if reawakened in 2186, revealed that Ilos was originally inhabited by the extinct inusannon before the Protheans discovered and colonized it. Ilos was the setting for the mission Ilos: Find the Conduit and hosted geth of every type. In Greek mythology, Ilos founded the city later known as Troy or "Illium"; the world was conceived during development as a lush jungle before being revised for a more ominous third-act tone.
