Messner was a trader who arrived on Fehl Prime in 2185, presenting himself as a traveling merchant selling hardware around the galaxy. In truth he was a Cerberus operative tasked with investigating the disappearances of human colonies and the Collectors' involvement, as depicted in Mass Effect: Paragon Lost.

Delivering a shipment to Fehl Prime, Messner met James Vega and Treeya en route to the colony's underground research labs, flattering Vega for his 2183 victory. Barred from the newly secured labs, he lent Delta Squad a M35 Mako to investigate a jamming signal on the colony outskirts. While the squad destroyed the alien bio-mechanical device producing the signal, a Collector ship arrived and began harvesting the population; Messner suggested using the colony defense cannons against it, but a low-powered shot failed to disable the ship. In the labs Delta Squad learned the tightened security had been meant to keep Messner out, the Alliance having identified him as a Cerberus agent. He admitted it, arguing they now shared the goal of stopping the Collectors.

When Delta Squad used a ship piloted by the krogan Brood to attack the Collector ship's drive core, Messner shot Brood in the head, causing the ship to crash and the squad to be captured. He revealed he had deliberately lured the Collectors to Fehl Prime with the jamming device to study them, sacrificing the colony to gain their temporary trust. He forced Treeya, who could interface with Prothean technology, to access a Prothean archive aboard the ship, revealing the Protheans' last stand, their transformation into Collectors, and a plan to liquefy humans into genetic material for a new Reaper. Messner recorded the intel on a bracelet for Cerberus. The Collectors then declared him no longer useful and prepared a pod for his "ascension"; he threw Treeya into it instead, but she seized the bracelet as the pod was ejected toward Fehl Prime. In the ensuing firefight Vega disarmed and stabbed Messner, refused his bribes, and left him to be killed by Collector fire.
