"UNC: Cerberus" was a Mass Effect assignment following "UNC: Missing Marines." After that mission, Admiral Kahoku contacted Shepard by message, having learned that the ambush which killed his marines was orchestrated by a group called Cerberus. He provided coordinates to three Cerberus research facilities on the planet Binthu in the Yangtze system of the Voyager Cluster, where Cerberus was unleashing alien monstrosities into the Attican Traverse.

The three bases were similarly built, each with an underground entrance topped by two defense turrets and, inside, a Cerberus Sniper, two Cerberus Commandos and two biotic Research Technicians working around central forcefields caging test specimens: three Rachni Workers in one base, one Rachni Soldier in another, and three Thorian Creepers in the third. A recommended tactic was to shut off the forcefields at a diagnostic station so the specimens and Cerberus personnel fought each other. Regardless of clearing order, Kahoku was found in the final facility, having been captured and murdered by Cerberus operatives soon after transmitting the operation's details; his body showed no combat trauma, only needle marks indicating execution by injection. Examining the body completed the assignment and triggered "UNC: Hades' Dogs."
