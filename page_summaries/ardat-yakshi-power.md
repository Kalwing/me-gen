Ardat Yakshi was a passive class power available only to the squad member Morinth in Mass Effect 2. It represented her ability to feed off the dying, granting unnatural increases to her health, weapon damage, and power recharge time. Across its first three ranks the power scaled from +5% health, +6% weapon damage, and -6% power recharge time at Rank 1 to +15% health, +18% weapon damage, and -18% power recharge time at Rank 3.

At Rank 4 the power evolved into one of two options. Erdua Yakshi emphasized stamina, providing +20% health, +18% weapon damage, and -25% power recharge time. Malian Yakshi emphasized her consumption of lives, providing +20% health, +25% weapon damage, and -18% power recharge time.

A known bug caused leveling the power to Rank 4 to change the headings and descriptions of ranks 1 through 3 to those of Samara's Asari Justicar class power, and to revert the overall ability description to Samara's power text; only the Rank 4 description itself was unaffected, regardless of which evolution was chosen.
