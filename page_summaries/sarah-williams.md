Sarah Williams, affectionately nicknamed "Sar" by her sister, was the youngest of Gunnery Chief Ashley Williams's four sisters. As of 2183 she was set to graduate from high school and kept up regular correspondence with Ashley regardless of Ash's posting. Like all her sisters she took self-defense lessons at her parents' urging, choosing aikido. She could be overheard on Ashley's mail calling Kaidan Alenko—or a male Shepard interested in Ash—"cute" after seeing him on a news vid about the SSV Normandy.

A couple of years earlier Sarah had a boyfriend named Mike who pressed her to take their relationship further; she objected violently and slammed him face-first into a tree during a walk in the woods on Amaterasu. Ashley rushed home and walked Sarah to school for several days. Sarah told everyone at school what had happened, angering Mike, who confronted the sisters on Ashley's last day of leave. Sarah insisted on handling it herself, kept her cool as Mike screamed at her, dodged his punch so that he faceplanted and injured himself, then helped stop his bleeding and told paramedics he "fell." Mike apologized before reaching the hospital and Sarah accepted with a hug.

By 2186 Sarah had married a military man named Thomas, a Second Lieutenant stationed on Demeter, surprising a family that thought she disliked military life. The couple were on their honeymoon when the Reapers invaded; Thomas was recalled to service while Sarah joined her mother and two other sisters, eventually reaching the Citadel. She later learned Thomas had been killed in action. If Ashley survived Virmire, she could update Shepard on the family, and after the failed Citadel coup Sarah arranged a memorial service for Thomas at the Refugee Docks' Memorial Wall, asking Ash to speak; Ashley and Shepard could attend and offer condolences.
