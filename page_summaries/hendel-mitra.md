Hendel Mitra was a former Alliance soldier serving as Security Chief of the Ascension Project. Tall and muscular with rusty-brown hair and light brown skin, he was of part Scandinavian and part Indian ancestry, born in the suburbs of the New Calcutta megapolis on Earth. His mother's exposure to dust-form element zero during pregnancy gave him biotic powers rather than birth defects. Detected as a biotic, he was sent to BAaT for training, where the harsh military program's efforts to separate children from their families succeeded too well: he became permanently estranged from his family, refusing contact even after BAaT closed, and later enlisted in the Alliance military. Kahlee Sanders believed his BAaT experiences drove his determination to help Gillian Grayson. He is hinted to be homosexual through Kahlee's comments.

In Mass Effect: Ascension, Hendel was troubled by Paul Grayson's drug addiction and contact with Gillian, but was fiercely protective of all the Project's children. When Gillian unleashed a biotic storm in the cafeteria, he hesitated to stun her and used his own biotics to shield himself, afterward blaming himself and considering resignation. His protectiveness paid off when he caught Dr. Jiro Toshiwa administering cerebrospinal medication that triggered a seizure in Gillian; despite being stunned repeatedly, Hendel stayed conscious and kept her alive with CPR until EMTs arrived. After Jiro was exposed as a Cerberus operative, Hendel accompanied Kahlee, Gillian, and Paul Grayson to Omega, where they were captured by the Cerberus agent Pel, who planned to sell Hendel and Gillian to the Collectors. The quarian Lemm'Shal nar Tesleya broke them out, and they fled to the Migrant Fleet. During quarantine aboard the shuttle Hendel tutored Gillian in her biotics; he toured the Idenna with Kahlee and marveled at its free trading economy. When Cerberus sent Paul Grayson and the quarian Golo to extract Gillian, Hendel fought with his biotics and was badly hurt, but recovered enough to help defuse explosives aboard the Cyniad before it could destroy the Idenna. When Kahlee left with Paul as a captive, Hendel stayed aboard the Migrant Fleet as Gillian's protector for the Idenna's mission to find a new quarian homeworld.

In the first edition of Mass Effect: Deception (acknowledged by BioWare as containing lore discrepancies), Hendel and Gillian remained trusted crew of the Idenna. When the batarian slave ship Glory of Khar'shan boarded the vessel and the quarian security head Ugho was killed, Hendel took command and led the counterattack, and with Gillian's biotics the quarians seized the slaver ship. After the captured slave Hal McCann told Gillian her father was dead, Hendel left with her aboard the batarian ship to learn what had happened.
