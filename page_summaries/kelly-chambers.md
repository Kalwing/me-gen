Kelly Chambers was Commander Shepard's yeoman aboard the Normandy SR-2 in 2185, a Cerberus operative in the Lazarus Cell holding a psychology degree. Besides managing Shepard's emails and relaying squadmates' meeting requests, she served as counselor, monitoring the psychological state of Shepard and the crew and offering insight on each new squad member and mission outcome. She rejected the idea that she or Cerberus was anti-alien, treating the crew's non-humans with respect. Depending on Shepard's response to her, she was addressed as "Yeoman Chambers" or "Kelly," a choice reflected on her CIC character model. She was a potential romance for male or female Shepard: after a private dinner in the Captain's Cabin, she could send a suggestive follow-up message ("KellyGrrl" / "slip into something more comfortable") and later perform a dancer's routine in Shepard's cabin.

During the Suicide Mission the Collectors abducted the Normandy crew. If Shepard delayed, the team arrived to see Kelly dissolved into raw organic material for the Human-Reaper; if Shepard went through the Omega 4 Relay immediately, the colonist Lilith was liquefied instead and Kelly was freed in time. A rescued Kelly vividly recalled her imprisonment.

In Mass Effect 3, if she survived and had dined privately with Shepard, Kelly was found in the Citadel refugee camp in Cargo Hold B. She revealed she had deserted Cerberus over its methods and declined to rejoin the Normandy due to recurring nightmares of her abduction, preferring to help refugees. She confessed to having sent reports on Shepard and the crew to the Illusive Man while serving as yeoman; if Shepard reprimanded her, she later took a cyanide pill and died. If Shepard reassured her and, crucially, told her to change her identity, she dyed her hair blonde and became "Felicia Hannigan," surviving the Cerberus attack on the Citadel; otherwise Cerberus killed her. If Shepard kept fish on the Normandy, she returned them. She later emailed Shepard connecting him with other defecting Cerberus engineers in disguise. Cronos Station recordings showed the Illusive Man and Miranda Lawson had handpicked her for the Lazarus Cell because her personality would make Cerberus look more sympathetic. Her name appeared on the Normandy memorial wall only if she died during the Suicide Mission itself.
