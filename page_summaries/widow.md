The Widow system was a star system whose only significant contents were the star Widow itself, additional gases drawn from the surrounding Serpent Nebula, a fuel depot, and the Citadel. It was the location of the Citadel across all three games of the original trilogy and thus the hub for a very large number of missions and assignments, including Citadel: Expose Saren and Race Against Time: Final Battle in the first game, Citadel: The Council and Dossier: The Master Thief in Mass Effect 2, and the Priority: The Citadel missions, Citadel: Aria T'Loak and the Leviathan investigation missions in Mass Effect 3.

The system also held Aria's Fleet, added after downloading the Mass Effect 3: Omega DLC and completing Citadel: Aria T'Loak, and a fuel depot added in Mass Effect 2 by either the Firewalker Pack or Kasumi - Stolen Memory. A mass relay connected the system to the wider galaxy. The page is largely an index of the content set on or around the Citadel; the term "widow" also refers to a sniper rifle and other unrelated subjects covered on a disambiguation page.
