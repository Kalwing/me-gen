Javik was the only known survivor of the ancient Prothean race, revived in 2186 after more than 50,000 years in stasis and driven by vengeance against the Reapers for his people's extinction. He was written mainly by John Dombrow and added through the From Ashes DLC, fully integrated into the Legendary Edition.

In Prothean society, "Avatars" each embodied a single virtue; Javik's was Vengeance. Born mid-war on a galaxy already losing to the Reapers, he was to lead roughly one million warriors who would outlast the invasion in stasis in an underground bunker on Eden Prime, then reclaim the galaxy. Indoctrinated traitors betrayed the bunker to the Reapers, and the Collectors slaughtered the sleeping Protheans. The supervising VI Victory initiated a neutron purge that damaged the bunker's sensors, making mass reactivation impossible; power was triaged so that only Javik would wake.

Commander Shepard and Liara T'Soni uncovered and opened his pod on Eden Prime while investigating Cerberus. Javik attacked them on waking, then agreed to join the fight to destroy the Reapers. Through touch he could read biological memories and instantly learned human language. He held imperialist, social-Darwinist views: he preached the "Cosmic Imperative," dismissed mercy, morality, and friendship as weakness, believed allies were merely resources, and thought the modern "primitive" races should be forced to help rather than asked. He refused to use names, referring to people by species or job. He took Grunt's former quarters aboard the Normandy and clashed repeatedly with the crew, especially EDI, whose capacity for choice he found troubling, and Liara, whose persistent questions and asari heritage he needled. He recounted Prothean history with the rachni (bred as weapons), the zha and synthetic zha'til, the densorin, and the asari, whom the Protheans secretly "guided" as this cycle's best hope, a claim that nearly provoked Liara to violence over events on Thessia.

If persuaded to use his Prothean Echo Shard, Javik relived hunting down and killing his own indoctrinated crew, concluding that war is atrocity committed for survival. Asked what he would do after the war, he might choose to return to the Cronian Nebula to take his own life and rejoin his comrades, or, if reconciled with Liara, co-author a book with her titled "Journeys with the Prothean," or live among the hanar on Kahje. In the Citadel DLC he starred alongside Shepard in "Blasto 7: Blasto Goes to War." If Shepard lacked sufficient forces, Javik could be killed by Harbinger in the final push; if he survived, he honored Shepard at the Memorial Wall. "Javik" derives from the Hindi word for "biological" or "organic."
