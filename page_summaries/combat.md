Combat was a game-mechanics overview page for the first Mass Effect, covering its real-time third-person combat: squads of Commander Shepard and two NPC allies, the Tactical Pause with the Power Wheel (console) or HUD screen (PC), squad movement and attack orders, sprinting with a fatigue meter, player-only grenades, biotic/tech/combat talents that vary by class, the four weapon types with their heat-buildup and overheat system rather than reloading, weapon accuracy and stability, melee "smash" attacks, health and toxic damage, recharging shields (kinetic barriers), cover, squad selection balancing tech/combat/biotic strength, the Casual, Normal, Veteran, Hardcore, and Insanity difficulty settings, game options such as Auto Level-Up and Target Assist, combat-related bugs including the Overheat Bug, and Legendary Edition changes.
