The Striker Assault Rifle was an assault rifle in Mass Effect 3. A fully automatic weapon that functioned more like a grenade launcher, it fired high-impact slugs that detonated on contact, and its rate of fire increased the longer the trigger was held. It was designed to be usable by non-krogan for marketing outside the Krogan DMZ, though its recoil tended to unbalance smaller species.

In single-player the Striker was added by the Groundside Resistance Pack, and in the Legendary Edition it could be bought from the Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies terminal in the Presidium Commons for 10,000 credits. In multiplayer it was a rare weapon requiring the Resurgence Pack. Its rising rate of fire came with dramatically increasing recoil and heavy reserve-ammo consumption, making an Assault Rifle Magazine Upgrade and Stability Damper useful. Its explosive rounds ignored armor damage penalties, could not score headshots or weak-point bonuses, and dealt splash damage; aiming at the ground near a target let the proximity-fused grenades airburst. The weapon stunned unshielded non-heavy units, slowed the wielder when fired (Drell and vorcha most, krogan and batarian least; several classes exempt), and paired well with Adrenaline Rush, ammo augmentations and Tactical Cloak.
