Noveria: Espionage was a Mass Effect assignment, acquired when Shepard first reached the Port Hanshan Mezzanine on Noveria and an asari named Mallene Calis called the Commander over. Calis worked for the Armali Council and wanted information about the company Binary Helix and its biotic engineering. She asked Shepard to wiretap Rafael Vargas, a Binary Helix salesman elsewhere in the bar, by approaching him as a potential customer and keeping him distracted while a device she provided injected monitoring viruses into his personal wireless network. Calis warned that Vargas was shrewd and would notice time-wasting, but that he had a tell, rubbing the back of his neck when uncomfortable, and suggested pressing him at those moments.

The assignment was mostly a means of gaining morality points. Shepard could refuse to help Calis (8 Paragon, no experience), agree and then quit before talking to Vargas, agree and then warn Vargas of the plan (letting Binary Helix feed the Armali Council false information), or fully carry out the wiretap. Completing the hack required passing only the first two persuasion checks with Vargas; on return, Calis paid 500 credits, raised to 750 if Shepard charmed or intimidated her. Renegade routes allowed lying to Calis that the job was done to collect the reward without performing the distraction. The assignment was written by Chris L'Etoile and is referred to internally as "Rich Man's House," likely a reference to a Diogenes quote.
