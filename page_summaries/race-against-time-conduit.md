"Race Against Time: Conduit" was the Mass Effect mission that followed "Ilos: Find the Conduit," into which the earlier "Race Against Time: Sovereign" journal entry was renamed. Vigil had revealed that the Conduit was not a weapon but a miniature mass relay serving as a back door onto the Citadel, itself an enormous mass relay. Saren Arterius intended to use the Conduit to reach the Citadel, unlock the station for Sovereign, and trigger a Reaper invasion that would destroy all organic life in the galaxy.

The mission was a linear drive in the Mako out of Vigil's chamber and down a path now cleared of the forcefields that had blocked it earlier. Shepard's squadmates remarked that even the Protheans, with all their power and knowledge, had fallen for the Reapers' traps, and vowed not to share their fate. Geth Rocket Troopers guarded the entrance to a long trench run, which was defended by numerous geth including Rocket Troopers, Troopers, a Shock Trooper, and a Geth Colossus, followed by further groups of Rocket Troopers. There were no item pickups during the driving sections, and the game would not allow Shepard to enter the aqueducts or the Conduit itself without the Mako; entering the Conduit on foot meant instant death.

At the end of the aqueduct the Mako halted at a ledge overlooking the distant Conduit, and a lengthy cutscene showed the Battle of the Citadel beginning: Sovereign and its geth allies arrived through the mass relay and fired a devastating broadside on the Citadel Fleet, the Citadel forces failed to seal the station's arms, and the commander of the Destiny Ascension ordered the Council evacuated. On the Presidium, a geth threw aside the body of an asari as Saren and his minions took the elevator toward the Council Chambers. Regaining control, Shepard had only 40 seconds to reach the Conduit while dodging four Geth Colossi, then drove into the Conduit, which automatically transported the Mako to the Citadel. The mission led directly into "Race Against Time: Final Battle."
