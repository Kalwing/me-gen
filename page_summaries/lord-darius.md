The self-styled Lord Darius was a human warlord operating in the Plutus system of the Hades Gamma cluster. Arrogant and greedy, he exploited his base's position on Nonuel, a secondary source of element zero, to extort the Alliance. If Commander Shepard was mostly Renegade, Admiral Hackett sent Shepard to negotiate with him. Darius was hostile from the outset and made outrageous demands: to be addressed as "Lord", an Alliance apology for "illegal" eezo mining on an asteroid in his territory (his attacks on the miners being "punitive" and "justified"), equipment to manufacture red sand plus fees and a share of the mined eezo in exchange for mining rights, and for Shepard to say "please". He offered an OSD formalizing the deal if Shepard accepted everything. Shepard could object to only one condition before Darius declared negotiations over and ordered an attack, or could simply open fire at any time.

If Shepard objected, Darius claimed he was actually an Alliance ally: after the batarians were driven from the Skyllian Verge, the Alliance had armed his syndicate, the region's most powerful, to take over and stabilize it, and was now reneging over his eezo and drug operations. Reporting back, Shepard realized Hackett had expected and wanted Darius killed ("or did you think you were the only one who did whatever was necessary to get the job done?"). Restored Galactic News reports covered both outcomes: pirate efforts to halt Alliance mining failing if Darius died, or inflated eezo prices if he was spared. He was voiced by Townsend Coleman.
