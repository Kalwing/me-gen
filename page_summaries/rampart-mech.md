The Rampart Mech was a Cerberus combat unit encountered in Mass Effect 3, used to contain and control Omega's civilian population after Cerberus's successful invasion of the station. Facing the problem of policing Omega's large and lawless populace, Cerberus first established detainment areas bordered by impassable force fields (believed to be plasma sheets suspended in a magnetic field), then needed units that could pass freely through those fields. Cerberus engineers recovered LOKI security mechs from the remaining Eclipse mercenary chapters and upgraded them with proprietary mini-fabrication technology, shielding salvaged from Omega's element zero processing plants, and additional ablative armor, producing the Rampart, a flexible unit designed for control and intimidation.

Rampart mechs could hunt targets anywhere on Omega, including heavily irradiated lower industrial areas. They were fitted with infrasound and scent markers that triggered fight-or-flight responses in several species to subdue or disperse crowds. When threatened they could channel all available power into a high-intensity shield that resisted hacking, biotic attacks, and weapons fire until reinforcements arrived, though deploying it prevented them from advancing. Most were armed with a standard shotgun and a flash-forged omni-blade; when destroyed, a Rampart's remains discharged glowing red energy that damaged and stunned nearby opponents, and a broken mech could destroy its shotgun's aluminum-alloy heat sinks to coat assailants in red-hot residue. In extreme situations the Rampart overclocked into a "hunter-killer mode," diverting power from its shields to move faster and single-mindedly destroy its target with the omni-blade, targeting unprotected nerve clusters, while venting heat through its dispersal sinks as an intimidating display.

The page also gives combat tactics: Ramparts moved far faster than the LOKI mechs they derived from, rarely sought cover, and possessed thrusters for vertical movement. They were vulnerable to headshots (a Javelin or M-11 Suppressor round could kill one), tech stuns from Overload and Energy Drain, Sabotage, incendiary ammo, and fire-detonation combos, but could not be lifted or ragdolled due to their armor. Their four downward-slanting red slit "eyes" resembled the Cerberus Phantom, and they could not be fought in the Armax Arsenal Arena.
