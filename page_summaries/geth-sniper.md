Geth Snipers were geth bipedal platforms built for long-range combat in the original Mass Effect. They were dark brown with red armor details and emitted a bright cyan light from their flashlight eye, were equipped with sniper rifles, and possessed the powerful Assassination ability. They were the least durable geth platforms, with relatively low, non-regenerating health and only two bars of shielding, which did regenerate like that of all Mass Effect enemies.

Their Assassination ability was very dangerous, able to instantly kill a low-level Shepard and deal heavy damage to the Mako; it was always preceded by a laser targeting beam, so players were warned not to leave cover until Snipers were dealt with, with Immunity and Barrier improving survival odds and Sabotage or Lift able to interrupt the shot. Because Snipers lacked secondary weapons, Sabotage rendered them helpless, and Master Sabotage could kill them outright given their fragility. Their passive Radar Jamming disabled the combat radar for anyone in the surrounding area and was often the first sign of geth presence; Combat Sensor, Combat Scanner or Combat Optics upgrades bypassed it, and the effect grew stronger as more jamming users were present. Geth Snipers could be turned with the most basic AI Hacking and were useful as allies when positioned in towers or away from other geth, but were quickly gunned down among their own kind.
