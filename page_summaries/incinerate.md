Incinerate was a tech power in Mass Effect 2 and in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes. It launched an explosive plasma blast (impacting instantly when used by squadmates) that dealt damage over time, was very effective against armor, and in Mass Effect 2 permanently stopped the health regeneration of enemies such as vorcha and krogan.

In Mass Effect 2 it had a 1.2-meter impact radius, a 6-second recharge (12 for squadmates), and scaled from 130 to 170 damage over 3 seconds across three ranks. Rank 4 evolved it into Heavy Incinerate (210 damage) or Incineration Blast (3-meter radius). It used a 2.4x multiplier against armor and reduced multipliers against organic health and unprotected synthetics, dealt instant damage to protections, caused flamethrower-equipped pyro enemies to explode when it hit exposed health, and panicked or knocked down most unprotected humanoids. Klixen and most synthetics resisted its fire damage to health. It was available to Engineers, Infiltrators, Mordin, and Dr. Amanda Kenson.

In Mass Effect 3 it had an 8-second base recharge and 300 base damage (330 multiplayer), with ranks improving recharge speed, damage (up to +30%), a 2-meter radius option, a rank 5 Burning Damage DoT (+40%/50% over 8 seconds), and rank 6 choices of a Freeze Combo bonus (+100% to chilled or frozen targets) or +50% armor damage. Unlike the Mass Effect 2 version it had no baseline area of effect (only the Radius evolution, hitting up to 4 targets) and no longer halted health regeneration despite its in-game text. It did 1.5x damage to armor and half damage to shields and barriers, dealt 75% of its damage up front, and primed almost all targets, including protected ones, for Fire Explosions. Alternating it with Overload or Arc Grenades produced near-endless chains of Fire Explosions and Tech Bursts, and it was very effective against Reaper units, destroying corpses to deny Cannibals. Single-player availability: Engineer, Infiltrator, EDI, Nyreen Kandros, Miranda Lawson (Kaidan being the only NPC squadmate with a cold source, Cryo Blast, to enable the Freeze Combo).
