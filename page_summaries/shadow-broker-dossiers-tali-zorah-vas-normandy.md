This Shadow Broker dossier, unlocked after the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, profiled Tali'Zorah vas Normandy as a mechanic and engineer showing typical quarian investment in her species' future, and noted that her father Rael'Zorah's work was useful for widening geth-quarian tensions.

A suit process log lists upgrades and installed applications, including relic-technology interface and radiation-detection upgrades authorized under Project Haestrom, a ShieldJack prototype defense suite authorized by Admiral Han'Gerrel vas Neema, an isolated infection-treatment process, and education vids such as "Dealing with Loss," "Understanding Body Language: Human Edition," "Human Courtship and Mating" and the entertainment vids "Fleet and Flotilla" and "Exiles: Portraits of the Lost Quarians." Nerve-Stim Pro is repeatedly installed, uninstalled and reinstalled, ending with a "Deluxe Edition."

An email chain records Tali, still Tali'Zorah nar Rayya during her Pilgrimage, contacting the Shadow Broker after finding a recording implicating Spectre Saren working with the geth and the Reapers, offering the information in exchange for protection. The Broker directed her to his Citadel enforcer Fist, but secretly ordered Fist: "When you get the intel, kill the quarian and any of our operatives who had access to the data." Tali then reported that Fist tried to sell her to Saren and that she had instead given the information to "a human Alliance commander named Shepard."

A keystroke log captures her repeatedly drafting and erasing a condolence letter to the parents of Myrr'Jorin, a soldier killed on Haestrom saving her life, struggling over wording such as "He gave his life to get data that will one day bring us back to Homeworld... and we all honor his sacrifice," before saving the draft and exiting without sending it.
