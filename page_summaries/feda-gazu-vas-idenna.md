Short page. Feda'Gazu vas Idenna was a quarian crewman of the Idenna who commanded the Cyniad scout vessel, appearing in the novel Mass Effect: Ascension. She led the scout team that met Golo on Shelba to buy much-needed technology. Though prepared for Golo's betrayal, she was surprised when she defused an explosive trap in his truck that overloaded her weapon and knocked out her shields. Pel then shot her from over a mile away with a Volkov sniper rifle, hitting her twice in the shoulder and knee before killing her with a shot to the head. Her environmental suit was stripped and used by one of Pel's team to ambush the Cyniad's remaining crew.
