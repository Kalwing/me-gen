Crooks was a Systems Alliance starship pilot assigned as the SSV Normandy's original pilot before the position was reassigned to Jeff "Joker" Moreau. During preparations for the Normandy's test flight at Arcturus Station, Crooks was aboard the vessel. When Joker decided to hijack the Normandy to demonstrate that he could fly it despite his medical condition, he locked Crooks in a shuttle and left him behind on the station, then carried out the test flight himself. Alliance soldiers later found Crooks, and he told Captain Anderson, Captain Dillard, and General Invectus that he had not been responsible for the hijacking. Following the incident, Crooks was permanently replaced by Joker as the Normandy's pilot, a change made at the insistence of Invectus, who had been deeply impressed by Joker's demonstrated skill. The events are drawn from the comic Mass Effect: He Who Laughs Best.
