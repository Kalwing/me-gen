Cerberus Turrets were automated defensive emplacements used by Cerberus to protect key locations and installations in Mass Effect 2. They appeared on the planet Aite, defending sites tied to Project Overlord, a Cerberus effort to build a VI capable of exploiting the geth's religious beliefs to gain control over them. The turrets were concentrated on the approaches to Vulcan Station and at small outposts around the planet. Each turret mounted a Mass Accelerator Machine Gun that could destroy the Hammerhead within a few seconds of sustained fire, and relied on armor alone for defense. They rose to fire only when the Hammerhead came close and retracted when it moved away, which could aid a retreat but also prevented targeting them at range. Recommended tactics were to stay at a distance, move in and out of their engagement radius, fire from the top of a Hammerhead boost and drop back behind cover, and avoid prolonged exposure. A separate Cerberus turret appears in Mass Effect 3.
