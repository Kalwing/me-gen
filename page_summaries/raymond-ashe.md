Colonel Raymond Ashe was a Cerberus operative, described as ruthless, with a deep hatred of aliens and a willingness to achieve his mission at any cost, including the lives of his own men or civilians. In Mass Effect: Invasion, as Omega was attacked by Adjutants, Ashe and General Oleg Petrovsky arrived aboard the Elbrus and destroyed the remaining Adjutant-controlled Cerberus transports. During the cleanup, Ashe ordered Aria T'Loak's mercenaries to kill the wounded before they could transform into Adjutants; Aria, offended by his open disrespect for her authority, struck him with a biotic attack. He was kept in chains on the station while Aria and Petrovsky oversaw Omega's defense from the Elbrus.

Ashe's imprisonment was part of his own plan. He killed his taunting guards, contacted the Illusive Man to say he was ready for "the real battle for Omega," slaughtered everyone in Omega Control to prevent any alert of incoming Cerberus reinforcements, and broadcast a declaration of coup demanding that all of Omega submit to Cerberus. After Aria was taken beyond the Omega 4 Relay to Avernus Station and presumed dead, Omega's gangs went to war and Ashe commanded the Cerberus forces attempting to secure the station. Aria returned, rallied the gangs against Cerberus, and Petrovsky resumed command, criticizing Ashe's disregard for Cerberus and civilian casualties.

The rift between Petrovsky and Ashe came to a head when Ashe released an Adjutant to attack Aria and her men inside the Afterlife Club. Petrovsky was outraged at the danger to the whole station; Ashe called the losses acceptable. As they fought, Aria flung the Adjutant at them; it infected Ashe. As he began to transform, Ashe asked Petrovsky what he would do, and Petrovsky, saying he would protect his men, shot Ashe in the head. In his report to the Illusive Man, Petrovsky claimed Ashe was missing and assumed to have met the end he deserved.
