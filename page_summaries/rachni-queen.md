Rachni queens were the matriarchs of the hive-minded, insectoid rachni, the largest of their species at about fifty feet long. They guided the workers and soldiers of their broods with a telepathic "song" that also let them speak through "receptive" beings of other species, and they carried the genetic memory of their mothers, effectively being born with the previous hive's collective knowledge. Queens could lay eggs even with no brood warrior present, carrying their fathers' genetic information, and could produce a colony's worth of eggs within days. They lived in deep underground nests on harsh worlds, which made it impossible for the Citadel Council to negotiate with them during the Rachni Wars; the krogan ultimately infiltrated the nests, killed the queens on every world, and drove the rachni to extinction.

In Mass Effect, a rachni vessel escaped the war and drifted with cryo-suspended eggs. Binary Helix brought one egg to Noveria expecting a worker to clone into an army, but it hatched a queen. When the corporation removed her newborns to train them, the rachni went insane without her song and heavily damaged Peak 15. Matriarch Benezia, on Saren Arterius's orders, used biotics to take the Mu Relay coordinates from the queen's genetic memory. After Shepard defeated Benezia, the queen appealed for forgiveness through a commando's body, implying a "sour yellow note" from space had "hushed one voice after another." Shepard could kill her with the lab's acid tanks or free her; freed, she fled into Noveria's wilds, and the Council warned she might threaten the Citadel again in a few generations.

In Mass Effect 2, if freed, a newscast reported rachni-design scout ships, and the queen sent thanks through an unnamed asari courier on Illium who had been saved by the rachni after a crash, promising the queen's aid "against the coming darkness." If killed, a report noted the Noveria cloning facility yielded no viable samples.

In Mass Effect 3, if she survived, the queen was captured by the Reapers in 2186 on Utukku, grown much larger and proving highly resistant to indoctrination (a "maddening sour note"), so the Reapers restrained her physically while converting her young into Ravagers. Shepard could free her for her support, at the cost of sacrificing Aralakh Company to cover her escape; a loyal Grunt leading Aralakh survived alone with severe injuries. Left behind, she would "embrace the silence." If the real queen had died in 2183, the Reapers built an artificial "Breeder" queen; freed, this Breeder later betrayed the Alliance Engineering Corps, and left behind she raged at Shepard through krogan corpses.
