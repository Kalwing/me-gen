The M-6 Carnifex was a heavy pistol in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3 and a pistol in Mass Effect: Andromeda. Highly accurate and lethal, it was effective against armor but weak against shields and biotic barriers, and in Mass Effect 2 it upgraded the M-3 Predator. It was a favored sidearm of mercenary leaders and Eclipse tech specialists, expensive but powerful, its marketing featuring a charging krogan and the slogan "Don't you wish Carnifex was at your side?"

In Mass Effect 2 Mordin Solus gave the Carnifex to Shepard on Omega during his recruitment, saying it had been recovered from a dead Blue Suns mercenary. Usable squadmates included Jack, Jacob, Miranda, Mordin, Tali, Kasumi and Liara. It dealt more damage than the Predator but fired slower with more recoil and less ammo, and was the best heavy pistol for squadmates.

In Mass Effect 3 it was found during Arrae: Ex-Cerberus Scientists and was one of the more powerful heavy pistols, accurate to medium range and usable as a lightweight sniping weapon with a scope mod. It was categorized as rare in multiplayer. In Andromeda it was described as the preferred choice of Milky Way mercenaries. Carnifex means "butcher" or "executioner" in Latin, and the pistol bore "Magnus" on its side. The M-77 Paladin and M-11 Suppressor were variants. During the final Mass Effect 3 mission, after Harbinger's attack, Shepard was left with only an unmodified Carnifex with unlimited ammunition; the game also had Shepard threaten Mordin with a Carnifex when trying to persuade him to sabotage the genophage cure.
