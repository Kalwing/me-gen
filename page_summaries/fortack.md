Fortack was the Lord High Researcher of Clan Urdnot and the owner of Fortack's Database on Tuchanka. He was forced to kill his predecessor before gaining the title.

If Urdnot Wrex led Clan Urdnot, Fortack complained to Commander Shepard that his talents were wasted on research into "things that don't explode," such as crop genetics and medical improvements that he felt could simply be bought from the salarians. If Urdnot Wreav led the clan instead, Fortack worked on weapons, saying there was always room for more on Tuchanka. In either case he welcomed Grunt after his rite of passage into Clan Urdnot, or Shepard if serving as Grunt's krantt, and noted that the Shaman had granted the customary upgrades for completing the Rite while taking credit that Fortack felt was due to him.
