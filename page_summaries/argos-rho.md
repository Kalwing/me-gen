Argos Rho was a star cluster in the Milky Way, located relatively close to Gemini Sigma, though the mass relay route between them required passing through three other clusters, making the practical travel distance far greater than a straight-line measurement. In Mass Effect 3 it connected via mass relay to the Horse Head Nebula and the Shadow Sea. The cluster was generally ignored by the major galactic powers, largely because the vast majority of its planets were uninhabitable and unsuitable for colonization. This obscurity allowed a wide range of factions, including pirates, Cerberus, and the geth, to operate out of the cluster largely unhindered.

The cluster contained the systems Gorgon, Hydra, and Phoenix, whose known worlds included Camaron, Sharblu, Canrum, Intai'sei, Tuntau, and the location Pinnacle Station. Its known systems were named after creatures from Greek mythology. Argos Rho hosted numerous missions and assignments across the trilogy, including "Citadel: Kakliosaur Fossil," the Pinnacle Station combat missions and convoy assignment, "Wrex: Family Armor," and several UNC assignments such as "UNC: Prothean Data Discs" and "UNC: Asari Writings." This page is largely a navigational and location reference with limited narrative prose.
