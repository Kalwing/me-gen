Short page. A Mass Effect 3 multiplayer character kit (the reptilian drell as a biotic Adept class), consisting almost entirely of gameplay stats and tactics. The only lore content notes that drell have dense muscle tissue granting wiry strength, unique hyoid throat bones that let them vocalize above human hearing range and make them very hard to strangle, and that although they lack the durability of other races they are fast and accurate. In-game the kit used Reave, Pull, Cluster Grenade and Fitness, and had the weakest starting barrier of all Adepts.
