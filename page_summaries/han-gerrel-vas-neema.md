Admiral Han'Gerrel vas Neema was a member of the quarian Admiralty Board and commander of the Heavy Fleet. He had a long history of provoking trouble along the turian border. A committed advocate of reclaiming the quarian homeworld, he argued that the Migrant Fleet had the largest fleet in the galaxy yet did nothing with it; Tali'Zorah countered that the ships might be needed against the Reapers, and he replied the quarians would need a homeworld to shelter non-combatants. Daro'Xen vas Moreh derisively called him "an aging warship," and he in turn dismissed Admiral Zaal'Koris vas Qwib Qwib as a "whining suit-wetter."

Han'Gerrel was a friend of Tali's father, Rael'Zorah, having served with him aboard the gunship Yaska before their Pilgrimage. During a batarian raid that killed six of ten crew, the two, left alone on the bridge and under orders to hold position, disobeyed to recover a tramp freighter; they received medals and were sent on Pilgrimage early. During Tali's treason trial on the Migrant Fleet, Han'Gerrel sympathized with her and believed her innocent. He was the one person Shepard and Tali could confide in about the nature of Rael's experiments on the Alarei; despite his pro-war stance the experiments horrified him, and he vowed to keep the truth quiet to protect what Rael had wanted for the fleet.

Han'Gerrel was among the admirals who pushed for war against the geth, driving the synthetics to ally with the Reapers and trapping the Migrant Fleet within the Perseus Veil. When Shepard helped disable a geth dreadnought, Han'Gerrel ordered the fleet to press the attack on the geth flagship while Shepard's squad was still aboard. Back on the Normandy, Hackett and Shala'Raan condemned his recklessness, Raan threatening a treason charge. Shepard could hear his explanation or punch him and send him off the ship. He later clashed with Raan over comm about fleet maneuvering. After the Reaper controlling the geth was destroyed, Han'Gerrel ordered an all-out attack on the disabled geth fleet; whether he broke off depended on Shepard having earned the quarians' trust, and if not, the assault destroyed the entire Migrant Fleet.
