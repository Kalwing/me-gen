The Director was a Cerberus operative in charge of the Barn space station and its experiments on Reaper technology, as depicted in Mass Effect: Infiltrator. He was also responsible for abducting numerous test subjects of various species for those experiments.

During a Cerberus experiment, the Director turned against fellow operative Randall Ezno. Ezno had been implanted with indoctrination technology and adapted to it faster than other Cerberus soldiers, but the indoctrination process failed. The Director judged Ezno a liability, sent the Barn's security forces against him, and abducted Ezno's handler, Inali Renata, for experimentation. After capturing Inali, the Director cut Randall off from the organization, and Randall vowed to shoot him.

Amid the chaos of prisoners escaping due to Randall's actions, the Director personally supervised the execution of one escapee, a turian who had inflicted considerable casualties, watching and laughing with subordinates as a turret was deployed on the prisoner's exposed position. After Randall fought through the X1 Wing, the Director unleashed X1, a giant mutated krogan outfitted with geth weaponry. Randall defeated X1 and escaped the Barn to a nearby desert planet, where the Director deployed something more powerful still: Inali herself. After defeating Inali and choosing to shoot or spare her, Randall swore to hunt down and kill the Director, who escaped to parts unknown. The Director interacted with Randall mostly via holoscreens and was seen in person only twice, briefly.
