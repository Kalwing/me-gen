UNC: Prothean Data Discs was a collection assignment in Mass Effect focused on recovering ancient Prothean data discs, a key subject of research into Prothean culture. The assignment began when Commander Shepard discovered a data disc anywhere in the galaxy. There were ten discs in total, but only seven had to be collected to complete the assignment, which then chained into UNC: Collection Complete. The discs were described as extremely fragile, with intact ones rare and usually found inside Prothean data recording devices; scientists had not yet found a way to transcribe their contents, but the discs were regarded as the most promising source of information on Prothean science and culture. The original completion summary erroneously read "a complete collection of asari artifacts," corrected to "Prothean" in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. The page otherwise consists of location tables.
