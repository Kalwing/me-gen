After Priority: The Citadel II, Shepard overheard an angry turian veteran and a human civilian in the Presidium Commons discussing a wounded batarian terrorist named Ghorek who was being held on the Citadel; overhearing the conversation activated the mission, and the Spectre Terminal updated with a data entry giving Ghorek's location. Shepard went to the Docks: Holding Area, to the back of Cargo Hold C among many batarian refugees, where a turian nurse stood outside a refugee accommodation. After the nurse described Ghorek's poor chances of survival, Shepard was allowed to speak with him. Ghorek explained that his terrorist attacks had been driven by a desire to avenge his homeworld, the batarian colony Aratoht, which was destroyed in the Alpha Relay explosion; his motivation was the same whether or not Shepard had carried out Arrival, though the exchange was more personal on both sides if Shepard had been there. Ghorek asked Shepard for mercy and to be taken off life support. Shepard could either disable the life support, after which Ghorek thanked the Commander before dying, or let the nurse administer a sedative. Taking Ghorek off life support drew no reaction from the nurse. Completion granted 5 Reputation, 210 experience, and 5,000 credits.
