Disruptor Ammo was an ammo power in the single-player modes of Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. The ammunition pulsed electromagnetically to tear through shields and shred synthetic targets, lasting until overridden by another ammo power.

In Mass Effect 2 it dealt bonus damage to all shields and to the health of synthetics such as mechs and geth, but not to synthetic armor or to organic health. Its three base ranks scaled the shield and synthetic damage bonus from +20% to +40%, added a synthetic overload (incapacitation) effect at rank 2, and added a weapon-overheat effect on unprotected infantry at rank 3. Rank 4 evolved it into Heavy Disruptor Ammo, which caused dying synthetics to critically overload and explode (+60% bonus), or Squad Disruptor Ammo, which shared the power with the whole squad. It was especially useful at high difficulties and for shield-vulnerable classes like the Vanguard and Adept; tougher enemies such as YMIR Mechs and shotgun krogan resisted the overheat effect. It was available to the Soldier, Infiltrator, and Zaeed Massani, who could share it via the squad evolution.

In Mass Effect 3 it became more versatile, damaging barriers as well as shields and, at higher ranks, the health of all enemy types, though it could no longer disable enemy weapons. Its six ranks scaled health damage from +5% to +15% and shield/barrier damage from +20% to +60%, with branching choices for squad bonus (50% effectiveness), ammo capacity, headshot damage, and improved stun odds. Its electrocution stun worked on almost all infantry regardless of protection, with exceptions such as Husks, Abominations, and Phantoms. It could prime enemies for Tech Bursts and had a limited ability to bypass shield gates because ammo-power bonus damage was applied before raw weapon damage. In single-player it was available to the Infiltrator, Soldier, Ashley Williams, and Zaeed Massani, and had no multiplayer version.
