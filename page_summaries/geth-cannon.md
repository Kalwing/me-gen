The Geth Cannon was a weapon from the armament of geth starships. A crashed geth ship on the planet Aite was discovered by Cerberus and repurposed as Prometheus Station, which supplied geth subjects for Project Overlord. After the project went wrong, the Overlord VI seized control of the station's cannon and used it to defend the ship against Commander Shepard's squad in the Mass Effect 2 DLC "Overlord." The massive cannon could destroy the M-44 Hammerhead in a single hit and had high health plus an externally powered shield that had to be brought down before the turret could be targeted. Shepard had to stay mobile, watch the red targeting lines, use one shot to drop each external generator's shield, and then destroy the cannon with the Hammerhead's missiles.
