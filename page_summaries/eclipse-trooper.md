Short page. Eclipse Troopers were the front-line soldiers of the Eclipse mercenary group and its most common unit, exclusively human and salarian (asari served instead as Sisterhood Initiates). Human Troopers carried M-8 Avenger assault rifles for sustained fire against armor, shields and barriers, while salarian Troopers used M-4 Shuriken machine pistols and strafed more. Troopers preferred medium-range cover and rarely closed to melee. Those hired by Donovan Hock on Bekenstein could also use a Flashbang Grenade. They gained light shielding on Hardcore and Insanity. The rest of the page is enemy tactics.
