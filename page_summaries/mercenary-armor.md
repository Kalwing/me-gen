Mercenary Armor was a combat hardsuit produced by Ariake Technologies in Mass Effect, available for humans, turians and krogan in light, medium and heavy versions. It was Urdnot Wrex's default armor. It used ablative weave technology, which increased shield capacity but lowered protection against biotic and tech damage relative to other armors. Light Mercenary Armor (human, turian) scaled from 12 to 30 damage protection, 91 to 325 shields and 12 to 32 tech/biotic protection across levels I to X. Medium Mercenary Armor (human, turian, krogan) ran 24 to 42 damage protection, 117 to 351 shields and only 1 to 21 tech/biotic protection. Heavy Mercenary Armor (human, krogan) ran 40 to 58 damage protection, 156 to 390 shields and 0 to 10 tech/biotic protection, illustrating the ablative weave trade-off of high shields for poor tech/biotic defense.
