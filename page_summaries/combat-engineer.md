The Combat Engineer was a class of Cerberus soldier introduced in Mass Effect 3, a specialized support unit that assisted primary forces while staying out of the line of fire. Combat Engineers wore lighter, more mobile modified-mesh armor than typical Cerberus combatants and, from cover and out of sight, focused on setting up and maintaining Turrets and on repairing mechanical units or armored allies. The Alliance specifically warned troops to watch for their Turrets, which could mow down an entire squad while the engineer escaped.

They were armed only with an M-5 Phalanx heavy pistol, which they used even at point-blank range as a last resort, had no melee attack, carried shields, and could evade by rolling. They could also repair the armor of their Turrets and of Atlas mechs.

Tactically, after dropping a Combat Engineer's shields, shooting the Turret stored on his back critically damaged or killed him and caused an explosion that could harm nearby units. Sabotage could hack a deployed Turret against him, and staggering him or catching him in Stasis while he set up a Turret made it explode, dealing heavy damage. On higher difficulties their repair rate could outpace incoming damage, so singling them out first was advised. During the campaign an enemy engineer could inadvertently aid Shepard by repairing a commandeered Atlas, potentially keeping it usable indefinitely.
