The Geth Plasma Shotgun was a geth shotgun appearing in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. The three-barrelled weapon fired miniature cluster rounds of superconducting projectiles with a longer range than standard shotguns. A two-stage trigger allowed either quick-fire capacitor shots or a charge-and-release attack that electrified the projectiles; on impact the rounds fragmented and arced electricity, flash-converting air to conductive plasma, overloading shields and barriers and causing heavy trauma to unarmored targets. It was designed as an "unshotgun" to let players deal reliable damage at longer ranges, with the overcharge feature added to preserve a shotgun's heavy-hitting feel.

In Mass Effect 2 it was acquired through the Firepower Pack DLC, prompting an e-mail from the Illusive Man noting that a Cerberus cell had acquired the weapon from the geth ("A cell of ours acquired an interesting weapon from the geth... firing superconductors that create plasma on impact"); in the Legendary Edition it could be bought from Kenn's Salvage on Omega. It could be used by Grunt, Jack, Jacob and Tali. It fired three plasma balls rather than the standard eight pellets, with reduced damage on the second and third if they struck the same target. A full charge expended two rounds, could be held indefinitely while sprinting or using powers, and paired well with Adrenaline Rush or Tactical Cloak since releasing a charge did not break cloak. It dealt no bonus headshot damage.

In Mass Effect 3 it was found during Priority: Rannoch (or bought from Elkoss Combine afterward) and was a rare multiplayer weapon needing no expansion. It dealt extremely high damage at all ranges but fired travel-time projectiles that had to lead moving targets, with basic tracking toward a reticule-locked enemy, making it effective against Phantoms. It reliably staggered infantry, had a high chance of bypassing Guardian shields, ignored armor damage reduction and the shield gate, and could not score headshots, in contrast to the otherwise similar Graal Spike Thrower. Charged shots reached double damage at maximum charge.
