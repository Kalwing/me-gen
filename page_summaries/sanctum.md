Sanctum was the second planet of the Decoris System in Sigurd's Cradle, in the Milky Way. Nominally a garden world, it proved the spacer adage that the label did not guarantee comfort: freezing ice storms swept its poles and temperate zones, leaving only a narrow, dry, windy strip of habitable land along the equator that supported the planet's minimal terrestrial plant life. Sanctum had not developed land animals, though invertebrates grew large in its pelagic seas. Carbon dioxide levels could reach 5,000 parts per million during thermal inversions, so travelers were advised to carry breath masks and consult the Sanctum World Weather Service. Piracy was at a fourteen-year global high, prompting security advisories.

Mining, called "ice cracking" anywhere but the equator, was the backbone of the economy and drew a gruff, hardy population of miners, mercenaries, and company men. The planet was rich in platinum and palladium deposits and in boron, used locally in semiconductor doping; scanning also yielded iridium and element zero. The capital was named Vulpes. Its corporate factions had learned that Cerberus was involved in the planet's finances, and Systems Alliance intelligence agents embedded in the corporate strata quietly urged the companies to confront Cerberus, but financial bickering kept the major stakeholders from acting against the Illusive Man.

Reaching Sanctum required completing N7: MSV Strontium Mule in Mass Effect 2. The planet hosted the missions Citadel: Alien Medi-Gel Formula and N7: Cerberus Lab and the assignment N7: Blue Suns Base. The Mass Effect 3 multiplayer map Firebase Glacier was located on Sanctum.
