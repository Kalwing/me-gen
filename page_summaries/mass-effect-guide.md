This page is a walkthrough for the main-plot missions of the first Mass Effect game, excluding optional assignments and the DLC packs Bring Down the Sky and Pinnacle Station. It notes that Legendary Edition bundles Bring Down the Sky by default but not Pinnacle Station, and revises graphics and combat.

The structure covers the Prologue (character creation, Normandy briefing, and Eden Prime) and Chapter 1: Citadel, where Shepard recruits most of the squad and must expose Saren Arterius's treachery. Chapters 2-4 (Finding Liara, Noveria, and Feros) can be done in any order; Noveria pits Shepard against geth and Matriarch Benezia, and Feros against geth, krogan, and the Thorian. Completing any two of the three unlocks Chapter 5: Virmire, where Shepard thwarts Saren's plan, converses with an ancient enemy, and makes a fateful choice. The endgame runs through Chapter 6: Lockdown (the Council grounds the Normandy, Anderson helps steal it), Chapter 7: Ilos (locked progression, Trench Run), and Chapter 8: the final battle against Saren. The epilogue resolves the Council's fate and the human representative choice between Udina and Anderson.

The guide also covers a UNC: Rogue VI assignment on Luna that unlocks specialization at level 20, and level caps and repeat careers: the original game capped a first playthrough at level 50, requiring a second career to reach 60, while Legendary Edition removed the first-playthrough cap and improved XP scaling. Characters carry over into Mass Effect 2 with starting bonuses, and decisions are remembered.
