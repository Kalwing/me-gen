Citadel: Asari Widow was a side mission in Mass Effect 3, acquired by investigating a PDA in the Ardat-Yakshi monastery during the mission Kallini: Ardat-Yakshi Monastery. The PDA was in the first chamber with an interior balcony reached from the start of that mission, the same room where Shepard could encounter Samara if she was alive. Unlike most item-retrieval missions in the game, failing to obtain the PDA permanently locked out the mission, as no equivalent could be bought from Spectre Requisitions.

The PDA held the last message of Tashya Porae, an asari commando who was badly wounded when she recorded it and included some personal regrets; the message was meant for her bondmate, Weshra, and could be replayed for clarity after the mission was acquired. Back on the Citadel, Shepard found Weshra, labeled "Asari Widow," standing alone on the stairs between Meridian Place Market and Apollo's Cafe in Presidium Commons, where she had been trying without success to learn her bondmate's whereabouts or even her assignment, which was classified. Shepard spoke to her to deliver the bad news and give her closure. Completing the mission granted 90 experience and 5 Reputation.
