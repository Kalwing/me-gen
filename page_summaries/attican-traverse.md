The Attican Traverse was a broad region of the Milky Way lying between Citadel-controlled space and the lawless Terminus Systems, regarded as the true frontier of the Citadel's domain. It contained many worlds once inhabited by the Protheans and numerous mass relays. Colonies established there faced constant raids and attacks from the nearby Terminus Systems, but the abundance of resource-rich worlds and Prothean ruins continued to attract colonists.

Although the Citadel officially claimed the region, forces from the Terminus Systems had seized many of its planets and systems. Unwilling to risk all-out war with the Terminus, the Citadel adopted a policy of military non-interference in the Traverse. The Council did, however, permit the Systems Alliance to expand there, since the large Alliance Navy could stabilize unsettled regions without the Council becoming directly involved. The region encompassed clusters including the Armstrong Nebula, Caleston Rift, Hades Nexus, Hawking Eta, Ismar Frontier, Kepler Verge, Maroon Sea, Ninmah Cluster, Nubian Expanse, Sentry Omega, Shadow Sea, and Styx Theta; the homeworld Suen; colonies such as Horizon, Mindoir, Ontarom, and Zorya; and space stations including Hephaestus Station and Jarrahe Station. The name "Attican" derives from Attica, the historical Greek region containing Athens.
