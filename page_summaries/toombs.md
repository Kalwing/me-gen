Corporal Toombs was one of the Alliance marines at Akuze and a survivor of the thresher maw tragedy there. Cerberus had set the thresher maws on his unit as an experiment; when Toombs was found alive afterward, Cerberus was delighted and hauled him off for further study, subjecting him to horrific ordeals over years, including injection with thresher maw acid.

Toombs eventually escaped confinement and, deeply traumatized, began systematically hunting the scientists responsible, hiring mercenary help. If Commander Shepard investigated a series of dead Alliance scientists, they crossed paths with Toombs on Ontarom as he was about to kill the last one, Dr. Wayne. Shepard could talk him down by promising a public trial for Wayne and psychological treatment for Toombs; allow Toombs his revenge; kill Wayne personally under Spectre authority; or shoot Toombs to save the scientist, then send the Fifth Fleet to take Wayne for trial. If Shepard had the Sole Survivor background and spared Wayne while stopping Toombs, Toombs noted that the vids said Shepard was the only one to escape Akuze, said "who am I to argue?" and killed himself.

If Toombs survived the 2183 encounter, he sent Shepard a message in 2185 titled "What the hell?" after learning of Shepard's ties to Cerberus, feeling betrayed and promising retribution: "I've got my own merc team now, Shepard. And I kill any Cerberus team I can find. If I run into you, don't expect any different." Galactic News reports could describe his activities: if both he and Wayne survived, an independent inquiry against Cerberus proceeded on their testimony; if Wayne died, the inquiry dismissed Toombs' claims as those of a disturbed man lacking evidence.
