The Protheans were an ancient race that vanished roughly 50,000 years ago after building a galaxy-wide empire. They were long credited with creating the Citadel and the mass relays, though in truth they only inherited that Reaper technology. Anthropoidal in form, they had two pairs of dual-pupiled eyes, three pairs of nostrils, a layered head carapace, three-fingered hands, and a flanging voice like turians. A unique trait was an experiential exchange system: by touch, a Prothean could absorb another's memories, skills, or language. They had quad-strand DNA, some resistance to low radiation, and at least some members wielded potent biotics.

After achieving spaceflight, the Protheans discovered ruins of the earlier inusannon and learned mass effect physics, gaining a centuries-long secret advantage. Facing a hostile machine intelligence, they decided all organic life must unite under their rule; resisting species were conquered and assimilated, coming to call themselves "Prothean." United, they held off the machines in the Metacon War. They observed and cultivated primitive species including asari, hanar, humans, quarians, salarians and turians, intending eventually to offer them the "choice" of joining the empire.

The Reapers arrived around 48,000 BCE, entering through the Citadel, decapitating the government, disrupting the relay network, and using Citadel census data to hunt every Prothean. Their unified empire and single military doctrine became fatal weaknesses. Indoctrinated Protheans acted as sleeper agents. Subject races like the densorin and the AI zha'til were destroyed; the Protheans themselves annihilated the zha'til by sending a star supernova. Some Protheans built a Crucible from designs left by prior civilizations, but indoctrinated builders sabotaged it. After centuries the Reapers killed or enslaved every Prothean and withdrew into dark space.

Survivors persisted in stasis. On Ilos, the Conduit research team under Ksad Ishan went dark; the VI Vigil cut power to non-essential pods until only about a dozen scientists remained, who later used the Conduit to reach the Citadel and alter the keepers' signal to trap the Reapers. On Eden Prime, nearly a million warriors and their commander Javik were stored; traitors betrayed the site, most pods were destroyed, and the VI Victory kept only Javik's pod alive. Shepard could revive Javik in 2186. Meanwhile the Reapers repurposed captured Protheans into the Collectors, directed by Harbinger to abduct humans for a Human-Reaper.

The Protheans held evolution, the "Cosmic Imperative," as the universe's driving force. Their influence shaped the asari (uplifted as a possible failsafe), humans (via the Mars archive) and the hanar, who worship them as the "Enkindlers." Notable Protheans included Javik, avatar of vengeance, and the guides Janiri and Lucen of asari myth. Prothean tech included mind-to-mind beacons, memory shards, indoctrination detection, kinetic barrier grids, the Particle Rifle, the flagship Penumbra Apex, and the Conduit.
