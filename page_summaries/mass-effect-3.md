Mass Effect 3, developed by BioWare, was the final game in the original Mass Effect trilogy, released March 6, 2012 in North America (later that month elsewhere) for PC, PlayStation 3, and Xbox 360 after a delay from a planned "Holiday 2011" window. PC versions required the EA App (formerly Origin); a Steam release came June 11, 2020. The game shipped in an N7 Collector's Edition, was bundled in the Mass Effect Trilogy boxed set (2012), and was remastered in 2021 as part of Mass Effect Legendary Edition, which also consolidated nearly all DLC. A February 14, 2012 demo featured a level on Earth at the start of the Reaper invasion with David Anderson as a temporary squadmate, then a mission on Sur'Kesh with Garrus and Liara defending a fertile krogan female from Cerberus. EA also sent "space edition" copies aloft on weather balloons from six cities.

The plot is the conclusion of Commander Shepard's story: Earth burns under a Reaper invasion and Shepard must rally the galaxy's civilizations to retake it. Player choices across the whole trilogy (over 1000 imported variables) shape which civilizations survive and which endings are available; a Shepard who died in Mass Effect 2 cannot be imported, and squadmates lost in the Suicide Mission do not return. Squad members number fewer than in Mass Effect 2, by design, focusing on deeper relationships: Kaidan Alenko or Ashley Williams, Tali'Zorah, Liara T'Soni, Garrus Vakarian, James Vega, Javik (From Ashes DLC), and EDI. New locations include Mars, Menae, Rannoch, Sur'Kesh, Thessia, a Geth Dreadnought, and Cronos Station, alongside returning ones like the Citadel and Tuchanka. The Normandy SR-2 returns, rebuilt by the Systems Alliance.

Gameplay, per designers Christina Norman and Casey Hudson, added richer RPG features, faster and more agile combat with rolls and heavy melee, smarter coordinated enemy AI and instant-kill "sync kills," any-class weapon use governed by a weight/cooldown system, returning weapon mods, more prominent grenades, larger skill trees with a level cap of 60, and expanded power combos. Mining was replaced by Search and Rescue; there were no vehicle segments. Optional Kinect voice controls and Roleplaying/Action/Story play styles were added. The Galaxy at War system tracks Galactic Readiness via War Assets and four-player co-op multiplayer (dropped from Legendary Edition). Clint Mansell scored the game, with Sam Hulick returning; Jack Wall did not. Single-player DLC included From Ashes, Extended Cut, Firefight Pack, Leviathan, Groundside Resistance Pack, Omega, and Citadel.
