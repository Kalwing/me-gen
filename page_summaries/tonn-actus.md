Tonn Actus was a turian pirate and profiteer who specialized in collecting krogan armor and artifacts, especially items from the Krogan Rebellions. Urdnot Wrex hated him because Wrex's family armor, handed down for generations, was part of Actus' collection.

Wrex had intended to hunt Actus through the Argos Rho cluster to recover the armor and kill the pirate personally, but Actus kept several scattered bases and Wrex's travels with Commander Shepard delayed the search. Wrex's best lead placed Actus on the planet Tuntau in the Phoenix system. Shepard could travel to Tuntau and raid Actus' hidden base; Actus was killed in the resulting firefight, letting Wrex reclaim his armor. In combat Actus carried an assault rifle and a shotgun, used Immunity, Shield Boost, and Carnage, and began the fight positioned on a top balcony near a fuel tank whose detonation could kill him quickly. It was possible to raid the base and kill him without Wrex present or without ever formally taking up the assignment, and still recover the armor and discuss it with Wrex afterward aboard the Normandy.
