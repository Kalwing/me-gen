Mass Effect 3: From Ashes was a DLC pack for Mass Effect 3, accidentally revealed February 21, 2012 when Microsoft made it available over Xbox Live, and released alongside the game on March 6, 2012. In its story, Cerberus attacks Eden Prime and takes over a human colony while stretched-thin Alliance forces cannot respond; intel shows Cerberus is after a Prothean artifact it has found, and Shepard infiltrates the colony to recover it. The artifact proves to be Javik, the last surviving Prothean, who joins as a permanent squad member and provides lore about the last days of the Prothean empire before the Reapers wiped out the species.

Content comprised the squadmate Javik, two missions (Priority: Eden Prime and Eden Prime: Resistance Movement), the Prothean Particle Rifle, and a new alternate outfit for every squadmate. It was free with the N7 Collector's Edition and Digital Deluxe Edition, sold separately for 800 BioWare Points or $10.00 USD, included with the Wii U Special Edition, and is part of Mass Effect Legendary Edition.
