Shadow Broker Vanguards were the biotic experts of the Shadow Broker's private army, tasked with defending the Broker's interests. They were encountered by Shepard, Liara T'Soni and the squad during the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, appearing on Illium and at the Broker's base on Hagalaz.

They were armed with the M-27 Scimitar shotgun and could use Warp. Defensively they had moderate health and barriers bolstered by Tech Armor and Barrier. They typically used Warp to weaken a target before charging in, so the recommended approach was to keep them at range; at melee range, repeated melee attacks could stun-lock them, and Liara's Stasis was highly effective, letting the player finish them quickly once the effect ended.
