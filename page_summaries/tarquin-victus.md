Lieutenant Tarquin Victus was a turian officer in command of the 9th Platoon and the son of General, later Primarch, Adrien Victus. After Adrien became Primarch, he asked Commander Shepard to help retrieve his son and his missing platoon on Tuchanka, calling it a delicate political matter he could not detail. If Garrus was brought along, he suspected Tarquin's father had promoted him without merit, a frowned-upon act in turian culture.

Shepard found Tarquin wracked with guilt: his platoon had been decimated because he tried to imitate his father's clever tactics rather than use brute force, moving through narrow ruins to avoid casualties. When Reaper ground forces spotted them there was no room to maneuver, and the platoon was shot down, his attempt to avoid losses causing more. His mission had been to stop Cerberus from detonating a bomb the turians had planted in the Kelphic Valley after the Krogan Rebellions as a last-resort weapon should the krogan rise again; detonation would trigger all-out war between the krogan and turians. Shepard convinced Tarquin to fulfill his duty, which he agreed to do with Shepard's support.

During the disarming, Tarquin discovered an error prevented conventional deactivation and climbed the bomb to remove the detonator manually. All the locks holding it released except one, which could only be removed in a way that would take him with it. Telling Shepard "victory, at any cost," he removed the final piece and fell to his death, completing the mission. Garrus, if present, noted other turians would simply expect this of him but that "he never hesitated" and would be remembered for it. Back on the Normandy, Primarch Victus expressed sorrow and regret for not being honest sooner, but pride that his son died a hero with his men's respect, and gratitude that Shepard gave Tarquin a chance at redemption.
