Noveria was a small, frozen, barely habitable terrestrial world in the Pax System of the Horse Head Nebula, the second planet of its system, with most of its hydrosphere locked in massive glaciers. It was a privately chartered colony owned by the Noveria Development Corporation, a holding company funded by investment capital from two dozen high-technology development firms and administered by an Executive Board representing their interests. The investors built remote "hot labs" in isolated locations across the surface for research too dangerous or controversial to be performed elsewhere, since Noveria was technically not part of Citadel space and therefore exempt from Council law. By special arrangement, Citadel Special Tactics and Reconnaissance agents were granted extraterritorial privileges, though the Executive Board's commitment to that principle was uncertain. Its unique situation made Noveria the subject of many wild conspiracy theories.

Noveria featured the Skadi Mountains, where the isolated lab Peak 15 was situated, and the Aleutsk Valley, a pass between Port Hanshan and Peak 15. Other locales included an equatorial resort offering accredited asajura fields, bouncebacking, and diving. Known non-sapient lifeforms included the nathak. The name was derived from "Nova Siberia" (New Siberia).

In Mass Effect, Shepard investigated Noveria in 2183 during the hunt for Saren, pursuing Matriarch Benezia to Peak 15 through missions including Noveria: Geth Interest, Noveria: Matriarch Benezia, Noveria: Peak 15, Noveria: Rift Station, and Noveria: The Hot Labs, plus assignments Noveria: Espionage and Noveria: Smuggling. By Mass Effect 3 and the Reaper War, the planet drew significant attention: home to some of the galaxy's most advanced research, it was a magnet for Cerberus interest, and its mass relay was the transit point between the humans' Exodus Cluster and the start of salarian space, which the salarians were expected to defend to the utmost. Related content included N7: Cerberus Fighter Base and the multiplayer map Firebase White.
