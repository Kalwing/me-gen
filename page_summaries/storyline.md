This page is a chronological plot synopsis of the first-era Mass Effect fiction, running from the First Contact War through the events of Mass Effect.

In Mass Effect: Evolution (2157), during the turian invasion of Shanxi, mercenary Jack Harper and his colleagues Ben Hislop and Eva Coré captured turian General Desolas Arterius, who was hunting a Prothean artifact, the Arca Monolith. Contact with the Monolith killed Ben (who was resurrected as a "meta-turian") and altered Harper. Desolas planned to use the Monolith and a second one hidden beneath Temple Palaven to transform the entire turian race into obedient super-soldiers, until his brother Saren realized the devices were a Reaper de-evolutionary trap and destroyed the temple, killing Desolas. Harper, now believing a dark force would test humanity, founded Cerberus as the Illusive Man.

In Mass Effect: Revelation (2165), an attack on the Sidon AI research base sent scientist Kahlee Sanders on the run and drew Lieutenant David Anderson, retired hero Jon Grissom (Kahlee's father), and the turian Spectre Saren Arterius into pursuit of batarian financier Edan Had'dah and the missing Dr. Shu Qian. Saren destroyed the refinery, killed Qian and Had'dah, stole Qian's research, and discovered references to a warship called Sovereign; Anderson's Spectre candidacy was sabotaged by Saren's report. Interspersed Foundation and Homeworlds comic entries followed the Cerberus agent Rasa (also called Brooks), Kai Leng, a young Kaidan Alenko at BAaT, Kasumi Goto's fatal Bekenstein heist against Donovan Hock, James Vega's family, Mordin Solus's genophage modification work, and Tali'Zorah nar Rayya's disastrous Pilgrimage.

The Mass Effect section recapped Commander Shepard's game: the Eden Prime attack in which Saren killed Nihlus Kryik and Shepard touched a Prothean Beacon; Shepard becoming the first human Spectre; recruiting Garrus Vakarian, Urdnot Wrex, Tali and Liara T'Soni; the missions to Therum, Noveria (Matriarch Benezia and the Rachni Queen), Feros (the Thorian and the Cipher) and Virmire (Sovereign revealed as a Reaper, and the death of Kaidan or Ashley); the theft of the Normandy to reach Ilos and Vigil's revelation of the Citadel trap; and the Battle of the Citadel, ending with Saren's death, Sovereign's destruction, and Shepard's choice whether to save the Council.
