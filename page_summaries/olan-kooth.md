Olan Kooth, also known as Agent Zeta, was a former salarian Special Tasks Group operative and a powerful biotic obsessed with augmenting his power by any means. Discovered by the STG as a child with off-the-charts biotic aptitude, he was trained into one of their deadliest operatives. As an adult he developed a compulsion to boost his biotics and began experimenting on himself when military-grade bio-amps could not keep up; he became mentally unstable and the STG cut ties with him, after which he worked freelance. During his travels he found a set of mysterious spikes and incorporated pieces of the technology into his body, channeling his biotics to unprecedented levels but causing permanent, ultimately fatal nervous-system damage unless he learned to harness the tech. Seeking more such technology, he traced rumors to the quarian scientist Shio'Leth vas Novarra, who had ties to the Andromeda Initiative.

In Mass Effect: Discovery, Zeta replaced an overloaded bio-amp with help from three LOKI mechs, which were fried on activation. He tracked Shio to the lower Citadel wards, but the quarian escaped amid panic and Zeta's amp exploded, nearly killing them both. His pursuit took him to a planet where Shio's ship the Novarra ran a strip-mining camp; he began annihilating it with raw biotic energy to interrogate Shio's husband Viegle, was delayed by the biotic Luna Shanks (whom he threw off a cliff), slaughtered the Novarra militia by biotically breaking their bones, and tortured Shio's location out of Viegle. On Omega he cornered Shio, but a heavily armed group including a still-living Shanks triggered a biotic explosion that destroyed much of his cybernetics; Shanks pulled Shio away, Zeta impaled her with a pole, she fired an M-22 Eviscerator into his face leaving severe burns, and he escaped after another biotic outburst. Having extracted the location of the Kholas Array from Shio, Zeta went there believing it held the tech in his amps, but was besieged by geth platforms. Sanjin Vinnick persuaded him to help guard Shio against the geth, but Shio told him the geth had no relevant tech for organics. Refusing to answer to his birth name, and resigned that he could no longer contain his power, Zeta warned Vinnick and Shio to run before suffering a massive biotic meltdown.
