This page listed the achievements, also called Accomplishments or Trophies, awarded across the Mass Effect series for completing tasks or reaching milestones. Achievements earned in the original games did not carry over to Mass Effect Legendary Edition, and Legendary Edition achievements were viewed through the Origin launcher.

The original Mass Effect had 49 achievements worth 1200 Gamerscore and 47 trophies. Its achievements required at least three complete playthroughs to earn them all, and many granted permanent gameplay bonuses such as faster experience gain, increased weapon damage, extra shield strength, or unlocked bonus talents usable in later careers. Examples included Medal of Honor for completing one playthrough (which unlocked Hardcore difficulty and character levels 51-60), Distinguished Combat Medal for completing Hardcore (which unlocked Insanity and a Saren Arterius gamer pic), Charismatic for using Charm or Intimidate to talk down Wrex or convince Saren to shoot himself, the various Ally achievements tied to squadmates such as Ashley Williams, Kaidan Alenko, Urdnot Wrex, Garrus Vakarian, Tali'Zorah nar Rayya, and Liara T'Soni, and Scholar for finding all alien race Codex entries. DLC achievements covered Bring Down the Sky and Pinnacle Station.

Mass Effect 2 had 62 achievements worth 1355 Gamerscore and 59 trophies, including recruitment and loyalty achievements for each squadmate (each unlocking a bonus power), Suicide Mission for using the Omega 4 Relay, and No One Left Behind for keeping the whole team alive. DLC achievements covered Zaeed, Kasumi, Overlord, Lair of the Shadow Broker, and Arrival, the last including Covert Action for rescuing Dr. Amanda Kenson undetected. Mass Effect 3 had 68 achievements worth 1550 Gamerscore and 69 trophies, with cryptically named story achievements, combat and multiplayer challenges, and DLC achievements for From Ashes, Leviathan, Omega, and Citadel. Mass Effect Legendary Edition had 127 achievements worth 2915 Gamerscore, blending modified trilogy achievements with new cross-game ones. Mass Effect: Andromeda had 55 achievements worth 1000 Gamerscore and 56 trophies.
