Geth Primes were heavy geth platforms that served as command-and-control units on the battlefield, larger and more powerful than any other geth infantry platform and equipped with heavy armor, shields, specialized abilities and varied weapons. In the original Mass Effect a Prime stood twelve feet high, was recognizable by white armor and huge stature, carried a highly damaging pulse rifle that could also launch rockets, and possessed six levels of shielding. A Combat VI boosted the rate of fire, accuracy and damage of all nearby geth, making Primes priority targets. They were immune to Hacking and resistant to most biotic and tech talents, and were among the few Mass Effect enemies with Damping, which could put the entire squad's abilities, including First Aid, on cooldown. Their Distortion Rockets could one-shot unprotected characters. In the Legendary Edition Primes no longer used Distortion Rockets, instead advancing while firing their pulse rifles.

In Mass Effect 2 Primes were armed with the M-76 Revenant light machine gun, could melee, jam radar, fire siege pulses and deploy Combat Drones. They had high shields, armor and health, did not seek cover, and closed to point-blank range while moving quickly. Disruptor Ammo, Armor Piercing Ammo, Warp Ammo, Overload, Warp, Energy Drain and Incinerate wore down their defenses; once stripped they could be knocked down or frozen. Radar jamming served as an early warning of a nearby Prime.

In Mass Effect 3 Primes were redesigned with three eyes and a red color scheme. They deployed a Combat Drone and up to two Geth Turrets and wielded a semi-automatic Pulse Cannon that dropped shields in one or two hits and briefly stunned, firing at near-limitless range in bursts of three. They had heavy armor and regenerating shields, were immune to Pull and Throw, took 40% bonus headshot damage, and exploded violently on death. Sabotage worked on them even at full defenses. Aside from Legion and the Geth VI, Prime units were the only individual geth shown verbally interacting with Shepard, potentially at the conclusion of Priority: Rannoch and at the London FOB.
