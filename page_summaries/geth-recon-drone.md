The Geth Recon Drone was a geth combat drone and the first enemy encountered in the original Mass Effect, where a swarm of them shot down Corporal Jenkins on Eden Prime. Recon Drones had the lowest health of any enemy in the game, dying to a couple of shots, though their small size and high mobility made them hard to hit; their shielding regenerated but was negligible. Despite their fragility, their armament was as strong as normal weapons and could easily kill Shepard or squadmates. Tech talents were very effective: Sabotage, Overload and Damping instantly destroyed them, often catching several at once. As flying units they were immune to all biotics except Warp.

In Mass Effect 2, Geth Recon Drones appeared on Haestrom, providing covering fire for Geth Primes as Shepard's squad advanced toward Tali. They were armed with a pulse weapon similar to those of Geth Troopers and, like the Geth Hunter, were equipped with a form of Tactical Cloak. They normally lacked shields but gained light shielding on higher difficulties. They appeared in swarms of three or four escorting Geth Primes and were meant to be cleared before attacking the larger geth. AI Hacking appeared to work on unshielded drones but a hacked drone kept attacking the player. Once their shields were down they were extremely vulnerable to force-based powers such as area Throw or Concussive Shot and to freezing attacks; Singularity could target them even while shielded, and the Arc Projector, Overload, Energy Drain and Stasis were all effective against the swarms.
