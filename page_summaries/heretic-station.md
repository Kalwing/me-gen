Heretic Station was a geth space station in the Sea of Storms system of the Phoenix Massing cluster, accessible during the mission Legion: A House Divided. Once called Haratar by the quarians, it was stripped of its useful technology by the Migrant Fleet when they fled the Perseus Veil roughly 300 years earlier, leaving little more than a cold metal superstructure that was removed from star charts by 2050 CE. Scans indicated it had been reconstructed and upgraded over an effort of at least ten years, implying some geth existed outside the Veil before their attack on Eden Prime; needing only a fuel source, it could have remained hidden near the barren worlds of Tassrah or the elcor of the Salahiel system.

Called "Heretic Station" by Legion, it housed a geth data core capable of tightbeam broadcasting over vast distances. It stored roughly 6.6 million copies of geth software, most kept bodiless in servers and downloaded to legged platforms as needed, with about 2.4 million platforms present (1 million in storage). The station measured 20.5 km long, 11.3 km wide and tall, with 8 m of exterior armor and a gross weight of 1.55 billion metric tons. It was the setting of the mission Legion: A House Divided.
