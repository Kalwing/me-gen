Cryo Blast was a tech power in Mass Effect 2 and in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes. When activated, the user's omni-tool fired a mass of supercooled subatomic particles that snap-froze targets within a radius. In Mass Effect 2 it had three ranks raising freeze duration from three to five seconds, with a rank 4 choice between Deep Cryo Blast (seven-second freeze) and Full Cryo Blast (three-meter radius). It could freeze even large foes such as Geth Primes and YMIR Mechs but not enemies without health bars such as Gunships, Harbinger, or Scions. Frozen enemies took double weapon damage and increased melee damage, and health regeneration on vorcha or krogan was paused. It was available to the Engineer, Sentinel, and Mordin, whose maxed version had the shortest recharge of any squadmate ability outside ammo powers.

In Mass Effect 3 the power had six ranks with evolutions for recharge speed, duration, radius, speed reduction, Cryo Explosion, and Frozen Vulnerability. It dealt no damage; it froze unprotected (red bar) humanoids solid or chilled protected enemies to slow them, and reduced armored enemies' damage mitigation by 25% (50% with Frozen Vulnerability). Frozen enemies were primed for Cryo Explosions but not inherently more vulnerable to damage. Phantoms, Swarmers, and Seeker Swarms were immune to the freeze. Kaidan Alenko's Cryo Blast was the only freeze or chill ability available to NPC squadmates in Mass Effect 3. Single-player availability was the Engineer, Sentinel, and Kaidan; multiplayer availability was the Human Infiltrator, Quarian Engineer, and Turian Havoc Soldier.
