Mordin Solus was a salarian geneticist, professor and former Special Tasks Group operative, born in the 2150s, who had led the science team that designed and deployed the modified genophage on the krogan. He was a consequentialist who disliked killing without cause, affable, fast-talking, and prone to talking to himself. A botched dispersal mission on Tuchanka cost him facial scars and his right cranial horn; in Mass Effect: Foundation this injury occurred when he fought a female krogan praying to Kalros at the target site and was caught in his own team's explosives. Troubled by the project's ethics, he briefly turned to religion, suffered a crisis of faith, then built a clinic on Omega, defending it with STG-acquired mechs and killing intimidating Blue Suns and vorcha until even Aria T'Loak respected his ruthlessness.

In 2185 Shepard recruited him on Omega, where he was curing a Collector-engineered plague spread by vorcha against the Blue Suns; he gave Shepard the cure and asked help finding his assistant Daniel Abrams. Aboard the Normandy he ran the tech lab and, if prompted, sang "I am the very model of a scientist salarian." His loyalty mission concerned rescuing his former student Maelon from Clan Weyrloc on Tuchanka. He had a nephew, a promising young geneticist, whom he used as personal motivation.

In Mass Effect 3 Mordin returned as an STG consultant and Urdnot inside source, met on Sur'Kesh during the rescue of fertile krogan females. He commandeered the Med-bay to synthesize a genophage cure, treating Eve (aided by Maelon's data if saved), got along with krogan, played matchmaker for Joker and EDI and for Garrus and Eve, and sang the "Krogan Queen" song. He proposed using the salarian Shroud facility, which the salarians had secretly sabotaged years earlier at Dalatrass Linron's direction. During Priority: Tuchanka he and Eve finalized the cure while Kalros brought down a Reaper. At the Shroud's base Mordin insisted on manually fixing the sabotage, admitting "I made a mistake" and "Had to be me. Someone else might have gotten it wrong," and died in an explosion in the control room, singing "Scientist Salarian" if he had sung for Shepard before. He could be talked down only if Wreav led the krogan and Eve had died; otherwise Shepard could shoot him to stop the cure. If he died before Mass Effect 3, Padok Wiks filled his role. His name was added to the Normandy memorial wall if he died. Drew Karpyshyn wrote early passes before Trick Weekes became his main writer; Michael Beattie voiced him, with William Salyers taking over in Mass Effect 3.
