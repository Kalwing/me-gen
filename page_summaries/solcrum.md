Solcrum is the largest moon of the gas giant Notanban, located in the Grissom System of the Armstrong Nebula in the Milky Way. It is reached in Mass Effect during the assignment UNC: Geth Incursions. The moon carries a Level 1 Heat Hazard warning: it has only a trace atmosphere of krypton and xenon, a crust of various metals with sodium deposits, and a surface that is scorching hot and heavily irradiated by the blue giant star Grissom, making unprotected surface excursions fatal.

On the Normandy's approach to Notanban, its passive sensor array intercepted a fragmentary coded transmission from Solcrum's surface. Attempts to decrypt it failed, and the message did not match any software coding language used by the Citadel races.

The moon's points of interest include a geth headquarters (the objective of UNC: Geth Incursions), an asari capsule holding several artifacts, all worthless except one of Matriarch Dilinaga's writings (the collection UNC: Asari Writings), and a crashed probe that requires medium Electronics to salvage. Mineral deposits include thorium, uranium, and magnesium. Defenders encountered on the surface include the Geth Colossus, Destroyer, Juggernaut, Rocket Trooper, Stalker, and Sapper. Notanban is visible to the north and Grissom prominent to the west, and a moderate wind mildly reduces visibility. The operational area sits on rugged terrain with most points of interest at the tops or bottoms of hills.
