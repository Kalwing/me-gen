Kaidan Alenko was a human Sentinel and Systems Alliance Marine, initially a Staff Lieutenant and head of the Marine detail aboard the SSV Normandy and an original member of Commander Shepard's squad, and a possible love interest for a female Shepard. Born in 2151, he gained biotic potential after in utero element zero exposure when his mother was downwind of a Singapore transport crash. He was sent to BAaT ("Brain Camp") at Gagarin Station / Jump Zero, where he formed a close bond with a girl named Rahna and was fitted with the damaging L2 implants that left him with severe migraines. When the turian instructor Commander Vyrnnus broke Rahna's arm for reaching for water, Kaidan killed him in a biotic rage; BAaT was shut down and its records sealed, and Rahna became afraid of him. He returned to the Alliance in 2173 on his own terms, refusing further biotic training, and rose to Lieutenant with several commendations before transferring to the Normandy under Captain Anderson.

Kaidan was politically conservative, cautious, and loyal to the Alliance with no sympathy for biotic extremists. He was affected by the death of his friend Richard L. Jenkins on Eden Prime. On Virmire, Shepard could save him at the cost of Ashley Williams; if he survived he became determined to make the Council heed the Reaper threat. Between games he suffered survivor's guilt after the Normandy's destruction and Shepard's apparent death, rose to Staff Commander, and in 2185 investigated Cerberus on Horizon, where he was reunited with Shepard but refused to trust a Cerberus-aligned Commander, leaving after a bitter confrontation and later sending a conciliatory email. If romanced, Shepard kept his photo in her quarters.

In Mass Effect 3, having reached Major and command of the 1st Special Operations Biotic Company, Kaidan rejoined the fight (or Ashley took his place if he died on Virmire). He was severely injured by the Cerberus gynoid on Mars, recovered at Huerta Memorial Hospital, and accepted Spectre status from Udina. During the Cerberus coup he was ordered to protect the Councilors and confronted Shepard, unaware of Udina's collusion; Shepard could talk him down or he would be killed. If he lived he could rejoin the squad or serve as a war asset, sending a farewell email. Aboard the Normandy he spoke with Shepard about Cerberus, the war's toll, his mother separated from his father, Tali, the geth, Liara after the fall of Thessia, and the hunt for Kai Leng. He could resume or begin a romance with either a male or female Shepard, with dates at Apollo's Cafe and a Canadian meal in Shepard's apartment during the Citadel shore leave. If in the squad at the final push to the conduit, he could be killed by Harbinger if the war effort was insufficient; if he survived and Shepard fell, he placed Shepard's name on the Normandy memorial wall. He was voiced by Raphael Sbarge.
