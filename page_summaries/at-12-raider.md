The AT-12 Raider was a shotgun appearing in Mass Effect 3. It was carried by the batarian military's Special Intervention Unit and described as a semi-automatic weapon that loaded slowly but fired rapidly with tremendous force. It had a very short effective range, even for a shotgun, and a large, ovoid pellet spread in which none of its eight pellets travelled toward the center of the crosshair. Rather than eliminating recoil, its integral compensators made the recoil predictable and vertical.

In single-player the Raider was initially a pre-order bonus for players who ordered Mass Effect 3 through EA's Origin service, accompanied by an in-game email from James Vega describing it as having "hell of a kick" and claiming contacts he made on Omega were shipping a crate to the Normandy. It was later included on-disc in the Wii U version, made available on all platforms with Mass Effect 3: Leviathan, and could be found on a corpse during the mission Namakli: Leviathan. In multiplayer it was a rare weapon randomly awarded through item packs and required no expansion packs.

Mechanically it was a two-shot weapon whose strength was a very high firing rate, letting a user pop from cover and land two heavy shots, together capable of more damage than a single M-300 Claymore blast, before reloading. Its wide spread made it nearly useless outside very close range and impractical for headshots except at point-blank distance. Like other conventional shotguns it suffered little damage loss against shield gates, making it strong against shielded infantry, but it performed poorly against armored targets such as Brutes without mods, ammo powers, or armor-weakening powers like Warp or Cryo Blast. Adrenaline Rush's instant reload could be exploited to fire four shots in quick succession, and the Marksman power suited it well on the Quarian Marksman Soldier or Turian Soldier. It weighed the same as the Venom Shotgun and Graal Spike Thrower, heavier than the M-11 Wraith.
