Ereba was an asari merchant. In Mass Effect 2 she ran Memories of Illium, a store on Illium selling model starships and fish, and was the object of the affections of the love-struck krogan Charr, who stood nearby reciting poetry. Ereba had been in a relationship with Charr but broke it off because she was unsure what she wanted, and suspected he was mainly interested in her for children, noting that krogan affected by the genophage sometimes procreate only with asari. Commander Shepard could persuade her either to take Charr back or to forget him; in either case she gave Shepard a store discount. If Shepard reunited them, the couple traveled to Tuchanka to start a new life, where Ereba doubted the ramshackle settlement and the xenophobia of other krogan and wanted to leave, but Charr convinced her to stay under his protection.

By 2186, in Mass Effect 3, Ereba worked on the Citadel in the Presidium Commons as a merchant for Nos Astra Sporting Goods. If Shepard had kept her with Charr, she was married to him and mentioned to a customer that he was off fighting the Reapers. Should Shepard pursue a missing krogan team, the Commander could recover a dying message from Charr consisting mostly of poetry with references to an unborn child, which he asked be brought to Ereba; on hearing it played back she was shaken and excused herself. If Shepard had broken the couple up or ignored their troubles, Ereba was instead overheard discussing the war on Palaven, but Charr still left a message for her, which Shepard could deliver or withhold; she was still rattled if it was delivered.

In Mass Effect 2 cut content, Ereba had a few ambient voicelines, since removed, in which she was clearly annoyed at Charr's poetry.
