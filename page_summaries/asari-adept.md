The Asari Adept was a playable character kit in Mass Effect 3's cooperative multiplayer mode, classed as an Asari Justicar. The page opened with lore describing the asari as the most influential and respected sentient species in the galaxy, native to Thessia, the first to discover the Citadel, and long-serving galactic mediators whose proficiency with biotics made them dangerous despite limited physical strength, favoring hit-and-run tactics.

In gameplay the Asari Adept had 500 barrier and 500 health and access to the powers Stasis, Warp, Throw, and Fitness. Its melee options were a punch sequence, a Heavy Biotic Blast area pulse, and a Biotic Punch grab that dragged an enemy over cover for execution; its dodge was a Biotic Dash that consumed barrier strength. The kit was built around biotic explosions, since Warp and Throw were both long-ranged, enemy-seeking, ammo-free powers that could be chained for a detonation roughly every three seconds. An evolved Stasis, especially with the bubble evolution, could immobilize mobile enemies such as Phantoms and Geth Hunters, force Guardians to drop their shields, and pair well with lightweight sniper rifles like the M-11 Suppressor for easy headshots. Against Reapers, Stasis was less useful on armored units, but biotic explosions dealt double damage to armor and cleared Ravager Swarmers.
