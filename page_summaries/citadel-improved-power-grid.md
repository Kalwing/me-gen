Citadel: Improved Power Grid was a short fetch assignment available after Priority: Palaven and N7: Cerberus Attack. A technician named Alison, standing to the right of the Purgatory entrance on the Citadel with a datapad, complained about keeping the club's systems running; overhearing her activated the mission. The improved power grid schematics, based on a repurposed old krogan design, could be examined on a console near the cannon controls during N7: Cerberus Attack on Tuchanka, where they were temporarily unavailable while Cerberus cut the power and had to be taken before firing the cannon at the Cerberus cruiser, which ended that mission. Alternatively they could be bought from Spectre Requisitions in the Citadel Embassies for 1,000 base credits. Turning them in to Alison gave 5 Reputation, 30 experience, 1,000 credits, and an update to the Alliance Fifth Fleet War Asset; afterward Alison was overheard proposing to donate part of Purgatory's profits to a krogan charity in gratitude. The mission had to be completed before Priority: Tuchanka.
