The Particle Rifle was an assault rifle in Mass Effect 3. After the Reapers destroyed the Prothean Empire's warships, the Prothean resistance developed weapons that did not depend on intact supply lines; the Particle Rifle was a stripped-down, powerful assault rifle modified to fire without thermal clips or specialized ammunition. Alliance scientists noted it shared principles with the Collectors' particle beam weapon, though it required a temporary cool-down if it overheated. It was described as an amalgam of two different eras of technology.

In single-player it was obtained after completing the DLC mission Priority: Eden Prime; in multiplayer it was an ultra-rare random reward requiring the Mass Effect 3: Rebellion Pack. The weapon used a self-cooling system like the guns of the original Mass Effect and could not permanently run out of ammunition. It was perfectly accurate with no recoil, fired a hitscan beam of moderate but finite range, and was one of the heaviest assault rifles, making it poorly suited to power-focused classes. Its defining trait was a damage ramp-up: initial damage was very low but quadrupled after about two to two-and-a-half seconds of continuous fire, giving it the highest sustained damage-per-second of any assault rifle, though taking most actions or being staggered reset the charge. It paired well with the Assault Rifle Magazine Upgrade mod and with Incendiary Ammo, was effective at staggering Guardians and CAT6 Heavies, and disintegrated enemy corpses in green flames, denying Cannibals their buffs. In multiplayer it was especially strong on the N7 Destroyer Soldier in Devastator Mode and on the stun-resistant Geth Juggernaut Soldier. James Vega used it effectively thanks to his Incendiary Ammo and toughness.
