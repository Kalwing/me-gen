"Apien Crest: Banner of the First Regiment" was a mission in Mass Effect 3. A turian officer needed the Banner of the First Regiment, a turian artifact, to inspire his troops; the task was to recover it from Castellus in the Apien Crest and deliver it to him at Purgatory on the Citadel.

The mission had the prerequisite "Priority: The Citadel I." It could be acquired by listening to a group of three turian officers chatting in an alcove inside Purgatory, to the right of where James was located at the bar; this required completing "Priority: Palaven" first, since Purgatory was otherwise unavailable. Alternatively, the mission could be acquired by conducting Search and Rescue operations in Castellus, which was possible even before Purgatory became available; in that case the journal entry was named "Citadel: Banner of the First Regiment" until Shepard spoke to the turians.

The Banner of the First Regiment was located on Digeris, the third planet in the Castellus system, and was retrieved by scanning the planet. Shepard then returned to the Citadel and spoke to the turian officer to finish the mission; if the banner was retrieved early, access to Purgatory still required completing "Priority: Palaven." Completing the mission gave 5 Reputation, 15,000 credits, and an update to the Turian Seventh Fleet War Asset.
