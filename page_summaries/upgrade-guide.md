The Upgrade Guide was a Mass Effect 2 reference article listing the locations and prices of all equipment and power upgrades in the game, organized by location and by equipment type or biotic/tech category. Upgrades purchased from vendors took effect immediately and did not need to be researched in the Normandy SR-2's tech lab, while research costs scaled with the number of upgrades already researched (for example, the first Heavy Pistol Damage research always cost 2,500 Palladium, then 5,000 and 7,500 for the next tiers). Engineers received up to a 25% discount on research projects, and completing the game with a single character granted +50,000 of each resource on subsequent playthroughs.

The guide estimated the total resources needed to research or buy all useful upgrades at roughly 195,500 palladium, 275,000 platinum, 200,000 iridium, 31,000 element zero, and 1,253,416 credits, with higher figures when all DLC upgrades were included. It noted that in Mass Effect Legendary Edition, DLC that was free in the original game must instead be purchased with credits or mineral resources, making some listed figures inaccurate. The bulk of the article is location-by-location tables covering assignments, the Citadel, the Derelict Reaper, the Disabled Collector Cruiser, Freedom's Progress, Horizon, Illium, Omega, loyalty missions, and other missions. This is a gameplay reference page with no in-universe lore content.
