The Cerberus Harrier was an assault rifle in Mass Effect 3, a Cerberus modification of the M-96 Mattock. Cerberus gunsmiths converted the rifle to fully automatic fire and reined in its recoil so it stayed on target, at the stated cost of slightly less power per round; in practice its per-bullet damage was marginally higher than the standard Mattock's. It was issued mainly to Cerberus's elite troopers, who trained constantly to make every burst count. In single-player it became available through the Mass Effect 3: Firefight Pack, and in the Legendary Edition it could be bought from the Spectre Requisitions terminal in the Citadel Embassies for a base 150,000 credits. In multiplayer it was an ultra-rare random reward requiring the Mass Effect 3: Rebellion Pack. The combination of full-automatic fire, excellent accuracy (especially aiming down sights), and negligible muzzle climb made it one of the game's most powerful weapons at any engagement range, though it was somewhat heavy and, more seriously, carried a very low spare thermal clip reserve. Players mitigated that weakness with the Assault Rifle Magazine Upgrade, ammo-capacity gear and Intel Terminal upgrades, armor-piercing rounds or piercing mods against armored targets such as Brutes and Atlases, and resupply sources like the N7 Demolisher Engineer's Supply Pylon. It performed poorly in squadmates' hands because their short three-to-four-round bursts wasted its limited ammunition. The assault rifle in Mass Effect Infiltrator, an M-96 Mattock Auto, was similar in function and appearance.
