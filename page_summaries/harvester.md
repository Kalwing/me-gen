Harvesters were huge, highly aggressive flying creatures native to several Milky Way worlds, including Tuchanka and Tarith. Roughly a third the size of a thresher maw, they attacked primarily by flying low and dropping klixen onto their targets, using the smaller creatures as shock troops or living ordnance, which suggested a fiendish intelligence.

In Mass Effect 2 a Harvester appeared in Clan Urdnot's proving grounds during Grunt's Rite of Passage, disgorging klixen for Grunt and his krantt to fight. Others could be dimly seen through the chlorine fog on Tarith, near the edges of the Blood Pack's mining operation. In these appearances the creatures could not be killed.

During the Reaper invasion of 2186, numerous Harvesters were corrupted by Reaper technology and pressed into service as troop transports, atmospheric superiority craft, and ground support. Reaper-corrupted Harvesters and the base creatures were sometimes called "wormnecks" or "worm-necks" by military and military-affiliated personnel. The Mass Effect 3 enemy unit derived from them is covered separately.

Cut content: Harvesters were first teased in the September 2006 issue of Electronic Gaming Monthly, a year before the original game's release, as a boss-level enemy meant to challenge players in the M35 Mako, capable of running, crushing, and acidic venom attacks. The Art of Mass Effect later revealed they were cut for time and budget reasons, though a Codex image and text entry remain in the game files of both the original and Legendary Edition. Cut animations in the Mass Effect 2 files show the creatures were originally intended to be fought on the ground and killed.
