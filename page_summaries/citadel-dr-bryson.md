Citadel: Dr. Bryson was the opening Mass Effect 3 mission of the Leviathan mission chain, with prerequisites the Mass Effect 3: Leviathan content and Priority: Palaven. Shepard received a message on the private terminal from Admiral Hackett ordering the Commander to meet Dr. Garret Bryson, an Alliance researcher on the Citadel who had uncovered important new information about the Reapers. This noncombat mission was essentially a long cutscene serving as an intro to the next mission, with EDI present to assist.

At Bryson's laboratory, Shepard learned he worked for Task Force Aurora, whose mandate was investigating myths and legends about the Reapers, with most of the team away chasing leads. As Bryson asked his assistant Derek Hadley for data on something called "Leviathan," Hadley shot Bryson dead and turned his pistol on Shepard before being subdued; an optional Paragon interrupt was non-lethal either way. C-Sec was contacted and arrived, and EDI helped sort through Bryson's files. Hadley had no memory of recent events; EDI concluded he had been indoctrinated. When the conversation turned to Leviathan, Hadley seized up, cryptically warned Shepard against pursuing it, and collapsed before C-Sec guards took him for medical attention. EDI played two video logs between Bryson and Hackett: Bryson believed the Leviathan of Dis uncovered by the batarians was a dead Reaper, and that its killer, the true "Leviathan," was being shadowed by the Reapers as Bryson's teams searched for it. Bryson had formally requested assistance tracking it down, which completed the mission and triggered Citadel: Leviathan I.
