Desolas Arterius was a turian general who served during the First Contact War and the older brother of Saren Arterius. He appears in the comic Mass Effect: Evolution. In 2157 he was given a secret assignment to recover an important artifact from a turian research vessel that had crash-landed on Shanxi. Leading a squad into an abandoned human settlement, he was ambushed by the human mercenaries Jack Harper, Ben Hislop, and Eva Coré; his squad was killed and Desolas was captured. The mercs used his datapad to locate a turian camp, and Harper used a fake message from Desolas to lure the turians into a trap. In the cave hiding the artifact, Hislop touched it and was struck by an energy bolt while Harper was blinded; the distracted Eva was overpowered, and Desolas retook command, taking Harper and Eva prisoner and sending Hislop's body for study.

When the Citadel Council ended the war, Desolas returned Eva and Harper to General Williams on Arcturus Station, expressing interest in Harper because of his exposure to the artifact, the Arca Monolith, though Williams stopped him from detaining Harper further. Desolas later recaptured Harper on Illium, having used the Monolith as bait. Taking Harper and Eva to the turian homeworld Palaven, Desolas revealed a plan to use the Arca Monolith to convert the turian race into an army of super-soldiers to dominate the galaxy, operating from Temple Palaven, which concealed another Monolith. Harper insisted Desolas misunderstood the Monolith's true purpose. Harper convinced Saren that Desolas was endangering the turians; when Desolas's converted turians turned against him, he became trapped in Temple Palaven. Saren ordered Palaven Command to conduct an orbital strike to prevent a "bioweapon emergency," destroying the Temple, the Monoliths, and Desolas.
