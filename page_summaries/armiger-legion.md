Armiger Legion was a passive class power in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode, named for the turian 26th Armiger Legion, described in-fiction as a respected and feared frontline assault squad. The power granted bonuses to weapon damage, weapon stability, and weight capacity, scaling across six ranks with branching evolutions covering power damage, headshots, assault rifle weight, and squad capacity. It was available to the Turian Havoc Soldier, Turian Ghost Infiltrator, and Turian Saboteur Engineer, and had no single-player equivalent.
