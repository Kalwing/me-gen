Garm was a Krogan Battlemaster who led the Blood Pack on Omega in Mass Effect 2. Garrus Vakarian described him as a "freak of nature" able to regenerate faster than most krogan. Garm once fought Garrus one-on-one during Garrus's vigilante career as Archangel, calling it the longest fight of his life, and then chased him across half of Omega with his men but failed to capture him.

With the Blood Pack's operations disrupted by Archangel's team, Garm allied with Tarak of the Blue Suns and Jaroth of Eclipse to destroy the vigilante. He did not care about Archangel's views, only that the vigilante was costing him men and money, and he vowed to personally kill the turian, being the only merc leader to have fought Archangel alone and survived. The three bosses also conspired to use their combined resources to overthrow Aria T'Loak afterward, a plan Jaroth proposed and Garm approved. Tarak's plan had Garm waiting until the freelancers and Eclipse had done their part. Garm tolerated freelancers wandering into Blood Pack quarters and answered their questions; when a young krogan freelancer accompanied a human-led team, Garm questioned why the krogan followed a human instead of learning from his own kind, then decapitated a vorcha with a shotgun to make his point, accepting the krogan's explanation.

After the freelancers and Eclipse were defeated by Archangel and Shepard's reinforcements, Garm and the Blood Pack assaulted the hideout through the basement, breaching into the living room. Garm charged upstairs straight for Archangel but was killed there along with his cohorts. Before his death he had been contacted by fellow Blood Pack leader Kalusk requesting men for Salamul's mining operation on Tarith, but the Archangel operation left him with no vorcha to spare. Garm was armed with an M-300 Claymore Heavy Shotgun, used biotic Warp, and had strong armor plus a powerful biotic barrier that he could fully restore once, but only after his armor was destroyed. Flashbang Grenade, Stasis, and the Arc Projector were effective against him.
