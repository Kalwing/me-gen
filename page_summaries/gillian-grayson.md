Gillian Grayson was a student at the Jon Grissom Academy and the adoptive daughter of Paul Grayson, a Cerberus operative and red sand addict. She showed strong biotic ability early and had high-functioning autism, being prone to silence and highly sensitive to social interaction and physical contact. Cerberus had given her to Grayson as a baby so he could infiltrate the Alliance's Ascension Project biotics research program. She was twelve during the Cerberus operation against her and had likely been exposed to element zero during the Cerberus-orchestrated industrial "accident" on Yandoa. Both the Illusive Man and her father regarded her as a potential savior of humanity, though this may have been a pretext used to manipulate Grayson.

In Mass Effect: Ascension, Dr. Jiro Toshiwa drugged Gillian at the Project, ostensibly to boost her biotics, which may also have worsened her autism. The medication let her throw fellow student Nick twenty feet, cause chaos in the cafeteria, and unleash a high-level singularity before Hendel Mitra stunned her; a cerebrospinal dose then caused a seizure that hospitalized her. Posing as a concerned parent, Grayson took Gillian, Kahlee Sanders, and Hendel Mitra to Omega, intending to hand her to the Illusive Man, but his former partner Pel had turned against Cerberus and planned to sell Gillian to the Collectors for technology. Lemm'Shal nar Tesleya, a quarian on his Pilgrimage, freed them and brought them to the Migrant Fleet, where they were isolated aboard a shuttle on the Idenna. Gillian enjoyed the confinement and liked wearing a quarian environmental suit. Hendel gave her one-on-one biotic tuition. When Grayson followed with Cerberus commandos and attacked the Idenna, Gillian left her hiding place, killed several operatives with her improved abilities, and even dropped a six-ton forklift on soldiers before her father stunned her. Kahlee convinced Grayson that Cerberus was poisoning Gillian and turning her into a weapon, and he agreed to let her stay on the Flotilla with Hendel; both were chosen to accompany the Idenna's captain on the search for a new quarian homeworld.

In Mass Effect: Deception (a first edition acknowledged by BioWare to contain lore discrepancies), Gillian had become a trusted crew member known as "Gillian nar Idenna," serving on the Idenna's security force with Hendel. When the batarian slave ship Glory of Khar'shan attacked, her biotics let the quarians capture it. Among the slaves was Hal McCann, a Cerberus operative who had known Paul Grayson; learning of her father's death, Gillian resolved to find his killer and was given the Glory of Khar'shan to fly to the Citadel.
