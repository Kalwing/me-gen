Citadel: Medical Supplies was a Mass Effect 3 mission available after Priority: The Citadel II. On returning to the Normandy from the Citadel, Specialist Traynor told Commander Shepard that the ship's medical officer, either Dr. Chakwas or Dr. Michel depending on who had been recruited, wished to speak with them. The doctor had noted a group in the Citadel refugee camp that held military medical supplies but needed basic civilian necessities, and asked Shepard to broker a trade.

In the refugee medical center in Cargo Hold B of the Docks: Holding Area, Shepard found a turian named Tactus, who recognized Shepard but refused to give up the refugees' supplies, citing distrust of the Alliance, his people's dire situation, and the supplies' high value. With sufficient reputation, Shepard could persuade him to trade by stressing mutual benefit or military necessity; alternatively, Shepard could give away their own supplies for nothing in return, gaining 15 Paragon points. Pressing Tactus to reconsider only produced a refusal, as he expected a better offer from the black market, though the discussion could be reopened later.

After a successful trade, reporting back to the medical officer completed the mission, granting 5 Reputation, 375 experience, and the Med Bay Surgery intel. Completing it through Charm or Intimidation could also yield an upgrade: with Dr. Chakwas, the Efficiency Increase Medi-Gel Upgrade, giving a 50% chance not to consume a unit of medi-gel when Shepard healed the squad; with Dr. Michel, an increase of two units to medi-gel capacity. In New Game Plus the extra capacity accumulated across playthroughs, at two units each, while the efficiency improvement did not carry over.
