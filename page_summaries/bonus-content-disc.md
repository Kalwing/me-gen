The bonus content disc was a DVD packaged with certain Xbox 360 releases of Mass Effect (2007). Three variants existed, each with a shared visual design but different contents: the pre-order bonus disc, the Limited Collector's Edition disc, and the 2008 Platinum Hits disc. All contained music tracks, documentaries, trailers, gamer pictures, and themes, but in differing quantity. The pre-order disc had one documentary, three music tracks, four trailers, twelve gamer pictures, eight themes and no galleries; the Limited Collector's Edition disc had four documentaries, ten tracks, six trailers, eighteen pictures, sixteen themes and design galleries; the Platinum Hits disc had seven documentaries, twenty tracks, ten trailers, eighteen pictures, a premium theme, downloadable content and galleries.

Each disc opened with a spoken introduction by project director Casey Hudson. Documentaries across the versions included Inside Mass Effect, Inside BioWare, The Vision of Mass Effect, The Making of Mass Effect, Interactive Storytelling, Sci vs. Fi: Mass Effect, Making Bring Down the Sky, Beyond the Game, and The Future of Mass Effect. The soundtrack selections, composed by Jack Wall and Sam Hulick, ranged from three tracks on the pre-order disc to more than twenty on the Platinum Hits disc, including "Strangers," which was exclusive to the Collector's Edition and absent from the official soundtrack release.

The design galleries were split into The Creatures of Mass Effect (207 images), The Environments of Mass Effect (200 images), and The Technology of Mass Effect (207 images), each image carrying recorded artist commentary. Gamer picture sets depicted characters such as Saren Arterius, Garrus Vakarian, Commander Shepard, Ashley Williams, Urdnot Wrex, Benezia, Councilor Tevos, and Jeff "Joker" Moreau, plus creatures and the N7 logo. Trailers spanned 2005 to 2008. The Platinum Hits disc's internal files also contained traces of two inaccessible galleries tied to the Bring Down the Sky DLC: "Creatures: Batarians" and "Environments: Asteroid 57."
