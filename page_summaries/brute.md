Brutes were synthetic-organic creatures that appeared in Mass Effect 3 as the Reapers' heavy melee troops, able to pummel smaller units into paste and destroy armored vehicles to reach the soldiers inside. Each was an amalgamation of turian and krogan victims of the Reapers, mutated by Reaper technology; because dextro-protein turian tissue is incompatible with levo-protein krogan tissue, implants regulated the Brute's body chemistry to combat organ rejection. The fusion of turian military skill and krogan blood rage made them formidable. Commander Shepard first encountered Brutes on Menae while searching for General Victus, and the rest of the galaxy's forces fought them on numerous battlefields.

A Brute's main attack was a running charge followed by a wide sweeping claw strike, both able to drop shields and health and stun anything in the path; the claw swipe could penetrate cover and terrain. It could also jump and slam its claws into the ground, and periodically beat its chest and roar to stagger nearby foes. Its large right claw could grab a victim and smash them into the ground for an instant kill, and a dying Brute invariably made one last claw swipe and could crush enemies beneath its falling body. Defensively, Brutes had high armor but no barriers or shields; thick metallic plates covered much of the body and could be shot off, the right claw's plating could not, and the head and rear haunches were unarmored and vulnerable. Recommended tactics included keeping distance, using ladders and obstacles the Brute could not pass, concentrating fire, exploiting its clumsiness, and using anti-armor powers such as Carnage and Incinerate, penetrating weapons such as the N7 Crusader and M-98 Widow, and repeated Warp and Throw to trigger Biotic Explosions. In multiplayer, Brutes appeared alongside Banshees during later waves.
