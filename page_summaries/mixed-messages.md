"Mixed Messages" was a minor task in Mass Effect: Andromeda, acquired after the mission Hunting the Archon by agreeing to help Jim, an exile jailed in The Docks observation-deck cell on Kadara for drunkenly urinating on an Outcast guard's boots. The mission was only available while Sloane Kelly ran Kadara Port and before the mission High Noon was completed. Jim asked Ryder to carry a message to Cele, an asari he called his girlfriend who worked at Tartarus, telling her he would soon have the credits for Sloane's protection fees and that she should wait for him, while hiding that he was in jail. Cele, working a dancer cage on Tartarus's upper floor, denied being Jim's girlfriend, said she was happy where she was and needed neither his money nor rescuing from a "pup" like him. Ryder could relay her answer or send her to do it herself; either way Ryder confronted Jim, either calling out his delusion or letting him down gently, and told him to focus on himself. Reward: 270 XP.
