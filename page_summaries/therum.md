Therum was a distant but resource-rich industrial world in the Knossos System of the Artemis Tau cluster, the second planet of its system and claimed by the human Systems Alliance. Its abundant heavy metals fueled a manufacturing boom on Earth. Core samples containing fossils of simple silicon-based organisms indicated the planet was more habitable in the past, which may explain the many Prothean ruins on its surface, most of them already looted by mining corporations. Its capital was named Nova Yekaterinburg (New Yekaterinburg).

In Mass Effect, Therum was the setting of the mission to find Liara T'Soni, an asari researcher trapped inside a Prothean ruin during a geth attack while Commander Shepard pursued Saren Arterius. Shepard fought through the geth, including an Armature, extracted Liara from a Prothean security barrier, and escaped the collapsing dig site.

The page notes that Therum grew out of cut content. In early Mass Effect demo footage the location was a planet named Caleston where Shepard rescued miners trapped underground by a geth attack; the approach and the battle outside the entrance were the same, but the demo had Shepard question why the geth would deploy an Armature merely to kill miners. Under the name Caleston, the world was originally intended to host a full Eldfell-Ashland Energy mining facility with on-site workers and a seedy bar, as referenced in The Art of Mass Effect.
