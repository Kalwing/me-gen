Grizz was a turian bodyguard hired by Aria T'Loak and stationed in the Afterlife Club on Omega, at the base of the staircase to the right of Aria's private booth. He sometimes hired agents on Aria's behalf for missions requiring discretion. At Aria's command, Grizz asked Commander Shepard to protect Patriarch, Aria's aged krogan predecessor, after Mordin Solus or Archangel had been recruited.
