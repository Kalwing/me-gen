Nodacrux was the second planet of the Vostok System in the Maroon Sea cluster of the Milky Way. It was a verdant world with abundant water, a temperate climate, a thick oxygen-nitrogen atmosphere and a rich ecosystem, seemingly perfect for life. Its high oxygen percentage made humans feel energized but had also allowed insect analogues to grow to frightful sizes. The planet was ultimately "almost but not quite" habitable: thunderstorms in the thick, oxygen-rich air were deafening and sparked constant wildfires, and large ubiquitous tufts of pollen floating in the high-pressure atmosphere caused severe or lethal allergic reactions in oxygen-breathing species.

Commander Shepard could visit after Feros: Geth Attack, in connection with the assignment UNC: ExoGeni Facility. An ExoGeni facility stood on the surface, staffed in part by Dr. Ross and defended by mercenaries. Other points of interest included a crashed probe for salvage, an ancient half-buried escape pod whose computer still held data on the Thracia Colony (for the UNC: Turian Insignias collection), a crashed ship and an abandoned camp both guarded by Thorian Creepers, where the rachni "song" could be heard, and a herd of Space Cows. Mineral deposits included heavy gold and light cobalt for the UNC: Valuable Minerals survey.

The planet had a rugged mountain complex difficult to navigate even with the Mako's climbing ability, with points of interest often perched atop or ringed by sheer cliffs. The western mineral deposit could only be reached by dropping off steep cliffs that could not easily be recrossed. The name may derive from the Latin roots crux (cross, pain, torture) and nodus (knot).
