Binary Helix (BH) was a corporation and a leader in genetic engineering and biotechnology, with a significant presence on Noveria that shielded it from Luddites, base-liners, and fundamentalist groups opposed to its work. It also researched biotic implants. Its motto was "A Better Foundation, A Stronger House." Killira T'Nama was its Chief Executive Officer and Yaroslev Tartakovsky its operations director.

In 2183, Binary Helix rented a lab on Peak 15 that was used to breed the rachni from an ancient egg found on a derelict ship. BH also developed the biotoxin Thoros-B, ordering Dr. Zev Cohen to make it affect more species so it could be marketed. Saren Arterius and Matriarch Benezia were key investors; Benezia held the title of executive secretary and was Saren's official executor, and Saren's investor status let him plant Alestia Iallis at Rift Station. Benezia intended to groom the reborn rachni into part of Saren's forces. Before the project matured, the rachni escaped containment, ravaged Peak 15, and triggered a lockdown. Commander Shepard rescued surviving scientists and guards, then triggered a neutron purge to euthanize the rachni. BH's stock collapsed, and by 2185 the company's future was in doubt.

Mallene Calis sought Shepard's help spying on BH representative Rafael Vargas at Port Hanshan; Vargas discussed BH's military genetic modification packages while insisting the company obeyed all Citadel Council regulations. A Citadel news story reported that BH had settled out of court with krogan investors who had sued after a study into a genophage cure showed no results, possibly linked to Saren's genophage cure on Virmire.

In 2185, Binary Helix joined Guanghui Solutions and Sonax Industries in invading Garvug for eco-engineering research, supplying logistical staff. After publicly criticizing its allies for chemical weapons use and withdrawing, BH secretly returned with Sonax and Guanghui to ambush and wipe out the insurgency's leadership. Separately, scientists at BH's Nautilus Facility beneath the ice of Europa discovered microorganisms in a rock sample, sparking a rights dispute with the Systems Alliance. The facility was later flooded, its cause unresolved, and the Alliance confiscated its data. Notable employees included Alestia Iallis, Han Olar, Dr. Palon, Rafael Vargas, and Captain Ventralis.
