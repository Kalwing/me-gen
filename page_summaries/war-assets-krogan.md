This war-asset list collected the might the krogan clans threw against the Reapers as shock troops. Its leadership asset was either Urdnot Wreav (25), who seized Tuchanka's armies using the genophage cure and openly planned a postwar krogan empire, or Urdnot Wrex (30), who had risen to clan leadership championing progressive reform and united Tuchanka after the cure. If the cure was secretly sabotaged while Wrex ruled, both his personal asset (-30) and Clan Urdnot (-300) turned negative as Wrex withdrew krogan support.

Grunt (25) joined if he survived the Suicide Mission and Attican Traverse: Krogan Team. Aralakh Company (25), a hand-picked cross-clan warrior unit, was led by Grunt if he survived; it was wiped out on Utukku (-25, or -75 in Legendary Edition) if the rachni queen or breeder was saved there. The Krogan First Division (50) came from N7: Cerberus Attack, and Krogan Mercenaries (75) returned from the Terminus Systems if Wrex ruled and the genophage was cured.

The large clan assets all required Priority: Tuchanka. Krogan Clans (300) noted decades of stockpiled weapons of mass destruction now aimed at the Reapers; it gained 40 from cloning toxin-immune kakliosaur battle-mounts, but lost 50 if Maelon's data was destroyed and Eve later died, lost 250 if Tuchanka: Bomb was not completed (the Kelphic Valley blast killing every krogan gathered there, including females and children), or lost 300 entirely if the cure was sabotaged. Clan Urdnot (300) had absorbed or destroyed Clans Nakmor, Jurdon, Quash and Raik and rallied krogan around the discovery of a fertile Urdnot female.
