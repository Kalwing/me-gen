Ice Giant was the opening mission of Mass Effect: Infiltrator, serving as the game's tutorial for cover, aiming, and powers. Cerberus Infiltrator Randall Ezno was deployed by shuttle to an unnamed ice planet to capture a high-value turian target. His handler and technician, Inali Renata, briefed and guided him over comms, and the operation doubled as a live field test of Randall's newest augmentations.

The mission ran across four checkpoints. Randall fought through geth opposition including Geth Troopers, Geth Juggernauts, and a Turian Sniper, using cover, Pull, Push, Cloak, Charge, and Slide to raise his Combat Analysis Style and Health ratings. Inali repeatedly coached him to stay in cover; Randall answered sarcastically ("Gee, ya think?"). When Inali noted he carried more implants than any other subject, Randall replied, "Well, more than any human subject," alluding to Cerberus's other experiments.

At the fourth checkpoint Randall defeated the turian target, who tended to flee behind cover and required longer-range weapons and powers to finish. After the fight Randall chose how to handle the capture, then dragged the turian back to the shuttle and returned to the Cerberus home base, the Barn. The captured turian later attempted to escape during Randall's disruption of the Cerberus base, and became the protagonist of Infiltrator's bonus mission, Incarceration.
