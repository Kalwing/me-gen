Mass Effect: Incursion was an eight-page mini-comic written by Mac Walters with art by Eduardo Francisco, made available June 21, 2010 through IGN and later collected with Mass Effect: Evolution and paired with Mass Effect: Inquisition on Dark Horse Digital. Walters created it to explain a moment in Mass Effect: Redemption where Aria T'Loak reacts negatively to a mention of the Collectors.

Set one week before the Collectors destroyed the SSV Normandy, the story follows Aria T'Loak and two batarian bodyguards investigating a disturbance on Omega. A bodyguard suggested it was just Blue Suns or slavers, but Aria sensed something worse and came herself. The three interrupted a meeting between Blue Suns mercenaries and a group of Collectors accompanied by a Praetorian, at which the Blue Suns were selling captive humans to the Collectors. Aria ordered that no one leave alive and joined the fight. All Collectors and captive humans died; Aria lost one bodyguard. Surviving Blue Suns were interrogated and executed. Afterward at Afterlife, Aria received a datapad of everything her agents had found on the deal: human population totals for Horizon, Omega, Freedom's Progress, the Citadel, Eden Prime, Terra Nova, and Earth. Aria concluded the Collectors wanted far more than a few slaves and that humanity had made a dangerous enemy. The events link into Mass Effect: Redemption.
