Torthak was a heavily scarred batarian warlord operating in the Terminus Systems, appearing in the comic Mass Effect: Foundation. He had been hearing from an associate about two humans asking around for Commander Shepard when that associate's underlings dumped one of the humans, Miranda Lawson, in front of him. Torthak used the moment to stab the associate in the back, taking over leadership of the local district.

He considered what to do with the captive Miranda, who headbutted him when he crouched near her; he easily subdued her, boasting of his toughness. A new grunt, Taleed, arrived and Torthak put him to work. When Miranda gave up nothing under interrogation, Torthak ordered Taleed to kill her and leave the body somewhere conspicuous to warn off her friends, unmoved by her taunt that he would not do it himself. A flashbang detonated at the warehouse moments after Torthak left, and he avoided the ensuing firefight. He later held a little girl hostage in front of Jacob Taylor and Miranda to block the humans and freed slaves from escaping. While Torthak's attention was on Miranda's taunts about his tactics, Jacob shot him in the chest, leaving him to die on the floor.
