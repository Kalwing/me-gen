The Mass Effect Trilogy was a special boxed set containing Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, and Mass Effect 3, featuring a premium foil box and exclusive artwork. It was released for Xbox 360 and PC worldwide on November 6, 2012 (November 9 in Europe), and for PlayStation 3 on December 4, 2012 worldwide (December 7 in Europe). Besides copies of the three games, it included platform-dependent downloadable content: on Xbox 360, Cerberus Network and the Mass Effect 3 Online Pass; on PC, on-disc Bring Down the Sky and Pinnacle Station plus Cerberus Network; and on PlayStation 3, on-disc Bring Down the Sky, Kasumi - Stolen Memory, Overlord, and Lair of the Shadow Broker, plus Cerberus Network and the Mass Effect 3 Online Pass.
