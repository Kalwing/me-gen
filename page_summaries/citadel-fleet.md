The Citadel Fleet was the main space defense force of the Citadel, made up of turian, salarian, and asari vessels, predominantly turian because of the turians' peacekeeping role. Its flagship was the asari dreadnought Destiny Ascension, the most powerful ship of the Council races. Its exact size was unknown, but it was large enough to patrol every mass relay linking Citadel space to the Terminus Systems while still keeping a force at the Citadel; Ambassador Udina claimed it could secure the entire Attican Traverse if the Council wished.

During the Battle of the Citadel, after the events on Virmire, the Council deployed the fleet to the relays believing Saren Arterius would not dare attack the Citadel directly. The dreadnought Sovereign and a geth fleet then launched a surprise assault. The Destiny Ascension ordered the Wards closed to fortify the station, but Saren had already used the Conduit to infiltrate the Citadel and shut down the station and the relay network, isolating the diminished fleet and blocking reinforcements. Sovereign broke through as the Wards closed behind it. The Council was evacuated to the Destiny Ascension, which could not fight clear of the geth ships. After Shepard reactivated the relays, letting the Alliance Navy and the Normandy arrive, Shepard chose whether to send the reinforcements to save the Council and the Destiny Ascension or to let them fall.

If the Council was saved, the Destiny Ascension survived and later made a 20-colony victory cruise; the turians lost 20 cruisers of 300 crew each, and the Alliance lost eight cruisers and 2,400 personnel. If the Council was left to die, most of the defending fleet was believed destroyed, and Khalisah al-Jilani cited 10,000 dead. Galactic News in 2185 reported plans for an Allied Citadel Defense Unit to replace the fleet, with the Systems Alliance supplying most ships, the Asari Republics ceding defense responsibility to the Turian Hierarchy, and the Hierarchy announcing it would no longer cap its dreadnought production. In 2185 the Citadel Fleet's 24th Fighter Group received the Galactic Unit Citation for being the first unit to engage Sovereign.
