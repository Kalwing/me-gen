Short character page (under ~150 words of prose). Keo was a bodyguard for the high-ranking Terra Firma party member Claude Menneau, a candidate for party leader in 2173. She was in her forties with an unusually pale appearance that Paul Grayson believed might have been cosmetically altered. She never trusted Grayson, Menneau's pilot, and stuck close to her fellow bodyguard Pel. Her mistrust was wise but misplaced: both Grayson and Pel were Cerberus plants under orders to kill Menneau. En route to Shanxi for a rally, Grayson killed Keo with a kitchen knife hidden in the fridge, then on Pel's mocking order claimed her pistol as a trophy and kept it for years. It was Grayson's first kill for Cerberus and continued to haunt his dreams, as recounted in Mass Effect: Ascension.
