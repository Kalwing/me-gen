This war-asset list collected the warships and tactical expertise the salarians brought against the Reapers. Major Kirrahe (20) joined if he survived Virmire and was not killed by Kai Leng, which required talking to Thane Krios before Priority: The Citadel II so Kirrahe rather than Thane would defend the salarian councilor; Kirrahe had been decorated with the Star of Sur'Kesh for his leadership at Virmire.

The Salarian First Fleet (150) was gained by sabotaging the genophage cure during Priority: Tuchanka; it revealed that the salarians had slowed dreadnought production because STG had procured the SSV Normandy's stealth systems and the SR-2's Reaper IFF algorithms to build stealth dreadnoughts. The Salarian Third Fleet (125) required saving the Destiny Ascension in Mass Effect and helping the salarian councilor survive an assassination attempt in Priority: The Citadel II; its scouting flotillas carried hull-mounted Thanix cannons developed with the turian military, and completing Citadel: Heating Unit Stabilizers added 8. Abandoning the Destiny Ascension instead yielded the smaller STG Task Force (70), which had quietly prepared for the Reapers' return after the Battle of the Citadel.

Dr. Mordin Solus (25), the ex-STG geneticist, was gained if he survived the Suicide Mission and was convinced to sabotage the cure; he then worked aboard the Crucible. The Salarian Special Tasks Group (35), the salarian intelligence service operating in small espionage and sabotage cells, was gained only by letting Captain Kirrahe die in Mass Effect and speaking with Lieutenant Tolan during Priority: Sur'Kesh.
