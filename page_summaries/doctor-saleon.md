Doctor Saleon was a salarian geneticist who formerly worked on the Citadel and came to Garrus Vakarian's attention during a C-Sec investigation into black-market trafficking in body parts. C-Sec could not tell whether a lab was illegally selling organs or a killer was dismembering people. Garrus ran a DNA check on a recovered turian liver and found the supposed donor alive; the turian had worked for Dr. Saleon. Garrus inspected Saleon's lab but found no cloned parts or growing machinery. During interrogations of Saleon's employees, one began bleeding, and medics found fresh incisions across his body. Saleon had been using his poor, desperate employees as living incubators, cloning organs and growing them inside their bodies, then removing and selling them for a small cut of the profits; failed organs were simply left inside, with dire health consequences.

When C-Sec moved to arrest him, Saleon destroyed his lab, seized some employees, and fled the Citadel by ship. Garrus wanted the vessel shot down, but C-Sec overrode the order because it was too close to the station, and Saleon escaped. He renamed himself "Dr. R. Heart" as a joke and bought and equipped the MSV Fedele to continue his work. Garrus obtained the ship's transponder codes and asked Shepard to investigate, requesting to come along. Boarding the MSV Fedele, Shepard's team found test subjects resembling Thorian Creepers in the cargo bay and Saleon in a rear compartment. If Garrus was present he recognized the salarian, who feigned innocence. Shepard could let Garrus execute Saleon or persuade him to arrest the doctor instead; either way, Saleon refused to surrender and was killed in the ensuing firefight. His voice actor differed depending on whether Garrus was present. An early cut pitch for reintroducing Garrus in Mass Effect 2 had him hunting Saleon's accomplices on Omega.
