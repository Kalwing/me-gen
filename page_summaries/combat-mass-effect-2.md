Combat (Mass Effect 2) was a game-mechanics overview page for Mass Effect 2's squad-based combat, covering its increased emphasis on cover, the shift from heat generation to thermal-clip ammunition that must be reloaded, the removal of standard grenades, squad commands and the Power Wheel, the HUD and enemy lock-on for tracking powers, the simplified shared-cooldown powers system, the five weapon types including new submachine guns and player-only heavy weapons with power cells, headshots and weak points, the ragdoll double-damage and range-scaling modifiers, expanded melee combat, health/armor/shields/barriers with default values for Shepard and squadmates, the four enemy protection layers (health, armor, shields, barriers) and their counters, squad selection, the Casual through Insanity difficulty settings with their health multipliers, level scaling that reduces player damage as Shepard levels, game options, bugs, and Legendary Edition changes.
