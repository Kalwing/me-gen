Canrum was the fourth planet of the Hydra System in the Argos Rho cluster, a small, rocky world with a trace atmosphere of methane and krypton. Its surface was composed mainly of magnesium and silicates with deposits of carbon.

Canrum was the site of the warlord Shiagur's defeat by turian peacekeeping forces during the Krogan Rebellions. Shiagur's band was not especially powerful, but she was a female warlord and one of the few remaining fertile females. Through viciousness and cunning she had turned her unique value into a position of power, with krogan males competing for the right to join her band and lie with her. When her death was announced, vengeful male krogan admirers near and far swore blood oaths against the turian crews that had participated. Ultimately several thousand of those turians were killed in open combat or by assassination, and to this day many krogan proudly claim to carry the "blood of Shiagur."

In Mass Effect 3, scans of Canrum revealed dangerous radiation coming from orbit. Chief Engineer Adams conducted further scans and discovered the remains of an ancient warhead marked with the Parthia Colony insignia, tied to the collection assignment UNC: Turian Insignias. The planet also featured a Haptic Optics Array as part of Search and Rescue. Canrum was referred to as "Sywinlon" in the first game's internal data. In Mass Effect 2 the krogan Grunt described the battle fought at Canrum as the last great battle of the Krogan Rebellions, dating it to the latter half of the 8th century CE.
