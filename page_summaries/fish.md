Fish were aquatic creatures, some edible, some decorative, that could be collected for the aquarium in Commander Shepard's personal cabin aboard the Normandy SR-2 in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. Stores restocked after each purchase.

In Mass Effect 2 the fish had to be fed after every mission or assignment or they would die and need to be replaced; feeding could be stretched across two consecutive missions if Shepard did not return to the Normandy between them. If Shepard flirted with Kelly Chambers often enough and invited her to dinner, she would offer to feed the fish. Available species included the Illium Skald Fish and Thessian Sunfish from Citadel Souvenirs, and the Prejek Paddle Fish from Memories of Illium on Illium.

In Mass Effect 3 the fish again required feeding after every mission. After the Normandy was impounded following the Collector mission, Kelly Chambers rescued any surviving fish if she and Shepard had dined together and she survived the suicide mission, returning them when met at the Citadel refugee camp before the mission to Tuchanka. Species sold at Kanala Exports included the Illium Skald Fish, Thessian Sunfish, Koi fish, Striped Dartfish, Belan Jellyfish, and Khar'shan Snapping Eel, with several unlocking after specific missions; the Prejek Paddle Fish was available only if Kelly had saved Shepard's Mass Effect 2 fish. An Aquarium VI upgrade module, sold at the Presidium Commons for 25,000 credits, would automatically feed and maintain the fish.

Related trivia noted that both Wrex and a krogan named Kargesh wondered aloud whether the Presidium lakes held fish, that Liara kept a tank of every acquirable fish in her apartment during Lair of the Shadow Broker, and that importing a save with a surviving Prejek Paddlefish through both sequels unlocked a bonus from the Intel Terminal in Liara's cabin. Cut content would have added a "camel fish" during the Overlord mission, offered by Dr. Archer.
