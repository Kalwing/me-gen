Batarian Commanders were enemies encountered in Mass Effect 2 during the mission N7: Javelin Missiles Launched, and were believed to be batarian terrorists. They were supported by Batarian Troopers, who provided covering fire, and the page noted they were functionally similar to Blue Suns Legionnaires.

Offensively, Batarian Commanders were armed with M-15 Vindicator assault rifles, making them accurate and deadly, and could load their rifles with Disruptor Ammo to increase damage output. Defensively they had moderate shielding on veteran difficulty and below, gaining more resilient shields on hardcore and insanity.

The tactics section identified the Commander's main weakness as the burst-fire nature of the Vindicator: although capable of serious damage, they could not keep a squad pinned as effectively as a Trooper, leaving openings between bursts to coordinate squad firepower and powers against them. Weapons that maintained a constant stream of fire could easily suppress and damage them. On harder difficulties they could deal very high damage, making suppression unrealistic, especially with supporting troops present; the page recommended picking off weaker enemies first while using powers on the Commanders. In close quarters they could deal higher damage, particularly while still shielded, so directing squad powers and gunfire against them together was advised.
