Irikah Krios was the wife of the drell assassin Thane Krios and the mother of Kolyat Krios, remembered for her sunset-colored eyes. As told in Mass Effect: Foundation, she was a lab researcher working on a vaccine for a rare hanar disease, work that drew "moral objections"; thugs broke into her lab and Thane intervened to help her fight them off, though she handled one attacker herself. Earlier she had prevented an assassination of an elderly salarian in a park by stepping into the targeting laser's path. Learning it was Thane behind the trigger, she began to call security but changed her mind on hearing his plea for redemption.

Despite her skepticism and contempt, she showed Thane compassion and eventually forgave him. They grew close, dated, and married in a small open-air ceremony. Irikah told Thane he was free to choose his own future, advice he heeded by asking the hanar to release him from his Compact. She gave him a coin that had belonged to her father, saying that rubbing it had brought her father peace of mind. After Kolyat was born and money was tight, she supported Thane's decision to freelance as an assassin.

One of Thane's jobs, killing the leaders of a batarian slaver ring, brought vengeful batarians down on the family; too afraid to target Thane directly, they paid the Shadow Broker to identify him and sent a hitman after his home. Sensing intruders, Irikah told Kolyat to hide and armed herself with a knife, but was killed. Thane returned to Kahje for her funeral, left Kolyat with relatives, and hunted down those complicit in her murder, letting them suffer before they died. He later threw her father's coin into the sea. By 2185 her memory formed much of the backstory Thane shares with Commander Shepard after his recruitment on Illium; she had reminded him of Arashu, the goddess of motherhood and protection.
