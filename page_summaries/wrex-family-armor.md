Wrex: Family Armor was an assignment in the first Mass Effect in which Commander Shepard recovered the ceremonial armor of Urdnot Wrex's family, Clan Urdnot. Wrex explained that the armor had been taken by the turians after the Krogan Rebellions and had passed to a profiteer named Tonn Actus. The assignment required completing Citadel: Expose Saren first and could be triggered either by repeatedly talking with a recruited Wrex aboard the Normandy until he opened up about his family, or simply by visiting the hidden structure on Tuntau (Phoenix system, Argos Rho).

The hidden structure lay in a crater south of the landing site. Its exterior entrance was guarded by two Pirate Snipers and a Pirate; inside, the main room held four Pirates at ground level plus two Pirate Snipers and Tonn Actus on an upper balcony. Actus tended to descend and rush Shepard once combat started, and the non-sniper pirates used Immunity frequently and healed while hidden, making damage-over-time powers such as Warp effective. Explosive fuel tanks on the balcony could be detonated with Overload or Sabotage before alerting the pirates, often killing Actus outright. Wrex's armor was kept in a Wall Safe (easy decryption) on the second floor; it was a plot item that did not enter the inventory, and other armor found in the safe could be freely converted to omni-gel.

Journal entries varied by whether Wrex was brought along, was alive but absent, or was already dead. If Wrex was alive, the completed assignment noted the armor was "not very good" but held sentimental value, and completing it before Virmire earned Wrex's trust: Shepard could then calm him without Charm or Intimidate (still unlocking the Charismatic achievement) when Saren Arterius's genophage cure was revealed, and Wrex's dialogue changed to affirm his loyalty rather than doubt.
