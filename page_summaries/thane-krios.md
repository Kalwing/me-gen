Thane Krios was a drell assassin, rumored the most skilled in the galaxy, who preferred to kill targets up close using stealth, firearms, hand-to-hand combat and biotics. Born in 2146, he was given by his parents at age six to the hanar to be trained as an assassin under the Compact between the two species, and made his first kill at twelve. A deeply spiritual man, he followed the old drell polytheistic religion, praying to Amonkira (lord of hunters) to find a target, Arashu (goddess of motherhood and protection) when defending another, and Kalahira (goddess of oceans and the afterlife) after a kill. His faith held body and soul as separate, letting him regard his body as a tool, though his species' eidetic memory forced him to relive every murder. He described his years of killing as a "battle sleep."

He met his wife Irikah during an assassination when she stepped into his targeting laser to save a stranger; intrigued, he sought her out and they fell in love. The hanar released him from his Compact to raise a family. He and Irikah had a son, Kolyat, but assassination work drew Thane increasingly away from home. Batarian slavers, in revenge for Thane's killings, paid the Shadow Broker for his identity and murdered Irikah. Thane hunted down everyone responsible, abandoning his clean-kill approach for torture, and afterward threw away a coin Irikah had given him, vowing to follow his path to its end.

In Mass Effect: Foundation, Thane was sent to the Citadel to judge the Cerberus agent Rasa; after spiking her drink and cornering her, he decided he "did not see murder in her soul," apologized, and carried her off with a prayer to Kalahira.

Commander Shepard first encountered him on Illium, dropping from the ceiling to assassinate Nassana Dantius. Dying of Kepral's Syndrome, Thane joined Shepard's mission against the Collectors as penance. His loyalty mission (Thane: Sins of the Father) had Shepard help stop Kolyat from becoming a hitman on the Citadel. He was a romance option for a female Shepard, calling her "siha," after "one of the warrior-angels of the goddess Arashu."

In Mass Effect 3, if he survived, Thane resided at Huerta Memorial Hospital under the name Tannor Nuara. He declined to rejoin the crew but intercepted Kai Leng during Cerberus's attack on the Citadel, dueling him to protect the salarian councilor and being stabbed through the stomach. He died in surgery, in the final stages of Kepral's Syndrome, with Shepard and Kolyat reciting a drell prayer of redemption; the prayer was Thane's wish for Shepard, not for himself. His name was added to the Normandy's memorial wall. Shepard later avenged him by killing Kai Leng.
