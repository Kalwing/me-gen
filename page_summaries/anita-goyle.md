Anita Goyle was the first human ambassador to the Citadel, a role she took extremely seriously as the representative of all humanity to the Citadel races. She was eventually succeeded by Donnel Udina. In 2165 CE she was described as a striking woman in her early 60s, of medium build, with long silvery hair, Middle Eastern features and emerald eyes, and a reputation for cutting through political obfuscation.

In Mass Effect: Revelation, Goyle met David Anderson on his return from Sidon and revealed that the facility had been secretly researching artificial intelligence. She instructed him to discreetly track down the source without drawing Spectre attention. When the truth about Sidon leaked to the Council, which planned heavy sanctions against the Alliance, Goyle realized the Council was actually alarmed by humanity's rapid expansion and was using the incident to keep humans in check. She delivered a passionate speech describing humanity's swift integration into Citadel society, the galactic economy and C-Sec, argued that facilities like Sidon were necessary to understand synthetic intelligence and races like the geth, and negotiated a better deal than the penalties proposed. After Kahlee Sanders exposed Dr. Shu Qian's involvement, Goyle chose to hand the recovered files to the Council as a show of good faith. Once the Council agreed to send Saren Arterius after Edan Had'dah and Qian, Goyle requested that Anderson accompany him as a possible Spectre candidate. She was surprised by the asari Councilor's quick agreement, later realizing the Council had anticipated and pre-discussed all her requests, showing how much she still had to learn about galactic politics. When Saren filed a report blaming Anderson for ruining the mission on Camala at the cost of hundreds of lives, Goyle did not make an example of him and let him return to his military career.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda and Andromeda: Initiation, Goyle appeared in Alec Ryder's memories: Alec pitched his AI research to her, and she weighed his arguments but denied his proposal because AIs were illegal in Council space. Her later corroboration of reports that Alec was pursuing AI research contributed to his dismissal from the Alliance, and Alec suspected Goyle or her aide of involvement in the theft of his AI kernel.
