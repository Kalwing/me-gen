Short page (under ~150 words of prose). Commander Jacobus was a turian mercenary serving Saren Arterius, tasked with searching for Prothean artifacts buried on an ice planet in the Crescent Nebula. When Tali'Zorah nar Rayya and Keenah'Breizh landed to investigate the geth presence, Jacobus tried to kill them, knowing Saren would kill him and his team if he failed. He pursued the quarians to Illium, ambushed and killed the crew of the ship Honorata, then chased Tali and Keenah to the Citadel, wounding both. He cornered them in an incinerator where Keenah had already died of his wounds, but Tali sprang a trap, sealed Jacobus inside, and activated it, obliterating him. Events are from Mass Effect: Homeworlds.
