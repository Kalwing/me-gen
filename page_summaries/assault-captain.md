Short page: the Assault Captain was a Cerberus officer who led a squad of Assault Troopers into battle. Randall Ezno encountered him once, on the Barn space station, during his escape (in Mass Effect: Infiltrator). The Captain had considerable health and a strong regenerating shield, tended to stay in cover unless Shepard was in his line of sight, was vulnerable to headshots, and wore a jetpack that let him close to Eviscerator shotgun range; Pull could flush him from cover or interrupt him in flight. He resembled the Trooper and Assault Trooper in appearance.
