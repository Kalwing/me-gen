This page transcribed the recorded artist commentary for the design gallery "The Creatures of Mass Effect: Humans," included on the 2007 Mass Effect bonus content disc. All twenty-seven images were narrated by concept artist Matt Rhodes, with helmet sketches by Derek Watts noted.

Rhodes covered the development of human armor, beginning with one of his first drawings on joining the project and progressing through male and female variants, an ultra-industrial hazard suit with heavy pipes and copper wiring, Tron-line lighting tests, and the settling of the iconic white-and-red scheme with the N7 logo. Armor was built up in layers from a base to medium sets, and kept close to the body rather than the bulkier Gears of War silhouette. Civilian and crowd costumes drew on references including Han Solo, Sean Connery's Outland (the puffy orange vest Rhodes fought to keep and which reached the game in altered form), The Fifth Element and Jean-Paul Gaultier's designs, Syd Mead furniture, and Neo-Tokyo styling. Medical officer designs ran from department heads to field commando medics, and dramatic dignitary costumes used black cloaks with red slashes.

The final images concerned character faces. Rhodes discussed test heads for Anderson, matched to recorded voice acting; Ashley sketches combining features from several actresses; Kaidan studies focused on expression and "worry lines"; and an early model's face that was for a time expected to be Commander Shepard. After many tested faces, Mark Vanderloo's face was the one everyone agreed on and became the default Commander Shepard, a face Rhodes said the team felt confident exploring the galaxy with.
