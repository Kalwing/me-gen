Batarian Engineers were the technical specialists of a batarian squad in the first Mass Effect. They carried heavy shielding but were armed only with pistols, and they used tech powers including Overload, Sabotage and Neural Shock. During the mission to stop batarians from crashing an asteroid into the human colony of Terra Nova, Engineers were deployed inside the fusion torch stations and the main facility to handle technical problems and reinforce their allies.

In combat the Engineers hung back behind cover rather than leading an assault, supporting other batarian troops with tech proximity mines used to debuff enemies and wear them down from a distance, and firing their pistols between mine salvos. The page warned that their Neural Shock was especially dangerous if it knocked the player down while a batarian pet varren closed in, that Sabotage could overheat the player's primary weapon so a backup was advisable, and that Damping, biotics and high tech resistance were effective counters to their abilities.
