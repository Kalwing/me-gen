This page transcribed the chapter "The Making of Mass Effect 3" from the 2020 book BioWare: Stories and Secrets from 25 Years of Game Development by Ben Gelinas. It collected development anecdotes about the trilogy's finale, in which Shepard gathered the galaxy's forces against the Reapers and confronted a compromised Illusive Man. The game reunited earlier squad mates, added new characters, refined combat, and introduced a co-operative multiplayer mode against geth, Reapers, Cerberus, and Collectors.

Voice-over producer Caroline Livingstone recalled that she and Jennifer Hale both wept during Hale's final FemShep session, which included Anderson's death and a quiet scene with Garrus. Xbox and PC versions supported Kinect voice control, requiring hundreds of commands across accents and languages; players sometimes killed Tali by accidentally saying "geth" or "quarian" aloud during a decision. FemShep received major marketing for the first time, appearing in a trailer and on a reversible cover.

Audio designer Joel Green recorded tanks at CFB Wainwright and fired a 105mm shell, whose concussive force informed the sound of the Black Widow sniper rifle; he kept the spent shell, which was later passed between designers. Multiplayer combat voices were recorded by local police and military personnel. Cerberus became a full enemy faction; concept artists explored an "anti-team" with Kai Leng as an anti-Shepard, but it stayed in concept. Designers Corey Gaspur (weapons) and Brenon Holmes (enemies) ran an escalating "arms race" that produced Phantoms able to cut rockets in half, and an Arc Pistol that secretly fired three bullets per trigger pull. Gaspur died during Anthem's development, and Anthem was dedicated to him and to Nathan Mayes.

Kakliosaurs began as a desk-toy mash-up by John Dombrow and appeared in the Citadel DLC. A severe bug let a Tali romance scene fire after her death in a geth-sided playthrough. Mass Effect 3: Special Edition shipped Wii U-exclusive on November 13, 2012, with Genesis 2. Casey Hudson acknowledged the widely criticized ending, saying more should have been done to reward players' investment.
