Acheron was a poorly explored, inhospitable star system in Mass Effect, unlocked as a destination after the Noveria: Geth Interest assignment. It consisted of four terrestrial worlds, one of them tidally locked and another a double-planet body. Prospecting had identified several notable resources, but no organizations or business interests had moved to claim them. The system was the site of a battle during the turians' Unification War early in their history.

The system's star, Acheron, was orbited by four planets. Farthorl was the first planet, Altahe the second, Grosalgen the third, and Imaneya the fourth. Altahe hosted the assignment UNC: Listening Post Theta and yielded collectible asari Matriarch's Writings and surveyable heavy metal and rare element deposits. Grosalgen offered a surveyable light metal deposit. A carbonaceous asteroid in the system held a collectible turian insignia: while scanning an asteroid field there, a small recon and recovery team led by Navigator Pressly determined that many of the wrecked ships were marked with the Epyrus Colony insignia, signs of the earlier battle.

The cluster and its systems were named for the Greek underworld, Hades. Acheron, though strictly a branch of the river Styx, was also frequently used as a synonym for Hades itself. A separate location, the Acheron Spine, appeared in Mass Effect: Andromeda.
