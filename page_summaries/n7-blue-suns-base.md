An optional N7 assignment in Mass Effect 2, unlocked after N7: MSV Strontium Mule. Cerberus Command traced the trap that caught the Strontium Mule to a Blue Suns base on the planet Sanctum, Decoris system, Sigurd's Cradle. The Blue Suns were broadcasting a fabricated distress beacon to lure merchant freighters into orbit so they could be ambushed and raided. Shepard was sent to shut down the beacon and strike a blow to the organization.

The base was the abandoned Brunfeld Mining Facility, which had been closed over worker safety concerns after a botched expansion of the northern mining tunnels into the mess hall. Shepard's shuttle landed at the southern entrance and the squad fought through waves of Blue Suns Troopers, Heavies and a Blue Suns Commander, then pushed through the mess hall and into the mining tunnels. Recovered datapads and interrogation logs authorized by Captain Narom revealed that the Blue Suns, acting on Commander Santiago's hunch, had tortured a client's agent to learn that the client wanted them to move a Prothean artifact from a dig site; deciding the artifact was worth far more than the transport fee, the Suns dispatched ships and men to seize the dig site themselves. A fired worker's message noted the Suns were hiring guns to kill a vigilante on Omega (Archangel).

The climactic battle pitted the squad against two YMIR Mechs simultaneously, then Captain Narom and Blue Suns Troopers. After clearing them, Shepard bypassed the central door and overloaded the transmitter, escaping as it exploded. The assignment deactivated the false distress beacon and eliminated Blue Suns resistance, rewarding experience, 7,500 credits and 2,000 iridium, and leading toward N7: Javelin Missiles Launched.
