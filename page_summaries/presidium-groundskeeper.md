The Presidium Groundskeeper was a turian living on the Citadel who was responsible for maintaining the grounds of the Presidium. Despite his job, he could be found on Level 28 of Zakera Ward in the Dark Star Lounge.

If Commander Shepard overheard two krogan on Level 27 of Zakera Ward talking about fish in the Presidium lakes, the Commander could ask the groundskeeper about it. He admitted he got that question often, and explained that the Presidium lakes held no fish because they were used as reservoirs. He said the only place he knew of to buy a live fish was Deleia Sanassi's gift shop, and as a parting remark he recommended checking out the new flowers coming in from across the Conduit.

The groundskeeper shared traits with the turian Lilihierax in his pride in his work and his laid-back, easy-going manner, contrasting with the typical image of the disciplined turian warrior. Cut voicelines revealed more of his background: a trained horticulturalist, he had left "everything behind" on Palaven and arrived on the Citadel only a month earlier, and he often commented humorously on Citadel nightlife, alcohol, and, being unused to asari or human females, on their appearance.
