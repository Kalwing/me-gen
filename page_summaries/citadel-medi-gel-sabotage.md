Citadel: Medi-Gel Sabotage was a Mass Effect 3 mission available after Priority: The Citadel II, following the Cerberus attack on the Citadel. Medi-gel dispensers in the refugee camp had been sabotaged, and Commander Shepard was asked to repair them by the Clinic Doctor in Cargo Hold B of the Docks: Holding Area. If Conrad Verner was alive, he appeared in front of Bay E24 and also had to be spoken to. Shepard located and repaired three of the four dispensers scattered around the docks, then reported back. In the basic version, the doctor thanked Shepard and lamented being unable to track down the saboteur.

If the doctor also complained about someone recruiting refugees for Cerberus, Shepard found Conrad Verner near Bay E24 by the Memorial Wall. Conrad admitted he had been preaching about joining Cerberus and, told that Cerberus had just tried to kill the Council, realized his mistake; his Cerberus contact had sabotaged the dispensers while Conrad distracted the refugees. Conrad handed over his Dark Energy Dissertation, a War Asset that contributed to the Crucible's construction, its value quintupled if certain first-game tasks had all been imported: completing UNC: Asari Writings and Feros: Data Recovery and purchasing the Elkoss Combine armory license.

After the dispensers were repaired and the doctor spoken to, a final scene played in which Conrad's contact drew a gun and Conrad dove in front of Shepard. If Jenna had been extracted from Chora's Den during Citadel: Rita's Sister in Mass Effect, she had sabotaged the contact's gun, saving Conrad's life, and the two wandered off together; otherwise Conrad was shot and killed. Completing the mission allowed crates of dextro-amino acid medi-gel to be shipped to the Turian Seventh Fleet, increasing its Effective Military Strength.
