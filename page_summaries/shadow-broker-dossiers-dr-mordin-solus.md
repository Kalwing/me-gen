This Shadow Broker dossier, unlocked after the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, collected intelligence on Mordin Solus, a salarian professor formerly attached to the Salarian Special Tasks Group whose modifications to the genophage re-established a 99.9% death rate among krogan births. The Broker judged his work with Shepard an obvious attempt at atonement.

The bulk of the dossier is an STG mission report for Project Firebreak, "Drop 16" at the Weyrloc Clan Trading Center on Tuchanka, in which Solus served as Mission Specialist under Commander Kirrahe. The team infiltrated a Weyrloc agricenter to disperse the genophage-modification payload into soil, water and food; a firefight killed Operatives Jirin and Chorel and wounded Specialist Maelon and others. Solus stabbed a Weyrloc guard through the eye with a pitchfork to stop an alarm, injuring his own face and right cranial horn, then refused evacuation to complete analysis at a secondary site (a Weyrloc hospital). The report repeatedly notes friction between Solus and Kirrahe, e.g. `Solus suggests Commander Kirrahe has foreign obstruction in cloaca`, answered by `Kirrahe suggests Solus is one tough cloaca`. Maelon was distressed to learn the agricenter was staffed by female krogan and formally protested that killing them violated mission parameters; the protest was struck from the record after he was sedated.

A bibliography lists Solus's published works (including classified STG papers such as "Indoctrination Progression and Mental Degradation" and threat-potential analyses of turians, humans, asari and batarians) and his amateur theatre credits, including Major-General Stanley in The Pirates of Penzance and Polonius in an "Interspecies Edition" of Hamlet.

A surveillance transcript from his Omega medical clinic during the plague records his triage philosophy. Mordin, over a dead batarian patient: "More patients than time. Impossible to save everyone. Go crazy if you try. So save who you can, move on." When Blue Suns demand the humans come out, he says only "Will speak with them," then returns after gunfire: "Blue Suns didn't expect resistance. Foolish of them." Learning his assistant Daniel Abrams took some of the cure and went into the slums alone, Mordin responds: "His decision. Hope he lives. Can't help him now. Too busy... Bring next patient."
