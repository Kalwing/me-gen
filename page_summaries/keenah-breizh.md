Keenah'Breizh was a quarian crewmember of the ship Honorata, which was carrying Tali'Zorah nar Rayya to Illium for her Pilgrimage, as recounted in Mass Effect: Homeworlds. Keenah landed with Tali on an unidentified ice planet in the Crescent Nebula to investigate a geth presence. They disabled a geth and extracted its memory core, which held a recording of Saren Arterius and Matriarch Benezia discussing the attack on Eden Prime and the coming of the Reapers. Tali convinced Keenah they should bring the information to authorities on Illium, and they fled when Commander Jacobus attacked.

At Illium, docking officials refused the Honorata clearance because of anti-quarian prejudice, giving Jacobus time to land first and set an ambush that killed the Honorata's crew except Keenah and Tali. The pair stowed away on a transport bound for the Citadel, hoping to give the geth recording to the Council. The transport crew found them but the captain turned them over to C-Sec rather than killing them. Officer Chellick ignored their claims of important information and ordered them to leave the station. After learning from Avina that an audience with the Council had a seven-month wait, they were spotted by Jacobus, who chased them across the Citadel and mortally wounded Keenah. Cornered in an incinerator and unable to keep up, Keenah died, and his body was destroyed when Tali activated the incinerator to kill Jacobus.
