Jella was a batarian woman who worked in the personnel and accounting department of Dah'tan Manufacturing on Camala. Hard-working but underpaid, she was a disgruntled employee who routinely helped outsiders steal from the company by altering purchase orders, manipulating supply records, and leaving inventory unsecured. She was paid well for this and preferred not to know who was behind the thefts.

When Edan Had'dah anonymously asked her to shut down the security system at one of the entrances to the facility, she found the offer too tempting to refuse and complied despite her suspicions. This allowed Skarr and several Blue Suns mercenaries to enter the facility undetected. Because Jella was Had'dah's only inside contact and the only link between him and the company, Skarr was tasked with personally ensuring she was killed.

Moments before the attack began, Jella felt sick and left her post. When the rampage started she hid in the ventilation shaft of the bathroom while her colleagues were killed, including her co-worker She'n'ya, whom Skarr mistook for Jella and killed, believing he had succeeded. From the air shaft, Jella overheard two mercenaries discussing Had'dah, a name she recognized, and learned of his involvement. The building was then destroyed with explosives, but Jella escaped in time and survived, though she was horrifically burned and incapacitated. Batarian rescue teams found her and took her to a hospital.

Saren Arterius, tracking Had'dah, visited the ruined factory and later went to see Jella during her treatment. Though she was in a medically induced coma, Saren sent the medical staff away and woke her to question her. Despite her life-threatening injuries, she gave him Edan Had'dah's name. Saren then deliberately withheld an injection that could have returned her to a coma and saved her life, waited until she died, and only then administered the fluid to cover his tracks.
