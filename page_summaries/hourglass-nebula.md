Short location page. The Hourglass Nebula was a cluster firmly within the Terminus Systems, largely remote despite serving as a relay stopover between the Omega and Illium clusters. Many of its worlds were named by salarian explorers who found barren rock or lucrative robo-mining opportunities. Its systems included Faryar, Osun (containing Purgatory), Ploitari and Sowilo (containing Hagalaz). Missions set here included Dossier: The Convict and Lair of the Shadow Broker. The real-world Hourglass Nebula (MyCn18) lies about 8,000 light-years away in the Musca constellation, catalogued by Annie Jump Cannon and Margaret W. Mayall.
