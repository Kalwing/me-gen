Combat Drone was a tech power in Mass Effect 2 and in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer that deployed a single hovering tech drone to draw enemy fire, briefly stun targets, and damage shields, with only one active at a time. This page details its Mass Effect 2 power ranks and the rank 4 Attack Drone and Explosive Drone evolutions; its Mass Effect 3 ranks 1 through 6 with the Detonate, Shock, Rockets, and Chain Lightning evolutions; drone damage, health, duration, and stagger force values; which enemies ignore it (Husks, Abominations, Brutes, Scions, Praetorians, and others); distraction, cover-breaking, "mobile mine," and early-warning strategies; drone colour and behaviour mechanics; and its availability to the Engineer, Tali'Zorah, and Legion in single-player and the Human Engineer and Volus Mercenary Sentinel in multiplayer.
