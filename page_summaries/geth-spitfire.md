The Geth Spitfire was a geth weapon in Mass Effect 3, classified as a heavy weapon in single-player and as an assault rifle in multiplayer. Like the Geth Plasma Shotgun it was not a true energy weapon: its minigun mechanism magnetically flung clusters of superconducting toroids, doughnut-shaped projectiles designed to shatter on impact, arc electricity between the fragments and flash-convert them to plasma, striking with the heat of a cutting torch. Its stopping power drew the attention of Alliance and quarian intelligence, who theorized it may have been designed to destroy other geth; the number recovered in the field suggested it was past prototyping and in production, implying a schism among the synthetics had forced its manufacture.

In single-player the Spitfire was found on Rannoch during Rannoch: Admiral Koris and Priority: Rannoch, and, with the Citadel DLC or in Legendary Edition, was automatically obtained during Citadel Wards: Ambush. As a heavy weapon it was discarded once its ammunition ran out and could not be carried to future missions; the Rannoch Spitfires held 200 rounds each while the Citadel one held 1000. It was especially useful in the Priority: Rannoch fight against three Geth Primes at once. Like the Geth Plasma SMG it started firing slowly and ramped up rapidly in fire rate and damage the longer the trigger was held, and any interruption reduced its efficiency. Both versions had an inherent 1.75x damage multiplier against shields and barriers. In multiplayer it was an ultra-rare weapon requiring the Reckoning expansion, was the single heaviest weapon in the mode, and had the highest sustained DPS of any weapon when fully modded; it drastically slowed the wielder except for the Geth Juggernaut Soldier and most krogan, and geth characters boosted its damage through the Networked AI passive.
