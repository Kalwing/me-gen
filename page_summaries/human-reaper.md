The Human-Reaper was an incomplete Reaper constructed by the Collectors inside the Collector Base under Harbinger's orders. The Collectors abducted humans from colonies along the edge of the Terminus Systems, stored them in organic pods at the base, and periodically broke them down into raw genetic material, which was pumped through tubes to the base's center and used to build the Human-Reaper. EDI concluded the Collectors were facilitating Reaper reproduction and estimated millions of humans would be needed to complete it. She speculated every Reaper is built to resemble the species used to create it, and that the Reapers had even unsuccessfully attempted to build one from Prothean genetic material.

When Commander Shepard's squad reached it, the Human-Reaper was an inactive larva: a vaguely humanoid upper torso supported by two feeder tubes per shoulder. Destroying the tubes caused it to fall, but it reactivated, climbed back up, and fought the squad, who destroyed it after dealing massive damage to the construct. It attacked by slamming and tearing away platforms, potentially knocking Shepard to their death, and fired a charged energy beam from its mouth that also killed any Collectors in its path; its weak points were its eyes, mouth cannon and chest.

In Mass Effect 3, the raid on Cerberus Headquarters revealed the Illusive Man had salvaged components of the Human-Reaper from the Collector Base regardless of whether the Base was saved or destroyed. Cerberus used the remains to augment Cronos Station, employing the brain for processing power (if the Base was saved) or the heart to power the station (if the Base was destroyed). After Cerberus fell, the Human-Reaper's remains became War Assets incorporated into the Crucible. During development the Human-Reaper was nicknamed "Baby." Writers gave conflicting accounts of its human appearance: Mac Walters said it was meant to be the core of a larger Sovereign-like Reaper, while Chris L'Etoile said Reapers were once meant to individually resemble the species harvested to create them before BioWare standardized all Reaper designs on Sovereign.
