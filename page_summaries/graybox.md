A graybox was a collection of snapshots of a person's memories. Launched by Synthetic Insights in 2160 CE and originally developed to treat Alzheimer's disease, its implant procedure was found to have dangerous side-effects: removing the graybox for maintenance or a software bug developing would cause extensive brain damage. Its use was therefore restricted to people who genuinely needed eidetic memories, such as researchers and intragalactic spies. When accessed with a specialized reader, its memories appeared as snapshots while the viewed memory played as a video.

Only two individuals were known to have grayboxes: partners Kasumi Goto and the late Keiji Okuda. Keiji was killed by Donovan Hock for his heavily encrypted graybox, which Hock had been trying to crack for the information Keiji recorded in it. In Mass Effect 2, if Kasumi was recruited by Commander Shepard, the Commander could help her retrieve the graybox from Hock on Bekenstein. Hock's decryption team had failed with brute-force methods by the time of the infiltration. After fighting through Hock's Eclipse security and Hock himself, Kasumi and Shepard retrieved and accessed the graybox. Its incriminating information, which would implicate the Alliance in something "big" if shown to the Citadel Council, had its encryption key uploaded to Kasumi's own graybox, and images of Reapers were interlaced with Keiji's romantic memories of Kasumi so the data could not be separated. A prerecorded holo-message from Keiji urged Kasumi to destroy the graybox for her safety, and Shepard could advise her to follow his wishes or keep it intact.

In Mass Effect 3, the information was revealed to be evidence of an Alliance black ops raid on a batarian research station holding Reaper technology; the raid became a massacre, the Alliance returned empty-handed, and a power failure was faked to hide the incident. If Kasumi had retrieved the graybox she leaked the information; otherwise Hock apparently cracked it. Either way the intel reached salarian Spectre Jondum Bau in connection with a case he was pursuing. At the end of the Reaper war, if the graybox was not destroyed, Kasumi could be seen holding either it or a holographic representation of Keiji, depending on Shepard's earlier advice. The Codex's stated 2140 release date is a developer error; the canonical release was 2160.
