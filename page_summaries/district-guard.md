District Guards were turians hired by Aria T'Loak to guard the quarantine zone on Omega during the plague outbreak in Mass Effect 2. After Shepard spoke with Aria about Mordin Solus and headed to the quarantine zone, the squad was confronted by a district guard at the transport hub. If Shepard convinced the guard to let them through, he radioed ahead for his companions to stand down; if Shepard failed, the guards opened fire on the squad's arrival.

The guards were armed with M-8 Avenger assault rifles. They had only health on lower difficulties and minor shielding on Hardcore and Insanity, and no biotic or tech powers. They compensated with disciplined use of cover and superior positioning, popping out to fire and ducking back down.
