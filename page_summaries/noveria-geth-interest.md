Noveria: Geth Interest was one of the three core-world missions in Mass Effect, acquired after Shepard gained Spectre status and reported to Dock 422. Udina announced that Anderson was stepping down as commanding officer of the SSV Normandy and that Shepard now commanded the ship. The Council had received reports of geth interest in the corporate research colony of Noveria and needed to know why. Anderson warned that Noveria was trouble, that only Spectres were respected there, and told Shepard to take care.

Noveria lay in the Pax System of the Horse Head Nebula, a private world outside Citadel space where only Spectre authority permitted the visit. On approach, Noveria's traffic controller warned that the Normandy was not on the arrival list and was being tracked by defense systems; Joker invoked Citadel business and a Council Spectre aboard, and the ship was cleared to land pending identity confirmation. Leaving the ship, Shepard's team was blocked from Port Hanshan by Captain Maeko Matsuo, Sergeant Kaira Stirling, and a turian, who demanded the team's weapons under Port Hanshan's no-firearms policy. The standoff was defused when Gianna Parasini confirmed Shepard's Spectre identity over the intercom, granting the privilege to carry guns. Passing through weapons scanners triggered an alarm, and Parasini, administrative assistant to port Administrator Anoleis, explained the port's heavy security protected corporate interests and privacy.

Parasini revealed that Matriarch Benezia had arrived recently and headed to Peak 15, but that Anoleis had to approve any request to leave Port Hanshan. Shepard could discuss whether Liara T'Soni could be trusted against her mother. The journal then renamed the mission Noveria: Leave Port Hanshan and added Noveria: Matriarch Benezia. Shepard gained free access to most of Port Hanshan except the garage, the Synthetic Insights offices, and Anoleis's office, and needed a garage pass to leave, obtainable by ratting out the hanar merchant Opold to Anoleis or by taking up Lorik Qui'in's offer. The mission allowed returning to the Normandy at any point before the final battle, and its concluding encounter was one of the game's most difficult; the walkthrough recommended bringing Wrex.
