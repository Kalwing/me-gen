The Leviathan of Dis was the name given to a gigantic corpse that disappeared from a crater on Jartar in the Dis system in 2163. It was believed to be the remains of a genetically engineered starship, originally dated at nearly a billion years old. There was little time to study it: shortly after its discovery by a batarian survey team, a batarian dreadnought visited the Dis system, after which the Leviathan vanished. The batarians thereafter strenuously denied it had ever existed, becoming more vocal when shown salarian researchers' documentation of it made before its disappearance.

In Mass Effect 3, provided he survived the 2183 batarian terrorist attack on Terra Nova, Balak told Commander Shepard that the Leviathan of Dis had actually been a Reaper corpse transported by the Batarian Hegemony to Khar'shan. After the Terra Nova attack failed, the Hegemony accelerated research on it, and the corpse indoctrinated numerous batarian scientists and officials. Those indoctrinated officials sabotaged Hegemony defenses during the 2186 Reaper invasion, allowing the Reapers to easily conquer batarian territory and crush their scattered navy.

After meeting Dr. Garret Bryson on the Citadel, Shepard learned from a recorded conversation between Bryson and Admiral Hackett that Task Force Aurora had been investigating the Leviathan of Dis. Bryson's true interest was the entity that had killed the Reaper corpse, which he identified as the real Leviathan. Cut content suggested an Alliance black-ops raid on a batarian Reaper-tech lab correlated with the Hegemony's research.
