Commander Rentola was a salarian officer, quartermaster at the salarian camp on Virmire and Captain Kirrahe's second-in-command in the 3rd Regiment Special Tasks Group (3rd STG). During Mass Effect he could sell Commander Shepard the few supplies he had left, which could include several product licenses. He was pessimistic and unimpressed by Shepard and the SSV Normandy, remarking, "We asked for a fleet, but instead they sent... you."

Rentola always survived the assault on Saren's base and afterward joined the other salarians on the Normandy's Cargo and Engineering deck, where he continued to supply Shepard until the ship docked. Even if Kirrahe survived with most of his men, Rentola remained cautious about calling the mission a success and deferred to Kirrahe's judgment; if Kirrahe died, Rentola reported that heavy losses meant his unit would soon be disbanded.

According to the Shadow Broker's dossier on Mordin Solus, Rentola later served with Mordin on the mission to deploy the modified genophage strain on Tuchanka, during which he was lightly wounded.
