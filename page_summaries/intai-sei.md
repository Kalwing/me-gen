Intai'sei was a planet in the Phoenix System of the Argos Rho cluster in the Milky Way. Its atmosphere, similar to Earth's, made it an early candidate for human colonization, but prohibitively high temperatures and an arid climate hindered terraforming and agriculture. A few human cities were founded, its capital being Thoreau Mesa, but most of the human population remained scattered across the deserts, operating wind farms and geological research stations.

Admiral Ahern kept a retirement home on Intai'sei, a prefab dropped by ExoGeni Corporation in a remote location, which he wagered as a prize for completing his special simulator scenario aboard Pinnacle Station. In Mass Effect the planet was added by the Pinnacle Station DLC as the fifth and outermost planet of Phoenix, though its orbital stats placed it between Tuntau and Sylsalto; Mass Effect 3 corrected this, moving it to the third orbital. Because Pinnacle Station was cut from Mass Effect Legendary Edition, Intai'sei no longer exists in the remastered first game. It featured in the Mass Effect 3 mission Citadel: Kakliosaur Fossil and as a Search and Rescue site for the Fossilized Kakliosaur artifact. Its name may derive from the Japanese "intaiseikatsu" ("secluded life"), and Thoreau Mesa may reference essayist Henry David Thoreau.
