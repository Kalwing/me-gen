N7 Armor was Commander Shepard's default armor set in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, designed to augment tech, biotic and weapons damage while adding health and shield strength. It was modular, allowing chestpieces, gauntlets, pauldrons, helmets and leg pieces to be swapped in the Normandy SR-2's armor locker. In Mass Effect 2 it was first obtained in Prologue: Awakening, derived from the first game's heavy model with a new helmet and right arm and a grainier finish; until additional parts were acquired it could only be customized cosmetically. Individual pieces gave small bonuses (health, power damage, weapon damage or shield strength). In Mass Effect 3 it was obtained on Priority: Mars, restored some metallic and matte finishes, and every N7 piece granted a 10% health bonus.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda, N7 Armor was a craftable four-piece set (ranks I-X) worn originally by Alec Ryder and refitted for Pathfinder Ryder, with stats focused on max shields, biotic power damage and biotic recharge speed. It could only be crafted or, after the Market Dominance perk, bought from the Nexus Armor Merchant. August Bradley commented disapprovingly if Ryder wore the N7 Chestplate, cautioning about Alliance veterans. The Onyx Armor from the first game shared the N7 symbol and red striping. The armor also appeared as crossover content in Dead Space 3, Kingdoms of Amalur: Reckoning, and Final Fantasy XIII-2.
