The Mad Prophet was a batarian doomsayer who preached on Omega. In Mass Effect 2 he stood on a crate near the entrance to Harrot's Emporium, addressing a small crowd with anti-human sermons and frequent quotations from a scripture he called "the Word." Omega's public speakers occasionally broadcast his fire-and-brimstone sermons, apparently paid for by the Followers of the Exalted Light of the Word. He returned in the Mass Effect 3: Omega DLC, encountered in 2186 during Aria T'Loak's campaign to retake the station from Cerberus, preaching to a large crowd in the Gozu District during the assault on Afterlife. His theme throughout was that humanity was a blight on galactic purity and "the hands of the Reapers." One of his lines echoes T.S. Eliot's poem The Hollow Men, and the character may reference Gorton Colu from Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic. The page collects his sermon lines.

Mad Prophet, preaching on Omega (Mass Effect 2): "Humans are a blight on galactic purity. You sir! You are a blight! And you! And you, human. And you!"

Mad Prophet, on the Protheans (Mass Effect 2): "The Protheans cast off the lesser races: the krogan, the vorcha, and the humans. ... The lesser races will be our downfall! The Word is clear."

Mad Prophet, quoting the Word (Mass Effect 2): "'Let none in my sight continue to live, lest their impurity displease me.' The Word is clear on this, friends. Humans are the hands of the Reapers, here to commit the galaxy to ruin!"

Mad Prophet, quoting the Word (Mass Effect 2): "'Bring unto me the children, that I may watch them grow to soldiers for the cause.' The way is clear, my friends. Draw your weapons for the Word, or face its terrible wrath!"

Mad Prophet, on the end times (Mass Effect 2): "The end times will come, not with a bang, but with a sigh. Those who dip their extremities in the well of sin and vice must repent with fire, oil, and gun!"

Mad Prophet, during Aria's assault (Mass Effect 3: Omega): "You were warned! You were all warned! Watch now as the hands of the Reapers, the human invaders, lay ruin to all we hold dear!"

Mad Prophet, during Aria's assault (Mass Effect 3: Omega): "Repent! Repent and restore your souls to glory before it is too late! This great station has fallen, and all within shall fall with it!"

Mad Prophet, during Aria's assault (Mass Effect 3: Omega): "Sally forth and blaze gloriously through the stars!"
