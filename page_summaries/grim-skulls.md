The Grim Skulls were a small mercenary organization operating in the Skyllian Verge, made up of a few dozen armed thugs and criminals with basic military training. They drew the attention of Saren Arterius when they attempted to buy a shipment of military-grade weapons stolen from a turian transport freighter. Because turians were among the sellers and humans among the Grim Skulls buyers, Saren chose to handle the matter personally rather than turn it over to local authorities. The smugglers' and Grim Skulls' operations were amateurish, and Saren wiped out most of the participants single-handedly while they argued over the deal in a warehouse on Juxhi. Before Saren executed him, an injured human member revealed that the Blue Suns had been the original buyers with a major operation planned, but had pulled out of the deal to avoid attracting Spectre attention. These events were detailed in Mass Effect: Revelation.
