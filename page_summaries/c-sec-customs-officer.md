The C-Sec Customs Officer was an unnamed female human working for Citadel Security Services' Customs and Immigration in Zakera Ward. Her job required confiscating biotic amps and weapons before allowing people to enter the ward, and her motto was "be alert, be safe"; she believed assumptions were dangerous. She had an ongoing dispute with an unnamed turian who accused her of racism whenever she made a request, including over his attempt to board a shuttle with a ceremonial blade. She refused to let two asari depart for Thessia, flagging them as a potential risk for geth infiltration. If Commander Shepard chose to help the asari, the Commander could either persuade the officer or use Spectre authority to order her to let them leave; afterward she addressed Shepard as "sir" regardless of gender. Despite the heightened security, she failed to recognize Legion as a geth, calling it Shepard's "personal synthetic assistant." Her posting and branch were identified through her uniform, a shuttle directory, and dialogue from Captain Bailey.
