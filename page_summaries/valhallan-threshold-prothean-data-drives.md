"Valhallan Threshold: Prothean Data Drives" was a fetch mission in Mass Effect 3, available after Priority: The Citadel II and only after the Cerberus attack on the Citadel. Commander Shepard could overhear an Alliance soldier talking to his superior about the Crucible near the door to the Normandy at Normandy Dock: Bay D24. The soldier knew the location of Prothean data drives that could aid the Crucible project, on the planet Garvug in the Valhallan Threshold. Alternatively, a Search and Rescue scan operation over the planet Paz in the Valhallan Threshold yielded the drives directly, in which case the journal entry was labeled "Citadel: Prothean Data Drives" and instructed Shepard to find someone on the Citadel who could use them.

The walkthrough was to scan Garvug (Paz) to obtain the artifact and return it to the soldier on the Citadel. Completion granted 5 Reputation, 15,000 credits, and an upgrade to the War Asset Alliance Engineering Corps. Trivia noted the soldier's wide speaking radius made the mission hard to avoid triggering, though it could be bypassed by rushing past him, save/reloading, or completing a Leviathan mission that deposited Shepard directly on the Normandy.
