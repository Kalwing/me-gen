Gozu District was a residential area in the lower levels of Omega, home to Kokomo Plaza and the Omega Clinic and served by a life support system independent of the rest of the station. It bordered the Doru and Kima Districts. For many years the Blue Suns provided security there, preventing crime and keeping residents in line. In mid-2185 this changed: the Blue Suns came under attack from a vigilante group led by the Archangel, and a bio-engineered plague that spared only humans and vorcha cut their Gozu numbers by half.

In late 2185, Tarak, leader of the Blue Suns, allied with the Blood Pack and Eclipse and planned an offensive against the Archangel from a base in Gozu District. At the same time a plague engineered by the Collectors was released into the district's residential blocks. Aria T'Loak, Omega's de facto ruler, ordered the district quarantined and posted guards at the entrances to the contaminated area, and the Blood Pack began pushing into the district as batarian and turian Blue Suns members died.

During Aria T'Loak's 2186 campaign to liberate Omega from Cerberus occupation, the final assault on the Cerberus command center at Afterlife advanced through Gozu District, where the Mad Prophet could be seen preaching to a large crowd in one of its corridors. The district's station ID was 429, its logo was apparently repurposed from an unused corporation logo in the first game, and several signs in the district read "Auxera," the meaning of which is unknown.
