Cyone was the second planet of the Kypladon System in the Silean Nebula, a garden world under the protection of several asari matriarchs. During the Krogan Rebellions it was assaulted repeatedly but never occupied by more than token forces; the asari cut off the krogan supply line at the Citadel relay and drove the invaders out. By 2186 antimatter generators on its surface and in orbital stations supplied fuel for military starships throughout asari space, and its capital city was named A polos. During the Reaper War the matriarchs pledged the planet would fall to no enemy, organic or synthetic. Cyone began supplying fuel to Systems Alliance forces in hope that an asari-human partnership might prevent the Reapers from seizing it, and the Alliance established several outposts in the system. A small turian detachment drawn from a corporate conglomerate's private militia also joined the defense, with negligible integration issues. The task force repelled several Reaper attacks, though commanders feared small Reaper forces had slipped through. Cyone was accessible after the mission Priority: Geth Dreadnought and featured in the missions Citadel: Chemical Treatment and N7: Fuel Reactors. The multiplayer map Firebase Reactor is located there.
