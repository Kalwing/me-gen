Seeker Swarm was a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode, available only to the Awakened Collector Adept. At Rank 1 it summoned three Seeker Swarms to cloud around the user; activating the power again launched a swarm at the target, dealing 150 damage and slowing the target's movement by 10% for 4 seconds, with a 24-second recharge. Higher ranks improved recharge speed and damage, and offered branching choices: Rank 4 between further recharge speed and a stronger, longer slow; Rank 5 between a Damage Reduction evolution (10% damage protection per active swarm) and a 40% damage boost; and Rank 6 between adding one more swarm or a large damage and slow increase. Fully evolved damage reached up to 315 per swarm.

The power had multiple functions: it could detonate Biotic Explosions on targets primed by Dark Channel or Dark Sphere, deal damage over time while slowing movement, and, with the Damage Reduction evolution, act as an armor ability mitigating up to 30% (40% with Rank 6 Swarm Count) of incoming damage. It behaved like the Volus Adept's Biotic Orbs or the Geth Juggernaut Soldier's Siege Pulse: the cooldown applied when first activated, after which swarms could be thrown in succession without triggering a cooldown, with unused swarms orbiting the user. New swarms could only be summoned once all previous ones were spent, and it was advised to resupply between waves because the summoning animation paused the user and pulled them out of cover. Like Throw, it could force a Guardian to hold its shield away from its body.
