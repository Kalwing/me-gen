Castis Vakarian was a retired C-Sec officer and the father of Garrus and Solana Vakarian. On Palaven he personally taught Garrus to shoot as a child, refusing to let his son give up when the sniper rifle proved difficult and telling him that quitting when things got hard would get him nowhere. While Castis was working on the Citadel, his wife was injured in a hit-and-run accident on Palaven, but she discouraged him from returning because of the distance. After Garrus joined C-Sec the two became colleagues and clashed over a case in which Castis released a suspect because of Garrus's rule-breaking methods; Castis insisted on adhering to the rules, and father and son grew distant. When Garrus later tracked down and physically assaulted that freed suspect after the Battle of the Citadel, Castis threatened to lock his son up personally.

By 2185 Castis had retired to Palaven. He received a call from Garrus during Garrus's last stand on Omega as the sole survivor of his vigilante group, and gave him combat advice, telling him that as long as he had one bullet left he could still finish the job, and urging him to come home afterward. Earlier, during Garrus's Spectre training, Castis had talked him out of becoming a Spectre because he distrusted operatives not held accountable by law, and he took Garrus's C-Sec resignation to join Shepard's hunt for the rogue Spectre Saren Arterius badly, fearing his son would become like Saren.

During his C-Sec years Castis befriended the human military attache Alec Ryder and kept up the friendship after Alec's discharge from the Alliance; in a reviewable memory he warned Alec about the Reapers, vouching for Shepard on Garrus's word that the Commander was level-headed and an N7. In Mass Effect 3, if Garrus survived the Collector mission, Castis heard the full story from his son, believed him, and pushed Primarch Fedorian into establishing a task force for Palaven despite Fedorian's disbelief. Castis and Solana were stranded on Palaven when the Reapers attacked; Solana broke her leg and communications were cut, but Garrus later learned both had made it off-world, their ultimate fate unknown.
