Zakera Ward was one of the five wards of the Citadel, serving as a hub location with several shops, a club, and a Citadel Security (C-Sec) office. It functioned as a starting and finishing point for numerous missions and assignments in Mass Effect 2, including Garrus Vakarian's and Thane Krios's loyalty missions. Rapid transport terminals shuttled visitors between its levels.

According to Captain Bailey, the ward's population was dominated by volus, elcor, and hanar, with humans concentrated in a commercial zone called Shin Akiba; krogan and quarians appeared only occasionally. Transients lived in the 633 Block, sheltering in HabCapsules.

Geographically, the point closest to the Presidium was Presidium Junction and the farthest was Zakera Point, with the Mid-Ward District and Keel Docking Ports roughly equidistant between them. The Factory District and 800 Block lay between the Mid-Ward District and Presidium Junction. Other landmarks included the Vol-Clan Exchange, the Courts of the Center, and the Shrines of the Enkindlers. A shuttle schedule listed travel times of about 30 minutes to the Presidium proper, Shin Akiba, or Zakera Point, 20 minutes to the Vol-Clan Exchange, and 10 minutes to the Courts of the Center and the Shrine of the Enkindlers.

In the Mid-Ward District stood a building of at least 28 levels, which Commander Shepard visited in 2185 to begin several missions and assignments. Level 23 held the Fishdog Food Factory. Level 26 held Rapid Transit, Saronis Applications, a Shipping Warehouse, the Sirta Foundation, and a stand. Level 27 held a C-Sec Office, Citadel Souvenirs, Rapid Transit, the Zakera Cafe, and the Zakera Level 27 Dock. Level 28 held the Dark Star Lounge, a Game Shop, the store Palaven, Rapid Transit, and Rodam Expeditions.
