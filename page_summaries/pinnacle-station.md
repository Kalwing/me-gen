Pinnacle Station was the second downloadable content pack for Mass Effect, developed by Demiurge Studios and released on August 25, 2009 for 400 Microsoft or BioWare Points. It added a landable Alliance training station to the Galaxy Map, in the Phoenix system of the Argos Rho cluster, along with 13 combat simulation scenarios and roughly two to three hours of gameplay in which the player competed in battle simulations to earn weapons and armor for the main game, unlock achievements, and win ownership of Shepard's Apartment on Intai'sei. Challenge modes included Survival, Time Attack, Hunt and Capture, and a scenario modeled on the First Contact War. It added three assignments (Pinnacle Station: Combat Missions, Pinnacle Station: Convoy, Pinnacle Station: Vidinos), three achievements (New Sheriff in Town, Best of the Best, Undisputed), and two locations. Bring Down the Sky was the only other Mass Effect DLC pack.

It was included in the PC edition of the Mass Effect Trilogy but not the PlayStation 3 port. Between the original release and later re-releases, BioWare discovered its source code archive for the DLC was corrupted, and Demiurge Studios' backups were also mostly unusable. Mac Walters of the Mass Effect Legendary Edition team described an "emotional rollercoaster" and estimated a rebuild would take another full six months with most of the team, calling its omission heartbreaking. As a result Pinnacle Station was cut from Mass Effect Legendary Edition. A known Xbox 360 bug made the station inaccessible when Bring Down the Sky was installed from the Platinum Hits bonus disc, requiring both packs to be deleted and reinstalled in a specific order.
