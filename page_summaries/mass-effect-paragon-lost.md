Mass Effect: Paragon Lost was an animated feature film produced by BioWare, FUNimation, T.O Entertainment, and Production I.G, screened in select theaters on November 29, 2012 and released digitally and on disc in December 2012. A prequel to Mass Effect 3, it followed the early career of Alliance Marine James Vega (voiced by Freddie Prinze Jr.). In 2183 the human colony of Fehl Prime, a major pharmaceutical producer, was attacked by Blood Pack mercenaries led by the krogan Archuk. Alliance shuttles were shot down; only Delta Squad survived, and Vega saved its commanding officer Captain Toni before dueling and helping kill Archuk, sparing his second Brood as a prisoner. Admiral Steven Hackett ordered Delta Squad to remain and defend the colony.

Two years later, with the colony rebuilt and fitted with an anti-ship cannon and kinetic barriers, Delta Squad investigated a jamming signal with asari anthropologist Treeya and the colonist Messner. They found an alien device; Treeya used a Prothean communication system to contact her mentor Liara T'Soni, who suggested Reaper tech before the link was jammed by an arriving Collector ship. Seeker Swarms incapacitated the colonists. The squad's low-power cannon shot failed, the cannon was destroyed, and squad members were killed or captured as they retreated to underground labs. There they learned Messner was a Cerberus agent, found Brood held as a test subject, and were attacked by a Praetorian incorporating Kamille's body. Brood led survivors to his hidden ship in exchange for repaying his debt to Vega; Messner shot Brood dead and the group was captured aboard the Collector ship.

Vega, Essex, and Milque escaped using a Seeker antidote Vega had secretly spiked into a shared drink, and planted antidote canisters to free the captured colonists. Treeya, forced by Messner to access a Prothean archive, witnessed the Protheans' transformation into Collectors and the plan to harvest humans to build a Human-Reaper. The Collectors turned on Messner; Treeya grabbed his data bracelet and was ejected in an escape pod. Vega killed the Collectors and left Messner to die, then faced a choice: evacuate the revived colonists or pursue Treeya and her intel. He chose the intel. The Collector ship crashed into Fehl Prime, killing all colonists aboard. On the Citadel, Vega was commended by David Anderson and Hackett, told the intel could yield an antidote and new weapons and that Commander Shepard was seemingly alive, and ordered to report for N7 training. He resolved to honor the lost colonists' sacrifice. The score was composed by Joshua R. Mosley and David Kates.
