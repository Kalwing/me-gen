Priority: Mars was the first full mission of Mass Effect 3, acquired automatically after the Reaper assault on Earth in the prologue. Admiral Hackett ordered the Normandy to the Prothean archives on Mars, where the dig site had gone silent, to recover data that might help defeat the Reapers. Shepard landed with James Vega and found Cerberus troops executing Alliance researchers; this was the first appearance of Cerberus as an enemy faction in the trilogy, introducing Assault Troopers, Centurions and Guardians. Among the dead was a researcher named Reeves.

Inside the facility Shepard reunited with Dr. Liara T'Soni, who revealed she had discovered Prothean plans for a device ("the Crucible") capable of stopping the Reapers, and that Cerberus was there to seize them. Shepard sent Vega back to the shuttle to protect the team's exit and took Liara as squadmate. The two fought through the research complex, a sabotaged cafeteria where the air had been vented, corridors, a clean room, an autoturret gauntlet and a tram station, learning that Cerberus operatives had been implanted with Reaper technology. Shepard posed as a Cerberus team to lure a tram, then fought through a large battle at the archive station.

In the archive chamber the Illusive Man appeared by hologram. The Cerberus infiltrator Dr. Eva Coré was caught stealing the Prothean data, triggering a rooftop chase. Eva reached a shuttle, but Vega crashed his shuttle into hers. Her organic disguise was destroyed, revealing a mech infiltration unit, and on the Illusive Man's order she gravely injured Kaidan Alenko or Ashley Williams before Shepard shot her down. Shepard ordered the body brought aboard the Normandy, which left Mars orbit just as the Reapers arrived. The wounded crewmate was taken to the medical bay, and Shepard ordered Joker to set course for the Citadel. Hackett confirmed Anderson's order to seek Council aid and requested the Crucible plans for Alliance analysis. Eva's body would later be reactivated by EDI.
