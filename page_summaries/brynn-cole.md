Dr. Brynn Cole was an ex-Cerberus scientist who studied technology left behind by the Collectors and led an enclave of Cerberus defectors on Gellix in Mass Effect 3. She had previously worked in physics, which she called a "researcher's paradise" with access to cutting-edge technology, before her Collector work, which involved dissecting Reaper-to-Collector communication and beginning work on a practical human implant. When colleagues who had finished their portions of the project began disappearing without a trace, she deduced the Illusive Man was responsible. Because she held a high position in Cerberus's hierarchy, she used her relative freedom to plan an escape, and after learning that four of her group were missing, she and her remaining colleagues cut ties with Cerberus and fled.

Cole had met Jacob Taylor through a mutual friend in Cerberus's Mumbai cell a year before her defection. If Jacob survived Shepard's suicide mission, Brynn went to him for help and protection; he agreed, they entered a relationship, and together they helped dozens of scientists and their families leave the organization. On Gellix her group sheltered in a Cerberus base, believing they had covered their tracks, but the Illusive Man sent troops to recapture them, dealing enough damage to cripple their escape plans. If Commander Shepard rescued Brynn and the scientists, she joined the Crucible Project, applying her Cerberus knowledge to help build the weapon. If Jacob had died in the suicide mission, she was less trusting of Shepard until the Commander sent additional troops. She later emailed Shepard a Crucible update thanking the Commander and praising Admiral Hackett.

If Shepard spoke with Jacob afterward at Huerta Memorial Hospital on the Citadel, Jacob revealed that Brynn was carrying their child and wished to name it after Commander Shepard, though he planned to talk her out of it; intercepted correspondence on Liara's Broker Terminal also showed the depth of their affection. If Shepard activated the Crucible, Brynn and Jacob could be seen together afterward, consoling a young woman amid the devastated surroundings.
