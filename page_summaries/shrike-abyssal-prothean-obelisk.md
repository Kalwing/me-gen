"Shrike Abyssal: Prothean Obelisk" was a fetch mission in Mass Effect 3. A volus diplomat at the Citadel Embassies needed a Prothean obelisk, and Commander Shepard was tasked with recovering it from the Shrike Abyssal and delivering it to him. An alternative journal entry framed it as a Prothean obelisk recovered from a volus site that needed to be matched with someone on the Citadel who could use it.

The mission required completion of Priority: Mars, and could be acquired either by overhearing the volus speaking with a human and a turian in the far left corner of the Embassies from the elevator, or by scanning the target planet first. The obelisk was located on the planet Talis Fia in the Urla Rast system of the Shrike Abyssal; Shepard scanned the planet and recovered the artifact with a probe. The relevant cluster only opened for travel after Priority: Palaven. Returning the artifact to the volus rewarded 5 Reputation and 20,000 credits.
