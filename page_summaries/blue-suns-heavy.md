Blue Suns Heavies were squad-support troops of the Blue Suns mercenary organization in Mass Effect 2, always human and armed with ML-77 Missile Launchers. Their role was to inflict heavy damage and keep opponents suppressed while staying as far from them as possible, ducking in and out of cover and firing tracking missiles that could stagger a hit target, arc around cover, and switch targets in mid-flight. They were encountered frequently at Blue Suns bases and places of interest. The article noted two types, helmeted and helmetless, with the helmetless ones seemingly acting as commanders and having substantially better defenses; Heavies normally lacked shields but gained them on higher difficulties or in the late game, and helmetless Heavies could carry Tech Armor that restored shields to full. Because they used missiles rather than firearms, Heavies did not suffer weapon overheat.

Recommended counters included sniper rifles, with heavy pistol fire effective once shields were down, or concentrating powers to stop their bombardment and knock them from cover; standing behind tall cover without attaching to it could halt their suppressing fire. The page also observed that their missiles could damage or kill other Blue Suns and even the Heavy herself at point-blank range, most notably on Korlus, and that biotic lifts such as Singularity or the Dominate power could neutralize them.
