Cooperative multiplayer was a game mode in Mass Effect 3 and the first multiplayer in the Mass Effect series. Up to four players joined online, customized characters, and fought the Reapers, Cerberus, geth and Collector forces. Progress fed the Mass Effect 3: Galaxy at War system, raising Galactic Readiness and letting max-level characters be promoted into War Assets that improved the single-player campaign's outcome, though co-op was optional for the best endings. Play required an Online Pass, later made free; the mode was not included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. BioWare launched the N7 HQ stat-tracking website on October 11, 2012. The mode originated from the cancelled shooter Mass Effect Team Assault, and its characters were voiced by police and military personnel local to BioWare.

Matches ran eleven waves of enemies across galaxy firebases at four difficulties: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum (Platinum added by the Earth DLC). Random objectives appeared at waves three, six and ten: Devices, Assassination, Hacking, Retrieval (Rebellion Pack), and Escort (Earth). Wave eleven required defending then extracting to a zone for bonus experience and credits. Players chose from six classes and twelve races for 65 unique characters, capped at level 20 (2,500,000 XP), with 84 power points available. The Store sold randomized item packs bought with earned credits or real currency. Rankings included N7 Ranking, Challenge Ranking, and per-class Promotion Ranking. Challenges (from Retaliation) awarded Challenge Points and lobby banners. Weekend Challenges rewarded Commendation Packs; Weekend Events rewarded item packs; the program ended with Operation Lodestar on March 10, 2013. Individual and squad medals gave bonus XP at milestones.

Standard enemy factions were Cerberus (smoke screens, elite Phantoms and Atlas mechs), all-synthetic geth (stun-locking, Geth Prime), Reapers (Brutes and Banshees), and, from Retaliation, the Collectors (kamikaze Abominations, possessed units, Praetorians and Scions). Free expansion packs were Resurgence (April 10, 2012), Rebellion (May 29, 2012), Earth (July 17, 2012), Retaliation (October 9, 2012), and Reckoning (February 26, 2013), the last multiplayer DLC. Maps ranged from Firebase Dagger, Ghost, Giant, Glacier, Reactor and White through pack-specific bases and Retaliation hazard variants.
