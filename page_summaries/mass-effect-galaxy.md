Mass Effect Galaxy was a Mass Effect spinoff, a top-down shooter developed by BioWare and published by Electronic Arts for iOS, released June 22, 2009. It used the device's accelerometer and touchscreen for movement, targeting, and abilities, and had a stylized "graphic novel" art style with 2D animated cutscenes by artist Joy Ang, distinct from the trilogy's photo-realistic look. It was removed from sale in February 2012. Its story focused on Jacob Taylor and Miranda Lawson, who later appear together as squadmates in Mass Effect 2.

Set before Jacob joined Cerberus in 2183, the plot began with rising galactic tension as batarian ambassador Jath'Amon arranged a meeting with the Citadel Council ostensibly to negotiate peace between the batarians and the Alliance. Jacob Taylor, a biotic soldier and former Alliance marine, was on vacation aboard the passenger liner Arcturian Jade when batarian terrorists attacked it. Armed with his retained assault rifle, Jacob fought through a plot to carry out an attack threatening the Citadel, working alongside Miranda. Locations included the Ahn'Kedar Orbital Platform, Cartagena Station, Tortuga, and the Citadel.

The game featured a conversation-wheel dialogue system that showed full lines and morality icons, and a limited Galaxy Map restricted to mission destinations. Linking the game to an EA account unlocked a Mass Effect 2 reward: extra dialogue with Ish, available when Miranda and Jacob are both in the party (automatic in Legendary Edition). A version 1.1 patch in July 2009 improved performance on lower-end devices.
