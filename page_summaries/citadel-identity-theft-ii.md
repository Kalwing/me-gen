Citadel: Identity Theft II was awarded automatically on returning to the Personal Apartment after Silver Coast Casino: Infiltration, with Elijah Khan now dead. Shepard ordered Brooks and EDI to check Khan's recovered data drives for anything useful while the squadmates waited in the apartment, EDI and Liara having swapped positions from the previous visit. Speaking to Brooks advanced the mission: she and EDI had cracked a communication from a few days earlier detailing large weapons purchases, including mechs. Glyph then reported that Shepard's Spectre access code had just been used at the Citadel Archives, which a squadmate (Garrus, or Liara if he was dead) noted they had been unable to enter despite ostensibly having the authority. A volus arrived delivering a double-pepperoni pizza that James had ordered, and needed the bill paid. Shepard then chose a squad for the next mission while the remaining crew split into two support teams to come along, leading into Citadel Archives: Escape.
