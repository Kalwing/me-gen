Sergeant Cathka was a male batarian employed by the Blue Suns as a mechanic on Omega. During the mercenary operation to kill the vigilante Archangel, he was technically part of the infiltration group, but with the gang's gunship out of commission he hung back near the gunship hangar by the third barricade to take charge of repairs and to organize the freelancers the gangs had hired for the assault.

During Commander Shepard's mission to recruit Archangel, Shepard met Cathka at the hangar. He recognized Shepard's team because Salkie had radioed earlier that this particular group stood out from the other freelancers. Taking a cigarette break from his repair work, Cathka willingly answered Shepard's questions about the plan: the freelancers were expendable distractions meant to occupy Archangel while the infiltration team either moved in to kill him at close quarters or rigged his base with explosives. He explained that the bridge was the only way in unless the infiltrators cleared the blocked tunnels leading to Archangel's base or the gunship, to be flown by the Blue Suns leader Tarak, was returned to service. He had no time for "great ideas" from freelancers, considering the Blue Suns plan solid, though he assured Shepard the infiltration team would do its part if the freelancers did theirs.

When the call came to send in the next wave of freelancers, Cathka relayed it and directed the group with him, remarking that Archangel was in for a surprise and that he should get back to work. Shepard could pick up a sparking tool nearby and use it to incapacitate Cathka, preventing him from completing the gunship repairs.
