Kolyat Krios was the drell son of Thane and Irikah Krios. As described in Mass Effect: Foundation, Thane proclaimed him a gift from Arashu at birth but was an absent, clueless parent; Kolyat was raised by Irikah while Thane's freelance assassination jobs grew longer and farther away. When Thane's enemies attacked, Kolyat hid on his mother's instruction and was never found, but never saw Irikah alive again. He witnessed Thane's grief and breakdown while in the custody of crime scene investigators.

In Mass Effect 2, Thane had stored money with volus bankers on the Citadel as Kolyat's inheritance, but Kolyat took it early and became a hitman. Hired by Elias Kelham through information from Mouse, he was contracted to assassinate the turian politician Joram Talid in the 800 Blocks. Commander Shepard and Thane tracked Talid from separate positions to stop Kolyat from becoming a killer. If they failed to coordinate, Kolyat killed Talid and escaped; otherwise Shepard interrupted him and, with Thane, stopped the execution. Kolyat, furious at Thane's lifelong absence, calmed as his father explained why Irikah was killed and why he could not return, and called Kolyat the only good thing he ever added to the world. In the C-Sec interrogation room Kolyat could face community service, work for Captain Bailey, or go to trial; Thane stayed in contact either way.

In Mass Effect 3, if reconciled, the two remained in contact. Kolyat appeared at Thane's side after Kai Leng mortally wounded Thane during the Cerberus attack on the Citadel; despite a blood transfusion, Kepral's Syndrome made recovery impossible. At Thane's deathbed, Kolyat finished his father's prayer to Kalahira, having spent the past year with the drell priests, and led a prayer for redemption that Thane revealed was intended for Shepard. In the Citadel DLC, Kolyat emailed Shepard requesting a memorial service, held at Shepard's apartment with Thane's old crewmates and the salarian Councilor. He planned to lobby the Councilor (Valern or Esheel) to fund salarian research into Kepral's Syndrome, and told Shepard he had found three intercepted video messages from Thane addressed to the Commander.
