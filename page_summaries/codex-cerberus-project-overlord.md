This Mass Effect 2 "Overlord" DLC Codex described the planet Aite, a garden world in the Terminus Systems' Phoenix Massing cluster with a mild climate, Earth-like wildlife, and soil and bacteria suited to imported plants, colonized for nearly a century but sparsely settled. It was unpopular for two reasons. First, its moon Litae was in an unstable orbit that would cause a planetary impact and extinction-level event within the next two centuries, so all investment was short-term and the biggest business was selling off local biota to the highest bidder. Second, the planet was extremely violent: briefly considered part of Citadel space during its first wave of colonization, it broke away as an independent world in 2133, and the Council released the doomed planet after less than a day of debate. With no real governing body, Aite's history was filled with wars between small frontier-town city-states over its resources, leaving the average citizen expected to fend for themselves against cutthroat corporations, strong-arm militia groups, and geth incursions. The fighting was so frequent that the planet's name had changed more than eleven times; standard Citadel galaxy maps used the name given by human colonists in the latter half of the century.
