Dis was a star system consisting of four terrestrial planets and one gas giant. Batarians held a vested interest in the region: they falsified survey data on the planet Klensal to make it appear resource-rich, luring human businesses into developing infrastructure in the system and bankrupting them when the "gold rush" proved a bust.

Batarians also discovered the remains of a billion-year-old starship on the planet Jartar and publicly denied its existence after recovering it. This wreck, the "Leviathan of Dis," was in fact a Reaper corpse. Personnel working on the corpse became indoctrinated and contributed to the downfall of the Batarian Hegemony during the Reaper invasion of 2186.

The system's bodies were Jartar (first planet), Nearrum (second), Klensal (third), Gremar (fourth), and Raysha (fifth), along with a metallic asteroid. Klensal was tied to the assignment UNC: Hostile Takeover and yielded a League of One Medallion and mineral surveys. The system is likely named for Dis Pater (shortened to Dis), an alternative name for the underworld in Greek mythology, and possibly also for the City of the Dead in Dante's Inferno.
