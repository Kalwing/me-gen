The M-8 Avenger was a common, versatile, military-grade assault rifle in Mass Effect 2, Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Andromeda, manufactured by the Elkoss Combine. Its modular design and inexpensive components made it a favorite of military groups and mercenaries, with a reputation for being tough, reliable, easy to use and easy to upgrade. It was accurate in short bursts and deadly on full auto, effective at penetrating shields, armor and biotic barriers.

In Mass Effect 2 it was the default assault rifle acquired during Freedom's Progress for a Soldier, or later via assault rifle specialization on the Collector Cruiser; usable squadmates included Garrus, Grunt, Legion, Samara/Morinth and Zaeed. Its main drawback was low damage per shot, though it had the largest ammo capacity of any assault rifle except the M-76 Revenant.

In Mass Effect 3 it was found during Prologue: Earth next to the radio and was one of five multiplayer starter weapons, categorized as common. It dealt low damage but was very light with a decent rate of fire, large ammo reserve and fast reload, and was recommended to be replaced once better weapons appeared. In Andromeda many Avengers were acquired for Initiative security forces. Its appearance was based on the default Hahne-Kedar Lancer from the first Mass Effect, and the N7 Valkyrie was a variant. Joker was seen using one to provide covering fire at the end of Mass Effect 2, and Jacob Taylor used a heat-based variant in Mass Effect Galaxy.
