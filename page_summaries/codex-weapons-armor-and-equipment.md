The Codex/Weapons, Armor and Equipment page compiled primary and secondary Codex entries on military technology from across the Mass Effect trilogy. Mass accelerators used precisely controlled electromagnetic force to propel a metal slug designed to squash or shatter on impact; element zero fields lightened the slug for much higher velocities, though recoil equal to impact energy remained the prime limiting factor. Small arms from pistols to assault rifles applied this on a micro scale, shearing rounds from a single internal metal block that could supply thousands of shots; the geth later introduced detachable heat sinks called thermal clips, which organic armies adopted after concluding that firefights were won by whoever put the most rounds downrange fastest. Kinetic barriers, or "shields," were repulsive mass effect fields from tiny emitters that deflected fast-moving objects but did not protect against temperature extremes, toxins, or radiation. Body armor combined ballistic cloth, ablative ceramic plates, kinetic-barrier emitters, and a microframe-run self-healing system that sealed tears with sterile medi-gel. Medi-gel was a genetically engineered bioplasm from the Sirta Foundation combining anesthetic, disinfectant, and clotting agent, technically in violation of Council anti-genetic-engineering law. Omni-tool weapons centered on the disposable silicon-carbide "omni-blade," developed after the Reaper invasion forced close-quarters husk fighting.

Heavy and specialist weapons covered included the Reaper-built Blackstar, which fired micro black holes; the Collector particle beam, an organic-component weapon Cerberus failed to replicate; the geth Spitfire, which magnetically flung superconducting toroids that flash-converted to plasma and may have been designed to destroy other geth; the M-560 Hydra missile barrage; the experimental M-622 Avalanche cryo weapon; and the M-920 Cain, a portable particle accelerator nicknamed the "nuke gun" that used replaceable graphite rods as neutron moderators. The Cerberus Arc Projector was an anti-synthetic "laser-guided lightning" weapon with a three-stage trigger. The M-490 Blackstorm gravitational singularity projector was credited to weapon designer Eli Wegner, a human who had served with asari commando units and set out to duplicate biotic singularities technologically; after six years of development it passed Systems Alliance AIHW trials and was marketed as the "black hole gun" before Wegner renamed it Blackstorm. Tech Armor trapped a stationary warp effect between two low-yield kinetic barriers, while the alternative "fortification" approach used superconductive devices to create an immobilizing Foucault-current magnetic field at the cost of wearer fatigue. Field modification kits, or "upgrades," let omni-tool-equipped soldiers customize weapons, armor, biotic amps, and grenades using battlefield salvage rendered into omni-gel.
