Chief Weyrloc Guld was the krogan leader of Clan Weyrloc on Tuchanka, determined to cure the genophage at any cost, including experimenting on humans and on willing members of his own clan. In 2185, while aiding Mordin Solus during his loyalty mission, Commander Shepard entered the Weyrloc base to rescue Maelon, Mordin's former student, who was in fact voluntarily carrying out the genophage-cure research. Guld sent a messenger offering to let Shepard leave unharmed so the Commander could spread word that Clan Weyrloc would rise above all clans and dominate the galaxy; instead, Shepard killed the Weyrloc soldiers in the way and then killed Guld himself. The Chief Scout at the main Urdnot camp said Guld's followers regarded him as a leader with a destiny because he had two living children, one of them a girl; having even one surviving child was unlikely under the genophage, and having two was read by some as a sign, though the Chief Scout dismissed it as luck. In battle, Guld wielded an M-300 Claymore heavy shotgun and could use Warp, and like all krogan he had a strong biotic barrier, heavy armor, high health, a charge attack, and rapid regeneration. The battlefield held numerous explosive canisters that could be used against him.
