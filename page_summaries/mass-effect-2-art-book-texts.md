This page transcribes all commentary texts from the Mass Effect 2 Art Book published in 2010; the writers are unattributed. The texts are organized into five sections describing the game's concept art and design intentions.

"Heroes and Villains" covers character design: several characters were born from production paintings, including the Illusive Man, whose impeccable futuristic suit was meant to convey the swagger of a charming billionaire. Grunt was given smoother horns and showroom-new armor to contrast with other krogan; Garrus would bear scars and bandages from a near-fatal injury for most of the story; Miranda was reconceived from blonde to black hair to suit the "femme fatale" look; Jack's tattoos told a visual story of her past; Samara's armor captured a "mystic warrior" archetype; Legion gained moving parts for expression and Shepard's N7 armor became integral to its appearance and backstory; Mordin balanced brilliant scientist with combat tech specialist; Tali's look was made more mature; and Thane had to be a sleek alien assassin and an attractive love interest.

"Alien Life Forms" describes the hybridization of humans and Reaper technology through escalating enemies culminating in the Reaper-human larva, whose designs spanned Reaper and human forms before a partially-formed human shape of Reaper construction was chosen. Scions were crude fusions of Reaper technology and human anatomy; Collectors were inspired by electron microscope photographs of insect heads; and Praetorians fused Collector DNA with multiple human heads. "Alien Worlds" covers the Citadel, Cerberus interiors and Minuteman Station, Illium, Purgatory, Korlus ("junk world"), and Omega, whose shape was inspired by a nuclear mushroom cloud, with Collector interiors drawing on bee hives. "Transport" covers the quarian Migrant Fleet's tadpole-like ships, the Collector ship inspired by Australian termite mounds, the new squad shuttle, the Hammerhead-reimagined Mako, the Gunship, and the Cerberus-built Normandy SR-2, which was meant to feel like a crude knockoff but ended up luxurious with wood inlay and leather seats. "Weapons" covers heavy weapons including the Collector beam weapon and the tactical nuke.
