The M-451 Firestorm was a short-ranged heavy weapon, effectively a flamethrower, appearing in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. It was effective against armor, shields and biotic barriers, and could suppress the health regeneration of enemies such as vorcha and krogan after a sustained second or two of fire. Mechanically it worked much like the Collector Particle Beam, consuming five units of ammunition at a time, but its range was capped at about 10 meters. It dealt no splash damage and hit only one target at a time, could induce panic in unprotected organic enemies (rarely), and never harmed Shepard or the squad regardless of difficulty. Synthetic enemies were more resistant to fire and never panicked, so Geth Destroyers could not be detonated by it.

In Mass Effect 2 the Firestorm was found on the body of a dead mercenary during Zaeed Massani's loyalty mission on Zorya; if Shepard did not collect it, it could be seen on Zaeed's back when boarding the departure shuttle. It offered above-average damage against single armored targets and never needed reloading, useful early in the game, though the Particle Beam was ultimately superior in range and damage. In Mass Effect 3 several Firestorms were found throughout Attican Traverse: Krogan Team but were specific to that mission and could not be carried off-world; the weapon burned slowly for a second before intensifying for much greater damage. The name references Ray Bradbury's novel Fahrenheit 451.
