Husks were synthetic-organic "zombies" created from the bodies of organic beings. Victims, living or dead, were impaled on mechanical spikes that Alliance marines nicknamed "dragon's teeth," which over time converted the body's organs, skin and water into cybernetic materials, turned the blood into a sickly green fluid, and made the body generate an electrical charge. When approached, the spikes released the husks, which charged and unleashed a powerful electrical blast that disabled shields and dealt heavy damage. Fully converted husks retained none of their former personality.

In Mass Effect: Evolution, the Arca Monolith on Shanxi during the First Contact War transformed those who touched it into synthetic thralls; General Desolas Arterius sought to build an army of "meta-turians," while mercenary Jack Harper, only partially exposed, kept his mind but gained altered eyes and the ability to sense the artifact and understand alien languages. Saren Arterius ordered an orbital strike on Temple Palaven, destroying Desolas and the meta-turians. In Mass Effect, husks were encountered from Eden Prime onward wherever humans fought the geth, who used dragon's teeth from Sovereign; Cerberus studied husks for a super-soldier program and exposed a pioneer team on Chasca, and Saren kept husks at his Virmire facility. Sovereign's possession of Saren produced a husk-like creature, suggesting dragon's teeth were Reaper technology; a cache was also found buried on Trebin.

By Mass Effect 2, husks were known to be of Reaper origin. The Collectors deployed husks plus advanced variants: Abominations (explode on contact), Scions (three husks fused, long-range cannons), and Praetorians (thirty husks fused). Dragon's teeth found aboard a derelict Reaper showed the variants had been used at least 37 million years ago, while a Reaper artifact in the Minos Wasteland on Aequitas created husks with an energy orb rather than spikes. Mass Effect: Invasion introduced Cerberus study of husk-like Adjutants at Avernus Station, which escaped and attacked Omega, infecting organics to make more Adjutants. In Mass Effect 3, Reaper victims deemed inadequate for DNA processing were converted into husks; Cerberus's Sanctuary logs on Horizon revealed the transforming nano-machines "ride" on adrenaline molecules and that each husk also serves as a transmitter node for Reaper signals. Reaper husk types included the Banshee (from asari Ardat-Yakshi), Brute (krogan/turian), Cannibal (batarian), Harvester, Marauder (turian), Ravager (rachni) and Swarmer. Activating the Crucible obliterated husks (Destroy), made them flee (Control), or made them docile (Synthesis).
