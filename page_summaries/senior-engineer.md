The Senior Engineer was a special Blue Suns mercenary stationed in the engine room of the MSV Strontium Mule in Mass Effect 2. He was unique among the Blue Suns mercenaries encountered by Commander Shepard in being the only one able to use tech attacks. He was killed during the firefight when Shepard's squad reached the ship's engine room.

He was armed with an M-300 Claymore heavy shotgun loaded with Incendiary Ammo, making him capable of massive close-quarters damage, and he could also deploy a Combat Drone. He was heavily armored and shielded and used Tech Armor to regenerate his shields. Recommended tactics included using Overload to strip his shields and then Kasumi's Shadow Strike to knock him down before finishing him with gunfire, while keeping distance to render his shotgun ineffective.
