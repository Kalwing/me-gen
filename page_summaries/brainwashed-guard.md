Brainwashed Guards were enemies encountered on the planet 2175 Aeia during Jacob Taylor's loyalty mission in Mass Effect 2. They were male, shipwrecked former crew members of the MSV Hugo Gernsback who had suffered neural decay after eating the planet's local vegetation. Ronald Taylor, Jacob's father and the ship's commanding officer, had somehow conditioned four of these men to serve as his bodyguards. Each of the four Brainwashed Guards used different armaments and powers. One was a tech user armed with an M-6 Carnifex who could deploy Combat Drones and use Incinerate, and who appeared to wear Predator Armor or Scorpion Armor carried over from the first Mass Effect. The others fought as soldiers, carrying the M-8 Avenger, M-23 Katana, and M-9 Tempest. All of the guards had similar shielding. In the final confrontation before Commander Shepard's squad reached Ronald Taylor, the guards were reinforced by a YMIR Mech. Though few in number, they laid down suppressing fire while the mech advanced on the player's position.
