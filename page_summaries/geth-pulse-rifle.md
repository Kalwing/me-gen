The Geth Pulse Rifle was a geth assault rifle appearing in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, distinct from the original game's Geth Armory pulse rifles, which it was visually identical to. It fired a rapid stream of lightweight phasic slugs, making it very effective against shields and biotic barriers but only moderately effective against armor. Geth pulse rifles were rarely recovered intact and proved unreliable and hard to repair over time; attempts to replicate the weapon failed, though technology recovered from Haestrom could enable a few to be built. Two features distinguished it: its mass effect field generation followed a sine curve, giving a variable rate of fire that alternated between fast and slow, and its slugs were lighter than standard Avenger rounds but encased in a phasic envelope.

In Mass Effect 2 it could only be acquired on Hardcore or Insanity difficulty during Tali's recruitment mission at Haestrom, found in Tali's room after the Geth Colossus was destroyed. It could be used by Garrus, Grunt, Legion, Samara or Morinth, and Zaeed. It was very accurate and excellent for suppressive fire, especially with Incendiary Ammo, but suffered from low damage per shot and weak armor performance, and refilled ammo efficiently from thermal clips.

In Mass Effect 3 it was found during Priority: Geth Dreadnought or bought from Elkoss Combine afterward, and was a rare multiplayer weapon requiring no expansion. It weighed the same as the M-8 Avenger but dealt less damage per shot, compensated by high fire rate, large clip capacity (up to 180 rounds with a Level V magazine upgrade), near-zero recoil and high accuracy, though it had a slow reload. The Assault Rifle Extended Barrel and, in multiplayer, Armor-Piercing Rounds were recommended, and geth multiplayer characters could boost its damage with the Networked AI passive. The Legendary Edition made it considerably heavier, matching the M-96 Mattock.
