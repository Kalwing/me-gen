Trebin was the fourth planet of the Antaeus System in the Hades Gamma cluster of the Milky Way, a modest terrestrial world with a nitrogen-argon atmosphere and a surface mostly of nickel with silver deposits. Its environment was relatively mild, but a lack of water had prevented any biosphere from forming. ExoGeni Corp recently carried out a test impact of a single water-ice comet into the surface, the first step of a long-term plan to thicken the atmosphere and introduce water. A survey team monitored the geological and meteorological effects; most released water was still atmospheric vapor, but thick cloud banks had formed and the arid world was expected to see its first rain soon.

In the first Mass Effect, Shepard's team could visit Trebin for the assignment UNC: Missing Survey Team. The survey team's progress had been hampered by repeated failures of their GPS satellites, caused by a transmitter tower beaming tightbeam signals into geosynchronous orbit and crashing the satellites nearby. Points of interest included the survey camp, a crashed probe guarded by Scavengers, turian wreckage bearing a Nimines Colony insignia flag, a Thresher Maw, and a mine entrance where survivors were found; husks were also present. A spot on the planet was one of several locations where the "song" associated with the rachni could be heard. In Mass Effect: Andromeda, Liam Kosta may reference this project, noting that "a corporation hit a desert world with an ice comet" to bring rain.
