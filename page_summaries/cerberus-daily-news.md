This was the index and overview page for the Cerberus Daily News, an in-universe news feed distributed through the Cerberus Network whose reports were set in 2185 and 2186 CE; it linked to the monthly archive pages running from January 2010 through March 2012 and noted that daily publishing stopped in March 2012, after which the Alliance News Network carried the feed for the remainder of that month.
