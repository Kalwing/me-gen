This page is a walkthrough of Mass Effect 3's mission structure, dividing plot progression into a Prologue and three Acts, each Priority mission gating the next. The Prologue covers Prologue: Earth (escaping the Reaper invasion aboard the Normandy SR-2), Priority: Mars (saving Liara T'Soni and retrieving Crucible data), and Priority: The Citadel I (rallying the galaxy's races). Act I runs Priority: Palaven (rescuing the turian primarch), Priority: Sur'Kesh (a war summit of turian, salarian, and krogan leaders, then protecting a krogan female from Cerberus), and Priority: Tuchanka (escorting the krogan female and a salarian scientist to the Shroud to disperse a genophage cure). Act II covers Priority: The Citadel II (stopping a Cerberus coup against the Council), Perseus Veil, Geth Dreadnought, Rannoch (deactivating the Reaper signal upgrading the geth), The Citadel III, Thessia (retrieving Catalyst information as the Reapers devastate the planet), and Horizon. Act III is Priority: Cerberus Headquarters (recapturing a Prothean VI that can identify the Catalyst) and Priority: Earth (the final battle and Crucible deployment).

The page then lists side missions grouped by the Priority mission that unlocks them, flagging "progress-sensitive" missions that expire when a later plot mission is completed and "timed" missions marked failed after a set number of missions (for example Tuchanka: Bomb, within three missions). It warns that no side missions can be done once Priority: Cerberus Headquarters begins. It notes DLC missions folded into progression (Priority: Eden Prime from From Ashes, Citadel: Aria T'Loak from Omega, Citadel: Dr. Bryson from Leviathan, Citadel: Shore Leave from Citadel) and Mass Effect 2 squadmate cameos in specific side missions: Kasumi Goto in Citadel: Hanar Diplomat, Jack in Grissom Academy: Emergency Evacuation, Grunt in Attican Traverse: Krogan Team, Jacob Taylor in Arrae: Ex-Cerberus Scientists, Zaeed Massani in Citadel: Volus Ambassador, and Samara in Kallini: Ardat-Yakshi Monastery. Attican Traverse: Krogan Team and Tuchanka: Turian Platoon each unlock Priority: Tuchanka, and Rannoch: Admiral Koris and Rannoch: Geth Fighter Squadrons unlock Priority: Rannoch. It notes Legendary Edition includes all former DLC while keeping prerequisites largely intact.
