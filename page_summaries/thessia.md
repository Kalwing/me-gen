Thessia was the asari homeworld, the second planet of the Parnitha System in the Athena Nebula, and the core of the largest economy in the Milky Way. It has been called the "crown jewel of the galaxy," the "apex of democracy," and the "beating heart of galactic love." Its republics had a remarkably low incidence of war, disease, violent crime and famine, supported by wealthy colonies and Thessia's vast element zero reserves, which were large enough to affect the price of eezo galaxy-wide. Traces of element zero in the water and soil were so common that most native life adapted to it, making the world home to many biotically active and eezo-resistant species. Travel to Thessia was strictly controlled, though smuggling persisted.

Rather than a single government, Thessia was host to varying republics, each with a formidable military, and had long been free of internal or external wars. The asari were renowned for cultural and political dominance and excelled at diplomacy, holding a strong presence in Citadel politics. This unfocused political structure left the Thessians unprepared for the Reaper invasion of 2186, which eliminated the communications infrastructure underpinning asari e-democracy and left millions without power, water or food, a deliberate Reaper strategy since husks alone struggled to subdue a population full of biotics. The planet became the setting of the mission Priority: Thessia.

Cultural details include a distinctive fashion sense with non-asari patrons such as Edan Had'dah; food and drink served with small amounts of element zero, prompting a separate visitors' menu, though the drink elasa could be consumed by asari and non-asari alike; and native plants such as the common irssal, a honey-smelling blossom whose seeds were carried by colonists toward the Andromeda galaxy. Thessia lay on the Illium–Thessia shipping lane. The name is adapted from Thessaly, whose "witches" Roman writers said could draw down the moon.
