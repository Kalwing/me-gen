This page catalogued all downloadable content released for Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, Mass Effect 3, Mass Effect: Infiltrator, and Mass Effect: Andromeda, noting that most DLC for the original trilogy games was included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. For the first Mass Effect, the two story add-ons were Bring Down the Sky (March 2008, three assignments) and Pinnacle Station (August 2009, three assignments), the latter not included in Legendary Edition.

Mass Effect 2 DLC was delivered largely through the free Cerberus Network service and included Normandy Crash Site, the Zaeed - The Price of Revenge and Kasumi - Stolen Memory squadmate packs, Firewalker Pack, Overlord, Lair of the Shadow Broker, Arrival, the Mass Effect: Genesis interactive comic, two Alternate Appearance Packs, and numerous weapon and armor packs (Cerberus Weapon and Armor, Arc Projector, Equalizer, Aegis, Firepower). Promotional items included Blood Dragon Armor (from registering Dragon Age: Origins), Inferno Armor, Terminus Weapon and Armor, Collectors' Weapon and Armor, several Dr Pepper armor parts, and the M-29 Incisor.

Mass Effect 3 single-player DLC included From Ashes (adding the prothean squadmate), the free Extended Cut ending cinematics, Leviathan, Omega, and Citadel, plus Mass Effect: Genesis 2 and weapon packs (Firefight, Groundside Resistance). Multiplayer DLC was all free and included Resurgence, Rebellion, Earth, Retaliation, and Reckoning, alongside purchasable and earnable card packs (Spectre, Veteran, Premium Spectre). Promotional weapons included the AT-12 Raider, Chakram Launcher, M-55 Argus, M-90 Indra, and the Reckoner Knight Armor from the Kingdoms of Amalur: Reckoning demo. Mass Effect: Infiltrator received the free Patch 1.03. Mass Effect: Andromeda's DLC listed here was limited to pre-order and Deluxe/Super Deluxe edition bonuses and free promotional items. Cut content notes two cancelled first-game DLCs, Cerberus Brainchild and Mars.
