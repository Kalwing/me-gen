Brief location/navigation page. The Crescent Nebula was a medium-sized star cluster at the edge of asari space, a Terminus Systems territory near the borders of a thin section of the Attican Traverse. It contained four systems (Lusarn, Ondeste, Tasale, and Zelene) and included the asari-settled world Illium, in the Tasale system, along with a mass relay. It connected via mass relay to clusters including the Eagle Nebula, Hourglass Nebula, Ismar Frontier, Minos Wasteland, Omega Nebula, and The Shrike Abyssal. The cluster hosted numerous Mass Effect 2 missions and assignments, among them Dossier: The Assassin, Dossier: The Justicar, Lair of the Shadow Broker, Miranda: The Prodigal, and the Illium Conrad Verner and Gianna Parasini assignments. It was originally named the "Illium Economic Zone" during development.
