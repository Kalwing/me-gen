Ardat-Yakshi, meaning "Demon of the Night Winds" in an old asari dialect, was a rare congenital genetic condition affecting the nervous systems of asari. The condition did not harm the afflicted asari herself, but during mating her nervous system completely overpowered that of her partner, causing brain hemorrhaging and, in extreme cases, death. The Ardat-Yakshi grew smarter, stronger, and deadlier after each encounter, and those who fed on victims developed an innate ability to influence and dominate minds, a talent that strengthened with repeated mating. Affected asari felt a compulsion to mate that grew into a full addiction, and often exhibited a lack of empathy reaching psychopathic levels, attributed to their inability to properly meld. Lethal-form Ardat-Yakshi who kept seeking mates could cause death tolls reaching tens of thousands over a lifetime.

The condition was inherited but could not be fully diagnosed until adolescence at the earliest. Identified Ardat-Yakshi were given an ultimatum: live in isolation in a monastery or face execution. Beyond protecting society, the asari enforced this policy because they viewed Ardat-Yakshi as an embarrassment to be hidden from non-asari. Ardat-Yakshi were born only of unions between two asari, contributing to prejudice against purebloods, and were themselves sterile. Monastery residents often produced goods such as asari honey marinade and asari honey mead for the galactic market, and some were granted supervised visits to Thessia.

In Mass Effect 2, Falere, Rila, and Morinth, daughters of the justicar Samara, all had the active form. Morinth embraced her condition while Falere and Rila chose seclusion. Samara stated they were the only three living Ardat-Yakshi, likely meaning the most dangerous form; roughly one percent of asari fell on a latent spectrum where mating was not always lethal. Morinth's centuries of use let her match Samara's biotic power, and she claimed the Ardat-Yakshi were the "genetic destiny" of the asari. If Shepard mated with Morinth, the Commander died almost immediately.

In Mass Effect 3, an Ardat-Yakshi monastery on Lesuss was attacked by Reapers seeking to convert the afflicted into Banshees. Asari High Command's commandos failed to report back, so Shepard investigated. Samara traveled there out of maternal concern and justicar duty; unable to execute her last living child Falere, she attempted suicide. If stopped by Shepard, she accepted Falere's plan to live in isolation at the ruined monastery. If Samara was absent, Falere survived alone or was shot by the Commander as a security risk. In Mass Effect Andromeda: Annihilation, the condition was said to have a viral component and contributed to the Fortinbras Plague.
