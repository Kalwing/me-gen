Krogan Battlemasters (also written Battle Master) were the elite officers of the krogan military: tough, disciplined, well-armed and biotically gifted, with a single Battlemaster reputedly a match for ten soldiers of any other species. They treated killing as a science, favoring economy of motion to deliver a single overwhelming blow, and held no regard for combat honor, accepting hostage-taking and genocide if these minimized krogan casualties. They were rare because of the scarcity of krogan biotics.

In Mass Effect: Revelation, the Battlemaster Skarr was hired by the batarian Edan Had'dah to kill Kahlee Sanders. Skarr tracked her to Elysium and the home of retired Admiral Jon Grissom, but fled when Grissom, David Anderson and the Spectre Saren Arterius fought him off; Saren refused to pursue, saying only an idiot fights a krogan biotic one-on-one. Skarr later shot down a transport and hurled an APC with biotics to capture Sanders, and was finally killed in a gunfight when Anderson infiltrated an element zero refinery to rescue her.

In Mass Effect, Shepard could recruit Urdnot Wrex, one of the last Battlemasters, whom the Shadow Broker had hired to kill Fist. Shepard's squad fought another Battlemaster sent by Saren to retrieve Liara T'Soni on Therum, and Saren fielded Battlemasters at Virmire and during the assault on the Citadel. In Mass Effect 2, Chief Weyrloc Guld was a Battlemaster trying to build a new krogan empire by curing the genophage; Gatatog Uvenk opposed Grunt over his tank-birth, and Grunt afterward named Shepard his battlemaster; Blood Pack Battlemasters appeared across the galaxy. In Mass Effect 3, Krogan Battlemaster Vanguards were mobilized for the war against the Reapers in 2186. Known Battlemasters included Urdnot Wrex, Skarr, Gatatog Uvenk, Garm, Kureck, Chief Weyrloc Guld and Salamul.
