Archuk was the leader of a squad of Blood Pack mercenaries who attacked the human mining colony of Fehl Prime in 2183. He was ruthless, a highly skilled warrior, and relished battlefield challenges.

Archuk's siege of the colony was brutal and efficient. Knowing the Blood Pack held superior firepower, he used it to pound the colony's kinetic barriers while standing fearlessly atop a promontory in full view of the colonists, mocking their attempts to defend themselves. When his second-in-command, Brood, destroyed the kinetic barriers with a powerful bomb, Archuk ordered his forces to charge the colonists' positions.

The arrival of the Alliance distracted Archuk from his objective, and his ground cars were destroyed by Kodiak drop shuttles. Furious, he fired missiles at the shuttles, downing two and forcing the third into a damaged crash landing, then ordered his vorcha to begin search-and-destroy operations. During the Alliance counter-assault, James Vega distracted Archuk by openly challenging him to a duel. Archuk accepted without hesitation and gained the upper hand, leaving Vega at his mercy. As Archuk prepared to kill him, his head was blown off by Milque's sniper rifle.
