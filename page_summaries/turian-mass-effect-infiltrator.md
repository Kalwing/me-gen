An unnamed turian was a recurring adversary in Mass Effect: Infiltrator. He first appeared as the main enemy of Randall Ezno, a top Cerberus operative, during a capture mission on an ice planet. After Ezno beat him in a firefight, taunting him that he was "not Archangel," the turian was captured and taken to a secret Cerberus facility known as the Barn, where he became a test subject for Cerberus scientists. As an enemy he had considerable health and heavy regenerating shields, favored cover, and took bonus damage from headshots; Pull could flush him out.

Later, after Randall Ezno and his volus ally released all the test subjects in protest at the brutal treatment overseen by Ezno's handler Inali Renata, the facility fell into chaos and the turian's restraints released. Feeling his strength return, he grabbed a weapon and set out to escape, becoming a playable character. He fought through Cerberus troops and other escaped subjects, an Ogre Mech and an endless supply of Orcus Mechs, wielding a fixed, non-upgradeable version of the M-37 Falcon (assault rifle and shotgun modes) and abilities including Charge, Push, Slide, Sprint and Tactical Cloak. He eventually reached a large empty room where, through a window, the facility's Director laughed at him as a turret deployed with no cover available, and the screen faded to black, leaving his fate unresolved.
