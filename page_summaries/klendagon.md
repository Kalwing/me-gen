Klendagon was the second planet of the Century System in the Hawking Eta cluster, an arid terrestrial world slightly larger than Earth but of lower density, reflecting a relative lack of heavy elements. Its crust was composed of tin and aluminum, with wide deserts of dust-fine sand. Its most striking feature was the Great Rift valley across the southern hemisphere, which did not appear natural: the geological record suggested it resulted from a "glancing blow" by a mass accelerator round of unimaginable power roughly thirty-seven million years ago.

In Mass Effect 2, accessible after the Collector Ship mission or Project Firewalker: Rosalie Lost, it was a rich mining world (palladium, platinum, iridium). The Illusive Man revealed that an Alliance Survey Team determined the Great Rift Valley was caused by a mass accelerator strike whose original target had been the Derelict Reaper, hit and disabled 37 million years ago. No trace remained of the race that fired the round, but both the weapon and the target had since been located, the weapon being defunct. Klendagon's in-game artwork was a real depiction of Mars, with the Rift standing in for Valles Marineris.
