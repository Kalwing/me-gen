News Stories collected the in-game news broadcasts heard across the original Mass Effect trilogy, distinct from the out-of-game Cerberus Daily News and Alliance News Network feeds. These reports were often obfuscated and sanitized for the public so as not to reveal the classified nature of events, and their content changed based on Commander Shepard's choices.

In Mass Effect, news played during Citadel elevator rides. Pre-mission items could point Shepard toward assignments, such as an ExoGeni survey team gone missing in the Hades Gamma cluster or Chairman Burns taken hostage by biotics. Post-mission items reported sanitized aftermaths: the Feros story at Zhu's Hope varied with colonist survival, the Noveria story covered a research colony's destruction and Bel Anoleis's possible arrest, and being rude to Conrad Verner produced a story of his death in a fight with turians. Deceased Eden Prime serviceman Nirali Bhatia was honored with an Alliance "profile in courage," and Admiral Kahoku was reported to have died of "natural causes."

In Mass Effect 2, stories were delivered through the Citadel NewsNet with Emily Wong, Galactic News, Illium News and a darker-toned Omega News. Many carried over consequences from the first game: the Destiny Ascension's survival or destruction, whether the Rachni Queen lived, the fates of Kaidan or Ashley on Virmire, the Terra Nova hostages on Asteroid X57, and the Cerberus investigation tied to Dr. Wayne. Others reported on Shepard's recruitment missions, sightings of Shepard alive after Freedom's Progress and Horizon, and numerous Illium advertisements including Blasto: The Jellyfish Stings and the Fishdog Food Shack.

In Mass Effect 3, Alliance News Network anchor Azad Carter reported cycles of seven stories on Citadel vidscreens, updated as missions completed. These tracked the Reaper invasion's progress: the destruction of the Batarian Hegemony, the fall of Earth, Palaven and Arcturus Station, the raloi delegation's withdrawal, the mercenary gangs uniting, the genophage cure and krogan-turian alliance, the outcome at Rannoch, the attack on Thessia, and the failed Cerberus coup at the Citadel.
