This hand-corrected manual lore document recounted how the Systems Alliance formed after the discovery of the Prothean ruins on Mars. The ruins, together with the newly usable element known as eezo, lifted humanity to mastery of its own solar system and drove rapid change across technology, infrastructure, art and especially religion. New beliefs took hold almost overnight: groups such as the interventionary evolutionists proclaimed the ruins as proof that all human history had been directed by alien forces, while established faiths, particularly the major Abrahamic religions, struggled to fit extraterrestrial life into their frameworks, with some absorbing the knowledge, some staying silent and a stubborn few denying the Mars evidence as conspiracy. The media fixated on the questions of who the aliens were, whether they were friendly and whether they were still out there.

While the discovery fractured most religions, it unified politics into an "us versus them" stance. In early 2149, political leaders across the system set aside their differences, and along with representatives from Mars and Luna, Earth's eighteen largest nations drafted and ratified the Systems Alliance Charter to establish a representative body to expand and defend human territory in space. The founding nations were the Argentine Republic, the Chinese People's Federation, the Commonwealth of Australia, the Federal Republic of Germany, the Federal Republic of Nigeria, the Federative Republic of Brazil, the French Republic, the Indian Union, Japan, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, the Republic of Korea, the Republic of Turkey, the Republic of Singapore, the Republic of South Africa, the Russian Federation, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom of Great Britain, and the United North American States; the Republic of Ireland joined a year later. These nations pooled their scientific and technological might to form the Systems Alliance military.

Relativity threatened to make expansion slow and disconnected, since primitive FTL drives caused time dilation and even advanced drives could not run indefinitely because of an eezo fuel side effect. The answer came from the translated Mars data cache, which in 2148 revealed that Charon, the largest moon of the planetoid Pluto, was actually a colossal piece of Prothean technology encased in ice, dormant for 50,000 years. A human science team near Pluto confirmed the finding and, under orders from Earth, began excavation to reactivate it. Activation proved surprisingly simple, but exploratory probes sent through the Charon Relay instantly lost contact, so people had to be sent. Because many high-ranking Alliance officers came from Earth's militaries, the decorated Rear Admiral John Grissom was chosen to lead. Around mid-2149, Grissom and several volunteers boarded an experimental jump ship and became the first humans to transit the Charon Relay, arriving instantly at a second mass relay in the Arcturus system, 36 light-years away, where they learned the relays formed a galaxy-spanning network. On their return, Grissom and his crew were hailed as heroes, and the sullen, retiring Grissom became the face of the Systems Alliance as humanity entered a new age of expansion.
