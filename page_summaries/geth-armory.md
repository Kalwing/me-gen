The Geth Armory was the manufacturer of geth weapons and armor, all of the highest quality. Few details of geth manufacture were known. In Mass Effect it produced the Rage, Berserker, and Battlemaster heavy krogan armors, which shared identical stats and differed only in highlight color (red, green, and light blue); built with geth materials, they offered unparalleled tech and biotic protection. A license to sell Geth Armory products was available from Morlan, a salarian merchant in the Lower Markets of the Citadel Wards.

Geth Armory weapons were extremely accurate thanks to adaptive fire control and outperformed organic designs. The Geth Pulse Rifle, the standard geth infantry weapon, fired a brief cyan energy beam ending in an electrical discharge and was particularly effective at stripping shields; it was the only geth weapon obtainable normally in the first game, with geth shotguns and sniper rifles accessible only via console commands. Pulse technology also extended to geth recon drones and was possibly related to the Siege Pulse cannon on Armatures and Colossi. In Mass Effect 2 the Armory produced the Geth Pulse Rifle (a secret weapon on Haestrom at Hardcore difficulty or above) and the Geth Plasma Shotgun. In Mass Effect 3 it produced five weapons, including the Javelin (a ferrofluid weapon called the Javelin by Alliance marines), the Geth Spitfire, and the Geth Plasma SMG. Higher-ranking krogan on Tuchanka and Kureck wore the Rage series, and Gatatog Uvenk wore a modified Battlemaster variant. Both the Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3 descriptions of the M-98 Widow sniper rifle called it geth in origin, though only Legion was seen using it and its true origins were unknown.
