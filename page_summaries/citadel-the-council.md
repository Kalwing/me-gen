Citadel: The Council was a Mass Effect 2 mission received automatically on completing Freedom's Progress and taking command of the new Normandy. Shepard's former commanding officer, David Anderson, left a message asking Shepard to come to the Citadel and speak for himself about the rumors of his return. The message's wording varied by whether Shepard had saved the Citadel Council in Mass Effect: if saved, Anderson noted Shepard had put "us" or "me" on the Council; if not, that Shepard had put humanity "on top."

Traveling to the Presidium by Rapid Transit, Shepard was met at the human embassy by Anderson. If the Council had been saved in Mass Effect, a Council meeting was underway; if not, the new Council refused to meet Shepard. The Council was skeptical of Shepard's mission to stop the Collectors from destroying human colonies and did not believe the Reaper threat was real. If the original Council had been saved, or if it had died and Anderson had become a Councilor, Shepard could choose to be reinstated as a Spectre; there was no such choice if Udina had become Councilor. In all cases, Udina arrived late and was upset that Anderson had arranged matters without telling him first. A photo of Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko, depending on who died on Virmire, was meant to appear on the Councilor's desk.
