Jath'Amon was a batarian ambassador who presented himself as meek, well-meaning, and an advocate of peace and diplomacy. Sometime before the geth attack on the Citadel in 2183, his arrangement of a meeting with the Citadel Council to negotiate peace terms between the batarians and the Alliance sparked rising galactic tensions. Cerberus later discovered that a batarian extremist group, which equated "peace" with "surrender," had marked the ambassador for assassination. Miranda Lawson was tasked with uncovering the truth; with Major Derek Izunami's help she recruited former Alliance marine Jacob Taylor to investigate, and during the inquiry Jacob met Illo Nazario, who revealed the extremists' real plan.

In truth, Jath'Amon was himself the leader of the batarian extremists. His goal all along was to assassinate the Council with an engineered virus released during the meeting, against which he would be vaccinated. When Jacob and Miranda learned the truth from Nazario, they rushed to the Council Chambers during the ambassador's speech and presented their evidence. Exposed, Jath'Amon dropped his peaceful demeanor and sprayed the virus into the Chamber from a concealed pump in his hover chair. Jacob defeated Jath'Amon's batarian troops and combat drones before detaining him. Upon defeat, Jath'Amon claimed he and Jacob were alike, both going to great lengths to protect their people, and was last seen swearing revenge on Jacob and the Citadel races as C-Sec led him away. In gameplay he was the final boss of Mass Effect Galaxy, protected by two turrets and armed with a missile launcher, with regenerating shields.
