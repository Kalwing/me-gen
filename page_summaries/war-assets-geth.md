This war-asset list collected the forces contributed by the geth after they allied with organics against the Reapers. All geth assets required completing Priority: Rannoch by choosing the geth's side; if Admiral Han'Gerrel was also convinced to cease fire, the quarian assets could be kept as well.

The Geth Corps (300) represented geth infantry: every platform was armed, shielded and combat-ready, sharing enemy tactics and positions instantaneously and using expendable turrets and drones to minimize losses of networked platforms, making it the largest and best-equipped infantry in the galaxy. A Paragon interview with Diana Allers after Rannoch added 5 as the Council sent rare materials to upgrade geth ground units.

The Geth Fleet (300) noted that every geth vessel, whatever its purpose, could fight, and that being unbound by the Treaty of Farixen the geth had built almost as many dreadnoughts as the turians, with the geth themselves serving as the ships' software and cyber-warfare suites. If the geth heretics were rewritten during Legion: A House Divided and peace was achieved (or the geth won), the fleet gained 150 from the added processing power; if the heretics were destroyed instead, it lost 150. A Renegade Allers interview added 5 as the Alliance sent Thanix cannons.

The Geth Prime Platoon (60) required Rannoch: Geth Fighter Squadrons plus siding with the geth or forcing a compromise. Geth primes housed more programs than other platforms and acted as mobile networking hubs, boosting and coordinating nearby geth; because destroying a prime lowered other geth's processing power, prime platforms were heavily shielded and armed.
