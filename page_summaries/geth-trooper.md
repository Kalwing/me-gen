Geth Troopers were the standard ground infantry of the geth throughout the Mass Effect trilogy, usually attacking in groups and supported by stronger geth units. In the first Mass Effect they were lightly equipped with no exotic weapons, colored dark grey with pale green synthetic "flesh" and a bright blue flashlight eye. They lacked heavy armor, making them weak to weapons fire, biotics, and grenades. Their shields recovered after several seconds or via shield boost, and at low health they deployed a geth hex shield, a roughly three-meter blue hexagon that fully obscured the unit and absorbed all damage until destroyed, during which the Trooper abandoned cover. Sabotage, Overload, Armor Piercing Rounds, and lifting biotics such as Lift and Singularity were effective; AI Hacking could turn one to briefly fight for Shepard.

In Mass Effect 2 they were the most common geth enemy, recolored white rather than steel grey and resembling the earlier Geth Shock Troopers. They carried the standard Geth Pulse Rifle and used Geth Shield Boost to refill depleted shields, effectively giving them a second shield bar. Their headshot zone sat slightly above the flashlight. They came in large numbers, often escorted by Geth Hunters. Disruptor Ammo, Overload, Energy Drain, and shield-penetration upgrades countered them best, and disabling or killing them before they activated Shield Boost was advised.

In Mass Effect 3 they returned steel grey with green skin and white armor markings, armed with a Geth Pulse Rifle and a melee attack. They had no shields or armor but could dodge attacks by jumping sideways, took forty percent additional headshot damage, and could be staggered. Most sniper rifles killed them with a single headshot. They swarmed reckless players and acted as distractions while stronger geth such as cloaked Geth Hunters and Geth Pyros closed in. Area-of-effect weapons, Overload, Energy Drain, grenades (especially Cluster Grenades), and Proximity Mines cleared clusters efficiently; staggered Troopers were far less likely to dodge.

In Mass Effect: Infiltrator, Geth Troopers were basic infantry armed with M-96 Mattock Auto assault rifles. Randall Ezno fought them during his first mission and again aboard the Barn space station after the geth invaded in search of a target Ezno had already secured. They were fragile with low health and weak regenerating shields, tended to stay in cover unless fired upon, and were vulnerable to headshots and to Pull, which flushed them into the open.
