Powers were the abilities used in combat in Mass Effect 2 by the player, squad members, and enemies, replacing the "talents" of the original Mass Effect. They were divided into four categories: biotic, combat, tech, and the newly introduced ammo powers that modified weapon damage. Category determined which upgrades applied. Many damage powers could substitute for weapons even on higher difficulties and dealt bonus damage against particular protection types, with biotic powers generally stronger against biotic barriers and tech powers stronger against shields. Most powers auto-locked onto enemies and either hit instantly or fired seeking projectiles, but enemy protections such as shields had to be stripped before crowd-control effects like levitation would apply.

Each power except Unity had four ranks, unlocked with squad points earned on level-up; unlocking all four cost ten points, and rank 4 forced a choice between two final evolutions. Commander Shepard could earn up to 51 squad points and, after completing Horizon, could reassign them using the Retrain Powers research project. Each class began with Unity (which used medi-gel to revive squadmates), five active powers including one class-unique power, and a passive class power (Biotic Mastery, Tech Mastery, Operative, Defender, Combat Mastery, or Assault Mastery) that boosted health and Paragon/Renegade gains.

Each class could also learn one bonus power via the Advanced Training research project after earning a squadmate's loyalty achievement; available bonus powers included Flashbang Grenade (Kasumi), Fortification (Grunt), Inferno Grenade (Zaeed), Armor-Piercing Ammo (Garrus), Shredder Ammo (Thane), Warp Ammo (Jack), Energy Drain (Tali), Geth Shield Boost (Legion), Neural Shock (Mordin), Barrier (Jacob), Dominate (Morinth), Reave (Samara), Slam (Miranda), and Stasis (Liara). Squadmates had three active powers (a starting power, a second power, and a loyalty power) plus a unique passive class power, and could earn up to 30 squad points (31 for Miranda and Jacob, 29 for Liara). The Lair of the Shadow Broker DLC added the ability to reassign squadmate points. Bio-Amp and Omni-Tool upgrades further improved biotic and tech cooldowns, damage, and duration, with squadmate-specific boosts for Jack and Mordin.
