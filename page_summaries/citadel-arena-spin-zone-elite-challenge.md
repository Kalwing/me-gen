Citadel Arena: Spin Zone Elite Challenge was a side mission in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, available after Citadel Docks: Retake the Normandy, once the Armax Arsenal Arena opened with the rest of Silversun Strip. After one arena match, Daksa Teller, Earth-Clan Talent Liaison for Elkoss Combine's Citadel Sports Division, emailed the Combat Sim Controls offering corporate sponsorship. Teller congratulated Shepard on recent arena victories, noted Shepard had not yet settled on a corporate sponsor, and offered a one-time cash stipend for top gear, asking Shepard to make an Elkoss "debut" by selecting the Spin Zone layout against Elite enemies and winning.

To complete the challenge, Shepard selected "Spin Zone" as the map and "Elite" enemies at the Combat Simulator Control terminal; Spin Zone was a bronze-level prize and Elite enemies a silver-level prize, both purchased with prior match winnings. Other settings were free, allowing other non-conflicting arena challenges such as the Thermal Clip Challenge or Medi-Gel Challenge to be completed in the same match. Afterward Teller sent a second email praising Shepard as "the paradigm," promising to file the paperwork and arrange a future meeting. Receiving and reading the second email granted 5,000 credits and +2 Reputation.
