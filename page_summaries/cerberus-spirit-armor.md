The Cerberus Spirit Armor was a full-body armor set in Mass Effect 3, added by the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC and obtained by scoring over 9,999 points in the Armax Arsenal Arena. In fiction it was worn by Cerberus Phantoms who were biotic or skilled with highly technical attacks; it gave small gains to shield strength and regeneration but chiefly amplified biotic amps and omni-tools so those attacks hit harder and more often, letting a Phantom dismantle heavily armored targets. Reaching the score triggered a congratulatory in-game e-mail, "Thanks for breaking the scoreboard," from Armax Arsenal Arena principal systems administrator Virginia Ruojian, who offered Commander Shepard a Cerberus Phantom's armor captured during the attack on the Citadel. The armor then appeared beside the arena's bronze, silver and gold token machines, alongside the Cerberus Shade Armor and Cerberus Nightmare Armor; only one of the three could be chosen, and the choice carried across later playthroughs with the same character. Spirit increased power recharge speed, power damage and shields, making it arguably the best powers-focused set for Adepts, Engineers, Sentinels and Vanguards when first available and rendering the Inferno Armor obsolete, though its recharge bonus mattered less for characters already running light loadouts because of diminishing returns. Together with the Cerberus Ajax, Nightmare and Shade sets its bonuses contributed to an 80% cumulative total, above the 60% of most full-body armors. Setting combat difficulty to Narrative was the fastest way to reach the required score. The male version was identical to Kai Leng's outfit without a helmet and was reused for the multiplayer N7 Slayer Vanguard, while the female version matched the N7 Shadow Infiltrator; the set was visually identical to the Shade and Nightmare armors, with the Legendary Edition giving all three dark face shields instead of gold.
