Short lore page. Cord-Hislop Aerospace was a well-respected starship manufacturer based on Elysium with corporate headquarters on Earth. In the novel Mass Effect: Ascension it was revealed to be a front used by Cerberus to fund its activities; few employees knew who they truly worked for, and the Illusive Man had dozens of agents at every level of the company. Paul Grayson was assigned to Cord-Hislop's sales department around the time he adopted Gillian Grayson and rose to the executive ranks as cover for his Cerberus work. In Mass Effect: Retribution, after Grayson had been transformed into a Reaper-controlled puppet, he visited Grissom Academy to learn about the Ascension Project, telling the staff (who knew him only as a Cord-Hislop executive) that the company was considering a merger with a turian competitor to explain why he traveled aboard a turian ship. The company is likely named after Eva Coré and Ben Hislop, friends of the Illusive Man who died after the First Contact War.
