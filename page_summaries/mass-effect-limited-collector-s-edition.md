The Mass Effect Limited Collector's Edition was a special release of the first game offered through participating retailers. It included a fiction book titled "Galactic Codex: Essentials Edition 2183" (a 36-page guide to the Mass Effect universe with a Citadel timeline and a guide to non-human races, biotics, and key Perseus arm locations), an art book titled "A Future Imagined" (36 pages of BioWare concept art), and a bonus DVD. The DVD contained the documentaries "The Vision of Mass Effect," "The Making of Mass Effect," "Interactive Storytelling," and "Inside BioWare," plus gamer pics, dashboard themes, ten soundtrack songs, five historical trailers, promotional demos and videos for other games, and commented design galleries of some 600 concept images. A separate Polish PC Collector's Edition, sold exclusively in Poland, included English and Polish copies of the game, a bonus DVD, an N7 t-shirt, an art book, a Polish game guide, a Normandy mouse pad, three postcards, a 2 GB USB flash drive, and a branded lanyard. In the US and Canada the edition was sold only through online retailers such as EB Games, Best Buy, Amazon, and Circuit City; in Europe it was available in stores.
