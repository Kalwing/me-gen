Short Mass Effect 3 mission from the Leviathan DLC. Dr. Alex Garneau was identified as the best source of information about Leviathan, and Shepard had to search possible locations across the galaxy to find him. The mission became available after completing Citadel: Leviathan I and activating at least one galaxy map search filter. The walkthrough directed the player to the Aysur system in the Caleston Rift and to land on the asteroid Mahavid in the southern quadrant of the asteroid belt, which triggered the next mission, Mahavid: Leviathan.
