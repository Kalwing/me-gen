Diana Allers was a human Alliance News Network war correspondent and a regular on the network's program "Battlespace." She was originally from Milgrom, Bekenstein, and considered herself a "colony kid." She was a potential romance option for both male and female Commander Shepard.

On Shepard's first visit to the Citadel during the Reaper War, Allers could be found at the lounge of Normandy Dock: Bay D24 attempting to extract information from ensign Copeland. She asked to serve as an embedded journalist aboard the Normandy, arguing she could improve Shepard's standing with the galactic public, and Shepard could accept or refuse. Aboard ship she lived in the starboard cargo bay and aided the war effort as long as she remained. If refused, she embedded with the Alliance dreadnought SSV Shasta, which was later destroyed in the Horse Head Nebula with all hands, including Allers; she left a large donation to the University of Milgrom's Rossberg School of Journalism.

Shepard could direct Allers to focus her reporting on rallying support for Earth or on exposing Cerberus atrocities, and she produced story synopses accordingly, covering topics such as the destruction of Adelaide, suspected indoctrination of human leaders, the attack on Jon Grissom Academy, Kai Leng, and Sanctuary's true nature. She argued with ensign Copeland over an opinion piece claiming that sacrificing Terra Nova would save five other colonies, and Shepard could intervene. She traded intercom banter with Samantha Traynor. She was prohibited from reporting on Javik and the Prothean discovery, and declined to cover the returning rachni or Banshees to avoid alienating krogan and asari audiences.

Allers requested interviews after Tuchanka, after the Cerberus attack on the Citadel, and after Rannoch. If Shepard did not cut the second interview short and pursued her, the post-Rannoch interview led to a romance that had to be kept secret to protect her career, ending only in a kiss. Her romance was unique: no committed partnership, no love scene, no post-romance cutscenes, no Paramour achievement, and it did not block other romances, though Liara T'Soni would call out an affair. She often ended broadcasts with "Good night, and stay strong," echoing Edward R. Murrow. She was voiced by and modeled on Jessica Chobot. In an emailed message to Shepard she wrote: "I want you to make it all normal again, Shepard. Because that's the best revenge I can think of."
