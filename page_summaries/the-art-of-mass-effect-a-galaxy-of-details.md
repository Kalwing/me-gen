This page transcribes the fourth part of The Art of Mass Effect (2007), with commentary written entirely by Casey Hudson. The section covers the small objects and props that fill out the game's world: geth hardware (the geth rifle designed to echo geth starships, geth drones, hex barriers, heavy drones and flyers, geth towers, and the four-legged geth armature), electronic equipment and appliances, mining gear, the asari Consort's pleasure pods, generic interactive cover objects, space debris such as escape pods and downed satellites, and living-quarters furniture. It also discusses weapon design principles, including the difficulty of human helmets (resolved with a clear visor and non-obscuring chin guard), the twin-barrel look shared by all pistols and rifles, disc-shaped grenades, shotguns, the omni-tool interface, and the mass relays, whose final design used heavy rotating rings and a tuning-fork shape to focus energy into a beam that projects ships across the galaxy.
