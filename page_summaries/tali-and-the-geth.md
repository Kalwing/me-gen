Tali and the Geth was an assignment in the first Mass Effect, acquired during the mission UNC: Geth Incursions. After purging the geth stronghold on Solcrum, Shepard could find geth data files on a terminal in a back room; accessing the terminal began the assignment, on the basis that the files might interest Tali given quarian history.

Returning to the Normandy, Shepard could speak to Tali, who asked for a copy of the data to use as her Pilgrimage gift to bring back to her people. Shepard could give her the copy or refuse; if refused, Tali would try to reason with the Commander, but once Shepard decided the classified information could not be shared, that decision was final. The assignment granted experience and one morality point: one Paragon point for giving Tali the data, one Renegade point for withholding it. It was then completed and renamed in the journal as "Tali's Pilgrimage" (capital P) if the data was given, or "Tali's pilgrimage" (lowercase p) if not; in the original release the name was always lowercase regardless of choice.

In Mass Effect 2, on Freedom's Progress, Shepard could verify their identity to Tali by mentioning the geth data, and Tali would confirm it had aided her Pilgrimage. If the data had been withheld, Shepard could not reassure her this way, though she remained only mildly suspicious and her Pilgrimage was still presumably a success with an unknown substitute gift.
