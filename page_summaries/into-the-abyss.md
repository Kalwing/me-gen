Into the Abyss was a mission in Mass Effect Galaxy, played as Jacob Taylor. On the Citadel, Jacob ran into Major Derek Izunami, his old commanding officer, who asked for help stopping a batarian terrorist attack against the Citadel. Jacob accepted out of respect for his old friend and travelled to Cartagena Station, deep in the Nemean Abyss, to meet his contact, Miranda Lawson.

After discussing the plot with Izunami and travelling to Cartagena Station, Jacob intercepted a hurried patrol whose officer warned that pirates had infiltrated the station and that all arrivals were confined until the threat was neutralized. Jacob offered his help and fought through pirates and Pirate Elites across four corridors to reach the bar called the Fringe, where Miranda waited. There he met the pirate leader, Clint "Black Eye" Darragh, and could either fight him (with his two bodyguards, triggering an optional fifth combat zone) or convince him to surrender to avoid a bloodbath. Afterward, Miranda explained three potential sources to investigate the terrorist threat and provided a corsair pilot for transport, leaving the order of investigation up to the player. The mission had five combat zones and was followed by Scum and Villainy.
