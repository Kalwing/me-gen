Homeward Sol was a human-centric grassroots movement opposed to human expansion beyond the Sol system. Its members were xenophobes and Earth-centrists, likened to the Tenth Street Reds but without the illegal drug trade. It funded itself partly through lawsuits filed against famous individuals goaded into harming its members, such as by punching them, but was also capable of sophisticated espionage and terrorism.

In Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation, following an interview by Khalisah al-Jilani, Homeward Sol supporters harassed Cora Harper during her arrival at Tamayo Point and her weeks-long hospital convalescence there; her SAM-E implant had to fabricate a bomb threat so authorities would clear the docking bay of protesters. Alec Ryder received threats from the group that he considered credible, borne out when two Homeward Sol members were arrested after a bombing on the Hyperion nearly destroyed his SAM project. The group was known to run pirate radio ads carrying encrypted secondary messages, one of which the Alliance intercepted.
