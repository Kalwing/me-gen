The Perseus Veil was a huge purple-and-gold nebula of opaque gas and dust at the galactic rim that formed the natural border between geth space and the Terminus Systems. After the Geth War with the quarians, the geth isolated themselves behind the Veil, far from organic eyes. The nebula was almost impossible to scan through or navigate, so Council intelligence could not survey geth activity; the geth could theoretically be preparing a devastating attack, or could have died out entirely, leaving Alliance defense spending against them wasted. It connected to the Far Rim by mass relay, and beyond it lay the Tikkun system with Rannoch, the Geth Dreadnought, a geth debris field, the Migrant Fleet, and other bodies.

Despite fears, prospectors occasionally mounted salvage ventures inside the Veil for rich pickings; one ended in tragedy when the geth used technomental domination to draw a salvage team in and then sent them back as husks against the organic society that had produced them. A leaked classified Spectre report claimed the dreadnought Sovereign, commanded by ex-Spectre Saren Arterius and crewed by geth, hid near the Veil before launching the 2183 Citadel attack. The Veil took its name from the Perseus Arm of the Milky Way. In Mass Effect 3, after completing Priority: Thessia the Perseus Veil became inaccessible.
