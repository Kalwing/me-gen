Fornax was a human-published magazine featuring acts of xenophilia. It cost five credits an issue and was available on Omega. Launched in 2167, it presented raunchy pictures of models from various races, with a predominance of unisexual asari models but also male and female quarians, drell, batarians, volus, and hanar. By its fifth year it became the first human magazine to offer full five-sensory stimulation. It had a monthly circulation of 127 million and was available in hard copy and by direct download, with specialty editions such as Genit-elcor and Krogasm serving smaller markets. According to his Shadow Broker files, Grunt purchased a Special Krogan edition. The name is Latin for "furnace" and also references a star constellation and the word "fornication."
