Weapon Upgrades were modifications applied to assault rifles, shotguns, sniper rifles and pistols in the first Mass Effect to augment their performance. Each weapon had one ammunition upgrade slot and at least one weapon upgrade slot, with better weapons offering two weapon upgrade slots in addition to the ammo slot. The equivalent systems in later games were Research in Mass Effect 2 and Weapon Mods in Mass Effect 3.

Upgrades were grouped by function. Detection upgrades (Combat Sensor, Combat Scanner, Combat Optics) countered enemy radar jamming by raising detection level, with Combat Optics also improving accuracy and stability; the benefit was shared with any squadmate carrying a scanner on a drawn weapon, and extended to the Mako's radar if a party member had one equipped on entering the vehicle. Sight upgrades (Improved Sighting) raised maximum accuracy. Cooling upgrades (Heat Sink, Frictionless Materials) improved heat absorption or damping, with Frictionless Materials also adding damage and being especially recommended for shotguns and sniper rifles. Barrel upgrades (High Caliber Barrel, Rail Extension, Scram Rail) increased damage at the cost of rate of fire and heat absorption. Stability upgrades (Recoil Damper, Kinetic Stabilizer, Kinetic Coil) reduced kickback to tighten the targeting reticle during sustained fire, with Kinetic Coil also boosting damage.

Frictionless Materials and Kinetic Coil shared identical stats, differing only in whether the bonus was labeled "Heat Damping" or "Weapon Stability"; used together on high-level weapons they allowed continuous fire without losing stability or overheating while greatly increasing killing power. Weapon stability governed how quickly the targeting circle swelled to maximum during continuous fire.

The page also records cut content: modifications were called "X-Mods" during development, and a cut upgrade called the Hyper Rail, obtainable only through PC cheats, extended the barrel and "overclocked" the mass accelerator to fire hypervelocity slugs useful against lightly armored vehicles at the cost of severe heat and recoil.
