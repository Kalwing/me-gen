This was a short fetch mission in Mass Effect 3, available after Priority: The Citadel II. An asari scientist seated near the Bank area of the Presidium Commons, found only after Shepard stops Udina's coup, needed a statue from the asari Hesperia period for classified Crucible research because it bore highly accurate Prothean inscriptions and she had no images of it. Shepard obtained the statue by scanning the gas giant Polissa in the Vernio system of the Athena Nebula; it could alternatively be recovered through a Search and Rescue operation, in which case the journal entry was labeled Citadel: Hesperia Period Statue. Delivering it to the asari granted 5 Reputation, 15,000 credits, and an upgrade to the Alliance Engineering Corps War Asset.
