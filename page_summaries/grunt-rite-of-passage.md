Grunt: Rite of Passage was Grunt's loyalty mission in Mass Effect 2, available after Horizon once Grunt had been woken following Dossier: The Warlord. In the Normandy cargo hold, Grunt paced restlessly and told Shepard his desire to destroy and kill had become an involuntary compulsion; he asked to be taken to Tuchanka because EDI lacked krogan medical records, which the krogan fiercely guarded after the salarians used them to build the genophage.

On Tuchanka, Shepard spoke with the leader of Clan Urdnot, either Wrex or (if he died on Virmire or was never recruited) his brother Wreav, who explained that Grunt was not sick but of the age to undergo the krogan Rite of Passage, and referred the party to the clan Shaman. Gatatog Uvenk protested Grunt taking the Rite because he was tank-bred, but could be made to stand down. Shepard agreed to serve as Grunt's krantt and the Shaman sent the party to the traditional Rite grounds.

The Rite consisted of three waves triggered by activating a keystone: a swarm of varren, then fire-breathing klixen delivered by unkillable Harvesters, and finally a Thresher Maw that had to be survived for five minutes, with extra accolades for killing it. Killing the Maw earned the krogan's respect; it had not been done since Wrex's own Rite. Afterward Uvenk and his krantt appeared, offering to accept Grunt into Clan Gatatog as little more than a trophy; regardless of dialogue choice the encounter ended in a fight against Uvenk, who had barrier and armor, plus four Gatatog Warriors. Once they were defeated, the Shaman welcomed Grunt as a full member of Clan Urdnot and awarded a shotgun damage upgrade (Synchronized Pulsar), and Fortack's Database granted a discount. Grunt also unlocked the Fortification power. The Shaman later emailed Shepard demanding Grunt be kept alive for the krogan's survival. In Mass Effect 3, if this mission was not completed and Shepard spared the Rachni Queen during Attican Traverse: Krogan Team, Grunt would die.
