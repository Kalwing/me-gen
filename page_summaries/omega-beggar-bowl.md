"Omega: Beggar Bowl" was a cut assignment planned during development of Mass Effect 2 and not considered canon. Set on Omega, it involved a conflict between several beggars and the batarian merchant Marsh.

Marsh would give Shepard the quest after being seen talking with a beggar. Finding that same beggar, the player could choose to punch him or set him free. Shepard could also speak with several more beggars, and as a result could confront Marsh and Intimidate him. Either carrying out Marsh's orders or confronting him would complete the quest. Its plot database reference was LE2.Hubs.Omg_Hub.RP_Beggar_Bowl.
