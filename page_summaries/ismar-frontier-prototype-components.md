Brief Mass Effect 3 side mission (under ~150 words of real content). A salarian scientist at Huerta Memorial Hospital is overheard on a comm call searching for missing prototypes for a biotic amplification system. Shepard recovers the components by scanning Metaponto in the Aquila system of the Ismar Frontier cluster, which grants an Advanced Biotic Implants upgrade redeemable at the Intel Terminal in Liara's quarters on Deck 3 of the Normandy SR-2. Returning the prototypes to the scientist (by simply entering his lab; there is no map icon or manual trigger) completes the mission for 90 experience and no credits. The scientist says it will save Alliance lives, but nothing further comes of it. The mission must be done before Priority: Tuchanka, as it becomes unavailable after Priority: The Citadel II.
