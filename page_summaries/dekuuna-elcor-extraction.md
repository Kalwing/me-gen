Dekuuna: Elcor Extraction was a side mission in Mass Effect 3, available after Priority: Rannoch. The elcor had requested an emergency extraction for warriors and civilians trapped on their homeworld Dekuuna during the Reaper invasion. It was acquired by speaking to the elcor ambassador on the Citadel Embassies floor of the Presidium, near the stairs after exiting the elevator; morality points were available regardless of Shepard's response.

The mission was a standard Search and Rescue operation. Dekuuna lay in the Phontes System of the Silean Nebula. Scanning between Dekuuna and its moon Oltan revealed the Elcor Flotilla at the moon and the Code of the Ancients on the planet; only scanning and retrieving the flotilla from Oltan was required to complete the mission, and Dekuuna itself could be ignored. Returning to the Citadel and speaking with the ambassador again finished the mission; the elcor had evacuated, but the ambassador conveyed that not enough civilians escaped. Completion gave 5 Reputation and 120 experience. In the Legendary Edition, the Elcor Flotilla War Asset only appeared in scans after the mission was obtained from the ambassador.
