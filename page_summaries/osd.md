An OSD, or Optical Storage Disc, was a small, portable data storage unit used to physically transfer data between computer systems. Its contents could also be scanned remotely by omni-tools. OSDs were standard components of upgrade kits and were once used to distribute commercial games at retail, a practice that had largely fallen out of favor by 2185.

Several OSDs were significant to Commander Shepard's missions. Fist's OSD, recovered from the crime boss's office on the Citadel, contained files documenting Citadel corruption and was sought by the reporter Emily Wong. Qui'in's OSD, obtained from Lorik Qui'in on Noveria, held an encryption key granting access to restricted data implicating Administrator Anoleis in corruption. Benezia's OSD, given to Shepard by Matriarch Benezia, contained the coordinates of the Mu Relay for transfer to the SSV Normandy's navigation computer. Hossle's OSD, obtained from Gavin Hossle between Zhu's Hope and ExoGeni HQ on Feros, carried his data on weapon modifications.

Later examples included the Kirosa Generational Archive, an OSD holding the complete genetic lineage of the salarian Kirosa family for use in high-level reproductive negotiations, which was lost during the chaos at the Dantius Towers on Illium. Sekat's personal datapad was an OSD whose data allowed Liara T'Soni to locate the Shadow Broker's base and mount a rescue of her associate Feron. The information derived from the Galactic Codex: Essentials Edition 2183.
