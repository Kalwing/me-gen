During the Reaper War the Alliance News Network published two blog pieces concerning the Systems Alliance's N7 Special Operations. Both were released to promote Mass Effect 3 Multiplayer content: the Firebase Hydra piece accompanied the Resurgence Pack, and the special-operations piece accompanied the Earth DLC, which introduced Firebase Hydra, batarian characters, and N7 characters.

The first, datelined Firebase Hydra, was a satirical item reporting that elite troops of the now-defunct Batarian Hegemony had issued an official dictate clarifying that medi-gel works normally on batarians and should be used on injured batarian fighters. Squad Commander Shar'lak Gurek, presented as spokesman for the batarians, also asked allies to warn batarians of incoming banshees and denied that batarian kinetic barriers benefit from geth pyro flamethrowers. Admiral Peter Mikhailovich, a veteran of the Skyllian Blitz, issued a public statement of support for the batarian fighters, noting they fought alongside former enemies without slaves to treat their wounds. Lieutenant Girard, recovering from a fight on Noveria, said he would use medi-gel on wounded batarians as readily as on other allied species.

The second piece, "Special Operations in the Systems Alliance," described Interplanetary Combatives Training (ICT), the Alliance's premier leadership and combat course, run from the Interplanetary Combatives Academy ("the villa") at Vila Militar in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Trainees who passed the initial course earned the internal designation N1 and could return for courses N2 through N6, held at space stations and other planets and covering skills such as zero-G fighting, military free-fall, jetboot flight, combat diving, and multi-species first aid. N6 focused on live combat in galactic conflict zones; only trainees who survived actual combat "in an admirable and effective fashion" received the N7 designation. Before the Reaper War, N7 graduation took place at Arcturus Station; during the war it occurred near the battlefield. N7 was traditionally the only school rank permitted on uniforms, and there was no stigma in not receiving an N-designation. During the Reaper War many N7 soldiers were assigned to train alien species in asymmetric anti-Reaper warfare; units led by an N7 officer were designated "N7 forces" regardless of whether their soldiers were themselves N7s.
