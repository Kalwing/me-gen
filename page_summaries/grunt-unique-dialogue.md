This page collects the squad-specific lines Grunt speaks in Mass Effect 2 when brought along on missions, in ambient "talk to squadmate" spots, and as spontaneous combat or exploration barks. Recurring themes are his eagerness for violence, contempt for caution and diplomacy, frequent requests for food at scenes of carnage, and reverence for Shepard as a leader who fights "big things."

Representative lines:

- Grunt, at the Citadel security checkpoint on Shepard's first visit: "What is this crap?"
- Grunt, on Omega after Shepard is poisoned by Forvan: "Get up. You're not dead."
- Grunt, replying to the Blood Pack leader Garm at Archangel's base about why he follows Shepard: Shepard "does all of this and more."
- Grunt, on Haestrom during Tali's recruitment when told the sun will overload their shields: "So we'll fight in the shade... but no hiding!"
- Grunt, right before the Geth Colossus fight on Haestrom: "This is why I follow you Shepard, big things!"
- Grunt, if chosen to lead the second diversion squad on the Suicide Mission, his final words: "Too many. Gave them hell but...shields dropped. Good fight, Shepard. Good fight."
- Grunt, holding the door on the second team during the Collector Base finale: "Shepard! You're missing it! It's goddamn glorious! I'll climb out on their bodies!"
- Grunt, approving if Shepard destroys the Collector base: "We fought a great battle, Shepard. And when you blew up the base instead of handing it to Cerberus -- hah!" and "if you killed the most dangerous thing in the galaxy, that leaves... us."
- Grunt, chasing Jack during her recruitment: "Jack is small, this will be funny." and "She's blowing everything up. I like her!"
- Grunt, using his charge attack in combat: "I AM KROGAN!" / "Burn and die!" / "Should've run, now you'll fry!"

He also mocks the justicar Code as "Asari are stupid. Your code just means you kill her tomorrow instead of today," calls asari intimacy "pointless," asks if anyone is hungry aboard the derelict Reaper and on Omega, and tells Shepard the geth "doesn't need its help" when Legion appears.
