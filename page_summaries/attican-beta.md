Attican Beta was a star cluster in the Milky Way, revisited in Mass Effect 3 after Priority: Tuchanka. Prior to the First Contact War, a remote, frigid terrestrial world in the cluster hosted half of a turian interferometric telescope array. It was linked by an expensive chain of FTL comm buoys to a matching telescope at Gromar in the Voyager Cluster, and the two instruments functioned together as a virtual "lens" with an effective aperture equal to the thousands of light years separating them. The turians used this array to map the Terminus Systems with great accuracy. In 2186, the array could be salvaged to contribute to the construction of the Crucible. The cluster contained the Hercules and Theseus systems, the latter home to the planet Feros, site of the Feros: Geth Attack mission and several assignments in the original Mass Effect. The cluster was named after the Greek region of Attica, and its systems were named for heroes of ancient Greece and Rome.
