The Batarian Brawler Vanguard was a playable multiplayer class in Mass Effect 3. The page opened with the standard batarian flavor text, noting that little was known about Khar'shan or its inhabitants and that the race was judged by the organized crime enforcers, pirate boarding parties, slaver gangs, and gladiatorial combatants the Hegemony allowed beyond its borders. Batarians were described as large brawlers who used nets and spiked weapons to capture or bleed targets, and who bludgeoned close opponents with spiked armor and enforcement gauntlets.

The class used the "Batarian Enforcer" archetype with a barrier of 750 and health of 750. Its powers were Biotic Charge, Lash, Blade Armor, and the passive Fitness. Its melee sequence was two one-handed punches and a final two-handed blow; its heavy melee, Enforcement Gauntlets, wound up a powerful omni-tool punch that sent unprotected foot soldiers flying and destroyed their heads on a fatal hit; and its grab dragged an enemy over cover for an Enforcement Punch execution. The Batarian Brawler Vanguard had no dodge maneuver and could take cover but not roll between cover.

Player notes emphasized that the class excelled at melee: starting the heavy melee right after a Biotic Charge let it reach impact with stacked damage reduction, and with powers evolved it could exceed 100% damage reduction by combining heavy melee, Blade Armor, and Biotic Charge bonuses. Because it lacked direct-damage abilities aside from Biotic Charge, the page recommended a light but powerful weapon such as the N7 Piranha for tougher foes like Praetorians and Atlas mechs. Lash could disable smaller foes and set up Biotic Charge detonations on unarmored units but was useless against armored ones, while Blade Armor was best maxed for protection and reflected damage. The notes covered tactics against Cerberus, Collectors, Geth, and Reapers, warning specifically against charging Banshees since batarians could not roll away. A bug caused the Batarian Brawler Vanguard and Batarian Slasher Adept to use male human vocalizations when grabbed by a Husk or Abomination.
