Vendetta was a Prothean Virtual Intelligence residing within a Prothean beacon in the Temple of Athame on Thessia. It was modeled after Pashek Vran, the overseer of the Protheans' Crucible project, and held valuable information about the Crucible's construction and implementation. During the Reaper assault on Thessia in 2186, Commander Shepard activated Vendetta. After verifying it was in a post-Prothean cycle and detecting Reapers, it attempted to shut down, declaring the cycle had reached its "extinction terminus," but Shepard urged it to wait.

Vendetta explained that the Crucible was not of Prothean design, and that the Protheans' attempt to use it had been sabotaged by an indoctrinated splinter group who argued the Reapers should be controlled rather than destroyed, much like Cerberus. It said the Protheans had found that similar patterns of evolution and dissolution recurred in every galactic cycle, too similar to be coincidence, and inferred the Reapers were merely "servants" of that pattern, not its creators, its intention being galactic annihilation. If Javik was present, Vendetta identified him as Prothean and revealed it had known of his mission. It agreed to help with the Catalyst once the Crucible was complete, then detected the indoctrinated Kai Leng and entered a fail-safe mode; Kai Leng, aided by an A-61 Mantis Gunship, defeated Shepard, took the VI, and delivered it to the Illusive Man.

At Cronos Station, Shepard learned from Vendetta that the Catalyst was the Citadel itself, which enhanced dark energy emissions and coordinated the mass relay network to extend the Crucible's range. Vendetta had been programmed to withhold this so the Reapers would not learn of it before the Crucible was finished, but the Illusive Man broke its security and informed the Reapers, who seized the Citadel and moved it into orbit of Earth. After Kai Leng's death, Vendetta reported the Reapers were preparing to complete the harvest of humanity and recommended preserving information for the next cycle. Like the other Prothean VIs Vigil and Victory, its name began with the letter V.
