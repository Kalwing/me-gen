The N7 Hurricane was a submachine gun in Mass Effect 3 and a pistol in Mass Effect: Andromeda. Some militaries passed on it for its low accuracy, but the Alliance valued its rapid firing rate for suppressive fire; a disciplined marksman could use the fully automatic weapon to cut through targets very quickly, and Alliance officers were pleased enough with field results that it became many squadrons' standard-issue SMG.

The Hurricane was the most powerful SMG in raw damage output in Mass Effect 3, combining an extreme rate of fire with high per-bullet damage, but its main weaknesses were very high recoil (worst when zoomed or scoped) and rapid thermal clip consumption, with each shot drawing two rounds from the clip. It was best fired from the hip or from cover, benefited greatly from the SMG Heat Sink and SMG Recoil System mods, and combined with SMG High-Velocity Barrel and Ultralight Materials became one of the best anti-armor weapons for its weight. It worked well for squadmates such as Liara T'Soni and EDI, who ignore recoil and clip limits, and in multiplayer for turian classes and the N7 Destroyer Soldier.

In single-player it came with the N7 Collector's Edition Pack via an email from Lieutenant D. Eugene of the Alliance Engineering Corps; in Legendary Edition it cost a base 75,000 credits at Spectre Requisitions, and in multiplayer it was ultra-rare. In Mass Effect: Andromeda a small stock was acquired for the Initiative's security forces at the insistence of its proponents.
