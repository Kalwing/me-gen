Omega was a lawless mining space station in the Sahrabarik system of the Omega Nebula, built in the mined-out husk of a metallic asteroid once briefly mined by the Protheans and later split open by an asteroid collision that exposed its element zero. A rush of corporations and outlaws followed, and processing facilities were built vertically off the asteroid, giving Omega its jellyfish-like silhouette; mass-effect field generators ring the station to deflect debris. With a population of around 7.8 million and eezo as its major export, Omega had no central government or unifying authority and was a hub of narcotics, weapons, and eezo trafficking. Only mercenary groups imposed limited order. Different species named it revealingly: the asari "heart of evil," the salarians "place of secrets," the turians "world without law," and the krogan "land of opportunity."

The earliest known ruler was a turian, killed by a krogan crime lord who ate his heart and wore his skull; the krogan ruled until around the 1980s CE, when his lieutenant Aria T'Loak defeated him in single combat and became the station's de facto ruler. In Mass Effect: Ascension, the Cerberus operative Pel ran a warehouse and smuggling operation there in 2183 before betraying Cerberus. In Mass Effect: Redemption, Liara T'Soni came to Omega tracking Shepard's body, learning from Aria it was headed to a lower-level mining plant for a Blue Suns–Shadow Broker transaction. In Mass Effect 2, Shepard recruited Mordin Solus, "Archangel" (revealed as Garrus Vakarian), and optionally Zaeed Massani; a vorcha-caused plague sparing only humans was cured with Mordin's help, and Garrus was extracted after being besieged by the Blue Suns, Eclipse, and Blood Pack, who covered up their defeat. In Mass Effect: Retribution (2186), Paul Grayson worked as Aria's enforcer under the alias "Paul Johnson," aided by Aria's daughter Liselle, before a Reaper-tech-implanted Grayson was ambushed in a trap set by the Illusive Man and permitted by Aria.

In Mass Effect: Invasion, Adjutants broke loose from Cerberus bases beyond the Omega 4 Relay; a Cerberus fleet under Petrovsky and Ashe helped clear them, a prelude to Cerberus conquering the station and seizing exclusive access to the Omega 4 Relay. Aria united the gangs but Petrovsky took the whole station hostage, and she accepted exile. The occupation gave Cerberus a major strategic advantage during the 2186 Reaper invasion. In the Mass Effect 3: Omega DLC, Aria and Shepard, aided by Nyreen Kandros and the Talons, disabled Omega's defenses, rallied the Talons, shut down Cerberus's power-siphoning reactor to drop internal force fields, prevented the station's destruction, and assaulted Petrovsky's base at Afterlife. Aria reclaimed Omega and vowed never to lose it again.

Omega was divided into districts including Carrd (elcor), Doru, Fumi, Gozu (residential, site of the Omega Clinic), Kenzo, Kima (where Archangel was cornered), Tuhi, and Zeta. Its style drew on Blade Runner and the Mos Eisley cantina, and its silhouette was based on a mushroom cloud.
