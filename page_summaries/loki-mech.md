The LOKI Mech was a bipedal humanoid security robot manufactured by Hahne-Kedar for guard duty where manpower was scarce or continuous organic shifts were unfeasible. Initially used only by the Alliance for colony guard duty, it came into wider use after the 2183 Battle of the Citadel, when civilian and military security suffered heavy losses from Sovereign's attack and Saren Arterius' actions. Eclipse mercenaries deployed them as expendable assault units, and the Blue Suns and Cerberus used them for added security detail.

LOKIs had a basic personality suite, could be programmed for various security tasks and equipped with lethal or non-lethal weapons, spoke pre-recorded phrases (casually warning intruders to leave even while firing), and were waterproof. A unit switching to security protocols showed its dual "eye" turning from white to red. Their simplicity made them cheap but incapable of complex tactics: they never took cover, advanced slowly and relentlessly, and had weak firewalls easily overridden by hacking, turning them on their owners. They were no match for a trained soldier and were deployed in overwhelming numbers or as support. As enemies they usually carried the M-4 Shuriken (fired without spread or recoil), had a close-range electrical stun pulse, gained armor on higher difficulties, and frequently exploded when destroyed by headshots or when triggering self-destruct; they could be dismembered, losing the use of legs or firing arm. Related variants included the reprogrammed "Hacked Mechs" on Lazarus Station and the heavily modified Rampart Mechs Cerberus used during the occupation of Omega. The name likely references the Norse god of mischief.
