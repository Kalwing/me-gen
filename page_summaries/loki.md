Loki was the fourth planet of the Asgard System in the Milky Way's Exodus Cluster, a small terrestrial world with little to recommend it. One hemisphere was largely covered by an ice cap, the other by craggy basaltic highlands, the rough varied terrain suggesting an ancient cataclysm, a hypothesis reinforced by its unusually elliptical orbit reaching 6.5 AU at perigee and 7.1 AU at apogee. Penetrating scans of the ice cap revealed a network of crisscrossing subterranean tunnels; because some were surprisingly regular in shape, early expeditions looked for signs of excavation, but concluded they had formed by natural processes. The thin atmosphere was mostly krypton and xenon, and near perigee frozen sulphur oxides could evaporate into clouds on the hemisphere facing Asgard. Loki was accessible after the Bring Down the Sky DLC in Mass Effect. In Mass Effect 3, its natural tunnels may have sheltered retreating Alliance forces, though any organics hidden there remained in peril from the inhospitable atmosphere and efficient Reaper scanning; Alliance Spec Ops Team Delta was a Search and Rescue asset associated with the planet. It was named for the Norse god of mischief.
