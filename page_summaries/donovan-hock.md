Donovan Hock was a weapons dealer, smuggler, and patron of the arts who spoke with a strong Afrikaans accent. In the comic Mass Effect: Foundation, two thieves broke into his property on Bekenstein; Hock personally killed one of them, Keiji Okuda, and stabbed a device into Okuda's head to forcibly extract his graybox, remarking that he had never stolen a person's memories before and telling the dying man to consider it an honor.

Okuda was the former partner of master thief Kasumi Goto, and his encrypted graybox contained a secret that, if exposed, would implicate the Systems Alliance. Hock obsessed over decrypting it and kept it in the vault of his lakeside Bekenstein mansion, which also held priceless works including Michelangelo's David and the head of the Statue of Liberty. He was a patron of the artist June Fasha. During Kasumi's loyalty mission in Mass Effect 2, Hock threw a lavish party for high-society criminals; Kasumi secured Commander Shepard a forged invitation under the alias "Solomon/Allison Gunn," with a golden Saren Arterius statue as tribute, while turning Kasumi herself away. If prompted to keep talking for a voiceprint, Hock delivered a speech describing himself and his guests as the "cleaners" who form the "support structure for the galaxy's gleeful delusions of peace," earning a standing ovation. After Kasumi and Shepard infiltrated the vault and recovered the graybox, Hock confronted them, first via hologram and then in person piloting a shielded A-61 Mantis Gunship. Kasumi disabled the shield and she and Shepard shot the gunship down, killing Hock in the explosion.

In Mass Effect 3, if Shepard did not help Kasumi retrieve the graybox, Hock apparently decrypted it and survived; Salarian Spectre Jondum Bau paid him for intelligence about an Alliance cover-up of a raid on a batarian outpost, and regarded him as an unpleasant man. A developer script summarized Hock as "a bad person who kicks puppies for fun." Cut content adds that he murdered his way to the top of the arms market and had been a child soldier from the Omega slums.
