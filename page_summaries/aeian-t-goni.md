Aeian T'Goni, labeled in-game only as "PTSD Soldier," was an asari huntress being treated for Post Traumatic Stress Disorder at Huerta Memorial Hospital in 2186. She was found in the hospital's entrance hall recounting her last assignment to an asari psychotherapist. She refused to shower, repeatedly asked whether her eyes were still their normal color, and requested a firearm and a transfer to a facility without humans.

Aeian had landed on the human colony Tiptree with a group of asari commandos to help colonists evacuate the Reaper invasion. Among them was her friend Neaira, whose medical condition (presumably Ardat-Yakshi) had prevented their relationship from progressing. While Aeian was showering at a human farm, the farm was attacked by husks and by Neaira, who had been turned into a banshee. Unarmed, Aeian fled to the hills with a human girl named Hilary. Her commander judged the farm overrun and never sent a shuttle back. After two days without food, Aeian and Hilary snuck back to retrieve a radio. Hilary, unaware the surviving farmers were indoctrinated, freed them, and they raised an alarm and attacked. Hilary then broke her leg and could not stay quiet; to avoid attracting Neaira, Aeian was forced to kill her. Aeian escaped with data that earned her a medal.

Afterward Aeian blamed herself for the deaths, reasoning that Neaira had been controlled by the Reapers while she herself had merely lowered her guard for base needs, and she felt intense guilt over the adrenaline rush she experienced killing the indoctrinated humans. If Shepard used Spectre authority to override security and grant Aeian a firearm, she committed suicide, demoralizing the hospital staff. A nurse in the Presidium Commons was later overheard saying Aeian screamed whenever they met because the nurse resembled Hilary. Aeian's name was revealed only through a message on the Citadel Embassies Spectre Terminal. Writer Patrick Weekes stated that the Hilary whom Aeian killed was Joker's younger sister, consistent with Joker's in-game remark that his 15-year-old sister Hilary and his father lived on Tiptree.
