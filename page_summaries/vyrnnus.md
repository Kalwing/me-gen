Commander Vyrnnus was a turian biotic and mercenary who fought in the First Contact War, claiming to have served at the helm of a dreadnought that killed the father of one human trainee. In 2166 the human corporation Conatix Industries hired Vyrnnus and several other turian mercenaries to instruct human teenagers in the Biotic Acclimation and Temperance Training (BAaT) program at the Jump Zero station (also called Gagarin Station). Conatix kept the turians' involvement secret, since public memory of the First Contact War was still raw and human biotics being trained by turians would have angered the Alliance public.

Vyrnnus openly despised humans and ran a severe regime that used hunger and thirst as training aids. Several BAaT trainees "snapped" under the strain and some died. According to Mass Effect: Foundation, one incident began when a boy was caught rigging an illicit off-station transmitter; Vyrnnus tied him to a post, lectured the students on the millions of credits spent on their education, and forced them to biotically assemble objects from a pile of blocks for hours, withholding water. As students collapsed from exhaustion, Kaidan Alenko covered for Rahna when she faltered. Vyrnnus noticed, taunted Rahna with a floating glass of water, then blasted her arm and called her pathetic. In the Mass Effect account he broke Rahna's arm outright.

Kaidan retaliated with a burst of biotic energy. Vyrnnus escalated the confrontation, lunging biotically, drawing a military-issue "talon" knife, goading Kaidan to finish the fight, and slicing Kaidan's right side. Kaidan answered with a biotically charged jumpkick to Vyrnnus's head. The blow was stronger than intended and broke Vyrnnus's neck. BAaT staff tried to give him medical attention, but he died before reaching the infirmary. His death caused a diplomatic incident with the turians and ultimately led to the BAaT program being shut down. By 2183 Vyrnnus was a figure from Kaidan's past that could also be shared with Commander Shepard.
