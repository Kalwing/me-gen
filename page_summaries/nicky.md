Nicky was a member of Delta Squad, an Alliance special forces unit deployed to the colony of Fehl Prime in 2185, as depicted in the animated film Mass Effect: Paragon Lost. He served as the team's technical specialist and was highly talented at his job, though shy and timid. He respected the chain of command and showed great deference to Lieutenant James Vega, persistently calling him "sir" despite Vega's objections.

Delta Squad was sent to Fehl Prime to stop a Blood Pack invasion. Worried by the last-minute orders, Nicky analyzed the mission thoroughly. The Kodiak shuttle was shot down during descent. In the counter-assault Nicky provided tactical readouts of enemy positions and deployed his Combat Drone, which distracted the Blood Pack leader Archuk long enough to buy Vega time. Nicky was then wounded by a sniper shot from Brood and was incapacitated for the rest of the firefight. He was commended afterward.

Two years later, with Fehl Prime flourishing but new defense cannons installed amid a spate of Terminus colony disappearances, Nicky suspected the guns were connected to those disappearances. Captain Toni ordered Delta Squad to investigate a jamming signal emitted by an alien artifact in nearby Prothean ruins; the artifact was destroyed by Essex after Nicky failed to interface with it. When a Collector ship arrived, Nicky could not contact the colony. The squad tried to use the colony defense cannons against the vessel, but Vega, impatient with the slow charge, ordered a half-power shot that was deflected and nearly killed the team. Retreating into a bunker, Nicky's database search for an antidote to seeker-swarm toxin instead flagged squad member Messner as a Cerberus operative, forcing an uneasy alliance. After a Praetorian attack drove the squad onto Brood's ship, the creature attacked the shuttle in flight; Nicky was mortally wounded by its leg while pushing Vega clear of a melee attack. As he died, he urged Vega to save the colonists.
