HOME Group was an incorporated collective holding numerous assets on and around Earth. It owned Home Away, an Earth-orbit space station meant to be the flagship of a planned fleet of similar structures. Its board members enjoyed generous perks such as meetings at exotic Earth locations.

In Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation, HOME Group was linked to UNIN through board member and UNIN politician Alis Silhaus, an arrangement that effectively put a publicly accountable government body in charge of funding Home Away for the delegate's personal gain. Home Away in turn funded the Systems Alliance black site Quiet Eddy in exchange for commercial-grade cybernetics technology. HOME Group and its affiliates were responsible for the initial theft of Alec Ryder's artificial intelligence kernel, learned of through Alliance contacts. Once the kernel was secured at Home Away, infighting broke out between HOME Group and several companies over intellectual property rights. After Cora Harper retrieved and erased the software, news reports framed the incident as "a successful security breach by a rival corporation"; HOME Group's stock fell, and spokeswoman Irene Amadiume reassured shareholders by announcing an additional million credits invested in security.
