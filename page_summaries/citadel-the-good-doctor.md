Citadel: The Good Doctor was an assignment cut from Mass Effect during development and is not canon. It would have had the player acquire medical supplies and deliver them to a doctor on the Citadel, with supplies available in "regular" and "advanced" categories and an option to negotiate a discount. No endpoint was defined, so whether it was repeatable is unclear.
