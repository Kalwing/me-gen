This page was a gameplay guide to the M35 Mako, the six-wheeled infantry fighting vehicle used by Commander Shepard's team to explore hostile and uncharted worlds in the first Mass Effect. The Mako could traverse almost any terrain, was environmentally sealed against EVA hazards, and served as mobile turret, cover, and transport. In the original release, kills made while inside the Mako granted only half XP on Normal and 40% on Hardcore and Insanity, making higher levels harder to reach; Mass Effect Legendary Edition removed this penalty.

The Mako mounted two weapons: a mass accelerator cannon (powerful heavy artillery that partially bypassed shields but needed several seconds to recharge, with a double zoom for precision sniping) and an overheating coaxial machine gun (weaker but more precise and excellent at stripping shields). Propulsion jets let it jump over rough terrain and slow-moving projectiles such as Armature Siege Pulses, and helped right the vehicle if it flipped. Its radar had greater range than Shepard's but could be jammed by geth unless a squadmate carried a combat sensor. The Mako had shields and a sturdy hull; it could only be destroyed while occupied, and losing the hull with the team inside was an instant game over. Field repairs cost 15 omni-gel, took 10 seconds, could be done once every 30 seconds, and scaled with the team's combined Electronics skill; returning to the Normandy fully restored it.

The guide covered tactics: softening enemies with Mako fire then disembarking to use it as cover (and for full XP), ramming to disable isolated foes or "drag and drop" larger units like Armatures, firing on the move by strafing side to side since enemy AI aimed where the vehicle had been, dodging via "rocking back and forth" or jumping (best against Thresher Maws), and going "hull down" behind terrain rises. It documented glitches including the Mako flipping or rocketing erratically, becoming stuck between rocks on Therum, and save/load tricks to restore shields and hull. A closing section listed Legendary Edition changes: much stronger weapons, faster shield regen, no fall damage, camera-relative steering option, higher physics threshold, new rear thrusters for climbing hills and fighting the upgraded Thresher Maw, removal of the XP penalty, and cannon fire that followed the reticle.
