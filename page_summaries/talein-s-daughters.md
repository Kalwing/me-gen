Talein's Daughters was an asari commando unit based on Thessia that ran operations throughout asari space. Members typically served two-year tours before reenlisting, and, as with many military units, were not always told mission specifics but were expected to carry out their jobs without question. The unit's motto was, "I am a daughter of Talein. Against chaos—we stand. Against death—we refuse." Unit lore told of an asari matron who single-handedly held a defensive line against a krogan horde descending on the colony Ellita to buy time for evacuation; told that her death would leave no one to raise her two daughters on Thessia to be good asari, she replied that the only lesson she had to teach them was how to fight, and she died in weapons fire after holding long enough for the last evac shuttle to escape.

Known operations included actions on Omega and Nos Astra and the defense of asari colonies attacked by geth. In 2180, the unit accepted the biotic human Cora Harper into its ranks as part of the Alliance's Valkyrie Program; commanding officer Nisira T'Kosh gave her no preferential treatment over her four years in the unit. Known members included Nisira T'Kosh (unit commander); Ygara Menoris (a former subcaptain who retired to found her own mercenary outfit); Bannyn T'Dahn (who served one tour around 2084 and reunited with Menoris a century later); Cora Harper; Janae (the unit's "baby," an asari maiden over 200 years old by 2185 who had unreciprocated romantic feelings for Cora and once tore an AA gun from its housing to crush a gang of slavers); Kalia (a medic); Tethys (the unit's sniper, considered old, able to hit an elcor in the eye from two kilometers, whom Cora suspected of being an Ardat-Yakshi); and Valenza (a religious commando who prayed between firefights and executed a cult leader after being sent to be "purified of evil").
