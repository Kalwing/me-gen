The Rachni Wars were a series of conflicts beginning around 1 CE, when a Citadel Council expedition of salarian explorers activated a dormant mass relay and opened a route to rachni-controlled space. The rachni, a hive-minded species of highly intelligent space-faring insects guided by queens who dwelt in deep underground nests on worlds too hostile for outsiders to survive, captured the explorers. Using element zero research gathered from Kashshaptu, the rachni reverse-engineered the explorers' FTL drives, built their own FTL fleets, and expanded rapidly into the galaxy. Negotiation proved impossible because no one could reach the hive queens, so the Citadel races assumed the rachni were irredeemably hostile and could be stopped only by war. The rachni's overwhelming numbers pushed Council defenses back for nearly a century.

To turn the tide, the salarians culturally uplifted the krogan, whose homeworld Tuchanka had been ruined by nuclear winter from a civil war. The salarians gave the krogan advanced technology and relocated them to a habitable world. The true purpose soon emerged: the krogan's rapid breeding cycle and toughness made them ideal reinforcements. Krogan forces drove the rachni back, struck at the queens in their lairs, and reclaimed conquered Council worlds. When krogan fleets pushed the rachni to their homeworld Suen and the rachni refused to surrender, the krogan bombarded Suen's cities and detonated bombs in the underground nests, creating massive sinkholes and obliterating the species. In 300 CE the rachni were declared extinct, though the Council left a listening post in the home system. The Council afterward prohibited activation of dormant relays leading to uncharted systems.

The page notes theories that an outside party manipulated the rachni into war. The Rachni Queen on Noveria described hearing "oily shadows" and a "tone from space" that "forced the singers to resonate with its own sour, yellow note," language reminiscent of Reaper indoctrination; an asari serving the rachni on Illium suggested the rachni were not inherently hostile. Dr. Garret Bryson instead speculated the Leviathan was responsible, possibly building an army against the Reapers, but noted the ancient rachni bore no Reaper cybernetics; his theory remained unconfirmed.
