Submachine guns (SMGs) were a class of primary weapon in the Mass Effect universe, characterised as rapid-firing weapons in fully-automatic and burst-fire models. They traded low accuracy and low damage per shot for large thermal clip capacity and high rate of fire, making them best suited to close- and mid-range combat.

In Mass Effect 2, SMGs dealt bonus damage against shields and biotic barriers but were weak against armour, and like most weapons benefited from firing at close range. Every player class except the soldier could use SMGs from the start, and squadmates including Miranda, Mordin, Samara or Morinth, Thane, Kasumi, Liara and Dr. Amanda Kenson wielded them. The three models were the M-4 Shuriken (Elkoss Combine, the default starting weapon), the M-9 Tempest (Elanus Risk Control Services, obtained from Dossier: Tali) and the M-12 Locust (Kassa Fabrication, obtained during Kasumi: Stealing Memory). Research upgrades added shield piercing, extra rounds and squad damage bonuses.

In Mass Effect 3, all models fired standard rounds at a high rate of fire, each trading between damage and accuracy or stability; SMGs were generally the least damaging weapon type, though some rivalled assault rifles. Accuracy improved when aiming down sights, firing from cover or using a scope, and SMGs were a light weapon type attractive to power-focused classes, especially with an Ultralight Materials mod. A high-level High-Velocity Barrel made any SMG effective against armour. Squadmates EDI, Liara, Aria, Kasumi, Miranda and Samara used them. Models included the Blood Pack Punisher, Collector SMG, Geth Plasma SMG, M-4 Shuriken, M-9 Tempest, M-12 Locust, M-25 Hornet (Cerberus), and the N7 Hurricane (Systems Alliance). Weapon mods covered ultralight materials, magazine upgrades, high-caliber and high-velocity barrels, heat sinks, scopes and recoil systems. Unlike heavy pistols, SMGs could not be modified for melee damage, though holding one allowed faster melee attacks than shotguns, sniper rifles or assault rifles.
