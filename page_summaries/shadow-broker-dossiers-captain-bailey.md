This page collects the Shadow Broker's Dossiers file on C-Sec Captain Bailey, comprising a summary line, one of his internal memos, and a log of his daily reading. The header describes him as a "C-Sec captain. Survivor of Reaper attack on Citadel. Prefers dealing with Zakera Ward's criminal element 'off the record.' Ample blackmail material."

The memo, sent to the Zakera Ward officer mailing list under the subject "New Recruit Basics," instructs officers to drill three rules into new recruits:

- Bailey, on knowing the district: "Contacts don't make themselves. There's a mandatory minimum of twenty hours on foot patrol during your work week. No exceptions."
- Bailey, on translators: "The Citadel gets more than nine million tourists a week. You need to understand whether that drunk from Omega's enjoying the nightlife or telling his buddies to flank you."
- Bailey, on scrutiny: "Everyone from the Council to your bus driver is watching the new C-Sec. Yak all you want in the locker room, but remember a mistake in uniform can get plastered over the whole damn galaxy's news network."
- Bailey, closing: "Come to me with any problem cases you think we need to lean on or boot out."

The second document is a list of materials Bailey read on 05/23/2184: a Council meeting summary, Citadel Newsnet, emails from his ex-wife about a cancelled Citadel visit and about his health, a Newsnet Article of the Day titled "VIP Looters: Dark Side of the Colonies" by Erlan Golta, a report from Detective Nepatu Casha on murder suspect Rose Jones (with Bailey's note that Jones is innocent per camera footage), a transit bulletin cancelling Zakera-to-Tayseri Ward service, a backup request and officer-transfer request from Captain Keethem of Tayseri Ward, a note from Tayseri Ward intendant Sonull Beresh mourning officers Lambart Yungira and Bretnas Somalda killed by gunmen, and an invitation from Keethem to meet at Ohera's Bar and Grill.
