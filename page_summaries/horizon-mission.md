Horizon was a major plot-driving mission in Mass Effect 2, triggered after Shepard recruited Archangel, Jack, Mordin Solus and the krogan tank from Korlus. The Illusive Man summoned Shepard to report that the colony world Horizon had gone silent, a telltale sign of Collector attack, and that a former squadmate, the Virmire survivor Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko, was there on an Alliance outreach program. Before the Normandy's arrival, the colony's Alliance-provided GARDIAN turrets could not be calibrated, the Collector Cruiser descended, communications were jammed, and seeker swarms stung and paralyzed colonists including Lilith and the Virmire survivor.

Mordin had developed a countermeasure to the seeker swarms, letting Shepard's team move through the colony. They fought Collector Drones, Guardians and Assassins, plus Husks, and encountered Harbinger for the first time as it possessed Collector troops. The presence of Husks confirmed the Collectors worked for the Reapers. In a garage the team met the mechanic Delan, who explained the GARDIAN towers were the key to driving off the Collector ship. At the spaceport, Shepard hacked the transmitter and had EDI realign the lasers, then defended the tower through waves of Collectors, Scions and Harbinger, culminating in a fight against a Praetorian. Once the lasers charged, they hammered the Collector cruiser, forcing it to retreat with its harvesting unfinished.

Afterward Delan berated Shepard for failing to save his friends. The Virmire survivor, spared abduction, reunited with Shepard but reacted with anger on learning Shepard worked with Cerberus, refusing to rejoin and revealing the Alliance had sent them after a tip that Horizon would be hit. The Illusive Man admitted he had leaked information about Shepard's Cerberus ties and lured the Collectors to Horizon rather than wait; he pledged to focus on the Omega 4 Relay and handed over three new dossiers. The Collectors escaped with about one third of Horizon's population; salvage teams captured Collector bodies for study. A colonist, Robyn Reeve, later emailed Shepard begging for help finding her abducted son and brother.
