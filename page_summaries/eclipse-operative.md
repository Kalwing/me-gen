Short page. Eclipse Operatives were the elite salarian tech specialists of the Eclipse mercenary organization. They possessed all the abilities of Eclipse Engineers but were far more durable, protected by shields, armor and good health plus Tech Armor for powerful, rechargeable shields. They carried the M-9 Tempest submachine gun to strip shields and barriers, and used Combat Drones and Incinerate for fire support. The rest of the page is enemy tactics.
