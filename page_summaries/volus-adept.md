The Volus Adept was a playable class in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer, added with the Retaliation DLC pack. Its background noted that the volus homeworld Irune had a high-pressure atmosphere supporting an ammonia-based biochemistry, forcing volus to wear pressure suits and breathers or specially sealed combat armor. The class was described as a cagey support specialist using the most advanced power armor credits could buy, small in size but wielding formidable biotic ability.

The Volus Adept had 500 barrier and 150 health under Volus Training, with the powers Stasis, Biotic Orbs, Shield Boost, and Fitness. Its regular melee triggered a short tactical cloak lasting three to four seconds rather than dealing damage; its heavy melee (Defensive Stance) rooted the character in place and generated a protective shield sphere that damaged nearby enemies, granted brief invulnerability on each pulse, and restored some shields. The class could not grab, could not take formal cover (only "soft cover" by walking into it), had reduced visibility due to its short height, and could dodge with a combat roll. Volus could not be grabbed by Husks or Abominations.

The Volus Adept was regarded as an ideal support character on higher difficulties. Shield Boost recharged the shields of all nearby allies (roughly 1,100 shields plus about 220 per second for three seconds) and could grant 50 percent damage reduction and brief invulnerability to the whole team, making it a stronger medic than the Geth Engineer's turret, though it could not counter the shield gate. Biotic Orbs dealt weak damage but boosted power recharge rate substantially (up to +60 percent fully evolved), letting the Adept cast Stasis and Shield Boost almost continuously with a light weapon. Stasis immobilized targets and could be detonated by Biotic Orbs for chains of biotic explosions. The class was extremely fragile without Shield Boost. A Medi-Gel Transmitter allowed a volus to revive the entire squad by dying in a suitable location.
