Cryo Ammo was an ammo power available in the single-player modes of Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. Its payload of supercooled subatomic particles snap-froze targets, making them vulnerable to being shattered by gunfire or biotics. In Mass Effect 2 the power had three ranks that raised freeze duration from three to five seconds, with a rank 4 choice between Improved Cryo Ammo (seven-second freeze) and Squad Cryo Ammo, which extended the effect to the entire squad. The freeze worked on any enemy with a health bar once shields, barriers, and armor were stripped. Low-rate-of-fire weapons such as the M-96 Mattock and heavy pistols had a much higher per-shot chance to freeze; shotguns could trigger the freeze per pellet and even through protection layers. It was available to the Soldier, Infiltrator, and Vanguard.

In Mass Effect 3 the power gained six ranks with evolutions for freeze chance, freeze duration, squad bonus (50% effectiveness), ammo capacity, headshot damage against frozen enemies, and a damage combo increasing all damage to frozen targets by 50%. It flash-froze unprotected (red bar) enemies, "chilled" protected enemies to slow their movement by 15%, weakened armor by 25% (party-friendly), and stopped frozen targets from regenerating health. Unlike in Mass Effect 2, frozen enemies were not inherently more vulnerable to damage. Fully frozen enemies were primed for Cryo Explosion combos. It was available in single-player to the Infiltrator, Soldier, and Vanguard, and had no multiplayer version (the multiplayer equivalent being Cryo Rounds).
