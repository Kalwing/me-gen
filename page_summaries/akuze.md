Akuze was a human colony on the outskirts of Systems Alliance space. In 2177, while Akuze was still in the early stages of colonization, all contact was lost with the pioneer team. An Alliance Marine unit sent to investigate found the settlement intact but no survivors. When the unit camped for the night, it was attacked by thresher maws. Fifty marines were lost and only one reached the landing zone alive.

In Mass Effect, if Commander Shepard was the sole known survivor, C-Sec officer Eddie Lang on the Citadel mentioned a monument at Akuze with a section dedicated to Shepard. Shepard could later learn of a second survivor, Corporal Toombs, who claimed the slaughter was not an accident: he said the black ops group Cerberus had deliberately set the thresher maws on the marines to study the creatures and observe how the unit reacted.

In Mass Effect 2, a survivor Shepard could see news of a Shepard Memorial being erected on Akuze, with a commemoration ceremony hosted by Admiral Steven Hackett planned for 2186 once the site was cleared of thresher maws. If Shepard had rescued Toombs in 2183, he sent a threatening message about Shepard's new alliance with Cerberus. During Lair of the Shadow Broker, after Shepard and Liara defeated Tela Vasir, the asari Spectre cited the Akuze massacre as evidence of Shepard's hypocrisy in allying with Cerberus, the group responsible for those deaths. In Mass Effect 3, a survivor Shepard could view a recording on Cronos Station of a Cerberus scientist and the Illusive Man confirming that Cerberus was behind the attack.
