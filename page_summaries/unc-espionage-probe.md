UNC: Espionage Probe was an assignment in Mass Effect given to Commander Shepard by Admiral Hackett. An old Alliance espionage probe carrying a nuclear payload had turned up in the Amazon system of the Voyager Cluster, and if the Citadel discovered such nuclear booby-traps it would embarrass the Alliance. Shepard's task was to find, disable, and recover the probe. The assignment could be triggered by entering the Amazon system, or picked up early by decrypting cargo logs aboard the freighter Borealis in Zhu's Hope on Feros, which showed months of human rations being delivered to an uncharted world in the cluster. In either case Hackett commed the Normandy to explain the situation; Shepard could accept his orders or claim to be too busy.

The probe lay in a mine on the planet Agebinium, marked on the map as an Alliance Homing Beacon. Reaching it triggered a trap set by Elanos Haliat, the mastermind behind the Skyllian Blitz. If Shepard had the War Hero background, Haliat declared he was taking revenge for Shepard's role in repelling the Blitz and the loss of standing among his pirates that followed; with other backgrounds he detonated the device as general revenge against the Alliance because his operation had been discovered. Haliat set the probe's nuclear device to overload, giving Shepard ten seconds before it went off.

Disabling the bomb required disarming three hard points on the probe, each through a lengthy mini-game or 100 units of omni-gel. Once the bomb was disabled, the entrance was found blocked by fallen debris, and a previously inaccessible airlock door became the only way out; an upgrade kit sat just inside it. On returning to the surface, Shepard found Haliat and assorted pirates waiting over the next rise with the Mako. Killing Haliat, his Pirates, and Pirate Snipers ended the assignment. The enemies were heavily armed with armor that could survive a direct Mako cannon hit, making the fight challenging. The assignment was written by Chris L'Etoile and is referred to internally as "Spectres of the Past."
