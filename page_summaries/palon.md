Dr. Palon was a toxicologist working at Rift Station on Peak 15, on the world Noveria. Commander Shepard found him sleeping at the back of the station's main area near the entrance to the maintenance section, past Petozi, and could wake him to talk. Jumpy but cooperative, Palon told Shepard about the labs, pointed the Commander toward Han Olar, and mentioned a biotoxin accident that had affected Dr. Zev Cohen's team without giving details. He had not seen Matriarch Benezia and said Dr. Iallis was the only asari present, though he would notice if Iallis went missing.

Asked about his own research, Palon held to his non-disclosure agreement, hoping to keep his job if he survived. He said he disliked working at Peak 15 but that it paid better than anywhere else, and that its isolation left "only the discovery", making it easy to forget why science was guided by ethics elsewhere. If fighting broke out between Shepard and Rift Station's guards, Palon cowered in his corner like the other civilians and angrily ordered Shepard away, fearing the guards would shoot him to reach the Spectre. If Shepard cleared the hot labs first, Palon and the other civilians went missing, and Han Olar suggested Captain Ventralis had had everyone else killed.
