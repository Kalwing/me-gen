Citadel Arena: Cerberus Challenge was a side mission in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, available after Citadel Docks: Retake the Normandy, once the Armax Arsenal Arena opened along with the rest of Silversun Strip. After Shepard completed some arena matches, a fan named Rutaana emailed the Combat Sim Controls. Rutaana explained that some associates had heard rumors that Shepard worked with Cerberus and were intensely disappointed, and asked Shepard to fight simulated Cerberus forces publicly, alongside Alliance soldiers, to win those fans back.

To complete the challenge, Shepard selected Cerberus as the enemy at the Combat Simulator Control terminal and entered the simulation with an Alliance-only squad: no squadmates, James alone, or James plus Kaidan or Ashley if the Virmire survivor was alive and available, as no other squadmates qualified as the specified "Alliance soldier friends." Other match modifiers were unrestricted, so the challenge could be completed alongside non-conflicting arena challenges such as the Medi-Gel Challenge or Spin Zone Elite Challenge. After the match, Rutaana sent a second email saying the match was what her friends needed to see, noting that some complained a simulation meant less while others cited Shepard's role in stopping the Citadel coup. Reading the second email granted +5 Reputation.
