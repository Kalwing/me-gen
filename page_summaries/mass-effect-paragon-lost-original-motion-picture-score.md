The Mass Effect: Paragon Lost Original Motion Picture Score was made publicly available on November 21, 2012. Of its 27 tracks, 26 were composed by Joshua Mosley and David Kates, and the 27th, "Warning Signs," was a song by The Anix. The soundtrack was made available on iTunes and Amazon. Track titles included "Alliance Marines," "Krogan Battle," "Vega's Tactical," "The Mass Effect," "Collectors," "The Cannon," "A Spy Revealed," "Enter Brood," "Ascension," "Paragon Is Lost," and "The Emergance of Vega."
