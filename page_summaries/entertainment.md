This page catalogs in-universe forms of recreation across the Milky Way and Andromeda: films, plays, books, magazines, musical acts, sports, and games. Notable theatrical works included Francis Kitt's award-winning all-elcor production of Hamlet, which ran over 14 hours and used elcor body language and pheromones, and his planned Macbeth with a krogan lead and a live pyjak; the elcor doctor Yorrik authored an unproduced 16-hour all-elcor Macbeth but died before reaching Andromeda. Starless (2173) was a gothic horror masterpiece whose vacuum-preserved sets on airless Faringor became a tourist draw.

The Blasto franchise followed the galaxy's first hanar Spectre through many films, including Blasto: the Jellyfish Stings, Blasto Saves Christmas, Blasto 6: Partners in Crime, and Blasto 7: Blasto Goes to War! (co-starring Shepard and Javik). Blasto lines quoted include: "This one doesn't have time for your solid waste excretions." / "This one has forgotten whether its heatsink is over capacity. It wonders whether the criminal scum considers itself fortunate?" / "Enkindle THIS."

Citadel, directed by Risa Uvarsen, dramatized Shepard's role in the Eden Prime War, with a scene varying by whether Shepard saved the Council. Fleet and Flotilla was an award-winning vid praised for its positive turian-quarian romance; Shepard and Tali could watch it during Citadel shore leave. Its quoted lines: Bellicus, "But Shalei. We can never be together. I have my duty, and you, have your people." / Shalei, "Not tonight. Tonight, I'm as free as the dust in the solar wind." / Shalei, "I want you to see behind this mask. I want you to see who I truly am." / Bellicus, "I already have." Last of the Legion was a turian war film watched aboard the Tempest, quoted: Jorax Halabrian, "I never left the Legion. It left me. But I know my duty." Nekyia Corridor was a record-breaking human simstim that outgrossed the asari classic Confederates.

Dozens of other films are listed via Cerberus Daily News box-office reports, including Blue Steel, Vaenia, and the "Taetrus effect" comedies. Games covered include Chess (popular with elcor; General Oleg Petrovsky's holographic board passes to Shepard if Omega is liberated), Kepesh-Yakshi, Galaxy of Fantasy, Quasar, Skillian Five poker, and Three-Card-Monte. Publications include Fornax ("the galaxy's finest xenophilia"), Ascension and Revelation by Drew Karpyshyn, and Justicar Heroes. Sports include biotiball, clawball, kailat ring-ball, the krogan sport kowla, and Armax Arsenal Arena combat simulators.
