Eden Prime was the second planet of the Utopia System in the Exodus Cluster, one of the first human colonies established beyond the Charon mass relay. Its biosphere was unusually well suited to Earth-native life, drawing heavy immigration and development by the Systems Alliance and various corporations. It became a model of sustainable, organized agrarian development, with populations housed in space-efficient arcologies towering over thousands of kilometers of fields and orchards. Its 2183 population was estimated near 3.7 million, with Collector data retrieved by Aria T'Loak recording 3,962,450 humans. In 2185 Earth's Council for the Preservation of Human History and Culture named it a "culturally significant site."

In 2183, Eden Prime was attacked by a geth force led by the rogue Spectre Saren Arterius. Commander Shepard, responding to the attack, encountered a Prothean beacon that warned of the imminent Reaper invasion; this warning and Shepard's quick action gave the galaxy its chance at survival. According to Zaeed Massani, only the farms had been rebuilt by 2185.

50,000 years earlier, Eden Prime had been a Prothean colony, overrun by Reaper Destroyers and Sovereign-class vessels supported by Collector troops. One bunker complex meant to hold a million Protheans in stasis was betrayed by indoctrinated traitors; disasters reduced the survivors to hundreds, and by 2186 only one surviving stasis pod was unearthed, drawing the attention of Cerberus. In 2186 Cerberus occupied the colony, abducting able-bodied men and women while researching how to open the pod. A local resistance movement formed to thwart them. Known native flora included lace-in-lilac, whose seeds were carried to Andromeda by Milky Way colonists. The planet featured in the missions "Prologue: Find the Beacon," "Priority: Eden Prime" and "Eden Prime: Resistance Movement."
