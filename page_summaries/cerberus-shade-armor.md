The Cerberus Shade Armor was a full-body armor set in Mass Effect 3, added by the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC and obtained by scoring over 9,999 points in the Armax Arsenal Arena. In fiction it was worn by Cerberus Phantoms who needed to be ready for a wide variety of threats, with capacitors giving modest gains to shields and shield regeneration and many small improvements boosting biotic amps, omni-tools, heat management, kinetic coils and synthetic muscle assistors for melee. Reaching the score triggered a congratulatory in-game e-mail, "Thanks for breaking the scoreboard," from Armax Arsenal Arena principal systems administrator Virginia Ruojian, who offered Commander Shepard a Cerberus Phantom's armor captured during the attack on the Citadel. The armor then appeared beside the arena's bronze, silver and gold token machines, alongside the Cerberus Nightmare Armor and Cerberus Spirit Armor; only one of the three could be chosen, and the choice carried across later playthroughs with the same character. Shade increased shields, melee damage, weapon damage, power damage and ammo capacity, making it arguably the best melee-focused set for Vanguards, Soldiers and Sentinels when first available and rendering the Collector Armor obsolete. Together with the Cerberus Ajax, Nightmare and Spirit sets its bonuses contributed to an 80% cumulative total, above the 60% of most full-body armors. Setting combat difficulty to Narrative was the fastest way to reach the required score. The male version was identical to Kai Leng's outfit without a helmet and was reused for the multiplayer N7 Slayer Vanguard, while the female version matched the N7 Shadow Infiltrator; the set was visually identical to the Nightmare and Spirit armors, with the Legendary Edition giving all three dark face shields instead of gold.
