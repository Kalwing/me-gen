Septimus Oraka was a retired turian general who had fought several campaigns against the krogan.

In 2183, Commander Shepard was sent to find Septimus at the request of the Consort Sha'ira. Septimus had visited the Consort for years and they had grown close, but after he wanted her to be "more than she could be," they had a falling-out. Angry, he began spreading lies about her and spent his time drinking in Chora's Den. Sha'ira asked Shepard to speak to him soldier to soldier and remind him of his honor. When Shepard found him, Septimus claimed Sha'ira had been the only woman who helped him forget the horrors of his past and had effectively deserted him. Shepard could convince him to straighten up and behave like a general. Realizing how he had acted, he vowed to apologize to her, and asked Shepard one favor: he had discovered a compromising secret about an elcor diplomat named Xeltan and had accused the Consort of leaking it, so he gave Shepard the details of how he had actually learned it, which would exonerate Sha'ira, and asked Shepard to give them to Xeltan. Sha'ira later reported that Septimus had sent a gracious apology note and that they had become friends again.

During the Reaper invasion in 2186, Darner Vosque asked Shepard to kill Septimus in exchange for Blue Suns support in retaking Earth, as Septimus had been harrying Blue Suns raids on C-Sec weapon shipments. Septimus offered to call off his attacks if Shepard convinced a black market dealer to trade weapons; Shepard could speak to Kannik and gather valuable artifacts in the Vular system of Kite's Nest to trade for advanced weaponry. Alternatively, Shepard could simply ask Aria to have Septimus killed, which also secured the Blue Suns. If present, Wrex mocked Septimus, who retorted with a paraphrased Winston Churchill line: "I may be drunk, krogan, but you're ugly. And tomorrow I'll be sober."
