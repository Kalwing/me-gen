Sticky Grenade was a grenade power in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes, available to the Infiltrator class (and to the Human Infiltrator and Quarian Infiltrator in multiplayer). The grenade was thrown to adhere to an opponent, then detonated to tear apart the target while shrapnel damaged nearby enemies, with a base 750 damage (1100 in multiplayer) and a small 2-meter radius. Like all grenade powers it had no recharge time but consumed one grenade per use from a shared grenade pool.

Its six ranks improved grenade capacity, damage and radius, added an armor-piercing bonus, and at rank 6 offered a Proximity Trap evolution that let a wall- or surface-attached grenade stay active for 15 seconds and detonate when an enemy approached. Throwing the grenade did not break an Infiltrator's Tactical Cloak, allowing stealthy placement, though enemies could detect the Infiltrator once a grenade dealt damage. Ammo powers endowed the grenade with their effects, and the detonation could both prime and trigger certain Power Combos with a single use. The page noted the grenade penetrated cover and terrain (but not Guardian or CAT6 Heavy shields), and was easiest to land on large, slow targets such as Atlas mechs, Ravagers and Brutes.
