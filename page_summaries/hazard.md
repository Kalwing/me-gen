Environmental hazards were dangers found on planets with hostile environments, appearing in Mass Effect and returning in Mass Effect: Andromeda. In the original game, worlds dangerous to explore on foot were ranked Level 1, 2, or 3 on the Galaxy Map. Exiting the environmentally sealed M35 Mako started a hazard timer that filled faster at higher levels; when full, the character took health damage until death. The timer reset upon entering the Mako or large structures, but small portable cabins gave no protection. Wearing Devlon Industries armor negated the timer entirely on Level 1 environments and slowed it on Level 2 for the whole party. Common hazards included heat, extreme cold, pressure, toxic agents such as pollen and bacteria, and radiation. Hazard worlds in Mass Effect included Eletania (toxic), Mavigon (cold), Metgos (heat), Nonuel (heat), Noveria (cold), Sharjila (pressure), Solcrum (heat), and Xawin (cold). In Legendary Edition, lava became a Level 8 generic hazard that damaged the Mako over time rather than destroying it instantly, though characters were still killed instantly and Nonuel's lava still destroyed the Mako; Therum's surface was reclassified Level 8.

Interior hazards included falling boxes and exploding power junctions, plus containment cells found in mercenary bases, mines, and derelict ships: fusion cells caused toxic damage, ion cells released a shield-damaging electrical discharge, and cryo cells released Bose-Einstein condensate causing cold damage. Fires were ranked Level 2 or 3 depending on size and ignored shields.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda the system returned with hazard levels 1 through 4, and the ND1 Nomad, unlike the Mako, also took damage. Encountering a hazard switched Pathfinder Ryder to Life Support, tracked on a lower-left display and narrated by SAM. Forward Stations and re-entering the Nomad replenished Life Support, though recharging inside the Nomad was very slow. Higher levels drained Life Support faster; Level 4 hazards such as lava depleted all Life Support and shields on contact and killed within about a second, and damaged the Nomad as fast as Ryder. Once Life Support was gone, shields then health took damage until death. Backup Life Support consumables and Nomad upgrades (Advanced and Supplemental Life Support) extended protection. Only Ryder was affected; squadmates and enemies were immune. Planet hazards were tied to Remnant vaults: Elaaden (heat), Eos (radiation), H-047c (radiation, so severe Ryder could not leave the Nomad and which had no vault), Havarl (none), Kadara (toxic, only in sulfur pools), and Voeld (cold). Activating a planet's vault removed or, for Voeld, partially reduced the hazard.
