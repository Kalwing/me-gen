Essex was a talented human biotic and a member of Delta Squad, an Alliance special forces unit, featured in the animated film Mass Effect: Paragon Lost. He was carefree, overconfident, and constantly boasted about his biotics, though he could casually dispatch enemies and took pleasure in it. He was persistently attracted to fellow marine Kamille, who repeatedly rebuffed him, and his favorite line was "Just trying to look cool before I die." He also mocked Lieutenant James Vega's devotion to Commander Shepard.

Delta Squad arrived at the colony of Fehl Prime in 2183 while it was under attack by Blood Pack mercenaries. Their shuttle was shot down, Captain Toni was incapacitated, and Vega assumed command. Essex and Kamille flanked the mercenaries while Vega distracted the enemy commander; Essex nearly blew the flank by fumbling a thrown rifle, but redeemed himself with devastating biotic attacks and sniper support from Milque. His adrenaline nearly killed Kamille when a startled varren was flung onto her, and Essex was beaten by the krogan Brood until Vega intervened; Vega then stopped Essex from killing the prisoner. Essex was reprimanded and commended after the Alliance victory.

Two years later, in 2185, the Alliance had reinforced Fehl Prime with an anti-ship cannon and kinetic barriers. Delta Squad investigated a jamming signal traced to an unknown alien device on the colony's outskirts, which Vega ordered Essex to destroy with his biotics, angering the archaeologist Treeya. The Collectors then attacked, abducting colonists. During the fighting the anti-ship cannon failed to penetrate the Collector ship's barriers and was destroyed, and Kamille was seized and carried off by a drone while Essex, distracted, could only watch. Essex stayed behind to cover the squad's retreat into the underground labs and was paralyzed by seeker plague, then recovered by his squad and given an experimental antidote from the labs.

When a Praetorian attacked, Essex was left behind but astonishingly recovered and returned to immobilize the creature with biotics while Vega finished it. Nicky died soon after. The squad, aided by an uneasy alliance with Brood, flew Brood's ship into the ascending Collector vessel, but Messner shot Brood, revealing himself as a traitor, and the ship crashed. Vega revealed he had spiked the squad's shared drink with the cure. Using a power cell Brood had given Vega, Essex amplified his biotics and fought the Collectors while Vega tried to save Treeya's pod, which was spaced. Facing the multilegged Collector boss, Essex was impaled from behind while grandstanding; with a final biotic effort he broke the creature's defenses and, aided by Vega's fire, sent it over a cliff, falling to his death with it.
