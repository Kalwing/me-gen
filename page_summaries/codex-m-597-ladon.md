The M-597 Ladon was a heavy weapon exclusive to the Wii U version of Mass Effect 3, occupying its own primary Codex entry. It combined human workmanship with Reaper technology: after the Battle of the Citadel, Alliance intelligence studied Reaper-based code recovered from Sovereign and decoded a notably advanced face- and object-recognition system. Preparing for the Reaper invasion, the Alliance adapted this software so a weapon could distinguish friend from foe, producing the Ladon, a missile system that surveyed the field, assigned a homing warhead to each hostile, and delivered incendiary payloads while avoiding allies, even if they had changed out of uniform or grown facial hair since it was last fired. Its main shortcoming was the few critical moments it took to process this information before each launch while the trigger was held down.

Despite its fearsome antipersonnel capability, soldier folklore made it a candidate for "most hated weapon." Its targeting display had been adapted so quickly that alien alphanumeric characters remained visible around the edges, a constant reminder of its partial Reaper origin. Rumors held that a Ladon could indoctrinate an owner who slept next to it, or that it would hit friendly targets the first time it was used against Reaper creatures. More than one Alliance unit left their Ladons at the bottom of a supply crate before heading on a mission.
