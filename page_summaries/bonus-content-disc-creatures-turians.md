This page transcribed concept artists' gallery commentary for "The Creatures of Mass Effect: Turians," from the 2007 Mass Effect bonus content disc, with remarks by Sung Kim and Derek Watts. Kim, who was working on Dragon Age when asked to contribute turian concepts, said the designers wanted a "bird race"; his early sketches gave the turian beak-like facial features and feathers but read as too bird-like, while a later swing away from bird traits went too far into a "what is that?" direction, though some elements from it survived. He tried a triangular, blocky upper body influenced by Batman: The Animated Series for an imposing look, then moved toward an elegant, long, agile, exoskeletal physiology, running out of time before finishing the lower body. A tighter head version gave the face plating, imagined as a beak evolved into plating over the face, while keeping bird-like eyes to convey intelligence.

Watts described adapting the finished turian design for Saren, the game's main villain. A cloth-and-robes concept by Matt Rhodes was cut because an armor-and-shields game made a cloth-wearing villain feel too easily killed, so the team went to armor. After talking with Casey, they gave Saren a large geth arm with hoses and blue lights as an iconic feature showing him being converted into a machine, bulked up the naturally sinewy turian frame to feel more massive, and added dirt, weathering and blood spatter. Roughly 25 head concepts were done by Watts, Rhodes and others; Saren's head incorporated a mosquito's stinger as the bone structure behind the eye, microscopic insect photos overlaid for grooves, jaw pistons, and a mechanical blue eye referencing a Transformers: The Movie poster. Watts also covered the "Saren monster" form seen after Saren is destroyed and revived, animated on all fours in a hopper pose, missing its jaw, with exposed wiring, which the modeler, James, found difficult to build.
