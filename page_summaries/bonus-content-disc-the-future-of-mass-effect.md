"The Future of Mass Effect" was a documentary included on the bonus content disc of Mass Effect, released in 2008. It featured project director Casey Hudson, lead writer Drew Karpyshyn, BioWare general manager and CEO Ray Muzyka, and principal lead designer Preston Watamaniuk. The menu described it as showing where the creators wanted to take the franchise.

In the transcript, Muzyka described Mass Effect as a large "toy box" of stars and planets and said post-release downloadable content was important to BioWare, citing the batarian-focused Bring Down the Sky as an example of developing selected worlds and continuing storylines. Hudson said the team wanted continuous content leading into Mass Effect 2 and that downloadable content would help set up the sequel. Watamaniuk said the team was working on a fight club or arena idea that had been cut from Mass Effect 1 for lack of time, intended as the next downloadable content piece. Hudson described this as a more combat-oriented, lighter-story experience set on a casino gaming fight club space station, and confirmed that a Mass Effect 1 save could be carried into Mass Effect 2 to continue the same character. He said the second game would be a darker period in which answers were harder to find, with many twists and turns.

Karpyshyn said Mass Effect explored universal themes such as love, loss, consequences, choice, whether the end justifies the means, and morality, and would continue pushing themes of technology and how synthetic or artificial life compares to organic life. He said player choices had to carry consequences through the trilogy, which forced the team to create alternate versions of many scenarios and effectively more content than a single playthrough would show, so that players who had made different choices would see significant differences, including characters being alive or dead. Hudson said returning characters would include Captain Anderson and Udina, and that main characters would recur throughout the trilogy unless they died because of the player.
