UNC: Locate Signs of Battle was a collection assignment in Mass Effect. Several League of One medallions and a few salarian ID tags were scattered across various star systems, the remains of a hunt that must have been extensive and taken years. The assignment appeared in the journal when Commander Shepard discovered the first medallion or ID tag. The in-game Codex claimed there were 12 League of One medallions and 4 salarian ID tags, but collecting 10 medallions and 3 ID tags was sufficient to complete it. The original page consists mainly of location tables and has little additional prose.
