The drell were a reptile-like race rescued from their dying homeworld by the hanar following first contact between the two species. They remained loyal to the hanar afterward and integrated into galactic civilization. Drell were omnivorous humanoids with an average lifespan of 85 galactic standard years, superficially resembling asari and humans but with denser muscle tissue giving them wiry strength. They had fused ring and middle fingers, two sets of eyelids, and skin infused with a mild venom that could be served in drinks and cause mild hallucinations on oral contact. They possessed eidetic memory, an adaptation to their resource-scarce ancestral world; external stimuli could trigger vivid, involuntary memory recalls so detailed that some drell mistook them for reality. Thane Krios could recall every assassination he had performed in flawless detail.

The drell evolved on the arid, rocky world of Rakhana. Around the 1380s CE, industrial expansion drove the planet toward lifelessness while the population reached eleven billion with no interstellar flight capability. The hanar made first contact around the 1980s CE and transported roughly 375,000 drell to their humid ocean homeworld, Kahje, over the following decade; the billions left behind perished in resource wars. On Kahje, drell required climate-controlled dome cities, and the leading cause of death became Kepral's Syndrome, an incurable, noncommunicable degradation of the lungs caused by long-term humidity exposure.

The debt drell owe the hanar is called the Compact, fulfilled by taking on difficult tasks such as combat; service is considered a great honor and is rarely refused. Most drell lived contentedly on Kahje as respected citizens, while those who left tended to be solitary adventurers who adopted other species' cultures. Drell were deeply religious, believing body and soul form a Whole that can be separated, and worshipped a polytheistic pantheon including Amonkira, Lord of Hunters; Arashu, Goddess of Motherhood and Protection; and Kalahira, Goddess of Oceans and Afterlife. Non-translatable terms included drala'fa ("the ignored"), sere (honorific for adult males), siha (a warrior-angel of Arashu, used as endearment), and tu-fira ("lost in another").

During the Reaper War of 2186, drell served in Adept, Vanguard and Infiltrator roles, and if Kahje was saved through Shepard's efforts the hanar sent their best drell combat specialists to the war effort. Over 4,500 drell joined the Andromeda Initiative aboard the ark Keelah Si'yah; centuries into the voyage a bioengineered virus, seeded via usharet-scented capsules on the pods of extroverted drell, mutated and became lethal, jumping to other species before a cure was developed.
