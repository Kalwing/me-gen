Gabriella "Gabby" Daniels was a former Alliance engineer, a Cerberus operative in the Lazarus Cell, and an engineer aboard the Normandy SR-2 in 2185. More serious and formal than her fellow engineer and best friend Kenneth Donnelly, she preferred to get straight to the point. The two graduated from the same tech academy class and served aboard the SSV Perugia at the Battle of the Citadel. Afterward, when Kenneth publicly defended Commander Shepard against the Alliance's denial of the Reapers, Cerberus recruited him; Gabby insisted on being included in the contract, believing he would fall apart without her.

Gabby loved starship engines and worked the engineering deck beside Kenneth, often chatting about ship upgrades and crew, scolding Kenneth for perverted comments, and teasing him about Jack before warning him she was a psychopathic murderer. She was among the Normandy crew abducted by the Collectors and would die if Shepard took too long to rescue the crew during the suicide mission.

If she survived, Gabby was taken into custody when the Alliance impounded the Normandy in 2186; Shepard could use Spectre status to free her back to the engine room. She and Kenneth were both approached again by Cerberus during the Normandy's downtime but refused on Gabby's intuition. Back at work on the Alliance Normandy SR-2, they resumed their old banter. Chief Engineer Adams praised Gabby as knowing propulsion theory better than most physicists and thanked Shepard for recruiting her. If both survived and Shepard sided with Gabby in their argument, she and Kenneth could begin a romance and later be caught embracing in the sub-deck. After the war, if Shepard used the Crucible with sufficient preparation, Kenneth, Gabby, and Major Coats oversaw reconstruction efforts in a devastated London. A programming error tied the Gabby and Ken banter to Shepard being good friends with Ashley, fixed by the Extended Cut. In an unmodified, non-imported Mass Effect 3, Gabby's name appeared on the Normandy's memorial wall.
