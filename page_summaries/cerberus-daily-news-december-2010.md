This archive collected Cerberus Daily News reports from December 2185. The war on Garvug moved into a legal phase: Second Star Broadcasting reporters exposed Guanghui Solutions and Sonax Industries concealing a cache of Prothean technology on the planet for private gain, and Citadel Council investigators indicted 14 of 24 board members from both companies, including Sonax supreme commander Lira Speight, for withholding the technology and instigating the war. An alleged assassination attempt by Spectre Lonar Maerun left Speight wounded and killed his companion Urjon Taltz; Sonax's parent company Umsung Holdings then rebranded the division as "Kore," retired the indicted board members, and installed Vice Admiral Sanele Mabuza. Speight fled Garvug with bearer bonds and platinum, and days later an Omega medical examiner, Ur'shok Aranhur, confirmed her death by stroke via a 99.998-percent geneprint match; she was 47. A running story followed biotic, apparently intelligent beetle-like insects called kirik on the planet Ekram: researchers led by Dr. Andile Ndiaye, with linguist Martha Cospiel, communicated with the hive through biotic pulse patterns, Ndiaye controversially proposed uplifting them, and the Citadel sent representatives to assess their sapience after the colony stranded the team by lifting their groundcar. On Taetrus, civilians were released from the safe camps into a difficult reconstruction overseen by former hastatim units, with tens of thousands missing or orphaned and Lieutenant Astrae Rubicum tasked with culling abandoned pets. Aish Ashland auctioned a cloned spare heart for charity, won for 115.6 million credits by Kanta Dutta for her ailing father Rakesh Dutta. Other reports covered elcor performance artist Nockrick Taur's arrest for disrupting a Citadel musical, the University of Serrice agreeing to return salarian artifacts to Mannovai after professor Baliya Haphia changed her position, the completion of Andrea Schumacher's Cairn time-capsule monument, a record-length biotiball game, Christmas, and the galaxy's longest chess game. The month closed with the New York Times Square New Year's Eve ball, relocated to the Presidium, being stolen by turian con man Rolan Quarn, who left a signed note, prompting a C-SEC search led by Captain Armando-Owen Bailey.
