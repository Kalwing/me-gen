Mass Effect 3: Citadel was a DLC pack for Mass Effect 3, announced February 21, 2013 and released March 5, 2013 for Xbox 360 and PC (March 6 for PlayStation 3 in Europe). It was the final DLC released for the game and is included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. On Xbox 360 it shipped as two packs (one paid, one free), both required.

The pack opened new areas of the Citadel and centered on a sinister conspiracy targeting Commander Shepard, whom the team must uncover and stop through battles and intrigue ranging from the Wards to the top-secret Council Archives, fighting alongside a clone of Shepard and the antagonist Brooks. Its second half let players reconnect with characters from across the trilogy: gamble at the Silver Coast Casino, fight in the Armax Combat Arena, furnish Shepard's Citadel apartment, host a party, and rekindle romances. Content included 17 missions (Shore Leave, Identity Theft I and II, Silver Coast Casino: Infiltration, Citadel Archives: Escape, Citadel Docks: Retake the Normandy, Citadel: Party, and ten Arena challenges), eight temporary squadmates if they survived earlier games (Urdnot Wrex, plus Miranda, Jacob, Jack, Grunt, Samara, Zaeed, and Kasumi appearing only in the Armax Arsenal Arena), 21 enemies (CAT6 mercenaries, the Clone, and Reaper- and Collector-type foes in the Arena), five locations, two weapons (M-7 Lancer, M-11 Suppressor), three Cerberus armor sets, seven weapon mods, and two War Assets (Normandy Repairs and Retrofit, Team Spirit). It cost 1200 BioWare Points on PC and $14.99 on consoles. BioWare marked its release with a promotional Twitter broadcast about four soldiers' shore leave during the Reaper War.
