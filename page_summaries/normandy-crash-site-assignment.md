Normandy Crash Site was a Mass Effect 2 assignment delivered as free Cerberus Network DLC. After acquiring the Normandy SR-2, Shepard received an e-mail from Admiral Hackett ("Normandy Crash Site Located") reporting that Alliance scans in the Amada System, Omega Nebula, had located the wreckage of the SSV Normandy on the planet Alchera. The Alliance asked Shepard to place a memorial monument on the site and to recover any sign of the 20 crew members still unaccounted for after the Collector attack that destroyed the ship.

The assignment was a solo exploration with no hostiles. As Shepard walked the frozen crash site, the Commander experienced flashbacks to areas of the SR-1: the ship docked at the Citadel in Dock 422, Joker at the cockpit controls, the sleeper pods, the Mako in the cargo bay, the CIC and Galaxy Map, Navigator Pressly saluting, and the squad member left behind on Virmire. Notable wreckage included the intact Mako, the destroyed Galaxy Map, port armor plating still reading "Normandy," the sleeper pod compartment, a cockpit section, and an upright escape pod.

Twenty dog tags were scattered across the map, most inside yellow crates, each belonging to a lost crew member (Bakari, Barrett, Chase, Crosby, the two Dravens, Dubyansky, Emerson, Felawa, Gladstone, Grenado, Grieco, Laflamme, Lowe, Negulesco, Pakti, Rahman, Tanaka, Tucks, Waaberi). A datapad belonging to Navigator Pressly held three journal entries charting his shift from distrust of the ship's alien crew to a declaration that he would die for any member of the crew regardless of their birthworld. Shepard's lost N7 helmet could also be recovered; if found it appeared on the desk in the Commander's SR-2 quarters. Four containers held 500 units of element zero total.

Shepard had to place the monument to complete the assignment; returning to the shuttle afterward ended the mission. If at least one dog tag was recovered, Hackett sent a follow-up e-mail thanking Shepard on behalf of the notified families. Rewards included 3,750 credits in Alliance funding, 500 element zero, and up to 240 experience. In Mass Effect 3, the names of all crew lost with the SR-1 appear on the Normandy SR-2's memorial wall regardless of how many dog tags were collected.
