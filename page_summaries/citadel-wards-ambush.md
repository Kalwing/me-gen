Following Citadel: Shore Leave, Shepard's conversation with Joker at Ryuusei's Sushi Bar was interrupted when armed mercenaries entered, knocked out the host, and fired into the ceiling, scattering the patrons. Shepard pulled Joker down behind an overturned table. When a mercenary dragged Brooks away, Shepard sent Joker to gather the crew and went after Brooks, killing a mercenary with a heavy melee and recovering a unique M-11 Suppressor pistol. After fighting through mercenaries and Disruption Drones and reviving Brooks, a mercenary with a Mattock took aim at Shepard; Brooks shoved the Commander clear and took the shot herself, and gunfire shattered the bar's glass floor, sending Shepard falling into the lower Ward and onto a platform, badly injured and reduced to one health segment. Brooks, having used all the medi-gel on herself, reported finding a secure terminal, and Shepard told her to alert C-Sec and stay off the possibly hacked comm. Shepard descended through maintenance platforms, a frozen-fish storage area, and a market, using stealth against roving CAT6 mercenaries, snipers, and drones. A crew member, either Liara T'Soni or Shepard's love interest, made contact on foot nearby, joined later by a second crewmate; Brooks objected to the unsecured channel. After bypassing a locked-down bridge with Spectre authorization, which set off an alarm, Shepard reached the Cision Motors skycar lot, where the first crewmate joined as a squadmate and a frightened volus manager was persuaded to open a door. Outside, an arriving C-Sec shuttle proved to be another ambush, backed by a Geth Spitfire. Urdnot Wrex crashed through a window, smashed the shuttle onto the platform, and cleared the ambushers; if Wrex was dead or the genophage cure had been sabotaged, James Vega arrived with a Cobra Missile Launcher instead, and EDI could stand in as the second crewmate. With a full squad and restored health, Shepard held off waves of mercenaries until Joker arrived with a shuttle and extracted the squad, beginning Citadel: Identity Theft I.
