This page collects dialogue cut from Mass Effect 2 that is set on Omega and the Citadel, recovered from dialogue and text-string files; none of it is canon. It includes ambient voicelines for asari dancers, patrons, and drunks in the Afterlife Club and a nearby back alley; a scene of a client hiring a dancer; ambient lines for the hitmen in Omega: The Patriarch; a cut Preitor Gavorn greeting if Shepard beat down the vorcha Shisk; a cut Sergeant Haron conversation accessible only by reaching the Presidium early; cut al-Jilani ambient reactions after the interview; unheard Presidium groundskeeper lines in the Dark Star Lounge; a cut game-vendor dialogue for Citadel: Crime in Progress; a conversation between two Citadel dockworkers, Lors and a friend; and cut or bugged Galactic News bulletins, some restored in Legendary Edition.

Representative lines:

Afterlife dancer: "Buy me a drink, and maybe you'll embrace eternity tonight."

Afterlife patron: "I seen a vorcha shoot a man. Oh, no. It was another vorcha."

Preitor Gavorn, to Shepard: "You're the human who laid out Shisk! The only thing those vorcha bastards understand is a good smack in the mouth."

Sergeant Haron, on the Citadel's tightened security: "Officially, sir/ma'am, only C-Sec are allowed to have weapons on the Citadel now." He adds that Executor Pallin "was killed by geth in the Battle of the Citadel. C-Sec's current commander is Decian Chellick."

Khalisah al-Jilani, after a bad interview: "Give the people nothing, and they make up their own facts, Shepard. You're not helping yourself."

Presidium Groundskeeper: "You ever had quarian 'thruster fuel?' Must be 99% alcohol. I think they distill it from raw antimatter."

Lors, a dockworker who fled Omega: "You know damn well I can't. T'Loak would skin me alive if I set foot on Omega again."

News presenter, on the Shadow Broker: "Is the Shadow Broker's criminal empire collapsing? ... Financial broker Barla Von, linked as a possible Shadow Broker source, was recently found dead."
