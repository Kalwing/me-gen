Grad Turrets were immobile defensive installations used by Cerberus, concealed inside ceilings and emerging only when an enemy came into range. They appeared on the Barn station, where Randall Ezno faced them during his escape in Mass Effect: Infiltrator. They could inflict particularly heavy damage and fired in two long bursts with a sizeable pause between, which players were advised to exploit. Being ceiling-mounted, they were unaffected by Pull, Leash, melee attacks, and Charge, and could not be knocked down with Slide, though Slide remained useful for dodging their fire. An evolved biotic storm was effective against them since they could not move, letting Ezno stay in cover, but they regenerated armor.
