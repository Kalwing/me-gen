Citadel: Hanar Diplomat began after Priority: Palaven, when salarian Spectre Jondum Bau emailed Shepard asking for discreet help investigating whether a member of the hanar diplomatic delegation on the Citadel had been indoctrinated. Meeting Bau outside Commander Bailey's office, Shepard learned that hanar operatives present at an Alliance raid on a batarian lab studying Reaper technology were believed to have been indoctrinated there. Bau's source was Kasumi Goto if Kasumi: Stealing Memory had been completed, or Donovan Hock otherwise. The mission had to be finished before Priority: Tuchanka, as the civilian Citadel would not reopen in the same state afterward.

Using the Spectre Terminal's Hanar Embassy Tracking option and a series of terminals across the Embassies, the Office Suite, and the Docks Holding Area, Shepard investigated suspicious money transfers by the hanar Bolohn and Oloon. Both were cleared: Bolohn's transfers went to an asari mistress, and Oloon was lobbying for the hanar to support the war effort. The true suspect was identified as Zymandis, a recent arrival from Kahje who had been on the Alliance raid team and on a "special research assignment" since. If Kasumi was alive, she cloaked and assisted throughout without Bau's knowledge, and referenced Keiji Okuda's graybox.

Bau and Shepard confronted Zymandis, who revealed he had uploaded a virus to shut down the automated defenses of the hanar homeworld. A human bodyguard attacked Bau. If Kasumi was present, she stopped the upload while Shepard freed Bau and Bau shot Zymandis, after which a rigged console exploded. Without Kasumi, Shepard had to choose between saving Bau with a Renegade interrupt or stopping the upload. Saving Bau granted the Spectre Unit War Asset (40 points); stopping the upload granted the Hanar and Drell Forces War Asset (50 points, upgradable to 58 via Citadel: Alien Medi-Gel Formula); Kasumi's help secured both. A loyal Kasumi then supported the Crucible as a technical expert and, with the Citadel DLC or Legendary Edition, became a playable squadmate in the Armax Arsenal Arena.
