The Human Sentinel was a cooperative multiplayer class in Mass Effect 3 (distinct from the Andromeda class of the same name). Its lore blurb noted humans joined the galactic community after discovering a Prothean data cache on Mars in 2148 and impressed the staid Council races with their adaptability to modern warfare. Sentinels were described as masters of nothing but proficient in everything, often relied upon to draw fire.

The class had 500 shields and 500 health and used the Alliance Training passive with the powers Throw, Warp, Tech Armor and Fitness. Its melee options were a punch sequence, dual omni-blades for heavy melee, and an omni-blade stab grab; it could combat-roll to dodge and roll between cover. Gameplay centered on biotic combos: Warp primed and Throw detonated biotic explosions on any enemy, while pairing the Acolyte weapon with Throw (and incendiary ammo) produced rapid fire explosions against shielded foes. Tech Armor added damage reduction and could stagger nearby enemies, though it did not boost biotic combo strength. Because the class was power-intensive, a light weapon loadout was recommended. The page details faction-specific tactics against Cerberus (Atlases, Dragoons, Phantoms, Guardians, turrets), Collectors (Warp against all enemy types, Praetorians, Abominations), Geth (Acolyte/Throw as an efficient killer, cloaked Hunters), and Reapers (Warp/Incendiary stacking against Brutes and Ravagers, and complex phased tactics against Banshees).
