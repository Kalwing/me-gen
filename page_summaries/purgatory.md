Purgatory was a maximum-security prison starship owned and run by the Blue Suns mercenary company, located in the Milky Way's Hourglass Nebula around Osun. Its facility administrator was Warden Kuril. It was where Commander Shepard could recruit Jack, a dangerous and highly unstable biotic, during the mission Dossier: The Convict.

Originally an "ark ship" built to carry agricultural animals, Purgatory was seized by the Blue Suns during a large-scale battle in the Skyllian Verge, then rebuilt over years into a prison for sapient inmates. Rumors held that the Blue Suns sold skilled or fit prisoners to batarian slavers. When media investigated the slaving claims, the Blue Suns turned the scandal into income: claiming full compliance with Citadel law, Purgatory's crew would land at ports announcing they could no longer afford to hold prisoners and would have to release them locally, prompting governments to grant massive discounts on fuel, food and repairs to make them leave, and some even paid to offload their own prisoners. The listed population was 4,350, though journalists estimated nearly three times that during overcrowding. The ship was minimally armed with GARDIAN defenses and relied on Blue Suns fighters. It held about 30 blocks of stacked, movable cells serviced by mechanized claws, with open-air communal showers and field-generator towers to separate fighting inmates. A fork in the walkways led to a supermax wing of criminals in cryogenic stasis, where Jack was held under guard by multiple YMIR Mechs.

On Shepard's arrival, Kuril said Cerberus had paid for Jack's release and directed Shepard to Outprocessing, where Shepard could intervene in the brutal interrogation of a prisoner called Bimmy. Kuril then betrayed Shepard over the intercom, declaring the Commander "worth more as a prisoner than a customer," and set the Blue Suns guards on the squad. Shepard fought through and released Jack, who went on a destructive rampage. By the time Shepard killed Kuril, the prison was falling apart from structural damage. Afterward the Council sent rescue ships for any survivors in escape pods.
