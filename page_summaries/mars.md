Mars was the fourth planet of the Sol System. Once considered a prospect for terraforming and colonization, it became a quiet backwater after the discovery of faster-than-light travel. Its southern pole was made a historical preserve centered on the Prothean ruins found there, and immigration and development were restricted while the search for Prothean artifacts continued. Mars was explored by robotic expeditions for nearly a century before the first manned research stations arrived in the 2080s. The first permanent settlement, Lowell City, was founded in 2103 by the European Space Agency in Eos Chasma, and within a decade the US and China had established their own settlements. Satellites near Promethei Planum reported unexplained magnetic field shifts that gave the region a "Bermuda Triangle" reputation.

In 2148, a prospecting team led by Mateus Silva exploring near the Deseado Crater unearthed a subterranean Prothean ruin, the source of the disturbances. The ruins contained a malfunctioning mass effect core, several starships, and refined element zero. After a global translation effort, the structure was identified as a former Prothean observation and biosciences station set up to study Cro-Magnon humans on Earth. The observers' motives and conclusions remained unknown, but the fragmented data cache accelerated human sciences by roughly two hundred years, paving the way for FTL drives and later mass effect field technology. Some of the refined element zero fell into the hands of criminal triads on Mars, who used it to create the drug red sand, which may have taken its name from the planet's coloring. The mission Priority: Mars took place there, and Mars could not be mined. A cut Mass Effect DLC would have taken place inside the Prothean ruins and on the Bonestell Platform orbiting the planet.
