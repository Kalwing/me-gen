UNC: Hostile Takeover was an assignment in Mass Effect in which Commander Shepard worked for Helena Blake, a high-ranking member of a criminal cartel who wanted two of her compatriots eliminated. After Shepard became a Spectre, the assignment could be obtained by meeting Blake near the Emporium in the Financial District of the Presidium, or by decrypting a C-Sec Academy console (Hard Decryption) in the office next to Chellick, which identified Blake as a powerful but unproven criminal element. Blake explained that her two targets were red sand dealers who sold defaulting customers to batarians as slaves, a practice she disapproved of; once they were dead she would run their organization "more tastefully." She provided the coordinates whether or not Shepard accepted.

The first crime boss was on Mavigon in the Han system (Gemini Sigma), a base on a cliff defended by three heavy turrets, on a world with a Level 2 Cold Hazard. Inside, a science-station layout held five mercenaries, two Snipers, and the Crime Boss, who used tech abilities including Damping and Neural Shock. The second boss was on Klensal in the Dis system (Hades Gamma), a mine defended by regular mercs and snipers in towers, with an identical interior force. Mercenaries were krogan, turian, and human, could regenerate and use Immunity on higher difficulties, and included one-shot snipers.

After clearing both bases, Shepard traveled to Amaranthine in the Fortuna system (Horse Head Nebula) to meet Blake in a two-story facility. Accepting her reward resulted in Shepard being escorted out and the door locked. Attempting to arrest her caused her and her mercenaries (a krogan merc, three human mercs, two salarian snipers, and Blake herself, behind a purple barrier) to attack. With high Charm (10+) or Intimidate (7+), Shepard could convince Blake to disband her gang permanently for morality points and free run of the base. If she was killed, a dialogue box described her dying with a wry, respectful smile, unable to speak as only blood came out.

In Mass Effect 2, if Blake survived, she could be found in the lower level of Afterlife on Omega: as a social worker thanking Shepard if she disbanded her gang, or, if not, having merged her gang with Aria T'Loak's organization. The Amaranthine base reused the layout of the Sharjila base in UNC: Asari Diplomacy and the Xawin base in UNC: Privateers.
