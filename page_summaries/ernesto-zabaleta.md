Short page. Lieutenant Ernesto Zabaleta was a former Systems Alliance marine found on the Citadel Ward Access corridor. He appeared only if Commander Shepard had the Spacer background, in which case he was on the Citadel looking for a handout and recognized Shepard because he had served aboard the SSV Einstein with Shepard's mother, Hannah Shepard. During a conference call, Hannah explained that Zabaleta had been among the ground troops at Mindoir and suffered post-traumatic stress from what he witnessed there, turning to alcohol and eventually leaving the service after repeatedly reporting for duty intoxicated. She suggested referring him to the Veterans Affairs Office on the Citadel, though he was reluctant. Shepard could give up, set him up with a grocery store account, or convince him to seek help.
