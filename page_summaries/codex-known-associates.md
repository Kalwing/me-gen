The page collected the Mass Effect 3 Codex biographies of Commander Shepard's known associates. Admiral Steven Hackett, born in Buenos Aires in 2134, rose from enlisted man to command the Fifth Fleet at the Battle of the Citadel and then the whole Alliance military. Admiral David Anderson, born in London in 2137, was the first N7 graduate and the Normandy's original captain before handing command to Shepard; he briefly served as the first human Councilor (or as Councilor Udina's military advisor) before returning to duty, in both accounts becoming entangled in a Cerberus plot against his friend Kahlee Sanders. Dr. Liara T'Soni, born on Thessia in 2077, was an asari Prothean researcher and biotic, only child of the late Matriarch Benezia, who became an information broker after serving aboard the Normandy. EDI, the Enhanced Defense Intelligence, was the rebuilt Normandy SR-2's AI, given full ship control by pilot Jeff "Joker" Moreau during a Collector attack.

Garrus Vakarian was a turian sharpshooter and former C-Sec officer who investigated Saren Arterius, later operated on Omega as the vigilante Archangel, and became head of a turian Reaper task force. Major Kaidan Alenko, a human biotic with failing L2 implants, led the Alliance's Special Operations Biotics Division. Lieutenant Commander Ashley Williams, born 2158, was the sole survivor of her unit on Eden Prime and later the Normandy's gunnery chief. Tali'Zorah, a quarian machinist born on the liveship Rayya in 2161, recovered geth evidence against Saren during her Pilgrimage and later survived a geth attack on Haestrom. Dr. Mordin Solus was a salarian Special Tasks Group geneticist who had worked to perpetuate the genophage and ran a clinic on Omega. Jacob Taylor was a former Alliance marine and Corsair who became a Cerberus operative under Miranda Lawson. Miranda Lawson, genetically engineered from her father's DNA in 2150, headed Project Lazarus, which revived Shepard. Dr. Karin Chakwas served as the Normandy's trauma surgeon under Anderson and Shepard, was kidnapped by the Collectors, and later rejoined the Alliance. Most associates had left Cerberus or had unknown current whereabouts.
