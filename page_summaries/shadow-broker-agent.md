Shadow Broker Agents were the rank-and-file soldiers of the Shadow Broker's private army, tasked with defending the Broker's interests. They were encountered by Commander Shepard, Liara T'Soni, and the squad during the mission to bring down the Shadow Broker, appearing on Illium and at the Broker's base on Hagalaz.

Agents were armed with the standard M-8 Avenger and could use Flashbang Grenades. They had moderate health, and on higher difficulty levels also carried light shielding. The Flashbang Grenade made them more dangerous than ordinary Troopers, as they used it to dislodge players from cover and strip shields; the recommended response was to move away from a landed grenade and have Shepard look away to avoid being blinded.
