Dark Channel was a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes. It plagued an opponent with a persistent, damaging biotic field that transferred to a nearby target when the current one was killed, continuing to jump to new targets until its duration expired; only one field could be active per user at a time. It had six ranks with evolutions for recharge speed, damage, duration, target slowing, and a Pierce evolution boosting damage to armor and barriers by 75%. By default it dealt 150% damage to armor, double damage to barriers, and half damage to shields, though these modifiers were lost after the field jumped to a new target. Targets afflicted were primed for Biotic Explosions regardless of protections. Like Reave, it was a hitscan power that impacted instantly and could not be dodged, and it could bypass the power-immunity shields of Banshees, Phantoms, Rampart Mechs, and Praetorians when spreading from a dead enemy. It dealt nearly four times more damage in multiplayer than in single-player and was effective against isolated Swarmer groups and hard barrier-and-armor targets. Availability was Javik and Shepard (as a bonus power) in single-player, and the Asari Huntress Infiltrator, Awakened Collector Adept, and N7 Fury Adept in multiplayer.
