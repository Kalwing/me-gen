Space Cows are orange-and-white herbivores with orange irises, four legs, and two small arm-like forelimbs each ending in two fingers. They graze naturally in herds, with wild populations on Chasca, Nodacrux, Ontarom, and Aite. They can be spoken to but do not respond, and are startled by nothing, not gunfire, vehicle ramming, or the killing of herdmates, though the Galactic Humane Society disapproves of harming them. Space Cows are edible by humans; Gil Brodie notes that overcooked Space Cow tongue is extremely tough.

In Mass Effect, the "Shifty Looking Cow" on Ontarom steals credits whenever Commander Shepard looks away. In Mass Effect 2, Shepard can kill Space Cows on Aite with the Hammerhead, prompting dry commentary from the vehicle's VI, which observes that "defenseless herbivores are no match for guided missiles" and eventually states that targeting systems do not require local wildlife to calibrate. Space Cows had no audio in the first Mass Effect but make bovine squealing sounds when killed in Mass Effect 2. Cut content indicates they were also called "hexa cows," referenced in Eden Prime files and a cut Ashley–Kaidan dialogue describing them as a local herd species with high milk yields and tough meat.
