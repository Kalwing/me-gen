This page documented Adrenaline Rush, a Soldier-class combat power in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3 (single-player and multiplayer) that granted a limited-duration weapon damage boost and, in single-player, a time-slowing effect to help line up shots. The page consists almost entirely of power-rank statistics, evolution options (Hardened, Heightened, Hardening, Dilation & Damage, and later-rank branches), and gameplay mechanics notes rather than narrative lore, so no full lore summary applies.
