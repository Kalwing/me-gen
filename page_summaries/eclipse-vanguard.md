Eclipse Vanguards were the biotic and close-quarters specialists of the mercenary organization Eclipse, encountered as enemies in Mass Effect 2. They were exclusively human and asari, with asari predominating because of their naturally greater biotic potential. The Vanguard's battlefield role was to close in and eliminate opponents through force and biotic power, though unlike geth units or the Krogan Berserker they used cover while advancing.

Asari Vanguards carried the M-27 Scimitar shotgun, which had short range but a damage bonus against shields and barriers, and they engaged at point-blank range. Human male Vanguards used the M-15 Vindicator and attacked from a distance. Both variants had the Warp power to complement their weapons. Defensively, Eclipse Vanguards were protected by the rarer biotic barrier, making them resistant to shield-stripping effects such as Overload and Disruptor Ammo, and were additionally shielded by Tech Armor, which they could use to fully replenish depleted barriers.

Recommended tactics were to keep asari Vanguards at a distance and kill them at long range as they advanced, since they were extremely dangerous in close combat on higher difficulties; human Vanguards' Vindicators could likewise kill Shepard or squadmates quickly. Warp, Concussive Shot, Reave, assault rifles and submachine guns were effective against their barriers. Combat Drones distracted asari Vanguards only briefly before being destroyed by shotgun fire, and Flashbang Grenades could disable their biotic attacks. Asari Vanguards called out before using Warp with lines such as "Weakening their integrity!", "Deploying Warp field!" or "Mind over matter!" Because they were among the most dangerous Eclipse units in any group, players were advised to concentrate fire on advancing Vanguards and to knock them down or disable them before they could restore their barriers with Tech Armor.
