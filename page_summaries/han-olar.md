Han Olar was a volus scientist at Peak 15's Rift Station on Noveria, found in the scientists' quarters. He was the sole survivor of the rachni attack in the hot labs and, as Captain Ventralis put it, "ain't all there any more"; Dr. Palon asked Shepard to be considerate of him. Severely traumatized, Han answered questions monotonously. He told Shepard's squad that Binary Helix had recovered an ancient egg from a derelict vessel drifting in space, from which a Rachni Queen hatched, a project he now believed might have been a mistake. When other scientists told him to stop discussing confidential matters, he replied he no longer cared about Binary Helix's secrets.

Han revealed he had survived by reaching the tram tunnels and closing the train door behind him while his colleague, a scientist named Dr. Zhonmua, was killed by the rachni; he described her death without emotion and said flatly, "I killed her." If Shepard made the biotoxin cure and was ambushed by Alestia Iallis, Han pointed the squad to the maintenance area the geth had come from and suggested asking Dr. Zev Cohen for a pass. If the hot labs were neutron-purged first, Han was found being threatened by Matriarch Benezia's asari commandos; afterward he told Shepard he was the only one left, twice over, and believed Binary Helix wanted to bury its rachni mistake and everyone involved, claiming Benezia had ordered Ventralis to lure Shepard's team to the hot labs expecting the rachni to kill them. He said he only wanted off the mountain.

If Shepard met him in 2183, Han later sent a bleak message titled "Therapy" in 2185, questioning whether Shepard was truly alive or whether he himself had died holding that door open on Peak 15, closing: "But if I'm not, then thank you."
