Citadel: Leviathan III was a mission of the Leviathan DLC for Mass Effect 3, acquired automatically on completing Namakli: Leviathan. As an investigation stage rather than a combat mission, it took place entirely in Dr. Bryson's lab on the Citadel. Commander Shepard met a grieving Ann Bryson upstairs and, after conversation, spoke with her again downstairs and followed her around the lab as she reminisced about her father and related the research they had found.

Near Dr. Bryson's husk-head experiment, Shepard could speak repeatedly with James Vega until he asked whether he could take the experiment; Shepard then chose whether to have it delivered to the Normandy's cabin. Ann eventually concluded that Leviathan could be tracked through the signal it sent via its artifacts to enthrall people, and volunteered to let Leviathan take control of her while EDI traced the signal. During the trace, two Renegade interrupts were available to prolong Ann's exposure and narrow the search for Leviathan's exact location. The first interrupt could be taken with no consequences, but the second incapacitated Ann and prevented her from becoming a War Asset. Neither interrupt was required to proceed to the next mission, Leviathan: Scan Locations.
