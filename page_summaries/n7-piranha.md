The N7 Piranha was a shotgun appearing in Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Andromeda. It was an assault shotgun designed for the Reaper War: when the N7 program began training alien resistance forces, lighter-bodied species wanted a low-recoil weapon with a wide pellet spread for dealing with hordes of husks, and the Piranha was the result, offering a strong sweet spot in close-range firepower with rapid-fire capability that tore apart husks and most other opponents.

In Mass Effect 3 it was a fully automatic shotgun with the same rate of fire whether the trigger was held or pumped, an eight-round magazine, and a large reserve, essentially a heavier-hitting M-27 Scimitar weighing as much as the M-11 Wraith or M-96 Mattock. Its weaknesses were relatively low damage per shot for a shotgun and a wide pellet spread that nearly required the Shotgun Smart Choke mod to be effective beyond point-blank range. Like conventional shotguns it excelled against shielded infantry but struggled against armor without appropriate mods, ammo, or armor-weakening powers. The Marksman power sharply tightened its spread and worked well with its large magazine. It was acquired in single-player via the Groundside Resistance Pack (or Spectre Requisitions for 125,000 credits in the Legendary Edition) and was a rare multiplayer weapon requiring the Earth DLC.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda it was an automatic assault shotgun, devastating at short range but losing accuracy with distance. In Milky Way lore it was a "proof of concept" prototype by the Bradford Engineering Group; the Andromeda Initiative procured its Piranhas after the company's military clients deferred on full production. Post-launch patches improved per-pellet and overall damage, max ammo, and projectile force impact.
