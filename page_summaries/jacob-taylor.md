Jacob Taylor, born 2157, was a human biotic and former Alliance Marine who served under Major Derek Izunami and once belonged to the Alliance-sponsored Corsairs. Present for the geth attack on Eden Prime and later the attack on the Citadel, he grew disillusioned with political bureaucracy and quit the Alliance after five years. He was a romance option for a female Shepard.

In Mass Effect: Galaxy, Jacob foiled a batarian terrorist takeover of the liner Arcturian Jade, then was sent by Izunami to the Nemean Abyss, where he met Miranda Lawson. Together they uncovered a plot by batarian extremists, led by Ambassador Jath'Amon, to unleash a plague biological weapon on the Council. Jacob confronted Jath'Amon during his audience with the Council; the ambassador released the plague and was taken into custody, while the asari scientist Batha began work on a cure. In Mass Effect: Foundation, roughly a month after the geth attack, a drinking Jacob was recruited by Miranda for a Cerberus job: recovering Commander Shepard's body. Traveling the Terminus Systems with Miranda, he wrestled with Cerberus' ethics, questioned its accountability, rescued enslaved humans and the beggar girl Lakshmi and her family, and confirmed Shepard's body had left the planet.

Jacob enlisted with Cerberus and became Miranda's lieutenant in the Lazarus Cell, running security on the Lazarus Research Station during the two-year project to resurrect Shepard; he and Miranda had a brief relationship that did not work out. In 2185, when the scientist Wilson betrayed the project and turned the security mechs on the staff, Jacob fought alongside the newly revived Shepard to escape; he and Miranda were the cell's only survivors. He joined the Normandy SR-2 as Armory Officer and a calm voice of reason. His loyalty mission, Jacob: The Gift of Greatness, concerned a distress signal from the MSV Hugo Gernsback, on which his father Ronald Taylor had served, missing ten years. In a romance, Jacob eventually confessed his love before the Omega 4 Relay.

If Jacob survived the Suicide Mission, he left Cerberus, spent time meditating in the Mediterranean, and met Dr. Brynn Cole through a Cerberus Mumbai cell. A year later he helped Cole protect a group of disaffected Cerberus scientists, sheltering on Gellix, where a relationship formed. In Mass Effect 3, if Shepard assists, Jacob is found wounded holding off a Cerberus assault; a romanced female Shepard can confront him about Brynn, but he defends himself and remains with the scientists, later aiding the Crucible project. He emails Shepard from Huerta Memorial Hospital to reveal Brynn is pregnant with a daughter, whom she wants to name "Shepard." In the Citadel DLC, if rescued from Gellix, Jacob assists in the Armax Arena, brings kids to the Castle Arcade, and can attend Shepard's party, where he discusses his plan to propose to Brynn. Jacob was written mainly by Lukas Kristjanson (after Chris L'Etoile's early drafts). Mass Effect: Galaxy was originally titled "Jacob's Story."
