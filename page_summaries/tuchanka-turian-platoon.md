"Tuchanka: Turian Platoon" was a Mass Effect 3 mission acquired immediately after Priority: Sur'Kesh, when Primarch Adrien Victus quietly told Shepard that a turian ship on a classified mission had crashed on Tuchanka and asked for help rescuing its platoon, away from the ears of the nearby Urdnot leader. The mission remained available until Priority: Thessia, after which it was considered failed.

Landing at the Downed Vessel site in the Krogan DMZ, Shepard made contact with Lieutenant Tarquin Victus, the Primarch's son, who was under Reaper attack and marked his position with a flare. The squad fought through rubble-choked terrain against Husks, Cannibals, Marauders, a Brute and repeated Harvester attacks, working past several crashed turian escape pods and driving off Harvesters before they could destroy the pods. The final battle in an open area pitted the squad against waves of Cannibals and Marauders, a Brute, and a Harvester serving as the boss; a Reaper Blackstar heavy weapon could be found nearby to bring it down.

After the fight Shepard spoke with a demoralized Tarquin Victus and his troops, choosing an empathetic or demanding tone for morality points, and received the timed follow-up mission "Tuchanka: Bomb." In the War Room Shepard debriefed with Admiral Hackett and spoke again with Primarch Victus. Depending on mission order, Garrus and Liara could be found reminiscing in the Port Observation Lounge with Garrus voicing concern over the Victus situation, or Tali could be found in Engineering discussing with EDI whether her morale, like Tarquin's, suffered from living in the shadow of a famous parent. If completed after Priority: Tuchanka, the Primarch emailed his commendation and asked Shepard to help his son stop the Kelphic Valley bomb.
