Scions were a type of Husk created by the Collectors from three human Husks fused together and grafted around a single mass effect/biotic weapon on the left arm. The blue "sacks" on their backs and heads contained redundant organs, brain matter, spinal tissue, and element zero sources that powered their weapons and shockwaves. Posthumous examination showed a human- or asari-like frame and skull overlaid with metallic resin, a husk-like skin tone, nano-scale cybernetic modification allowing operation in hard vacuum, and hoses replacing veins and muscle. Because the nervous system was decentralized across several victims—one primary providing the frame, several "secondaries" the flesh—Scions were highly resistant to gunfire, and even a headshot was not a certain kill. The dust-form eezo nodules that let them produce warp effects were thought to be deposited during transformation rather than requiring a biotic victim.

In Mass Effect 2 Scions operated alone or in pairs, hanging back behind rushing Husks and Abominations as slow-moving ranged fire support. Their shoulder-mounted cannon fired a long-range version of Shockwave that penetrated cover, stunned targets, dealt heavy damage, and left a bluish residue that delayed friendly shield recharge; at close range they released a deadly area burst. They were heavily armored with no barriers, shields, or health, immune to most disabling powers except Stasis. Weak spots included the head on the right shoulder, the hump on the back, and a bright blue circle on the back. In Mass Effect 3 they appeared in the Retaliation multiplayer pack and the Armax Arsenal Arena, firing explosive salvos of three with a large blast radius, using a one-hit-kill melee execution, and, like other Collector units, able to be possessed to become far more powerful. Bursting the shoulder sacs caused a large damage spike. In horticulture, "scion" refers to a method of grafting plant tissues.
