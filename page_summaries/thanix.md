The Thanix series was a line of turian-designed weapons used primarily in ship-to-ship combat. Its origin lay in the aftermath of the Battle of the Citadel in 2183, when human and turian volunteers spent three months clearing debris from the station's orbit. During the cleanup the turians secretly salvaged Sovereign's main gun along with much of its element zero core. Eleven months later they introduced the Thanix, a scaled-down version of the Reaper weapon.

The Thanix's core is a liquid alloy of iron, uranium and tungsten suspended in an element-zero-powered electromagnetic field. The molten metal is accelerated to a significant fraction of light speed and solidifies into a projectile as it is fired, striking with enough force to pierce any known shield or armor. It can fire reliably every five seconds (the Mass Effect 2 codex voice-over says fifteen). Its relatively small size lets it be mounted on most fighters or frigates, including the Normandy SR-2, giving them firepower rivaling a cruiser.

In Mass Effect 2 the Normandy SR-2 could be upgraded with a pair of Thanix Cannons; with the upgrade, the Normandy could destroy the Collector Cruiser during the Suicide Mission with minimal damage. By the Reaper invasion of 2186, the Thanix and its variants had seen widespread use across Alliance fleets and beyond, with an up-scaled version built for dreadnoughts. A ground-based Thanix missile, based on similar principles, was used during the final battle for Earth.
