X1 Wing was a mission in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, set aboard the Cerberus facility known as the Barn. After the volus hacker unlocked strategic cells as a distraction, freed asari and krogan test subjects rebelled against the station's Cerberus employees and troopers. Randall Ezno used the chaos to fight through the X1 Wing toward the communication tower to contact the Alliance, while the Director taunted him over holoscreens about Inali's fate and her growing addiction to the experiments; Randall refused to believe him and pressed on.

The mission ran across six checkpoints. Early checkpoints pitted Randall against corridors of enraged Krogan Prisoners who charged him, and rooms of Asari Prisoners armed with assault rifles fighting Cerberus engineers and troopers. Biotic Pull was repeatedly recommended to halt krogan charges, and Tactical Cloak to reposition safely. Middle checkpoints added Cerberus reinforcement squads of Troopers, Riot Troopers, Centurions and Engineers, plus Grad Turrets in a war-zone room. Checkpoint 5 took place in a dark room where pressing a button in a central cell activated three encircling Grad Turrets, followed by three waves of mixed Cerberus troops. The final checkpoint resembled the first, with Krogan Prisoners (some carrying M-333 Particle Beams) and Orcus Mechs fighting each other. Completing each checkpoint quickly, avoiding all charges and damage, and switching weapons after each kill earned the "Veteran" medal. Afterward Randall was taunted again by the Director and moved on toward the X1 chamber. The next mission was Colosseum.
