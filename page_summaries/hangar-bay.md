Hangar Bay was a mission in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, following the Ice Giant mission and preceding Medical Bay. After his mission on the Ice Giant, Cerberus operative Randall Ezno returned by shuttle to the Cerberus station known as the Barn, a facility dedicated to weapons and biotics advancement through adapted Reaper technology and alien experimentation. The mission had two checkpoints.

In the first checkpoint, Randall opened a radio communication with Inali Renata and moved through the Hangar Bay toward her office for a medical diagnostic. The hangar was then attacked by Turian Troopers, presumably dispatched by the Turian Hierarchy. With the aid of Cerberus troops and friendly Orcus Mechs, Randall cleared the hangar; side walls held consoles and wall safes yielding 50 credits each. In the second checkpoint, Inali contacted Randall again but the communication was cut, prompting him to run for her office. More turian troops, including Turian Snipers, attacked. UDI, the Barn's AI, opened a door after ten seconds to a corridor full of turians. After receiving his Combat Analysis, Randall gained access to the station's Medical Bay and moved on to try to meet Inali. Enemies encountered were Turian Troopers and Turian Snipers.
