Urdnot Wrex was a famed krogan mercenary, bounty hunter, and one of the last Krogan Battlemasters, combining biotics with heavy weapons. Born into Clan Urdnot, he killed a thresher maw during his Rite of Passage and became the youngest krogan in a thousand years to lead an Urdnot tribe. After the Krogan Rebellions and the genophage, most krogan wanted to keep fighting, a view championed by Wrex's father, the warlord Jarrod. Wrex instead urged the krogan to focus on breeding. Jarrod called a Crush in the holy Hollows and ambushed Wrex's warriors; Wrex escaped, sinking his knife into Jarrod's chest, and afterward turned his back on the krogan, spending three centuries as a bodyguard, soldier of fortune, and bounty hunter. He once worked with a merc named Aleena and was briefly hired by Saren Arterius to raid a volus cargo ship without knowing who Saren was.

In Mass Effect, Wrex joined Commander Shepard on the Citadel, initially hired by the Shadow Broker to kill Fist, whom he executed. He hunted his stolen family armor, held by the turian profiteer Tonn Actus. On Virmire, learning the Special Tasks Group planned to destroy Saren's krogan-breeding facility and its possible genophage cure, Wrex confronted Shepard; he could be talked down (helped by recovering his armor) or be shot dead by Shepard or Ashley Williams.

If Wrex survived, by 2185 he was leader of Clan Urdnot, uniting clans on Tuchanka, protecting fertile females in exchange for loyalty, and facing opposition from Gatatog Uvenk. He welcomed Grunt into the clan and declined to rejoin the Normandy. In Mass Effect 3 (2186), Wrex demanded a genophage cure as the price of a krogan alliance against the Reapers, revealed the surviving fertile female "Eve" held on Sur'Kesh, and joined the mission to rescue her. His trust in Shepard depended on whether the Commander had preserved Maelon's cure data. On Tuchanka he led the clans against a Reaper Destroyer guarding the Shroud; Wreav's vehicle was eaten by Kalros. Wrex named Shepard a brother/sister and champion of the krogan, charging Ravagers while screaming "I AM URDNOT WREX, AND THIS IS MY PLANET!" After the cure, he pledged krogan forces to Palaven and Earth and, at the final battle in London, revealed Eve was pregnant and would name the first child "Mordin."

If Shepard let the salarian sabotage stand, Wrex discovered the fake cure and confronted the Commander on the Citadel with a smuggled shotgun; he was killed by Shepard or by Commander Bailey, dying with "I know... what you... did... Shepard," and the clans withdrew all support. In the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, a living Wrex helped foil the clone conspiracy, body-slamming an ambushing shuttle, remarking "Why shoot something once when you can shoot it 46 more times?", and complained of being hounded by every fertile female on Tuchanka to sire their firstborn. Wrex was voiced by Steven Barr, and Lukas Kristjanson wrote him in the first game.
