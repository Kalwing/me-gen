CAT6 was a private military corporation whose name came from "Category 6", the term for soldiers dishonorably discharged from the Systems Alliance. Its mercenaries were known for extensive criminal records and histories of steroid abuse, and they named their squads after sharp objects, such as Rapier, Hatchet and Dagger squads, with an elite contingent called Razor squad.

In 2186, during the events of the Mass Effect 3 Citadel DLC, CAT6 was hired by a Mysterious Figure to assassinate Commander Shepard. They first ambushed Shepard and Joker at Ryuusei's Sushi Bar on the Silversun Strip, then helped the Figure break into the Citadel archives. The Mysterious Figure was revealed to be a clone of Shepard, who hijacked the Normandy SR-2 with an army of CAT6 troops and ordered the mercenaries to "slow down" the original Shepard after Shepard's boarding party sneaked aboard; one merc was overheard questioning whether they were even allowed to kill Shepard, and another noted the clone treated them as cannon fodder. In the final confrontation in the Normandy's cargo hold, every surviving mercenary tried to support the clone. None of the CAT6 members who fought Shepard survived except the one piloting the Normandy, who was subdued by security drones directed by EDI.

CAT6 forces fielded Atlas mechs, CAT6 Heavies, CAT6 Snipers, CAT6 Specialists and Disruption Drones. Despite being introduced in the same DLC as the Armax Arsenal Arena, CAT6 were among the only enemy types that could not be fought in the combat simulator.
