Nova was a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes, available to the Vanguard class (and the Human Vanguard in multiplayer). It transferred the energy of the user's biotic barrier into a close-range explosive blast, with barrier strength determining the blast's intensity; using it consumed the remaining barrier. Base statistics were 350 damage, 450 N force, and a 4 m radius, improvable through six ranks that increased radius, damage and force, added a power-recharge buff or a reduced-consumption "Half Blast" option, and finally offered Pierce (double damage to barriers, shields, and armor) or Sustain (25% chance not to consume barriers).

Nova dealt 1.5x damage to biotic barriers by default (3x with Pierce). It had no cooldown and could be used whenever barrier remained, penetrated cover and terrain like Shockwave, worked vertically as well as horizontally, and hit a maximum of three targets. During its animation the player was invulnerable to almost all damage, which could be used to buy time for Biotic Charge to cool down; the animation could also be dodge-cancelled with precise timing. Nova could detonate all four types of power combos. Its damage scaled with the percentage of shield remaining rather than raw shield strength, so shield-boosting effects did not increase it. Charge and Nova were considered the Vanguard's signature paired moves, with Nova commonly following a Charge since the player was nearly guaranteed to have shields for it.
