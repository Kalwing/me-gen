This page contained under ~150 words of real prose and covered cut, non-canon content. Batarian State Arms (BSA) was a hardware manufacturing company. Because the batarian government did not trust private industry to build its military hardware, BSA existed as a vast nationalized institution infamous for waste and corruption. It produced effective if unethical chemical weapons and a line of Nuclear, Chemical, and Biological (NBC) protective armor. In the first Mass Effect it was a hidden manufacturer whose equipment was fully functional in the game files but only reachable via the PC console or third-party software. Its lines included the Terminator assault rifles (sometimes accessible through the Bring Down the Sky PC DLC), the Executioner shotguns, the Judgement pistols, the Lightning Strike sniper rifles, and Partisan/Skirmish light, medium, and heavy armor. A separate Batarian State Arms shop appeared on the Citadel in Mass Effect 3.
