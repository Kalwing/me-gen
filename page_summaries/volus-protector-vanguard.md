The Volus Protector Vanguard was a playable class in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer. Its background repeated that the volus homeworld Irune had a high-pressure atmosphere supporting an ammonia-based biochemistry, requiring volus to wear pressure suits and breathers or specially sealed combat armor.

The class had 500 barrier and 150 health under Volus Training, with the powers Biotic Charge, Biotic Orbs, Shield Boost, and Fitness. Its regular melee triggered a tactical cloak; its heavy melee (Defensive Stance) rooted the character and generated a damaging protective shield sphere that pulsed brief invulnerability and restored some shields. It could not grab, could not take formal cover (only "soft cover"), had reduced visibility from its low height, could dodge with a combat roll, and could not be grabbed by Husks or Abominations.

With Biotic Orbs evolved for recharge speed and orb count, the Protector could use Biotic Charge more frequently than a Human Vanguard under Nova's power recharge evolution, letting it travel the battlefield safely, protect the squad by charging and then following up with Shield Boost, and effectively "tank" bulky enemies such as Banshees and Atlases, though those enemies had instant-kill grabs. Its tactical cloak enabled "stealthy" charges for escaping tight situations or surprise attacks and for slipping behind Guardians. Biotic Charge and Biotic Orbs together could detonate biotic explosions set by teammates in quick succession, and Charge helped compensate for the volus inability to vault by reaching enemies behind low platforms. The class was excellent at supporting close-range allies, using Charge to keep up with faster characters and its low height and cloak to reach optimal Shield Boost positions. As with all Vanguards, charging Phantoms, Atlases, Praetorians, Brutes, and Banshees risked instant-kill attacks, and charging Ravagers with open sacs was inadvisable.
