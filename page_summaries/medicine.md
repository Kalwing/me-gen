Medicine was an unlockable tech talent in Mass Effect, available to Engineers and Sentinels but not the Quarian Machinist class. It lowered the cooldown of First Aid (by 10% at rank 2 up to 30% at rank 11) and unlocked the Neural Shock ability at ranks 1, 7 and 12 (Neural Shock, Advanced Neural Shock, Master Neural Shock).

Neural Shock induced paralysis and dealt toxic damage, working only on organic foes including humanoids, Husks, Thorian Creepers, rachni and varren. It had a fixed 45-second recharge and 60% accuracy cost at all ranks, doing 40/80/120 toxic damage and knocking out enemies for 1/3/5 seconds. It could drop any organic enemy instantly, making it useful against charging krogan, and its toxic damage suppressed enemy health regeneration. It had no area of effect and no proximity mine, requiring precise aim. Some enemies also possessed Neural Shock and could use it to disable Shepard or squadmates without landing a projectile.
