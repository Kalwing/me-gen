Multiplayer map page. Firebase Vancouver is a Mass Effect 3 multiplayer map included in the Mass Effect 3: Earth DLC, set on rooftops in Vancouver. Its areas are the Helipad (starting location and a possible extraction zone), Lower Rooftops, Upper Rooftops (lower level a possible extraction zone), Offices (a medical wing), Ventilation Deck, and Ventilation Housing. Like Firebase Dagger, it has two possible extraction locations, close together. The Ventilation Deck is difficult to navigate for classes that cannot climb cover, and area-of-effect powers can singlehandedly halt massed infantry at chokepoints such as the ladder by the Offices.
