This page is an index and overview of content that was reworked, abridged, or cut from the first Mass Effect before release, reconstructed from leftover audio, text, and other files. It is explicitly non-canon.

Many missions lost additional challenges or larger areas. The main mission Find Liara T'Soni originally took place on an urban hub world called Caleston, starting in the Throw Down mining station, with dozens of characters and interlinked assignments (later renamed Therum). A trio of non-mandatory "Global Plots" were abandoned entirely: chasing biotic human test subjects, dealing with an AI threatening the galactic economy, and a conflict between the hanar and the lystheni. An additional hub world called Misery was cut, along with assignments about mapping worlds, thresher maw hunting, and recovering evidence. Planned DLC focused on Mars and Cerberus (including "Cerberus Brainchild," which would have introduced the Illusive Man, and "Evil Corp" targeting Jormangund Technology) was scrapped in favor of Bring Down the Sky and Pinnacle Station.

Other cut material included: a Normandy workbench crafting mechanic using omni-gel; an interactable garage crewman, Ian Ferguson; a Citadel combat simulator in what became C-Sec academy; numerous cut Citadel assignments; extra objectives on Feros, Noveria, and Virmire; an Ilos section about restoring power to the ancient complex; and a different finale in which Kirrahe assists Shepard, Sovereign speaks to Shepard one last time, and Anderson, Shepard and Pallin have an altered reunion. Casey Hudson's 2003 pitch (codenamed SFX) included procedurally generated planets, an upgradeable ship with resource collection, and an online component. Also cut were Mako vehicle modifications, a dialogue "interrupt" button seen in the X06 2006 trailer, a "Movie Mode" dialogue option, six Codex entries, several weapon and armor manufacturers, four training skills, and two talents (Rout and Camouflage). Some elements later resurfaced in Mass Effect 2, 3, and Andromeda.
