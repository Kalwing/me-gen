This page catalogs single-use equipment items used during Mass Effect 3 multiplayer matches, each lasting one mission and grouped into ammo, armor and weapon bonuses. Ammo bonuses added a separate "ammo damage" component to shots, heavily modified by the target's current defense type. They included Armor-Piercing Rounds (damage and penetration versus armor, no bonus to shields or barriers), Cryo Rounds (slows, weakens armor, can freeze unshielded enemies to prime Cryo Explosions), Disruptor Rounds (4x damage to shields and barriers, primes Tech Bursts), Drill Rounds (long-range penetration through cover; from Retaliation), Explosive Rounds (delayed detonation; from Retaliation), Incendiary Rounds (damage-over-time, primes Fire Explosions, stops enemy health regeneration), Phasic Rounds (10x versus shields and barriers; from Retaliation), and Warp Rounds (armor weakening, bonus biotic damage against Biotic Explosion-primed targets, 2x versus barriers).

Armor bonuses covered the Adrenaline Module (movement speed), Cyclonic Modulator (shield strength up to +150%), Power Amplifier Module (power damage), Power Efficiency Module (recharge speed), Shield Power Cells (shorter shield recharge delay), and Stabilization Module (weapon stability). Weapon bonuses were the per-class Rail Amps (assault rifle, pistol, shotgun, SMG, sniper rifle; +10/20/30% damage), the Strength Enhancer (melee damage), and Targeting VI (headshot damage). Several items became available in Store packs only after downloading the Resurgence Pack, and drill, explosive and phasic rounds only after the Retaliation Pack. Each item had three or four escalating levels.
