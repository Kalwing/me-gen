Citadel: Cerberus Ciphers was a Mass Effect 3 mission with prerequisites Priority: The Citadel II and N7: Communication Hub. Shepard could overhear turian Officer Dellk at a terminal at the end of a corridor in the Citadel Embassies, past the Earth Councilor's office, saying he needed updated ciphers to break Cerberus codes; the map sometimes showed his location incorrectly, with reports placing his marker near the couch with the elcor ambassador. If Dellk was never encountered, the ciphers and journal entry could be acquired directly at Ontarom.

The Cerberus encryption algorithms were on a terminal during N7: Communication Hub, which became available only after Priority: Thessia, a gap of at least six Priority missions from acquisition to completion, the longest in the game. The terminal sat behind a tubular wall near a comm tower to the west of the mission's start. If not obtained there, the codes could be bought from Spectre Requisitions for 1,000 credits, or 900 at the maximum 10% discount. Returning the codes to Dellk completed the mission for 5 Reputation, 30 experience, 1,000 credits, and a small boost to the Alliance Fifth Fleet War Asset. A bug could cause the codes to be lost if Shepard died before completing the mission while the source terminal stayed deactivated, forcing purchase. Regardless of how the cipher was acquired, Shepard claimed during the handover that it had been found on a Cerberus engineer.
