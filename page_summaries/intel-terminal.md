The Intel Terminal was an upgrade terminal in Liara T'Soni's cabin aboard the Normandy SR-2 in Mass Effect 3. Intel items found during missions or through system scanning were stored there, each offering two possible upgrade paths that granted a permanent stat bonus, of which only one could be chosen and kept. Glyph alerted Shepard when new intel was available. All Search and Rescue intel upgrades became accessible by the completion of Priority: Thessia; others were found during missions or, in a few cases, unlocked by importing a save with certain conditions met. Intel upgrades did not carry over between playthroughs in New Game+ and had to be earned again.

Available intel included the Armor Modkit (obtained via Feron's contacts; ammo capacity or shield strength, requires Lair of the Shadow Broker), the Prejek Paddlefish (from an imported Mass Effect 2 save with the surviving fish and Kelly Chambers; weapon or power damage), the Weapon Upgrade Kit (scan Verush), Remains of Reaper Destroyer (scan Borr; store discount or power recharge), Advanced Biotic Implants (scan Metaponto), Improved Geth Resistors and Miniaturized Armor Capacitors (both from the Geth Consensus during Rannoch: Geth Fighter Squadrons), Intel from Battle of Arcturus (scan Arcturus Station), Gallae's Electronic Signature (from Kallini: Ardat-Yakshi Monastery), Intact Reaper Weapon (scan Trategos), Volus Intelligence Archives (scan Oma Ker), Research Data From Pragia (scan Pragia), and Med Bay Surgery (complete Citadel: Medical Supplies; heal scars or +5% health). Most bonuses were around +5%.
