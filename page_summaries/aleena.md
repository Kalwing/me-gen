Aleena was an asari commando who had a successful career as a mercenary and was a close friend of Urdnot Wrex. The two first met when they were hired to assassinate the same turian and split the reward, with Wrex taking the head and Aleena the rest. Their friendship was tested when Wrex accepted a new contract from a volus diplomat to kill someone from the volus's past who "knew too much," who turned out to be Aleena. They joked about it, but the contract still stood.

Out of mutual respect for each other's skills, Wrex and Aleena agreed to fight it out. Aleena chose an old salarian space station overrun with mercenaries and pirates so that no bystanders would be innocent. Wrex pursued her through the station for days, running out of ammunition and killing several mercenaries to take their weapons. He finally trapped Aleena in the medical bay while she tried to heal her wounds, but as he broke in he realized the station's core was about to go critical and barely escaped before the facility was vaporized. There seemed no way Aleena could have survived, yet Wrex later received a cheerful message from her reading "Better luck next time..."

When Shepard asked what Wrex told the volus diplomat, Wrex said he told the truth: that Aleena was alive and furious. He turned this to his advantage, noting he was the only one who could protect the volus from her, and was hired as a personal bodyguard on the spot. The arrangement lasted until the volus died of natural causes, which Wrex considered the easiest job he ever had. Producer Mike Gamble has stated that Aria T'Loak and Aleena are not the same asari.
