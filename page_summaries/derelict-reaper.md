The Derelict Reaper was a two-kilometer-long Reaper found orbiting the failed star Mnemosyne in the Thorne system of the Hawking Eta cluster in Mass Effect 2, accessible after the Collector Ship mission. It gave off only faint, localized power signatures far weaker than its size implied, maintained a mass effect field that kept it from falling into the failed star, and had massive unrepaired holes blasted and melted through its hull, indicating it had "died" or been reduced to minimal function long ago.

Approximately 37 million years earlier, an unknown spacefaring race fired a mass accelerator round of extraordinary speed and power that struck and disabled the Reaper; the round continued on and impacted the planet Klendagon, creating its Great Rift Valley. This was likely the unknown race's final act of defiance before their extinction. Despite the damage, the Reaper's indoctrination mechanism remained active. Cerberus discovered the Reaper and sent a research team led by Dr. Chandana to study it; the team was indoctrinated and converted into husks.

The Illusive Man sent Commander Shepard to investigate the loss of contact with Chandana's team and to recover the Reaper's Identify Friend/Foe (IFF) tag, needed to safely use the Omega 4 Relay (the Reaper IFF mission). When Shepard's squad boarded, the ship's kinetic barriers engaged and trapped them; disabling them required destroying the ship's core, which also released the mass effect field. Fighting through husks, Shepard retrieved the IFF and reached the core, where the squad found a lone geth that was overwhelmed by husks. Shepard destroyed the core and escaped with the inactive geth just before the Reaper fell into Mnemosyne's atmosphere and was destroyed. A Cerberus log described the Reaper as "a dead god who still dreams."
