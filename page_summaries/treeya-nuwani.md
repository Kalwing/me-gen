Treeya Nuwani was an asari anthropologist and archaeologist living on the human colony Fehl Prime, featured in Mass Effect: Paragon Lost. She was a peaceful person who hated warfare, absorbed in her research and oblivious to Lieutenant James Vega's romantic interest in her. She resented the Alliance's interference with her work and was initially frosty toward Delta Squad.

During the Blood Pack's 2183 attack on the colony, Treeya stayed at the side of her close friend Christine, a civilian who fought bravely and was grazed in the shoulder; Vega and Delta Squad drove off the mercenaries and the colony survived. Two years later, with new defenses being installed amid a wave of Terminus colony disappearances, Treeya accompanied Vega's squad to some ruins where something was jamming long-range communications, traveling in a civilian named Messner's secondhand M35 Mako. She was distraught when the squad decided to destroy the source artifact and nearly fell into an abyss rushing at it. Afterward she used ancient technology in the ruins to contact her mentor, Liara T'Soni, about a fragment of the artifact; Liara suggested a Reaper connection. The conversation was cut short when the Collectors arrived to abduct the colonists.

Treeya survived a stealth reconnaissance through the occupied colony with Vega, was horrified to find the colonists paralyzed, and was restrained by Vega from trying to stop the Collectors taking Christine and April. She helped tend the wounded Essex, and after Messner was revealed as a Cerberus operative who had planted the artifact to lure the Collectors, she was appalled. During time in an underground lab she and Vega bonded as he doubted his decisions, and she reassured him. Nicky found a cure for the Collector paralysis venom but was fatally wounded by a Praetorian and died in Treeya's arms. Fleeing aboard Brood's ship, Treeya got kills with a flamethrower during the interdiction fight, then Messner shot Brood and the ship crashed inside the Collector vessel. Messner forced Treeya to interface with the Collector databanks; she witnessed the Protheans' defeat and conversion into Collectors and learned the location of the Collector base. When the Collectors branded Messner for "ascension," he shoved Treeya into the pod meant for him, but she stole his bracelet containing all the gathered intel before her pod was ejected into space. Descending toward the planet, she urged Vega over the comm to leave her and save the colonists. Vega instead chose to save her and the intel. Bruised and limping, she attended his debriefing on the Citadel, embarrassed to have survived while thousands of colonists died, and later comforted him on Fehl Prime as they saluted the Memorial Wall.
