The Horse Head Nebula was a star cluster of mostly uninhabited, barely-surveyed planets with modest resourcing prospects. Though within Earth Systems Alliance space and near Citadel space, it sat in a legal grey area for Citadel races; Noveria, one of its worlds, was not part of Citadel space. Cerberus maintained major operations in the cluster: Minuteman Station was located there in 2185, and by 2186 the organization's headquarters (Cronos Station) and at least one outpost lay in out-of-the-way positions within the nebula. Its systems included Pax (containing Noveria), Anadius (containing Cronos Station), Fortuna, Strenuus and Maganlis. Missions set here included the Noveria arc in Mass Effect and Priority: Cerberus Headquarters and N7: Cerberus Fighter Base in Mass Effect 3. The real-world nebula is Barnard 33 in IC 434, first identified by Williamina Fleming in 1888; Mass Effect 3 renders the name "Horsehead Nebula" without the space. After acquiring the Normandy SR-2, the Minuteman Station and its surrounding cluster became inaccessible.
