Quoyle was a drell living on Kahje, appearing in Mass Effect: Homeworlds. When Liara T'Soni came to Kahje to examine a submerged Prothean ruin used as a hanar shrine and was blocked by a hanar official, Quoyle intervened, suggesting that Liara's generosity, shown by a sizable contribution to the hanar's upcoming Cresting Bloom celebrations, should earn her a brief visit to the shrine. With the official satisfied, Quoyle personally escorted Liara to the shrine, while warning that he would shoot her if she attempted any mischief.

When the shrine's defenses suddenly attacked Quoyle's craft, he managed to guide it to the dock while Liara used her biotics to keep it from flooding. Inside the shrine they found a Cerberus Phantom who had killed the shrine's staff. Quoyle was slashed by the Phantom's blade and stayed behind while Liara examined the Prothean archives and confronted the Illusive Man. Afterward Quoyle bandaged his wound and was helped out of the shrine.
