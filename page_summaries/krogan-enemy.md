Krogan were common soldier enemies seen across the galaxy in the first Mass Effect game, used as shock troops by mercenary bands and pirate groups for their ability to charge, deal heavy weapons fire, and inflict powerful melee attacks. Saren, having found a genophage cure, also bred an army of krogan to supplement his geth, so krogan and geth were usually encountered together in his forces.

In combat these krogan wielded either an assault rifle and shotgun or a sniper rifle and pistol, the latter limited to Inamorda's bodyguards; assault krogan closed in while snipers held back for Assassination shots, and despite rushing they had no true melee attacks. Every krogan enemy regenerated once before dying unless killed in the air or by cold, incendiary, toxic or high-explosive rounds. They were dangerous on all difficulties and especially on Insanity, where most had Immunity and could one-shot the player with melee. Recommended tactics were keeping them airborne or staggered with biotics, Neural Shock or high-force weapons, using rapid-fire toxic damage or Warp to deny regeneration, and in open areas levitating and ejecting them from the map for instant kills.
