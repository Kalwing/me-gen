Mass Effect: Foundation 4, tagged "Revolt at Jump Zero!," was published October 23, 2013, written by Mac Walters with art by Tony Parker. It depicts a young Kaidan Alenko's biotic training. In 2168, Kaidan was a student at Jump Zero (Gagarin Station, nicknamed "Brain Camp") in the Biotic Acclimation and Temperance Training (BAaT) program, where students were barred from outside contact. A peer, Shelby, rigged a transmitter to send messages home to Earth; Kaidan brought his close friend Rahna to use it.

The next day the brutal turian instructor Commander Vyrnnus assembled the cadets, displayed Shelby bound to a post, and set them to biotically assembling building blocks for hours, forcing restarts on any mistake. Students collapsed from exhaustion. When Rahna faltered, Kaidan steadied her piece; Vyrnnus taunted her by floating a glass of water just out of reach, then biotically broke her arm when she reached for it. Kaidan flew into a biotic rage that knocked Vyrnnus down. In the ensuing biotic duel, Vyrnnus drew a knife and slashed Kaidan's side; Kaidan retaliated with a powerful blast and a jump-kick to the face that killed the commander. The horrified Rahna rejected Kaidan, terrified of him. During his expulsion from BAaT, Kaidan got a brief final goodbye, but Rahna simply waved and walked away. A month later, back on Earth, his father told him that even right choices carry consequences.

In the framing sequence, years later, Kai Leng visited Rasa's quarters after she missed a debrief. Rasa, having spent the night studying the dossiers of Shepard's crew, vented that Cerberus should focus on Shepard rather than the crew's personal issues. Leng dared her to raise it with the Illusive Man and left, reminding her not to miss the next debriefing; glancing at Kaidan's profile, she promised she would not.
