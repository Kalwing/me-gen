Equipment covered armor, weapons, upgrades, and related gear across the Mass Effect trilogy, most purchasable for credits from vendors. Legendary Edition folded all previously DLC-exclusive content into the base games and changed how some gear was obtained.

In the first Mass Effect, Shepard and squadmates used six equipment types: weapons, armor, biotic amps, omni-tools, ammo upgrades, and armor upgrades, rated in Roman numerals from I to X. Items were found in containers (some needing special skills), looted from enemies, or bought. Inventory was capped at 150 items (300 in Legendary Edition); surplus items could be converted to omni-gel (4 units each, max 999) or sold. Armor came in Light, Medium, and Heavy classes and four race-specific types (human/asari, krogan, quarian, turian), with three stats and some hidden manufacturer abilities such as Devlon Industries' hazard immunity and Sirta Foundation Phoenix armor's health regeneration. Weapons used mass accelerator technology with no ammunition but a heat/overheat mechanic, across assault rifles, sniper rifles, shotguns, and pistols; only the Soldier class trained in all types. Most weapons and armor accepted one modification slot, high-grade items two; Geth Armory weapons accepted no upgrades. Medi-gel and grenade capacity could be upgraded five times each from a base of five to a maximum of ten.

In Mass Effect 2, the thermal clip system replaced heat management: weapons needed detachable heat sinks scavenged from battlefields, and weapon categories expanded to six (adding submachine guns and heavy weapons, the latter using power cells and carried only by Shepard). Armor became piecewise-customizable from the default N7 Armor, and upgrades were bought at shops or researched aboard the Normandy SR-2.

In Mass Effect 3, thermal clips returned with a minor improvement allowing ammo pickups without reloading. Weapon use was no longer class-restricted; instead a weight system tied load to power cooldown rates, with Soldiers having the highest capacity and Adepts and Engineers the lowest. Weapon mods returned with two slots per weapon and a maximum level of 5. In Mass Effect: Infiltrator, Randall Ezno bought armor sets and four weapons (M-96 Mattock, M-22 Eviscerator, M-29 Incisor, M-333 Particle Beam) from an in-game store, while the turian protagonist had fixed gear.
