Kahje was the homeworld of the hanar, a water world with roughly 90% of its surface covered by a vast ocean called the Encompassing. Heat from its energetic white star kept the planet permanently wrapped in cloud, and its land consisted only of small islands. Its atmosphere, primarily oxygen, hydrogen, and nitrogen with traces of ammonia, was well suited to life but too humid for the drell, whom the hanar had evacuated there two centuries earlier from their dying homeworld; the drell lived in climate-controlled domed cities such as Cnidaria City, the only place they could avoid Kepral's Syndrome. Kahje was dotted with Prothean ruins that shaped hanar religion and society, the largest centered on the underwater volcano Mount Vassla, where winners of the Nyahir holiday's religious events were inscribed in bioluminescence for a year.

In Mass Effect: Homeworlds, Liara T'Soni traveled to Kahje's Prothean archives seeking a way to stop the Reapers and recovered encryption keys accessing archives on other worlds, including Mars. In 2186, during an investigation of an indoctrinated hanar diplomat named Zymandis, Commander Shepard's success or failure in stopping his sabotage of Kahje's automated planetary defenses determined whether the planet was overrun by the Reapers or saved along with the hanar and drell fleet. Kahje lay in Inner Council Space near Palaven and Irune and was the terminus of an Illium-Kahje shipping lane.
