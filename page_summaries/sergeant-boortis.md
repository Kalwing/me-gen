Sergeant Boortis was a human Blue Suns mercenary who, along with Captain Vorhess, hijacked the MSV Strontium Mule in Mass Effect 2. Greedy and treacherous, Boortis planned to betray Vorhess, compiling a list of crew members loyal to the captain whom he and his cohorts intended to kill so they could seize the ship's valuable cargo for themselves. He used Commander Shepard's arrival as a means to dispose of Vorhess, intending to ambush the weakened survivor on the ship's bridge. The ambush failed, and Boortis and all his fellow conspirators were killed by Commander Shepard.

Boortis was armed with an M-76 Revenant loaded with Disruptor Ammo, and had strong shields, armor, high health, and the Tech Armor ability. Because of his firepower at all ranges he was recommended as a priority target; the narrow corridors could be used to funnel him and his men into one-on-one fights, and the Arc Projector could take him down in a few shots while also hitting his squad.
