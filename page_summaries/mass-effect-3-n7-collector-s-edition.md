The Mass Effect 3 N7 Collector's Edition was a premium retail package offering exclusive content for Mass Effect 3. It included a 70-page hardcover art book with development-team illustrations, a limited edition copy of the comic Mass Effect: Invasion with unique cover art, a premium N7 fabric patch, an exclusive 4x6 lithographic print of the SR-2 Alliance Normandy, and a premium metal case with commemorative art of both male and female Commander Shepard. Digital bonuses were the N7 Collector's Edition Pack (four N7 weapons: N7 Eagle, N7 Hurricane, N7 Crusader, N7 Valiant; a robotic dog for the Normandy; new outfits for Ashley, Kaidan, James Vega and Liara T'Soni; an N7 Hoodie for Shepard; and one Online Pass), the Mass Effect 3: From Ashes mission and character, a free digital soundtrack, an Xbox LIVE Normandy avatar prop (Xbox 360 only), and a collection of forum and social badges, avatars and perks. Additional retailer-specific pre-order bonuses were also available.
