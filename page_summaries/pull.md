Pull was a biotic power in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes. It generated a mass-lowering field that levitated enemies into the air, rendering them helpless while they drifted slowly toward the caster so allies could finish them off. Cast by Shepard, it arced toward the crosshair; cast by a squad member, it traveled in a straight line and hit instantly.

In Mass Effect 2 it had a 3-second recharge for Shepard and 9 seconds for others, with duration rising from 5 to 9 seconds across ranks. Rank 4 evolved into Heavy Pull (12-second duration) or Pull Field (affecting all targets in a 3 m radius for 9 seconds). Any enemy lifted or ragdolled by biotics could not attack and took double weapon damage. On Hardcore and Insanity, all enemies had an extra protection layer that had to be stripped before Pull could lift them. Unprotected Husks died instantly when lifted. Large enemies such as Geth Primes and YMIR Mechs could be ragdolled but not lifted. Warp could detonate Pulled targets for large bonus damage. It was available to the Adept, Vanguard, Jack, Jacob Taylor, Samara and Morinth.

In Mass Effect 3 it had the fastest base recharge of any multiplayer power (4 seconds, reducible to as little as 0.77 seconds). Ranks improved recharge, duration and radius; Rank 5 offered Lift Damage or Expose (increasing all damage to lifted targets), and Rank 6 offered Double Pull (two projectiles seeking two targets) or a further recharge boost. Pull yanked shields from the hands of Cerberus Guardians and CAT6 Heavies; hitting Guardians from behind lifted them and made them permanently drop their shields. Double Pull could overload orange Cerberus energy walls. In single-player it was available to the Adept, Vanguard, Javik, Samara, Jack and Jacob Taylor, and in multiplayer to the Asari Justicar Adept, Drell Adept and Drell Vanguard.
