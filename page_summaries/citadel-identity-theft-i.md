Citadel: Identity Theft I was acquired immediately after Shepard escaped the ambush in Citadel Wards: Ambush. Back at the Personal Apartment, Brooks and the crew regrouped, and Shepard broke contact with Commander Bailey to avoid exposing him as a target. Brooks explained she had written a program to track officer IDs against fraud, and it had detected someone hacking Shepard's file and going through personal records and classified files; the recovered M-11 Suppressor pistol became the investigation's starting point. Liara examined the pistol and had Glyph compile data while Joker and the rest of the crew arrived.

The crew present always included James, EDI, and Cortez, with Javik, Garrus, Tali, Ashley or Kaidan, and Wrex (only if the genophage cure was genuine rather than faked) appearing if recruited. Several crew criticized the destruction of the sushi bar floor during the ambush, and Joker resented having been used as a decoy. After Shepard spoke to one crew member, a short timer passed and Liara reported her findings: a favor had traced the gun to casino owner Elijah Khan, suspected of using his money to smuggle weapons onto the Citadel, and she played an intercepted call between voice-modulated conspirators who were not Khan. The mercenaries were identified as CAT6, slang for Alliance dishonorable discharges.

The group planned a small, stealthy, no-gunplay infiltration of Khan's Silver Coast Casino during a charity event for war refugees that evening. Only Brooks could plausibly accompany Shepard and help disable the camera on the panic-room door, and Liara noted the event was black-tie. The mission led into Silver Coast Casino: Infiltration.
