The yahg were a sentient race of towering humanoids native to Parnack, known for their violent, aggressive nature. Consummate predators, they possessed unrivaled perceptiveness and mental adaptability. They evolved as Parnack's apex predator and had four pairs of eyes, each pair adapted to track and predict prey movement, giving them such acute reading of body language that it was nearly impossible to lie to a yahg. They had scaly, partly armored skin ranging from red to brown, triangular toothed mouths, two large horns, three-fingered hands and three-toed feet, and muscular bodies taller than a krogan. The Prothean Javik claimed the yahg were much smaller during his cycle 50,000 years earlier. One yahg lived at least sixty years as the Shadow Broker.

Parnack was discovered by Citadel Council surveyors in 2125 CE, when the yahg were a pre-spaceflight civilization with roughly 20th-century Earth technology. A Council delegation attempted diplomacy, but the yahg perceived the ambassadors' treating them as equals rather than subordinates as contempt and massacred the entire delegation. The Council severed contact and declared Parnack off-limits, maintaining that yahg aggression and obsession with control made them unfit for the galactic community.

Later the Shadow Broker's agents abducted a yahg for study, killing several before finding one willing to leave. After displays of intelligence rivaling the salarians, the Broker had it replace one of his operatives. Its violent nature became a problem and an execution order was issued, but the yahg killed the Shadow Broker mid-message, assumed the role itself, rescinded the order, and held the position for six decades, its agents often entering its office and never returning. In 2185 CE Liara T'Soni raided its base to rescue the drell Feron, and the yahg Broker was killed.

In Mass Effect 3, a salarian research facility on Sur'Kesh captured and experimented on several yahg, planning to secretly uplift them as deniable proxies as had been done with the krogan; during a Cerberus assault one broke containment and went on a rampage. Admiral Hackett noted the Reapers avoided Parnack because the yahg were pre-spaceflight, and it was speculated the yahg might dominate the next cycle, having nearly completed their first spaceship.
