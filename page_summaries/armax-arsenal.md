Armax Arsenal was the main supplier of elite turian military units. Its weapons and armor were high quality, high priced, and very difficult for most non-turians to acquire, though it also made a basic-grade omni-tool available to the general public. The company produced Predator L/M/H Armor and ran a popular simulated combat arena on the Citadel that broadcast matches across the galaxy. In Mass Effect, a license for Armax Arsenal gear could be purchased from Expat at the Citadel for 30,000 credits.

In Mass Effect, all Armax Arsenal weapons integrated phasic chambering technology for superior stats, and its lines included the Crossfire assault rifles, Avalanche shotguns, Brawler pistols, and Punisher sniper rifles. Its armor used woven armalite for outstanding shield performance and higher tech and biotic resistance than most competitors. In Mass Effect 2 the company manufactured the ML-77 Missile Launcher, a rapid-fire launcher with friend-or-foe seeking projectiles popular with the Blue Suns and protected by Fabrication Rights Management technology; it also produced three N7 armor pieces, the Capacitor Chestplate, Shield Harness, and Heavy Damping Gauntlets, all focused on kinetic barriers. In Mass Effect 3 it made customizable armor components and the Gemini biotic amp. Additional products included the C77 Tyrus, a 13-ton infantry fighting vehicle with an eezo core, incendiary grenades sold in bulk, and mass-produced uranium cores made in zero-g labs. Lemm'Shal nar Tesleya carried a heavily modified Armax Arsenal shotgun on his Pilgrimage.
