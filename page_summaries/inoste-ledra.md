Inoste Ledra, full name Gorot II Heranon Mal Dinest Got Inoste Ledra, was a salarian merchant at Zhu's Hope on Feros. He had come to the human colony hoping to make a profit but was caught up in the geth attacks. He could sell Commander Shepard supplies, including a license for goods from Haliat Armory, and, like the other colonists, behaved strangely, claiming he had been told to make all his stock available to Shepard.

This was because he, like the rest of Zhu's Hope, had been infected with Thorian spores; the Thorian needed Shepard's help to drive off the attacking geth. Along with the other colonists, Ledra fought to defend the crashed freighter once Shepard returned from the ExoGeni Corporation headquarters. If he survived, he thanked Shepard effusively, apologizing for his inaction while the Thorian filtered his every thought. If enough colonists survived, he planned to stay in Zhu's Hope to recoup his losses; if many died, he told Shepard he was leaving the colony as it was no longer profitable to remain.
