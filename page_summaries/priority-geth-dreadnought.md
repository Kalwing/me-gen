Priority: Geth Dreadnought was a Mass Effect 3 mission acquired automatically after Priority: Perseus Veil and the meeting with the quarian Admiralty Board. The quarians had attacked the geth to retake their homeworld Rannoch, but the geth had been upgraded by the Reapers and the quarian fleet was trapped. Shepard was sent to board and disable the geth dreadnought broadcasting the Reaper control signal. Tali'Zorah vas Normandy was a mandatory squadmate unless she had died in Mass Effect 2, in which case Admiral Xen guided the mission by comm instead. There was no time penalty for delaying the mission despite in-dialogue urgency.

The mission opened with a solo, low-gravity spacewalk along a circular docking tube toward the dreadnought, a sequence inspired by the hallway scene in the film Inception. After the tube detached and separated Shepard from the squad and the Normandy, the team fought through the geth defense network, the operations center (introducing Geth Hunters that used optical camouflage), and maintenance shafts, avoiding Geth Trip Mines throughout. Tali's Sabotage, which temporarily hacked synthetics to fight for Shepard, and Energy Drain were central tools, since the mission featured only synthetic enemies.

In the Main Battery corridor, the dreadnought's titanic main gun fired periodically, sending an electromagnetic shockwave down the hall that heavily damaged anyone not in cover. After crossing the battery twice and surviving a collapsing elevator platform, the team reached the Drive Core, where the Reaper control signal was being distributed through Legion (or a generic "Geth VI" if Legion was absent), held wired into the core. Freeing Legion shut the signal and the drive core down. The team then fought off geth reinforcements including Geth Primes before escaping to a geth fighter in the hangar. Afterward Legion or the Geth VI took up residence in the Normandy's war room, and the missions Rannoch: Admiral Koris, Rannoch: Geth Fighter Squadrons, and N7: Fuel Reactors, plus the Argos Rho cluster, became available. Rewards included the Arc Pistol, Geth Pulse Rifle, and the Archon Visor.
