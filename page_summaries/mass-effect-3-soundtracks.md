This page lists the four Mass Effect 3 soundtrack albums: the Mass Effect 3 Original Videogame Score plus three supplemental albums for Extended Cut, Leviathan and Citadel. The main score was composed by Sascha Dikiciyan and Cris Velasco (who had scored the ME2 packs Kasumi's Stolen Memory and Arrival), Sam Hulick (ME and ME2), Christopher Lennertz (ME2 packs Overlord and Lair of the Shadow Broker), and film composer Clint Mansell, known for Requiem for a Dream and The Fountain. The band Faunts returned with the credits song "Das Malefitz." ME and ME2 composer Jack Wall did not return. Project Director Casey Hudson described the goal as combining "epic scope and delicate emotion." The score was initially exclusive to the N7 Collector's Edition and Digital Deluxe Edition, then sold on iTunes from April 24, 2012 for $6.99, and released free by BioWare in May 2021 for a limited time. Its 25 tracks included "Leaving Earth," "Mars," "An End, Once and For All" and the Collector's Edition extra "Creation 1."

The Extended Cut album added seven Sam Hulick tracks (with Clint Mansell on the extended "An End, Once And For All"), free on July 6, 2012, including "Wake Up," which was from the original soundtrack but omitted from the released score. The Leviathan album consisted of one track, "Leviathan," by Dikiciyan and Velasco, free from September 1, 2012. The Citadel album added twenty tracks by Sam Hulick, Cris Velasco and Sascha Dikiciyan, free for registered owners from March 11, 2013, including "Citadel Underbelly," "The Anti-Shepard," "Shepard's Tango," "Liara's Theme," four Combat Simulator tiers and "Farewell and Into the Inevitable."
