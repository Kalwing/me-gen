"Garrus: Find Dr. Saleon" was a squadmate assignment in Mass Effect unlocked by repeatedly speaking with Garrus Vakarian aboard the Normandy about his history in C-Sec. Garrus eventually recounted the case of Dr. Saleon, a salarian geneticist who fled the Citadel with hostages because C-Sec authorities would not fire on his vessel; Garrus believed Saleon used the hostages as test subjects and regretted letting him escape. Shepard received the assignment along with coordinates for Saleon's ship, the MSV Fedele, in the Herschel system of the Kepler Verge. Garrus specifically asked to come along, though bringing him was optional.

Boarding the MSV Fedele, Shepard's team fought through Saleon's Test Subjects, creatures resembling Thorian Creepers that were not vulnerable to Anti-Thorian Gas grenades and attacked with toxic spit and melee at close range. Containment canisters in the main hold could be detonated to keep them at bay. In the cockpit section, Shepard found Saleon, who now called himself Dr. Heart. He thanked Shepard for "rescuing" him, but Garrus confirmed his identity and Saleon denied it. Shepard could either order Garrus to stand down, planning to hand Saleon to the military for questioning, or authorize Garrus to kill him. Either way Saleon turned hostile, drawing his gun, and was killed with a single shot before he could effectively retaliate. If Garrus was not brought, a squad member exposed Saleon and he fled, dying the same way.

After Saleon's death, on-screen text read: "Saleon's medical equipment is stained with the blood of many species. Pale blue, violet, orange... and more than a few dark red. But his work has ended here. Time to head back to the Normandy." The room contained several lockers, a medical station, a malfunctioning object, and a wetware kit. Completing the assignment gave Garrus closure, though if he was left behind he later regretted missing the chance to confront Saleon. Cut content indicates Mass Effect 2 originally planned to reintroduce Garrus on Omega hunting Saleon's accomplices.
