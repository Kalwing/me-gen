Short DLC-overview page (under ~150 words of prose). Kasumi - Stolen Memory was a Mass Effect 2 DLC pack released April 6, 2010 and included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. It added the squad member Kasumi Goto, the missions Dossier: The Master Thief and Kasumi: Stealing Memory (a heist on Bekenstein against master criminal Donovan Hock), the M-12 Locust weapon, a research upgrade, a casual outfit, an achievement, and the Boltzmann system location in the Serpent Nebula. It was priced at $6.99 USD / 560 BioWare Points and was included on the disc of the PlayStation 3 version.
