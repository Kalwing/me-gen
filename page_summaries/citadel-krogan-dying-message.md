Citadel: Krogan Dying Message was a Mass Effect 3 mission acquired after Attican Traverse: Krogan Team. Partway through the mission to Utukku, behind some webbing just before a one-way drop, Commander Shepard found the interactable body of a dead krogan who had asked that his final message be delivered to an asari merchant named Ereba. Shepard took the recording to Ereba, who worked at Nos Astra Sporting Goods in the Meridian Place Market of the Citadel Presidium Commons. The krogan was revealed to be Charr, whose relationship with Ereba could have been influenced by Shepard during the earlier assignment Illium: Blue Rose of Illium. On hearing the message Ereba wept, and Shepard could stay or leave her to listen; at the end of the recording she said she had to go. The message implied that Charr and Ereba had, or could have had, a child together. Completing the mission granted 5 Reputation and 90 experience. The exact wording of Charr's message depended on whether Ereba had been convinced to take Charr back or to break off the relationship.

- Charr's recorded final message, played if Ereba and Charr had parted or the Illium assignment was never done (also the outcome in a non-imported game): "O Blue Rose of Illium, if these humble words reach you, then I have joined my ancestors. My dream was to be by your side, a weed beside your beauty, twining together in the warm Tuchanka sun. But though you needed room to grow, still, I will remember the perfume of the scent and the soft touch of your petals. If my heart could not shelter you from the storm, then let my broken bones build a wall around your garden, so you can grow safe and happy."

- Charr's recorded final message, played if Ereba had been convinced to take Charr back: "O Blue Rose of Illium, if these humble words reach you, then I have joined my ancestors. My dream was to be by your side, a weed beside your beauty, twining together in the warm Tuchanka sun. But if my last days must be with krannt instead of kindness, still, I will remember the perfume of your scent and the soft touch of your petals. Let my broken bones build a wall around your garden, so you and the flower we planted together can grow safe and strong."
