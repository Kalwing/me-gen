This page is a chronological plot synopsis of the Mass Effect 2-era fiction.

In Mass Effect Galaxy, Jacob Taylor and Miranda Lawson foiled a batarian bioweapon plot by ambassador Jath'Amon to infect the Citadel Council with a blood-plague weapon during peace talks. In Mass Effect: Incursion, Aria T'Loak discovered the Blue Suns selling humans to the Collectors on Omega. The Mass Effect 2 prologue depicted the Collector attack that destroyed the SSV Normandy and killed Shepard.

Foundation and Homeworlds comic chapters tracked the Cerberus agent Rasa vetting Shepard's prospective squad (Mordin, Zaeed Massani, Thane Krios and others), her poisoning and rescue by Thane, a failed mission with Kai Leng to capture Jack, and the theft of Shepard's Spectre files from Tela Vasir; young Garrus Vakarian's path to becoming Archangel on Omega was also recounted. In Mass Effect: Redemption, Liara T'Soni and the drell Feron raced the Shadow Broker's agent Tazzik and the Collectors to recover Shepard's body, eventually delivering it to Cerberus while Feron was captured. In Mass Effect: Ascension, Kahlee Sanders, Paul Grayson, Hendel Mitra and the biotic child Gillian Grayson fled Cerberus to the Migrant Fleet, foiling an attack orchestrated by the traitors Pel and Golo. In Mass Effect: Paragon Lost, Lieutenant James Vega's Delta Squad failed to save the Fehl Prime colony from a Collector attack, Vega choosing to secure vital intel over evacuating the colonists.

The Mass Effect 2 recap covered Shepard's revival by Cerberus, command of the Normandy SR-2 with EDI and Joker, the recruitment of a team, the Horizon attack, the derelict Collector ship, the Reaper IFF from a dead Reaper, the abduction of the Normandy crew, and the suicide mission through the Omega 4 Relay against the Human-Reaper, ending with Harbinger vowing another way. Lair of the Shadow Broker followed Liara and Shepard killing the yahg Shadow Broker so Liara could take his place. In Arrival, Admiral Hackett sent Shepard to rescue Dr. Amanda Kenson, leading to the destruction of the Alpha Relay and the Bahak system's 300,000-plus colonists to delay the Reapers.
