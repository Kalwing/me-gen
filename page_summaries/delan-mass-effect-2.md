Delan was a human mechanic living on the colony Horizon in Mass Effect 2, distinct from the hanar merchant Delanynder of the first game. He had moved to Horizon because he believed the Systems Alliance brought nothing but trouble, a view he felt was confirmed when the Alliance installed GARDIAN anti-ship batteries against probable Cerberus attacks, which he thought only made the colony a target. The battery targeting calibration was badly off, and when the Alliance sent a representative to fix it, Delan believed the representative was a spy. When communications went down, the Collectors, long believed to be a myth, attacked, and Delan hid in a shed while they paralyzed and rounded up the colonists.

When the Normandy SR-2 responded, Commander Shepard found Delan hiding in a building. He could be told to stay put while Shepard powered up the defense towers to drive off the Collector ship; if asked to help, he refused, saying a mechanic could do nothing against Collectors. The Collectors escaped with roughly one-third of Horizon's colonists. A dismayed Delan ran out, distraught over lost friends and loved ones, and became further disillusioned when one of Shepard's former squadmates, working with Cerberus, appeared unharmed. Having had enough of the Alliance, Delan walked away. Using explosive weapons on him would make him "die" without leaving a body, and he would still appear at the end of the level. He was voiced by Keith Szarabajka, who also voiced Harbinger.
