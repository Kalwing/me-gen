After Shepard convinced the drunken turian General Septimus to stop spreading lies about the Asari Consort Sha'ira during Citadel: Asari Consort, Septimus offered a small job: an elcor named Xeltan had believed the rumors and taken the false information to the Elcor Ambassador. Septimus gave Shepard a datapad with proof of Sha'ira's innocence and asked Shepard to find Xeltan and tell him the truth to exonerate her. Xeltan was found in the elcor embassy speaking with Ambassador Calyn. Speaking to Xeltan before dealing with Sha'ira and Septimus did not trigger the assignment. Shepard delivered the datapad and received experience and credits regardless of how the dialogue ended; choosing the reassuring "Don't worry" response granted an additional 2 Paragon points. Completing the assignment before returning to Sha'ira earned an extra reward from her.
