Chorban was a self-proclaimed salarian scientist who built a scanner to obtain readings of the keepers, the Citadel's mysterious caretakers. Commander Shepard first found him at the Citadel Tower attempting to scan a stationary keeper. Chorban and his volus partner Jahleed had stolen the plans for an advanced medical scanner from the company they worked for and reconfigured them to scan keepers, since very little was known about the creatures. Worried the authorities would take a dim view of his curiosity, he kept the research covert, and he offered Shepard the chance to continue the scanning for him. The two partners later fell out, and Shepard could mediate their conflict; Chorban could be found in the lower markets of the Wards with two salarian bodyguards. If Shepard already knew about the research, he sent the guards away and spoke openly; if Shepard had learned of his activities only from Jahleed, Chorban assumed the Commander had been sent to seize his keeper data, refused to believe otherwise, and attacked with his bodyguards before losing and begging for his life. In every case he admitted that he and Jahleed had lost perspective but remained eager to study the keepers, and again offered Shepard the scanning task. His possible fates were that Shepard took up or continued the keeper scans, that he was discouraged from the research, or that Shepard killed him if the meeting began as a fight. In Mass Effect 2, if he had survived and Shepard had scanned all or most of the keepers, Chorban emailed his findings, entitled "Concerns re: keepers": the scans showed the keepers were bio-engineered, and comparison with material from Saren's flagship Sovereign indicated they had been engineered millions of years ago by the same makers, suggesting the Citadel was not built by the Protheans; genetic readings implied the keepers were set to react to some signal roughly every 50,000 years; and the last such interval coincided with the Protheans' disappearance and was due again around the present, so old technology might have "nasty surprises" waiting. He noted that nobody else on the Citadel would listen to him.
