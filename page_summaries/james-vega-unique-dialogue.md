This page collects James Vega's squad-specific and spontaneous dialogue across Mass Effect 3 missions, along with his list of nicknames. Vega's lines are triggered by which other squadmates are present and by prior story choices, and range from combat callouts to commentary on the rachni, the genophage, Cerberus, and the Prothean. The page also documents his habit of renaming people because "some people just don't match their names."

Nicknames: Loco (male Shepard), Lola (female Shepard), Sparks (Tali), Doc (Liara), Scars (Garrus), Esteban (Cortez), Buggy (Javik), L2 (Kaidan), LC (Ashley).

Representative lines:

James, meeting Tali during Priority: Geth Dreadnought: "Sparks. You've got lights. And you're small, and jumpy. It just came out."

James, on the shuttle during Attican Traverse if the Rachni Queen was spared: "You let a rachni queen go? Are you kidding?"

James, seeing the Reaper at the Shroud during Priority: Tuchanka: "I hope somebody has a plan B."

James, after a near miss from a Reaper's weapon on Tuchanka: "I just got shot by a Reaper."

James, to Javik after a Sun Tzu quote during Priority: The Citadel II: "That's what I love about you, buggy. You're like Sun Tzu with more eyes."

James, killing a Phantom at Cerberus Headquarters: "Take that, you flippy little bitch!"

James, distracting a guard during Silver Coast Casino: Infiltration: "Sir, I'm with the Citadel Station Building Inspectors. I need to look at your basement."

James, when Shepard confirms things are "dicey" during Citadel Wards: Ambush: "That's when things get fun."

James, to his clone during Citadel Archives: Escape: "You're a pale imitation of the real thing."

James, in the Normandy cockpit as Joker hesitates during Priority: Earth: "Come on, buddy, we can't stay here."
