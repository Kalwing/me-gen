Garvug was the first planet of the Paz System in the Vallhallan Threshold, Milky Way. In 354 CE it was granted to the krogan as a "bargain world," a frozen rock no one else wanted, though it technically bore life with an equatorial farm belt and insulated marine life. Within a century the krogan had adapted completely, breeding hundreds of younglings per family in underground bunkers; within another century overfishing and pollution destroyed its coral reefs and surplus krogan left for the stars. The Citadel Council cited Garvug as an object lesson that the krogan could not be trusted to limit their own numbers. By the time of the games it was a frozen wasteland hosting corporate eco-engineering projects attempting sustainable agriculture and aquaculture, with krogan and vorcha packs a constant threat and mercenaries well paid for protection.

Known cities included Dhazil, Ikrit, and Jundrag. Corporations Binary Helix and Guanghui Solutions (seeking to repair the planet) and Sonax Industries (seeking mining rights) backed a coup against the ruling Clan Hailot in exchange for unrestricted planetary access, opposing the Garvug State Forces. After six months of guerrilla warfare failed to capture deposed leader Hailot Wrund, the corporations feigned withdrawal, then resumed precision strikes once the rebels retook Dhazil, killing most rebels including Wrund. It later emerged the war was driven by a discovered cache of Prothean technology; the legal fallout caused upheavals among the complicit parties, with Sonax Industries renamed Kore by parent company Umsung Holdings. In gameplay, Garvug offered the mission "Valhallan Threshold: Prothean Data Drives," a Search and Rescue artifact (Prothean Data Disks), and mineral deposits of palladium, platinum, iridium, and element zero. It was also an N7 HQ Galaxy at War mini-game location. The mission required completing "Tali: Treason" in Mass Effect 2 and "Priority: The Citadel II" in Mass Effect 3.
