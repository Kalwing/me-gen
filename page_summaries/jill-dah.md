Gunnery Chief Jill Dah was a human marine serving in the Systems Alliance. Born in 2136, she served in then-Lieutenant David Anderson's unit during the First Contact War in 2157 and was later assigned to the frigate SSV Hastings. She was still assigned to that ship in 2165, together with Anderson. Standing 6'3", she was known for her aggression and strong opinions, which earned her the nickname "Amazon," or "Amy" among fellow marines.

When the Hastings responded to the distress call from the research post on Sidon, Dah was assigned to Anderson's ground team and took point during the mission. While escaping an underground room rigged with explosives, she was shot in the leg by a batarian mercenary. Anderson had to carry her back to the elevator, and the two barely escaped before the explosives detonated. Despite severe injuries to her right leg, Dah made a full recovery, recovering enough to take in the view of the Citadel from the Hastings' bridge.
