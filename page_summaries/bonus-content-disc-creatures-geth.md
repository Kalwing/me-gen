This page transcribed the recorded artist commentary for the design gallery "The Creatures of Mass Effect: Geth," included on the 2007 Mass Effect bonus content disc. All eighteen images were narrated by concept artist Matt Rhodes, with contributions from Derek Watts, Sung Kim, and Casey Hudson noted in the sketches.

Rhodes said the geth were originally conceived as just another alien species, and some early heads were creature-based, including one derived from a bat with flared nostrils and pointed teeth, plus a seal-skin or natural-armor covering. A turning-point sketch introduced a skinless android with an exposed "plastic muscle" chart, intended to provoke a visceral, sick emotional response; the office reaction was positive and the concept was moved into the geth role. Further passes emphasized artificial white muscle fiber, glowing internal lights, human-skull faces missing the lower jaw, and white "milk" blood, referencing Bishop from Aliens. One long-standing combination concept, featuring a hood with a single eye and wrapped tubing, was not chosen as the trooper but was built as its own creature and became the in-game hopper.

Several artists contributed elements: Derek produced a design built mostly from tubes and piping that read as mechanical but not organic; Sung Kim did sketches breaking up the human face and pushing the skeleton as far as possible; and Casey drew an early head, hood, and legs that were carried almost directly into the final geth, with the legs translated into the geth armature. The final geth trooper kept the arched neck and protruding "pigeon" chest, lost some large back tubes, and retained Casey's early head.
