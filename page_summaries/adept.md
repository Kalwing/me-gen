The Adept was a playable character class in Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, and Mass Effect 3, and a profile in Mass Effect: Andromeda, focused on biotic power. Described as the ultimate biotic, the Adept manipulated the physical world through mass effect fields, disabling and debuffing enemies while dealing heavy damage.

In the original Mass Effect, Adepts possessed all biotic abilities and could wear specialized bio-amps, but could equip only light armor, gained weapons training only with pistols, and had no tech skills. Their powers included Singularity, Lift, Throw, Warp, Barrier, and Stasis. The Adept class talent reduced biotic recharge time and increased biotic protection, and an Adept could specialize into Bastion or Nemesis to reach levels 7 through 12.

In Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, Adepts were biotic specialists who lacked advanced combat training but were the best at defeating enemies without firing a shot, outfitted with L5x implants that could spawn a micro-singularity. Mass Effect 2 gave them Warp, Throw, Singularity, Pull, Shockwave, and Biotic Mastery, with weapons training in heavy pistols, submachine guns, and heavy weapons. Mass Effect 3 added Cluster Grenade and Fitness; the Adept had a low weight capacity, comparable to the Engineer, with a bonus rising from 10 to a maximum of 50 through Biotic Mastery.

Mass Effect: Andromeda replaced fixed classes with flexible Profiles that Pathfinder Ryder could swap at any time, including in combat. The Adept profile reconfigured Ryder's implant for biotic combat and offered bonus powers Biotic Echoes, letting biotic combos trigger additional explosions on each enemy touched, and Biotic Jump & Evades, replacing the jump jet with biotic movement. According to franchise statistics, the Adept ranked fourth of six in popularity as a Shepard class, chosen by 7 percent of players in March 2012 and 11 percent by July 2021 in Legendary Edition.
