"Race Against Time: Final Battle" was the concluding mission of Mass Effect and the last rename of the Race Against Time series, acquired after Shepard traveled through the Conduit on Ilos. Saren Arterius was attempting to transfer control of the Citadel to Sovereign so the Reaper invasion could begin, and Shepard had to stop him and destroy Sovereign.

The Mako emerged from the Conduit onto the Presidium, crushing two geth. After fighting husks released from Dragon's teeth, Shepard could question the VI Avina, who reported the Presidium's damage, Saren's approach to the Council Chambers, C-Sec's inability to respond, and the evacuation of the Council and nearly 10,000 inhabitants to the Destiny Ascension. Sovereign forced its way past turian cruisers, destroyed one, and wrapped its limbs around the Citadel Tower. With the elevator disabled, Shepard's team suited up and crossed the tower's exterior in zero-g, fighting through the Maintenance Shaft, Exhaust Plain (including a Geth Dropship), and Exterior Defense Grid, battling waves of Geth Destroyers, Juggernauts, Geth Turrets, and krogan Warriors, Warlords and a Battlemaster.

In the burning Council Chambers, Shepard defeated Saren's last geth line and confronted him at the Master Control Terminal. Saren revealed Sovereign had implanted him and fully indoctrinated him; if Shepard had persuaded him on Virmire, dialogue options could make Saren fight the implants and shoot himself, otherwise Shepard fought him on his hover platform. Shepard then uploaded Vigil's data file, gaining control of the Citadel's systems, and received a mayday from the Destiny Ascension while Joker reported the Arcturus Fleet waiting at the relay.

At the Big Decision, the Paragon choice sent the Alliance fleet, led by the Normandy, to save the Destiny Ascension and the Council before turning fire on Sovereign; choosing "Let the Council die" or "Concentrate on Sovereign" let the Ascension be destroyed. Saren's corpse was then reanimated by Sovereign into a husk that Shepard fought in a final mobile battle. When it fell, Sovereign's shields dropped, the fleet opened fire, and Joker's Normandy fired the missile that tore Sovereign apart. Debris smashed into the Council Chambers; Anderson's team searched the wreckage and found an injured but triumphant Shepard. In the epilogue the Council (if saved) or Udina offered humanity a seat on the Council, and Shepard could nominate Anderson or Udina as councilor. The outcomes carried into Mass Effect 2 and 3, affecting the Council's composition, Anderson's role, the Destiny Ascension as a War Asset, and fleet strength during the Reaper War.
