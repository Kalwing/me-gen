Dr. Garret Bryson was a Systems Alliance scientist and head of Task Force Aurora, a group created to investigate ancient legends about the Reapers. He treated his work as a mission to find truth against galactic skepticism, noting that as late as 2148, within his lifetime, some humans still believed aliens to be a myth.

Commander Shepard met Bryson in his lab on the Citadel on orders from Admiral Hackett. Bryson introduced his group and mission and was about to explain a research breakthrough when he was shot in the chest and killed by his assistant Derek Hadley, who was incapacitated shortly afterward. With EDI's help, Shepard picked up the investigation. Video logs of correspondence between Bryson and Hackett revealed the task force had been researching the Leviathan of Dis, a Reaper corpse discovered by the batarians; Bryson found it significant that something had been able to kill a Reaper long ago, which he dubbed the "real" Leviathan. He later reported that the Reapers themselves had been shadowing his field teams as if also searching for Leviathan, prompting his formal request for assistance that brought Shepard.

Other logs showed Bryson had safeguarded his research, hiding decryption keys "close to his heart" by scribbling the numbers into one of his daughter Ann Bryson's childhood drawings. Further logs pursued other Reaper-killer leads, some dead ends like rachni activity and others worth following like creature sightings. After her rescue from Namakli, Ann Bryson said Shepard's work uncovering the Reapers validated years of her father's work. She described him as a distant but intensely passionate man who loved fieldwork, regretted that their last conversation was a petty argument over filing specimens, and said he would have hated dying in his lab.
