Short lore page (under ~150 words of prose). Conatix Industries was a defunct human company that developed biotic implant technology specializing in telekinesis and was a key early investor in the BAaT program, studying biotic abilities and building implants for humans. It originally relied on accidental element zero exposures for test subjects but ran short of first-generation subjects, prompting rumors that it later took a "pro-active" approach to eezo exposure, given its unusually fast detection of new biotics around 2163. Rather than seek Citadel experts and appear to make Earth look weak, Conatix discreetly hired turian mercenaries, including Commander Vyrnnus, to train the biotics. The company folded shortly after BAaT was shut down, and smaller private firms in the same field grew out of it.
