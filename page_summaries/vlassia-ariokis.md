Captain Vlassia Ariokis, service number 6732-IC-1917, was an Alliance Intelligence operative stationed at the Quiet Eddy black ops facility, appearing in the novel Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation. Short in stature but highly perceptive and radiating a lethal aura, she moved economically and regarded assassinations simply as judgment calls that had to be made.

On Illium, Ariokis killed the mercenary Ygara Menoris to obtain an artificial intelligence kernel in her possession, since she and her superiors had no budget to purchase the technology at auction and the asari was unlikely to be intimidated. After deleting the kernel from Ygara's ship, she drew the pursuit of Andromeda Initiative agent Cora Harper, who followed her shuttle trail to Pamyat chasing the twice-stolen code.

Ariokis transmitted the kernel to the Quiet Eddy facility as soon as she came within shuttle range, but chaos met her arrival hours later. The AI Medea disabled her shuttle during landing; Ariokis survived the crash and fought off hostile augments in the hangar. Given the location of a safe haven at the Gamma Labs, she cloaked her way through the facility and joined the survivors there.

When Cora reached the Gamma Labs and learned the situation, she asked Ariokis for help with a medical procedure. Noticing that Cora had been talking to her comms, Ariokis pulled a gun on her when Cora admitted it was her implant AI. Ariokis recognized it as built from the same kernel she had taken on Illium and assumed it was hostile because of Medea's actions, but SAM-E and Cora corrected her. Learning that Cora intended to overclock her body with SAM-E's help, Ariokis deduced she was present to kill Cora if the procedure went wrong. The procedure succeeded, and Ariokis witnessed Cora decapitate an augment through sheer biotic power, briefly stunning her. After helping move the other survivors to Cora's shuttle, Ariokis came to Cora's rescue when the exhausted biotic was about to be beaten by multiple augments, killing the attackers with a missile launcher and dragging the wounded Cora back to the shuttle.
