Citadel: Jahleed's Fears was a Mass Effect assignment on the Citadel, available after Citadel: Expose Saren once Commander Shepard had been given leeway to find irrefutable proof of Saren Arterius's treachery. It began by speaking with Jahleed, a volus scientist who had taken refuge in the C-Sec offices and refused to leave, claiming that his colleague, a salarian named Chorban, was trying to kill him. Jahleed asked Shepard to meet Chorban in the Lower Markets.

Chorban was found in the Lower Markets, and the assignment branched depending on whether Shepard had already spoken to Chorban and accepted Citadel: Scan the Keepers. If Shepard had agreed to scan the keepers, Chorban explained that he had only meant to threaten Jahleed to obtain data from him, and Shepard could tell him to stop or keep scanning. If Shepard had never spoken to Chorban, or had declined the scanning, Chorban and two salarian bodyguards attacked; explosive canisters were scattered nearby. After defeating Chorban and his guards, Shepard could discourage him from further scheming, kill him for Renegade morality, or agree to scan the keepers for him.

The journal then updated to Citadel: Jahleed's Secret, summarizing Chorban's revelations. Returning to Jahleed, he apologized for lying and departed, completing the assignment. If Shepard had not accepted Chorban's assignment, extra options were available: turning Chorban in to a C-Sec officer for stealing from his workplace, using Charm or Intimidate to make him turn himself in to his company, or demanding payment, which gave about five times the normal reward but no morality points. The overall morality outcome depended on order: starting Citadel: Scan the Keepers first allowed the maximum Paragon points, while starting Jahleed's Fears first allowed the maximum Renegade points. Talking to Chorban before Jahleed gave more insight into what the two partners had been doing. Enemies included an Assassin, a Salarian Bodyguard, and Chorban.
