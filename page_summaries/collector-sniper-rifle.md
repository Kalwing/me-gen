The Collector Sniper Rifle was a sniper rifle in Mass Effect 3 whose long-range particle beam was capable of sustained fire, letting it switch between anti-personnel and anti-materiel roles simply by holding down the trigger. It did not accept thermal clips, instead cooling by venting heat into a liquid-containing sleeve that radiated it through fin-like protuberances on the barrel. It was exclusive to multiplayer, randomly rewarded from item packs as a rare weapon, and required the Mass Effect 3: Retaliation DLC.

Its self-cooling ammunition worked like the Prothean Particle Rifle's, but with a much smaller magazine (35 rounds at level I rising to 45 at level X), a high-powered scope, and no charge-up time to reach maximum damage. Its small clip and very high firing speed made it easy to deplete, starting a long cooldown animation. It was the only sniper rifle with no accuracy penalty for firing unscoped, though it kept a damage penalty. It was a hitscan weapon that hit instantly; the trailing beam was purely a visual effect and dealt no damage, so players had to track targets with the crosshair rather than the beam. Its rapid low-damage fire made it ineffective against large armored targets like Brutes but effective against lighter enemies, unhindered by shield gates, and if enemy armor could be fully negated it could serve as a primary weapon even on Platinum. Because it disintegrated its kills, it denied Cannibals the chance to devour fallen allies. The Awakened Collector Adept dealt bonus damage with it via its Vengeful Ancient passive.
