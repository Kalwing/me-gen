The Krysae Sniper Rifle was a turian antimateriel rifle modified during the Reaper War of Mass Effect 3 to kill Reaper enemies. Its scope used a rangefinder that automatically adjusted to keep a target in proportion to the shooter, useful when a sniper was forced to close range, and it fired specialized ammunition that was both armor-piercing and explosive. In a desperate measure to aid the war effort, the turians released the weapon's specifications over the extranet so that nearly anyone with a fabricator could manufacture it.

The Krysae was unique among sniper rifles in firing explosive projectiles with a proximity fuse, dealing area-of-effect blast damage rather than penetrating cover or scoring headshots. It dealt 50 percent bonus damage to armored targets but its rounds detonated near any nearby enemy or cloaked foe, making precise shots past intervening targets difficult while making it effective against Swarmers, Phantoms and Geth Hunters. Enemies killed by it were completely destroyed, denying Cannibals bodies to consume.

In single-player the rifle was available after downloading the Mass Effect 3: Firefight Pack; in Legendary Edition it could be bought from the Cipritine Armory terminal in the Presidium Commons for a base 10,000 credits. In multiplayer it was a rare weapon requiring the Rebellion Pack, randomly awarded through item packs. A balance patch on July 24, 2012 sharply reduced its damage, rate of fire and spare ammo while doubling recoil, relegating it to a crowd-control support role; those changes did not apply in Legendary Edition, where it dealt roughly 25 percent more damage with better handling. The weapon was originally designed with a scope that auto-zoomed on targets, but according to lead combat designer Corey Gaspur the feature was abandoned because it made players nauseous.
