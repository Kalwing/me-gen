Unity was a default starting power in Mass Effect 2, available only to Commander Shepard from Prologue: Awakening onward. It used medi-gel from Shepard's limited reserve to revive fallen squad members and, when upgraded, to heal conscious but damaged squadmates and Shepard as well. It had no ranks and a 3.00-second recharge time, shared with all other powers.

Shepard began able to carry three units of medi-gel, upgradable to nine. By default Unity revived fallen squadmates to full health and shields but had no effect on Shepard; researching the Emergency Shielding and Trauma Module upgrades allowed it to restore shields and health to Shepard and to damaged conscious squadmates. Casting Unity when no one was damaged in a way medi-gel could currently repair simply failed, with a red tooltip explaining why. Medi-gel could be administered under fire and, depending on upgrades, could restore health and shields even while damage was being taken. At least one medi-gel was always expended when someone was damaged; no more than two were used per cast, and two only when both squadmates were down, with an exception when Shepard had only one medi-gel left, in which case a single unit restored everyone. Unity could not be used while storming, and could be interrupted by staggers or stuns during its activation animation, though not once the healing effect began. Revived squadmates forgot prior hold-position orders and chose their own cover. The page notes this power is distinct from the Mass Effect Spectre Training ability of the same name and from the First Aid talent.
