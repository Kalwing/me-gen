"Namakli: Leviathan" was a mission in the Mass Effect 3 Leviathan DLC, following "Leviathan: Find Ann Bryson." Researcher Ann Bryson's dig site on Namakli was under Reaper attack, and Shepard's team went in to rescue her and learn what she knew about the Leviathan artifacts. Cortez dropped the landing party off but was chased away by Harvesters, which then circled overhead and bombarded Shepard's position throughout the first half of the level as an inescapable environmental hazard, forcing the squad to keep moving.

Shepard, contacting Ann repeatedly by radio, fought through Cannibals, Husks, a Banshee, Ravagers, a Brute, and Marauders while repairing a bridge control panel and working around a damaged elevator shaft that Ann bypassed by opening a door one level up. Reaching Ann, the squad found she was the only survivor of her team. She explained what she knew of Leviathan and the orb-shaped artifact she had found, which was emitting energy toward the surrounding Reaper creatures. Ann lost control and began speaking in a deep voice not her own; Shepard's squadmates shot and destroyed the artifact, breaking the enthrallment. The team then fought a running retreat to Cortez's shuttle, prioritizing a Harvester so he could land.

Aboard the shuttle Ann did not remember being enthralled and wanted to contact her father; Shepard broke the news of Dr. Bryson's death. Stunned, Ann asked to meet Shepard at her father's lab. The mission confirmed the artifacts allowed Leviathan to control minds, making it urgent to deal with the artifact already sitting inside the Citadel. Rewards included 12,500 credits, medi-gel, the AT-12 Raider shotgun, and several weapon mods.
