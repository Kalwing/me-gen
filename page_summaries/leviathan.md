The Leviathans were an ancient aquatic race that dominated the galaxy before the Reapers, regarding themselves as its first and only apex race and other species as tools meant to serve them and provide "tribute". Massive and resembling Earth cuttlefish or squid, with two sets of three eyes and bony ridged plates, they lived at ocean depths over 3200 meters and possessed a natural ability to physically and mentally dominate lesser-minded organisms, communicating telepathically and creating thralls; over-exertion of this power caused nosebleeds, brain damage or death in humans. They may have enhanced themselves cybernetically to travel space and breathe air.

The Leviathans used an enthralled land-based species to achieve spaceflight and spread through the galaxy. Observing that their thralls' synthetic creations consistently rebelled and wiped out their makers, the Leviathans built an Intelligence with a mandate to preserve life at all costs. The Intelligence created pawns to harvest organic data, then concluded the Leviathans themselves were part of the problem and turned on them, slaughtering most and using their genetic material to build the first Reaper, Harbinger, designed in their likeness. Survivors went into hiding, observing the cyclical harvests and erasing evidence of their existence through organic "artifacts" they called "fragments". They had the power to destroy Reapers, shown when they killed the Leviathan of Dis, but declined to aid the galaxy.

During the 2186 Reaper invasion, Admiral Hackett's Task Force Aurora under Dr. Garret Bryson pursued them as a potential Reaper-killer. The Leviathans killed Bryson through his enthralled assistant Derek Hadley, and Shepard tracked their trail through Mahavid (where Dr. Alex Garneau died) and Namakli (where Dr. Ann Bryson was rescued) to 2181 Despoina. There a Leviathan recounted its race's history within Shepard's mind. Persuaded that the Reapers now knew of them and feared Shepard, the Leviathans agreed to fight the Reapers, not to help "lesser species" but to defend their domain. They disabled a Reaper capital ship, freed groups of Collectors from Reaper control, and had their artifacts deployed behind enemy lines to subvert Reaper forces.

Virtually nothing was known of the Leviathans before Shepard's encounter: no true name, no ruins, only rock paintings on Namakli and scattered artifacts. Their technology was believed to exceed the Reapers' in some respects, including faster-than-light communication pulses and organic tools, compared to the Thorian. The Leviathan that spoke to Shepard was voiced by Anthony Skordi.
