Ontarom was the first planet of the Newton System in the Kepler Verge. Its surface was uncomfortably hot, but its nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere and abundant shallow seas made it an ideal candidate for habitation by most known species. Its moon Thonal had an orbit that had been slowly decaying since the system formed; its proximity was producing tidal effects and, through its passage through Ontarom's magnetic field, increasingly powerful electrical storms, with an eventual collision expected. Proposals to emplace tens of thousands of mass effect drives to lighten the moon and correct its orbit were dismissed as prohibitively expensive. A multi-racial effort, including ExoGeni Corp. and Heyuan Genomics for the Alliance, worked to catalogue and preserve the genetic diversity of Ontarom's young biosphere.

When humans settled Ontarom, the asari derided it as foolish, but sixteen years before the Reaper War the Systems Alliance established the first dish field there, which grew into a crucial communications hub with hundreds of quantum communicators whose data was broadcast planet-wide and beamed out via comm buoy. Trade between the Alliance base and nearby human settlements sustained planetary unity, and the Alliance trained and hired colonists to maintain the communicators. A coalition of local businesses began building a civilian broadcast hub with military technical help, making Ontarom a symbol of civilian-military cooperation. By Mass Effect 3 it had not yet been attacked by Reaper forces.

In Mass Effect, set in 2183, Shepard could visit Ontarom for the assignment UNC: Dead Scientists (an underground science facility) and related tasks; its operational area was a large even plateau cut by deep gorges. It was one of only two uncharted planets (with Chasca) where the party could go helmetless outside. The Mass Effect 3 multiplayer map Firebase Dagger was located there.
