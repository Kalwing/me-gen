Grenades were hand-tossed explosive charges appearing in different forms across Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, Mass Effect 3, and Mass Effect: Andromeda, usable by the player, allies, and enemies depending on the game. In the first Mass Effect, the Systems Alliance issued the Mark 14 grenade, which had retractable stabilizing fins allowing it to be thrown like a gliding discus or lobbed over cover. Most grenades adhered to flat surfaces, had a 10-second fuse, could serve as makeshift mines or booby-traps, and could be detonated early by a hardsuit's communications gear. Field modifications included high explosive, flashbang, thermite incendiary, proton charges, Bose-Einstein "cryo" condensates, and cobalt-salted micro-nukes.

In gameplay for Mass Effect, only Shepard used grenades; Saren and Tali appeared to throw them in cutscenes. Four main upgrade types existed: Cryo, Fusion, Incendiary, and High Explosive, with a fifth Anti-Thorian Gas type granted by Juliana Baynham on Feros. Grenade type could be swapped even after a throw within the 10-second timer. Grenades were looted from crates and enemies, were not refilled between missions, and could be fully replenished at a device in Admiral Ahern's Intai'sei home won through his Pinnacle Station scenario. Grenades damaged the user and allies.

In Mass Effect 2, two DLC-only loyalty grenade powers existed, Flashbang Grenade and Inferno Grenade, functioning on cooldowns rather than by quantity. Some enemy soldiers in DLC missions threw flashbangs with a delayed whining fuse.

In Mass Effect 3, grenades returned as Grenade Powers that were lobbed with gravity-affected arcs, had no recharge period, drew from a shared limited pool, and were replenished only from environmental caches. Powers included Cluster Grenade, Frag Grenade, Inferno Grenade, Lift Grenade, Sticky Grenade, and many multiplayer-only variants. Squadmates never used grenades automatically except a bugged Urdnot Wrex in the Citadel DLC. Enemy grenade use was common and dangerous, ignoring shield and health gates; users included Assault Troopers, Centurions, Cannibals, Geth Bombers, CAT6 Specialists, Collector Troopers, and Possessed Scions. Some enemies such as Centurions deployed smoke grenades that blocked line of sight and power targeting. In Mass Effect: Andromeda, grenade powers (Frag, Omni, Sticky) consumed power cells refilled at supply caches.
