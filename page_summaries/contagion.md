Contagion was a Mass Effect: Andromeda mission acquired after Hunting the Archon via an e-mail from Captain Nozomi Dunn asking Ryder to meet on the Hyperion. Dunn revealed that a medtech working for Dr. Harry Carlyle, named Charlie, had cheated the screening process to smuggle his aunt Ruth Bekker aboard the Nexus and had recently woken her from cryostasis. Ruth carried TH-314, a deadly, airborne, cross-species disease with three stages that became highly contagious in its final stage and had no cure. Dunn wanted the matter handled discreetly, without informing Director Jarun Tann, to avoid public panic.

Using SAM to track Ruth's biological signature, Ryder retraced her movements through the Nexus Commons, learning from Lator Valon at The Vortex that she had gone to Ops seeking someone named Teron. Teron Aminus reported Ruth was acting strangely and had entered the stage 2 paranoia phase. Carlyle then reported Ruth had attacked a dockworker, Samuel Francoeur, and stolen a shuttle, which Francoeur had damaged, leaving a radiation trail. Scanning the trail across the Anasa, Solminae, Tecunis, and Zaubray systems showed the shuttle failing and heading for Kadara.

The shuttle was found crashed and abandoned west of Kurinth's Valley. Its flight recorder showed Ruth, aware she had reached stage 3, had intended to self-destruct the shuttle but was kidnapped first. Angaran footprints led to a building held by Roekaar, who planned to weaponize Ruth ("the human is very sick... it can infect any species from the Milky Way. But we angara are immune"), exposing captured Milky Way subjects and releasing them to spread the disease for Akksul. Ryder learned the angara were immune, offering hope of a cure, and could reassemble a disassembled stasis pod. Confronting the Roekaar leader, who held Ruth hostage with a degraded virus sample: refusing to let him go killed both Ruth and the leader but secured the samples and body; letting him go spared Ruth, who agreed to return to stasis. Rewards were +530 XP and +73 AVP.
