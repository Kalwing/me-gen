FENRIS Mechs were mechanical constructs designed by Hahne-Kedar specifically for security. Like other Hahne-Kedar mechs they were identifiable by white armor and red lights. Many humans viewed them as the mechanical analog of attack dogs, and they mimicked dog-like behavior when attacking, possibly connected to Hahne-Kedar being an Alliance-based corporation. FENRIS Mechs were less common than the LOKI Mech variant but were seen on Alliance offworld colonies and deployed by the Blue Suns mercenary group, sometimes alongside LOKI Mechs.

The mechs carried advanced sensory equipment allowing them to detect weapons and narcotics, functioning like bomb- and drug-sniffing dogs. Like all mechs, their software could be hacked, altered, or overridden by an experienced hacker and turned against those they were meant to protect.

In combat, a FENRIS Mech accelerated to a set speed and lunged with its rear legs, bringing a target down with its front legs in a pounce-like motion, then stunned the target with a taser embedded in its head. Severely damaged mechs deactivated and remained stationary; if shot while deactivated they exploded. They were deployed in packs and swarmed enemy positions. They had only weak armor, especially on higher difficulties, but their speed made them among the fastest enemies in Mass Effect 2, fast enough to outrun some powers. The page notes they were likely named after Fenrir (Fenrisulfr), the monstrous wolf of Norse mythology and, appropriately, the son of the god Loki.
