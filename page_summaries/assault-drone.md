Short page: Assault Drones were autonomous drones in Mass Effect 2 used to guard areas against hostile incursions. They were armed with mass accelerator machine guns, did not seek cover, and were highly maneuverable, easily repositioning to flank an opposing force. They carried only a moderate amount of kinetic shielding and were easily destroyed once it was depleted, making shield-stripping powers such as Overload and Disruptor Ammo effective. Being flying enemies, they were immune to kinetic biotic powers like Throw, and they typically attacked in swarms.
