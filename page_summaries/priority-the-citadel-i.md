Priority: The Citadel I followed Priority: Mars. Shepard was to inform the Citadel Council of the Reaper threat and the Prothean device (the Crucible) and convince them to send help to Earth. The Normandy arrived at the Citadel and the critically injured Kaidan Alenko or Ashley Williams was rushed to Huerta Memorial Hospital. Commander Armando-Owen Bailey, newly promoted, welcomed Shepard and directed them to Councilor Donnel Udina's office.

On the Citadel Shepard could visit the hospital and choose a Normandy physician, keeping Dr. Karin Chakwas if she survived the Suicide Mission or recruiting Dr. Chloe Michel; recruit journalist Diana Allers as a war asset; and speak with reporter Khalisah al-Jilani, also a potential war asset. Firing Range, Spectre Requisitions and Spectre Console facilities became available in the embassies.

In the Council Chambers, Liara was already presenting her findings. The non-human councilors were skeptical of the Prothean weapon and, with their own worlds under attack, refused to commit military aid to Earth. Afterward the Turian Councilor reported that the turians had lost contact with Primarch Fedorian, who had been about to call a war summit, and asked Shepard to rescue him. Shepard's Spectre status, if not already restored, was reinstated here.

Returning to the Normandy, Shepard experienced the first of the recurring dream sequences, pursuing the child from Earth through a dead forest and watching him burn. Liara visited the cabin, and new Comm Specialist Samantha Traynor introduced the Alliance-revamped Normandy SR-2 and its new war room, where Admiral Hackett waited to debrief Shepard. The mission ended with the galaxy map opened for the next destination, Priority: Palaven. Hackett's reinstatement email cited Emergency War Powers regulations and granted Shepard diplomatic authority to form treaties with non-human races.
