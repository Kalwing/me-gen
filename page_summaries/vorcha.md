The vorcha were a primitive, aggressive race from Heshtok, known for their unique biology and for living in the galaxy's darker and more dangerous places, such as Omega. Many were trained as mercenaries by the krogan Blood Pack for their savagery and adaptability, while the rest of galactic civilization regarded them as pests and scavengers. They were the shortest-lived known sapient species, with an average lifespan of only 20 years.

Vorcha biology centered on clusters of non-differentiated cells, similar to those of Earth's planarian worm, granting limited regeneration and rapid individual adaptation. When injured or stressed, these cells migrated to the affected area and matured over about a week into specialized forms: thicker skin after burns, stronger heart and leg muscles under high gravity, or lungs better suited to a barely-breathable atmosphere. This made vorcha immune to almost all disease and able to regrow lost limbs over months, but a vorcha could generally adapt to only one environment in its brief life. As a consequence the species no longer evolved; the vorcha equivalent of DNA had been unchanged for millions of years, since individuals could adapt where a species would.

Heshtok was a small, hostile, overcrowded world stripped of resources by successive generations of the fast-breeding species, producing a tight-knit, clan-based society locked in constant war between rival clans over scarce necessities. This incessant warfare made each generation stronger and more aggressive but kept vorcha society extremely primitive. The vorcha did not invent space travel; they were discovered by another spacefaring species and spread rapidly through the Terminus Systems, often as mercenary foot soldiers, most notably for the Blood Pack, who would sweep up pockets of vorcha and beat them into stronger, faster, smarter soldiers. In 2185, some vorcha on Omega allied with the Collectors and shut down the station's life support hoping to rule it. During the 2186 Reaper invasion, the Reapers struggled to harvest Heshtok because the vorcha were not cowed by intimidation and could adapt to resource destruction, so the Reapers focused on immobilizing the population.

Vorcha society was built around combat, which they used, individually and in groups, as their default form of communication, making them primarily non-verbal. They lived communally in clans; when a clan grew too large, younger members left to found a new one. Asari mining corporations once adopted vorcha orphans and raised them non-violently on Parasc, proving their violent communication was not entirely innate, though their short lifespans kept such careers brief. The vorcha had no recognized single government and thus no membership in galactic leagues, and no formal military. During the Reaper War, other races supplied ships and officers to lead vorcha forces: the human- and turian-trained Void Devils fighter group fought well and retreated with discipline at Heshtok, but an alien-led vorcha fleet was annihilated at Lihrat. Known vorcha included Gryll, Kreete, and Shisk. First contact between vorcha and an Alliance crew occurred in 2184.
