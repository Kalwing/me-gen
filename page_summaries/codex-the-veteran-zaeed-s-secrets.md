These Codex entries covered subjects tied to Zaeed Massani's loyalty mission. The M-451 Firestorm was a flamethrower derived from tools human colonists used in the 2160s to clear vegetation and ice, whose fuels performed erratically in cold or non-Earth atmospheres. Systems Alliance intelligence stole the turian "Hieras" flamethrower design, which used low-octane hydrocarbons thickened with dentra oil taken from whale-like marine animals, and humans reverse-engineered a synthetic equivalent that could be fabricated from heavy weapon fuel cells with an omni-tool. The resulting sticky spray burned at about 1,600 degrees Celsius over a wide area, produced choking smoke, and consumed the oxygen inside breached armor, making it among the Alliance's most feared weapons despite its inefficiency.

The full Blue Suns history stated the company was founded in 2160 by human mercenaries Zaeed Massani and Vido Santiago as a Skyllian Verge protection racket. The founders clashed, and after a dispute over recruiting slave-trading batarians, Vido ambushed Zaeed and shot him in the head. Believing Zaeed dead, Vido took full control, hired batarians freely, and installed one, Solem Dal'serah, as titular head of operations to placate batarian investors and draw assassins away from himself. Over decades Vido diversified the Suns into arms, training, and supply sales across Citadel space, the Verge, and the Terminus Systems, and obscured his own identity so thoroughly that few mercenaries knew who he or Zaeed was.

Zorya was a lush garden world first colonized in 2160, its habitable zones choked with pollen and spores ranging from benign to deadly, driving colonists to clear-cutting and slash-and-burn farming while spore-thick rural areas were populated only by vorcha. Lax ecological law let mining and manufacturing pollute cheaply. The Blue Suns dominated Zorya's colony security forces and held nearly unlimited influence with local politicians and judges, blocking rival contractors, though piracy, drugs, vice, and political violence remained commonplace.
