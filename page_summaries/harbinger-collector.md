This page covers Harbinger as an enemy encountered in Mass Effect 2 combat. While possessing the Collector General, Harbinger could also take direct control of an individual Collector soldier on the battlefield, announcing "ASSUMING DIRECT CONTROL." The possessed soldier gained substantial strength and biotic abilities; its skin cracked open with magma-like glow, and the transformation appeared very painful, causing the Collector to twitch and shake.

When Harbinger possessed a Collector, the Harbinger-Collector lost all health but gained fully charged barriers and armor, with no health bar beneath. Harbinger tended to possess the weakest or most damaged minion, and the delay before possessing another was very short inside the Collector Base. Though it carried a weapon, it never fired it, attacking only with three biotic powers: a fast seeking Warp-style projectile dealing heavy damage and staggering targets; a Singularity variant that penetrated thin cover and collapsed when a target entered it, or lingered on empty cover; and a short-ranged Nova-like pulse used only at melee range that could deplete Shepard's shields in one hit and cause staggering or flailing. Higher difficulty increased attack frequency, becoming a near-constant barrage on Insanity. Harbinger never panicked from fire, was immune to freezing and Stasis, almost always advanced to close range, and never took cover unless it could not find a path forward.

Recommended tactics included keeping distance, strafing in cover to make attacks miss, drawing fire away from squadmates who could not dodge, and killing other Collectors first to deny Harbinger new hosts. Effective tools included the Geth Plasma Shotgun and Widow Anti-Material Rifle against its barriers, Kasumi's Flashbang Grenade (which fully suppressed its abilities) and Shadow Strike, Combat Drones for distraction, the M-622 Avalanche and M-490 Blackstorm for prolonged disabling, and Warp Ammo slowdown for headshots. If a Collector under Pull, Stasis, Dominate or a freeze was possessed, those effects still applied; a possessed Collector pulled over open space would fall to its death. On higher difficulties a Vanguard was advised never to Charge Harbinger unless it could be killed immediately. When possessing a Collector in combat, Harbinger delivered a series of battle quotes.
