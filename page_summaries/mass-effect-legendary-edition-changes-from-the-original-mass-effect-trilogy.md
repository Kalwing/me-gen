This page catalogued the differences between the original Mass Effect trilogy and the Mass Effect Legendary Edition, from trivial to substantial, with such changes usually marked in orange text on gameplay-focused wiki pages. Overall improvements included a unified launcher, higher resolutions, more detailed models, real-time reflections, remixed audio, a Photo Mode, numerous bug fixes (some restoring cut content), a character creator for all three games based on the Mass Effect 3 creator with new options drawn partly from Mass Effect: Andromeda, all difficulty levels unlocked from the start, native controller and 21:9 support and DirectX 11 on PC, a raised or removed save limit, and a new mixed set of achievements.

Mass Effect received the most work: a redesigned main menu and HUD, retrofitted assets from Mass Effect 3 and the From Ashes DLC, a turian character model for Elanos Haliat (originally given a human model), a biotic shield explaining Benezia's invulnerability in early rounds, improved UNC worlds, and a dramatically reworked Mako with tweaked physics, more weapon damage, new thrusters, faster shield recharge, and no XP penalty for kills. Combat changes included a new hacking minigame, no omni-gel needed on lock-failure, reliable cover, improved weapon accuracy and aim-down-sights for all classes, individually directable squadmates, button-mapped melee, universal headshot damage, and tougher Thresher Maws. Virmire's final fight added Saren fighting alongside geth waves; the final battle gained more cover and geth waves. Bug fixes corrected the Batarian codex trigger, Emily Wong's presence, and the Casbin console.

Mass Effect 2 changes mostly corrected bugs in importing a Mass Effect playthrough, sometimes restoring cut content such as Galactic News reports about Charles Saracino, the Rogue VI, Sha'ira, and the Sirta Foundation. DLC weapons and armor were integrated into merchants rather than dumped into inventory, morality representation was made accurate, credits carried over at 10% (up to 100,000), thermal clip drop rates and ammo restoration increased, and the Vanguard's Charge benefited from the Biotic Cooldown upgrade. Miranda no longer asked about the council nominee. Mass Effect 3 changes removed Multiplayer and adjusted War Assets so all are treated at 100% effectiveness with only Total Military Strength shown (7400 minimum for the Destroy/Shepard-breathes ending); weapon stats reverted to the pre-patch base game; DLC gear was sold by merchants; Tali's photographed face was replaced; and fixes restored Joker's Paragon handshake goodbye, Ashley's Marksman ability, and the Priority: Mars Eva fight loadout.
