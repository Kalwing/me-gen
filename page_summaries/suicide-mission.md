This is a disambiguation page. It notes that "Suicide Mission" is the name given both formally and informally to the battle that serves as the finale of Mass Effect 2, in which Commander Shepard leads a counterattack against the Collectors, and links to related articles: Omega 4 Relay, Collector Base, Storyline II, the Mass Effect 2 Guide, Stop the Collectors, and the individual mission walkthroughs (The Normandy Attacked, Collector Base: Infiltration, Collector Base: The Long Walk, and Collector Base: Final Battle). Entering the Omega 4 Relay, which no ship had previously returned from, is considered the start of the Suicide Mission.
