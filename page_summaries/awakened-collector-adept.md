The Awakened Collector Adept was a rare survivor of the Collector race and a playable class in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer. According to the page, when the Reaper-killer known as Leviathan fought the Collectors, it severed their connection to Harbinger using a thrall device. Most Collector forces died as a result, but a few individuals survived and afterward fought for the memory of their people, a race the Reapers had broken. The page also noted that the Protheans, from whom the Collectors were derived, appointed avatars to embody and model a single virtue for their society; the Awakened Collectors' virtue was vengeance.

In gameplay terms the Awakened Collector Adept used the "Vengeful Ancient" class, with a barrier of 750 and health of 500. Its powers were Dark Sphere, Seeker Swarm, Dark Channel, and the passive Ancient Warrior, and its passive Vengeful Ancient could grant up to a +65% boost to power damage as well as bonuses to Collector and Prothean weapon damage, making it a strong Particle Rifle user when specced for weapons. It was capable of potent biotic damage, priming multiple Biotic Explosions with Dark Sphere and Dark Channel and detonating them with Seeker Swarms. Its weaknesses were lengthy power cooldowns and long activation animations.

Its melee options included Vengeful Strike, a charged biotic area attack that knocked down unprotected enemies; Ascension Mode, a heavy melee that for 45 seconds raised damage output and recharge speed at the cost of taking more damage; and a grab that dragged an enemy over cover and executed them with a semi-circular omni-blade stab. For movement it used Forewing Leap, a short winged dodge similar to the Propulsion Packs of turian classes, with very quick initial movement but a significant post-use delay. It could take cover and roll between cover. Player notes covered tactics against Cerberus, Collectors, Geth, and Reaper forces, including using Dark Sphere to reveal cloaked Geth Hunters and to hit Guardians and Phantoms through their defenses.
