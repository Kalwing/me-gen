On returning to the Citadel after completing Therum, Noveria, or Feros, Commander Shepard was approached outside the C-Sec Academy lift from Dock 422 by Khalisah Al-Jilani, a reporter for Westerlund News. She said she wanted to show people back home how Shepard would represent humanity as a Spectre. Shepard could refuse, in which case the assignment ended once she left, or agree to the interview. Al-Jilani asked leading questions about Shepard's Spectre status, whether the Citadel Council's interests conflicted with those of the Alliance, and about the development and command of the SSV Normandy. A further question depended on Shepard's completed missions: after Noveria she asked whether the Council had ordered Shepard to sabotage the labs at Peak 15, confused reports having reached the Citadel because of stonewalling by Binary Helix and Administrator Anoleis; after Feros, if the colonists had been killed, she asked why the colony was destroyed. She also asked whether humanity would gain the respect it deserved, and sought confirmation that Shepard was hunting a rogue Spectre. Shepard could use Charm or Intimidate to gain the upper hand, end the interview politely, walk away on realising her agenda, or take a Renegade option to punch her in the face. Afterward, aboard the Normandy, Admiral Hackett transmitted either praise or a reprimand for Shepard's conduct. If Shepard had named Saren Arterius as behind the geth attack on Eden Prime, Hackett noted it had caused a minor diplomatic stir with the Council, which had been trying to downplay Spectre involvement in Eden Prime. A later Citadel elevator news report gave the media's response. Khalisah reappeared for further interviews in Mass Effect 2 in the Zakera Ward and in Mass Effect 3 in the Citadel Embassies, referencing whether Shepard had punched her before. The assignment was written by Chris L'Etoile.
