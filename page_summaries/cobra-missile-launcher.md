The Cobra Missile Launcher was a heavy weapon in Mass Effect 3. Though visually identical to the M-560 Hydra, it was a single-shot launcher that fired a missile directly at its target, powerful enough to destroy a target on contact and producing a small area of effect.

The weapon was exclusive to multiplayer and did not appear in single-player. All players received Cobra launchers at the start of each match provided they had missiles in supply, starting with two per match. Purchasing item packs replenished missile supply and could award capacity boosts. Cobra Missile Launcher Capacity Upgrade cards, found in item packs, permanently raised the carried maximum to six missiles for all of a player's characters, and the Armored Compartments gear item added up to three more, for a maximum of nine.

The Cobra dealt very high damage, higher than the M-920 Cain while firing faster, and could kill any enemy with one hit on any difficulty. Its splash damage penetrated cover and terrain but did not harm the user or allies, making fire at the ground near a moving target a viable tactic. Damage dropped with distance, sometimes failing to kill boss enemies. As a weapon it benefited from weapon damage bonuses and from Infiltrator cloaking. Since the July 24, 2012 multiplayer update it fired instantly on the trigger pull, with no automatic fire. Killing enemies with the Cobra yielded significantly less experience than conventional kills, and allied missiles produced a distinctive whistling sound in flight. It was best reserved for clusters of powerful enemies such as Atlases and Banshees or for mission-critical emergencies.
