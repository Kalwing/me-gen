The Alpha Relay was a mass relay in the Bahak system of the Viper Nebula. It was named "Alpha" by Dr. Amanda Kenson and her research team, who determined it was the oldest known mass relay in the galaxy. Outwardly it appeared to be a standard relay, but it was unusual in range and versatility: it normally sent and received mass at the range of a secondary relay, but with certain controls adjusted it could draw on an unprecedented amount of dark energy to send cargo to sixteen other relays and even across a great distance to the Citadel. The Batarian Hegemony had long known of this capability and covered it up to avoid retaliation by other races who would see the relay as a threat.

After discovering the Reaper artifact Object Rho embedded in a nearby asteroid, Kenson's team learned the Reapers would soon arrive in the system and use Alpha to coordinate a galactic invasion. The team concluded Alpha had to be destroyed, even though this would kill more than 300,000 batarian colonists, because its loss would force the Reapers to detour and delay their arrival by months or years. They established Project Base on the Object Rho asteroid, smuggled in engines and navigation systems from Omega, and planned to ram the asteroid into the relay. Before they could act, the team was indoctrinated by Object Rho. During the events of Arrival in 2185, Commander Shepard activated "the Project" despite Kenson's attempt to sabotage the asteroid's propulsion. The asteroid struck the Alpha Relay and tore it apart, producing an explosion comparable to a supernova that wiped out the Bahak system. In Mass Effect 3, if Shepard did not attempt to rescue Kenson, a wounded batarian reveals that the Alliance destroyed the Alpha Relay to delay the Reaper invasion.
