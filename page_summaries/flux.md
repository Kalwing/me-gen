Flux was a newly opened and already popular nightclub on the Citadel, reached by a set of stairs off the Upper Wards near the Markets. It was owned and operated by a volus named Doran, with a human waitress named Rita. The club had a bar and dance floor on the ground level and a casino on the mezzanine where quasar could be played. Patrons on the ground floor could be overheard gossiping about Chora's Den, the attack on Eden Prime, and humanity's growing galactic influence.

In Mass Effect, Commander Shepard could dance with a love interest on the dance floor, and if the matter of Schells' gambling device was resolved, Doran could later be seen enjoying the dance floor himself. Rita's sister Jenna had worked at Flux before taking a job for Fist at Chora's Den, and returned to Flux once her reasons for being there were resolved. Flux's gambling machines were secretly being siphoned of credits, a trail leading to an artificial intelligence on the Presidium that wished to leave the Citadel. After the Normandy was locked down, David Anderson met Shepard in Flux to discuss getting the ship free to resume the pursuit of Saren Arterius. A twitchy diplomat outside the club sought someone to procure stims for him.

In the first edition of the novel Mass Effect: Deception, Flux was one of the locations Kai Leng visited while searching for Hal McCann, where a patron named Marcy flirted with him before he moved on to the Dark Star Lounge. Assignments associated with Flux included Citadel: Rita's Sister, Citadel: Scan the Keepers, Citadel: Signal Tracking, Citadel: Schells the Gambler, and Citadel: Negotiator's Request.
