"Omega: The Patriarch" was an assignment in Mass Effect 2, available after completing Dossier: Archangel or Dossier: The Professor. On the upper level of Afterlife, Aria T'Loak's lieutenant Grizz asked Shepard to move Aria's old adviser, the Patriarch, to safety because the Blood Pack was trying to kill him. Grizz revealed that Aria protecting the Patriarch herself would look like weakness, so Shepard's involvement masked her intentions.

The Patriarch, a krogan, was found in a small room off the lower level of Afterlife, where he told Shepard how he had lost control of Omega to Aria. After Shepard warned him of the coming attack, there were three outcomes: convince him to let Shepard's squad fight as his krantt, restoring his standing as a powerful krogan (Paragon; Grunt, if present and loyal, would suggest this directly); call him a trophy and goad him into charging out alone to a glorious death (Renegade); or tell him to hide until the danger passed (neutral, the outcome Aria wanted).

Unless the Patriarch was sent into hiding, two krogan mercenaries confronted Shepard at an Afterlife exit and were quickly gunned down by the squad in a cutscene, one attempting to use an M-451 Firestorm. Grizz's and Aria's responses varied by route; both the Paragon and Renegade routes led Aria to give Shepard coordinates to a cache on an uncharted world, but only if Omega: Archangel: Datapad Recovered was already complete. Rewards were 40 (50) experience and 3000 credits. It was internally called "(Omega:) The Oracle." Cut ambient mercenary voicelines existed but went unused.
