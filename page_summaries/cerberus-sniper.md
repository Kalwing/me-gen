Cerberus Snipers were operatives employed by Cerberus to defend its installations and research facilities in the first Mass Effect. They guarded the exterior and interior of key sites on Binthu and Nepheron, where Cerberus conducted research on captured organisms including Rachni Workers, Rachni Soldiers and Thorian Creepers. Armed with sniper rifles, they preferred to keep their distance and frequently used the Assassination ability, though its red sighting laser telegraphed the shot and made it easy to dodge. Because they held back at range, dislodging them usually required biotic or tech attacks. The page notes equivalent enemies elsewhere in the series: the Sniper in Mass Effect: Infiltrator and the Nemesis in Mass Effect 3.
