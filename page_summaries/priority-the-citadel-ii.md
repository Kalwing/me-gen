Acquired immediately after the events on Tuchanka and triggered by Shepard's next approach to the Citadel, this Mass Effect 3 mission responded to the salarian councilor's warning that Councilor Udina was a security risk. The Normandy was refused landing clearance; Joker contacted either Thane Krios or Armando-Owen Bailey inside the station, who reported that Cerberus had attacked and seized C-Sec Headquarters and the Docks. Shepard deployed with a squad and fought uphill through Assault Troopers, Centurions, Combat Engineers, Guardians, Turrets and newly encountered Phantoms and Nemeses, discovering that Cerberus had again used sleeper agents planted inside a target, as on Mars.

Reaching the C-Sec Executor's office, Shepard found the executor dead and the salarian councilor (Valern if the Council was saved during Sovereign's attack, otherwise Esheel) hiding under cloak. The councilor revealed that Udina was leading a coup against the Council before Kai Leng arrived. If Thane was alive and had been visited at Huerta Memorial, he intervened and was fatally stabbed shielding the councilor; if not, a cloaked Kirrahe took the blow and died; if neither was present, Leng killed the councilor. Bailey unlocked a C-Sec car, Leng disabled it with his sword, and Shepard pursued Udina and the Council on foot through the Presidium Commons, an Atlas fight, and a final zone where Cerberus reinforcements arrived by elevator cars that could be halted by shooting their power conduits.

At the landing pad the Virmire survivor (Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko), now a Spectre, blocked Shepard's path with weapon raised. A trust total built from prior choices (romance, saving the Council, keeping Kirrahe or Thane alive, hospital visits, kindness on Mars) plus a reputation check determined whether they stood down or forced a confrontation. Udina refused to step away from the door console, shoved the asari Councilor, and drew on her; Shepard or the Virmire survivor shot him dead. Kai Leng reported the failure to the Illusive Man.

Afterward, Thane could be attended in his final moments at Huerta Memorial with Kolyat present; Bailey lamented C-Sec's inability to catch Leng; Priority: Perseus Veil and numerous Citadel meetings and War Assets (asari fleets, and salarian forces if the councilor was saved) opened up. Anderson emailed the Alliance dossier on Kai Leng, and reading it before meeting Miranda let Shepard warn her of Leng, preventing her later death. The Virmire survivor could rejoin the squad or serve as a War Asset under Hackett.
