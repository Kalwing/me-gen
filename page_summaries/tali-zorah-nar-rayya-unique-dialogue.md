This page collects Tali'Zorah's squad-specific lines across all three games: unique dialogue at scripted story points, "talk to squadmate" location comments, spontaneous combat and banter lines, and secret dialogue only reachable by editing her into the party. The material recurringly touches quarian themes (the Migrant Fleet, homelessness, the geth, her mistrust of the Council and Cerberus), her fear of spiders, her drone Chatika vas Paus, and her running commentary comparing alien worlds to the flotilla. Representative lines:

- Tali, on Noveria when Captain Matsuo says Shepard is far from home: "Home is a state of mind."
- Tali, first seeing the Thorian on Feros: "Keelah, what is that?"
- Tali, at the salarian camp on Virmire before the assault: "These salarians are willing to die to stop Saren... and so am I." / (if Wrex lives) "I will fight with fire for you, Commander."
- Tali, on Ilos: "We shouldn't be here. Not us. Not the geth. Not Saren."
- Tali, if chosen to lead the second diversion squad and killed during the Suicide Mission: "There were too many. Lost my shields. Suit ruptures... too many... Keelah se'lai..."
- Tali, summoning her combat drone: "Go for the optics, Chatika! Go for the optics!" / "Nothing's faster than Chatika vas Paus!"
- Tali, using AI Hacking on geth: "We made you, we can break you!"
- Tali, in the Silver Coast Casino when a female Shepard comments on her enviro-suit: "At least you can dress up. What can I do? I'm already wearing my two sexiest belts!"
- Tali, to the Shepard clone in the Citadel Archives after it threatens to open her helmet: "just a cheap imitation of the real thing."
- Tali, spontaneously on the Attican Traverse krogan mission when Swarmers appear: "spiders, spiders, spiders, spiders!"
- Tali, cursing Kai Leng on Thessia: "Bosh'tet!"
- Tali, disembarking the Normandy after the Citadel ambush: "Worst shore leave ever."
