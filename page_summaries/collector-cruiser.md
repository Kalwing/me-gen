A Collector Cruiser was a massive vessel able to carry large numbers of Collectors, husks, Abominations, Scions, Praetorians, and Seeker Swarms, along with enough stasis pods to capture every human colony in the Terminus Systems and gather more from Earth; the Collectors were known to possess several, and it was the only known Collector ship type. The ships dwarfed both Normandy vessels and combined organic protrusions with a metallic superstructure, with cave-like hive interiors and vast open areas traversed by floating hexagonal platforms; despite their size they could land vertically on planets, using an enormous thruster to take off quickly. Their main weapon was a large particle beam capable of multi-directional fire, either in quick succession or as a single devastating blast, that ignored kinetic barriers and could critically damage an Alliance frigate in a few shots; some cruisers also carried "eye"-shaped lateral point-defense weapons for retaliatory fire. Their sensors could detect stealth-equipped ships such as the Normandy, and an Identify Friend/Foe system let them traverse the Omega 4 Relay unharmed, though sustained GARDIAN turret fire could still bring one down.

The Collectors used cruisers to abduct human colonists, approaching quietly, disabling communications and sensors, releasing Seeker Swarms to paralyze the population, and gathering them into pupae-like pods. A cruiser ambushed and destroyed the SSV Normandy shortly after Sovereign's defeat, killing Commander Shepard, Navigator Pressly, and twenty other crew. The same ship attacked Horizon, capturing about a third of the colony before the resurrected Shepard drove it off using the colony's defenses. It was later found seemingly derelict after reportedly being disabled by a turian patrol; Shepard's team boarded it, confirmed from a Collector corpse that the Collectors were Protheans, saw millions of pods suggesting Earth was a target, and escaped a sprung trap with database intel revealing the need for an IFF code to use the Omega 4 Relay. After Shepard acquired a Reaper IFF, a cruiser docked with the Normandy using a Reaper virus from the IFF, and its boarding parties of Scions, Praetorians, and Collector soldiers killed or captured the entire crew except Joker, who escaped with EDI's help. During the assault on the Collector Base, Joker fought a cruiser in a dogfight; an upgraded Thanix cannon destroyed it at long range in two shots, otherwise the Normandy crippled it at point-blank range while taking severe damage, and the explosion disabled the Normandy's mass effect fields, forcing the crash landing on the base. In Mass Effect: Paragon Lost, a cruiser lured to Fehl Prime by a planted artifact harvested the colony, deflected and destroyed its defense cannon with an eye weapon, and after its drive core was disabled crashed onto the colony, killing everyone aboard.
