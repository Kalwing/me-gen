The M-76 Revenant was a heavy assault rifle (effectively a machine gun) in Mass Effect 2, Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Andromeda. It unleashed a storm of high-velocity slugs with low accuracy but very high ammo capacity and firepower, effective against armor, shields and barriers. This custom-made weapon used sophisticated Fabrication Rights Management technology to prevent replication, so only the richest and most powerful warlords could afford it. In Mass Effect 2 it upgraded the M-15 Vindicator.

In Mass Effect 2 the Revenant was only usable by a Soldier-class Shepard who chose it during the Collector Ship mission; squadmates could not use it. It had the largest magazine and spare ammo capacity of any assault rifle, excelling at taking down large armored enemies like Scions, YMIR mechs, gunships and Harbinger, though the Assault Rifle Accuracy upgrade was needed to tame its spread. It was carried by some Blue Suns commanders, Warden Kuril, Jentha, Geth Primes, Aria's henchmen and the Shadow Broker.

In Mass Effect 3 it was found during Priority: The Citadel II in the C-Sec locker room bathroom and was categorized as rare in multiplayer. It carried fewer rounds per clip, reloaded slower and had harsher recoil and reduced accuracy, but still had one of the highest capacities of any assault rifle, suited to suppressive fire; firing it slowed the wielder's movement. In Andromeda, Initiative engineers studied and illegally duplicated it as a fully automatic armor-shredder. A revenant is a corpse or spirit brought back from the dead.
