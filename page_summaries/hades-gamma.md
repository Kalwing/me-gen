Hades Gamma was a cluster in Earth Systems Alliance space along an outer arm of the Milky Way, appearing in both Mass Effect and Mass Effect 3. The Anansi-Ishtar shipping lane ran through its Farinata/Cacus side, passing near Farinata and using Treyarmus as a discharge point. Quarian starships often exploited the heavy shipping traffic near Treyarmus to sell salvage or refreshments, and the dense merchant traffic made the route a popular hiding place for unregistered ships wishing to obscure their signals. The cluster contained the systems Antaeus, Cacus, Dis, Farinata, and Plutus, and in the first game hosted numerous UNC assignments. During the Reaper invasion of 2186 the Systems Alliance military abandoned the cluster early, and it was considered hostile territory by the time Commander Shepard began the search for allies to save Earth. The cluster appears to represent the Crab Nebula; its systems are all named for characters or places from Dante's Inferno.
