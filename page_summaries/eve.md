Eve, also known as Urdnot Bakara, was a female krogan shaman and the only survivor of Maelon's experiments on Tuchanka in 2185 to cure the genophage. She and other female krogan were recovered by the salarian Special Tasks Group after it was shut down and taken to Sur'Kesh. Her birth name was Urdnot Bakara, surrendered when she became shaman of the female clan; as a rite she had been locked in a dark cave for a week and clawed her way out, aided by a crystal shard she found. Having never revealed her true name to an outsider, she was named "Eve" by her salarian doctor, Mordin Solus or Padok Wiks, who felt human mythology was appropriate aboard a human ship. Her guiding line was "In the darkest hour, there is always a way out."

In Mass Effect 3, Primarch Victus called a war summit, and the krogan refused a turian alliance unless the genophage was cured. The Urdnot leader exposed the salarian conspiracy holding the surviving females on Sur'Kesh, and Shepard and Victus convinced Dalatrass Linron to release them. At the STG base Shepard's squad found Bakara the only survivor, the others having died of complications. Cerberus attacked, tipped off to the transfer, and the squad guided her pod to safety; freed, she seized the Urdnot leader's shotgun and killed two troopers, telling Wrex "I can handle myself" or Wreav "I'm not your property Wreav."

Aboard the Normandy she recovered in the Medical Bay while the cure was synthesized, offering commentary on female krogan and Tuchanka and giving Shepard her crystal shard. She befriended her salarian doctor. On Tuchanka, a confrontation at the Hollows over a salarian's involvement was averted by her speech that the clans must fight the Reapers together. During the joint turian-krogan attack on the Destroyer guarding the Shroud, the operation went wrong; Eve proposed summoning the thresher maw Kalros using the two largest Maw Hammers to distract the Reaper while the cure was completed. She entered a lab with her doctor to finish the cure.

If Shepard saved Maelon's data, Eve survived the procedure and Shepard gained strong krogan support; she later revealed her birth name and pledged lifelong friendship, and emailed from the Kelphic Valley: "We are finally free. The age of suffering is over... There is reason to hope. This, I owe to you, Commander." If Shepard destroyed Maelon's data, Eve died of complications regardless of whether the cure was sabotaged, sparking conflict among the clans and a funeral pyre at the Hollows. Even with the genophage cured, she could later die if Shepard failed to help Lieutenant Victus disarm a rearmed Krogan Rebellions bomb, which obliterated her and the krogan gathered in the Kelphic Valley. If Wrex survived, he later revealed Eve was pregnant with their children, insisting on naming their firstborn after Mordin if he sacrificed himself. Alive or dead, she featured in the ending: handing a krogan baby to its parent, or, if Wreav led and synthesis was not chosen, refusing to cooperate with his plans for domination, leading to a standoff between his faction and her females.
