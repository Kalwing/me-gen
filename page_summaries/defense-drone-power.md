Defense Drone was a tech power in Mass Effect 3's single-player mode. It deployed a holographic drone that hovered above the user's head and electrocuted enemies within a short range, dealing light damage. At rank 1 it had a 2.36-second recharge, 25 drone damage, and a 5-meter range, with a baseline duration of 45 seconds. Higher ranks increased recharge speed, damage, duration (doubling it to 90 seconds), range, and attack rate, with rank 6 evolutions offering either Incapacitate (30% chance to knock down an electrocuted enemy) or Chain Lightning (damaging up to two additional nearby targets).

Unlike the Combat Drone, the Defense Drone had no hitpoints, could not be targeted by enemies, and followed the user until combat ended. Its main value at higher difficulties was crowd control: its shock could briefly stun basic infantry such as Cannibals and Centurions roughly every 2.25 seconds, keeping a single target perpetually staggered regardless of shields, barriers, or armor. It could interrupt melee animations (except those of the CAT6 Specialist) but not sync kills or Husk grabs. Cerberus Phantoms were immune to the stun whether or not their barriers were up, though they were vulnerable to the Incapacitate knockdown when unprotected. Most heavily armored enemies and the two final bosses of the Citadel DLC were immune. The drone could not shoot through cover and would not target cloaked enemies on its own, though it could hit them the instant they decloaked, making it useful against Geth Hunters. It could attack while the user was stunned or cloaked without breaking Tactical Cloak, and it could kill Swarmers without spending ammunition. The rank 5 Attack Rate evolution was bugged, actually decreasing attack rate by 50%, and was advised against. Tali's Defense Drone remained active beside her body and kept attacking even if she was downed. The power set up and detonated no Power Combos.

In single-player it was available to Tali'Zorah vas Normandy and to Commander Shepard as a bonus power. It was not available in multiplayer.
