The Patriarch was a krogan adviser and "trophy" of Aria T'Loak, and the former ruler of the Omega space station. He claimed to have lived since the Krogan Rebellions, telling the asari Jinn that he remembered the hordes at their peak and younglings in breeding pools taking up weapons to kill weaker siblings. He came to power on Omega by killing the turian who ran the station before him.

When Aria arrived at Omega, the Patriarch hired her as an exotic dancer, unaware of her ambition. She rose to become one of his most trusted lieutenants over about a hundred years, until he grew suspicious and tried to eliminate her. His men, having grown accustomed to benefits from working with Aria's asari, abandoned him. Aria challenged him to destructive single combat in the Afterlife Club and won by crushing one of his hearts, seizing Omega. She kept the warlord alive as a "trophy" and a warning to rivals, mockingly naming him "Patriarch", a word with no meaning in her language, though she eventually came to respect it.

By the time of Mass Effect 2 the Patriarch spent his days telling old war stories and had completely submitted to Aria's authority. Aria asked Commander Shepard, through her bodyguard Grizz, to protect him from Blood Pack assassins sent to kill him and undermine her. Shepard could persuade him to go into hiding as Aria wished, kill the assassins on his behalf as his krantt to partly restore his political standing on Omega, or chastise him for his defeatist attitude, prompting him to charge off for a last stand against the assassins, in which he died. If he survived and regained status, a krogan in the Silver Coast Casino during the Mass Effect 3: Citadel missions could be heard praising the Patriarch's greatness. He was voiced by Jim Cummings, who also voiced Urdnot Wreav.
