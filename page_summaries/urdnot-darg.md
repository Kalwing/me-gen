Urdnot Darg was a male krogan of Clan Urdnot on Tuchanka, employed by the clan as a scout with the rank of Scout, Second Class. During Mordin Solus's loyalty mission in 2185, the Chief Scout gave Commander Shepard intelligence about Clan Weyrloc and asked the Commander to watch for one of his missing scouts. Shepard found Darg during the mission and discovered he had been captured by the Blood Pack and tricked into becoming a test subject for Maelon, who was researching a genophage cure at the Weyrloc camp. Darg had been poisoned in the process. Shepard could convince him to return to the Urdnot camp or leave him alone.

If Shepard successfully convinced Darg to leave, he later sent the Commander a grateful e-mail titled "Back from female camp." In it he wrote that he remembered little of what happened but that the chief scout said Shepard "pulled my quad out of the fire" after he was caught and poisoned at the Weyrloc camp. He promised that the next time he had a chance to kill a human, he would not, "unless I go into blood rage or something." He added that he had visited the female camp after recovering and that it was "pretty good," and that although he had been thinking of joining the Blood Pack before his capture, he had decided to stay with the clan instead.
