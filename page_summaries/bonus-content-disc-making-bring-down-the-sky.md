The page transcribed the documentary "Making Bring Down the Sky" from the 2008 Mass Effect bonus content disc. Interviewees were project manager Corey Andruko, lead level designer Dusty Everman, principal lead programmer David Falkner, principal project manager Yanick Roy, art director Derek Watts, and BioWare/EA executive Greg Zeschuk.

Greg Zeschuk said the downloadable content was meant to show players something new and expand their view of the Mass Effect universe. Corey Andruko said the same core team built the module, with planning beginning before Mass Effect was finished, and that development took about eight months total; because the team knew the intellectual property, the content could be integrated seamlessly. David Falkner said they wanted a bridge story immediately after the main game that felt like a continuation of Shepard's adventure.

Dusty Everman explained that the module could not alter the core planets or main story, so it mirrored and hinted at events coming in Mass Effect 2 and the novels; the player arrives near Terra Nova at the right moment for an adventure involving the batarians, who had been previously mentioned. Everman characterized the batarians in the module as terrorists, reflecting real-world bad blood between humans and batarians. Yanick Roy said the batarians were chosen as the villains because they had been introduced in the novel Revelation, and were written as killing civilians for its own sake; the setting used an asteroid with the planet looming as the player is about to crash.

Derek Watts described designing the batarians with four eyes rather than eight, distinctive nostrils, and a particular head shape, crediting artist Matt for the model, and said the rocket launch site reused the mining entrance from Therum with added supports. Everman detailed new challenges: shielded turrets, a minefield, tougher Mako combat, and batarians with war varren and extra combat dialogue.
