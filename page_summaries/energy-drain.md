Energy Drain was a tech power in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, in both single-player and multiplayer. It traveled instantly in a straight line from the user to the target, draining an enemy's kinetic barrier or synthetic power to restore the user's shields.

In Mass Effect 2 it dealt its listed damage to synthetic armor, barriers, and synthetic health, and double damage to shields, with no organic health damage. It could provide the largest shield restorations in the game, but only restored the user's shields when it hit enemy shields or unarmored synthetics. Hitting a shield or synthetic also temporarily increased the user's maximum shield or barrier by an amount equal to the power's listed damage for 30 seconds. Rank 4 evolutions were Heavy Energy Drain (much greater disruption to synthetics and shields) and Area Drain (radius widened to 3 meters to hit multiple targets). It was available to Shepard via Advanced Training and to Tali'Zorah vas Neema as a loyalty power.

In Mass Effect 3 it dealt triple damage to shields and barriers, normal damage to synthetic armor, and half damage to organic health and armor. It restored shields based on actual damage dealt whenever it hit an enemy with barriers, shields, or any synthetic, and could hit up to two targets. It no longer boosted maximum shields. It primed enemies for Tech Bursts, could detonate fire, cryo, and tech combos under certain conditions, and reliably stunned or electrocuted unshielded infantry. Rank 6 offered a Damage or Armor Boost evolution, the latter granting a temporary 40% damage reduction for 10 seconds. It was available to Tali'Zorah vas Normandy and to Shepard as a bonus power, and in multiplayer to the N7 Paladin Sentinel, Salarian Engineer, and Salarian Infiltrator. It was widely recommended as an anti-shield and survival tool for classes lacking shield-stripping ability, such as Adepts, Vanguards, and Infiltrators.
