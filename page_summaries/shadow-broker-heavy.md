Shadow Broker Heavies were the heavy hitters of the Shadow Broker's private army, tasked with defending the Broker's interests. They were encountered by Shepard, Liara T'Soni and the squad during the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, appearing on Illium and at the Broker's base on Hagalaz.

They were armed with the ML-77 Missile Launcher, though its missiles did not seek. They had moderate health and, on higher difficulties, additional light shielding. They tended to hang back and only expose themselves briefly to fire, so sniper rifles or other long-range weapons were recommended; they could also inflict friendly-fire damage on their own squad. Like other missile-armed enemies they fired two shots in quick succession then retreated to cover, and once their shields were down Pull, Shockwave or Incinerate could dislodge them.
