Lantar Sidonis was a turian and former member of Garrus Vakarian's vigilante team that fought crime on Omega. As told in Mass Effect: Homeworlds, Garrus found Sidonis being beaten by a Blood Pack krogan in the lower levels of Afterlife, helped him fight off the mercs, and the two formed a squad of twelve vigilantes that significantly lowered Omega's crime rate. Sidonis was later captured by the Blue Suns and coerced into luring Garrus away with a false lead on the Blood Pack; while Garrus was gone the Blue Suns attacked the hideout and killed the rest of the squad, and Sidonis vanished before Garrus found the bodies.

In Mass Effect 2, Garrus tracked Sidonis's whereabouts and learned his disappearance had been arranged by someone called "Fade," who turned out to be the former C-Sec officer Harkin. After interrogating Harkin, Garrus and Commander Shepard forced him to arrange a meeting with Sidonis at the Citadel's Orbital Lounge, where Garrus set up a sniping position. Shepard could lead Sidonis into Garrus's line of sight, in which case Garrus shot him dead and C-Sec investigated how the weapon reached the Citadel; Sidonis had no known family and no funeral. Alternatively, Shepard could warn Sidonis, who was revealed to be suffering survivor's guilt over his unwilling betrayal; he apologized to Garrus and later turned himself in to C-Sec, confessing to the murders, though conviction was uncertain since the killings occurred on Omega outside Council jurisdiction.
