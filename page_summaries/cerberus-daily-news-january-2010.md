This archive covered only the final week of January 2010 (entries dated 26 through 31), the earliest Cerberus Daily News reports, with three of the days translated from the German Mass Effect Wiki. Blue Suns mercenaries, led by company head Solem Dal'serah, seized Eldfell Ashland Energy Corporation facilities on the planet Zorya over unpaid fees, valued by experts at more than 1.2 billion credits; energy magnate Jonah Ashland called for the dispute to be settled in court. The human colony Cyrene, a moon settlement circling the gas giant Idmon with about 5,000 residents, vanished without a trace, becoming the seventh human colony to disappear in the Terminus Systems since 2183; investigator Simon Haute described it as "a genuine Roanoke," while Alliance inspector Owen Adams admitted there were no leads and Terminus locals reacted with indifference, blaming slavers or pirates. Federal investigators on the planet Trident uncovered a Cerberus cell and seized its laboratory, freeing two dozen prisoners who said the human-survivalist group had dosed them with biotic-suppressing drugs that risked brain damage; survivors described a "torture den" where captives were forced to perform biotic actions to exhaustion and at least one man died of a cerebral hemorrhage. Only one operative was captured, six were killed, and two committed suicide. Finally, the 24th Fighter Group of the Citadel Fleet received the Galactic Unit Citation, the highest honor for a military unit, for its role in the Battle of the Citadel two years earlier; the group was the first to engage the geth flagship Sovereign and, unable to pierce its kinetic barriers, sacrificed most of its pilots distracting the warship while the Destiny Ascension evacuated the Citadel Council. Only one-sixth of the unit survived.
