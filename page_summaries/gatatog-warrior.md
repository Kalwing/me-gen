Gatatog Warriors were krogan members of Clan Gatatog encountered by Commander Shepard and Grunt on Tuchanka during Grunt's loyalty mission, shortly after his Rite of Passage. Arriving with their leader Gatatog Uvenk, they attempted to persuade Grunt to join Clan Gatatog instead of Clan Urdnot; when he refused, they opened fire, and Shepard killed all of them along with Uvenk. In combat they engaged at short range with shotguns using Incendiary Ammo, closed distance with the krogan charge, and fired Carnage rockets at range. They had strong armor and krogan regeneration and were highly aggressive, advancing rather than taking cover. Incendiary powers, Warp, Reave, and the Firestorm were noted as effective against them.
