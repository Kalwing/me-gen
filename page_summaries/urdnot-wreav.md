Urdnot Wreav was Urdnot Wrex's broodbrother, sharing the same mother but born a decade apart. He became leader of Clan Urdnot if Wrex died on Virmire or never joined Commander Shepard's crew. More traditional than Wrex, Wreav prized his own clan over the krogan race as a whole and preferred war to peace, summed up in his line, "Tuchanka and the krogan live together in war. The planet hates us, and we hate it."

In Mass Effect 2, Shepard first met Wreav on Tuchanka, where he thanked the Commander for killing his brother, since it made him clan leader. Because he was a traditionalist, rivals like Gatatog Uvenk posed little challenge. He permitted Grunt to join Clan Urdnot after the Rite of Passage, pleased that the resulting hatred would be "influential." In Mass Effect 3, if Wreav was leader he was aggressive and racist toward turians and salarians, treated Eve and female krogan as property, and kept Jorgal Thurak as his second. Liara, via Glyph, revealed that Wreav had commissioned more weapons of mass destruction than any krogan in known history and had used some against other clans, though Urdnot retained a conventional stockpile. His diplomacy relied on the threat of krogan invasion. During the land assault on the Shroud, Wreav's tomkah was sunk by Kalros, the mother of all thresher maws, and he was believed dead, to Wrex's indifference: "he was a pain in the ass, anyway."

Once the genophage was cured, Wreav claimed credit, spreading stories of "the day Wreav cured the genophage" to cement his authority. At the final battle on Earth he ordered krogan to cooperate with allied forces while studying their tactics, implying future hostile intent. If Shepard hid the salarian sabotage and Wreav was leader, he never learned the cure was fake and pledged full krogan support, and Shepard also gained the salarian First Fleet. During Priority: Tuchanka he told Shepard to "fight like a krogan," and during Priority: Earth he might demand ownership of Australia in exchange for krogan military aid.
