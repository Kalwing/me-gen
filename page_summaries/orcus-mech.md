The Orcus Mech was a Cerberus security mech unit found on the Barn space station and fought by Randall Ezno during his escape, as depicted in the Mass Effect: Foundation comics tie-in content. It was a bipedal, humanoid robot, an apparent modification of the LOKI Mech, distinguished by larger shoulder plates and armed with the M-96 Mattock Auto rather than the M-4 Shuriken or M-3 Predator.

Orcus Mechs were the only enemy that possessed health alone, with no shields or armor of any kind; to compensate for this fragility they were always deployed in large groups. They carried powerful rifles that discouraged rushing them, so the recommended tactics were to pick them off at range with the Mattock or Incisor, use chain kills to clear several at once, exploit their vulnerability to headshots, and use biotic powers like Salvo or Pull to avoid being overwhelmed. Some groups in the Medical Bay emerged from the ground and closed distance quickly.
