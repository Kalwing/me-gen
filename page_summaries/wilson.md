Wilson was a Cerberus medical officer who worked on the Lazarus Project alongside Miranda Lawson. While Miranda ran the project overall, Wilson was attached to the bio wing as Chief Medical Officer, overseeing the effort to rebuild the dead Commander Shepard. While the reconstruction was still underway, the Lazarus Research Station came under attack by rogue security mechs, forcing the not-yet-fully-healed Shepard to fight out of the facility.

Shepard first found Wilson with a gunshot wound to the leg and treated him with medi-gel, after which more mechs swarmed the position. Wilson suggested overloading nearby volatile crates and helped carve a path toward the station's escape shuttle. At the shuttle bay doors he found Miranda waiting, and she shot him in the throat, claiming that Wilson had been the one who hacked the mechs. Shepard, Jacob Taylor and Miranda then evacuated the station.

Wilson's motive was initially unknown; logs he narrated show frustration with Miranda's standoffish manner, suggesting personal grievance or an attempt to extract privileged information. Files later recovered at the Shadow Broker's base revealed that Wilson had in fact been working for the Shadow Broker. Per the wiki's trivia, Wilson continued a BioWare tradition (like Corporal Jenkins before him) of an early squadmate-style character killed and effectively replaced by someone with the same abilities, in this case Miranda. He was voiced by Steve Blum, who also voiced Grunt. Cut voicelines have Wilson notice his access code has been revoked, realize only Miranda could have done it, and panic before she silences him.
