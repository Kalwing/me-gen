The Seeker Swarm was a Collector enemy unit introduced in Mass Effect 3: Retaliation. Collector Captains deployed Swarms to disable players' abilities, and a Captain could have only one active at a time; Possessed Captains deployed the enhanced Seeker Plague variant.

Once launched, a Seeker Swarm chased players in order to explode near them. The blast had a fairly small radius but penetrated terrain, dealing a small amount of damage, disabling cooldown-based powers for a short time, and significantly reducing weapon accuracy for anyone caught in it. NPC squadmates were immune to the disabling effects. Powers without cooldowns, such as Nova, Phase Disruptor, and grenade-type powers, were unaffected, as were already-readied "charge" powers such as Biotic Orbs and Siege Pulse. The Marksman power, while active, made its user immune to the disabling effect. In multiplayer the disable lasted from 6 seconds on Bronze up to 12 seconds on Platinum, with the same figures for Seeker Plagues; being hit again while already disabled reapplied the full duration.

Seeker Swarms moved fast and were hard to target due to their shape and erratic movement, could climb short drops and ladders, and had low but deceptively high-for-their-size health. They could not be frozen or placed in Stasis. Area-of-effect powers such as Shockwave, Electric Slash, and Inferno Grenade destroyed them quickly, as did explosive weapons like the Adas Anti-Synthetic Rifle, M-37 Falcon, and Striker Assault Rifle, and Singularity could immobilize and eventually destroy one. As a swarm prepared to detonate it briefly stopped and vibrated; moving away during that pause could make it dissipate while preserving power use. As summoned units, swarm kills did not count as enemy kills for certain purposes, did not contribute to krogan Rage or vorcha Bloodlust, and did not need to be cleared to finish a multiplayer wave. Swarms lasted until killed or detonated.
