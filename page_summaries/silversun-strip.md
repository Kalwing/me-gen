The Silversun Strip was a hub location on the Citadel Wards introduced in Mass Effect 3's Citadel downloadable content. It contained the Armax Arsenal Arena, Shepard's Personal Apartment, the Silver Coast Casino, Castle Arcade, a bar, and a Rapid Transit point, and served as a second social hub where Shepard could meet current and former squad members after receiving e-mail invitations. These encounters depended on the ally's own mission being complete, and some allies had a first meeting on the Strip and a later one in the Apartment.

Points of interest included the Personal Apartment (with the Home Spun kiosk and, for a Spacer Shepard, the ability to call Hannah Shepard), the Armax Arsenal Arena (with a vorcha Three-card Monte hustler outside and procurement and weapon-upgrade terminals), Castle Arcade (featuring Shattered Eezo, the Claw Game, Relay Defense, and a powered-down Towers of Hanoi), the Silver Coast Casino (Quasar, roulette, varren racing, and Kasumi Goto's suspicious device), and the bar where Citadel: Party supplies were sold and Garrus Vakarian, Ashley Williams, Joker, and Urdnot Wrex could be met.

Missions staged here included Citadel: Shore Leave, Citadel: Identity Theft I and II, Silver Coast Casino: Infiltration, Citadel: Party, and numerous Armax Arsenal Arena challenges. Notable background NPCs included Francis Kitt promoting a krogan-led production of Macbeth and an out-of-work elcor actor who quoted Hamlet. Arena and arcade high-score boards listed characters such as Aria T'Loak, Barla Von, Khalisah al-Jilani, Bray, Matriarch Aethyta, Schells, Armando-Owen Bailey, Mouse, and James Vega. Several overheard conversations referenced Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode.
