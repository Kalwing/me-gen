Bring Down the Sky was the first downloadable content pack for the original Mass Effect, released on March 10, 2008. It was one of only two DLC packs for the game, the other being Pinnacle Station. The mission took place in the newly added Asgard System of the Exodus Cluster, where a batarian extremist group had hijacked a mobile asteroid station and set it on a collision course with the nearby human colony world of Terra Nova, threatening millions of civilians. Commander Shepard and the Normandy crew were tasked with stopping the asteroid before it completed its descent. The pack notably introduced the batarians to the games.

The content comprised one new system, three assignments (X57: Bring Down the Sky, X57: Missing Engineers, and X57: Avoid the Blasting Caps), roughly 90 minutes of gameplay, and the Colonial Savior achievement worth 50 gamerscore. It originally sold for 400 Microsoft Points on Xbox 360 and 400 BioWare Points on PC, with the PC version initially requiring a one-time code for free redemption. The Xbox 360 price was later cut to about US$1, and the pack was eventually bundled free with the Platinum Hits Edition of Mass Effect, the PC and PlayStation 3 editions of Mass Effect Trilogy, and all editions of Mass Effect Legendary Edition. PC players who no longer had a code could obtain the DLC installer through EA's help page without authorization.
