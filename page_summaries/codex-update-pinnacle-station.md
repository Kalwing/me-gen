The page collected four secondary Codex entries added by the Pinnacle Station DLC for the first Mass Effect. The Combat Simulator entry explained that military organizations trained soldiers for varied environments using holographic projectors to depict opponents and obstacles, with kinetic barriers preventing participants from passing through the simulated terrain and also reproducing weapon damage and shockwaves; government-sponsored combat simulators were legally required to use extensive safety protocols to prevent serious or permanent injury. The Pinnacle Station entry described the site as a turian space station retrofitted by the Council as a military training facility, where invitees took part in a series of simulated combat encounters and competed against other participants. The Prefabricated Structures entry noted that colonizing corporations often deployed "pre-fab" residential structures, which featured full living quarters and a large solarium for observing the planet's surface, to quickly establish a base of operations. It added that while most human colonies had Alliance military or corporate defense-contractor support, many civilian colonists kept a small stock of personal armaments in their homes as a last resort, often critical to holding off a pirate attack until support arrived.
