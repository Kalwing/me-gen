Chairman Martin Burns was head of the Alliance Parliamentary Subcommittee for Transhuman Studies, whose purview most notably covered human biotics. When the Subcommittee held a vote on whether to pay reparations to L2 biotics with severe health problems, Burns voted against and presumably vetoed the proposal. A group of L2 biotics wrote letters asking him to reconsider but were ignored. Growing desperate and angry, the L2s kidnapped Burns, took him aboard the MSV Ontario, and attempted to hide in the asteroid belt of the Farinata system.

The Normandy caught up with the Ontario before it reached the belt, and Commander Shepard led a rescue party aboard. Burns was held at gunpoint in the back of the freighter, begging for his life and claiming he had changed his mind, but the L2s were prepared to execute him anyway. If pushed, the L2s killed Burns before being killed by the squad; alternatively, Shepard could argue that the L2s needed him alive to enact real change, and if he survived, Burns claimed he had not realized how desperate they were and vowed the reparations would come.

In Mass Effect 2, if Burns was rescued, he kept his word: L2 biotics received reparations for their implants. In a news report he thanked biotics everywhere and promised more help for people "forgotten by the system," and he sent Shepard a message, routed through Udina, titled "Thank you again." In it he admitted he had taken the job for power and had been wrong, pledged to help humanity reach its potential with minimum bloodshed, and offered his aid to Shepard despite the political heat he had taken.
