"Noveria: Rift Station" was a Mass Effect mission, following "Noveria: Matriarch Benezia," in which Commander Shepard reached Rift Station, held by the battered survivors of a Binary Helix science team, and had to break into the Secure Lab where Matriarch Benezia was directing a captive Rachni Queen. It could be triggered by approaching the sealed "Watchdog Protocol" doors in the restricted area, by being accosted by Han Olar after Alestia Iallis' attack during "Noveria: Quarantine," or by reaching the Secure Lab during "Noveria: The Hot Labs."

There were two routes in. Fighting through the Restricted Area yielded extra combat, experience and credits against Assault Drones and up to nine ERCS Guards, but turned all Rift Station characters permanently hostile, ending trade with Petozi and locking out "Noveria: Quarantine." The peaceful route ran through a maintenance back door at the Quarantine Lab, obtained from Dr. Zev Cohen at the Med Bay after completing the Quarantine mission for him; this passed a lone Rachni Soldier and provoked no guards. If Shepard pursued the Hot Labs lead before entering the Secure Lab, Captain Ventralis and the entire security force revealed themselves as Benezia's employees and attacked in the mess hall; Olar later reported that Ventralis had most of the civilians killed to cover up Binary Helix's rachni mistake.

The mission ceased to matter once Shepard reached Benezia (handled in "Noveria: Matriarch Benezia"). After Benezia died, approaching the Rachni Queen's tank opened a conversation through a dead commando, leading to the choice to flood the tanks with acid and exterminate the rachni or release the Queen to begin the species anew; the mission completed on that decision. In Mass Effect 2, freeing the Queen led to an asari contacting Shepard on Illium. In Mass Effect 3, a freed Queen provided Rachni Workers (+100) as a War Asset; if killed here, a replacement Queen called the Breeder appeared, and sparing the Breeder ultimately cost the rachni assets (-100) and Alliance Engineers (-100). The mission was written by Chris L'Etoile and was internally called "(Noveria:) Science Station."
