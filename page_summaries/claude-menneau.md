Claude Menneau was a candidate for the leadership of the Terra Firma party in 2173, standing after Inez Simmons resigned over the Nashan Stellar Dynamics kickback scandal. He had two bodyguards, Pel and Keo, and travelled in his own private vessel flown by a pilot, Paul Grayson. Grayson considered him charismatic in public but not a likeable man in person. Leading Charles Saracino in the polls by three points, Menneau mysteriously disappeared along with his vessel and bodyguards. In fact the Illusive Man, head of Cerberus, wanted Saracino to lead Terra Firma, and Pel and Grayson were Cerberus operatives planted to shadow Menneau and watch for an opportunity to remove him. While Menneau slept in his cabin en route to Shanxi, Grayson dealt with Keo and Pel entered the cabin and killed Menneau. The ship and the bodies of Keo and Menneau were disposed of, and Menneau's body was presumably never found, though theories and rumours about his disappearance persisted for years. Charles Saracino then ascended to the party leadership unopposed.
