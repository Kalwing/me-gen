The Conduit was a device sought by Saren Arterius and Matriarch Benezia during Mass Effect, believed by them to be the key to bringing back the Reapers. Commander Shepard first learned of it from audio files Tali retrieved from one of Saren's geth. Captain David Anderson assumed it was a weapon, while Liara T'Soni, a Prothean expert, knew only fragments suggesting it was linked to the Prothean extinction.

Its true nature was revealed by Vigil, a damaged virtual intelligence on Ilos. The Conduit was a miniature mass relay built by Prothean researchers on Ilos as a prototype during their experiments with relay technology. It functioned as a one-way "back door" onto the Citadel, independent of and unconnected to the rest of the relay network. Its receiving end on the Citadel had been mistaken for a piece of Prothean art, the Relay Monument, and was never removed by the Council; Garrus noted the keepers ignored the statue, and Kaidan reported a low humming from it. The surviving Prothean scientists used the Conduit to reach the Citadel after the Reapers had left, and altered the signal the keepers received from Sovereign that would have opened the Citadel relay to dark space. Because the corridor was one-directional, the researchers were trapped on the station and, as Vigil suggested, left to starve.

Pursuing Saren back to the Citadel, Shepard's team drove the Mako straight into the Conduit seconds before it shut down, dodging the Geth Colossi guarding it, and arrived on the Citadel to launch a counterattack. Living matter could not survive the trip on foot, requiring an enclosed vehicle. Technically the Conduit comprised both the Ilos prototype relay and the Relay Monument, forming the sending and receiving ends of one system. By 2185, in Mass Effect 2, the public had learned some details of the Conduit, and a Presidium Groundskeeper referred to it while recommending Shepard view nearby demael flowers.
