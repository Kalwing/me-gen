Pamyat was a star system in the Milky Way, reachable in Mass Effect 3 after Priority: Rannoch given a Star Chart obtained from the Baria Frontiers store or from Project Firewalker: Volcano Station in Mass Effect 2. It had once been a hotbed of small-scale pirate activity preying on the meager resources local miners extracted, but by late 2184 or early 2185 the pirates had apparently been cleared out, an unlikely development that spacer Cora Harper likened to picking fleas off a dog with tweezers.

Despite its poor reputation, the system rated highly for resources needed by the Normandy SR-2, which had the tools to reach deposits other miners could not; iridium was present in noteworthy amounts. Sources conflicted over whether the asari or the hanar charted Pamyat first, but it was decisively first colonized by humans. When the hanar landed on a planetoid for repairs in 1879 CE they were unable to establish ownership, allowing the Systems Alliance to stake a legal claim in 2156 CE. None of its worlds were livable; Dobrovolski's aluminum and other supplies from Sheol ensured raw materials for the fabricators that produced habitats.

The system contained the star Pamyat orbited by Komarov (first planet), Dobrovolski (second), Patsayev (third), and Volkov (fourth), plus the Chazov Belt asteroid field, which included the planetoid Quiet Eddy. "Pamyat" is Russian for "memory," and the system's planets are likely named for Soviet cosmonauts who died during spaceflight.
