Emergency S.O.S. was a minor mission in Mass Effect: Andromeda. Pathfinder Ryder received an urgent S.O.S. signal while driving through the Kurinth's Valley region on Elaaden, near the location for Old Skinner. SAM traced the signal to a valley beyond a high mountainous area and set a navpoint.

Following the signal, Ryder found a building occupied by two human Outlaw men sitting on a sofa with no apparent emergency. Speaking to them revealed the "emergency" was that one of their plants, said to have medicinal properties, was dying because its ultraviolet light was broken; the men, who scanned as cognitively impaired, were selling the plant to people needing medicine and asked Ryder to find them a replacement UV light. The room also held an Adapted Initiative Core Tech and three datapads that could be scanned for research points, along with the UV light and the unknown plant.

Ryder traveled to the Haarfel region via the Forward Station and obtained a UV light from a salarian refugee near the site of Task: Broken Family, then returned to the two men to complete the mission, which awarded 540 XP. Completing it opened the follow-up mission Task: Herbal Entrepreneurs; obtaining the UV light before acquiring this mission caused that follow-up to start automatically on completion.
