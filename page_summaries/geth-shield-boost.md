Geth Shield Boost was a tech power in Mass Effect 2 that co-opted geth shield technology to briefly reinforce the user's kinetic barrier with a high-energy geth shield that absorbed a large amount of damage. It had a 12-second recharge and 60-second duration, and its shield strength rose with rank from +25% of maximum shields at Rank 1 to +75% at Rank 3. At Rank 4 it evolved into either Heavy Geth Shield Boost (+100% shield strength, able to absorb heavy weapon impacts) or Improved Geth Shield Boost (+75% shield strength plus a +10% weapon damage bonus for the duration, from re-routing the shield generator into the weapons' power source).

The power was available to Shepard via Advanced Training and to Legion as a loyalty power, unlocked on completing Legion: A House Divided. It restored depleted shields but did not recover health damage, and it was not affected by the two Geth Shield Strength upgrades, which only improved Legion's base shields, though it did benefit from certain tech research upgrades that improved duration and reduced recharge. It was functionally the same as Fortification and Barrier, differing only in the Improved evolution's additive weapon damage buff, and like those powers could be used without interrupting Shepard while storming. Most geth enemies possessed a version of this power that let them fully restore their shields once after depletion. A loyal Legion would constantly reactivate the power in battle even with Squad Power Use disabled, which could be avoided only by resetting Legion's powers at the Shadow Broker's Base and investing no points in it.
