Ben Hislop was a human mercenary and munitions master who appeared in Mass Effect: Evolution. He signed up to fight turians after his brother was killed during the turian bombardment of Shanxi's settlements during the First Contact War, and fought in the resistance on occupied Shanxi. Recruited into Jack Harper's prohumanity movement, he formed a mercenary trio with Jack and Eva Coré, conducting guerrilla warfare under the employ of General Williams, and served as Jack's right-hand man.

On Shanxi, Ben's unit ambushed a turian patrol and he personally fought and captured the turian general Desolas Arterius with Jack's help. He wanted to execute Desolas but Jack kept the general alive for interrogation. On a mission to find the artifact Desolas sought, Ben antagonized him while Jack's scheme defeated the turian subordinates. Reaching the artifact inside a cave, Ben and Jack were ambushed by hooded figures revealed to be cyberized turians, which proved freakishly strong. When Ben touched the artifact to try to shut it off, he was electrocuted; Jack pried him free but was also affected, leaving both incapacitated. Desolas seized them and ordered Ben's body taken for study.

When Harper and Eva pursued Desolas to Illium, they found Ben alive but mutated into a synthetic form like the "meta-turians." He retained some sense of his identity but followed orders from Desolas and Saren, bringing the artifact, the Arca Monolith, to Palaven, where Desolas tried to replicate Harper's enhancement without creating mindless husks. The artifact commanded Ben to excavate a second artifact to accelerate the transformation. When Eva tried to haul Ben out and then to destroy the artifact, it commanded Ben to slam her head against a wall, killing her. Coming to his senses, a horrified Ben charged Desolas and rammed him into the artifact before he could shoot Harper; Saren tore Desolas away. Prolonged contact with the released energy instantly killed Ben, and his body was later vaporized when Saren ordered an orbital bombardment of the temple. Harper later included Ben's and Eva's names in his manifesto as he became the Illusive Man.
