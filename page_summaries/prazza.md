Prazza was a male quarian serving on Tali'Zorah vas Neema's team during their mission to Freedom's Progress. He, Tali, and a squad of other quarians were sent to the colony to find Veetor, a quarian who had fled there.

When the quarians encountered Commander Shepard and Cerberus operatives conducting their own investigation of the colony, Tali forced Prazza to cooperate with them. During the brief meeting Prazza expressed strong distrust and animosity toward Cerberus.

Shortly after the two groups separated, Prazza disregarded Tali's authority and led his team ahead on their own, apparently intending to reach Veetor and remove him before Shepard could, driven by his hatred of Cerberus. His decision resulted in his own death and the deaths of most of his squadmates at the hands of a reprogrammed YMIR Mech that Veetor had turned on in his panic.
