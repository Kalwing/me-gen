Aldrin Labs was a human equipment manufacturer based on the Luna colony. By providing basic, reliable gear at an affordable cost, it became a primary supplier of armor, omni-tools, and bio-amps to the Systems Alliance military. It also manufactured the hand-held stunners that all Ascension Project staff were required to carry.

The company produced three armor lines: Agent (for turians), Hydra (for humans and quarians), and Onyx (for humans, krogan, quarians, and turians), in light, medium, and heavy weights. In Mass Effect, because Aldrin Labs armor used kinetic plastite, its stats were reduced compared with most other manufacturers' armor. An armory license could be bought from the C-Sec Requisitions Officer for 100 credits. The company's omni-tool, the Bluewire Tool, and its Solaris biotic amp likewise had inferior stats, the former due to an encryption overlay and the latter due to its neural sequencer. In Mass Effect 2, Aldrin Labs manufactured the Stabilization Gauntlets for the N7 Armor, which incorporated micro-gyros linked to the hardsuit's targeting aids and increased weapon damage by 5%. The company's name may reference the astronaut Buzz Aldrin.
