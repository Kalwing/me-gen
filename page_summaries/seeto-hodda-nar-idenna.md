Seeto'Hodda nar Idenna was a young quarian aboard the liveship Idenna, serving mostly on security detail, as recounted in the novel Mass Effect: Ascension. During Kahlee Sanders' quarantine aboard the Migrant Fleet, Seeto befriended her; Kahlee liked his exuberance and curiosity. He greatly admired his captain, Ysin'Mal vas Idenna, and shared the captain's belief that the Fleet needed to change its policies to survive. When Kahlee was released, Seeto gave her, Hendel Mitra, and Gillian Grayson a brief tour of the Idenna, including his own sleeping quarters and the ship's trading deck, offering them insight into quarian culture.

Seeto's father was a navigator and his mother sat on the Idenna's civilian Council. Unusually for a quarian, he also had an older sister who joined the Rayya's crew after her Pilgrimage, suggesting Seeto may have been born during a period when the prohibition against single births in quarian families had been lifted.

Seeto never got the chance to go on his own Pilgrimage. He was part of the security detail sent to meet the Cyniad when it returned to the Idenna, not realizing it was carrying a Cerberus strike team. Seeto was killed along with his comrades when Golo and the other Cerberus operatives stormed the ship.
