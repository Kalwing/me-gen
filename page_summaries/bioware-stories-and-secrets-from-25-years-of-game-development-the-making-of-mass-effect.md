This page was a transcription of the chapter "The Making of Mass Effect" from the 2020 book BioWare: Stories and Secrets from 25 Years of Game Development, written by Ben Gelinas.

Mass Effect began as a vision document in fall 2003, shortly after BioWare released Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic. Code-named SFX, it was conceived by Casey Hudson and a core KOTOR team as an original science-fiction RPG in a setting wholly owned by BioWare. The design evolved from letting players control any party member to controlling a single customizable Spectre, Commander Shepard, issuing commands to two AI squadmates; lead designer Preston Watamaniuk wanted the focus on Shepard as a battlefield commander. An early 2004 backstory draft had humans landing on Mars in 2250 and finding alien evidence and a substance called Black Sand that enabled faster-than-light travel; the political hub was named Star City before becoming the Citadel. Multiplayer was envisioned from 2003 and discussed for every game, per Mac Walters, but was not added until Mass Effect 3.

Some of the earliest writing was done in a Jade Empire development build, with no conversation wheel, Friendly and Hostile options standing in for Paragon and Renegade, and Shepard portrayed silently by Wu the Lotus Blossom. The prototype featured a Noveria weapon-smuggling mission, a hanar merchant, the Normandy, the krogan, Saren, Harkin, and the line "I should go." Greg Zeschuk proposed the name "Mass Effect" after other candidates such as BioWar and The Epsilon Effect were rejected. Casey Hudson created the Prothean vision sequence using photographed grocery-store meat, electronics parts, and red wine in his basement. Art director Derek Watts derived the N7 designation from his N77 ski bindings. Tali was originally named Talsi. Concept artist Matt Rhodes based the krogan face on a bat and designed 250 to 270 helmets; lead animator Jonathan Cooper moved from Scotland to work on the game. BioWare patented the conversation wheel (US Patent US20070226648A1). The Reaper sound originated from a microphone dropped into a bear-proof garbage can at Elk Island National Park by audio designers Michael Kent, Steve Sim, and Michael Peter, whose trip ended with police towing Peter's car over expired California plates; Casey Hudson declared the recording "the sound of the Reapers." The Mass Effect main theme by Jack Wall and Sam Hulick was slowed and reworked as ambient sound inside the Normandy SR-2 in Mass Effect 3. Elevation Partners, an investor group including Bono, was a major BioWare shareholder during development and was shown the game's opening. A cut Cerberus outpost called Misery was designed by Mac Walters, and a Mako physics bug caused the vehicle to spin into the air like a tornado.
