Eppo Wen was a capable volus information broker based on Illium, wealthy enough to afford high-end mech security and a volus-compatible airlocked home in a residential neighborhood. She presented as a polite, portly old lady but was dangerous when angered, though she believed killing former customers was bad for business.

In Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation, Wen hosted Cora Harper for a mutually beneficial arrangement. Wen had sold information about potential mercenary competitors to the asari Ygara Menoris, who paid with a bogus account; three of Ygara's thugs then tried to blast through Wen's wall, foiled only because the house was reinforced with Silaris armor. Wen made her services cheap to Cora because she wanted Ygara dead, asking only for the asari's death and, as a bonus, a photo of the corpse. She supplied Cora with Ygara's room number and entry code at the Bulwark hotel in Nos Astra, recent transactions, port registrations for Ygara's ship the Audacity, and a fake ID.

Wen refused to deal in artificial intelligence, wanting no Council Spectres in her home, so she ignored the potential payday of the AI tech Ygara had stolen. Her research revealed the entities who hired Ygara to steal the tech from HOME Group had also directed her to steal it from the Andromeda Initiative, and that all auction buyers were human factions or their proxies, leading Wen to conclude whoever ordered the theft anticipated a double-cross and ensured the tech would stay in human hands. Ygara Menoris ended up dead, giving Wen half of what she wanted, though Cora forgot to send the corpse photo amid the day's events.
