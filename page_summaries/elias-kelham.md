Elias Kelham was a human criminal on the Citadel who moved there around 2175 and ran rackets at Shin Akiba. Originally a small-timer, he rose in power after higher-level crime bosses were killed during Sovereign's attack on the Citadel in 2183, and he bribed C-Sec Captain Bailey to be left alone provided he did not cause too much trouble.

During the search for Thane Krios's son Kolyat, Commander Shepard and Thane learned from Mouse that Kelham had hired Kolyat for an assassination. Kelham was brought in for questioning at the C-Sec office in Zakera Ward. He had a high tolerance for interrogation and, confident in his power, influence and lawyer, would threaten his interrogators if questioning dragged out. He cooperated quickly only if a ruthless Shepard immediately threatened him with Spectre authority, prompting Thane to remark it might be the shortest interrogation in history; otherwise it took physical force, guile or promises of reward. Regardless of method, Kelham eventually revealed that Kolyat's target was Joram Talid, a turian politician. He was left unconscious if brutalized, or berating his late-arriving lawyer otherwise.
