The Blue Suns Commander was a powerful enemy unit of the Blue Suns mercenary group in Mass Effect 2, equipped with better armor and weapons than other Blue Suns forces. Human and batarian Commanders carried the M-76 Revenant light machine gun, while turian Commanders used the M-300 Claymore heavy shotgun, and all could load Incendiary or Disruptor Ammo. A Commander could provide a stats boost to nearby Blue Suns troops. Defensively they had regenerating shields, high armor and access to Tech Armor, making them more durable than other Blue Suns in combat.

The article recommended engaging Commanders at range, with sniper rifles ideal and assault rifles serviceable up close, concentrating a squad's fire on the Commander until biotics or tech could crowd-control it, and keeping Claymore-wielding variants at a distance. Overload, Incinerate and Reave were listed as effective, and on higher difficulties draining shields and destroying armor with powers at range was advised to conserve ammunition and kill them quickly.
