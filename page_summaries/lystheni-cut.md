This page documents non-canon cut content: the lystheni, an offshoot of the salarians whose culture and history would have been developed in the unfinished Mass Effect assignment Global Plot 3. Described as similar to salarians but distinct in culture, language, and ideology, the lystheni called themselves "the old blood." They were said to have separated from other salarians during the formation of the Citadel Council and to have evolved separately, despising everything the Council stood for and deliberately ignoring its laws.

For generations the lystheni lived among and harvested asteroids within hanar space in the Attican Traverse. When the hanar began allowing other species to mine those asteroids, conflict grew, escalating to lystheni bioweapon use against the hanar and the hanar declaring martial law on lystheni colonies. In the assignment, while searching for missing asari scientist Dr. Euthalia, Shepard would investigate a lystheni asteroid facility. Euthalia had been kidnapped and forced to develop an anti-hanar bioweapon, but instead released a virus affecting only lystheni, turning them into violent husks. Shepard would encounter both healthy and mutated lystheni, none of whose language any squadmate could understand, and would have to choose between stopping a data transmission dangerous to the hanar or saving Euthalia's lystheni-sympathizing assistant Nuriam, the unwilling virus carrier. Afterward Shepard could persuade a hanar senator to end mistreatment of the lystheni and begin a peace process. Related material survives as Global Plot 3 voicelines.
