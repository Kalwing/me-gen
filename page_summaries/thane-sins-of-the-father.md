Thane: Sins of the Father was Thane Krios's loyalty mission in Mass Effect 2, acquired after his recruitment when Kelly reports he wishes to speak with Shepard. Contemplating his mortality, Thane has learned that his estranged son Kolyat has taken a hitman contract on the Citadel, and asks Shepard to help stop Kolyat from repeating his own mistakes. It is a no-combat mission with substantial experience, credit and morality rewards.

On the Citadel, Shepard consults Captain Bailey at C-Sec, who traces a lead to a petty criminal named Mouse, a Citadel street kid whom Thane once used as a child informant. Mouse (who is also selling Shepard VIs) reveals, through charm, intimidation, a Renegade interrupt or a 3,000-credit bribe, that Kolyat was hired by Elias Kelham, a small-time criminal who rose after the geth attack killed many crime bosses. Bailey, who takes bribes from Kelham, agrees to arrest him so Shepard and Thane can interrogate him before his lawyer arrives, playing good cop/bad cop. Kelham confesses to ordering a hit on Joram Talid, a turian running for local office on an anti-human platform.

Shepard then shadows Talid from the catwalks above, periodically updating Thane on his position while Talid moves between a shop and a nightclub; failing to keep track results in Kolyat killing Talid and Thane losing loyalty. Along the way Shepard must get past a stock boy, Jim Reynolds. Kolyat wounds Talid's krogan bodyguard and corners Talid in his apartment, where Shepard, Thane and Bailey arrive. A Paragon interrupt lets Shepard disarm Kolyat; otherwise Shepard may wound Kolyat or kill Talid. Father and son argue, then talk privately. Shepard tells Bailey not to pursue old drell murder cases. Kolyat's fate depends on Shepard: community service (Charm), private employment under Bailey (Intimidate), or prison. Completing the mission with Thane loyal grants the achievement "Cat's in the Cradle" and unlocks Thane's Shredder Ammo power; Bailey later emails an update that Kolyat is doing all right. The mission also flags that anti-human bias in Citadel politics may force support of criminals like Kelham.
