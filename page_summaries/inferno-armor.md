The Inferno Armor was a non-customizable bonus armor set available in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. Built specifically for Cerberus field officers, the powered suit carried a VI dedicated to recognizing signs of stress and medical trauma, which helped assess soldiers and gave the wearer an edge in high-risk situations and in negotiations. Its microframe computer managed the wearer's biotic amp and omni-tool power, while microservos assisted movement to counteract the armor's weight.

In Mass Effect 2 the armor granted a Negotiation Bonus of +10%, Tech and Biotic Power Damage +10%, and Storm Speed +10%. It was distributed as a pre-order bonus through retailers other than GameStop and was also included in the Equalizer Pack via the Cerberus Network. It came with a confirmation e-mail from the Illusive Man, who wrote that Miranda had recommended sending it and that it had been well received by Cerberus squad leaders in the Verge; the suit was delivered to Shepard's quarters aboard the Normandy. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition it could instead be researched in the Normandy's Tech Lab for 2,500 Palladium. The tech and biotic damage bonus was noted as valuable for Sentinels, Engineers and Adepts.

In Mass Effect 3 the same VI-equipped suit could be purchased from Kassa Fabrication in the Presidium Commons for 50,000 credits, or through Procurement terminals in the Normandy's shuttle bay or the Armax Arsenal Arena for 55,000 credits. It favored power-oriented classes, maximizing power abilities at the cost of defensive and weapon-damage bonuses, and was considered a strong choice for Sentinels and for Engineers and Adepts. Its power recharge speed bonus suffered from diminishing returns for light loadouts. The Inferno Armor was noted to resemble an Envirosuit from the first Red Faction game.
