UNC: Hostage was an assignment in Mass Effect that took place aboard the derelict freighter MSV Ontario in the Farinata system of the Hades Gamma cluster. A group of fanatical biotic extremists had kidnapped Chairman Burns of the Systems Alliance Parliamentary Subcommittee for Transhuman Studies and were holding him hostage, protesting his vote against providing reparations for L2 biotics who suffered side effects from their implants. Commander Shepard received the assignment either as a personal request from Admiral Hackett upon entering the Farinata system, or from a Citadel elevator news report.

The freighter's entry areas were empty; the six Biotic Terrorists and their leader were in the main cargo hold. A three-minute time limit began only when the terrorists spotted Shepard, at which point the squad had to fight across the hold to the far side where Burns was held. The Terrorists carried only pistols but used biotic powers frequently to knock down Shepard and squadmates, and liked to rush the squad in the tight space near the door. Explosive crates in the hold could be used against them. It was possible to skip the fight entirely by running straight through to Burns' door in under thirty seconds.

At Burns' holding room, opening the door triggered a confrontation with the Biotic Terrorist Leader, who held Burns at gunpoint with a shotgun. The extremists argued that a grand gesture was the only thing that got attention for suffering L2s. Choosing "Take them down!" started a close-quarters firefight in which the Leader was the primary threat. Alternatively, Shepard could talk them down with Charm (requirement 6, eight Paragon points) or Intimidate (requirement 5, nine Renegade points), pointing out that the biotics still needed Burns' political connections; either led to a peaceful resolution, and the surviving terrorists could not afterward be fought. If Kaidan Alenko was in the squad, he revealed he was also an L2 and vouched that they could trust him, and Shepard could promise to make sure Burns followed through.

In Mass Effect 2, if Shepard reasoned with the extremists and Burns survived, Burns sent a message of thanks confirming Shepard had changed his view, and a Galactic News report noted he succeeded in getting reparations for biotics harmed by L2 implants. The assignment is referred to internally as "Hostage Situation."
