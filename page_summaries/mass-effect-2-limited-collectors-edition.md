Short product page (under 150 words of prose). The Mass Effect 2 Limited Collectors' Edition was a retailer special edition of Mass Effect 2. It contained a bonus DVD with documentaries (Prelude to E3, E3 Reveal, The Making of Mass Effect 2, The Enemies of Mass Effect 2, The Stars of Mass Effect 2), a 48-page hardcover Mass Effect 2 Art Book, limited edition issue #1 of the comic Mass Effect: Redemption, unique in-game Collectors' Edition armor and weapons, a Cerberus Network card for bonus content (also included with the standard edition), all packaged in a premium tin with exclusive artwork.
