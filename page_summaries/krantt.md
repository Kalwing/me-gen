Krantt, also spelled krannt, was a krogan term for a warrior's most trusted allies, those willing to kill in his name and honor. Krantt served as a krogan's proxy and representative in rites and politics, and anybody could serve as krantt, including non-krogan. A krantt was regarded not as a servant but as a second or bodyguard, and the ability to command loyal krantt was itself considered proof of power, since only the strongest warriors could inspire such loyalty. The Urdnot Shaman on Tuchanka observed that raw strength in a warrior was commendable but meaningless if that warrior could not inspire his comrades to greatness. A krogan undergoing the Rite of Passage was granted the right to bring a krantt along, both as backup and as evidence that he was fit to command.

In Mass Effect 2, Shepard could choose to become the krantt of both Grunt and, temporarily, Patriarch. Aria sent Shepard to protect Patriarch, her predecessor as ruler of Omega, from a planned assassination; Aria wanted Patriarch forced into hiding, but Shepard could instead become his krantt and eliminate the hit-men on his behalf, restoring his standing as an authority figure on Omega. During Grunt's Rite of Passage into adulthood on Tuchanka, the Urdnot Shaman asked whether Grunt had any krantt to aid him, and Shepard, as his commander and shipmate, could volunteer. The Shaman accepted Shepard; if Shepard explained the offer as simple crew loyalty, the Shaman remarked that the concept seemed alien to humans, but if Shepard answered with confident aggression, the Shaman concluded that Shepard understood the concept despite not being krogan, noting that even those who were not the strongest warriors could still draw strength from the loyalty of others.
