"Omega: Struggling Quarian" was an assignment in Mass Effect 2, available after Freedom's Progress. At Kenn's Salvage on the lower level of Omega, Shepard met a quarian named Kenn who was trying to raise enough money to leave the station but was being undercut by Harrot, a powerful elcor merchant. The assignment opened when Shepard asked about Harrot after haggling with Kenn or keeping the conversation going.

The simplest resolution was to give Kenn the 1000 credits he needed to leave, rewarding 5 Paragon points. Alternatively, Shepard could confront Harrot; with a high Paragon or Renegade score, Shepard could physically threaten Harrot to leave Kenn alone, or convince Harrot to buy Kenn out of business. Either resolution granted 5 morality points in the corresponding direction and discounts at both Harrot's Emporium and Kenn's Salvage. To maximize morality, Shepard could threaten Harrot and then still give Kenn his 1000 credits.

Kenn abandoned his kiosk either because Shepard bought his ticket or because Harrot bought him out; no one replaced him behind the counter. The reward was 40 (50) experience. It was internally called "(Omega:) Extinguishing Sun." Notably, the persuasion options subverted the usual alignments: the Renegade choice produced a peaceful buyout while the Paragon choice had Shepard intimidating Harrot.
