Short enemy-reference page (under ~150 words of real prose). Troopers were basic Cerberus soldiers stationed on the Barn space station in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, fought by Randall Ezno during his escape. They had a moderate regenerating shield layer over their health, tended to stay in cover unless fired on (Pull could flush them out), and took bonus damage from headshots.
