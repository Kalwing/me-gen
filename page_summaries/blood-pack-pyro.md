Thin page: a Mass Effect 2 enemy-unit stub, mostly gameplay tactics. The Blood Pack Pyro was a member of the Blood Pack mercenary group armed with a flamethrower. Pyros were always vorcha, encountered on many worlds alongside other vorcha and sometimes krogan. They were relatively harmless until close enough to use their flamethrowers, which could force Shepard and the squad out of cover and stagger them. They wore light protection, gained a weak armor layer on Hardcore and Insanity difficulties, regenerated health on all difficulties, and could rarely use the vorcha ability to restore armor. Damaging an unprotected Pyro's fuel tank caused it to panic and explode, harming nearby enemies.
