Citadel: Old, Unhappy, Far-Off Things was a Mass Effect assignment available only to a Commander Shepard with the Spacer background, after one of the core missions had been completed: finding Liara T'Soni, Noveria: Matriarch Benezia, or Feros: The Thorian. Walking through the Wards Access corridor, Shepard was addressed by name by an older man, who introduced himself as Lieutenant Zabaleta and claimed to know Shepard's mother. He asked for 20 credits, saying he was down on his luck and stranded on the Citadel because he could not afford a ticket home; Shepard could give the money, lie about having none, or refuse outright.

Calling the SSV Kilimanjaro from the Normandy's comm room reached Hannah Shepard, to whom the ship was assigned, and she explained that Zabaleta suffered from post-traumatic stress disorder after being at Mindoir and said he should go to the Veterans' Affairs Office for help. Returning to Zabaleta, the lieutenant again asked for credits, this time for whiskey to stop the dreams. Shepard could give him money, set him up with an account at a grocery store to stop him drinking, refuse again, or, with 4 Charm or 5 Intimidate, convince him to go to the Veterans' Affairs Office. Whatever the outcome, Zabaleta then left the location for good.

The assignment could not be completed if it was begun after finishing the final plot world, because the Normandy could not be boarded during the lockdown to use the comm. It was written by Chris L'Etoile; its title referenced the poem "The Solitary Reaper" by William Wordsworth, and it was named "Drink Before the War" in the game's internal data.
