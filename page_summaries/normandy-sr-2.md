The Normandy SR-2 was the starship serving as Commander Shepard's base of operations after the SSV Normandy SR-1 was destroyed. It entered service in 2185, built by Cerberus at great and secret expense in the Voyager Cluster on the orders of the Illusive Man for the revived Shepard's team to stop the Collector abductions of human colonists; it served as headquarters of the Lazarus Cell. Based closely on the SR-1's layout, it was almost twice the mass of the original, added civilian-grade comforts (leather chairs, a kitchen), retained the SR-1's stealth characteristics, but could not land on every planet, requiring shore parties to use a Kodiak shuttle.

The ship had five decks: Deck 1, the Captain's Cabin ("the loft"), with a fish tank, model ship case, space hamster, and souvenir displays; Deck 2, the Combat Information Center, with bridge, galaxy map, armory, research/tech labs, and a communications room with a quantum entanglement array for real-time holographic contact with the Illusive Man; Deck 3, the crew's quarters, with the XO's office, mess hall run by Mess Sergeant Gardner, medical bay run by Dr. Karin Chakwas, the AI core, life support, main battery, and observation lounges; Deck 4, engineering, housing the oversized Tantalus drive core (three times the SR-1's, vertically oriented); and Deck 5, the hangar for the Kodiak shuttle and M-44 Hammerhead.

EDI (Enhanced Defense Intelligence) was the ship's AI, functioning as electronic-warfare defense with behavioral blocks preventing direct interface with ship systems, and serving as the Illusive Man's eyes and ears. She was designed in part from Sovereign's remains, making her partly Reaper-based. Armament included Javelin disruptor torpedoes and an upgradeable Turian-designed Thanix cannon (a miniature of Sovereign's weapon); defenses included kinetic barriers, cyclonic barrier technology multicore shielding, Silaris ablative armor, and GARDIAN lasers. Weapon, armor, and shield upgrades, plus the Reaper IFF recovered from the Derelict Reaper at Mnemosyne, determined crew survival in the final mission; the IFF also let the Collectors locate and board the ship.

By 2186 Shepard had defected from Cerberus and turned the ship over to the Systems Alliance, which refitted it, stripped Cerberus equipment, and recommissioned it as the SSV Normandy SR-2 in Alliance colors. Intended as Admiral Anderson's command center, it instead rescued Shepard from the Reaper invasion of Earth and became the mobile base for the war effort and the Crucible mission. In the final battle for Earth the Normandy deployed Shepard's team, then was caught in the Crucible's energy wave mid-jump and crash-landed on an unknown world; the severity of the crash and crew survival depended on galactic readiness.
