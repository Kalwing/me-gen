Kasumi Goto was described as the galaxy's most enigmatic thief, a master of stealth and infiltration with enemies everywhere but few who could put a name to her face. In Mass Effect: Foundation she was shown in an intimate relationship with her partner Keiji Okuda, who nicknamed her "Little Bird." She planned a heist against arms dealer Donovan Hock on Bekenstein; during it Hock stabbed Keiji in the head and stole his graybox, a neural implant storing memories and secrets. The dying Keiji begged Kasumi to survive and recover the graybox; she cloaked and left his body, vowing to make good on her word.

In Mass Effect 2, Cerberus tracked Kasumi down and she agreed to join Commander Shepard's mission against the Collectors on the condition that Shepard help her recover Keiji's graybox from Hock. Shepard met her on the Citadel's Zakera Ward using the password "Silence is golden." She settled in the Normandy's port observation deck among stolen effects. She rivaled Kelly Chambers for insight on the crew, being the only person to notice if Morinth replaced Samara, and displayed an unreciprocated crush on Jacob Taylor. Her loyalty mission, Kasumi: Stealing Memory, saw Shepard and Kasumi infiltrate Hock's gala, battle Eclipse mercenaries, and recover the graybox; one secret within could trigger galactic war.

In Mass Effect 3, six months after surviving the Suicide Mission, Kasumi resurfaced on the Citadel, feeding the salarian Spectre Jondum Bau a tip about indoctrinated hanar operatives aware of an Alliance black ops raid into batarian space. She secretly assisted Bau and Shepard tracking down the culprit, who was attempting to disable the hanar homeworld Kahje's defenses. Kasumi stopped the virus but the booby-trapped console exploded, seemingly killing her. If Shepard had recovered the graybox, she revealed the raid was Keiji's dangerous secret: the Alliance had raided batarians who found Reaper technology. She then revealed she had faked her death to escape Bau, and Shepard could convince her to aid the Crucible project by promising expensive equipment nobody would miss. If the graybox was never retrieved, she did not reveal herself or join the war. During the final battle she reported the Crucible ready. In the synthesis ending, if she kept Keiji's memories, she was seen holding hands with an apparition of Keiji; in destroy or control endings she held his graybox. In the Citadel DLC she plotted to rob the Silver Coast Casino and appeared at Shepard's party, constantly cloaking and decloaking in unexpected places.
