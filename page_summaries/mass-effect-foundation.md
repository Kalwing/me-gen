Mass Effect: Foundation was a comic series written by Mac Walters and published by Dark Horse Comics, the fifth Mass Effect comic series produced. Unlike the earlier four-issue series, Foundation ran thirteen issues, the first released on July 24, 2013. The series was stated to connect to the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC pack, and issue 5 tied directly into Mass Effect 2. Its recurring framing device followed the Cerberus agent Rasa (later known as Brooks) as she compiled dossiers on Commander Shepard and the individuals who would become Shepard's Mass Effect 2 squad, with each issue exploring a different character's backstory.

Three paperback volumes collected the run: Volume 1 (issues 1-4) released February 5, 2014; Volume 2 (issues 5-8) released June 4, 2014; and Volume 3 (issues 9-13) released November 5, 2014. This page is largely an index listing all thirteen issues and the collected editions.
