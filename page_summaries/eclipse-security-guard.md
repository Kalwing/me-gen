Short page. Eclipse Security Guards were Eclipse mercenaries who appeared in the Kasumi: Stealing Memory mission as bodyguards for Donovan Hock. During the effort to crack Hock's vault, Shepard fought guards in the communication room to obtain the vault password and encountered more guards while infiltrating Hock's private rooms for a DNA sample. They were equipped only with M-3 Predator pistols, had low health and low shields on higher difficulties, and wore the unique black-and-white armor scheme also used by the Eclipse Troopers guarding Hock's home.
