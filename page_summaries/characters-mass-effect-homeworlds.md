This page was a roster index for the four-issue comic series Mass Effect: Homeworlds, listing links to the characters appearing in each issue. It held roughly eleven entries: issue 1 covered James Vega, Emilio Vega and Joshua Sanders; issue 2 covered Tali'Zorah nar Rayya, Keenah'Breizh and Jacobus; issue 3 covered Garrus Vakarian and Lantar Sidonis; and issue 4 covered Liara T'Soni, Quoyle and the Illusive Man. It contained no original prose beyond the per-issue link tables.
