Priority: Sur'Kesh followed Priority: Palaven. The krogan demanded a cure for the genophage before they would aid the war effort, and the price of krogan support for Palaven was set at a genophage cure, now within reach thanks to Maelon's research. A few krogan females had survived Maelon's procedures and were being held on the salarian homeworld Sur'Kesh. Dalatrass Linron and the Clan Urdnot leader (Urdnot Wrex if he survived Virmire, otherwise Urdnot Wreav) came aboard the Normandy for a summit, and Shepard was tasked with extracting the females.

At the salarian STG base Shepard could speak with Captain Kirrahe (promoted to Major if he survived Virmire, otherwise replaced by the more critical Lieutenant Tolan). Mordin Solus, or Padok Wiks if Mordin had died, was revealed as Clan Urdnot's inside source and introduced the sole surviving krogan female. The Urdnot leader then warned that Cerberus was attacking the base to prevent a genophage-free krogan population.

Shepard fought through the multi-level facility against Cerberus Assault Troopers, Centurions, Combat Engineers with automated turrets, Guardians and an Atlas mech, while protecting the female's containment pod from damage. An imprisoned yahg broke loose during the fight, and audio logs and research terminals detailed STG experiments on the yahg and varren and the female krogan's backstory. After destroying the Atlas and clearing the final wave, the female demonstrated she could fight for herself, killing two Cerberus soldiers, and the team extracted her by shuttle. A dying Cerberus soldier expired before Shepard could interrogate him.

In the aftermath Hackett confirmed the Crucible's construction had begun. The Urdnot leader insisted all krogan receive the cure and moved to the war room. The female krogan was named "Eve" and stayed in the med-bay with the salarian scientist. The missions Tuchanka: Turian Platoon and Attican Traverse: Krogan Team became available.
