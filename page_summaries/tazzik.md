Tazzik was a salarian hitman employed by the Shadow Broker, unusually tall and rugged for a salarian, to the point that a Blue Suns krogan was surprised by his appearance.

In Mass Effect: Redemption, Tazzik arrived on Omega in 2183 to oversee the Blue Suns operation moving Commander Shepard's body for transport to the Collectors, and was recognized as a reputable Broker agent by Feron. Despite interference from Liara T'Soni, he escaped Omega with Shepard's body and brought it to the Broker's main base on Alingon. During the handover, Feron walked in claiming the Broker wanted full payment rather than half; Tazzik was ready to believe him and had ordered the body taken back when the Collector negotiator outed Feron as a traitor. Feron then signaled Liara, who attacked and escaped with Tazzik's ship and Shepard's body. Tazzik was last seen elbowing Feron to the ground and beating his face bloody.

In Mass Effect 3, if Liara saved Feron from the Shadow Broker, then following the destruction of the Shadow Broker Base in 2186 Liara tasked Feron with tracking Tazzik down. Displeased at taking orders from Feron, Tazzik nonetheless complied, acting as bodyguard for Feron and a group of Shadow Broker agents gathering intel across the Terminus Systems. He remained unaware that Liara was the new Shadow Broker, apparently assuming nothing had changed beyond Feron's status shifting from liability to asset.
