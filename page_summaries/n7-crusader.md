The N7 Crusader was a shotgun appearing in Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Andromeda. In-universe, bruised and bloody Alliance marines on Torfan credited their survival against waves of batarian mercenaries to the Crusader's precision and stopping power. Patterned on riot shotguns, it had a moderate rate of fire that rewarded careful aiming and was primarily issued to highly trained soldiers.

Unlike a normal shotgun, the Crusader fired a single high-powered slug rather than a spread of pellets, firing four semi-automatic shots before reloading. It had innate 50 cm shot penetration, punching through thin obstacles, Guardian shields and armor plating with no damage penalty. It was very accurate, with accuracy improving when aimed down sights, and its damage per shot exceeded the M-99 Saber and rivaled sniper rifles like the M-92 Mantis, though it was one of the heaviest weapons in the game with heavy vertical recoil and a 0.1-second delay between trigger pull and firing. Its single-projectile design made it strong against armor but more vulnerable to shield gates, mitigated by Disruptor Ammo.

In Mass Effect 3 single-player it came with the N7 Collector's Edition Pack, delivered via an email from Lieutenant D. Eugene of the Alliance Engineering Corps, who had led the Normandy's Alliance retrofit; in Legendary Edition it cost 125,000 credits at Spectre Requisitions. In multiplayer it was ultra-rare. In Mass Effect: Andromeda the Initiative's N7 Crusaders were heavily modified versions supplied by a sympathetic Systems Alliance agent. The name references the medieval Crusades.
