Human Armor was the category of armor wearable by humans and asari in the first Mass Effect. Shepard's maximum armor weight class depended on the Commander's class. Ashley Williams started with medium armor and could unlock heavy armor, while Kaidan Alenko and Liara T'Soni could wear only light armor. The page catalogs the armor sets in light, medium and heavy weight classes, each sold across levels I to X and rated for Damage Protection, Shields, and Tech/Biotic Protection. Many sets shared statistics under different visual names (for example, Skirmish Armor was statistically identical to Partisan Armor).

Manufacturers and their representative lines included Aldrin Labs (Hydra, Onyx), Ariake Technologies (Mercenary), Armax Arsenal (Predator L/M/H), Batarian State Arms (Partisan/Skirmish), Cerberus Skunkworks (Freedom/Hoplite), Devlon Industries (Explorer/Liberator/Survivor), Elanus Risk Control Services (Duelist/Guardian), Hahne-Kedar (Mantis/Predator/Scorpion/Ursa), Hahne-Kedar Shadow Works (Spectre, Janissary), Jormangund Technology (Crisis/Hazard), Kassa Fabrication (Colossus), Rosenkov Materials (Titan), Sirta Foundation (Phoenix), and Elkoss Combine (Assassin/Gladiator). Higher-end sets such as Predator, Colossus and Titan only appeared at higher levels (VII to X, or IV to X). Several sets, including Partisan/Skirmish, Freedom/Hoplite, Light and Medium Spectre, Janissary, and Crisis/Hazard, could only be acquired through console commands. In general, heavier classes traded Tech/Biotic Protection for greater Damage Protection, with Colossus offering the highest damage protection and Cerberus Skunkworks and Jormangund lines offering the strongest tech/biotic and shield values respectively.
