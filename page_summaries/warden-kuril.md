Warden Kuril was the turian warden of the Blue Suns prison ship Purgatory. Originally a law enforcement officer on Palaven, Kuril grew frustrated with criminals repeatedly escaping custody, so he left and joined the Blue Suns, conceiving the idea of holding prisoners in space where escape was impossible. He was one of the few "barefaced" turians seen in the series, and was voiced by Fred Tatasciore.

When Commander Shepard came aboard Purgatory to recruit the biotic prisoner Jack, Kuril gave Shepard a tour and directed the Commander to Outprocessing to collect Jack. When Shepard opened the door, Kuril revealed he had betrayed Cerberus and ordered his mercenaries to capture and hold Shepard for ransom. Shepard instead released Jack from cryo; the biotic went on a rampage that wrecked prison blocks as she fought her way out. Deciding Shepard was too much trouble, Kuril chose to kill the Commander. In the confrontation Shepard called him a "two-bit slaver," and Kuril shouted that everything he did was for the good of the galaxy. Despite being protected by three external shield generators, which had to be destroyed before he could be harmed, Kuril was killed by Shepard.

In combat Kuril was one of the few NPCs armed with the M-76 Revenant, which he fired accurately, especially on higher difficulties, though he had no offensive powers. He relied on strong armor and shields, Tech Armor that could fully restore his shields once (only after his armor was gone), the three external shield generators, and elevated cover with no approach path, forcing engagement strictly at range. The page recommends disruptor and overload effects against his shields, incineration against his armor, flashbang or inferno grenades to prevent his shield restore, and heavy weapons such as the M-490 Blackstorm or M-920 Cain to end the fight quickly, and advises clearing the waves of Blue Suns Troopers, Centurions and Commanders before destroying the generators.
