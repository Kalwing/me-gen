The Venom Shotgun was a shotgun featured in Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Andromeda. It was developed by the salarian Special Tasks Group to meet the unpredictable needs of operatives stationed in hostile areas. Its double barrel fired ammunition that detonated on impact, while a third barrel below could be charged to fire a round of microgrenades. Given the covert nature of STG assignments, the Venom was designed to force an exit strategy and was issued to teams whose primary objective was to extract compromised undercover operatives; during the Reaper War it was reissued to frontline soldiers.

In Mass Effect 3 the Venom functioned essentially as a grenade launcher with a charged-shot feature: an uncharged shot fired one explosive round that detonated on impact, while a fully charged shot fired a high-damage round that split into bomblets (five in single-player, three in multiplayer). Because it fired explosive rounds, penetration and anti-armor mods and ammo were useless with it, and it could not score headshot or weak-point bonuses. Its drawbacks were heavy weight and poor accuracy at range, though accuracy could be improved by aiming down sights or fitting a Shotgun Smart Choke. Charged shots dealt over six times an uncharged shot's damage in single-player, bypassed shield gates, and could one-shot Brutes or Phantoms at level X with a High Caliber Barrel and the right ammo power; they were also effective against Phantoms and could blow apart bodies to deny Cannibals. It was acquired via the Groundside Resistance Pack (or the Aegohr Munitions terminal in Legendary Edition for 7,000 credits) and was an ultra-rare multiplayer weapon requiring the Reckoning pack.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda the Venom was a semi-automatic shotgun firing explosive rounds and, after a short charge, clusters of microgrenades that rebounded off obstacles. It could not use a Shotgun Receiver Mod and, like other grenade-launching weapons, had no Concussive variant.
