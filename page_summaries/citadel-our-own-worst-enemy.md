Citadel: Our Own Worst Enemy was a Mass Effect assignment available after Race Against Time: Sovereign. After Captain Anderson summoned Commander Shepard to meet him at Flux, a group of people chanting slogans gathered in the Upper Wards near the stairs by Flux, where the player first encountered Emily Wong. At the front stood Charles Saracino, current leader of the Terra Firma Party and a vehement critic of the Alliance's policy toward alien races, who believed humanity should resist alien influences by any means necessary, even militarily in the worst case. Saracino was running for a seat in the Alliance Parliament and considered the support of the first human Spectre invaluable.

If Ashley Williams was in the party she voiced her own objections to Saracino, which Shepard could encourage or curtail; Kaidan Alenko, Liara T'Soni, Garrus Vakarian, and Urdnot Wrex also had their own complaints. Conversation with Saracino provided information about Terra Firma, Shanxi, and the First Contact War. Shepard could agree or refuse to endorse Saracino for the parliamentary seat, and separately could agree or refuse to make a public statement of support for his candidacy. Refusing to endorse Saracino gave two Paragon points, while endorsing him gave two Renegade points; either way Saracino was grateful for Shepard's respect of the democratic process and returned to the protest.

The journal entry was marked complete upon receipt, its text reflecting Shepard's decision. News reports cut from Mass Effect 2, later restored in Legendary Edition, showed the Terra Firma Party gaining parliamentary support if Shepard had supported Saracino, or Saracino pleading guilty to tax evasion if not. The assignment was written by Chris L'Etoile.
