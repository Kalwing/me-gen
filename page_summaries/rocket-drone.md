Rocket Drones were autonomous drones equipped with rocket launchers, used in 2185 to guard certain areas in Mass Effect 2. They attacked from a distance at varying heights and often moved to flank enemies. Most possessed only shielding and were destroyed once it was stripped, though the Rocket Drones on Kopis also had health; being unable to take cover, they compensated with high maneuverability. Shield-stripping powers and weapons such as Overload and Disruptor Ammo were effective against them, and because they were flying enemies they were immune to some biotic powers. On Kopis the Hammerhead was used to fight them, its guided missiles countering their unguided rockets. During the Lair of the Shadow Broker they were affected by Singularity (flying in circles) and instantly destroyed by Stasis. In Legion: A House Divided, Shepard could override inactive rocket drones and turn them against the geth at the Heretic Station. Drones in the Project Firewalker assignments resembled the Geth Rocket Drone from the original Mass Effect.
