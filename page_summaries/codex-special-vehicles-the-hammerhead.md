This Codex entry described the M-44 Hammerhead, a highly maneuverable mass-effect-assisted armored infantry fighting vehicle. Instead of wheels it used three solid-fuel rocket thrusters to hover over the battlefield at up to 120 kilometers per hour, letting it keep formation with fast armored units, skim calm water, and leap terrain obstacles; backup microboosters preserved full mobility even after the loss of two main thrusters. It kept the features of interplanetary fighting vehicles, including an airtight interior, 360-degree kinetic barriers, and a guided missile system for accuracy during aggressive maneuvers, along with countermeasures for laser detection, chaff, active thermal masking, and ground-penetrating weapons-sniffing radar. Its navigation controls emulated those of tanks so tank drivers could operate it without extra training, and factory-issue units carried no altimeter, being intended for a standard cruising altitude of two meters above the ground.
