Citadel Arena: Medi-Gel Challenge was a side mission in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, available after Citadel Docks: Retake the Normandy, once the Armax Arsenal Arena opened with the rest of Silversun Strip. After one arena match, a fan using the hanar-style handle BlastoFan17485 emailed the Combat Sim Controls, claiming Shepard's rise on the leaderboards could not have been achieved through legitimate play and that Shepard's medi-gel contained blood oxygenators to combat fatigue. BlastoFan dared Shepard to enter a match without medi-gel.

To complete the challenge, Shepard first purchased the "No Medi-gel" match modifier, a silver-level prize, from the prize kiosks, then completed a combat simulation against any enemy at any difficulty with that modifier active. The challenge was not mutually exclusive with other arena challenges, such as the Geth Challenge or Spin Zone Elite Challenge, and could be completed in the same match. Afterward BlastoFan17485 sent a second email conceding that the victory was legitimate, having reviewed the video feeds and biometric data with an acquaintance and found that Shepard's vital signs never smoothed out as performance enhancers would cause. BlastoFan then proposed an ongoing correspondence about arena tactics, particularly kill streaks against Reaper super elites.
