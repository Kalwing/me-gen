Part 1 of a breakdown of the Normandy SR-2. The SR-1 was a prototype deep-scout stealth frigate co-developed by the Alliance and the Turian Hierarchy under Citadel Council sponsorship, commissioned in 2183. It was first commanded by an Alliance officer who clashed with the Turian chief engineer over the drive core's limits and was removed in favor of David Anderson, then passed to Commander Shepard when Shepard became the first human Spectre. About a month after the Battle of the Citadel, an unidentified starship intercepted the Normandy despite its active stealth systems. Joker began evasive maneuvers, but the attacker's beam weapons cut straight through the ship's defenses, knocking out kinetic barriers and weapons in a single hit. Within seconds the ship was crippled, the upper decks breached, a starboard drive cell destroyed, and over 20 crew killed, including Shepard. The SR-1 was completely destroyed above Alchera.

The Normandy SR-2 entered service in 2185 as the SR-1's successor. The Illusive Man had Cerberus build it at deliberately secret expense in the Voyager Cluster for the revived Shepard's team, to fight the Collectors' galaxy-wide human abductions; it served as headquarters of the Lazarus Cell. It was closely based on the SR-1's layout but almost twice the mass, with more crew-oriented comforts (civilian-grade accommodations, leather chairs, a kitchen). It kept the SR-1's stealth characteristics, but its greater mass meant it could not land on every planet, so shore parties used a shuttle. It carried over the SR-1's lithium-heat-sink stealth array and the oversized Tantalus drive core.

The SR-2's most significant upgrade was EDI, the Enhanced Defense Intelligence, an AI represented by a floating blue holographic sphere at terminals throughout the ship. EDI provided information about the Normandy and Cerberus and functioned as the ship's electronic-warfare defense. Because of the danger of a rogue AI, EDI was given behavioral blocks and could not interface with ship systems directly. When the Collectors boarded and abducted most of the crew, an emergency response by EDI and Joker released those blocks, granting EDI full control of the Normandy. Before the crew defected from Cerberus, EDI also served as the Illusive Man's eyes and ears aboard, monitoring listening devices and sending reports, while the Illusive Man restricted Shepard's access to some of EDI's files. EDI gained access to anti-Reaper algorithms and revealed she was designed in part with technology from Sovereign's remains, making her herself partly based on Reaper technology. After the defection, EDI, a fully sentient AI with free will, followed Shepard's lead and severed her ties to Cerberus, though she still downplayed her sentience to unfamiliar Alliance personnel.
