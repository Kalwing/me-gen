Collector Drones were the basic foot soldiers of the Collector race in Mass Effect 2; the equivalent basic infantry unit in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer was renamed the Collector Trooper. Drones were armed with a Collector weapon that visually resembled the Collector Particle Beam but was numerically identical to the M-8 Avenger, and like other Collector troops they had wings and often flew in before landing to attack, after which they stayed grounded and did not fly again. They could have barriers at higher difficulty levels. Drones did not attack while airborne and could be attacked in the air; hitting an unprotected one with powers such as Throw or Concussive Shot could knock it to an environmental death. They were fairly accurate and could wear down shields surprisingly fast, so taking them out first was advised, both because they were easier to kill and because it denied Harbinger cheap targets to possess. Their name likely derives from the term for hive-dwelling insects such as male bees or ants, from an Old English root meaning "male honeybee."
