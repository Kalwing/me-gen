The N7 Eagle was a heavy pistol in Mass Effect 3 and a pistol in Mass Effect: Andromeda. When the Alliance's Offensive Handgun Project received funding to update a design, its engineers redesigned the Phalanx pistol into the Eagle, a compact, fully automatic pistol with high accuracy, punch and a rapid firing rate. It was named after the Desert Eagle, a 20th- and 21st-century Earth handgun popular in action movies.

The Eagle was the only fully automatic heavy pistol, effectively an SMG in pistol form, firing slower but harder than the M-12 Locust with more muzzle climb and very low weight. It could mount the Pistol Heavy Barrel for extra damage and the Pistol Scope for sniper-level accuracy, had a large thermal clip reserve, and paired exceptionally well with the Marksman power. In Legendary Edition it dealt only about half its patched single-player damage with a slightly smaller magazine. Squadmates fired it in five-round bursts; in multiplayer it excelled for Turian and Quarian Marksman Soldiers and the N7 Destroyer Soldier.

In single-player it came with the N7 Collector's Edition Pack, delivered via an email from Lieutenant D. Eugene of the Alliance Engineering Corps; in Legendary Edition it cost a base 80,000 credits at Spectre Requisitions, and in multiplayer it was ultra-rare. In Mass Effect: Andromeda it was an update of the M-5 Phalanx, with the military N7 variant supplied to the Initiative by a sympathetic Systems Alliance agent.
