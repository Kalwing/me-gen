Matano was a star system composed of five worlds, four terrestrial and one a gas giant, accessible in Mass Effect after the Feros: Geth Attack mission. Its planets were named for Inca deities: Inti (first), Chasca (second), Apo (third), Illapa (fourth) and Supay (fifth). The Systems Alliance had claimed Chasca, charged ships that drew water from Supay, and took on patrol duties against pirates in the system. Illapa was slated to become a helium-3 production hub if Chasca's development continued.

The system contained points of interest for Shepard's surveys and assignments. Chasca hosted the assignment Investigate Samples, which led into UNC: Colony of the Dead, and yielded Prothean Data Discs and valuable minerals. A metallic asteroid in the inner belt (radius 47 km) held a large platinum deposit and heavy metal for UNC: Valuable Minerals. A rocky asteroid in the outer belt (radius 34 km), described as unusually sculpted with sweeping curves, was the site of a badly damaged derelict ship where a recon team found no survivors but recovered a Prothean data disc. Supay provided light metal for survey. The system was probably named after Lake Matano.
