Citadel: GX12 Thermal Pipe was a short assignment available after Priority: Palaven. Chief Engineer Adams, on the Normandy's engineering deck, asked Shepard to obtain a GX12 thermal pipe to make the ship's engines safer. If Priority: Sur'Kesh had been completed, Mordin Solus (or Padok Wiks) and Eve occupied the med bay and Adams temporarily moved to the crew quarters, only resuming his request and station after two further missions involving landing on a world. Shepard purchased the E-Gel Thermal Conduit for 1,000 credits at the Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies kiosk at the Bank in the Presidium Commons, or, if that kiosk had already been visited, from the Normandy's Alliance Requisitions terminal at a 10% surcharge. Adams installed the pipe over the course of a subsequent mission, with Traynor notifying Shepard when the work was done. Completing the assignment gave 5 Reputation and 375 experience; if Kenneth and Gabby were alive and had been pardoned, schematics for Optimized Eezo Capacitors were sent to the Crucible project.
