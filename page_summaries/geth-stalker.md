The Geth Stalker was a variant of the Geth Hopper fielded by the geth that followed Saren Arterius during the events of Mass Effect. Its platform carried a camouflage pattern of black on white, and its body core glowed blue intermittently during normal operation. The Stalker was built as a fast harassing unit, combining the extreme agility of the Hopper chassis with a suite of disabling technical attacks that disrupted enemy weapon heat systems and personal shields. These abilities needed several seconds to charge before firing. The Stalker could also fire energy pulses from its eye that dealt toxic damage, but it generally preferred its disabling powers.

Like all Hoppers, Stalkers were lightweight and lightly armored, leaving them vulnerable to gunfire; the difficulty lay in landing a hit while they leapt rapidly between positions, usually bouncing back and forth between two points across an indoor space. A single round striking a Stalker forced it to cancel its attack and jump elsewhere. Physics-based biotic powers such as Throw inflicted heavy damage, and Lift could suspend them in midair for sniper fire. The color of the energy gathering around a Stalker's eye telegraphed its next power: orange indicated Sabotage, green indicated Damping, and yellow indicated Overload. Stalkers were most often encountered in interior environments.
