This page collects cut and non-functional dialogue from the first Mass Effect covering the first half of the Noveria missions, from Port Hanshan through the Aleutsk Valley to Peak 15's garage, reconstructed from leftover files. It is explicitly non-canon. Content includes cut Investigate options with Maeko Matsuo, Opold and Lilihierax; ambient conversations overheard in the Port Hanshan lounge; blocked Charm and Intimidate options with Administrator Anoleis (which would not actually pay out); a Joker weather-report exchange at the closed garage gate; extra Mako-ride squad interjections against geth drones, gunships and anti-air guns; and a cut boathouse rest stop on a frozen lake, including a conversation about krogan cold tolerance and an assassin ambush at a jammed gate.

Lilihierax, cut line pointing to him as the Mako repair contact on Noveria: "Mako, eh? Don't see many IFV's..." Shepard: "We've got a Mako that needs work."

Joker, cut weather report at the garage gate: "We got a monster storm sitting up around the Skadi Range. Blinding snow. Sub-zero temperatures. Gale force winds. Pretty average by local standards." He signs off: "Remember: if your feet stop feeling cold, that's a bad sign. Normandy out."

Wrex, cut boathouse conversation on the frozen dead krogan: "We're not cold-blooded, but we do come from a warmer world than most. To me, it feels fifteen degrees colder. And those morons didn't bring any winter gear."

Unnamed human male assassin at the jammed gate: "Stay where you are. Ah-ah, no weapons, please. Fighting would make this messy. Well, messier." When asked who sent him: "Saren, actually. Though I'm sure Lady Benezia would send her regards. I won't insult you by asking for surrender. We're here to kill you."

Rulamin (salarian businessman), overheard on a comm in the lounge: "It's the quietest place I've visited off homeworld. Everything is built with a good view, and there are constables everywhere."
