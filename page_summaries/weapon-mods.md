Weapon Mods were customization attachments in Mass Effect 3 used to augment any of the five standard weapon types: assault rifles, shotguns, sniper rifles, submachine guns, and heavy pistols. Available mods included high-caliber barrels, scopes, and bayonet attachments. Only two mods could be applied to a weapon at once, and the same mod could not be applied twice. Once found, a mod could be fitted to every weapon of its type at a weapon bench; finding a duplicate immediately upgraded that mod to the next level, up to level V (some mods could not reach level V on a first playthrough). Shops always carried at least two upgrade levels of a given mod. In Legendary Edition, all single-player DLC mod requirements were waived because the DLC was included by default.

Assault rifle mods included the Stability Damper (reduced kickback), Magazine Upgrade, Piercing Mod, Extended Barrel, Precision Scope, Omni-Blade, Thermal Scope (revealed enemies through walls and smoke, +50% weight), High-Velocity Barrel (+50% weight), and Ultralight Materials. Heavy pistol mods included the Melee Stunner, Piercing Mod, Magazine Upgrade, High-Caliber Barrel, Scope, Ultralight Materials, Heavy Barrel, Cranial Trauma System (increased headshot lethality), and Power Magnifier (enhanced tech and biotic power damage). Shotgun mods included the Spare Thermal Clip, Shredder Mod, Blade Attachment, High Caliber Barrel, Smart Choke (tightened pellet spread), Omni-Blade, High-Velocity Barrel, and Ultralight Materials. Sniper rifle mods included the Concentration Mod (biometric aim assist with time dilation), Spare Thermal Clip, Extended Barrel, Piercing Mod, Enhanced Scope, Thermal Scope, High-Velocity Barrel, and Ultralight Materials. SMG mods included Ultralight Materials, Magazine Upgrade, High Caliber Barrel, Heat Sink (chance to negate heat), Scope, High-Velocity Barrel, Recoil System, and Power Magnifier. Many DLC mods added 50% weapon weight, and several mod pairs were mutually exclusive (for example Extended Barrel with High-Velocity Barrel, or Scope with Power Magnifier).

Extended Barrel and High Caliber Barrel damage bonuses stacked additively with class and power damage bonuses. Piercing, Shredder, and High-Velocity mods worked by ignoring part of the damage reduction that bullets suffered against armored targets, benefiting rapid-fire low-damage weapons more; the "reduced damage" wording only meant shots fired through solid objects lost potential damage. Weapons such as the N7 Crusader, N7 Typhoon, Javelin, Executioner Pistol, M-98 Widow, and Black Widow penetrated by default with no damage penalty. NPC squad members did not use or benefit from scope mods, which also reduced the player's peripheral vision and targeting sensitivity while zoomed.
