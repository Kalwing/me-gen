This war-asset list collected the raw materials, equipment and expertise devoted directly to building the Crucible, the Prothean-designed superweapon whose blueprints were recovered on Mars. Most entries came from scanning planets in Reaper-controlled systems or from side missions.

Power and fuel assets included Advanced Starship Fuel (75, an improved helium-3 mix from N7: Fuel Reactors), Emergency Fuel Pods (30), the Element Zero Converter (50) and Optimized Eezo Capacitors (15, from Kenneth Donnelly and Gabriella Daniels), plus Advanced Power Relays (50) and a Radiation Shielding Sheath (25) to protect the device. Manufacturing and structural assets included Volus Fabrication Units (45), a Volus Engineering Team (50) from Elkoss Combine, and Shadow Broker Starship Tech (50) repurposed for safe discharge of huge energies.

Weapons and sensor assets included Javelin Missile Launchers (50), dark-energy rockets produced for allied dreadnoughts, and the Interferometric Array (45), which let the Crucible tune into mass relay command switches and map every Reaper in the galaxy. Data assets tied to the Protheans included Prothean Data Files (75) and the Dark Energy Dissertation (1, rising to 4) by Dr. Conrad Verner, whose theory that dark energy affects the passage of time helped decode Prothean instructions. Advanced AI Relays (45) came from salvaging the geth dreadnought if Admiral Xen was supported over Tali'Zorah, and the Haptic Optics Array (50) and Jovian Dissertation (5) added further engineering insight.

The two highest-value assets were mutually exclusive: the Reaper Brain (110), the reactivated central processor of the human proto-Reaper if the Collector Base was saved, which also lowered the EMS requirement for the best "Control" ending; and the Reaper Heart (100), the proto-Reaper's incomplete power core if the base was destroyed, which lowered the requirement for the best "Destroy" ending. Both were studied under strict drone-assisted supervision for use in powering the Crucible.
