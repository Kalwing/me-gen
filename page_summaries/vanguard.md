The Vanguard was a combat class available across Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, and Mass Effect 3, and as a profile in Mass Effect: Andromeda. Vanguards combined the offensive biotic powers of the Adept with the weapons and armor training of the Soldier, forming the front line of the squad. They used biotics to disable and soften enemies while shielding themselves long enough to finish foes at close range with gunfire. In Mass Effect they wore light armor (upgradable to medium), could gain specialist training in shotguns and pistols, and equipped advanced bio-amps; the class talent increased shotgun and pistol damage and biotic protection, and could specialize into the combat-oriented Shock Trooper or the biotics-oriented Nemesis. All Krogan Battlemasters, including Urdnot Wrex, belonged to this class.

In Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, Vanguards were known for a high-risk, high-reward style, closing quickly on enemies and destroying them at short range. They were outfitted with L5n implants enabling the Biotic Charge, which struck an opponent with great force while bringing the Vanguard into close combat. Powers included Incendiary Ammo, Cryo Ammo, Charge/Biotic Charge, Shockwave, Nova (ME3), and Pull. In Mass Effect 3 the Vanguard had a weight capacity bonus of 15 to 70 depending on Assault Mastery rank. In Mass Effect: Andromeda the Vanguard profile reconfigured Ryder's implant and physiology to absorb energy from enemies on melee impact and channel it into shields, with bonus powers Siphoning Strikes and Biotic Jump & Evades. By July 2021 the Vanguard was the second most-picked class among Mass Effect Legendary Edition players, used by 21%.
