Oriana Lawson was Miranda Lawson's genetically identical younger sister, raised by an adoptive family and living a normal life, initially unaware of her origins or of Miranda. She was grown by Miranda's father, Henry Lawson, in 2166 when Miranda was a teenager, to replace Miranda in his plans for a dynasty. Realizing this, Miranda took the infant Oriana to Cerberus, who arranged the adoption.

In 2185, as Oriana neared her 19th birthday on Illium, Henry paid Niket to find and retrieve her; Niket used the Eclipse mercenaries. Accompanied by Commander Shepard, Miranda stopped the Eclipse from taking Oriana. Shepard could later suggest Miranda actually speak with her sister, and the two bonded, discovering shared traits such as playing the violin and loving the adagio of Nielsen's Fifth; Oriana wanted to work in colony development. Oriana afterward emailed Shepard her thanks, asking that Miranda not be told and that Miranda stop reading Shepard's mail.

By 2186, Henry captured Oriana and took her to Sanctuary on Horizon, presented as a refugee haven but actually a facility experimenting on refugees to turn them into husks or indoctrinate them, seeking a means to control the Reapers. Miranda tracked her there. Shepard's team arrived pursuing Kai Leng and found Oriana held as a human shield by Henry, who claimed she had tried to shoot him. Shepard could talk Henry down or shoot Oriana in the leg to give Miranda a clear shot; Miranda then threw Henry from the tower with her biotics. If Miranda survived the Collector base, she comforted the traumatized Oriana and took her away; if Miranda was mortally wounded by Kai Leng, she died telling Oriana to have the normal life she never could. If Miranda died on the suicide mission, Oriana herself, taking the surname Lawson, broadcast the warnings exposing Sanctuary and left video logs of Henry's activities before the tower standoff, in which she too could die by falling with her father. In a Control ending, Miranda and Oriana were seen working together on Reaper schematics.
