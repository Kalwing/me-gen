The Migrant Fleet, also called the Flotilla, was the massive collection of starships that became home to the quarians after the geth drove them from Rannoch about three centuries before the trilogy. It consisted of roughly 50,000 ships housing some 17 million quarians, the largest array of spacefaring vessels in the galaxy; it could take days to pass an entire mass relay, and some vessels dated from the original flight. It was rarely welcoming to outsiders, since any risk to the Fleet was a risk to the species, and quarians normally left only for the Pilgrimage.

Conditions aboard were extremely cramped, with cargo holds converted into individual living compartments; the cruiser Idenna held nearly 700 quarians where a comparable Alliance cruiser held around 80. Larger crews raised a captain's social status. Three heavily defended Liveships at the Fleet's center provided most of its food, growing crops such as the high-protein celery keleven; losing one would starve millions. The Fleet was nominally under martial law but functioned democratically: each ship had an elected civilian council, representatives sat on the Conclave (civilian government), and a five-member Admiralty Board could override the Conclave only by then resigning or facing arrest, which had happened only four times in three centuries. The navy was small but aggressive, shooting to kill ships whose motives could not be established. On arrival in a system quarians strip-mined planets and undercut local labor, making their approach unpopular. The Fleet was organized into four groups: the Patrol Fleet (Shala'Raan vas Tonbay), the Civilian Fleet including the Liveships (Zaal'Koris vas Qwib-Qwib), the Heavy Fleet (Han'Gerrel vas Neema), and Special Projects research vessels (Daro'Xen vas Moreh).

In Mass Effect: Ascension (2183), Kahlee Sanders, Gillian Grayson and Hendel Mitra were brought aboard the Idenna by Lemm'Shal nar Tesleya to escape Cerberus. Captain Ysin'Mal argued the Fleet was dying for lack of spaceworthy ships and would be unsustainable within 90 years; a Cerberus attack led by the traitor Golo finally persuaded the Fleet to send cruisers on long-term missions. In Mass Effect 2 (2185), Shepard and Tali travelled to the Fleet for Tali's treason trial over geth parts sent to her father Rael'Zorah, culminating in retaking the lab ship Alarei. In Mass Effect 3 (2186), Daro'Xen's scanning countermeasure derived from Rael'Zorah's research crippled geth ships, prompting a 3-2 Admiralty vote to invade geth space; the quarians armed the Civilian Fleet, fitted Thanix weapons, destroyed a geth Dyson sphere, and drove the geth back to Tikkun. The geth accepted Reaper upgrades and turned the war until Shepard disabled the Reaper signal on Rannoch. Depending on Shepard's choices the Fleet was destroyed, the geth were annihilated, or quarians and geth made peace; if the quarians survived, the Fleet joined the defense of Earth. Notable vessels included the Alarei, Idenna, Neema, Rayya, Qwib-Qwib, Moreh, Tonbay and the Liveships Rayya, Shellen and Chayym.
