Jentha was a Blue Suns commander on Omega in 2185. She handled the interchange with the freelancers and underlings on behalf of her leader Tarak, who was too busy coordinating the operation to kill Archangel. Jentha had suggested to Tarak that the Blue Suns cooperate with the other groups in the operation, the Blood Pack and Eclipse, but he preferred to let each group and the freelancers take their shots separately while the Blue Suns waited. She believed Archangel would not last long, as the turian had been holed up for over a day, and she held a personal hatred of him because his vigilantism had cost the Blue Suns substantially. After Archangel attacked Tarak at his home, Tarak became paranoid and jumpy, constantly second-guessing Jentha and making her life miserable.

Commander Shepard encountered Jentha while posing as a freelancer to recruit Archangel. If Zaeed Massani was in the squad, Jentha acknowledged that Zaeed and Tarak knew each other; otherwise she questioned why Shepard's team was not outside preparing for the next wave. She justified her leader's distrust of outsiders and shared information about the plan of attack, Blue Suns operations, and Archangel if questioned.

Later, when Shepard and Archangel fought off the mercenary groups, Jentha was deployed from Tarak's gunship in the first wave of Blue Suns troops, alongside Troopers, Centurions, and Legionnaires. She was armed with an M-76 Revenant, could apply Disruptor Ammo, had high shields, armor, and health, and could fully restore her shields once her armor was destroyed. She was killed in the ensuing firefight. Jentha was notable for a unique human hairstyle unavailable to female Shepard without modding; in Mass Effect Legendary Edition it was changed to one available to female Shepard.
