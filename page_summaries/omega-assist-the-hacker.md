"Omega: Assist the Hacker" was a side mission in the Mass Effect 3: Omega DLC. After entering Aria's Bunker, Commander Shepard could speak with Aria's hacker, who asked Shepard to trigger three flagged terminals scattered through Cerberus-controlled areas of Omega to increase the bunker's operational capabilities and control over the station.

All three consoles were located within the Talon Territory mission area. The first was to the right at the top of the ladder to the airlock, next to lift controls. The second was in the lower monitor area's right corner in a room full of monitors and Cerberus troops. The third was next to an Injured Talon in one of two rooms off to the right, near the room that deactivated the Cerberus lockdown. Finding all three prompted the hacker to report that she had blocked enemy access to critical areas, making the frontal assault less treacherous. The hacker and the mechanic in Aria's bunker were among the few NPCs in the game who reacted when Shepard walked away mid-conversation, and leaving too quickly could prevent the journal from updating with their missions.
