Defensive Training was a passive Combat skill in Mass Effect: Andromeda and Cora Harper's starting Combat skill; it was not available in multiplayer. In the game's fiction it represented Cora's military training and suit systems keeping her alive in dangerous situations. Ranking it up granted stacking survivability bonuses: +20% max shields at rank 1, +20% max health at rank 2, and improved health and shield regeneration at rank 3. Rank 4 branched between Defense Systems (+25% max shields, +30% health regeneration) and Physical Training (+25% max health, +30% melee damage). Rank 5 branched between Active Barrier, which partially restored shields (20%) for Cora and nearby allies whenever she killed a foe with a power, and Hold the Line, which reduced incoming damage by 20 and added +25% max health and shields when Cora was at low health. Rank 6 branched between Barrier Projection (+20% max shields for Cora and all allies while she was alive) and Saving Barrier (restored 20% of Cora's shields when her health was low); rank 6 was unavailable until her loyalty mission, Cora Harper: At Duty's Edge, was completed.
