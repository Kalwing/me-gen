Graphite (chemical symbol C) was a collectible resource in Mass Effect: Andromeda, described as a crystalline carbon mineral that is an electrical conductor and useful in many manufacturing processes. It could be detected via orbital scan and mined with the Nomad, or purchased from certain merchants, and was found on the planets Elaaden and Eos. Graphite was used to craft a small number of items, including the M-90 Indra sniper rifle, the N7 Valiant and P.A.W. assault rifles, the Scorpion and Talon pistols, and several ND1 Nomad upgrades (Advanced Life Support, Improved Boost, Supercharged Boost). One crafted copy of each listed item required 8,890 total graphite.
