Huerta Memorial Hospital was located on the Presidium of the Citadel, occupying one of the spokes connecting opposite sides of the torus, with the Presidium Commons visible below. It was named after President Christopher Huerta of the United North American States on Earth. Touted as a leader in premium medical care, it could accommodate all major Citadel races and some exotic ones, staffing 12 full-time doctors and more than 50 support staff, with many experts specializing in xenomedicine.

In Mass Effect 3 the hospital served as a Citadel hub. The patient lounge featured an Avina terminal, Doctor Chakwas, Dr. Chloe Michel, Aeian T'Goni, and Thane Krios before the attack on the Citadel; Dr. Ravin sought a modified medi-gel formula for hanar patients, and Jacob Taylor appeared after the ex-Cerberus scientists were rescued. The inpatient wing held Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko recovering from injuries sustained on Mars, and Thane Krios on his deathbed after the Citadel attack. The hospital anchored several side missions, including Citadel: Alien Medi-Gel Formula, Citadel: Biotic Amp Interfaces, Citadel: Cerberus Turian Poison, Citadel: Chemical Treatment, and Silean Nebula: Rings of Alune.
