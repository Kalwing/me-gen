Citadel: Barla Von was a Mass Effect 3 mission in which Barla Von, a volus agent of the Shadow Broker stationed behind the counter at the Presidium Commons Bank on the Citadel, provided Commander Shepard the location of Shadow Broker forces trapped in the Dranek system inside the Krogan DMZ, so they could be recruited to the war effort. The mission became available after Priority: Palaven. During Shepard's second or later visit to the Citadel, Liara, found at Apollo's Cafe in the Presidium Commons, told Shepard to contact Barla Von about a mercenary group that could be recruited for the war. The mission did not appear in the journal until Shepard spoke with the volus for details.

The Krogan DMZ opened only after Priority: Sur'Kesh, several events after the mission could first be acquired. Shepard traveled to the Dranek cluster and scanned the planet Rothla to find the Shadow Broker Wet Squad, a War Asset. After securing it, Shepard returned to the Citadel and reported to Barla Von to complete the mission, earning 5 reputation and 120 experience. The mission had to be finished before launching Priority: Tuchanka, because intervening events reshaped the Citadel's contents before Shepard could return in a noncombatant capacity, one consequence being that Barla Von was absent from his post from then on.
