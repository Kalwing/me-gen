This page was a roster index for Mass Effect 3, listing links to its characters grouped as protagonist, squad members, temporary squad members, allies, and adversaries. It held roughly fifty entries, including Commander Shepard, squadmates such as Ashley Williams, EDI, Garrus Vakarian, James Vega, Javik, Kaidan Alenko, Liara T'Soni and Tali'Zorah, a large temporary-squad and allies list including David Anderson, Aria T'Loak, Urdnot Wrex, Admiral Steven Hackett and Mordin Solus, and adversaries such as Maya Brooks, the Catalyst, The Illusive Man, Kai Leng and Donnel Udina. It contained no original prose beyond the link tables.
