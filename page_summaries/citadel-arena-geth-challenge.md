Citadel Arena: Geth Challenge was a side mission in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, available after Citadel Docks: Retake the Normandy, once the Armax Arsenal Arena opened with the rest of Silversun Strip. It was typically the first arena challenge Shepard received. After one arena match, Dave Shanel, self-styled "Anti-President" of the Citadel University Engineering Club, emailed the Combat Sim Controls. He said Shepard's match had drawn a following at the club, that a member poll had chosen geth over Reapers or Cerberus because those felt too much like the news, and asked for a match against geth on the Blasteroid map. The club could not pay but offered to build Shepard a weapon mod, as some members worked in weapons research.

To complete the challenge, Shepard played a combat match in the Armax Arsenal Arena against geth on the Blasteroid map; because it was the first challenge received, not all match options were available until non-mission matches had been completed. The challenge was not mutually exclusive with other arena challenges, such as the Medi-Gel Challenge. After a successful match, Shanel sent a follow-up email, and reading it awarded the SMG Power Magnifier weapon mod and +2 Reputation, with a joking suggestion that Shepard test the mod before trusting it.
