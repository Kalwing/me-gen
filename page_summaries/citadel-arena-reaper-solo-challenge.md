Citadel Arena: Reaper Solo Challenge was a side mission in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, available after Citadel Docks: Retake the Normandy, once the Armax Arsenal Arena opened with the rest of Silversun Strip. After Shepard completed two arena matches, the "Hour 20" news team emailed the Combat Sim Controls through executive producer Lara J. Delante of "Hour 20 with Azad Carter" on the Alliance News Network. The team wanted an iconic lead-in for its war-progress segments, noting that its real Reaper battle footage suffered from bad lighting, unstable cameras, and significant risk. Because the Armax Arena recorded its sessions, they asked Shepard's permission to film Shepard fighting Reaper enemies alone, with likeness-release forms attached and compensation offered.

To complete the challenge, Shepard purchased the Reaper Combat Set enemy pack, a silver-level prize, using winnings from a previous match, then set the simulator to Reaper enemies with no squadmates and completed the combat. Other options could be arranged to finish additional arena challenges simultaneously, such as the Medi-Gel Challenge or Thermal Clip Challenge. Afterward Delante sent a second email confirming good footage was captured and that payment was attached. Receiving and reading it granted 5,000 credits and +2 Reputation. This challenge was a prerequisite for Citadel Arena: Prothean Challenge.
