Project Firewalker: Prothean Site was the final Firewalker assignment, acquired after Project Firewalker: Volcano Station. From data recovered at Karumto, Cerberus located Dr. Cayce's last known position on Kopis, a moon orbiting Makhaira in the Hades Nexus, where he had set out to excavate a Prothean site. Scans showed Prothean artifacts protected by a powerful energy barrier and hidden automated defenses.

The mission began with Shepard's squad in the Hammerhead. The kinetic barrier over the dig site was too strong for weapons, so it had to be brought down by destroying the generators powering it, following the power lines while evading Rocket Drones. Four eezo deposits could be collected outside the base. Once the last generator fell, the barrier dropped and the squad continued into the ruins on foot, passing dead Blue Suns mercenaries hired to guard the site.

Logs and datapads revealed the tragedy: Dr. Robert O'Loy had been indoctrinated by the Reapers and was leaking the team's data to a geth ship, and had made a deal with the Collectors, trading the Prothean find for the safety of his wife Helen's colony. Dr. Cayce discovered O'Loy's betrayal, killed him, then found the Reapers "still in my head" and resolved to destroy the relic and himself. Shepard hacked the console and examined the relic—similar to the one on Eletania—which glowed green and shrank to a manageable size, afterward displayed on the coffee table in Shepard's cabin. A follow-up email from Project Firewalker reported that an energy burst during the retrieval contained coded data advancing research on energy transfer and biotics, and that the artifact was inert and not dangerous.
