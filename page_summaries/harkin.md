Harkin was one of the first human C-Sec officers, described by Captain Anderson as "a disgrace to the species." Over 20 years in C-Sec he was known for roughing up suspects in custody, taking bribes, and abusing drugs and alcohol. The human embassy repeatedly covered for him to protect humanity's reputation, but once enough humans served in C-Sec that protection ended, and Executor Pallin suspended him for drinking on the job shortly before the Eden Prime attack.

In Mass Effect, Commander Shepard sought Harkin at Ambassador Udina's suggestion to find a lead on Garrus Vakarian. Harkin was found drinking at Chora's Den, complaining about C-Sec keeping a file on him, and amused that Shepard served under Anderson; against a female Shepard he was sexually harassing. Before revealing Garrus's location he forced the squad to hear a story claiming Anderson had been a Spectre whose disastrous first mission got him expelled by the Council. He eventually directed Shepard to Dr. Michel's med clinic in the Upper Wards. His story proved inaccurate: Anderson had only been a Spectre candidate, and his candidacy was sabotaged by Saren Arterius.

By 2185 Harkin had been fired from C-Sec and become a racketeer on the Citadel under the alias "Fade," having facilitated Sidonis's disappearance into the Terminus Systems. Tracking him down during Garrus's loyalty mission, Shepard and Garrus fought Blue Suns mercenaries allied with Harkin and reprogrammed mechs. Cornered and interrogated by Garrus, Harkin arranged a meeting with Sidonis. Unless Shepard intervened, Garrus shot Harkin in the leg to hinder his escape; if prevented, Garrus instead headbutted him viciously. EDI later reported that C-Sec took Harkin into custody. A cut assignment, Citadel: Oculon Syndicate, had featured Harkin as an underworld contact.
