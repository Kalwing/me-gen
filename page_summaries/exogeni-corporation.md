ExoGeni Corporation was an interstellar company specializing in planetary exploration and colonization. It funded several human colonies and in the process secured resource rights to worlds with potential undiscovered resources or alien technology, such as its colony on Feros. Juliana Baynham described it as a genius at "repurposing anything and everything." ExoGeni founded the Zhu's Hope settlement on Feros, funding it in exchange for valuable finds from the Prothean ruins, and converted an ancient tower above the Skyway into a base of operations, housing most researchers near the labs and using Zhu's Hope as a port.

Dissatisfied with returns from Feros until the Thorian was discovered beneath Zhu's Hope, ExoGeni "repurposed" the colony as a control group, deliberately allowing the colonists to be infected so it could study the Thorian's mind-controlling properties. The geth attack killed many employees and cut communications; once ExoGeni learned what had happened, it decided the situation was out of control and told its sole surviving representative, Ethan Jeong, that it planned to purge the colony to keep the secret. Jeong found ExoGeni held a dossier on Shepard. Depending on how Shepard handled Jeong, the company pulled or increased funding after the Thorian's destruction, with a Citadel news story covering the stock impact.

ExoGeni also conducted planetary studies: it was one of two Alliance corporations studying the Ontarom biosphere, ran a long-term plan to introduce water to Trebin, was involved in colonizing Chasca (supplied from Feros), and operated satellites billing ships for water-ice removal from Supay. A small facility on Nodacrux, led by a Dr. Ross, received Feros samples and found a way to make Thorian Creepers docile, but the Thorian's death sent them berserk; restricted communications delayed any rescue. The corporation also secretly researched Reaper technology with disastrous results: a survey team on Trebin died or became husks after excavating an artifact, and Cerberus collaborated with ExoGeni to deliver dragon's teeth to Chasca, converting that entire colony into husks.

In 2185 an ExoGeni researcher named Dr. Farrin hired the Blue Suns to guard Prothean artifacts recovered from Joab, but the mercenaries betrayed the team. If Feros remained viable, ExoGeni expanded advertising for third-stage colonization. By 2186 an ExoGeni team still on Feros studying Prothean remnants had expertise valuable to the Crucible project. In Andromeda, ExoGeni products such as M-Spec magnets turned up as low-value salvage, and Nakmor Drack recalled the company running dangerous mining operations. Known employees included Ethan Jeong, Juliana Baynham, Lizbeth Baynham, Gavin Hossle, Dr. Ross, and Dr. Farrin.
