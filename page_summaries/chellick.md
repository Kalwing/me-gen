Chellick was a C-Sec detective on the Citadel and a former colleague of Garrus Vakarian; cut conversations gave his first name as Decian. In Mass Effect: Homeworlds, where his name was spelled Chellik, he handled a case of two quarians who had stowed away on a turian freighter, among them Tali'Zorah nar Rayya. He talked the ship captain out of pressing charges but had no sympathy for the stowaways, holding prejudiced stereotypical views of their race; he ignored Tali's attempt to tell him about something valuable in her possession, uncuffed the pair, and ordered them off the Citadel by the next day. In Mass Effect, Chellick worked to combat illegal arms trading on the Citadel with the help of an informant named Jenna, who worked undercover at Chora's Den. Disguised as a drunk, he bumped into Commander Shepard leaving the club and whispered for Shepard to come to C-Sec. In his office he complained that Shepard had nearly blown Jenna's cover and offered to release her from the job if Shepard would instead meet his krogan contact Jax, who was smuggling weapon modifications onto the station, and receive the mods while posing as a buyer in the Markets. If Shepard kept the cover intact, Chellick was very pleased, let Jenna quit, and passed some modifications to Shepard; if Shepard shot Jax, Chellick lost all his leads and angrily refused further contact. In Mass Effect 2, an Emily Wong news vid referred to him as Executor Chellick, implying he had succeeded Executor Pallin as head of C-Sec, though this is generally regarded as a mistake, since Pallin canonically survived and remained Executor in Mass Effect: Retribution. Cut conversations with Sergeant Haron established that Chellick let Shepard remain armed on the Citadel because the Commander tended to find trouble; Garrus considered Chellick the "safe" choice to lead C-Sec, but not the one he would make.
