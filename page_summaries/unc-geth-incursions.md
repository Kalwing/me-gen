UNC: Geth Incursions was an assignment in Mass Effect in which Commander Shepard destroyed a series of geth outposts in the Armstrong Nebula of the Skyllian Verge before the incursion could become a full-scale invasion. It became available after Shepard received command of the Normandy (prerequisite: Citadel: Expose Saren, Feros: The Thorian, or Virmire: Assault) by traveling to the Armstrong Nebula, where Admiral Hackett transmitted the details of four geth outposts. It could also be acquired by decrypting a geth terminal at the ExoGeni building on Feros or in the Genophage Labs on Virmire (each requiring at least 9 Decryption), which revealed the geth were massing a force in the cluster on Saren's orders, away from "prying eyes."

The four outposts were on Maji (Vamshi system), Casbin (Hong system), Antibaar (Tereshkova system), and Rayingri (Gagarin system), and could be tackled in any order. Each was defended by Geth Snipers, Geth Rocket Troopers, and heavier units such as Geth Colossi and Armatures in guard towers and barricades, with Geth Dropships arriving afterward to deposit reinforcements including Geth Primes, Shock Troopers, and Destroyers. Maji added a Colossus drop; Casbin featured a lingering dropship and an optional post of three Armatures; Antibaar was tightly ringed by mountains and jammed radar; Rayingri was an underground research facility infested with husks that could open doors, followed by a geth ambush and a dropship outside.

After all four outposts fell, Shepard's squadmates deduced from an incoming signal that the true base of operations lay elsewhere in the cluster, on Solcrum, the moon beside Notanban in the Grissom system. Solcrum's underground base was guarded by patrolling Geth Colossi and manned by elite geth: Geth Destroyers, a Geth Juggernaut, and Geth Hoppers (Stalkers and a Sapper). Inside, a terminal had transmitted a recording of a quarian lament to the geth homeworlds. Shepard could hack a data terminal in the back room for geth data files and later pass them to Tali'Zorah in the Normandy's Engineering Bay, informing story decisions about the geth. Completing the assignment prompted a congratulatory message from Hackett stating the Alliance would investigate the incursions further. This connected to the supplementary assignment Tali and the Geth. The assignment is referred to internally as involving "Tali and the Geth."
