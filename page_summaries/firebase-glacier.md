Multiplayer map page. Firebase Glacier is a Mass Effect 3 multiplayer map, the smallest in multiplayer, set at a research facility. Its in-game description ties it to the batarian colony of Lorek in the Omega Nebula, whose fierce resistance to Cerberus and Reaper occupation made it a training camp for other anti-Reaper forces; a developer note clarifies the map is actually located on the planet Sanctum. In single-player, the Galaxy at War mission N7: Cerberus Lab takes place here. Mass Effect 3: Retaliation added a Hazard version set during a Seeker Swarm containment breach, in which a massive, invulnerable, slow-moving swarm circles the map damaging anyone near it; without Retaliation, an original version with a different central-room and ammo-dump layout is used. The map's areas are the Balcony, Science Lab, Hub, Upper Basement, and Lower Basement. It is described as the fastest map for solo speedrunning the game.
