Magnesium (Mg) was a collectible crafting resource in Mass Effect: Andromeda, described as a light and sturdy metal whose alloys were integral to many complex Andromeda Initiative structures. It could be detected by orbital scan and mined with the Nomad on the planets H-047c and Havarl, or purchased from certain merchants. It was used in a small number of craftable items: the Equalizer pistol, the Inferno sniper rifle, the Scattershot shotgun, and the Sweeper assault rifle, totaling 6,900 magnesium for one full rank I-X copy of each.
