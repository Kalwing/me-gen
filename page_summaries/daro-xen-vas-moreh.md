Admiral Daro'Xen vas Moreh was a member of the quarian Admiralty Board known for her clinical intellect and keen scientific interest in the geth, whom she regarded as machines built solely to serve their creators. She was said to have performed surgery on her childhood toys without guilt. She wished to return the geth to quarian control.

In Mass Effect 2, Xen was one of three admirals judging Tali'Zorah's treason trial, alongside Zaal'Koris vas Qwib Qwib and Han'Gerrel vas Neema. She viewed the trial as "a farce born of fear," called Zaal'Koris a coward and Han'Gerrel "an aging warship," and considered war with the geth foolish, though she had no qualms about experimenting on active geth. If Shepard brought Legion, she found it fascinating and mused about experimenting on it, prompting Legion to state it was not available and to call her control plans "unsound," a view Tali shared. Afterward she messaged Shepard that had Rael'Zorah's research been presented, humanity would have benefited; instead the quarians would reclaim their homeworld and the largest synthetic army in the galaxy.

In Mass Effect 3, Xen's research and development corps created white-noise jamming against geth ladar systems, prompting the Admiralty to vote for war, and she led the Special Projects group. If Tali was absent, Xen provided electronic support during the mission to disable a geth dreadnought, gave Shepard a prototype Arc Pistol, and later left in a geth fighter. She created the Sync Laser marking technology that cut through geth jamming, critical to the assault on the Reaper base on Rannoch. She repeatedly expressed interest in dissecting Legion or the Geth VI, argued with Tali over search-and-rescue versus salvage priorities, and briefed Shepard on the rescue of Admiral Koris. If she survived the geth-quarian war, she was sent to help build the Crucible, where Shala'Raan vas Tonbay described her as dissatisfied with the device but lending her technical skills. She was voiced by Claudia Black.
