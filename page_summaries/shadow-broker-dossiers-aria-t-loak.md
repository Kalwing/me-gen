This page collects the intelligence file on Aria T'Loak found in the Shadow Broker's Dossiers terminal, comprising a summary line, an intercepted audio transmission, and an internal Afterlife planning message. The file's header describes Aria as the "De facto leader of Omega. Three centuries of commando experience. Aloof, paranoid, excellent organizational and management skills."

The audio transcript is a recording taken in a private room of the Afterlife nightclub at 04:14 AM, in which Aria confronts a shackled turian named Kalus Enrix and, through him, addresses the Shadow Broker directly before apparently killing Enrix.

Representative lines:

- Aria, to the captive turian: "Don't bother. Those shackles don't have a release catch."
- Aria, on how long the spy went unnoticed: "This was very good. The turian's been at Afterlife for a week. Usually, I spot them earlier."
- Aria, addressing the Broker through a hidden speaker: "I know you're listening through that speaker hidden in Enrix's collar. I bet Enrix doesn't even know who he's really spying for, does he, Shadow Broker?"
- Aria, stating her grievance: "You don't play favorites, Broker. I admire that. But what I don't like is how you think anything twitches a muscle on Omega without my knowing it!"
- Aria, on the Broker's operatives: "Feel free to waste as many lives as you want, sending your agents down to my world."
- Broker's file note: "Kalus Enrix's death has not yet compromised our other agent placed in the Afterlife Nightclub."

The second document is an "Afterlife Entertainment" message from Aria to events coordinator Churkas Flin, listing bookings for the coming month: spacing out the Sinyah Sisters and the Bekke Burlesque act, restocking the fight pit with krogan and humans instead of vorcha, flagging an Eclipse infiltrator posing as a band's singer ("Bring him to me after last call"), booking hanar jugglers for a laugh, and considering making the casino-ship a "VIP Only" event where invited guests "who need killing" are left to kill each other.
