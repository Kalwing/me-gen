"Eden Prime: Resistance Movement" was a Mass Effect 3 mission in which the colonists of Eden Prime fought the Cerberus occupation and Commander Shepard aided them by gathering Cerberus intel. It required the mission "Priority: Eden Prime" and was acquired by accessing a terminal in the right-hand building on landing, with Liara noting the intel could greatly help the resistance.

The mission involved collecting three pieces of intel from terminals across the colony buildings while completing the main mission. The intel documents revealed Cerberus operations: able-bodied colonists were being abducted under the cover story that they were doing safe manual labor at a research camp, with increased food rations used to quiet complaints; Cerberus had thinned its northern troops while maintaining a false show of strength because it lacked the manpower to fight the whole colony; and Cerberus had a source inside the resistance who warned of a planned attack on their security center, which they intended to use to destroy the resistance leadership. That source was Dr. William Cambiata, whom Cerberus planned to let escape with staged near-miss fire so the locals would see him as a hero and possibly put him in charge.

Collecting all three intel pieces resulted in an email stating the resistance pushed Cerberus off Eden Prime and improved the Eden Prime Support War Asset. Failing to collect them left the mission permanently incomplete, since Shepard could never return to Eden Prime once the Prothean Artifact lift was raised.
