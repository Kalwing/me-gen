After the Citadel Council hearing in Citadel: Expose Saren, Shepard could ask David Anderson for further leads against Saren Arterius, and he directed Shepard to Barla Von, a Shadow Broker agent located in the Presidium financial district bank; the mission was then briefly named Citadel: Shadow Broker. Barla Von, giving the information away for free, explained that Saren had betrayed the Shadow Broker and turned a former Broker agent named Fist to his side, and that the Broker had retaliated by hiring the krogan bounty hunter Urdnot Wrex to deal with Fist. The mission then became Citadel: Wrex. At C-Sec Academy, Shepard found Wrex confronting C-Sec officers who had detained him for threatening Fist; after a tense standoff the officers left and Wrex, finding his goals aligned with Shepard's, agreed to join the squad. Alternatively, if Shepard completed Citadel: Garrus first, Garrus Vakarian urged Shepard to recruit Wrex, and Shepard could go straight to C-Sec without visiting Barla Von. If Fist had already been dealt with at Chora's Den, Wrex was instead found leaning against the Dock 422 elevator, frustrated that Shepard had handled his contract; out of professional pride he gave Fist's bounty to Shepard and asked to join, wanting to be "where the action is" and anticipating a major conflict, while expressing disgust at the krogan serving Saren. Shepard could refuse a krogan on the team, in which case Wrex waited at Chora's Den to be recruited later, though the bounty was still paid. In Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, if Wrex had joined and survived Virmire, he led Clan Urdnot on Tuchanka and became the de facto krogan leader negotiating a genophage cure, assisting Shepard on Sur'Kesh and Tuchanka and fighting on Earth in the final battle. Otherwise his brother Wreav led the clan, with the Illusive Man claiming Wrex had returned to Tuchanka to unite the clans.
