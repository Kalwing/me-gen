Frag Grenade was a grenade power in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes, described as a shrapnel-packed grenade that ripped enemies apart. At rank 1 it dealt 600 damage (900 in multiplayer) over a 6.50 metre radius. Its six ranks allowed the player to increase grenade capacity, boost damage by 20 percent at rank 3, and at rank 4 choose between a 30 percent damage increase or a 30 percent larger impact radius. Rank 5 offered additional grenade capacity or bleed damage against organics over time, and rank 6 offered armor-piercing or shield-overload bonuses; the bleed and rank 6 evolutions were multiplicative, calculated from total rather than base damage.

Grenade powers had no recharge time and could be used at any moment as long as grenades were available, with each use consuming one grenade from a pool shared among all of a character's grenade powers. Frag Grenades were effective against groups of weaker enemies, staggered lighter foes caught in the blast, and could be bounced or arced around terrain to hit targets in cover. Player-thrown grenades detonated one second after contacting a horizontal surface or instantly on hitting an enemy; squadmate-thrown grenades in single-player travelled and detonated instantly. Frag Grenades could detonate non-biotic tech, fire, and cryo power combos but could not prime combos. The rank 6 Shield Overload evolution increased damage only to shields, not barriers. The power was available to Anderson during the prologue, though he carried no grenades, rendering it useless. Single-player users were the Soldier class, David Anderson, James Vega, and Grunt; multiplayer users were the Battlefield 3 Soldier and Human Soldier.
