Delan, short for Delanynder, was a hanar merchant who operated the Emporium on the Citadel Presidium in the first Mass Effect. Like all hanar he was extremely polite to everyone and was particularly welcoming toward Commander Shepard. He described the Emporium as selling only the "finest and most luxurious items that credits can buy." If asked why the hanar referred to themselves as "this one" and "it," Delan offered insight into hanar culture, giving Shepard a better understanding of the species. He sold licenses for the Sirta Foundation, Devlon Industries, and the Serrice Council, along with a Medi-gel capacity upgrade, in addition to his normal wares. The first time Shepard spoke to him, he referred to the shop as "my" shop, a pronoun hanar do not normally use. He was distinct from the human mechanic Delan encountered on Horizon in Mass Effect 2.
