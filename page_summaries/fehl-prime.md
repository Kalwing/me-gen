Fehl Prime was a human colony in the Terminus Systems and one of the top producers of pharmaceuticals for the Systems Alliance, which made its defense a military priority. The habitable planet had at least three moons, a desert-like colony region, and areas of massive bioluminescent fungus-like flora. It was also the site of an intact, functional Prothean interstellar communication device believed to be at least 70,000 years old, not being actively studied as of 2185.

In 2183, Fehl Prime was besieged by Blood Pack mercenaries led by the krogan Archuk. The Alliance sent several special forces squads, most of which were lost during the landing attempt. The only surviving unit, Delta Squad, defeated the Blood Pack, killing all except the krogan Brood, who was taken prisoner. Delta Squad was reassigned to guard the colony, which was rebuilt over the next two years and given a heavy anti-ship cannon and military-grade kinetic barriers.

When a jamming signal was detected, Delta Squad found and destroyed an alien bio-mechanical device on the colony's outskirts. Meanwhile a Collector ship arrived, incapacitating the colonists with Seeker Swarms and taking them aboard. Delta Squad fired a low-power shot from the defense cannon to stop the ship; the shot was deflected by its shields and the cannon was destroyed by return fire. Retreating to underground labs, the squad learned research there sought an antidote to the Seeker Swarms' paralytic agent, and with the captive Brood's help planned a counterattack.

As it departed, the Collector ship demolished the colony with its main proton cannon. Delta Squad boarded using Brood's shuttle and disabled the ship, but the colonists were lost; James Vega, acting squad leader, chose to rescue the asari Treeya, who held vital intel on the Collectors, rather than retrieve the colonists from the crashing ship. The Alliance erected a monument on Fehl Prime to the dead and later commended Vega for his choice, even though Commander Shepard ultimately ended the Collector threat and rendered the intel unnecessary. These events are dramatized in Mass Effect: Paragon Lost and referenced in Mass Effect 3.
