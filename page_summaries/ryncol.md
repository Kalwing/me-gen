Ryncol was an extremely potent liquor created by the krogan for the krogan. Its exact composition was unknown, but Grunt commented that it "hits aliens like ground glass." Humans could survive drinking it, though it was unsafe for volus; one datapad complaint compared a leaking YMIR Mech to "a volus after a mug of ryncol." When Commander Shepard asked the Dark Star Lounge bartender for five consecutive drinks, they were served ryncol, with the bartender joking Shepard would "set off radiological alarms."

Elcor and quarians could also survive ryncol, though quarians needed weeks of immuno-suppressants and anti-virals before even one shot, and it made them feel like "stained glass" and "sipping knives"; ancient quarians, before Migrant Fleet living ruined their immune systems, could drink it without issue. Ryncol had "pure" and cocktail variants and was sold in 190-proof bottles. The turian Dark Star bartender mixed a green ryncol cocktail from separate components that knocked Shepard out, leaving the Commander waking groggily on the Dark Star bathroom floor. Jack claimed Shepard's creme de menthe stash could sicken people worse than pure ryncol. Ryncol was highly flammable, and Grunt described using a thrown bottle as a Molotov cocktail against a C-Sec aircar on Silversun Strip. It was also highly acidic, capable of melting through machinery, and vomit from its consumption was purplish.
