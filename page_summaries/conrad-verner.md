Conrad Verner was a self-proclaimed "huge fan" of Commander Shepard who recurred across the trilogy only in save files imported from Mass Effect. In 2183 he approached Shepard in the upper Markets of the Citadel Wards, holding a romanticized view of Spectre life and eventually asking to be signed on as a fellow Spectre and squad member. Shepard could charm him into going home to his wife, intimidate him by putting a gun in his face (causing him to renounce Shepard as his hero), or tell him to get lost, in which case an elevator announcement later reported he had picked a fight with turians and been killed.

In 2185 Conrad appeared in the Eternity bar on Illium, impersonating an Alliance officer in replica N7 armor and demanding the bar's deed from bartender Matriarch Aethyta. He believed he was aiding an "undercover officer" investigating red sand sales, but was actually being used by a con artist after the deed. He remembered Shepard pointing a gun at him regardless of the 2183 outcome. After Shepard exposed the fake officer at Gateway Personal Defense (earning a discount), news reported Conrad had founded a refugee and orphan charity called "Shepards." If dismissed, a news report described him falling from a bus and into a bio-mass recycler.

In 2186, if Conrad had survived both prior encounters, Shepard met him in the Citadel refugee sector recruiting for Cerberus, near Avina, while investigating sabotage of medi-gel stores that Cerberus had carried out as a distraction. Persuaded to stop, Conrad revealed a doctorate in Xenoscience, sent Shepard his dissertation on dark energy, and connected Shepard with Gavin Hossle for ancient tech schematics useful to the Crucible. He admitted his "wife" was invented and that he kept a candlelit shrine to Shepard. When his Cerberus contact drew a weapon, Conrad tried to shield Shepard; he survived only if Shepard had earlier dealt with Jenna, who had sabotaged the agent's gun, and was otherwise shot dead. Mass Effect: Andromeda revealed a sister, Cassandra Verner, who joined the Andromeda Initiative and described him as "a couple quarts low on eezo."
