Jasox was a human mercenary hired to distract Urdnot Wrex from his target, Fist. He was the sole survivor of a group Wrex slaughtered in a warehouse near Fist's apartment; Jasox scored a sniper hit on Wrex's armor, irritating the krogan, and took a shotgun blast in the back moments later. As Wrex moved to finish him, Jasox removed his helmet and confessed his role as a distraction. He reminded Wrex that they had once worked a job together where Jasox pulled Wrex from a burning ship, hoping to be spared. Wrex countered that Jasox had shown up late for that job, forcing Wrex to kill the crew alone; Jasox replied he need not have shown up at all.

Walking out of the carnage together, Jasox remarked that the point-blank shotgun blast would leave a mark, and proposed grabbing a drink at a nearby bar, offering to pay. Growing suspicious, Wrex deduced the human was still trying to keep him from his target, which Jasox admitted, saying he had to try. Wrex, who had intended to return the merc's sniper rifle, decided against it and said so. As they parted, Jasox called out good luck; Wrex disregarded the blessing. Events are from Mass Effect: Foundation.
