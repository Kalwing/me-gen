Mass Effect: Foundation 12, tagged "A Prayer for the Wicked," was published June 25, 2014, written by Mac Walters and Jeremy Barlow with art by Tony Parker. In the framing sequence, the Illusive Man learns from Miranda Lawson that the agent Rasa is becoming untrustworthy while assembling the dossier on Thane Krios; the Illusive Man is willing to cut losses. Thane's dossier is obtained through a simple interview in which he recounts his life.

The bulk of the issue is Thane's backstory. As a drell assassin bound by his hanar Compact, Thane lined up a sniper shot on an elderly salarian war criminal hiding in a park when a drell woman, Irikah, stepped into his scope and warned the target, letting him flee. Disturbed by his own feelings, Thane tracked Irikah and saved her when two thugs broke into her lab to torch it, killing the drell attacker while she handled the turian. He confessed to being the sniper she had foiled and begged her for redemption. Over time she forgave him and the two grew close, married, and had a son, Kolyat. Following Irikah's advice, Thane asked his hanar handler to be released from his Compact; the hanar warned that not all are destined for happiness but complied.

Freedom left Thane listless. Irikah gave him a coin that had belonged to her father to rub for peace of mind. Unqualified for civilian work, Thane took menial jobs, knew little of fatherhood, and eventually returned to contract killing with Irikah's support, his jobs taking him ever farther from home. After he targeted a gang of batarian slavers, the survivors bought his identity from the Shadow Broker and, too afraid to face him directly, attacked while he was away. Kolyat hid, but Irikah was killed, leaving only bloodstains. Thane hunted the culprits one by one, giving them slow, torturous deaths unlike his usual clean method. He extracted the name of the last, Stiv Kay, sniped him, and finished him slowly. Returning to his seaside home, Thane threw Irikah's coin into the sea and vowed to walk the path he had been given.
