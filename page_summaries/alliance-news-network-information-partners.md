The Alliance News Network's information partners were correspondents across the galaxy who filed weekly reports in the days before the Reaper invasion of 2186 and dispatched news when possible during the war. The page collects their bylined articles. Amita Qasid reported (24 August 2186) that a leaked defense-committee document lent credence to "Citadel Conspiracy" claims, citing Commander Shepard's testimony that Saren Arterius's flagship was an AI hostile to organic life separate from the geth; Shepard had been relieved of duty, and Prime Minister Shastri deflected questions. Pietro Pagano reported (31 August) that Dalatrass Linron narrowly retained leadership of her salarian bloodline over the rival Narra. Diana Allers reported (7 September) that the Migrant Fleet refused Council weapons inspectors after allegedly acquiring dual-use ship materials at Korlus; Admiral Han'Gerrel vas Neema said quarian armaments were not the Council's business. Iris Dunnigan reported (14 September) an STG assessment calling Cerberus a "significant threat" that had grown drastically in size and power. Shirin Kazemi reported (21 September) a flood of batarian ships fleeing into the Exodus Cluster, claiming a hostile species had bombarded Khar'shan. Chisilix Palanurus filed two reports: Primarch Fedorian's address (28 September) naming the attackers "Reapers" and declaring war, and the Hierarchy's failed relay assault toward Palaven (5 October) after the loss of Taetrus.

The page also gathers unbylined "Shipboard News" articles Shepard could read aboard the Normandy SR-2 as the war progressed, contingent on player choices: a Cerberus raid on the turian colony Aephus; biotic Aresh Aghdashloo dying heroically defending an evacuation shuttle on Elysium; an Eden Prime resistance under Edward Crabb pushing Cerberus off-world with Alliance intel; Kal'Reegar and his quarian squad dying to hold a ground comm relay for turian forces, praised by a spokesman for Primarch Victus; Ruxius Rilius's column questioning the Migrant Fleet's secret movements; Rana Thanoptis, indoctrinated years earlier while working for Saren, killing asari officials before dying in custody; Urdnot Wrex being killed by C-Sec after confronting Shepard over a fake genophage cure, with C-Sec commander Armando-Owen Bailey whitewashing it as a gambling dispute; and Zhu's Hope colonists on Feros, led by spokesperson Lizbeth Baynham, repelling Reaper landings to cover nearby evacuations.
