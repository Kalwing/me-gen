The Alliance Navy was the space combat branch of the Systems Alliance military, with the Alliance Marines a specialized ground branch within it. Charged with protecting human colonies, stations, and mass relays, it concentrated its strength in fleets stationed at relay nexuses so it could respond quickly to attacks. It held over 200 vessels and several dreadnoughts at the time of the First Contact War in 2157, and by the Reaper War in 2186 it fielded nine dreadnoughts across at least eight fleets, each subdivided into flotillas of a cruiser and four to six frigates. Fighter carriers, dreadnought-sized ships armed mainly with fighters, were a human innovation. Its size and skill drew Citadel Council interest and helped make humanity a candidate Council species. After the Battle of the Citadel in 2183, Admiral Steven Hackett was made head of the Navy.

The First Fleet, largest in the Navy and based at Arcturus Station, guarded the Charon Relay; during the Reaper invasion it was cut in half, and Admiral Ines Lindholm sacrificed a tenth of the survivors to let the rest escape. The Second Fleet, under Admiral Kastanie Drescher, liberated Shanxi in 2157 and was later sacrificed by Hackett to let the Third and Fifth Fleets flee Arcturus. The Third Fleet was led by Admiral Nitesh Singh aboard the SSV Logan. The Fourth Fleet, stationed at Earth, was wiped out. The Fifth (Arcturus) Fleet, Hackett's command, frequently tasked Commander Shepard with missions and received the Palladium Star after the Battle of the Citadel; its 63rd Scout Flotilla under Rear Admiral Mikhailovich was to have received the Normandy. The Sixth and Seventh Fleets, near Terra Nova and Eden Prime, were bypassed entirely by the Reapers; the Sixth later rallied after Shepard's visit to Horizon. The Eighth Fleet was ambushed near Ontarom.

Ship naming conventions assigned dreadnoughts mountain names, cruisers city names, frigates battle names, and carriers names of great human figures; warships carried the "SSV" prefix. Dreadnought classes in service were the Everest and Kilimanjaro. The 2nd Frontier Division garrisoned Eden Prime and was nearly destroyed by Saren Arterius's geth attack in 2183. During the Fall of Earth the Navy suffered its worst defeat, losing Arcturus Station and 45,000 people. The Alliance Naval Exploration Flotilla, damaged by a 2185 mining-fraud scandal, later aided Crucible construction on Tyr.
