Bray was a batarian bodyguard who served Aria T'Loak. During Aria's 2186 campaign to retake Omega from Cerberus, depicted in the Mass Effect 3: Omega DLC, Bray helped coordinate the offensive. He first met Commander Shepard as Aria's contact on the Citadel, acting as co-driver of the skycar in which Aria pitched her invasion, and provided Shepard the coordinates of Aria's fleet stationed just outside the Citadel.

During the assault, Bray manned the helm of a captured Cerberus cruiser that slipped through the enemy fleet before being crippled by Omega's new defense cannons. He evacuated in a separate escape pod from Aria's, landed on Omega, and reported that only six pods survived. Aria ordered him to rally the survivors while she and Shepard disabled the anti-starship batteries. Bray then opened the hangar doors so Jarral could land troops, locked down Aria's Bunker, and directed Shepard to clear a Cerberus Combat Engineer jamming the cannons.

Aria assigned Bray to watch Nyreen Kandros, whom he lost track of while offloading supplies, drawing Aria's threat that only her manpower shortage spared his life. His patrol later "caught" Nyreen on the bunker perimeter, unaware she had already joined Aria's cause. Bray's team scouted maintenance tunnels and found Cerberus engineers moving to destroy Omega's central support columns; he delayed them until Aria and Shepard arrived, then guarded the facility. After the retaking of Afterlife, Bray was ordered either to dispose of General Oleg Petrovsky's body or to take the General away, and afterward waited at a docking bay to escort Shepard off Omega while Aria delivered her victory speech. He later emailed Shepard that the Talons had become Omega's new security force, honoring Nyreen Kandros's memory, and signed off noting that at least one batarian did not want Shepard dead. Trivia notes Bray held scores at the Armax Combat Arena and Castle Arcade on the Silversun Strip.
