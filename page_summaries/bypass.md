Bypass was the term for defeating imposed security restrictions on objects such as crates, terminals, minerals and doors, usually performed with an omni-tool, and it worked differently in each game of the original trilogy.

In Mass Effect, bypassing required a party member, either a squadmate or Shepard, with sufficiently leveled Decryption and/or Electronics, and triggered a minigame in paused time. On PC the minigame presented rotating concentric rings through which the player moved an arrow to the centre within a time limit; on Xbox and PlayStation it was a "Simon Says" sequence of three, five or seven face-button presses. Omni-gel could skip the minigame, but only if tech skill was high enough to at least attempt a manual hack. In the original release, failing the first attempt usually removed the manual override button, leaving omni-gel as the only alternative, a behavior removed in the Legendary Edition. Some tasks used alternative minigames, including a sequence for the mining laser during Liara T'Soni's recruitment and for the anomalous Citadel signal, and a Towers of Hanoi puzzle to restart Mira's VI core on Noveria.

In Mass Effect 2, Bypass and Hacking were no longer tied to abilities and any Shepard could perform them. Bypass used a circuit-board display with three to five pairs of hidden-icon nodes to match, connected by circuit lines; Hacking required selecting boxes matching a Target Code Segment from three scrolling columns, three times. Both ran in real time, so taking damage, or starting with damaged health or shields, caused failure. Purchasable Bypass and Hack Modules (on Illium and Omega) extended the time limits, successful attempts awarded credits that depleted the longer they took, and an exploit could sometimes repeat an object for extra credits. The Legendary Edition allowed retries on some puzzles and reduced the number of required matches.

In Mass Effect 3, the minigame was replaced with a glowing orange holographic loading animation, typically only on doors, during which the player kept full control of Shepard and could move around or take other actions, though moving too far away cancelled the bypass.
