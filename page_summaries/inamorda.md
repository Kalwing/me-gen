Inamorda was a quick-tempered krogan bounty hunter found on the Port Hanshan Mezzanine on Noveria, on the second floor of the hotel, who brushed off attempts at conversation. He was the intended recipient of an experimental weapon mod that the hanar Opold had smuggled onto the planet. When Shepard mentioned Opold's name, Inamorda became receptive and offered to buy the package directly rather than deal with the hanar; Shepard could haggle over the price.

If Shepard kept the item and told Opold so, Inamorda led a gang of krogan in an attack on Shepard's group upon their next return to the Normandy. If Shepard told Inamorda the item had been turned over to Administrator Anoleis, the krogan did not attack but alluded to an unpleasant conversation with Opold to come. In the fight, Inamorda used Immunity and Shield Boost on Insanity, and unusually his thugs used sniper rifles and pistols, employed Shield Boost and Adrenaline Burst, and did not attack in melee though they still charged. The docking bay gangways made flinging his group to their deaths with biotics an effective tactic.
