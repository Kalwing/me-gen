This page described the Alliance Infiltration Unit Infiltrator, a Mass Effect 3 multiplayer character kit. The unit was an unshackled AI modeled on the Cerberus synthetic Eva that Commander Shepard's team recovered on Mars. Although quantum blue-box AIs were considered illegal by the Council, the Systems Alliance secured a special allowance to deploy the infiltration unit during the Reaper war. The units favored shotguns, were designed for close-quarters combat, and could self-repair when downed, using the powers Tactical Cloak, Repair Matrix, and Snap Freeze. The remainder of the page consists of multiplayer gameplay notes and tactics rather than narrative lore.
