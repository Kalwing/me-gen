Citadel: Rita's Sister was a Mass Effect assignment begun at Flux, where Shepard overheard the volus manager Doran speaking with his waitress Rita about her sister. Rita worried that her sister Jenna, working undercover for C-Sec, was in over her head. Speaking to Jenna at the bar in Chora's Den only irritated her, and she refused to quit. On leaving, a turian bumped into Shepard and, feigning drunkenness, said to see him at C-Sec.

At the C-Sec offices near traffic control, the turian introduced himself as Detective Chellick and criticized Shepard for nearly blowing Jenna's cover. He explained Jenna was gathering information on illegal weapons mods. Shepard could Charm or Intimidate Chellick into releasing Jenna at once, or take on a task to close her case: meeting a krogan arms dealer named Jax in the Lower Markets near Morlan's shop. Shepard could arrest Jax, kill him, or buy the weapon mods peacefully. Fighting Jax gave 9 Renegade points and more experience; playing along gave 8 Paragon points, an upgrade, credits, omni-gel, and experience, with Chellick logging the transaction as evidence.

Any resolution removed Jenna from Chora's Den and returned her to Flux. Speaking to Rita ended the assignment; players were warned to collect Chellick's reward before talking to Rita to avoid a bug that ended the assignment prematurely with no reward. Urdnot Wrex blocked the C-Sec Offices entrance as part of Citadel: Wrex, so players wishing to delay his recruitment were advised to postpone this assignment. In Mass Effect 3, if this assignment and both Conrad Verner assignments were completed, Jenna saved Conrad's life during a mission by intercepting a Cerberus contact's bullet; otherwise Conrad died. The assignment was internally named "Help My Sister" and resembled a cut sub-plot, Citadel: Save My Sister, featuring sisters named Rita and Ari.
