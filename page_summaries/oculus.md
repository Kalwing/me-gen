The Oculus was a fast-moving cybernetic craft used by the Reapers for space and atmospheric superiority, first seen deployed by the Collectors to protect the Collector Base. Oculi used in Collector service were piloted by a single Collector drone stripped down to its nervous system, the remaining tissue hardened to withstand vacuum and integration into the Oculus shell. Each carried a powerful beam weapon similar to other Reaper and Collector weaponry, and could invade an enemy ship by jettisoning part of its armor and cutting into the hull. The Collectors likely acquired the technology from the Reapers.

In Mass Effect 2, the Normandy SR-2 was attacked by several Oculi left on guard in a debris field while approaching the Collector Base. Most were destroyed by the Normandy's weapons or by flying into debris, but one penetrated the hangar deck, lost its outer shell, and was destroyed by Commander Shepard on foot. Its particle beam could slice through a hull lacking Heavy Ship Armor and could kill Jack if the Normandy lacked the upgrade. In combat the Oculus had extremely strong armor serving as its health, took headshot damage on its red "iris" only when not firing, was immune to Stasis, could see through Tactical Cloak, and eventually switched targets from Shepard to squadmates.

In Mass Effect 3, Oculi were deployed en masse by the Reapers during the 2186 invasion as atmospheric and exoatmospheric fighters and interceptors, seen attacking Alliance fighters and Mantis Gunships over Earth and Palaven and during the battle to retake Earth, though none directly engaged Shepard. "Oculus" is Latin for "eye"; the design was based on a stereo speaker with HAL 9000 as the reference for the eye, and the center of a Destroyer's primary beam weapon is identical to an Oculus.
