The clone was a rogue Cerberus-made duplicate of Commander Shepard and the culprit behind Shepard's identity theft during the events of Mass Effect 3: Citadel. Shepard's team of three confronted the clone for the last time in the hangar bay of the Normandy SR-2, where it fought alongside Brooks and an army of CAT6 mercenaries. Across every outcome of the battle the clone plummeted from the Normandy to its death.

The clone's weapon, powers, and melee attacks mirrored whatever class the player had chosen, ranging from an N7 Valkyrie with Carnage for a Soldier clone to a Black Widow with Tactical Cloak for an Infiltrator clone and Biotic Charge with Nova for a Vanguard clone. It had very high armour and shields and carried five medi-gels, healing itself to full once its armour was moderately damaged. It was immune to Stasis, could not be grabbed, ragdolled, or knocked down, and resisted tech electrocution stuns. Every version except the Vanguard would grapple Shepard at close range, a contest that could be won by rapidly tapping melee, and each had a grenade with a lingering incendiary after-effect.

The fight occurred in two stages. Round 1 lasted until the clone was reduced to two medi-gels, at which point Cortez radioed "Moving in, Commander" and a cutscene led to Round 2, during which the clone grew less aggressive but its shield was 50% stronger and the Normandy rocked from side to side, hampering aim. The clone focused solely on Shepard, ignoring squadmates, drones, decoys, turrets, and enemies affected by Dominate, but was vulnerable to Energy Drain and Overload against its shields and to anti-armour powers such as Warp and Incinerate once its shields were down. In Legendary Edition its medi-gel count was no longer shown on the HUD.
