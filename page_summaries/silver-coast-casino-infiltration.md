"Silver Coast Casino: Infiltration" was a Mass Effect 3 Citadel DLC mission following Citadel: Identity Theft I. Arms dealer Elijah Khan was hosting a charity gala at his casino, and Shepard worked with Maya Brooks to bypass security and reach his panic room. The mission involved no combat: Brooks crawled through a ventilation shaft while Shepard, accompanied by any squadmate except EDI (barred as an AI/mech), mingled with guests and, on cue, distracted patrolling guards and cameras. Using resonance emitter lenses provided by Liara (which turned Shepard's eyes yellow), Shepard followed colored underground circuits, bypassed junction boxes, pressure pads, and camera boxes, and raised the security threat level if seen acting suspiciously, with critical mission failure if caught outright. Two ATMs could be hacked for 3,000 credits each.

After Brooks was briefly caught near the panic room and Shepard intercepted a guard to call it a false alarm, the team entered the safe room to find Khan already dead. Brooks accessed his terminal and found a deletion order; Shepard noticed the comm had not been wiped and activated it, bringing up a face distorted beyond recognition by signal degradation. The figure vowed to take everything Shepard was and owned before cutting the call, which Brooks could not trace. Shepard ordered the drives pulled for EDI to analyze. Rewards totaled roughly 30,000 credits including Alliance funding.
