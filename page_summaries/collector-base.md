The Collector Base was a massive space station on the accretion disk of a black hole in the galactic core, reachable only through the Omega 4 Relay and, Commander Shepard speculated, shielded by Reaper technology. It was surrounded by the Tartarus Debris Field, a graveyard of ships destroyed attempting the relay, patrolled by hidden Oculi, and kept at least one Collector Cruiser docked. It housed countless Collectors, Seeker Swarms, husks, Abominations, and Scions, along with the Collector General, who was directly controlled by the Reaper Harbinger. After infiltrating and escaping a Collector Cruiser, Shepard pinpointed the base near the Milky Way's core and obtained a Reaper IFF to cross the relay safely. During the suicide mission the Normandy SR-2 survived the Collectors' security systems, destroyed an intercepting cruiser, and crash-landed on the base. Shepard split the squad into teams to fight through Collector troops, husks, and Seeker Swarms, found the kidnapped Normandy crew stored in pods among thousands of humans, and learned the Collectors were breaking victims down into an organic paste piped to another part of the base. At the core Shepard found a Human-Reaper larva built from thousands of abducted humans and seemingly destroyed it by shattering its feeding tubes.

Shepard then opened a terminal to overload the base's reactor, but the Illusive Man asked instead for a radiation pulse that would kill the Collectors while leaving the technology intact for Cerberus to use against the Reapers. Depending on Shepard's response, Garrus, Legion, Zaeed, Grunt, and Mordin argued to preserve the base, while Tali, Kasumi, Thane, Jacob, and Jack objected to keeping it, and Samara and Miranda were neutral. The Human-Reaper climbed back out and was destroyed again before Shepard escaped as the countdown expired, with Harbinger vowing to continue its plans. If Shepard destroyed the base the star behind the Illusive Man appeared pure blue; if the base was preserved, deep red. In Mass Effect 3, Cerberus sent teams through the Omega-4 Relay to salvage Reaper technology from the base whether it had been destroyed or preserved, and incorporated the recovered Human-Reaper into Cronos Station, using it as the station's brain if the base was preserved or its heart if it was destroyed. After the Alliance captured Cronos Station, the Human-Reaper's remains became Crucible War Assets. In an unmodified, unimported Mass Effect 3 game the base had been destroyed by Shepard.
