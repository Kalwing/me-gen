"N7: Lost Operative" was an optional Mass Effect 2 assignment on the planet Lorek in the Fathar system of the Omega Nebula, triggered by a message from Cerberus Command (typically after recruiting Archangel) or by scanning the planet. Cerberus deep-cover operative Tyrone Rawlings had been captured by Eclipse mercenaries, who wanted a cipher he carried to decrypt data that could prove Cerberus involvement in rachni experiments. Interrogation reports by Cpl. Geero recorded that Eclipse lost many agents disabling the ship carrying Rawlings to extract him, that he resisted advanced and invasive interrogation, and that he died during questioning without giving up the cipher.

Shepard and squad assaulted the Eclipse base, fighting Eclipse Troopers, multiple Eclipse Vanguards, and the merc Morl, who deployed a Combat Drone. There were no thermal clip pickups beyond drops. After bypassing the holding cell and reaching the interrogation room, Shepard found Rawlings dead and accessed the Eclipse computer holding the encrypted data. EDI explained the data documented illicit Cerberus operations over the previous five years and could severely damage Cerberus's ability to operate openly; full decryption would take a year or more.

Shepard chose to upload the data to Cerberus Command, to Alliance Command, or to keep it on the Normandy. The choice affected neither credits nor morality, only the follow-up e-mail: Cerberus thanked Shepard for protecting its fragile reputation, Anderson called Alliance possession "a huge win," and EDI logged a Normandy copy for slow decryption. Rewards were roughly 7,500 credits and 2,000 platinum.
