This page is a short archive with minimal prose. It reproduces the Alliance News Network headline reports delivered via the Mass Effect 3 main menu during March 2012, set in-universe in 2186 during the Reaper invasion. Week Two headlines covered blackouts and bombardment on Earth, batarian refugees in the Exodus Cluster reporting devastated planets, comm buoys going dark across Sol, Arcturus, and Exodus with sightings of insectoid attackers and "deformed" batarians, and the start of war in turian space over Taetrus, with Primarch Fedorian vowing "Whatever civilization we face, it picked the wrong foe." Week Three headlines identified the enemy as ancient sapient starships called Reapers, reported a third front opening in asari space with an offline antimatter generator in the Loropi system, noted salarian fleets held back by the Narra bloodline's threat of rebellion while Dalatrass Linron refused martial law, and reported the turian defeat at the Taetrus relay with Reaper forces pushing through to Palaven.
