Arrival was the assignment in the Mass Effect 2 DLC of the same name, added after the Horizon mission. Admiral Hackett contacted Commander Shepard privately in the captain's cabin and asked, as a personal favor and alone, to infiltrate a batarian prison on the planet Aratoht in the Bahak system of the Viper Nebula and rescue Dr. Amanda Kenson, an Alliance deep-cover operative and scientist who claimed to have found proof of an imminent Reaper invasion via a Reaper artifact in the system. Kenson had been arrested on terrorism charges.

Shepard fought or sneaked past batarian prison guards and War Beasts, overheard that Kenson's people had planned to crash an asteroid into a mass relay, and freed Kenson from an interrogation chamber. Kenson temporarily joined as a squadmate, hacked an escape route, and the pair stole a shuttle. Aboard it, Kenson explained "the Project": a plan to destroy the local Alpha Relay by crashing an asteroid into it before the Reapers arrived and used it to spread across the galaxy, an act that would trigger a supernova-like explosion destroying the entire system and its colony of 300,000 batarians. She said the artifact, Object Rho, had given her Reaper visions like Shepard's from the Prothean beacons.

At Project Base, a countdown clock marked time until the Arrival. Exposure to Object Rho revealed that Kenson and her whole team were indoctrinated; Kenson drew a weapon, and Shepard was forced to fight through waves of Project personnel and a YMIR Mech before being knocked out and confined to a med lab. Shepard escaped, reached the Project control room, activated the engines to send the asteroid toward the relay, and pursued Kenson to the reactor core to stop her from causing an eezo meltdown. Cornered, Kenson detonated herself, knocking Shepard unconscious and advancing the clock. Shepard fought across the asteroid's surface to a comm relay, was addressed by Harbinger, who declared "Prepare yourselves for the Arrival," and was retrieved by Joker and the Normandy moments before the asteroid destroyed the Alpha Relay. The Bahak system and its 300,000 inhabitants were destroyed, and the Reaper invasion was delayed. Aboard the Normandy, Hackett debriefed Shepard in person, warned that the batarians would seek blood and that Earth would eventually call Shepard to answer for the deaths, but affirmed Shepard had done the right thing.

In Mass Effect 3, if a Mass Effect 2 save lacked this completed assignment, the Alpha Relay was still destroyed but blame shifted to the Alliance's 103rd Marine Division, which suffered casualties reducing its War Asset value, and dialogue changed with Balak, Ghorek, and Anderson.
