Salamul was a Krogan Battlemaster leading a squad of Blood Pack mercenaries on the planet Tarith in Mass Effect 2. He was the overseer of the Blood Pack mining operations there and was in communication with another Blood Pack base on Zada Ban, sending it resources gained from the mining. The Tarith operation was hit hard by hordes of Harvesters and Klixen, forcing the crew to evacuate the planet.

After arriving on Tarith to investigate an anomaly, Commander Shepard learned the nature of the Blood Pack operation by reading data pads scattered through the level. Shepard encountered Salamul as he attempted to evacuate, a firefight ensued, and Salamul was killed. He was armed with an M-300 Claymore Heavy Shotgun and could use Warp, and was protected by a strong biotic barrier, heavy armor, krogan regeneration, and krogan charge. In combat he moved aggressively toward the squad, rarely took cover, tried to flank, and closed to devastating shotgun range; Warp attacks were recommended to stop his regeneration and strip his barrier and armor.
