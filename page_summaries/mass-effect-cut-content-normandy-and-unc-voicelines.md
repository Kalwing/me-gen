This page collects cut and non-functional dialogue from the first Mass Effect set aboard the SSV Normandy or tied to UNC assignments, reconstructed from leftover files (some surviving only in Polish or Italian audio, or as translations). It is explicitly non-canon. Content includes a large set of ambient Joker cockpit monologues, the Normandy workbench crafting menu, dialogue with cut garage crewman Ian Ferguson, generic squad remarks for uncharted worlds, an animated cutscene for UNC: Lost Module in which a "space monkey" runs off with the data module, squad lines for UNC: Rogue VI and UNC: ExoGeni Facility, a cut Renegade option to refuse UNC: Dead Scientists, and the 2006 "X06 Demo" dialogue showcasing the conversation system.

Joker, ambient cockpit tour parody (reconstructed from translation): "As we're flying along in this... infinite vast expanse of nothingness, passengers on the left can look out their portholes and see... nothing for miles and miles and miles."

Joker: "The chef recommends dehydrated ice creams with fruits and something that was supposed to be a meat-- meat loaf. It didn't cook up well."

Ian Ferguson, in the cargo hold about Wrex: "That krogan makes me a wee bit nervous. He's not done anything, but the way he just glares at everyone... well. He's on my list of concerns."

Admiral Hackett, cut Renegade refusal of UNC: Dead Scientists — Shepard: "I've got higher priorities than retired scientists, Admiral. Find someone else." Hackett: "Understood, Commander. Sorry to trouble you. Fifth Fleet out."

Wrex, uncharted-world remark on a rock world: "I haven't seen this many rocks since I buried my mother."

Garrus, X06 Demo on Caleston/Therum: "The mission objective didn't say anything about civilians, Shepard." Ashley: "You're just saying that because they're human!"
