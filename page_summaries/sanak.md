Sanak was a batarian who worked as an enforcer for Aria T'Loak on Omega, frequently seen in her private booth at the Afterlife Club and often paired with Aria's daughter Liselle on field assignments. Openly hostile to humans, he was annoyed when Aria partnered him and Liselle with the human mercenary Paul Johnson—secretly the Cerberus defector Paul Grayson—and was told they did not need to like each other to work together. The three carried out a retaliatory strike against the rival turian gang the Talons, confiscating a shipment of red sand. Sanak protested Johnson's order to store the sand in his apartment but backed down when Liselle approved, and while moving it he noticed signs that Johnson was a recovering addict.

When Liselle was murdered and Johnson disappeared, Sanak was ordered to investigate and suspected Johnson had relapsed and killed her. His tech experts uncovered Johnson's true identity as Paul Grayson and his connection to Kahlee Sanders. After a massive crackdown on his operations, the Illusive Man manipulated Aria into helping him take revenge on Grayson, sending Kai Leng to negotiate. Sanak distrusted Leng and advised Aria against working with Cerberus, but was overruled. He led an assault on a Cerberus space station under turian occupation, killing the turians and capturing Sanders and David Anderson as bait for Grayson. Ordered to guard Anderson while accompanying the strike squad sent to kill Grayson, Sanak was killed when Anderson, freed after the heavily modified Grayson butchered Aria's men, fired three pistol shots into his torso. The events are drawn from Mass Effect: Retribution.
