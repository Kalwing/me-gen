Eldfell-Ashland Energy (EAE) was a human mining corporation with a significant galactic presence. In 2137 the Ashland Energy Corporation extracted helium-3 from Saturn's atmosphere, beginning a new era of fusion research, and merged with Eldfell Construction to vertically integrate: Ashland recovered raw materials while Eldfell built power plants, refineries and pipelines. EAE expanded by absorbing companies to create a "cradle-to-grave corporate experience," with subsidiaries such as Second Star Living recruiting colonists and the education group Excelsior training workers. It could keep 90% of a colony's services in-house, though its primary revenue came from mining hydrogen, helium-3 and eezo and trading their futures.

In 2170 an Eldfell-Ashland element zero freighter exploded over the human colony of Yandoa, the culmination of several eezo-related industrial accidents over four months; the dust-form eezo cloud caused birth defects and biotic births among survivors. It was later revealed that Paul Grayson, under Cerberus orders, caused the Yandoa crash.

In 2185 EAE hired Zaeed Massani to liberate its refinery on Zorya from the Blue Suns, with the facility either heavily damaged and workers freed or completely destroyed with all workers killed, depending on outcome. The same year, Eclipse mercenaries raided EAE's Helyme mining facility seeking the destination of the freighter Ter Alan; Commander Shepard liberated the facility but most workers were killed. In November 2185 EAE clashed with the quarian Migrant Fleet over mining rights to palladium-rich asteroids in the Nubian Expanse; after the quarians protested, EAE's local mining fleet, including the cruiser MSV Stanislaw, was destroyed in a reported "alien attack," and EAE blamed the quarians and planned to petition the Council to ban the Migrant Fleet from Citadel space. In 2186 the Reaper War destroyed numerous EAE helium-3 facilities, causing severe financial distress. Its operations included Caleston, Gei Hinnom, Helyme, Jonus, Naxell and Zorya.
