The Sirta Foundation was a human biomedical non-profit organization renowned for humanitarian work and genetics research. It made its fortune eliminating several genetic diseases endemic to human populations and created medi-gel, which, though technically illegal under Council law, was too useful to ban. Another product was the Sirta Home Series AutoMedBed, an expensive emergency unit with a sophisticated medical VI. Sirta refused on principle to produce weapons, but offered protective and utility gear: armor, omni-tools, and bio-amps. Its Phoenix full-body armor incorporated a medical VI that regenerated the wearer's health at the cost of lower stats; in Mass Effect 2 it produced N7 armor pieces such as Strength Boost Pads, Stimulator Conduits, and Life Support Webbing, plus the Chameleon omni-tool line and the Unity biotic amp. An armory license could be bought from Delan on the Presidium, Petozi on Noveria, or Commander Rentola aboard the SSV Normandy after his rescue from Virmire, for 100 credits. Sirta operated shops on Zakera Ward and inside Huerta Memorial Hospital.

In 2183, biotic fanatics attacked a Sirta facility on Chohe and took its staff hostage; Commander Shepard could resolve the situation on assignment from Admiral Hackett, with outcomes depending on how many scientists survived. By 2185, heavy casualties had brought Sirta near bankruptcy until asari colonists on Lusia sold it platinum from a moon of Pronoia below market value, credited with saving the company; if most scientists survived, Sirta instead prospered and cured an inter-species flu virus. In 2186, security fields installed at the Citadel amid the refugee influx used advanced biometric authentication systems developed by the Sirta Foundation. The manufacturer was internally named "Sitta" in early development before localization renamed it "Sirta."
