In single-player Mass Effect 3, Commander Shepard's armor could be customized by swapping pieces of the default N7 Armor for parts purchased from merchants, found on missions, or supplied through downloadable content, using the armor locker aboard the Normandy SR-2. Each piece altered both appearance and combat effectiveness, and the player could further adjust color, reflectiveness, pattern, two separate tints, and the color of any lights. Full armor sets could also be equipped without piece swapping, providing unique bonus combinations. Shepard could additionally select casual outfits worn aboard the Normandy and on the Citadel.

Customizable slots were head, chest, shoulders, arms, and legs. Head options included the N7 Helmet and Breather Helmet, Death Mask, Kuwashii Visor, Sentry Interface, Kestrel Helmet, Capacitor Helmet, Archon Visor, Umbra Visor, Recon Hood, Mnemonic Visor, Securitel Helmet, and Delumcore Overlay, with effects such as bonus health, shields, weapon or power damage, headshot damage, melee damage, ammo capacity, shield regeneration, or power recharge speed. Chest, shoulder, arm, and leg pieces were branded to manufacturers including Kassa Fabrication, Serrice Council, Rosenkov Materials, Armax Arsenal, Ariake Technologies, and Hahne-Kedar, each granting a stat bonus. Many pieces were acquired during specific missions such as Priority: Mars, Priority: Tuchanka, Priority: Sur'Kesh, Grissom Academy: Emergency Evacuation, Priority: Cerberus Headquarters, and Arrae: Ex-Cerberus Scientists, or purchased from vendors such as Cipritine Armory, Aegohr Munitions, Nos Astra Sporting Goods, Batarian State Arms, Kanala Exports, and Spectre Requisitions after certain story milestones.
