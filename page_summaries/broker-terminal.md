The Broker Terminal was a message terminal in Liara T'Soni's cabin aboard the Normandy SR-2 in Mass Effect 3. It held correspondence and intercepted communications collected through Liara's work as the Shadow Broker, covering events across the galaxy during the Reaper War. The page reproduces its contents in full.

Many entries concerned the Crucible project: Kahlee Sanders wrote to David Anderson about the breathtaking device she was helping build; Dr. Lok reported to Admiral Hackett on nervous staff reactions to rachni working on the Crucible; Mordin Solus messaged an STG colleague about the Crucible's scale and Alliance engineers slavishly following the Mars Archive plans; Dr. Cole and Dr. T'Kisha described engineering and material shortages solved by anonymous Shadow Broker deliveries of platinum and a comm server retrieved by Kasumi Goto. Kahlee Sanders also called Jack about securing an L3-X1 biotic amp for the Grissom Academy students.

Other logs included Grunt addressing new Aralahk Company recruits, Sergeant Cavanagh apologizing for firing on allied geth primes, a purchase list for Zaeed Massani, Jacob Taylor's love letter to Dr. Brynn Cole, three unsent letters from Morinth (signed Mirala) to her sisters Rila and Falere reporting Samara's death, and Alliance correspondence to Miranda Lawson thanking her for intelligence on Cerberus. Councilor Gujir was recorded authorizing seizure of Senate eezo stockpiles to evacuate a colony. A smuggler manifest in the Yungiss system led to batarian slaves being granted asylum at the Broker's request. Feron reported to the Broker on scouting the Terminus Systems with Tazzik. An extensive set of Liara's "Prothean Notes" (2171-2186) traced her academic career, her realization that the Collectors were husked Protheans, and her shifting, disillusioned view of the Protheans after meeting Javik, ending with a list of possible book titles for a work she hoped to co-write with him.
