Space stations are artificial structures built and maintained to support space travel, defence, or resource gathering. Humans built a large number of them as military installations during their expansion through the Milky Way, valuing their proximity to mass relays. Stations may be constructed from scratch or built onto existing bodies such as asteroids, and normally hold a fixed position within a system or orbit a planet. Large structures like the Citadel, which does not orbit a fixed point, use navigation systems and engines to maintain position. Most stations include drive discharge points so visiting ships can safely dump their core charge. Because of their expense, stations are frequently repurposed.

The page groups stations into broad categories. Trade/Gateway stations sit near relay nexuses and become commerce and communications hubs with docking facilities; some, such as those around Pluto, develop sizable populations, and orbital ports like the planned Asteroid X57 provide transfer points for goods and travellers. Defence stations in orbit around colonies give early warning of attack and allow control of the ground situation; Noveria's orbital battle stations can target and destroy approaching vessels and strike surface targets with antimatter warheads. Research stations offer platforms for long-term or low-gravity experiments, astronomical observation, and work too dangerous or classified for a colony, such as AI development or BAaT's biotic training, with the option of destroying the station to contain a catastrophic breach.

The article lists many known Milky Way stations, including Arcturus Station, Avernus Station, the Barn, the Citadel, Cronos Station, Gagarin Station (also called "Jump Zero"), the Jon Grissom Academy, the Lazarus Research Station, Minuteman Station, Omega, Pinnacle Station, and others, plus Khi Tasira and the Nexus in Andromeda.
