Nos Astra was the capital city of the asari colony world Illium, in the Crescent Nebula. It included a port, a bar, and a commercial area with several shops. Though outwardly a gleaming, beautiful city, characters remarked it was not as safe as it looked and could be as dangerous as Omega, and Illium was compared to Noveria for its permissive trade policies.

In Mass Effect: Evolution, shortly after the First Contact War, Jack Harper and Eva Coré arrived at Nos Astra on the trail of Desolas Arterius, a turian general in possession of the Arca Monolith. Jack, briefly exposed to the Monolith, could sense its presence on Illium from across the galaxy. He and Eva took a skycar to a building where Jack believed the artifact was located, but it was a trap: Desolas wanted to test the Monolith's link with those it affected, and Jack's ability to trace it confirmed his theories. The two were subdued by Desolas's forces and taken from Nos Astra to Palaven.

In Mass Effect 2, Commander Shepard traveled to Nos Astra in 2185 to recruit the assassin Thane Krios and the asari justicar Samara. Shepard could also encounter former teammate Liara T'Soni and choose to help her hunt the Shadow Broker, reunite with the fan Conrad Verner at the bar Eternity, and meet Gianna Parasini depending on events on Noveria in 2183. The city ran regular shuttle schedules; local destinations included the Nos Astra Commercial Spaceport, Dantius Towers, Manjoros Plaza, and Saefos Valley, and offworld routes served Thessia, the Citadel, Palaven, Irune, Kahje, and Amaterasu. Flights to Elysium, Mindoir, and Shanxi were delayed due to Collector attacks on human colonies. Missions and assignments based there included Miranda: The Prodigal, Dossier: The Assassin, Dossier: The Justicar, and Lair of the Shadow Broker. Notable figures present included Nassana Dantius, Shiala, Seryna, Detective Anaya, Pitne For, Niftu Cal, Captain Wasea, and Oriana.
