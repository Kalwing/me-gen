Medical Bay was a mission in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, following Hangar Bay and preceding Security Checkpoint. Randall Ezno entered the Medical Wing of the Cerberus station known as the Barn to reach Inali Renata's lab. Finding her office empty, he was told by the facility's Director that Inali had "volunteered" for a project beyond his clearance. An unnamed volus then contacted Randall, warning that Inali was in danger and offering assistance by hacking the station AI, UDI, to clear a path.

Advancing through the Med Bay, Randall fought waves of Orcus Mechs that malfunctioned and attacked Cerberus staff, then discovered Inali being vivisected by the Director, who was implanting new technology in her body behind a sealed window. Unable to intervene, Randall swore to "kill them all." The Director fled and branded Randall a hostile, ordering every Cerberus employee and trooper to shoot him on sight. Randall fought through mechs, Troopers, Engineers and a Centurion, killing former colleagues including Biggs and Wedge, and encountered moral choices to spare or kill Cerberus personnel and a hostage. He fought to an old datacore where the volus partner was held, but UDI retook control and locked down the room, forcing a major battle before Randall pressed on toward the Security Checkpoint and the Comm Relay.
