Citadel: Doctor Michel was a Mass Effect assignment available after Citadel: Expose Saren, when Commander Shepard returned to the Med Clinic on the Citadel and overheard Dr. Chloe Michel being blackmailed. Someone had learned of her dismissal from a previous employer and the circumstances of it, and threatened to go public unless she handed over medical supplies, which would cost her her license. The supplies were to be delivered to Morlan, a salarian shop owner in the Lower Markets. Shepard offered to deliver them and uncover the blackmailer. If Shepard had earlier saved Michel from Fist's thugs, she first thanked the Commander for the rescue.

At Morlan's shop, after a brief conversation, a krogan working for a man named Banes stepped in, saying "I told Banes you'd screw this up," and confronted Shepard. Shepard could defuse the encounter with a Charm or Intimidate check for extra morality points, or resolve it in a fight that yielded additional experience and either 120 credits or a random weapon drop. Michel's reward of 268 XP and 120 credits was the same regardless of method, along with a discount at her clinic and better prices on anything sold to her. Shepard could then ask her about Armistan Banes, a figure central to the assignment UNC: Missing Marines. If the mission was completed successfully, in Mass Effect 2 Michel emailed Shepard expressing relief that the Commander was alive and asking whether Shepard and Garrus could visit.
