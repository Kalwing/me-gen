This archive collected Cerberus Daily News reports from November 2185. The war on Garvug reached its conclusion through what corporatists called the "Wooden Horse Gambit": guerrillas under Hailot Wrund briefly retook the capital Dhazil before a massive surprise fleet, including Binary Helix ships thought to have left the coalition, emerged from behind a moon and levelled the city with mass cannons. Corporatist stocks soared, with Sonax up nearly 1,000 percent on the "win by losing" strategy. Hailot Wrund was killed after his skycar was shot down. The coalition then explained the ruse: the Binary Helix rift had been staged, special forces disguised as civilians had located insurgent leaders, and the board had voted 8-3 to prolong the war, per CFO Abayomi Osoba. In a two-part interview with the Future Content Corporation's Emily Wong, Sonax supreme commander Lira Speight admitted the deception, said the video of executed krogan had been staged, and expressed regret only over the death of General Noah Vardos. Another running story was the Nubian Expanse mining dispute: Eldfell-Ashland Energy stonewalled the Quarian Conclave over palladium asteroid rights, then accused the Migrant Fleet of destroying a cruiser, three frigates, and six freighters; the quarians denied it, the Citadel opened an investigation, and sole survivor Camilla Gutierrez of the MSV Stanislaw said a single, enormous starship unlike anything in the Flotilla had attacked her vessel. Other reports covered the Omega gang war winding down after a leaders' meeting; the Mannovai artifacts debate, with University of Serrice professor Baliya Haphia defending relics she had rescued from a batarian bombardment against MP Shayar Edcronus's repatriation campaign; the nanotech diet aid "The Skale" by Black Horse Foods, linked to dozens of deaths after hackers disabled its safeguards and then recalled; a flooding sabotage at Binary Helix's Nautilus Facility on Europa; a new interspecies-conflict wing at the Tianar Museum of Military History under curator Hunner Genchak; drell actor Fell Fannus receiving a lifetime achievement award; the watchdog group BALANCE under Dr. Trent Leeds challenging a Genex Chemical colony contract; and asteroid miner Pashtol Nima found alive after two years marooned on a deserted station.
