Batarian Rocket Soldiers were batarian fighters armed with an assault rifle capable of switching fire mode to launch rockets, making them more effective against vehicles. They were deployed inside and outside the fusion torch stations around the asteroid X57 during the mission to stop batarians from crashing it into the human colony of Terra Nova. In combat they stayed at a distance, fired rockets at vehicles and infantry, remained in cover, and used melee attacks when engaged closely.
