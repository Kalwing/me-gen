The Cabal Vanguard was a turian Mass Effect 3 multiplayer character drawn from the turian biotic units called Cabals, which were isolated from regular infantry and whose dictum was "the intangible is unstoppable." Because turian biotics were relatively rare, Cabals were small, tight-knit teams trained for the deadliest infiltration missions, both biotically and technically proficient, and equipped with venom-infused gauntlets fatal to any enemy who came within range. The character had a barrier of 750 and health of 500, the Turian Veteran class, and the powers Poison Strike, Nightshade Blades, Biotic Focus and Venom Gauntlets.

She was the only Vanguard without Biotic Charge; her replacement, Poison Strike, launched her forward a set distance, passing through walls and objects while damaging enemies she passed through and granting brief invulnerability without restoring her barrier, and it was her only means of detonating power combos, able to trigger all four types. Her heavy melee teleported her to a target for a venom-tipped blade stab that decapitated on a fatal hit and could also be used untargeted to teleport a short distance; her regular light melee was an infinite series of gauntlet slashes, a trait shared with the N7 Fury and the Vorcha. Her dodge teleported a short distance at the expense of barrier strength. All her offensive powers and melee attacks inflicted poison damage over time, which affected synthetics, restricted enemy regeneration and prevented cloaking. Her ability to pass through walls and reach higher or lower elevations, combined with the movement-speed boost from Biotic Focus, made her a highly mobile hit-and-run and flanking character and an effective medic, especially with the Double Dash evolution, though she was fragile between attacks and struggled against enemies such as Brutes, Banshees, Scions and Atlases. Her voice lines matched the asari multiplayer characters, minus asari-specific lines and the turian voice flange.
