Garrus Vakarian was a turian, former C-Sec Investigation Division officer, and a recurring squadmate across the Mass Effect trilogy. Trained militarily at fifteen, he followed his by-the-book C-Sec investigator father into law enforcement. In 2183 he was assigned to investigate Saren Arterius after Ambassador Udina alleged Saren had gone rogue, but Saren's Spectre status kept his activities classified. When Executor Pallin closed the investigation for lack of evidence, Garrus defied the order to pursue a lead. Commander Shepard could find him at Dr. Chloe Michel's Med Clinic in the Wards, where he took out a thug with a headshot; he then left C-Sec to join the Normandy and hunt Saren, having no qualms about pursuing a fellow turian. His personal assignment involved tracking down Dr. Saleon, a salarian doctor who had escaped him. Garrus objected to C-Sec's regulations and even suggested killing Saren rather than risking his exoneration. Depending on Shepard's guidance, he considered reapplying for C-Sec or for Spectre candidacy (his father had once blocked his Spectre bid).

After the Battle of the Citadel and Shepard's death, disillusioned with the Council's dismissal of the Reaper threat, Garrus went to Omega, took the name "Archangel," and formed a twelve-member vigilante squad that waged war on the Blue Suns, Blood Pack, and Eclipse. His lieutenant Sidonis was coerced into betraying him, luring Garrus away while the gangs bombed the hideout and killed his team. In 2185 the three merc groups allied to corner Archangel on a bridge in Kima District, where Shepard, recruiting him for the Collector mission, revealed Archangel to be Garrus. A Blue Suns gunship badly wounded the right side of his face, requiring grafts and cybernetics. His loyalty mission, "Eye for an Eye," let Shepard help him hunt Sidonis (interrogating "Fade," revealed as Harkin) and choose whether to allow or stop the killing. Garrus became a romance option for a female Shepard.

In 2186 Garrus, if he survived the Collector base, returned to fight the Reapers. He had convinced his father and then Primarch Fedorian to form a turian Reaper task force. Shepard found him on Menae, Palaven's moon, as a military consultant; he helped locate Primarch Adrien Victus and rejoined the Normandy, manning the main battery. Victus later put him in charge of coordinating all turian fleet activity, a burden that made him doubt his fitness as a leader and grapple with the "ruthless calculus of war." He coordinated turian dry-dock ships for the Crucible and, during the final battle, could be wounded on the run to the conduit. Recurring lines: "This mission just got a lot more complicated"; on the Presidium shooting cans with Shepard, "I'm Garrus Vakarian, and this is now my favorite spot on the Citadel"; and, if romanced, his final order: "come back alive. It'd be an awfully empty galaxy without you." If neither Garrus nor Tali romanced Shepard, the two could become a couple. Mac Walters wrote him in the first game; he was made romanceable after fan feedback.
