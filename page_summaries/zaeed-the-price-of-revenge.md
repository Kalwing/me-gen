Zaeed - The Price of Revenge was a downloadable content pack for Mass Effect 2, released on January 28, 2010. It had been intended to launch alongside Mass Effect 2 on January 26, 2010, but was delayed. Like most original-trilogy DLC, it was later folded into Mass Effect Legendary Edition. Access to the pack required download and/or purchase of the Cerberus Network.

The DLC added the mercenary Zaeed Massani as a recruitable squad member. Its backstory established that Zaeed served in the Alliance military before becoming one of the deadliest guns-for-hire in the Terminus Systems, waging war for profit for 20 years, and now wished to retire from mercenary life. The pack contained two missions, Dossier: The Veteran and the loyalty mission Zaeed: The Price of Revenge, the latter set on the planet Zorya. It also added one new location, the Ismar Frontier cluster, and one new weapon, the M-451 Firestorm incendiary sprayer.
