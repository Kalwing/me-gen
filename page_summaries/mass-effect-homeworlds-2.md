Mass Effect: Homeworlds 2, tagged "The Galaxy Betrayed!," was published May 30, 2012, written by Patrick Weekes and Jeremy Barlow with art by Chris Staggs. It focuses on Tali'Zorah nar Rayya, told mostly in flashback as an injured Tali collapses in a Citadel clinic in 2183 and recounts how her Pilgrimage went wrong.

Tali had traveled aboard the quarian ship Honorata toward Illium seeking something to bring home when Keenah'Breizh reported a geth presence on an unidentified ice planet in the Crescent Nebula. They landed, and Tali disabled a geth and extracted its memory core. Activating it, she heard a recorded conversation between Saren Arterius and Matriarch Benezia discussing the attack on Eden Prime and the imminent return of the Reapers. Realizing its importance, she resolved to bring it to authorities on Illium. Commander Jacobus, a turian mercenary hired by Saren to excavate Prothean artifacts on the ice planet, learned of the intrusion and vowed to hunt the quarians down or be killed by Saren.

Tali and Keenah fled to the Honorata and traveled to Illium, but port officials denied them docking for days out of anti-quarian prejudice, giving Jacobus time to land and prepare an ambush. When the Honorata finally docked, its crew was attacked and killed; only Tali and Keenah escaped, stowing away on a transport to the Citadel. Discovered, they were handed to C-Sec rather than killed because "as quarians they aren't worth the bullets." The officer Chellick ignored their claim of vital information and ordered them off the station. An Avina terminal quoted a seven-month wait for a Council audience. Jacobus then attacked them, chasing them into a maintenance area. Tali was badly wounded and Keenah mortally so; reaching an incinerator, Tali sealed the doors and activated it, destroying Jacobus, Keenah's body, and a computer playing her father's farewell message. At the clinic, a volus information broker offered to route her data to the Shadow Broker and directed her to a bar to hide.
