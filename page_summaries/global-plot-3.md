Global Plot 3 was a lengthy multi-location assignment cut from Mass Effect during development and considered non-canon. Its theme was a hidden war between the hanar and the reclusive lystheni, a group of salarians isolated from the galaxy for centuries, and the threat of bioweapons. On the Citadel, an asari diplomat named Elarya would tell Shepard that a prominent asari scientist, Dr. Euthalia, had gone missing traveling in hanar space in the Attican Traverse, and that the unusually silent hanar prompted the asari to send a Spectre. Elarya provided Euthalia's last known coordinates.

In hanar space, Shepard would find a recorded transmission showing Euthalia's ship contacting a hanar colony as it came under attack, proving hanar concealment, and would then be summoned to meet a hanar senator. The senator revealed the hanar were at war with the lystheni over asteroid claims, and that the lystheni had kidnapped Euthalia to force her to build a bioweapon against the hanar; he gave coordinates for an asteroid facility. Landing there, Shepard's squad found it eerily quiet before being attacked by mutated beings and ambushed by lystheni speaking an untranslatable language. An asari intervened to stop the squad shooting a wounded lystheni, identifying herself as Dr. Euthalia and describing a viral outbreak that turned lystheni into the mutated creatures. As the group advanced she grew increasingly sympathetic to the lystheni, describing hanar oppression. They then met the real Dr. Euthalia; the first asari was her assistant Nuriam, who was appalled to learn the real Euthalia had deliberately caused the infection using Nuriam as a carrier. Enraged, Nuriam fled alone while Euthalia joined Shepard.

Shepard then encountered lystheni transmitting data hazardous to the hanar, which could only be stopped by destroying the facility's transmitters, meaning blowing up the whole facility. Shepard had to choose between stopping the data immediately or delaying the destruction to rescue Nuriam, over Euthalia's protests. If pursued, Nuriam was found badly wounded and under attack by infected lystheni; Euthalia refused to heal her, and she would die unless Shepard used Charm or Intimidate, asking that her body be used to find a cure. In the epilogue the hanar senator met Shepard and Euthalia, confirmed Nuriam's recovery if she survived, and Shepard could threaten to involve the Council if the hanar continued their aggression. Reporting back to Elarya earned credits plus a bonus for starting the peace process. Cut plot database entries reference unique Joker dialogue, a conversation with Sha'ira, a vaccine subplot, three endings ("Sided with hanar," "Sided with lystheni," "Peace"), and otherwise unattested characters Arandur, Suranos/Suranon, Tumeeron, and Zephyra.
