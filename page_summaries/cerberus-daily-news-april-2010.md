This archive collected Cerberus Daily News reports from April 2185. The war on Garvug dominated coverage: after paying the local ruzad a "review fee," GBC News correspondent Robin Lasky was released from Tuchanka; corporate forces under Sonax Industries supreme commander Lira Speight stormed the Clan Hailot ancestral fortress, but clan chieftain Hailot Wrund escaped to the countryside and waged a guerrilla campaign, taunting his pursuers over radio and video. Corporatists captured the Garvug System's comm buoys, and a decapitation strike on a convoy was believed to have killed Hailot Wrund until he released a video claiming the analyzed DNA "was some other hump." The Systems Alliance ruled 5-4 in Ford v. Huerta to uphold President Huerta's term, triggering mass riots across the United North American States that Speaker Lisa Ford both disowned and praised. A Cerberus cell on Bekenstein was found to communicate through steganographic messages hidden in everyday extranet broadcasts, as explained by Alliance counter-terror expert Meng Zhao. Four human C-SEC officers were charged with excessive force after beating three salarians, one of whom, Haruta Sio, died. The month closed with the Vallum Blast: the Taetrian colonial capital Vallum was obliterated by a privately owned ship accelerated to near-FTL speed and aimed at the Radiatum parliament building, leaving a five-kilometer crater, killing the planet's Primarch and possibly over a million people, and drawing responsibility claims from fourteen separatist groups. General Timus Aurelos toured the wreckage. Lighter items covered the core-fusion explosion of planet DC1938 with a single infant survivor, a Citadel Council war-game exercise that angered Terminus Systems denizens, the box-office hit "The Demon and the Nightmare," the Chao Hsu and Valerie LeBarre wedding on Luna, student suspensions at Thessia's Erana University over a human-history costume party, a mixed martial arts upset by Zarren Casias over Luis Reyes, and the wrap of principal photography on Morgan Bierster's "Nekyia Corridor."
