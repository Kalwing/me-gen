Lair of the Shadow Broker was a Mass Effect 2 mission in which Commander Shepard helped Liara T'Soni track down the Shadow Broker. It became available after the DLC install and the Horizon mission, triggered by a Cerberus message on the Galaxy Map titled "Give Liara Intel," offering intel on the Broker's base because of his past work for the Collectors. The mission ran without a break from Illium to its end and featured mostly shielded enemies.

On Illium, Shepard delivered the intel to Liara in her office; the data also revealed that Feron, Liara's drell partner who sacrificed himself two years earlier, was still alive. Arriving at Liara's apartment, Shepard met the asari Spectre Tela Vasir investigating an assassination attempt on Liara. Clues led to Sekat, an information broker at the Dracon Trade Center, which exploded as the squad arrived. Fighting through the Shadow Broker's private army with Vasir, Shepard found Liara alive; Liara exposed Vasir as a long-time Shadow Broker asset who had killed Sekat and taken his data. Vasir fled, leading to a firefight, a skycar chase through Nos Astra, and a rooftop battle at the Azure hotel that ended with Shepard killing Vasir, who justified her betrayal as the price of intel that saved lives and accused Shepard of hypocrisy for working with Cerberus.

The trail led to the Broker's ship orbiting in the perpetual storm band of Hagalaz. Shepard, with Liara as a mandatory squadmate, fought across the ship's exterior amid lightning capacitors and maintenance drones, defended a door during a long five-wave siege, and pushed inside to free Feron. Confronting the Shadow Broker revealed him to be a yahg, taken from his homeworld and having killed and replaced the original Broker decades earlier. After a three-stage boss fight, Liara dropped an electrified fluid tank to overload his barrier and kill him. Liara assumed the Broker's identity and network, becoming the new Shadow Broker, and granted Shepard permanent access to the base. If Shepard had romanced Liara in Mass Effect, she later returned Shepard's dog tags recovered from the Normandy wreck. Rewards included 90,000 credits, element zero and palladium, and Heavy Pistol Damage and Damage Protection upgrades. Cut content included early plans to rescue Liara from the Broker or to make Shepard the Shadow Broker.
