Niket was a childhood friend of Miranda Lawson's, the only friend she had while living with her father and the only person she stayed in contact with after fleeing. He also understood what her father was really like. During Miranda's loyalty mission he was placed in charge of moving her twin sister Oriana and Oriana's adoptive family to safety after word came that their father had discovered their location.

Niket had grown frustrated with Miranda after learning she had joined Cerberus and taken her sister away without telling him. He betrayed her, believing she had kidnapped Oriana for revenge and arguing that Oriana would be better off wealthy than raised in poverty as he had been. The night Miranda told him she had stolen her sister, he called her father, who bought him out on the condition that Niket set the terms of the transfer so Oriana could be returned without traumatizing her adoptive family. Miranda refused to believe he had turned on her until the last minute, reasoning that her father would have tried to buy him off years earlier when she first ran. Because Niket had kept all other information about Oriana from her father, Miranda would shoot him to tie up loose ends unless Shepard intervened, in which case Captain Enyala killed him instead. Cut content indicates his life could have been spared through a Charm or Intimidate persuasion.
