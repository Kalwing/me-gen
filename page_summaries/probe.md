Probes were used in Mass Effect 2 to discover resources or landing sites on uncharted planets, launched as part of the planet-scanning minigame. Scanning revealed mineral concentrations and anomalies, and a probe was fired to collect them; the planet could be rotated by scanning near its edge. The Normandy SR-2 carried a finite number of probes, 30 initially and up to 60 after recruiting Thane and researching the modular probe bay. Additional probes were bought at fuel depots for 100 credits per 5 (roughly 20 credits each), though purchases still cost the full 100 even when fewer than 5 below maximum capacity.

As a running joke, when surveying Uranus, EDI would say "Really, commander?" for the first probe and "Probing Uranus" for the second, before reverting to her standard lines.
