Survivalist was a passive biotic skill in Mass Effect: Andromeda, described in-universe as a combination of Peebee's training and experience helping her survive dangerous situations. It was the starting biotic skill of the squad member Pelessaria B'Sayle (Peebee) and was not available in multiplayer.

Its six ranks progressively raised maximum shields and health, then improved shield and health regeneration and reduced regeneration delay. Rank 4 offered a choice between Resilience (larger bonuses to maximum shields and health, with biotic evades) and Reflexes (bonuses to regeneration rate and delay reduction). Rank 5 offered Barrier Detonation, causing her barrier to explode and damage nearby enemies when her shields dropped, or Barrier Reinforcement, restoring shields to her and allies when she killed an enemy with a power. Rank 6, locked until her loyalty mission Peebee: A Mysterious Remnant Signal was completed, replaced her evade with a biotic teleport and offered either Advanced Reflexes (large damage bonuses at low health) or Advanced Resilience (further maximum health and shield bonuses). Specced for defence, Peebee could become one of the squad's most durable members, second only to Drack in staying power, functioning as a reliable support character despite modest weapon and power damage.
