The League of One was a salarian intelligence service active around the time the salarians discovered the Citadel. Trained primarily in espionage and assassination, the team was never more than a dozen strong and was expert at infiltrating the tightest defenses and eliminating all obstacles. Only a few top members of government and military knew its members' identities; League members wore no distinguishing garments and held no rank, their only mark of membership a small medallion presented upon induction.

This secrecy lasted until the formation of the Citadel Council. To dispel rumors and reassure their new asari partners, the Salarian Union released all classified documents on the League, suddenly exposing its members to the salarians' enemies. Before harm could come to them the team mysteriously disappeared, widely assumed to be a cover story, but a few months later the salarian inner cabinet was murdered with no incriminating evidence, though responsibility was clear. The Special Tasks Group dispatched a team of hunters, who did not return; the STG then sent ten of its best military operators with broad discretionary powers, resembling Spectres, and only two returned, reporting no evidence of the League. No further incidents occurred and the League was assumed wiped out, though recently declassified documents suggest a possible thirteenth member who eluded the salarian military.

League of One medallions have since been found in escape pods, on an abandoned fighter, on a collapsing space station, hidden within probes, possibly buried on Nepmos, and on a mummified salarian corpse on Klensal. A cut Mass Effect 2 concept by Mac Walters proposed a salarian assassin squadmate descended from the League and obsessed with avenging its eradication.
