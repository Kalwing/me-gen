Credits were the standard galactic currency used to buy weapons, armor, armory licenses, and upgrades. The credit was established as the standard galactic trade currency by the Citadel's Unified Banking Act, legislation enacted by the Citadel Council and drafted by the volus for their financial expertise. The Act set regulatory guidelines for the credit's value and its exchange rate against member races' currencies. The credit used a managed floating exchange rate, recalculated in real time by the central bank to maintain the average value of all participating currencies, so some regional currencies were worth more and some less. When the Systems Alliance joined the Citadel, its national treasuries were linked into the credit network, and all participating economies had to price items in both local currency and credits. Because hard currency could be stolen or counterfeited, electronic fund transfers were the norm; physical transactions were hard to trace, making them useful for tax evasion or illegal purchases.

In Mass Effect, Commander Shepard was capped at 9,999,999 credits, after which mission rewards stopped and sold items were effectively given away; converting unwanted items to omni-gel was advised. Mass Effect 2 shared the same cap but made credits sparse, with a normal full playthrough plus DLC yielding roughly 1,280,251 credits; Legendary Edition changed this by requiring purchase of formerly DLC-exclusive equipment. In Mass Effect 3, credits worked similarly, with about 1,295,005 obtainable in a single playthrough with all DLC, and the Citadel DLC allowed effectively unlimited credits via Castle Arcade, the Silver Coast Casino, and Armax Arsenal Arena tokens. In Mass Effect: Infiltrator, credits were a Cerberus-branded currency earned by Randall Ezno through completing checkpoints, scanning vaults and computers, or trading Intel Points, spendable only in the in-game Store (with real-money credit packs available). In Mass Effect: Andromeda, credits were a universal Heleus Cluster currency (used even by the angara), found in combat, exploration, and missions, and spent on weapons, armor, mods, augmentations, Nomad upgrades, blueprints, and resources; APEX Strike Teams and multiplayer used separate credit accounts alongside Mission Funds and micro-transaction Andromeda Points.
