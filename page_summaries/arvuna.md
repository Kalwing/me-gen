Arvuna was a moon of the planet Dranen, located in the Aysur System of the Caleston Rift in the Milky Way. It became accessible in Mass Effect 3 after the mission Leviathan: Find Garneau. Classified as a water world because oceans or ice covered 90% of its surface, Arvuna hosted prodigious sea life as well as venomous arthropodal pests in its tropical zone whose metallic carapaces, similar to those found on Palaven, resisted radiation from Dranen's magnetosphere. Several well-shielded human colonies existed on Arvuna, though they were alienated from the Citadel Council and politically insignificant to the Traverse and Terminus Systems.

During the Reaper War the Reapers had not reached Arvuna, concentrating instead on the Balor system, which cut Arvuna off from the cluster's mass relay but served as evidence that the Reapers could not be everywhere at once. The Mass Effect 3 multiplayer map Firebase Hydra was located on Arvuna. Cerberus Daily News stories referenced the moon, including one about fishermen and marine biologists capturing a Deinorostrum there. Arvuna was originally slated for Mass Effect 2 and appears in that game's guide and files, but was cut before release; its earlier description differed significantly, mentioning avian species and human settlements totaling over a third of a billion inhabitants.
