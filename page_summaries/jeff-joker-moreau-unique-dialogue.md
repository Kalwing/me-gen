This page collects Joker's optional conversational dialogue across all three games: mission-completion remarks, his "The Squad" opinions of the last two squadmates Shepard took out, randomized cockpit banter triggered by standing behind him, lines during the Collector attack on the Normandy, and Mass Effect 3 exchanges about EDI's body and a possible relationship. The lines are heavily sarcastic and full of 20th–21st century pop-culture references.

Representative lines:

Joker, after Noveria in Mass Effect: "One will freeze your balls off, the other will sell them out from under you."

Joker, mission completion after Dossier: The Assassin: "Oh, another dangerous alien aboard, Commander. Thanks. Why can't you collect coins or commemorative plates or something?"

Joker, mission completion after Dossier: Archangel: "Hey Commander, we got Garrus back! That's great, because he was totally my favorite... with that pole up his ass."

Joker, cockpit banter in Mass Effect 2: "Yeah, this is 98 percent of my job. I just watch buttons flash. Sometimes I press one."

Joker, cockpit banter: "You know what pisses me off? Calling this the cockpit. Alliance ships have bridges. Asari ships have cockpits. Oh wait. No they don't."

Joker, during the Collector attack when EDI asks for ship control: "What?! You're crazy! You start singing Daisy Bell and I'm done!"

Joker, during the Collector attack: "See, this is how it all starts. When we're all just organic batteries, guess who they'll blame? ... I have to spend all day computing pi because he plugged in the Overlord."

Joker, after EDI gets her new body in Mass Effect 3: "If I knew that EDI was going to install herself into a sexy robot body, do you honestly think I'd be able to keep quiet about it? Look at that! I would have baked a cake!"

Joker, on the Council in Mass Effect 3: "After years of ignoring your warning, they're finally willing to step up and tell us they just can't help. Did they at least validate our parking?"

Joker, on biotics: "I'm just tired of them stealing the spotlight from people with actual disabilities. I break ribs if I sneeze too hard - being able to move crap with your mind is not a handicap."

Joker, if Shepard discouraged the EDI romance (Citadel party, to Miranda): "And if we ever broke up? Well, nobody wants an angry ex equipped with a Thanix cannon."
