This page collected dialogue unique to Gunnery Chief Ashley Williams when she accompanied Commander Shepard's squad, drawn from Mass Effect and Mass Effect 3 and sorted by mission and by type: scripted conversation triggers, ambient "talk to squadmate" lines, and unprompted spontaneous remarks. Her lines consistently voiced wariness of aliens and artificial intelligence, blunt military pragmatism, loyalty to Shepard, and personal history with her lost unit the 212 and with Kaidan Alenko's death on Virmire. Locations covered included the Citadel, Therum, Noveria, Feros, Virmire and Ilos in the first game, and Eden Prime, Tuchanka, the Ardat-Yakshi monastery on Kallini, Rannoch, Thessia, Horizon, the Leviathan sites, the Citadel DLC and the final battle on Earth in the third. Recurring situations included her jealousy or affection if romanced, arguments with Charles Saracino and Nassana Dantius, exchanges with Tali over their ranks aboard the Geth Dreadnought, and reactions to Henry Lawson, Shepard's clone, and the Illusive Man's manipulations.

Representative lines:

- On the Citadel Wards, with Kaidan and a male Shepard, after Shepard jokes that humanity has everything aliens want: "If you expect me to get into a tinfoil miniskirt and some thigh-high boots, I want dinner first. Sir."
- During Citadel: Signal Tracking, when Shepard asks the rogue AI whether the matter can be resolved peacefully: "What are you, crazy? It's an AI, like the Geth, it would kill us all if it could!"
- Ambient remark overlooking Chora's Den: "A million light years from where humanity began only to find a bar filled with men drooling over half-naked women shaking their asses on stage. I can't decide whether that's funny or sad."
- On Virmire, approaching Saren's base: "Nothing like a nice, relaxing stroll on the beach… blasting bad guys with my boomstick!"
- On Feros, after Gavin Hossle describes the danger of recovering his data: "Deadly for him, fine for us. What could be better?"
- During Priority: Thessia, taking in the Reaper towering over the squad: "Demons do exist and they want to kill us all."
- During Priority: Cerberus Headquarters, on learning the Illusive Man planted Shepard's old crew: "That son of a bitch set you up."
- During Silver Coast Casino: Infiltration, distracting a guard: "Hi. Ashley Williams. Office of Special Tactics and Reconnaissance. I'm looking for the best. Are you the best?"
