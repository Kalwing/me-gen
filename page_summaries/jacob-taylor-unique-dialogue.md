A catalogue of dialogue unique to Jacob when he is in Shepard's squad in Mass Effect 2 (with some Mass Effect 3 lines), organized into location-triggered lines, "talk to squad mate" conversations, and spontaneous combat and ambient barks. A recurring thread is Jacob defending Cerberus and the Illusive Man, especially during Jack's loyalty mission, where he insists the Teltin facility "went rogue." Representative verbatim lines:

- Jacob, on the Collector Ship entering the massive pod chamber: "Earth... they're going after Earth."
- Jacob, on the Collector Ship when the Illusive Man's foreknowledge is revealed: "It doesn't make sense for Cerberus to sell us out. It just doesn't."
- Jacob, during Jack's loyalty mission on the container logs: "This is... unbelievable."
- Jacob, dying if he leads the second diversion squad in the Suicide Mission without secured loyalty: "There were too many, but I got them through. You guys finish up for me, all right?"
- Jacob, if Shepard destroys the Collector Base and he survives: it was a "hell of a way to tell the boss you're quitting," adding he wished he could have seen the Illusive Man's face.
- Jacob, using Pull: "Get over here!" (a nod to Scorpion from Mortal Kombat), also "Gravity is one mean mother, huh?"
- Jacob, using Barrier: "Throwing up a barrier!" / "Nothing can hurt me now!"
- Jacob, caught in friendly fire: "Get me out of your god-damn sights!"
- Jacob, hearing Jedore's canned PA orders on Korlus: "Who does that?"
- Jacob, on Jack's recruitment mission seeing a prisoner beaten: "Commander, you seein' this shit?"
- Jacob, if a female Shepard dumps him for Garrus: he calls the turian a "Cuttlebone."

Other tagged lines: confronting Ish on Omega about a past ambush and warning Shepard against helping him; on Horizon, dismissing Ashley/Kaidan's accusation as "typical Alliance attitude"; and, if brought to the Shadow Broker's base, the Broker notes his and Shepard's deaths would cripple Cerberus.
