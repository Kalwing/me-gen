"UNC: Dead Scientists" was a Mass Effect assignment obtained either from a security terminal on Noveria's Port Hanshan Mezzanine (initially labeled "Doctor at Risk") or from Admiral Hackett when Shepard traveled to the Newton system. Someone was killing every Alliance scientist who had worked on a classified project years earlier on Akuze, the planet where Shepard's entire unit was wiped out if Shepard had the Sole Survivor background. The last surviving project member, Dr. Wayne, was believed to be hiding in the Newton system of the Kepler Verge.

The assignment played out on the planet Ontarom, where Shepard fought through human mercenaries and snipers outside and krogan-backed mercenaries in a cramped, crate-filled facility. In a back room Shepard found Corporal Toombs holding Dr. Wayne hostage. Toombs revealed that after a thresher maw attack killed the rest of his squad, he had been captured by scientists working for Cerberus and used as a test subject; he had been killing the responsible scientists in revenge. Wayne repeatedly called Toombs crazy and demanded Shepard shoot him. With enough Charm (10+ points), Shepard could talk Toombs down and have the Alliance arrest Wayne; with enough Intimidate (8+), Shepard could shoot Wayne. Failing to defuse the standoff resulted in Toombs killing Wayne and then himself. If Toombs and Wayne both survived, both testified against Cerberus in Mass Effect 2 (Wayne given immunity for cooperation), and Toombs could later message Shepard expressing disgust at Shepard joining Cerberus and threatening to kill Cerberus operatives, including the Commander. If Wayne was shot via Intimidate, Toombs' testimony was dismissed for insufficient evidence.
