The page transcribed art director Derek Watts' gallery commentary for "The Technology of Mass Effect: Human Weapons," from the 2007 Mass Effect bonus content disc. Watts said the game needed a pistol, assault rifle, sniper rifle, and shotgun, plus grenades and vehicle-scale enemies.

He described a human turret/drone that sits as a tripod, then folds its wings and flies after the player, incorporating recurring design motifs: 45-degree arcs, stenciled type, and double barrels. The double-barrel motif originated from an early Eden Prime concept showing a man firing two handguns. Weapons were designed to unfold when held and collapse small onto the player's hip or lower back. Four rear cells on the guns were intended for modular upgrades the team called "X-Mods."

Watts said he added "M7" to the assault rifle early and it stuck, forcing designers to build a whole class around it; the design drew on the real XM8, and the modeler built it in about five days. He noted that drawing guns revealed the office's gun collectors. The shotgun was kept short, roughly a slightly extended sawed-off, and carried a "T88" marking. The sniper rifle mainly needed very long barrels and a large scope, with references including real military weapons and guns from Final Fantasy: The Spirits Within and Appleseed; the built model proved too long when held and had to be shortened. The grenade was designed to be thrown like a frisbee or disc, with a rotating light so the player could see it stick to a wall. A human ball turret used for Uncharted Worlds mercenary camps had its extending legs squared off due to collision problems.
