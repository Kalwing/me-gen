The M-3 Predator was a heavy pistol in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3 and a pistol in Mass Effect: Andromeda. Manufactured by Elanus Risk Control, it was valued as a powerful, deadly and relatively inexpensive weapon. Because it was weak against kinetic barriers, it saw little military use but was very popular in the Terminus Systems, where such defenses were less common. It was reliable and accurate, effective against armor but weak against shields and biotic barriers.

In Mass Effect 2 the Predator was obtained from a locker at the start of Prologue: Awakening and could be used by numerous squadmates including Jack, Jacob, Miranda, Mordin, Tali, Kasumi and Liara. It dealt less damage than the M-6 Carnifex or M-5 Phalanx but fired far faster, and like other pistols had a high chance of triggering ammo-power effects such as Disruptor or Cryo Ammo with a single shot.

In Mass Effect 3 Admiral Anderson gave the Predator to Shepard when the Reapers reached Earth; it was also one of five multiplayer starter weapons, categorized as common. It dealt low damage but was lightweight with a 15-round clip, fast fire rate and rapid reload, and was generally recommended to be replaced once better options appeared. The Andromeda Initiative acquired and modified several hundred Predators for use in Andromeda, where it served as a rapid-fire sidearm and combo primer. Its appearance was based on the primary pistol model from the first Mass Effect.
