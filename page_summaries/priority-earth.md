Priority: Earth was the final mission of Mass Effect 3 and the conclusion of Commander Shepard's trilogy. It was acquired after Priority: Cerberus Headquarters; from that point Sol was the only reachable system, and merely traveling there triggered the mission with no way to back out. Allied fleets assaulted the Reapers above Earth while Hammer, the ground force, tried to land troops near the Citadel transport beam.

The mission had two main stages. First, Shepard's shuttle landed to knock out a Reaper anti-aircraft "Hades Cannon" so Hammer could land; the team fought through streets and a ruined house full of Cannibals, Marauders, and Brutes to a crashed assault team, recovered two M-920 Cain shots to destroy the cannon, held off a Banshee (named "Morinth" if that character survived Mass Effect 2) and endless Cannibals, and were extracted, reuniting with Anderson and meeting Major Coats. At the Forward Operating Base, Shepard held final conversations with surviving squadmates, allies, and leaders (Victus, Wrex or Wreav, Kirrahe, and others depending on prior choices) before Anderson gave the battle plan: a frontal armored assault on the conduit past a Reaper Destroyer.

The second stage advanced through occupied London to a missile battery, where Thanix missiles were coordinated with EDI on the Normandy to destroy the Destroyer while the team fought Brutes, Banshees, and a Harvester and dodged the Reaper's beam. Anderson then arrived by APC for the final push, but Harbinger descended on the conduit and annihilated the ground forces. In the Extended Cut, squadmates survived or died based on Effective Military Strength (below 4200 EMS in Legendary Edition they died). A wounded, solitary Shepard fought through Husks and a Marauder with an infinite-clip pistol to reach the beam.

On the reconfigured Citadel, Shepard reunited by comm with Anderson and found him held hostage by a transformed Illusive Man, who could now control both of them. The confrontation's outcome hinged on the final choice, with full Paragon/Renegade options requiring Paragon/Renegade choices at every prior meeting with the Illusive Man. Paragon made him realize he was indoctrinated and shoot himself; Renegade interrupts let Shepard kill him. Shepard then opened the Citadel arms to let the Crucible dock. The Catalyst appeared and offered Destruction, Control, or Synthesis (Refusal was also possible), with availability and galactic damage set by EMS. Every activated ending crippled the Normandy, and its crew's survival, the epilogue scenes, and the placing of Shepard's name plaque above Anderson's on the memorial wall all depended on EMS and prior choices. After the credits a Stargazer told a child tales of "the Shepard."
