Mira was the virtual intelligence running the Peak 15 research facility on Noveria, coordinating lab functions, security and search queries. Unlike a simple question-and-answer VI such as Avina, Mira handled much of Peak 15's sophisticated lab work, which explained its very large processing core. Mira had been helping the scientists develop a cure for a biotoxin leak before it was shut down due to facility problems. Purging Mira's core was standard procedure during serious lab emergencies; when Shepard expressed surprise that this "killed" Mira, it replied calmly, "To be 'killed', I would need to be alive."

When Commander Shepard's team reached Peak 15, they found the power off and Mira inactive. Shepard entered Mira's core and repaired it through a Tower of Hanoi-style puzzle requiring at least fifteen moves. Because of Shepard's Spectre status or the emergency, Mira granted secure data access and explained that a "contaminant leak," actually the hatched rachni egg, had triggered a Code Omega shutdown. With Mira's landlines and reactor offline, Shepard and the squad reconnected the landlines and restored the reactor, after which Mira warned of "loose contaminants" in the decontamination chamber. As more of Mira's systems came online it provided further information; its medical terminal at Rift Station, where Dr. Zev Cohen worked, could administer first aid, and Mira oversaw the neutron purge system in the hot labs that Shepard activated to kill the remaining rachni. If Shepard did not obtain the purge codes from Yaroslev Tartakovsky, Mira in the hot labs control room explained the purge system and warned of the radiation levels involved. Mira's dialogue and appearance included nods to the Microsoft Office "Clippy" assistant.
