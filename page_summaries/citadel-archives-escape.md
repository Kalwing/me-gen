Citadel Archives: Escape was a mission in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, following Citadel: Identity Theft II. Commander Shepard learned that the culprit behind the identity theft was a clone of Shepard, and pursued the clone into the Citadel Archives before it could escape. Shepard, accompanied by chosen squadmates plus Brooks, rode skycars to the archives, where James breached a wall with a bomb; if Wrex was present he rushed in first. The team fought CAT6 mercenaries, and the clone appeared on a catwalk holding Brooks hostage and ordered Shepard's group to surrender.

The clone revealed it had been grown by Cerberus during the Lazarus Project as a source of spare parts should the real Shepard need them, but the Illusive Man abandoned the project after recovering the original Shepard. The clone remained comatose until roughly six months earlier, around the time Shepard was arrested, then learned to behave human through neural implants. It intended to impersonate Shepard by flying the Normandy, contacted Traynor using Shepard's own codes to prepare an emergency departure, and ordered its men to execute Shepard and the squad.

Shepard's crew fought through successive CAT6 units, including Specialists, Snipers, Heavies, and an Atlas mech, aided by covering fire from the unselected squadmates and from Cortez and Brooks. The VI Glyph was persuaded to track the clone. Reaching a vault, Shepard found the clone with Brooks, who was revealed as ex-Cerberus: she had assembled the dossiers for the Suicide Mission a year earlier, became disillusioned when Cerberus sought alien help as the Illusive Man descended into madness, and had activated the clone six months prior. The clone updated the archives' handprint records, which could not be perfectly cloned, sealed Shepard and the squad in an iridium vault, and departed for the Normandy, taunting Shepard with the phrase "I should go." Shepard summoned Glyph to free the team, leading directly into the next mission, Citadel Docks: Retake the Normandy. Rewards included 15,000 credits, the M-7 Lancer assault rifle, and several weapon mods.
