Priority: Rannoch was acquired after Rannoch: Admiral Koris or Rannoch: Geth Fighter Squadrons, when Admiral Raan reported the location of the Reaper base controlling the geth. Jamming towers on Rannoch prevented orbital bombardment, so Shepard landed with Tali'Zorah (a required squadmate if alive) and Legion (replaced by a Geth VI if Legion had not survived Mass Effect 2) to use a prototype targeting laser to guide the Normandy's bombing run. The team fought through geth forces including Geth Troopers, Rocket Troopers, Hunters, Pyros and multiple Geth Primes, culminating in a fight against three Primes standing together.

Shepard designated the target and the Normandy destroyed the Reaper base, but a Reaper Destroyer climbed out of the crater. Legion secured a geth ship and the squad escaped while Shepard manned its turret, calling in an orbital strike that briefly downed the Reaper. EDI synced the quarian fleet to the Normandy's targeting systems, and Shepard stayed behind to finish the Destroyer personally, dodging its beam cannon and completing three targeting solutions plus a final slow-motion shot while the fleet fired. In its death throes the Reaper addressed Shepard by name, said Harbinger regarded the Commander as a worthy opponent, and spoke of Reapers representing "order" against organic "chaos" and the harvest as necessary "intervention." Depending on dialogue it issued the threat, "Finish your war. We will be waiting."

With the geth freed from Reaper control, the quarian-geth war reached a standoff, with Legion and Tali representing their races. Legion wished to upload the Reaper code to grant the geth true intelligence; Tali feared for her people, who had resumed attacking. Shepard chose to allow the upload (Legion sacrifices itself; the quarians are exterminated and Tali dies), to stop Legion (the geth are annihilated by quarian bombardment), or, with a very high Paragon or Renegade reputation and enough trust-building choices, to broker peace, in which Legion achieves sentience and dissolves its 1183 runtimes to spread true intelligence, and both fleets join the war. Priority: The Citadel III then became available.
