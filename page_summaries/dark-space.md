Dark space, or intergalactic space, was the physical space between galaxies, analogous to interstellar space between stars within a galaxy. It was almost completely dark, containing little to no stars, and made up nearly the entire volume of the universe, with galaxies as small islands of light separated by several million light-years, compared with the Milky Way's roughly 100,000-light-year disc. It was not entirely devoid of objects: the binary brown dwarf 44N81/44N82 and an almost barren planet with calorie-inefficient lichen lay on the outskirts of the Andromeda galaxy.

According to Vigil, the Reapers entered long hibernation states in dark space beyond the galaxy's horizon to conserve energy and avoid accidental discovery and destruction while waiting for their vanguard to open the Citadel mass relay. The last attempt to open the relay to dark space ended in victory for the assembled organic forces and the destruction of the Reaper vanguard, after which the chosen human Council representative, either Donnel Udina or David Anderson, vowed to drive the Reapers back into dark space. After Shepard purged or destroyed the Collector Base, the Reaper fleet was seen in dark space moving toward the Milky Way, having been in transit since Sovereign's destruction. In Mass Effect: Andromeda, the Andromeda Initiative launched cryosleep arks across dark space in 2185, partly to escape the impending Reaper arrival; because of the lack of discharge points in dark space, the Initiative developed technologies such as hydrogen ramscoops and the ODSY Drive, and after over 600 years six arks completed the journey.
