N7: Blood Pack Base was a Mass Effect 2 assignment on the planet Zada Ban in the Xe Cha system of The Shrike Abyssal, acquired via the Star Chart from the Baria Frontiers store or automatically after shutting down the N7: Blood Pack Communications Relay on Tarith. Intel indicated a crude Blood Pack base staffed by numerous vorcha, likely a weapons manufacturing facility. On landing, EDI detected a high power source inside that could be used to destroy the base.

Shepard's squad fought through Blood Pack Troopers, Boom-Squads and Pyros across ramps, a bridge and outcroppings, then entered the facility and killed the krogan overseer Kalusk. Datapads from a Blood Pack contact named Salamul complained to Kalusk about incompetent vorcha, missing workers, and requests for reinforcements including castoffs from Garm. Inside the final room were four containment cells on two large objects; destroying all four triggered large explosions, forcing the squad to evacuate down the hallway to end the assignment. Rewards included 125 experience, 7,500 credits, 2,000 palladium and a Heavy Weapon Ammo upgrade. The mission summary reported destroying a Blood Pack weapons production facility and decimating its vorcha soldiers.
