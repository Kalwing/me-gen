The Shadow Broker was an enigmatic figure at the head of a vast organization that traded in information, always selling to the highest bidder. The Broker balanced its sales so that no client ever gained a decisive advantage, forcing customers to keep trading and keeping the organization in business. The Broker operated only through agents, and its identity was unknown to the public. In 2183 Barla Von called the Broker "he" for convenience but believed it was a group, since no single individual could monitor so much information.

The Broker's dealings touched many events. In 2161 it tasked its associate Brooks to kill Roth, a Cerberus funder, and transfer his funds to Barla Von; Brooks was shot and left for dead, derailing the plan. The Broker also arranged the conspicuous destruction of the turian frigate Verrikan to embarrass the Hierarchy, hiring Zaeed Massani for the job. Tali'Zorah came to the Citadel to trade her evidence that Saren Arterius had gone rogue, routed through the Broker's agent Fist, but Fist had switched allegiance to Saren, so the furious Broker hired Urdnot Wrex to kill him. Admiral Kahoku bought the Broker's services to locate Cerberus, learning only that they were active in the Voyager Cluster. Batarian mercenaries once used the Broker to learn Thane Krios's identity, leading to the murder of his wife Irikah.

After the Normandy SR-1's destruction, the Collectors hired the Broker to retrieve Shepard's body; the Broker sent its enforcer Tazzik, who succeeded despite interference from Liara T'Soni and Feron. Liara and Feron tracked Tazzik to the Broker's base on Alingon, where they found the Broker was communicating through a machine; Feron was captured. Pursuing Feron, Liara eventually teamed with Commander Shepard to raid the Broker's ship on Hagalaz. The Shadow Broker was revealed to be a yahg, a pre-spaceflight species from Parnack; the original Broker had had a yahg abducted for study around 2125, and the captive, operating as "Operative Kechlu," killed and replaced his handler, then pre-empted the Broker's attempt to eliminate him and seized the role, running the organization for about six decades. Having learned of the Reapers and the truth about the Collectors, he had dealt with the Collectors in a bid for his own survival. Shepard and Liara killed the yahg by turning his own kinetic technology against him, and Liara became the new Shadow Broker, taking his Info Drone (renamed "Glyph"). In 2186, facing a Cerberus takeover attempt, Liara crashed the Broker's ship into a Cerberus cruiser and relocated operations aboard the Normandy SR-2. Few knew of the leadership change; the black market only sensed the Broker had "gone soft." An audio log revealed Alec Ryder had reached the Broker through Barla Von for help developing SAM. The Broker's troops wore black-and-white armor bearing a triangular red emblem resembling a yahg's maw, and its forces included Agents, Engineers, Heavies, Vanguards, and Rocket Drones.
