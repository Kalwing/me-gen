Aite was the second planet of the Typhon System in the Phoenix Massing cluster, a Terminus Systems world visited during the Mass Effect 2 Overlord DLC. It was an Earth-like garden world with habitable terrain ranging from deserts to jungles to tundra, a mild climate, wildlife no more dangerous than Earth's, and soil and bacteria that accepted imported plants. It also had faint planetary rings, unusual for a non-giant planet, containing rocks up to a meter long within a dust cloud stretching nearly 23,000 km from the planet.

Despite being a garden world with a colony nearly a century old, Aite was sparsely settled for two reasons. First, its largest moon, Litae, was in an unstable orbit predicted to impact the planet and cause an extinction-level event within two centuries, so all investment was short-term and the biggest local business was selling off native biota to the highest bidder. Second, the planet was extremely violent. Like the rest of the Phoenix Massing, Aite was briefly considered Citadel space during its first colonization wave, but when the colony declared independence in 2133 the Council let it go after less than a day of debate. Without a real governing body, Aite's history became a series of wars between frontier city-states over resources, with citizens expected to fend for themselves against corporations, militia groups, and geth incursions. The fighting was so frequent that the planet's name changed more than eleven times; standard Citadel maps used the name given by its human colonists. Aite could not be mined. It served as the setting for the Overlord assignment and its Atlas, Prometheus, and Vulcan Station missions.
