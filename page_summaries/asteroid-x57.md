Asteroid X57 was a metallic asteroid, 22 kilometers long, that featured in the Mass Effect DLC Bring Down the Sky, set at Terra Nova in the Asgard System of the Exodus Cluster. Originally located at the trailing Lagrange point of the gas giant Borr, X57 was targeted for conversion into a new orbital port facility to support Terra Nova's growth. Because of a funding shortage, planners decided to mine out the asteroid and use the recovered resources to finish its interior for habitation, decelerating it from Borr's orbit with fusion torches so it would fall into orbit around Terra Nova.

Communications with the engineering team were lost, the fusion torches reignited, and the asteroid began accelerating toward Terra Nova. A group of batarian terrorists led by Balak had seized the site, murdering many of the engineering and survey crew; recovered logs and bodies documented the deaths of surveyors and engineers including C. Hymes, R. Montoya, G. Mendel, and T. Slajs. The batarian Charn could provide a pass card to the locked main facility, whose entrance codes the batarians had changed. Human survivors included Kate Bowman and mining supervisor Simon Atwell.

Commander Shepard was dispatched to stop the impact, which would have caused cataclysmic upheavals on Terra Nova lasting thousands to millions of years, as X57 was roughly twice the size of the asteroid that killed Earth's dinosaurs. The associated assignments were X57: Bring Down the Sky, X57: Missing Engineers, and X57: Avoid the Blasting Caps. After the DLC's completion, X57 was brought under control and slated to be maneuvered into geosynchronous orbit around Terra Nova once repairs were finished; despite the batarians destroying months of work, experts predicted the port could still open before the end of 2184. The site also contained X57 Radio, a small pirate music station run by the engineers whose slogans referenced Star Trek, Babylon 5, and Pump Up the Volume.
