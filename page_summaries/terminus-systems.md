The Terminus Systems lay on the far side of the Attican Traverse, beyond space administered by the Citadel Council or claimed by the human Systems Alliance. They were home to a loose affiliation of minor species united only by their refusal to acknowledge the Council's authority or adhere to the Citadel Conventions. That independence came at a cost: the region was fraught with war as governments and dictators rose and fell, and it was a haven for illegal activity, especially piracy and the slave trade. The prevalence of batarian criminal gangs made the batarian language a lingua franca across the Terminus. The region had no government, but the station Omega served as its trade capital.

At least once a year a Terminus fleet raided the neighboring Attican Traverse, typically small strikes against poorly defended colonies. The Council rarely retaliated, fearing that sending patrols into the Terminus would unify its disparate species into a common front and trigger a long, costly war.

Despite the dangers, Citadel races colonized the Terminus extensively; humans, turians, salarians, elcor, asari and volus all held sizable colonial presences, often fully independent of any interplanetary government. The region also encompassed the homeworlds of the quarians (Rannoch) and the vorcha (Heshtok). Before the Alliance expanded into the Voyager Cluster, the turians had mapped the Terminus in detail using an interferometric array stretching from the Attican Beta cluster to the planet Gromar. Its clusters included the Crescent Nebula, Omega Nebula, Far Rim, Perseus Veil, Sigurd's Cradle and the Shrike Abyssal, and its notable sites included Omega, the Mu Relay, the Omega 4 Relay and the Nemean Abyss. "Terminus" is Latin for "boundary stone."
