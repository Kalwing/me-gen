Shanxi was a Systems Alliance colony near the Shanxi-Theta mass relay, a jumping-off point for local traffic and best known for its role in the First Contact War of 2157, when it was the site of several battles between the Systems Alliance and the Turian Hierarchy. Prior to 2186 it was the only human colony ever occupied by an alien race.

After a turian patrol fleet wiped out an Alliance expedition passing through the Shanxi-Theta relay, one ship escaped back to Shanxi. The colony commander sent a retaliatory force that destroyed the turian ships, but the turians escalated, sending more vessels through the relay and capturing Shanxi, though the defenders got a message drone out to Arcturus Station first. Besieged from orbit, the Alliance garrison fought a guerrilla campaign; the turians dropped orbital debris to level city blocks and starve out marine fire teams, and the garrison, with troops starving and civilians dying, eventually surrendered. The occupying turians then faced their own logistical problems because their biology required all food to be imported. Believing they had defeated the bulk of humanity's navy, they were unprepared when the Second Fleet under Admiral Kastanie Drescher counterattacked and drove them off. The turians mobilized for full-scale war, but the Citadel Council, alarmed at Alliance capability, intervened and brokered a truce.

In Mass Effect: Evolution, a turian research vessel carrying an artifact crash-landed on Shanxi during the siege. General Desolas was sent to recover it; he was briefly captured by the human mercenaries Jack Harper, Ben Hislop and Eva Coré before the artifact incapacitated Harper and Hislop, letting the turians escape, recover the artifact and take the humans prisoner, who were later freed in a post-war exchange at Arcturus Station.

Shanxi bred lasting human-turian mistrust but also proved humanity's tenacity and military strength, impressing the galactic community since, apart from the krogan, the turians had found no equals in war. Xenophobic groups like Terra Firma cited Shanxi against alien contact; party leader Charles Saracino used it rhetorically, and a 2173 Terra Firma rally there was to be attended by candidate Claude Menneau, who vanished en route. Ashley Williams's grandfather, General Williams, was the commander who surrendered Shanxi, an act that ended his career though no charges were brought. During the Reaper War the Shanxi-Theta relay's traffic information was listed as "unavailable."
