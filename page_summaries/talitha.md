Talitha was a human woman captured by batarian slavers during the 2170s raid on the colony Mindoir, the same raid that killed Commander Shepard's family in the Colonist background. She was six when the slavers slaughtered her family and forced her to watch her parents be incinerated. She tried to play dead, but the batarians threw her into the holding pens and implanted a control device in her head. Treated like an animal, she was branded with batarian symbols on her back without painkillers, used for manual labor, and abused. She learned to call herself and other humans "animals," since her masters said animals had no names. After thirteen years as a slave, Alliance marines raided the encampment, killed the batarians, and freed the captives; Talitha was so conditioned that she tried to help her dead masters.

Talitha ended up on the Citadel, where in Mass Effect she barricaded herself at Dock 422 with a pistol, threatening suicide. Lieutenant Girard called Commander Shepard to handle her. Speaking in the third person, confused, she could be coaxed to tell her story. Shepard concluded she had developed Stockholm Syndrome; she believed her suffering had happened only to a more unfortunate girl, and thought other humans saw her only as an abused animal. She wanted to die because remembering caused her pain. On learning Shepard was a fellow Mindoir survivor she first called Shepard a liar, then wondered why Shepard had not been broken as she was.

Approaching too quickly caused Talitha to shoot herself. With careful persuasion, Shepard could get her to face her past, take a sedative, and be taken to a psychiatric facility; alternatively Shepard could judge her too dangerous and order Girard's sniper to kill her. In 2185, if Shepard had the Colonist origin and Talitha was sedated, Shepard received an email from her titled "Thanks," reporting that her doctors, one connected to Cerberus, were helping her, that she had been placed in a special school, and hoping Shepard could help others taken as she was. The name "Talitha" is Aramaic for a maiden, from the Biblical phrase "talitha kum."
