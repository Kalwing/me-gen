Klencory was the second planet of the Newton System in the Kepler Verge, a rock and ice world with a chlorine and argon atmosphere and a frozen surface composed mainly of potassium with iron deposits. It was famously claimed by the eccentric volus billionaire Kumun Shol, who said a vision of a higher being told him to seek on Klencory the "lost crypts of beings of light," entities supposedly created at the dawn of time to protect organic life from synthetic "machine devils." Shol excavated the toxic surface for two decades at great expense, and no government valued the world enough to evict his small army of mercenaries.

By Mass Effect 3, Shol's once-ridiculed visions seemed less far-fetched, and his mercenaries remained dug in awaiting husks; the codex judged they would likely be obliterated by a Reaper orbital bombardment passing through on its way elsewhere. In Mass Effect 3, after Priority: Thessia, scans of Klencory revealed the cockpit of a volus trading vessel with no sign of the rest of the ship; the salvage team recovered one of Matriarch Dilinaga's writings from the ship's systems, counting toward the collection UNC: Asari Writings: Matriarch's Writings.
