This page has minimal prose and is a gameplay reference. Alliance Officer was a passive class power in Mass Effect 3's single-player mode, available to squad members Ashley Williams and Kaidan Alenko and not used in multiplayer. At rank 1 it boosted weapon damage, health, and shields or barriers by 15 percent, with ranks 2 and 3 raising the bonuses further. For Ashley the power scaled toward weapon damage or durability, then power damage or sniper rifle damage, and finally a squad-wide weapon damage bonus or an assault rifle bonus. For Kaidan it scaled toward power damage, then weapon damage or durability, then biotic or tech damage, and finally squad bonuses to tech and biotic power damage or to squadmate shield recharge. The rank 6 "Squad Bonus" evolutions applied to the whole party, including Ashley or Kaidan themselves, unlike the similarly named evolutions on Shepard's own class powers.
