The Firewalker Pack was a downloadable content pack for Mass Effect 2, announced on February 25, 2010 and released on March 23, 2010. Its central addition was the M-44 Hammerhead, a hover tank used for planetary exploration on hostile worlds. All combat and resource gathering in the pack's content took place within the Hammerhead; although a squadmate selection screen was shown, the choice had no effect beyond incidental comments.

The pack added five assignments under the Project Firewalker banner: Rosalie Lost, Geth Incursion, Survey Sites Located, Volcano Station, and Prothean Site, several linked to galaxy-map objectives such as Artifact Collection, Recover Research Data, and Prothean Ruin. Seven locations were opened for these missions, including the Dranek system in the Krogan DMZ, Chomos in the Phoenix Massing, the Verr system in Hawking Eta, the Yakawa system in the Caleston Rift, the Hoplos system in the Hades Nexus, and the Elysta system in the Ismar Frontier (the latter available only if the Zaeed DLC was not downloaded). The pack also placed a Prothean relic as a cabin item aboard the Normandy and added a fuel depot to the Widow system.

The Firewalker Pack required download or purchase of the Cerberus Network, and was later included as part of Mass Effect Legendary Edition.
