Private Bethany Westmoreland was a Systems Alliance soldier serving aboard the Normandy SR-2 during Mass Effect 3. Together with Private Sarah Campbell, she guarded the doors separating the CIC from the conference and war rooms. The two passed the time by voicing opinions on the aftermath of Commander Shepard's major missions.

After Shepard's tepid meeting with the Citadel Council following Mars, Bethany supposed Earth would have focused on its own borders had Thessia been attacked first. After Primarch Adrien Victus was retrieved from Menae, she thought he was only cooperative because his homeworld was burning, and noted the Normandy had to host the war summit because the salarians would not let krogan aboard their ships; she was nonetheless impressed by turian tenacity after seeing feeds from Palaven. Following Eve's rescue from Cerberus forces on Sur'Kesh, she felt the salarians should have defended their own homeworld better. After Tuchanka was resolved, she was mainly relieved the turians and krogan were off the ship. After the failed Cerberus coup on the Citadel, she thought Councilor Udina got what he deserved for dealing with terrorists. She found it hard to fight only geth while the Reapers held half the galaxy.

After Shepard destroyed a Reaper Destroyer on Rannoch, Bethany shared Sarah's excitement that Reapers could be beaten. She briefly lost morale after the failure on Thessia, believing the asari should have joined the war earlier, but recovered after the success on Horizon, where she questioned what kind of soldier would help turn people into husks. Once Cerberus was destroyed, she was eager to fight the final battle on Earth, not wanting to tell her grandchildren she spent the war guarding a door.

In quieter moments she remarked that Citadel residents would soon notice the war as refugees arrived, disagreed with Sarah about taking revenge on those who sat the war out, and recounted the Collector attack on the Normandy and the abduction of its crew as told to her by Joker. She noted Cerberus had once been popular as the only group fighting the Collectors while the Council did nothing, and held a low opinion of black ops work in general.
