The ML-77 Missile Launcher was a heavy weapon in Mass Effect 2, a rapid-fire launcher firing seeking projectiles effective against armor, shields and biotic barriers. Missile launchers were appearing with increasing frequency among Terminus Systems mercenary bands, though their origin was unknown; each projectile carried a friend-or-foe recognition system that ensured it would find a hostile target, though not necessarily the one in the crosshairs. It was popular with the Blue Suns for clearing snipers and entrenched enemies in urban fighting, and was nearly impossible to duplicate because it used Fabrication Rights Management (FRM) technology, a nod to real-world digital rights management.

In gameplay it was unlocked at the Normandy SR-2 research station after researching one Heavy Weapon Ammo upgrade, costing 5,000 iridium. Its missiles homed on any active enemy in the battle space regardless of line of sight or the player's target lock, choosing their own targets somewhat erratically, and could not lock onto destructible objects. With no enemies present the missiles scattered randomly. They dealt moderate area explosive damage with a stagger effect, instantly killing husks and abominations, but also caused friendly fire. Fully upgraded it carried over twenty missiles. It was considered weak for general combat compared to the M-100 Grenade Launcher or M-622 Avalanche, but useful for attacking while fully in cover against dangerous targets such as gunships, YMIR Mechs and the Oculus. In Mass Effect 3 it was cut from player use and appears only with Geth Rocket Troopers.
