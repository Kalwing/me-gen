Search and Rescue was the system in Mass Effect 3 that replaced the Planet Scanning mini-game of Mass Effect 2. The Normandy SR-2 carried an improved scanning pulse that, at the push of a button, radiated outward a limited distance to detect objects of interest—War Assets, artifacts, intel, and salvage—as the player flew around a system; multiple pulses were often needed to cover a whole system. Points of interest appeared as red markers; space wreckage was collected by investigating it, while planetary finds required flying into the atmosphere, scanning the surface, and launching a probe, of which the Normandy carried an infinite supply. Wreckage could also be salvaged for fuel without scanning by flying over it and interacting when a tone sounded.

In total, 1,290 War Assets could be obtained through Search and Rescue, plus 200 from the Leviathan DLC, 440 from artifacts, and 50 from the Paragon route of Aria: Blue Suns; up to 100,000 credits could be salvaged, plus 10,000 from Leviathan and 185,000 from artifacts, along with 7 intel items. Every scanning sweep in a system without an active mission filled a Reaper-awareness meter that filled faster near system entry points; when full, all Reapers in the system pursued the Normandy, and the only escape was to leave via the system boundary or a mass relay. Completing a mission reset the awareness meters on all compromised systems. Reapers moved slowly at first, allowing a quick scan-and-flee approach, and the ship's re-entry position could be manipulated by orienting it on the cluster map so its front faced the central star.
