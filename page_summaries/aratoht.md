Aratoht was the second planet of the Bahak System in the Viper Nebula, and the setting of the "Arrival" mission in Mass Effect 2. Jon Grissom, on a fact-finding mission to judge whether the garden world was worth contesting with the batarians, described it as "like Mount Everest inside an oven." His team concluded that the planet's air pressure and oxygen content were too low for large-scale human habitation, and roughly two decades before the events of the game the Alliance withdrew its claim, concentrating instead on worlds that could support human life without domed habitats and rebreathers. Human governments considered this wise; batarians considered it cowardly.

The Batarian Hegemony rose to the challenge, colonizing the polar regions, shipping in large numbers of laborers, and beginning an extensive terraforming effort using cyanobacteria and invasive plant species. Large-scale dumping of cyanobacteria raised atmospheric oxygen by a fraction of one percent, a modest gain the Hegemony trumpeted as a sign of eventual victory. Short-term profits came mainly from mining the extremely metal-rich planetary crust, with much of the labor performed by indentured servants or slaves, a fact absent from Hegemony propaganda.

Alliance intelligence confirmed that the colony held several batarian military installations, both on the surface and throughout the system, though details were heavily restricted by the Hegemony's Ministry of Information Control. These bases were considered uncomfortably close to human space. Human merchant ships rarely visited, being outcompeted by locally protected companies, and the average Aratoht citizen saw humans only on the news, usually in stories of trials and executions of accused spies. EDI's travel advisory warned that the Batarian Hegemony treated any Alliance military presence in batarian space as hostile, and that because the Normandy SR2 resembled the Alliance's Normandy SR1, use of stealth systems was strongly recommended. The system later became the center of a diplomatic crisis after an Alliance cruiser incident.
