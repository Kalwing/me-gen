This page contains under ~150 words of real prose. Abrudas was a turian lieutenant who served in the First Contact War in 2157, under the command of General Desolas Arterius. Her soldiers found a downed turian research vessel that had crashed on Shanxi. Acting on a false message from Desolas, she led a squad to rendezvous with the General, fell into a trap, and unwittingly gave human mercenaries the location of the downed research vessel. She was the first female turian visually depicted in the Mass Effect franchise, and the events are detailed in Mass Effect: Evolution.
