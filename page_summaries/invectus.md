General Invectus was a turian military commander who attended the test flight of the SSV Normandy at Arcturus Station as a representative of the Turian Hierarchy, as depicted in the comic Mass Effect: He Who Laughs Best. While travelling to Arcturus Station, Invectus rode in a shuttle piloted by Systems Alliance pilot Jeff "Joker" Moreau, who hoped to become the Normandy's pilot. Invectus treated Joker with disdain, telling him he would never allow a "cripple" to pilot a starship as sophisticated as the Normandy.

After Joker hijacked the Normandy to prove he could fly it, Invectus was impressed by his ability to circumvent security protocols, adapt under fire, and complete the ship's trials with time to spare. He personally defended Joker's conduct and insisted he be made the Normandy's official pilot, implying that doing otherwise would harm human-turian relations. He is distinct from the similarly named planet Invictus.
