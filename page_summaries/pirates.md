Pirates operated on many worlds in the lawless Terminus Systems, making their living by scavenging shipping lanes or running lightning raids on lightly defended outposts, and were often involved in slaving, gun running and other crime. They typically hid on uncharted worlds, in abandoned bases or deep underground. Though by nature independent bands looking out only for themselves, they acknowledged leaders who proved their ruthlessness or battle prowess. Their ships were no match for a real warship but were a genuine danger to unarmed merchant vessels.

The Alliance ran several pirate suppression campaigns, the most aggressive in the 2170s after pirate bands with batarian sponsorship attacked Elysium during the Skyllian Blitz. Tracking anchorages via FTL vectors from Theshaca, the Alliance destroyed pirate bases on Torfan, sending a message to the batarians, and later set up listening posts in the Styx Theta cluster to monitor pirate activity. The quarian Migrant Fleet was frequently preyed upon by privateers, and the quarian military responded mercilessly, making examples of pirates and slavers alike.

Notable pirates included Clint "Black Eye" Darragh, Elanos Haliat, Eluam Ran'perah, Red Qorayk, Tonn Actus, and Zaysh. Planets known for pirate activity included Agebinium, Alsages, Antirumgon, Inti, Maji, Prescyla, Theshaca, and Tuntau.
