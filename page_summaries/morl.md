Morl was a salarian member of the Eclipse mercenary band in Mass Effect 2. His team captured a Cerberus operative carrying top-secret information about Cerberus's involvement in rachni experiments, and tried to extract the decoding cipher through interrogation but killed the operative in the process. Commander Shepard encountered Morl while attempting to rescue that operative, and Morl was killed in the resulting firefight.

In combat Morl wielded the M-9 Tempest, was accurate enough to strip Shepard's shields quickly, and could use Incinerate and deploy Combat Drones. He had strong shielding from Tech Armor plus armor and moderate health, activating Tech Armor to restore shields only once his armor was gone. Recommended tactics were Overload to drop his shields, Incinerate or Warp against armor, then gunfire, or a Flashbang Grenade to deny his tech abilities.
