This page summarized Mass Effect film and television projects, all with official BioWare support. One animated film was released: Mass Effect: Paragon Lost, produced by T.O. Entertainment and FUNimation, a prequel to Mass Effect 3 following the early career of Alliance Marine James Vega, who leads an elite Special Forces squad defending a remote colony against a Collector invasion seeking to capture the population. A live-action Mass Effect feature film based on the first game's story was developed but shelved.

After the film's cancellation, development pivoted to television. On November 7, 2024, a Mass Effect TV series was officially announced, with executive producers Michael Gamble, Avi Arad, Karim Zreik, and Daniel Casey (also a writer). The live-action series would be set in the canon of the original trilogy, take place after its conclusion, and feature an entirely new cast. On June 6, 2025, Doug Jung was hired as showrunner and executive producer. In April 2026 it was reported the series was being rewritten to appeal to non-gamers and was "on the verge of starting," though Casey said he was not aware of such a pivot.
