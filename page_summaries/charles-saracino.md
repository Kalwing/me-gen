Charles Saracino was the head of the Terra Firma party. Commander Shepard met him on the Upper Wards of the Citadel, where he was taking part in a Terra Firma protest marking Armistice Day, the anniversary of the end of the First Contact War. Like most members of Terra Firma, Saracino believed Earth must stand firm against alien influences "politically, socially, and in the worst case, militarily," and cited Shanxi as an episode humanity should never forget when dealing with alien races. If Urdnot Wrex, Garrus Vakarian, Ashley Williams or Liara T'Soni were in Shepard's party without another human squadmate present, one of them ended up arguing with Saracino and Shepard had to intervene.

Saracino was seeking one of the "spacer" seats in the Alliance parliament at Arcturus Station, but ran into a problem with the eligibility rules: to vote for a spacer candidate, a citizen had to spend more than six months a year in space while never remaining in any settled system for more than a month. Shepard fit those conditions, so Saracino asked whether the Commander would be interested in voting for him.

In Mass Effect: Ascension, set in 2183 after the Battle of the Citadel, Paul Grayson reminisced about his first kill, Keo, one of the bodyguards of Claude Menneau, who had been Saracino's chief rival. The Illusive Man had Menneau assassinated, believing Saracino would be easier to influence.

In 2185, if Shepard had endorsed his candidacy, a news report quoted Saracino as pleased that Terra Firma had gained seats in a recent parliamentary election and promising continuing pro-human changes. If Shepard had refused to endorse him, a report instead stated that Saracino had been charged with tax evasion, had pled guilty, and was awaiting sentencing. These Mass Effect 2 news reports were cut from the game's original release and restored in Mass Effect Legendary Edition.
