The Weyrloc Clanspeaker was a krogan of Clan Weyrloc who spoke on behalf of Chief Weyrloc Guld. He was encountered inside the krogan hospital on Tuchanka alongside Weyrloc Clanguards, Blood Pack Troopers and vorcha during Mordin Solus's loyalty mission. Confronting Mordin and Shepard, the Clanspeaker said Shepard deserved death for spilling the blood of many Weyrloc krogan but relayed that Weyrloc Guld, the chief of chiefs, was offering the crew one chance to flee. When the offer was refused, he launched into a long, bloodthirsty speech about Weyrloc's plans for the galaxy, including plunging the Citadel into a star and eating salarian eggs as delicacies.

The lengthy speech gave Shepard the opportunity for a Renegade interrupt: Shepard fired one shot at the fuel tank beneath the Clanspeaker, and after the krogan mocked Shepard's aim, a second shot ignited the leaking gas and incinerated him, removing him from the subsequent fight. His last words were a scream that "The Bloodpack will avenge me!" If not killed by the interrupt, he fought as a standard krogan, armed with a shotgun, using Carnage as often as the Clanguards and able to grant a stat boost to his men; he had high armor and health and would charge and regenerate. The suggested tactic was to hold cover at the bottom of the stairs and focus fire on him and the Clanguards while dealing with vorcha that descended from the right.
