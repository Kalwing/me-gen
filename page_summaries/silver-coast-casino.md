The Silver Coast Casino was a casino on the Citadel's Silversun Strip, owned by the weapons dealer Elijah Khan and introduced in Mass Effect 3's Citadel downloadable content. It occupied three floors: an entrance stairway, a main floor holding Quasar booths (overseen by a krogan pit boss named Krud), roulette tables, and varren racing booths broadcast live from Tuchanka, and an upper floor with more seating, a second bar, and a dance floor. Surveillance cameras and guards watched the floor, a public announcer issued advisories, and three decorative waterfalls carried a strict no-touching rule, with the announcer threatening to call them "hanar urinals" if patrons persisted.

During the investigation into the identity-theft operation against Commander Shepard, the Spectre's team infiltrated a black-tie charity fundraiser at the casino to reach Khan in his secure office and panic room, with Maya Brooks moving through the vents while Shepard distracted or disabled security. They found Khan dead, and a Mysterious Figure appeared on his viewscreen to claim responsibility and threaten Shepard, leaving clues on Khan's computer. Afterward, Kasumi Goto could be found staging a heist in the casino if she had remained after the incident with Jondum Bau and Zymandis; Shepard could inspect her "suspicious device" near the varren racing games and either let her continue or talk her out of it. Various past and present crew members, including Garrus Vakarian and Ashley Williams, could be met there after accepting invitations, and Citadel: Party supplies were sold at the bar. The casino's dance music reused Chora's Den's dancing track from the first Mass Effect. It was the setting for the missions Citadel: Party and Silver Coast Casino: Infiltration.
