"N7: MSV Estevanico" was an optional Mass Effect 2 assignment on the planet Zanethu in the Ploitari system of the Hourglass Nebula, acquired by scanning the planet and detecting a distress anomaly. Surface scans matched the wreck of the Alliance merchant freighter MSV Estevanico, which had vanished without a trace roughly a year earlier. Alliance sources offered a reward for any data recovered on the ship's fate.

The mission was an entirely linear traversal of the decrepit, half-collapsed freighter perched on a cliff. There were no enemies; the hazard was the ship itself, which groaned, popped, and rocked with increasing violence as Shepard crossed narrow beams and fallen debris toward the ship's mainframe, collecting several containers of iridium along the way. EDI warned over the comm that exploring the wreck could unbalance it. A hanging tile had to be shot down to form a ramp (shooting it from directly underneath caused a unique environmental death). Accessing the mainframe triggered a cutscene in which Shepard retrieved the data as the freighter finally slid off the cliff, with the Drop Shuttle extracting Shepard just in time. The page documents notorious bugs where Shepard becomes stuck floating above the final beam, forcing an autosave reload.

An e-mail from Dr. Richard Talos, curator of the Alliance Museum of Galactic Exploration on Earth, thanked Shepard: the recovered data showed the Estevanico had been attacked and overrun by Blood Pack mercenaries and vorcha soldiers, making its crew the first Alliance crew on record to encounter the vorcha. Salvage crews were dispatched. Rewards were 7,500 credits and 2,000 iridium.
