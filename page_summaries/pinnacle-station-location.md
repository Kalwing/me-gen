Pinnacle Station was an asteroid-based facility in the Phoenix System of the Argos Rho cluster, in an asteroid belt near Vebinok, with a population of 1,200. It was originally built as a concealed command center during the Krogan Rebellions and later retrofitted as a military training facility for all high-level special operations teams employed by the Council, its combat simulator letting teams train under a range of hazardous conditions.

The station had three notable sections. Past the airlock lay the command centre, staffed by the station's operators and commanding officer, whose raised-and-rear placement over his subordinates echoed the turian construction of the SSV Normandy's CIC, and which offered a view of Vebinok and the asteroid field. The observation deck was where competitors rested between scenarios, checked scores, and watched others; Tech Specialist Ochren, the simulator's designer, set up and monitored scenarios there. The simulator itself used combat VIs, holographic images and kinetic barriers to produce small-scale scenarios, firing kinetic slugs that hurt like real rounds and could cause real damage if they penetrated shields. It offered volcanic, subterranean, tropical or warehouse settings with different objectives, and enemies were simulated geth, pirates or mercenaries.

Notable inhabitants included station commander Admiral Tadius Ahern, Ochren, Sergeant Dahga, Guard Captain Vidinos, krogan competitor Khel Burrum, and human competitor Lieutenant Bryant. During the Reaper War the station was repurposed as a command and control center for Council forces, and it appeared as a mission location in the N7 HQ Galaxy at War minigame. Because the Pinnacle Station DLC was cut from Mass Effect Legendary Edition, the asteroid no longer exists in the remastered first game.
