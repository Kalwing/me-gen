Aralakh was a gateway system that the krogan accorded a place of significance in their culture. The name meant "eye of wrath" in the krogan language and was also given to a place on Tuchanka and to an elite krogan warrior unit. Access to the system required prior completion of "Mordin: Old Blood" or "Grunt: Rite of Passage" in Mass Effect 2, and of "Priority: Sur'Kesh" in Mass Effect 3.

All of Aralakh's terrestrial worlds were inhospitable; even the krogan homeworld Tuchanka was barely livable by other species' standards. The Krogan Rebellions had stripped most of the system's resources for the war effort, though some deposits remained. The Council Demilitarization Enforcement Mission (CDEM) kept a careful watch on krogan hotspots to prevent another uprising like the Rebellions, and part of its role was regulating helium-3 fuel sales skimmed from the system's gas giants.

The planets orbiting the star Aralakh were Durak (first), Kanin (second), Kruban (third), Tuchanka (fourth), Ruam (fifth), and Vaul (sixth); the system also contained a mass relay and a fuel depot. Tuchanka was the focus of extensive gameplay content: in Mass Effect 2 it hosted the missions "Grunt: Rite of Passage" and "Mordin: Old Blood" and several assignments, and in Mass Effect 3 it hosted "Priority: Tuchanka," "Tuchanka: Bomb," "Tuchanka: Turian Platoon," "N7: Cerberus Attack," and Citadel-related missions.
