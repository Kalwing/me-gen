The Geth Armature was a mobile anti-vehicle and anti-personnel geth unit used in high-risk areas, often dropped directly from a Geth Dropship and typically accompanied by Geth Rocket Troopers. It consisted of a quadruped "tank" or "walker" armed with a slow-recharging Siege Pulse assault cannon capable of killing infantry in one shot, plus a faster-firing secondary weapon dangerous at close range. Armatures were sentient synthetic lifeforms capable of independent thought, learning, and action; when inactive they folded into a compact state, making them ideal for garrisons and ambushes. They had heavy armor, regenerating kinetic barriers, and high resistance to tech and biotic powers. The Geth Colossus was a larger, more heavily armed version.

In gameplay Armatures were usually encountered in Mako-driving areas. Effective tactics included ramming them with the Mako to knock them down, dodging the Siege Pulse with propulsion jets, ramming them into hazards such as lava or off cliffs for an instant kill, and on foot using Lift, Sabotage, Overload, Tungsten Ammunition, and Assassination. Armatures debuted in the X06 demo during the battle on Caleston, where Shepard noted the geth would not deploy one merely to kill trapped miners.
