Aria T'Loak was the de facto ruler of Omega, the nominal capital of the Terminus Systems, and was known as the "Pirate Queen." After a colorful past, she arrived on Omega and worked as an exotic dancer at the Afterlife Club as a cover, then overthrew its owner, formed alliances, and eventually deposed the krogan ruler of Omega, whom she spared and derisively named the Patriarch as an example of her power. She ruled with an iron fist, backed by a strong and loyal organization, had commando training, was a very powerful biotic, and had survived over a thousand years through patience and caution.

In the comics, Aria disrupted a Collector–Blue Suns deal over human captives (Incursion); gave Liara T'Soni and Feron the location where the Blue Suns were taking Shepard's body (Redemption); and briefly detained Tiran Kandros, cousin of her then-girlfriend Nyreen Kandros (Discovery). In Mass Effect 2, Shepard met her in Afterlife, could warn her of a mercenary plot to overthrow her, and could protect the Patriarch from Blood Pack assassins on her behalf. In Retribution and Deception, her daughter Liselle was killed by Kai Leng during a Cerberus operation to capture Aria's mercenary Paul Grayson; the Illusive Man exploited Aria's suspicion of Grayson to draw her into working with Cerberus, and Aria ultimately failed to avenge Liselle.

In Mass Effect: Invasion, Aria allowed Cerberus to use Omega as a supply hub, then fought an Adjutant outbreak alongside General Oleg Petrovsky. Petrovsky fitted her with biotic dampeners and held her captive aboard the Elbrus while Colonel Raymond Ashe seized Omega Control in a Cerberus coup. Aria escaped, retook the Elbrus, and waged guerrilla war, but conceded rather than let Cerberus destroy the station level by level, vowing to return.

In Mass Effect 3, Aria fled to the Citadel and lounged in the Purgatory Bar. She tasked Shepard with uniting the Blue Suns, Eclipse, and Blood Pack in exchange for committing her forces to retaking Earth. In the Omega DLC she led a fleet assault on the Cerberus blockade with Shepard, crash-landed on the station, allied with Nyreen Kandros and the Talons, and fought through Cerberus forces and Adjutants. Nyreen died destroying a group of Adjutants. Aria cornered Petrovsky in Afterlife and either choked him to death or spared him, then reclaimed Omega and committed her eezo supplies and a fleet to the war. In the Citadel DLC she held high scores at the Armax Arsenal Arena and Shattered Eezo.
