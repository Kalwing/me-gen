Juliana Baynham was an ExoGeni Corporation scientist assigned to the growth labs and a survivor of the geth attack on Feros in 2183. She had come to Feros with her daughter Lizbeth, wanting to "really make a difference," but like many colonists had become disillusioned. She could be found at the Skyway Weigh Station.

Despite working at ExoGeni headquarters, Juliana was unaware of the corporation's real interest on Feros: the Thorian and its mind-controlling abilities. After the geth attacked, she hid with other survivors on the Skyway but was separated from Lizbeth. She and Ethan Jeong became the de facto leaders of the survivors, with Juliana acting as the voice of morality and reason against Jeong's expedient focus on ExoGeni's interests. If Commander Shepard found their refuge before going to ExoGeni headquarters, Juliana asked for a search for her daughter.

During Shepard's absence, communications were restored and Juliana discovered that ExoGeni planned to purge the colony. She tried to send a transmission asking for help, but Jeong forced her from the radio and had her restrained just as Shepard returned. After Jeong was dealt with, Juliana was shocked to learn of the Thorian but quickly grasped the situation, offering Shepard Thorian-specific nerve gas grenades to incapacitate the colonists at Zhu's Hope.

Later, Juliana could be found at Zhu's Hope reunited with Lizbeth. If the colony was saved, she noted resupply ships were already inbound and hoped to make Zhu's Hope the biggest colony yet; if Jeong survived, she still called him corrupt but credited his negotiating skills. If too many colonists died, she dejectedly planned to board the next transport off the planet and start over elsewhere, unhappy to be proven wrong about Feros's potential.
