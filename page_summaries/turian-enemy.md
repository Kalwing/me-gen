Short enemy page. In Mass Effect Galaxy, "Turians" (recognizable by their grey color) were the guards of the arms dealer Illo Nazario, whom Jacob Taylor fought through on Tortuga while investigating Illo's dealings with the batarians. They had moderate health and regenerating kinetic shields, attacked at short to medium range with assault rifles, and had no special attacks; Jacob's powers fully affected them but were better saved for Turian Elites.
