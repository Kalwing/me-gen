"Eva Coré" was presented as a doctor and a recent addition to the science team studying the Prothean Archive on Mars, arriving at the facility one week before the Reaper invasion of Earth. After fleeing Earth, Commander Shepard was sent by Admiral Hackett to Mars to retrieve intel from Liara T'Soni and the research team before humanity lost the Sol system. Shepard's squad found that Cerberus had seized the site, and concluded a mole must have helped; a security terminal revealed Dr. Eva as the suspected infiltrator who had enabled the disposal of the research team. She took command of the Cerberus troops and began downloading and erasing the Prothean data.

Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko discovered Eva in a terminal booth amid a projection of Crucible schematics; she disabled the Virmire survivor with a kick, destroyed the console, and led the squad on a chase through the station before boarding a Cerberus shuttle. James Vega crashed the Normandy's shuttle into hers to stop her escape. From the wreckage Eva emerged revealed as a gynoid infiltration unit controlled by the Illusive Man. She grabbed the Virmire survivor by the helmet and, after contacting the Illusive Man, critically injured them before turning on Shepard, who drew a pistol and destroyed the drone. If Shepard failed to shoot her in time, she killed Shepard with an omni-blade to the face or by stamping on their head.

During the chase Eva could not be killed; her shields regenerated instantly, and she used Incinerate, a Shuriken SMG, and Shield Boost. On Shepard's orders Vega carried her charred body back to the Normandy, where it was stored in the AI Core on Deck Three. The ship began malfunctioning, and after the Menae mission EDI walked out of the AI Core wearing Eva's renovated body, explaining she had accidentally triggered a backup power source and CPU while analyzing the unit; Eva violently resisted, and subduing her caused the ship-wide fluctuations and a fire in the AI Core. Video logs on Cronos Station revealed Eva had been built as a successor to EDI, designed for intense loyalty to Cerberus rather than independent thought. The unit may have been named after the Illusive Man's late acquaintance Eva Coré.
