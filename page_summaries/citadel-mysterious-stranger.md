Citadel: Mysterious Stranger was an assignment planned during development of Mass Effect 2 and cut before release; the information is drawn from cut content and is not canon. Set on the Citadel, it involved a romantic relationship between an asari dancer and a Terra Firma politician. In the Dark Star Lounge, Commander Shepard would have overheard an asari dancer called Nara Selephais, who had been on a date with a human man and wished to see him again but could not find him. Offering to help, Shepard would be given a few hints to track him across the Citadel and would learn that the man, Akio, was a politician or diplomat for the Terra Firma party. Akio would ask Shepard to keep his identity secret from Nara. Returning to her, Shepard would choose between lying to honor Akio's request or telling her the truth, either decision concluding the assignment.
