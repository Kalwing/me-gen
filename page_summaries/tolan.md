Lieutenant Tolan was a member of the salarian Special Tasks Group. He met Shepard on Sur'Kesh in Mass Effect 3 only if Kirrahe had not survived the events on Virmire in 2183, effectively taking Kirrahe's narrative place. Tolan was part of the STG squad that rescued Eve and the other female krogan who had been cured of the genophage through Maelon's experiments.

Tolan initially seemed pleased to meet Shepard but refused to shake the Commander's hand, voicing misgivings about curing the genophage. As he talked, he revealed doubt and suspicion toward Shepard, particularly regarding Shepard's determination to fight the Reapers. He expressed an isolationist and imperialist outlook resembling his government's stance on the Reaper threat, lamenting that the revelation of the cured krogan would force the salarians into the war. He admitted he had wanted to execute the female krogan to keep the genophage cure secret, but was overruled by the scientists on his team. Tolan and his team later joined the war effort.
