This page transcribed the recorded artist commentary for the design gallery "The Creatures of Mass Effect: Krogan," included on the 2007 Mass Effect bonus content disc. All nine images were narrated by concept artist Matt Rhodes, who credited body sketches to Mike Spalding.

Rhodes framed the krogan as the setting's Klingon-like "war-like beasts" that the rest of the galaxy tolerated, and said designers' shorthand of "space lizards" was taken literally at first before being worked past. Spalding's gorilla-like, ape-shaped bodies established an imposing gargantuan form that carried through. Other rejected directions included a tribal approach with long organic dreadlocks and a hunched posture, a Star Trek-style version with big wrapped boots and a cone head, and a version judged too lumbering and "dopey." The face was approved relatively early, and the search was for a body to match it, favoring a hard shell over softer exposed tissue.

Adding a large hump to the back was described as the key step: it sold the idea of the krogan as "slaves to their war-like natures" whose genetics prevented them from overrunning the galaxy, and it complemented the shaved head. The approved "naked krogan" shrank the back slightly so it fit a human skeleton, keeping the hard armor plates, soft underbelly, and large intimidating mouth, which required extensive work to animate. Krogan armor was designed to fit the galactic economy, using slats, circles, and curved shapes to emphasize the massive back and tail while keeping the creature believable rather than laughable.
