Swarmers were small synthetic-organic creatures encountered in Mass Effect 3, presumably derived from Rachni Workers and mutated by Reaper technology. They were spawned in groups by puncturing the large orange sacs on Ravagers or by destroying Gestation Pods. They attacked with a suicide leap that damaged targets through both an explosion and an acid damage-over-time effect, and in large numbers could hinder shield regeneration with their constant attacks.

Swarmers had very little health and could be killed by a single hit from any weapon, a melee strike, or any power dealing direct damage; they also had a limited lifespan and eventually died on their own. Despite lacking protections, they were unaffected by Cryo Blast and other non-damaging freezing powers. If a Swarmer died on contact with a player character it left a puddle of acid dealing damage over time, but a Swarmer killed first left no puddle and dealt no damage.

Slow-moving and fragile, Swarmers were best countered with area-of-effect attacks, explosive weapons such as the M-37 Falcon, Scorpion or Krysae, and powers like Singularity, grenades, Dark Channel, Reave or Annihilation Field. Because most non-grenade powers in Mass Effect 3 could hit only a capped number of targets (usually three or fewer), massed Swarmers were effective at absorbing powers meant for more dangerous enemies; grenades and certain uncapped detonations (Tech Armor, Proximity Mine, Flare) were exceptions. As summoned units, Swarmer kills did not count toward completing multiplayer waves or triggering krogan Rage or vorcha Bloodlust, but in single-player their presence prevented squadmate revival and saving. The page notes their arachnoid bodies, acidic fluids and egg-bursting leaps deliberately echoed the "facehuggers" of the Alien films, matching the rachni's overall Alien-inspired design confirmed by artist Matt Rhodes.
