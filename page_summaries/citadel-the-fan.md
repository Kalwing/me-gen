Citadel: The Fan was a Mass Effect assignment involving Conrad Verner, who approached Shepard as a seemingly harmless fan in the Upper Markets on the Wards after Shepard first gained free rein of the Citadel. It played out in three parts, with Conrad reappearing in the same spot only after Shepard completed mission chains on Therum, Feros, and Noveria; speaking to him for the first time after all three worlds made completion impossible, and access to the Citadel was later cut off after leaving for Ilos.

In the first interaction, Conrad asked for an autograph to impress his wife, which Shepard could give or refuse without incident. In the second, after a core world, he congratulated Shepard on becoming a Spectre and asked for a photo as proof he had met humanity's first Spectre; refusing ended the assignment, after which an elevator news item reported that Conrad had tried to prove himself by attacking a group of turians and was killed. In the third, Conrad asked Shepard to sign him up as a Spectre. Telling him to get lost led to the same fatal outcome; otherwise Shepard could Charm him into staying home with his wife or Intimidate him into accepting he was not Spectre material, both granting morality rewards and letting him live.

If Conrad was Charmed or Intimidated and the save was carried to Mass Effect 2, he appeared on Illium in knock-off N7 armor, threatening a bartender and claiming Shepard had shoved a gun in his face. In Mass Effect 3, if Conrad survived both games and Citadel: Rita's Sister was completed, Jenna saved his life by using an omni-tool trick learned from Chellick to hack a Cerberus agent's gun as Conrad leapt to take a bullet for Shepard, after which he and Jenna became acquainted. Glitches could flag Conrad as both dead and alive, or as both Intimidated and Charmed, for save importing.
