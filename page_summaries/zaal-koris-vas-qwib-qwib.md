Admiral Zaal'Koris vas Qwib-Qwib was a member of the quarian Admiralty Board, outspoken in his sympathy for the geth. He firmly opposed reclaiming the quarian homeworld, arguing that the sentient geth had as much right to live as the quarians and that the Migrant Fleet should colonize a new world instead. This earned him the label of geth apologist; Han'Gerrel vas Neema called him a "whining suit-wetter" and Daro'Xen vas Moreh considered him a coward. He was initially distrustful of Rael'Zorah and his daughter Tali, though he later admitted he never hated Tali and only opposed her father's aim of war against the geth. His home vessel, the Qwib-Qwib, was a formerly owned ship whose embarrassing registry name he refused to change over "petty insults."

During Tali'Zorah's treason hearing in Mass Effect 2, Koris objected to Commander Shepard defending her, saying a human had no business in sensitive quarian military matters; Shala'Raan overruled him because Tali had been declared crew of the Normandy SR-2. If Shepard brought Legion, Koris instead demanded the geth be removed from the courtroom and accused Tali of intimidation, but withdrew when reminded the Rayya's captain had permitted Legion aboard. Koris was quick to conclude Shepard and Tali had died retaking the Alarei and moved to have Tali posthumously exiled, prompting objections from Raan and Gerrel before the pair returned alive.

In Mass Effect 3, the other admirals attacked the geth over Koris's objections, driving the geth to the Reapers; Koris feared this had doomed the quarian race and took command of the Civilian Fleet. His ship was shot down while destroying a geth planetary cannon; he survived the crash but was cut off from his crew. Shepard could obey his plea to save his crew instead of him, or convince him to return. If rescued, Koris talked panicking captains into staying, contributed to the war effort, and backed Shepard in brokering quarian-geth peace, lending his authority when Shepard ordered all quarian ships to cease fire. If left, he died charging a Geth Prime with a primed grenade, and some Civilian Fleet captains fled and were cut down, reducing quarian combat effectiveness.
