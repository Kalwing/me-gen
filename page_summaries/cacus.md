Cacus was a star system containing five worlds, four terrestrial and one a gas giant, accessible in Mass Effect after reaching 80% Morality and arriving in any opened Hades Gamma system. It carried some traffic as part of the Anansi-Ishtar shipping lane, but its worlds held little in the way of resources and were mostly unexploited, which let the system function as a stand-in for truly remote places or a jumping point to deep-space mysteries. Its planets, ordered outward from the star, were Zayarter (first), Chohe (second), Xamarri (third), Faringor (fourth) and Treyarmus (fifth). Faringor was used as a movie location; Treyarmus was a stopover for freighters where quarians often sold refreshments or trinkets; Chohe had habitats built by solitary people or corporations researching its native life and was the site of the assignment UNC: Besieged Base, plus asari writings and mineral surveys; and Zayarter had mysterious lights that disappeared on closer inspection. The system was probably named for the "centaur" Cacus, a likely reference to Dante's Inferno, in which Cacus is one of the guardians of the eighth circle of Hell. Supplementary notes discussed Keplerian orbital ratios among the planets and argued the star had likely not yet entered the main sequence, since several airless worlds were too hot for a main-sequence star of its mass.
