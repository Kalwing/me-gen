Jorgal Thurak was a krogan of Clan Jorgal and a representative of the Blood Pack. If Urdnot Wrex had been killed on Virmire or had never joined Commander Shepard's mission against Saren Arterius, Urdnot Wreav led Clan Urdnot and called all krogan clans to the Hollows on Tuchanka in 2186, where Thurak and other Blood Pack krogan were present. Thurak was first heard giving a radio warning to Wreav of the Reapers attacking the Hollows as Wreav's tomkah arrived.

On seeing Mordin Solus or Padok Wiks, Thurak immediately objected to the salarian scientist's presence, refusing to let what the salarians had done to the krogan be forgotten. This sparked an argument with Wreav: Thurak claimed Clan Jorgal had existed as long as there had been krogan and owned pet varren older than Clan Urdnot's "whelps," while Wreav called Jorgal old and weak. Thurak retorted that Jorgal had the experience to know a salarian could not be trusted. When Wreav bellowed that the reckoning for the genophage would come when he said so, Thurak drew a shotgun on him but was interrupted by Eve.

After the krogan agreed to unite against the Reapers and cure the genophage, Thurak joined the convoy for the groundside attack on the Reaper guarding the Shroud. His tomkah was one of the few still intact after a shot-down turian fighter crashed into the convoy. Later, while covering Wreav as he retrieved Shepard's squad, Thurak and his tomkah were dragged beneath the earth by Kalros, the "mother of all thresher maws," and he was presumed dead. Wreav was indifferent, noting that Jorgal was "not a friend" anyway. If Wrex survived Virmire, Thurak's role was instead filled by Wreav.
