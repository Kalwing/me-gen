"Omega: Batarian Bartender" was an assignment in Mass Effect 2, available after Freedom's Progress once Commander Shepard had access to Omega. It began by asking the bartender in the lower area of the Afterlife nightclub for a drink and choosing to buy one.

The batarian bartender, Forvan, served Shepard a drink that caused the Commander to fall unconscious; squadmates commented on this on waking. A passerby named Henner then explained that Forvan held a personal bias against humans after an incident in which several batarians were killed, and had been poisoning drinks served to humans in revenge.

Shepard could confront Forvan with several outcomes. Shepard could simply leave. With a high Renegade score, Shepard could force Forvan to drink his own poisoned drink, causing him to collapse. With a high Paragon score, Shepard could incite the crowd; Forvan would deny the allegations, draw a gun on Shepard, and be shot dead by a turian patron named Ogrinn. Shepard could also tell Forvan he was finished poisoning people, then still use Charm or Intimidate, or finally tell him he was not worth the trouble, letting him run away for Paragon points.

Regardless of resolution, Forvan was replaced by a salarian bartender who served Shepard free drinks on future visits to Afterlife. The assignment rewarded 40 (50) experience. It was internally referred to as "(Omega:) Bekke."
