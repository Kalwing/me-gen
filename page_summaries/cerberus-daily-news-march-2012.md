This final Cerberus Daily News archive collected three brief items from early March 2186, also distributed through the Alliance News Network, which took over the feed afterward. Batarian ships jammed the Exodus cluster relay as batarians fled Hegemony space now that the comm buoys were offline, with the Alliance saying they were fleeing their government. ANN Earth then reported its own comm-buoy difficulties and asked audiences to stand by. Batarian refugees reaching the Exodus cluster, including government figures, blamed unknown attackers who had devastated their planets, insisting it was neither the Alliance nor the Council.
