Lieutenant Gregory Adams was a human Systems Alliance engineer of the 22nd century, hand-picked by Captain David Anderson to serve as Chief Engineer of the SSV Normandy. He had served on every class of Alliance starship, most recently the SSV Tokyo, which he considered a good ship but inferior to the Normandy. Aboard the Normandy SR-1 he maintained a friendship with Navigator Pressly, remained calm and even-tempered, and could brief Commander Shepard on the ship's IES stealth system and engines. He expressed condolences after Corporal Jenkins died on Eden Prime, welcomed non-human crew, was impressed by Tali'Zorah nar Rayya's technical skill and took her under his wing, and was untroubled when Shepard took the Normandy to the Terminus Systems.

After the SR-1 was destroyed, Adams and most surviving crew were reassigned. Unlike Dr. Chakwas and Joker, he declined to join the Cerberus-backed Normandy SR-2 in 2185, citing prior run-ins with Cerberus and disbelief that Shepard was alive; Tali noted his absence was hard to get used to.

In Mass Effect 3, Adams returned to lead the SR-2 engine room after the ship was turned over to the Alliance. He was the first to recognize EDI as an AI rather than a VI. He apologized to Shepard for refusing the earlier call and later discussed his regret with Chakwas or Dr. Michel in the crew quarters. When EDI went offline after the Palaven mission, Adams brought a fire extinguisher to the AI Core and helped as EDI took over Eva Coré's body. If Mordin was absent, Adams contacted EDI about the IFF upgrade and its evolving encryption keys. He offered Shepard a GX12 thermal pipe to make the drive core safer; installing it also let Daniels and Donnelly develop eezo capacitor improvements usable on the Crucible. A firm proponent of synthetic life as valid sapient life, Adams debated the topic with Donnelly and the ship's doctor, with Shepard able to weigh in. After Thessia he could be found with Kaidan praising Liara's resilience. Before the final battle he told Shepard the Normandy was ready and closed with, "It has been an honor and privilege to serve with you." Roger Jackson voiced Adams, who also voices Harkin.
