Kalusk was the krogan commander of the Blood Pack base on Zada Ban. He was killed in a firefight when Commander Shepard arrived to shut down the base, appearing in the final area containing the generator. He carried an M-6 Carnifex heavy pistol with Incendiary Ammo and had the Carnage ability despite lacking a shotgun, along with strong shielding and armor plus krogan charge and regeneration. He was the most powerful enemy in the assignment and one of only two krogan in Mass Effect 2 with a blue shield bar rather than armor or barriers.
