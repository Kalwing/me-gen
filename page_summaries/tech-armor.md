Tech Armor was a tech power available to the Sentinel class in the single-player and multiplayer modes of Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. It generated a suit of energy or holographic armor that protected the user and could be detonated to send out a damaging, knockback energy pulse centered on the character.

In Mass Effect 2, Tech Armor raised the user's maximum shields by a percentage of base shields (up to 100% at rank 4's Power Armor evolution) and fully restored current shields on activation. It lasted until destroyed, which happened when shields were completely knocked out, triggering the pulse; as long as one shield point remained, the armor held and shields regenerated to their augmented strength. The rank 4 choices were Assault Armor, rigged for a much larger, more damaging pulse plus a post-detonation shield boost, and Power Armor, which maximized deflection and added a power damage bonus. It could be reactivated any time powers were off cooldown to restore shields, but could not be activated while sprinting and was not remembered across saves. Its pulse instantly killed unarmored Husks.

In Mass Effect 3, Tech Armor instead provided flat damage reduction (35% base, up to roughly 45–50% with Durability ranks) that protected health as well as shields and stacked with powers like Fortification and Barrier, at the cost of a 50% power-speed penalty (reducible at rank 6). It no longer broke when shields fell and only entered cooldown when deliberately detonated; the omnidirectional blast dealt 1.5x damage to shields, could hit through walls and cover, and struck any number of targets in radius. Rank 5 offered Power Damage or Melee Damage bonuses while active.
