Ensign Copeland was a yeoman assigned to the Normandy SR-2 in Mass Effect 3, holding idealistic views on war and unprepared for its harsh realities. He hailed from the colony Terra Nova. During Shepard's Citadel visit to brief the Council on the Mars findings, Copeland was in Normandy Dock: Bay D24, where he repeatedly stonewalled Battlespace reporter Diana Allers about Shepard's whereabouts. If Shepard let Allers aboard the Normandy, Copeland disappeared from the lounge along with her.

Aboard the ship, Copeland could confront Allers over her opinion piece arguing Terra Nova should be sacrificed to save other colonies, accusing her of being traitorous since Shepard had saved that colony years earlier. If Shepard sided with Copeland, Allers had to retract the piece; if Shepard sided with Allers, Copeland grudgingly accepted her compromise to label it as personal opinion. Copeland frequently visited the Port Observation lounge to hear squadmates' stories. He was amazed by Garrus Vakarian's account of the Rachni Queen on Noveria but rejected Garrus's admiration of the Reapers' method of turning people into husks, calling it simply evil; Garrus warned him to respect the enemy's capabilities. If Javik was present, the Prothean cited Copeland's "childish views of war" as proof humanity had much to learn.
