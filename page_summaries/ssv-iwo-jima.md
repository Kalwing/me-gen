The SSV Iwo Jima was a Systems Alliance human frigate, named after the World War II Battle of Iwo Jima as was the convention for all human frigates. The events involving the ship are recounted in the novel Mass Effect: Revelation.

The Iwo Jima was dispatched to the batarian colony of Camala to evacuate Alliance scientist Kahlee Sanders. Minutes before the planned rendezvous, the Alliance ground team escorting Sanders was ambushed by the krogan bounty hunter Skarr and the Blue Suns. The escort vehicle was overrun by Blue Suns rovers, crashed, and overturned, forcing Sanders and the marines to evacuate it and take cover. As a Blue Suns rover moved to flank them, the Iwo Jima arrived and used her GARDIAN defense lasers to destroy one rover. Skarr then struck the frigate at point-blank range with hundreds of explosive rounds fired from a portable mass accelerator cannon. Because her shields had been lowered so she could land and recover the shore party, shrapnel from the hypervelocity rounds punctured her hull and shredded the crew inside. The Iwo Jima careened out of control, crashed on Camala's surface, and was destroyed on impact.
