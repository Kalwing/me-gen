Nyreen Kandros was the leader of the Talons mercenary organization on Omega in 2186 and the first female turian to appear in a Mass Effect game. Born to a family with a long tradition of military service, she left the turian military after her biotic abilities emerged and was shipped off to the Cabal units, where her talents were underused. After years as a mercenary she was drawn to Omega, where Aria T'Loak took an interest in her, helping her hone her biotics and reconcile with her unwanted abilities. The two became romantically involved but parted over irreconcilable differences; Nyreen stayed on Omega unknown to Aria and eventually took control of the Talons, transforming them from a lawless gang into a disciplined force devoted to protecting Omega's civilians. Unlike the group's previous leader she had a strong moral compass.

In Mass Effect: Discovery she encountered her cousin Tiran Kandros (a hooded turian) and his human companion Luna Shanks being harassed by batarians on Omega, drove the batarians off, and brought Tiran to Aria on Afterlife, vouching for him. Learning of Tiran's mission infiltrating the Andromeda Initiative, she took the organization at face value and urged Tiran to sign up for real to escape the "family curse" they had dreamed of escaping as children. She later led Tiran and Shanks to an armory and fought alongside them against Agent Zeta, distracting the salarian biotic while Shanks triggered a biotic explosion; Shanks was stabbed but survived, and Zeta escaped.

In the Mass Effect 3: Omega DLC, when Aria returned to liberate Omega from Cerberus occupation, Nyreen was reunited with her and revealed she had become the Talons' leader and directed the resistance against Cerberus. She became an invaluable part of Aria's forces and tried to rein in Aria's recklessness, though her composure faltered against Adjutants, Cerberus-created Reaper creatures; she vividly recalled having to kill infected friends and allies. During the final assault on the Afterlife command center, Nyreen led her Talons through Gozu District, scouted ahead, and arrived at the plaza outside Afterlife as Adjutants charged toward civilians. She drew the creatures in with her biotics and detonated a belt of grenades, her biotic field containing the blast; she was incinerated along with the Adjutants.

Nyreen was originally envisioned as an asari gang lord with no ties to Aria, then reworked into a turian to serve as a Paragon foil to Aria for the DLC. She was voiced by Sumalee Montano, and could quote Hamlet's "to thine own self be true" to Shepard.
