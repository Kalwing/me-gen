The Alternate Appearance Packs were downloadable content for Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3 that added new squadmate outfits and armor. For Mass Effect 2, the first pack was released on 23 March 2010 and included three new outfits, one each for Garrus Vakarian, Jack, and Thane Krios; the alternate outfits did not require a squadmate's loyalty mission to be completed. BioWare accepted community suggestions in August 2010 for a second pack, which was released on 8 February 2011 and contained new outfits for Grunt, Tali, and Miranda, including krogan-built plating for Grunt, an armored envirosuit upgrade with a visor heads-up display for Tali, and black Cerberus composite-ceramic armor for Miranda. Both Mass Effect 2 packs cost 160 BioWare Points on PC or 1.99 USD on consoles.

For Mass Effect 3, Alternate Appearance Pack 1 was released on 20 November 2012, with its contents immediately available on download. It included three new outfits, one each for Garrus Vakarian, Liara T'Soni, and EDI, plus the Cerberus Ajax Armor set for Commander Shepard. It was priced at 160 BioWare Points on PC or 1.99 USD on consoles.
