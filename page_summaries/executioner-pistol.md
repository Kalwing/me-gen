The Executioner Pistol was a heavy pistol in Mass Effect 3. It was invented by Blood Pack weapons experts, the first improvised from spare parts and scrap metal during an Omega territory dispute. The result was a handheld cannon firing high-impact, armor-piercing slugs one at a time due to its limited heat sink. Many Blood Pack mercenaries carried one as a backup weapon, though some enthusiasts used it as a primary gun with a one-shot-one-kill approach.

In single-player it was available after downloading the Groundside Resistance Pack; in Legendary Edition it could be bought from the Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies terminal in the Presidium Commons for a base 10,000 credits. In multiplayer it was a rare random reward requiring the Reckoning pack.

The Executioner was a single-shot weapon whose shot hit harder than the M-92 Mantis sniper rifle at a fraction of the weight, killing most soft targets in one hit on lower difficulties. The Pistol Magazine Upgrade gave no benefit since the shot count rounded down. It was easier to use at close range than a sniper rifle, lacking scoped weapons' hip-fire penalties, but was less accurate at extreme range and dealt less damage than the Javelin or M-98 Widow. Its rounds had a latent penetration of 50 centimeters, enough to pierce Guardian shields, thin cover, and armor plating with no damage penalty, extendable further with piercing mods or Armor-Piercing Rounds (which at rank IV reached two meters). It kicked significantly when fired. The page compares it unfavorably with the Arc Pistol, which deals nearly as much damage per charged shot with a six-shot capacity, less weight, and better accuracy and stability, though the Arc Pistol is weaker in Legendary Edition. Recommended mods included the Pistol Scope, Pistol Ultralight Materials, and Pistol Heavy Barrel (up to +40% damage). The weapon had a high per-shot chance to trigger cryo and disruptor ammo effects, and powers that auto-reload such as Adrenaline Rush paired well with it. In non-Legendary versions it could not down Dr. Eva on Priority: Mars in the required single shot, and it made a strong early-game option and squadmate weapon. After being fully deployed on unholstering, a vorcha snarl could be heard.
