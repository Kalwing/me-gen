The Cipher was the cultural context, knowledge, and experience of the Prothean race, its archetypes, biological instincts, and common experiences, needed to correctly interpret the visions stored in Prothean beacons. After the beacon on Eden Prime uploaded its knowledge into Lt. Commander Shepard, the resulting dreams and visions grew clearer over time but remained elusive in meaning without this unique racial perspective. Liara T'Soni first raised the idea, comparing it to what Carl Jung called the collective unconscious, and noted the difficulty of acquiring it given that the Protheans had been dead for millennia. Both Saren Arterius and Shepard used the Cipher to interpret the beacons on Eden Prime and Virmire; without it, a non-Prothean mind lacked the attitudes, mindset, and cultural experience to make sense of the imagery, and describing the Cipher was said to be like explaining colour to a creature without eyes. Shepard gained the Cipher through Shiala, an asari formerly working for Saren who had been offered as a "sacrifice" to the Thorian on Feros. The Thorian had itself gained the Cipher by watching and studying the Protheans on Feros and consuming some of them when they died, assimilating their knowledge; when Shiala melded with the Thorian she shared its consciousness and so gained the Prothean Cipher, which she then transferred directly into Shepard's mind. The experience left the Commander badly shaken but was necessary to find the Conduit. With the Cipher and Liara's help, Shepard could make more sense of the vision and understand the Prothean language. The Cipher later proved useful in understanding archived video footage containing codes for the stasis pod holding the surviving Prothean Javik, and in activating another beacon on Thessia. The information came from the in-game Codex.
