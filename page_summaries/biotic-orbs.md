Brief gameplay page with minimal lore prose: Biotic Orbs was a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode, used by the Volus Adept and Volus Protector Vanguard, that summoned three biotic orbs to float around the caster and be launched at targets. Each floating orb increased power recharge speed, the orbs were effective against armor and barriers, and the power could detonate all four types of power combos.
