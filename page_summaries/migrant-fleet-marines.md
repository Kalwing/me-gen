The Migrant Fleet Marines were a military body within the quarian Migrant Fleet, tasked with naval-infantry and police operations. In 2185, Kal'Reegar and roughly a dozen of his Marines escorted Tali'Zorah vas Neema to the former quarian colony of Haestrom, then under geth control, to protect a science team studying the anomalous behaviour of the system's sun. A company of geth troops slaughtered most of the Marines, leaving Reegar as one of only two survivors of the task force. Separately, a squad of quarian marines attempted to retake the small science ship Alarei after it was overrun by geth; the raid failed and every marine involved was killed.

In Mass Effect 3, sometime after the fall of Thessia to the Reapers in 2186, and only if the quarians survived their war with the geth and Kal'Reegar survived Haestrom, Shepard could receive an Alliance News Network update reporting that Reegar and his squad were killed on Palaven while repairing and defending a critical communications tower used to relay mission information back to the Turian Hierarchy.
