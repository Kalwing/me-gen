The Geth VI was a network of geth virtual intelligences created by the "true" geth to study the Reapers. It appeared in Mass Effect 3 in place of Legion if Legion had been sold to Cerberus or never activated in Mass Effect 2, or if Legion joined Shepard's team but did not survive the Collector Base suicide mission. Commander Shepard first met it during Priority: Geth Dreadnought, and it accompanied Shepard into the geth server and supported the assault on the Reaper base on Rannoch. If Legion had died on the suicide mission, Tali, if present on the dreadnought, described the VI as a "backup" of Legion, likening it to quarian virtual reconstructions of the dead.

Physically the Geth VI was nearly identical to Legion, recreating non-standard armor elements such as Shepard's N7 shoulder piece and the hole in its torso via hologram. Aboard the Normandy SR-2 it stated it was a unique mobile platform containing 1,183 geth programs built to study the Reapers and communicate with organics, but it had no data on events after Saren Arterius' attack on Eden Prime, its last memory being of searching Eden Prime for Shepard. It repeatedly insisted "We are not Legion," though Shepard kept calling it Legion. It held a more negative view of organic civilizations than Legion and was less friendly.

During the final operation on Rannoch a peaceful outcome was impossible when the Geth VI was present. If Shepard chose to let the geth die, the VI violently attacked Shepard, stating its people "were correct to distrust organics," and tried to upload the Reaper upgrade code before Tali killed it with a knife in the back (or Shala'Raan killed it with a shotgun if Tali was absent); the quarians then won the space battle over Rannoch. If Shepard uploaded the Reaper code to save the geth, the VI thanked Shepard, said its "skepticism was misplaced," and dissolved itself into the geth consensus to grant true intelligence to all geth, who then defeated the quarian fleet. Subtitles still labeled the platform "Legion" if Shepard recognized it as such; otherwise it was only ever called "Geth VI." Javik would still refer to it as Legion on the Normandy even in playthroughs where knowledge of Legion should have been impossible.
