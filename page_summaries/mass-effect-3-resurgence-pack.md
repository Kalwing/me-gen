Mass Effect 3: Resurgence Pack was the first free multiplayer DLC pack for Mass Effect 3, released April 10, 2012 for Xbox 360, PC and North American PlayStation 3, April 11 for European PlayStation 3, and included on-disc with Mass Effect 3: Special Edition for Wii U. It added two maps (Firebase Condor and Firebase Hydra); six multiplayer characters (Asari Justicar Adept, Krogan Battlemaster Vanguard, Batarian Enforcer Soldier, Batarian Enforcer Sentinel, Geth Hunter Infiltrator, Geth Engineer); three weapons (Striker Assault Rifle, Kishock Harpoon Gun, Geth Plasma SMG); and four consumable items: Strength Enhancer, Targeting VI, Stabilization Module and Shield Power Cells, each a one-mission bonus.
