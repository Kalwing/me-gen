Hades Nexus: Obelisk of Karza was a Mass Effect 3 fetch mission acquired after Priority: The Citadel II and Priority: Rannoch. A researcher sitting on a bench in the Presidium Commons Courtyard, near the Meridian Place billboard, needed the Obelisk of Karza for the Crucible project, but his team had run out of time digging for it and it now lay in off-limits territory. The Hades Nexus became accessible only after Priority: Rannoch, at which point Shepard scanned the moon Kopis in the Hoplos system to recover the obelisk, an artifact containing Prothean language translations likened to a Rosetta Stone for Protheans. It could alternatively be found during a Search and Rescue scan, initially logging as Citadel: Obelisk of Karza. Delivering it to the researcher granted 5 Reputation, 15,000 credits, and an update to the Alliance Engineering Corps War Asset.
