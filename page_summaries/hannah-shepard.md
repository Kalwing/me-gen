Hannah Shepard was Commander Shepard's mother if the Commander had the Spacer background. In 2183 she served as executive officer aboard the Alliance dreadnought SSV Kilimanjaro; a previous posting had been on the carrier SSV Einstein. In Mass Effect, Shepard could call her from the Normandy's FTL comm to ask about a former comrade, Lieutenant Zabaleta, who had guarded the Einstein's CIC during Hannah's watch and become her friend. When Zabaleta began drinking heavily to cope with post-traumatic stress after the Mindoir raid, Hannah stuck up for him, and she advised Shepard to direct him to the Veteran Affairs Office for treatment.

By 2185 Hannah, now captain of the SSV Orizaba, was reported to have turned down promotion to Admiral, believing that commanding a starship honored her child's memory. Learning Shepard was alive only third-hand through Alliance brass, she sent a pointed message to the Normandy SR-2 asking where Shepard had been and urging the Commander to stay safe and use a secure channel next time.

During the Reaper War, Shepard had not heard from Hannah since the Reaper attack on Earth. After the mission on Rannoch, Hackett told Shepard he had located Hannah and recruited her for the Crucible project; she had been promoted to Rear Admiral and was proud of her child leading the fight. In the Citadel DLC, after Shepard foiled an identity-theft attempt, Hannah asked the Commander to call, and the two spoke briefly about Shepard's childhood, with Hannah again expressing her pride.
