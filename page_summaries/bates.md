Bates was a Systems Alliance soldier assigned to Unit 212's Dog Squad on Eden Prime, appearing in Mass Effect: Foundation. Two days before Saren Arterius' geth attack, he was one of the grunts on downtime at a bar, watching Sergeant Donkey's interactions with Chief Ashley Williams and joking about who would pair off with whom. Dog Squad was then assigned to defend a recently unearthed Prothean Beacon, patrolling the area between the dig site and the colony. Bates complained about going on foot while the other three squads took transport, and Donkey put him on point duty for it.

On the night of the geth attack, Bates voiced unease, noting that Bravo Squad had failed to make contact, which he took as a sign something was wrong. The squad discovered Bravo Squad's remains below the cliff they had been standing on, and Donkey partnered Bates with Ashley to investigate; they found no survivors. Bates became distraught over the corpse of a friend but pulled himself together after Ashley consoled him, and he approved of her pep talk when Donkey named her squad leader.

At dawn, Dog Squad prematurely engaged the geth at the mission site, and Bates laid down diversionary fire. The tactic failed and the squad was killed one by one. Bates saw a gigantic, ominous starship in the distance, and when only he and Ashley remained he refused to answer her calls for support, crawling away from the battlefield in the chaos, terrified and out of his depth. In the aftermath he was captured by Kai Leng and forced to divulge what he knew. The day after the attack, Bates assured Leng he had told him everything, and Leng decided to kill him and make it look as though he had died with the rest of his squad.
