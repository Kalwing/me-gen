The Infiltrator was a playable character class in Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, and a combat Profile in Mass Effect: Andromeda. It combined tech and combat abilities, focusing on disabling and killing enemies quickly, unlocking alternate routes, and gaining advantageous positions.

In Mass Effect, the Infiltrator possessed a mix of combat and tech talents, could gain specialist training with pistols and sniper rifles, started in light armor with potential for medium armor, and could equip advanced omni-tools. Its class talent reduced heat buildup with pistols and sniper rifles and increased explosion damage from tech proximity mines (Sabotage, Overload, Damping). Infiltrators could specialize as Commando or Operative to reach levels 7-12.

In Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, Infiltrators gained the signature ability Tactical Cloak, hiding them from visual and technological detection, and time slowed briefly when scoping with a sniper rifle. Their powers included Disruptor Ammo, Cryo Ammo, Incinerate, AI Hacking (Mass Effect 2) or Sabotage and Sticky Grenade (Mass Effect 3), and Operative/Operational Mastery. Mass Effect 2 weapon training covered heavy pistols, sniper rifles, submachine guns and heavy weapons. In Mass Effect 3 the class had a weight capacity bonus ranging from 15 to a maximum of 70, comparable to the Sentinel and Vanguard.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda, which replaced fixed classes with swappable Profiles, the Infiltrator was one of seven profiles available to Pathfinder Ryder, enhancing mental and visual acuity, with bonus powers Battlefield Awareness (see enemies through walls when scoped) and Cloak Evades (brief cloak when evading). Statistically, the Infiltrator was the second most-picked Shepard class in the first two games (11% of players as of March 2012), rising to third rank at 15% in Legendary Edition statistics from July 2021.
