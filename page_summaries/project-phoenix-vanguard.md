The Project Phoenix Vanguard was a playable multiplayer class in Mass Effect 3, one of the Project Phoenix operatives. Project Phoenix was a result of the Illusive Man's early experiments in enhanced human physiology, producing highly skilled combat and biotic specialists. After the Illusive Man adopted indoctrination to ensure his troops' loyalty, many Phoenix operatives defected to the Alliance to fight the Reapers. Their command of Cerberus lash and stasis technology, backed by strong biotic skills, made them valuable on the battlefield.

The Vanguard had 500 barrier and 500 health and used Biotic Charge, Lash, Smash and Fitness under Phoenix Training. Its regular melee was a shock baton, its heavy melee swung biotically charged lashes, and its grab dragged an enemy over cover for a charged biotic punch. Lash worked like a damaging, long-range Pull that could be evolved for shield and barrier penetration. The class could single-handedly create biotic explosions on armored enemies by pairing Smash with Biotic Charge, with Charge also restoring barriers for survivability. Smash produced a short-range, cover-piercing shockwave effect and, with its Biotic Combo evolution, left a priming effect on any target including large armored units, uniquely letting this Vanguard benefit from Warp Rounds' biotic damage bonus against hard targets like Atlases.
