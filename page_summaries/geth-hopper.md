The Geth Hopper was a specialized geth platform used for stealth attacks, cyberwarfare and sabotage in the original Mass Effect. As cyberwarfare platforms, Hoppers could disrupt hardsuit computers, shields and weapons, and like the Geth Sniper they used a radar jammer; they typically ambushed in packs, relying on rapid movement to cause disarray. The Hoppers were a geth subtype the quarians did not recognize, hinting that the geth were evolving beyond their original forms. A Hopper's body was built from a springy synthetic substance resembling organic muscle tissue that could be compressed and released to launch the platform enormous distances, giving Alliance soldiers the name; tiny hooks on its hands and feet let it climb walls and ceilings. Recognized subtypes were the Geth Stalker (disabler), the Geth Sapper (assault), and the Geth Ghost (sniping).

Hoppers were lightweight and lightly armored, vulnerable to gunfire and especially to physics-based biotics such as Throw, though their constant leaping made them hard to hit; Lift could hold one in midair for a sniper shot. Some Hoppers used a powerful sniper laser resembling Assassination to attack shields, marked by a red targeting beam. Hoppers debuted in the X06 demo footage, where Ashley Williams shouted a warning on seeing one outside the mine on Caleston, later incorporated into Therum. Cut content indicates Hoppers were considered for Mass Effect 2: the filename "HopperCanyon" appears in Tali'Zorah's recruitment mission, they are referenced in unused plot data, and a Hopper-like model exists in Legion's loyalty mission files.
