Citadel: Captain Bailey was an optional Mass Effect 2 mission. Captain Bailey ran the local C-Sec office in Zakera Ward. When Shepard tried to leave the docking area, an alarm sounded because security had tightened and the scanners registered Shepard as dead. Sergeant Haron ordered the scanner shut down, apologized, and directed the Commander to Bailey at the nearby C-Sec Office. Bailey's console also listed Shepard as dead; asked how he knew the Commander was not an impostor, he said the scanners were accurate and the best in the galaxy. Shepard could have Bailey reinstate the Commander's IDs, which Bailey said saved about nine days of running around, or choose to leave the status unchanged.

Either way, Bailey suggested Shepard head to the Presidium to speak with the Council, with dialogue varying by whether the character was imported from Mass Effect and whether the Council had been saved. The mission was optional because full Citadel access and Council contact were available without talking to Bailey. For an imported save with the Council saved and Anderson or Udina as Councilor, Shepard could go straight to the Council to have Spectre status reinstated; if the Council had been killed, reinstatement was possible only if Anderson was on the Council. Afterward Bailey remained available to provide information. Trivia noted his line that "spending a year dead is a popular tax dodge" likely referenced Hotblack Desiato in Douglas Adams's The Restaurant at the End of the Universe.
