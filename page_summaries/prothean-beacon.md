Prothean Beacons were rare pieces of surviving Prothean "paleo-technology." They formed a system of near-instantaneous communication across the Prothean empire, interfacing directly with a person's brain rather than through audio or video. The discovery of a beacon on Eden Prime prompted the Alliance to dispatch the SSV Normandy to recover it.

Commander Shepard's "vision," triggered by accidentally accessing the Eden Prime beacon, was a warning about the Reapers sent by the researchers on Ilos to reach any survivors and give them hope. The message was coded so that only Prothean minds could understand it, a precaution meant to keep the Reapers from learning Ilos had survived. Because the Protheans did not yet fully understand indoctrination, they failed to anticipate that an organic Reaper agent could receive it. The Eden Prime beacon was also badly damaged, reportedly in Sovereign's attack, so Shepard received only random, vivid images, suffering fifteen hours of unconsciousness and recurring nightmares. Comprehending the message as intended required the Cipher, which enabled both Shepard and Saren Arterius to understand the archetypes. Liara T'Soni noted Shepard's exceptionally strong will; the beacon exploded after transmitting to Saren and then Shepard, possibly from the strain, possibly because Shepard instinctively resisted the joining.

An intact beacon with a user interface existed in Saren's lab on Virmire; Shepard coped more easily with its vision, and it was presumably destroyed in the nuclear blast that leveled the base. A Prothean video terminal on Joab held a similar vision, but showed a pair of Collectors instead of a Reaper at the end. Another beacon was hidden on Thessia inside a statue of the goddess Athame, known only to the highest levels of asari government, which secretly used its data to make the asari the galaxy's most advanced race in violation of Council policy. During the Reaper invasion Shepard sensed it with the Cipher and activated it using temple artifacts, releasing the Prothean VI Vendetta, which suggested that the repeating patterns of galactic evolution were not caused by the Reapers, who were merely "servants" of that pattern, before Kai Leng stole the data on the final Crucible piece.
