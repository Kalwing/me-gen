Citadel: Cerberus Retribution was a Mass Effect 3 mission acquired after Priority: The Citadel II. On the stairs leading to the C-Sec Outpost in the Presidium Commons, two arguing citizens were planning to kill someone in C-Sec custody; one was conflicted while the other sought justice for their murdered spouses. Talking to them activated the mission. While Shepard questioned them, they received a call from Captain Aaron Sommers saying he was in position at C-Sec and needed them to distract security so he could move in for the kill.

Inside the C-Sec office, Sommers, a retired Alliance marine, realized his plot was exposed and explained that a C-Sec officer was in custody for aiding Cerberus during their attack on the Citadel. The officer was giving C-Sec intel on Cerberus in exchange for a lighter sentence, but Sommers argued he deserved death for murdering innocents and executing his own friends, and that as a fellow Alliance veteran who had dishonored the uniform it was up to Sommers to deliver justice. Shepard could persuade Sommers to call off the hit (Paragon), sanction the killing under Spectre authority (Renegade), warn him he would be arrested, or feign ignorance and let the plot proceed. The outcome granted morality points plus 210 experience and 5,000 credits.
