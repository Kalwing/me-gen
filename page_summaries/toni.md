Captain Toni was the nominal leader of Delta Squad, an Alliance special forces unit deployed to Fehl Prime. Lieutenant James Vega described him as a "hard-assed son of a bitch" but a "good leader." Though his squad were highly trained, Fehl Prime in 2183 was their first real engagement; Alliance Command deployed the rookies with little idea of the threat, and Toni synced the mission briefing to their omni-tools after they geared up.

Krogan Blood Pack mercenaries were besieging the colony, one of humanity's top pharmaceutical processing facilities, hammering its kinetic barriers. Toni's plan was Kodiak strafing runs, dropping marines between the colony and the mercenaries, then "vigorously negotiating." The air strikes worked well until the Blood Pack brought up missile launchers and shot down Toni's shuttle during landing. Toni dislocated his right leg in the crash; Vega set it, giving him a thermal clip to bite on, but Toni's scream alerted the enemy and he fell unconscious. Vega assumed command, assigned pilot Mason to guard Toni, and led the counterattack that defeated the Blood Pack. A recovered Toni, wheelchair-bound, later urged a modest Vega to accept Anderson's and Hackett's praise during the debriefing.

Two years later the colony was flourishing with new defense cannons and barriers, and Toni, walking with a cane, led Delta Squad's guard duty. He ordered the squad to investigate an anomalous signal near ruins that was disrupting offworld communications; with the APC down for a suspension overhaul, a civilian named Messner lent his secondhand Mako. The Collectors then attacked, their Seeker Swarms rendering the military-grade kinetic barriers useless. Toni fought with a flamethrower until he was stung in the neck and paralyzed, and was carried aboard the Collector ship with the other colonists. He revived after Delta Squad released a paralysis antidote, took up Collector weapons with other able-bodied men to defend the colonists, and contacted Vega by omni-tool. With the Collector ship crashing, Vega chose to save the intel about the Collectors rather than the stranded colonists. Toni never got word from Vega; sensing the young man's turmoil, he urged the decision be made "now or never," then died alongside April and her mother Christine as the ship exploded on the planet's surface, muttering "God have mercy" as he realized no evac was coming.
