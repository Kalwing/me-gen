Armor was protective body covering intended to prevent injury in combat, usually customized for the wearer's species. It was also termed a combat hardsuit. Beyond letting the wearer survive environments such as outer space, each suit was reinforced with ceramic plating on non-flexible body parts and carried a kinetic barrier generator and an onboard computer able to give environmental readouts, interface with terminals to download data, and check wildlife against the xenobiology database.

In Mass Effect, turians, krogan, quarians, and humans had species-specific armor, while asari used human armor. Three general categories rated protection: light, medium, and heavy. Light armor offered basic protection with minimal accuracy penalty and was usable by all classes from the start; Adept, Engineer, Asari Scientist, Sentinel, and some other classes were limited to it. Medium armor increased protection and movement penalties and was available to Soldiers and Krogan Battlemasters immediately, or to Infiltrators, Vanguards, and Turian Agents through the Tactical Armor talent. Heavy armor gave the highest protection and heaviest penalties, reachable only by Soldiers and Krogan Battlemasters with seven points in Combat Armor. Armor attributes were Damage Protection (percentage health-damage reduction), Shields (damage absorbed before health), and Tech/Biotic Protection. Armors of levels I-VI had one upgrade slot; VII and above had two. Manufacturers included Hahne-Kedar, Kassa Fabrication, Rosenkov Materials, Sirta Foundation, and Devlon Industries, the last providing immunity to environmental hazards. Some armors carried hidden shield multipliers, and glitches allowed equipping restricted armor on any party member.

In Mass Effect 2, Shepard started with default N7 Armor, which could be customized piece by piece at the Normandy SR-2 armor locker or swapped for DLC sets such as Blood Dragon, Terminus Assault, Collector, Inferno, Cerberus Assault, or Kestrel Armor; only Kestrel was as customizable as N7. Squadmate armor was cosmetic only, with alternate appearances unlocked through loyalty missions or DLC. Cybernetic, medical, shield, and squad upgrades further improved survivability.

In Mass Effect 3, Shepard again began with N7 Armor, customizable or replaceable with sets including N7 Defender and Reckoner Knight Armor. Unlike Mass Effect 2, squadmate armor sets in Mass Effect 3 conferred gameplay bonuses that varied by set.
