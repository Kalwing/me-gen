The Ruzad was a shotgun in Mass Effect: Andromeda. A massive, slow-firing krogan weapon, it delivered a forceful blow that staggered any enemy who survived. In krogan culture "Ruzad" translated loosely as "judge," a name the krogan considered more obvious than humorous or ironic. It was a straightforward weapon of its class with no unique traits; the large bayonet under its twin muzzles was purely cosmetic and gave no melee bonus.

The Ruzad had to be researched before crafting and was outperformed in every stat by the Disciple shotgun, which auto-unlocked crafting tiers as Ryder leveled, leaving no gameplay reason to choose the Ruzad over the Disciple aside from aesthetics. Squadmate Drack wielded a Ruzad as his weapon of choice. The multiplayer version received one balance update, an increase in damage per pellet. Singleplayer stats ran from rank I (355 damage) to rank X (580 damage) with a fixed rate of fire of 70 and a three-round clip; the multiplayer version dealt substantially higher damage. Full development from rank I to X cost 1,450 research, 300 omni-gel canisters, and quantities of iron, nickel, and element zero.
