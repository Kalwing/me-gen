The Krogan Rebellions were a major war between the krogan and the Citadel Council that began in 700 CE and lasted decades, preceded by over three centuries of aggressive krogan expansion and booming population growth. In gratitude for krogan service in the Rachni Wars, the Council had given them the conquered rachni worlds and several pristine habitable planets, but the krogan's high birth rate and lack of predators let them spread "like a plague" and annex territory from other Citadel races. When the krogan tried to settle the asari colony Lusia and refused Council demands to leave, their representative Overlord Kredak stormed out of the Council chambers daring them to take the worlds back, and war broke out.

The Council had pre-drafted STG operators and asari huntresses into the Office of Special Tactics and Reconnaissance, and the Spectres opened the war with crippling strikes: extranet viruses, sabotaged antimatter refineries, and headquarters stations rammed by pre-placed suicide freighters. But the krogan replenished losses easily and victory seemed inevitable, so the Council turned to the newly arrived turians, who established hidden command centers such as Pinnacle Station. The krogan retaliated by using asteroids to render three turian worlds uninhabitable, which only hardened turian resolve, since turian doctrine was to stop an enemy from ever becoming a threat again. The turians obtained the salarian-engineered genophage, intended by the salarians only as a deterrent, and deployed it in 710 CE, cutting successful krogan births to one in a thousand. The fertile female warlord Shiagur drew the strongest males to her band but was killed by turian peacekeepers at the Battle of Canrum, the last krogan push; vengeful krogan later hunted down the turians responsible. Besieged on all sides, the krogan surrendered, though rogue warlords fought on or became pirates. The turians were rewarded with a Council seat. The krogan remained deeply bitter, as Wrex put it, "to thank us for wiping out the rachni they neutered us all." A movement to remove the Krogan Monument failed. In 2185 a mass grave of about 430 turian soldiers from the Rebellions was uncovered on Uresium and repatriated to Palaven.
