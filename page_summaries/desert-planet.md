Desert Planet was a mission in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, following the Access Corridor mission. Randall Ezno stole a shuttle to reach a nearby desert planet, LV426, and entered a Cerberus outpost for a pickup arranged by the Systems Alliance. At the outpost he was trapped by the Cerberus Director, who deployed a heavily modified, huskified Inali Renata against him. She fought with an assault rifle and powerful biotics such as Salvo, and used a tactical cloak and fast movement to flank him, regenerating her barrier quickly when not taking damage.

Randall defeated Inali, and depending on the player's moral inclination he either spared her or killed her. Either way, he vowed to take revenge on the Cerberus Director who had experimented on his former handler and friend, and left the planet to pursue that goal and to provide the Alliance with intel on Cerberus activities. The planet's name is a reference to LV-426 from the first two Alien films.
