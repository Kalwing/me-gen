The Pariah was a mid-tier Outlaw enemy unit in Mass Effect: Andromeda, a rogue asari fighting for plunder and glory within Outlaw formations, typically appearing one or two at a time alongside groups of Raiders and Sharpshooters. Classified as a Milky Way enemy, she was a heavily armored close-range combatant who used biotics to create a shield and attempt to flank.

Pariahs fought at short range with Disciple shotguns designed for asari commando forces, wearing down kinetic barriers in preparation for a biotic attack. Many had mastered a "backlash" technique, generating a near-negative-mass aegis in front of them that accelerated incoming projectiles and particle beams back at attackers, though at a cost of reduced movement speed; the aegis did not block melee or attacks from the sides and rear. Tech attacks such as Overload and Energy Drain stripped their moderate shields and momentarily collapsed the Backlash aegis, staggering the Pariah. They were extremely aggressive and closed distance immediately, so falling back was advised. Prior to Patch 1.08, Pariahs had armor instead of health and shields.
