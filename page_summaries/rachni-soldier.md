Rachni Soldiers were a combat caste of the rachni, encountered by Commander Shepard's team during Mass Effect. They were much larger and slower than Rachni Workers, with thin tentacles ending in lobster-like claws. Soldiers used their bodies as weapons: they spat acid capable of inflicting heavy toxic damage comparable to a sniper round, and their tentacles could impale nearby victims. Their acid attacks bypassed shields and, against the Mako, caused direct hull damage. The page describes them as cunning ambush predators most at home in vents and tunnels, favoring the confined underground spaces where rachni nested.

Most of the page is combat guidance for players. Because Soldier attacks ignored shields, armor upgrades that increased damage protection, heavy armor, and the Immunity power were recommended defenses. The acid spit dealt physical damage plus high toxic damage; toxic damage did not directly drain health but blocked health recovery, so the advised remedy was to equip Medical Exoskeletons or high toxin-resistance upgrades after a fight, or to use First Aid, and swap back to protective upgrades afterward. A Medic Shepard with the First Aid specialization could use medi-gel freely even during combat. Biotic powers were effective at disabling Soldiers, knocking them onto their bellies for a few seconds; Singularity was singled out as strong against groups. Since Soldiers were dangerous in melee and closed distance quickly, engaging them at range and not lingering in cover was advised. Soldiers encountered while Shepard was in the Mako could be knocked down with the main cannon and finished with the machine gun, while keeping the vehicle moving to avoid acid hull damage. The page also references art-book and bonus-content depictions of the rachni.
