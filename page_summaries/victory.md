Victory was a Prothean VI that served as overseer of a bunker facility on Eden Prime. During the Reaper invasion 50,000 years before the present, Victory kept the base commander Javik apprised of ongoing threats. Prothean survivors gathered at the facility hoping to enter cryogenic stasis and emerge after the Reapers withdrew into dark space to rebuild the Prothean Empire.

As Collector ground troops closed in, Javik ordered Victory to broadcast the readiness signal to all lifepods. Victory reminded him of refugees still en route, which Javik dismissed as a necessary sacrifice. Victory reported that Reaper forces had destroyed roughly 300,000 lifepods, a third of the Protheans, and that the northside bulkhead could not be sealed. With the bunker about to be overrun, Victory ordered Javik into his stasis pod and triggered a neutron bombardment, denying his request for a few more minutes for his men to escape and using his own words against him: "Their sacrifice will be honored in the coming empire."

The blast destroyed the Reaper threat but damaged the base's sensors, making automatic reawakening impossible; the surviving Protheans could only be revived manually when a new culture discovered the facility, risking a power shortage to the stasis pods. Over Javik's protests, Victory said it would triage the base's power supply, and it eventually cut power to all pods except Javik's, appointing him the voice of their people before he entered stasis. Victory's ultimate fate is unknown; if Shepard reawakened Javik in 2186, Victory appeared only in flashbacks. Unlike other degraded Prothean VIs, Victory appeared as a holographic Prothean and could project anywhere in the facility. Whether, like Vigil and Vendetta, it was based on a real Prothean's personality imprint is unknown.
