BioWare: Stories and Secrets from 25 Years of Game Development was a book published in November 2020 by Dark Horse Comics LLC. It was written by Ben Gelinas with the assistance of a wide range of BioWare veterans and presented the history of the studio from its inception to the date of publication, focusing on concept art and anecdotes from every BioWare game, including cancelled projects. Among the studio's other games, the work revisited the creation and development of each of the Mass Effect games.

The book contained a foreword by Mass Effect trilogy director Casey Hudson, who was BioWare's general manager at the time of publication. It was divided into four parts: "Doctors Explore Forgotten Realms" (covering BioWare: Origins, Shattered Steel, Baldur's Gate, and MDK2); "Prairie Upstart Becomes Industry Legend" (Baldur's Gate II: Shadows of Amn, Neverwinter Nights, Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic, Jade Empire, Sonic Chronicles: The Dark Brotherhood, and Revolver); "From Ambition to Reality" (Mass Effect, Agent, BioWare Labs, Mass Effect 2, Dragon Age: Origins, and Dragon Age II); and "Exploring Thedas, the Milky Way, and Far Beyond" (Star Wars: The Old Republic, Mass Effect 3, Dragon Age: Inquisition, Shadow Realms, Mass Effect: Andromeda, Anthem, and closing sections on the future of BioWare and about the author).

The Mass Effect-related chapters were The Making of Mass Effect, BioWare Labs, The Making of Mass Effect 2, The Making of Mass Effect 3, and The Making of Mass Effect: Andromeda, transcriptions of which were made available. The book carried the print ISBN 978-1-50671-879-8 and ebook ISBN 978-1-50671-880-4. Its jacket copy described it as a quarter century of memories and milestones featuring never-before-seen art, photos, and design documents anchored by candid developer stories, including details of cancelled projects and the genesis of characters and games.
