This war-asset list collected the personnel, data and equipment that skilled operatives took with them when they abandoned Cerberus as the Illusive Man's methods grew more extreme. Equipment assets came from N7 missions: Cerberus Research Data (50), reports on Reaper unit composition used by Alliance military VIs, and an Advanced Fighter Squadron (75), fast fuel-efficient one-person craft captured on Noveria, repainted and pressed into Alliance service after being swept for bugs.

Personnel assets largely came from the Arrae: Ex-Cerberus Scientists mission. Ex-Cerberus Scientists (25) rescued from Gellix joined the Crucible project, as did their leader Dr. Brynn Cole (25), who had studied Collector technology and helped dozens of families flee Cerberus. Dr. Gavin Archer (25), a synthetic intelligence specialist from Terra Nova, contributed insights into parallel processing and the geth after David Archer was dealt with in Overlord and Grissom Academy was completed. Jacob Taylor (25) joined after an attack on a former-operative safehouse, working as a consultant on Cerberus tactics; he declined reinstatement into the Alliance marines.

Ex-Cerberus Engineers (10) were contacted by Kelly Chambers, who convinced former Cerberus engineers to come out of hiding and help build the Crucible. Miranda Lawson (25), the operative who led the Lazarus Cell that revived Shepard, brought extensive knowledge of the Illusive Man's operations to the Alliance. Cerberus Escapees (40-120, with a bug allowing 160), humans and aliens who broke out of a secret Cerberus holding facility, could be obtained only through the now-unavailable Mass Effect: Infiltrator mobile game.
