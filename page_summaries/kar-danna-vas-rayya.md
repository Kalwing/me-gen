Captain Kar'Danna vas Rayya was the quarian captain of the Rayya, Tali'Zorah's birth ship in the Migrant Fleet. In Mass Effect 2, after the Normandy docked with the Rayya, Captain Danna greeted Commander Shepard and Tali and explained the treason charges brought against Tali. On the orders of Admiral Shala'Raan vas Tonbay, he withheld the fact that Tali's father, Rael'Zorah, was unaccounted for and presumed lost aboard the Alarei.

If Legion was brought aboard, Danna was more hostile, with his security detail training weapons on Shepard's squad, and he insisted that neither geth nor Cerberus were welcome in the Flotilla. Shepard could persuade him to allow Legion aboard or leave the geth on the Normandy. Danna addressed Shepard as "Captain" despite the Commander no longer serving in the Alliance, reasoning that any commanding officer's decisions on their own ship carried great weight.

In Mass Effect 3, if the quarians lost their war against the geth over Rannoch, the entire Migrant Fleet, including the Rayya, was destroyed. The ship's final moments were heard as distress calls from Captain Danna, reporting that the Rayya was falling from orbit with its drive core and escape pods inoperative. This distress call was not heard if Admiral Shala'Raan vas Tonbay had substituted for Tali on Rannoch because Tali died during the Collector Base suicide mission.
