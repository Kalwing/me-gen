Brood was a one-eyed krogan member of a Blood Pack mercenary squad and second-in-command to Archuk, appearing in the animated film Mass Effect: Paragon Lost. He had the bloodlust typical of krogan but also a personal code that compelled him to honor his debts in a warrior's way.

In 2183 the Blood Pack attacked the human colony of Fehl Prime. Brood destroyed the colony's kinetic barriers by throwing a satchel bomb, then joined the ground charge. When Alliance shuttles arrived, Brood crawled from his wrecked vehicle and helped shoot the shuttles down with missile launchers, killing most of the Marines. The attack was blunted by an Alliance strike team; Brood grazed a human sniper's neck and, on Archuk's order, targeted a tech-drone operator. After Archuk was killed, Brood shot down the biotic Essex and pinned him, but Lieutenant James Vega incapacitated Brood with a sidearm. Vega chose to spare Brood and take him prisoner rather than let Essex execute him.

Two years later, during the Collector attack on Fehl Prime, the remnants of Delta Squad found Brood held in a containment tank in the colony's underground research labs, kept as a test subject because he was deemed too dangerous to transport to the Citadel for trial. While fleeing a Praetorian, Vega freed Brood, who repaid the debt by offering his hidden private ship, "Garm's Way," and its arsenal of heavy weapons. Brood proposed flying straight through the Collector ship's weapon ports. He piloted the vessel into the Collector ship and blundered toward its drive core, but the Cerberus operative Messner shot him repeatedly in the head, destroying his remaining eye and causing the ship to crash; Delta Squad was captured. Mortally wounded but still moving, Brood later thanked Vega for letting him die a warrior's death and gave him his last power cell before dying. Vega gave him a Spanish benediction, "vaya con Dios, hermano."
