An optional N7 assignment in Mass Effect 2, available after Freedom's Progress, and additionally given by Aria T'Loak on Omega after Shepard helped her with the Archangel datapad and the Patriarch, or acquired by scanning the planet. An Eclipse smuggling depot was detected on the planet Daratar, Faryar system, Hourglass Nebula, and Cerberus offered to pay handsomely for any intact cargo crates recovered, warning that Eclipse would rather destroy the cargo than lose it to a rival.

When the Normandy SR-2 was detected in orbit, the Eclipse mercenaries triggered a "Farewell Protocol": they fled the depot in A-61 Mantis Gunships, leaving three YMIR Mechs behind with orders to destroy the 20 cargo crates before Shepard's squad could recover them. The squad had to destroy all three mechs before they finished destroying the crates; payment scaled to the number of crates saved. A mech could only be pulled onto the squad one at a time, and once a mech finished its assigned crates it turned on the squad. Only mech fire could damage the countable crates.

Strategies to save all 20 crates included killing the mechs in a specific order, hitting all three at once with a single M-920 Cain shot before they spread out, or "pulling" each mech individually with the ML-77 Missile Launcher without triggering the crate-destruction script. Rewards were experience, 500 element zero and 3,750–7,500 credits depending on surviving crates. A follow-up email from Cerberus Command ("Nice Work on Daratar") confirmed the credits had been wired.
