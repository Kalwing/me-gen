Humans, from the planet Earth, were the newest sentient species of notable size to enter the galactic stage and the most rapidly expanding and developing. They discovered a Prothean data cache on Mars in 2148 and the mass relay network shortly after. Physiologically robust bipedal mammals, humans were roughly on par with turians in unarmed combat, less agile than the closely-resembling asari, and stronger but slower than salarians. Human biotics resulted solely from pre-natal element zero exposure, which carried high medical risk. Humans lived to about 150 years, reached maturity around 18, and had eradicated almost all diseases. They were noted for unusually high genetic diversity, making their genetic material useful as an experimental control group; recessive traits such as blonde and red hair and blue and green eyes had grown rarer through centuries of ethnic mixing.

Human spaceflight began in earnest in the late 21st century: Armstrong Outpost on Luna (2069), Lowell City on Mars (2103), and Gagarin Station near Pluto. The privately-funded Manswell Expedition launched in 2075 with 300 cryogenically frozen colonists, was lost, and was rediscovered alive in Alpha Centauri in 2186. The 2148 Mars discovery let explorers open the Charon Relay to Arcturus; the fledgling Systems Alliance drove expansion. The First Contact War began in 2157 when turians attacked a human fleet activating a dormant relay and occupied Shanxi; Admiral Kastanie Drescher's Second Fleet retook the colony, and the Citadel Council brokered peace, making the Alliance humanity's supranational governing body. Humanity gained a Citadel embassy in 2165. Expansion into the Skyllian Verge brought lasting conflict with the batarians, who became a rogue state. The geth attack on Eden Prime in 2183 began the Eden Prime War, culminating in the Battle of the Citadel, after which humanity gained new prominence and, depending on Shepard's choices, a Council seat. Terminus colonies vanished to Collector abductions around 2185. In 2186 the Reapers invaded through batarian space, overran Earth, and began the harvest that killed millions before Shepard united the galaxy and the Crucible retook Earth.

Culturally, humans were seen as intelligent, ambitious, adaptable, individualistic and unpredictable, with military prowess among the galaxy's greatest despite only 3% volunteering for Alliance service. The Systems Alliance, based at Arcturus Station, governed most humans, though independent fringe colonies, Earth nation-states and the corporate-linked Union of Incorporated Nations (UNIN) also held power.
