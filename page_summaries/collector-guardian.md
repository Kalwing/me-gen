Collector Guardians were elite Collector combatants in Mass Effect 2, distinct from the Cerberus Guardian unit of Mass Effect 3. They carried a more powerful, burst-fire variant of the Collector Assault Rifle, similar in mode to the M-15 Vindicator, that could deplete shields in a single burst on higher difficulties, and like other Collector troops they had wings often used to drop into combat. Guardians had strong biotic barriers and could also deploy circular yellow energy shields resembling the geth Hex Shields, which blocked most conventional weapon fire and directly-hit projectile powers but could be broken by sustained damage; Guardians could shoot out through their own shields. Rapid-fire weapons and shotguns destroyed the deployable shields quickly, while hitscan and arcing powers such as Reave, Overload, Energy Drain, Neural Shock, Warp, and Incinerate, plus Stasis, Flashbang Grenade, Inferno Grenade, the Collector Particle Beam, the Arc Projector, and large-area-of-effect heavy weapons like the M-920 Cain and M-490 Blackstorm, could bypass or collapse them. Like all Collector soldiers, Guardians could be possessed by Harbinger.
