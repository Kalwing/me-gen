Freedom's Progress was a human colony in the Terminus Systems, built on blocky terrain with a night sky dominated by two moons and home to nocturnal lifeforms whose cries resembled Earth wolves. It was a typical human settlement with minimal defenses beyond a small military force supplemented by mechs and security drones, including LOKI Mechs, YMIR Mechs, FENRIS Mechs, Assault Drones, and Rocket Drones. Collector data recorded a population of 912,810 in 2183.

Two years later, the Collectors attacked the colony. Commander Shepard arrived several hours after they left and found the entire population missing with no signs of conflict. On the colony Shepard encountered Tali'Zorah vas Neema, Prazza, and other quarians searching for Veetor'Nara, a quarian who had gone missing during his Pilgrimage. Veetor, made paranoid by suit damage and infection, had reprogrammed the colony's security drones to attack anyone in the area in case the Collectors returned. Veetor had recorded the abduction on his omni-tool, giving the Illusive Man proof that the Collectors were behind attacks on multiple human colonies.

News of the disappearance eventually reached the wider galaxy, though details remained scant and were redacted by sources such as the Systems Alliance. Rumors circulated that it might have been a politically motivated terrorist attack by former Council races, prompting the Citadel Council to make formal information requests to the Special Tasks Group. Veetor's footage of the Collectors abducting humans reached the Shadow Broker and could be seen on screens at the Intel Center in the Shadow Broker Base. Aid was dispatched to the colony, though it was unclear whether any survivors remained, and raiders and pirates staked out the routes leading there.
