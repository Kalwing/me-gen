Tasale was a star system described as a gateway in more than one sense: it connected to the rest of its cluster and served as a crossing point between the Asari Republics and the Terminus Systems. Its principal feature was the strategically important asari trade world Illium, the second planet orbiting the star. The system's worlds rated moderate to rich in resources needed by the Normandy SR-2, especially palladium, though most deposits went untapped by commercial interests. Helium-3 mining infrastructure was being built on Naxell to service Illium, hampered by cutthroat competition from outside the cluster. The system contained a fuel depot and a mass relay.

The planets orbiting Tasale were Beregale (first), Illium (second), Ponolus (third), Teukria (fourth, present only in Mass Effect 3), Thail (fifth), and Naxell (sixth). Illium was the hub for numerous Mass Effect 2 missions and assignments, including the recruitment of Thane Krios and Samara, Miranda's loyalty mission, and Liara's confrontation with the Observer. In Mass Effect 3, access required completing Horizon in Mass Effect 2 and the Leviathan mission Find Garneau.
