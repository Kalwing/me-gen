This overview page collected the various turret emplacements across the Mass Effect series. Turrets were generally stationary heavy artillery posts guarding bases and positions on remote worlds, equipped with shields and firing rockets at hostiles in range; the rockets moved slowly enough to dodge but could badly damage Shepard's squad or the Mako. They could be temporarily sabotaged, hacked or disabled by technical skills.

In the original Mass Effect these included Alliance Heavy Turrets (roof-mounted in groups of three, vulnerable to simultaneous sabotage or being hacked to destroy each other), Geth Turrets and Geth Heavy Turrets, Mercenary and Pirate Turrets hidden in surrounding hills, the Heavy Turrets around Asteroid X57's fusion torches, unmarked training positions on Luna, the generator-powered listening-post turrets that could be wired to the Mako in UNC: Listening Post Alpha, and the Citadel Defense Turrets, which Shepard could hack to fire on a Geth Dropship. In Mass Effect 2, Cerberus Turrets defended Project Overlord outposts using pop-up automatic mass accelerator cannons, and the unique Geth Cannon guarded Prometheus Station on Aite, powerful enough to destroy a Hammerhead in one shot. In Mass Effect 3, Turrets were portable emplacements deployed by Cerberus Combat Engineers with heavy shields, armor and a rapid rate of fire. In Mass Effect Galaxy, Turrets were immobile synthetic enemies with heavy firepower; in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, Grad Turrets were ceiling-hidden pulse-fire defenses in the Barn; and in Mass Effect: Andromeda, Turrets were units fielded by the kett, outlaws and Remnant.
