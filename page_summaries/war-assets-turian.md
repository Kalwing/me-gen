This war-asset list collected the Turian Hierarchy's fleets and soldiers, needed to fight the largest war in galactic history. The 79th Flotilla (40), distinguished at Digeris during the Krogan Rebellions, relied on speed and stealth to harass enemy ships. The Sixth Fleet (135), the largest concentration of dreadnoughts of any known species and normally a Citadel peacekeeping fleet, was unlocked by Priority: The Citadel II; choosing the Volus Bombing Fleet over saving the colony in Citadel: Volus Ambassador cost it 10. The Seventh Fleet (90) was assigned to guard the Crucible during its construction and deployment; recovering the Banner of the First Regiment added 40, and shipping dextro medi-gel added 5.

Ground assets included the 43rd Marine Division (90), founded during Palaven's Unification Wars and experienced in joint operations with allied militaries; the Turian Engineering Corps (110), which had repaired Sovereign's damage to the Citadel in record time and viewed the Crucible as a career challenge; and Turian Blackwatch (75), an elite special-operations force pulled from Palaven by Primarch Adrien Victus, with rumors that Saren Arterius had served in it before becoming a Spectre. The Turian Spec Ops Team (40) under Guard Captain Vidinos had hunted separatist leaders during the War on Taetrus, where separatists rammed an FTL ship into the capital. The Leviathan DLC added Dextro Rations (10) from the agribusinesses of Triginta Petra, favored by turian and quarian troops and useful for restoring squad morale.
