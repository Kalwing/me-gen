Maya Brooks was a former Cerberus operative who left the organization to pursue her own pro-human agenda. "Maya Brooks" was an admitted alias; she also used the names "Captain Channing", "Hope Lilium" and "Rasa". She specialized in infiltration and espionage, using numerous distractions to cover her real objectives.

In Mass Effect: Foundation, her backstory was told. As a child in 2160-2161 CE she was a forced laborer in an asteroid mine near Themis, disguised as a boy by a handler named Brocktun, whom she eventually stabbed to death. She encountered a lone Cerberus agent called Brooks, followed and aided her, then shot her in the back when refused passage, stealing her ship and identity. Years later she joined Cerberus, telling the Illusive Man she "wanted to stand for something too." As "Rasa" she manipulated Fist and Urdnot Wrex on the Citadel, interviewed Ashley Williams as "Captain Channing" after Eden Prime, and assembled the dossiers on Commander Shepard's crew. She worked with Kai Leng on failed missions to capture Jack, then joined Miranda Lawson on the Lazarus Project, where her intelligence on Shepard proved invaluable. Tela Vasir compromised her with a virus-laden disc, and Thane Krios drugged and then spared her. She ultimately stole Shepard's clone from the Lazarus Research Station and fled, vowing to make the clone more loyal than the original.

In Mass Effect 3: Citadel, Brooks approached Shepard in Ryuusei's sushi bar posing as an Alliance Staff Analyst, warning of a breach of Shepard's classified files by her tracking program "Mr. Biscuits". After a mercenary ambush she guided Shepard through C-Sec lockdown and joined the Normandy crew at Shepard's apartment. She helped investigate CAT6, casino owner Elijah Khan (whom she secretly shot) and the Citadel Archives, where the identity thief was revealed as Shepard's Cerberus-made clone. Brooks was exposed as the clone's accomplice; she had orchestrated the whole plot to steal Shepard's Spectre code and kill the real Shepard, disgusted that Cerberus recruited aliens. She sealed the crew in iridium vaults to suffocate and helped seize the Normandy SR-2, but Glyph enabled their escape. During the final fight she abandoned the clone to its death and was arrested by Steve Cortez. Depending on Shepard's dialogue she either stood down or attempted to flee and was shot. She never became a War Asset. The names reference "tabula rasa" (clean slate) and Sanskrit "maya" (illusion).
