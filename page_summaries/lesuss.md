Lesuss was the first planet of the Mesana System in the Milky Way's Nimbus Cluster, a garden world just outside the comfort zone of its asari population: its gravity was slightly too high, its diseases too virulent, and its soil too poor for growing food. Although it had been settled long ago, its night side showed little light pollution, and the asari government was uncharacteristically silent about it. It lay far from regularly traveled trade routes and was inhabited only by a small asari colony.

The world's main feature was a monastery housing a large population of asari with active or latent Ardat-Yakshi genes, a disorder that killed anyone who mated with the afflicted asari. Some colonists had exiled themselves voluntarily, but many were sent to Lesuss by their families to protect society from their deadly potential. The monastery emphasized individual sacrifice for the good of the community. Latent Ardat-Yakshi, and active sufferers who demonstrated they could control their condition, could be offered a chance to reintegrate into asari society after sufficient time on Lesuss, while active Ardat-Yakshi whose psychological profiles showed capacity for neither empathy nor reeducation were confined to the monastery for life.

During the Reaper War, Asari High Command lost contact with several commando squads sent to Lesuss, prompting Commander Shepard to investigate. The monastery, home to Samara's daughters Falere and Rila, had been attacked by Reaper forces converting its residents into banshees. Lesuss featured in the missions Kallini: Ardat-Yakshi Monastery and Citadel: Asari Widow.
