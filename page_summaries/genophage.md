The genophage was a biological weapon deployed against the krogan by the turians during the Krogan Rebellions to sharply reduce krogan numbers through an introduced genetic mutation. Rather than reducing female fertility, it reduced the probability of viable pregnancies: most fetuses never developed nervous systems and many krogan died in stillbirth. Every cell in a krogan was infected to prevent gene therapy from countering it. The weapon was developed by the salarians, who intended it as an undeployed deterrent, believing its consequences too terrible to use; the turians, favoring "massive retaliation," deployed it immediately upon completion. Krogan numbers dwindled rapidly, fertile females became prizes of war, and the warlord Shiagur used her fertility to attract the strongest males to her army. After centuries, awareness of impending extinction bred a dangerous fatalism among the krogan. There was no known cure; some krogan invested in companies such as Binary Helix, and a black-market trade in krogan testicles arose from the unsubstantiated belief that transplants could counteract the effects.

In Mass Effect, Saren Arterius developed a genophage cure, possibly aided by Binary Helix or Sovereign, and used it to breed a krogan army on Virmire. Shepard and Captain Kirrahe destroyed the facility and the cure with a nuclear weapon, potentially causing a rift with Wrex. In Mass Effect 2, Mordin Solus explained that the krogan had been evolving resistance, so he and STG colleagues engineered a new genophage variant to hold population growth at pre-industrial levels. Mordin's loyalty mission revealed his former student Maelon, kidnapped by Blood Pack krogan on Tuchanka, working on a cure amid dead test subjects (including humans and a female krogan); Maelon claimed to work willingly and feel no guilt. Shepard could destroy or keep his data.

In Mass Effect 3, STG recovered surviving krogan females and brought them to Sur'Kesh, where only "Eve" survived. Cerberus sought to preserve the genophage to prevent a krogan-turian alliance. With Maelon's research, a cure could be dispersed through the Shroud facility on Tuchanka. Dalatrass Linron revealed STG had sabotaged the Shroud and offered salarian support if Shepard concealed this. Mordin (or Padok Wiks if Mordin died) overrode the sabotage and sacrificed himself to release the cure. If Shepard hid the sabotage, they could let Mordin proceed or shoot him to make the cure fail; if Wreav led and Eve was dead, Mordin could be talked out of it and fake his death. A Citadel Archives document dated 710 CE showed turians activating the Shroud over the objection of a lone salarian who called it genocide. In Mass Effect: Andromeda, krogan colonists departed still afflicted but with a natural resistance mutation, bolstered by gene therapy during cryosleep, raising offspring survival from 0.1% to 4%.
