Opold was a hanar shopkeeper in the Port Hanshan Plaza on Noveria in 2183. He sold basic supplies, upgrades, non-human armor, and armory licenses for the Elkoss Combine and Rosenkov Materials.

Opold asked Commander Shepard to carry a package through Port Hanshan's security systems, exploiting the fact that Shepard's Spectre status meant the alarms would not be triggered by its contents, a weapons mod. He was anxious because the package had already been delayed and its recipient, a short-tempered krogan named Inamorda, was growing impatient. Opold paid Shepard for the help, and the Commander could pressure him for a larger payout. If Shepard instead traded with Inamorda directly or reported Opold to Administrator Anoleis, the hanar became angry but still traded with Shepard grudgingly.

Shepard could also keep the weapons mod, in which case Inamorda and a gang of krogan ambushed the Commander outside the SSV Normandy. Afterward Opold was unconcerned, saying the mod no longer mattered and that the galaxy was better off without Inamorda.
