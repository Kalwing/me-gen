Citadel: Inspirational Stories was available after Priority: The Citadel II. Film director Solik Vass, barred along with his camera drone from the refugee camp by a turian border guard who deemed him disruptive, asked Shepard at the Docks Holding Area security checkpoint to take morale-boosting photographs for a war-effort film. The mission was granted even if Shepard refused. Of eight photo opportunities in the docks, four were suitable and three were needed: a salarian consoling a grieving human woman at the Memorial Wall, a woman talking with a human or batarian man between Bays E24 and E26, two doctors tending patients on crates in Cargo Hold B, and James playing cards with three others between Bays E26 and E28. The four unsuitable spots involved crates and turian security officers. Returning the footage to Vass gave 5 Reputation, 210 experience, and 5,000 credits. Bugs could cause photo prompts, particularly at the card game, to disappear after taking unsuitable shots or after saving and reloading.
