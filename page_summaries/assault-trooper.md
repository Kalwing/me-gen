Assault Troopers were the standard rank-and-file soldiers of Cerberus, encountered throughout Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Infiltrator. They were described as the backbone of Cerberus forces: recruits who survived a grueling basic training were then put through an intensive psychological conditioning program that left them fearless, disciplined, and unrelenting. They were issued custom-designed armor and rifles and were trained to fight with practiced teamwork, typically rushing into an engagement first and keeping opponents occupied while heavier Cerberus units closed in.

In Mass Effect 3 the Assault Trooper functioned as Cerberus cannon fodder. They carried M-25 Hornet submachine guns and electrified batons for melee strikes, and they threw slow-fuse frag grenades to kill enemies or force them from cover. In multiplayer they could execute downed players with a stomp. They had moderate health and no shields, relying on cover and combat rolls to survive, which made them vulnerable to almost any combination of attacks, biotic explosions set up with Singularity, Pull, or Stasis, and area powers such as Shockwave or a Vanguard's Nova. Their grenades, which bypassed shield and health gate protection on higher difficulties, were considered their most dangerous weapon.

In Mass Effect: Infiltrator, Assault Troopers were presented as elite Cerberus infantry that Randall Ezno faced on the Barn during his escape. That version wore the same armor as regular troopers but was more resilient, carried regenerating shields, wielded M-22 Eviscerator shotguns, and used jetpacks rather than jetboots to close distance or flank; like other humanoid foes they took bonus damage from headshots.

According to The Art of the Mass Effect Universe, the developers deliberately made Assault Trooper armor look more robust than earlier Cerberus designs to convey that these were battle-hardened veterans. The Codex stated their rifles were custom-made, and promotional images depicted them with weapons such as the M-96 Mattock, M-12 Locust, and M-22 Eviscerator, though in Mass Effect 3 they only ever wielded the M-25 Hornet.
