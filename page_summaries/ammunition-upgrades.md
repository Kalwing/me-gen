Ammunition Upgrades were weapon modifications in the first Mass Effect designed to enhance the effectiveness of a weapon's ammunition. They dropped as loot and were sold by merchants; in the original game many lower-tier types stopped dropping at higher player levels, while in Mass Effect Legendary Edition all types dropped throughout the game. Each type came in ten upgrade levels, with a given named mod covering a range of those levels.

Anti-organic ammunition (Anti-Personnel Rounds and Shredder Rounds) gave a damage bonus against organic enemies with no downside, useful against rachni and Thorian Creepers. Anti-armor/synthetic ammunition (Armor Piercing Rounds and Tungsten Rounds), derived from dense tungsten, dealt extra damage to synthetics such as the geth; Tungsten VII outperformed any level X mod against synthetics, making it valuable through the endgame. Incendiary ammunition (Incendiary Rounds and Inferno Rounds) applied burning damage over five seconds and an accuracy penalty, and could stop krogan health regeneration. Cold ammunition (Cryo Rounds and Snowblind Rounds) provided shield bypass or a damage bonus with accuracy and rate-of-fire penalties; Snowblind's slow fire kept weapons from overheating but barely raised damage per second. Explosive ammunition (High Explosive Rounds) added blast radius, damage, and weapon force at the cost of a large increase in overheating. High-impact ammunition (Hammerhead Rounds and Sledgehammer Rounds) increased knockback; Sledgehammer also carried polonium to cause radiation sickness and block regeneration. Shield-piercing ammunition (Phasic Rounds and Proton Rounds) released charged particle bolts that bypassed kinetic barriers but did less damage. Toxic ammunition (Chemical Rounds and Polonium Rounds) added a toxin that had to be "healed through" before real healing occurred, plus damage over time. Anti-biotic/tech ammunition (Radioactive Rounds) slowed a target's ability cooldowns.

Cut content, accessible only through PC cheats, included Harpoon Rounds, which condensed on impact to penetrate armor then shredded into shrapnel and were considered unethical, and an unnamed "X" mod. Internal data also listed alternative names "Hollow Point Ammo" for anti-organic ammunition and "Field Jacketed" for high-impact ammunition.
