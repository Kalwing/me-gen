The Collector Chitin Armor was a non-customizable bonus armor in Mass Effect 2, known simply as Collector Armor in Mass Effect 3. Cerberus adapted the suit for Commander Shepard from salvaged Collector technology; the Collectors' chitinous armor was described as flexible and tougher than ballistic fibers, self-healing due to its organic construction, and made comfortable to wear despite its weight by muscle-like tissue that assisted movement. In Mass Effect 2 it granted +10% regeneration rate, +10% storm speed, and +20% health, and was distributed through the Collectors' Weapon and Armor and Recon Operations Pack DLC with an e-mail from the Illusive Man announcing that Cerberus researchers had built an experimental armor suit and a matching thermal-clip assault rifle and delivered them to the Normandy's armory; in Legendary Edition it could be researched in the Normandy's Tech Lab for 10,000 palladium. It offered no weapon or power damage bonuses, making it best suited to Soldiers, Vanguards, and Sentinels who took heavy fire. In Mass Effect 3 it boosted health and shields by 20%, was described as incredibly difficult to find on the galactic market, and could be bought at Nos Astra Sporting Goods in the Presidium Commons for 50,000 credits without discounts or through Procurement terminals in the Normandy's shuttle bay or the Armax Arsenal Arena; it worked well combined with a defensive buff such as Barrier, Fortification, or the Sentinel's Tech Armor.
