Mass Effect: Invasion was a four-issue comic mini-series published by Dark Horse Comics. It followed Aria T'Loak, the Pirate Queen of Omega, as her station came under attack from a new threat unleashed by the human-survivalist organization Cerberus. The series was written by BioWare lead writer Mac Walters, scripted by John Jackson Miller, and drawn by Omar Francia, with cover designs by Massimo Carnevale and Paul Renaud. A limited edition of issue #1, with unique cover art, was included in the Mass Effect 3 N7 Collector's Edition. On April 4, 2012, Dark Horse released a trade paperback collecting all four issues alongside Mass Effect: Conviction. In May 2021, BioWare included the Collectors' Edition cover version of issue #1 in a bonus content download featuring items from the Deluxe versions of Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, available only until May 31, 2021 or until the download limit was reached.
