Lash was a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes. It latched a biotic field onto an enemy and jerked the target toward the user, dealing damage in the process, with a base recharge of 6 seconds, 150 damage and 1200 N of force. Its evolutions improved recharge speed and damage, added a detonation-boost option, a damage-over-time effect, a chance to skip cooldown, and a Shield Penetration rank that lifted unarmored targets through shields and barriers with reduced force.

The power carried a significant force rating that scaled with its damage, capable of flinging unshielded, unarmored enemies such as husks out of many multiplayer maps for instant kills or causing large physics-based collision damage. It could stagger opponents as heavy as Banshees and Scions, saving allies from sync kills. Phantoms resisted it by stabilizing with their armor rockets, and Geth Hunters were not forced to decloak, though the damage-over-time evolution could reveal them. Its direct damage landed before the force effect, so destroying a target's shields with the hit would fling them at full force. In single-player Lash detonated targets primed for power combos; in multiplayer it could not detonate armored targets. Its casting animation was long, leaving the user exposed, and dodging before impact cancelled the projectile. It was available in single-player to Aria T'Loak and to Shepard as a bonus power, and in multiplayer to the Batarian Brawler Vanguard, Batarian Slasher Adept, Project Phoenix Adept and Project Phoenix Vanguard.
