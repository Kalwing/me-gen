Inali Renata was the initial Cerberus handler of Infiltrator Randall Ezno in Mass Effect: Infiltrator. She had been with Cerberus for some time, sharing Randall's posting at the Barn installation, and guided his missions over comms. She secretly maintained discreet Alliance ties and collaborated with a volus prisoner at the Barn, entrusting him with critical information to pass to Randall should anything happen to her.

In 2186, after Randall's successful test mission, Inali directed him to her office for medical diagnostics as the Barn came under assault by turian forces. She was overheard greeting someone in her office when her feed went dead; by the time Randall reached the office it was empty. The Director of the Barn told Randall that Inali had "volunteered" for a classified project. In reality the Director had her abducted and operated on. Randall witnessed her undergoing surgery in a secure medical bay, mechanized arms stabbing and cutting into her, which turned him against Cerberus. The volus guided Randall to her location and afterward directed him to a comm relay to contact the Alliance. The Director taunted Randall over holoscreens, claiming Inali was becoming "addicted" to the experiments, and killed operatives reported she had cried out for Randall during the initial operation.

The Director transformed Inali into a husk-like form retaining half a will of her own; she could still speak but could be manually controlled by the Director. As the game's final boss she had the strongest defenses in the game, heavy armor, regenerating biotic barriers, a charging melee attack, a homing Salvo biotic identical to Randall's, an M-96 Mattock Auto, and a repositioning cloak. If Randall defeated her he either killed her or spared her in her twisted form; in later releases this choice was tied to his dominant morality rather than left to the player. Either way, Randall begged her forgiveness before leaving.
