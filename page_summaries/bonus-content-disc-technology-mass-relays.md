The page transcribed art director Derek Watts' gallery commentary for "The Technology of Mass Effect: Mass Relays," from the 2007 Mass Effect bonus content disc. Watts explained that the game needed a device to transport ships quickly across the galaxy, called a mass relay, and that early ideas placed it behind or even buried within Pluto, to be dug up.

He described progressively refined concepts: a carrier-deck structure with a central passage, sketches of mechanical systems with a rotating disc between blades that the Normandy would pass through, and a Max model animating a ship being fired through a large pad flanked by rotating discs. The team reused a rejected concept originally drawn for the asari flagship Destiny Ascension as the basis for the relay.

The near-final concept placed the structure horizontally with large blades and a rotating central section generating an energy force, influenced by the rotating rings in the film Contact; two oval rotating elements would create an energy field that grabs the Normandy and launches it down the relay's length. Watts noted an advantage of this design: the ship could emerge anywhere in the vicinity of the destination relay rather than directly from a single point, addressing designers' concern that military vessels would otherwise camp the exit and destroy anything arriving. The final version included a concept of the launch effect and energy field.
