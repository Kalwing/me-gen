Captain Preitor Gavorn was a turian hired by Aria T'Loak to keep the vorcha out of the Afterlife Club on Omega. He stood guard near the club entrance armed with an M-97 Viper, and could be heard in an in-station advertisement for "vorcha removal."

A vorcha named Shisk, loitering beneath Afterlife with several others, mentioned Gavorn to Commander Shepard, telling the Commander to inform Gavorn that they would "kill no one today" and that they knew his tricks.

After the vorcha threat was dealt with, Shepard could speak to Gavorn, who coyly asked whether the Commander had anything to do with the many dead vorcha. Shepard could either note that his job had gotten easier or deny any knowledge; either way Gavorn told the Commander to "keep up the good work." Asked about vorcha plans, he laughed and dismissed the species as a "pestilence," saying that if the vorcha could actually plan, everyone would be in trouble. He added that despite the current lull he would soon have work again, given how quickly the vorcha bred. Gavorn had some dialogue that was cut from the game.
