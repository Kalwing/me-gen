Multiplayer map page. Firebase White is a Mass Effect 3 multiplayer map located in a Cerberus base on Noveria in Alliance space. Its in-game description ties it to defending the Horse Head Nebula, whose mass relay bridges the human Exodus Cluster to the hanar homeworld of Kahje. In single-player, the Galaxy at War mission N7: Cerberus Fighter Base takes place here. Mass Effect 3: Retaliation added a Hazard version set during a whiteout that reduces outdoor visibility (making white-colored Cerberus troops especially hard to see); without Retaliation an original version with fewer level connections is used. Its areas are the Landing Pad (start and extraction), Stairs, Upper Level, Lower Level, Hallway, Interior, and Launch Ramp. The corner in the back doorway between the Hallway and Upper Level is called the most reliable solo camping spot in the game, and White is the most popular map for soloing Platinum difficulty.
