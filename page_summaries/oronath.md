Oronath was a character cut during development of Mass Effect and not considered canon. He was to be Throw Down's medical doctor on Caleston. His office on Throw Down Plaza was small and poorly equipped because the original medical bay had been bought by Smiley for a private residence; after a deal with the station owner, the Eldfell-Ashland Energy corporation, Oronath was relocated to a much smaller building.

Oronath could still provide Shepard medical assistance, though he often apologized for his inadequate equipment. Asked about Liara T'Soni, he said he had not seen her for some time and might deliver a long monologue about the religion of the Enkindlers and Prothean ruins. During a job for Fred Mazzei, Shepard could come to collect money Oronath owed the Union and find him amid a dilemma: Union and Cartel men had gathered demanding he treat their own lethally wounded man rather than the rival gang's. Shepard could pick a side, making the other hostile, or use Charm or Intimidate to get everyone to accept that Oronath could save both, for which he was grateful. Oronath claimed to have no money; Shepard could pay the 100 credits he owed or have him provide medi-gel packs to sell to Kar Kath. After the Union-Cartel conflict ended and Smiley was eliminated, Shepard could tell Oronath he was free to return to the real medical bay, for which he was very grateful.

As a hanar, Oronath would have had two unique traits: blue coloring rather than the usual purple, and a female-sounding voice, though everyone on Caleston referred to him with male pronouns.
