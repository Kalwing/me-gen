Dekuuna: Code of the Ancients was a side mission in Mass Effect 3. It became available after Priority: The Citadel II, acquired by overhearing an angry elcor standing just outside the Purgatory elevator on the Citadel, who needed the elcor holy book, the Code of the Ancients, to inspire his people during the Reaper War. Alternatively, if the player never approached Purgatory after the failed Cerberus coup, the item and journal entry could be obtained through a Search and Rescue operation, in which case the mission was labeled Citadel: Code of the Ancients.

The Code of the Ancients was located on the elcor homeworld Dekuuna, in the Phontes system of the Silean Nebula, a system that only became accessible after Priority: Rannoch. Scanning the planet retrieved the book regardless of how the mission was acquired. Returning it to the elcor by the Purgatory elevator completed the mission, awarding 5 Reputation and 15,000 credits and updating the Citadel Defense Force War Asset.
