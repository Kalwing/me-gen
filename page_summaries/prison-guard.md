Prison Guards were enemies encountered during the Mass Effect 2 assignment Arrival, found throughout the batarian prison on Aratoht. During the initial stage of the assignment Commander Shepard could avoid them by sneaking past, but freeing Dr. Amanda Kenson set off an alarm that alerted the guards to the break-out.

Offensively they were comparable to generic enemy troopers, armed with the M-8 Avenger for suppressing fire and equipped with Flashbang Grenades that forced Shepard out of cover, damaged shields and caused temporary blindness. Defensively they had only weak shielding, and only on higher difficulty levels. Because Shepard fought them alone, they relied on numbers to flank and overwhelm, and used their grenades heavily on harder difficulties; the fighting areas offered ample cover to reposition around the grenades.
