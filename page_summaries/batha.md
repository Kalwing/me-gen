Batha was an asari scientist specializing in biosciences and vaccinations, appearing in Mass Effect Galaxy. She worked at a settlement near the Batarian Fringe in the Nemean Abyss and was good friends with the krogan Nax, who effectively served as her bodyguard. When Batha went missing, Nax immediately set out to find her.

Batha, along with other scientists including Doctor Hendricks, had been kidnapped by batarians and taken to a research facility on the Ahn'Kedar Orbital Platform. She was told the batarians were suffering from a terrible blood plague that had devastated entire batarian populations and, moved by their plight, agreed to help create a vaccine. She did not realize the "blood plague" was actually a bioweapon the batarians intended to unleash on the Citadel.

While Batha worked in a separate facility on a prototype formula, her colleagues' labs came under assault by Jacob Taylor, who fought through the batarian captors. Jacob provided a distraction so that Nax, with whom he had joined forces, could blast through to Batha and carry her out on his shoulders. After Jacob eliminated the pursuing batarians, Batha thanked him, to Nax's dismay. She said she was close to completing the vaccine but that it was not ready; she could get closer with items from the sick bay of Jacob's ship, but the final vaccine also required a large amount of unrefined element zero, which Miranda had a lead on at a stockpile on Bekke. Batha eventually completed the formula in time to cure Illo Nazario and to inoculate the rest of the crew against the batarian bioweapon. She later used it to inoculate exposed victims on the Citadel.
