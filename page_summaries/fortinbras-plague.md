The Fortinbras Plague was a highly virulent artificial contagion that threatened to kill everyone aboard the quarian-led ark Keelah Si'yah roughly 30 years before its scheduled arrival in Andromeda. The elcor doctor and Shakespeare enthusiast Yorrik named it after the Hamlet character because it "always swarms in the end and destroys it all." It was a chimera virus assembled from traits of many diseases affecting Council and non-Council species, highly mutagenic, classified in the Metastolizomai family, transmissible by air and contact, with an R-nought of 22 to 26. Its main shape came from the volus disease Yoqtan, with RNA payload sequences drawn from Asari Cyanophage, Ayalon B, Titan's Tears, and measles, plus dormant "junk" sequences from a dozen sources including Kepral's Syndrome and Ardat-Yakshi. Symptoms were similar across species: projectile vomiting, fever, a sweet body odor, bluish sores and fistulas, ravenous hunger, throat and chest swelling, and terminal hallucinations, insanity, or rage from brain swelling, with death about 18 hours after observable infection in a batarian victim. Infected volus exploded into blue liquid inside their suits.

The plague was created by the ark's own captain, Qetsi'Olam vas Keelah Si'yah, and smuggled aboard in canisters ostensibly scented with usharet flowers, placed near the cryopods of the most gregarious drell. About 150 years before the crisis Qetsi secretly revived herself and activated it, relying on Malak'Rafa's computer worm to hide the event from ship systems. The worm malfunctioned, letting cryo temperatures rise slightly so the virus replicated and mutated for decades. It was meant to be a symptomless carrier virus lethal only to humans, asari, turians, and salarians, intended to reduce Council-race populations and secure the minor races' ascendancy with quarians on top. It instead adapted to attack drell brains and became lethal broadly.

In Mass Effect: Annihilation, the worm prematurely woke over a thousand colonists, making quarantine impossible. Yorrik devised a retrovirus cure requiring eezo and a naturally immune subject; the eezo came from batarian smuggler Borbala Ferank, and the only immune person found was Qetsi herself. Enough retrovirus for one person was made, and Qetsi's second-in-command Senna'Nir injected himself and moved through all six racial sections of the ark without his suit to spread the cure, dying in the high-pressure volus section. The cure succeeded, and survivors returned to cryosleep for the last 30 years of the journey. Only Senna, Qetsi, Malak, and the drell detective Anax Therion knew the full truth, kept secret to prevent reprisals against the quarians.
