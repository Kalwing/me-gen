This page covers the general controls, mechanics, and AI behavior of NPC party members across the major Mass Effect games rather than individual characters. A squad is the group Commander Shepard or Pathfinder Ryder takes on field missions or into civilized hubs, typically three members: the protagonist plus two NPC squadmates, though some missions require a specific member, reduce the squad to two, or leave the protagonist alone. In Legendary Edition, squadmates that originally required DLC no longer do.

The page lists selectable squadmates per game: six in Mass Effect (Kaidan Alenko, Ashley Williams, Garrus Vakarian, Urdnot Wrex, Tali'Zorah nar Rayya, Liara T'Soni); up to twelve in Mass Effect 2 with all DLC; and three to seven in Mass Effect 3; plus six in Andromeda (Cora Harper, Liam Kosta, Vetra Nyx, Peebee, Nakmor Drack, Jaal Ama Darav). Selection screens and reselection rules differ by game: Mass Effect locks the choice on leaving the Normandy, while later games allow reselection at various terminals, hubs, and mission starts.

Common mechanics are covered in detail. Squadmates deal roughly half the player's weapon damage but are highly accurate, unaffected by recoil, and effectively have infinite ammo though they still reload. Abilities manually ordered by the player are "proxied" through the player for targeting, ignoring the squadmate's own line of sight, whereas automatic power use is not proxied. The page also details commanding controls per platform, reviving downed squadmates (Unity, First Aid, medi-gel, the Mako, or kneeling to revive in Mass Effect 3 and Andromeda), equipment management, and extensive notes on squad AI quirks: poor line-of-sight judgment, imperfect pathfinding and cover use, reluctance to switch weapons in combat, aggressive advancing when not ordered into cover, and per-game improvements to cover discipline and responsiveness. It notes that in Mass Effect, bringing a squadmate with Electronics or Decryption lets the team open secured objects even if Shepard lacks those talents.
