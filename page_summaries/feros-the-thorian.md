Feros: The Thorian was the second leg of the Mass Effect Feros arc, following Feros: Geth Attack and written by Lukas Kristjanson. Saren had sent the geth to Feros to destroy the Thorian, a parasitic plant creature that had taken mental control of most of the colonists at Zhu's Hope.

Escaping ExoGeni HQ, Shepard fought through geth, then used a glitchy shuttle bay door control (setting it to 31–34 PSI) to shear the claw holding the Geth Dropship, dropping the ship into the planetary rubble and restoring comms. Joker reported the crazed colonists were clawing at the Normandy's hull. Lizbeth Baynham confessed full knowledge of the Thorian project and joined the team. On the skyway back, the team diverted to the ExoGeni Bunker, where Ethan Jeong had ExoGeni Security restrain Juliana Baynham and ordered the colony purged. With sufficient Charm or Intimidate, Shepard could convince Jeong to instead support and expand the colony; otherwise Shepard shot him when he drew his pistol. Juliana provided Anti-Thorian Gas grenades that would harmlessly incapacitate the colonists, though Shepard could also kill them.

Fighting back to Zhu's Hope meant battling Thorian Creepers and mind-controlled colonists (16 total, tracked by an on-screen counter); gas grenades and melee knocked colonists out non-lethally. Fai Dan, forced by the Thorian to try to kill Shepard, resisted and shot himself in the head. Freighter crane controls exposed a stairwell into the Thorian's lair. There the enormous Thorian expelled a green asari clone who demanded respect and explained the Thorian had absorbed the essence of the Protheans and traded that knowledge, plus the asari Shiala, to Saren in exchange for being left alone; Saren broke the deal and attacked. Shepard destroyed six Thorian Neural Nodes across five ascending levels, fighting waves of creepers and seven Asari Clones, until the Thorian fell into the abyss. A pod then released the violet-skinned asari Shiala, formerly of Matriarch Benezia's service, who gave Shepard the Cipher (triggering a Prothean vision) and whose life Shepard could spare or take.

The colony's fate was determined by a formula: colony health (one point per completed sidequest plus one per surviving colonist) plus colony leadership (5 if Shiala survived, 12 if Jeong agreed to help). A total of 13 or higher meant Feros survived. Consequences carried into Mass Effect 2 (a Zhu's Hope colonist, Shiala if spared, appearing on Illium) and Mass Effect 3 (Shiala and a saved colony joining the war effort).
