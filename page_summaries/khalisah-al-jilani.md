Khalisah Bint Sinan al-Jilani was a reporter for Westerlund News known for hostile, agenda-driven interviews with Commander Shepard across the trilogy. In Mass Effect she approached Shepard at the C-Sec Academy on the Citadel; her questions turned combative, pushing the line that Shepard was a shill for the Citadel Council and that the Council treated humanity as a "poor relation," citing Noveria's Peak 15 and Feros. Shepard could answer diplomatically (which annoyed her), aggressively (which she often endorsed), walk away, or punch her. Emphasizing human independence could impress her, ending with "Strong words. This is a good time for strong words." Admiral Hackett later relayed Earth's reaction to the interview.

In Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation (2184), al-Jilani produced reports attacking the Andromeda Initiative, including "Al-Jilani Investigates: Andromeda... Exposed!", insinuating sinister motives behind Alec Ryder's military expulsion and taking up delegate Alis Silhaus's offer to smear backer Jien Garson. She interviewed Initiative agent Cora Harper on Tamayo Point, spinning it to suggest Cora had been subverted by asari influence until Cora flared her biotics in anger. Acting on a tip from Ygara Menoris, al-Jilani aired the interview prematurely while Cora was retrieving illegal Initiative code from the Home Away station, exposing her to station security.

In Mass Effect 2, al-Jilani ambushed Shepard outside the Dark Star Lounge with loaded questions about the Battle of the Citadel and the 2183 Council's fate. Shepard could punch her, charm the audience by framing the dead as heroes, rebuke her, or walk away. In Mass Effect 3 (2186) she sought an interview with the Council during the Reaper war, was stonewalled by Commander Bailey, and loitered near the Citadel Embassies criticizing Shepard for abandoning Earth. Her reflexes had sharpened: a third punch attempt was dodged, and if Shepard had punched her both prior times she declared "not this time you military thug!" — Shepard could then headbutt her or be knocked down by an uppercut. If Shepard never punched her, they could persuade her to stay on the Citadel and keep asking tough questions to aid the war effort; she conceded she was glad Shepard was on humanity's side. During the Citadel coup she used a Westerlund News van to call for C-Sec reinforcements. In the Citadel DLC, an unfinished pre-war interview she recorded with Admiral David Anderson could be heard in his apartment. Per Legendary Edition statistics, 68% of players punched her at least once.
