Asari Clones were asari commandos extruded by the Thorian on Feros. Unlike ordinary asari they had green skin. The Thorian used them both for defense and as a means of communication with outsiders such as Commander Shepard, because the plant creature could not speak except through its thralls. Despite being grown inside the Thorian, each clone emerged wearing the brownish uniform common to all asari commandos and carrying a shotgun and a pistol.

The Clones were created from Shiala, a powerful biotic who had served Matriarch Benezia and then followed Saren Arterius after falling under indoctrination. Saren traded Shiala to the Thorian in exchange for the Prothean Cipher. Shiala melded with the Thorian to obtain the Cipher and remained part of the creature until Shepard confronted the Thorian and freed her.

In combat the Clones relied chiefly on powerful biotics that could knock squad members down and leave them exposed to Thorian Creepers' acid attacks; Damping suppressed their biotics and Sabotage disabled their weapons. Their shotguns were dangerous at close range while their pistols were fired rapidly, and a Clone had a chance to switch weapons when approached in melee. They carried robust shields, against which Neural Shock and Warp were effective, and they could not be ragdolled by Weapons Force ammo upgrades though sufficiently leveled biotic and tech talents could still throw them. Because of the layout of the Feros structure, a strong enough Throw could knock a Clone off the platform edge for an instant kill, a hazard that applied equally to Shepard. In the Legendary Edition the Clone rarely used Throw and no longer targeted Shepard with it. Although created by the Thorian, the Clones were not instantly killed by anti-Thorian gas grenades, so those grenades were better saved for Thorian Creepers.
