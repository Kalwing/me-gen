Shotguns was a combat talent in the original Mass Effect, inherently available to Soldiers, Vanguards, the Krogan Battlemaster, and the Quarian Machinist, and trainable as a bonus talent by any class for more shotgun damage. Possessing the talent let the player aim down sights with a shotgun to zoom the view, though this did not tighten pellet spread; unlike other weapon talents, the accuracy bonuses had little practical effect, so the passive benefit was effectively confined to the damage bonus, which scaled from +5% at rank 1 to +20% at rank 11.

Higher ranks unlocked the active ability Carnage (rank 4), upgraded to Advanced Carnage (rank 8) and Master Carnage (rank 12). Carnage fired a concentrated fireball-like blast with perfect accuracy that dealt massive area-of-effect damage to anything it struck without harming Shepard or allies, useful at long range where shotguns normally failed, though the projectile's travel time made moving targets easy to miss. Mechanically it was a standalone ability: a shotgun had to be equipped but no shotgun round was actually fired, no heat was produced, and ammunition on-hit and force effects were lost. It inflicted weapon damage +50% within 2 m (Carnage), +100% within 2 m (Advanced), or +150% within 3 m (Master), with a 45-second recharge and 40% accuracy cost. Switching weapons, changing the shotgun model, or modifying its upgrades cancelled a pending Carnage. Enemies such as Geth Shock Troopers and Geth Destroyers used Carnage at the start of battles. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition any class could scope with any weapon without the talent (at reduced movement speed), and Carnage was reworked to purge all weapon heat on use, deal 200-250% of weapon DPS, and have a short 6-second window to fire.
