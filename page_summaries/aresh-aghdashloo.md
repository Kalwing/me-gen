Aresh Aghdashloo was a human biotic who was one of many child test subjects at the rogue Cerberus Teltin Facility on Pragia. Like most of the children there, he had been sold to Cerberus by batarian pirates. Horrific and often lethal experiments were performed on the subjects, primarily to ensure that the most important subject, Jack, would survive. Aresh was one of the few other survivors of the mass breakout from the facility, and the experience left him deeply mentally scarred.

In Mass Effect 2, Aresh became convinced that the atrocities committed at Teltin must have served some greater purpose, and he set about rebuilding the facility. He hired Blood Pack mercenaries, led by the krogan Kureck, to defend the site. During Jack's return to Pragia, Shepard and Jack fought through these mercenaries and confronted Aresh, who revealed his plan to restart the facility. Shepard could convince Jack to spare his life or could kill him personally.

In Mass Effect 3, if Aresh had been spared, he survived the destruction of the Teltin Facility and later took up work on the colony of Elysium. In 2186, Shepard received a news alert reporting that Aresh had saved a shuttle of children evacuating the Reaper-occupied colony and died in the process, having reportedly killed several dozen Reaper creatures during the defense. His surname, Aghdashloo, was revealed at this time. It matches that of Shohreh Aghdashloo, the actress who voiced Admiral Shala'Raan vas Tonbay.
