Jordan Noles was a C-Sec officer working in the E-Crimes division on the Citadel. After the Cerberus attack on the Citadel in 2186, she loitered outside the C-Sec outpost at the Presidium Commons and requested Commander Shepard's help. She was concerned about security leaks involving old batarian diplomatic codes and needed Shepard's Spectre authority to track where they had been used so she could disable them.

At Huerta Memorial Hospital, Noles found the access codes had been used either to cut off power to life-support systems or to requisition medical supplies. At Normandy Dock: Bay D24, the codes had been used either to engineer an accident causing Alliance casualties the previous week or to tamper with food requests at the refugee center. At the Docks holding area, Shepard tracked the final use of the codes. If they had been used for sabotage, Noles' comms were cut and she arrived to find Shepard had already dealt with the culprit, the batarian terrorist Ka'hairal Balak, who was either killed or allowed to walk free under Spectre authority. If the codes had been used to secure provisions, Noles again found Balak responsible, provided he had been defeated and left for dead at Asteroid X57; if he had been arrested or killed then, she traced and arrested an unnamed batarian instead, whom she would uncuff if Shepard dropped all charges while persuading the batarians to join the war effort.

Regardless of outcome, Noles was grateful for Shepard's help and might repeat the sentiment by mail. She was also a known gamer with top-ten scores in Shattered Eezo and at the Armax Arsenal Arena, and had investigated an incident involving holographic vorcha spilling into the Arena crowd, blaming it on mass effect fields after decoy holographic emitters were found at the scene.
