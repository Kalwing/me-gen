The page gathered Codex entries on spacefaring species without Council power. The batarians developed the Skyllian Verge for decades before Alliance colonization; after the Council refused in 2171 to declare it a batarian interest zone, the Hegemony closed its Citadel embassy, became a rogue state, and funded pirate and slaver attacks on human colonies, culminating in the 2176 Skyllian Blitz against Elysium and the Alliance's 2178 assault on Torfan. The Collectors lived beyond the Omega-4 relay, insectoid bipeds who imprisoned whole populations in stasis for harvesting; they were remotely controlled by a "Collector General," itself a conduit for the extragalactic Reaper Harbinger, which was building an embryonic Reaper from the human genome. Their servitors included the mechanical Oculus.

The drell were reptilian humanoids saved two centuries earlier when the hanar evacuated 375,000 of them from the dying world Rakhana to Kahje; many served the hanar with fierce loyalty, and many died of Kepral's Syndrome. The elcor were slow, deliberate, monotone high-gravity natives of Dekuuna. The geth were networked AIs built by the quarians roughly 300 years earlier; when they showed self-awareness the quarians tried to exterminate them and lost, driving the quarians into exile. Geth intelligence grew with proximity and numbers; the "heretics" were the minority that followed Saren Arterius, as revealed through the geth program Legion. The hanar were extremely polite ocean-dwellers with public "Face Names" and private "Soul Names" who often revered the Protheans. The keepers silently maintained the Citadel and held a constant population from an unknown source.

The krogan evolved on lethal Tuchanka, were uplifted by the salarians for the Rachni Wars, then overpopulated and fought the century-long Krogan Rebellions, ended when the turians deployed the salarian-made genophage, which left only one in 1,000 pregnancies viable. Praetorians were armored hovering tanks made from multiple encased humans. The quarians lived aboard the roughly 50,000-ship Migrant Fleet, home to about 17 million, sending youths on the Pilgrimage to earn a berth; they had almost no immune system. Reapers were dismissed in-universe as myth; their indoctrination slowly reprogrammed organic minds through electromagnetic and sonic conditioning until the victim degenerated. Sovereign was Saren's two-kilometer dreadnought, defeated over the Citadel by the Normandy and the Fifth Fleet. The volus were an ammonia-breathing turian client race who authored the Unified Banking Act. The vorcha were short-lived, adaptable scavengers with regenerative neoblast cells, mostly working as krogan mercenaries. The yahg were massive apex predators from Parnack, quarantined by the Council after they attacked its 2125 first-contact team. Varren were feral Tuchanka pack animals.
