Edan Had'dah was a batarian aristocrat, entrepreneur and skilled negotiator operating from the batarian-administered planet Camala. He made his fortune investing heavily in survey teams and archaeologists searching for profitable Prothean technology and relics, which he sold to researchers, and his wealth and network of contacts brought him immense power in batarian society. He was known for his vanity and extravagant lifestyle, including flamboyant asari-made clothing and a fondness for hanar liquors.

In 2162, one of Had'dah's scout teams operating dangerously close to geth space discovered a mysterious artifact. Visiting the site afterward, Had'dah recognized its potential but was also subtly indoctrinated, driving him to uncover its secrets. He set aside his hatred of humans to recruit the best available expert, Dr. Shu Qian, who began studying the artifact from his lab on Sidon while maintaining close contact with Had'dah.

In 2165, after Kahlee Sanders threatened to expose Dr. Qian, Had'dah hired the Blue Suns to attack the Sidon facility and cover up evidence of Qian's defection. He then hired a krogan bounty hunter named Skarr to find and kill Sanders; when Had'dah tried to intimidate him, he was shocked by Skarr's intelligence and biotic power and agreed to a larger payout. After Skarr failed, Had'dah ordered him to destroy the Dah'tan Manufacturing facility, the last link between Had'dah and the Sidon events, and later sent him to capture Kahlee to assist Qian's work.

A surviving Dah'tan worker pointed the Spectre Saren Arterius toward Had'dah. Saren attacked the eezo refinery on Camala where Had'dah and Qian were hiding. Confronted, Had'dah claimed Qian's obsession had made him a liability. After Saren killed Qian, Had'dah bargained for his life, arguing that Saren would need his help and resources to unlock the artifact or lose years of progress. Saren replied that he was a patient man who did not mind waiting, then shot Had'dah dead.
