Nepmos was the first planet of the Erebus System in the Styx Theta cluster of the Milky Way. It was a resource-rich terrestrial world somewhat larger than Earth, wrapped in a dense atmosphere of sulphur dioxide and argon. Its crust was very thin and unstable, nowhere more than eight kilometers deep, so subsurface magma flows menaced ground travel and earthquakes were frequent. Although heavy metals and radioactives were plentiful, this geological instability discouraged investment by mining concerns. A further complication was that the Styx Cluster lay uncomfortably close to the "Five Kiloparsec Ring" around the galactic core, a region rich in molecular hydrogen that fuels most of the galaxy's star formation and is too dangerous to travel safely.

Commander Shepard could visit Nepmos after Noveria revealed geth interest, in connection with the assignment UNC: Listening Post Alpha. An Alliance listening post stood on the surface, and coordinates from Lieutenant Durand opened access to a mine that had been infested by rachni. A mining camp abandoned by the Tenkarra Mining Consortium remained on the site, its shacks and equipment stenciled with the company logo but empty of any crew; the "song" associated with the rachni could be heard there, and particle bores had been shut down after breaking into tunnels just below the surface. One of the miners had carried a League of One medallion, recoverable for the UNC: Locate Signs of Battle collection. Other points of interest included a crashed probe and safebox for salvage, plus two unmarked rachni colonies. Rachni Soldiers, Brood Warriors and Workers were encountered on the planet.

Mineral deposits on Nepmos included rare uranium, heavy palladium and light titanium, supporting the UNC: Valuable Minerals survey. The page also notes technical issues: approaching the rachni colonies a second time while corpses were still present could crash the PC version, and leaving the colony on Xbox 360 and PS3 caused severe frame-rate drops and could make a squad member disappear until Shepard returned to the Normandy.
