Freedom's Progress was the third and final mission of Mass Effect 2's prologue, acquired automatically after Shepard's briefing with the Illusive Man following Prologue: Awakening. All colonists of the human colony had vanished, and the Illusive Man sent Shepard, Jacob Taylor, and Miranda Lawson to investigate. During the shuttle ride, Shepard could earn Paragon points by emphasizing the search for survivors or Renegade points by prioritizing the mission.

Moving through the abandoned settlement, the team fought waves of LOKI Mechs, FENRIS Mechs, Assault Drones, and Rocket Drones that had been reprogrammed for defense. In prefab 92 they met Tali'Zorah vas Neema and a group of distrustful quarians searching for Veetor, an unstable quarian on Pilgrimage whose damaged suit and subsequent infection had made him paranoid enough to reprogram the colony's mechs. The two squads agreed to cooperate. Prazza and other quarians pushed ahead against Tali's orders to reach Veetor before Cerberus could question him, and were slaughtered by a reprogrammed YMIR Mech at the cargo docks, which Shepard's team then destroyed.

The team found Veetor in building 7, babbling about monsters and watching garbled security footage. A Renegade or Paragon interrupt calmed him. Veetor had pieced together footage revealing the Collectors were behind the abductions; the group theorized he was spared because the Collectors scanned only for human vital signs. Miranda wanted Veetor taken into custody for interrogation while Tali demanded he be returned to the Flotilla for medical care, with the final decision left to Shepard. In Tali's loyalty mission, a Veetor returned to the Flotilla could testify on her behalf.

In the aftermath, the Illusive Man debriefed Shepard about the Collectors' link to the Reapers, explained that former squadmates and Spectre resources were unavailable, and recommended recruiting Mordin Solus on Omega to counter the Collectors' Seeker Swarms. He reintroduced Joker as pilot of the Cerberus-built Normandy SR-2, and EDI introduced herself. The mission's end concluded the prologue and opened the first recruitment missions: Dossier: The Professor, The Convict, Archangel, and The Warlord, plus DLC dossiers and assignments. Rewards included 1,000 experience, 20,000 credits, a Heavy Weapon Ammo Upgrade, and 2,000 iridium.
