The Collector Captain was a Collector unit encountered in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer with the Retaliation Pack DLC, and also in combat simulations in the Armax Arsenal Arena added by the Citadel DLC; its Reaper-possessed form was the Possessed Captain. Captains were armed with Collector Assault Rifles, deployed Seeker Swarms for battlefield support, used organic blades for melee attacks, and could instantly kill downed players with a stomp. Like other Collector opponents they could be possessed, gaining additional durability and offensive power. They often took cover during combat, had moderate health and barriers, could be staggered by gunfire or powers when unprotected by barriers, and took 40% additional damage from headshots. Comparable to "commander" types such as Marauders and Centurions but tougher and more aggressive, Captains tended to advance straight at the player firing long rifle bursts and were single-minded about rushing to stomp downed players. Their melee attack staggered players, and the Collectors always struck twice with their blades, though a batarian's Blade Armor caused the Captain to stagger back after the first blow, denying the full damage of both strikes. Captains were most vulnerable while doubled over creating a Seeker Swarm, when they could not dodge or retaliate, and killing one quickly enough during that window could prevent the swarm from appearing.
