This page collects gameplay information and combat tactics for fighting thresher maws as enemy units in Mass Effect and Mass Effect 2, as opposed to the general lore page.

In Mass Effect, thresher maws appear on uncharted worlds and can be fought in the Mako or on foot. Their nests are identifiable by a roughly 50–75 meter circular area of flat ground with low hills and shallow pits; maws only surface on the flat portions and retreat underground if the player leaves the circle for about five seconds. The page stresses never driving into or meleeing a maw, since its claws can cause instant death, and that its acid spit bypasses shields entirely and can destroy the Mako or kill Shepard in one hit. Recommended tactics include keeping distance, circle-strafing or using the Mako's boosters to hop over each acid projectile while firing, and using cover behind hills. A quirk let players kill the same maw twice for double experience by killing it before its health bar appeared. In the Legendary Edition, maws spit a spread of acid balls that lead the target, stay underground longer with a visible dirt trail, and can deploy immobile Sensory Tentacles; killing in the Mako no longer carries an experience penalty, and maws do not heal when the player withdraws.

In Mass Effect 2, a single thresher maw must be fought on foot by Grunt and his krantt during Clan Urdnot's Rite of Passage on Tuchanka. It stays outside the playable area, lobbing fast high-damage acid blobs that delay shield and health regeneration, and periodically dives and tunnels to a new position. It has heavy armor with no weak points (the blue tongue and mouth take no bonus damage) and is immune to Stasis. Effective options include Warp, Reave and Incinerate against its armor, stone pillars for cover, well-upgraded heavy pistols such as the M-5 Phalanx or M-6 Carnifex, upgraded sniper rifles, and heavy weapons like the M-920 Cain or Collector Particle Beam.
