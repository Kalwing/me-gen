"N7: Javelin Missiles Launched" was an optional Mass Effect 2 assignment in the Skepsis system of Sigurd's Cradle, available after "N7: MSV Strontium Mule" or "N7: Blue Suns Base," or by scanning the moon Franklin. Batarian radicals had seized an Alliance colony's Javelin Mk.II missile base on Franklin and launched two missiles at the colony on the planet Watson. Scans predicted total destruction of the colony with zero probability of survivors, giving the landing party five minutes after shuttle touchdown to reach the control panel and deactivate the missiles.

Shepard and squad fought through Batarian Troopers, Batarian Commanders, and a final Commander in the silo control room, bypassing several time-consuming door locks under the countdown transmitted by EDI. Hacking the console deactivated one missile automatically. EDI then reported only one kill-switch code remained, forcing a choice: save the spaceport to protect Alliance interests, or destroy the spaceport to save the residential district and hundreds of lives. Destroying the industrial area left the colony non-viable and requiring evacuation; saving it kept the colony viable for repopulation. The decision awarded no Paragon or Renegade points and had no further consequences. A journal bug displayed the opposite outcome to the one chosen, though the game recorded the actual choice.

Rewards were 7,500 credits and 2,000 palladium. Trivia noted the mission's monitor art reused prefab buildings from the Mass Effect 2 cinematic trailer and a screenshot of the Blue Suns base from "Disable False Distress Signal," and that a sword prop could be found near the control room.
