Orinia was the turian ambassador to the Citadel in 2186 CE and a former general who had served during the First Contact War. She featured in the novel Mass Effect: Retribution. She was the first major turian female character in the Mass Effect series; prior to Retribution, all notable turian characters had been male.

When Kahlee Sanders passed Paul Grayson's evidence on Cerberus to David Anderson, Anderson, unable to trust anyone in the Alliance, brought it to Orinia. She noted that Cerberus had been the reason turians opposed adding humanity to the Council, but Anderson countered with the example of Saren Arterius, and she agreed to listen. At Anderson's request, she let Kahlee shelter in the turian embassy for protection.

Having only a limited number of troops, Orinia had to choose targets carefully for the operation against Cerberus. She asked a former subordinate, C-Sec Executor Pallin, for help, and he assembled a turian-exclusive task force; with Anderson's assistance they organized the strikes against Cerberus. She refused Kahlee's request to join the mission, noting the commanders had spent thousands of hours preparing their teams. After the successful strikes, she reported that Udina was furious, and, once persuaded that the turians needed human insight and that time was short to find a missing turian team, she allowed Kahlee and Anderson to visit the base holding Grayson, sending a half-dozen scientists with them and warning that the shuttle would leave in one hour regardless.
