Short character/kit page (under ~150 words of real prose). The Human Kineticist was a biotic warrior class (a Mass Effect: Andromeda multiplayer character) who dealt heavy telekinetic damage and could enter a powered-up state but had weak defenses. Characterized as a scientist eager to test her theories in the field, she used the powers Pull, Throw and Lance with the passives Biotic Ascension, Barrier and Veteran Bonus, a Biotic Punch melee, and a Max Health bonus stat (450 health, 250 shields). She excelled against unshielded enemies and biotic combos but struggled against shields and armor, and was relatively weak against the armor-heavy Outlaw faction.
