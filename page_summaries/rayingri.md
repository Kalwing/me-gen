Rayingri was the third planet of the Gagarin system in the Armstrong Nebula, a small, barren terrestrial world. Despite a reasonably temperate climate and a number of useful resources, no mining corporation was willing to risk investment there. Roughly ten thousand years earlier a rogue planetoid, dubbed Vahtz by the initial salarian surveyor team, had entered the system and become trapped in a decaying orbit around Rayingri. As it neared the end of its slow inward spiral, earthquakes and cyclonic windstorms grew increasingly common. The two bodies were expected to tear each other apart within a few hundred years, and some asari travel consortiums had already announced sponsorship of cruises to observe the eventual spectacle.

In Mass Effect, Rayingri was explorable via the Mako and hosted the assignment UNC: Geth Incursions, centered on an overrun research outpost, along with collection and survey objectives for a League of One Medallion and valuable minerals (titanium, polonium, thorium). Points of interest included the burnt corpses of an independent "mom and pop" salvage team showing signs of geth heavy particle beam hits at close range, an abruptly abandoned scavenger camp, a crashed probe, and a downed escape pod containing a League of One medallion whose owner was missing. The "song" associated with the rachni could be heard in two areas of the planet. Geth forces present included Destroyers, Dropships, Rocket Troopers, Shock Troopers, Snipers, and Troopers, alongside Husks. Viewed from the surface, the Gagarin sun appeared to the north and the planetoid Vahtz to the south.
