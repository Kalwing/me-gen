An optional N7 assignment in Mass Effect 3, obtained after Priority: Tuchanka once the turian-krogan alliance was cemented, when Specialist Traynor flagged it. Admiral Hackett asked Shepard to land on the icy world of Noveria, in the Horsehead Nebula, and disable the defenses of a Cerberus fighter base. The mission uses the multiplayer map Firebase White as it appeared before the Retaliation Pack.

Cortez dropped the squad on a small platform above the main landing pad. Shepard cleared Assault Troopers and Centurions, reached a command center and had a squadmate deactivate a security console, then withstood a Cerberus counterattack, of Troopers, a Centurion and a Guardian, with only one remaining squadmate for a time. After activating a second console, the squad cleared the landing pad of Troopers, Centurions, Combat Engineers and a Nemesis, and finally destroyed an Atlas mech sent in as the last unit, which ended the mission and returned Shepard to the Normandy to debrief with Hackett. A recovered log noted that all on-base personnel had been "processed" and integration protocols updated.

Completion rewarded the Advanced Fighter Squadron war asset, 20,000 credits, the M-300 Claymore, a Pistol Magazine Upgrade, the Heating Unit Stabilizers and +5 Reputation.
