Blue Suns Troopers were the rank-and-file soldiers of the Blue Suns mercenary organization and one of the most commonly encountered enemy types in Mass Effect 2. They were predominantly human, batarian, and turian personnel, armed with either assault rifles or shotguns. Assault-rifle Troopers sought cover and were effective at stripping shields, barriers, and armor, laying down sustained fire helped by the large heat-sink capacity of their weapons, which spaced out their reloads. Shotgun Troopers tried to close the distance and kill their targets at short range.

On normal difficulty Troopers had no defensive layers; on higher difficulties they gained shields. Turian Troopers were slightly more durable than their human and batarian counterparts, and turians more often filled the close-quarters role. Troopers used basic infantry tactics: suppression fire to pin the player, popping out of cover to take shots, and flanking. They rarely entered close combat and were generally easy to handle when not backed by elite units such as Legionnaires or Centurions.

Shotgun Troopers behaved somewhat like an Eclipse Vanguard but were considerably weaker, tending to move into open areas where they could be picked off. They could inflict significant damage up close, but their slow rate of fire and predictable patterns made them manageable, and powers such as Throw or Concussive Shot knocked them over when unshielded. Full Cryo Blast was effective against clustered groups. The main danger Troopers posed was distracting a squad's fire from more dangerous threats. Assault-rifle Troopers were considered a higher priority target than shotgun Troopers because of their greater range and rate of fire, and on harder difficulties they brought down the player's shields quickly.
