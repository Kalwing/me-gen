David Archer was the brother of Cerberus chief scientist Dr. Gavin Archer. He was an autistic mathematical savant with rapid calculation ability and eidetic memory, and was very sensitive to loud noise. During Cerberus' Project Overlord on Aite, it was discovered that David could communicate with the geth on a fundamental level by reproducing their speech patterns. To salvage the failing project, which aimed to give Cerberus complete control over the geth through an "overlord" VI, Gavin Archer forcibly merged David into the VI program. David's mind could not handle the merger, causing the hybrid VI to go berserk and rampage across Aite in an attempt to escape, leaving David in extreme overload. As a human/VI hybrid he could interface with and control machinery including security mechs and geth, and imprinted memories and visual data directly onto Commander Shepard.

Shepard's deactivation of the rogue VI program freed David. If Shepard stopped the experiments, David was removed from Cerberus and his brother's care and taken to Grissom Academy. If Shepard allowed the experiments to continue, David stayed with his brother and Project Overlord continued.

In Mass Effect 3, if David was delivered to Grissom Academy, he could be met there in 2186 during the Cerberus attack, helping fellow students Octavia and Isaac maintain a kinetic barrier generator and vouching for Shepard. He gave Shepard access to a weapons room and afterward went with Kahlee Sanders to help build the Crucible, earning the scientists' respect with his theories of interstellar cross-modulation. If EDI or Garrus was present he recognized and apologized to them for Project Overlord, and both told him no apology was needed. If David had been left with Cerberus, Gavin Archer explained that David eventually stopped responding to stimuli and was euthanized as an act of mercy; if Project Overlord was never addressed, David died when Cerberus destroyed Atlas Station with nuclear weapons.
