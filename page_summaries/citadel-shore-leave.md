Citadel: Shore Leave was the Mass Effect 3 mission that opened the main storyline of the Citadel DLC, available after Priority: The Citadel II. Shepard activated it by reading a private terminal message from Admiral Hackett ordering the Normandy into dry dock on the Citadel for retrofits and putting the crew on shore leave, with a note that Admiral Anderson kept an apartment on the Wards.

Traveling to the Personal Apartment, Shepard was contacted by Anderson from Earth, who handed the apartment over permanently, saying he no longer needed it. After exploring, Shepard read a message at the private terminal from Joker proposing dinner at the Ryuusei sushi restaurant in the Wards. At the restaurant, the host let Shepard bypass the queue. Joker discussed the Normandy's dry docking, and Shepard could remind him it was shore leave or agree with his distrust of the engineers for morality points. Joker then asked why Shepard had invited him, and Shepard replied that Joker had sent the invitation, leaving both confused.

An Alliance soldier in dress uniform who had been trying to get Shepard's attention reached the table and introduced herself as Staff Analyst Maya Brooks of Alliance intelligence. She warned that a new group was trying to kill Shepard, hacking Shepard's account and communications and targeting the Spectre personally. Shepard told her to calm down and start from the beginning, which began the next mission, Citadel Wards: Ambush. As in the main game, Ashley or Kaidan, Javik, and Tali were only available as squadmates for the DLC if they had been recruited. Preparations such as purchases and power selection could not be made once Shepard docked at the Personal Apartment, and the following mission imposed a weight capacity penalty.
