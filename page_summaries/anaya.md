Detective Anaya was an asari who lived and worked in Nos Astra on Illium, employed by Illium Law Enforcement at a district police station. When Commander Shepard arrived at the Illium spaceport in 2185, Anaya was investigating the murder of Dakni Kur, the business partner of Pitne For. She briefed Shepard on the details of the killing, granted permission to enter the crime scene, and suggested that Eclipse mercenaries were responsible.

Anaya told Shepard that the justicar Samara was also investigating the crime. Anaya was unhappy about Samara operating in her district and wanted her gone quickly. Ordered by superiors who feared Samara might cause a cross-species incident, Anaya reluctantly moved to detain the justicar. Samara willingly submitted to a day of detention, but Anaya believed her own life was at risk for obstructing the investigation. After Samara obtained the information she needed and pledged herself to Shepard's service, Anaya expressed relief and gratitude, saying she was glad to have met a justicar and would tell the story to her grandchildren, whom she now expected to live long enough to have.

If Shepard passed along information about the recent murder of a volus, possibly Dakni Kur, Anaya declared the evidence inadmissible until Samara vouched for Shepard, forcing her to concur. Anaya approved of the outcome whether the suspect escaped or was killed. Shepard could also hand over evidence of Pitne For's illegal smuggling activities in exchange for a monetary reward. Anaya formed an opinion of Shepard based on how she was treated, resenting rudeness but appreciating professional conduct and advising Shepard to value the justicar. After Samara joined the squad, Anaya sent Shepard an email titled "Still alive," thanking the Commander, reporting that the Eclipse mercenaries had gone quiet and her superiors had backed off, and promising to forward any further data on Samara's target.
