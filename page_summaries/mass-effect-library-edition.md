The Mass Effect Library Edition was a set of two hardcover anthology volumes published by Dark Horse Comics, each collecting all Mass Effect comics released up to a certain point. Both volumes were also republished in trade paperback as the Mass Effect Omnibus. Volume 1, published May 15, 2013, collected all issues through August 2012: Mass Effect: Redemption, Evolution, Invasion, Homeworlds, Incursion, Inquisition, and Conviction; its Omnibus edition (November 16, 2016) used the cover of Redemption 1. Volume 2, released May 19, 2015, collected the remaining original-trilogy issues before Mass Effect: Andromeda media: Mass Effect: Foundation, He Who Laughs Best, and Blasto: Eternity is Forever; its Omnibus edition (February 8, 2017) used the cover of Foundation 3.
