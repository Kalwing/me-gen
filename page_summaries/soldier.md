The Soldier is a player and squadmate class appearing in Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, Mass Effect 3, and (as a profile) Mass Effect: Andromeda. Soldiers are pure combat specialists focused on durability and firepower, with no biotic or tech abilities, relying on squadmates to cover those areas.

In the first Mass Effect the Soldier gained improved health and limited health regeneration through its class talent, could train in all weapon types, was the only class with the Assault Rifles talent, and was the only class able to upgrade to heavy armor (starting in medium). Ashley Williams, Richard L. Jenkins, and the default Commander Shepard all use this class. A Soldier Shepard could specialize into Commando or Shock Trooper to unlock levels 7 to 12 of the class talent.

In Mass Effect 2 Soldiers had the most thorough weapons training, could use all special ammo types, and high-level operatives received ocular synaptic processors for lethal accuracy. Their powers included Adrenaline Rush, Concussive Shot, Disruptor Ammo, Incendiary Ammo, Cryo Ammo, and the Combat Mastery class power. Mass Effect 3 kept this kit and added Frag Grenade and Fitness. The Mass Effect 3 Soldier has the highest potential weight capacity of any class, with a Combat Mastery bonus reaching up to 100.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda the Soldier is one of seven swappable profiles available to Pathfinder Ryder, optimizing armor and weapon mastery, with the bonus power Marksman's Focus granting an escalating damage bonus per kill. According to BioWare statistics, Soldier is by far the most player-picked class: 65 percent of characters in March 2012, dropping to 40 percent but still the most popular in Legendary Edition as of July 2021.
