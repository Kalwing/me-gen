This short archive collected five brief Cerberus Daily News items from late February 2186, also carried by the Alliance News Network. The delayed Susskind Supercollider came online with results physicists called "promising," and the stolen New Year's Eve ball was found intact on the Citadel with a note from thief Quarn claiming C-Sec needed funds and that anything could be smuggled onto the station. An official ship for the Citadel Committee on Paleotechnology was shot down over Garvug, raising fears that data files on Prothean inventions had fallen into criminal hands. The final two items reported that communication with the batarian homeworld Khar'Shan had gone dark, cutting the batarian economy off from its colonies; the Hegemony blamed comm-buoy disruption on solar output, while the governor of the Camala colony suspected an Alliance stealth-vessel attack.
