Urdnot Dagg was a krogan soldier who led Aralakh Company during the Reaper War if Grunt did not survive the Collector base suicide mission or was never released from his tank. Either Urdnot Wrex or Urdnot Wreav, as leader of Clan Urdnot, chose Dagg to command the company because he was a respected and feared soldier whom his superiors believed would bring greater unity to Aralakh Company.

In 2186 Dagg led Aralakh Company on a mission to Utukku to investigate a missing krogan scout team, assisted by Commander Shepard. On first meeting Shepard, Dagg expressed both admiration and jealousy that Shepard had the Reapers as enemies, wondering what the Commander had done to earn such a reputation; if Shepard replied that Dagg came highly recommended, he explained his selection as leader. He boasted that every krogan under his command wanted blood, and if a female Shepard quipped she would leave some for them, he barked approvingly that "the female has bite!" Eager for battle, Dagg assumed Shepard was intentionally charging into combat when the Commander first fell into the rachni nest. During a moment when Shepard's squad could observe Aralakh Company fighting rachni, Dagg had more trouble than Grunt would have, pinned down and unable to break cover.

Dagg later sacrificed himself, fending off a swarm of Ravagers to buy time for Shepard's squad to escape. Unlike Grunt, there was no way to save Dagg; whether Shepard chose to save Aralakh Company or the Rachni Queen/Breeder, Dagg always died, overwhelmed by Ravagers.
