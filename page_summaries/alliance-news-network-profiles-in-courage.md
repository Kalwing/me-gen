Profiles in Courage was a special edition of the Alliance News Network show, broadcast during the Reaper War in 2186, following a four-member squad of N7 Special Ops agents on shore leave on the Citadel after a week of combat. The broadcast was released to promote the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, and the mixed-species squad reflected Mass Effect 3 Multiplayer. The story is considered canon only in playthroughs where the geth are spared at Rannoch. The squad comprised De-MEL-Isher (@De_Mel_Isher), a human N7 engineer who fought mainly with grenades; Snarfles (@VorchaDaddy), a vorcha soldier; Amda Talons Flufyria (@TalktotheTalons), a female turian vanguard and Cabal member; and Unit 1025 (@ThisUnit1025), a geth infiltrator aiding organics.

Told through ANN posts and the agents' exchanges, the story followed the squad from the Silver Coast Casino, where Snarfles lost his credits on a Quasar machine and Unit 1025's hacking attempt triggered casino security, and where De-MEL-Isher briefly dated the krogan crime lord Thax Vorak; to the Armax Arsenal Arena, where the squad hacked the combat simulator so Talons would win, Unit 1025 altered the holographic enemies rather than let geth be depicted as targets, and a grenade breached a wall; to the Silversun Strip's Castle Arcade; and finally to a stolen sky-barge that Unit 1025 crash-landed in the Presidium lake. Along the way Talons biotically poisoned several people including the Alliance N7 specialist Richards, a former squadmate of De-MEL-Isher who had been picking up Unit 1025's dropped interface port, and Snarfles admitted his asari girlfriend on Omega was invented to impress Talons, who then reciprocated his feelings.

The page also reproduces in-universe news items: a C-Sec statement by Commander Bailey insisting no hostile AI was awakening at the Silver Coast Casino after the Thax Vorak poisoning, with casino owner Elijah Khan denying any AI incidents; a Citadel Weekly item quoting E-Crimes expert Jordan Noles blaming holographic vorcha escaping the Armax arena on mass effect fields; and a Citadel Weekly profile of a celebrated varren, "Prince Grimrender of Gembat," whose life had touched Feros colonists, Tuchanka merchants, and finally Thax Vorak.
