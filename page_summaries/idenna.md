The Idenna was a starship of the quarian Migrant Fleet, a thirty-year-old decommissioned batarian Hensa-class cruiser with a crew of 693 (compared to about 80 on Alliance cruisers of similar size). She commanded several scout vessels, including the Bavea and the Cyniad.

In Mass Effect: Ascension, the Idenna served as a refuge for Kahlee Sanders, Gillian Grayson, and Hendel Mitra after they came aboard the Fleet. Her captain was regarded as a maverick and visionary who wanted to use Fleet vessels to search for new homeworlds. After Cerberus attacked the Idenna, the Admiralty Board finally accepted this policy and chose the Idenna as one of the first ships to leave the Flotilla. Her crew was cut from 694 (including newest member Lemm'Shal nar Tesleya) to around 50, and she was extensively refitted with new technology to survive five years without outside contact, a mission likened to Star Trek's Enterprise.

In Mass Effect 2, the scout Cyniad crashed on Gei Hinnom in the Sheol System, Hades Nexus, killing commanding officer Lieutenant Commander Gorlat vas Idenna; varren then killed the rest of the crew except Lieutenant Forzan vas Idenna, who was rescued by Commander Shepard and the Normandy SR-2. Captain Ysin'Mal vas Idenna sent Shepard a message of thanks for aiding the search for a new quarian homeworld.

In the first edition of Mass Effect: Deception (acknowledged by BioWare as containing lore discrepancies), the Idenna was attacked by the batarian slave ship Glory of Khar'shan, which used "drive killers" to halt her and board. The Idenna's security force, including Gillian Grayson and Hendel Mitra, repelled the boarders and captured the batarian ship. Notable crew also included Seeto'Hodda nar Idenna, Feda'Gazu vas Idenna, Hilo'Jaa vas Idenna, and Lita'Orn nar Idenna.
