Junthor was the first planet of the Gagarin System in the Armstrong Nebula of the Milky Way. It was a large terrestrial world with a thick carbon dioxide and chlorine atmosphere, its surface composed mainly of aluminum with nickel deposits. Surveyors found the ruins of an ancient spacefaring civilization near the equator, reduced by wind erosion to little more than husks of former arcologies and megastructures. At the center stood a single column whose inscriptions resisted translation for centuries. When asari linguists finally rendered it, the elaborate relief carvings read only, "Walk among these works, and know our greatness," while crude scratches on the reverse base read, "Monsters from the id." Scans also revealed a wrecked freighter orbiting one of Junthor's smaller moons; a recon team found no survivors but recovered one of Matriarch Dilinaga's writings, tied to the collection assignment UNC: Asari Writings. The "monsters from the id" phrase referenced the film Forbidden Planet, and the column's boast echoed Percy Bysshe Shelley's poem "Ozymandias."
