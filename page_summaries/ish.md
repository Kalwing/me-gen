Ish was a salarian information broker who operated on Omega. In Mass Effect: Galaxy, Miranda Lawson arranged a meeting between Ish and Jacob Taylor when Jacob first arrived at Tortuga. Ish informed Jacob of the whereabouts and activities of the turian weapons smuggler Illo Nazario and supplied the access code to the backdoor of Illo's settlement, warning that Nazario had many clients and enough thugs to take on an army. When Jacob later moved against Illo, he was ambushed by batarians, whom he and Miranda believed Ish had tipped off.

In Mass Effect 2, set in 2185, Commander Shepard encountered Ish outside the Afterlife Club on Omega, accompanied by another salarian named Sel. Ish asked Shepard to pick up packages for him. If Jacob was in the party, he confronted Ish about the earlier ambush and, along with Miranda, advised Shepard not to trust the broker. If Mass Effect: Galaxy was completed or the Legendary Edition was played, extra dialogue with Jacob and Miranda present explained the Tortuga events. Ish was evasive about his real motives, admitting only that he was gathering information on people on Omega, especially Aria T'Loak. Retrieving his packages on the Citadel and Illium revealed that Anto Bek'tall, one of Aria's most trusted bodyguards, was making unauthorized outside deals. Shepard could warn Anto that Ish was investigating him, which led Anto to kill Ish, or convince Ish to abandon his plans and leave Omega to avoid Aria's reprisal. Ish was voiced by George Szilagyi.
