"Dossier: The Justicar" was a Mass Effect 2 recruitment mission provided by the Illusive Man after Horizon. Samara was described as a justicar, a rare member of an asari monastic order trained in enhanced combat biotics and weapons, recently sighted on Illium outside normal asari space. On Illium, Liara T'Soni directed Shepard to Tracking Officer Dara, who placed Samara at the commercial spaceport. There, Detective Anaya was investigating the murder of the volus Pitne For's business partner, Dakni Kur, a case Samara was also pursuing. Anaya had been ordered by her superiors to detain Samara, which would likely get her killed.

At the crime scene, Shepard watched Samara interrogate and then kill an Eclipse Lieutenant over the name of a ship carrying a fugitive she was hunting. Samara introduced herself and declined to join Shepard until her own mission was complete. Shepard offered to obtain the ship's name so Samara could leave Illium lawfully, which under the Justicar Code would satisfy her obligation. Questioning Pitne For revealed he had been smuggling the illegal biotic-boosting chemical Minagen X3 and red sand to the Eclipse, who now wanted him dead. Pitne For gave Shepard a pass card to the Eclipse base.

Shepard fought up through the Eclipse hideout, past toxic Minagen X3 canisters, encountering Elnora (a young Eclipse initiate who confessed to killing the volus), an A-61 Mantis Gunship, and the drugged, delusional volus Niftu Cal who believed himself a "biotic god." The mission ended with a boss fight against Captain Wasea, who wielded an M-300 Claymore and hurled Minagen containers; her datapad confirmed the fugitive had left aboard the AML Demeter. Told the ship's name, Samara swore an oath of service to Shepard for the length of the mission, overriding the Code, kneeling with her biotics flaring. Back on the Normandy, Samara was given the Starboard Observation Deck to meditate. Elnora's audio statement could be given to Anaya, and Pitne For's shipping manifest to either Anaya or Pitne For for credits and morality points. Rewards included the M-27 Scimitar Assault Shotgun, platinum, and a Biotic Damage (Hyper-Amp) upgrade. The mission was written by Brian Kindregan.
