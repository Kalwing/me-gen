Luna Shanks headed the personal security detail of Andromeda Initiative founder Jien Garson. Portly and gruff, she possessed considerable physical and biotic strength plus an unexpected diplomatic streak. She first appears in the comic Mass Effect: Discovery, where she dispersed a violent anti-Initiative rally outside the headquarters on Earth with her biotics and helped up a beaten turian who turned out to be seeking to join the Initiative. Garson overheard and had Shanks escort him upstairs; the turian introduced himself as Sanjin Vinnick, and Garson accepted him on condition he retrieve exiled quarian scientist Shio'Leth vas Novarra.

Shanks accompanied Vinnick, starting on the Citadel, where she strong-armed informants toward the lower ward flophouses, finding only a wrecked hovel and an encrypted datapad. Vinnick obtained the coordinates of the quarian strip-mining ship Novarra through military contacts on an encrypted channel, deepening Shanks' suspicion of him. Aboard, they met Captain Jakin and the quarian Viegle, who had known Shio. A biotic salarian, Agent Zeta, attacked the camp hunting Viegle; Shanks shielded Viegle with her body, sent him off with Vinnick, and dueled Zeta, who blasted her off a cliff. She survived clinging to a metal bar, but Zeta slaughtered Jakin's crew and biotically crushed Viegle, who died revealing Shio was his husband and had fled to Omega.

On Omega, Nyreen Kandros blurted Vinnick's true name, Tiran Kandros, her cousin; Shanks drew her Shuriken before being subdued. After hearing him out, she recounted how Garson recruited her from her father's construction company, where she worked as a biotic laborer because he lacked a male heir. In the final confrontation, Shanks armed herself with an Eviscerator and set up a biotic explosion against Zeta, but the salarian impaled her from behind with a metal bar; she emptied her shotgun into his face before he flung everyone aside and escaped. Left incapacitated on a stretcher with some permanent loss of feeling but alive, she urged Tiran not to pursue Zeta to the Kholas Array. He went anyway and later visited her recuperating at a medical facility on Earth.
