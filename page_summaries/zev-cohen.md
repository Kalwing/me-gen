Dr. Zev Cohen was a microbiologist at Noveria's Peak 15 research facility, found in the medical bay at Rift Station tending to fellow scientists beside a Mira terminal that could administer emergency first aid to Commander Shepard's squad. His team had been working on a biotoxin called Thoros-B, derived from an exotic lifeform discovered on the frontier. Because Binary Helix saw no profit in a toxin that killed only one species, Cohen's team engineered it to affect multiple species.

While that work was underway, Mira's core went offline and quarantine was broken. Thoros-B infected several scientists, though it proved not contagious. After the station's doctor was killed, Cohen took over medical duties despite little training, and was also tasked with administering stims to the perimeter guards on request. He grew worried about how stressed Captain Ventralis and his soldiers had become, having been awake for days, but felt he had no choice since the perimeter had to be maintained.

When Shepard's squad arrived, Cohen asked for help producing the biotoxin cure because he could not access the quarantined lab. If Shepard made the cure, the squad was ambushed by Alestia Iallis; Cohen professed to be as shocked as they were that she was an asari commando, explaining that Alestia had recently been transferred to Peak 15 by Saren Arterius. Accused of setting them up, Cohen insisted he abhorred conflict so much he never even fired people personally, leaving that to his receptionist. He believed the guards had been expecting Shepard, having grown more on edge since the team arrived. Grateful for the help, he offered a reward of medi-gel packs, which Shepard could increase by charm or intimidation, and as a project lead he could lend his pass to the sealed maintenance area connecting the quarantine lab to the secure lab.

If fighting broke out between Shepard and Rift Station's guards, Cohen and most other civilians refused further dealings with the squad and would not accept the cure even if completed. If Shepard cleared the Hot Labs first, Cohen and the other civilians went missing; Han Olar suggested Captain Ventralis had them all killed. The game's internal data refers to the character as "Talin Somaai."
