Short resource stub. Scale Fibers was a collectible crafting resource in Mass Effect: Andromeda, consisting of scales layered with a natural fiber similar to spider silk that could be chemically modified into a strong, elastic material. It was obtained by hunting challyrions and rylkors in the wild or by purchase from certain merchants, and was used in a small number of crafted items including the Dhan shotgun, the Naladen and Inferno sniper rifles, the Rozerad pistol, the Soned assault rifle, and the HyperGuardian armor set.
