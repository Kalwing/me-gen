Priority: Thessia followed Priority: The Citadel III and was acquired after speaking with the asari Councilor in the late Donnel Udina's office. The asari, with the Reapers overrunning their systems, revealed an artifact at a temple on Thessia that might reveal the location of the Catalyst. Shepard landed with Liara T'Soni (a mandatory squadmate) and could bring Javik, who added detail about Prothean meddling in asari prehistory.

The team held a barricade with a turret while an asari biotic sealed a breach, destroying a Barrier Engine and fending off Brutes and husks; Lieutenant Kurin then withdrew her forces despite orders to hold. Shepard fought across ruined temple grounds against Marauders, Cannibals, Banshees, a Ravager and Harvesters, aided at points by M-560 Hydra missile launchers and by asari support including sniper Specialist Cayla (her partner Jineva was killed) and the gunships Talon One and Talon Five, both eventually shot down.

Inside the Temple of Athame the science team was found dead. Shepard realized the artifact statue concealed a Prothean Beacon, explaining the asari's advanced development: they had secretly studied Prothean technology for thousands of years. Activating four temple artifacts revealed the beacon, which produced a Prothean VI called Vendetta. Vendetta confirmed the extinction cycle and that the Crucible design had merely been passed down through cycles, then detected an indoctrinated presence and retreated. Kai Leng arrived, identified as the scientists' killer, and delivered a hologram of the Illusive Man, who explained his goal of controlling rather than destroying the Reapers. Shepard fought Kai Leng, depleting his shields three times, but Leng overpowered the squadmates, ordered the gunship to destroy the temple, stole the VI and escaped. Shepard was left listening over comms as Kurin and the asari defenders were overwhelmed.

In the aftermath EDI and Traynor tracked Leng to Horizon, making Priority: Horizon available. Shepard received a taunting email from Leng disguised as asari military command, and could speak with Anderson, Garrus and Joker about the disaster and their families on Palaven.
