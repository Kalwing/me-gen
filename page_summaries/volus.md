The volus were an associate race on the Citadel with their own embassy (shared with the elcor) and a client race of the turians. They hailed from Irune, a world with a high-pressure greenhouse atmosphere supporting an ammonia-based biochemistry, a gravitational field 1.5 times Earth's, and air mixtures poisonous to carbon-based life. Volus therefore wore pressurized suits and breathers around other species; in low-pressure atmospheres their flesh would split open. Because they were never seen unsuited, little was known of their appearance, though they had cloacae like salarians, coppery-blue saliva they could spit, and some could use biotics. Volus genders were treated by the volus as an intrusive and irrelevant question. All volus contracted the disease Yoqtan in childhood as a rite of passage; it killed only the weakest.

The volus were the third race to post a Citadel embassy after the asari and salarians, roughly 2,384 years earlier. Their mercantile prowess made them instrumental in building a stable galactic economy: they authored the Unified Banking Act that established the credit as the standard interstellar currency and continued to monitor and balance the galactic economy. After first contact with the turians during the Krogan Rebellions in the first millennium CE, the volus petitioned for client status within the Turian Hierarchy. Despite their contributions they were never offered a Council seat, a lasting grievance especially for ambassador Din Korlack, because they could not supply fleets, resources, or disaster aid.

Volus culture was dominated by trade of land, resources, and even tribe members. Irune had abolished warfare as an institution of state, with disputes settled by social castigation, bargaining, or economic sanctions. The volus revered the Book of Plenix, which called for charity and forgiveness of debts should they be drawn into war. Volus had two given names but no family names, since claiming to own a person was offensive, and they referred to other races by homeworld ("Earth-clan") and to quarians as "Migrant-clan" or "clanless." Their religion was polytheistic, with deities including Cherk Sab (luck) and Zada Ban (punishment), and a language dialect called Iperian Volus. Their government, the Vol Protectorate, was a client state of the Turian Hierarchy, paying tax and providing auxiliary troops in exchange for military protection and deferring to the turians on foreign policy; it had issued only three writs of exile in its history, two against agitator Gaffno Yap.

Militarily the volus relied on turians for ground defense, favoring airpower and flotillas of heavy-bomber frigates. They produced only one dreadnought, the Kwunu, armed entirely with Thanix weapons and funded by the Elkoss Combine. During the 2186 Reaper invasion, Reaper husks were deployed on Irune, whose cities had been built on trade routes rather than defensible positions; refugees fled to turian colonies, and after the Miracle at Palaven a joint turian-krogan force landed to liberate the planet. Around 3,000 volus joined the Andromeda Initiative aboard the ark Keelah Si'yah, where the Fortinbras Plague, using Yoqtan as a vector, infected some despite their suits, causing them to explode within their armor before a cure was found.
