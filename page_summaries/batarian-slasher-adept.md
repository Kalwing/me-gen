This page contained under ~150 words of real prose and is largely a Mass Effect 3 multiplayer character entry. The framing text noted that little was known about the batarian homeworld Khar'shan, so the species was judged by the batarians the Hegemony let travel abroad: organized crime enforcers, pirate boarding parties, slaver gangs, and gladiatorial combatants. Batarians were described as large brawlers who used nets and spiked weapons to capture or bleed targets, bludgeoning close opponents with spiked armor and enforcement gauntlets. The Batarian Slasher Adept used the Batarian Enforcer class with Warp, Cluster Grenade, Lash, and Fitness. Unlike other Adepts and other batarian characters, it had shields rather than a biotic barrier and lacked Blade Armor, which a senior tester attributed to a possible developer oversight.
