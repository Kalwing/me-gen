X1 was a heavily modified krogan test subject faced by Randall Ezno during his escape in Mass Effect: Infiltrator. It was the Director's oldest test subject, held in the Cerberus space facility known as the Barn. The subject was a krogan that had undergone extensive genetic and cybernetic augmentation: it was several times larger than a normal krogan, with greatly increased forelimb size and muscle mass, and had been fitted with energy weapon gauntlets and integrated shield generators. X1 appeared incapable of speech, and it was unknown whether Cerberus controlled it through indoctrination or whether it had simply gone insane from the experimentation.

X1 was the first major boss of the game. It was extremely durable, with large health and armor bars and strong regenerating shields. Its gauntlet blasters fired weapons similar to those of the Ogre Mech; shooting a blaster made X1 turn around to expose a weak point on its back, and shooting that point dropped its shields by a quarter. Unusually, X1 lost its armor before its shields, which could only be depleted by hitting the exposed back. Once fully unshielded it resorted to charging and melee. High-DPS weapons such as the M-96 Mattock Auto or M-333 Particle Beam were recommended to stagger it. Partway through the fight the Director spoke over a cut-scene as walls closed in to confine Randall and X1, but X1's frenzied charging broke the walls and reopened the arena.
