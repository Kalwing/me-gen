Mission Funds were an alternative currency in the Andromeda Galaxy used by APEX Strike Teams. They were earned by Strike Teams or multiplayer groups only upon successfully completing APEX Missions, and could be spent to purchase new Strike Teams, equip existing Strike Teams, or buy specific equipment in the multiplayer store. They were distinct from Mass Effect: Andromeda's multiplayer Credits, which drew on a separate account from the single-player credits used by Pathfinder Ryder.
