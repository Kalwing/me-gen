Mess Sergeant Rupert Gardner was an operative of Lazarus Cell and the cook and all-around handyman aboard the Normandy SR-2. He was one of the more openly pro-Cerberus crew members, telling Commander Shepard he was "damn proud" of the organization and dismissive of the Alliance and Council. Before joining Cerberus, Gardner had been a family man working element zero rigs along the frontier colonies until batarian raiders cost him everything; he then joined Cerberus to make a difference. He described himself as a jack-of-all-trades who handled any task that "falls through the cracks," including janitorial work and plumbing, and admitted he could not fight but was willing to help however he could.

Gardner asked Shepard to buy high-quality ingredients for the crew's meals, purchasable at the Zakera Cafe on the Citadel; if provided, crew members including Ken, Gabby, and Kasumi Goto commented on the improved food. He offered Shepard a calamari gumbo based on an asari recipe, joking that it seemed mildly cannibalistic for a tentacled race to eat squid but admitting the asari "know good grub."

If Shepard delayed traveling through the Omega 4 Relay after the crew's abduction, Gardner was processed into organic material with the rest of the crew; if the relay was used immediately, he could be saved along with Kelly Chambers and Dr. Chakwas. In Mass Effect 3, if he died at the Collector base his name was added to the Normandy's Memorial Wall; if he survived, his whereabouts were unknown. In an unimported Mass Effect 3 game his name appears on the memorial wall by default.
