Brief game-mechanics page. Rebel Soldier was a passive Combat skill in Mass Effect: Andromeda and the starting Combat skill of the squadmate Jaal Ama Darav. Its six ranks improved Jaal's offensive capabilities, granting bonuses to weapon damage and force, power damage and force, rate of fire and recharge speed, weapon accuracy of effect, and choices such as Suppressive Fire (enemies Jaal shoots deal less damage) or Shredding Rounds (damage over time). Rank 6, offering Momentum or Destabilization, was locked until completion of his loyalty mission Jaal Ama Darav: Flesh and Blood. It was single-player only.
