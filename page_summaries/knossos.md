Knossos was a star system of four terrestrial planets and one gas giant in the Artemis Tau cluster. It held little of note beyond the industries of Therum and the helium-3 collection infrastructure on Archanes. At least two spacefaring cultures had used the system in pre-Council times: the zeioph kept crypts on Armeni, and the Protheans had a presence on Therum. The system's planets were Phaistos (first), Therum (second), Zakros (third), Armeni (fourth), and Archanes (fifth), most named for ancient Minoan cities and monuments.

Therum was the site of the mission Find Liara T'Soni, where Shepard recovered the asari archaeologist Liara T'Soni from Prothean ruins. The system also contained a carbonaceous asteroid where Navigator Pressly discovered an abandoned salarian fighter carrying a League of One medallion, and a metallic asteroid with platinum deposits. The system was likely named for the classical Minoan and later Greek city of Knossos, the oldest of the Greek city-states referenced by the Artemis Tau cluster; cut dialogue from Caleston's plotline stated Knossos was to be the name of the Prothean ruins where Liara was found.
