Short enemy page. Feral Hunters were encountered on 2175 Aeia during Jacob's loyalty mission. They were the shipwrecked male crew members of the MSV Hugo Gernsback who had suffered neural decay from eating the planet's native vegetation. They were armed with assault rifles, shotguns, and SMGs, had no offensive or defensive powers, moderate health, and some shielding on higher difficulties. Shotgun-armed Hunters advanced on the player's position while those with M-9 Tempest SMGs dealt high accurate damage; lacking powers, they tended to stay in cover and fire only when engaging others.
