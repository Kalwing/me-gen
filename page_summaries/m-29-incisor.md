The M-29 Incisor was a sniper rifle in Mass Effect 2, Mass Effect 3, and Mass Effect: Andromeda. It was one of a new wave of military and police sniper rifles designed to overload active defenses such as shields, firing three rounds per trigger pull. In Mass Effect 2 the burst was advertised as so fast that all three rounds struck before the barrel moved a millimeter, keeping accuracy and stopping power high, with the burst's noise no longer than a single shot; later descriptions admit the second and third rounds "climb" under real combat conditions.

In Mass Effect 2 it was initially exclusive to Digital Deluxe editions and later available via the Aegis Pack DLC, accompanied by an email from the Illusive Man saying Miranda had reviewed Shepard's hit-to-kill ratios and requested extra long-range anti-shield firepower; in Legendary Edition it was sold at Rodam Expeditions. Garrus, Legion, Thane, and Zaeed could use it. It dealt lower damage than other sniper rifles but had higher multipliers against shields and barriers, was ammo-inefficient in the original release, and was most effective for squad members, who suffered no recoil or spread.

In Mass Effect 3 it was found during Tuchanka: Bomb and sold at Kassa Fabrication; multiplayer rated it uncommon. It was the only burst-fire sniper rifle, its slower burst now letting enemies dodge later rounds, and was generally considered an unattractive option compared to the lighter, more accurate M-97 Viper, M-13 Raptor, M-90 Indra, and N7 Valiant, with a large spare thermal clip supply as one of its few advantages. In Mass Effect: Andromeda it dropped the M-29 designation and again fired climbing three-shot bursts distributing damage along a vertical line, later patched for reduced recoil.
