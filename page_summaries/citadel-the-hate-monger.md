Citadel: The Hate Monger was an assignment cut from Mass Effect during development and is not canon. Set on the Citadel, it would have had Shepard mediate a conflict between a hanar and a male security guard, each of whom could be spoken to and would air their frustration. The assignment would have ended once the guard was persuaded or the hanar was convinced to stop the "hate". It closely resembled the completed assignment Citadel: Presidium Prophet.
