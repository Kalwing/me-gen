The M-4 Shuriken Machine Pistol was a submachine gun in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, manufactured by the Elkoss Combine. As kinetic barriers grew more common, so did submachine guns; the Shuriken had a reputation for being deadly and easy to use, very effective against shields and biotic barriers but weak against armor. It was a compact machine pistol firing bursts rather than fully automatic fire.

In Mass Effect 2 it fired three-round bursts, could be pulsed for a higher rate of fire at the cost of accuracy, and was automatically granted to all classes except the Soldier during the Freedom's Progress mission. Usable squadmates included Miranda, Mordin, Samara or Morinth, Thane, Kasumi and Liara. Despite poor accuracy it had the second-highest damage and rate of fire among SMGs, and squadmates fired it with perfect accuracy, making it their most effective SMG on higher difficulties.

In Mass Effect 3 it fired six-round bursts with a high rate of fire and was given automatically to Adepts and Engineers at the start of Priority: Mars, otherwise found on a dead soldier there. It was also one of five multiplayer starter weapons, categorized as common. It was lightweight and good for power-focused builds, with fast burst fire and reload, but suffered from high recoil (best mitigated by firing from cover) and low damage that limited it on higher difficulties. Shepard was seen using the Shuriken in many cutscenes regardless of class, making it effectively the default handgun of Mass Effect 2.
