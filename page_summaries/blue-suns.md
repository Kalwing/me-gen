The Blue Suns were a "private security organization" that was in reality a ruthless, discreet, and expensive mercenary outfit operating in the Skyllian Verge. Members bore a blue sun tattoo, removed with acid wash during high-risk jobs. The group was mostly batarian, turian, and human, and used YMIR, FENRIS, and LOKI mechs. It was founded in 2160 by Zaeed Massani and Vido Santiago as a protection racket, Zaeed leading the men and Vido handling administration. A dispute over hiring batarians led to a 2165 power struggle in which Vido seized control and nearly killed Zaeed. Vido then hired batarians freely and installed Solem Dal'Serah as the public head of operations while remaining hidden as "Co-Executive Officer."

In Mass Effect: Revelation (2165), the Suns were hired by Edan Had'dah for the Sidon raid; Saren Arterius killed several, including Groto Ib-ba, during his investigation, and Suns under Skarr captured Kahlee Sanders before dying when an element zero refinery was destroyed. In Incursion (2183) a Suns–Collector exchange of humans on Omega was wiped out by Aria T'Loak. In Redemption the Suns recovered Commander Shepard's body from Alchera for the Shadow Broker but were ambushed by Liara T'Soni and Feron. In Foundation the Illusive Man hired Suns as a containment crew during Subject Zero's rampage.

In Mass Effect 2 (2185) Shepard repeatedly clashed with the Suns: they controlled Omega's Gozu District until the plague weakened their non-human members; they besieged Archangel (Garrus Vakarian) alongside Eclipse and Blood Pack under coordinator Tarak, aided by Sidonis's betrayal, and were destroyed with Tarak's gunship. The Suns ran the Purgatory prison under Warden Kuril, who betrayed Shepard during Jack's recruitment and was killed. Suns officer Jedore funded Okeer's krogan project on Korlus and killed him. On Zorya, Zaeed pursued Vido Santiago through a Blue Suns-controlled refinery. Other operations included Harkin's Citadel racket, Prothean dig-site raids on Joab and Kopis, and pirate raids via the MSV Strontium Mule and Sanctum base. In Mass Effect 3 (2186), with Aria's help Shepard secured the allegiance of the Citadel Blue Suns under Darner Vosque, who had been raiding C-Sec weapons shipments and targeting General Septimus Oraka.
