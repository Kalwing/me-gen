Sniper Rifles was a combat talent in the first Mass Effect, inherent only to the Soldier, Infiltrator, and Turian Agent classes but trainable as a bonus talent by any class. It increased damage and accuracy with sniper rifles, enabled zooming through the built-in scope, and unlocked the Assassination active ability for periodic very powerful shots fired with perfectly stable aim. Across twelve levels the talent raised damage from roughly 5 percent to 20 percent and accuracy from 10 percent to 30 percent, with Assassination unlocking at level 3, Advanced Assassination at level 8, and Master Assassination at level 12.

Assassination boosted the next sniper shot's damage, quickly dispatching enemies that would normally take multiple rounds. While active, the scoped view had no sway and could not be disrupted by incoming fire. The three tiers granted +50 percent, +125 percent, and +225 percent weapon damage respectively, each with a 20-second duration and 45-second recharge. Some enemies, including mercenaries, Geth Hoppers, and Geth Snipers, also used Assassination, telegraphed by a distinct red beam; on higher difficulties or with damaged shields a single such shot could kill the player instantly, so the counter was to take cover, raise a Barrier, use Immunity or Shield Boost, or kill the attacker first. Switching weapons, changing the equipped rifle, or modifying its upgrades cancelled an active but unfired Assassination. Sabotage disabled all special weapon abilities, though an already-charged Assassination shot could still be fired before the weapon jammed. Soldiers and Infiltrators could shorten Assassination's recharge via the Commando specialization.

In Mass Effect Legendary Edition, any class could zoom or scope with any weapon without training and sniper rifles no longer swayed regardless of accuracy. Assassination there purged all residual weapon heat, had a much shorter six-second window, and dealt greatly increased damage calculated from weapon damage-per-second rather than a single shot, making it viable even with rapid-firing rifle models. The three Legendary tiers dealt 200 percent, 250 percent, and 300 percent of weapon DPS.
