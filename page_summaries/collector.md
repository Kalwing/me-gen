The Collectors were an enigmatic race living beyond the Omega 4 Relay in the Terminus Systems, seen on Omega only every few centuries and widely regarded as myth by Citadel citizens. They were known for trading startlingly advanced technology in exchange for living beings in oddly specific numbers and varieties, such as left-handed salarians, batarian twins, or healthy human biotics; the fate of those handed over was unknown. Physically they resembled human-sized bipedal insects with a chitinous exoskeleton, four eyes, vestigial limbs, and wings for short flights, communicating in insectoid chirps but able to speak other languages when needed. Exploring a derelict Collector ship, Commander Shepard's team found the Collectors shared the Protheans' quad-strand genetic structure, revealing them as Protheans subjugated by the Reapers and altered by an "extensive genetic rewrite" with three fewer chromosomes. Aboard the Normandy SR-2, Mordin Solus determined the Reapers had indoctrinated the Protheans and offset their decline with cybernetics, leaving each Collector a mindless clone lacking glands, digestive system, and self-awareness, "closer to husks than slaves."

The Collectors arose in the final years of the Prothean harvest and were deployed against the last Protheans, with Collector Drones destroying stasis pods in the Eden Prime bunkers; only Javik survived. Afterward they withdrew beyond the Omega 4 Relay. Modern sightings began around 1600 CE. In 2173 the quarian Golo tried to sell his own people to them, and the warlord Okeer traded many krogan for tech used to create Grunt. In 2183, weeks after the Battle of the Citadel, Collectors on Omega were attacked by Aria T'Loak; a Collector cruiser then destroyed the SSV Normandy, and the Collectors contracted the Shadow Broker to recover Shepard's body, which Liara T'Soni and Feron secured for Cerberus. By 2185 the Collectors were abducting entire human colonies using Seeker Swarms, hitting at least twelve colonies including Horizon and Freedom's Progress, prompting the Illusive Man to send the revived Shepard to investigate.

Traveling through the Omega 4 Relay, Shepard's team reached the Collector Base near the galactic core and found the Collectors processing tens of thousands of humans to build a Human-Reaper. Shepard destroyed it; as the Collector General died, Harbinger announced it was "releasing control," confirming a Reaper had directed the Collectors all along. Concurrently, James Vega's actions on Fehl Prime yielded intel on the race at the cost of the colony. During the 2186 Reaper invasion the Collectors returned aboard low-velocity transports called Black Arks as reserves, fighting at Palaven and elsewhere and deploying Abominations, Praetorians, and Scions. The Leviathans severed Harbinger's control over some Collectors; most died, but a few "awakened" survivors joined the war against the Reapers to avenge their Prothean ancestors.
