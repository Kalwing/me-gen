"Pureblood" was a term for the child of two asari parents. It was considered negatively charged and offensive, arising from asari biology and outlook. After leaving their homeworld and realising they could reproduce with any species, asari developed the view that mating with other races granted greater insight into that species and had an evolving effect on their offspring and thus the asari race itself. Pureblooded asari, by contrast, were seen by some as wasteful because "nothing has been gained," though saying so to a pureblood's face was regarded as cruel and insulting.

Liara T'Soni discussed her own possible pureblood status with Commander Shepard. Her mother, Matriarch Benezia, never named Liara's other parent; Liara believed her "second parent" was another asari, which she treated as an explanation for Benezia's silence and the public humiliation Benezia would have wished to avoid. Liara found the topic painful and wondered about the identity of her "father." She does eventually discover that her other parent was indeed another asari.

Ardat-Yakshi were asari born with a rare genetic defect preventing them from properly melding with others' nervous systems; in its most severe form it killed anyone they mated with. Ardat-Yakshi could only be purebloods, which the justicar Samara, herself a pureblood, theorized was the origin of the stigma against purebloods. Samara had three daughters, all Ardat-Yakshi: Morinth, Rila and Falere.
