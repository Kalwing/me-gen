Niftu Cal was a volus in Nos Astra on Illium, and a member of Pitne For's trade group. Commander Shepard encountered him in the Eclipse base during Samara's recruitment mission, standing before a vending machine. When he noticed Shepard behind him he waddled into the Commander, then paced back and forth boasting of newfound godlike biotic powers and immortality. Conversation revealed that the mercenaries had pumped him full of biotic-enhancing drugs, probably Minagen X3 or red sand.

Shepard could persuade the "biotic god" to stop fighting and rest; if he refused, Shepard could tap him on the head, sending him off balance into drowsiness, and he would later appear at the spaceport with his wits restored, thanking Shepard. Alternatively, Shepard could encourage him to charge the mercenaries, whereupon he sent a feeble Warp field at Captain Wasea that dissipated in front of her; she retaliated with a biotic blast that hurled him into boxes across the room, never to be seen again. If ignored entirely, he did not reappear at the spaceport. His line "I am a biotic god!" was later referenced in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer through the Volus Adept class description and the "Biotic God" banner.
