Rana Thanoptis was an asari neurospecialist employed by Saren Arterius and assigned to his base on Virmire to study Sovereign's effect on organic minds. She discovered that indoctrination affected not only test subjects such as the captive salarians but also support staff; her first test subject was her own predecessor. She told Shepard that Saren feared indoctrination might be affecting him too. Rana claimed she had little choice working under Saren, that her position had proven more "permanent" than expected, and that she wanted to escape before becoming a test subject herself.

During the assault on Saren's base, Commander Shepard's squad found her hiding under her office desk. She begged for her life and offered information, including access to Saren's private lab where another Prothean Beacon was stored. Once she had divulged what the squad needed, Shepard could either shoot her (she pleaded that she was only following orders) or tell her to start running; if released, she was horrified to learn the facility would be destroyed and fled.

If spared, Rana appeared again on Korlus in 2185, where Shepard sought Dr. Okeer. She had joined Okeer's project, creating a mental imprint routine to educate his tank-bred krogan, though most did not survive. She said Okeer was not curing the genophage but trying to help his people somehow, and revealed she had shut off the facility's security cameras when she saw Shepard approach. Released again, she said she "knows how Shepard works" and intended to get far away, likely referring to the Virmire nuclear detonation. If she survived Virmire, an ANN Report in 2186 stated she had murdered several top asari military officials and then committed suicide; while in custody she reported hearing "voices," a typical symptom of indoctrination.
