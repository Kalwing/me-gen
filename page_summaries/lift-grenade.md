Lift Grenade was a biotic grenade power in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes. Lobbed into a group of enemies, it dealt high damage (450 base, 900 in multiplayer, scaling with rank) over a 5 m radius and sent unprotected enemies floating helplessly into the air like Pull, with the blast penetrating cover and terrain. It inflicted 1.5x damage to biotic barriers, had no recharge time but consumed one grenade from a shared pool, and could detonate all four types of power combo if the target was unprotected. Upgrades increased damage, capacity, duration, blast radius (up to 8 m, among the largest in the game), and a Slam evolution that knocked floating targets to the ground and stunned them. It was available to the Sentinel, Javik, Nyreen Kandros, Urdnot Wrex and Jacob Taylor in single-player, and to the Asari Vanguard and Krogan Sentinel in multiplayer.
