This page transcribed art director Derek Watts's gallery commentary for "The Technology of Mass Effect: Space Vehicles - Turian," from the 2007 Mass Effect bonus content disc. Watts said the turians, a party-member race and a military species, would need fighters, cruisers, dreadnoughts and possibly shuttles and other vehicles, so the team drew quick concepts before the design and writing pinned down exactly what was required. A recurring idea was large armor plates attached to the main hull, like a tank's applique armor, meant to deflect or absorb impacts while the important components sat behind them.

One larger dreadnought-style concept had a front-center cockpit, tall vertical sections that might have carried cannons, and a plated body running back to the engines; another buried the main mass driver behind heavy plating. A hump-backed design was imagined as a transport that could pull ground vehicles up off the surface, drop them off and fly away, though this was never written into the story. Watts described arriving at the final turian cruiser: plates sweeping from a central section to two large wings at the back that could fold up and out during combat, side armor plates to absorb impacts, forward cockpit sections, and a central channel for the mass driver, with protective plates on the engines too. The final turian fighter had rear wings that folded out in atmosphere for stability and slowing, an unresolved cannon placement, and a rear engine block; its shape deliberately echoed the bone structure on the back of the turian head. Only a small model was built and used briefly in cinematics, with the possibility of reuse later in the series.
