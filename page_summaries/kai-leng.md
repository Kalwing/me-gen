Kai Leng was an assassin for the human-supremacist organization Cerberus and the Illusive Man's most trusted wet-work operative. Earth-born of Chinese and Russian/Slavic heritage, he was formerly a lieutenant and N7 marine of the Systems Alliance known for anti-alien attitudes. Alliance records showed he enlisted on November 14, 2172 at age 16 with faked credentials, received the N7 designation in 2176, and that same year was dishonorably discharged and imprisoned for 20 years for first-degree murder after killing a krogan in a Citadel bar fight with a service blade. Cerberus liberated him from prison, and the Illusive Man used him as an assassin for over a decade; his cybernetics included Phantom-class implants, and he fought with a tactical cloak, a gauntlet projectile weapon, a palm kinetic barrier, and a sword resembling a ninjatō.

In Mass Effect: Foundation he was repeatedly paired, to his annoyance, with the agent Rasa on missions involving Fist, the aftermath of Eden Prime, and a failed attempt to capture Jack, during which Jack twice threw him with her biotics. In Mass Effect: Retribution he captured Paul Grayson on Omega, personally killed Liselle, daughter of Aria T'Loak, oversaw the Reaper nanotechnology experiment on Grayson, and proposed dosing him with red sand. When turians raided the facility he chose to rescue the Illusive Man rather than kill Grayson. He later offered David Anderson and Kahlee Sanders a ride off Omega, was tasered and bound by Anderson, escaped at Grissom Academy by enlisting the student Nick, and though shot in both legs by Anderson still escaped after Anderson killed Grayson.

In Mass Effect 3 Leng was deployed during Udina's Citadel coup, where he stabbed Thane Krios or shot a cloaked Major Kirrahe (or assassinated the salarian councilor if both were unavailable), then escaped after erasing footage of himself. He killed scientists at the Temple of Athame on Thessia, seized the Prothean VI Vendetta and its Catalyst data, and leveled the temple with his gunship. He then sent Shepard a taunting message ("The legend of Shepard needs to be re-written... It ends with your death. -KL"). Traynor tracked him to Horizon, where he wounded a Lawson daughter and secured Henry Lawson's Sanctuary research but left behind evidence (or a tracer from Miranda) leading to Cerberus Headquarters. In the Illusive Man's office at Cronos Station he fought Shepard a final time; despite summoning waves of troops he was overwhelmed, and when he attempted a stealth kill from behind, Shepard countered and killed him with an omni-blade, dedicating the kill to Thane, Miranda, or Kirrahe.
