Lift was an unlockable biotic talent in the first Mass Effect, available to Adepts, Sentinels, Vanguards, and the Asari Scientist. Using it caused enemies to float helplessly in the air, making them more vulnerable to attack, with each point spent increasing the force. Master Lift could launch enemies into ceilings for extra damage or out of the valid map area for an instant kill, and worked on nearly any free-standing enemy including a Geth Colossus, though advanced enemies often resisted. Lift's force was added to a target's current momentum, so lifting a charging enemy could fling them into a wall or off a cliff, an effect amplified in low gravity. It combined powerfully with Throw, could pull enemies out of cover, and stopped Geth Hoppers and the final boss from jumping, but had no effect on already-flying enemies such as drones. Ranks progressed from Lift (6 s duration, 60 s recharge) through Advanced Lift to Master Lift (12 s duration, 40 s recharge, 6 m radius).
