Lemm'Shal nar Tesleya, later Lemm'Shal vas Idenna, was a young quarian born aboard the Tesleya who set out on his Pilgrimage from the Migrant Fleet, as told in Mass Effect: Ascension. Capable and fiercely devoted to his people, his most prized possession was a heavily modified Armax Arsenal shotgun given to him by the Tesleya's captain on departure.

Lemm arrived on Omega six weeks into his Pilgrimage seeking adventure, quickly grew disillusioned, and left. On the planet Kenuk he met two quarians from the Bavea, a scout ship of the Idenna, who told him of the Cyniad, another Idenna scout vessel whose crew had gone missing after leaving to broker a deal. Hoping their safe return might make a fitting Pilgrimage gift, Lemm followed the trail back to Omega and, after paying his way past batarians at Fortune's Den, reached the quarian exile Golo. Golo admitted brokering the deal but claimed not to know what the human Pel had done with the quarians afterward. Distrusting Golo, who secretly warned Pel, Lemm attacked Pel's warehouse early and found not the Cyniad crew but four captives: Kahlee Sanders, Paul Grayson, his daughter Gillian Grayson, and the biotic Hendel Mitra. Recognizing Kahlee from the quarians' investigation into Saren Arterius, Lemm rescued all but Paul Grayson.

Shot and badly wounded in the leg during the escape, Lemm had to wear a sealed boot against infection and left his distinctive shotgun behind, which later gave Grayson a clue to the captives' whereabouts. Using the code phrase "the quest for knowledge sent me away from my people; now the discovery of wisdom has brought me back," he brought the humans aboard the Idenna in a stolen shuttle. For delivering Kahlee Sanders, Captain Ysin'Mal vas Idenna admitted Lemm to his crew, renaming him Lemm'Shal vas Idenna. Lemm fought off a Cerberus attack on the Idenna and, despite his age, was chosen for the ship's dangerous five-year mission away from the Fleet. His last task before departure was escorting Kahlee Sanders and Paul Grayson to the colony Cuervo, but Grayson escaped and stunned them both; Lemm woke unharmed on Daleon and agreed to drop Kahlee at the Ascension Project before returning to his crew.
