Avernus Station was a Cerberus research base constructed beyond the Omega 4 Relay after the events of Mass Effect 2, its primary purpose the study of Reaper technology. The events surrounding it were depicted in the comic Mass Effect: Invasion.

At some point a colony of Reaper creatures known as Adjutants broke loose aboard Avernus Station and overwhelmed the personnel. The Adjutants reached the transport ships docked at the station and crossed the Omega 4 Relay to arrive at Omega. Some Cerberus operatives survived the initial outbreak and fled to the station's exterior, hoping the high levels of cosmic radiation there would kill the Adjutants; this proved wrong, and the Adjutants followed them outside. The survivors were saved by the arrival of the Elbrus, a Cerberus warship commanded by General Oleg Petrovsky. Petrovsky and Aria T'Loak assisted the troopers in eliminating the remaining Adjutants and re-securing the station. The station was named after Lake Avernus, a volcanic crater lake in southern Italy that the ancient Romans believed to be an entrance to Hades.
