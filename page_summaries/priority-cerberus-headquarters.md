Priority: Cerberus Headquarters was a Mass Effect 3 mission, acquired immediately after Priority: Horizon and the events at Sanctuary, in which the Alliance assaulted the Illusive Man's base to recover the Prothean VI Vendetta, believed to hold the key to the Catalyst. The base was revealed to be Cronos Station, a space station orbiting Anadius in the Horse Head Nebula. Admiral Hackett warned Shepard over vidcomm that the attack would expose the Crucible to the Reapers and had to be treated as the first phase of the assault on the Reapers themselves; beginning the mission was the game's point of no return, after which side missions, DLC, and the Citadel became inaccessible. EDI was a mandatory squadmate.

Shepard's team fought through the station's hangar bay (stopping Cerberus from venting it into space), rotation and drive sections, and the remains of the Human-Reaper larva from Mass Effect 2. Depending on earlier choices, two named enemies could appear: Legion as a unique "Legion Assassin" if Shepard had turned it over to Cerberus, and an indoctrinated Jack as a unique Phantom if Grissom Academy had not been completed; both had to be killed. Video terminals along the route revealed details of the Lazarus Project, EDI's origin as the Rogue VI, Kai Leng, and Cerberus's Reaper implant research.

In the Illusive Man's inner sanctum, Shepard confronted him by hologram for the third time; Charm or Intimidate options carried consequences for the final confrontation on the Citadel. Vendetta then revealed that the Catalyst was the Citadel itself, but the Illusive Man, having overridden Vendetta's security, already knew this, had fled to the Citadel, and had alerted the Reapers, who seized the station and moved it to Earth. Kai Leng then attacked; after a running battle through the collapsing sanctum against Leng and three waves of Cerberus reinforcements, Shepard killed him, finishing him with an omni-blade after an optional Renegade interrupt. The team returned to the Normandy for a debrief with Anderson, and Priority: Earth became available, with Sol the only reachable system. Rewards included the M-37 Falcon, and the Reaper Heart or Brain War Asset depending on the Collector Base choice.
