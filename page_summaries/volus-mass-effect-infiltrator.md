An unnamed volus was a supporting character in the mobile game Mass Effect: Infiltrator. A skilled computer hacker, he made vidcomm contact with Cerberus operative Randall Ezno after Randall was told that their mutual acquaintance Inali Renata had "volunteered" for a secret project, and he served as Randall's sporadic mission support during the upheavals at the Barn facility.

When Randall inserted a keycard Inali had left at her desk into a nearby terminal, the volus gained control of numerous facility systems and bypassed most of the lockdowns in Randall's path, proving capable enough to outmatch the facility's UDI artificial intelligence. He guided Randall to the Medical Bay, where experiments were being performed on Inali; the events there turned Randall against Cerberus, and the volus told him he could save both himself and Inali by contacting the Alliance. He also alerted Randall to other Cerberus victims held in the facility and urged him to rescue them.

The volus then directed Randall to an old datacore in the Medical Bay, revealing that he himself was a prisoner at the Barn, holed up inside that datacore. Continuing to help from his cell, he guided Randall toward the station's communication tower to contact the Alliance, and hacked UDI again to unlock strategic cells, freeing many asari and krogan test subjects and turning the facility into a warzone as a diversion. When Randall reached the communication tower, the volus arranged a pickup on the nearby desert planet LV426, warned him of geth activity in the sector, and led him to the hangar bay to steal a shuttle. Not wishing to draw attention to himself, he cut communications once Randall was aboard, asking him to take the intel to the Alliance and wishing him luck.
