The Blood Pack Punisher was a submachine gun in Mass Effect 3. It featured a secondary barrel that fired one armor-piercing round for every burst from the main barrel. It was developed by Blood Pack gunsmiths who found that their vorcha recruits frequently failed to optimize ammunition loads in combat; the twin-barrel configuration made armor penetration automatic. In sustained fully automatic fire the armor-piercing round triggered every eight rounds, marked by a backfire effect and a jolt of recoil, and it would not fire in semi-automatic use. The special round dealt five times a normal round's damage with an additional double multiplier against armor, but did not penetrate objects like a piercing mod.

In single-player the Punisher was available after downloading the Mass Effect 3: Firefight Pack, or in Legendary Edition from the Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies terminal in the Presidium Commons for a base 10,000 credits. In multiplayer it was an ultra-rare item-pack reward requiring Mass Effect 3: Reckoning. It was one of the heaviest SMGs alongside the N7 Hurricane and Collector SMG, with lower muzzle climb, better accuracy, more spare clips, and a lower rate of fire than the Hurricane, making it a cheaper option that paired well with Marksman and with turian or quarian classes.
