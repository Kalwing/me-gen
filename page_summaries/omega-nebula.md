The Omega Nebula was a star-forming nebula that contained the Omega space station, the central hub of the Terminus Systems, along with the unmapped Omega 4 Relay. Most of its explorable environs were the territory of criminal elements. Batarian interests controlled much of the resourcing and colonization efforts, and there was a substantial pirate and mercenary presence on various worlds.

Its mass relay connected to the Caleston Rift, Crescent Nebula, Eagle Nebula, Hawking Eta, Hourglass Nebula, Ismar Frontier, Pylos Nebula, Rosetta Nebula, Shadow Sea, Sigurd's Cradle, The Phoenix Massing, Titan Nebula, and Valhallan Threshold. Its systems included Amada, Arinlarkan, Batalla, Fathar, Kairavamori, and Sahrabarik; Sahrabarik held Omega itself, a fuel depot, the Omega 4 Relay, and the planets Urdak, Imorkan, and Bindur. Other notable sites were Alchera and the Normandy crash site, and the MSV Strontium Mule in Arinlarkan.

Many missions and assignments took place there across Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, including Dossier: Archangel, Dossier: The Professor, Dossier: The Veteran, Omega: Aria T'Loak, Samara: The Ardat-Yakshi, and the Mass Effect 3 Omega missions to assist the Hacker, the Mechanic, and Harrot. In real-world astronomy the Omega Nebula was Messier 17 (NGC 6618), also known as the Swan, Checkmark, Lobster, or Horseshoe Nebula, a Sagittarius constellation nebula discovered by Jean-Philippe de Cheseaux in 1745 and roughly 6000 light years from Earth.
