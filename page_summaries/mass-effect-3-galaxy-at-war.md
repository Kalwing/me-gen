Mass Effect 3: Galaxy at War was an optional gameplay system in Mass Effect 3 for managing Commander Shepard's resources in the war against the Reapers. It provided a color-coded strategic map of the galaxy and directly affected the single-player war effort and how the final confrontation played out; the same outcomes were achievable through single-player alone. Access required an Online Pass included with new copies or free to download. The system is not in Mass Effect Legendary Edition, which handles the mechanic through War Assets instead.

Galactic Readiness measured Shepard's influence and ability to use the galaxy's resources; War Assets were the armies, fleets, and supplies acquired in play. Together they set the Effective Military Strength (EMS) rating: at 100% Readiness, EMS equalled all available War Assets, and at the 50% floor, EMS was halved. Readiness could be raised through several interfaces. Co-op multiplayer added roughly 8-9% to a chosen region or 3-4% overall for "Unknown Location," with 2% for a failed post-Wave-5 match; level 20 characters could be promoted to a single-player War Asset worth 75 points each, indefinitely. Mass Effect: Infiltrator on mobile granted the Cerberus Escapees War Asset (up to 120 points, occasionally bugged to 160) and Intel Points toward Readiness. The Mass Effect 3: Datapad iOS app let players deploy fleets for Readiness and receive character messages; removed June 6, 2013 and replaced by BioWare's N7 HQ website, which absorbed the Codex and Galaxy at War mini-game. Readiness decays over time.
