A Fuel Depot was a massive structure used for storing starship fuel, and could be purchased by a ship's captain. Fuel was expended when a ship travelled between systems within a cluster and required replenishing.

In Mass Effect 2, refueling cost 1 credit per unit with a minimum 25-credit charge, and pressing refuel at a depot on the Galaxy Map restored fuel continuously in 25-unit increments until full; refueling could only be cancelled by flying away or leaving the Galaxy Map. Running out of credits while refueling still completed the refuel, leaving a negative balance, while starting with a negative balance provided fuel for free but blocked probe purchases. Depots also sold probes, five at a time for 100 credits, used to find landing sites and extract minerals. EDI gave a verbal warning at half fuel; if fuel was exhausted, the Normandy expended minerals (Iridium or Palladium first, then Platinum, never Element Zero) to fly to the nearest depot, and inter-system travel was blocked until refueling, though mass relays still worked. Depot locations included Aquila, Aralakh, Balor, Chandrasekhar, Dakka, Enoch, Hekate, Imir, Nariph, Osun, Pranas, Raheel-Leyya, Sahrabarik, Skepsis, Tasale, Tassrah, Widow (Firewalker Pack), and Xe Cha.

In Mass Effect 3, many depots had been destroyed by the Reaper invasion; scanning detected destroyed depots yielding 50 to 800 units of recoverable fuel. Intact depots remained in certain systems and still charged 1 credit per unit. Running out of fuel instantly transported the Normandy to the nearest mass relay system and locked non-relay travel until refueling. Docking at the Citadel refueled the Normandy automatically and for free. Intact depots were located in the mass relay systems of the Annos Basin, Apien Crest, Athena Nebula, Krogan DMZ, Horsehead Nebula, Ismar Frontier, Ninmah Cluster, and Silean Nebula. The Art of Mass Effect presented a similar-looking vessel as a turian ship design, also depicted as a turian warship in Mass Effect: Evolution.
