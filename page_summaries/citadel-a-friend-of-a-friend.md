This page contains under ~150 words of real prose. Citadel: A Friend of a Friend was an assignment cut from Mass Effect during development. It would have taken place on the Citadel and would have opposed a doctor with someone named Laz. After an introductory scene, Commander Shepard could talk to the doctor or to Laz and choose a side; taking the doctor's side, Shepard would deal with Laz using Charm or Intimidate, or by paying him off, to complete the assignment. The wiki notes it strongly resembles the shipped assignment Citadel: Doctor Michel, which shares the theme of helping a doctor against a third party on the Citadel.
