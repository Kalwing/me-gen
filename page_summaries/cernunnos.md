Cernunnos was the first planet of the Balor System in the Caleston Rift, a sizable gas giant believed to be an extrasolar capture because of its close orbit to its red dwarf star. Unusually, it lay within the star's life zone yet was too massive to become tidally locked. Its crushing surface gravity ruled out surface life, but its moon Caleston was habitable and bore life. Cernunnos was skimmed for its abundant hydrogen, which refineries on Caleston processed into a metastable metallic form used as starship fuel; by Mass Effect 3 the Reapers had destroyed this operation. Planet descriptions differ slightly between games, with Mass Effect 2 citing a hydrogen-nitrogen atmosphere and Mass Effect 3 emphasizing high nitrogen content. In Mass Effect 2 the planet was reached via the Collector Ship mission or Project Firewalker: Rosalie Lost, and in Mass Effect 3 via the Leviathan DLC; scanning yielded moderate deposits of palladium, platinum and iridium and no element zero. It may be named for the Celtic god Cernunnos. Cut content indicates Cernunnos and Caleston were planned for the first Mass Effect, where Caleston was to be a major location placed in the Ankyra system of the Hades Gamma cluster and referred to as "Planet X."
