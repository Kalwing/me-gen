Priority: Perseus Veil was a short, conversation-only mission acquired after Priority: The Citadel II. Following the failed Cerberus attack on the Citadel, Admiral Hackett told Shepard that the quarians were willing to aid the war effort with technical specialists and support ships, and directed the Normandy to the Far Rim. Shepard travelled to Dholen and docked with the Quarian Envoy Ship.

In the Normandy's war room the admirals of the Migrant Fleet explained that they had launched an open war against the geth, pushing them back toward the quarian homeworld Rannoch, after the Reapers gave the geth upgrades. The admirals asked the Normandy to help disable a geth dreadnought that was broadcasting the Reaper signal.

If Tali'Zorah had survived Mass Effect 2 she rejoined the squad; if she had not been exiled, she had been appointed an admiral in place of her late father Rael'Zorah. Even if exiled, Tali still appeared because of her knowledge of the geth, with the other admirals keeping her presence hidden, prompting Shepard to call her their "dirty little secret." Shepard could speak with Tali privately in the captain's cabin, continuing or locking in a romance, and on a first playthrough this unlocked Energy Drain as a bonus power.

In the aftermath, Admiral Shala'Raan vas Tonbay took up residence in the war room, Tali'Zorah vas Normandy became a temporary squad member, and the mission Priority: Geth Dreadnought became available.
