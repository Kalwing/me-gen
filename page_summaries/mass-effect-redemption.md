Mass Effect: Redemption was a four-issue comic series published by Dark Horse Comics that explored key events leading up to Mass Effect 2. Its narrative picked up just as Commander Shepard disappeared and was left without the crew of the SSV Normandy in the lawless Terminus Systems, with Dr. Liara T'Soni working to recover Shepard alive. The story introduced readers to new aliens, locations, and factions later seen in Mass Effect 2 and had major ramifications for the setting, including Liara's involvement with the Shadow Broker, continued in the Lair of the Shadow Broker DLC. It was written by BioWare lead writer Mac Walters, scripted by John Jackson Miller, drawn by Omar Francia, with cover designer Daryl Mandryk, colorist Michael Atiyeh, and letterer Michael Heisler. Announced July 21, 2009, it was published in print from January 6, 2010, with a trade paperback collecting all four issues released June 9, 2010. Issue #1 was bundled with the Mass Effect 2 Limited Collectors' Edition, and its alternate cover was later offered in a May 2021 bonus content download. Miller stated Redemption did not assume any specific canonical Shepard, accommodating the reader's past choices. The short comic Mass Effect: Incursion linked to the Redemption storyline. In an oversight, turian blood was colored red rather than blue in the comics.
