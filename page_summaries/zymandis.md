Zymandis, whose soul name was Regards the Works of the Enkindlers in Despair, was an indoctrinated hanar diplomat found in the Private Offices of the Citadel Embassies during Mass Effect 3. Years earlier he had taken part in a Systems Alliance raid on a batarian science lab studying Reaper technology, where he was indoctrinated. He subsequently joined the hanar diplomatic staff on the Citadel.

Spectre Jondum Bau's investigation, joined by Commander Shepard, led to Zymandis. He revealed a twisted reasoning: because the Protheans became the Collectors, and the Collectors serve the Reapers, the hanar should likewise serve the Reapers. To that end he attempted to upload a computer virus that would cripple the automated planetary defense systems protecting Kahje, the hanar homeworld.

If Kasumi Goto was present, she disabled the virus while Shepard took out Zymandis's human accomplice, after which Jondum Bau shot and killed Zymandis; the upload was stopped and Kahje saved. If Kasumi was not present, Shepard had to choose between saving Jondum Bau from the accomplice and stopping the virus. Zymandis's face name and soul name reference Percy Bysshe Shelley's poem "Ozymandias" ("Look on my works, ye Mighty, and despair!").
