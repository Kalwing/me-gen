Citadel: Target Jamming Technology was a Mass Effect 3 mission acquired after Priority: The Citadel II and Rannoch: Admiral Koris. A turian C-Sec officer near Apollo's Cafe in the Presidium Commons was overheard over his comm unit asking for target-jamming technology to save lives in future attacks. The mission could alternatively begin during the search for Admiral Koris on Rannoch.

The Jamming Tower Data was retrieved from a terminal on the right side wall of the left AA Gun setup in the AA Gun Control facility on Rannoch; its parent mission became available only after Priority: Geth Dreadnought. If missed, the data could be bought for 1,000 credits, before discounts or markups, at the Spectre Requisitions terminal. Delivering it to the C-Sec officer gave 5 Reputation, 30 experience, and 1,000 credits, and updated the Citadel Defense Force War Asset.
