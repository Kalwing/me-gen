Benning was the second planet of the Euler System in the Arcturus Stream and the nearest garden world to Arcturus Station, serving as that station's primary food supply and an important staging area for starship maintenance and repair. Occupying the planet would be key to any attempt to retake the system's mass relay. In Mass Effect 3 it was reached after the Attican Traverse: Krogan Team or Tuchanka: Turian Platoon missions.

During the battles of Arcturus and Earth, the Reapers sent a small force to Benning to destroy its comm buoys, spaceports, and starships. With the population immobilized and communications cut, the Reapers' main force concentrated on the larger prize, confident they could return before repairs were made or help arrived. Heat sources in Benning's space later indicated a non-Reaper presence.

The codex entry stated that Benning had been a thriving human colony, but the Reapers rounded up a large portion of the populace for processing. Pockets of civilian resistance still eluded the Reapers and rescue attempts were underway. A "resistance radio" established during the early days of the attack continued to broadcast, urging survivors to band together and discussing rumors of safe havens, though those rumors had begun to result in ambushes. Throughout the occupation, Benning's automated agricultural systems kept operating, producing unprecedented stores of food in the planet's granaries and warehouses. The missions N7: Cerberus Abductions and Benning: Evidence took place there, and the multiplayer map Firebase Ghost was located on Benning.
