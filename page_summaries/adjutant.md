Adjutants were hostile, husk-like creatures originating from beyond the Omega 4 Relay. In the comic Mass Effect: Invasion, set in 2186, a Cerberus transport ship returning from a research base beyond the Omega 4 Relay docked with Omega and released dozens of Adjutants onto the station. They attacked everything in sight and advanced to the doorstep of the Afterlife Club, the seat of Aria T'Loak's rule. It was revealed that Cerberus had kept a colony of Adjutants at its Avernus Station research base, where they had broken free and commandeered transport ships to escape. Although the first wave was stopped, more waited on the far side of the relay, learning to pilot better-armed Cerberus ships. Aria and Cerberus general Oleg Petrovsky eventually reached Avernus Station and contained the creatures, but the whole escape had been orchestrated by the Illusive Man to distract Aria so Cerberus could conquer Omega.

Adjutants could "infect" other beings with their Reaper-originated technology, transforming a victim, such as an attacked salarian, into a new Adjutant. A newly transformed Adjutant was significantly weaker while it rewrote its body's genetic code, the ideal moment to kill it; the transformation could otherwise be stopped only by killing the infected or decapitating an infected corpse. Adjutants aimed to transform others rather than kill them, since the dead were useless to them.

Cerberus lost control of the Adjutants shortly after Omega fell, and they rampaged across the station, devastating much of its population and gangs before being contained in a section of Omega's mines. In Mass Effect 3, during Aria T'Loak's campaign to retake Omega from Cerberus, Commander Shepard, Aria, and Nyreen Kandros encountered rogue Adjutants in that mining facility. It was discovered that Cerberus had developed the Adjutants from a Reaper-created virus, believed to date from several cycles earlier, and planned to implant Omega's citizens with control devices and infect them to create an obedient Adjutant army. During the final assault on the Afterlife Club, Petrovsky unleashed perfected Adjutants and a last wave of troops, but Aria and Shepard defeated them. Adjutants were originally designed for Mass Effect 3, also called "Pariahs," but were cut because their planned teleportation ability proved too much for the game's physics.
