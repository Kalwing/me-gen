Warp was a biotic ability appearing in Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, and Mass Effect 3, described in-universe as rapidly shifting mass effect fields that shredded a target apart, functioning like the disruptor torpedoes used by starships and preventing most affected organic targets from healing or regenerating health. This article, distinct from Warp (skill) in Mass Effect: Andromeda, catalogued the power's mechanics across the trilogy.

In the original Mass Effect, Warp was a biotic talent that lowered a target's armor (damage protection), increased damage taken to health, and dealt damage over time that ignored shielding. It was valued on Hardcore and Insanity difficulty against krogan and against Geth Armatures and Geth Colossi, and was the main counter to the Immunity ability. Only one source of damage-protection reduction could apply at a time and effects did not stack, so Warp had to be applied before Overload. Talent ranks scaled from Warp (6 damage per second, 50% protection reduction, 7-second duration) through Advanced Warp and Master Warp (10 damage per second, 75% reduction, 20-second duration). Usable classes were Adept, Vanguard, Krogan Battlemaster, and Asari Scientist. Some asari biotic enemies could cast Warp on Shepard's squad. A bug shared with Throw and Lift could cause Warp to fail against more than one target.

In Mass Effect 2, Warp ripped enemies apart at the molecular level, stopped health regeneration, was effective against armor and biotic barriers, and detonated biotic powers such as Pull or Singularity for combo damage. Ranks 1-3 raised damage from 120 to 160 points; rank 4 evolved it into Heavy Warp (higher damage) or Unstable Warp (larger detonation radius). It dealt double damage to barriers and armor and had a knockback effect against most enemies. It was available to the Adept, Sentinel, Liara T'Soni, Miranda Lawson, and Thane Krios.

In Mass Effect 3, Warp did 1.5x damage against armor, 2x against barriers, but half damage against shields, delivering 75% up front and 25% as damage over time. Rank 6 offered Pierce or Recharge Speed; the Expose evolution increased weapon and biotic power damage taken by a target. Contrary to its description, it did not halt health regeneration, and it had a buggy interaction with Incendiary Ammo. Single-player users were the Adept, Sentinel, Liara T'Soni, Samara, Miranda Lawson, and Jack, plus numerous multiplayer kits.
