Admiral Tadius Ahern was a Systems Alliance officer who commanded Pinnacle Station, a Citadel Council training facility that had once been a turian command center during the Krogan Rebellions. A veteran of the First Contact War, Ahern carried a grudge against turians but still trained turian officers in the station's combat simulations. During the war he had led a near-suicidal mission with a small squad to retrieve a data module against dozens of turian mercenaries; the mission succeeded and left him prejudiced but also gave him a candid appreciation of the strengths and limits of turian military doctrine. He rose to admiral and was given Pinnacle Station because someone who understood how both humans and turians fought was needed, aiming to teach turians to "think outside the box."

In Mass Effect, Ahern commented sarcastically on Commander Shepard's performance through the simulator tiers, offering back-handed compliments even after Shepard set new records. Once Shepard completed every combat scenario, Ahern unlocked a final special scenario: a recreation of his own First Contact War module-recovery mission. Shepard could take it on with the safeties off as a wager, with Ahern staking his retirement home on Intai'sei against Shepard's life. He was reluctant to risk killing the first human Spectre in his simulation. If Shepard won, Ahern honored the bet, gave up the apartment and allowed Shepard to simply call him "Ahern." A computer in the apartment let Shepard order random, sometimes very rare weapons, armor and upgrades from a local trade convoy across three price tiers. His quoted line was, "It's better than a desk job, and a hell of a lot better than retirement."
