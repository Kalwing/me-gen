UNC: Valuable Minerals was a collection assignment in Mass Effect tied to the Systems Alliance's need for resources to maintain its fleets and expand into the Traverse. The assignment appeared in the journal after Commander Shepard surveyed the first mineral deposit. Resources were located by scanning planets from the Galaxy Map and by landing to conduct surface-level surveys of mineral deposits, the latter requiring a bypass minigame. Each find rewarded both experience and credits, scaling up as the game progressed. After discovering all the required minerals, Shepard received the "UNC: Survey is complete" journal entry. The page otherwise consists of location tables.
