Asteria was the first planet of the Hekate System in the Hades Nexus cluster of the Milky Way, reachable in Mass Effect 3 after Priority: Rannoch. It was a habitable world known for arid, sulfurous deserts, with southern latitudes reaching temperatures around 65 degrees Celsius. To avoid this heat, colonists settled near the poles. Its seas held primitive animal life, but little of that life could survive on land, leaving the soil to hardy heat-tolerant plants. Asteria supported thriving human and asari agrarian colonies but had little manufacturing or mining industry. A travel advisory warned that atmospheric carbon dioxide concentrations could reach 2,500 parts per million, recommending supplemental oxygen for children and the elderly. An Alliance bulletin prohibited civilian traffic in the Hekate system after geth were encountered there. The Alliance frigate Hong Kong could be found at Asteria as part of the Search and Rescue operation. Asteria had been referenced in Mass Effect 2 materials and the codex entry for the neighboring planet Bothros, but it only appeared on the galaxy map in Mass Effect 3.
