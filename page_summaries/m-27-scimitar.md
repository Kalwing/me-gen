The M-27 Scimitar Assault Shotgun appeared in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, manufactured by Ariake Technologies. It featured twin mass effect generators that gave it a more rapid rate of fire than a traditional shotgun. Originally created for the Eclipse mercenary band, it became popular with Blood Pack mercs as well. It was deadly at short range but ineffective at long range, effective against shields and biotic barriers but weak against armor, and was likely named after the sword. In the first Mass Effect the Scimitar line had been made by Elkoss Combine, and the change to Ariake in Mass Effect 2 is regarded as an error.

In Mass Effect 2 it upgraded the Katana and was found during Samara's recruitment mission on Illium, in the mercenary base, before the Gunship fight, and after the Niftu Cal encounter near Captain Wasea; if missed it was awarded at mission's end. Grunt, Jack, Jacob, and Tali could use it. Its high fire rate and large magazine gave it the highest theoretical damage-per-second of any shotgun, but only with sustained near-point-blank fire; it lacked the Eviscerator's range and struggled against armor without incendiary ammo, though it excelled against masses of Husks.

In Mass Effect 3 it was found during Priority: Palaven in the turian base camp and sold at Kassa Fabrication; multiplayer rated it uncommon. One of the lightest shotguns, it had an eight-shot magazine suited to sustained combat, an X-shaped spread best tightened with the Shotgun Smart Choke, and low per-shot damage. It paired exceptionally well with the Marksman power and was comparable to the N7 Piranha, trading raw damage and reserve ammo for a tighter spread.
