The Kholas Array was a gigantic geth structure composed of three primary mass relays joined together with "strange" technology, described in Mass Effect: Discovery. Using sensors in the combined relay corridor as a form of faster-than-light "telescope," the array could observe intergalactic distances in almost real time. The Migrant Fleet considered it a tall tale.

The array was accidentally discovered on the fringes of the Perseus Veil by Shio'Leth vas Novarra during his Pilgrimage. The geth aboard showed no interest in other galaxies; they appeared to be searching for something in dark space that Shio could not identify. Shio pointed the array at Andromeda hoping to find a new home for quarians, downloaded the recorded information, evaded the hostile geth, and shared the data with the Andromeda Initiative.

A flaw in the initial observations forced Shio to return to Kholas with Tiran Kandros to correct the information. Agent Zeta, a salarian biotic who had incorporated technology similar to the array's into his body, also traveled there after coercing the location from Shio, hoping to stave off the physical and mental degradation caused by his own biotic power. Despite heavy geth resistance, Shio and Tiran obtained what they needed and escaped. Agent Zeta succumbed to his power and suffered a biotic meltdown; the extent of any damage to the structure was unclear.
