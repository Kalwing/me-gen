Elnora was an asari living in Nos Astra on Illium and a member of a local Eclipse gang. Commander Shepard could encounter her hiding behind a desk in the Eclipse base during the mission to recruit Samara. Despite wearing the Eclipse insignia, Elnora claimed to be a new recruit who had joined because she liked the idea of being "Elnora the mercenary," then grew disillusioned and had only pretended to shoot at Shepard in the earlier firefight. The story was a ruse to lower the team's guard while she drew her gun. Shepard could shoot her immediately or wait for her to stand down. If spared, she was ordered to leave, and could first be questioned about an Ardat-Yakshi and the ship her fellow mercenaries used to smuggle that individual off Illium.

Shepard may earlier have learned that the local police under Detective Anaya were investigating the murder of volus merchant Pitne For's business partner. After dealing with Elnora, Shepard could examine an audio log revealing her excitement at becoming a full Eclipse member and that she had murdered a volus the night before as part of her initiation, taking sadistic satisfaction in the kill and anticipating her friends' envy. Shepard could bring this evidence to Detective Anaya: if Elnora had escaped, Anaya promised to track her down; if she had been killed, Anaya expressed relief that Elnora's path of violence had been cut short.
