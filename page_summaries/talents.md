Talents were the combat skills and abilities of the first Mass Effect, the equivalent of the "powers" of the sequels. Each class had twelve talents: four starting talents and eight unlockable ones, categorised as biotic, tech or combat. Passive talents could unlock further active abilities (for example, Fitness unlocked Immunity), and Shepard could unlock a Specialization Class to extend a base class talent to ranks 7 through 12.

Players received 100 talent points across character levels 1 to 60, plus two automatically assigned points; NPC squadmates received 80. Starting talents were capped at character level plus one until level 11, when they could be maxed with 12 points; unlockable talents had a similar cap tied to the level at which they became available. Shepard also gained free points in Charm and Intimidate for advancing the Paragon and Renegade meters, and extra training on becoming a Spectre. Point assignments became permanently irreversible once a character's Squad menu screen was closed, and Shepard's choices carried across future playthroughs, though squadmate points reset with each new game. The original release capped a first playthrough at level 50; Legendary Edition removed the cap and improved experience scaling.

Class talents shared their names with the six playable classes (Soldier, Engineer, Adept, Sentinel, Infiltrator, Vanguard); Ashley Williams shared the Soldier talent and Kaidan Alenko the Sentinel talent with variations, while other squadmates had unique class talents (Quarian Machinist for Tali'Zorah, Asari Scientist for Liara T'Soni, Krogan Battlemaster for Urdnot Wrex, Turian Agent for Garrus Vakarian). Specializations unlocked after the mission UNC: Rogue VI on Luna, offering two options per class (such as Commando or Shock Trooper for soldiers). Active abilities unlocked from talents included Assassination, Carnage, Marksman, Overkill, Adrenaline Burst, Immunity, Shield Boost, Unity, AI Hacking, Neural Shock, Overload and Sabotage. When creating a second character, players could add one of fifteen bonus talents unlocked via achievements, usable regardless of class prerequisites but without class bonuses. Two talents, Rout and Camouflage, appeared in text files as cut content.
