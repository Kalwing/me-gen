The MSV Strontium Mule was a three-deck freighter commanded by Captain Jarrett Barnes, encountered as a derelict in the Arinlarkan system of the Omega Nebula in Mass Effect 2, following the N7: Archeological Dig Site assignment. The ship answered a distress signal that proved to be a Blue Suns trap; the crew escaped with heavy damage to the ship's FTL drives and comm arrays, but two weeks later the Suns captured the vessel and killed the crew, mostly in their own quarters. Before his death Barnes recorded a final log including encrypted authorization codes to the ship's most valuable cargo; Blue Suns Captain Vorhess recovered the codes from the black box and began decrypting them in anticipation of a large payout.

When Commander Shepard's squad boarded through the cargo-hold emergency hatch, the Blue Suns' ambush failed. Recognizing Shepard, Sergeant Boortis refused to reinforce Vorhess on the bridge in a bid to reduce the number of payout beneficiaries, but both Vorhess and Boortis's men were killed, leaving the ship's assets to Shepard. The associated task was the assignment N7: MSV Strontium Mule.
