The Adjutant was an enemy encountered in Mass Effect 3, specifically in the Omega DLC. "Adjutant" was the Cerberus codename for experimental subjects created at a secret facility beyond the Omega-4 relay. The creatures were engineered from Reaper technology to serve as obedient pack hunters and shock troops, and the Adjutants found on Omega could infect any life-form to create a new Adjutant.

Adjutants operated as pack hunters. They initially remained hidden, observing potential victims and distracting them with subtle whispers before ambushing a chosen target. Their charged biotic projectiles, similar to a Singularity, detonated on impact into a lingering vortex that created a violently unstable mass effect field, causing severe disorientation and leaving the target helpless. Enhanced muscle tissue let them leap surprising distances to close in and infect a target quickly, and at close range they lunged and swiped like Brutes. Defensively they had above-average armor and barriers, could sidestep power projectiles like geth infantry, took extra damage from headshots, and carried fragile blue sacs on their backs that burst under gunfire for additional damage.

The baseline form of the creature matched no known species. Xenobiologists suspected the Reapers had originally created and deployed the underlying virus several cycles before the Prothean extinction, meaning it still carried genetic traces of its original host. If true, the Adjutants, like the Collectors, were a glimpse of a long-forgotten species and its struggle against the Reapers.

In gameplay, the power Dominate worked on the Adjutants in the Mines segment of the mission Omega: Aria T'Loak, but not on those unleashed during the final battle in Afterlife. Adjutants were one of the few enemy types that could not be fought in the Armax Arsenal Arena combat simulator. In military terminology, an adjutant is a staff officer who assists a commanding officer in issuing orders.
