The Possessed Captain was a Collector enemy unit encountered in Mass Effect 3, an enhanced variant of the standard Collector Captain that appeared once Collector forces were reactivated as Reaper thralls. It served as a "commander" infantry type comparable to the Marauder and Centurion, but was tougher and more aggressive than either.

Offensively, Possessed Captains carried Collector Assault Rifles and could release Seeker Plagues, swarms that damaged targets and blocked their use of powers. They attacked in melee with organic blades, striking twice per attack, and could instantly kill downed players with a stomp. They tended to advance straight toward opponents while firing long bursts. A batarian's Blade Armor could stagger a Captain after its first melee blow, denying the second and giving batarians a close-quarters advantage against Collectors.

Defensively, the Possessed Captain had biotic barriers twice as strong as a normal Captain's and roughly twice the health, described as the largest health bar of any enemy in the game. It took cover during combat, could be staggered by gunfire or powers when its barriers were down, and took 40% additional damage from headshots. Because its only protection layer was barriers, biotic characters and Overload could eliminate it quickly. It was most vulnerable while generating a Seeker Plague, doubling over helplessly for several seconds, though the finished Plague could then block incoming shots and powers.
