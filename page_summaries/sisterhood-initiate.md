Sisterhood Initiates were exclusively asari members of the Eclipse mercenary organization in Mass Effect 2, encountered only on Illium during Samara's recruitment mission. They closely resembled standard Eclipse Troopers but could use biotic barriers instead of shields on higher difficulties. They were armed with M-8 Avenger assault rifles for sustained fire and carried only light barriers, making them a minor threat; powers such as Concussive Shot, Warp, and Warp Ammo were effective for stripping their barriers. Their asari-only, Illium-only nature suggested that Eclipse recruits from asari-owned or asari-dominated worlds joined an Eclipse "sisterhood" rather than the mainstream organization, or that the arrangement was specific to Illium.
