Project Engineers were enemies encountered during the Mass Effect 2 assignment Arrival, attacking Commander Shepard throughout Project Base. They first appeared during the Object Rho sequence in up to five waves alongside Project Guards, Elites and Pyros, and later backed up larger Project forces.

They were armed only with M-6 Carnifex heavy pistols, relying mainly on a powerful Incinerate that could stun Shepard and leave the Commander exposed. They had good shielding on all difficulties, tougher on Hardcore and Insanity, and used Tech Armor to restore shields. They performed like Eclipse Engineers, usually staying back in cover and popping out to fire Incinerate; effective counters included closing to short range where they were fragile, area powers and weapons such as Singularity or the Arc Projector, and detonating a nearby Project Pyro. They were noted for calling out "Stop, drop, and roll!" when launching Incinerate.
