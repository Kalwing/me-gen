This page catalogued the combat abilities available to Randall Ezno in the mobile game Mass Effect: Infiltrator. Randall could buy skill upgrades from the in-game store using credits, across three categories: Abilities, Biotics, and Melee. The turian protagonist of the bonus mission could use some of these abilities but could not upgrade them, and was not a biotic.

The Abilities category held three powers. Sprint used stimulator conduits adapted from Sirta Foundation technology to briefly increase speed. Slide used Cassharn slide stims for a speed boost to move below enemy fire into cover or knock enemies' legs out; Randall was invulnerable during a Slide, making it useful in boss fights, and upgraded versions could knock down most enemies. Cloak used light-refracting mass effect fields to render Randall invisible to visual and technological detection, replenished his shields while active, and, unlike the main series' Tactical Cloak, did not break when he fired a weapon or used another ability. The Stealth Suit armor cut Cloak's recharge time by 10 percent.

The Biotics category held four powers. Pull levitated enemies and drew them helplessly toward Randall, stripping Riot Trooper shields and useful against enemies in cover, though bosses such as Geth Juggernauts, Grad Turrets, and Ogre Mechs were immune. Leash disabled an enemy and flung it with a force of 600 Newtons, instantly killing Riot Troopers and enemies thrown into pits. Storm created a persisting sphere of destruction that damaged enemies over time and was effective against stationary targets like Grad Turrets. Salvo fired multiple target-seeking projectiles, effectively turning the user into a mobile missile launcher; Inali Renata was the only other character who could use it.

The Melee category held four moves. Punch, powered by Kestrel cybernetic implants reinforced with synthetic spider silk, knocked enemies off their feet when Randall was close and out of cover. Push knocked down enemies from cover using beryllium-tungsten alloy bracers. Charge stormed through humanoid enemies using sub-dermal capacitors and dealt extra damage to downed foes.
