Blue Suns Pyros were flamethrower-equipped troops fielded by the Blue Suns mercenary organization, encountered by Commander Shepard's squad on the planet Zorya during Mass Effect 2. They engaged at short range using the M-451 Firestorm flamethrower. On normal difficulty they carried no defensive layers, but on higher difficulties they gained shields. Turian, human, and batarian personnel filled the Blue Suns ranks generally, though the Pyro role emphasized aggressive close assault.

In combat a Pyro advanced toward its target rather than taking cover, seeking to close the distance and bring its flames to bear. A squad member caught in the flames staggered backward and became vulnerable to follow-up attacks, so the recommended approach was to keep distance and shoot the Pyro down while retreating. Because the Pyro refused cover and pushed into open ground, it was straightforward to isolate and neutralize when unsupported.

The signature weakness was the fuel tank carried on the Pyro's back. Well-placed shots could rupture the tank, and once the Pyro was unshielded, tech powers such as Overload and Incinerate detonated it instantly, producing an explosion that damaged and knocked down nearby units regardless of allegiance, comparable to triggering an explosive environmental object. Most weapons could rupture the tank once shields were gone, with accurate weapons or the Arc Projector cited as most effective. Very high single-shot weapons, such as the M-98 Widow or the M-92 Mantis loaded with Disruptor Ammo, could break the remaining shield and set off the tank in one hit on higher difficulties; the Arc Projector could achieve the same on most difficulties without needing a direct hit on the tank.
