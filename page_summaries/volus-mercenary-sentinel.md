The Volus Mercenary Sentinel was a playable class in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer. Its background reiterated that the volus homeworld Irune had a high-pressure atmosphere supporting an ammonia-based biochemistry, requiring volus to wear pressure suits and breathers or specially sealed combat armor.

The class had 500 shield and 150 health under Volus Training, with the powers Decoy, Combat Drone, Shield Boost, and Fitness. Its regular melee triggered a tactical cloak lasting three to four seconds on a separate cooldown; its heavy melee (Defensive Stance) rooted the character and generated a damaging protective shield sphere that pulsed brief invulnerability and restored some shields. It could not grab, could not take formal cover (only "soft cover"), had reduced visibility from its low height, could dodge with a combat roll, and could not be grabbed by Husks or Abominations.

The Volus Mercenary Sentinel was noted as the only class in the game with no direct offensive power; its only damage came from its weapon, Combat Drone, and an evolved Decoy (Shock or Exploding Decoy). Because it was less power-dependent than the other volus classes, it could carry somewhat heavier weaponry. Its battlefield role was pure support: using Decoy and Combat Drone to distract or lure enemies away from allies while staying hidden through Tactical Cloak and its small profile. Combining an area-of-effect weapon with Decoy's Shock evolution and Combat Drone's Shock and Chain Lightning evolutions could indefinitely stagger and lock down large enemy groups. Shield Boost recharged all nearby allies' shields (roughly 1,100 plus about 220 per second for three seconds), could grant 50 percent damage reduction for six seconds, and conferred about a second of invulnerability on activation, making the class a stronger medic than the Geth Engineer. The Acolyte was recommended to compensate for the lack of anti-shield powers.
