The Geth Colossus was the largest geth ground unit, similar to the Geth Armature but much larger, with more health, stronger armor, and better weaponry. Its armor was brighter, a platinum-silver color with ridges on the back. It was equipped with mass accelerator machine guns and a powerful Siege Pulse mass accelerator cannon, and could fire the Siege Pulse without facing its target. In Mass Effect it was a monumentally dangerous foe almost always encountered from within the M35 Mako; effective tactics included whittling it down with the Mako's guns at long range, circle-strafing, ramming it to stun it, or engaging on foot with Shield Boost or Barrier active plus Tungsten Ammunition, Warp, Lift, and Sabotage.

In Mass Effect 2, one Colossus served as the level boss at the end of the mission to recruit Tali'Zorah vas Neema on Haestrom; this unit was Armature steel-gray rather than white, and the quarian marine Kal'Reegar called it an "Armature-class" unit. It had very strong shielding and armor, homing main-gun shots, and would collapse into a self-repair mode to regenerate shields (though armor damage was permanent). Its Siege Pulse no longer killed in one hit even on Insanity. It was immune to Stasis and could target Shepard through Tactical Cloak. Recommended counters included the M-920 Cain, the Arc Projector, Incinerate, and Warp. Multiple weaker Colossi lacking the self-repair mechanism appeared in the Firewalker Pack, fought with the M-44 Hammerhead, which could ram and topple them.
