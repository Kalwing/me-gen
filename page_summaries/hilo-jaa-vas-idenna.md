Hilo'Jaa vas Idenna was the quarian pilot of the Cyniad, a scout ship of the Migrant Fleet cruiser Idenna, in 2183. He piloted the Cyniad to a rendezvous with the quarian criminal Golo on the planet Shelba, but the meeting was an ambush orchestrated by the human Cerberus agent Pel. Hilo's crew was killed and he was captured, intended to provide the access codes Cerberus needed to reach the Migrant Fleet.

Golo, out of spite, cracked Hilo's faceplate after Hilo addressed him as "Golo vas Nedas" and called him a traitor. With his environmental suit compromised, Hilo contracted several severe alien diseases and fell into a fever, complicating Pel's interrogation on Omega and leading Pel to resort to brutal torture. When Pel received a better offer from the Collectors, Hilo was abandoned and left for dead in Pel's warehouse. Another Cerberus agent, Paul Grayson, found him delirious and repeating the Cyniad's pass phrase. After giving Grayson the Idenna's frequency, 43223, and the pass phrase "My body travels to distant stars, but my soul never leaves the fleet," Hilo'Jaa vas Idenna died of his sickness and injuries.
