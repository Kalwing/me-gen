Priority: Palaven followed Priority: The Citadel I. Primarch Fedorian of the Turian Hierarchy had called a war summit that would let the Alliance request help building the Crucible, but Fedorian was caught in the Reaper attack on Palaven. Shepard travelled to Menae, Palaven's moon, with Liara T'Soni and James Vega to extract him. The Kodiak made a hot landing at a turian forward camp under assault by husks, the first sustained fighting against Reaper ground forces.

General Corinthus informed Shepard that Fedorian was already dead. To learn who the new Primarch was, Shepard's team repaired a comm tower overrun by husks, defending it in waves while one squadmate made repairs. Garrus Vakarian, if he had survived Mass Effect 2, made his first appearance here, joining the squad in place of Liara after Joker reported Normandy system malfunctions. Corinthus revealed that General Adrien Victus was next in the line of succession. Shepard's team then defended an airfield and a barricade against husks, Marauders, Cannibals and a Brute, using a mounted turret, before advancing down a long path past crashed turian frigates and aircraft toward Victus's last known position.

Shepard found Victus's position under heavy attack, fought through Cannibals, Marauders and multiple Brutes (a Reaper Blackstar heavy weapon was available), and reached Victus, ending the mission. Victus reluctantly accepted the role of Primarch and returned with Shepard to the Normandy, taking up residence in the war room. In the aftermath, Joker reported a fire in the AI Core: EDI had taken control of Dr. Eva Coré's mech body, becoming a mobile squadmate stationed in the cockpit. The mission Priority: Sur'Kesh became available, Garrus joined the crew to run the main battery, and the Grissom Academy mission and several Citadel areas and DLC missions opened up.
