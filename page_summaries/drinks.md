The page catalogs beverages consumed across the Mass Effect galaxies, spanning sustenance, personal preference and social lubrication. It lists dozens of named drinks with their species associations, effects and in-game contexts rather than telling a single story.

Alcoholic entries include Akantha, a popular asari liquor with a smoky flavor that cuts hangovers; asari honey mead, brewed in an Ardat-Yakshi monastery and the most expensive liquor in Aria T'Loak's bar; batarian ale, a potent green drink; Serrice Ice Brandy, a 1,000-credit favorite of Dr. Chakwas; turian brandy, which Tali'Zorah drinks through an "emergency induction port" (a straw); Elasa, a Thessian drink nicknamed "Sorrow's Companion" that Anderson drank in Chora's Den during his 2165 divorce; horosk, a hard turian drink strong enough to induce comas; ryncol, an extremely potent krogan liquor; and Noverian Rum, which Aria lamented being unable to obtain. Species-specific intoxicants include the hanar's mindfish (a hallucinogenic skin oil), the angaran marljeh and tavum, the volus bina, and krogan drinks such as burukh, sovak juice (used to name newborns via belching contests) and "liquefied turian," a reverse-chirality dare drink with adverse effects on all who tried it. Methanol is safely ingested by some species and mined by Terminus pirates on Antirumgon.

Cocktails feature heavily, many mixed by Samantha Traynor during Shepard's Citadel party using her college bartending knowledge: the Full Biotic Kick, Quad Kicker, Tasty Tankard, Frozen Pyjak (made from a bartender's spill pad and ice), Perfection, and the Weeping Heart (a martini with mild drell-skin venom). Umi Henon's concoctions in Andromeda, including Combat Juice and an angaran wine and ryncol mix, caused blackouts and wild dancing in Ryder. The Vortex on the Nexus served the Dirty Squirrel, Lucky Leprechaun, Tall Moose and Rotten Scoundrel via bartender Dutch Smith.

Non-alcoholic entries include coffee, credited by SAM for its own existence through Alec Ryder's consumption; soda; and sports drinks Tupari (with an aggressive marketing campaign claiming it revives ancestors) and its rival Paragade. Wine entries cover champagne, Thessia Red (the Illusive Man ordering a "'47"), and various bottles Anderson purchased. Whiskeys named include Mount Milgrom scotch, one 645-year-old bottle of which reached Andromeda and could be stolen by Reyes Vidal from Sloane Kelly's storeroom. Mass Effect: The Official Cocktail Book (2024) collects real-world recipes for many of these drinks.
