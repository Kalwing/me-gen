Amul Shastri was the Prime Minister of the Systems Alliance from at least 2185 until 2186. The date he took office and the length of his tenure were never recorded. By 2185 he had been involved in a public dispute with the CEO of a media company, who launched a campaign of mocking viral videos against Shastri until the Prime Minister backed down. Later that year Shastri met a salarian dalatrass during the salarian holiday of Betau and exchanged gifts with her; aware of his love of flying, she gave him a classic Tess Auburn 2166 aircar. He also attended the opening ceremonies of a public memorial and museum on Eden Prime after Earth's Council for the Preservation of Human History and Culture declared the colony a culturally significant site.

On August 24, 2186, Shastri addressed reporters at a news conference concerning a leaked report that appeared to support Commander Shepard's claims that the warship Sovereign, destroyed during the Battle of the Citadel, was not of geth construction but was one of many hostile machine intelligences. Shastri waved off questions, stated that the geth were not the only threat in the galaxy, and assured the public that the Alliance was prepared for anything.

Later in 2186, during the Reaper invasion, Shastri was killed along with the entire Alliance Parliament when Reaper forces destroyed Arcturus Station. With his death and the loss of the Parliament, Councilor Donnel Udina became the de facto political leader of the Alliance.
