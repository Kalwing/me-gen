Admiral Ravis Kandros was a high-ranking official of the Turian Hierarchy, a member of the prominent Kandros family, and the mother of Lieutenant Tiran Kandros. In Mass Effect: Discovery she organized a meeting between Tiran and other Hierarchy officials to discuss a possible link between forbidden geth technology and the Andromeda Initiative. Commander Narkkin dismissed it as a mother pulling favors for her son, but others recognized that the Initiative's scope resembled the establishment of a sizeable private military force. Narkkin pushed for direct intervention; Ravis and Tiran instead convinced the group to let Tiran infiltrate the Initiative to gather evidence first.

Ravis privately asked Tiran whether he was committed. He wanted to prove himself worthy of the Kandros name without leaning on her reputation, and she shared that she had once wanted to escape her own mother's shadow, telling him he had already earned his place but to come back alive. During the mission Tiran drew on Ravis's intelligence resources via encrypted channels, obtaining coordinates for the ship Novarra while seeking a quarian named Shio'Leth vas Novarra, and later a dossier on the ex-STG salarian Agent Zeta. Once the Hierarchy learned of Zeta's involvement, Ravis tried to pull Tiran out, judging the risk no longer worthwhile; Tiran insisted he was close to a breakthrough, and she relented while warning of a long discussion on his return. After Zeta's escape from Omega, Ravis informed Tiran he had been declared AWOL and accused of gross dereliction of duty, warning he would be arrested if he continued.

When Tiran turned himself in, Ravis and the two other officials from the original meeting served as judges at his trial. Because he had already declared himself guilty, he was discharged without honor, effective immediately. Ravis initially disapproved of his choice to join the Initiative for real, suggesting he remain a civilian contractor, but came to accept it, embracing him before his departure and regretting only that she would never get to read the chapter of Kandros history he intended to write.
