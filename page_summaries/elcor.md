The elcor were a Citadel species native to the high-gravity world Dekuuna. Massive quadrupeds standing on four muscular legs for stability, they moved slowly as an evolved response to an environment where a fall could be lethal, which made their psychology deliberate and conservative. They were incredibly strong with thick, tough skin and highly attuned olfactory senses, and had at least three stomachs. Their "lips" were multiple vertical slats. Elcor speech sounded to other species as a flat monotone; among themselves, scent, slight body movements and subvocalized infrasound conveyed nuance, so elcor prefixed all dialogue with non-elcor with an emotive statement to clarify tone. They lived for centuries, with a hundred years considered young and around four hundred old.

Prehistoric elcor traveled Dekuuna in tribal groups led by the oldest members, which developed into a culture of Elders. The elcor were making their first forays into space when the asari made contact; with asari help they reached the nearest mass relay and established Citadel trade within a lifetime, becoming a prominent Citadel species, though they still shared an embassy with the volus. Their government, the Courts of Dekuuna, followed detailed recorded precedents compiled by Elders, making elcor policy highly predictable and their government extremely stable. Their economy was small but well developed and self-sufficient, trading only in finished goods.

Because their slow psyches made them poor at split-second combat decisions, elcor relied on sophisticated VI combat systems. Elcor warriors carried no small arms; their broad shoulders served as platforms for vehicle-scale weapons, earning them the enemy nickname "living tanks." They maintained only a small fleet, mostly for troop transport. Elcor culture prized the arts, including an award-winning all-elcor 14-hour production of Hamlet, and revered the Code of the Ancients. During the 2186 Reaper invasion the elcor navy was decimated defending Dekuuna, trapping many elcor on the homeworld; some survivors escaped if Shepard sent help, and elcor military remnants fought on through the final battle on Earth. The quarian ark Keelah Si'yah carried 3,311 elcor toward Andromeda.
