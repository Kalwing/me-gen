Elite Prison Guards were enemies encountered in the Mass Effect 2 assignment Arrival. After Commander Shepard freed Dr. Amanda Kenson from the batarian prison on Aratoht, Elite Prison Guards attacked the pair alongside groups of regular Prison Guards as they fought their way out. They were equipped with the M-15 Vindicator, could arm Disruptor Ammo, and were highly accurate at range but less effective in close combat. They had shields on all difficulties, stronger on higher settings, and could use Tech Armor to restore depleted shields. Because Shepard fought them alone at points in the mission, the recommended tactics were to prioritize and kill them quickly before they could re-shield, or to close the distance and exploit their weakness up close after dealing with accompanying guards.
