Noveria: Matriarch Benezia was a sprawling Mass Effect mission to capture Matriarch Benezia, a top advisor to Saren, who had come to Noveria on his behalf. Shepard learned from Gianna Parasini that Benezia was at Peak 15 in the Skadi Mountains. After obtaining a garage pass to leave Port Hanshan, Shepard took the Mako from the port garage, first fighting through geth (two Destroyers and two Stalkers) and being questioned afterward by Captain Maeko Matsuo.

The drive through the Aleutsk Valley to Peak 15 imposed a cold hazard and ran a series of cliffside U-turns defended by geth: turrets, Shock and Rocket Troopers behind hex shields in tunnels, a Geth Armature, and more turrets outside the Peak 15 garage. Inside Peak 15, Shepard fought a Geth Juggernaut, a repair drone, and krogan, then encountered a new organic enemy: the rachni, in the form of exploding Workers and acid-spitting Soldiers bursting from floor grates. The station's VI, Mira, reported extensive facility damage. Shepard had to restore the offline VI and complete the sub-missions Noveria: Reconnect Landlines, Noveria: Reactor Repair, and Noveria: Contamination before taking the tram to Rift Station.

At Rift Station, Shepard met Captain Ventralis, who was maintaining a lockdown and provided a Hot Labs pass card. Shepard could follow Ventralis's lead to the Hot Labs or explore the main level, where scientists and guards sheltered and Dr. Zev Cohen gave the Noveria: Quarantine mission; the route chosen changed how the story unfolded. Entering the Secure Lab was the mission's point of no return. There Benezia stood on an upper platform; after a conversation (with an exchange between mother and daughter if Liara was present), Benezia placed the squad in stasis and her Asari Commandos attacked. She was invulnerable through three waves of Commandos and Geth Snipers. Once weakened, she briefly regained herself and revealed that the real threat was Sovereign, that Saren was merely its tool, and that Saren had sent her to extract the location of the Mu Relay from the Rachni Queen held in a tank in the lab. Indoctrination then overwhelmed her and she attacked again with three final Commandos before dying, renaming the entry Noveria: Death of a Matriarch.

Shepard then decided the fate of the Rachni Queen, releasing or killing her, before taking the tram back to the Normandy briefing room. The Council reacted with fear if the Queen was released and condemnation if she was killed, the turian councilor asking whether Shepard took pleasure in committing genocide.
