Prologue: Awakening was the second mission of Mass Effect 2, acquired automatically after Prologue: Save Joker. It served as a combat tutorial and established Commander Shepard as a Cerberus operative two years after the destruction of the Normandy SR-1 and Shepard's death. Shepard's body had been recovered by Cerberus and rebuilt through the Lazarus Project into a cybernetically enhanced operative. The opening credits showed the reconstruction; Shepard then woke prematurely once, then again on an operating table at the Lazarus Research Station as alarm bells rang and the station's own security mechs turned hostile.

Under-equipped and alone, Shepard was guided by radio by an authoritative woman later revealed as Miranda Lawson, head of the Lazarus Project and the station's commanding officer. Shepard collected a pistol, armor and thermal clips and fought through waves of Hacked Mechs, picking up an M-3 Predator Heavy Pistol and an M-100 Grenade Launcher along the way. Scattered laptops and datapads held audio and video logs from Miranda and Dr. Wilson describing the effort to revive Shepard, including that Shepard was returned without mental tampering. Wall safes and datapads could be bypassed or hacked for credits.

Shepard met Jacob Taylor on a bridge fighting mechs; Jacob confirmed he was on Shepard's side, revealed he was a biotic, and later disclosed that the Lazarus Project was a Cerberus operation, with morality points awarded for dialogue choices. Shepard then reached Dr. Wilson, who appeared wounded in the leg and was healed with medi-gel before joining temporarily. At the shuttle bay door, Miranda was waiting on the other side and shot Wilson dead without ceremony, explaining that he was the traitor who had hacked the security mechs, endangered the project, and caused the deaths of most of the staff.

During the shuttle ride to another Cerberus facility, Miranda questioned Shepard about pre-service history, psychological profile, and the squadmate lost on Virmire to assess memory. At the facility Shepard spoke with the Illusive Man by hologram, who explained that entire human colonies and thousands of colonists had vanished and tasked Shepard with investigating Freedom's Progress alongside Miranda and Jacob. The Lazarus Station was afterward destroyed by remote detonation to erase incriminating data. Shepard gained Jacob and Miranda as squad members and the mission awarded roughly 1,000 experience and 7,500 credits.
