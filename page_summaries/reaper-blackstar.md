The Reaper Blackstar was a heavy weapon in Mass Effect 3, of Reaper design and so advanced that Alliance scientists could only speculate about its function. The gun appeared to exploit an element zero core and mass effect fields to fire gravitational singularities, micro black holes, that reverted to their natural lethality on impact with a solid object. Researchers theorized the blast tore apart the strong nuclear forces binding the target's atoms, producing a localized fusion reaction in light atoms and a localized fission reaction in heavy atoms, altering nuclei and changing the target's chemical composition. This destroyed organic tissue, corroded surviving armor, and left a visible trail of light-emitting particles. Its single-launch capacity was considered a liability by some, but its capacity for instantaneous, large-scale destruction made it valuable when overwhelming firepower was required.

In single-player the Blackstar could first be found in Priority: Palaven, and also in Tuchanka: Turian Platoon and Mahavid: Leviathan. Like all Mass Effect 3 heavy weapons it was discarded once its ammunition was depleted, and it was acquired with only one round. It required a charging period, though shorter than that of the functionally similar M-920 Cain, and dealt massive area damage; players were advised to fire from behind cover rather than while in cover, and to aim at the ground amid a group rather than directly at a single target, since even Brutes, Harvesters and Banshees died instantly within a considerable radius. The Blackstar had unusual friendly-fire properties: it damaged Shepard's squadmates but not Shepard. Its design was inspired by a Reaper's leg, and its strong-nuclear-force premise was recycled by writer Chris Hepler from an undeveloped exploration of the science behind the Crucible.
