The Elbrus was a Cerberus cruiser commanded by General Oleg Petrovsky. Heavily armed and carrying weapons forbidden by the Citadel Conventions, it was dispatched to Omega by the Illusive Man to help contain a dangerous threat that Cerberus had released.

In Mass Effect: Invasion, when Omega came under attack from Reaper creatures called Adjutants that had broken free from Cerberus research bases beyond the Omega 4 Relay, the Elbrus was sent ahead of a Cerberus fleet to aid Aria T'Loak. It quickly destroyed the unarmed transport vessels the Adjutants had hijacked and led the defense against a second wave of armed Adjutant ships. Overwhelmed by the numbers, Petrovsky took the Elbrus through the Omega 4 Relay to stop the Adjutants at their source, Avernus Station. There the Elbrus cleared Adjutants from the station's exterior and served as a base of operations for Cerberus forces reclaiming it. During the return trip to Omega, Aria escaped confinement and hijacked the ship, then slipped away when it docked before Cerberus troops could kill her.

The ship was named after Mount Elbrus, a dormant volcano in Russia, to parallel Petrovsky's name origins; Cerberus ships used a mountain naming motif.
