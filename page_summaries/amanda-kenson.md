Dr. Amanda Kenson was a human scientist from the University of Arcturus who became notable for claiming, with her team, that the construction of the mass relays predated Prothean civilization. By analyzing dust trapped near mass relays and comparing it to dust clouds elsewhere in the same systems, she concluded that only a fraction of relays could be dated to about 50,000 years ago and that the rest were far older. This discovery made galactic headlines.

Afterward, Kenson led an expedition to investigate rumors of Reaper technology on the fringes of a batarian system. She sent a message to her friend, Systems Alliance Admiral Steven Hackett, reporting evidence of an imminent Reaper invasion. When Hackett learned she had been captured by batarians and accused of terrorism, he asked Commander Shepard to rescue her. Shepard broke into her prison on Aratoht, freed her, and armed her with an M-4 Shuriken; Kenson helped fight through the facility, hacked a security console to disable the orbital tracking grid, and escaped with Shepard aboard a commandeered shuttle. She then explained that her group, "the Project," had found a Reaper artifact she called Object Rho, which gave visions of the coming invasion, and that the Project intended to destroy the Alpha Relay in the Bahak system by ramming an asteroid into it. Rather than proceed to Arcturus Station, she took Shepard to Project Base.

On the asteroid, Kenson showed Shepard a countdown clock indicating the Reapers would arrive in just over two days, then exposed Shepard to Object Rho. She then drew a weapon on Shepard: she and the entire Project staff had been indoctrinated by prolonged exposure to the artifact and now served the Reapers, seeking to prevent the relay's destruction. Shepard disarmed her and broke her wrist. Kenson had Shepard sedated, but the Commander woke and fought through the base while she tried to destabilize the reactor and later to override power to the Project's engines. In a final confrontation at the master control panel, lamenting that she could no longer "hear the whispers," she triggered a detonator that obliterated her body and knocked Shepard back. As a squadmate she was armed with an M-4 Shuriken and could gain an M-12 Locust, and was protected by a biotic barrier.
