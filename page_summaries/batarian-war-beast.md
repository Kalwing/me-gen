Batarian War Beasts were trained varren used by batarian terrorists and extremists. In the first Mass Effect, batarians brought them along during the assignment to stop batarians from crashing an asteroid into the human colony of Terra Nova, using them to help take over the asteroid and secure their positions; the page noted the operation shifted from a simple slave grab into a mission of revenge. Because the War Beasts behaved exactly like varren, they charged and attempted to bite opponents. They had no shielding but a respectable amount of health, and biotics were extremely effective against them, especially against several at once, while shotguns were effective at close range.

In Mass Effect 2, Batarian War Beasts also roamed prison complexes, and some were encountered on Aratoht during a prisoner extraction assignment. Like regular varren, they used their teeth to tear chunks off enemies. On higher difficulties they gained a layer of armor over their health. They were identical to ordinary Mass Effect 2 varren and were to be handled the same way, kept at a distance to avoid their melee attack, and eliminated quickly in the close quarters of that mission. Warp, Incinerate, or Incendiary Ammo helped strip their light armor, after which powers such as Throw or Cryo Blast neutralized them.
