Mass Effect: Ascension is a novel written by Drew Karpyshyn and published by Del Rey Books on July 29, 2008. It is the second Mass Effect tie-in novel and carries the ISBN-13 978-0345498526.

The story follows scientist Kahlee Sanders, who has left the Systems Alliance to join the Ascension Project, a program that trains gifted biotic children to control their powers. Its most promising student is twelve-year-old Gillian Grayson, a borderline-autistic girl who is secretly an unwitting pawn of Cerberus. The outlawed black-ops group has been sabotaging the program by running illegal experiments on the students. The novel is set in the context of humanity's post-Mars rise, after the discovery of a Prothean cache on Mars allowed humans to join other species in exploiting Prothean technology, while a rogue militia seeks domination rather than participation.

When the Cerberus plot is exposed, Gillian's father removes her from the Ascension Project and flees into the lawless Terminus Systems. Determined to protect Gillian, Kahlee accompanies them, unaware that the elder Grayson is himself a Cerberus operative. Kahlee is forced to travel across the galaxy, facing hostile enemies, to try to save the girl from her own father.

An in-game version of the book, the "Ascension Novel," can be purchased from the Zakera Cafe in Mass Effect 2 and references the events and author of this novel. Full plot details are covered on the separate Storyline page rather than in this article.
