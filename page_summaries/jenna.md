Jenna was a waitress at Chora's Den on the Citadel in 2183 who had previously worked at Flux. She was secretly an informant for C-Sec, working on behalf of Detective Chellick. She insisted she could take care of herself and became annoyed when Commander Shepard tried to warn her how dangerous Chora's Den was, asking the Commander to leave so she could work: "I'm not a stripper, I don't get paid to stand around and look pretty." Her sister Rita, who also worked at Chora's Den, worried about her safety.

Shepard could help Jenna by cooperating with Chellick to close his investigation. In return, Chellick pulled Jenna out of Chora's Den. She was immediately rehired by Doran at Flux, where the customers loved her. Rita was relieved her sister was out of danger but had forgotten that Jenna earned the best tips.

In Mass Effect 3, if Shepard had dealt with Jenna in 2183, she later saved Conrad Verner's life during the Cerberus coup attempt on the Citadel. Using an omni-tool trick she had learned from Chellick, she hacked the gun of a Cerberus agent as Conrad leapt to take a bullet for Shepard. Jenna and Conrad then became acquainted, bonding over his relationship with the Commander. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition, Jenna's hair changes from red to blonde if she returns to work at Flux.
