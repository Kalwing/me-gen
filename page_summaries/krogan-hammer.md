The Krogan Hammer was a melee weapon in Mass Effect: Andromeda. A mechanized hammer whose force was enhanced by precision pistons and mass effect fields, it was commonly used by elder krogan warlords; the version available to the player was modified for especially strong humans or young krogan. Tied with the Asari Sword as the game's most powerful melee weapon, it was swung vertically and smashed into the ground, knocking human-sized enemies back a significant distance and releasing a shockwave that hit multiple surrounding enemies for full damage, making it the best melee option for crowd control. Its drawback was speed: over a second passed between upswing and damage, leaving the wielder open to counterattack or letting the target escape.

Three characters could wield it: Pathfinder Ryder, Nakmor Drack, and the Krogan Gladiator. It suited a Vanguard Ryder, who gained large melee bonuses and could pair Charge, Nova and a Hammer strike as an opening combo; the knockback worked through cover and could push enemies off ledges into instant death, though it also quickly destroyed cover. Melee damage scaled from 400 at rank I to 880 at rank X, with zero weight, and it required iron, lithium and element zero to research and develop.
