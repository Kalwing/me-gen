Singularity was a Biotic skill in Mass Effect: Andromeda, the Andromeda incarnation of the trilogy's biotic power of the same name. Tapping the button deployed a gravity well that lifted and damaged unshielded and unarmored enemies and primed enemies for combo detonations; tapping again ended the effect early. At rank 1 it dealt 35 damage per second over a 3-meter radius for 8 seconds, with a 24-second recharge in single-player (20 in multiplayer).

Its rank upgrades improved recharge speed, damage, duration, and radius, added +100% damage versus shields (Anti-Shield), and offered a rank 6 choice between Exploding Singularity, which detonated after the effect ended for area damage in a 7-meter radius, and Expanding Singularity, which grew the radius by 200% over the skill's lifetime. Biotic explosions from primed units did not end the skill. Although a target needed no armor or shields to be lifted, all targets in the area of effect were primed even if protected, though protected enemies could still move out of the field. It functioned mainly as a crowd-control tool against masses of unprotected units and, combined with a detonator, as an effective way to strip shields. It was available to Pathfinder Ryder (unlocked with 9 points in Biotic skills or Biotic Training) and in multiplayer to the Human Adept and Human Commando.
