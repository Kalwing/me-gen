The Cerberus Assault Armor, renamed simply Cerberus Armor in Mass Effect 3, was a non-customizable armor set appearing in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. It was designed for shock troops expected to turn the tide of battle against creatures or forces that would decimate ordinary soldiers. Its users demanded shields and armor thick enough to survive a superior foe plus, in Mass Effect 2, a rechargeable pack to extend a heavy weapon's power cell, and in Mass Effect 3, compartments for extra thermal clips. Its one drawback was its weight, which the troops carried as a point of pride, with the saying "Out of shape going in, in shape coming out."

In Mass Effect 2 the armor increased heavy weapon ammo capacity, shields and health by 10 percent each. It was released as free downloadable content through the Cerberus Network on February 9, 2010, arriving with a confirmation e-mail from the Illusive Man saying his armor technicians had assembled a package and had it delivered to Shepard's quarters on the Normandy. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition it could instead be researched in the Normandy's Tech Lab for 5000 Palladium. Players considered it a good choice on Insanity difficulty for its health and shield boosts.

In Mass Effect 3 the Cerberus Armor could be bought at Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies in the Presidium Commons for 50,000 credits without discounts, or from the procurement terminals in the Normandy's shuttle bay or the Armax Arsenal Arena for 55,000. Because of its early availability it held some value for Soldiers and, to a lesser extent, Infiltrators, though the defensive bonuses were somewhat wasted on Infiltrators who were often invisible to enemies. The set was also available on the Xbox Avatar Marketplace for $3.99.
