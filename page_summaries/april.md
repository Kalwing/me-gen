April was a colonist on Fehl Prime in 2185 and the daughter of Christine. She was a lively little girl who idolized the Systems Alliance Marines and worshipped Lieutenant James Vega in particular, their relationship almost like that of brother and sister. She sometimes pretended to be a marine by putting on James's combat helmet.

During the installation of the colony's defense cannon, April came to meet Vega with her mother, snapped him a military salute, and pretended to be a professional. Vega gave her a badge to wear. She teased Vega about looking at Treeya differently from others; Vega deflected by hoisting her onto his shoulders and telling her to act as a lookout for bad guys, which she happily did, imagining herself an Alliance private. When she asked about the large gun the Alliance had installed, Vega promised her he would use it to keep the colony safe.

When the Collectors attacked the colony, April and Christine were paralyzed by the Seeker Swarms, and Delta Squad was forced to watch as the Collectors carried them away to their ship. Later, after Delta Squad infiltrated the Collector ship and released the antidote from the processing plant, April reawakened along with the other colonists. She never gave up hope in Vega, and contacted him over the comm using Toni's omni-tool, telling him to come and save them and that she would always believe in him. Vega then had to make a difficult choice, and the one he made caused the death of April and the other colonists when the Collector ship crashed onto the planet's surface before the colonists could be evacuated.
