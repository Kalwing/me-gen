Citadel: Presidium Prophet was a Mass Effect assignment acquired after Citadel: Expose Saren, when the Citadel Council initially dismissed the charges against Saren Arterius. Near the Wards Access corridor on the Presidium, on the way to the Citadel Tower and the Relay Monument, Commander Shepard overheard an argument between a turian C-Sec officer and a religious hanar. The officer said the hanar was preaching in a non-designated area without an evangelical permit and was harassing the public; the hanar insisted it was not preaching but merely stating the truth about the Enkindlers, which needed no permit.

After hearing both sides, Shepard chose whom to help. Buying the hanar a permit for 150 credits let it leave to preach legally elsewhere and gave no morality change. Alternatively, Shepard could Charm or Intimidate one party into leaving, each option granting 8 Paragon or 9 Renegade points. Persuading the hanar to leave, by arguing the Enkindlers would not want their gift of language used to break society's laws, satisfied the officer, who thanked Shepard and gave a reward of omni-gel plus morality points and experience. Persuading the officer to leave, by getting him to admit no one had actually complained, prompted the hanar to reward Shepard with medi-gel packs before it resumed preaching to passers-by.

A bug could prevent the officer from initiating his reward and completing the assignment if he was spoken to first and the hanar then left peacefully. The assignment resembled a cut sub-plot, Citadel: The Hate Monger, which featured similar characters.
