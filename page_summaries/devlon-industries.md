Devlon Industries was a manufacturer best known for producing basic armor models able to withstand a range of environmental extremes such as cold, heat, and toxic exposure. It also marketed a complete line of military-grade weapons. Its armor lines included Explorer Armor, Liberator Armor, Thermal Armor, and Survivor Armor. In the first Mass Effect, if any squad member wore Survivor, Explorer, Liberator, or Thermal Armor, the timer on Level 1 environmental hazards was negated and the Level 2 timer slowed for the entire shore party, because all of Devlon's armors were environmentally sealed. The tradeoff was reduced overall efficiency compared with most other armors. A license for Devlon Industries gear could be purchased from Delan on the Citadel for 6,250 credits.

Devlon's Mass Effect weapons used advanced fusion technology, which enhanced their stats relative to most competing weapons. The company's weapon lines were the Raptor assault rifles, the Firestorm shotguns, the Stinger pistols, and the Striker sniper rifles. The Stinger II was only obtainable by talking to Cole and his group during the prologue mission Find the Beacon. In Legendary Edition, Devlon weapons received improved accuracy, slower heat generation, a "Heat Sink Capacity" stat display, and manufacturer-specific firing patterns such as burst fire.

In Mass Effect 2, Devlon Industries manufactured the M-92 Mantis sniper rifle, a powerful, highly accurate weapon that could kill most targets in a single shot but had a slow rate of fire. It was effective against armor but weaker against shields and biotic barriers, was primarily used by police and planetary militia groups, and saw more frequent use in the Terminus systems where kinetic barriers were less common. The armor stat tables covered light, medium, and heavy variants of the Explorer/Liberator/Survivor lines for humans, turians, krogan, and quarians, plus turian-specific light and medium Thermal Armor.
