This page collects cut and non-functional dialogue from the first Mass Effect covering the Ilos mission and the Citadel finale, reconstructed from leftover files. It is explicitly non-canon. Cut material includes an Ilos section about restoring power to an ancient power plant and finding security overrides to enter the bunker, squad remarks about a hackable turret, an alternate Normandy-lockdown escape (via Udina's office or the dock controls), salarian reinforcements led by a surviving Kirrahe during the Tower climb, an extended final confrontation with Saren, and an altered post-battle reunion cutscene in which Executor Pallin appears alongside Anderson.

Kirrahe, arriving with reinforcements during the Citadel Tower climb: "I told you, Shepard. We salarians pay our debts. Make for the Council chamber, Commander. We'll try to draw fire away from your team."

Shepard, cut elevator line during Race Against Time: "Saren's locked the elevator! Suit up - we're going outside!"

Saren, cut final dialogue after his transformation: "You have no idea what stands before you. I am not Saren. This form is only a shell. A vessel to channel my power!"

Saren/Sovereign: "This flesh repulses me. It is vulnerable. Fragile. Weak. Like all organic life. My kind knows true strength: the strength of metal and steel."

Sovereign, cut boast: "We are aware of your presence. We tolerate your existence. But you are less than insects to us. You are bacteria. Germs. We are the pinnacle of creation! Immortal. Infallible. Perfect. I am Sovereign! And this station is MINE!"

David Anderson, cut reunion in the ruins: "You gave us a hell of a scare, Shepard. But you'll make a better hero than a martyr."

Anderson, on the dropped charges: "They dropped the charges after Sovereign attacked the Citadel. Guess they figured helping you escape wasn't such a crime after all."
