The Milky Way is the barred spiral galaxy in which the events of the original Mass Effect trilogy take place. The page describes it as containing between two and four hundred billion stars and serving as the setting the player explores through story missions and optional assignments across the mass relay network. The galaxy is navigated via the Galaxy Map aboard the SSV Normandy in Mass Effect and the Normandy SR-2 in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3.

The bulk of the page is a reference index organizing the galaxy by game (Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, Mass Effect 3) and then by cluster, listing the systems, planets, moons, asteroid belts, mass relays, fuel depots and space stations found in each. Clusters catalogued include the Local Cluster (Sol, with Earth and Luna), Serpent Nebula (Widow system and the Citadel), Exodus Cluster (Utopia, with Eden Prime), Armstrong Nebula, Artemis Tau (Knossos, with Therum), Attican Beta (Theseus, with Feros), Sentry Omega (Hoc, with Virmire), Pangaea Expanse (Refuge, with Ilos), the Omega Nebula (Sahrabarik, with Omega and the Omega 4 Relay), Krogan DMZ (Aralakh, with Tuchanka), Apien Crest (Trebia, with Palaven and Menae), Athena Nebula (Parnitha, with Thessia), Perseus Veil (Tikkun, with Rannoch and the Migrant Fleet), Horse Head Nebula (Anadius, with Cronos Station), and many more. Entries are marked to indicate which game each location appears in.
