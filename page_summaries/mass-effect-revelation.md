Mass Effect: Revelation was a prequel novel to the Mass Effect trilogy, written by Drew Karpyshyn, lead writer of the Mass Effect game, and first published May 1, 2007 by Del Rey Books as the first novel set in the Mass Effect universe. It filled in background for the game, including locations such as the Citadel, the internal politics of the Council races, and relevant science, and introduced characters such as David Anderson. Much of the plot revolved around the galaxy's views on artificial intelligence, a major game plot point, and it set up the trilogy's main villain, Saren Arterius, and his involvement in the events sparked at Sidon. In the story, ship commander and Alliance war hero David Anderson investigated the ruins of a top-secret military research station, its staff slaughtered and the young scientist Kahlee Sanders missing hours before the attack. Sanders became the prime suspect; Anderson, partnered with a rogue alien agent he could not trust and pursued by an assassin, worked across uncharted worlds to uncover a sinister conspiracy. The book appears in Mass Effect 2 as a purchasable souvenir at the Zakera Cafe, described in-game as a military history novel about Saren's early missions, though the codex entry incorrectly identifies the destroyed facility as Shanxi rather than Sidon.
