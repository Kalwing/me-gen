Tikkun was the quarian home star system, reachable in Mass Effect 3 after Priority: Perseus Veil. Before the Morning War it had a conventional layout: a homeworld, helium-3 fuel infrastructure on a gas giant, and several planets used for resource extraction. In the centuries after the quarians were driven out, the geth slowly built a Dyson bubble around the star, part of their plan to construct a megastructure large enough to house every geth program in existence.

The system contained the planets Rannoch (first planet and the quarian homeworld), Adas (second), Kaddi (third), and Haza (fourth), plus the asteroid Uriyah, along with a mass relay, a Geth Debris Field, a Geth Dreadnought, and the anchored Migrant Fleet. During the geth-quarian war of 2186, every world in the system became a battlefield; both sides took heavy damage on and around these planets, and much of the geth Dyson bubble was reduced to ruin. Missions set here included Priority: Geth Dreadnought, Rannoch: Geth Fighter Squadrons, Rannoch: Admiral Koris, and Priority: Rannoch.

The name derives from Hebrew: "tikkun" means "a fix" or "correction," and appears in the phrase "tikkun olam," meaning "healing and restoring the world." Several worlds in the system followed a naming scheme based on Jewish culture and belief.
