Varren were omnivores with a preference for living prey, originally native to the krogan homeworld of Tuchanka. Like most Tuchanka life they were savage, clannish, and durable survivors: pack hunters when prey was easy, scavengers when outmatched. Their adaptability, viciousness, and rapid breeding made them ubiquitous and dangerous pests wherever the krogan traveled, wreaking havoc on native ecologies. The krogan had a millennia-long love-hate relationship with varren, alternately fighting them for territory and raising them as treasured companions and beasts of war; batarians also used them in war. A common silver-scaled subgenus earned the nickname "fishdogs." Varren coloration varied between eras and worlds, from white-bellied dark-backed animals in 2183 to brown-striped varieties by 2185.

Shepard first encountered varren in the Prothean ruins on Feros, where colonists like Davin Reynolds hunted them for meat and Shepard was asked to kill a rabid alpha varren; the animals were vicious enough to attack geth. Batarians deployed "Batarian War Beasts" on Asteroid X57 during Bring Down the Sky, and Simon Atwell noted varren did not always wait for prey to die before eating. On Tuchanka, a tame retired pit champion named Urz stood beside the trader Ratch and would follow Shepard around Clan Urdnot's camp if given pyjak meat. Mordin Solus was seen combating "scale-itch," a varren-borne sexually transmitted disease, aboard the Normandy. Varren were a popular food source, advertised at Illium's "Fishdog Food Shack" and in Citadel ads for "McSorley's Cloaca Margarine." The salarians studied varren (designated "species 408") as potential biological weapons. In the Citadel DLC, Jack noted that varren on Thessia could develop element-zero-fueled biotics, making them more aggressive; she adopted a red-skinned one named Eezo. Besides pit fights, varren were trained for less violent racing. The Andromeda Initiative brought only tissue samples, cloned into edible meat.
