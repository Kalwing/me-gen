Camala was the second planet of the Indris System in the Kite's Nest, a relatively small but eezo-rich garden world recently colonized by the batarians. In an unusually open gesture, the batarians allowed immigration of free citizens of other species, provided they came from independent colony planets such as those in the Traverse and Terminus and retained non-citizen status during their stay. The economic lure of element zero drew many settlers, and the planet soon boasted spaceports and refineries that considerably enriched the Batarian Hegemony. Camala enjoyed relative peace and prosperity, its main inconvenience being fresh water shortages caused by a dry climate. Its surface consisted mostly of inhospitable desert and rock scattered with hundreds of mining and refinery operations.

By the mid-2160s Camala was the wealthiest batarian colony and a key supplier of element zero. Its capital was Ujon, and the city of Hatre was a populous port. The planet was home to the batarian aristocrat Edan Had'dah and was the site of the Dah'tan Manufacturing facility. Another resident, Jalosk Dal'Virra, witnessed the beginning of the Reaper invasion and escaped the planet, securing passage for himself and his children aboard the Andromeda Initiative ark Keelah Si'yah.

During the Reaper invasion of 2186, Camala's affluent cities became a nightmare. Alliance intelligence indicated that early in the invasion a high percentage of the population was captured and converted into husk creatures for future offensives. Processing camps ran night and day, and troop transports flew legions of Cannibals out every hour. For this reason Alliance command targeted Camala for military strikes. Camala also appeared as a mission location in the N7 HQ Galaxy at War mini-game for Mass Effect 3.
