Sirta Supplies was a store terminal on the Citadel inside Huerta Memorial Hospital, across from the reception desk, in Mass Effect 3. It sold eight gift items available only on Shepard's first visit, intended for the hospitalized squadmate, Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko. If Ashley was the patient, all items except the TM88 Peruvian Whiskey were available; if Kaidan was the patient, only the TM88 whiskey was offered. Shepard automatically handed over any purchased gifts during the first conversation after the squadmate awoke, one mission after Priority: The Citadel I, after which the remaining gifts could no longer be bought.

The items were a bouquet of flowers grown in the Citadel's hydroponic gardens; a levo-amino candy assortment; and several poetry volumes: Rumi's His Remembrance, the elcor performance artist Nockrick Taur's Tear-songs of the Plains, the reclusive asari poet Techllis Bel's And Love Fell, a Walt Whitman collection Among the Multitude (partly funding the charitable Whitman Foundation), and a limited Oxford Press edition of The Collected Alfred Tennyson. The TM88 Peruvian Whiskey was marketed as calming yet stimulating to blood flow and as the only alcoholic drink endorsed by the Medical Board of Sur'Kesh.
