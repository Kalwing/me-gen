Scientists were a minor human enemy type in Mass Effect and Mass Effect 2: research personnel who also knew their way around weapons, lightly armed and quick to fall in combat. In Mass Effect, two ExoGeni Corporation scientists were holed up inside their own facility on Nodacrux after their illegally created Thorian Creepers went berserk and killed the rest of the staff. If Shepard found their hiding place, one of them, Dr. Ross, explained the situation and tried to bribe the Commander for their freedom; accepting let them go, while refusing led to a fight and their deaths. They carried only pistols and had little health and no shields, backed by two tougher Mercenary companions, and Dr. Ross always ran for a table barricade beside a destructible containment cell that could be shot to send her flying.

In Mass Effect 2, following Shepard's incapacitation at Project Base, a scientist monitoring the Commander's sedation discovered Shepard was fighting off the drugs, fled the medical bay, and called security. She raised a barrier to trap Shepard in the medical bay, but the LOKI Mech controls were on Shepard's side of the room; Shepard remote-controlled the mechs to kill her and drop the barrier. She was armed with an M-3 Predator and had only health on lower difficulties, light shielding on higher ones.
