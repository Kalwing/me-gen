This page described the combat abilities available to Jacob Taylor in the mobile game Mass Effect Galaxy. Jacob could use and upgrade three types of skills, grouped into three categories: biotic attack, Heavy Weapon attack, and Tech attack. Each set offered a different way to approach combat.

Abilities were triggered from a powers wheel on the left of the screen. Tapping an ability button launched the power at the targeted enemy, after which the power entered a cooldown before it could be reused. Power icons showed one of three colors: blue when no enemy was in range and the power could not be used, orange when the power was available and an enemy was in range, and grey when the power was cooling down, with the wheel also showing the remaining time. The powers wheel could additionally pause the game, which recalibrated the iOS device's accelerometer when combat resumed. Each of the three powers could be upgraded once, with Jacob gaining an upgrade after each major investigation mission; an upgraded power showed a small plus sign beside its icon.

The biotic attack was Stasis, which froze an enemy in place so it could not move or fire while Jacob continued shooting it without resistance. It was upgraded after the mission on Bekke to last longer, and was recommended against stronger enemies. The Heavy Weapon Attack fired an exploding grenade in a straight line that detonated on impact and dealt area damage; unlike the auto-targeting assault rifle, it required manual aim, and Jacob could hurt himself if he stayed in the blast radius. It was upgraded after the mission on Tortuga for more explosive damage, and was best used against enemies near objects or in tight quarters. The Tech Attack was Shield Overload, which automatically discharged a targeted enemy's shield bar; it was upgraded after the mission on Ahn'Kedar to deplete more shielding, and was recommended against heavily shielded enemies.
