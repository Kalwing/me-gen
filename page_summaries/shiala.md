Shiala was an asari commando and powerful biotic who served as an acolyte of Matriarch Benezia for nearly two centuries. She accompanied Benezia aboard Sovereign when the matriarch tried to steer Saren Arterius toward a gentler path. When Benezia was indoctrinated, Shiala also became one of Saren's thralls and followed him to Feros. There Saren discovered that the Thorian held the Prothean Cipher he needed to understand the Eden Prime vision. He traded Shiala to the Thorian; she was absorbed into its single consciousness, used her biotics to acquire the Cipher, and transferred it to Saren's mind. Saren then abandoned her and ordered the geth to destroy the Thorian to keep Shepard from gaining the Cipher.

When Commander Shepard's team reached the Thorian's lair, the creature produced biotic clones of Shiala that communicated with and attacked the squad. After the Thorian was destroyed, Shiala was freed and restored to herself. She explained Saren's actions and Benezia's enslavement, then touched Shepard's mind to transfer the Cipher directly, warning that comprehending it would take time. Shepard then chose her fate: spared, she stayed to help the colonists of Zhu's Hope; executed for switching sides too often, she knelt without resistance and was shot in the back of the head. Shiala was the only individual to overcome Sovereign's indoctrination without suicide, likely because she had been linked to and "indoctrinated" by the Thorian instead.

If spared, she appeared on Illium two years later investigating a medical contract made with the Feros colonists. Her skin pigmentation had turned green like her clones, her biotics had become unstable, and she suffered disturbing dreams of the Thorian. If Shepard helped negotiate with the company, she expressed romantic interest; if the Feros colony had died, her manner was colder and she did not flirt. In Mass Effect 3, if she and the colony survived, Shiala emailed Shepard that the Thorian spores let the colonists sense one another and fight the Reapers as one mind. She still felt the Reapers' influence but said her connection to the colonists drowned it out, and pledged that she and the colonists would leave Feros to fight alongside Shepard.
