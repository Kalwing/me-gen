Citadel Security Services, or C-Sec, was the volunteer police service handling law enforcement on the Citadel. It answered to the Citadel Council and was headed by an Executor, usually a turian, who liaised between C-Sec and the Council. The incumbent Executor was Venari Pallin, whose office sat in the Presidium Embassies. The C-Sec Academy was located in the Lower Wards because of higher crime there. The turians first proposed a Citadel police force, and more than half of C-Sec's roughly 200,000 constables were turian, with humans the largest minority, followed by salarians and asari. C-Sec's duties included public order, pirate suppression, customs enforcement, and search-and-rescue, organized into Enforcement, Investigation, Customs, Network or E-Crimes, Special Response, and Patrol divisions. Recruits required sponsorship by a Councilor or an associate race's ambassador and were usually posted first to the calmer Presidium. C-Sec and the Council's Spectres often clashed over the Spectres' freedom from legal procedure.

In 2183, after Eden Prime, the Council tasked C-Sec with investigating Saren Arterius. Garrus Vakarian, the officer in charge, found no evidence but continued investigating against Pallin's orders, eventually saving Dr. Chloe Michel from thugs at the Med Clinic with Shepard's help and learning of the quarian Tali'Zorah nar Rayya before joining Shepard. Detective Chellick ran a weapons-smuggling investigation that Shepard could aid or ruin, and C-Sec detained the krogan Urdnot Wrex after Fist reported his threats. In Mass Effect Galaxy, before 2183, Jacob Taylor discovered that terrorists tied to batarian ambassador Jath'Amon had infiltrated C-Sec itself.

Between 2183 and 2185, C-Sec underwent radical restructuring after casualties from Saren's attack, with humans filling many vacancies and Pallin tightening security against geth infiltration through no-fly lists and weapon bans. Captain Armando-Owen Bailey assisted Shepard with several Citadel operations, including providing an interrogation room for Thane and helping with Garrus's past. In 2186, Bailey, sent by Councilor Udina to investigate Pallin, found two dead officers and Pallin, who accused Udina of lies before drawing his gun; Bailey killed him and was promoted to Commander. During the Reaper invasion, C-Sec was overwhelmed by refugees at the docks and caught off guard by the Cerberus attack, which hit C-Sec headquarters first and exposed Cerberus sleeper agents among the officers. With Shepard and the Normandy crew, Bailey retook C-Sec headquarters and the Citadel. Afterward C-Sec remained short of manpower while investigating whether Udina acted alone, disarming Cerberus booby traps, and debating whether to stop policing minor crimes; a civilian militia could be organized to help. Notable officers included Bailey, Chellick, Officer Eddie Lang, Sergeant Haron, Jordan Noles, Garrus Vakarian, the suspended and later fired Harkin, and Venari Pallin, killed in 2186.
