Rahna was a human biotic, born to a wealthy Turkish family, chosen for Biotic Acclimation and Temperance Training (BAaT) at Gagarin Station (Jump Zero) in 2168 when the Alliance and Conatix Industries first organized the program. She was an acquaintance and close friend of Kaidan Alenko, who described her as smart, charming, and gentle, protected and loved by their circle of friends; the two became "dorm buddies" during their first months at BAaT. In Mass Effect: Foundation, Rahna declined a chance to smuggle a message to her family via a friend's unsanctioned transmitter, reasoning her parents would report it and that she did not know where her brother was stationed.

Rahna was terrified of the turian instructor Commander Vyrnnus and his brutal regime. During a marathon training exercise, an exhausted Rahna, thirsty and standing in line for water, reached for a glass with her right hand instead of using biotics. Vyrnnus biotically shattered the glass and broke her arm badly enough that bone protruded. Kaidan flew into a rage and killed Vyrnnus with a "full on biotic kick." In the aftermath Rahna was terrified of Kaidan rather than grateful; she refused to speak with him and rejected his justifications as he left BAaT, saying that killing a problem did not make things right, and waved a final goodbye as she walked away. By 2183 Kaidan still referred to her in conversations with Commander Shepard. What became of Rahna after BAaT was shut down is unknown.
