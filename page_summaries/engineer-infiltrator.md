Brief stub. Engineers were Cerberus's technical personnel and an enemy unit in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, encountered by Randall Ezno on the Barn space station during his escape. Lacking the armor of mainline Cerberus soldiers, they were fragile, with low health and only a weak regenerating shield layer, dangerous only at short range though some carried assault rifles, and vulnerable to headshots. Unlike the Cerberus Research Technicians and Combat Engineers of Mass Effect and Mass Effect 3, these Engineers had no tech powers.
