Overlord was a downloadable content pack for Mass Effect 2, released on June 15, 2010, and later included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. It added four linked missions set on the planet Aite in the Typhon System of the Phoenix Massing cluster, unfolding across five new level areas with two new achievements.

The story began when Shepard was sent to investigate a Cerberus research base that had gone silent and arrived to find geth overrunning it. The sole survivor, Chief Scientist Archer, explained that an experiment to fuse a human volunteer with a virtual intelligence had produced a dangerous hybrid "VI overlord". The rampaging VI had already attacked three other Cerberus bases, seizing control of any technology it found in an attempt to break free and unleashing geth across the planet. Unless Shepard infiltrated the VI's fortress and shut it down, the intelligence would beam itself off-planet and spread to other systems. The four assignments were titled Overlord, Overlord: Vulcan Station, Overlord: Prometheus Station, and Overlord: Atlas Station.

The pack also provided the M-44 Hammerhead hover tank for planetary exploration (added only if the Firewalker Pack had not already been downloaded) and the Heavy Skin Weave upgrade, which raised the maximum health bonus from upgrades to +60%. It sold for $6.99 USD on Xbox 360 and 560 BioWare Points on PC, and was included on the disc of the PlayStation 3 version. Cut content notes indicate early plans for a larger cluster of four systems and twenty planets, plus shotgun and heavy weapon research upgrades.
