This page transcribed art director Derek Watts's gallery commentary for "The Technology of Mass Effect: Space Vehicles - Other Races," from the 2007 Mass Effect bonus content disc. Watts explained that a design once considered for the asari ship Destiny Ascension was instead used for the mass relay, and that a separate painted concept, with a hole in the middle from which lit ships emerged and wings off to one side, became the final Destiny Ascension seen often in the game's cinematics; he noted the team tried to match each ship believably to its race.

Other images were early concepts drawn for the salarians, who at that stage were planned as a party-member race with many ships and were given a softer, more organic look with an open central section, side wings and a cockpit. Watts showed numerous quick throwaway sketches, each producible in a couple of hours, including several alternative asari ships with sunken or layered engine arrangements and aggressive cockpits, a sleek cruiser-sized design whose guns might fire through two ovals, and a bladed salarian ship that could have been modified into a geth ship or used as a shuttle. He remarked that many designs were drawn only to be shown around the office for blunt feedback from artists, designers and programmers and then discarded, with the exercise still turning up useful elements.
