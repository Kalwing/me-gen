Citadel: Alien Medi-Gel Formula was a side mission in Mass Effect 3 set on the Citadel. A doctor at Huerta Memorial Hospital, Dr. Ravin, needed a medi-gel formula adapted for non-human physiology. The mission and its objective could both be obtained during the assault on the Cerberus base at Sanctum in the mission N7: Cerberus Lab, where a console in a room labeled LT5 revealed a "failed" Cerberus experiment: the formula produced a medical breakthrough for hanar but had no effect on humans. Alternatively, after the turian primarch was rescued from Menae, Shepard could acquire the mission by overhearing Dr. Ravin's conversation at Huerta Memorial Hospital, though Ravin was not present when the hospital first opened during Priority: The Citadel I. The mission required the prerequisites N7: Cerberus Lab and Priority: Palaven, and could not be completed until after Priority: Palaven.

Shepard recovered the hanar medi-gel formula from Sanctum and delivered it to Dr. Ravin at Huerta Memorial. The mission had to be finished before Priority: Tuchanka, the last chance to visit the Citadel in its then-current state, as it became unavailable after the civilian areas reopened following Priority: The Citadel II. If the formula was missed during the mission, it could be bought at the Spectre Requisitions terminal in the Citadel Embassies for a base price of 1,000 credits. Completing the mission granted 30 experience, 1,000 credits, and updated the Hanar and Drell Forces War Asset.
