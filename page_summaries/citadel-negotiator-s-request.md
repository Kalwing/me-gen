Citadel: Negotiator's Request was a Mass Effect assignment available after Race Against Time: Sovereign. When Captain Anderson summoned Commander Shepard to meet him at Flux, an Alliance negotiator named Keeler waited near the entrance. Keeler said he urgently needed a legal but regulated mental stimulant to improve his performance in an important treaty negotiation with the salarians; through conversation it became apparent that he was an addict. He claimed that if he could not attend, his assistant Thomas Kent would take over, and he disparaged Kent as an alien sympathiser who would bend over backwards to accommodate the Citadel races, whereas Keeler believed humans needed to be more aggressive with the salarians.

Shepard had several options with varying morality rewards. Shepard could talk Keeler out of it, using Charm for 8 Paragon points or Intimidate for 2 Renegade points, after which Keeler left to get treatment and let Kent take over. Shepard could fetch the stimulant from the Med Clinic and give it to him, earning 2 Paragon points only if Keeler had also been talked into treatment after the sessions. Or Shepard could buy a depressant sedative from Dr. Michel and give him that instead, leaving him drowsy and insulting before he staggered off, for 9 Renegade points.

A later elevator news story reported the negotiation's outcome. If Keeler was given the stimulant, the deal was said to bring the Alliance billions of credits while the Salarian Union complained of unreasonable requests and hardline tactics. If Keeler received the depressant or was convinced to get treatment immediately, the Salarian Union was impressed by Kent's efforts to find mutually beneficial courses of action. The assignment was named "Negotiator's Fix" in the game's internal data.
