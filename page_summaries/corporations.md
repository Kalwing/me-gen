Directory/list page. It briefly described corporations as key business entities in interstellar society, funding research and development, founding colonies, providing security, reporting news, and supplying goods and services, ranging from multi-world enterprises with subsidiaries to lone-proprietor shops. It noted that many arms manufacturers signed exclusive contracts with alien governments, that requisitions officers needed armory licenses for particular companies' products, and that larger mercenary groups were themselves corporate entities. The bulk of the page is a categorized index of known corporations (bioengineering, clothing, colonization, electronics, entertainment, media, mining and energy, private military, food, shipping, utilities, vehicles, weapons, and other), including entries such as Binary Helix, ExoGeni Corporation, Elkoss Combine, Hahne-Kedar, Kassa Fabrication, Armax Arsenal, Sirta Foundation, Blue Suns, Eclipse, Cord-Hislop Aerospace, and Westerlund News.
