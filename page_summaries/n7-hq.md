N7 HQ was a BioWare companion website for Mass Effect 3, launched on October 11, 2012, offering stat-tracking and gameplay data for the single-player campaign and multiplayer mode. Players could view their Profile, Challenge Awards, Inventory, Achievements, a partial Codex, and the Mass Effect 3: Galaxy at War system. On June 6, 2013, the Mass Effect 3: Datapad iOS app was removed from the App Store and its functions were taken over by N7 HQ.

The Profile section tracked rankings, challenges, unlocked multiplayer characters and their skill trees, inventory by rarity, and leaderboards, and let players view other players' public profiles. The Galaxy at War section let users manage Effective Military Strength and Galactic Readiness from a browser: up to five fleets could be deployed to fifteen mission locations across five galactic regions, with short (1 hour, +2.80%), medium (3 hours, +2.98%) and long (5 hours, +3.17%) missions boosting Galactic Readiness. The browser version was simplified from the Datapad mini-game, removing credit and upgrade mechanics and fixing rewards at their highest level. Named mission locations included Pinnacle Station, Noveria, Camala, Menae, Thessia, Trident, Caleston and Omega, each with briefing and debriefing text. Online war assets included the N7 Special Ops Team (gained by promoting multiplayer classes) and Cerberus Escapees (gained through the mobile game Mass Effect: Infiltrator). The site's Codex entries assumed Donnel Udina became the human Councilor in 2183.
