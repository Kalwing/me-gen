The Breeder was an artificial rachni queen genetically engineered by the Reapers to produce Ravagers for their armies. She was created from "pieces" of real rachni and was physically more or less identical to a true rachni queen, but her mind had been twisted by her unnatural creation, leading her to call herself a "monster" and to speak in the first-person singular rather than the collective plural used by a true queen.

Commander Shepard encountered the Breeder in Mass Effect 3 while searching for a krogan scout team that had vanished investigating a possible rachni presence on Utukku. She appeared only if Shepard had killed the original rachni queen on Noveria in 2183. Entrenched deep in the tunnels of Utukku, she was guarded by Reaper technology and by barriers controlled by local nodes, and she spoke through the mouths of the dead krogan scouts, many of whom were suspended on the walls in webbing.

The Breeder was far more insistent than the original queen that she be spared, offering to spawn children loyal to Shepard's cause in exchange for freedom. If Shepard chose not to spare her, she became enraged and screamed for her minions to kill the Commander. Unlike the trustworthy queen, the Breeder proved treacherous if freed, resulting in heavy losses to the war effort. Per Mass Effect Legendary Edition statistics from July 2021, of the roughly 7 percent of players who killed the original queen on Noveria, 67 percent killed the Breeder and 33 percent saved her.
