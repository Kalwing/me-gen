The Reckoner Knight Armor was a non-customizable armor set in Mass Effect 3, created as a cross-promotion with the game Kingdoms of Amalur: Reckoning and also included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. In the game's fiction, the armor was originally worn by the Rhode Island Knights, winners of Earth's Urban Combat Championship 2186. A military-grade version was later presented to devoted Knight fans at the Alliance's Rhode Island base in thanks for their vocal support during the EUCC matches. Beneath its medieval-gothic exterior the suit carried biofeedback systems that monitored the wearer's health and combat performance, micro-servos that boosted close-quarters damage, an enlarged power cell that increased projectile velocity, and a strong shield system, making it well suited to melee-focused play.

In the original Mass Effect 3, the armor unlocked after downloading and playing the Kingdoms of Amalur: Reckoning demo with a linked EA/BioWare account; PS3 owners had to play the demo before launching Mass Effect 3. After EA shut down the demo servers, there was no sanctioned way to obtain it in the original game short of contacting EA customer support, though the assets remained in the game files. In Legendary Edition it was sold by Kanala Exports on the Citadel for a base price of 40,000 credits. An accompanying email from Joker told Shepard that a shipment of armor had arrived on the Normandy from Alliance soldiers formerly stationed at Rhode Island, left with a note reading "You'll need this more than we do."

The armor was designed by Todd McFarlane, creator of Spawn. Like the Blood Dragon Armor, it was framed as EUCC sporting gear before a military version was produced. The Rhode Island email was a nod to 38 Studios, the Providence, Rhode Island developer of Kingdoms of Amalur: Reckoning. The Chakram Launcher assault rifle was obtainable from the same promotion.
