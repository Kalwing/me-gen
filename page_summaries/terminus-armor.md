Terminus Armor was a non-customizable armor set appearing in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. It was environmentally sealed with an independent air supply for use in space and extreme planetary conditions, and its onboard microframe computer ran battle-management software. To avoid detection by passive thermal sensors, the suit channeled the wearer's body heat to the base of the feet and dispersed it into the ground. Manufactured in the Terminus Systems, the suit was not legal in Citadel space.

In Mass Effect 2 the armor was included in the Terminus Weapon and Armor downloadable content pack, accompanied by an email from the Illusive Man saying his armor technicians had found the high-performance suit and delivered it to Shepard's quarters. It increased storm speed by 10%, shields by 15%, and all weapons' reserve ammo by 10%, but gave no weapon-damage bonus. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition it could instead be bought from Harrot's Emporium for 12,000 credits.

In Mass Effect 3 the set granted bonuses to melee damage, shield strength and ammo capacity, making it well suited to close-range tanking Vanguards and shotgun-wielding Infiltrators. It could be purchased at Batarian State Arms in the Citadel Holding Area for 50,000 credits, or through Normandy or Armax Arsenal Arena Procurement terminals for 55,000 credits.
