Collector Ship was a main-story mission in Mass Effect 2, part of the Stop the Collectors arc. After the Horizon mission a five-mission countdown ran, after which Shepard lost Galaxy Map access and was summoned by the Illusive Man, who dispatched the Normandy to investigate a Collector cruiser found adrift, apparently disabled by a turian patrol. The mission would not trigger until eight squad members had been recruited, and completing it became the only way to advance.

Boarding the largely deserted, highly linear vessel, Shepard's team passed dead bodies and salvageable Collector technology. At a control terminal, EDI reported the Collectors had been making genetic comparisons between their species and humanity; analysis on the Normandy revealed the Collectors' DNA matched that of the Protheans. Shepard then received advanced weapon training, choosing proficiency in shotguns, assault rifles, or sniper rifles, or, if already proficient, a unique weapon (M-300 Claymore, M-76 Revenant, or M-98 Widow, depending on class). The choice was permanent across all playthroughs with that character.

Deeper in, the squad found a vast chamber of countless empty pods and concluded the Collectors intended to harvest all of Earth. Accessing a command console let EDI mine data, but it was a trap: the platform launched into the air and Collector forces attacked on flying platforms in one of the game's hardest fights, involving Collector Drones, Guardians, Scions, and a Harbinger-possessed Collector. Afterward EDI determined the distress signal was non-turian and contained an error detectable only with Cerberus protocols the Illusive Man himself wrote, proving he had knowingly sent Shepard into the trap. Fighting out through further waves of Collectors, Husks, Abominations, and a Praetorian, the team reached a shuttle and escaped as the cruiser powered its weapons.

Back on the Normandy, the Illusive Man defended his gamble: EDI's analysis revealed the Collectors survived the Omega 4 Relay using an advanced Identify Friend/Foe (IFF) device. He directed Shepard to a derelict Reaper in the Thorne system of the Hawking Eta Cluster, orbiting the brown dwarf Mnemosyne, to recover an IFF, warning the Omega 4 passage would likely be one-way. Jacob Taylor, Miranda Lawson, and Mordin Solus discussed the results; EDI calculated the Collector homeworld lay in the galactic core. A blind FTL jump left the Normandy in the Balor System of the Caleston Rift, and two upgrades became available on Illium. Cut content accounted for Legion's presence in squad reactions and included alternate Illusive Man debriefing lines.
