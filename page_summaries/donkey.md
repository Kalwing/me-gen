Sergeant Donkey, nicknamed "Donk," was a Systems Alliance soldier assigned to Unit 212's Dog Squad on Eden Prime, appearing in the comic Mass Effect: Foundation. He persistently pursued a sexual relationship with his squadmate Ashley Williams. Two days before the geth attack on Eden Prime, Donk and his squad were drinking at a bar when a lieutenant arrived and briefed them on a new mission: a Prothean Beacon had been unearthed on the planet and Unit 212 was to defend it. Donk was assigned leader of Dog Squad for the operation, over Ashley, who was the group's nominal leader, and Dog Squad was ordered to patrol the terrain from the colony to the beacon while the other squads went ahead by transport.

Donk privately admitted to Ashley that he would rather she led the squad and called the lieutenant an ass; Ashley believed the officer was prejudiced against her because of her grandfather Williams' name. Donk relayed that the whole squad respected her composure under pressure and urged her to stay in the service. On the night of the geth attack, Dog Squad found the remains of Bravo Squad. Donk split the group into search teams, taking Pennyloafer while pairing Ashley with Bates, but found no survivors. As Able and Charlie squads reported hostile contact, Donk relinquished command to Ashley, saying the mission needed real leadership. At dawn Dog Squad engaged the geth prematurely; Donk provided a diversion while Ashley and Rasputin flanked, and Sergeant Donkey was killed by weapons fire as Ashley ordered a retreat.

A day later, Ashley was interviewed on the Citadel by an Alliance psychologist about Eden Prime and recalled Donkey's remark that "luck is for the lonely," conceding he had been right. Trivia notes that the rank of sergeant does not exist in the Alliance rank structure.
