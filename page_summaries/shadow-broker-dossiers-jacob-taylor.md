This Shadow Broker dossier, unlocked after the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, profiled Jacob Taylor as an ex-Alliance soldier with biotic training and a solid service record, whom the Broker judged had been placed on Shepard's team mainly as a stabilizing personality.

It compiled his most recent workout stats (hundreds of crunches, squat thrusts, leg lifts and half sit-ups) and his preferred workout viewing, including Blasto: The Jellyfish Stings and its making-of, Old Yeller: Centennial Remastered Edition, and Asari Confessions 26: True Blue.

A military letter dated February 19, 2184, from Col. Hashim Banks of the First Earth Division Special Forces, expressed regret at Taylor's resignation from the Alliance military, praised him as an exemplary marine, and offered him a promotion to First Lieutenant with a pay raise if he would join Banks's company.

A personal letter from his father, Ronald Taylor, written as a just-in-case message before the Gernsback traveled too far from its last relay, admitted that he had not been around enough for his son, said the expedition's money was meant to buy a quiet life far from space with Jacob and his mother, and told Jacob he was proud of the man he had become. The letter closes: "I love you, son, and I'm proud of the man you've become. I wish I could have told you that one last time." The Gernsback was the ship whose survivors Taylor later encounters.
