The Geth Bomber was a geth unit introduced in the Mass Effect 3 multiplayer Retaliation Pack DLC and fightable by Shepard in the Armax Arsenal Arena combat simulator. Bombers closed to close range with a target, then performed a quick forward flyover dropping four bombs that detonated after a few seconds for moderate damage each; if they could not bomb, they used a weak close-ranged shock attack. Their full barrage was extreme, bypassed shield and health-gate protection, and stunned, capable of taking down even krogan classes. They were protected by moderate shields and armor, had a small profile that made them hard to hit, jerked sideways to evade fire, and had no head or weak point for bonus headshot damage. Like Geth Hunters and Pyros, they were completely silent. Effective counters included maintaining distance, Sabotage (a hacked Bomber would bomb nearby geth), Overload, Energy Drain, the Acolyte pistol, area-detonation weapons such as the Scorpion, and invulnerability powers timed against the barrage.
