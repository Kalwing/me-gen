Captain Enyala was an asari commando and a captain in the Eclipse mercenary band on Illium. Commander Shepard encountered her while assisting Miranda Lawson on her loyalty mission.

Enyala was hired by Miranda's father to "rescue" his daughter Oriana, Miranda's twin. She worked with Miranda's friend Niket, who had been bribed into betraying Miranda. Niket agreed to cooperate with Enyala only on the condition that he handle Oriana's transfer personally, so that there would be no trauma to Oriana's adoptive family. When Miranda confronted Niket and threatened to kill him, Shepard could choose to stop her; if Shepard did, Enyala killed Niket herself. Enyala was then killed in the ensuing battle.

In combat Enyala fought as a Vanguard, wielding an M-300 Claymore Heavy Shotgun alongside her biotics, using Warp to weaken targets before closing to shotgun range. She was treated as a boss, with a strong biotic barrier, heavy armor, and high health, and she could regenerate her barrier if left alone. She was surrounded by Eclipse allies during the fight and tended to advance directly on Shepard. Using Dominate on Enyala produced a unique line, "I'm sorry, sister," each time she was forced to turn on fellow Eclipse, and she could sometimes be heard telling her troops, "Remember, Lawson is mine."
