The Geth Juggernaut was a heavy geth infantry platform seen in the original Mass Effect and in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, distinct from the Mass Effect 3 multiplayer Geth Juggernaut Soldier character and its class power. In Mass Effect it was recognizable by red armor, bright red flashlight eyes and huge stature, towering over the battlefield like the Geth Prime. Juggernauts carried pulse rifles modified to fire Distortion Rockets, functioning as a stronger version of the Geth Rocket Trooper. They possessed four to six bars of regenerating shielding and very high health that did not regenerate unless aided by a Geth Repair Drone; they moved little and rarely used cover. Their Distortion Rockets were slow and dodgeable at range but could kill a weak or low-level Shepard in one hit. Sabotage left them defenseless until their weapon cooled, Overload and Warp lowered defenses, and Master-level AI Hacking could turn one into a powerful ally. On Feros they appeared in small groups supported by Geth Armatures, making them very hard to kill outside the Mako.

In Mass Effect: Infiltrator, Geth Juggernauts were large units armed with a Geth Plasma Shotgun and protected by both shields and armor, making them effectively the game's first boss-type enemy. They were dangerous only at short range because of the shotgun, and their slow movement gave players time to shoot; regular Geth Troopers, with longer-ranged weapons, were the priority target. Juggernauts were often deployed by geth dropship, could fire over small cover due to their height, had a strong close-range melee attack, and could not be stunned while their armor remained active. In the Legendary Edition, larger bipedal geth platforms were redesigned along the lines of the Geth Prime's Mass Effect 3 appearance, with three small eyes, removed shoulder armor and a leaner build.
