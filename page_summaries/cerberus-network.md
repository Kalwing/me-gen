Cerberus Network was a free online DLC and news service bundled with new copies of Mass Effect 2 and the Mass Effect Trilogy, and also downloadable separately at no additional cost. It was accessed from a terminal on the Mass Effect 2 main menu, where it displayed the Cerberus Daily News and any new downloadable content. On consoles, every profile on a machine could use DLC downloaded through it, but only the profile that activated the Network could fetch new updates; downloads themselves were delivered through BioWare's social site on PC, Xbox Live on the 360, or the PlayStation Store on the PS3. Content distributed through the Network included Cerberus Network-only downloads such as the Normandy Crash Site, Zaeed - The Price of Revenge, Cerberus Weapon and Armor, the Arc Projector, the Firewalker Pack and Mass Effect: Genesis; promotional and retail bonus items like Blood Dragon Armor, Inferno Armor, Terminus Weapon and Armor and the M-29 Incisor, unlocked once their codes were redeemed; and paid add-ons that did not themselves require the Network, including Kasumi - Stolen Memory, the Overlord, Aegis, Firepower and Equalizer packs, Lair of the Shadow Broker, and Arrival. After BioWare's website was deactivated, activation codes could no longer be redeemed there; players instead redeemed them through the "Redeem Product Code" section of their EA account settings.
