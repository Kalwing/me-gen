Project Base was an installation secretly built by a Systems Alliance deep-cover research team on the asteroid 157-Golgotha in the Bahak system of the Viper Nebula. The team, led by Dr. Amanda Kenson, constructed it around a Reaper artifact embedded in the asteroid, dubbed "Object Rho." Their original intent was to study the artifact, but visions it emitted convinced them a Reaper invasion was imminent. Believing the Reapers meant to use the Alpha Relay to strike anywhere in the galaxy, the team resolved to destroy the relay. They acquired scrap engines and navigation technology through contacts on Omega and installed them into the asteroid so it could be launched into the Alpha Relay at the press of a button; this plan was "the Project."

During their study of Object Rho the entire team was indoctrinated. Eager to witness the Reapers' arrival, they abandoned the plan to launch the asteroid. After Kenson was captured by batarians on a scouting mission, Commander Shepard rescued her and returned her to Project Base with less than two days before the Reapers arrived. Realizing the team was indoctrinated, Shepard fought waves of Project Guards, Engineers, Elites, Pyros and a YMIR Mech before being knocked out by an energy pulse from the artifact, waking two days later with only hours left. Despite the team's opposition and Kenson's attempt to overheat the fusion core and destroy the propulsion systems, Shepard activated the Project and escaped moments before the asteroid struck and destroyed the Alpha Relay, annihilating the entire Bahak system and its batarian population.
