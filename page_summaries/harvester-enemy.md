The Harvester was an aerial synthetic-organic enemy in Mass Effect 3, derived from the natural creatures of the same name that had been mutated by Reaper technology. Reaper Harvesters were typically deployed for air superiority in a planet's atmosphere, and the sight of one in flight was one of the first signs that a Reaper invasion was underway. Their massive wingspan let them close distance on prey quickly, and their appearance nearly always signaled that Reaper ground troops were close behind.

A Harvester's mouth held two heavy cannons that fired in an alternating pattern. These fast-firing guns were similar to those of a Ravager but fired in pulses of five rather than three and dealt damage over a wide area. Harvesters could also spawn additional enemies during combat. Defensively they had only armor, with no shields or barriers, and took no bonus damage from headshots or from the large blue disc on the chest. On certain mission junctures the armor bar did not display, making them hard to target though still killable; the bar returned when they were scripted to fly away. A Harvester's death produced a large explosion that could damage nearby enemies but not the player or allies.

Recommended tactics stressed keeping the squad out of the Harvester's field of fire while dealing with other threats first, since its damage was very high and its area-effect cannons could splash between allies. Incendiary Ammo, Armor-Piercing Ammo, and heavy weapons brought them down fast. Biotic powers such as Warp, Reave, Dark Channel, and Warp Ammo were useful, as were biotic explosions; tech users benefited from fire explosions and fire abilities. Vulnerability powers like Warp, Cryo Blast, and Proximity Mine with the Damage Taken evolution were effective. Infiltrators with Tactical Cloak could take one down easily with sniper rifles, and a Combat Drone, Sentry Turret, or Decoy could draw its fire briefly before being destroyed. Harvesters were rarely encountered and were the only non-unique enemy in the base game absent from multiplayer.
