Brief gameplay page with minimal lore prose: Biotic Sphere was a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode, used by the Asari Justicar Adept. It created a defensive shield around the caster and nearby allies that reduced their incoming damage while increasing damage dealt to enemies inside it. Higher ranks added shield-regeneration, larger radius, greater damage reduction, enemy weakening, and a Warp Effect evolution that applied Warp to enemies within the sphere and set them up for biotic detonations.
