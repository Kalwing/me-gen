The Reaper IFF was a Reaper-designed Identify Friend/Foe device the Normandy SR-2 needed in order to pass safely through the Omega 4 relay. After the Collector Ship mission, the Illusive Man directed Commander Shepard to a derelict Reaper orbiting the brown dwarf Mnemosyne in the Thorne system of the Hawking Eta cluster, where a Cerberus science team led by Dr. Chandana had gone silent while investigating the hulk.

Aboard the Cerberus ship, work logs revealed the team's progressive indoctrination: shared false memories, headaches, hallucinations of a "gray thing" from the walls, and Chandana "listening" to the specimens. Passing into the Reaper, the team found it had activated kinetic barriers; EDI advised destroying the mass effect core to drop them, though that same core powered the fields keeping the Reaper from falling into the brown dwarf. The interior and exterior were defended by hordes of Husks plus Scions and Abominations, and dragon's teeth were visible. A geth sniper wearing pieces of N7 armor picked off Husks, recognized Shepard as "Shepard-Commander," and later dropped a barrier to let the team reach the core before being overwhelmed. One log referenced a "dead god" that "still dreams," an allusion to Lovecraft's Cthulhu.

At the Mass Effect Core, Shepard destroyed the core (instantly possible with the M-920 Cain) while fighting continuous Husk waves, an iris repeatedly shielding the target. Completing the mission started a countdown before the Normandy crew would be endangered if Shepard delayed going through the Omega 4 relay, so players were advised to finish other recruitment and loyalty missions first. Rewards included 50,000 credits and the Heavy Skin Weave, Sniper Rifle Damage, and Shotgun Damage upgrades.

Afterward the team recovered the disabled geth. Miranda stored it in EDI's AI Core; Jacob wanted it spaced, Miranda wanted it given to Cerberus. Shepard could activate it (gaining it as the squadmate "Legion," 15 Paragon points) or sell it to Cerberus for 50,000 credits and 15 Renegade points, permanently losing Legion. In Mass Effect 3 this choice determined whether Legion or a Geth VI "backup" appeared during Priority: Cerberus Headquarters and related missions, and affected the possibility of securing both geth and quarian allegiance. Cut content included an indoctrinated Chandana husk boss fight and an option to leave the disabled Legion behind.
