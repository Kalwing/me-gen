An optional N7 assignment in Mass Effect 3, available after Priority: Sur'Kesh, when Specialist Samantha Traynor mentioned intelligence about Cerberus activity on Tuchanka. Cerberus had seized an old military installation in the Krogan DMZ and was firing its large cannon into orbit to clear a path for an incoming Cerberus cruiser. Shepard landed to neutralize the operation. The mission uses the multiplayer map Firebase Giant, and Admiral Hackett briefed the squad on the way down.

Shepard's squad fought Cerberus infantry, Assault Troopers, Centurions, Combat Engineers, Guardians, a Nemesis and Turrets, first securing the cannon's control room, which stopped the ground-shaking cannon fire. Cerberus then cut power to the console, forcing Shepard to shoot the latches off two power modules and reactivate them, then fight back to the console and activate it. On the map Shepard could recover the Old Grid Schematics for the Citadel: Improved Power Grid mission and the Armax Arsenal Gauntlets.

Once the console was activated, Cortez reported the Cerberus cruiser beginning its bombardment and relayed its coordinates; the installation's guns automatically shot the cruiser down, and the mission ended as it broke up in orbit. Back on the Normandy, speaking with Hackett earned Shepard the Krogan First Division war asset, along with 20,000 credits and +5 Reputation.
