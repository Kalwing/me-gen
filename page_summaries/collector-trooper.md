The Collector Trooper was a Collector enemy unit added to Mass Effect 3's multiplayer by the Retaliation Pack DLC; it also appeared in combat simulations in the Armax Arsenal Arena in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC. It was distinct from the Collector Drone, the basic Collector infantry unit of Mass Effect 2.

Collector Troopers were equipped with Collector SMGs for ranged combat, used organic blades for melee, and threw Frag Grenades to flush enemies from cover. Like other Collector opponents they could be possessed, gaining additional durability and offensive abilities. They had a moderate amount of health, frequently took cover, could perform combat rolls to avoid attacks, could be staggered by powers or gunfire, and took 40% additional damage from headshots.

Tactics that worked on a Cerberus Assault Trooper generally worked on Collector Troopers, though compared with their Geth, Reaper, and Cerberus counterparts they laid down greater volumes of fire and had slightly higher health, and they usually appeared alongside a Collector Captain or a deadlier foe such as a Scion or Praetorian. Their weapon tracers were light gray and hard to see. Their melee blades always struck twice, but a batarian's Blade Armor caused the Trooper to stagger back after the first blow, preventing the second and giving batarians a close-quarters advantage.
