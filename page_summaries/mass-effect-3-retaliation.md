Mass Effect 3: Retaliation was a free multiplayer DLC pack for Mass Effect 3, released October 9, 2012 on all platforms in North America and October 10 for PlayStation 3 in Europe. Its fiction framed it as Harbinger unleashing the Collector legions from the Black Arks as an all-new fourth enemy faction. It added fifteen enemies, including the Collectors (Abomination, Collector Captain, Trooper, Web, Praetorian, Scion, Seeker Plague, Seeker Swarm), their "Possessed" variants, and reinforcements to existing factions (Cerberus Dragoon, Geth Bomber). It also added sixteen multiplayer characters (four at launch, twelve over time) covering asari, batarian, drell, geth, krogan, quarian, turian, volus and vorcha kits; three Collector weapons (Assault Rifle, SMG, Sniper Rifle); three ammo bonuses (Drill, Explosive, Phasic Rounds); five Gear items; six Hazard maps (variants of existing maps with environmental conditions such as acid rain, sandstorm, whiteout and reactor meltdown, granting a 15% experience bonus); and the Challenges system with titles and banners.
