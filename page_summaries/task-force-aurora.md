Task Force Aurora was a Systems Alliance research group created by Admiral Hackett with a mandate to investigate legends and rumors about the Reapers predating the public revelation of their existence. It was headed by Dr. Garret Bryson.

During the Reaper invasion in 2186, Hackett directed Commander Shepard to assist Bryson's investigation. Bryson revealed that the task force had been researching Leviathan, a mythical being theorized to have killed the Reaper known as the Leviathan of Dis. Bryson was then killed by his assistant, and Shepard picked up the trail, reasoning that anything able to kill a Reaper would be a valuable asset. The search led to the asteroid Mahavid, where Shepard arrived too late to save Dr. Alex Garneau, killed by enthralled miners, and to the planet Namakli, where Shepard rescued Bryson's daughter Dr. Ann Bryson from a Reaper attack on the dig site. Affected by a Leviathan artifact, Ann helped pinpoint Leviathan's location on 2181 Despoina. There, Shepard made contact with Leviathan, one of several survivors of an ancient race that gave rise to the Reapers; the Leviathans agreed to aid the galaxy's races and demonstrated their power by disabling an attacking Reaper capital ship.

Known members included Garret Bryson (head), his assistant Derek Hadley, Dr. Alex Garneau (leader of Project Basilisk, investigating Mahavid), Dr. Ann Bryson (leader of Project Scarab, investigating Namakli), and her Namakli colleagues Boyles, Hopkins, and Kirkwood.
