Grenade Upgrades in Mass Effect modified grenades to produce a single effect at any time. Anti-Thorian Gas was designed to disable Thorian-infected subjects without killing them and instantly killed Thorian Creepers, though it did not affect asari clones; it was obtained only on Feros from Juliana Baynham and was recommended for sparing use. Cryo Explosive grenades contained a small Bose-Einstein condensate that snap-froze nearby targets, reducing enemy accuracy by -20% at level I up to -47% at level X. Fusion Explosive grenades dispersed radioactive polonium causing radiation poisoning, scaling toxic damage from 20% at level I to 65% at level X. High Explosive grenades maximized blast radius and impact, useful for flushing lightly-armored enemies like Geth Troopers from cover and for crowd control, with damage, radius, and weapon force bonuses scaling from level I (10%, 50cm, 10%) to level X (37%, 140cm, 37%). Incendiary Explosive released a clinging thermal paste that burned through nearly all substances, dealing damage per second over five seconds from 15 at level I to 51 at level X. Related systems included Grenade Carrying Capacity Upgrades and tech proximity mines.
