This Mass Effect 3 Codex section chronicled the Reaper invasion. The Reapers took Earth within hours, arriving from batarian space and jumping relay to relay to bypass the Sixth and Seventh Fleets; more than a dozen capital ships screened the main force at Arcturus Station while others destroyed the First and Fourth Fleets and severed Earth's comm buoys, satellites, and undersea cables. Capital ships annihilated cities including Adelaide, Hamburg, Al Jubail, and Fort Worth while destroyers melted roads and herded survivors for the harvest. In batarian space the Reapers first struck the Vular system, then descended on the homeworld Khar'shan; the Hegemony's response was uncoordinated, its comm buoys were destroyed, and refugees flooded into the human Exodus Cluster. The Reapers hit turian space first at Taetrus, site of the Vallum Blast, ambushing the Hierarchy fleet as it emerged from the relay and broadcasting images of the ruined capital Vallum. Military planners rejected destroying mass relays (each rupture would devastate its solar system) and using starships as FTL projectiles (mass effect drives carry hardwired anti-collision safeties).

At the Battle of Palaven, Fleet Admiral Irix Coronati's "Fifteen-Minute Plan" used the carriers Undaunted and Resolute to gather drone reconnaissance, then jumped dreadnoughts into the Reaper fleet to down several capital ships before Reaper destroyers jumped to Palaven, forcing a costly ground war and a turian retreat. At the Battle of Rannoch, the quarians retreated behind the star Tikkun and sent strike teams to destroy the Reaper transmitting upgrades to the geth; outcomes varied, with an emergency ceasefire ordered by Commander Shepard and Admiral Shala'Raan vas Tonbay letting quarians and geth survive and ally, or alternatively the near-annihilation of the quarians, or the destruction of the geth. In the Cerberus Coup, Councilor Udina secretly coordinated with Cerberus to seize the Citadel and command its fleet to relieve Earth; the plan collapsed when Executor Pallin and the salarian councilor learned of it, the Illusive Man sent assassins under Kai Leng, and Udina was killed, costing humanity its councilor amid unproven rumors of his indoctrination. On Thessia, asari hit-and-run tactics failed against superior Reaper numbers, orbital bombardment forced a conventional defense, and the lightly defended homeworld fell. In the Miracle at Palaven, a turian and krogan counterattack leaked a false battle plan and used the dreadnought Indomitable as bait to draw Reaper capital ships away; landed krogan commandos delivered warp bombs and fission weapons to Palaven's resistance, who smuggled them into Reaper ships opened to indoctrinate turian leaders, destroying many at the cost of the civilian captives inside and reclaiming large territories. General Minin Resvirix eulogized the turian dead.
