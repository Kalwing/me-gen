The M-7 Lancer was an assault rifle in Mass Effect 3, a rare collector's item introduced shortly after the First Contact War. It had been refurbished by an unknown master weaponsmith to use modern high-velocity rounds while retaining an earlier era's heat-generation system, so it needed no thermal clips. Few of these finely crafted weapons existed.

With the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC or Legendary Edition, the Lancer was obtained during Citadel Archives: Escape, found in a display case on the capsule holding information about the First Contact War. In multiplayer it was categorized as ultra-rare and required the Mass Effect 3: Reckoning pack.

Instead of thermal clips the Lancer used a self-cooling system like the Particle Rifle, with a finite magazine but an unlimited regenerating ammo reserve; emptying the magazine fully forced a lengthy, uninterruptible heat-venting animation. Its magazine grew from 38 rounds at level 1 to 57 at level 10, and could reach 102 with a magazine mod. Aside from self-cooling it was essentially an M-8 Avenger with greater damage and recoil, doing excellent damage for its low weight, making it ideal for power-reliant builds and for the ammo-scarce Platinum difficulty. It was based on the Hahne-Kedar Lancer line from the first Mass Effect, a callback to that game's cooldown-based weapons.
