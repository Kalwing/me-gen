The Destiny Ascension was the flagship of the Citadel Fleet and the pride of asari engineering, an asari dreadnought four times the size of the largest human vessel and crewed by up to 10,000 asari under Matriarch Lidanya. It was unmatched in scale among Mass Effect dreadnoughts; only the combined fleets of the Citadel could field one. Though Citadel-race technology varied little because most was reverse-engineered from the Citadel and mass relays, the Ascension's design was distinctly asari-first, and its specifications were highly classified. A Citadel archive recording showed the asari vessel that first discovered the Citadel to be of similar design, indicating the class dated back to at least 580 BCE. Diplomats given six-hour tours reported seeing no more than 10% of the ship, and one volus visitor claimed it had almost as much firepower as the rest of the asari fleet combined; its huge crew made it effectively a mobile city.

Like most dreadnoughts it was most effective at range. Its main gun was a super-heavy mass accelerator cannon able to score a critical hit on virtually any known Milky Way vessel. Its defenses used Citadel space's standardized GARDIAN network of automated anti-missile and anti-fighter laser turrets, which could repel attack almost indefinitely unless overwhelmed by cyber warfare and massed force. Because its point defenses could be brought down, it was vulnerable to close-combat frigates and fighters and needed a small support fleet. It carried an FTL drive, conventional thrusters, and backups including ion and antiproton drives, but deploying these was so expensive that the ship's operating cost usually exceeded its long-range value. In practice it served as a deterrent, meant to make aggressors choose diplomacy over war with Citadel space.

During the Battle of the Citadel in 2183, Sovereign's geth fleet attacked and the Council was evacuated to the Ascension per standard emergency procedure, but its main drives were quickly disabled by geth cyber warfare and overwhelming force, its kinetic barriers reduced toward 40%, leaving it helpless. Commander Shepard chose whether to divert the Alliance Fifth Fleet to rescue it (at heavy human cost) or concentrate on Sovereign, in which case the Ascension and Council were destroyed; the default outcome in an unimported Mass Effect 2 game was its destruction. If saved, it spent months in repairs and then undertook a 20-colony "victory cruise" completed in 2185, widely seen as propaganda justifying its expense. If destroyed, the asari ceded their share of Citadel defense responsibility to the turians. In the Reaper War, a surviving and upgraded Ascension was finally used for its true purpose of large-scale ship-to-ship combat in the battle to retake Earth.
