Blood Dragon Armor was a non-customizable armor set appearing in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. In-fiction it was originally created for Earth's Urban Combat Championship league and had undergone as much field testing as modern military hardware. It used a proprietary power cell said to cost as much as a EUCC rookie's contract, and its microframe computer adapted to any top-tier omni-tool, kinetic barrier, or biotic amp for high, error-free performance. The chest and shoulder piece bore the logo of the Edmonton Blood Dragons, and the interior carried the signatures of the entire team, some with messages such as "[expletive] the geth!" and "Shepard, get well soon!!!" How the Illusive Man obtained the armor was never explained.

In Mass Effect 2 the set increased power damage by 15 percent and shield strength by 10 percent, making it a strong choice for Adepts, Engineers, and Sentinels. It was unlocked by entering the registration code for Dragon Age: Origins, or formerly through the BioWare Social Network's Dragon Age DLC Bundle, after which the player received an email from the Illusive Man saying the armor, mentioned as arranged by Jacob, had been delivered to the Normandy's armor closet. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition it could instead be bought from the Omega Market for 15,000 credits.

In Mass Effect 3 the armor carried a similar description and was purchased from Kanala Exports in the Presidium Commons for 50,000 credits, or from Procurement terminals in the Normandy shuttle bay or the Armax Arsenal Arena for 55,000 credits, before discounts. Its bonuses favored power-reliant builds with added protection, benefiting Adepts, Engineers, Sentinels, Infiltrators, and Vanguards. Trivia noted the Edmonton Blood Dragons name likely referenced BioWare, headquartered in Edmonton, Alberta.
