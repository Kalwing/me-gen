Mass Effect: Genesis 2 was an interactive backstory comic produced by Dark Horse Comics for Mass Effect 3: Special Edition for Wii U, and released April 2, 2013 for Xbox 360, PC, and PlayStation 3. An updated version of Mass Effect: Genesis, it stood in for both Mass Effect and Mass Effect 2 for new players, summarizing the first two games and letting the player make six choices from each. It was prompted after character creation but was optional, with separate male and female Shepard versions. It could not be used when importing a Mass Effect 2 save into Mass Effect 3.

The six Mass Effect choices matched the original Genesis. The six Mass Effect 2 choices were: how many squadmates Shepard recruited; whether squadmate conflicts were resolved or ignored; whether Shepard stayed true to old friends or moved on; whether Shepard went through the Omega 4 Relay immediately or upgraded the Normandy SR-2 first; whether Shepard started a new romance; and whether the Collector Base was spared or destroyed. The comic omitted Feros, the geth, squadmate recruitment and loyalty missions, other romances, missions such as Horizon, and all DLC content, meaning Kasumi Goto and Zaeed Massani would not appear in Mass Effect 3. Resolving conflicts implicitly resolved all loyalty missions in specific canonical ways (heretics destroyed, Maelon's data destroyed, Morinth killed). Garrus Vakarian always survived the Suicide Mission and always appeared in Mass Effect 3; the "worst" outcome left 8 of 10 squadmates dead. The page includes a table of Suicide Mission survival results by choice combination. It was priced at 320 BioWare Points on PC and $3.99 on Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3, and is included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition.
