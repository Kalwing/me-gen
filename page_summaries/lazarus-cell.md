The Lazarus Cell was one of many compartmentalized cells operating within Cerberus during Mass Effect 2. As with all Cerberus cells, its members had no knowledge of other cells, a security measure preventing captured agents from compromising the wider organization. Its leading operative was Miranda Lawson, who reported to the Illusive Man, with Jacob Taylor as her executive officer.

After Commander Shepard's death, the cell was tasked with recovering and reanimating Shepard's remains in the Lazarus Project, laboring aboard the Lazarus Research Station for two years and successfully reviving Shepard. In the project's final stages the cell's medical officer, Wilson, hacked and reprogrammed the station's mechs to attack the staff, killing most of the cell's personnel; only Lawson, Taylor and the prematurely awakened Shepard survived and fled before the station was destroyed by remote detonation.

The survivors traveled to Minuteman Station, where the Illusive Man placed Shepard in command of the cell and tasked it with discovering what had caused the disappearance of tens of thousands of human colonists in the Terminus Systems. The cell's investigation of Freedom's Progress confirmed the Collectors were responsible. Afterward the cell's headquarters moved to the newly completed Normandy SR-2 and it was restaffed with 24 new members according to EDI. Lawson retained her position as leading operative and continued reporting to the Illusive Man, while Shepard, Doctor Chakwas, Joker and the recruited specialists were not official members of the cell or of Cerberus. The cell's fate depended on player choices, ranging from the Collectors killing most of its personnel to Shepard saving the entire crew; in all cases it accomplished its mission to stop the Collectors, and a surviving Shepard could either cut ties with Cerberus or remain on good terms with the Illusive Man.
