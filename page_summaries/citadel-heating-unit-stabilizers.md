Citadel: Heating Unit Stabilizers was a short fetch assignment available after Priority: Palaven and N7: Cerberus Fighter Base. A salarian named Sellea, standing in front of the C-Sec Outpost in the Presidium Commons, worried aloud in a comm conversation about unstable weather threatening his sister's colony's egg clutches; overhearing him activated the mission. The advanced heating unit stabilizer schematics could be taken from a wall panel opposite the security terminal during N7: Cerberus Fighter Base, or bought from the Spectre Requisitions terminal for 1,000 credits. Turning them in to Sellea updated the Salarian Third Fleet War Asset. Speaking to him again afterward let Shepard overhear that his sister's egg clutch had stabilized and that she wished to name her firstborn after the person who helped. The fighter base was inaccessible until after Priority: Tuchanka, and the item could not be returned to the Citadel until after Priority: The Citadel II.
