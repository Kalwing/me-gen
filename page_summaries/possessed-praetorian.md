The Possessed Praetorian was a Collector enemy unit added to Mass Effect 3 multiplayer with the Retaliation Pack DLC, and it also appeared in the Armax Arsenal Arena combat simulator introduced by the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC. It was an enhanced variant of the standard Praetorian, the large floating Collector construct built from fused husks.

Offensively it fired powerful heat-seeking missile-like projectiles that curved around and over terrain, sought multiple targets, caused short stuns, and fired continuously for several seconds (fewer missiles in single-player). It followed these with twin particle beams like a normal Praetorian. It closed distance with a fast, long-range pounce and then either raked enemies with rapid claw strikes or grabbed a victim, lifting them to crush their head for an instant kill. In single-player the Possessed variant replaced the first of the Praetorian's usual three beam shots with a guided missile volley.

Defensively it shared the normal Praetorian's capabilities but with armor and barriers 50% stronger, making it by far the most durable enemy in the game, with superior barriers and over twice the armor of a Geth Prime or Banshee. Recommended tactics were to stay at range, avoid the missile volleys and pounce, attack from the rear while it fired its beams, and use damage-amplifying debuffs such as Warp, Cryo Blast, and Tactical Scan given its high durability.
