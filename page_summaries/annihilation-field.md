Annihilation Field was a biotic power available in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode, used by the N7 Fury Adept and the Asari Valkyrie Sentinel. It was not available in single-player. Distinct from the later Annihilation skill in Mass Effect: Andromeda, the power spun a fiery biotic effect around the user to burn nearby enemies, and while active it could be recast to blast a short-range area and to detonate combos.

At rank 1 the field had an 8-second recharge, 100 damage per second, a 45-second duration, a 4-meter radius, and 500 blast damage. Rank 2 increased recharge speed by 25 percent. Rank 3 increased damage by 20 percent. Rank 4 offered a choice between a 30 percent damage increase and a 30 percent larger impact radius. Rank 5 offered either causing targets in the field to take 15 percent additional damage from all sources, or a 20 percent movement speed boost while active. Rank 6 offered either a 65 percent damage increase or a Drain evolution that doubled duration and drained 100 percent of damage done to enemy shields and barriers to restore the user's own shields.

Mechanically, the power dealt 1.5x damage to armor and barriers, and its detonation blast passed through walls and cover. The field persisted even while the user was downed, still damaging or killing nearby enemies. Only enemies within the radius were primed for a Biotic Explosion, so moving away from a target lost the priming effect, though a player could walk back and forth to re-prime enemies for repeated explosions. With the Rank 6 Drain evolution, detonating explosions on primed targets could restore the user's barrier for the full explosion damage, sometimes instantly. Unprotected organics caught in the field could panic, reducing the risk of being flanked at close range. Manual deactivation could detonate all four combo types. Annihilation Field was one of only three powers, alongside Warp and Reave, that could set up and detonate Biotic Explosions on any enemy at any time, making it valuable in biotics-heavy squads, and it was also an effective automatic defense against Swarmers.
