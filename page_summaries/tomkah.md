The tomkah was a large infantry fighting vehicle fielded by the krogan on Tuchanka. It resembled the Alliance M35 Mako and M29 Grizzly in outward form but was considerably larger, standing almost twice as high as a standard Alliance IFV, its size likely reflecting the need to carry krogan-sized passengers. Its only visible armament was a large dorsal double-barreled turret set toward the rear, most likely a mass accelerator cannon. Its armor was strong enough to withstand a ten-ton bomb.

A part called the combustion manifold was vital to the tomkah's operation. The vehicle could run without it, but mechanics were very reluctant to send an affected vehicle into the field, and building a replacement took roughly three days' work.

The name may reference Tonka Trucks, the American toy construction vehicles; the tomkah's oversized wheels echo the toys, and "Tonka" is a Dakota-Sioux word for "big" or "great." According to The Art of the Mass Effect Trilogy, the design that became the tomkah in Mass Effect 2 was originally a rejected concept for the M35 Mako in the first game.
