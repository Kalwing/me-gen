Mass Effect: Retribution was the third Mass Effect novel written by Drew Karpyshyn, released July 27, 2010 and set after the events of Mass Effect 2. In the story, the Illusive Man, leader of the pro-human black ops group Cerberus and one of the few who knew the truth about the Reapers, launched a plan to uncover the enemy's strengths and weaknesses by studying someone implanted with modified Reaper technology. His chosen subject was former Cerberus operative Paul Grayson, who had earlier freed his daughter from the group with the help of Ascension project director Kahlee Sanders. When Sanders learned Grayson was missing, she turned to Alliance war hero Captain David Anderson, and together they searched for the secret Cerberus facility holding him, pursued by others also seeking Grayson. As the experiments continued, the Reaper technology twisted Grayson's mind, with insidious whispers threatening to take over his identity and unleash the Reapers on the galaxy.
