Short gameplay page: Assault Rifles was a combat talent in the first Mass Effect, available by default only to the Soldier, the Turian Agent, and the Krogan Battlemaster. Higher ranks improved damage and accuracy with assault rifles and, at certain levels, unlocked Shotguns for the Krogan Battlemaster and Sniper Rifles for the Turian Agent and Soldier. The talent let a character aim down sights with assault rifles to shrink the reticle and improve accuracy, and it unlocked the Overkill active ability (upgradable to Advanced and Master Overkill), which reduced weapon overheating and boosted accuracy for a short duration. In the Legendary Edition any character can aim down sights with any weapon without a talent, and Overkill was reworked to a shorter six-second duration with greatly increased damage.
