The Preaching Hanar was a hanar on the Citadel Presidium in 2183, standing near the Wards access corridor and arguing with a C-Sec officer. Even by hanar standards it was deeply religious, and it had decided to preach about the Enkindlers, the hanar name for the Protheans, on the Presidium.

The C-Sec officer told it that evangelising was illegal on the Presidium, that the Citadel had designated preaching areas elsewhere, and that the hanar lacked an evangelical permit. Despite the officer's rudeness the hanar remained calm and polite. It argued that it only wished to share the truth of the Enkindlers with everyone, and that because stating the truth was not preaching, no permit was required.

Commander Shepard could resolve the dispute in several ways: buy the hanar an evangelical permit so it could preach legally; tell it that it was misusing the gifts of speech and sentience the Enkindlers had given its people, prompting it to admit its enthusiasm had got the better of it and to leave; or persuade the C-Sec officer to let it stay, after which it remained on the Presidium preaching to passers-by about the glory of the Enkindlers.
