Flare was a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's single-player mode, in which the user focused and expended all biotic energy to unleash a large flare that threw enemies within its range and caused massive damage. At rank 1 it dealt 400 damage with 200 N of force over a 6-metre radius on a 30-second recharge. Later ranks increased recharge speed, damage and force, and radius, with a rank 5 choice between faster recharge or stronger combo detonations and a rank 6 choice between larger radius or a 75 percent bonus to damage against shields and barriers.

Flare dealt 0.5x damage to armor and 1.5x damage to barriers baseline. It could detonate Biotic Explosions but could not trigger other power combos. It was described as the most destructive biotic power in the game, offset by a longer-than-average cooldown, and functioned like an unlimited but slow-firing grenade whose blast penetrated thin cover and destroyed environmental objects. It could stagger or knock down lighter enemies and was one of the few biotic powers effective against shielded targets. It could be blocked by the defensive abilities of Phantoms, Banshees, Rampart Mechs, and Praetorians, and did not damage Guardians or CAT6 Heavies when hitting their front shields.

Flare was available to Aria T'Loak, and to Commander Shepard only as a bonus power selected from the Normandy med bay between missions, since it could not be chosen when starting a new game. When used by Aria it traveled straight to targets and hit instantly; Shepard's version launched a glowing projectile similar to Warp. It was not available in multiplayer.
