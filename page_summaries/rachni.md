The rachni were an insect-like, hive-minded species from the tidally locked planet Suen, believed extinct after the Rachni Wars roughly two thousand years before 2183. Intelligent and highly aggressive, they had chitinous exoskeletons, four walking limbs, green blood, and beak-like mouths of five mandibles. Their forms included Workers (small, suicide-explode with toxin), Soldiers (larger defenders with toxic spit), Brood Warriors (large biotic males) and Queens (largest and most intelligent, guiding the hive).

At the height of the Prothean Empire the Protheans bred rachni as war weapons until they became uncontrollable and turned on their masters; the Protheans destroyed up to 200 worlds but enough rachni survived on Suen to recover. The rachni developed civilization, spaceflight, and cryogenic suspension, and researched element zero found in the "howling gulf" crater on Kashshaptu. Around 1 CE salarian explorers opened a relay into rachni space and were captured; the rachni reverse-engineered FTL drives and expanded explosively, starting the Rachni Wars. Negotiation was impossible because the queens could not be reached in their deep nests. The krogan, uplifted by the salarians in 80 CE, turned the war by striking the queens in their lairs; when the rachni refused to surrender at Suen, the krogan bombarded their cities and detonated bombs in their nests, creating massive sinkholes. The rachni were declared extinct in 300 CE.

In Mass Effect, Binary Helix recovered a drifting rachni ship carrying cryo-suspended eggs and hatched one at Peak 15 on Noveria, expecting a worker but getting a queen. Matriarch Benezia, on Saren Arterius's orders, mined the queen's genetic memory for the Mu Relay coordinates. Newborn rachni removed from the queen's "song" went insane and overran the facility. The captured queen, speaking through a dead commando, said the rachni had been driven mad by a "sour yellow note" from space, asked Shepard to euthanize her broken children, and promised peace if freed. Squadmates split: Liara T'Soni and Tali'Zorah favored release, Garrus Vakarian wanted the Council alerted, Kaidan Alenko favored release pragmatically, while Urdnot Wrex and Ashley Williams opposed it; the choice was Shepard's. Cerberus later cloned rachni on Binthu and at listening posts on Nepmos and Altahe, with commander Flores concluding the rachni were too intelligent to exploit.

In Mass Effect 2, a freed queen contacted Shepard through an asari on Illium, building an army against "the coming darkness" (the Reapers) and repairing damaged starships. In Mass Effect 3, the Reapers found the queen on Utukku, indoctrinated most of her children, and converted them into Ravagers spawning Swarmers. Aralakh Company, led by Grunt or Urdnot Dagg, assaulted the hive; Shepard could save the queen (her workers then aiding the Crucible) or let her "embrace the silence." If the real queen was dead, the Reapers built an artificial "Breeder" queen who would later betray the Alliance engineers. Dr. Garret Bryson theorized the Leviathan may have provoked the Rachni Wars to raise an army against the Reapers.
