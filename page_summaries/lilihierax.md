Lilihierax, known as 'Li' because humans found his name hard to pronounce, was a turian shuttle mechanic at Port Hanshan on Noveria, found loitering near the garage. A former military tech, he oversaw a team of twelve. Commander Shepard could talk to him about local events: the scandal involving Administrator Anoleis, Synthetic Insights and Lorik Qui'in, Peak 15's poor reputation and the facility lockdown, and Matriarch Benezia's movements. He reported that Benezia had taken a shuttle to Peak 15 before contact was cut. Li was one of the few able to enter and leave Port Hanshan during lockdown, but refused to lend Shepard his tracked garage pass, fearing he would lose his job and be sued. Easy-going and proud of his work, he contrasted with the typical disciplined turian warrior image, much like the Presidium Groundskeeper. Cut content indicates he was originally to sell Mako upgrades. He was voiced by Keith Szarabajka, who also voiced Delan, Elias Kelham and Harbinger.
