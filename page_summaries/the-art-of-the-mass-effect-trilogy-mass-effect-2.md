This page transcribes the Mass Effect 2 section of the developer commentary in the 2021 edition of The Art of the Mass Effect Trilogy, with text credited to Casey Hudson, Derek Watts, Chris Hepler, Karin Weekes and Ryan Cormier. It is a behind-the-scenes reference collecting BioWare's notes on the concept art and design process for the game.

The commentary walks through character designs for Commander Shepard's piece-based armor, Jacob Taylor, Miranda Lawson, Garrus Vakarian, Tali'Zorah, Grunt, Thane Krios, Mordin Solus, Legion, Jack, Samara, the Illusive Man, and DLC companions Kasumi Goto and Zaeed Massani. It records design intentions such as krogan crests growing with age, Thane's balaclava evoking a ski mask, Jack's tattoos covering her scars, the Illusive Man's deliberately "inhuman" eye implants, and Zaeed's abandoned prosthetic-leg and dog concepts.

Enemy and creature entries cover the Scion, Praetorian, Proto-Reaper (built from harvested human DNA), and the insect-inspired Collectors, plus the YMIR, FENRIS and LOKI mechs, the Oculus (referencing HAL 9000) and the engineer's tech drone.

Environment sections describe Omega (shaped like a mushroom cloud, with Afterlife modeled on Las Vegas clubs), Illium's utopian Nos Astra, the Citadel Wards, Tuchanka, Horizon, 2175 Aeia, the prison ship Purgatory, Pragia, the Migrant Fleet and its liveships (with a note that quarians follow a vegan diet for water-conservation reasons), Korlus, the Lazarus Research Station, the Derelict Reaper, and the Collector Ship.

The Normandy SR-2 entry explains that the plot's requirement to destroy the original ship freed the team to redesign it with multiple floors, a "muscle car" paint job and a supercollider-style engine room. Weaponry notes cover the five conventional gun classes and the new heavy weapons. DLC sections address Arrival (originally an underwater base inspired by The Abyss), Kasumi – Stolen Memory, Zaeed – The Price of Revenge, Lair of the Shadow Broker (including the Shadow Broker's dual human/alien design), Overlord and David Archer, and Project Firewalker with the M-44 Hammerhead derived from the Mako.
