Balor was a gateway star system in Mass Effect, accessible after certain missions in Mass Effect 2 (Collector Ship or Project Firewalker: Rosalie Lost) and Mass Effect 3 (Priority: Rannoch or Citadel: Leviathan I). Its star was a red dwarf whose solar output was normally weak, but during solar flares its luminosity could double or triple. The page noted this fit the description of a flare star, a class that also includes Sol's nearest stellar neighbors Proxima Centauri and Barnard's Star.

The system's worlds were described as rich in resources, though mining corporations had only begun to exploit them; some regions remained unexplored and others were beyond economically feasible extraction. The system contained a fuel depot and a mass relay. Its named bodies were Cernunnos, the first planet; Bres, the second planet; Elatha, the third planet; and Partholon, the fourth planet. Caleston was a moon orbiting the planet Cernunnos. The Fomor Belt was an asteroid belt lying between the orbits of Cernunnos and Elatha.

The page observed that Balor may have been named after a king of the Fomorians in Irish mythology, with several of the system's planets also named after figures from the Irish Mythological Cycle. It also noted that the Caleston Rift cluster became automatically available after Priority: Rannoch, but that with the Leviathan DLC installed Balor could be accessed much earlier, though travel to Aysur remained restricted in that case; scanning Caleston before visiting Dr. Bryson's Lab was said to break story immersion because the resulting information was introduced only upon formally starting the Leviathan mission chain.
