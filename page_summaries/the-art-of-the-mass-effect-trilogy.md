The Art of the Mass Effect Trilogy: Expanded Edition was a 264-page concept art book published by Dark Horse Comics in 2021, collecting BioWare artwork and developer commentary for Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. It was originally released in 2012 as The Art of the Mass Effect Universe; the 2021 edition added over 90 pages of art focused on the trilogy's downloadable content and Mass Effect 3's multiplayer, including Lair of the Shadow Broker and Citadel. Developer commentary in both editions was written by Casey Hudson (game director), Derek Watts (art director) and Chris Hepler (writer), with additional Expanded Edition commentary by editors Karin Weekes and Ryan Cormier. After a preface by "The Mass Effect Team at BioWare" and separate introductions by Hudson and Watts, the book is divided into the three games, containing felt sketches, design concepts, production paintings, 3D renders and commentary. ISBNs: ebook 978-1-50672-164-4, hardcover 978-1-50672-163-7, BioWare Gear Edition 978-1-50672-576-5.
