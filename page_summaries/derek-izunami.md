Major Derek Izunami was a Systems Alliance Major and Jacob Taylor's former commanding officer, appearing in Mass Effect Galaxy. He met Jacob when Jacob arrived at the Citadel shortly after a batarian attack on the Arcturian Jade. Izunami had information pointing to another attack, this time on the Citadel itself. He claimed that batarians viewed humans as usurpers and that the planned peace agreement between the Council and Jath'Amon would be seen by some batarian factions as a surrender.

Izunami proposed an investigative mission into the Nemean Abyss, deep in the Terminus Systems, and needed someone unconnected to the Alliance military because unauthorized tactics might be required to prevent the attack. Jacob accepted and was assigned a ship. Izunami instructed him to meet a contact at Cartagena Station, in a bar called The Fringe.
