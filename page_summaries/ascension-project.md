The Ascension Project was a renewed human biotics training program that succeeded BAaT. It focused on the children of the victims of three element zero "accidents" that occurred across the Systems Alliance in 2170 on four separate colonies, including Yandoa; although the industrial accidents happened under suspicious circumstances, no link between them was ever found. On Yandoa alone, thirty-seven children were born with biotic abilities and were all sent to the Ascension Project. Established around 2176, the Project was based at the Jon Grissom Academy, a medium-sized space station orbiting the human colony of Elysium in the Skyllian Verge.

Unlike BAaT, the Project was not a military-exclusive venture; it invited civilian support and welcomed visits and input from attendees' families. It had three main aims: to develop and monitor new L4 implants (biotic amplifiers with built-in VI interfaces for monitoring implanted subjects); to help biotics maximize their field potential; and to help biotics integrate into human society. The last aim held an important curriculum place, with students given philosophical and moral guidance on the responsibilities of using biotics. Staff were nonetheless equipped with Aldrin Labs stun guns to subdue students who misused their abilities, though these were used on a student only once.

At some point the Project was infiltrated by Cerberus operatives seeking research on human biotics in order to "save" humanity. Cerberus left Dr. Jiro Toshiwa to monitor events there, particularly to study Gillian Grayson's progress. Known students included Gillian Grayson, Nick, and Seshaun; known staff included Dr. Kahlee Sanders, Security Chief Hendel Mitra, Dr. Jiro Toshiwa, Dr. Sanchez, and maths professor Jacob Berg. During the Grissom Academy evacuation in Mass Effect 3, Samantha Traynor told Shepard about the program; if Shepard was a Vanguard, Sentinel, or Adept, she said Shepard would have joined it had it existed during the Commander's youth.
