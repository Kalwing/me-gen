This page was a roster index for the first Mass Effect game, listing links to its characters grouped as protagonist, squad members, temporary squad members, allies, and adversaries. It held roughly twenty entries, including Commander Shepard, squadmates such as Ashley Williams, Garrus Vakarian, Kaidan Alenko, Liara T'Soni, Tali'Zorah and Urdnot Wrex, allies such as David Anderson and Jeff "Joker" Moreau, and adversaries such as Saren Arterius, Matriarch Benezia and Sovereign. It contained no original prose beyond the link tables.
