Benning: Evidence was a side mission in Mass Effect 3 in which Commander Shepard helped Ambassador Dominic Osoba find out what happened to his son, who was lost during a mission on the human colony world Benning. The mission became available after the missions Priority: Palaven and N7: Cerberus Abductions. On the Citadel Embassies, Osoba could be overheard trying to gather information about his missing son, whose squadmates had returned from Benning without him and refused to say what had happened. Shepard could offer to help. Alternatively, the mission was acquired by picking up a Dog Tag during a Hackett-sanctioned operation on Benning, in which case it was labelled Benning: Dog Tags in the journal.

On Benning, the Dog Tag lay on the ground near a support column by building 22, in an open area to the northwest of the starting position of the parent mission, surrounded by buildings numbered 22, 25, and 27. The tag was easily missed and produced no dialogue when collected. If missed, it could later be purchased from Spectre Requisitions in the Spectre Office at the Citadel Embassies for a base price of 1,000 credits.

The mission had to be completed before Priority: Tuchanka, as that was the last chance to visit the Citadel in its then-current state; it was no longer available after the civilian areas reopened following Priority: The Citadel II. Delivering the Dog Tag to Dominic Osoba gave the ambassador closure about his son's fate. Completing the mission granted 5 Reputation and 90 experience.

The soldier was identified as Bilal Osoba. At the Purgatory nightclub, drunk soldiers could be overheard recounting the heroics of a fallen squadmate; only after the Dog Tag was delivered to Dominic Osoba did they raise their glasses to Bilal Osoba by name. As Dominic noted, overhearing the soldiers was not sufficient evidence on its own, and the mission still required the Dog Tag to be obtained and delivered. The tags bore an image of the SSV Normandy, the same asset used for the crew tags in the Mass Effect 2 assignment Normandy Crash Site.
