This war-asset list covered the Systems Alliance, humanity's military and economic reach, with its navy led by Admiral Steven Hackett. Naval assets included the First Fleet (90, halved retreating from Sol under Admiral Ines Lindholm), the Third Fleet (90, under Admiral Nitesh Singh at Arcturus), the Fifth Fleet (90, famed from the Battle of the Citadel, saved by the sacrifice of the Second Fleet), and the Sixth Fleet (90, from Priority: Horizon). Saving the Council in Mass Effect reduced the First, Third and Fifth Fleets by 25 each. Admiral Boris Mikhailovich (25), promoted after leading the 63rd Scout Flotilla against Sovereign, was available only if the Council was not saved. Other ships included the Alliance Cruiser Shanghai (40, evacuated Uqbar), the Naval Exploration Flotilla (75), Spec Ops Team Delta (35), and the Normandy SR-2 itself (50), upgradable via imported Mass Effect 2 purchases (Thanix Cannon +15, Silaris armor +25, Cyclonic Barriers +25) and Citadel DLC (+35 each for repairs and crew morale).

Ground and engineering assets included the Alliance Engineering Corps (130, reduced to -100 if the rachni breeder was spared and the rachni betrayed them), the 103rd Marine Division (100, reduced by -50 if the Arrival DLC was not played), the Krogan-supported Arcturus First Division (60), Rogue Fighter Pilots (20, via Steve Cortez), Communications Arrays (50, via technician Grace Sato), the N7 Special Ops Team (75 per multiplayer class promotion), and the Biotic Company or support unit from Grissom Academy depending on whether Shepard sent the students to war.

Individual assets included Dr. Karin Chakwas (10), Kahlee Sanders (15-20), David Archer (5), Jack (25), Ashley Williams (25) or Kaidan Alenko (25) if they survived Virmire and Priority: The Citadel II, reporters Diana Allers (5) and Khalisah bint Sinan al-Jilani (5, if never punched), Shiala's Zhu's Hope militia (30), Kasumi Goto (25), Zaeed Massani (25), Dr. Ann Bryson (20) and Eden Prime supply lines. Mineral Resources (10-100) and numerous Prothean-artifact scan assets fed the Crucible.
