Kenn was a young quarian merchant who ran Kenn's Salvage on Omega station. He had come to Omega during his Pilgrimage but became stranded when all his money was stolen and he could no longer afford passage off the station. A rival merchant, Harrot, kept him from earning enough by forcing him to sell his goods at inflated prices, costing him business. Kenn sold the T6-FBA coupling that the Normandy SR-2's engineers needed to ease their maintenance work.

If asked, Kenn told Commander Shepard about his situation. Shepard could persuade him to lower his prices, or intimidate Harrot into nullifying their agreement so Kenn could set his own prices, after which a grateful Kenn offered a discount. Kenn still needed a thousand credits for passage off Omega, which Shepard could pay. If Shepard instead convinced Harrot to buy out Kenn's shop, the quarian disappeared from his kiosk but Harrot's store-wide discount applied to the former shop. If Shepard simply gave Kenn the fare without haggling, he left with no discount offered.
