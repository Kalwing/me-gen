The Weapons Locker was an interface in Mass Effect 2 used to configure the weapon loadouts of Commander Shepard and the squad. Aboard the Normandy SR-2 it appeared as a large cabinet marked with the M-8 Avenger assault rifle icon, and its interface came up automatically before every mission and assignment. Additional weapons lockers were placed during certain missions and assignments, typically shortly after a new weapon was found, so that Shepard or any squadmates present could revert to a previous weapon; newly acquired weapons were otherwise equipped automatically on every team member able to use that weapon class.

Accessing a locker during a mission or assignment also replenished thermal clips, and repeated access could top off clip capacity, though more than two uses was rarely necessary. Changing any equipped weapon, for any character, stripped all active ammo powers from the entire squad, including Shepard's, so those powers had to be reapplied afterward.
