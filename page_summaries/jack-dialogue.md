A collection of Jack dialogue and party-banter clips spanning Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3: a cut Mass Effect 2 conversation about red sand, the Mass Effect 3 scene introducing her varren Eezo, Normandy confrontations with Miranda, her time as an instructor at Grissom Academy leading a biotic student squad, a reunion with Garrus, and the Citadel DLC reconciliation with Miranda. It is useful as voice reference and for Jack's personal continuity. Representative lines:

- Jack, in the cut red sand conversation, when Shepard notes she takes drugs herself: "If I don't, I wake up screaming, with furniture slamming into the wall."
- Jack, on why an asari would use red sand despite already being biotic: "You get a great high, too."
- Jack, introducing her naturally biotic varren from Thessia: "Who's my badass biotic? Who's my badass biotic?"
- Jack, agreeing to hold off her fight with Miranda for the mission: "Sure, I'll do my part. I'd hate to see her die before I get a chance to fillet her myself."
- Jack, when Shepard keeps visiting to ask questions in Mass Effect 2: "You don't need to know someone to sleep with them. You just have to know where to put it."
- Jack, riding her Grissom Academy student: "You couldn't destroy wet tissue paper. Drink your juice, Rodriguez."
- Jack, on staying with her students in Mass Effect 3: "The little shits are useless without me, so I've got to stick with them — I'm dependable now, apparently. I blame you for this."
- Jack, reuniting with Garrus: "As charming as ever. Good to see you again, Garrus." — Garrus: "You still look like [expletive]."
- Jack, in the Citadel DLC after Miranda concedes Cerberus was wrong about her: "Wow. Thanks. I still really hate you, but you have fantastic tits." — Miranda: "All right, I can live with that."
- Jack, to Shepard at the Citadel party: "The duty rosters can wait. Right now you're on shore leave, Shepard." And: "Everybody knows you can't dance."

Kelly Chambers' observation is also quoted: "She pushes people away yet approaches sex casually… If you want her respect, think twice." Miranda, during the sub-deck confrontation, insists "it wasn't Cerberus, not really" and that Jack "was clearly a mistake."
