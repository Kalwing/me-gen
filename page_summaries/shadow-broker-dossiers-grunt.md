This Shadow Broker dossier, unlocked after the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, covered Grunt, the vat-bred "prototype" krogan created by the warlord Okeer. The Broker assessed him as a minimal threat to the continuation of the genophage but recommended leaking knowledge of the "perfect krogan" and his location to the Salarian Special Tasks Group.

The dossier consists mainly of intercepted extranet and terminal activity showing Grunt teaching himself about his heritage and about warrior cultures. His krogan-history searches (krogan history, great wars, the genophage, Okeer, Tuchanka, Urdnot Wrex) repeatedly show terms erased or modified, as do searches that drift toward "large predators," "earth lizard wrex" and "dinosaurs." His human-history activity includes downloads of documentary and news footage on major military figures and awards, and then a turn to literature: he searches for "homer kipling hemmingway" and downloads works by Ernest Hemingway, completing the audiobooks of For Whom the Bell Tolls and The Old Man and the Sea (100%), abandoning The Sun Also Rises at 14%, and deleting A Farewell to Arms, before searching "sharks."

A transactions log records purchases from an "Ultra Black Ops Mega Catalog" including case lots of engine oil, boxes of Fishdog Food Factory "Tastee Bites" and Batax's Hot Fish Spice, and video downloads: an unauthorized biography of Warlord Okeer, When Fauna Attacks! Volumes 1-10, Vaenia, Asari Confessions 26: True Blue, a Fornax "Krogan Edition" spotlight, and a "Garr the Krogan Battlemaster" action figure "with real smash your enemies action."
