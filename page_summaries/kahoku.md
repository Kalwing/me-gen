Rear Admiral Kahoku was a Systems Alliance officer who fought with distinction against the batarians during the Skyllian Blitz, earning his men's loyalty. In Mass Effect he could be found in the Citadel Tower, frustrated at being unable to secure an audience with the Council, trying to learn the fate of a group of his marines who had vanished on a recon mission in the Artemis Tau cluster while searching for the ship of Armistan Banes. Commander Shepard, after becoming a Spectre, could hear his name from Captain Anderson, encounter it while investigating Dr. Chloe Michel's blackmail, or report to him after finding his marines dead on Edolus, where they had been lured to a thresher maw nest. Kahoku then vowed to uncover the truth.

Kahoku purchased information from the Shadow Broker that led him to Cerberus, agreeing to pass on findings to a Broker agent. He managed to relay the coordinates of Cerberus facilities to Shepard before Cerberus caught him. If Shepard's squad raided the research facilities on Binthu, they found Kahoku's corpse imprisoned with Cerberus test subjects, needle marks on his arm suggesting his cause of death; Shepard could avenge him by assaulting the Cerberus base on Nepheron. A Shadow Broker agent then requested the intelligence gathered, which Shepard could hand over or keep. A Citadel news report attributed his death to natural causes and noted he was married with three children. In Mass Effect 2, two years later, a news report could suggest Cerberus was suspected in his death, and the dying Spectre Tela Vasir might cite his murder as an example of Cerberus crimes. He was voiced by Brian George.
