A Mass Effect: Andromeda romance mission available only if Ryder romanced Jaal Ama Darav and completed The Journey to Meridian and Jaal Ama Darav: Flesh and Blood. Jaal sends an email ("From Your Romantic Friend Jaal") asking Ryder to meet him on Aya for a surprise.

Ryder meets Jaal at a balcony overlook near the Resistance Headquarters on Aya. Jaal, covering Ryder's eyes, leads them toward a waterfall in a secluded, visitor-free spot he arranged so they would not be disturbed. There is an interrupt option to kiss Jaal. After talking about Ryder's favorite place, Jaal professes that he has fallen in love with Ryder, then, flustered, asks about sex. Ryder can decline, in which case the moment stays tender and Jaal praises Ryder warmly, with an interrupt to "Declare Love" before a hug and fade to grey. If Ryder accepts, Jaal disrobes and enters the water, Ryder follows (shown nude), Jaal again praises Ryder, and after the optional "Declare Love" interrupt the two have sex before the scene fades.

Afterward Jaal sends "A Letter of Love and Admiration," with slightly different text for a female or male Ryder, recalling taking Ryder to the most beautiful place on the most beautiful planet, admiring Ryder's body and also intellect and work ethic, and ending "I love you. Jaal." The mission gives no rewards.
