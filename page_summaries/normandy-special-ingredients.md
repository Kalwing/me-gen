Normandy: Special Ingredients was a short Mass Effect 2 assignment. After the mission Freedom's Progress, Shepard could speak to Mess Sergeant Gardner on the crew deck of the Normandy SR-2 and ask whether he needed anything. Gardner explained he wanted better ingredients to improve the crew's meals before the suicide mission, and that most of the supplies were available at the Citadel. Shepard traveled to Level 27 of the Citadel, visited the Zakera Cafe store, and purchased the High-Grade Provision for 500 credits, then returned it to Gardner to complete the assignment. The reward was 40 experience. Completing it caused crew members such as Crewman Goldstein to later remark on the improved food quality.
