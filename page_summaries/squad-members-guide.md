This gameplay guide gives a general overview of the six squadmates Commander Shepard can acquire in the first Mass Effect, summarizing their abilities, strengths, and weaknesses in combat. Members are listed roughly in the order they become available.

Kaidan Alenko is a Sentinel offering both tech and biotic utility with fast Sentinel recharge times; with Throw, Stasis, Neural Shock, Lift, and Sabotage he is a strong disabler, and his Medicine talent makes him an effective squad medic, though his light armor and mediocre weapon damage are weaknesses. Ashley Williams is a pure combat Soldier, the only squadmate able to train all four weapon types and, with Garrus, one of only two true snipers; maxed Immunity plus heavy armor makes her extremely durable, but she lacks any tech, Decryption, or disabling ability. Garrus Vakarian, a Turian Agent, brings high weapon damage plus Overload for shields, Sabotage for suppression, Damping against enemy tech and biotics, and full hacking access, but has no heavy armor, Barrier, or Immunity and must stay in the back line. Urdnot Wrex, a Krogan Battlemaster, is the most resilient member with Immunity, Barrier, heavy armor, and knockdown resistance, plus Warp, Throw, and Stasis, his only real gap being a total lack of tech skills including First Aid.

Tali'Zorah nar Rayya, a Quarian Machinist, is the tech specialist with Overload, Sabotage, Damping, and AI Hacking, devastating against geth and able to cast almost constantly at high level, though she relies on light armor and shields and is the hardest member to equip well. Liara T'Soni, an Asari Scientist, is unmatched at crowd control through Singularity and Lift, has the full biotic suite plus optional Overload, and her class passive speeds recharge for herself and the squad's First Aid, though the game's UI hides Overload if she learns all her biotic abilities. The page ends with a talent reference table mapping each member's starting and unlockable weapon, armor, combat, tech, biotic, and health talents.
