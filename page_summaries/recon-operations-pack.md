The Recon Operations Pack was a downloadable content pack for Mass Effect 2, released on January 18, 2011, and available only on the PlayStation Network. It bundled the Collector Armor, the Collector Assault Rifle, the Recon Hood, the Sentry Interface, and the Umbra Visor. The pack sold for $1.99 USD on the PlayStation Network and was also included in the N7 Complete Arsenal Bundle.

Two in-game emails from the Illusive Man accompanied the visors. The Umbra Visor message described a night-vision device said to help put biotic and omni-tool attacks on target, delivered to Shepard's quarters. The Sentry Interface Visor message described a visor with onboard processors that managed the wearer's armor shield strength, recommended after Cerberus software experts reviewed Shepard's loadout telemetry, and likewise left in Shepard's quarters.
