Nisira T'Kosh was the commanding officer of the Talein's Daughters huntress unit, an asari well over 600 years old by 2184. A seventy-tour veteran, she had survived the Ailanthus campaign and the Siege of Arta. She had a slightly stocky build, bluer than average skin, facial markings resembling eyebrows, and two husbands sharing her bed, a krogan and a turian. Among her unit her reputation was that of a stern, demanding but ultimately caring mother, nicknamed "Mama Nisi." She did not tolerate whining, never allowed time off during an ongoing operation, and drilled her commandos in rapid threat assessment, often telling them to plan something "to death" and then plan it more. She blessed Ygara Menoris's departure to found her own mercenary group. Cora Harper related that Nisira once assassinated a krogan warlord with a pack of playing cards, deemed a "choking hazard."

Nisira had little prior contact with humans but accepted Cora Harper into her unit in 2180 as long as she could keep up. Over four years of mentoring she grew impressed with Cora's record, approved one tour extension while insisting Cora try new things afterward, and ultimately recommended her to the Andromeda Initiative.

In Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation, Nisira took a vidcall from Cora on Thessia to clarify lingering issues, explaining she had referred Cora to the Initiative simply because the organization could use someone so biotically capable; she was amused to learn of Ygara's betrayal. After Cora's near-fatal rescue of survivors from Quiet Eddy, Nisira traveled to Tamayo Point, performed a mind-meld on the comatose Cora and confirmed her consciousness persisted, and later granted Cora's friend Janae a week of "family leave" pay. Nisira never told Cora the real reason she had sent her away; Janae perceived it was because Nisira knew Cora was not truly interested in the Daughters.
