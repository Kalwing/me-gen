The page transcribed art director Derek Watts' gallery commentary for "The Technology of Mass Effect: Space Vehicles - Geth," from the 2007 Mass Effect bonus content disc. Watts said the geth were the main enemy and needed several ship designs for cinematics and cutscenes.

He described a geth cruiser derived by enlarging and modifying the geth transport, noting that elements of the transport concept were reused in the geth rifle. A geth dropship, roughly helicopter-sized, was designed to deliver troopers to the ground with movable arms and rear thrusters; it was designed and modeled, then cut for lack of programming time, then re-added late as a vehicle-scale enemy whose tail could rotate to lower it near the ground so troopers could exit, with a front cannon and articulated legs, though much of that articulation was removed because it was added so late.

Watts explained that ship concepts were drawn in bulk and later sorted by which race they suited; many organic-looking sketches began as possible Normandy designs before being reassigned to the geth, with references including the Black Boa and Copperhead ships from Final Fantasy: The Spirits Within. The final geth transport had a distinctive head that supported it when landed, a neck-like hole echoing the geth armature, rear boosters, and pivoting sections to conform to terrain; it appears attached to the side of a building on Feros.
