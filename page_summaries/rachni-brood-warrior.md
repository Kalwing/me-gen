Rachni Brood Warriors were rachni much larger and stronger than normal Rachni Soldiers, described as the "elder males" of the hive who usually mate with the queen. Historical information stated they only attacked when the hive was particularly strained for warriors.

A Brood Warrior had abilities comparable to a standard Rachni Soldier but dealt more damage, and it was unique among rachni in possessing biotic ability. Brood Warriors used Warp, could occasionally place a squad member in Stasis, and appeared to use a form of Barrier; their hind legs and tentacle-arms glowed with biotic energy when they used these powers.

In combat they engaged at long range with their abilities and tried to retreat if rushed at close quarters, but if cornered they swiped their long tentacles for massive melee damage. Like Rachni Soldiers, they dropped onto their bellies for a few seconds when hit by biotics, creating an opening. A few Brood Warriors were encountered while Shepard was in the Mako; a single main-gun shot would not kill one outright. They were usually accompanied by Rachni Soldiers, whose acid spit passed straight through the Mako's shields.
