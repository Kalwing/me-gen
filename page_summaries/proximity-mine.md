Proximity Mine was a power in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes. The player fired a sticky mine that traveled in a straight line toward the crosshair and attached to terrain, then detonated when an enemy stepped within range. An untriggered mine self-destructed after about ten seconds, and recasting while one was active immediately detonated the original. It detonated instantly on a direct hit.

At Rank 1 it dealt 400 damage over a 3 m radius with a 10-second recharge. Rank 2 increased recharge speed by 25 percent and Rank 3 raised damage by 20 percent. Rank 4 split between more damage or a larger radius. Rank 5 offered Damage Taken (increasing all damage to the target by 20 percent for 8 seconds) or Slow (reducing the target's movement speed by 30 percent for 8 seconds). Rank 6 chose between a 50 percent damage boost or a 40 percent recharge boost.

Unlike most powers, Proximity Mine could hit an unlimited number of enemies like a grenade, making it strong against groups of Swarmers and useful for staggering lighter enemies, hitting foes through cover or walls, and knocking enemies out of cover. It could detonate fire, cryo and tech power combos and paired well with area-effect weapons such as the Scorpion. The Rank 5 Damage Taken evolution helped bring down tough enemies like Brutes, Ravagers, Banshees, Atlases and Geth Primes. It could be planted at enemy spawn points in the Armax Arsenal Arena or near corpses to trap feeding Cannibals. A Geth Hunter Infiltrator with maxed power damage detonating a mine while in Tactical Cloak could deal 3,340 damage. In single-player it was available to Garrus Vakarian and as a bonus power for Commander Shepard; in multiplayer to the Geth Hunter Infiltrator, Salarian Infiltrator, Turian Soldier and Volus Engineer.
