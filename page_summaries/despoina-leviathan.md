Despoina: Leviathan was the final mission of the Mass Effect 3: Leviathan DLC, available after Leviathan: Scan Locations once scanning 2181 Despoina revealed a faint trace of Leviathan's energy signature. The mission began when Shepard's team landed on the ocean planet. During descent, a mysterious pulse disabled the Normandy shuttle, and Steve Cortez set it down on one of the floating shipwrecks littering the ocean. Datapads among the wrecks told the story of the MSV Monarch's crew, who crash-landed after a similar pulse, ran short of rations, and were gradually enthralled by organic spherical artifacts, turning violent and refusing to leave.

A Sovereign-class Reaper descended and Reaper forces (Husks, Cannibals, Marauders, and Brutes) attacked repeatedly while Cortez repaired the shuttle, though the pulse kept striking and forced him to land again. With the shuttle unable to fly and the Normandy unable to safely approach, the team resolved to press on and find Leviathan. Cortez proposed using a Triton ADS mech stored in a sealed section of the wreck; Shepard carried the shuttle's power cells to sockets to charge the bay door open while fending off Reaper waves. Once inside, Shepard piloted the Triton, which handled like an Atlas, to clear out endlessly reinforcing Brutes and enemies.

Shepard then dove in the Triton to the ocean floor, losing contact with Cortez partway down due to interference. Near the probe Shepard had sent earlier, at a depth around 3,200 meters, Shepard encountered Leviathan. The entity explained its race's history and reasons for staying out of the war, and initially declared that Shepard must remain while the Reapers harvested everyone else, but was ultimately convinced to help for its own reasons. During the ascent, a Brute on the surface turned against other Reaper forces, and as Shepard escaped in the repaired shuttle, a ship-disabling pulse struck the pursuing Reaper, sending it crashing into the ocean. Shepard woke aboard the shuttle; if Ann Bryson had survived her ordeal relatively unharmed, she spoke with Shepard over the comm. Completion granted the Leviathan Enthrallment Team War Asset (and Ann Bryson as an asset if not incapacitated), and made Dominate available as a bonus power in the Med Bay.
