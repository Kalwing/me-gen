Mass Effect: He Who Laughs Best was a single-issue comic released May 4, 2013 for Free Comic Book Day, written by Mac Walters and Jeremy Barlow with art by Garry Brown. It depicts how Jeff "Joker" Moreau became pilot of the SSV Normandy before the events of Mass Effect.

Joker was flying a shuttle carrying the turian General Invectus to Arcturus Station, making small talk and disparaging the Alliance's pilot choice for the Normandy's upcoming test flight, a job he had applied for. Invectus dismissed him, saying a "cripple" would never control the fleet's most sophisticated starship. Meanwhile Captain Dillard complained to David Anderson about the Turian Hierarchy's mistrust and the delay waiting for a turian overseer. A distress call reported the Normandy hijacked, its pilot sealed in the cockpit behind kinetic barriers; the ship was still following its charted flight plan. The pilot identified himself as "Crooks," a man found locked in a shuttle in the station's cargo dump. Dillard ordered patrols to force the Normandy down or target its engines, over Anderson's objection.

Joker came over the comms, saying he had read the manifest, memorized the obstacle course, and would return the ship when finished. He evaded every shot the patrol fired and brought the Normandy back to the station, gushing that he was in love with it. Anderson demanded an explanation; Joker said that despite being the most qualified pilot on the station, people forgot his credentials the moment they saw his crutches, so stealing the ship was his only chance to fly it. Dillard promised a court martial, but Invectus intervened, praising Joker for running circles around station security while under fire and keeping his cool, and, citing turian-human relations, urged them to make him the pilot. Anderson agreed Joker would be punished but said he would trust the Normandy to no one else. Joker was left stunned that the theft had worked out in his favor.
