Carnage was a power in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes, distinct from the Carnage talent tied to shotguns in the first Mass Effect. It fired a vicious blast that ripped a target apart and dealt major collateral damage to nearby enemies, and it was especially effective against armor. Unlike its first-game namesake, it did not require the user to have a shotgun equipped.

At rank 1 it had a 10-second recharge, 315 damage (350 in multiplayer) and a 1.5 m radius (1 m in multiplayer). Its six ranks let the player improve recharge speed, damage and radius, add an Incapacitate effect that knocked enemies down, and boost damage against armored units; a build specialised for power damage, including passive bonuses, could deal over 700 damage.

Carnage dealt 50 percent bonus damage to armor but only half damage against barriers and shields. It was not classed as a tech power and did not benefit from tech-power damage modifiers; in multiplayer only the Mental Focuser gear item improved it. Its projectile tracked targets like Throw or Concussive Shot but travelled and recharged more slowly, though fast enough that close enemies often could not dodge. The projectile carried a strong force component that could stagger Guardians and even Atlases, and it inflicted a minor undocumented fire damage-over-time effect after a hit. When used by a squadmate it produced no real projectile and instead struck instantly.

The power could detonate fire, tech and cryo Power Combos and set up Fire Explosions, and it was not limited in how many enemies it could hit within its detonation area. The rank 5 Incapacitate evolution knocked down most unprotected infantry in its area of effect, and infantry killed by a direct hit often exploded.

In single-player Carnage was available to James Vega, to Commander Shepard as a bonus power, and to Aria T'Loak, Urdnot Wrex and Zaeed Massani. In multiplayer it was used by the Battlefield 3 Soldier, Krogan Battlemaster Vanguard, Krogan Soldier and Vorcha Soldier.
