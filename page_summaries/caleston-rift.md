The Caleston Rift was a star cluster within the Attican Traverse. Human and volus entities had staked mining and colonization claims on various worlds in the cluster, and although most planets had rich resources, development on some was hindered by inhospitable environments; local governments issued advisories because of noticeable levels of piracy. Hephaestus Station, the construction and launch point of the Andromeda Initiative intergalactic ark Keelah Si'yah, lay somewhere within the Rift. Its systems included Aysur, Balor, Solveig, Talava and Yakawa, with Balor containing the gas giant Cernunnos and its moon Caleston and a mass relay connecting the cluster to regions such as the Far Rim, Hades Nexus, Hawking Eta, Nubian Expanse, Omega Nebula, Pylos Nebula and Shadow Sea.

The Rift hosted the assignments N7: Endangered Research Station, N7: Mining the Canyon and Project Firewalker: Volcano Station, and the Mass Effect 3 missions Leviathan: Find Garneau and Mahavid: Leviathan. In Mass Effect 2 the cluster became available automatically after the mission aboard the Collector Cruiser, with the Firewalker Pack limiting early access to the Balor, Aysur and Yakawa systems until the Horizon mission, after which the Solveig and Talava systems unlocked. In Mass Effect 3 it opened automatically after the problems on Rannoch were dealt with, or, with the Leviathan content installed, as early as after Priority: Palaven once Dr. Bryson's Lab had been visited, though travel to Aysur remained restricted in that case.
