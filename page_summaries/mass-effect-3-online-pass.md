The Mass Effect 3 Online Pass was DLC that unlocked access to the game's multiplayer mode on consoles. It came as a redeemable code included with all new copies of Mass Effect 3 and the Mass Effect Trilogy, or could be bought separately through the Xbox Live Marketplace or PlayStation Network. Once activated it did not expire. Before May 30, 2013, buying it separately cost 800 Microsoft Points on Xbox 360 or $9.99 USD on PlayStation 3; on that date EA ended its Online Pass program, making the pass free to download.
