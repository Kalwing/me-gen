Collector Base: Infiltration was the first of three segments of the Mass Effect 2 suicide mission's assault on the Collector Base, triggered under Stop the Collectors: Defeat the Collectors after the Normandy crash-landed on the station. In the Normandy's briefing room, EDI projected a scan showing the station's weak point in the central control center, the likely location of the captured Normandy crew and any remaining colonists, and explained that a tech specialist had to pass through a thermal vent to open the route for two squads. Shepard chose the specialist, ideally a loyal Legion, Tali, or Kasumi, and a second fire-team leader to serve as a diversion, with Miranda volunteering; ideal choices for that role were Garrus, Miranda, or Jacob. A non-ideal specialist, or a non-ideal second fire-team leader, would cause the specialist to be shot. After a speech to the troops, Shepard's chosen squad disembarked and fought waves of Collector Drones, Assassins, Guardians, and possessed Collectors, with repeated appearances by Harbinger, while under time pressure to keep the specialist in the vent from overheating. Shepard advanced through a series of gates opened by green buttons, each activation resetting and extending the specialist's timer, until reaching the final gate, whose opening ended the mission regardless of remaining enemies or unopened gates. The diversion team then charged into the safe area and held their ground while the specialist opened the door for Shepard's squad; an ideal specialist sealed the door behind them, while a non-ideal one closed it but was shot dead before finishing, and a non-ideal second fire-team leader also caused the specialist's death even if the specialist was loyal and ideal. The squad emerged into a room holding giant tubes and a disturbing secret, leading into Collector Base: The Long Walk.
