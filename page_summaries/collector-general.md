The Collector General was an entity that oversaw the Collectors' operations from beyond the Omega 4 Relay. Unlike the humanoid drones it had an insectoid, centauroid body with a larger head, eight bladed forelimbs, six legs of which four rested on the ground, and retractable wings that let it hover, its many limbs suited to operating numerous command interfaces. It could possess any Collector drone regardless of distance, giving the drone glowing eyes and unlocking its biotic potential. In Mass Effect: Redemption, one month after the SSV Normandy's destruction, the General struck a deal with the Shadow Broker for possession of Commander Shepard's body and was incensed by delays caused by the Illusive Man and Liara T'Soni also seeking it. At the exchange on the Broker's base on Alingon, the Broker's agent Feron stalled for full payment upfront; the General, boasting that it was already everywhere, accused the Broker of betrayal and had its on-site drone relay to the enforcer Tazzik that Feron had betrayed the Broker, but Liara escaped with the body.

In Mass Effect 2 the General, declaring "I am assuming direct control," oversaw the human harvest and possessed drones on Freedom's Progress and Horizon, taking control on Horizon when it saw the resurrected Shepard investigate the operation. It sprang the derelict Collector Cruiser trap, during which a virus bearing its likeness nearly infected the Normandy SR-2. During the assault on the Collector Base it rallied every available drone to protect the Human-Reaper and the base and possessed multiple drones during the battle, but Shepard's fire-team tactics forced it to spread itself too thin, and the Human-Reaper was destroyed. Harbinger then berated the General for its failure, asserted the Reapers "will find another way," and released its control; the General's eyes lost their glow for the first time and it appeared confused or dismayed, suggesting some intelligence and emotion remained, before it died when Shepard destroyed the base or triggered the radiation pulse. If Shepard had activated the Arrival Project first, Harbinger appeared possessing the General via hologram during the escape from the asteroid bound for the Alpha Relay. In Mass Effect: Paragon Lost, a General-like drone physically oversaw the harvest of Fehl Prime, negotiated and then reneged on a deal with the Cerberus agent Messner, and was pushed into an abyss by the biotic Delta Squad soldier Essex after impaling him with a forelimb.
