Joram Talid was a turian politician campaigning in 2185 for the position of Citadel Zakera Ward Intendant, running on a platform of eradicating organized crime under the slogan "Take Back Your Station." In reality he was extremely anti-human and, contradicting his anti-crime stance, shook down human-run businesses for money. He claimed human C-Sec officers were corrupt and turned a blind eye to rising crime he attributed to humans. He railed against the rapid establishment of the human embassy on the Citadel while other races had waited centuries or millennia, against human-sympathetic Council members, and against humanity's Council seat, gained after the Battle of the Citadel in 2183, whereas the turians had to defeat the krogan to earn theirs. He mocked humanity's rapid advancement and disposability of anything a few years old, questioning its ability to contribute to a 2,000-year-old Council, and promised he would not rest "until the humans have been removed from power."

The human criminal Elias Kelham hired Thane Krios' son Kolyat to assassinate Talid. During Thane's loyalty mission, Commander Shepard could kill Talid, save his life, or let Kolyat succeed in killing him. Talid was barefaced, both literally and in the colloquial sense of an unscrupulous politician.
