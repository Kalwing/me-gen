Tereshkova was a binary star system explorable in the first Mass Effect, named after Valentina Tereshkova, the first woman to fly in space. The system centered on the star Tereshkova-A, and its combined stellar energy was low. The system appeared to be in a state of change: the planet Patamalrus was projected to lose its atmosphere and Mawinor to be ejected from the system entirely.

Seven bodies orbited the star. Antibaar, the first planet, was tied to the assignment UNC: Geth Incursions, held a Prothean data disc, and could be surveyed for heavy and light metals. Patamalrus (second) and Thegeuse (fourth) each yielded a survey deposit of heavy metal. Hunsalra was the third planet, Solmarlon the fifth, and Mawinor the sixth. A dark carbonaceous asteroid roughly 42 km in radius drifted in the system's asteroid field; scanning it produced anomalous readings, and after maneuvering by Joker a small team recovered debris from one of the larger rocks that included a League of One Medallion, part of the assignment to locate signs of battle.
