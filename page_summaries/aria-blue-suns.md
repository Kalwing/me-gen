"Aria: Blue Suns" was a Mass Effect 3 mission, part of Aria T'Loak's trilogy of tasks to unite mercenary bands under her control in exchange for the Terminus Fleet War Asset. It became available after Priority: Palaven, once the turian primarch had been retrieved from Menae, by speaking with Aria at the Purgatory bar on the Citadel. It was written by Neil Pollner.

Blue Suns leader Darner Vosque wanted turian General Septimus Oraka, who was investigating Vosque's raids on C-Sec weapons, removed before he would commit the Suns to Aria. Shepard met Vosque at the Docks holding area, then contacted Aria, who instructed Shepard to persuade Oraka to back off rather than kill him. Oraka, who had relocated from Chora's Den to the Presidium Commons courtyard, said he needed high-grade weapons to prepare the Citadel for the coming Reaper attack, and directed Shepard to a salarian named Kannik at the Cipritine Armory in Meridian Place Market.

Kannik would deal only in exchange for rare artifacts, one of which was located on the planet Vana in the Vular system, Kite's Nest. Returning the artifact to Kannik and then speaking with Oraka completed the mission on the Paragon path and awarded 5 Paragon points. Alternatively, Shepard could order a hit on Oraka for 5 Renegade points, completing the mission instantly. Completion granted 50 points of Terminus Fleet war assets, 5,000 credits, and morality points. A known bug could prevent the war assets from updating on the Paragon path. Darner Vosque was voiced by Brian Bloom.
