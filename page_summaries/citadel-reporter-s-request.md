Citadel: Reporter's Request was a Mass Effect assignment and the first in a chain involving the reporter Emily Wong. After Shepard's first audience with the Citadel Council and gaining access to the Upper Wards, Wong waved Shepard down near the stairs leading to Flux. She said she was investigating crime and corruption on the Citadel and asked Shepard to pass along any useful information. If she was not met beforehand, the assignment was instead acquired on obtaining an OSD.

After meeting Garrus Vakarian, Urdnot Wrex, or both, Shepard stormed Chora's Den in pursuit of Fist, the club's owner and a former agent of the Shadow Broker. Fist's OSD, a data disk holding information about his illegal activities, was left on the floor after he was defeated. Shepard first had to race to rescue a quarian, but could return to Fist's office later to collect the OSD.

Bringing the OSD to Wong in the Wards yielded a reward. If Shepard had spoken with her before raiding Chora's Den, a Charm or Intimidate option added credits, and the Intimidate option also gave 2 Renegade points. Denying Wong the OSD still completed the assignment but gave no reward. A bug in the original release could make Wong absent from the Wards if Shepard acquired the OSD without first speaking to her, making completion impossible; this was reportedly fixed in Legendary Edition. The assignment was internally named "Expose," and the next assignment in the chain was Citadel: Planting a Bug.
