The A-61 Mantis Gunship was a vectored-thrust military aircraft that first rolled off assembly lines in 2170 and appeared in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. Its modular construction let it be reconfigured as a low-altitude gunship, a fighter, a high-altitude bomber, or a single-stage-to-orbit spaceplane; it could not serve as a true deep-space fighter because it had no FTL drive. An element zero core lightened its engines with a mass effect field, allowing vertical takeoff, hovering on minimal fuel, and greater range and speed than the helicopters and jump-jet aircraft it replaced. Standard defenses included heavy armor plating, kinetic barriers (often stripped out by mercenary groups), a thermal decay system, and an electronic countermeasures suite. In ground-attack configuration it typically carried two sets of two forward missile bays armed with Inferno PKRs and a pair of belly-mounted M350 mass-accelerator autocannons, and it could deploy armed personnel or FENRIS Mechs.

In Mass Effect 2, mercenary groups and criminals used Mantis Gunships against Commander Shepard. On Omega, Blue Suns sergeant Cathka was repairing one while Shepard recruited Archangel; a Renegade interrupt could sabotage the repair, though the gunship still badly wounded Archangel. An Eclipse gunship attacked during Samara's recruitment on Illium. On Zorya, during Zaeed Massani's loyalty mission, a Mantis carried Vido Santiago away. On Bekenstein, Donovan Hock attacked Shepard and Kasumi Goto with a Mantis fitted with a self-recharging kinetic barrier, which Kasumi disabled so it could be destroyed. The gunship was immune to Stasis, and Hock's model matched a Geth Colossus in durability.

In Mass Effect 3, Alliance-marked Mantis Gunships fought Reapers over Earth, including a downed one in Vancouver harbor that Shepard used to contact the Normandy. Cerberus-colored gunships appeared on Sur'Kesh and during Udina's coup on the Citadel. Two asari Mantis gunships aided Shepard on Thessia before being shot down, and another supported Kai Leng's escape there. During Priority: Earth, multiple Mantis gunships supported Hammer's final charge to the conduit but were destroyed by Harbinger. The craft was playable in the multiplayer prototype Mass Effect Team Assault.
