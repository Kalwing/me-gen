This brief enemy page had under ~150 words of substantive prose: Cerberus Anti-Tank infantry were used by Cerberus to defend key installations and research facilities, and were deployed to guard the exterior rather than the interior of the underground facility on Nepheron, carrying assault rifles with a switchable rocket firing mode that could destroy a vehicle in a few hits or kill infantry in one or two, using Immunity, and yet being trivial to defeat with the Mako's main gun while jump-jetting around their missiles.
