This page collects assorted unused text strings from Mass Effect 2, none of it canon. It opens with a placeholder "test conversation" between Anderson and Shepard used to test facial animation and dialogue features, with joke lines such as Anderson's "I can't believe I animate as well as I do" and Ashley's "I'm a spunky soldier with a healthy dose of xenophobia!"

The bulk is cut Codex entries, most later posted to the N7 HQ website: the Collector Oculi (a repurposed Collector drone reduced to its nervous system to pilot the machine); the Drell (375,000 relocated to Kahje by the hanar two centuries ago via the Compact, average lifespan 85 years, vulnerable to Kepral's Syndrome); Systems Alliance and its Military Ranks; Eldfell-Ashland Energy (formed after Ashland extracted helium-3 from Saturn in 2137 and merged with Eldfell Construction); a Sovereign entry describing it as Saren's two-kilometer dreadnought of mysterious origin; Quantum Entanglement Communicators; Synthetics (AI theory, technological singularity, transcendency cults); Enforcement Gauntlets; the M-100 Grenade Launcher; Omni-gel; and Thermal Clips (adopted after the Alliance found geth sustained fire outmatched marines).

Also included: cut emails from Captain Kirrahe (if he survived Virmire) and the Illusive Man (if Legion was left on the Derelict Reaper, plus a removed "Probe Retrieval" assignment); cut planet and station descriptions (Arvuna, Asteria, Leyya, Rosh, Susskind Station) and cut clusters including the Perseus Veil; three planned squadmate research upgrades (Kasumi, Zaeed, Morinth); developer placeholder text such as an "Impossible" difficulty where "All enemies are invisible and Shepard cannot move" and joke Omega-4 relay and end-of-game test prompts; DLC planning notes for Overlord, Lair of the Shadow Broker, and Arrival; a cut "Kasumi: Memento or Mori" loyalty mission involving Nava Corp bio-viruses; a detailed cut Tali: Treason journal with per-Admiral trial objectives (Shala'Raan, Zaal'Koris, Han'Gerrel, Daro'Xen); a purchasable pet robot "Sparky"; and an "Altered Names" table (Donovan Hock as "Shuriken Hock," Thane as "Krios Than," Zaeed as "Hariz al-Kherij," Jacob as "Jacob Mather," Normandy SR-2 as "Leviathan," Bekenstein as "Ciceraan," Pragia as "Pragus").
