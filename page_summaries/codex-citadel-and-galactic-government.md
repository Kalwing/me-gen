The page compiled Codex entries on the Citadel and galactic governance. The Citadel was an ancient deep-space station presumed built by the Protheans, serving as the galaxy's political, cultural, and financial capital, with species maintaining embassies on the inner Presidium ring and millions living in the five Ward arms. It could seal its arms into an impregnable shell and was maintained by the enigmatic keepers. The Citadel Council was an executive committee of the Asari Republics, Turian Hierarchy, and Salarian Union, no one race able to defy the other two; the asari were seen as diplomats, salarians as intelligence-gatherers, turians as the military. Embassy-holding species were associate members who could raise issues but not vote; the human Systems Alliance became an associate member in 2165. "Citadel Space" informally meant any region controlled by a Council-acknowledging species, though less than one percent of stars had been explored, expansion depending on the mass relay network. Spectres were agents of the Office of Special Tactics and Reconnaissance answering only to the Council and operating outside galactic law; the order was founded after the salarians joined and publicized after the Krogan Rebellions.

Secondary entries described the 2183 Battle of the Citadel, when the rogue Spectre Saren Arterius attacked through a hidden relay with geth and krogan from Ilos, timed with the geth fleet and his flagship Sovereign. The Destiny Ascension evacuated the Council but sealed the wards; Commander Shepard followed from Ilos, fought Saren's forces, and reopened the arms. In the Paragon account the Normandy and Admiral Hackett's Fifth Fleet saved the Destiny Ascension and the Council, taking heavy losses before destroying Sovereign, and the grateful Council offered humanity a seat, accepted by Donnel Udina and David Anderson. In the Renegade account the fleet let the Council ships perish and humanity filled the resulting power vacuum. Other entries covered the post-Rebellions Citadel Conventions, which regulated weapons of mass destruction in four tiers; Citadel Security (C-Sec), a 200,000-strong volunteer police force in six divisions whose Executor Venari Pallin distrusted Spectres; the Ward slums called the Foundations; the Presidium Ring and its kilometer-high Tower; the light-reflecting Serpent Nebula; station statistics (13.2 million population, 44.7 km open length); the Wards' rotation-held atmosphere; and the Treaty of Farixen, which fixed a 5:3:1 dreadnought ratio among turians, other Council races, and associates.
