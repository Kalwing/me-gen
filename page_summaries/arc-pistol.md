The Arc Pistol was a heavy pistol in Mass Effect 3, an innovation of Admiral Daro'Xen. It was a scaled-down Arc Projector that used only thermal clips, solving the parent weapon's power problems. The Arc Pistol used a nonvisible laser to ionize the air and create a path for a high-ampere electric shock, and it could be charged up for a more damaging blast.

In single-player, the Arc Pistol was found during the mission "Priority: Geth Dreadnought," where Tali or Xen set it down for Shepard to take; it could also be purchased afterward from Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies if missed. In multiplayer it was categorized as rare, required no expansion packs, and was obtained randomly through item packs.

Despite its origin, the Arc Pistol behaved mostly like a conventional weapon; its shots did not jump between targets. It was considered one of the most powerful and versatile heavy pistols in the game, usable as a semi-automatic weapon with the same rate of fire as the M-3 Predator, and more damaging than the M-6 Carnifex or M-77 Paladin when charged, while weighing less. It had above-average accuracy, low muzzle climb, no accuracy loss from repeated firing, and a large ammo reserve and magazine. Its charged shot dealt increasing damage the longer the charge was held and, unusually, fired additional projectiles, up to three, similar to a shotgun. There were four charge thresholds: uncharged fired one projectile at 1x damage; slightly charged fired one at 3x; moderately charged fired two at 3x each; fully charged fired three at 3x each, for nine times the uncharged damage, and consumed three ammo over at least one second. Firing a charged shot broke Tactical Cloak. In Legendary Edition the weapon dealt significantly less damage and weighed slightly more because it was not based on the latest patched version. Squadmates did not use its charged-shot feature, making it a poor choice for them.
