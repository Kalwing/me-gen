The Dragoon was a heavily armored Cerberus unit encountered in Mass Effect 3. Dragoons were close-quarters combatants armed with the M-25 Hornet SMG and biotic lashes, which they used to lunge rapidly toward targets, slam the ground for an omni-directional melee attack, and stomp downed players for an instant kill. They moved far faster than any other Cerberus enemy and their lashes ignored cover, letting them push players out of position. Defensively they had powerful armor but no shields or barriers; they could not be grabbed over cover, and were immune to physics-based powers such as Pull, Throw, Concussive Shot, Singularity, and Stasis. Headshots dealt 40 percent extra damage. Electrical tech powers like Overload, Energy Drain, and Disruptor Ammo could stun them, except during their melee and lunge animations, when they were partially or fully immune to stagger.

Recommended counters included anti-armor tools such as Armor-Piercing Rounds, Carnage, Flamer, Incinerate, Homing/Inferno Grenades, Reave, Warp, high-powered snipers, pistols, and shotguns, and weapons firing stunning or explosive projectiles like the Graal Spike Thrower and M-37 Falcon. Dragoons entirely ignored Combat Drones, Decoys, Sentry Turrets, and Geth Turrets, making those powers effective for stunning and damaging them. They almost always traveled in groups of two or three and often coordinated with Phantoms to flank and quickly down isolated players. Trivia noted that Dragoons may have been the end result of Project Phoenix, sharing the Smash biotic armament and armor design with ex-Cerberus Phoenix operatives (colored yellow rather than blue), and that their armor closely resembled the default armor of Randall Ezno, protagonist of Mass Effect: Infiltrator. The word "dragoon" historically referred to mounted infantry.
