Jace Reyn was a freelance mercenary and former Blue Suns soldier, appearing in the comic Mass Effect: Foundation. According to Zaeed Massani, Jace was an inexperienced kid but "as strong as a varren and twice as loyal," and he stayed loyal to Zaeed during Zaeed's fall from the Blue Suns leadership. After a skirmish with black-armored Eclipse mercenaries, Zaeed approached Jace with a job proposal to bring down the ship Verrikan. Jace proudly showed off his custom weapon, an M-8 Avenger modded with everything he could find, which he named "Bessie" in imitation of Zaeed's rifle "Jessie." Zaeed reckoned Jace was his biggest fan whether he liked it or not.

Jace stuck by Zaeed during the boarding action on the Verrikan, weathering setbacks cheerfully. Once inside, Zaeed tasked Jace and the merc Tristana with acting as a diversion team while he headed for the frigate's bridge. The two took up positions between a door and a barricade, killing turians as they advanced. Jace found the fight fun, having ample ammunition, but Tristana considered it a waste of time. She unlocked the door, slipped through, and sealed it behind her, leaving Jace to hold the position alone, to his terror.
