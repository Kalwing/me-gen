The Lazarus Research Station was a deep-space station used by Cerberus as headquarters for the Lazarus Cell and as the site of the Lazarus Project, the effort to revive Commander Shepard. Along with Minuteman Station, it was one of two facilities that housed and operated on Shepard's body and clone.

In Mass Effect: Foundation, the station's systems were breached in 2184 during a successful operation by the agent Rasa to steal Shepard's clone, with mechs, security feeds and sprinkler systems all affected. In Mass Effect 2, in 2185, the station suffered a catastrophic security breakdown when LOKI and YMIR mechs, allegedly reprogrammed by Wilson, turned on the personnel. During the crisis Shepard revived and escaped by shuttle with Miranda Lawson, the station's commanding officer, and Jacob Taylor, her executive officer, in the mission Prologue: Awakening. A later Illusive Man report revealed the station was destroyed by remote detonation, with a Cerberus team dispatched to ensure no incriminating evidence survived.
