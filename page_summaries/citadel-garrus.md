Citadel: Garrus was obtained automatically during the brainstorming session after the Council hearing in Citadel: Expose Saren, tasking Shepard with recruiting C-Sec officer Garrus Vakarian to help build the case against Saren Arterius. Garrus could be tracked through Harkin, a human barfly at Chora's Den, who, after claiming that Anderson had once been a Spectre and was dismissed for botching his trial mission, revealed Garrus had gone to Dr. Chloe Michel's Med Clinic. Alternatively, completing Citadel: Wrex first gave Garrus's location directly and removed the need to see Harkin. Near Chora's Den, Shepard could be ambushed by two Assassins, and inside, Urdnot Wrex could be seen threatening Fist, the Den's owner.

At the Med Clinic, Fist's thugs were threatening Dr. Michel while Garrus waited nearby; Garrus shot one thug and joined the fight. Afterward, Michel explained she had treated an injured quarian fugitive carrying information about the geth, and that Fist wanted the quarian silenced. Garrus concluded the data linked the geth to Saren and demanded to join Shepard, calling Saren a traitor and a disgrace to the turians. If Wrex had not been recruited, Garrus automatically joined at the end of the conversation. If Wrex was already aboard, Shepard could refuse Garrus, who then waited in the clinic until Fist was dealt with and afterward at C-Sec Academy, where he revealed he had quit C-Sec out of frustration with its bureaucracy and could be recruited at any later point.

If never recruited, Garrus still appeared on Omega in Mass Effect 2 to be recruited against the Collectors, with only minor dialogue differences, and the Illusive Man could be asked about his whereabouts after Shepard's revival. Cut content included an alternate clinic cutscene in which Garrus, rather than Shepard, shouted "Let her go!", and unused lines for the assassins outside Chora's Den.
