Navigator Charles Pressly was an officer aboard the SSV Normandy, serving as its navigator in 2183. His grandfather had served in the First Contact War, and Pressly was uncomfortable around non-humans, particularly turians, though he remained loyal to his commanding officer and would set aside those prejudices to serve the Alliance faithfully. He joined the Alliance shortly after finishing school, wanting to follow his grandfather's footsteps. Before the Normandy he served on the frigate SSV Agincourt, one of the first reinforcements to reach Elysium after the Skyllian Blitz; if Commander Shepard had the War Hero background, Pressly would reminisce about the Commander's involvement in the Blitz. After the Elysium crisis he received his officer's commission and was selected by Captain David Anderson to serve as the Normandy's Navigation Officer.

When Anderson gave Shepard command of the Normandy, Pressly became executive officer, in charge of the ship during Shepard's off-ship activities. Though loyal to the Alliance, he was more loyal to Shepard and treated the theft of the Normandy as an adventure, saying part of him loved it despite expecting a court-martial if it failed. At the start of the mission to Ilos he detected Saren's position on the planet's surface but argued against a landing, judging there was not enough open terrain to set down the Mako.

One month after Sovereign's attack on the Citadel, the Alliance sent Shepard and the Normandy to patrol distant systems for geth. Pressly believed there were no geth and that slavers were the real threat. While investigating a system where ships had gone missing, an unidentified ship attacked the Normandy; Pressly, on the bridge, was knocked unconscious by an exploding console. After Cerberus revived Shepard two years later, Shepard was told Pressly had died in the attack. His datapad was later recovered among the ruins of the Normandy crash site's command center; though partly corrupted, its messages showed he had come to regret his earlier distrust of Shepard for allowing aliens aboard, writing that he would gladly die for any of the non-humans on the ship and that his doubts had come from ignorance. According to Tali, Pressly had contracted chicken pox at some point before his death. In Mass Effect 3, Pressly was listed on the Normandy SR-2's memorial wall on the crew deck.
