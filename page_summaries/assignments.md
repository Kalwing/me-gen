This page is an index/navigation list, not a prose article. It catalogues the optional side missions ("Assignments") of Mass Effect and Mass Effect 2, organizing them by category (character-specific, Citadel, charted planet, galaxy-wide, downloadable content, and cut content) and linking to their individual walkthroughs. It contains under ~150 words of real prose and no substantive lore beyond noting that assignments have little to no bearing on the overarching narrative but can deepen understanding of the setting.
