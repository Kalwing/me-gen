Seeker Swarms were technological devices used by the Collectors, resembling large light-brown insects with four wings, four limbs, and a stinger. Deployed in swarms from Collector ships, they sought out organic targets and stung them, leaving victims in a paralytic, stasis-like state. This allowed the Collectors to disable the population of entire colonies before moving in to gather the bodies for transport.

After the Battle of the Citadel, the Collectors used Seeker Swarms against human colonies in the Terminus Systems. On Freedom's Progress every colonist was taken; the sole survivor was the quarian Veetor'Nara, who had been on his Pilgrimage and, though mentally unbalanced by the attack, recorded footage and biological data of the swarms. He passed this to Commander Shepard, who had begun working with Cerberus against the abductions. After analysis, the Illusive Man asked Shepard to recruit Mordin Solus, who developed an experimental countermeasure making its wearer theoretically invisible to the swarms. It was successfully tested on Horizon during a Collector attack, but proved inadequate against heavy concentrations of Seekers such as those in the Collector Base, where biotic fields were used to hold the swarms back.

In Mass Effect: Paragon Lost, Collectors arrived on Fehl Prime in 2185 and deployed swarms that bypassed the colony's new military-grade kinetic barriers, stunning every human on sight, including James Vega's associates Captain Toni, April, and Christine, and his squadmate Essex, who later recovered thanks to a cure. The swarms were destroyed when their Collector Cruiser was, and Vega later handed intel on the Collectors and the cure to the Alliance.

In 2186, Shepard encountered no Seeker Swarms directly, though "Collector swarms" were reported in the fighting on Palaven. Across galactic battlefields, Collector Captains created swarms that advanced in small dispersible clusters, Possessed Captains created more powerful Seeker Plagues, and some battlefields featured a massive, indestructible swarm that roamed the area harming friend and foe alike. The Awakened Collector Adept could deploy and launch Seeker Swarms as a power.
