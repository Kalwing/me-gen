Concussive Shot was a combat power in the single-player and multiplayer modes of Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, described as a massive explosive blast that knocked down and damaged enemies, leaving them open to further attacks. It was neither a tech nor a biotic power, so it did not benefit from tech or biotic damage and recharge upgrades. It dealt heavily increased damage against barriers (350% in Mass Effect 2, 400% in Mass Effect 3) and multiplied its physics force against targets suspended by Pull or Singularity, often causing environmental deaths. In Mass Effect 2 it knocked down unprotected infantry, instantly killed unprotected Husks and glowing Abominations, and evolved into Heavy Concussive Shot or Concussive Blast; it was available to the Soldier, Garrus Vakarian, Grunt, and Zaeed Massani.

In Mass Effect 3 the projectile arced and homed toward the crosshair, could detonate Fire, Cryo, and Tech combo explosions, and evolved through six ranks with options including Recharge Speed, Force & Damage, Radius, Shatter, Shredder, Hammer, and Amplification, the last letting the shot both prime and detonate combos using the active ammo power. It was available in single-player to the Soldier, David Anderson, Garrus Vakarian, Ashley Williams, Grunt, and Zaeed Massani, and in multiplayer to the Human Soldier and Turian Soldier.
