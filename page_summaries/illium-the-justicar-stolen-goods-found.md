Stolen Goods Found was a short Mass Effect 2 assignment on Illium, acquired during the mission to recruit the justicar Samara (Dossier: The Justicar). After Detective Anaya granted Shepard access to the crime scene, a terminal past the police tape displayed a shipping note revealing that a transport operator, an asari named Eria, was skimming cargo from a shipment and diverting the "surplus" to its owner, Mr. Thax (Thax Vorak, a male krogan Citadel business magnate). Uploading the message to Thax activated the assignment.

After recruiting Samara and returning to Nos Astra, an urbane krogan approached Shepard asking whether the Commander had sent the note. Admitting it granted 2 Paragon points; being evasive granted 2 Renegade points; either way Shepard automatically earned 5 additional Renegade points. Rewards were 40 (50) experience and 1,500 credits.

The asari responsible could be overheard in the transport hub both before and after the assignment. Beforehand she complained she could not reach the stolen merchandise because it sat inside the sealed crime scene; afterward, if the message was sent, she was heard telling an accomplice to give Thax a full refund and go somewhere safe. If Shepard finished the recruitment mission without uploading the message, she instead boasted about her successful skimming operation.
