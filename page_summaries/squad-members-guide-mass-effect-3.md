This gameplay reference page summarized every squad member Commander Shepard could recruit in Mass Effect 3, describing each one's powers, class passive, weapon options, durability and combat role so players could assemble effective teams, especially on higher difficulties. It emphasized understanding Power Combos and noted that squadmate armors and outfits in Mass Effect 3 carried gameplay bonuses (weapon damage, power damage, power recharge, or shields).

The core squad covered James Vega (Arms Master, a durable point man with Fortification, Frag Grenade, Incendiary Ammo and Carnage, but slow power recharge); Liara T'Soni (Pure Biotic, fragile but with Singularity, Stasis, Warp, Warp Ammo and very fast recharge); Garrus Vakarian (Turian Rebel, long-range damage with Overload, Concussive Shot, Armor-Piercing Ammo and Proximity Mine); EDI (Unshackled AI, the only squadmate with Incinerate, plus Overload, Defense Matrix and Decoy); Javik (Vengeful Ancient, durable biotic with Dark Channel, Lift Grenade, Pull and Slam, requiring the From Ashes DLC or Legendary Edition); Ashley Williams (Alliance Officer with Marksman, high weapon damage and sniper access); Kaidan Alenko (Alliance Officer, a versatile tech/biotic hybrid with Overload, Cryo Blast, Reave and Barrier); and Tali'Zorah vas Normandy (Quarian Machinist, with Energy Drain, Sabotage and two drone types).

David Anderson appeared as a temporary combat-support squadmate in the Earth prologue. DLC-mission-exclusive squadmates included Aria T'Loak and Nyreen Kandros (Omega) and Urdnot Wrex (Citadel). The Armax Arsenal Arena simulator allowed Grunt, Jack, Jacob Taylor, Kasumi Goto, Miranda Lawson, Samara and Zaeed Massani to be used as simulated combat partners if they were still alive, each starting with powers at rank 3 and one fewer skill point. The page also included weapon-access and power-combo reference tables.
