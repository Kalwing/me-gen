"Dossier: Archangel" was a Mass Effect 2 recruitment mission, available after Freedom's Progress as part of the Illusive Man's first batch of dossiers. Archangel was described as a mercenary commander noted for technical expertise and strategic brilliance who had carried out high-profile attacks on Omega's gang leaders. On arriving at Omega, Shepard was directed by Aria T'Loak to Archangel, a vigilante whom three mercenary groups, Eclipse, Blue Suns, and Blood Pack, had allied to kill. Shepard signed up as a freelancer to infiltrate the merc staging area in Kima District, passing through areas controlled by the three gangs and meeting their leaders Jaroth (Eclipse), Garm (Blood Pack), and Tarak (Blue Suns). Along the way Shepard could sabotage a YMIR Mech so it would turn on the mercs, and could use a Renegade interrupt to jam a welder into Sergeant Cathka, crippling Tarak's gunship.

Shepard crossed the last barricade, turned on the freelancers, and fought up to Archangel's sniper nest, where the vigilante removed his helmet and was revealed to be Garrus Vakarian. Garrus explained that he had resigned from C-Sec in frustration, come to Omega, and led a twelve-man vigilante band against the merc gangs, earning the name "Archangel" from locals; his team had since been wiped out in a betrayal, their bodies lying shrouded around the hideout. Shepard and Garrus then defended the hideout against successive assaults by Eclipse (ending with the YMIR mech and Jaroth), the Blood Pack (requiring Shepard to close three basement shutters, ending with Garm), and finally the Blue Suns, who deployed Jentha and Tarak's A-61 Mantis Gunship. Garrus was badly wounded by a rocket during the gunship fight before Shepard shot the gunship down.

Garrus was recruited and evacuated to the Normandy, where he received cybernetic reconstruction but kept a permanent facial scar and damaged armor for the rest of the game; he took up residence in the main battery. Nalah Butler, widow of one of Garrus' squad members, emailed Shepard asking that Garrus be helped to stop blaming himself for the team's deaths. Rewards included the M-15 Vindicator Battle Rifle, element zero, credits, and Tech Damage and Submachine Gun Damage upgrades.
