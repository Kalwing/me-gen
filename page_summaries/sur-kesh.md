Sur'Kesh was the salarian homeworld, the second planet of the Pranas System in the Annos Basin of the Milky Way. It was likened to the jungles of Earth: attractive, teeming with life, uncomfortable to live on and dangerous to the unwary. The planet alternated between large oceans and flora-covered landmasses, with a humid climate, lush vegetation and rich biodiversity comparable to Earth's former rainforests. It tended to be wetter than Earth, and salarian cities invested heavily in collecting and providing fresh water, as befitted an amphibious species.

The technophilic salarians suffered significant pollution and waste problems early in their development but adopted social solutions just as rapidly, using complex breeding rules to maintain a crowded but sustainable population. Their drive for intellectual stimulation had long ago led them to study every aspect of their environment and develop ways to thrive without harming their habitat. Areas near cities and industrial centres were meticulously maintained so that sunlight reached ground level and jungle paths stayed clear. Burngrass, a soil-enriching, adaptable native weed, became a major export because of its value for terraforming.

Because Sur'Kesh lay far from dark space, it had not been invaded by the Reapers during the events of Mass Effect 3, but its rulers were aware they lay in the path of attack, and many salarians considered their forces disadvantaged because their military doctrine of striking first could not be applied. The planet was the setting of the mission Priority: Sur'Kesh, and the Mass Effect 3 multiplayer map Firebase Jade was located there.
