Kahlee Sanders was a female human First Lieutenant of the Systems Alliance, one of its top computer and systems technicians and an expert in synthetic intelligence, with rare naturally blonde hair. Born in Earth's Texan Megapolis in 2139 to an unknown mother and Admiral Jon Grissom, she enlisted at twenty-two after her mother's death. In Mass Effect: Revelation she served on Dr. Shu Qian's AI research team at the base on Sidon, discovered Qian's obsessive study of an artifact found near geth space, and fled to Elysium with copied data after the base was destroyed. Nearly captured by the Blue Suns, she sought help from her estranged father, was attacked by the krogan battlemaster Skarr, and was saved when Saren Arterius drove Skarr off. She lied to Saren that the research was into biotics, then escaped Elysium with Lieutenant David Anderson, giving him an OSD naming Qian as the traitor. After the Dah'tan lead on Camala, she was captured by Skarr and coerced into assisting Qian until Anderson rescued her; she and Anderson were mutually attracted but did not pursue a relationship.

In Mass Effect: Ascension she joined the Ascension Project's board on the Jon Grissom Academy, growing devoted to the biotic children, especially Gillian Grayson. She exposed the Cerberus agent Jiro Toshiwa, was nearly sold to the Collectors by Pel, and was rescued by Lemm'Shal nar Tesleya, reaching the Migrant Fleet. Aboard the Idenna she defended the ship against Paul Grayson and Cerberus, appealing to Grayson's love for Gillian until he turned on Golo. In Mass Effect: Retribution she brought Grayson's Cerberus files to Anderson; they enlisted the turian ambassador Orinia, were taken hostage to Omega by Aria T'Loak's men, and were offered a ride by the Cerberus assassin Kai Leng, whom Anderson incapacitated. At Grissom Academy, Anderson and Leng killed the Reaper-mutated Grayson; Kahlee, nearly indoctrinated, afterward joined Anderson's research team and kissed him.

During the Reaper War in 2186 Kahlee signaled for her students to be evacuated before Cerberus attacked the Academy. Samantha Traynor identified the responding signal as a Cerberus fake. If Shepard intervened, Kahlee opened a cargo hatch for the boarding team, helped locate remaining students, discussed forming a Biotic Strike Team, and commandeered Cerberus shuttles to aid the escape. She expressed deep concern for Anderson's safety. If Shepard did not reach the Academy in time, Anderson presumed her killed. She was voiced by Grey DeLisle.
