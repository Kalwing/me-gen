The Nemesis was a Cerberus sniper-specialist unit in Mass Effect 3, recognizable by minimal armor and a red targeting laser (distinct from the Mass Effect class specialization also called Nemesis). Codex lore stated that customized implants let the nemesis withstand the crippling kickback of the M-98 Widow antimateriel rifle, turning it into a weapon capable of instant death, while a suite of scanning equipment made the sniper adept at staying in cover, so a nemesis was usually spotted only after firing. Self-destruct mechanisms triggered on death, and Cerberus troops' tendency to suicide rather than be captured, meant the Alliance never recovered an example of nemesis augmentation technology for study. Early design iterations were male; the unit was made female and given the red lens light to make its silhouette distinct from other Cerberus enemies at a distance.

In gameplay the Nemesis carried an M-13 Raptor with a laser sight that behaved like a Widow or Mantis, was extremely accurate and high-damage (capable of instant kills on higher difficulties against downed shields), was protected mainly by shields, and rarely left cover, relocating frequently and sometimes using rocket-propelled boots to reach new ground. It had no short-range offense, so closing distance, staggering it with gunfire or instant powers like Overload, Energy Drain, or Stasis, or counter-sniping while it aimed were the recommended counters. In Greek mythology Nemesis was the goddess of revenge.
