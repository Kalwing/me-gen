Mass Effect 2 Character Concepts is an internal BioWare development document containing early concepts for the game's squadmates, partially transcribed from material shown by writer Mac Walters. Many concepts were later refined or merged into final squadmates while others were dropped. The page also includes the start of a separate document, "Acquisition & Loyalty Missions." Parts are missing.

The "Party Member Templates" section lists numbered concepts with an enabling mission and a rationale. Notable entries: "Cerberus Superwoman," a genetically engineered female human protégé of the Illusive Man ("the best daughter money can buy"), became Miranda Lawson; "Cerberus Bodyguard," a taciturn veteran soldier and former lover of Superwoman, fed into Jacob Taylor; "The Geth Frankenstein," rebuilt from a damaged geth boss's memory core and partitioning itself into three personalities, became Legion; "The Design-A-Krogan," a tank-bred krogan raised from a chosen parent's genetic material (Wrex suggested), became Grunt; "The Salarian Avenger," a League of One descendant obsessed with revenge and hunted by Kirrahe's squad, and "The Assassin" concept merged into Thane Krios; "The Spacejacker" / "Young Thief," a ship-stealing rogue and lesbian romance option, became Kasumi Goto; "The Mercenary" / "The Convict," a gruff tattooed soldier-of-fortune, became Zaeed Massani; "The Fallen Paragon," a broken genius doctor drinking himself to death, and "The Professor" fed into Mordin Solus; "The Crusader" / "New Alien," an alien religious "Teutonic Knight," influenced later designs; and "Garrus the Bounty Hunter" / "The Rebel General" retained Garrus as a vigilante leader.

Detailed descriptions expand on the Cerberus Female (manipulative, aristocratic intelligence analyst who pursues male Shepard), Cerberus Male (soft-spoken "Old Man" fire support who could attempt suicide if not helped), the Salarian Assassin ("Mac"), Quarian Infiltrator, and Young Thief. The "Narrative Overviews" and "Acquisition & Loyalty Missions" sections describe Garrus's cut acquisition and loyalty missions built around a mole who betrayed his vigilante team, and Thane's loyalty mission about stopping his son from becoming an assassin for revenge. An "Altered Names" list gives alternate names such as Jacob Mather, Krios Than, and Serafina "Sera" Guerrero de Vallado for Miranda.
