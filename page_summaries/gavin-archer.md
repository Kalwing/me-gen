Dr. Gavin Archer was the chief scientist of Cerberus's Project Overlord at the facility on Aite and the project's only surviving researcher. The project sought to merge a human mind with a VI to control the geth. Archer's brother David Archer, an autistic savant with a photographic memory and mathematically gifted mind who could already communicate rudimentarily with geth, volunteered as the test subject. When the Illusive Man threatened to shut Overlord down for lack of results, Gavin forcibly connected David to the VI, causing him great pain. David could not handle the merger and became "more like a virus," seizing control of the facility's technology and threatening a "technological apocalypse" if he uploaded off-world.

In Mass Effect 2 (DLC "Overlord"), Gavin contacted Commander Shepard from a locked computer room, directing the Commander to stop the rogue VI by destroying the base's satellite dish and infiltrating Atlas Station via Vulcan and Prometheus Stations. When Shepard subdued David in the Atlas core, Gavin entered, initially wanting to continue the experiment ("If my work spares a million mothers mourning the loss of a million sons, my conscience will rest easy"), then relenting and asking to care for his brother. Shepard could send David to Grissom Academy or leave him connected; Gavin could be pistol-whipped or punched and was warned against pursuing David.

In Mass Effect 3 (2186), Gavin was among ex-Cerberus scientists fleeing the Illusive Man, who had placed a kill order on him after he erased all Project Overlord data. Outcomes varied: if David was safe at Grissom Academy, Gavin lent his AI expertise to improving the Crucible. If David was left at Overlord, Gavin eventually performed a mercy killing when David stopped responding, then defected from guilt. If David died in a Cerberus attack on Grissom Academy, or if Shepard revealed the geth were no longer a threat (making David's suffering pointless), or if Shepard lied about David's fate, Gavin drew a pistol, walked away, and shot himself. He was voiced by Simon Templeman.
