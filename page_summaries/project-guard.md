Project Guards were enemies encountered during the Mass Effect 2 assignment Arrival, attacking Commander Shepard throughout Project Base. They first appeared during the Object Rho sequence in up to five waves alongside Project Elites, Pyros and Engineers, and afterward were found in pairs or larger mixed groups.

They were comparable to any other trooper, armed with the M-8 Avenger for constant gunfire and carrying Flashbang Grenades to blind Shepard, dislodge the Commander from cover and destroy shields. They gained weak shielding on higher difficulties and had moderate health from body armor. Since Shepard fought them alone, they used their numbers to outflank and overwhelm, so careful cover and target selection mattered; facing away from and moving clear of their grenades reduced the effects, and detonating a nearby Project Pyro could catch a Guard in the blast.
