This is an index page listing the races and species of the Milky Way and Andromeda galaxies, with under 150 words of real prose. It groups Milky Way entries into Citadel races (asari, drell, elcor, hanar, humans, keepers, salarians, turians, volus), non-Citadel races (batarians, Collectors, geth, krogan, Leviathans, quarians, raloi, Reapers, virtual aliens, vorcha, yahg), historical races (arthenn, densorin, inusannon, oravores, Protheans, rachni, thoi'han, zeioph, zha'til), and various non-sapient creatures. Andromeda entries cover intelligent races (angara, Jardaan, kett, Remnant) and creatures. It contains no standalone lore beyond these listings.
