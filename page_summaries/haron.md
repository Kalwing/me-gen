Sergeant Haron was a turian C-Sec officer reporting to Captain Bailey, working at the security checkpoint to the C-Sec Office in Zakera Ward. He was in charge of scanning all new arrivals on the Citadel, checking DNA and screening for unauthorized weapons. When Commander Shepard arrived on the Citadel in 2185, Haron scanned the Commander, was surprised that Citadel records listed Shepard as dead, apologized for the delay, and directed Shepard to Captain Bailey.

During Shepard's investigation into Thane Krios's son Kolyat, Haron and another officer worked to delay Elias Kelham's lawyer, whose summons was likely triggered by Kelham's VI when a C-Sec officer came within ten meters. When Shepard and Thane apprehended Kolyat with his target Joram Talid, Bailey and Haron were the first officers to arrive at Talid's apartment; Bailey ordered Haron to take Kolyat into custody and later to give Thane and Kolyat a private room. A cut conversation, accessible only through a removed Presidium-first path, had Haron hint that Venari Pallin died in the Battle of the Citadel and was replaced by Chellick.
