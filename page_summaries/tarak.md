Tarak was the leader of the Blue Suns on Omega in 2185. The vigilante Archangel had personally tried to kill him in his own home; fed up with the vigilante's disruption of Blue Suns operations, Tarak formed a temporary alliance with the Blood Pack and Eclipse, whose leaders Garm and Jaroth had been similarly harmed. The assassination attempt left Tarak short-tempered and paranoid, harrying subordinates such as Jentha. Though none of the other mercenary bosses would admit it, Tarak was the de facto leader of the joint operation, being an experienced negotiator and deal-broker. He also joined a scheme, proposed by Jaroth and confirmed with Garm, to move against Omega's ruler Aria T'Loak using the combined strength of the three gangs once Archangel was dealt with.

Having cornered Archangel at his base, Tarak decided to send waves of expendable hired freelancers to distract the vigilante while specialized infiltration teams got into position, planning to kill any surviving freelancers afterward and let Eclipse and the Blood Pack exhaust their forces while the Blue Suns hung back, against Jentha's advice. He loathed relying on outside help but was growing desperate. He used his personal A-61 Mantis Gunship against Archangel early in the assault, but the vigilante disabled it, forcing Tarak back to base; he ordered Sergeant Cathka to finish repairs under threat of being sent against Archangel himself. If Zaeed Massani approached with a group of freelancers, Tarak recognized him from past work and briefly explained himself before passing them to Jentha.

Commander Shepard's team, disguised as freelancers, rushed to Archangel's aid and destroyed the Eclipse and Blood Pack forces. Tarak entered the battle in his gunship, offloading troops and firing missiles, one of which exploded near the vigilante and took him out of action. Shepard's team shot down the gunship, killing Tarak.
