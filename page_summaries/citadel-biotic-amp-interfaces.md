Citadel: Biotic Amp Interfaces was a Mass Effect 3 mission with prerequisites Priority: Palaven and Grissom Academy: Emergency Evacuation. An unnamed asari standing at the Lab Services section of Huerta Memorial Hospital was trying to reach Grissom Academy to request improved biotic amp interfaces she could adapt for asari use, but no one answered. Overhearing her troubles activated the mission; alternatively, finding the Biotic Amp Interfaces first during the student rescue triggered it. A second journal entry framed it as schematics recovered from Grissom Academy needing someone on the Citadel who could use them.

The interfaces were on a terminal in an otherwise empty corridor during Grissom Academy: Emergency Evacuation, past a corridor where Assault Troopers harassed Grissom students behind a shield, including Octavia and others, with David Archer present if he had been sent there. Shepard collected them and delivered them to the asari on the next hospital visit, earning 5 Reputation, 30 experience, and 1,000 credits, and updating the Citadel Defense Force War Asset. If missed, they could be bought from the Spectre Requisitions terminal in the Spectre Office at the Citadel Embassies. After completion, the asari told someone to alert Matriarch Aethyta that her huntresses had received the amp interfaces. The mission had to be completed before Priority: Tuchanka, as it was no longer available after the civilian areas reopened following Priority: The Citadel II.
