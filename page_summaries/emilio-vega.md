Emilio Vega was James Vega's uncle and a father figure who lived in Escondido, California, and encouraged James to join the Systems Alliance Marines. Before James's mother died she made Emilio promise to look out for her son. Emilio helped James enlist in 2176. After James argued with his father, Joshua Sanders, and feared Josh would report to the military that James had bought drugs for him in San Diego, Emilio reassured him that Josh would never admit to a drug deal made through a minor, and told James that only he could choose how to live his life. James went on to join the Alliance. During the Reaper invasion of 2186, James sought news of his father and uncle, both still on Earth, but Citadel authorities could not contact them.
