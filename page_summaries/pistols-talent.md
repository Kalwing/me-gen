Pistols was a combat talent in the original Mass Effect, available to all classes except the Asari Scientist, Krogan Battlemaster, and Turian Agent; Sentinels lacked the talent itself but gained pistol bonuses from their class talent. Training improved damage and accuracy with pistols and unlocked the activatable Marksman ability. Talent ranks raised pistol damage from 5% to 20% and accuracy from 10% to 30% across twelve levels, with milestone ranks unlocking Marksman (rank 3), other weapon proficiencies for certain classes (ranks 4-6), Advanced Marksman (rank 8), and Master Marksman (rank 12).

Marksman was a timed pistol buff boosting accuracy by 60%, increasing rate of fire, and reducing heat, with duration scaling from 10 to 16 seconds across its three tiers and a fixed 45-second recharge. A Sentinel Shepard could only get the basic rank. Switching weapons, changing the equipped pistol, or modifying its upgrades immediately cancelled Marksman. Soldiers and Infiltrators could shorten its recharge with Commando specialization.

In Mass Effect Legendary Edition, Marksman purged residual weapon heat on activation, had a fixed 6-second duration, and instead of boosting fire rate increased base damage (25% to 75%) and headshot damage (50% to 100%) across its tiers; headshots, newly possible in Legendary Edition, made the talent more potent, though squad members did not deliberately aim for heads.
