This page was a roster index for the Mass Effect mini-comics, listing character links per title grouped as protagonist, allies, and adversaries. It covered five mini-comics with roughly fourteen entries total: Mass Effect: Incursion (Aria T'Loak); Mass Effect: Inquisition (Captain Bailey, Executor Pallin); Mass Effect: Conviction (James Vega, David Anderson); Mass Effect: Blasto: Eternity is Forever (Blasto, Cerulean Star, Kronus); and Mass Effect: He Who Laughs Best (Jeff "Joker" Moreau, David Anderson, Crooks, Dillard, Invectus). It contained no original prose beyond the link tables.
