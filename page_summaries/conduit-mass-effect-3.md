Short lore page. The Conduit of Mass Effect 3 was a massive structure the Reapers built in war-torn London in 2186 to transport corpses and other matter directly from the ground to the Citadel, which had been relocated above Earth. Allied galactic forces called it a "conduit" and attempted to storm it in order to reopen the arms of the Citadel for the next stage of the war, but were largely annihilated by Harbinger; only Commander Shepard and David Anderson reached the Citadel.

The structure followed basic mass relay principles but differed in several ways: it emitted a constant beam of blue light into the sky rather than lighting up only during transmission, had no visible element zero core, allowed living matter to survive the trip unaided (arriving at ballistic but survivable speeds), and appeared to have multiple destination termini, since Shepard and Anderson materialized at slightly different points on the station. In some outcomes of Shepard's final decision, the conduit appeared to have been remodeled or rebuilt using prevailing galactic architecture.
