May O'Connell was a colonist at Zhu's Hope on Feros in Mass Effect. Following a recent geth attack she was placed in charge of restoring power to the colony, but the generator she was working on had failed. If asked, she requested that Shepard find new power cells; when Shepard offered spare cells from the SSV Normandy, she noted dryly that the generator was an old model and the Normandy's cells were too sophisticated for it.

Like the other colonists, May was under the influence of the ancient Thorian. During the assault on Zhu's Hope, Shepard could kill or knock out the affected colonists; killing her meant the colony would fail to secure a power supply unless she had already been given the power cells. If she survived, she was relieved that the Thorian was gone and that she could think again without pain, though if too many colonists died she considered repairing the generator pointless since the survivors would be leaving anyway. She was voiced by Jennifer Hale, who also voiced female Commander Shepard.
