Part 2 of a hand-corrected survey of Reaper types, ordered by increasing size. The Hades Cannon was a four-legged anti-air and anti-spacecraft weapon shaped like a small Reaper, firing a highly accurate energy beam able to shoot down fast drop shuttles; during the invasion of Earth in 2186 these cannons made troop landings nearly impossible until Commander Shepard destroyed one with the M-920 Cain, clearing the way for allied forces.

Destroyers were smaller Reapers about 160 m tall, the most common true Reapers, with four legs and head-mounted arms; they opened front plates to fire a ship-killing beam, were faster and more agile than capital Reapers, could chase or board smaller ships, and acted as walking tanks on the ground. The human-Reaper larva was an unfinished Reaper grown from human bodies on Harbinger's orders, built from humans the Collectors abducted and reduced to genetic material; it had a humanoid upper body, was roughly 200 m tall even as a larva, and was destroyed by Shepard's team before it could develop.

The Leviathans were included for context as the Reapers' creators: ancient ocean-dwelling creatures around 450 m long resembling giant squid, who ruled the galaxy billions of years ago, treated other species as tools through mind control, built the intelligence that became the Reapers, and later hid in ocean worlds while erasing evidence of their existence.

Processors were roughly 600 m machines that collect and process organic material into raw material for new Reapers; they were remotely controlled tools, not alive. Intruders were mid-level Reaper ships between Destroyers and capital ships, with stronger beams, shields, and point defense. Troop transports (200 m to a kilometer) moved husks and captured populations and were also remotely controlled. The Devastator functioned as a flying carrier, launching large numbers of Oculus drones and Destroyers, trading durability for internal drone storage.

Reaper capital ships were the largest class, around 2 km long and squid-shaped, leading the fleet and attacking whole planets with a hull-length cannon no dreadnought could survive. Sovereign was a unique, fully alive capital-class Reaper about 2 km long that served as leader and scout, waking periodically over millennia to gauge galactic advancement, and tasked with starting the next invasion via indoctrination and activation of the Citadel; the geth worshipped it as a god though it regarded all other life as temporary tools. Harbinger, the first Reaper, remained in dark space and directed events through the Collectors, taking direct control of individual Collectors in battle, and devised the plan to harvest humans and build the human-Reaper.
