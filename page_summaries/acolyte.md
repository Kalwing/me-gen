The Acolyte was a heavy pistol in Mass Effect 3. It was designed for the asari resistance, and its barrels fired advanced ammunition similar to an impact-triggered resonant warp bomb, devastating against shields and biotic barriers. The specialized warp field meant it did not pierce armor as effectively, on the expectation that the wielder's own biotics would make up the difference.

In single-player the Acolyte became available with the Mass Effect 3: Groundside Resistance Pack, and in Legendary Edition it could be bought from the Nos Astra Sporting Goods terminal in the Presidium Commons for a base 10,000 credits. In multiplayer it was a rare weapon requiring the Mass Effect 3: Earth DLC and was awarded randomly through item packs.

The weapon fired only charged shots: the trigger had to be held for about a second and released. It carried a 500 percent damage bonus against shields and barriers and, despite its description, suffered no penalty against armor. Its explosive projectile arced downward, could bounce off walls, and could detonate in the air near targets; the proximity detonation grew larger after the first bounce, letting careful aim strip shields from a crowd or hit Guardians and CAT6 Heavies behind their shields. Because it fired explosive munitions, it could not penetrate objects or score weak-point damage, but it bypassed enemy shield gates so leftover damage could carry through to health or armor. It was especially devastating to Phantoms, which could not dodge or block its rounds. Hits usually staggered light infantry and could cause severe electrocution-style stuns. As one of the lightest weapons in the game, it suited cooldown-conscious biotic and melee classes and all volus multiplayer classes. Squadmates could use it, being an exception to the rule that they never fire charged shots. If not playing Legendary Edition, having the Acolyte equipped made the confrontation with Dr. Eva on Mars nearly unwinnable due to its charge time, and the player had to switch weapons immediately.
