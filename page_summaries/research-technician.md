Research Technicians were personnel employed by Cerberus to conduct research at the organization's installations in Mass Effect. They were biotics and were armed with pistols. Technicians worked at multiple installations on Binthu, where they conducted research on organisms including Rachni Workers, Rachni Soldiers, and Thorian Creepers for unknown reasons. In combat they typically opened with a biotic attack, then used their pistols as covering fire while their talents recharged. The layout of the Binthu labs and the obscuring forcefields at their centers made it possible to sneak up on a technician for a first-strike attack. In Legendary Edition, unlike most biotic enemies, Research Technicians could still target Shepard directly with their disabler Throws.
