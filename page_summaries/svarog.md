Svarog was the first planet of the Pax System in the Horse Head Nebula of the Milky Way, and the system's only gas giant. It was described as a small hydrogen-helium gas giant orbiting very close to its star, an unusual configuration for a system that also contained a habitable world. Its high temperature, powerful winds and high orbital velocity made it a poor candidate for helium-3 mining, which was why nearby Noveria, despite having plentiful deuterium for its fusion plants, imported all its helium-3 from out-system.

Like most pegasids, Svarog was thought to have formed outside its star's frost line and migrated inward on an unstable orbit. Its small size for a hydrogen-helium gas giant was attributed to hydrodynamic escape, and because it orbited closer than 0.015 AU its atmosphere was expected to be consumed before long. Scientists continued to debate how the system's other planets survived Svarog's inward migration, since its gravity may have disrupted them during their formation. The page notes that the planet is likely better described as a dense ice giant like Neptune, and that in Slavic mythology Svarog is the god of the Sun. Reaching Svarog in Mass Effect 3 required completing Priority: Sur'Kesh.
