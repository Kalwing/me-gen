Smash is a biotic power exclusive to Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode. The user drives a biotic lash into the ground to cause area-of-effect and heavy direct damage. Its base statistics were an eight-second recharge, 550 damage, 450 N force, and a 1.5 m radius, scaling across six ranks with options for recharge speed, force and damage, a detonatable biotic effect, an alternative electrical effect, armor and barrier effectiveness, and an impact radius increase that raised the number of targets hit.

Smash dealt 1.5x damage to all forms of protection and reliably staggered all but the largest enemies. Most unprotected enemies not killed outright were tossed into the air to float down in a slow arc. It normally hit a maximum of two enemies, or three with the rank 6 Impact Radius evolution. Like Shockwave it could travel through walls and cover, but unlike Shockwave it could be aimed above and below the player. Its cooldown did not begin until one second into the animation, allowing a roll or melee to cancel it, and a heavy melee could be chained immediately after for extra area damage. The player's Smash was faster than the version used by Cerberus Dragoons and would always strike first if activated simultaneously. The power granted no invincibility during its lengthy animation, though the user could keep moving at reduced speed while casting. A dodge-cancel performed just as the whips swung down still caused damage, allowing the user to circle larger targets.

Smash was available in multiplayer to the Project Phoenix Adept and Project Phoenix Vanguard, and had no single-player use.
