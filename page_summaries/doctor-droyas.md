Doctor Droyas was a krogan researcher working at Saren Arterius' base on Virmire in the first Mass Effect. He was furious when Commander Shepard's squad broke into his lab during the assault on the base, declaring that his work on countering the genophage was the "glorious salvation" of his species. He worked alongside an asari assistant and appeared to be conducting research involving husks, amid a variety of machinery.

In combat Droyas was easier to defeat than most krogan enemies because he wore no armor and lacked regeneration and Immunity. He was dangerous mainly for his tech abilities, including Neural Shock, which could instantly stun Shepard and leave the Commander exposed to the husk test subjects, as well as Overload, Sabotage, Damping, and a self-heal form of First Aid. He could charge but had no actual melee attack. Cut content included an unused dialogue in which Shepard, and Wrex if present, could briefly converse with Droyas as he defended his anti-genophage work; in the final game he attacks immediately while speaking a line or two from it.
