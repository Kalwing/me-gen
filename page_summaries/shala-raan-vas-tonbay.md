Admiral Shala'Raan vas Tonbay was a member of the quarian Admiralty Board and an old friend of Tali'Zorah's father Rael'Zorah, having known the Zorah family for over 25 years. Tali called her "Auntie Raan." Raan and Tali's mother had synchronised suits so both could be in the clean room for Tali's birth, leaving Raan sick for a week, and Raan was the one who placed the infant Tali in her environmental bubble.

In Mass Effect 2, Raan presided over Tali'Zorah vas Neema's treason hearing aboard the Rayya but recused herself from judging due to her ties to the family, acting instead as moderator and spokesman. She deliberately withheld news of the geth attack on the Alarei so Tali's surprise would be genuine before the judges. She was undecided on the geth question. Depending on Shepard's advocacy, Raan announced either that Tali was guilty and exiled or that the Board had acquitted her.

In Mass Effect 3, if Tali died at the Collector Base, Raan filled her role as a quarian envoy aboard the Normandy SR-2. She had given provisional support to Han'Gerrel and Daro'Xen's push for war with the geth. During the mission to disable the geth dreadnought she followed the plan and tried to stop Han'Gerrel from ordering the Heavy Fleet to fire on the ship while Shepard's team was aboard, then threatened to charge him with treason. She tasked Daro'Xen with countering the Reaper-upgraded geth, asked Shepard to rescue Zaal'Koris after his crash landing on Rannoch, and later provided a Sync Laser and the coordinates of the Reaper base; her ship was shot down during the assault.

If Shepard chose the geth and Raan had replaced Tali, she shot herself in the head. If Shepard chose the quarians and Tali was dead, Raan killed Legion or the Geth VI with a shotgun when the geth platform attacked, expressing regret. If peace was secured, a newly sentient Geth Prime welcomed the quarians to Rannoch and Raan suggested settling the southern continent for its farmland. Afterward she renamed herself Shala'Raan vas Rannoch and sent Shepard a progress report on the fleet's recovery and support of the war effort. If Tali was dead, Raan committed the quarian forces to the final battle on Earth.
