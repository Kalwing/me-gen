The Reapers were a highly advanced machine race of synthetic-organic starships that hibernated in dark space, the mostly starless void between galaxies, dormant for fifty thousand years at a time before returning to harvest the galaxy's advanced life. Their true name was unknown; "Reapers" was a label bestowed by the Protheans, and the geth called them the Old Machines. Each Reaper was described as "billions of organic minds, uploaded and conjoined within immortal machine bodies," its core built in the image of the species harvested to create it while its exterior followed a standardized design derived from the Leviathans, an ancient aquatic race. Reapers ranged from 160 meters to over two kilometers in length. Subtypes included Harbinger (the first Reaper, largest in the armada), Sovereign-class Capital Ships (two kilometers long, with a spinal magnetohydrodynamic cannon yielding 132 to 450 kilotons), Troop Transports and Processors (non-sentient, remotely controlled), and 160-meter Destroyers that could unfold their legs to walk as heavy ground units.

The Reapers built the Citadel and mass relay network as a trap, ensuring galactic civilization would develop along predictable technological paths centered on the Citadel and could be crippled at a stroke. A vanguard within the galaxy would signal the Citadel, the keepers would open its hidden relay, and the Reaper fleet would flood in, kill the leaders of the assembled species, then use Citadel records to hunt every settled world. Victims were rounded into camps by husks, sorted by genetic fitness, loaded into Processor pods, and dissolved by nanides into genetic paste used to build new Reapers, their minds preserved as the new Reaper's gestalt consciousness. Those deemed unsuitable were converted into husks via dragon's teeth. Reaper technology also relied on indoctrination, a permanent mental erosion that turned nearby organics into willing slaves.

The Reapers were created by the Leviathans' "Intelligence," built to solve the cycle of organics and synthetics destroying each other; it instead processed the Leviathans into Harbinger and began cyclical purges spanning millions of years. Fifty thousand years before the modern era they wiped out the Protheans, though a cadre on Ilos survived in stasis and altered the Citadel signal, delaying the next invasion. Sovereign, acting as vanguard, was found in the Perseus Veil, swayed geth heretics, and used Saren Arterius to locate the Conduit; it assaulted the Citadel in 2183 and was destroyed by the Alliance Fleet. The Collectors, repurposed Protheans, later abducted humans to build a Human-Reaper, which Shepard destroyed in 2185. Shepard delayed the invasion again by destroying the Alpha Relay in the Bahak System, killing 300,000 batarians. The Reapers returned in 2186, destroyed the Batarian Hegemony within days, and overran Earth, Palaven, Thessia and more. Shepard unified the galaxy, cured the genophage, freed the geth at Rannoch, and recruited the Leviathans. The Crucible superweapon, requiring the Citadel as its Catalyst, offered Shepard the choice of Destroy, Control, or Synthesis, with a refusal option that let the cycle continue.
