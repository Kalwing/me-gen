Model Ships were a collectibles feature in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3 with no impact on story or gameplay. Mass Effect 2 had eleven models; the Normandy SR-2 model started on display in the Captain's Cabin, and the rest were bought from vendors, except the Flotilla Vessel (found during Tali's loyalty mission) and the Shadow Broker Base (found during Lair of the Shadow Broker). Purchased models went on a display rack, sometimes at a discount for talking to shopkeepers or doing small tasks. In Mass Effect 3, models acquired in Mass Effect 2 returned scattered around the Normandy, and additional ships became available on the Citadel for 1,000 credits (or 1,100 through Alliance Requisitions in the shuttle bay) after completing priority missions. The model sold as a "UT-47" actually matched a "human heavy cruiser" concept and was labeled an "Alliance Shuttle" in Mass Effect 3. The display drew comparison to the ship models in Star Trek. Mass Effect 3: Citadel added a second Normandy SR-2 model, a Citadel model, a freighter and an original-trilogy Alliance ship design in Shepard's apartment.
