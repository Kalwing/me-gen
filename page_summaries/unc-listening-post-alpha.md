UNC: Listening Post Alpha was an assignment in Mass Effect, the second leg of the Cerberus rachni experiment chain (prerequisites: Noveria: Matriarch Benezia and UNC: Listening Post Theta). Listening Post Alpha, on Nepmos in the Erebus system of the Styx Theta gateway cluster, was reportedly under attack by unidentified life forms and requesting assistance. Entering the Erebus system triggered a distress call from Lieutenant Durand; the assignment could also be picked up from a terminal in the base's comm room.

On the surface, Commander Shepard found three marines on barricade-platforms defending the post against a swarm of rachni. No experience, credits, or loot were gained from these rachni. A central generator could power twin rocket turrets on the base perimeter if connected to the Mako, which disabled the vehicle in exchange; the turrets could one-shot rachni but fired slowly. The rachni, all Rachni Soldiers emerging from holes in front of the post, attacked in three waves with two one-minute rest periods between them. During the lulls Durand told Shepard the assault had gone on for days and that the few surviving marines were all that remained of a platoon of ninety. After the third wave, a marine reported that a UAV had found a hollow space about 500 meters below the surface, and Durand asked Shepard to investigate. Completing this conversation was enough to unlock the Gorgon system for the third leg, UNC: Depot Sigma-23 (the other requirement being UNC: Listening Post Theta).

Clearing the nearby Rachni Infested Mine was optional but required to close the journal entry. The mine's first room held dormant holes; the main room had eight holes releasing up to twenty rachni (these did grant experience). Two branch tunnels beyond held Rachni Soldiers and Rachni Brood Warriors, the latter emerging last and using stasis and a form of barrier. Killing both Brood Warriors triggered a message that the rachni on the planet had been eliminated; there was no need to return to Durand. The assignment was written by Chris L'Etoile, is referred to internally as "Rachni Rebirth: Listening Post Alpha," and may be a homage to Starship Troopers.
