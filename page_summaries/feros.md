Feros was a habitable world, the second planet of the Theseus System in the Attican Beta cluster of the Milky Way, with two large moons, Orcan and Vardet. Two-thirds of its habitable surface was covered by the ruins of a crumbling Prothean megatropolis that had been picked over by looters many times since the Prothean extinction. Feros was a poor colonization prospect: little open ground remained for agriculture, the only sizable fresh water sources were the poles (tapped by decaying Prothean aqueduct systems), the dead cities were of uncertain stability, ground level was buried under a dozen meters of fallen debris, and the air was fouled by dust. Evidence suggested Feros had been a much colder world in the past.

In 2178, the human ExoGeni Corporation announced a permanent pilot colony on Feros to thoroughly explore the ruins. The pioneer settlement, Zhu's Hope, was placed on the upper levels of several intact skyscrapers, using surviving Prothean aqueducts and rooftop hydroponic gardens to support the population. In Mass Effect, Feros was visited after Shepard gained Spectre status, comprising the missions Feros: Geth Attack and Feros: The Thorian and assignments including Data Recovery, Geth in the Tunnels, Power Cells, Varren Meat, and Water Restoration. It became accessible again in Mass Effect 3 after Priority: Tuchanka.
