Rosenkov Materials was a human corporation with a reputation as one of the premier armor manufacturers in Citadel space. In Mass Effect, a licence for Rosenkov Materials gear could be purchased from Opold at Port Hanshan on Noveria for 6,250 credits. The company's weapons used superior materials to produce improved damage, accuracy, and shorter cooldown compared with rivals. Its Mass Effect weapon lines were the Kovalyov assault rifles, Sokolov shotguns, Karpov pistols, and Volkov sniper rifles; in Mass Effect Legendary Edition weapon accuracy and heat performance were revised across the board.

In Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, Rosenkov Materials manufactured a single small arm, the M-97 Viper sniper rifle, a rapid-fire military weapon developed in response to the rising prevalence of kinetic barriers, popular with infiltrators and assassins and effective against armor. It upgraded the Mantis sniper rifle.

For armor, Rosenkov produced the Titan Armor line in Mass Effect (light, medium, and heavy variants for various species), which included an integrated shield VI increasing protection against non-tech and non-biotic damage. In Mass Effect 2 the company made the Asymmetric Defense Layer for the N7 Armor, increasing health by 5%. In Mass Effect 3 it made customizable armor components (chest, shoulders, arms, legs) that boosted power recharge. According to trivia, the mercenary Pel used a Volkov sniper rifle to ambush the Cyniad crew on Shelba, shooting Feda'Gazu vas Idenna from over a mile away after an explosion disabled her shields.
