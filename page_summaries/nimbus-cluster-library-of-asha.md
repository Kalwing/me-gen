Nimbus Cluster: Library of Asha was a fetch mission in Mass Effect 3, available after Priority: The Citadel II. An asari military trainer at Purgatory on the Citadel wanted ancient texts from the Library of Asha, feeling her people needed a refresher on the ways of the huntress because too many maidens were dancing instead of fighting. The mission could be acquired by overhearing her comm conversation near the staircase to the dance floor, or the artifacts could be picked up during a Search and Rescue operation, in which case it was labeled Citadel: Library of Asha.

To retrieve the texts, Shepard scanned the planet Carcosa in the Agaiou system of the Nimbus Cluster. Delivering the Library of Asha texts to the Military Trainer granted +5 Reputation, 15,000 credits, and an upgrade to the Asari Sixth Fleet War Asset. Speaking with her again, she said she wanted the texts made required reading so every maiden would think like a huntress, adding that she had Matriarch Aethyta's backing on the issue. A known bug could prevent the trainer from appearing on the map.
