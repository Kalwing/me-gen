Kate Bowman was an engineer on Asteroid X57 near the human colony of Terra Nova. Her brother, security officer Aaron Bowman, had persuaded her to work there, claiming it would be an adventure; their father, Reverend Michael Bowman, had served with the Alliance on Earth. Colleague Simon Atwell, who called her "Katie," described her as brilliant but headstrong. When batarian terrorists led by Balak seized the asteroid and activated fusion torches to send it on a collision course with Terra Nova, Kate and Aaron hid in the main facility, and she quietly transmitted distress calls.

The SSV Normandy picked up her signals, and Kate fed Commander Shepard intelligence on enemy movements. After two of the three fusion torches were shut down, her hiding place was discovered; Balak demanded to know who was sabotaging the torches and shot Aaron dead when she refused to answer, turning her back so she would not have to watch. Kate and other hostages were then locked in an office with a bomb, which she tried to defuse herself. Her fate depended on Shepard's confrontation with Balak: if Balak was allowed to leave, Kate and the hostages survived; if not, the bomb killed them.

If she survived, Kate met Shepard afterward, thanked the Commander, grieved her brother, and asked Shepard to remember Aaron if they crossed paths with Balak again. In 2185, Citadel news reported her death if the asteroid drop was never stopped, a memorial ceremony held by her father if the hostages died, or, if she lived, that she was to read a thank-you message to her rescuers at an anniversary celebration. In that case she also emailed Shepard, expressing colonists' worry about events in the Terminus Systems and gratitude that Shepard was there to help.
