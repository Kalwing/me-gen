Citadel: Found Forged ID was acquired on the Citadel in one of two ways: by picking up forged IDs before the two YMIR Mech fight during Garrus Vakarian's loyalty mission Garrus: Eye for an Eye, which named it Citadel: Found Forged ID, or by hearing an asari speak near the Level 27 stairs after Thane Krios's loyalty mission Thane: Sins of the Father, which named it Citadel: False Positives. Thane's loyalty mission had to be finished for the assignment to be completed. Shepard spoke with an asari named Kalara Tomi, seated with a companion, who complained she had been wrongly flagged as a geth-infiltration security risk, preventing the pair from leaving the Citadel. An imported Mass Effect save in which Shepard had abandoned the Council made Kalara hostile toward Shepard and humanity.

Shepard could resolve it by giving Kalara the forged IDs for 1,000 credits and no morality points, unless the journal was first updated by choosing "Let me help," which then allowed giving the IDs for +5 Renegade. Alternatively, Shepard could speak to the C-Sec Customs officer at the Citadel entrance and charm or intimidate him (+5 Paragon or Renegade), or use reinstated Spectre authority (+5 Paragon), to clear the asari to leave; telling Kalara honestly how it was done granted a further +2 Paragon. Rewards were 1,000 credits and 40 (50) experience. Several bugs could allow the assignment to complete twice or cause Kalara to vanish across disc changes on the Xbox 360 version.
