The M-96 Mattock was a semi-automatic assault rifle in Mass Effect 2, Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Andromeda. It was a hybrid weapon with an assault rifle's low heat production and a sniper rifle's punch, favored by marksmen for bringing down hardened targets. Its lack of a full-auto setting was advertised as a feature that curbed a soldier's tendency to spray inaccurate fire under stress. Colonist militias had used the design for decades. In Mass Effect 2 it upgraded the M-8 Avenger.

In Mass Effect 2 it was acquired through the Firepower Pack DLC, accompanied by an email from the Illusive Man explaining that EDI and Miranda had suggested revisiting older, proven technology; in the Legendary Edition it was sold at Kenn's Salvage on Omega. Usable squadmates included Garrus, Grunt, Legion, Samara/Morinth and Zaeed. It fired as fast as the trigger could be pulled, making it exceptional during a Soldier's Adrenaline Rush, but had a low ammo reserve.

In Mass Effect 3 it was found during Grissom Academy: Emergency Evacuation on a couch before Seanne Bellarmine, with a second upgrade sometimes in the security room unlocked by David Archer if he was rescued in Overlord; it was categorized as uncommon in multiplayer. It offered highly accurate, damaging semi-automatic fire effective at medium range but was weak against armor without mods and a poor squadmate weapon. The M-55 Argus, Cerberus Harrier and L-89 Halberd were variants. A mattock is a pickaxe-like hand tool.
