Klensal was the third planet of the Dis System in the Hades Gamma cluster, with a thin atmosphere of carbon dioxide and ethane. Its first geological surveys, performed by batarians, suggested great mineral wealth, prompting human mining concerns to spend billions of credits rushing to the distant system and sinking test bores. In reality Klensal held only an average level of mineral wealth. Merida Industria, a small Mexican company on its first extrasolar mining venture, was forced into bankruptcy protection. Investigation revealed the batarian crew had deliberately falsified their surveys to lure human rivals into a costly boondoggle; this was unethical but not illegal, and the batarian government disavowed the "personal actions of a few misguided patriots." The planet remained littered with abandoned mining bases, often used as temporary meeting places for criminals.

In Mass Effect, Klensal was tied to the assignment UNC: Hostile Takeover, involving a syndicate hideout and a crime boss with mercenaries and snipers, plus a mummified salarian carrying a League of One Medallion and salvageable minerals (platinum, beryllium). In Mass Effect 3 it offered a Search and Rescue salvage worth 10,000 credits.
