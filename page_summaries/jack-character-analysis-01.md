Part 1 of a YouTube character analysis of Jack, covering her origin and history; the author frames Jack as one of the best-written characters in the series and a frequent romance choice, and notes some content is interpretation rather than hard canon. Names attached to her: Jack, Subject Zero, Jacqueline Nought, and Jennifer. Born a biotic and modified into a weapon, she is one of the most powerful and notorious human criminals in the setting.

In 2165 Cerberus, a human-supremacist paramilitary group, abducted the infant later called Jennifer, drawn by biotic potential from accidental element-zero exposure in the womb, and told her mother the child had died. Cerberus sent her to the Teltin facility on Pragia, whose goal was to enhance human biotic potential "no matter the risk." Designated Subject Zero and later named Jack, she was the focal point of all research; other children were expendable, with lethal enhancements tested on them first and only beneficial ones applied to Jack. Scientists tortured her physically and mentally to study how pain and distress affected biotics. She was kept behind soundproof one-way glass, able to see and hear the other children but not the reverse, and spent hours screaming and beating on the glass. The other children hated her because Cerberus regularly killed them in experiments meant to advance her abilities. Their only interaction was forced gladiatorial combat: she was shocked for hesitating and injected with narcotics for killing without hesitation, hardwiring a link between euphoria and killing. The abuse made her one of the strongest human biotics alive at a very young age.

Eventually the subjects rioted and attacked the guards, damaging the facility and freeing Jack, whose pent-up rage sent her into a blind fury; she nearly killed everyone present, then stole a shuttle and escaped Pragia. A passing freighter picked her up, used her, and sold her into slavery. In the violent, crime-filled years that followed she committed murder, assault, kidnapping, drug use, theft, arson, piracy, and vandalism, and joined a cult. She attacked another Cerberus facility using biotic children to free them, fought off Cerberus agents while covering the children's escape, but was overrun and captured by Blue Suns mercenaries, to Cerberus's frustration, and taken to the prison ship Purgatory. There guards and prisoners beat and raped her; after healing she murdered every attacker. Warden Kuril decided she was not worth the Blue Suns' effort and offered to sell her back to Cerberus. In 2185 Commander Shepard, revived and working with Cerberus, arrived to complete the purchase and recruit Jack against the Collectors. When Kuril tried to capture Shepard a fight broke out, Jack escaped and rampaged, and after being talked down from killing Shepard over the Cerberus connection she agreed to join the team.
