Stop the Collectors was the main plot mission of Mass Effect 2, acquired automatically after Freedom's Progress once Commander Shepard confirmed the Collectors were behind the abduction of entire human colonies in the Terminus Systems. It consisted of a chain of missions directed by the Illusive Man and ended only when Shepard confronted the Collector Base beyond the Omega 4 Relay.

Shepard first assembled a team using the Illusive Man's dossiers, recruiting a core group (Archangel/Garrus Vakarian, the Convict/Jack, the Professor/Mordin Solus, the Warlord's creation Grunt, plus DLC recruits the Veteran and the Master Thief), and was reunited with Joker and given the Cerberus-built Normandy SR-2, staffed by EDI. After the Collectors attacked Horizon, the Illusive Man provided further dossiers (Tali, the Assassin, the Justicar). Shepard then boarded a disabled Collector ship, where EDI discovered the Collectors used a Reaper IFF to pass the Omega 4 Relay, and the ship proved to be a trap.

To obtain a Reaper IFF, Shepard traveled to a derelict Reaper orbiting the brown dwarf Mnemosyne, fought through Husks with help from a lone geth platform (recruitable as Legion), retrieved the IFF and destroyed the Reaper. While the IFF was being installed and the squad was away in the shuttle, the IFF broadcast the Normandy's position and a Collector cruiser boarded the ship; EDI was unshackled and the player briefly controlled Joker as he purged the Collectors, but the entire crew except Joker was abducted. Shepard then faced a choice: go through the relay immediately to save everyone, or complete more missions and lose half or all of the crew.

Using the Omega 4 Relay, the Normandy emerged in a debris field, fought Oculi and a Collector cruiser (with squad deaths possible if the ship was not upgraded), and crash-landed on the Collector Base. Inside, Shepard learned the Collectors were building a Human-Reaper from abducted humans, faced a final choice to destroy the base or preserve it for Cerberus via a radiation pulse, and had a closing conversation with the Illusive Man mirroring the aftermath of the Battle of the Citadel.
