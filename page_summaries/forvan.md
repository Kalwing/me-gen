Forvan was a batarian bartender stationed on the lower level of the Afterlife Club on Omega during Mass Effect 2. He held a grudge against humans and habitually poisoned the drinks he served to human customers. His motive stemmed from the deaths of his brothers, who had been stationed at a mining colony on Bekke when Jacob Taylor and his crew attacked it, an assault allegedly carried out to prevent a batarian terrorist attack on the Citadel. Following his brothers' deaths, Forvan poisoned human patrons to "return the favour."

Commander Shepard could order a drink from Forvan and, after being served, fell unconscious while Forvan walked away with a smug glance. Shepard woke outside Afterlife, where a human passerby named Henner explained what Forvan had done and why. Shepard could then confront the bartender. If Shepard loudly and correctly accused Forvan of routinely poisoning drinks, Forvan drew an M-6 Carnifex and aimed it at Shepard, prompting an irate turian bar patron who overheard the accusation to execute him. Alternatively, Shepard could force Forvan to drink from his own poisoned stock, causing an ironic death, or could dismiss him as not worth the Commander's time, letting Forvan leave alive after he vowed never to do it again. Regardless of the outcome, Forvan was eventually replaced by a salarian bartender whose drinks posed no threat beyond intoxication.
