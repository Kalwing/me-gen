Spectre Requisitions is a store located inside the Spectre office in the Citadel Embassies in Mass Effect 3. It sells high-end weaponry, upgrades, and certain unretrieved items needed to complete missions. Because the Citadel becomes inaccessible after Priority: Cerberus Headquarters, a missed Delumcore Overlay can afterward only be bought through the Alliance Requisitions terminal in the Normandy's shuttle bay. In Legendary Edition some item prices were adjusted from the original release.

Most of its listed items are framed as morally or politically sensitive acquisitions requiring cover stories. The Biotic Amp Interface Tech comes from a dismissed Grissom Academy instructor and addresses amp burnout in asari commandos. Cerberus Encryption Codes and Chemical Burn Treatments are sold quietly by the salarian Special Tasks Group, to be attributed to other sources. Cerberus Turret Control Schematics help C-Sec disable devices Cerberus left behind after their coup. Geth Target-Jamming Tech is offered by an Eclipse covert ops team and to be treated as recovered on Rannoch. Heating Stabilizers use hanar technology to save the salarian colony of Senoquol's egg incubators. Information on Bilal Osoba provides the dog tags of Ambassador Dominic Osoba's son, missing on Benning. Other items include a Non-Human Medi-Gel Upgrade, Power Grid Schematics for Purgatory, a Reaper Code Fragment from a scavenged Sovereign processor to help asari war strategists predict Reaper movements, and a Turian-Specific Toxin antidote for a wounded turian general at Huerta Memorial Hospital.
