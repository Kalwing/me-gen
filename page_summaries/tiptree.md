Tiptree was a small human colony attacked by a small Reaper force shortly after the Reaper invasion began in 2186. An asari commando unit was dispatched to evacuate it, gathering colonists from across the continent while husks and marauder scouts were landed against them. Most of the commando team was destroyed.

One survivor, Aeian T'Goni, developed post-traumatic stress disorder after watching Neaira, a fellow asari commando who had been turned into a banshee during the evacuation, slaughter human farmers. While Neaira killed the farmers, the unarmed T'Goni escaped into the hills with a human girl named Hilary, whom she judged to be "maybe 15" and who said she wanted to be a pilot. Reaper forces rounded up the remaining colonists and held them prisoner at a farmhouse. Hilary tried to free them, but the captives had been indoctrinated and turned on her, forcing T'Goni to kill them to stop them becoming husks or indoctrinated infiltrators. She was also forced to kill Hilary, whose whimpering after she broke her leg threatened to reveal their hiding place.

Several evacuation ships carrying mostly children, sent off-world by their parents before the attack, escaped Tiptree and reached salarian colonies. Tiptree was where Joker's father and his 15-year-old sister lived when the Reapers invaded; writer Patrick Weekes confirmed that the Hilary whom T'Goni killed was Joker's younger sister. The colony's only in-game appearance is a hologram Joker pulls up in Mass Effect 3, which also shows a large moon around the planet. The name may reference science fiction author Alice Bradley Sheldon, who wrote as James Tiptree Jr.
