Cerberus Weapon and Armor was a Mass Effect 2 downloadable content pack released on February 9, 2010, containing one armor set and one weapon. The Cerberus Assault Armor was designed around shields, thick armor plating and auxiliary ammo packs, and was issued to shock troopers expected to turn the tide of battle against forces that would decimate normal soldiers. The M-22 Eviscerator shotgun came as standard issue, lethal at close range and favored by Cerberus operatives who liked to fight up close. The pack required download or purchase of the Cerberus Network.
