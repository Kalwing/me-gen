The Skyllian Blitz was a major assault on the human colony of Elysium in 2176 CE. A large band of pirates, slavers, and batarian warlords, partly funded by batarian financiers, drove into the Skyllian Verge intending to destroy Elysium. The attack was partly a batarian reprisal for humanity's expansion in the Verge and partly a consequence of tensions from the Alliance's pirate suppression campaigns. The decision to finally launch it was instigated by one man, Elanos Haliat, who intended to use the Blitz's prestige to cement himself as leader of the Terminus Systems' many pirate bands.

The people of Elysium and the Alliance rallied to defend the colony. Small ground teams of Alliance Marines on leave and Elysium civilians held off the pirates on the surface while the Alliance Navy, including the SSV Agincourt, engaged enemy vessels overhead. Navigator Pressly claimed the pirate ships were no match for the Alliance and that he could not keep count of how many the Agincourt destroyed. The ground fighting was far harder, but the defenders held out for several hours until reinforcements arrived, at which point the surviving enemy ships fled.

The aftermath led two years later to a major Alliance operation on Torfan to destroy batarian pirate bases. The successful defense of Elysium is often cited as a testament to Alliance military training and adaptability. Some batarians remained resentful of the Blitz's failure, calling it just retribution for humanity's actions against them.

Commander Shepard's connection to the Blitz depends on background. A War Hero Shepard rallied the colonists, single-handedly held off enemy forces when they broke through the defenses, sealed the breach, was awarded the Star of Terra, and became widely regarded as a hero. A Ruthless Shepard instead took part in the retaliatory attack on Torfan. In a conversation with Joker during Mass Effect 3, it is noted that Shepard was present during the Skyllian Blitz regardless of background, even if given no special commendation.
