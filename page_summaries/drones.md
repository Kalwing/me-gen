Drones were small, lightly-armed combat robots equipped with very small mass effect fields for levitation, basic VI programming allowing battlefield adaptation, and a small amount of shielding. They were typically deployed in wings as cannon fodder to create distractions so marines could operate safely; soldiers called friendly drones "flocks" and enemy drones "swarms." Drones were a key element of the Systems Alliance military initiative and were also used extensively by the geth.

In the first Mass Effect, Alliance drones encountered on Luna during the UNC: Rogue VI assignment included Advanced Assault Drones (shielded, pistol-grade weaponry) and Advanced Rocket Drones (slow-recharging rocket launchers). Other Alliance types were the Surveillance Drone (a spy unit, one shot down by the geth), the Message Drone (used to alert the Alliance to the attack on Shanxi), and Defense Drones stored on Asteroid X57, some reprogrammed as hostile by batarian extremists. Geth drones, whose individual sentience was unknown, included Geth Recon Drones (weak shields, biotic-resistant, cloak-capable; recon drones killed Richard L. Jenkins on Eden Prime), Geth Assault Drones (black armor, pulse weapons, seen on Feros and Virmire), Geth Rocket Drones, and Geth Repair Drones that healed larger geth units. "Geth Flyers" was a catch-all term used during the Virmire mission.

In Mass Effect 2, Assault Drones and Rocket Drones defended Freedom's Progress, Cerberus installations on Aite, and mercenary positions, while Geth Recon Drones appeared on Haestrom during Tali's recruitment mission. Mass Effect 3 featured the Combat Drone, Combat Drone (enemy), Defense Drone, Disruption Drone, Geth Bomber and Info Drone.
