Geth Turrets were an enemy unit in Mass Effect 3, deployed by Geth Primes to provide fire support on the battlefield. They functioned like the Sentry Turret or Geth Turret powers rather than the Cerberus Combat Engineer's turret. A single Prime could keep up to two turrets active at once and could summon them even through intervening terrain, catching enemies in unavoidable crossfire. The turrets had a moderate rate of fire and did moderate damage, but left alive for long they could kill an unwary player. Defensively they had only a weak layer of regenerating shields and no other protection, and they had a timed lifespan, deactivating on their own and self-destructing shortly after the Prime that summoned them was destroyed.

Recommended tactics were to destroy the turrets quickly since their damage accumulated fast, especially when multiple enemies were attacking. Powerful weapons or high-level tech powers could remove them in one hit; an un-upgraded Energy Drain instantly destroyed one on lower difficulties, and Overload with the chain evolution used on a Prime would clear its turrets and combat drones. Sabotage could hack a turret, though it rarely survived long. Tactical Scan killed turrets instead of applying its normal effect, while Stasis had no effect despite the turrets not being armored. When a Geth Prime extended its arm, players were advised to scan their surroundings for flanking turrets, which on some maps were placed atop the player's cover. Like Swarmers and other summoned units, Geth Turrets did not count as true enemies for "on kill" power bonuses and could persist past the end of a multiplayer round until their Prime died.
