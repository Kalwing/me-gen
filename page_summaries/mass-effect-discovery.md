Short reference page (under ~150 words of prose). Mass Effect: Discovery is a four-issue comic series set around the events of Mass Effect: Andromeda, following turian Tiran Kandros during his infiltration of the Andromeda Initiative. The first issue was released May 24, 2017 and the last on October 25, 2017, with all issues collected into a trade paperback on January 17, 2018. Four variant covers were made in classic sci-fi pulp style by artist Kate Niemczyk, with colors by Michael Atiyeh.
