Citadel: Leviathan I was the second mission of the Leviathan DLC for Mass Effect 3, following automatically from Citadel: Dr. Bryson after that mission's cutscenes. Before his death, Dr. Bryson had asked for help locating Leviathan, a mysterious entity reputed to have killed a Reaper, and this stage had Commander Shepard search Bryson's lab on the Citadel for clues.

Investigating objects around the lab and the upstairs bedroom supplied search filters for a galaxy-map search program, first accessed through an audio log on a table next to EDI. A full search turned up six filters: Blackout Crimes, Creature Sightings, Rachni Activity, Locations with Element Zero, Prothean Activities, and Reaper Activities. Only one filter was needed for Shepard to leave and begin investigating candidate sites, but selecting the Creature Sightings, Locations with Element Zero, and Reaper Activities filters together while leaving the other three disabled pinpointed the correct location and, in the original release, earned the "Conspiracy Theorist" achievement, which was absent from Legendary Edition. Each activated filter gave 60 XP. The lab also held four weapon mods: the Pistol Cranial Trauma System, the SMG High-Velocity Barrel, the Pistol Ultralight Materials mod, and the SMG Recoil System.

A datapad in the lab, dated August 17, 2185, compared Leviathan to the Thorian discovered on Feros. Like the Thorian, which evolved to use organic tools rather than mass relays or Prothean technology and used pain conditioning to control its victims, Leviathan was thought to have survived undetected for so long by doing something similar, described by analogy to Reaper indoctrination. With the investigation complete, Shepard left the lab to begin the next mission, Leviathan: Find Garneau.
