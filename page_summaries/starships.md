This codex-style overview defined starships as spacecraft capable of interstellar travel using thrusters and FTL drives, and described the classification of military vessels. Large warships fell into four extant weights. Frigates were light escort and scouting vessels, able to land on planets, carrying a squad of marines and extensive GARDIAN systems; their most important role was reconnaissance, since luminal sensors always lag FTL attackers, so fleets surrounded themselves with spheres of scouting frigates. In fleet combat frigates fought in "wolf pack" flotillas of four to six, circle-strafing targets whose barriers had been dropped by fighter-launched disruptor torpedoes. Destroyers were an obsolete light escort class, fielded by the Alliance as of 2157 but replaced by frigates by the 2180s and thereafter seen mostly as second-hand private vessels such as Ygara Menoris's Audacity.

Cruisers were middleweight combatants, faster than dreadnoughts and more heavily armed than frigates, serving as the standard patrol unit and leading frigate flotillas; turian and human cruisers each carried about 300 crew. Carriers were dreadnought-sized ships carrying large fighter complements; humans, recent graduates from surface to space combat, were the first to build ships using fighters as main armament. Carriers stayed clear of battle, as a single torpedo through the flight deck could gut one. Dreadnoughts were kilometer-long capital ships whose power lay in the length of their main gun; an 800-meter mass accelerator could fire a 20-kilogram slug at 4025 km/s every two seconds, each slug carrying roughly 38 kilotons of energy. The Treaty of Farixen capped dreadnought counts by navy, with the turians permitted the most.

Small vessels included one-man fighters and interceptors, shuttles (some FTL-capable, such as the UT-47 Kodiak), and ten-man corvettes for combat and survey work. The page also listed starship attributes such as ablative and Silaris armor, disruptor torpedoes, kinetic and cyclonic barriers, mass accelerator and Thanix weapons, GARDIAN systems, sensors and thrusters.
