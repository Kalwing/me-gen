This page collects cut and non-functional dialogue from the first Mass Effect covering the Virmire missions and their aftermath, reconstructed from leftover files. It is explicitly non-canon. Content includes squad remarks about disabling the base's artillery with the Mako, long one-on-one crew conversations (identical to Normandy talks) held in the salarian camp, cut store/topic dialogue with Rentola, a full conversation with krogan scientist Doctor Droyas before he turns hostile, guard-the-bomb lines for whichever of Kaidan or Ashley is left behind, and an entire cut branch in which Shepard rescues both Kaidan and Ashley.

Ashley, cut crew talk on the timing of the assault: "Don't know what I think about us attacking today, of all days. Kind of an ill omen." She explains it is Armistice Day, the anniversary of the end of the First Contact War, which her family always marks.

Kaidan, cut crew talk: "I've seen what cutting corners can do to someone. And I'd hate to have that happen to you, Shepard."

Doctor Droyas, cut confrontation in Saren's labs: "Are you blind? We have a cure for the genophage! Saren has led us through the darkness to a new dawn! You will not destroy the work we have started!"

Saren, cut line after the bomb is set: "This facility was more than reinforcement. It was inoculation! So be it. You may have taken the day. But this is not over. Goodbye, Shepard. You have ensured we will meet again."

Kaidan or Ashley, cut guard-the-bomb line: "Go, Commander. I'll be fine here."

Ashley, cut "rescued both" debriefing: "If they don't court-martial you. You endangered the entire mission. Why didn't you just leave one of us behind?"

Kirrahe, cut "rescued both" cargo-hold talk: "Perhaps we will have the opportunity to work together again some day."
