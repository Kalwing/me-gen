The page compiled the in-game Codex entries on the three Council species. The asari, from Thessia, were the first species to discover the Citadel and proposed the Citadel Council when the salarians arrived; they served as the Council's mediators and centrists. An all-female race, they reproduced by attuning their nervous systems to a partner of any gender or species, could live over 1,000 years, and passed through Maiden, Matron, and Matriarch stages. Their conscious control of nerve impulses made them naturally strong biotics. Thessia was long organized as republican city-states governed by an inclusive "electronic democracy" with no politicians, deferring to Matriarchs in a crisis. The Justicar Order acted as extra-judicial executioners bound by the Oaths of Subsumation. Asari huntresses fought as small special-operations units rather than a conventional army. The mainstream religion was the pantheistic siari ("All is one"); an older monotheism worshipped the goddess Athame. Ardat-Yakshi were asari with a genetic disorder that made melding lethal to partners.

The salarians were the second species to join the Council, warm-blooded amphibians with a hyperactive metabolism, short lifespans (rarely over 40), photographic memories, and a talent for research and espionage. They were haplo-diploid egg-layers, roughly 90 percent male, with cloistered females called Dalatrasses who dominated politics; the Salarian Union functioned like medieval European noble houses. The salarians uplifted the krogan for the Rachni Wars and later developed the genophage. Their intelligence service, the Special Tasks Group, ran covert cells; the earlier assassination team called the League of One vanished after being declassified and reportedly killed the government's inner cabinet.

The turians, from Palaven, were invited to the Council about 1,200 years ago as galactic peacekeepers and provided most of its military forces. Palaven's weak magnetic field gave its life a metallic skin, and turians were dextro-amino-acid based like quarians. Their society was a hierarchical meritocracy of 27 citizenship tiers topped by Primarchs, valuing personal accountability and honor, with the military serving as an all-encompassing public-works body. They took the volus as a client race. Turian religion held that groups and places had inspirational "spirits." The Unification War, a turian civil war contemporaneous with the Council's founding, ended with the Hierarchy reasserting control; turians still wore colony facial markings, and "barefaced" became slang for the untrustworthy and for politicians. Lingering animosity with humans dated to the 2157 First Contact War, called the Relay 314 Incident by turians.
