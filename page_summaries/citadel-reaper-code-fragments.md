Citadel: Reaper Code Fragments was a Mass Effect 3 mission acquired after Priority: The Citadel II and Rannoch: Geth Fighter Squadrons. At the Embassy Offices in the Citadel Embassies, near the elevator, an Asari War Strategist spoke over comms about her inability to predict Reaper movements, wishing for code samples from Sovereign to devise counter-strategies. Overhearing her added the mission to the journal. Because Shepard began in Armando-Owen Bailey's nearby office after Priority: The Citadel II, the War Strategist's wide trigger radius made the mission almost unavoidable.

The objective was to obtain a Reaper code fragment and deliver it to her. The fragment was found near the first infected data cluster in the geth consensus during the Rannoch missions, at an orange-glowing segment that the Geth Combat Software could not destroy. It could alternatively be purchased from the Spectre Requisitions terminal if missed, and could be acquired from the geth consensus before resolving the main events at Rannoch.

Delivering the fragment to the Asari War Strategist gave 5 Reputation, 30 experience, and 1,000 credits, and updated the Asari Second Fleet War Asset. If the fragment was bought from Spectre Requisitions, the player sometimes had to leave and re-enter the Embassies level before giving it to her. Overhearing her again had her recommend redirecting forces to new coordinates based on the intel. Saving far from her before the mission notification and reloading could temporarily bypass her trigger, allowing acquisition during the trip to the geth consensus.
