The M-300 Claymore Heavy Shotgun appeared in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, likely named after the two-handed Scottish sword. In Mass Effect 2 it was a very rare krogan shotgun of human design used only by krogan, because the kickback of a single shot had enough force to break a human's arm. Protected by Fabrication Rights Management technology, it was nearly impossible to reproduce and prohibitively expensive. It dealt very high short-range damage, was effective against armor, shields, and biotic barriers, and held only one round per thermal clip.

In Mass Effect 2 it could be obtained during the Collector Ship mission by a Soldier or Vanguard, or researched for Grunt after recruiting him; only Grunt could wield it among squadmates, and he dealt 40% less damage with it. Its single-shot magazine made it a high-risk, high-reward weapon, greatly aided by reload-cancelling (cutting the delay between shots from about 2.4 to 1.4 seconds) and by Vanguard Charge or Soldier Adrenaline Rush for guaranteed point-blank kills.

In Mass Effect 3 the manufacturer reworked its kinetic dampening system and relaunched it at a lower price without reducing stopping power; despite the ME2 lore, turians, asari such as Captain Enyala, and Shepard were shown using it. It was found during Attican Traverse: Krogan Team and sold at Cipritine Armory; multiplayer rated it rare. It had the single most damaging shot of any weapon in the game, beating even the Javelin, but that damage was split among eight pellets in a widening X-spread, and it was among the heaviest weapons with only one shot before reloading. Stasis, Adrenaline Rush, Tactical Cloak, and shield-stripping powers greatly improved it. On the weapon's side the name reads backwards as "300-M," resembling "BOOM."
