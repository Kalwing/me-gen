Cerberus Commandos were highly trained shock troopers used by Cerberus to perform operations and defend locations in the first Mass Effect. They formed a key part of the defense of Cerberus installations on Binthu and Nepheron, facilities conducting research on organisms such as Rachni Workers, Rachni Soldiers and Thorian Creepers.

Commandos were armed with assault rifles and shotguns and were deadly at close range. Rather than hold cover, they frequently charged to get around their enemy's cover and made sweeping, flanking attacks. They had relatively high health as well as shields, though powerful attacks and well-used cover were usually enough to weaken and neutralize them. Because they were fairly accurate and came in groups, crowd-control talents were useful against them; alternatively, on Binthu, releasing the captive test subjects could turn the fight into a three-way battle that drew fire away from the player.
