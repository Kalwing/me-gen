This gameplay-focused page covered varren as combat opponents. In Mass Effect, varren were encountered only on Feros, where colonists killed them for meat; a lair could be cleared beneath Zhu's Hope and another group infested the ExoGeni headquarters garage, attacking en masse after the encounter with Lizbeth Baynham. Their bites ignored shields and damaged health directly, they attacked in large numbers, and biotic powers such as Throw, Lift, and especially Singularity, along with High Explosive Rounds and cryo grenades, were the recommended counters.

In Mass Effect 2, varren infested numerous worlds Shepard visited in 2185, appearing feral or as attack animals aiding the Blood Pack. They had a powerful close-range bite, moderate health, and low armor on higher difficulties. Because they were among the fastest enemies in the game and could outrun many power projectiles, instantaneous powers like Slam, Reave, or Neural Shock were advised, along with keeping them at distance. Reave and Incinerate stripped armor and stunned them; shotguns or heavy pistols with anti-armor ammo (the M-300 Claymore and Geth Plasma Shotgun could one-shot them even on Insanity), Squad Cryo Ammo, and heavy weapons such as the M-622 Avalanche and Arc Projector were effective against groups. Varren were not panicked by Incendiary Ammo, largely ignored Combat Drones, and did not stagger from Shepard's melee, making melee a last resort.
