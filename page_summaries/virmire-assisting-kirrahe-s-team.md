Virmire: Assisting Kirrahe's Team was an assignment in Mass Effect that ran concurrently with the mission Virmire: Assault. While Captain Kirrahe's salarian STG force attacked Saren's base head-on to divert the geth, Kirrahe asked Commander Shepard, infiltrating from the rear, to watch for opportunities to frustrate and slow the geth defenses. The assignment was gained automatically during Kirrahe's comm check.

Kirrahe divided his men into three fireteams named Jaëto, Mannovai, and Aegohr, after the salarians' first three colony worlds. There were four distinct actions Shepard could take, which had to be completed in the order they occurred: disrupting geth communications at a comms tower to protect team Mannovai, destroying a satellite uplink to send the geth's air support off to recharge, destroying geth flyers hovering over fuel tanks near the refueling platform, and, on entering the base, choosing whether to disable the base alarms or trigger them on the far side to send enemies toward Kirrahe's teams. Each action awarded morality points, and radio chatter from Ashley or Kaidan (whichever was sent with Kirrahe) or from Kirrahe himself announced the results. Most outcomes were flavor only, but Captain Kirrahe's survival depended entirely on the destruction of the geth flyers; if Shepard ignored them, Kirrahe died and Ashley or Kaidan took over comm duties, with the journal renaming the assignment "Virmire: Captain Kirrahe is Dead."

If Kirrahe survived, he appeared aboard the Normandy with the surviving salarians and told Shepard he might see them again one day. In Mass Effect 2, Mordin Solus, who had served under Kirrahe in the STG, either spoke fondly of him and his "Hold the Line" speech or briefly eulogized him. In Mass Effect 3, a surviving Kirrahe, now a Major, aided Shepard on Sur'Kesh and later, on the Citadel, saved the salarian Councilor from Kai Leng at the cost of his own life (this Citadel appearance required Thane Krios to have died in Mass Effect 2 or never to have been spoken to). If Wrex survived Virmire but later died due to a sabotaged genophage cure, Kirrahe and his STG unit fought in London in place of Wrex's krogan; if Kirrahe died, Colonel Vaykom led the salarian contingent there instead.
