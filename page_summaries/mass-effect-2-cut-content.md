This page is an index of content made for Mass Effect 2 that was not implemented in the final version or is otherwise inaccessible, none of which is considered canon. Compared with Mass Effect and Mass Effect 3, the known cut content is less extensive. A large portion consists of unusable squadmate reactions during missions that take place before those squadmates can be recruited, a consequence of a late-development change to recruitment mission availability. Additional material relates to earlier development stages of Kasumi's and Zaeed's recruitment and loyalty missions and to several DLC packs.

For main-plot missions, the page notes alternate dialogues in Prologue: Awakening; a first vorcha appearance and unused Illusive Man debriefing lines at Freedom's Progress; a playable colonist Lilith segment and Tali reactions on Horizon; Legion reactions on the Collector Ship; a cut boss fight with an indoctrinated Dr. Chandana and an option to leave Legion on the Reaper in Reaper IFF; and unused EDI, Harbinger, and squadmate voicelines in the final battle. Recruitment cut content includes Kasumi originally being recruited on Illium with a rooftop gunship battle, and Zaeed originally having a full recruitment mission on Korlus with a Mako section. Loyalty mission cut content includes Collectors appearing in Jack's mission, a savable Niket in Miranda's mission, Legion testifying at Tali's trial, and an alternate Zaeed mission called "Zaeed: Headliner" set on Tuchanka.

The page also lists cut minor assignments (Citadel: Mysterious Stranger, Illium: Drunk Asari, Omega: Beggar Bowl, Tuchanka: Female Quest), DLC cut content, and miscellaneous items: a planned early Mako, a cut Mordin-Grunt confrontation, a pet robot "Sparky" (later realized as KEI-9 in Mass Effect 3), a Hammerhead training simulation, numerous cut Codex entries (mostly preserved on the N7 HQ website), cut star clusters including the Perseus Veil and Annos Basin, and many unheard news bulletins, some restored in Legendary Edition. It links to numerous detailed sub-pages of cut voicelines.
