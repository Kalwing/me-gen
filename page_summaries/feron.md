Feron was a drell information trafficker who described himself as "an information trader. Not a slaver." In Mass Effect: Redemption, set after the destruction of the Normandy SR-1, Liara T'Soni hired him to help find Commander Shepard; he reported that Shepard was neither dead nor alive. Ambushed by Blue Suns sent by the Shadow Broker to delay Liara, the pair were saved by an unknown sniper, then encountered Cerberus agents, including Miranda Lawson, and were brought to the Illusive Man, who revealed the Shadow Broker was working with the Collectors. Feron admitted he had been ordered to lead Liara away from Shepard but chose to help her instead.

Feron took Liara to Afterlife on Omega, where Aria T'Loak resided, and learned where the Blue Suns would bring Shepard's body. He spotted the Broker's enforcer Tazzik but missed his ship with a turret, letting Tazzik flee Omega with the body. Feron revealed he had never stopped working for the Shadow Broker, which they could exploit to track Tazzik. The two infiltrated the Broker's base on Alingon, with Feron passing Liara off as his prisoner; he downloaded the Broker's dealings while Liara distracted a hologram of the Broker. Feron confessed Cerberus had recruited him and that he and the Illusive Man had planned for Feron to snatch the body. When a Collector exposed him as a traitor, Feron held off Tazzik so Liara could escape, and was captured.

In Mass Effect 2, Liara mentioned a "friend" lost during her search. During Lair of the Shadow Broker, Liara, Shepard, and Cerberus intel led to the Shadow Broker Base on Hagalaz, where Feron was found being tortured on a device that could not be removed without killing him. Liara and Shepard killed the Shadow Broker; Liara took over the role, and Feron chose to stay and assist her, mentally scarred and using his drell memory recall to survive the torture. In Mass Effect 3, if Shepard helped Liara take the Broker ship, Feron helped evacuate it during a Cerberus attack, became one of Liara's agents controlling Terminus operations (to Tazzik's dismay), and emailed Shepard offering information via Glyph. If Shepard did not help overthrow the Broker, Liara hired mercenaries to storm the lair and Feron did not survive.
