Mass Effect 3: Extended Cut was a free DLC pack for Mass Effect 3, released June 26, 2012 for all platforms (July 4 for PlayStation 3 in Europe) and built into the Wii U version. After many players complained the original ending was confusing and lacked closure, BioWare co-founder Ray Muzyka announced new content, and BioWare re-prioritized its post-release DLC schedule to make it. The pack added new cinematics and epilogue scenes expanding the original ending, varying by the player's choices and Effective Military Strength (EMS).

New cutscenes showed Shepard's squadmates being incinerated by Harbinger's beam or evacuated by the Normandy depending on EMS; Admiral Steven Hackett learning someone reached the beam; Shepard launched into the Citadel tunnel; the Crucible docking under attack at low EMS; a dead character replacing Joker in the final flashback, ending on the actual love interest; Hackett ordering the fleets to evacuate Earth's orbit, with a surviving squadmate convincing Joker to leave so the Normandy is caught in the energy wave in transit; two stranded soldiers on Earth affected differently by each ending; the Charon Relay's core breaking up while the relay survives and relays are later repaired (though low-EMS Destroy and Control still show relays exploding); species and crew aftermaths; and Shepard's name placed on the Normandy memorial wall beside Anderson's, left off in high-EMS Destroy to keep Shepard's fate ambiguous alongside the shot of Shepard breathing in the rubble.

Dialogue changes largely rewrote the Catalyst's lines, let Shepard investigate Reaper origins and the Crucible's abilities, and added a fourth option: refusing to activate the Crucible (via dialogue or by shooting the Catalyst), dooming the galaxy to another Reaper purge and triggering scenes of a Liara time capsule and an alternate post-credits Stargazer scene in which a later cycle achieves peace using the previous cycle's records. Fixes lowered the EMS needed for all endings from 4,000 to 3,100, restored ambient Normandy dialogue between Ken/Gabby and Garrus/Tali, and replaced the post-credits DLC advert with a thank-you message. EA later confirmed it would never charge for or remove the content.
