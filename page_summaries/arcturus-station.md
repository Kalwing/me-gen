Arcturus Station was a five-kilometer-diameter Stanford Torus-type space station located at the trailing L5 Lagrangian point of the gas giant Themis in the Arcturus system. Construction began in 2151 and concluded in 2162, using nearby metal-rich asteroids, and the station was inaugurated in 2156. Since the First Contact War it served as the military and political headquarters of the Systems Alliance, housing the Alliance parliament and the nerve center of the military armada. It held a permanent population of 45,000, guarded the mass relay leading to Earth, sat at the nexus of several mass relays, and functioned as a shipyard where the dreadnought SSV Aconcagua was built. Admiral Hackett commanded the Fifth Fleet from Arcturus. Joker grew up there before joining the Academy.

In Mass Effect: Evolution, after the First Contact War, General Desolas Arterius arrived to exchange prisoners with General Williams; Jack Harper and Eva Core were traded for turian soldiers, examined in a medical bay, and given Williams' personal shuttle to pursue Desolas. In Mass Effect: He Who Laughs Best, in 2183 the station hosted the first test flight of the SSV Normandy, attended by captains David Anderson and Dillard and Hierarchy General Invectus. Jeff "Joker" Moreau trapped the Normandy's original pilot on the station, locked himself in the cockpit, hijacked the ship, evaded scrambled fighters, and returned it undamaged, prompting Invectus to pressure the Alliance into assigning Joker as the Normandy's pilot.

In Mass Effect 3, Arcturus Station was the site of a battle between the Alliance's Second, Third, and Fifth Fleets and Reaper forces heading toward Sol. As the Alliance held off roughly a dozen Reaper capital ships, the bulk of the Reaper fleet rushed through the mass relay. Admiral Steven Hackett, unable to win, abandoned the station and sacrificed the Second Fleet so the Third and Fifth Fleets could escape. The Reapers later destroyed Arcturus Station, fusing its fragments into wreckage that became a grave for 45,000 people. According to Donnel Udina, the entire Alliance Parliament and Prime Minister Amul Shastri were aboard and killed. Intelligence from the Battle of Arcturus could be salvaged from the remains.
