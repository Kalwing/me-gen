Roth's Decoy was an unnamed Cerberus operative who posed as Roth, a major financier of Cerberus, while the real Roth went into hiding to avoid assassination. In 2161 CE the decoy took up residence in Roth's quarters aboard an asteroid-mining facility orbiting the gas giant Themis, while Roth left ostensibly for Earth. The next day a female assassin infiltrated the building and, believing the decoy was Roth, forced him at knifepoint to transfer a large sum of money to the banker Barla Von. The decoy deduced she was sent by the Shadow Broker and tricked her into confirming it, then knocked her into a window, broke free, and drew a bladed claw-like hand weapon.

He revealed his role as a decoy and asked whether she thought the Shadow Broker was the only one with such agents. They fought; the assassin slashed his back and disarmed him, but he drew a gun and shot her in the torso. As he closed in, he asked why the Broker wanted Roth dead when Roth had not provoked him. She said Roth funded a pro-human terrorist organization, Cerberus, citing turians killed on a freighter Cerberus had blown up; the decoy claimed those turians were slavers and that protecting humanity was not terrorism, adding that at least Cerberus stood for something. Distracted at the last second by a young boy calling for his "mommy," the decoy was struck by the assassin's thrown knife in the chest. He refused to reveal the true Roth's location, assuming she would not kill him in front of her son, but she said the boy was not hers. He then admitted Roth had left for Earth. The assassin killed him with a kick to the head and left his body in Roth's quarters. The story is drawn from Mass Effect: Foundation.
