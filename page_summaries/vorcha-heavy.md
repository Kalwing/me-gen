The Vorcha Heavy was an enemy type in Mass Effect 2, a rocket launcher-wielding soldier mechanically identical to the Blood Pack Boom-Squad. Unlike Boom-Squads, Heavies were not part of the Blood Pack, wore black garb rather than red, and displayed no gang insignia. They were independent elements seeking to exploit the opportunity created by the Collector plague, which infected all races on Omega except vorcha and humans.

In 2185, a few Vorcha Heavies supported Blood Pack troops in securing Omega's Gozu District and preventing any cure from reaching the population. They were defeated by Commander Shepard, who dispersed a cure developed by Mordin Solus through the district's ventilation systems.

Their standard armament was the ML-77 Missile Launcher, which they fired once or twice in rapid succession before ducking back into cover. Like most vorcha they wore light armor and relied on natural regeneration, taking cover quickly and popping up briefly to fire missiles. They had only health on normal difficulty but gained an armor bar on higher difficulties. Tactics effective against Boom-Squads applied equally to Vorcha Heavies.
