Sovereign, which identified itself to the geth as Nazara, was a Reaper: a fully sentient, immensely powerful AI dreadnought roughly two kilometers long, crewed by geth and krogan and serving as the flagship of the rogue Spectre Saren Arterius. Initially believed to be a geth construct or a Prothean relic, it was in fact a vanguard left behind by the Reapers to trigger their return from dark space. For 50,000 years it had lain in near-constant hibernation, waking periodically to assess organic civilization. Its duty was to signal the keepers on the Citadel to open the Citadel relay to dark space and usher the Reapers through. Prothean sabotage had broken that mechanism, forcing Sovereign to work for centuries through organic agents and indoctrination, a subtle mind-controlling field, to uncover what had gone wrong.

Around 2162 a survey team working for the batarian entrepreneur Edan Had'dah discovered Sovereign orbiting an uncharted world near the Perseus Veil. Had'dah recruited the defector AI scientist Dr. Shu Qian; both were gradually indoctrinated. Investigating an attack on the Sidon research facility, Saren Arterius seized their research, killed Qian and Had'dah, and sought Sovereign himself, becoming its "prophet" to the geth while falling under its indoctrination. Sovereign and Saren brought a geth army to Eden Prime in 2183 to seize a Prothean Beacon that might reveal the Conduit; the assault devastated the colony and drew the SSV Normandy. On Virmire Sovereign spoke directly to Shepard, then implanted Saren with control cybernetics after the encounter shook his loyalty. Saren found the Conduit on Ilos, reached the Citadel, and Sovereign led a geth armada against the Citadel Fleet, attaching itself to the Citadel Tower to open the relay manually. After Saren's death Shepard used Vigil's data file to block Sovereign's control; Sovereign reanimated Saren's corpse as an avatar, but when it was destroyed Sovereign's shields failed and the Alliance Fifth Fleet, with a killing shot from the Normandy, destroyed it, averting the Reaper invasion.

Representative lines:
- Sovereign, to Shepard on Virmire: "We impose order on the chaos of organic evolution. You exist because we allow it, and you will end because we demand it."
- Sovereign, of the Citadel: "This station is mine."
- Sovereign, in Saren's lab: "We are each a nation." / "We are legion."

Turian researchers reverse-engineered Sovereign's molten-metal main gun into the Thanix Cannon. Cerberus recovered fragments as anti-Reaper algorithms for EDI. In Mass Effect 3, Dr. Garret Bryson kept a shielded Sovereign fragment for Task Force Aurora's Leviathan research. Sovereign (or a Reaper like it) is implied to have indoctrinated the rachni queens, causing the Rachni Wars.
