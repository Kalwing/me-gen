This archive collected Cerberus Daily News reports from February 2185. The Citadel Council was accused by the Vol Protectorate of manipulating galactic economic data by withholding its sources; three simultaneous break-ins at the Council's Ministry of Finance leaked the reports onto the extranet, and asari Spectre Tela Vasir was assigned the case, later arresting six members of a group called TruthHax while four others were found shot dead. A running story followed the extrasolar comet CR1331 Kingu and the hanar colony world Belan: initially declared harmless by the ISAB, the comet instead passed close enough that its gravity triggered planet-wide tsunamis, drowning non-hanar citizens, collapsing cities, and prompting the Council to declare Belan a disaster zone; the official death toll reached 110,000 with estimates running to 3.5 million, and relief efforts and a star-studded telethon followed amid criticism of the government response. Socialite Aish Ashland was caught substituting her estranged fraternal twin Mina Ashland's DNA at the Hollywood Bank of Fame, calling it an apology for years of mistreatment. Other reports covered Super Bowl CCXIX (New York Giants over Beijing Dragons, 24-19, featuring krogan quarterback Bragus Thul); the welcome of the avian raloi of planet Turvess into Citadel space, marred by a krogan sports brawl and an H7N7 flu outbreak; the arrest of turian lawmaker Ellis Valterus for funding a krogan clan's dreadnought reconstruction; teenager Jorge Asensio, who stole a Mantis spaceplane, outran Alliance fighters, and froze to death after bailing out over Antarctica; and the escape of Cerberus operative Skye Turnick from custody on Trident in a paramilitary gunship raid, sparking a manhunt led by Special Agent Angela Rhow. Coverage of the "Nekyia Corridor" production noted a Super Bowl trailer and lead actor Nathan Gold being struck by lightning during filming, while turian separatists on Taetrus claimed responsibility for a hijacked commercial cruiser that crashed into a farming village.
