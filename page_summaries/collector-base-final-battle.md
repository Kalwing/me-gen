Collector Base: Final Battle was the climactic mission of Mass Effect 2, triggered after Collector Base: The Long Walk as part of Stop the Collectors: Defeat the Collectors, with no manual saving allowed and autosaves at each stage. Beforehand, Commander Shepard selected a squad to continue on and assigned the remaining team members to hold the line against the Collectors; the game calculated a collective defensive value for that group, and if it was too low, specific squadmates were designated to die in a fixed order beginning with any non-loyal members, with Mordin taking priority if present. Shepard's platform advanced through six fly-in platforms carrying Collector Drones, Guardians, Assassins, Abominations, and Scions, with Harbinger repeatedly assuming direct control, then entered the central chamber where a Human-Reaper larva was introduced. Shepard destroyed the larva's four feeding tubes, dropping it into the chasm, then contacted the ground team, ordered them to fall back to the Normandy, and told Joker to prepare the ship for departure.

As Shepard opened the platform's core to overload the reactor, the Illusive Man interrupted and asked Shepard to instead trigger a radiation pulse that would kill the Collectors but preserve the facility against the Reapers; destroying it was a Paragon action and preserving it a Renegade action. After Shepard's choice, the Human-Reaper reactivated and attacked, and was destroyed by fire on its eyes, chest, and mouth while Collector reinforcements flew in, one possessed by Harbinger. The Normandy then raced to outrun the base's end: a massive explosion if destroyed, or a blue radiation blast that killed all organic matter but left the base intact if preserved. Closing scenes varied with who survived the suicide mission, with surviving companions pulling Shepard aboard or, if Shepard was alone, Joker failing to save the Commander, who fell to his or her death. Afterward Shepard, or Joker if Shepard died, met the Illusive Man, who was furious if the base was destroyed and complimentary if it was preserved. A final scene showed the Normandy in space with the crew mourning any dead or repairing the ship, and Joker handing Shepard a datapad of Reaper schematics, before a fleet of hundreds or thousands of Reapers was revealed advancing on the Milky Way. Preserving the facility added a Reaper Brain to Shepard's Mass Effect 3 War Assets and favored the Control ending, while destroying it added a Reaper Heart and facilitated Destroy; each choice could also affect which final choices were available at the end of Mass Effect 3. Surviving squadmates could return or become War Assets in Mass Effect 3, and the death of either Legion or Tali made a peaceful resolution of the geth-quarian war impossible. Cut content included many unused EDI voicelines for the mission and a scrapped option to take control of enemy turrets during the final boss fight, during which Harbinger would have used different taunts.
