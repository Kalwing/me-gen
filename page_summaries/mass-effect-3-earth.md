Short DLC page (under 150 words of prose). Mass Effect 3: Earth was a free multiplayer DLC pack for Mass Effect 3, released July 17, 2012 on Xbox 360 and PC worldwide and on PlayStation 3 in North America (July 18 in Europe); it was included with the Wii U Special Edition. It added three Earth-based Firebase maps (Rio, Vancouver, London), six N7 multiplayer characters (Fury Adept, Destroyer Soldier, Demolisher Engineer, Paladin Sentinel, Shadow Infiltrator, Slayer Vanguard), three weapons (Acolyte Pistol, N7 Piranha Shotgun, N7 Typhoon Assault Rifle), five weapon mods, eleven gear upgrades, a new Combat Drone escort objective occurring on waves 3, 6, and 10, and the Platinum difficulty setting, on which the chosen enemy faction appears at full force immediately and other factions join as the match progresses. Seven of a planned twelve mods were cut for balance and later released with Mass Effect 3: Retaliation.
