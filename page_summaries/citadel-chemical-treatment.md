Citadel: Chemical Treatment was a Mass Effect 3 mission with prerequisites Priority: The Citadel II and N7: Fuel Reactors. At Lab Services in Huerta Memorial Hospital, a human doctor and the salarian Dr. Silon discussed a patient injured by a chemical agent Cerberus had deployed during their attack on the Citadel. Overhearing the conversation activated the mission. It could alternatively be acquired directly while restarting a fuel reactor on Cyone, and an alternative journal entry framed the treatment plan as recovered from a reactor on Cyone and needing someone on the Citadel who could use it.

The plans were on a datapad in the upper-left area at the start of N7: Fuel Reactors, overlooking the radiation below; that parent mission became available only after Priority: Geth Dreadnought. If not taken during the main mission, the plans could be purchased from the Spectre Requisitions terminal for 1,000 credits. Shepard returned to Huerta Memorial and handed the treatment plans to Dr. Silon, who called them experimental but was willing to try. Completion gave 5 reputation, 30 experience, 1,000 credits, and an update to the Alliance First Fleet War Asset.
