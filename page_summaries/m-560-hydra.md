The M-560 Hydra was a heavy weapon in Mass Effect 3, a missile launcher that combined anti-armor and anti-personnel roles. Before its introduction, missile launchers either brought down a single armored target or neutralized multiple unarmored ones; the Hydra did both. It released a barrage of miniature missiles, each guided by an independent homing system that sought exposed enemies, and each missile detonated three shaped charges in sequence: the first overloaded kinetic barriers, the second destroyed armor, and the third exploded inside the target. Its name referenced the multi-headed water serpent of Greek myth.

The Hydra fired eight homing missiles per shot and caused no splash damage to Shepard or squadmates. Like the M-920 Cain and Reaper Blackstar it had to be charged before firing, but the charge could be held indefinitely and was not broken by taking hits. It was found in single-player during Tuchanka: Bomb while defending Lieutenant Victus, during Priority: Thessia, during Despoina: Leviathan near the crashed Kodiak, during Priority: Earth in the Thanix missile area, and twice during Omega: Aria T'Loak. It had no multiplayer availability. It was especially valuable in the final mission against hordes of Brutes, Banshees and a Harvester, though against hardened single targets it was best used only after clearing nearby enemies so missiles would not scatter to other foes.
