Ferris Fields was a human colony in the Terminus Systems, located near New Canton. A brother of the Normandy SR-2's Crewman Hadley lived there. In late 2185 the Collectors abducted the colony's population; an investigation found no sign of the colonists. Aboard the Normandy, Crewman Matthews recognized the colony's importance to his friend Hadley and offered sympathy, while Crewman Goldstein and Crewman Hawthorne confirmed Hadley had a brother there and expressed outrage; engineers Kenneth Donnelly and Gabriella Daniels also voiced outrage but did not mention Hadley's connection. In Mass Effect 3, Steve Cortez revealed that his husband had been stationed at Ferris Fields when the colony was taken.
