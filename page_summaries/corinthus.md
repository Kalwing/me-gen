Short character page. General Corinthus was a turian military commander in Mass Effect 3, responsible for organizing the turian counterattack against the Reapers on Menae, a moon of Palaven. When Commander Shepard arrived to extract Primarch Fedorian, Corinthus reported that the primarch's shuttle had been shot down and Fedorian, a close friend, was presumed dead. Once communication with the Turian Hierarchy was reestablished, Corinthus learned that General Adrien Victus was next in the line of succession and tasked Shepard with retrieving him. In a later e-mail titled "Impressive work," Corinthus admitted that although he had doubted the value of Shepard flying off with one of the turians' best generals, watching turian and krogan fighters jointly take down a Reaper destroyer convinced him Shepard had brokered an alliance many thought impossible, and that Palaven might yet be saved.
