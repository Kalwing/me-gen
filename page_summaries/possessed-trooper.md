The Possessed Trooper was a Collector enemy unit encountered in Mass Effect 3, an enhanced variant of the standard Collector Trooper fielded once Collector forces were reactivated. It was armed with a Collector SMG, the same weapon as a normal Collector Trooper, whose tracers were light gray and very hard to see. Unlike its regular counterpart it lacked grenades, instead creating a Collector Web to snare enemies; it remained immobile and unable to dodge or retaliate while producing the web, and the web could otherwise be ignored unless it blocked a path.

Defensively the Possessed Trooper had moderate health, roughly 50% more than a standard Collector Trooper, plus an added layer of barriers. It took cover during combat, dodged attacks by rolling, could be staggered by gunfire or powers when its barriers were down, and suffered 40% additional damage from headshots. Like other Collectors it struck twice with its melee blades, and a batarian's Blade Armor could stagger it after the first blow to negate the second. In practice it was treated as slightly less dangerous than a normal Collector Trooper because its web could not flush a target out of cover the way grenades could.
