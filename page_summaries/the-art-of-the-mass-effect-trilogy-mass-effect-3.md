This page transcribes the Mass Effect 3 section of the developer commentary in the 2021 edition of The Art of the Mass Effect Trilogy, credited to Casey Hudson, Derek Watts, Chris Hepler, Karin Weekes and Ryan Cormier. It is a behind-the-scenes reference documenting the concept art and design decisions for the trilogy's final game.

Character entries cover the returning love interests Ashley Williams, Kaidan Alenko and Liara T'Soni (given new Alliance colours and heavier gear), the newly introduced James Vega, Admiral Anderson in field fatigues, a re-armored Garrus Vakarian, a matured Jack, EDI's infiltration-unit body (described as the Mass Effect version of Maria from Metropolis), the Child, and Kai Leng, whose early metal-legged design stemmed from Anderson crippling him in the novel Mass Effect: Retribution. The Illusive Man section notes a scrapped plan to turn him into a Reaper creature for the final battle.

Enemy sections describe Cerberus forces (trooper, engineer, sniper, centurion, guardian, Atlas) and Reaper forces built from converted species: the banshee (asari matriarch), cannibal (batarian), brute (krogan/turian hybrid), marauder (turian), harvester, the Reaper-converted rachni, the cut "pariah/adjutant," the roughly 160-meter Reaper destroyer, and Sovereign's leaf-insect-nymph silhouette.

Environment entries cover Earth (modeled on Vancouver), Mars and the Prothean archive, Sur'Kesh, Tuchanka and its lost krogan city, Rannoch, Thessia, the Citadel, Menae, Cerberus Headquarters, and the Normandy SR-2 repainted in Alliance blue. Other sections address the Crucible (evoking a cross between the Trinity bomb and a NASA probe), weaponry and the new omni-blade, and the Extended Cut DLC's expanded endings. DLC coverage includes the Citadel DLC (CAT6, Nyreen, Maya Brooks), Leviathan, Omega and the Talons, Javik and the Protheans, and the multiplayer firebases and character kits.
