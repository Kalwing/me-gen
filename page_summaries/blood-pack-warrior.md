Thin page: a Mass Effect 2 enemy-unit stub, mostly gameplay tactics. Blood Pack Warriors were the elite troops of the Blood Pack mercenary organization. They were exclusively krogan and served as officers and overseers to the more numerous vorcha, and were rarely encountered without vorcha support. They accompanied Blood Pack commanders such as Kureck and Garm. Warriors carried the M-27 Scimitar shotgun, which could be loaded with Incendiary Ammo, and specialized in close-quarters combat, advancing quickly while firing and periodically using Carnage blasts and the krogan charge. They possessed moderate armor that grew more durable on higher difficulties, along with consistent health regeneration that became nearly instant on Insanity.
