This is a walkthrough reference page listing the exact conversation and action triggers that award Paragon and Renegade points throughout Mass Effect 3, which feed a single Reputation meter. It proceeds mission by mission, beginning with the Earth prologue (dialogue with Anderson, the Alliance Council, the child, and James, plus 15 Reputation for completing the prologue) and Priority: Mars, and continues through the campaign. Each entry names the specific dialogue option, notes Paragon or Renegade value, and marks interrupt bonuses (typically 5 points). It notes that interactions with Javik require Mass Effect 3: From Ashes or Legendary Edition, and that entries involving characters who can die assume those characters survived. The page is a data table with no narrative prose.
