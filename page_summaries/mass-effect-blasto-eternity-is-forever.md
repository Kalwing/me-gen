Mass Effect: Blasto: Eternity is Forever is a single-issue comic featuring Blasto, the fictional hanar Spectre who stars in several films within the Mass Effect universe. It was written by Mac Walters with art by Omar Francia and released exclusively through Dark Horse Digital on November 7, 2012.

In the story, Blasto defeats a group of batarian mercenaries seeking galactic domination and returns to the Citadel, where his turian handler assigns him to hunt Kronus, a mad krogan scientist. Three Spectres sent after Kronus have turned up dead, including one dispatched to the prison ship where Blasto had personally incarcerated him. The handler orders Blasto to search Virmire in the Hoc system and pairs him with a new partner, Cerulean Star, a famous asari actress who is secretly also a Spectre.

Reasoning that Virmire's reputation as uninhabitable following a nuclear blast makes it an ideal hideout, Blasto scans and finds a newly built structure on one of its islands. Inside, the pair fight through krogan patrols. Cerulean Star discovers from a console that Kronus is building a machine to freeze time, and stays behind to disable it while Blasto confronts Kronus. Kronus freezes Blasto with a gauntlet and gloats that Blasto will be trapped for eternity. Cerulean Star, brought in as a captive, reveals Kronus underestimated her, breaks free, and forces him at gunpoint to unfreeze Blasto. Kronus activates the facility's self-destruct and flees, but an explosion knocks him over a railing to his death. Blasto and Cerulean Star escape and swim to shore. Blasto admits she was more capable than he expected; she notes that asari believe in embracing eternity, and Blasto replies that embracing is the one thing he excels at.
