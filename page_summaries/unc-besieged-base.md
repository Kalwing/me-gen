"UNC: Besieged Base" was a Mass Effect assignment that became available only at high Paragon standing (80%, or 90% if 80% Renegade had been reached first). Admiral Hackett briefed Shepard that biotic fanatics had seized a Sirta Foundation medical research station on the planet Chohe in the Cacus system of the Hades Gamma cluster and had drugged the researchers to use them as human shields. The objective was to eliminate the biotics while minimizing innocent casualties; unusually among Hackett's assignments, certain briefing responses let Shepard refuse it.

The fight took place inside prefab structures, with all enemies (Biotic Terrorists and a Biotic Terrorist Leader) possessing biotic powers and six drugged scientists wandering into the line of fire. Recommended tactics included setting squad power usage to Defense Only or Disabled, avoiding area-of-effect attacks, using precise powers like Stasis and Neural Shock, and luring the biotics into the entrance tunnel away from the scientists. Containment cells could explode when hit with Sabotage or Overload. After clearing the base, Shepard returned to the Normandy and reported to Hackett, who responded with congratulations if no civilians died, moderate approval if only some were saved, or strong criticism if all were killed, with corresponding morality points. A save-flag bug (fixed in Legendary Edition) caused later news reports in Mass Effect and Mass Effect 2 to state the Sirta Foundation never recovered and would likely shut down, regardless of how many scientists survived.
