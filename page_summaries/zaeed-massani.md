Zaeed Massani was a feared, heavily scarred bounty hunter and mercenary soldier. He co-founded the Blue Suns private security outfit with his partner Vido Santiago, running guns, extortion, and arson. When other Suns wanted to enter the batarian slave trade, Zaeed objected; Vido settled the dispute by shooting Zaeed point-blank in the face. Zaeed survived, drank heavily during recovery, and rebuilt his reputation as an independent contractor, eventually working for the Shadow Broker. In one Foundation-era job he assembled freelancers Kendo Holin, Tristana, and Jace Reyn to crash the turian frigate Verrikan; the plan unraveled when the inside man Polonis was caught, but Zaeed took security chief Garkko hostage, reached the bridge, and set the ship on a crash course. Tristana, secretly working for Vido, turned on him, and Zaeed killed her and escaped by escape pod as the Verrikan exploded.

In Mass Effect 2, Cerberus and the Illusive Man contracted Zaeed to join Shepard's mission against the Collectors, with his fee paid personally by the Illusive Man; Shepard recruited him on Omega. He took the Normandy's starboard cargo hold, bringing a battered M-8 Avenger, a Blood Pack helmet, and a turian frigate model, and used a crate marked "55" as a knife dartboard. His loyalty mission, Zaeed: The Price of Revenge, sent the squad to Zorya to hunt Vido Santiago. Zaeed started a compressed-gas fire; Shepard could either put out the fires to save refinery workers (letting Vido escape by gunship and enraging Zaeed, whose loyalty could then only be salvaged with great difficulty) or pursue Vido, whom Zaeed shot in the leg and burned to death with a thrown heatsink. His neck bore a Blue Suns tattoo and his right eye and surrounding face were surgically grafted reconstruction.

In Mass Effect 3, if he survived the Collector base, Zaeed returned taking jobs that hindered Cerberus after failed negotiations with them. He was found to have kidnapped volus ambassador Din Korlack; Shepard remotely convinced Zaeed he had been duped, and he killed his fellow mercs and saved Korlack, who provided intelligence on a Cerberus attack on a turian colony and the volus bombing fleet. If Zaeed was not made loyal in Mass Effect 2, he died saving Korlack and did not appear on the Normandy memorial wall. He later pledged support at the Citadel Refugee Docks, discussed Blue Suns Citadel leader Darner Vosque, and bought parts to restore his rifle "Jessie." In the Citadel DLC he was found battling a claw machine at Castle Arcade, could be invited to Shepard's party, flirted clumsily with Samara, and plotted booby traps with Garrus. If Shepard chose to destroy the Reapers, Zaeed was shown lounging on a tropical beach with Jessie.

Key quotes: Zaeed's tagline: "Rage is a hell of an anesthetic." Zaeed, on Vido: "For twenty years, I've seen that bastard every time I closed my eyes. Every time I sighted down on a target. Every time I heard a gunshot. Don't you call that a goddamned 'grudge.'" Zaeed, in Mass Effect 3: "Let's gut the bastards." Zaeed, on the Reaper ground war in London: it didn't feel like a real ground war when the enemies were taller than "goddamn skyscrapers."

In Mass Effect: Andromeda, Zaeed may have fathered a son, Bain Massani, a private security contractor who joined the Andromeda Initiative; Zaeed was absent from his life. Jay Turner wrote Zaeed in Mass Effect 2 and Cathleen Rootsaert in the Citadel DLC. He was voiced by Robin Sachs, a BioWare regular who died on February 1, 2013; the multiplayer Operation TRIBUTE honored him. Zaeed was available only through the Zaeed - The Price of Revenge DLC, included in Legendary Edition, and in an unimported Mass Effect 3 he never joined the team.
