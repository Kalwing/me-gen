Cerberus Skunkworks was a hidden equipment manufacturer in the first Mass Effect, drawn entirely from cut content and not considered canon. Its gear was described as specialist hardware produced by Cerberus operatives for black-ops wetwork: advanced particle-beam weapons with strong shield penetration, damage and accuracy, and field hardsuits with advanced protective and anti-hacking measures. The armor line was said to offer the best tech/biotic protection of any in the game across all races and levels, a distinction that in normal play instead falls to the runner-up Predator armors of Armax Arsenal. The equipment is fully functional in the game files but obtainable only through the PC console or third-party software. Its weapon lines, named for Greek mythological creatures, were the Gorgon assault rifles, Hydra shotguns, Harpy pistols and Titan sniper rifles, each spanning ten levels with high damage values. Its armor came in Light, Medium and Heavy "Freedom/Hoplite" variants covering combinations of humans, turians and krogan, likewise across ten levels, each providing high damage protection, shields and tech/biotic protection. All items could be acquired only through console commands. The manufacturer name references the Skunk Works, the alias of Lockheed Martin's Advanced Development Programs division, and the "Hoplite" armor references the ancient Greek infantry.
