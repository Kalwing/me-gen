The M-597 Ladon was a heavy weapon in Mass Effect 3 that blended human workmanship with Reaper technology. After the Battle of the Citadel, Alliance intelligence studied fragments of Reaper code recovered from Sovereign and successfully decoded its face and object recognition system, which was notably advanced. Preparing desperately for the Reaper invasion, the Alliance adapted this software into the Ladon, a missile system that surveyed the battlefield, assigned a homing incendiary warhead to each hostile, and avoided friendly targets even if allies had changed clothing or grown facial hair. Its major shortcoming was the processing delay before each launch while the trigger was held.

The Ladon's antipersonnel capability was fearsome, but Alliance soldiers built a grim mythology around it, making it a contender for "most hated weapon." Alien alphanumeric characters remained visible around its targeting display, a reminder of its partly Reaper origin, and rumors claimed it could indoctrinate an owner who slept beside it or would hit friendlies the first time it was used against Reaper creatures; more than one unit left Ladons buried in supply crates before missions.

The weapon was exclusive to Mass Effect 3: Special Edition and found in Grissom Academy: Emergency Evacuation, Tuchanka: Bomb, Kallini: Ardat-Yakshi Monastery, Priority: Geth Dreadnought, Priority: Cerberus Headquarters and Priority: Earth. It locked onto up to six enemies before firing powerful homing rockets, with targets selectable via the gamepad touchscreen; physical obstructions could still block its rockets. It was likely named for the serpent-like monster of Greek mythology, a multi-headed offspring of Typhon and Echidna.
