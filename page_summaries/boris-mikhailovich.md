Rear Admiral Boris Mikhailovich was a Systems Alliance admiral in the Fifth Fleet who commanded the 63rd Scout Flotilla. In Mass Effect he met Commander Shepard at the Alliance docking bay when the Normandy returned to the Citadel and announced a surprise inspection of the ship; if Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko was in the squad, that squadmate saluted him on sight. After the inspection he expressed anger that the Normandy had been placed under Shepard's command rather than assigned to his flotilla. He considered the frigate overdesigned and too expensive, costing as much as a heavy cruiser, and dismissed the value of its stealth system, saying warships were meant to find and destroy the enemy, not count how often enemy garrisons went to the bathroom. He also criticised the cost of the drive core, the ship's turian design innovations, and its multi-species crew. Shepard could respond diplomatically or aggressively, influencing how positive his report to the Joint Military Council would be, or use Spectre authority to refuse him access entirely. If convinced of the Normandy's usefulness, he said his report would be less negative than he had planned and wished the Commander luck.

During the Battle of the Citadel, Mikhailovich led the 63rd Scout Flotilla against Sovereign. The 63rd suffered the fewest losses of any Alliance flotilla and provided critical support to the Fifth Fleet. He was promoted from Rear Admiral to Admiral after the battle and tasked with rebuilding and upgrading his ships. In Mass Effect 3, Admiral Boris Mikhailovich aided the war against the Reapers, but only if the Council had not been saved during the Battle of the Citadel. An Alliance News Network report mentioned an Alliance admiral named Peter Mikhailovich, though it was not known whether the two were related.
