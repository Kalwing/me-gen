The Scorpion was a heavy pistol in Mass Effect 3 and a pistol in Mass Effect: Andromeda. Originally issued to the salarian Special Tasks Group so small units could contain much larger enemy forces, it later saw galaxy-wide service. It fired low-velocity, squash-head projectiles filled with high-explosive and an adhesive: rounds stuck to a target on impact and detonated roughly three seconds later, dealing no damage on the initial hit, and rounds fired into a surface became proximity mines that triggered when an enemy came near, even cloaked ones. In Andromeda it was described as a mini-grenade launcher; the Initiative procured and modified a small number of the salarian weapons.

In Mass Effect 3 single-player, the Scorpion was found during Priority: Sur'Kesh next to the power switch and was otherwise purchasable from Aegohr Munitions afterward. In multiplayer it was an ultra-rare drop requiring no expansion packs. Its explosive rounds ignored armor penalties but could not penetrate solid objects or score headshot or weak-point bonuses, and could damage grouped or behind-cover enemies through splash. Drawbacks were the detonation delay and a very low clip and reserve capacity, offset by perfect projectile accuracy with negligible recoil, strong stagger on most enemies including Brutes and Geth Primes, and a very high chance to trigger Disruptor or Cryo Ammo effects on a direct stick. It paired well with Tactical Cloak, since damage occurred only on detonation while the cloak broke only on firing. Players were warned not to have it equipped when killing Dr. Eva at the end of Priority: Mars in non-Legendary versions, as its zero-damage initial hit guaranteed Shepard's death. In Andromeda it could not use a Pistol Receiver Mod and thus could not pierce cover.
