Kassa Fabrication was a human-controlled private company known for making some of the finest body armor in the galaxy, most notably Colossus Armor, which offered the highest protection against non-tech/biotic damage and the most efficient shields thanks to its micro-generators, with distinctive red and black coloring. The company spared no expense on personal protection suits, reflected in the exorbitant cost of its products. It also produced top-quality weapons including the Harpoon Sniper Rifle, and had recently begun manufacturing omni-tools, though those had not yet reached the standard of its armor lines.

In Mass Effect, a license for Kassa Fabrication equipment could be purchased from the C-Sec Requisitions Officer and from Petozi on Noveria for 30,000 credits. All Kassa weapons performed well across the board, being both powerful and accurate due to incorporated mass effect enhancers. Its Mass Effect weapon lines were the Breaker assault rifles, Armageddon shotguns, Razer pistols, and Harpoon sniper rifles. In Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3 the company manufactured a single small arm, the M-12 Locust submachine gun, a compact weapon developed for the Alliance but favored by gang enforcers and hitmen for its recoil-reducing mechanism and autotargeting.

In Mass Effect 2, Kassa made the Aegis Vest component for N7 Armor (interlocking ceramic plates in a tungsten alloy weave, +5% health). In Mass Effect 3 it made shield-focused customizable armor components (chest, shoulders, arms, legs). Kassa also produced the Polaris omni-tool and the Polaris biotic amp, the latter using reverse-engineered technology giving it decreased power but longer-than-average duration. The Prima official guide listed two unreleased Kassa weapons, the Thorn pistol and Apocalypse shotgun.
