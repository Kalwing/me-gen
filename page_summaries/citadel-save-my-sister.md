Citadel: Save My Sister was a Mass Effect assignment cut during development, set on the Citadel, in which Commander Shepard would have tried to reunite two estranged sisters, Ari and Rita, working separately, with the plotline resolving in either Rita being fired or Ari being re-hired; it closely resembled the released assignment Citadel: Rita's Sister.
