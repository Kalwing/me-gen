The Krogan Vanguard was a playable APEX strike team class in Mass Effect: Andromeda multiplayer, described in-fiction as a krogan who got drunk and missed his Rite of Passage on Tuchanka and, driven by shame, joined the Andromeda Initiative. He had 600 health and 195 shields, the powers Charge, Fortify and Nova, the passives APEX Training, Rage and Veteran Bonus, a Headbutt melee, and a bonus stat reducing health and shield regen delay.

Compared to the Human Vanguard he was slower but far more survivable with a much stronger melee, and nearly every Charge was meant to be followed by a headbutt, with Rage plus Fortify making him hit hard while shrugging off light fire. Rage rank 2 let him carry heavy weapon loadouts without going overweight. His primer skills were locked behind rank 6 evolutions and only worked at close range, so combo detonations relied on Charging in point-blank or on ammo boosters; Nova was of limited use given the melee focus. Player notes warned of insta-kill grab enemies, and covered cloaked Destined among the kett, Berserkers among the Outlaws, and floating Remnant units, all awkward for a Charge-based melee build.
