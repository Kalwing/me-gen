Dah'tan Manufacturing, also styled Dah'Tan Manufacturing, was a batarian corporation based on Camala that specialized in making computer components. When Dr. Shu Qian was researching the alien artifact discovered near the Perseus Veil, he bought all his components from Dah'tan to avoid hardware compatibility problems and deliberately chose a batarian manufacturer to avoid drawing suspicion given the sensitive nature of his work. In Mass Effect: Revelation, because using Dah'tan as a supplier risked linking Edan Had'dah's name to Dr. Qian's facility at Sidon, Had'dah ordered the krogan Skarr to attack the Dah'tan facility and burn it to the ground, intending to kill his contacts there, including a batarian woman named Jella, and destroy all records. Most of Dah'tan's employees were killed in Skarr's attack, but Jella alone survived due to a case of mistaken identity. By 2185 Dah'Tan Manufacturing had opened offices in the Sin'Kelar cluster in preparation for expansion into the Nemean Abyss and had advertised as far as Omega to attract jobseekers to the untamed reaches of the galaxy.
