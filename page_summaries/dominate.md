Dominate was a biotic power available in the single-player modes of Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. It brainwashed an organic enemy, manipulating its perceptual system so that it saw its allies as enemies and fought on the caster's side for a short time. In Mass Effect 2 it functioned as the organic equivalent of AI Hacking, affecting only one target at a time, stunning the target briefly before taking effect, and granting the dominated enemy a temporary biotic barrier at higher ranks. The rank 4 evolutions were Enhanced Dominate, which greatly extended duration against a single target, and Group Dominate, which affected multiple organics near a target point. Dominated enemies in Mass Effect 2 did not build resistance with repeat use. Used on a Husk it killed the target instantly rather than controlling it; used on an Abomination it mind-controlled it normally. It could indirectly seize control of Harbinger if cast on an unprotected Collector just before Harbinger possessed it.

In Mass Effect 2 the power was obtained by Shepard through Advanced Training only after allowing Morinth to kill Samara at the end of the loyalty mission Samara: The Ardat-Yakshi, in the current or a prior playthrough; Morinth also used it as a squadmate, with a doubled twelve-second recharge time.

In Mass Effect 3 Dominate was a bonus power for Commander Shepard, unlocked only by downloading and completing the Mass Effect 3: Leviathan DLC (included by default in Legendary Edition). It could not be chosen when starting a new game and had to be selected from the Normandy's Med Bay between missions. The Mass Effect 3 version could be used on armored, shielded, and barrier-protected organics, excluding Banshees and Harvesters, but targets grew resistant with each use and became permanently immune after three casts. It no longer granted a protective barrier, though the rank 6 Rage evolution gave damage resistance. Dominated enemies displayed a white aura. Rank 5 offered Lasting Damage or Frenzy; rank 6 offered Vulnerability or Rage. Atlases, despite organic pilots, counted as synthetic and could not be dominated. The power was not available in multiplayer.
