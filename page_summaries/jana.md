Short page (under ~150 words of prose). Jana was the Illusive Man's assistant in 2186, drawn from the first edition of Mass Effect: Deception, a novel BioWare acknowledged contained lore discrepancies. Her duties included assigning Cerberus operatives to minor tasks, filtering the Illusive Man's calls and messages, and guiding the rare visitors permitted to meet him. She was described as having black hair, large brown eyes, and a full figure, and had been a military officer of unspecified affiliation before joining Cerberus. Kai Leng questioned how human Jana really was and believed the Illusive Man was grooming her for a greater role. A separate blonde-haired scientist also named Jana appears in Cronos Station video logs discussing indoctrination.
