Gameplay guide, not lore. This page explained how to unlock the Scholar achievement in the first Mass Effect, which required discovering a set of Codex entries on alien species before certain characters became unavailable. It listed each required subject and where to obtain it: the Council races (asari, salarians, turians) unlocked automatically at the Council hearing or by talking to the Embassy Receptionist Saphyria; the extinct races (Prothean, rachni) via Nihlus's briefing aboard the Normandy and Avina terminals or Noveria NPCs; and non-Council races (batarians, elcor, geth, hanar, keepers, krogan, quarians, volus) through conversations with ambassadors such as Din Korlack and Calyn, shopkeepers like Delan, Avina terminals, Ashley Williams, Captain Anderson, Chorban, and the Wrex and Tali recruitment missions. It also noted a notification glitch on Xbox 360 where the achievement might not pop, and credited Armando Castillo's GameFAQs guide.
