A Shepard VI was a virtual intelligence based on Commander Shepard, of which more than one version may have existed.

In Mass Effect 2, during Thane Krios's search for his son, Captain Bailey directed Shepard and Thane to Mouse, a former "duct rat" who was selling a VI personality he had built based on Shepard. The VI made snarky comments based on its owner's activities, such as deleting files, but was so buggy it crashed every thirty minutes. While interrogating Mouse outside the Dark Star Lounge, Shepard could take a copy, ask for a cut of the sales, or demand Mouse stop making them; Mouse agreed to whatever Shepard wanted but insisted the product was legal because Shepard was dead when he made it, and said three volus were regularly patching the software.

In Mass Effect 3 (2186), after the mission to rescue the turian primarch, Shepard could find a holographic Shepard VI, "model 1.7 AGB," on sale at the Citadel refugee camp. A sales clerk, unaware she was talking to the real war hero, unlocked a demo because a child had spilled soda on the VI's hardware. The demo spoke a fixed set of lines in a synthesized voice, varying by the Commander's personality and reputation, after which the real Shepard wondered aloud whether they really sounded like it.

Paragon lines included: "Good to meet you! I'm Commander Shepard, Alliance Navy."; "There's nothing this galaxy can't beat if we all work together."; "Except the Reapers. Ever see the size of one of those things?"; and "My personality matrix can predict what the real Commander Shepard would say with seven percent accuracy." Renegade lines included: "I find the best advanced battlefield strategy is to have more bullets than the other guy."; "Technically, it's thermal clips, not bullets. But who says, 'I filled him with five detachable heat sinks'?"; "I sound seven percent more like Commander Shepard than any other bootleg VI copy."; and "You want help solving your problems? Get me out of this damn demo mode." Cut lines included "Let's get this straight: I don't 'do' task scheduling. I'm not that kind of program. Call me if you spot any rogue AIs."
