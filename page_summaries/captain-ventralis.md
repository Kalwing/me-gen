Captain J. Ventralis was the human head of the Elanus Risk Control Services (ERCS) personnel assigned to security at the labs on Noveria. Commander Shepard met him after taking the passenger tram to Rift Station.

Ventralis was holding the Rift Station perimeter against rachni that continually advanced along the tram tunnels. He explained that he and his men had been on guard for several days, were exhausted, and were taking stims to stay awake. A rachni attack interrupted the conversation, and Shepard was puzzled why the creatures kept assaulting a natural kill zone, something Ventralis could not explain. He warned Shepard not to poke around the sealed maintenance area. When asked about Matriarch Benezia, Ventralis said she was in the hot labs and gave Shepard a pass to access them. He could also grant permission to enter the quarantined lab to develop a biotoxin cure for the sick scientists, warning that the squad had to be scanned before leaving. Shepard could report the attack by the asari commando Alestia Iallis in the quarantined lab, which shocked Ventralis, who had believed her "just another egghead."

If Shepard went straight to the hot labs and was mobbed by rachni, the squad returned to find Ventralis's post deserted; he waited in the barracks, apologized, and admitted he was acting under Benezia's orders, having sent Shepard to the hot labs hoping the rachni would kill the team. He then opened fire and was killed. If Shepard broke into the Restricted Area, the drones and guards turned hostile, and Shepard could return to kill Ventralis at his post. Ventralis was voiced by Chris Edgerly and wore medium-weight Explorer Armor rather than the standard ERCS Guardian Armor. In cut content his first name was "Juleth" and he was originally written as a female turian.
