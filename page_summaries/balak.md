This page covered Balak as an enemy encountered in the first Mass Effect, distinct from the general character page Ka'hairal Balak. Balak was a batarian terrorist who attempted to crash Asteroid X57 into the human colony of Terra Nova. During the mission, after Commander Shepard shut down all three fusion torches, Shepard confronted Balak and his remaining team at the main facility. Balak gave the Commander an ultimatum: let him leave, or the captured scientists would die. When Shepard refused to release him and vowed to stop him, Balak detonated a bomb that killed all the hostages held in an adjoining room and then engaged Shepard in combat.

Shepard fought through the rest of Balak's terrorists, varren, and defense drones. A wounded Balak then accused Shepard of being no better than himself for throwing away the scientists' lives. Shepard could choose to leave him for the Alliance, let him die, or kill him outright. The page noted that whichever fate the player chose for Balak would carry consequences into subsequent games.

The tactics section described Balak as essentially a stronger version of the Batarian Trooper. He relied heavily on Immunity for defense and advanced from cover to cover. He would periodically overheat the player's weapons with Sabotage and put powers on cooldown with Damping, so the page recommended boosting the party's tech protection and carrying backup weapons, and ordering squadmates to switch weapons if theirs overheated. With supporting guards and drones the overall fight could be challenging, though Balak alone was not especially difficult. On higher difficulties, the page warned about his Assassination ability when using a sniper rifle, which could kill even a Shepard who normally survived sniper fire; suggested defenses included Sabotage, Immunity, a high-strength Barrier, keeping him staggered with powers, and staying in cover.
