Tali: Treason was Tali'Zorah's loyalty mission in Mass Effect 2, acquired some time after recruiting her when Kelly Chambers noted Tali seemed distraught. The Migrant Fleet's Admiralty Board had charged Tali with treason for smuggling active geth into the flotilla; on her father Admiral Rael'Zorah's orders she had been sending deactivated geth samples home. The Normandy, a registered Cerberus vessel, docked with the liveship Rayya via Tali's passphrase. Captain Kar'Danna met the squad; if Legion was brought, Shepard needed Charm or Intimidate to keep it aboard. Admiral Shala'Raan greeted Tali as "Tali'Zorah vas Normandy," revealing the Board had already stripped her quarian ship-name, a bad omen. Raan, recused from judging due to her closeness to the family, opened proceedings, and Shepard served as Tali's advocate.

The admirals sprang the news that the lab ship Alarei had been overrun by geth and that Rael'Zorah was presumed dead. Shepard and Tali proposed to retake the Alarei, and the hearing recessed. Speaking with the admirals, Veetor, and Kal'Reegar (present only if Veetor was returned to the Fleet and Kal'Reegar survived Haestrom) revealed the trial was a facade for the admirals' agendas: Xen wanted to study AI to reclaim the geth, Gerrel wanted war to retake Rannoch, Koris wanted Tali and her father made examples of, and Raan admitted she had deliberately withheld news of Rael's death so Tali's shock would win sympathy.

Aboard the Alarei, Shepard's team fought through waves of Geth Troopers, Hunters, a Geth Prime, and Combat Drones in close quarters, with Tali's AI Hacking key to survival. Logs showed Rael had ordered safety protocols bypassed. Tali found her father's body and a recorded message ordering her to destroy the main geth hub and deliver the data to Xen and Gerrel. The final console proved Rael had deliberately reactivated and networked geth toward sapience for weapons tests, while clearing Tali of any knowledge. Tali begged Shepard not to reveal it, to preserve her father's name.

Back on the Rayya, Shepard could clear Tali via persuasion (30 morality, loyalty gained), lie that no evidence was found (Tali exiled but loyal), rally the crowd with Veetor and Kal'Reegar, or present the evidence (Tali cleared, Rael posthumously exiled, the Fleet Balkanized, loyalty lost). Tali kept the name "vas Normandy" regardless. Rewards included the Energy Drain power for Tali and tech upgrades. The outcome influenced whether quarian-geth peace was possible in Mass Effect 3.
