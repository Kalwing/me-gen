Geth Shock Troopers were heavy geth infantry in the original Mass Effect, indicated by their white armor. They were also capable engineers: as seen on Eden Prime, they could set up and arm large explosives. On the battlefield they typically made up nearly half of geth ground forces. Shock Troopers possessed four to five bars of shielding depending on location and difficulty, which regenerated over time, while their health, like that of most synthetics, did not. They could take cover, tried to flank, and could use Shield Boost to fully restore their shields.

During fights they would often create a geth hex shield, a roughly three-meter opaque hexagon that completely concealed and protected the geth behind it while allowing that geth to fire through it, absorbing weapons fire from Shepard and squadmates and changing color from blue at full strength through orange to red before collapsing. Although armed with assault rifles, Shock Troopers could still launch Carnage, an ability normally restricted to shotguns, dealing heavy damage that was avoidable at medium to long range. Overload and Sabotage were effective given their strong shields and lack of a secondary weapon, leaving them defenseless until their weapons cooled; the tech proximity mines from those powers and Damping ignored hex shields, striking the geth behind them. Advanced or Master AI Hacking could turn a Shock Trooper into a resilient, high-damage ally, though a hacked one would still attack Shepard if no other enemies were in its line of sight.
