Powers were the combat abilities used in both single-player and multiplayer Mass Effect 3. They followed the general pattern of Mass Effect 2's powers but allowed two additional ranks, up to a maximum of rank 6, with ranks 4 to 6 each offering two choices and rank 6 always passive. Ammo powers returned with some changes, and grenade powers were added as a category. Power projectiles were faster and recharged more quickly than before, but many enemy types could now dodge them; squadmate powers still hit instantly and could not be evaded.

Commander Shepard could earn up to 181 points by level 60 (enough to fully max eight powers with points to spare), or up to 61 carried over from a high-level imported Mass Effect 2 save. Shepard's and most squadmates' powers could be reassigned at two terminals in the Normandy SR-2 medical bay; the first reassignment was free and later ones cost up to 25,000 credits, while reassigning Shepard's bonus power always cost 5,000. Each class had First Aid, Fitness, a passive class power (Biotic Mastery, Tech Mastery, Operational Mastery, Offensive Mastery, Combat Mastery, or Assault Mastery), and six active powers.

Bonus powers were unlocked through squad conversations or DLC missions rather than achievements, with each squad member able to unlock two; because Ashley Williams and Kaidan Alenko could not both appear in one playthrough, unlocking all of their bonus powers required separate games. Available bonus powers included Inferno Grenade and Marksman (Ashley), Proximity Mine and Armor-Piercing Ammo (Garrus), Fortification and Carnage (James Vega), Warp Ammo and Stasis (Liara), Defense Matrix and Decoy (EDI), Defense Drone and Energy Drain (Tali), Barrier and Reave (Kaidan), Slam and Dark Channel (Javik), Dominate (Leviathan DLC), and Lash and Flare (Aria via the Omega DLC). Squadmates had four active powers and one passive class power and could attain 90 points by level 60. Citadel DLC and other returning squadmates such as David Anderson, Aria T'Loak, Nyreen Kandros, Urdnot Wrex, Miranda, Jacob, Jack, Grunt, Samara, Zaeed, and Kasumi had their own power sets.
