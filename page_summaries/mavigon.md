Mavigon was the third planet of the Han System in the Gemini Sigma cluster of the Milky Way, a small rock and ice world with a thin ammonia and methane atmosphere. Its frozen surface was mainly tin with deposits of potassium, gold and cobalt. Rudimentary ammonia-based life clustered around geothermal vents deep underground. Severe storm cycles and a heavy blizzard at the operational area limited long-range visibility, making navigation difficult, and the planet carried a Level 2 Cold Hazard.

In Mass Effect, Mavigon was explored during the assignment UNC: Hostile Takeover, in which Shepard assaulted a Syndicate hideout defended by three Alliance Heavy Turrets and staffed by mercenaries, mercenary snipers and a crime boss. Points of interest included engine debris, an abandoned camp with locked crates, and a mummified salarian whose remains held the identification tag of Captain Stranaka, one of the salarian soldiers charged with hunting down the League of One, collected for UNC: Locate Signs of Battle. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition the planet's description text was corrected to reflect the Level 2 hazard rather than the previously stated Level 1. Mavigon was also referenced by the prerequisite mission Priority: Sur'Kesh in Mass Effect 3.
