Guardians were a class of Cerberus soldier encountered in Mass Effect 3, described as "human tanks." They were slow-moving troops carrying enormous polycrystalline-composite shields so heavy that they required an armored suit with hydraulic assists and a dedicated power supply, combined with enviro-mapping systems used to flank opponents and flush them from cover. Their relentless advance was meant to demoralize enemies and draw fire, but once their shield was removed they became little more than cannon fodder.

Offensively, Guardians carried the M-358 Talon pistol, a close-range "shotgun pistol," could bash with the shield, and used electrical batons only after losing the shield. Their shields blocked most weapon fire and powers from the front, prevented Power Combos, and made them immune to grab kills while raised; they could not dodge with the shield up but could combat-roll once it was gone. Guardians often operated in pairs and supported Combat Engineers by shielding them while they placed turrets.

Counters noted on the page included a small "mail slot" opening near the top of the shield that a precise sniper shot could pass through for a headshot (required for the Mail Slot Achievement), shooting their exposed hands or feet, flanking with powers such as Pull, Lash, Stasis from behind, Reave, or Annihilation Field, grenades or Proximity Mines detonating behind the shield, and innately penetrating weapons like the M-98 Widow, Javelin, Reegar Carbine, Geth Plasma Shotgun, Kishock Harpoon Gun, and Chakram Launcher, or Armor-Piercing Ammo and piercing weapon mods. Cerberus Shield Pylons could restore and reinforce their shields, so destroying pylons first was advised. The Riot Trooper was the Guardian's counterpart in Mass Effect: Infiltrator.
