This Mass Effect 3 "Omega" DLC Codex covered the Cerberus takeover of Omega and related subjects. Aria T'Loak, a powerful asari biotic rumored to be at least a thousand years old, had long been Omega's sole ruler through combat skill and manipulation before Cerberus drove her into exile. The Illusive Man's plan used experimental creatures called adjutants, which "escaped" from a Cerberus facility beyond the Omega-4 relay, seized a Cerberus convoy, and attacked Omega; Cerberus then asked Aria for help against its own test subjects. Aria allied her forces and joined General Oleg Petrovsky aboard his ship, but when the adjutants overran the combined fleet, both escaped through the Omega-4 relay. Petrovsky's subordinate Colonel Raymond Ashe had stayed behind to seize the station's defenses, and Cerberus took Omega, gaining control of the Omega-4 relay and the space beyond; without Aria, the station's factions turned on Cerberus and each other. Aria, held captive by Petrovsky, escaped back to Omega and organized a resistance. Petrovsky feigned a Cerberus withdrawal to lure her out, then summoned a large fleet and threatened to destroy the station rather than lose it; Aria left, vowing to return and take revenge.

Adjutants were Cerberus's codename for Reaper-technology-based experimental subjects that could infect any life-form to create new adjutants; xenobiologists suspected the underlying virus was a Reaper creation from cycles before the Prothean extinction. General Oleg Petrovsky was a military strategist and classical scholar who had commanded a unit against turians in the First Contact War and later found more common ground with Cerberus than the Alliance. The Rampart mech was a Cerberus combat and crowd-control unit built from recovered Eclipse LOKI mechs, upgraded with Cerberus fabrication technology, Omega shielding, and ablative armor, and capable of a "hunter-killer" mode. Nyreen Kandros was a turian biotic and former Cabal soldier, once mentored by Aria, who returned as leader of the reformed Talons, Omega's symbol of resistance. Turian Cabals were small elite biotic units (10 to 15 soldiers led by a kabalim) kept isolated by the Hierarchy because of a cultural bias against biotic soldiers dating to the Unification Wars, with many recalled to Palaven for the Reaper war.
