"Aria: Blood Pack" was a Mass Effect 3 mission, one of three in which Aria T'Loak asked Commander Shepard to bring a mercenary band under her control in exchange for committing the Terminus Fleet to the war effort. It became available after Priority: Palaven, by speaking with Aria at the Purgatory bar on the Citadel. It was written by Neil Pollner.

Aria directed Shepard to meet her batarian contact, Narl, at Apartment 86 in the Presidium Commons. Narl explained that he had told the Blood Pack he was turning the Commander over to Kreete, a vorcha Blood Pack leader, but that this was a ruse. The real goal was to lure the Blood Pack out because Kreete was planning to betray Aria. Narl needed Shepard to play along and pretend to be captured; Shepard could respond positively or negatively for mandatory morality points, but the plan proceeded either way.

Three vorcha arrived, including Kreete. As the deal was about to close, Narl revealed that the true arrangement was between Aria and Gryll, who was next in line to lead the Blood Pack. Gryll confirmed the deal and ordered Kreete killed. Shepard and Narl shot Kreete and the other unnamed vorcha. Shepard advised Gryll not to double-cross Aria, and Gryll agreed, saying he was ambitious but not crazy. Completing the mission updated the Terminus Fleet War Asset by 50 points and awarded 5,000 credits, 210 experience, 5 Reputation points, and morality points.
