Krogan Berserkers were tank-bred krogan created by the warlord Okeer in his attempt to breed "perfect krogan" free of the genophage's genetic markers and of the "weak genes" he believed nurturing feeble newborns had spread through the species. While in their tanks the specimens were shown alien anatomical weak points and past battles to prepare them for war and instill hatred of Okeer's enemies, and were taught to speak, move and fight by what one Berserker called a "voice" or "scratching sound." Most specimens failed and were handed to Blue Suns commander Jedore for her army, while Okeer kept the single specimen he judged perfect, who became Grunt. The Berserkers proved uncontrollably violent, driven only to fight and kill, and were prone to blood rage, so Jedore used them as live-fire training targets for her mercenaries. They matured extremely fast; one Shepard encountered on Korlus was only seven days old.

As enemies on Korlus, Berserkers were ferocious in close quarters, armed with shotguns and the Carnage ability, constantly advancing and using a stunning charge. They had good armor and very fast health regeneration. Incinerate, Warp and incendiary or Warp Ammo stripped their armor and blocked regeneration; precise ranged weapons, sniper rifles and staying at distance were advised, and once disarmored they could be knocked off the area's narrow walkways to their deaths.
