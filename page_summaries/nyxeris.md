Nyxeris was an asari assistant to Liara T'Soni on Illium in Mass Effect 2 and, secretly, an agent of the Shadow Broker known as the Observer. As part of her cover she minded the door outside Liara's office and supplied her nominal employer with supplemental data, professing that it was an honor to work with her and remarking that Liara could wield more political power if she were not focused on personal goals. As the Observer she was ruthless but pragmatic; scattered logs showed she issued kill orders and framed associates for them, but would rescind orders when there was a benefit.

During Shepard's assignment "The Observer" for Liara to investigate the Broker's agent, Nyxeris' identity could either be exposed or she could remain undercover for lack of evidence. If Shepard told Liara the Observer was female, Liara summoned Nyxeris into her office. On Shepard's next visit Liara stated she had killed Nyxeris, noting that "her barriers needed practice. Practice I'm afraid she won't be getting," and that had Nyxeris been ordered to assassinate her, Liara would never have seen it coming. Her name combines Nyx and Eris, Greek goddesses of night and discord. With Lair of the Shadow Broker installed, telling Liara the relevant intel could send her home before the assignment was finished, causing Nyxeris to simply walk off screen as the mission auto-completed.
