This Mass Effect 3 Codex section compiled entries on the Reapers and their forces. Reapers, once dismissed as a myth of star-devouring devils, were confirmed as a real and devastating sentient-machine threat. Harbinger, believed the oldest and largest Reaper, directed the Collectors from dark space to abduct humans from vulnerable colonies and was tentatively identified among the Reapers attacking Earth. Sovereign was the first Reaper met by the modern Citadel races; assumed to be a geth or Prothean flagship commanded by rogue Spectre Saren Arterius, it was in fact intelligent and controlled Saren. Its attacks on Eden Prime and the Citadel removed any doubt about Reaper superiority, but when Saren's death corrupted the link between them, Sovereign's shields failed and it was destroyed; later Reapers no longer shared that design flaw. Indoctrination was described as gradual reprogramming of organic minds using electromagnetic fields and infrasonic and ultrasonic noise, producing headaches, feelings of being watched, hallucinations, and eventual mental collapse, and capable of creating deep-cover agents from political or military leaders.

Reapers were classed into four types: two-kilometer Sovereign-class capital ships, each harvested from a single species per extinction cycle; 160-meter destroyers, the bulk of the fleet, made from unharvested species; remotely operated troop transports carrying husks; and remotely operated processors, or "slaughter ships," for mass DNA harvesting. Ground forces included husks (humans impaled on mechanical spikes and fitted with cybernetics), banshees (from Ardat-Yakshi asari, powerful biotics that seem to teleport and implode on death), brutes (turian-krogan fusions), cannibals (batarians that heal by eating the dead), marauders (turians that armor and command lesser troops), ravagers (rachni turned into walking artillery that spawn swarmers), and harvesters (winged flyers whose appearance signals an invasion). Harvesting sorted captured populations into camps, turned or enslaved the unfit, and reduced the rest to transportable paste in processor ships; intelligence estimated more than 400 processor ships on Earth killing about 1.86 million humans per day, projecting Earth's depopulation within a decade. Reaper capabilities included FTL more than twice Citadel speed, power sources that seemed to violate physical law and required no captured fuel, capital-ship main guns rated between 132 and 454 kilotons that no dreadnought had survived, and kinetic barriers able to withstand a small fleet. Catalogued vulnerabilities included reliance on kinetic rather than quantum shields, capital-ship barriers straining under three dreadnoughts and typically failing under four, greater effect from heat-focused weapons such as the Thanix, destroyers killable by a single cruiser or many fighters, and reduced barrier strength whenever a Reaper cut its mass to land on a planet.
