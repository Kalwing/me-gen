Marauders were synthetic-organic creatures created by the Reapers from harvested turians, appearing in Mass Effect 3 as support units, front-line combatants, and squad leaders in the Reaper army. Lean and armored, they commanded and protected other Reaper troops and were considered a significant threat on their own, but especially dangerous when leading a task force. Alliance marines observed Marauders reinforcing husks and cannibals by enveloping them in a ribbon of energy that formed a scabby shell of armor; because of this, standing orders directed Alliance soldiers to target the Marauder first when it appeared alongside husks or cannibals.

Marauders were armed with Phaeston assault rifles, fired in bursts at short to medium range, and could gun-butt enemies up close or execute downed foes with a stomp. They had moderate health and shielding and were the only Reaper units to rely on shields rather than armor or biotic barriers. While armoring an ally, both the Marauder and its recipient were immobile for several seconds but highly resistant to stun. Marauders could dodge with combat rolls, climb ladders, and levitate themselves up or down short vertical terrain. Headshots inflicted 40% more damage. In gameplay they functioned as squad leaders comparable to the Cerberus Centurion and Collector Captain, and their shields could be stripped with a single Overload, Energy Drain, or Sabotage before a follow-up headshot from a weapon such as the M-98 Widow or M-92 Mantis.

Marauders were the only Reaper enemy to use weapons analogous to the conventional firearms of organic civilizations; according to Aeian T'Goni at Huerta Memorial Hospital, those weapons could not be used by anyone except Marauders. During Priority: Earth they were seen operating mounted guns against friendly forces, and near the conclusion of Namakli during the Leviathan mission they were shown manipulating a Leviathan orb artifact.
