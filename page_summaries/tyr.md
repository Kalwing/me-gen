Tyr was the third planet of the Asgard System in the Exodus Cluster, accessible after the Mass Effect mission Bring Down the Sky. Compositionally similar to Earth, it lay over 4 AU from its star Asgard and had an atmosphere composed mainly of nitrogen and ethane. Although a potential terraforming target, the presence of the "shirt-sleeves" habitable world Terra Nova in the same system relegated Tyr to a support role.

Nearly a hundred human and alien corporations built extensive tele-operated mining, refining and manufacturing facilities across Tyr's surface. Together with the platinum lodes of Terra Nova, their output drove the local hydrogen fuel cell industry, and the Asgard system came to supply nearly 4% of the galactic market. During the Reaper War the Reapers immediately targeted these facilities to cut off the Alliance's supply of hydrogen fuel cells. In Mass Effect 3, Tyr was a Search and Rescue site for the Alliance Naval Exploration Flotilla. Its capital city was named Kaldidalur.
