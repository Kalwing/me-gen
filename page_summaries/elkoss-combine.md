The Elkoss Combine was a volus manufacturer based in the Terminus Systems that produced functional but inexpensive versions of items sold by high-end manufacturers, including armor, weapons, and omni-tools. It made Assassin Armor and Gladiator Armor for humans and turians. An armory license could be purchased from Expat in the Citadel Wards or from Opold on Noveria for 100 credits.

In the first Mass Effect, Elkoss Combine weapons reportedly suffered from poor field efficiency, with lower heat absorption and accuracy than competing firearms; its lines included the Avenger assault rifles, Scimitar shotguns, Edge pistols, and Reaper sniper rifles. Its armor used reverse-engineered technology, causing reduced shields and tech/biotic protection while keeping regular damage protection competitive. Its Cipher omni-tools likewise had reduced cooldown bonuses and a nullified medical recharge bonus. In Legendary Edition, accuracy and heat performance for its weapons were improved.

In Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, Elkoss Combine manufactured two small arms: the M-4 Shuriken Machine Pistol, a burst-fire submachine gun effective against shields and barriers but weak against armor, and the M-8 Avenger Assault Rifle, a common, versatile, military-grade rifle prized for being tough, reliable, and easy to upgrade. In Mass Effect 2 the company also produced the Off-Hand Ammo Pack modification for N7 Armor.

Additional Elkoss products included Elkoss Heatsinks (frictionless heat sinks with an element zero core), Elkoss Sapphire Skycars (a family skycar line), and the Kwunu, a volus dreadnought built with Elkoss funding. The corporation held numerous subsidiaries, among them First Irune Investment Trusts, Fishdog Food Shack, Genetic Paradigms, Galactic Beauty Solutions, Coros, and Cision Technologies.
