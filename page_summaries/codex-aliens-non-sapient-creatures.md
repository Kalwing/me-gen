The page collected Codex entries on common non-sapient life-forms. Husks were created when the geth impaled dead and living bodies on mechanical spikes that some Alliance soldiers called Dragon's Teeth; the spikes drained water and trace minerals, replaced them with cybernetics, and reanimated the corpses as mindless killing machines. It was unclear why a synthetic race would harvest such minimal resources, though their use as shock troops was obvious. Scions were a more extensive Collector transformation of humanoid victims: bone overlaid with metallic resin, one arm replaced by a rifle mount, and a back-mounted sack of brain and spinal tissue drawn from several individuals, making them highly resistant to gunfire, even headshots. Their weapons produced a warp effect consistent with eezo nodules deposited during transformation.

Thresher maws were subterranean carnivores that spent their lives eating or hunting for food, never fully leaving the ground and attacking with head and tentacles, toxic chemicals, and infrasound shockwaves. They reproduced via spores that could lie dormant for millennia and survive deep space and atmospheric re-entry, spreading them across many worlds. The Alliance first encountered threshers on the colony Akuze in 2177, where nearly an entire marine assault force was killed after contact was lost with the pioneer team; the Alliance recommended engaging them with vehicle-mounted heavy weapons.

Varren were omnivores that preferred living prey, native to the krogan homeworld Tuchanka and, like most Tuchanka life, savage, clannish, and highly adaptable. They hunted in packs when prey was easy and scavenged when outmatched, and their adaptability and rapid breeding made them widespread pests wherever the krogan had been. The krogan alternately fought varren and kept them as war beasts and companions; a silver-scaled subgenus was nicknamed "fishdogs."
