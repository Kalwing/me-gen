Oleg Petrovsky was a Cerberus general, an experienced military strategist and a classical scholar with deep knowledge of military history, which shaped his concern for humanity's future against the Reapers and his support for Cerberus. As an Alliance corporal in the First Contact War in 2157, he took command of his unit and held off repeated turian attacks for weeks, witnessing the brutality of the turian war machine; though praised and promoted, he later found more common ground with Cerberus and rose to become the Illusive Man's chief military strategist.

In Mass Effect: Invasion, the Illusive Man dispatched Petrovsky and Colonel Raymond Ashe aboard the starship Elbrus to help Aria T'Loak contain Adjutants unleashed on Omega by a Cerberus transport. Petrovsky was first found euthanizing an Adjutant-wounded turian, arguing that killing the attacked was the only way to stop their transformation. When the defense failed he took the Elbrus through the Omega 4 Relay to clear Cerberus's Avernus Station at the source. On the Illusive Man's orders he restrained an over-exerted Aria and had her dropped at a research station so Cerberus could seize Omega unopposed, though he grew suspicious that the Adjutant release had been a deliberate ruse costing his men. Aria broke free, returned, and united Omega's gangs; Petrovsky and Ashe clashed over Ashe's disregard for troops and civilians, and after Ashe released an Adjutant that infected him, Petrovsky killed his transforming subordinate. Petrovsky then revealed he had already won: he had evacuated most Cerberus forces so the fleet could destroy the station, and gave Aria the choice of destruction or exile. Aria conceded, and Petrovsky secured Omega for Cerberus, predicting she would one day return.

In the Mass Effect 3: Omega DLC, Aria returned aboard a captured Cerberus cruiser; Petrovsky instantly recognized the attack and revealed Omega's upgraded defenses, which cut through the Silaris armor of her ship until she and Commander Shepard boarded and disabled the cannons. He anticipated Aria's moves, letting the squad fight through Adjutant-infested mines to the reactor before trapping Shepard, Aria, and Nyreen Kandros in a force-field, boasting he had beaten them. Aria and Nyreen opened a gap for Shepard to reach the reactor controls, where Petrovsky forced a choice between cutting power (killing civilians via lost life support) or rerouting it (risking Aria and Nyreen); an Engineer Shepard could reroute quickly via omni-tool. Petrovsky made Afterlife his command post, caught Aria in a stasis trap, and unleashed perfected Adjutants and soldiers on Shepard, who freed Aria and destroyed the defenders. Defeated, Petrovsky surrendered. Shepard could hand him to the Alliance (a War Asset worth 30 points), let Aria strangle him to death, or execute him with a headshot (+30 to the Alliance First Fleet). If spared, he cooperated with Alliance interrogators, giving up a Cerberus base location and likely receiving asylum; if killed, Bray disposed of his corpse while Aria's technicians still extracted Cerberus intelligence.

Petrovsky was created by John Jackson Miller for the Invasion comics and was voiced by Brian George, who also voiced Admiral Kahoku.
