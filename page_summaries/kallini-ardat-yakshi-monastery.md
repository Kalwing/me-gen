Kallini: Ardat-Yakshi Monastery was a Mass Effect 3 mission, acquired after Priority: The Citadel II when Liara relayed that Asari High Command wanted Commander Shepard to investigate a distress signal after several asari commando squads vanished. Though titled for the Kallini system, the monastery was actually on Lesuss in the Mesana system of the Nimbus Cluster. On arrival Shepard learned the signal came from an Ardat-Yakshi monastery, which the asari government had been reluctant to disclose. If Samara had survived Mass Effect 2, a recently arrived vehicle indicated her presence, and Shepard met her inside.

Exploring the darkened monastery, the team found asari corpses and logs revealing that Matriarch Gallae and Superintendent Jethra had blocked extranet access to keep students from panicking over Reaper rumors and had refused to evacuate. The mission introduced the Banshee, a Reaper conversion of Ardat-Yakshi, first encountered in the courtyard, followed by Cannibals, Marauders, and Husks. In a cutscene the surviving Ardat-Yakshi Falere begged Shepard and Samara to save her sister Rila from a bomb in the Great Hall, then rushed ahead. Activating the bomb woke Rila, who attacked Falere as indoctrination took hold. After a fight against two Banshees and Husks, Rila took the detonator, insisted her family leave, and declared "The Ardat-Yakshi are not the Reapers' slaves" before setting off the bomb and destroying the monastery. Shepard dragged Falere to the elevator to escape.

In the aftermath, if Samara was present she drew her pistol, saying the Justicar Code required her to kill any Ardat-Yakshi living outside the monastery, and attempted suicide; a Paragon interrupt stopped her, Falere chose to remain, and Samara joined the war effort. If Samara was absent or dead, Shepard chose to let Falere stay or kill her. The mission added the Asari Commandos war asset (and Samara, worth 25 points, if she survived), and the next planned commando squad was redirected to Admiral Hackett. Captain Coreen Lemaes emailed thanks that the monastery had been destroyed. The mission was written by Sylvia Feketekuty.
