Indoctrination was the term for the brainwashing effect the Reapers and their technology exerted on organic beings. A signal or energy field around a Reaper subtly influenced the minds of nearby organics; it was first reported to Shepard by Eden Prime farmers who described a "horrible noise" from Sovereign, and Matriarch Benezia noted that even Sovereign's confusing interior angles served indoctrination. The effect extended to Reaper artifacts, such as the one aboard the MSV Cornucopia that brainwashed its crew. The salarian lieutenant Ganto Imness called it a greater threat than a krogan army. As Vigil explained, the Reapers used indoctrinated slaves from conquered worlds as sleeper agents against the Protheans, then abandoned them to die when they withdrew.

The mechanics were poorly understood but believed to involve an electromagnetic field and infrasound/ultrasound stimulating the brain and limbic system, producing headaches, hallucinations, paranoia, or superstitious awe, and ultimately letting the Reaper use the victim's body to amplify its signal as voices in the mind. Rana Thanoptis, an asari neuroscientist on Virmire, described it as a subtle whisper that compels action without reason; over roughly a week of exposure the subject stops thinking and merely obeys, becoming a mindless husk-like servant, the fate of her predecessor and Kirrahe's captured men. Saren Arterius discovered a balance between control and usefulness: the more control Sovereign had, the less capable the subject, so Saren made himself an invaluable resource to find the Conduit and win a reprieve.

Mental damage was severe and permanent. Only those with immense mental strength could resist, and only briefly. Benezia kept a "haven" in her mind but was trapped watching herself commit atrocities, choosing death on Noveria: "I am not myself, I never will be again." The last Rachni Queen was immune or highly resistant, forcing the Reapers to physically restrain her while she bred warriors. The asari Shiala was the only sentient being to fully escape, because the Thorian's control supplanted Sovereign's. Ironically, indoctrination contributed to Sovereign's defeat: after Virmire, Saren's faltering resolve required cybernetic implants to reinforce control, and when Saren died the Reaper's diverted energy to animate his corpse left Sovereign's shields down and vulnerable to the Alliance Navy, a flaw other Reapers such as Harbinger did not display.

The page also covers similar phenomena: Thorian enthrallment (pain-based conditioning, resistible by disciplined minds, granting cohesion among thralls, ending at the Thorian's death); geth programming (the Reaper virus given to the heretics, and the 2186 geth submitting to a broadcast Reaper code on Rannoch, amplified through a shackled Legion/Geth VI aboard a geth dreadnought); Cerberus experiments (Reaper-grade neural implants used in "integration" at the Barn and Project Phoenix, and Henry Lawson replicating the Reaper signal at Sanctuary on Horizon under the Illusive Man, who was later implanted with Reaper nanotech aboard Cronos Station and briefly controlled Shepard and David Anderson on the Citadel); and Leviathan enthrallment via opalescent artifacts, which caused no long-term mental damage aside from memory loss and was the basis for Reaper indoctrination.
