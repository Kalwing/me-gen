This page covers combat tactics for fighting Tela Vasir, the asari Spectre and Shadow Broker agent faced as the boss of Lair of the Shadow Broker in Mass Effect 2. After a skycar chase around Illium's skyways ends with her crashing into Hotel Azure, a bleeding Vasir takes a hostage and then fights Shepard's team in a plaza, eventually succumbing to her wounds after a protracted battle.

Vasir is described as one of the toughest straight-up fights in the game. Offensively she wields an M-15 Vindicator, uses Shockwave to knock targets out of cover, summons reinforcements of Shadow Broker Engineers and Rocket Drones, and has an extra-powerful, wide-radius Biotic Charge with a long charge time that can be interrupted. Defensively her barrier (stronger than a Praetorian's) and armor are extraordinarily strong, and she repeatedly Charge-teleports around the battlefield. Once her defenses are down she becomes vulnerable to biotics such as Pull, Throw and Dominate, to force effects like Concussive Shot and Tech Armor detonation, and to freezing. She is largely stun-resistant while protected, immune to Flashbang Grenade and the Arc Projector stun, and immune to the hold effects of Singularity and the M-490 Blackstorm until only her health remains.

Recommended tactics include using Liara's Stasis (which can stun Vasir even through her protections and can end waves early), Jack's Warp Ammo against her barrier, anti-barrier weapons such as the Collector Particle Beam or Geth Plasma Shotgun, and knocking her off the edge of the map for an instant kill. Class-specific advice is given for Vanguard, Soldier, Engineer, Sentinel, Infiltrator and Adept.
