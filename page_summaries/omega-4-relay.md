The Omega 4 Relay was a mass relay in the Sahrabarik system of the Omega Nebula, surrounded by hazard beacons and automated warnings. Over the previous thousand years many ships had attempted to pass through it and none had returned; the only ships to travel freely back and forth were the mysterious Collectors. Theories about its far end ranged from a black hole to an earthly paradise, but most assumed the Collectors captured or destroyed those who passed.

In Mass Effect 2 it was revealed that the relay led to the galactic core, where the Collector Base sat on the edge of a black hole. Standard relay transit produced a drift of several thousand kilometers, which was fatal in the galactic core, so the area was choked with debris from ships that had used the relay without a Reaper IFF. The Reaper IFF triggered advanced safety protocols in the Omega 4 Relay that ensured a ship survived close approach to the black hole. It was the only known mass relay to glow red rather than blue, and if Shepard survived the suicide mission, Alliance officials were reported to be extremely concerned about the relay's recent use.
