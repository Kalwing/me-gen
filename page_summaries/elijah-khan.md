Elijah Khan was an arms dealer and owner of the Silver Coast Casino on the Citadel, appearing in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC. Liara T'Soni traced an M-11 Suppressor used by CAT6 mercenaries in an attempt on Commander Shepard's life back to Khan, and found correspondence in which Khan, displeased that CAT6 had publicly used his weapons, returned their downpayment and warned that he held information about his client. Shepard traveled to Khan's casino to obtain the identity of the gun's purchaser, but on infiltrating his office found Khan already dead. It was later revealed that Maya Brooks, an ex-Cerberus agent, had executed Khan to tie up loose ends.
