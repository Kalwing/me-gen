"Aria: Eclipse" was a Mass Effect 3 mission, part of Aria T'Loak's trilogy of tasks to unite mercenary bands under her control in exchange for the Terminus Fleet War Asset. It became available after Priority: Palaven, once the primarch had been retrieved from Palaven, by speaking with Aria at the Purgatory bar on the Citadel. It was written by Neil Pollner.

Aria sent Shepard to secure the release of the jailed Eclipse founder and leader Jona Sederis. Commander Bailey, at the Citadel Embassies, insisted that Shepard speak with Sederis first; Shepard did so via a vidcomm conversation at the Presidium Commons C-Sec outpost, gaining morality points. Afterward, Shepard could either pressure Bailey to release Sederis, which ended the mission with Sederis resuming command of Eclipse, or track down her salarian second-in-command Sayn, who led the band in her absence.

Sayn was found in the processing area of the Docks holding area, near the Shepard VI, where he was plotting to break Sederis out of prison. Shepard could remind him that he was currently in charge and convince him either to let Sederis rot in jail or to have her killed upon her "release." Either way, Sayn took over. Regardless of the outcome, the Eclipse leadership submitted to Aria's authority, the Terminus Fleet War Asset was updated by 50 points, and Shepard received 210 experience, 5,000 credits, and morality points. A bug could softlock the mission and Aria's entire tri-mission arc if the player saved and quit immediately after talking to Sayn.
