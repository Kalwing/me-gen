This page collects a promotional social-media broadcast BioWare ran in 2186 (real-world 2012) to advertise the Mass Effect 3: Leviathan DLC. In the fiction, Bernard Plim, a self-proclaimed anti-conspiracy activist claiming twenty-three years of experience, founder and president of the Conspiracy Accountability League, hacked the Alliance News Network to publicize his claims. He operated through his blog, the hijacked @AllianceNewsNet channel, and his personal @BernardPlim channel, repeatedly begging readers for credits and dodging C-Sec. His central claim was that Admiral Hackett, Dr. Garret Bryson and a secret group called Task Force Aurora were secretly studying the Reapers and the Leviathan of Dis, and that the Alliance and Citadel Council were hiding this. He cited the 2163 batarian discovery of a crashed starship on Jartar/Jatar that then "disappeared" after a batarian dreadnought visited, plus supposed salarian vid footage, and a coffee-shop receipt reading "Bryson, Garneau – Leviathan discussion." His hack was shut down by C-Sec, who arrested him; he was released and vowed to continue. Trivia notes Task Force Aurora is the real Leviathan-DLC plot thread, and that Plim appears on the Armax Arsenal Arena and Castle Arcade leaderboards in the Citadel DLC.

Representative lines:

Bernard Plim, opening the hack: "Top of the line security, huh? Hmph. I don't think so! ... I am Bernard Plim and I speak the truth!"

Bernard Plim, boasting on his channel: "I would have liked to be in the room at the ANN when they discovered that they'd been hacked! Wasn't hard. #babystuff #findleviathan"

ANN (hacked), pushing his claims: "I have NEW EVIDENCE that Task Force Aurora is also studying the LEVIATHAN OF DIS! Admiral Hackett – WHY?!"

Bernard Plim, on the crashed ship's cover-up: "Batarians are liars and assholes (my opinion *only* based on personal experience on Omega ...)"

ANN (hacked), as C-Sec closes in: "AHHHHHH!! C-SEC is here! ANN is an ALLIANCE PATSY!! Send help! #FindLeviathan"

Bernard Plim, after release: "BERNARD PLIM SAYS... 'TRUTH HUNTER's ... HUNT ON!!!'"
