The Project Phoenix Adept was a playable multiplayer class in Mass Effect 3, one of the Project Phoenix operatives. Project Phoenix was a result of the Illusive Man's early experiments in enhanced human physiology, producing highly skilled combat and biotic specialists. When the Illusive Man turned to indoctrination to guarantee the loyalty of his troops, many Phoenix operatives defected and joined the Alliance to fight the Reapers. Their mastery of Cerberus lash and stasis technology, combined with powerful biotics, made them valued battlefield assets.

The Adept had 500 barrier and 500 health and used the powers Singularity, Lash, Smash and Fitness under Phoenix Training. Its regular melee was a shock baton strike hitting up to two enemies; its heavy melee swung biotically charged Cerberus lashes; and its grab dragged an enemy over cover for a charged biotic punch. Singularity primed biotic explosions on all enemies and could be chained into double explosions with Lash or Smash. Lash functioned like a damaging, long-range Pull that could be evolved to penetrate shields and barriers. Smash was a short-range, cover-piercing attack devastating against infantry and armored bosses but leaving the user briefly vulnerable. The class's main weakness was dealing with heavy enemies such as Banshees, Brutes, Atlases, Geth Pyros and Geth Primes, since Smash's short range forced dangerous proximity.
