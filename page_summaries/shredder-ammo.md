Shredder Ammo was an ammo power in Mass Effect 2 that increased weapon damage against the health of organic targets. It tore through soft, unshielded organic enemies, adding +40% weapon damage at rank 1, rising to +60% at rank 3, with a 1.5-second recharge and a duration lasting until overridden by another ammo power. Rank 4 evolved it into Improved Shredder Ammo (+80% weapon damage) or Squad Shredder Ammo, which extended the +60% bonus to the entire squad.

The power had the highest damage bonus of all ammo powers, but the bonus applied only to the red health bar, so targets had to be organic and stripped of shields, armor, or barriers first, making it less versatile than ammo powers with multiple bonuses. It was most useful on lower difficulties where organics lacked extra protection, and on higher difficulties against lightly protected foes such as vorcha and varren or high-health enemies such as krogan, especially when paired with a strong weapon like the M-12 Locust against frozen or ragdolled targets. It was noted as an effective "one-point wonder" for a large damage boost at minimal squad-point cost. Shredder Ammo was available to Shepard via Advanced Training and as Thane Krios's loyalty power. A separate Mass Effect ammo upgrade called Shredder Rounds was unrelated.
