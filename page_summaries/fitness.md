Fitness was a survival-oriented talent in Mass Effect that Soldiers, Infiltrators, and Krogan Battlemasters could unlock. It increased maximum health at most ranks, from a 10 percent bonus at level 1 to 30 percent at level 11, and at level 4 granted the Immunity power, upgraded to Advanced Immunity at level 8 and Master Immunity at level 12.

Immunity, when activated, reduced most damage done to health (not shields) by a set percentage, making it valuable when shields were down or against damage shields did not mitigate, such as rocket explosions. In the original game it increased damage protection by 50, 60, or 80 percent at its three tiers, with durations of 10 to 20 seconds, recharge times of 60 down to 30 seconds, and a 30 percent accuracy cost. At high levels it could be kept active almost indefinitely. It could be countered by stunning or immobilizing the user before activation, or by powers that reduced damage resistance such as Warp or Overload, and it did not protect against the tech-based electrical attacks of Husks. Powerful krogan enemies, and on higher difficulties human and turian enemies, also used Immunity, indicated by a whitened health bar. Squadmates activated it only after taking health damage, so ordering it manually before combat was often wise.

In Legendary Edition, Immunity had a fixed six-second duration and 45-second cooldown and could no longer be maintained continuously, but offered higher damage resistance of 75, 85, or 90 percent. Commandos and Soldier-based Shock Troopers gained Immunity Specialization at level 9, reducing its recharge time by 25 percent.
