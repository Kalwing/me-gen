The Arcturian Jade was a luxury passenger liner featured in Mass Effect Galaxy. Its only identified crew were its captain, Reginald Tudge, and a porter. After retiring as an Alliance marine, Jacob Taylor took a vacation aboard the vessel. While the Arcturian Jade was travelling toward the Citadel, batarian terrorists attacked the ship. Jacob, who had kept his assault rifle from his time as a marine, used his combat experience to fight the batarians and defend the passengers and crew.

Jacob intercepted the batarians as they moved toward the bridge. He confronted and questioned their leader, who explained his motives: he wanted to teach humanity a lesson in obedience, resenting what he described as the human "sense of entitlement" and the Council's refusal to address batarian demands. The leader admitted he had ordered the attack knowing that the ship's proximity to the Citadel made the operation a suicide mission. After Jacob defeated the batarians and saved the ship, Reginald Tudge offered him his choice of cabins for the remainder of the voyage to the Citadel.

Jacob later returned to his vacation aboard the Arcturian Jade after foiling Jath'Amon's attack on the Citadel. On this occasion he was unexpectedly joined by Miranda Lawson, who brought a bottle of champagne. The associated mission was titled "Save the Arcturian Jade."
