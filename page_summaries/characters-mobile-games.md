This page was a roster index for the Mass Effect mobile games, listing character links per title grouped as protagonist, allies, and adversaries. It covered two games with roughly thirteen entries total: Mass Effect Galaxy (protagonist Jacob Taylor; allies Miranda Lawson, Derek Izunami, Nax and Batha; adversaries Jath'Amon, Clint "Black Eye" Darragh and Illo Nazario) and Mass Effect: Infiltrator (protagonist Randall Ezno; allies Inali Renata and a volus; adversaries a turian and the Director). It contained no original prose beyond the link tables.
