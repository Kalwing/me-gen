Fai Dan was the human leader of the Zhu's Hope colony on Feros. He explained the situation on Feros to Commander Shepard, including details of the geth attack, and was polite toward the Commander, chastising Arcelia Silva Martinez for being rude. He provided basic information about the planet and colony but could offer no motive for the geth attack. Other colonists repeatedly directed Shepard to Fai Dan for information, yet he was reluctant to discuss some subjects, especially Ian Newstead. He cared deeply for the colonists and anxiously sought Shepard's help, asking the Commander to destroy a geth transmitter in the tunnels that was coordinating the attacks.

Like the other colonists, Fai Dan had been infected with Thorian spores but retained more autonomy than the others, and the Thorian may have used him as a local controller because he was their leader. After Shepard returned from the ExoGeni headquarters and disarmed the Thorian-controlled colonists, the squad encountered Fai Dan, who approached with a pistol while struggling against the Thorian's influence. He lamented the colonists' infection, feeling responsible as their leader, and pointed the pistol at Shepard, saying the Thorian wanted the Commander stopped. Before Shepard could fire in self-defense, Fai Dan overcame the Thorian's control and shot himself in the head. His voice actor, Armin Shimerman, also voiced the salarian Councilor Valern.
