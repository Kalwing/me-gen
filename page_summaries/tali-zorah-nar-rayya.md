Tali'Zorah nar Rayya was born in 2161 aboard the liveship Rayya to Rael'Zorah of the quarian Admiralty Board. A mechanical genius, she left the Migrant Fleet on her Pilgrimage, the quarian rite of passage into adulthood. In Mass Effect: Homeworlds she traveled aboard the Honorata with Keenah'Breizh, extracted a geth memory core containing a recording of an attack planned on Eden Prime and the coming of the Reapers, and was hunted across Illium and onto the Citadel by the turian mercenary Jacobus, working for Saren Arterius. Keenah died; Tali trapped Jacobus in an incinerator and burned him alive, then sought an information broker to reach the Council.

In Mass Effect, Fist betrayed her to Saren's agents; ambushed near Chora's Den, Tali threw a tech proximity mine and was saved by Commander Shepard. Her data provided proof that got Saren's Spectre status revoked and the first mention of the Reapers and the Conduit. She joined Shepard's crew, working in the Normandy's engine room with Engineer Adams, and shared insight into quarian nomad life and the geth. In an optional assignment she could receive rogue geth data to complete her Pilgrimage; refusing it caused her to leave the crew afterward.

In Mass Effect 2, having completed her Pilgrimage, she joined the Neema and became Tali'Zorah vas Neema. Shepard met her on Freedom's Progress hunting Veetor'Nara, then recruited her on Haestrom, where a geth patrol killed her marine escort; she and Kal'Reegar survived and she brought data proving Haestrom's sun was dying prematurely. She joined Shepard for Shepard's sake, not Cerberus's. Her loyalty mission, Tali: Treason, changed her name to Tali'Zorah vas Normandy. She is a romance option for a male Shepard, culminating before the Omega 4 relay when she removes her mask; if she survives, "it was totally worth it."

In Mass Effect 3, if she survived the Suicide Mission, she returned to the Fleet, becoming an Admiral in her father's place (or a secret adviser if exiled) during the war to retake Rannoch. She served as geth software expert aboard the geth dreadnought and on Rannoch, where she claimed a patch of land and Shepard handed her a rock. At the climax, if Shepard upgraded the geth she watched the Migrant Fleet burn, removed her mask, whispered "I'm sorry," and threw herself off a cliff. If Shepard stopped the upload she stabbed the geth unit with her boot knife; if Legion survived and peace was brokered, she confirmed the geth had souls. Surviving, she rejoined the Normandy and could become the quarian ambassador on the Citadel. If romanced, she left Shepard a photo of her unmasked face, and at the final battle, gravely wounded, told Shepard "I have a home" and begged him to return.
