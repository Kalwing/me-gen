This archive collected Cerberus Daily News reports from August 2185. On Garvug, Binary Helix representative Grigori Petrenko criticized Sonax Industries over suspected chemical weapons, a leaked video showed Sonax soldiers executing krogan into a mass grave, and Binary Helix withdrew logistical support from the corporatist alliance. Sonax brigadier general Noah Vardos, a close friend of supreme commander Lira Speight, was found dead of a gunshot at a base near Dhazil; the death was ruled a suicide, with a note blaming himself for failing to catch three krogan tied to a warp-bomb attack, and Colonel Oleg Horvat took his post. A recurring story followed an alien "ghost ship" in the salarian Antilin System: an AI-piloted vessel roughly 8,000 years old that housed close to a billion aliens who had uploaded their consciousness into quantum computers and lived in an accelerated virtual world tended by the ship's AI. MIT exo-biologist Jordan Detweiler made contact by passing an intelligence test; debate raged over whether to supply power to save the civilization, with turian scientist Elgus Paramon arguing it should be allowed to perish, hackers who boarded the ship left comatose after 180 virtual years passed, and the Citadel Council ultimately classifying the matter top-secret after the virtual aliens offered advanced science in exchange for aid. General Partinax declared an end to major combat operations in the Diluvian Wildlands on Taetrus, and reconstruction of the Signis executive building was completed. Illium Fashion Week was consumed by fallout after editor Carson Noble made racist remarks about quarian-inspired designs by Scarlet Jane, who boycotted the Luminary Gala. Other reports covered C-SEC officer Tarmiel East's viral bare-handed fight with krogan drug-runner Hunjon Sard, the "Mother's Star" diamond, the capture of a 35-meter deinorostrum on Arvuna, the psyotics body-modification trend, plastic surgeon Erzsebet Vidmar ("Vidmar the Vampire") arrested for murdering asari for telomerase, Aish Ashland's secret marriage to singer Jenellen Lepp, and turian con man Rolan Quarn, who impersonated dead CEO Jaxum Borlin for two years before being exposed, linked to dozens of other stolen identities, and escaping custody.
