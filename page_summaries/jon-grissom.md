Rear Admiral Jon Grissom was a famed Systems Alliance hero. In 2149, when the Charon Relay was discovered orbiting Pluto and activated, none of the probes sent through had enough power to transmit a message back, so the Alliance concluded a crewed team had to be sent. Grissom was chosen as team leader and was hailed as a hero on his return, celebrated as one of the great pioneers of human space exploration. Personally, he was a sullen, retiring man who disliked the attention. Though he took a leading role in the First Contact War, in 2160 he became a recluse on Elysium and refused all interviews and contact with Alliance media.

In 2157, Grissom was en route to Arcturus to meet the new graduates of the N7 program when news of the attack on Shanxi broke; he discussed the developing situation with graduate David Anderson. After the war, during his retirement on Elysium, his daughter Kahlee Sanders came to him for help after the destruction of Sidon. Grissom hid her, tried to put Anderson off the case, and guarded the house with a shotgun by night. When Skarr arrived, Grissom tried to fight him off and was caught in the krogan's line of fire. He survived but refused medical treatment to avoid attracting attention to Kahlee, then used his connections to get her secretly off the planet with Anderson as her protector.

The Jon Grissom Academy was named after him. In 2183, Jiro Toshiwa's knowledge that Kahlee was Grissom's daughter, a classified fact, exposed him as a traitor. Grissom died of natural causes in late 2185 or early 2186, six months before the incident involving Paul Grayson. Hundreds of dignitaries, including Anderson, attended his funeral; Kahlee attended as a member of the Academy faculty to preserve the secret that he was her father. A memorial plaque bearing his name and likeness was erected in the Academy's mess hall. His name may reference astronaut Gus Grissom.
