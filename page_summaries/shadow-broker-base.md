The Shadow Broker Base was a massive unnamed ship that traveled through the volatile atmosphere of Hagalaz, whose electrical storms concealed its presence. From here the Shadow Broker ran his information empire.

The exterior was hazardous, patrolled by the Broker's private army and tended by Maintenance Drones, with no handrails or guardrails; large conducting rods channeled lightning into hull-mounted capacitors to protect patrols. The interior held the engines, which had to run constantly to keep the ship aloft, a central operations center, a prison block and torture facilities for interrogation, and the Broker's office, Intel Center, and private quarters, where a custom VI Drone helped manage the network of informants and agents. Motion dampeners absorbed the noise and vibration of the storm.

After the yahg Shadow Broker's defeat and Liara T'Soni's ascension as the new Broker, Commander Shepard gained free access to the base, where drones repaired the battle damage over subsequent visits. Veetor's Freedom's Progress footage of the Collector abductions could be seen on screens there. The Intel Center held numerous terminals: Advanced Training (respec a squadmate for 1000 element zero), an Armor Locker, Delivery Pickup (daily shipments of weapon upgrades or randomized resources), Dossiers (intelligence files on the squad and others), the Info Drone, Investment Opportunities, a Research Terminal, a collectible Shadow Broker ship model, shutter and sound-system controls, Survey Information (buy locations of resource-rich planets), and the Video Archive of surveillance clips from across the galaxy.

Sometime before the 2186 Reaper invasion, Liara learned the Illusive Man had tracked her and intended to seize the base for Cerberus. Knowing the aging ship could not travel in space, she and Feron loaded as much information and communications equipment onto a shuttle as they could, escaped, and remotely crashed the base into a Cerberus cruiser, destroying both ships. Assorted tech salvaged from the wreckage was later used to help build the Crucible.
