The krogan were large reptilian bipeds native to Tuchanka, a harsh world of scarce resources and vicious predators. They thrived there, but as their technology advanced their birth rate exploded and wars over dwindling territory escalated into a global nuclear war roughly four thousand years ago that left Tuchanka a radioactive wasteland and the krogan reduced to primitive warring clans. Two thousand years later the salarians discovered and "uplifted" them, relocating some krogan and giving them technology including the Shroud to stabilize Tuchanka's atmosphere, so that krogan numbers could be thrown against the rachni in the Rachni Wars. The rapidly breeding krogan drove back and eradicated the rachni, were hailed as saviors, granted conquered and Citadel worlds, and honored with the Krogan Monument on the Presidium.

Unchecked by Tuchanka's conditions, the krogan population swelled and they forcibly seized inhabited worlds. When they settled the asari colony Lusia and Overlord Kredak defied the Council, the Krogan Rebellions broke out, lasting nearly three centuries. The krogan used redirected asteroids as weapons; in desperation the Council enlisted the Turian Hierarchy, who deployed the salarian-engineered genophage, causing near-total infant mortality (roughly one viable birth per thousand). The krogan surrendered, the turians gained a Council seat, and krogan numbers steadily declined, leaving a scattered, fatalistic people who mostly worked as mercenaries.

Krogan biology featured redundant organs (two hearts, four lungs, four testicles, hence the slang "quad"), a neuroconductive backup circulatory system making them nearly impossible to paralyze, thick hazard-resistant hides, shoulder humps storing fluids, 240-degree vision, lifespans exceeding a thousand years, and a berserk "blood rage." Culture prized strength, self-reliance and territory; the weak were "not worth killing." Fertile females were prized, lived in separate clans, and were the level-headed counterparts to the males. Rites (Rite of Life, Passage, Honor, Firsts, Union, Authority) were preserved by the nameless clan shaman; the dead were consigned to the Void on funeral pyres or displayed as skulls in the sacred Hollows. Ancient deities included Vaul and Uzin. In 2185 Mordin Solus revealed the krogan were evolving against the genophage and his team applied a modified version. In the 2186 Reaper War, Shepard could cure the genophage at the Shroud (with Eve, Mordin or Padok Wiks) in exchange for krogan aid, or let the salarian sabotage stand; outcomes ranged from krogan repopulation under Urdnot Wrex to Urdnot Wreav being deceived. Separately, Clan Nakmor joined the Andromeda Initiative, later founded New Tuchanka on Elaaden under Overlord Nakmor Morda after a mutiny and a broken Nexus leadership deal.
