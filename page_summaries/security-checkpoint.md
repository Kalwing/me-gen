Security Checkpoint was a level in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, following the Medical Bay mission and preceding the X1 Wing. Guided by his volus ally, the rogue Cerberus operative Randall Ezno fought his way toward the exterior of the Cerberus base, aiming to reach the base's Communication Relay Tower so he could contact the Alliance. The level contained two checkpoints.

At the first checkpoint, Randall crossed a short corridor defended by Orcus Mechs and Riot Troopers. Enemy squads emerged from doors along the corridor, creating a risk of crossfire for a player who advanced too quickly. Biotic powers such as Pull and Leash were recommended against shielded foes, and switching weapons between kills helped earn the "Veteran" ranking. At the far end, Randall had to equip a helmet to gain access to the outdoor security checkpoint.

At the second checkpoint, Randall crossed a quiet platform and was caught in a Cerberus trap. An Ogre Mech appeared in the center of the platform, supported by an endless wave of Riot Troopers attacking at close range. A sniper rifle was effective against the mech from a distance, while short-range weapons and biotics suited the troopers. Sustained fire to the mech's head could destroy its eye, removing its ability to target and causing it to fire its Storm Launcher randomly, potentially harming its own allies. The player was advised to stay in cover, use Tactical Cloak when focused or overwhelmed, and relocate across the platform if pinned down. The level ended when Randall defeated the Ogre Mech and entered the X1 Wing. Enemies encountered were the Orcus Mech, Riot Trooper, and Ogre Mech.
