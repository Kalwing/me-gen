Poison Strike was a biotic power exclusive to Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode, available only to the Cabal Vanguard (a turian Cabal Vanguard). Activating it launched the character a set distance forward encased in a biotic barrier, slashing through any enemies in her path for instant biotic damage (500 at rank 1) and applying a poison damage-over-time effect (75 per second for 5 seconds). The user was invulnerable during the power's animation. Base recharge was 8 seconds and range 12 meters. It hit a maximum of three targets per use and did not lock onto a target or restore the user's barrier.

Rank upgrades improved recharge speed, damage, range, poison duration, and poison strength, with a rank 6 choice between increased poison damage (plus paralyzing up to two unshielded enemies for 4 seconds) and a "Double Dash" evolution that reduced dash range but triggered a cooldown only every other dash. Poison Strike filled a mobility role similar to Biotic Charge, Havoc Strike, and Shadow Strike, but could be used to pass quickly through walls and obstacles. Like Charge, it could detonate all four types of Power Combos, making it a universal detonator. Aiming slightly into the floor shortened the dash for more precise positioning, useful for reviving teammates, and with Double Dash the Cabal became an effective medic.
