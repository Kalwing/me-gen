Synthetic Insights, Ltd. was one of only four corporations licensed by the Citadel Council to develop artificial intelligence. It had known operations on Illium and Noveria, with extensive holdings on Noveria where tight security allowed it to work without harassment from politically hostile organisations, prompting heavy investment there. Its manager at Noveria's Port Hanshan was Lorik Qui'in. Products attributed to the company included the graybox, released to market in 2160, and the Synthetics Insights SleepEasy Series of security VIs, which could run mechs and featured a fast response matrix and an industry-standard cyberwarfare defensive suite. The company may have been one of the capital contributors to the Noveria Development Corporation, though Qui'in noted it relied on the goodwill of the NDC executive board to operate.

In Mass Effect, Commander Shepard recalled hearing the company mentioned in the news, possibly consulted or criticised over the geth attacks on human colonies. In 2183, its Noveria offices were temporarily shut down by Administrator Anoleis pending an inquiry into Qui'in's knowledge of the administrator's corruption; Qui'in kept incriminating data about Anoleis on a terminal inside the searched offices.

By Mass Effect 2, trust in AI research had fallen since the geth attack on the Citadel, dragging down the company's reputation so that two years later it barely hired anyone. It maintained a strict non-discrimination employment policy and refused to use slave labour or indentured servants. On Illium, Shepard could mediate a dispute between an indentured quarian, her contract broker and Synthetic Insights, with outcomes ranging from the company seizing the chance as an anti-slavery PR stunt, to being pressured into hiring the quarian under threat of increased bureaucratic scrutiny, to Shepard failing to move their representative at all.
