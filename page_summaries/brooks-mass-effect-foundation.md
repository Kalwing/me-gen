Brooks was a human agent employed by the Shadow Broker who appeared in the comic Mass Effect: Foundation. It was unknown whether "Brooks" was her true name, as she never directly identified herself and was only referred to as "Miss Brooks" upon access to her personal starship. The article distinguished her from Maya Brooks of Mass Effect 3: Citadel.

In 2161 CE, Brooks was dispatched to an asteroid-mining facility orbiting the gas giant Themis to eliminate Roth, a major financier of the human survivalist organization Cerberus. Searching for Roth, she encountered a young boy working in the mines, spared him a beating from the mine's foreman, then interrogated and killed the foreman for Roth's location. She used the boy to infiltrate a clinic staff room by posing as his mother and claiming he had fallen ill, obtained Roth's address from the clinic's computer, and disguised herself as a doctor to enter Roth's quarters to deliver medication. Inside, she forced a lone man at knifepoint to wire the banker Barla Von a considerable sum, but the man revealed he was only a decoy, broke free, drew a bladed claw weapon, fought her, and shot her. The boy distracted the man long enough for Brooks to disable him; she forced him to reveal that the real Roth had left for Earth, then killed him and left with the boy. A facility administrator confirmed Roth had gone but gave no destination.

Preparing to depart, Brooks firmly refused the boy's plea for a ride off the station, explaining that she worked alone because she did not trust people and knew that if captured he could be forced to betray her. As she turned to board her ship, the boy shot her in the back, mortally wounding her, then revealed that he was actually a girl who, though she had learned not to trust people, had hoped she could trust Brooks. The girl boarded Brooks' ship and left her behind to die.
