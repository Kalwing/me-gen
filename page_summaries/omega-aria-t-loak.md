"Omega: Aria T'Loak" was a Mass Effect 2 mission acquired automatically the first time Commander Shepard arrived at Omega, after the prerequisite "Freedom's Progress." Aria T'Loak, ruler of Omega, wanted all important new arrivals to pay their respects at Afterlife, and was a potentially valuable source of information.

On docking, a salarian named Fargut tried to ingratiate himself before being muscled aside by Moklan, a batarian mercenary working for Aria, who instructed Shepard to visit Aria; neither Fargut nor Moklan interacted with Shepard again. Entering Afterlife past an elcor bouncer, Shepard could be accosted by a batarian named Kylan and his cohorts, with morality points available for dealing with them. Aria sat atop a platform on the far side of the main club floor. Her guards scanned Shepard to verify identity against impostors; morality points were available for resisting the scan and after Aria explained Omega's one rule ("Don't fuck with Aria"). Shepard could then ask about Omega and the dossiers or end the meeting, with questions still available later. The mission completed after the initial meeting. Although not formally part of the mission, speaking with Aria was required to advance "Dossier: Archangel," as she pointed Shepard to the relevant people; it was not required for "Dossier: The Professor."
