The Castle Arcade was an electronic gaming arcade on the Citadel's Silversun Strip. It contained three playable arcade games, Shattered Eezo, the Claw Game and Relay Defense, plus non-playable Kepesh-Yakshi and Towers of Hanoi consoles. Playing the games earned prizes including weapon mods, Personal Apartment decorations and music tracks, and credits, and various teammates could be met there.

Shattered Eezo was a robot boxing game in which the player's blue mech fought successively harder CPU-controlled orange mechs, regenerating only part of its health between rounds while each new opponent started at full health. Beating the high score of 1024 held by Aria T'Loak awarded an Assault Rifle Thermal Scope mod. When meeting Jacob Taylor at the arcade, Shepard could fight him as the orange mech. The Claw Game had the player grab colour-shifting orbs, with orange orbs giving weapon mods, purple a plush volus doll, and blue and green music tracks or credit payouts; Zaeed Massani was found here trying to win the volus doll for a child who had already left, and a conversation with him had to be completed before Shepard could play. Relay Defense had the player shoot turian fighters and cruisers on a 3x3 grid while sending allied shuttles to a mass relay to build a score multiplier of up to x4; its high score was held by Mouse rather than Aria, and beating it gave a Shotgun Ultralight Materials mod. Other listed high scores included Bailey and Schells on Relay Defense and James Vega, Barla Von and Al-Jilani on Shattered Eezo.

Kepesh-Yakshi, translating from asari as "blade of the night winds" and nicknamed "space-chess" by Shepard, was a strategy game played with a "homeworld" planet and opposing ships that shocked players who sacrificed pieces carelessly; Samantha Traynor was seen playing it against a rival named Polgara T'Suzsa when Shepard met her at the arcade after an email invitation. A Towers of Hanoi console also existed, referencing the reactor puzzle at Peak 15, but Shepard declined to play it.
