"Dossier: The Master Thief" was a short Mass Effect 2 recruitment mission (requiring the Kasumi - Stolen Memory DLC) acquired after Freedom's Progress. The dossier described Kasumi Goto as the best thief in the galaxy, a master of stealth and infiltration who operated off the grid with a clean criminal record, awaiting rendezvous with Shepard on the Citadel in Zakera Ward. Shepard found Kasumi's advertisement terminal at the Normandy's dock; she spoke through it while transmitting from the catwalks above. Recruitment was completed simply by welcoming her aboard. Through dialogue Shepard could learn about her history with Cerberus, her deal with them, the planned heist against Donovan Hock, grayboxes, and the evening wear she had procured for Shepard. After the conversation the terminal retracted, Kasumi spoke to Shepard in person, and then cloaked. She could afterward be found in the port observation lounge on the crew deck. Unlike normal recruitment missions, completing it did not advance squadmate conversations or trigger a loyalty mission. Cut content indicates Kasumi was originally to be recruited on Illium in a lengthy gunship-chase mission.
