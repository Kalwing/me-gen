Virmire: Assault was the climactic mission of the Virmire chain in Mass Effect, in which Commander Shepard infiltrated and destroyed Saren Arterius's research base while Captain Kirrahe's salarian teams staged a diversionary frontal assault to draw off the geth. It followed Virmire: Saren's Plan and was acquired on leaving the salarian camp. The mission ran concurrently with the assignment Virmire: Assisting Kirrahe's Team, in which Shepard could sabotage geth communications, satellite uplinks, and air support; destroying the geth flyers was required for Kirrahe to survive.

Shepard and two squadmates advanced west past a communications tower, satellite uplink tower, and refueling platform to reach the research facility, fighting geth and krogan. Entry to the base was possible through an upper warehouse door or lower maintenance underworks, where a console offered a choice to disable base alarms (more enemies for Shepard, more experience) or trigger alarms elsewhere (more enemies against Kirrahe's teams). Inside, Shepard passed cell blocks holding indoctrinated salarians, including Private Menos Avot and Lieutenant Ganto Imness, whom Shepard could release or leave to die. In Saren's genophage lab the krogan researcher Doctor Droyas and an asari assistant attacked alongside husks. The scientist Rana Thanoptis, encountered in an office, provided information on indoctrination and Sovereign and could be spared or killed; if spared she reappeared in Mass Effect 2's Dossier: The Warlord and, in Mass Effect 3, was revealed via an email to have been indoctrinated all along and to have killed herself.

Shepard activated a Prothean beacon in Saren's private lab, receiving the full vision without losing consciousness. A red hologram then revealed that Sovereign was not a Reaper ship but an actual Reaper; after a brief exchange Sovereign ended the conversation and shattered the lab's glass. Joker reported Sovereign executing an impossible maneuver. Shepard fought through krogan and geth to disable two anti-aircraft towers so the Normandy could approach. The squadmate not sent with Kirrahe brought the Normandy's drive core, converted into a nuclear bomb, and placed it by the geothermal taps. When geth dropships assaulted the bomb site, Shepard had to choose to save either Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko; the other stayed behind and died in the blast. Saren attacked from a hover-platform and, after taking damage, grabbed Shepard, then fled when the Normandy arrived. Joker outran the detonation and the base was destroyed. In the debriefing the surviving squadmate argued they were the wrong choice; the Council offered half-hearted condolences. The Virmire survivor never returned as a squadmate in Mass Effect 2 (appearing only briefly on Horizon) but became available again in Mass Effect 3.
