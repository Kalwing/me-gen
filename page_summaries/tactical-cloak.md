Tactical Cloak was a tech power in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3 (single-player and multiplayer) that rendered the user invisible to most enemies for a short time and granted a damage bonus to attacks made from concealment. Performing any offensive action broke the cloak, and activating it halted shield regeneration for its duration.

In Mass Effect 2 it was the Infiltrator's power. Its first three ranks improved the damage bonus and slightly extended duration, and Rank 4 evolved it into Assassination Cloak (a large damage bonus for attacking while cloaked) or Enhanced Cloak (a longer duration for bypassing enemies or setting up a strike). The damage bonus applied to weapons and melee but not to powers or most heavy weapons, making it most valuable on single-shot weapons like sniper rifles and shotguns. Cloaking did not stop automated attacks such as missiles, and certain enemies (the Oculus, Geth Colossus and Thresher Maw) continued to pursue a cloaked Shepard.

In Mass Effect 3 the power was available to Shepard's Infiltrator class, to Kasumi Goto (with her own rank layout), and to many multiplayer Infiltrator characters. Its six ranks offered choices between recharge speed and durability, duration and damage, recharge and melee damage, and at Rank 6 a Bonus Power option (fire one power while remaining cloaked) versus a weapon-type damage bonus that varied by character (sniper rifle, assault rifle, biotic power or shotgun). Key differences from Mass Effect 2 were that the damage bonus now applied to all damage including powers and grenades (but not power combos), lingered about two seconds after a deliberate decloak, and that the recharge time was proportional to how long the cloak had been active, with a three-second minimum. Grenade powers did not break the cloak, though enemies could detect a player after grenades were thrown.
