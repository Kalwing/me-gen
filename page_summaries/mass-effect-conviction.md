Mass Effect: Conviction is an eight-page mini-comic written by Mac Walters, with art by Eduardo Francisco and colors by Michael Atiyeh. It was released for free through Dark Horse Digital in September 2011 and is no longer available standalone, but is collected in compendiums including the graphic novel release of Mass Effect: Invasion, Volume 1 of the Mass Effect Library Edition, and Mass Effect: The Complete Comics. It details how Alliance soldier James Vega came to be part of Commander Shepard's trial on Earth.

The story takes place days after the destruction of Bahak, with the galaxy in turmoil over Shepard's alleged actions. The Citadel Council denounces Shepard, Councilor Udina deflects blame from the Systems Alliance, and the Batarian Hegemony demands retribution. On Omega, James Vega plays cards in a bar while watching a newscast about the uproar, then rips the screen from the wall and offers to pay the krogan bartender for the damage. The batarians he was playing with accuse him of being a "Shepard Lover" and attack; Vega fights the group, smashing one with the screen and crashing through a window with another.

Admiral Anderson arrives with a contingent of soldiers and tells Vega he is being brought in for more training. In an aircar approaching the Normandy SR-2, Anderson tells Vega he must get past "the incident on Fehl" and become the soldier they expect. Vega demands to be thrown in the brig instead; Anderson reveals that Vega will be guarding the brig, and one special prisoner: Commander Shepard. A single omniscient narration panel assumes Shepard destroyed Bahak, though the rest of the comic treats it as alleged; players without the Arrival DLC may instead learn Alliance marines were responsible.
