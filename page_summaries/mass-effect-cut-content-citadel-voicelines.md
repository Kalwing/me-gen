This page collects cut and non-functional dialogue from the first Mass Effect relating to Citadel missions and assignments, reconstructed from leftover dialogue files (some with only partial text surviving). It is explicitly non-canon. Highlights include ambient conversations for the Presidium, Chora's Den, the Council Chambers and Citadel traffic control; alternate versions of the Dr. Michel/Garrus clinic scene and the Chora's Den assassin ambush; an alternate-reality Avina tour describing the Krogan Monument being removed after the Krogan Rebellions; and lines explaining why Shepard knew Schells had cheated.

Avina, alternate history of the Krogan Monument: "This site was once the location of a memorial statue commemorating the krogan soldiers who gave their lives to protect Citadel Space during the Rachni Wars. In the wake of the Krogan Rebellions, several embassies petitioned to have the memorial removed... The memorial was removed despite their reservations."

Unnamed volus diplomat, ambient on the Presidium: "If I have to fill out one more form, I think I might just go work the mines in Caleston."

Unnamed salarian, overheard in Chora's Den about the Council: "The Council spends so much time bickering and posturing they don't get to make any real decisions."

Unnamed asari diplomat, in the Citadel Tower when a human enters: "Humans in the Tower? Interesting. Try not to break anything."

Citadel traffic controller, ambient near C-Sec academy: "Destiny Ascension, we're going to have to ask you to gain some distance. Your mass is disturbing ward rotation."

Garrus, alternate clinic scene (line originally given to Garrus rather than Shepard): "Let her go!"

Doran, having Schells thrown out of Flux: "If I ever see your cheating hide in here again, I'll do more than toss you out." Schells: "I didn't cheat. I just found my own way to win."
