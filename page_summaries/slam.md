Slam is a biotic power appearing in the single-player modes of Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. It biotically lifts a target and slams it to the ground for damage. It travels instantly to the target and has no projectile like Throw, Pull, or Singularity, so it cannot be dodged, though it also cannot be arced around cover.

In Mass Effect 2 it was described as a biotic body-slam inflicting massive damage. Across three ranks its lift duration and slam force increased (400 to 600 newtons), with a recharge of nine seconds, reduced to three seconds for Shepard. Rank 4 evolved it into Heavy Slam (greater force, up to 800 newtons and grievous harm) or Crippling Slam (less force but incapacitating the target for eight seconds after impact). Targets were instantly ragdolled and took double weapon damage until they rose. Damage from the slam itself was noted as inconsistent, sometimes applying during the lift, sometimes the impact, sometimes neither. Slam worked well on targets already lifted by Pull or Singularity, smashing them straight down, and could be detonated by Warp during the brief lift window. It was available to Shepard via Advanced Training and to Miranda Lawson as a loyalty power.

In Mass Effect 3 Slam had a four-second base recharge and 900 N base force, scaling through six ranks with options for recharge speed, force, impact radius, biotic detonation damage, and a rank 6 Stun that incapacitated a slammed target for five seconds. Its lift effect only worked on unprotected (red bar) targets; against shielded or armored enemies it did negligible damage and no stagger. It could detonate all four power combo types, with detonation occurring at the top of the lift. In Mass Effect 3 it was available to Javik and to Shepard as a bonus power; it was not available in multiplayer.
