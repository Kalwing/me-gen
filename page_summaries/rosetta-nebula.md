The Rosetta Nebula was a largely uncharted cluster in the Terminus Systems, accessible in Mass Effect 2 after starting Jacob's loyalty mission, Jacob: The Gift of Greatness. Because of the Citadel prohibition against indiscriminately opening mass relays, earlier exploration of the area went undiscovered until modern times. At least one system in the nebula was known to have hosted a spacefaring pre-Citadel civilization before its extermination by unknown parties.

The nebula connected via mass relay to the Omega Nebula and Sigurd's Cradle. Its systems included Alpha Draconis (containing the planet 2175 Aeia), Enoch, and Phi Clio. It hosted the mission Jacob: The Gift of Greatness and the assignment N7: Archeological Dig Site. Originally the nebula had a fourth system, Iota Urania, found in game files with five planets (Walden, Erewhon, Mizora, Pala, and Cascadia) but cut from the final game. After completing N7: Archeological Dig Site, a Citadel news report referred to the region as the "Aeia Cluster." The nebula's art appears to be an inverted image of the real-world Orion Nebula (M42) and bears little resemblance to the actual Rosette Nebula.
