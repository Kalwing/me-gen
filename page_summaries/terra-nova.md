Terra Nova was a human colony world in the Asgard System of the Exodus Cluster, the first planet of that system. One of the Class-1 colonization prospects discovered by the first wave of Alliance surveys in 2150, it was the second human extrasolar colony and the first beyond the Charon Mass Relay. Though banded by a harsh equatorial desert, its polar regions were temperate. Development was modest until extensive platinum deposits were found in the 2170s; because platinum was needed for the clean-burning hydrogen fuel cells that power private vehicles, a "platinum rush" of immigrants and investment followed, driving a roughly 30% population rise over twelve years. By the events of Mass Effect it had the highest population of any Alliance colony.

By Mass Effect 3, repeated threats of batarian attack had prompted many colonists to emigrate, making the name "Exodus Cluster" apt. During the Reaper invasion the Reapers landed on Terra Nova after a brief battle with the Alliance's Sixth Fleet, which retreated rather than be destroyed, hoping to regroup and retake the planet. Intelligence held the local Reaper force to be light, and debate continued over whether liberation was feasible.

Terra Nova had no natural satellites; the asteroid X57 was planned as an artificial one. It was the setting of the Mass Effect DLC Bring Down the Sky (the X57 assignments and related Search and Rescue content, including the Alliance cruiser Shanghai). Its capital was Scott. Terra Nova is Latin for "New Earth," and it was the only planet in the Asgard system not named after a Norse god.
