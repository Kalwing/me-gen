Kasumi: Stealing Memory was Kasumi Goto's loyalty mission in Mass Effect 2, acquired after recruiting her on the Citadel and undertaken on Bekenstein in the Boltzmann system of the Serpent Nebula. The arms dealer Donovan Hock had killed Kasumi's partner and lover Keiji Okuda and stolen his graybox, a neural implant storing memories and classified information he had seen. Kasumi planned a heist to recover it, and once completed she could devote her attention to Shepard's mission against the Collectors.

Only Shepard and Kasumi took part. Traveling to Hock's party, they used a cover identity (Solomon or Alison Gunn) and formal attire, smuggling weapons and armor inside a statue of Saren offered as tribute. Kasumi entered cloaked after Hock barred her at the door. To open Hock's vault, Shepard completed three tasks: recording Hock's voice by drawing him into a speech, obtaining his DNA from his private quarters (via subterfuge using guard Samuels and Chief Roe, or by breaking in from the balcony), and cutting power to the kinetic barrier by pulling a statue near a fireplace. The vault held stolen art including Michelangelo's David and the head of the Statue of Liberty. Grabbing the graybox triggered a hologram of Hock and an assault by Eclipse mercenaries led by Chief Roe. Fighting out through a garage full of YMIR mechs and past an M-080 cannon, Shepard and Kasumi reached a landing platform where Hock attacked in a shielded A-61 Mantis gunship supported by mercs and LOKI mechs. Kasumi performed acrobatics to disable the gunship's shields, and it was destroyed along with Hock.

On the shuttle ride back, Kasumi examined the graybox and relived memories of Keiji, along with images resembling Reapers and a warning that publicizing the information would severely harm the Alliance. Shepard could persuade her to keep or destroy the box; either way she became loyal. In Mass Effect 3's Citadel: Hanar Diplomat, the secret was revealed as an Alliance raid on a batarian lab studying Reaper tech, covered up to prevent an Alliance-batarian war. The mission unlocked the M-12 Locust submachine gun, a Tech Damage upgrade, and the Flashbang Grenade power. It was written by Jay Turner; the vault password "Peruggia" referenced the man who stole the Mona Lisa.
