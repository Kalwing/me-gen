The Krogan Warlord Sentinel was a playable class in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer, presented in-fiction as an elder krogan statesman who held no regard for combat honor, resorting to hostage-taking and genocide, and who carried a battle hammer while his maturity granted unsurpassed regeneration. It had 1250 shields and 1000 health, and the powers Tech Armor, Biotic Hammer, Electrical Hammer and Warlord Rage. Its melee was a Krogan Headbutt that could chain into two hammer swings, the last imbued with the equipped hammer power; a sprinting Krogan Charge dealt damage in a small arc. It could not take cover, vault, dodge, or perform grabs, and moved slower than other krogan classes, but could not be grabbed by Husks or Abominations and had the second-highest hit-stun resistance in the game after the Geth Juggernaut.

It was the only krogan class with both health regeneration and 1250 base shields, making it one of the best tanking characters. The Krogan Charge was replaced by a hammer strike consuming holographic charges based on the current hammer power, which locked onto moving enemies. Melee mods (multiplicative) were far better than power mods since hammer powers added damage to melee attacks; a Biotic Hammer with the Shotgun Omni-Blade V could deal roughly 7,000 to health and shields, 8,000 to barriers and 10,000 to armor. Electrical Hammer's Chill Effect set up devastating Biotic Hammer follow-ups. Extensive player notes covered every faction, stressing that its slow heavy melee left it exposed to sync-kill enemies such as Phantoms, Atlases, Scions, Praetorians, Banshees and Brutes, best handled by striking first or staying just out of reach. It uniquely never used the "For Tuchanka!" voice line common to other krogan classes.
