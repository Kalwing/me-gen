Terra Firma was a human political party that opposed humanity's growing integration into the galactic community, holding that humanity needed to stand alone to remain strong. Members cited Shanxi and the First Contact War as proof that aliens could not be trusted, marked Armistice Day with an annual protest, and opposed the teaching of alien languages in schools. The party was founded after first contact with the turians, when humans learned how many races made up Citadel space, out of genuine concern that human individuality might be diluted. Its manifesto was not especially extremist, but it attracted xenophobes and reactionaries, and it declined to curb its members' racist rhetoric on free-speech grounds.

In Mass Effect: Ascension (2173), Terra Firma was caught in a kickback scandal involving Nashan Stellar Dynamics, forcing party leader Inez Simmons to resign. Leadership candidates Charles Saracino and Claude Menneau campaigned vigorously; Menneau led the polls by three points until he vanished en route to a rally on Shanxi, his ship and crew never found. With his rival gone, Saracino ran unopposed and was elected party leader.

In Mass Effect, Commander Shepard could discuss the party with Ashley Williams aboard the Normandy; uncomfortable around aliens herself, Ashley reacted angrily to comparisons with Terra Firma, calling its members jackals who had strayed from the founders' ideals. Shepard could also encounter a Terra Firma protest on the Citadel led by Saracino himself during the assignment Citadel: Our Own Worst Enemy, where Saracino, seeking a political seat, asked Shepard to endorse him. "Terra firma" is Latin for "solid Earth." A Shadow Broker dossier later listed the Illusive Man's liaisons Staci and Stephi Strong as the "Terra Firma Twins."
