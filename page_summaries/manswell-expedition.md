The Manswell Expedition was a lost human colony rediscovered in 2186 on a planet in the Alpha Centauri system. It originated as a private spaceflight venture funded by Victor Manswell, a billionaire frustrated with the pace of official space exploration. Around 300 people were placed in cryogenic freeze and the expedition departed Earth in 2075, roughly 75 years before humanity discovered the mass relays and made contact with the other galactic races. Contact was lost after launch until an asari expedition, observing a primitive alien species in the region, stumbled upon the colony more than a century later.

The asari were ambushed by colonists who took one of their scientists prisoner. Hoping to avoid escalation, the asari asked the Systems Alliance for help keeping the peace, which was initially hard because the Alliance had no record of the colony; record checks eventually identified it as the Manswell Expedition of 2070. Believing the colonists likely had no knowledge of the wider galaxy, the Alliance used first contact protocols, sending not a military force but a team of military experts, medics, sociologists, anthropologists, linguists, psychologists, and geneticists. The colonists saw this as an alien plot and fought back, wounding several on both sides before the violence was quelled. After being convinced the newcomers were humans from Earth, they allowed an Alliance representative into their camp and released the asari scientist unharmed.

The Alliance offered to help reintegrate the colonists into human society and the galactic community. Opinion in the colony split roughly evenly: some felt 120 years of change would be too much to adjust to and chose to stay, while others were eager to rejoin, one amazed to learn they had living descendants. The storyline was tied to the Cerberus Daily News feature "Undocumented Human Colony Found in Alpha Centauri System."
