This page collects non-canon dialogue cut from Mass Effect 2's Reaper IFF mission, the Normandy crew abduction, and the Suicide Mission, reconstructed from game text-string and dialogue files. Highlights include a Cerberus VI's airlock announcements on the Derelict Reaper, Dr. Chandana's full indoctrination monologue (only partly used in the released game), squad reactions to hearing Legion speak, a cut choice letting Shepard leave the disabled geth platform behind instead of recovering it, and a scrapped version of the Collector Base finale in which Mordin could be a biotic specialist and EDI delivered extra lore about the Human-Reaper.

Cerberus VI, in the Reaper airlock: "Please stand by. Equalizing pressure with exterior conditions. Remember, safety is everyone's concern. We have gone five days without a workplace death."

Chandana, indoctrinated, over the IFF comm: "It was lonely. It called to us. It wanted to remember. The Masters had been gone so long. The Masters were lost when it was shattered... We have become an echo of Their echo. We have become more than we were. Join us. Know us. Remember all our lives... They were called imshai. Those who lived here before... Shepard. They know you. They wish you to understand. They are shepherds, too."

Tali, urging Shepard to leave the intact geth behind: "Leave it there! You know what they are! If it gets into Normandy's computers.."

EDI, on the Human-Reaper during the Suicide Mission: "Shepard, the energy readings of that creature are analogous to those detected from Sovereign. It is a Reaper."

EDI, cut finale lore: "This explains why the captive humans were rendered into their base components. Destructive analysis. They were dissected down to the atomic level... The knowledge and essence of billions of individuals compiled into a single synthetic identity."

EDI, on why humans were targeted: "The ultimate goal would be to upload all humans into this Reaper-mind. The Collectors would harvest every human settlement across the galaxy. The obvious final goal would be Earth."

Harbinger, during the cut Human-Reaper fight: "A minor setback. It changes nothing!" and "You insult a future you cannot comprehend!"
