Interrupts in Mass Effect 3 let Commander Shepard take direct, often physical action during conversations. A Renegade interrupt (red icon) played a metal "gong" and a Paragon interrupt (blue icon) an uplifting hum. They were optional, brief, awarded reputation points, and required no minimum reputation, with one class-exclusive exception in the Omega DLC. The page collects known interrupts across plot missions, Galaxy at War missions and casual interactions, preserving many exact lines.

Representative quotes:
- Shepard to James, Prologue: Earth: "Stow it, Lieutenant. You don't want to go - we get it - but this isn't a democracy!"
- Shepard interrupting Dalatrass Linron, Priority: Sur'Kesh: "Will be nothing compared to what happens if the Reapers win."
- Shepard to the dying Reaper, Priority: Rannoch: "You're the ones who should worry. Tell your friends we're coming for them." then, after the quarian salvo: "Never mind, I'll tell them myself."
- Wrex to Shepard if the genophage cure was sabotaged, Priority: The Citadel III: "What's the matter? Ashley not around to do your dirty work? You a coward and a traitor?"
- Shepard to Vendetta, Priority: Cerberus Headquarters: "You didn't fail. You gave us a chance. And we're not done yet."
- Shepard completing the soldiers' toast, Purgatory bar with James Vega: "Damn few. And they're all dead!"
- Shepard to Khalisah al-Jilani, Citadel Embassies: "I've had enough of your tabloid journalism," followed by a headbutt.

Key non-verbatim beats: on Priority: Tuchanka, Shepard may shake Wrex's hand, shoot Mordin to preserve the sabotage, or cry "Mordin, no!" as he heads to the Shroud. On Priority: The Citadel II, interrupts resolve the Udina standoff, letting Shepard shoot Ashley/Kaidan or Udina. On Priority: Geth Dreadnought, Shepard can punch Admiral Gerrel off the Normandy. On Priority: The Citadel III, two interrupts let Shepard wound and then kill a vengeful Wrex. On Priority: Cerberus Headquarters, a Renegade interrupt has Shepard shatter Kai Leng's sword and stab him. On Priority: Earth, interrupts let Shepard hug Anderson and shoot the Illusive Man. Kallini lets Shepard restrain a suicidal Samara or shoot Falere. Omega interrupts govern sacrificing civilians and the fate of General Petrovsky. Citadel DLC adds interrupts to shoot the ward replica onto the clone's troops, spare or execute Maya Brooks, and numerous romance kisses, dance moves and the 183-rep pull-up challenge with James.
