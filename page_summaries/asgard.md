Asgard was the first extrasolar system developed by humanity using the Charon Relay. Its colony, on the planet Terra Nova, proved rich in platinum, and the resulting platinum rush helped drive construction of hydrogen and helium-3 production infrastructure on the system's gas giants. As of 2183, Asgard's hydrogen fuel cells had captured nearly 4% of the galactic market. Threats of batarian attack, however, caused many colonists to emigrate rather than risk their fortunes there.

During the Reaper invasion of the galaxy in 2186, much of the system's production facilities were destroyed. The Alliance withdrew to assess whether to wait for reinforcements to retake the system or to join the fighting elsewhere. Alliance forces still put up a fight in defeat, evidenced by enemy wreckage littering Borr and by ground units still hiding in Loki's subterranean tunnels.

The system contained the star Asgard and its planets: Terra Nova (first planet), Borr (second), Tyr (third), and Loki (fourth). Asteroid X57, an asteroid orbiting Terra Nova, was the site of the Bring Down the Sky assignment in the first Mass Effect, along with the related tasks X57: Missing Engineers and X57: Avoid the Blasting Caps. Accessing Asgard in Mass Effect 3 required having played Bring Down the Sky. The system was named for the home of the Norse gods, with three of its planets named after members of that pantheon. By Keplerian ratios of Loki and, more approximately, Tyr, Asgard was described as a near twin of Sol.
