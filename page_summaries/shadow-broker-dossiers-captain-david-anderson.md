This page collects the Shadow Broker's Dossiers file on Captain David Anderson, comprising a summary line, an intercepted military message, an intercepted personal letter, and a list of his recent purchases. The header reads: "Captain David Anderson is a rejected Spectre candidate. Close working relationship with Commander Shepard before destruction of the Normandy SR1. Monitor communications for renewed contact with Shepard."

The first intercept is from Captain Farrah Gapoli, Assistant in Charge of Operations at Arcturus Station, pressuring Anderson to drop his advocacy for Shepard:

- Gapoli: "I have been asked to step in on the matter of your continued vocal and insistent support of Commander Shepard, formerly of the SR1 Normandy, within the Alliance military and to third party contracts."
- Gapoli: "We respectfully ask you to cease and desist your continued investigation that has been deemed closed and sealed for two solar years."
- Gapoli: "Your continued agitation in this matter is deemed a high security risk with potential repercussions for galactic security."

A wiki note observes that the message refers to Shepard's "involvement in the attack" using masculine pronouns even for a female Shepard, changed to "their" in Legendary Edition.

The second intercept is a personal letter from Cynthia Barris of Atlanta, Earth, recalling Anderson's appointment to the Hastings, describing her son Jason's graduation in San Francisco as a new "Aerospace Engineer," relaying Jason's admiration of Anderson as "a heroic figure off fighting grand battles in space," and inviting Anderson home for the holidays: "And David, please be careful."

The recent transactions list includes catalog orders from Dionysus Imports, wine and vodka purchases (Modis Valley, Illium Elite, Red Janey), home maintenance services, and video downloads titled "Saren: A Hero Betrayed," "Damaged: The Truth Behind the Citadel Crisis," "The Path of Lies: A History of the Alliance Military," and "Afraid of the Dark: Reapers, Collectors and other Myths."
