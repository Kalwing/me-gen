The page transcribed art director Derek Watts' gallery commentary for "The Technology of Mass Effect: Geth Weapons," from the 2007 Mass Effect bonus content disc. Watts described the geth as the game's primary enemy, requiring variations for different weapons, ships, and vehicle-scale combat.

He detailed a geth drone concept originally intended to launch four or five units from a slot in the back of the geth armature; this was simplified to drones that simply float and fire, using the large geth eye. A flying geth turret that lands and folds its legs behind it like a jet had to be simplified because the modeler lacked time, and was made to reuse elements from the human version.

Watts explained that geth rifles were designed by cutting up the existing geth transport concept in Photoshop and building guns from its elements, producing five or six variations in about two hours. The final rifle used the grey-purple geth material and had to fold small enough to collapse onto the player's back; the modeler built it in roughly a week. A larger geth rifle functioning as a rocket launcher was derived from the rifle with a longer barrel and more hoses.

He also covered the geth armature concept, whose head and eye influenced the geth trooper design; an early organic, skin-covered look for the geth that became flesh rather than metal; an outsourced geth platform whose front head was removed because Saren kept hitting it while shooting; the "Hex" geth shield, simplified to standalone shootable shields; the sniper-tower-scale geth tower; and the geth turret, a large vehicle-combat enemy whose leveling claws were removed due to collision problems.
