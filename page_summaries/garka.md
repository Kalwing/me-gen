Garka was a male batarian on Omega who worked for Aria T'Loak as an enforcer of her rule, and could be seen standing guard in her private box at the club Afterlife in Mass Effect 2. Like most of Aria's guards, he briefly pointed a gun at Commander Shepard during the Commander's first visit, then holstered his M-4 Shuriken and scanned Shepard with his omni-tool to confirm the Commander's identity, continuing even when Shepard objected or physically threatened him, before informing Aria and standing aside once the scan came back clean. After Aria's warning about Omega's one rule, if Shepard claimed to like it, Aria said Garka would remind them if they forgot, and Garka added that he would then throw the Commander out the airlock.

If Shepard returned a datapad retrieved while recruiting Archangel, Aria flung it in Garka's face and demanded to know how the information it held had slipped past their intelligence; Garka stuttered that he would look into it.
