Citadel: Planting a Bug was a Mass Effect assignment given by the reporter Emily Wong, available on the second visit to the Citadel after completing her earlier request, Citadel: Reporter's Request. Wong waited at the Citadel Tower below the Council's audience chamber. She explained a planned story on overworked Citadel traffic controllers and asked the player to plant a listening bug, which she described as harmless, in the C-Sec traffic control center. Agreeing was required to take the assignment; refusing ended it permanently.

The assignment could be completed by planting the bug in an inconspicuous spot near a keeper in the C-Sec Academy control room, or by not planting it, with a return to Wong required either way. Planting it and choosing supportive dialogue awarded 2 Paragon points, experience, and credits. A player could instead abort by telling Wong not to hold her breath, ending the assignment with no rewards. With four ranks of Charm, the player could refuse on the grounds that controllers might lose their jobs or accuse Wong of chasing ratings, earning 8 Paragon points plus the same experience and credits as planting it. With five ranks of Intimidate, the player could lie that the bug was planted, gaining 9 Renegade points, equal experience and credits, and omni-gel.

An elevator news report played afterward if the bug was planted as agreed or if the player refused over job losses. Internally the assignment was named "Don't Mean to Bug You." Unused ambient voicelines existed for the traffic controllers, portraying them coordinating many simultaneous tasks and communications, which supported the idea that they were genuinely overworked.
