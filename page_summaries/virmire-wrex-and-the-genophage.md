Virmire: Wrex and the Genophage was an assignment in Mass Effect that occurred at the salarian camp on Virmire, gained automatically after arrival if Wrex was a squadmate. It required the earlier assignment Citadel: Wrex. When Captain Kirrahe revealed that Saren had discovered a cure for the genophage and was breeding an army of krogan, Wrex became furious that Kirrahe intended to destroy the cure. Kirrahe refused to proceed with the assault until Wrex's state of mind was resolved, so Shepard had to confront Wrex, who could be found on the beach blasting his shotgun.

Approaching Wrex triggered the confrontation, in which he insisted the cure be preserved and eventually drew a pistol on Shepard. The outcome carried a large morality swing. Shepard's options were: to signal Ashley to shoot Wrex (available only if she had been told to be ready beforehand, Renegade); to shoot Wrex personally (Renegade); to stall until Ashley shot Wrex in the back on her own initiative to protect Shepard, which angered Kaidan; to talk Wrex down with 8 points of Charm or Intimidate by arguing that a cured krogan would only be Saren's puppet; or, if Shepard had already helped Wrex recover his family armor, to calm him without a persuasion check by choosing the lines "I wouldn't do this otherwise" or "We are." In the rare case where Shepard had only four squadmates (Kaidan, Tali, Wrex, and Ashley, requiring that Garrus and Liara never be recruited and that Barla Von's help be sought), Wrex simply submitted. Keeping Wrex alive earned the Charismatic achievement; if he died, Shepard automatically recovered his equipment.

The choice had lasting consequences. If Wrex lived, he became leader of Clan Urdnot and pressed his beliefs; if he died, Urdnot Wreav led the clan instead. In Mass Effect 3, a living Wrex was a War Asset worth 30 Military Strength (5 more than Wreav) and provided the "Krogan Mercenaries" asset if the genophage was genuinely cured, but attacked and had to be killed if Shepard left the salarian sabotage of the cure unfixed. Wreav, worth 25, could be fooled with a fake cure without penalty. According to Legendary Edition statistics, Wrex survived 94 percent of the time.
