Harrot was an elcor merchant on Omega who ran Harrot's Emporium, a salvage shop in the marketplace. In Mass Effect 2, he had a deal with Kenn, a quarian running Kenn's Salvage, forbidding Kenn from setting competitive prices and so hampering Kenn's efforts to earn enough to leave the station. After speaking with Kenn, Commander Shepard could confront the cigar-smoking Harrot, who grew suspicious and either denied or acknowledged the arrangement. If Shepard threatened to break his legs, Harrot was easily intimidated into releasing Kenn from the agreement; if Shepard persuaded him to buy Kenn's business instead, he confessed he had not considered it and saw a win-win. In both cases he gave Shepard a discount, but not if Shepard had already offered to cover Kenn's ticket costs beforehand. Harrot had once sold a defective YMIR Mech to a prospective Taitus miner who cursed him in his logs after it failed in the field.

In Mass Effect 3: Omega, Harrot was found in Aria T'Loak's bunker shouting for attention, having hidden from Cerberus forces and rampaging Adjutants in a hidden backroom until Aria's patrol liberated him. He planned to win Aria's favor by returning her favorite couch, which the Cerberus General had thrown out of Afterlife, and asked Shepard's help finding it. He reopened the Emporium as a kiosk in the bunker selling war materiel, and if the couch was found he was seen entering the reopened Afterlife. If Shepard had a prior discount, he asked why the Commander seemed familiar. In Legendary Edition, Harrot's redesigned appearance was applied to all elcor across the trilogy.
