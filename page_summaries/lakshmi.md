Lakshmi was a street urchin in the Terminus Systems featured in Mass Effect: Foundation. A brunette girl almost ten years old with a distinctive Y-shaped scar over her right eye, she came from an impoverished background and begged in the streets to survive. She encountered Miranda Lawson and Jacob Taylor; Miranda gave her nothing, but Jacob promised credits for directions. Lakshmi warned them the place they sought was dangerous, as her cousin's family had been abducted there and enslaved, and when batarian thugs attacked she lured the pair to safety and gave them decorated red and green fabrics to mask their faces.

Later, a batarian food vendor took her credits but refused her the leg meat she wanted, accusing her of theft, before a piece of a crashing Cerberus ship killed him and destroyed the stall. Another batarian shoved her and demanded she fetch her uncle Taleed. When an injured Jacob came looking for Miranda, Lakshmi helped him, warned off hoodlums with her uncle's reputation, and reluctantly revealed she knew where Miranda was being held. Her mother had her prepare a bed for Jacob, and Lakshmi later showed him her uncle's weapons stash when he vowed to rescue her enslaved aunt and cousins. She guided him to the slavers' warehouse, where she saw her uncle ordered to kill Miranda and was herself seized as a hostage by the batarian ringleader Torthak. Jacob shot the batarian and freed her; her family, minus her uncle, was reunited. Lakshmi hugged Jacob in gratitude and gave Miranda a yellow rose before running off.
