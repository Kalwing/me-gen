Race Against Time was the overarching main mission of Mass Effect, representing Shepard's ongoing effort to stop Saren Arterius. Saren was searching for the Conduit, an ancient relic dating to the Prothean extinction; through the visions burned into Shepard's mind by the Eden Prime beacon, Shepard came to believe the Conduit was a weapon capable of wiping out all life in the galaxy.

The mission appeared in the Journal after completing two of the three plot missions on Noveria, Feros, or Therum, when the Citadel Council requested a priority holo-meeting in the Normandy's comm room to share the latest intelligence on Saren. It did not correspond to any single point in the game and was completed only by finishing the game. Revealing the location of Ilos required completing Therum, Feros, Noveria, and Virmire in full.

The mission's name updated at story milestones: "Race Against Time" on acquisition, "Race Against Time: Sovereign" after meeting Sovereign on Virmire, "Race Against Time: Conduit" after speaking with Vigil on Ilos, and "Race Against Time: Final Battle" after passing through the Conduit and re-entering the Citadel.
