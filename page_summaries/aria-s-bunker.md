Aria's Bunker was a location on Omega's D-Deck. During Aria T'Loak's campaign to liberate Omega from the Cerberus occupation forces led by General Oleg Petrovsky, the bunker served as her headquarters. It was stocked with ample provisions, drew on a power source independent from the rest of Omega, possessed formidable defenses and a secret hangar bay, and provided access to key areas of the station through a network of secret tunnels and passages. It also featured an armor locker and a weapons bench, and the merchant Harrot later reopened his emporium there.

After General Petrovsky overheard Aria mention the bunker's location, he sent successive waves of Cerberus troops to capture it. The attackers were initially held off by exterior defense turrets, but while Aria was recruiting the Talons to her cause, the bunker's main entrance was compromised and sealed off. Aria and her troops then evacuated the bunker through the access tunnels in order to mount their final assault on the Cerberus-held Afterlife Club and confront Petrovsky. The bunker was the hub for several Omega DLC missions, including "Omega: Aria T'Loak," "Omega: Assist the Hacker," "Omega: Assist the Mechanic," and "Omega: Assist Harrot."
