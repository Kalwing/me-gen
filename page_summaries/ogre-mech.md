The Ogre Mech was a Cerberus mech used to guard the Barn space station. They were fought by Randall Ezno, and potentially by an unnamed turian during their escape. The Ogre was a very durable enemy with high health, armor, and regenerating shields; it could only be damaged by fire directed at its head or one of its shoulders. Its left arm housed a Pulse Rifle that fired in multiple bursts, and its right arm housed a Storm Launcher; it also had a charged beam attack telegraphed by throwing its arms up before firing. Dealing significant damage to the head could destroy its eye and targeting ability, causing it to fire wildly around the room and possibly harm its allies, while heavy damage to an arm could sever that limb and remove the associated weapon.
