Haestrom was the third planet of the Dholen System, in the Far Rim, and formerly a quarian colony. The quarians established the settlement before the geth revolt roughly 300 years earlier to study the instability of Dholen, the system's parent star, which appeared likely to erupt prematurely into a red giant. As a scientific outpost of minimal military value, Haestrom was poorly equipped to resist geth forces during the insurrection and fell quickly. The planet was lost to the geth in 1896 CE, after which all communication from its surface and attendant space stations ceased.

For the following three centuries the geth established several space stations near Dholen but showed no sign of treating the star as a threat. Dholen's magnetic eruptions and solar output overwhelm most communications in the region, and it was unclear how the geth compensated. Spy probe scans indicated extensive orbital construction around Haestrom housing thousands of geth platforms and an unknown number of geth software "minds." The number of geth on the surface was unknown because Dholen's interference hampered remote scanning. Resource estimates based on geth mining, refining and fabricating practices suggested the planet had at least 20 more years of use before exhaustion; intelligence analysts speculated the geth kept some resources in reserve for repairs.

Captured geth planetary survey data indicated that despite war damage, Haestrom's architecture remained as it had been before the conflict, preserving a quarian architectural style that no longer existed anywhere else in the galaxy. Because Dholen had overwhelmed the planet's protective magnetosphere, humans venturing onto geth-controlled Haestrom faced severe danger: minutes of radiation exposure would overload shields and hours would kill, and solar output rendered surface-to-orbit communication nearly impossible. A travel advisory designated Haestrom a geth stronghold, noting that only military spy drones using cutting-edge stealth technology had returned unharmed from geth space, and prohibited all civilian traffic. Haestrom could not be mined. The planet was the setting for the mission Dossier: Tali in Mass Effect 2, where a quarian research team led by Tali'Zorah investigated Dholen's accelerated aging under geth assault.
