This archive covered only the last week and a half of March 2186 (entries dated 22 through 28). Most reports followed the fallout from findings that the mass relays predate the Prothean civilization: Dr. Aurana T'Meles of the University of Serrice publicly converted from skeptic to supporter of Dr. Amanda Kenson's dating work, disappeared species such as the arthenn and zeioph were ruled out as builders, and batarians broke into T'Meles's Serrice office to steal Kenson's research, with one burglar identified as Palash Tarn, a former member of the Batarian Special Intervention Unit. On the hanar homeworld Kahje, religious leader All-Bright Olos the 10th urged calm, saying it was "no heresy to say the Enkindlers themselves may have been Enkindled." A wave of relay-related security incidents followed: researchers Zahi Moon and Ellis Veech were arrested for trying to tamper with the Lowas relay's command switches, Council peacekeeping fleets were repositioned to monitor relays against loitering and debris dumping, and the resulting traffic delays cost the galactic economy an estimated 612 billion credits. Separately, Systems Alliance admiral Steven Hackett testified before the Citadel Council that the Alliance Joint Chiefs had relaxed their opposition to increased turian dreadnought construction, judging future threats to be external to the signatories of the Treaty of Farixen; batarian ambassador Nel'Tarras Tilshan reacted angrily, calling dreadnoughts weapons for devastating planets.
