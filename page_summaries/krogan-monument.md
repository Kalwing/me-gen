The Krogan Monument was a large statue of a krogan warrior standing in the lake near the Citadel Tower on the Presidium, commissioned by the Citadel Council to commemorate krogan contributions in the war against the rachni. After the Krogan Rebellions there was controversy over whether it should be removed, but the Council ruled that the krogan role in saving Citadel space was a historical fact not to be forgotten.

Wrex admired the Monument, finding it far more impressive than the Relay Monument. If Commander Shepard spoke with him in front of it, Wrex said soberly that the statue embodied krogan values of honor, loyalty, courage and fortitude, all lost to his people since the Rebellions and the genophage; a human squadmate and Liara would remark that Wrex was unlike other krogan, and Wrex answered "I'll take that as a compliment." Tali resented that the krogan, who had turned on the Citadel races and nearly conquered them, received a Presidium statue while the quarians lived in exile; Wrex countered that the quarians were still alive, whereas in two hundred years the Monument might be all that remained of his people. In the Citadel DLC, if Grunt survived and was recovering at Huerta Memorial Hospital, he and Aralakh Company squadmates visited the Monument drunk in 2186, climbed it, photographed themselves, and carjacked the responding C-Sec officers. In Mass Effect 2, Morinth kept a scale replica in her Omega apartment.
