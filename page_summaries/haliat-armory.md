Haliat Armory was one of the smaller turian weapons manufacturers, distinct from the Mass Effect character Elanos Haliat. The turian Hierarchy granted it permission to sell excess units on the galactic market. Rather than competing with high-end manufacturers, Haliat specialized in a line of basic-level weapons, though its products were of high quality and carried high stats. In Mass Effect, a license for Haliat Armory gear could be purchased from Ledra at Zhu's Hope on Feros for 6,250 credits.

All Haliat Armory weapons incorporated absorbent recoil technology, giving better across-the-board performance than most other weapons. Its lines were the Thunder assault rifles, Tornado shotguns, Stiletto pistols and Equalizer sniper rifles. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition, weapon accuracy values were improved and heat generation slowed; the Tornado had inferior stats to the single-slug Avalanche shotgun, and the Equalizer had inferior stats to the semi-automatic Volkov sniper rifle while being described as more accurate than others in its class.
