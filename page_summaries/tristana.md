Tristana was a former asari Justicar working as a prolific freelance assassin, appearing in Mass Effect: Foundation. She had once had a relationship with Zaeed Massani, who called her a "stone-cold bitch" and claimed he ended it before it killed him.

Zaeed approached her mid-assassination with a job: bring down the turian frigate Verrikan. She thought it was not a job one walked away from easily, but was won over by the payout. At the briefing she openly doubted taking a frigate with only four soldiers; Zaeed insisted the raid would rely on stealth, not strength. During the mission the mercenaries reached the frigate's outer hull, but Zaeed's inside man was caught and the intruders were blown off the airlock, saved by tether cables; their salarian tech specialist was lost. Tristana and the mercenary Jace Reyn were assigned to draw turian fire while Zaeed made for the bridge; Tristana used biotics against swarming turians, then slipped through a door, locked it behind her, and abandoned Reyn to hold alone. She reached the bridge in time to blow the head off Zaeed's turian hostage, spattering his face, and the two fought off more than a dozen turians together.

Tristana declared the payout now fifty-fifty and turned on Zaeed once he set the ship to crash on Impera: Vido Santiago had hired her to kill Zaeed after the Verrikan job. When the planet's gravity took hold she lost her biotic grip and the ex-lovers fought hand to hand as the ship broke apart; she took a knife below the right breast while choking him. A hull breach sucked both out; each grabbed a hold, Zaeed above her. She told him he always knew how to show a girl a good time; he agreed but said his heart belonged to someone else, then kicked her in the face, breaking her grip and sending her plummeting to the surface.
