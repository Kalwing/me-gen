The M-55 Argus was an assault rifle in Mass Effect 3, a high-powered rifle favored by senior C-Sec officers and an excellent close-range weapon. Its bursts of fire conserved ammunition during lengthy conflicts, and other law-enforcement agencies across the galaxy were adopting it as their standard rifle, as much for its intimidation factor as its suppression power. According to Lead Combat Designer Corey Gaspur, the Argus was a modified M-96 Mattock.

In single-player the Argus was available to players who pre-ordered Mass Effect 3 (except in Australia, where it was exclusive to EB Games and Origin) and on the Wii U version; obtaining it this way triggered an email from EDI noting that the rifle had received more positive ratings than any other assault rifle in law-enforcement polls. It could also be found in a shack on the abandoned ship during Despoina: Leviathan. In multiplayer it was categorized as rare and required no expansion packs.

The Argus was a semi-automatic burst-fire weapon firing one three-round burst per trigger pull, alongside similar weapons like the M-15 Vindicator, N7 Valkyrie and M-29 Incisor. It was the most damaging but also the heaviest of these, with each full burst doing slightly more damage than the M-99 Saber. Its weaknesses were recoil and inaccuracy, best countered by firing from cover, pulling aim down, or using stability mods and the Marksman power. It carried a large reserve of thermal clips but was a poor choice for squadmates, who fired only a single round per shot.
