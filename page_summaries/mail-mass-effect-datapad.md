This page collected the brief in-character messages Commander Shepard received in Mass Effect 3 if the player also ran the iOS companion app Mass Effect 3: Datapad linked to the same Origin account. The messages came from squadmates and allies and varied by conversations held, decisions made, mission outcomes, crew recruited, and who was alive in the playthrough. They were grouped by sender. The content is short one-line notes reflecting game state; representative examples:

EDI: "Shepard, when you have time, I have another question for you. Actually, I have 1,623,058, but I'm pacing myself."

Javik: "I will help you, but evolve faster. Typing messages like this is an insult."

Joker: "YOU ARE THE BEST COMMANDER EVER ALSO I AM A LITTLE DRUNK RIGHT NOW"

Tali (drunk on shore leave): "hey Shepard im in teh lounge havinga drink, wanna hwlp?"

Wrex, on the genophage cure procedure: "Damn. This BETTER cure the genophage. The salarian's 'procedure' hurts like hell."

Wrex, if Shepard sabotaged the genophage cure: "I know what you did."

Anderson, from Earth: "Losing comms. It's hell down here, Shepard. Get us some help. Will be in touch--when I can."

Zaeed: "No way we're all getting out of this war alive. Be fun to try, though."

Geth Primes: "Our cognition is vastly improved. We thank you, Shepard-Commander. The geth will reciprocate this trust."

A recurring pre-final-mission line sent by several characters: "Doubt any of us will be getting much sleep anytime soon. But I believe in you."
