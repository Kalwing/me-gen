The M-25 Hornet was a submachine gun in Mass Effect 3 and a pistol-class weapon in Mass Effect: Andromeda. It was a long-range SMG created by Cerberus and issued as standard equipment to Cerberus troops, who were trained to handle the recoil of its three-round bursts. Cerberus designed it to conserve ammunition and provide covering fire during prolonged conflicts.

In Mass Effect 3 it was found during Priority: The Citadel II on the body of a C-Sec officer and was purchasable from Batarian State Arms; multiplayer rated it rare. It combined high damage, a high rate of fire, very quick reload, and a manageable weight, with only the N7 Hurricane exceeding its damage against soft targets. Its main drawbacks were the lack of fully automatic fire, high recoil, and a small clip, though the quick reload and mods like the SMG Heat Sink offset the last. It performed well for turian characters and the Geth Juggernaut Soldier thanks to their stability bonuses, while squadmates used it poorly, firing single bursts from cover.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda the Hornet was a heavy three-round-burst SMG originally made by Cerberus in the Milky Way; the Initiative's stockpile had been seized from a Cerberus base by the Systems Alliance and then bought by the Initiative at a semi-legal auction. It again boasted one of the highest damage-per-second ratings in the game, limited by burst fire and recoil, making it ideal for the Beam Emitter augmentation, which it suffered less from than most weapons. Very light, it could serve as a main weapon while leaving room for a sidearm.
