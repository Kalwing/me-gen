Keiji Okuda was a thief and the crime partner and lover of Kasumi Goto until a heist got him killed by Donovan Hock. A former member of the Alliance Intelligence Agency under the alias Abraham Rumoi, he had a graybox neural implant fitted so he could remember information needed for his work. He and Kasumi met when both were hired to steal the same painting; they kept it instead of delivering it. Over time Keiji acquired highly sensitive information that would badly damage the Alliance's reputation if made public, and he came under prosecution for stealing and selling classified material, though prosecutors were barred from using his graybox memories against him under an Alliance self-incrimination statute. After the trial he vanished.

As shown in Mass Effect: Foundation, Keiji visited his and Kasumi's Illium apartment after his release, then agreed to a heist on Bekenstein targeting Donovan Hock. During the job Hock stabbed Keiji in the head, forcibly extracted his graybox, and left him to bleed out. Keiji survived long enough to tell Kasumi to recover the graybox so they could "be together again," his last words directing her to survive. Hock then spent years trying and failing to crack the graybox.

In Mass Effect 2, Kasumi enlisted Commander Shepard's help to raid Hock's estate on Bekenstein; the mission ended with Hock dead and the graybox recovered. Accessing it, Kasumi saw a hologram of Keiji revealing the hidden data and begging her to destroy it and his memories; Shepard could persuade her to comply or to keep it. In Mass Effect 3, the secret was revealed: the Alliance had conducted a black ops raid on a batarian group studying Reaper technology, and its exposure could start an Alliance-batarian war. In the synthesis ending, if the graybox was saved, an apparition of Keiji was seen reunited with Kasumi.
