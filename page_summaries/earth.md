Earth was the homeworld and capital of humanity, the third planet of the Sol System in the Milky Way's Local Cluster. Its orbit was cluttered with debris from early "bootstrap" space development, making kinetic barriers advisable above 85 km altitude.

Before the Reaper invasion, Earth was described as entering a new golden age. Resource wealth from a dozen settled colonies and a hundred industrial outposts flowed back to the planet, fueling industry, commerce and art, while arcology skyscrapers and telecommuting greened the great cities. Earth remained divided among nation-states affiliated under the Systems Alliance, and while every human enjoyed a longer and better life than ever, the gap between rich and poor widened; advanced nations had eliminated most genetic disease and pollution, while less fortunate regions remained smog-choked, overpopulated slums no further advanced than 20th-century technology. Sea levels had risen two meters over 200 years due to 21st-century environmental damage, though recent decades had seen improvement. Earth's cultural influence was disproportionate to humanity's brief time on the galactic stage. Known governments included the United North American States, the European Union, the Chinese People's Federation, and the Union of Incorporated Nations (UNIN), composed of former UN countries too weak to be founding Alliance members. Collector data placed Earth's human population in 2183 at 11,490,225,106.

The Reaper attack in 2186 ended this former life. Earth's great cities became storehouses of human DNA for Reaper harvesting, and Reaper gunships with megaton-scale firepower annihilated industrial centers in seconds. The nation-state militaries retained only partial communication with Alliance fleets, leaving resistance efforts uncoordinated, and the loss of the comm-buoy network cut Earth's economy off from the galaxy, sending shockwaves through galactic markets and hampering aid. The bluefin tuna was thought extinct by 2186, though a cryogenically frozen sample was bound for Andromeda. Earth featured in the missions Prologue: Earth and Priority: Earth.
