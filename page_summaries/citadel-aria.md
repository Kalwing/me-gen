Citadel: Aria was an assignment developed for the original Mass Effect but cut before release, and is not canon. It centered on an asari called Aria on the Citadel with whom Commander Shepard could have had repeated interactions, discussing major events such as Eden Prime, Noveria, Feros, and Caleston (the development name for Therum), and flirting with her, during which she would at one point describe melding. The player could have experienced at least three sessions with Aria, though what would have triggered the assignment's completion is unclear. Like other cut Citadel assignments, this Aria and her storyline closely resembled Sha'ira the Consort and the assignment Citadel: Asari Consort.
