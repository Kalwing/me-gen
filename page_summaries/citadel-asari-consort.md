Citadel: Asari Consort was an assignment in the original Mass Effect, available once Shepard could first explore the Citadel Presidium after the meeting at Ambassador Udina's office. At the Consort's Chambers on the Presidium, Shepard spoke with Nelyna and was then summoned by the Consort, Sha'ira, an asari who feared that a former client and friend, the turian General Septimus, was spreading false accusations that could damage her reputation after a falling-out. Sha'ira asked Shepard, as a fellow soldier, to speak to him.

Shepard found Septimus drinking at Chora's Den in the Wards. He was infatuated with Sha'ira, who had rejected his advances because of her position, and through dialogue Shepard convinced him to behave more like a general. Septimus then admitted he had told an elcor diplomat, Xeltan, that the Consort had leaked his secrets, when Septimus had actually uncovered them himself and had revealed Xeltan's secret out of spite. Speaking to Xeltan at the elcor-volus embassy completed the linked assignment Citadel: Xeltan's Complaint, after which Shepard returned to Sha'ira.

Sha'ira rewarded Shepard with credits, experience, and a "gift of words," a personalized affirmation whose second and third phrases varied with Shepard's pre-service history (Spacer, Colonist, or Earthborn) and psychological profile (Sole Survivor, War Hero, or Ruthless). If Shepard had completed Citadel: Xeltan's Complaint before returning, Sha'ira also gave a Prothean-design trinket usable at ruins on the planet Eletania in the Hercules system of the Attican Beta cluster, which triggered a brief vision of Protheans studying cavemen on Earth; skipping Xeltan left her door locked and forfeited the trinket. Expressing dissatisfaction with the reward prompted a sexual encounter, with no morality effect. In Mass Effect 2, a completed assignment produced a private email from Sha'ira reaffirming the gift of words, though a data-import bug made Galactic News report she was leaving the Citadel over intelligence-leak rumors regardless of the outcome; this was fixed in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. Internally the game called the assignment "Asari Companion," and it resembled the cut sub-plot Citadel: Aria.
