Hal McCann was a former Cerberus operative, drawn from the first edition of Mass Effect: Deception, which BioWare acknowledged contains lore discrepancies. A loyal operative, McCann had served aboard the space station where the Illusive Man conducted Reaper technology experiments on Paul Grayson. When a turian strike force attacked the station, McCann survived by playing dead until the turians removed him and other corpses to their ship. He then killed both pilots, flew the ship to Omega and sold it, hoping to use the money to re-establish contact with Cerberus. Before he could, he gambled away his money and his freedom and was sold to batarian slavers.

When the slave ship Glory of Khar'shan unsuccessfully attacked the quarian vessel Idenna and was itself captured, McCann was freed and encountered Gillian Grayson and Hendel Mitra. Concealing his Cerberus affiliation, he told Gillian her father had been killed and persuaded her to seek information on the Citadel, his own intended destination. On arrival, Hendel and Gillian brought McCann to David Anderson and Kahlee Sanders' apartment, which was being monitored by Cerberus assassin Kai Leng. Leng recognized McCann and Gillian and informed the Illusive Man, who ordered both killed before they could damage Cerberus. Knowing McCann was an avid gambler, Leng searched the Citadel's clubs and cornered him. They fought, and McCann prevailed partly due to Leng's preexisting injuries, but rather than finishing him or escaping he stayed to gloat. Leng stabbed McCann in the foot, then crushed his neck with a baton, leaving his body in a bathroom stall.
