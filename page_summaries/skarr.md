Skarr was a Krogan Battlemaster and mercenary, and a biotic powerful enough to lift a four-ton armored vehicle and throw it more than a dozen meters. In 2165, Edan Had'dah hired him to kill Kahlee Sanders after the Blue Suns failed. Skarr tracked Kahlee to Jon Grissom's home on Elysium, injured Grissom with his boot knife, and nearly killed David Anderson before Saren Arterius, who had followed him, drove him off.

Returning angry to Had'dah, Skarr took a new contract to destroy the Dah'tan Manufacturing facility on Camala and kill Had'dah's contact there, Jella, so nothing would link Had'dah to the events at Sidon. Skarr led the assault, but Jella barely escaped and later gave Had'dah's name to Saren. When Kahlee Sanders was being shipped off Camala in supposed secrecy, Skarr acted on a tip planted by Saren, intercepted the APC carrying her, and used a mass accelerator cannon to bring down the frigate SSV Iwo Jima before capturing Sanders. He stayed close to her at Had'dah's element zero refinery hideout to prevent escape. Badly injured when Anderson and Saren attacked the refinery, Skarr survived long enough to attack Anderson; after a hard fight he was wounded by Sanders and then killed by Anderson with a point-blank shot to the head.
