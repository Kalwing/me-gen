UNC: Hades' Dogs was the final assignment in the Cerberus chain in Mass Effect, following UNC: Missing Marines and its sequels. Cerberus had been conducting illegal genetic research in an effort to create an unstoppable army. After Commander Shepard found Admiral Kahoku dead in a Cerberus containment facility on Binthu, Shepard learned the location of Cerberus' main base of operations: the planet Nepheron in the Columbia system of the Voyager cluster.

The base was marked "Underground Facility" on the map. Its entrance was defended by two Cerberus Snipers and two Cerberus Anti-Tank infantry. Inside, past an anteroom holding a technician kit, the main room contained five Cerberus Commandos and three Cerberus Snipers (in the Legendary Edition, five self-healing snipers and fewer Commandos). The room offered chest-high crates for cover but no lootable containment cells. Beyond it lay two back rooms: the right held extensive loot (storage lockers, a medical station, a medical kit, an upgrade kit), while the left held the assignment's objective, a terminal. Activating it caused the optical database to flash itself; Shepard hurriedly copied as many encrypted files as possible to the hardsuit's internal computer before the system wiped and shut down.

On next accessing the Galaxy Map, a Shadow Broker agent contacted Shepard requesting the files, which Kahoku had promised the Broker in exchange for information on finding Cerberus. Shepard could transmit them for credits and nine Renegade points, or refuse for eight Paragon points, with the agent warning that the Shadow Broker would remember the refusal.

In Mass Effect 2, if the Cerberus assignments were completed, Miranda Lawson stated the bases belonged to Cerberus' military division, and Tali'Zorah commented that Cerberus had thought enslaving Thorian creepers and rachni was a good idea. A Citadel news feed referenced an investigation into Cerberus activity connected to Kahoku's death, possibly aided by testimony from Corporal Toombs and Doctor Wayne. In Lair of the Shadow Broker, the asari Spectre Tela Vasir was aware of Shepard's anti-Cerberus operations. The assignment is referred to internally as "Cerberus: Finale."
