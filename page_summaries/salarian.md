The salarians were warm-blooded amphibians native to Sur'Kesh and the second species to join the Citadel after the asari, discovered during the Tecunis Expedition a few decades after the asari found the station. They possessed a hyperactive metabolism, letting them function on one hour of sleep a day, think and move quickly, and retain photographic memories, but limiting their lifespan to roughly 40 human years. Their aptitude for observation and non-linear thinking made them the Council's premier researchers, inventors, and intelligence agents.

Salarians were haplo-diploid egg-layers: unfertilized eggs produced males, fertilized eggs produced females, and social codes restricted fertilization so that about 90 percent of the species was male. Females were cloistered, and powerful clan matriarchs called Dalatrasses ran politics through negotiation and dynastic interbreeding within the Salarian Union, a matrilineal government structured like medieval European nobility. Young salarians psychologically imprinted on the Dalatrass who isolated herself with their clutch. Salarians had no concept of romantic love or sexual attraction; Mordin Solus noted their reproduction was not hormone-based. Full names encoded homeworld, nation, city, district, clan, and given name. They celebrated Betau, their New Year. Modern salarians were largely irreligious, though some believed in a wheel of life resembling reincarnation. The Lystheni were an offshoot unwelcome in Council space.

Salarians thrived on Sur'Kesh 50,000 years before the modern age; Javik said the Protheans considered them "lizard people" and a delicacy. Their first three colonies were Aegohr, Mannovai, and Jaëto. After joining the Council, the salarians opened the records of the League of One intelligence service, whose exposed members then slaughtered the Union inner cabinet before being hunted down. The salarians uplifted the krogan with technology and a new homeworld to defeat the rachni, then, to end the Krogan Rebellions, developed the genophage that the turians deployed.

The salarian military was small and volunteer-based, focused on maneuver warfare and on winning wars before they began. It always struck first without warning, executing preemptive strikes hours ahead of an enemy attack and never issuing formal declarations of war. Its field arm was the Special Tasks Group (STG), whose effectiveness during the Krogan Rebellion inspired the Spectre program. Biotics were assigned to intelligence rather than combat. The salarians cultivated a strong alliance with the turians for protection. Their navy held sixteen dreadnoughts, fewer than the Treaty of Farixen allowed, owing to increasingly complex designs; after studying Normandy stealth technology they built stealth dreadnoughts, and their warships used costly ultraviolet antiship lasers, GARDIAN systems, and hull-mounted Thanix cannons. STG weapons included the Scorpion pistol and Venom shotgun. Notable salarians included Mordin Solus, Kirrahe, Councilor Valern, and Dalatrass Linron.
