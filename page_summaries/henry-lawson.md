Henry Lawson was a powerful businessman and the father of Miranda Lawson and her genetic twin Oriana. Obsessed with making his mark on history through a "genetic dynasty," he took his own DNA and doubled his X chromosome to create Miranda, ensuring she had desirable traits; she was not his first such offspring but the first he kept. He spoiled her growing up, but every gift served his long-term plan. Dissatisfied with her, he used her DNA to engineer a more ideal heiress, Oriana. Miranda rebelled, taking Oriana and fleeing after a violent shootout with his guards, and went to Cerberus for protection, prompting Henry to withdraw his support for the organization.

Knowing better than to pursue Miranda while she was under the Illusive Man's protection, Henry kept hunting for Oriana. In 2185 he contacted Miranda's childhood acquaintance Niket to find her, and Niket located Oriana's adoptive family on Illium and hired Eclipse to capture her. If Commander Shepard intervened, the plot failed and Niket was killed by either Miranda or Captain Enyala.

In Mass Effect 3, following Miranda's defection from Cerberus (or her death on the Collector Base), Henry renewed his support for the organization and built a base on Horizon called Sanctuary, luring refugees as subjects for experiments on husks and Reaper indoctrination in hopes of controlling them. He copied the husk-control signal but believed applying it to the Reapers themselves would be far harder. He located Oriana and brought her to Sanctuary. The Reapers detected his work, because he underestimated the strength of the Reaper signal, and assaulted Sanctuary just as Shepard arrived tracking Kai Leng. The Illusive Man sent Kai Leng to retrieve Henry's research data, which he took, abandoning Henry to the Reapers. Cornered in his laboratories, Henry grabbed Oriana as a human shield and tried to bargain. He boasted, "I will be seen as the savior of the human race," and claimed few had the "stomach" to do what survival required; he blamed Oriana's attempt to shoot him on Miranda's "poisonous influence," and Shepard could reply with disappointment that she missed. With Miranda absent, Shepard could let him go in exchange for Oriana or shoot either of them, though otherwise Oriana dragged them both through the cracked window to their mutual deaths. If Miranda was alive, she biotically hurled Henry through the damaged glass to his death regardless of Shepard's wishes; depending on her prior relationship with Shepard, Henry might shoot Miranda in the stomach as he fell, killing her shortly afterward without stopping his own death.
