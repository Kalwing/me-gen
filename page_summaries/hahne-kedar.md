Hahne-Kedar was an Earth-based manufacturing corporation and a major supplier to the Systems Alliance military. Described as reliable and efficient, its weapons were considered stock quality at best, while its armor lines were generally recognized as above average. The company maintained at least one subsidiary, the Hahne-Kedar Shadow Works. Commander Shepard began Mass Effect holding an armory license for Hahne-Kedar.

In Mass Effect, Hahne-Kedar weapons used reverse-engineered technology and consequently had lower damage output than rival manufacturers' products. Its lines included the Lancer assault rifles, Storm shotguns, Kessler pistols and Avenger sniper rifles. Its armor lines, produced for humans, krogan and turians, comprised Mantis, Predator, Scorpion and Ursa, plus the turian-exclusive Silverback; a field destabilizer used across all Hahne-Kedar armor gave increased shield efficiency compared with most competitors. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition the weapons received accuracy improvements and slower heat generation.

In Mass Effect: Revelation, because Hahne-Kedar weapons were standard Alliance issue, Kahlee Sanders identified an impostor posing as an Alliance security officer on Elysium by the non-Hahne-Kedar pistol he carried. In Mass Effect 2, Hahne-Kedar manufactured the FENRIS, LOKI and YMIR classes of mechanical infantry units, and an N7 mission involved shutting down a Hahne-Kedar factory producing defective machines. In Mass Effect 3, Hahne-Kedar produced components of customizable armor, its chest, shoulder, arm and leg pieces each granting a ten percent weapon damage bonus.

The dual name, read "H-K" when shortened, was noted as a possible reference to the German weapons manufacturer Heckler & Koch or to the assassin droid HK-47 from Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic. In the first game, Hahne-Kedar armors of the same weight and rank class differed only in appearance, not statistics. The Official Game Guide erroneously stated the Mantis Armor could be worn by quarians and misspelled the company as "Hahne-Kadar."
