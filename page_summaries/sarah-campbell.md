Private Sarah Campbell was an Alliance soldier serving aboard the Normandy SR-2 in 2186. Together with Private Bethany Westmoreland she guarded the doors separating the CIC from the conference and war rooms, and the pair passed the time offering opinions on the aftermath of Commander Shepard's major missions. After the tepid Council meeting following Mars she could not believe they would do nothing to help; after Primarch Adrien Victus's rescue from Menae she thought him a good leader and praised turian tenacity; after Eve's rescue from Sur'Kesh she judged that Cerberus forces had outmatched the salarians because of their Reaper implants. She watched vids of Kalros killing a Reaper Destroyer on Tuchanka and wished she had been there, cursed Cerberus and Councilor Udina after the failed Citadel coup, and understood the priority given to the geth-quarian war because losing the Migrant Fleet's supply lines meant losing the war. She was impressed that Shepard downed another Destroyer on Rannoch without a thresher maw, briefly lost morale after Thessia, and recovered after the success on Horizon, calling the Cerberus troops there inhuman for volunteering for Reaper tech.

Campbell derided Citadel civilians who acted as if there were no war and wanted payback for races that sat it out. She had a friend classified Category 6 and discharged after the Eden Prime attack who told her he was joining Cerberus instead; she reported it to her superiors to keep the association off her record. She had no love for the human-supremacist organization, needing a better reason than "it has scales" to kill an alien, and had heard from her old sergeant that Cerberus began as an Alliance black-ops arm. She kept a favorite mug for her shifts, which Shepard could recover during the Normandy's hijacking by the Shepard clone.
