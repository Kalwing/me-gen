The Arc Projector was a heavy weapon in Mass Effect 2. It ionized targets with a nonvisible laser to ready them for a high-voltage electrical attack; as the lightning-like bolt struck its first target, an autotargeting system painted successive targets with the ionization laser so the electricity arced between them along the path of least resistance, allowing an entire enemy strike team to be shocked to death with a few trigger pulls.

The weapon was a downloadable content item available if the Cerberus Network had been activated, first equippable once Shepard was given command of the Normandy SR-2. In Mass Effect Legendary Edition it was included by default with no DLC or Cerberus Network activation required, available at the same point. The Illusive Man sent it to the Normandy's armory with an email explaining that Cerberus operatives had used the device successfully against a geth scouting party in the Skyllian Verge and that he wanted field reports on its performance, with plans to train other operatives using Shepard's tactics.

Mechanically, the Arc Projector required two seconds to charge before firing, and staggering hits from biotics, missiles, or melee interrupted the charge. It had limited range for a heavy weapon, dealt heavy damage to shields, moderate damage to armor and barriers, and least to health, plus roughly 20 to 30 percent extra damage to synthetic targets. Its chain lightning jumped to nearby targets, hitting up to five enemies, with each successive enemy taking slightly less damage; the chaining often ignored cover and could strike cloaked Geth Hunters and shielded Collector Guardians. The weapon carried most effects of Overload, including detonating mechs and pyros on death and stunning unprotected synthetics, and could inflict a Neural Shock-like stun on organics that worked even on protected enemies and permanently stopped health regeneration. It always triggered that stun on krogan except during their charge animation, while klixen were immune. The Arc Pistol in Mass Effect 3 was a heavy pistol variant of the Arc Projector, created by Admiral Daro'Xen vas Moreh.
