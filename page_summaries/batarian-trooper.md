Batarian Troopers were the basic grunts of batarian mercenary or terrorist groups, usually armed with an assault rifle. In the first Mass Effect they appeared during the mission to stop batarians from crashing an asteroid into the human colony of Terra Nova, deployed across the asteroid to defend positions on the exterior and interior of the various facilities, carrying assault rifles and shotguns. These Troopers were typically first into combat and the quickest to leave cover, staying exposed longer and popping out more often than their fellow soldiers. They had access to the immunity ability and used it frequently, fought at varying ranges, moved from cover to cover for better positions, and tried to flank their enemies.

In Mass Effect 2, Batarian Troopers were encountered during the mission N7: Javelin Missiles Launched and were believed to be batarian terrorists. They were combat-similar to Eclipse and Blue Suns troopers. They carried M-8 Avenger assault rifles to lay down constant fire on Shepard's squad and had no offensive powers. They lacked shields on Veteran difficulty and below but gained them on higher difficulties. They posed a threat mainly through numbers, and were often found grouped tightly together in the facility, making area-of-effect heavy weapons such as the Arc Projector and M-490 Blackstorm effective; on Hardcore and Insanity, Disruptor Ammo and Overload helped break their shields. The mission's time limit was described as more than sufficient on most difficulties, allowing players to pick them off from cover.
