Armistan Banes was a researcher hired by the Systems Alliance who disappeared under mysterious circumstances. In Mass Effect, his name arose when a krogan blackmailer, attempting to extort medical supplies from Dr. Chloe Michel on the Citadel, claimed to be acting on Banes' behalf. Dr. Michel told Commander Shepard that she and Banes had worked together years earlier but had lost contact, and that as far as she knew Banes had been contracted by the Alliance military for research in the Attican Traverse. A squad member suggested Shepard ask Captain Anderson.

When asked, Anderson was surprised, questioned where Shepard had heard the name, then decided he did not want to know. He revealed that Banes had been dead for some time, a fact not publicly known, and that Banes' corpse had been found on a derelict ship in the Sparta system. Anderson could say no more and directed Shepard to Rear Admiral Kahoku, whose crew had recovered the body. Speaking with Kahoku, Shepard learned that the marines who found the corpse had themselves gone missing. Because of the "classified project" Anderson mentioned and the fate of Kahoku's marines, Banes may have been involved in a Cerberus-sponsored project. To pursue the full chain of leads, the assignment Citadel: Doctor Michel had to be completed before UNC: Missing Marines, since Kahoku left the Citadel afterward.

In Mass Effect 3, if Dr. Michel was invited aboard the Normandy SR-2 instead of Dr. Chakwas, and Shepard visited the crew quarters directly after the Sur'Kesh mission with Mordin Solus or Padok Wiks temporarily aboard, Dr. Michel and Engineer Adams could be overheard talking. Dr. Michel clarified that she had once worked with Armistan Banes, that she had believed he was with the Alliance, but that he had turned out to be a Cerberus agent.
