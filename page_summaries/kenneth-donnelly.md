Kenneth "Ken" Donnelly was a Scottish former Alliance engineer, a Cerberus operative in the Lazarus Cell and an engineer aboard the Normandy SR-2 in 2185. He worked in the engine room with his best friend Gabriella Daniels; they had graduated from the same tech academy class and served aboard the SSV Perugia at the Battle of the Citadel. After the Alliance discredited the Reapers, Kenneth openly defended Commander Shepard to the point of near-insubordination, which drew the Illusive Man's attention; Gabby insisted on being included in the Cerberus contract. Both seemed largely ignorant of Cerberus's darker operations, Kenneth saying he joined to "kick the Collectors right in the daddy-bags." He and Gabby needed FBA couplings for their maintenance problems, purchasable from Nashan Stellar Dynamics, after which he invited Shepard to Skyllian-five poker. He frequently made crude comments about the women aboard, drawing scolding from Gabby. He was among the crew abducted by the Collectors and died if Shepard took too long to rescue them.

In Mass Effect 3, if he survived, Kenneth was taken into Alliance custody when the Normandy was impounded; Shepard could use Spectre status to clear him back to the engine room. Chief Engineer Adams praised his talent and dedication but noted his disregard for the chain of command. Kenneth and Gabby (if alive) resumed their banter and continued his crude remarks about EDI's new body, asari aging, and Samantha Traynor. He also showed a serious side, deriding the Illusive Man's taste for bourbon over Islay scotch, and helped adapt eezo capacitor improvements for the Crucible. He refused to discuss the Collector abduction. Eventually Gabby confronted him for never appreciating her; if Shepard sided with her, Kenneth began complimenting Gabby and the two could be "caught" together in the sub-deck. Before the final battle he wished he could fight beside Shepard. After the war, if Shepard used the Crucible with sufficient preparation, Kenneth, Gabby, and Major Coats oversaw reconstruction in London. If Gabby died in the Collector attack but Kenneth survived, he could still be recruited but was withdrawn and silent, no longer engaging in banter.
