Rakhana was the homeworld of the drell. Once a world teeming with life, with arid plains home to spectacular insect and reptile analogues, it was ruined when the drell industrialized early and did not recognize the environmental damage until too late. Disastrous industrial expansion beginning around the 1380s CE steadily destroyed the planet's sustainability over the following centuries. With topsoil depleted and oceans grown too acidic to sustain life, the drell, who numbered around 11 billion at their height but lacked the means to leave their planet, faced a massive population crash.

Around the 1980s CE the hanar made first contact with the drell and mounted a large-scale rescue operation, evacuating drell to the hanar homeworld Kahje over the following decade. Roughly 375,000 drell escaped in the exodus (the codex also cites approximately 2025 CE for the crash and rescue). The billions left behind gradually perished, warring over dwindling resources. To repay the debt, the drell entered an agreement with the hanar called the Compact, under which the drell perform tasks the hanar cannot physically manage; high-ranking hanar are often inseparable from their drell attendants. Present-day Rakhana is a dry, barren cemetery world of rocky deserts, home to only a few thousand drell in scattered clusters of no more than a few hundred, though the population can double during pilgrimage season. Rakhana once bore flowers called usharet, sweet-smelling scarlet blossoms of cultural significance to the drell, used in perfumes and in artful topiaries.
