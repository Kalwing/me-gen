"Dossier: The Professor" was a Mass Effect 2 recruitment mission acquired automatically after Freedom's Progress. The dossier described Dr. Mordin Solus as a salarian biological weapons expert, formerly of the Special Tasks Group, whose technology might hold the key to countering Collector attacks, currently running a medical clinic in the slums of Omega. Completing it first unlocked the Normandy's tech lab, needed to use tech upgrades found on other missions.

On Omega, EDI warned that Mordin's district was quarantined due to a plague that affected every species except humans and vorcha. After gathering information from Aria T'Loak at Afterlife, Shepard talked or fought past the turian guard into the slums, where Blue Suns mercenaries had sealed victims in to contain the outbreak and the Blood Pack was moving into Blue Suns territory. Shepard could aid a sick batarian who blamed humans for the plague, and fought through Blue Suns and Blood Pack forces (vorcha, krogan, varren, and Pyros) to reach Mordin's clinic.

Mordin, talking rapidly as ever, quickly deduced Shepard's Cerberus ties and revealed he had already developed a cure but needed to distribute it through the district's air-control system, which was held by Blood Pack vorcha. When the air flow then shut down entirely, Shepard volunteered to clear Environmental Control, reactivate the ventilation fans, and disperse the cure. Mordin gave Shepard the M-6 Carnifex Hand Cannon and asked that his assistant Daniel, who had gone into vorcha territory alone, be rescued alive. After fighting through more Blood Pack forces and vorcha working with the Collectors, Shepard restored both fans and distributed the cure. Mordin, satisfied and eager for a new challenge, joined the crew and set up the Normandy's tech lab. If rescued, Daniel emailed Shepard thanking him and reporting the clinic was recovering. Rewards included the M-6 Carnifex, element zero, palladium, platinum, iridium, and Medi-Gel Capacity and Assault Rifle Damage upgrades.
