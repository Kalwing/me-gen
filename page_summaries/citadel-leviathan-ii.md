Citadel: Leviathan II was a mission of the Leviathan DLC for Mass Effect 3, acquired after completing Mahavid: Leviathan. An artifact recovered to Dr. Bryson's office was tied to Leviathan, and Commander Shepard returned to the lab on the Citadel to investigate it. After EDI created a shield around the artifact to suppress its energies, Shepard searched for the next lead.

At a Project Information terminal in the second room, EDI revealed that Garneau had been working on Project Basilisk when he died, but that Derek Hadley had wiped the project information from the servers and backups before he killed Bryson. Two more galaxy-map clues could be found. The first came from a requisition locker: examining a PDA in a drawer next to the husk experiment yielded an access card, and comparing a missing item with one still present in the locker, which included an Assault Rifle Omni-blade upgrade, prompted EDI to add a search filter. The second came from ruling out a relay system: after a wall console proved unhelpful, a datapad showed that Ann Bryson had flown on a long-range ship called the Icarus, and its specifications let EDI and Shepard conclude that the destination lay outside a relay network, since a cheaper ship would otherwise have been used. A message from Ann Bryson to her father could be heard on his personal terminal upstairs.

Datapads in the lab, logged by Derek Hadley, recorded energy-emission tests on artifact P3X118 that found it inert and safe and recommended abandoning the hunt for Leviathan in favor of studying the artifact. With the precise next location determined, Shepard could return to the Normandy to investigate the planet, leading to Leviathan: Find Ann Bryson.
