Krogan Prisoners were krogan kidnapped by Cerberus and used for experimentation. Randall Ezno encountered them aboard the Barn space station during his escape (Mass Effect: Infiltrator). As enemies they had high health and armor and a fast-regenerating health bar, but did not take cover and had no regenerating shields. They could carry M-333 Particle Beams or M-22 Eviscerator shotguns and closed distance rapidly, with a Berserker charge that dealt heavy damage and forced the player out of cover. Recommended tactics were sustained high-DPS fire from the M-96 Mattock Auto or M-333 Particle Beam combined with abilities like Salvo, targeting the head for bonus damage, using biotic Pull rather than Leash (to which they were immune) to stop their charge, and using Tactical Cloak to disengage if they got close.
