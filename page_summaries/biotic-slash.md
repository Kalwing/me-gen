Brief gameplay page with minimal lore prose: Biotic Slash was a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode, used by the N7 Slayer Vanguard. It charged the user's sword with biotic energy to slash nearby enemies in a wide swath, flattening unshielded opponents, dealt increased damage to armor and barriers, travelled through physical objects, and could detonate all four types of power combos. It was described as a biotic counterpart to the Electric Slash power.
