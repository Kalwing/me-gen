Captain Dillard was a Systems Alliance military official responsible for overseeing the test flight of the SSV Normandy at Arcturus Station, as depicted in the comic Mass Effect: He Who Laughs Best. Dillard accompanied Captain David Anderson and met with Turian Hierarchy representative General Invectus while preparations for the test flight were underway.

When the Normandy's crew sent a distress signal reporting that the ship had been hijacked, Dillard had to respond to the unexpected situation. After learning that the assigned pilot, Crooks, was locked in a shuttle and therefore not responsible, Dillard assumed an enemy agent had seized the ship and deployed a fighter patrol to pursue and disable it. Jeff "Joker" Moreau then revealed that he was the one flying the Normandy, and he evaded the patrol's fire and returned the ship unharmed. When Joker was apprehended, Dillard told him he would be court-martialed and imprisoned for stealing the ship. Invectus, however, was impressed by Joker's piloting and insisted that he be made the Normandy's pilot.
