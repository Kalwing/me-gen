Vido Santiago was a human mercenary and, with Zaeed Massani, a co-founder of the Blue Suns. Zaeed commanded the mercenaries while Vido managed finances, and the two clashed when Vido wanted to hire cheap batarian labor. Around 2165 Vido betrayed Zaeed, paying six of their men to hold Zaeed down while he shot him in the head, then took control of the Blue Suns and ran them from the shadows. He later ceded de facto leadership to the batarian Solem Dal'serah, improving public relations and his own safety, and took the title "Co-Executive Officer."

In 2185, Vido was contacted by a Dr. Farrin of ExoGeni to safely transport a Prothean artifact on Joab. Judging the artifact more valuable than the fee, Vido betrayed Farrin, had Captain Narom torture Farrin's intermediary for the dig site location, and had the artifact stolen and moved to the hijacked MSV Strontium Mule. He also seized a refinery on Zorya, using its workers as slave labor. If Commander Shepard granted Zaeed's request to hunt him down, Vido confronted the pair as they stormed the facility, taunting Zaeed: "Actually, take your shot. Give my men a reason to put you down like the mad dog you are. Again." The fight started a fire endangering the Eldfell-Ashland refinery's civilian workers, forcing Shepard to choose between stopping Vido for good and saving the workers. If the workers were saved, Vido escaped in a gunship, muttering he would see Zaeed in another twenty years. If they were left to die, Zaeed wounded Vido before he reached the gunship; Vido begged for his life, calling the failed assassination "nothing personal" and a business decision, but Zaeed ejected a heat sink into a pool of flammable liquid and Vido burned to death. Cut Mass Effect 3 content had a Reaper Harvester carry Vido away if he escaped.
