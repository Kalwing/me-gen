The Sentinel was a playable class across Mass Effect, Mass Effect 2, and Mass Effect 3, and a selectable profile in Mass Effect: Andromeda, combining tech expertise with biotic aptitude. Kaidan Alenko was an L2 Sentinel.

In the first game, Sentinels were support-oriented, able to equip both combat-specialized biotic amps and omni-tools, and had biotic and tech abilities to bypass locks and security, disable or control enemies, and heal the party. They were moderately skilled with pistols but average with other weapons, and had durable shields with access to shield-boosting talents including Barrier. They were the only playable class without an armor talent, so they lacked Shield Boost and could not wear heavier armor. Their class talent reduced ability cooldowns and improved pistol accuracy and damage, culminating in Marksman for a Sentinel Shepard, though such a Shepard could not reach the Advanced or Master levels of Marksman. Sentinel Shepard could specialize into Bastion or Medic. Class talents included Throw, Lift, Barrier, Stasis, Decryption, Electronics, First Aid, and Medicine.

In Mass Effect 2, Sentinels brought both tech and biotic abilities and were equipped with an advanced ablative armor system, Tech Armor, which when overloaded stunned nearby enemies, allowing a more aggressive, cover-light "Juggernaut" playstyle. Their powers were Throw, Warp, Tech Armor, Overload, Cryo Blast, and Defender, with training in heavy pistols, submachine guns, and heavy weapons.

In Mass Effect 3, Sentinels gained complete weapons training, kept the detonatable Tech Armor, and used Throw, Warp, Lift Grenade, Tech Armor, Overload, Cryo Blast, Offensive Mastery, and Fitness. Their weight capacity bonus was comparable to the Infiltrator and Vanguard. In Andromeda the Sentinel profile linked Ryder's armor to its computer systems for flash-forged shielding, with Tech Armor as its bonus power granting +30 damage resistance. The Sentinel was consistently the second least-picked Shepard class.
