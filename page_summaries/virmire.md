Virmire was a lush frontier world located in the Milky Way, in the Hoc System of the Sentry Omega cluster, positioned as the third planet and described as being on the frontier of the Attican Traverse. Its vast seas and orbital position in the inner life zone had created a wide equatorial band of humid, tropical terrain, making it ideal for colonization by carbon-based species. Despite this suitability, the political instability of the nearby Terminus Systems had blocked all colonization efforts, since the high risk of raids by pirates and slavers made it an unappealing homestead.

The Citadel had repeatedly opened negotiations to settle Virmire with the various criminal gangs and petty dictatorships of the Terminus Systems, but every attempt collapsed due to internal power shifts among the opposing parties. The Citadel eventually wrote off the colonization of Virmire as impossible without significant political change. The Terminus powers themselves were considered unlikely ever to settle the world, as most lacked the resources to support the settlement of a virgin planet and found it more expedient to steal from their neighbors than to build for themselves.

In 2183, Virmire became the site of Saren Arterius's hidden research base, where he bred an army of krogan using a cure for the genophage and conducted indoctrination experiments. Commander Shepard traveled there after a salarian STG reconnaissance team sent a garbled distress signal, and the resulting mission chain ended with the base's destruction by a nuclear device and the death of one of Shepard's squadmates. Reaching Virmire required completing two of the three major plot worlds Feros, Noveria, or Therum. The planet's native lifeforms included Pod Crabs. Virmire was inspired by the islands of Palau, with rock formations based on Thailand's Phi Phi Islands, and was originally intended to be an island paradise and galactic vacation spot before the story was changed. The comic Mass Effect: Blasto: Eternity is Forever takes place on Virmire.
