This page catalogues the armor and weapons available in the mobile game Mass Effect: Infiltrator, in which the Cerberus operative Randall Ezno buys gear from an in-game store using credits earned or redeemed through found Cerberus intel. Each armor set comprises a body piece and a helmet that offer separate bonuses and can be mixed and matched. Weapons are also bought from the store and improved through purchased upgrades. The turian protagonist of the bonus mission uses a fixed basic armor and weapon that cannot be changed or upgraded.

Four armor sets are described. Cerberus Assault Armour favors offense, granting +10% biotic damage with +10% weapon damage from its helmet, and each piece costs 2000 credits. Aegis Defensive Armour combines N7 vest plating with tungsten alloy, giving +10% health and +10% shield strength from the helmet. The Stealth Suit, used by covert special forces, cuts tactical cloak recharge by 10% and adds +10% headshot damage via the helmet. Infiltrator Armour, which Randall starts with, grants smaller +3% bonuses to melee damage, weapon damage, and shield strength across both pieces.

Weapons include the M-96 Mattock Auto (a fully automatic version of the classic rifle, Randall's starting assault rifle), the M-22 Eviscerator shotgun (which fires serrated metal edges and violates intergalactic weapons treaties), the M-29 Incisor burst-fire sniper rifle, and the M-333 Particle Beam. The M-333 is an experimental Cerberus radiation weapon reverse-engineered from Collector technology, effective against shields and barriers but weak against armor, handles like a heavy weapon that prevents sprinting, does not require pausing to cool when it overheats, changes beam color as it levels, and is the only weapon exclusive to Infiltrator. Each upgradeable weapon has five levels: level 1 is basic function, with subsequent tiers (Tungsten Jackets, Target Optics VI, Scram Rails, Antarctic Heatsinks, and equivalents) adding penetration, accuracy, damage, and cooling for costs from 500 up to 5000 credits. The M-37 Falcon is the fixed weapon of the turian bonus-mission protagonist, switching between assault rifle and shotgun modes, each with its own heat level; it cannot be upgraded and Randall cannot use it.
