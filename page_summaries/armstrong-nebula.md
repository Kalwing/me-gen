The Armstrong Nebula was a star cluster located in the Skyllian Verge, connected by mass relay to the Attican Beta cluster. Most of its worlds were unexplored, primitive, or notable only for a few geological features. Denizens from the Terminus Systems frequently used the nebula's out-of-the-way locations as anchorages, drive discharge sites, and other covert operations, and pirates were common there, countered by a limited Alliance patrol presence.

During Mass Effect, Alliance surveillance drones, together with data Commander Shepard could obtain from hacked terminals on Feros or Virmire, revealed that geth forces were using the cluster as a staging ground. Admiral Hackett could only speculate whether the geth were preparing to attack human colonies or gathering intelligence, but the terminal data indicated that Saren Arterius intended to use them for a major offensive against Citadel Space. This tied into the assignment UNC: Geth Incursions and related tasks (UNC: Asari Writings, UNC: Locate Signs of Battle, UNC: Prothean Data Discs, UNC: Turian Insignias, and UNC: Valuable Minerals) set across the cluster.

The nebula contained the systems Gagarin, Grissom, Hong, Tereshkova, and Vamshi. It was named after Neil Armstrong, the first human to walk on the Moon, with its systems named for landmarks in human space exploration. Its cluster-view design was identical to that of the Arcturus Stream.
