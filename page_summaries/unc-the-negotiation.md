UNC: The Negotiation was an assignment in Mass Effect. Admiral Hackett asked Commander Shepard to negotiate a treaty with a warlord named Darius over mining rights to an asteroid rich in Element Zero, authorizing Shepard to make any financial or diplomatic concessions necessary. The assignment became available only at 80% Renegade (or 90% Renegade if 80% Paragon had been reached first). The rendezvous was on Nonuel in the Plutus system of the Hades Gamma cluster, at the Warlord's Outpost.

Darius opened the talks on the offensive, angered that the Alliance had sent a "grunt" rather than a trained negotiator, and insulted Shepard regardless of gender or psychological profile. He had four demands: that Shepard call him "Lord," that the Alliance apologize for intruding and mining on his territory, that he receive eezo, money, and equipment for making red sand in exchange for mining rights, and that Shepard ask nicely and say "please." Choosing only the uppermost dialogue replies capitulated to each demand and secured a peaceful deal, during which Shepard could learn that the Alliance itself had installed Darius in the region to stabilize it after the batarians were driven out. Objecting to the first three demands required a later apology to continue; refusing the "please" started a firefight regardless.

If negotiations concluded peacefully, Darius let Shepard leave with an OSD detailing the arrangement. If a fight broke out, Shepard faced Darius and nine Darius Retainers of several races and loadouts (including three krogan), all able to regenerate health and shields, in a large room with three exploitable power junctions; an Overload on Darius could detonate the junction on his balcony. The base exit locked until all hostiles were dead and a terminal in the upper room was accessed. During the debrief, Hackett revealed he had expected Shepard to kill Darius rather than negotiate, but would honor a treaty if one was made; the setup was obvious but the Alliance could not officially condone an assassination. The Renegade completion text quoted "Millions for Defense, but not one cent in tribute." The assignment is referred to internally as "Shepard Unchained."
