Cannibals were synthetic-organic creatures derived from batarians and humans and mutated by Reaper technology. During the Reaper attack on Earth in 2186, Cannibals formed a significant portion of the Reaper ground forces alongside Husks. They served as front-line Reaper units created from corrupted batarians, and their nickname referred to their habit of devouring the bodies of fallen comrades. Consuming a corpse triggered a biochemical process through which a Cannibal spontaneously healed itself and grew new chitinous armor plating. The transformation also appeared to give the creature greater awareness of its surroundings, leading to more strategic behavior and more careful use of battlefield cover.

Offensively, Cannibals wielded a large gun held in place by a human corpse that served as an arm; from that same arm cannon they could also launch grenades, though the Cannibals encountered during the Earth prologue did not throw grenades. They could also attempt melee grabs, and in multiplayer they executed downed players by regurgitating stomach acid. Defensively they were large, slow, and unable to dodge powers, making them easy targets, and they were not panicked by burning effects. Their health regeneration, gained only by eating a corpse, could be paused by freezing or halted permanently by incendiary ammunition applied after they had fed. The armor-plated state gained from feeding or from Marauder buffs was a separate condition that protected body regions, often shielding their heads from headshots, until the plates were shot off or penetrated by cover-piercing weapons. On missions such as Priority: Earth, Cannibals appeared with both armor plating and regenerating health by default. Powers and Power Combos ignored their plating, and weapons that left no corpses, such as the Particle Rifle, Reegar Carbine, and Krysae Sniper Rifle, denied them the ability to heal.
