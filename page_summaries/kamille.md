Kamille was a member of Delta Squad, an Alliance special forces unit, and a sincere, disciplined soldier. She was in a relationship with Mason and repeatedly rebuffed Essex's persistent advances. In Mass Effect: Paragon Lost, Delta Squad arrived at the colony of Fehl Prime in 2183 during a Blood Pack mercenary attack; their shuttle was shot down and Lieutenant James Vega took command after Captain Toni was incapacitated. During the counterattack, Kamille and Essex flanked and devastated the enemy; Essex accidentally threw a varren onto her with his biotics, and he was taken down by Brood while helping her. Vega disabled Brood, and Kamille credited the victory to him.

Two years later Fehl Prime had recovered, with a heavy anti-ship cannon and kinetic barriers, and Kamille was openly with Mason. When Delta Squad investigated a jamming signal, they found an unknown alien device, which Essex destroyed on Vega's order over Treeya's objections. The colony was then attacked by the Collectors, who had been abducting Terminus colonies. The squad found the colonists paralyzed by seeker swarms. An attempt to destroy the Collector ship with the anti-ship cannon failed because the cannon fired at half power, and the ship destroyed it. Covering the retreat to underground labs, Kamille ran out of thermal clips and was carried off by a single Collector. She was processed into a Praetorian; her head and largely intact torso remained recognizable among the creature's other heads. Mason approached the abomination and was incinerated by its particle weapon. Vega and Essex later destroyed the Praetorian, and any remains of Kamille disintegrated in an industrial vat. She was voiced by Laura Bailey.
