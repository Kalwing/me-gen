The Praetorian was a slow-moving aerial Collector construct assembled from thirty deformed husks fused together, armed with twin particle beams and protected by an armored carapace and a strong biotic barrier. It was likely named after the Praetorian Guard of the Roman Emperors.

In 2185 Commander Shepard fought Praetorians during Mass Effect 2, once at the end of the Horizon mission and once during the escape from the Collector Cruiser; several also attacked the Normandy SR-2. In that game the Praetorian attacked with a roughly three-second particle beam and with "Death Choir," a ground strike followed by a near-instant-kill area blast that also fully recharged its barrier. It was invulnerable during Death Choir, which it used after every quarter of its armor was stripped by weapons fire (but not by armor-damaging powers such as Incinerate, Warp, or Reave). It was immune to Stasis, Charge, and Dominate. Recommended tactics included armor-piercing or Warp Ammo, the M-9 Tempest or Geth Plasma Shotgun against its barrier, keeping distance and moving perpendicular to its beams, and circling large cover it would not fly over.

In the animated film Paragon Lost, the Collectors deployed a Praetorian on Fehl Prime during the colonist abductions, built in part from Delta Squad marine Kamille; its attacks and barrier glowed green rather than blue, and it was destroyed by James Vega after the ship Essex disabled its barrier.

Praetorians returned in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer with the Retaliation Pack and in the Armax Arsenal Arena combat simulator. There they swept their twin particle cannons in a wide line that penetrated thin cover and dealt continuous heavy damage (the most damaging enemy ranged attack in the game), used a long-range pounce, and could grab enemies for an instant kill. They were highly mobile despite their size, had very high armor and a sizeable barrier, put up a spherical barrier that blocked most power damage when hit while hovering, and could themselves be "possessed" for extra durability. They often appeared in groups of two.
