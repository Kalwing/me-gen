Inferno Grenade was a power in Mass Effect 2 and a grenade power in Mass Effect 3's single-player and multiplayer modes. It launched a volatile grenade that exploded on impact, sending searing waves of flame and fragments in all directions; the fire damage was especially effective against armor, dealing roughly twice damage to it.

In Mass Effect 2 the power detonated on contact with the first object hit, could be evolved at Rank 4 into Heavy Inferno Grenade (higher temperature, more damage) or Inferno Blast Grenade (wider splash zone, more fragments), and had a recharge time of six seconds for Shepard and nine for Zaeed. It could deal friendly-fire damage, halt health regeneration in organic enemies such as vorcha and krogan, panic unprotected organics, stun protected ones, and hit enemies crouched behind cover through indirect placement, including collapsing Collector Guardian shields. Synthetic enemies such as the geth resisted fire and did not panic. It was available to Shepard via Advanced Training and to Zaeed Massani as his loyalty power.

In Mass Effect 3 it cluster-bombed a small area with incendiary munitions, dealing 100 damage per second (150 in multiplayer) over eight seconds in a five-meter radius at Rank 1, with evolutions increasing damage, radius, grenade capacity, armor damage and shrapnel. Grenade powers had no recharge but consumed one grenade from a shared pool per use. It dealt 1.5x damage to armor, primed enemies for Fire Explosions (but could not itself detonate power combos), and spread shrapnel through terrain without line of sight. Unlike in Mass Effect 2 it did not stop enemy health regeneration. In single-player it was available to Ashley Williams, Zaeed Massani and Shepard (as a bonus power); in multiplayer to the Batarian Enforcer Soldier and Krogan Soldier. A bug caused it to combine destructively with Incendiary Ammo into an overly damaging effect.
