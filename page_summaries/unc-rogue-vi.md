UNC: Rogue VI was an assignment in Mass Effect. A virtual intelligence had taken over an Alliance training ground on Luna, Earth's moon, and stopped responding to shutdown commands. Admiral Hackett asked Commander Shepard to eliminate the VI and reclaim the facility. The assignment triggered after Shepard reached level 20 (level 11 in Legendary Edition with Legendary Mode) on next traveling to another system, immediately opening access to the Local Cluster and Sol system. Enemies were synthetic combat drones (Advanced Assault Drones and Advanced Rocket Drones, the latter able to one-shot Shepard), largely immune to physics-based biotics, making tech powers such as AI Hacking, Overload, and Sabotage valuable; Tali'Zorah was an especially strong squadmate.

The facility had three bunkers, each with two rooftop turrets and, inside, a main room full of drones and back rooms containing four VI Conduits apiece (eight per bunker). Each time a system of VI Conduits was destroyed, a new defense activated: toxic gas in bunker one, kinetic barriers over doorways and conduits in bunker two, and additional combat drones in bunker three, which held the hardest fight. Destroying all the VI Conduits shut down the rooftop turrets and any remaining drones. Completing the mission unlocked Shepard's specialization class.

After the VI was destroyed, terminals displayed a message repeating in binary that translated to "HELP," and the post-assignment journal entry hinted the VI may have been deliberately sabotaged to make it achieve sentience. In Mass Effect 2, Miranda Lawson revealed the VI was named Hannibal, and conversations with EDI more or less confirmed the Luna incident had been an experiment in creating controllable AI that went wrong. In Mass Effect 3, a Cerberus report in Priority: Cerberus Headquarters revealed the VI was later modified with technology salvaged from Sovereign, and the resulting AI became EDI. The assignment is referred to internally as "Ghost in the Machine."
