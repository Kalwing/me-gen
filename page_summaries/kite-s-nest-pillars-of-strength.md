Kite's Nest: Pillars of Strength was a side mission in Mass Effect 3, acquired after Priority: The Citadel I by listening to a batarian preacher standing near the entrance of Cargo Hold C in the Docks: Holding Area. The preacher wanted the Pillars of Strength, a batarian artifact, recovered from the Kite's Nest to improve his people's morale. The mission and artifact could alternatively be obtained by conducting a Search and Rescue operation in the Kite's Nest cluster, even before the Citadel docks became available; in that case the journal entry was named "Citadel: Pillars of Strength" until the player heard the preacher, at which point it reverted.

To complete it, Shepard scanned the batarian homeworld of Khar'shan, the second planet in the Harsa system, to retrieve the pillars, then returned to the Citadel. The pillars could not be handed to the preacher until after Priority: Palaven, since the refugee area only opened then. Delivering them rewarded 15,000 credits, 5 Reputation, and an update to the Batarian Fleet War Asset, with the preacher thanking Shepard for restoring faith to his people.
