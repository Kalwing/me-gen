The raloi were an avian species originating on the planet Turvess. They were avian in appearance and possessed feathers. They made first contact with the asari in 2184 after launching their first space telescope and discovering the asari cruiser Azedes in their system. During the ceremonies held to welcome the raloi to the galactic community, an outbreak of an alien flu virus (identified in full reports as the "H7N7 avian flu virus") spread among them, forcing the raloi to wear environmental suits whenever in contact with other species. The elcor had apparently known of the raloi far longer, preserving a mnemonic, "Happy Turians Treat Raloi and Rachni to Dinner," considered common knowledge among elcor at least three generations earlier.

As reported by Cerberus Daily News, in 2185 the Citadel Council formally welcomed the raloi to the galactic community. Council-sponsored shuttles brought visitors and gifts of goodwill to Turvess. Krogan representatives of the Council delegation were ejected from the proceedings after introducing a violent sport called Kowla that caused multiple deaths and injuries. A raloi delegation, including Representative Ari'ka, was then sent to the Citadel for a three-month stay to study galactic law, history, alien biology and culture, and mass effect physics.

In 2186, during the Reaper invasion, the raloi delegation withdrew from the Citadel. Unwilling and unable to fight the Reapers, the raloi chose to isolate themselves on Turvess and destroy their satellites and orbital observation equipment, hoping the Reapers would mistake them for a pre-spaceflight civilization and spare the planet.
