Mass Effect 3: Omega was a DLC pack for Mass Effect 3, released November 27, 2012 for Xbox 360 and PC (November 27 worldwide for PlayStation 3 except Europe, November 28), never released for Wii U, and later included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. In its story, Cerberus, led by General Oleg Petrovsky, one of the Illusive Man's most accomplished agents, had seized the space station Omega after luring Aria T'Loak away. Aria teamed with Commander Shepard to retake her domain, joined by Nyreen Kandros, the turian leader of the Talon mercenary group, who fought to free Omega's enslaved population. New enemies included the Cerberus-manipulated Reaper creature called the Adjutant and the Rampart Mech.

The pack contained five missions (Citadel: Aria T'Loak; Omega: Aria T'Loak; and three "Assist" missions for the Hacker, the Mechanic, and Harrot), two temporary squadmates (Aria and Nyreen), two locations (Omega and Aria's Bunker), two weapons (N7 Valkyrie and Chakram Launcher), five weapon mods, four new War Assets (Omega Eezo Hoard, Omega Raiding Fleet, General Oleg Petrovsky, Aria's hacker), two bonus powers (Flare and Lash), and a cabin item, General Petrovsky's chess set. It cost 1200 BioWare Points on PC and $14.99 USD on consoles. BioWare ran a promotional Twitter broadcast, "Fight for Omega," about the communication war on the station.
