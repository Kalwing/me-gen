An optional N7 assignment in Mass Effect 2, available after the mission Horizon and following the N7: Blood Pack Base assignment. Cerberus Command directed Commander Shepard to the planet Tarith, fourth planet of the Lusarn system in the Crescent Nebula, where the Blood Pack ran a mining operation that also hosted a high-powered communications relay using known Blood Pack mercenary protocols. Scans detected massed krogan and vorcha signals inside the mine along with unidentified life signs, later revealed to be hostile klixen.

Shepard's squad landed and advanced on foot toward the relay on a nearby hill, crossing valleys filled with thick chlorine fog. A network of beacons marked the path; harvesters repeatedly dropped klixen into the squad's route, and Blood Pack mercenaries resisted near the objective. Datapads recovered along the way, signed by the Blood Pack officer Salamul and his contact Kalusk, revealed that the vorcha were getting lost in the fog, that the mined nodes were exhausted, that Garm on Omega could spare no reinforcements, and that an agent on Illium was keeping a planetary power grid dark to allow an eventual invasion force in. Salamul had ordered workers to fall back to evacuation shuttles.

The final fight took place in an open space where Salamul, protected by health, armor and a barrier, charged the squad alongside Blood Pack Boom-Squad and Troopers. After defeating them, Shepard bypassed the Communications Relay to end the assignment. Decrypted relay data showed that Tarith's mined resources were being shipped to Zada Ban in the Xe Cha system for weapons manufacture, indicating the Blood Pack was preparing a large-scale invasion; this pointed toward the N7: Blood Pack Base assignment on Zada Ban. Rewards included experience, 7,500 credits and 2,000 platinum. The mission summary noted an encounter with an unclassified hostile alien species and the elimination of Blood Pack forces.
