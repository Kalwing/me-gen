Colosseum was a mission in the mobile game Mass Effect: Infiltrator with a single checkpoint. Taunted by the Director, Randall Ezno stumbled into the X1 Research Wing and faced the first major boss of the game, the test subject X1, a massively framed mutated krogan with significant genetic and cybernetic augmentation, energy-weapon gauntlets, and built-in shield generators.

The fight required dealing damage to X1's armour rapidly and relentlessly, best achieved with an evolved M-333 Particle Beam. When enough damage was done, X1 staggered and exposed his back, giving an opportunity to destroy his shield generators, which had to be done quickly; this was repeated several times. Partway through, the Director spoke to Randall through a screen, and the walls closed in to lock Randall and X1 in a smaller room, though X1 could break the walls and widen the battlefield again. The second phase was similar, but Randall had to wait for X1 to destroy part of the wall to escape, and X1 added a low-damage charge attack; the same approach of staggering him and unloading damage while his shields were down still applied. After defeating the modified krogan, Randall ran to the Communications Relay to contact the Alliance. The mission fell between X1 Wing and Comm Relay.
