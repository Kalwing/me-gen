UNC: Missing Marines was an assignment in Mass Effect and the first in the Cerberus chain. Admiral Kahoku asked Commander Shepard to find a missing recon team in the Sparta System of Artemis Tau; contact had been lost shortly after the team was sent to investigate suspicious activity. The assignment could be gained by speaking to Kahoku near the steps to the Citadel Council chamber after becoming a Spectre, by picking up the marines' distress call when the Normandy approached Edolus, or by asking Captain Anderson about Armistan Banes after completing Citadel: Doctor Michel, since the crew that recovered Banes' body had gone missing.

On Edolus, Shepard followed the distress signal north of the landing zone to find a wrecked M29 Grizzly, several dead marines, and a transmitter. Approaching triggered an attack by a Thresher Maw, which had to be fought on foot or from the Mako at range while avoiding its acid spit. Once it was dead, the squad concluded that someone had deliberately placed the transmitter to lure the marines to their deaths. Shepard could examine a marine for a codex entry, then returned to the Citadel Tower and reported to Kahoku to complete the assignment, which led into UNC: Cerberus. The assignment is referred to internally as "Cerberus: The Test."
