Brief game-mechanics page. Resilience was a passive Combat skill in Mass Effect: Andromeda and a starting Combat skill of the squadmate Jaal Ama Darav, improving his defensive capabilities. Its six ranks granted bonuses to maximum shields and health, shield and health regeneration, and melee damage (including bonus damage to shielded enemies and shields restored on melee hits), plus evade-cloak options and damage resistance while shields are up. Rank 6, offering Retaliation (an automatic electric burst that primes nearby attackers) or Obscuration (auto-cloak when badly wounded), was locked until completion of his loyalty mission Jaal Ama Darav: Flesh and Blood. It was single-player only.
