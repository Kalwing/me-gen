Samesh Bhatia was the husband of Nirali Bhatia, an Alliance marine who served and died on Eden Prime during the geth attack of 2183. He waited for Commander Shepard in front of the Embassies reception desk in the Citadel Presidium, after Tali's rescue, asking for help recovering his wife's body so he could give her a proper cremation and burial and find peace. He explained that the body had not been returned to him without explanation and directed Shepard to Clerk Bosker in the Embassy Lounge.

If Shepard agreed to help, Bosker revealed that Nirali's corpse was being held because it registered injuries from unique geth weaponry, and the Alliance intended to study it to develop new technology. Shepard could either assure Samesh the body would be released promptly or convince him to allow the tests, in which case he was distraught, demanding to know why a loyal soldier could not be treated with respect. If Ashley Williams was in the squad, she spoke to Samesh as he left, telling him either that Nirali died to keep him safe and would want her sacrifice honored, or that Nirali loved him and listened to recordings of his voice every night; he was grateful for her support.

If Shepard retrieved the body in 2183, Samesh sent a message to Shepard's private terminal in 2185, passed along by Udina, thanking the Commander again. He wrote that seeing Nirali's body treated properly had helped him more than Shepard could imagine, and that he had opened the restaurant she had always wished to start, back on Earth, where her picture hung on the wall and Alliance soldiers ate for free.
