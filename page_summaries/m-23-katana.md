The M-23 Katana was a shotgun appearing in Mass Effect 2, Mass Effect 3, and Mass Effect: Andromeda, manufactured by the Earth-based firm Ariake Technologies. It was a common military and mercenary shotgun, deadly at short range but ineffective at long range, very effective against shields and biotic barriers but weak against armor, and popular on colonies plagued by varren infestations. It was likely named after the sword, and had a unique draw/holster sound effect resembling a blade being sheathed.

In Mass Effect 2 it was granted automatically on Freedom's Progress to Soldier or Vanguard Shepards or those with Shotgun training, and could be used by Grunt, Jack, Jacob, and Tali. The availability of the M-22 Eviscerator made the Katana essentially obsolete from the start, though its larger clip size still appealed to shotgun-focused Vanguards and Soldiers.

In Mass Effect 3 it was given automatically to Vanguards, Soldiers, and Sentinels at the start of Priority: Mars, or found on a dead Cerberus trooper; it was one of five multiplayer starter weapons, rated common. With five shots, a good reload, and fair damage, it was one of the better starting weapons alongside the M-92 Mantis, but its slow fire rate, high weight, and poor long-range accuracy made it sub-optimal against other shotguns once alternatives appeared. In Mass Effect: Andromeda it was described as a popular Milky Way shotgun whose maker was eager to trade with the Andromeda Initiative; it received per-pellet damage increases, remained relatively heavy, and its multiplayer S variant made it competitive with other average shotguns.
