The Phaeston was an assault rifle in Mass Effect 3, named after a turian spirit of creation. It was engineered to balance accuracy and firepower in a machine gun, with kickback dampeners in the shoulder stock that let it hit harder than other weapons its size without losing precision. Its fully automatic fire and relatively light weight made it the turian infantry's primary go-to weapon. Citadel advertising paired it with the logo of the turian arms manufacturer Armax Arsenal, implying Armax as its likely maker.

In single-player it was found during Tuchanka: Turian Platoon next to a dead turian, and could otherwise be bought afterward from Cipritine Armory; with the Omega DLC it also appeared in the Talon outpost. In multiplayer it was an uncommon random reward requiring no expansion. Compared with the M-8 Avenger it was heavier but had greater ammo capacity, rate of fire, and damage per round, firing 50 rounds before reloading even without mods, making it good for sustained suppressing fire. Barrel and piercing mods helped offset its low per-shot damage. An upgraded Phaeston was a balanced, lightweight primary weapon well suited to power-dependent classes, though it became largely obsolete once options like the M-7 Lancer were available.
