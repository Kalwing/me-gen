Hades Nexus: Prothean Sphere was a Mass Effect 3 fetch mission acquired after Priority: The Citadel II by listening to a refugee in Bay E28 of the Docks: Holding Area, who begged a turian guard for food in exchange for the location of Prothean ruins on Gei Hinnom in the Hades Nexus. The cluster became accessible only after Priority: Rannoch, at least four Priority missions after the mission could first be acquired. Shepard scanned Gei Hinnom in the Sheol system to recover the sphere; it could alternatively be found during a Search and Rescue scan, logging as Citadel: Prothean Sphere. Returning it to the refugee, Shepard authorized him to sell the artifact to the Alliance, granting 5 Reputation, 15,000 credits, and an update to the Alliance Engineering Corps War Asset. Speaking to the refugee again revealed he used the money to buy food for his family and for others trapped on the station.
