This Shadow Broker dossier, unlocked after the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, profiled Zaeed Massani as a founder of the Blue Suns who lost leadership of the organization through poor interpersonal skills and political judgment, retained a high mission success rate even after a near-death experience, and had completed several jobs offered by agents whose Shadow Broker connection was concealed from him.

A partial audio transcript recorded on the Normandy's lower decks has Massani telling Kelly Chambers a war story about a botched raid on a slaver camp: a "hotshot turian" who ignored his hand signal was shot through the visor and decapitated by a sniper, the team jammed the slavers' radios but the slavers returned by shuttle with a bomb, and Massani's crew killed the captain and guards as contracted and left, expecting the Blood Pack pilot not to bomb his own base. Massani: "The Blood Pack always did like them incredibly goddamn stupid."

A personal log, written after Cerberus approached him in a merc coding system, has him weighing retirement properties once the Illusive Man's money comes through. He rejects Elysium (batarians in the Verge), Illium, an overcrowded Earth and a barely-rebuilt Eden Prime, considers Bekenstein for its weather, food and mostly-human population, and concludes: "Hell, maybe I should just buy a ship full of explosives and commit suicide by Omega. Easiest retirement plan I've come up with so far."
