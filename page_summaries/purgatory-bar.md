The Purgatory Bar was a popular dance club and bar on the Citadel in Mass Effect 3, owned by Aria T'Loak, though it was not registered as a nightclub according to Avina. Citizens of many races visited it to drink and dance and forget the war with the Reapers. The music heard throughout was "Staggered Injection" by Ekstrak. Although dialogue placed it on the Wards, the exterior view indicated it was actually on the Presidium, several floors above the Presidium Commons.

Aria used Purgatory as her base of operations after Cerberus took Omega. She loathed the club, calling it "hell," and disparaged it compared to Omega's Afterlife Club, but could still be found there even after retaking Omega. If Shepard got drunk in Purgatory, the Commander slept it off and woke next to Aria in the VIP Lounge; getting drunk a second time left Shepard in the elevator outside and unable to use the bar until leaving and returning.

Points of interest included the VIP Entrance, VIP Lounge (Aria T'Loak), Lower Bar and Dance Floor. Characters found there at various times included James Vega drinking at the bar, Joker near the bar and later dancing near EDI if encouraged toward a relationship with her, EDI observing dancers, Jack near the dance floor if she survived the Suicide Mission and was evacuated from Jon Grissom Academy, and Steve Cortez, who could invite Shepard to the dance floor after the attack on the Citadel. Ambient conversations covered the Banner of the First Regiment, an elcor mourning the loss of the Code of the Ancients, and drunken soldiers toasting Bilal Osoba's valiant sacrifice. Missions that could be acquired here included Apien Crest: Banner of the First Regiment, Aria: Blood Pack, Aria: Blue Suns, Aria: Eclipse, Citadel: Improved Power Grid, Dekuuna: Code of the Ancients and Nimbus Cluster: Library of Asha. With Leviathan or Citadel DLC installed, transit panels allowed rapid travel to the Normandy or Silversun Strip.
