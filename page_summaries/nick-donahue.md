Nick Donahue was a biotic student at the Ascension Project in Jon Grissom Academy. In the novel Mass Effect: Ascension he was twelve in 2183 and had received his L4 implant at eleven. One of the more talented biotics in the program, he was also a known bully. After he biotically pushed his friend Seshaun over for teasing him, Hendel Mitra placed him in lockdown for three weeks. On a dare from Seshaun and others, Nick went to the cafeteria to mock Gillian Grayson and knocked over her milk with his biotics; Gillian responded by hurling him twenty feet and wreaking havoc. Nick survived with a concussion and a bruised spine.

In Mass Effect: Retribution, set in 2186, Nick was older and had become the most talented biotic child at Grissom Academy following Gillian's departure. When the Reaper-modified Paul Grayson attacked the Academy, Nick "worked together" with Kai Leng against Grayson, and Kai Leng used him as a weapon, resulting in Nick being shot in the stomach. Kahlee Sanders and David Anderson saved his life and he recovered. Nick had an undeniable crush on Kahlee, and his dislike of Anderson stemmed from their relationship.

In the first edition of Mass Effect: Deception, acknowledged by BioWare to contain lore discrepancies, Nick accompanied Anderson and Sanders to the Citadel as they presented evidence of Reaper involvement in Grayson's attack to the Council, then ran away to join a biotic supremacist group called the Biotic Underground. Trivia notes that, like Gillian, Nick was portrayed as eighteen in Deception despite being twelve in Ascension and fifteen in Retribution, which took place only months earlier.
