Mass Effect 2 was a science-fiction action role-playing game developed by BioWare, the sequel to Mass Effect and the second game in the trilogy. It released for Xbox 360 and PC on January 26-29, 2010, and for PlayStation 3 on January 18, 2011. The PS3 version used a variant of the Mass Effect 3 engine, shipped with several DLC packs, included the interactive comic Mass Effect: Genesis in place of the original game, and suffered a save-file corruption bug fixed by a February 21, 2011 patch. In 2021 the game was remastered as part of Mass Effect Legendary Edition, which bundled nearly all of its DLC and retail and promotional bonus items.

Set two years after Shepard repelled the invading Reapers, the plot follows a new threat: entire human colonies are being abducted on the fringes of known space. Shepard works with Cerberus, led by the Illusive Man, to assemble an elite team and command the most powerful ship ever built for a mission through the Omega-4 relay that is expected to be suicide. In the final mission Shepard and squad members could die depending on the player's preparation, resources, and choices; if Shepard died, the character could not be imported into Mass Effect 3. Potential squad members included Garrus Vakarian, Grunt, Jack, Jacob Taylor, Kasumi Goto, Legion, Miranda Lawson, Mordin Solus, Samara or Morinth, Tali'Zorah vas Neema, Thane Krios, and Zaeed Massani, with success depending on gaining their loyalty. New locations included Omega, Illium, the Migrant Fleet, Freedom's Progress, Horizon, Tuchanka, a Collector Cruiser, a Derelict Reaper, and the Collector Base.

Gameplay changes included a thermal-clip ammunition system, six weapon classes, class-restricted weapons, heavy weapons, manual cover, body-part targeting, regenerating health and shields, and removal of Mako planetary exploration in favor of planet scanning. Talents became "Powers" with shared but much shorter cooldowns, a level cap of 30, and specialization choices. Dialogue added Paragon and Renegade Interrupts. Character imports from Mass Effect carried over decisions such as who was rescued on Virmire, the fate of the Council, and romances, though level and abilities did not transfer. The game received extensive DLC through the Cerberus Network and paid packs, including Zaeed - The Price of Revenge, Kasumi - Stolen Memory, Overlord, Lair of the Shadow Broker, and Arrival, the last of which linked to Mass Effect 3.
