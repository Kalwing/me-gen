Security Chief Roe was the leader of the Eclipse mercenary unit assigned to guard the arms dealer Donovan Hock on Bekenstein, encountered during Kasumi Goto's loyalty mission in 2185. Datapads left by her junior officers indicated a harsh, "Draconian" leadership style. Uninterested in the party Hock was hosting, she chose to monitor events from an unknown vantage point rather than mingle with the guests. During the party, Kasumi was able to briefly assume Roe's identity over the comm to get herself and Commander Shepard into Hock's private quarters. When Shepard and Kasumi infiltrated Hock's vault, Roe led a team to attack them and was killed in the battle. In combat she was armed with an M-6 Carnifex heavy pistol, used Incinerate, and deployed Combat Drones, and she was protected by strong shields, heavy armor, and high health; she could restore her shields with Tech Armor, but only once her armor had been fully depleted. Her tech abilities and defenses made her a dangerous opponent on higher difficulties, especially since Kasumi was Shepard's only squadmate for the mission. The M-12 Locust acquired before or during the fight quickly cut through her shields and the drones she summoned.
