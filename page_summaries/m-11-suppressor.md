The M-11 Suppressor was a heavy pistol in Mass Effect 3, distinct from the M-11 Wraith shotgun. It was the product of the Alliance's Offensive Handgun Project, which developed an infiltration weapon for close-quarters situations where silence was essential. The Suppressor featured a built-in integral sound moderator that reduced both noise and muzzle flash; civilian variants were illegal but circulated in some sectors.

With the Citadel DLC installed, the weapon was added automatically at the start of Citadel Wards: Ambush, after a cinematic in which Shepard killed a CAT6 Specialist wielding one. The Suppressor Shepard used during that mission was a unique model that existed only there, carried a very large weight penalty as a handicap, and did not upgrade or carry across playthroughs. In multiplayer it was a rare weapon requiring the Reckoning expansion.

Built on the same model as the M-6 Carnifex and M-77 Paladin, the Suppressor combined high per-shot damage, a high rate of fire comparable to the M-3 Predator, very low recoil, good accuracy and stability, a six-round clip, and an unparalleled 4x headshot damage modifier. Its fast fire rate let a full magazine be emptied within the duration of a Tactical Cloak bonus. With a Pistol Scope it could function as a "pocket sniper rifle," matching or outperforming lighter sniper rifles when headshots were landed, and its rapid follow-up shots gave it an edge against shield-gated targets. As a suppressed weapon it drew less attention from nearby enemies, similar to Tactical Cloak. The multiplayer version dealt roughly half the single-player base damage.
