Rolan Quarn was a veteran turian impersonator from Erinle with numerous reported exploits. He first drew public attention when he was arrested for masquerading as the CEO of a major Illium company: after meeting Jaxum Borlin of Delumcore Systems, who then died on holiday, Quarn lifted Borlin's biometric data and passed himself off as a tanned Borlin returning from vacation, successfully running the company for two years until a routine traffic-stop tissue sample exposed the discrepancy. Throughout that time he never touched Borlin's personal accounts, living only on what he earned running the company.

His trial revealed further impersonations: he had worked as a stunt driver, sous-chef, martial arts instructor, ambassador, varren tamer, news anchor, and stand-up comedian. He composed a message of thanks to his Delumcore employees before slipping off his tracking bracelet and leading police on a two-hour manhunt around Nos Astra. He later resurfaced on the Citadel, having stolen the Times Square ball on New Year's Eve 2186 under C-Sec's noses and leaving a signed, handprinted note claiming responsibility; the ball reappeared months later, still on the Citadel, with another note mocking C-Sec's underfunding.

During the Reaper invasion, in Mass Effect 3: Citadel, Quarn lurked at the Silver Coast Casino, staking out Elijah Khan's fundraiser and observing potential victims while going barefaced. Commander Shepard, on a covert mission to reach Khan, could mingle with him to learn about Khan and the crowd, deduce his nature, and half-declare an intent to turn him in, only for Quarn to reveal he knew of Shepard's ulterior motives. Their meeting ended in mutual respect.
