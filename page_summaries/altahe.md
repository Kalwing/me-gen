Altahe was the second planet of the Acheron System in the Styx Theta cluster, reachable in Mass Effect after the Noveria: Geth Interest prerequisite. It was a Roche World: one half of a pair of small, unusually dense terrestrial worlds, Altahe and Ontahe, orbiting each other so closely that they effectively shared a single atmosphere of nitrogen and ethane, though one could not fly between them. The surface was warm and made mainly of silica dust and dark basaltic rock, with extensive deposits of heavy metals and radioactives, and tidal effects from Ontahe produced constant heavy wind. Ontahe was the smaller partner, likely in synchronous rotation so it always appeared in the same place in Altahe's sky; at the Mako landing site it sat near the horizon, suggesting a landing far from the equator.

The planet hosted the assignment UNC: Listening Post Theta and an anomaly, UNC: Asari Writings, where a downed satellite carried one of Matriarch Dilinaga's writings and the "song" associated with the rachni could be heard near the wreckage. Other points of interest included a crashed pod for salvage and an unmarked rachni colony. Mineral deposits included samarium and gold. Lifeforms present were Rachni Workers, Rachni Soldiers, and Rachni Brood Warriors; these rachni appeared regardless of Commander Shepard's decision about the Rachni Queen on Noveria, because they had been sent to Depot Sigma-23 before the Peak 15 lockdown. The planet name references the Roche Limit, and the image used for Ontahe is a recycled real-world picture of Mars.
