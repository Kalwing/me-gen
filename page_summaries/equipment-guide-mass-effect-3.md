This page is a gameplay reference cataloguing where weapons, weapon mods, armor pieces, intel, and Citadel mission items can be acquired in single-player Mass Effect 3. It is the Mass Effect 3 counterpart to the Mass Effect 2 Upgrade Guide, and it points readers to related pages for general equipment, Armor Customization, and Weapon Mods.

The guide organizes acquisitions primarily by location and mission. It lists item pickups mission by mission across the campaign's Priority and secondary missions, including Priority: Mars, Priority: Palaven, Priority: Sur'Kesh, Priority: Tuchanka, Priority: The Citadel II, Priority: Geth Dreadnought, Priority: Rannoch, Priority: Thessia, Priority: Horizon, and others, giving precise in-level descriptions of where each collectible sits (for example the M-92 Mantis found near a dead soldier early on Mars, the M-98 Widow on the asari sniper's perch on Thessia, and the Graal Spike Thrower leaning against a railing on Tuchanka). It also covers items purchasable from Citadel merchants and a few other vendors.

The page notes that the Legendary Edition folds in all previously DLC-exclusive content by default and changes how some formerly DLC-locked equipment is obtained, with version differences flagged in orange notation. Several entries include timing warnings, such as collecting the Sniper Rifle Enhanced Scope on Palaven before the second-camp fight ends and the mission closes automatically. The content is a walkthrough aid rather than narrative lore, so no characters, dates, or story consequences are established beyond references to figures encountered in the relevant missions such as Eve, General Corinthus, Bailey, Tali'Zorah nar Rayya, and Legion.
