This DLC Codex entry detailed the yahg, a race of massive apex predators native to the world Parnack whose rise to sentience had not reduced their violent nature. A group of yahg could not cooperate until one leader was established through social maneuvering or brute force, after which former rivals served the victor with unflinching loyalty and no lasting grudges, a legacy of their origin as a pack species. Their four pairs of eyes had evolved to track and predict the movements of prey, giving the yahg acute sensitivity to movement and light and making them expert readers of body language across species; the Council's first-contact teams found it nearly impossible to lie to a yahg. When the Council discovered the yahg in 2125, their technology was equivalent to 20th-century Earth standards. The Council's ambassadors approached the yahg as friends and allies rather than as subordinates, which on Parnack read as contempt from newcomers, and the yahg attacked once it became clear the diplomats considered themselves sovereign people instead of new yahg underlings. The Council afterward placed Parnack off-limits, fearing that the yahg's size, aggression, and obsession with control made them poorly suited for integration into the galactic community.
