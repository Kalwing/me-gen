Salkie was a batarian Blue Suns mercenary on Omega who was in charge of logistics in the mercenary assault on Archangel. He met Commander Shepard's team after their shuttle landed during the mission to recruit Archangel. If Jonn Whitson had also signed up as a merc, he arrived around the same time, and Salkie briefly wondered whether he was with Shepard before sarcastically directing the enthusiastic human onward and debriefing the better-armed people in front of him.

Salkie was relieved that the recruiter had sent people who knew how to fight. He gave Shepard a tactical overview of the battlefield, expressed his belief that it was only a matter of time before Archangel was captured or killed, and, when asked, revealed details of the plan, the history of the battle, and the difficulties of defeating Archangel. He then instructed the Commander to report to Sergeant Cathka.
