This war-asset list collected the contributions of galactic species and factions that had previously stayed on the fringes of Citadel politics. The Terminus Fleet, a mix of mercenary and pirate vessels under Aria T'Loak, joined after Shepard first met Aria on Purgatory; its description updated to reflect the Blood Pack, Blue Suns or Eclipse depending on which "Aria" recruitment missions were completed, each worth 50. Rachni Workers (100) required saving the Rachni Queen in both Mass Effect and the Attican Traverse mission; the rachni integrated into the Crucible project as skilled engineers.

Volus contributions included the Dreadnought Kwunu (50), the volus' only dreadnought, armed entirely with Thanix weapons and jointly gifted to the Vol Protectorate and Turian Hierarchy; the Volus Bombing Fleet (75), heavy-bomber frigates unlocked by convincing Din Korlack; and Volus Fabrication and Engineering assets. Liara T'Soni's role as Shadow Broker provided the Shadow Broker Support Team (40) and Wet Squad (25), an assassination unit. The Elcor Flotilla (40) offered troop transport with shoulder-mounted heavy weapons.

The Batarian Fleet (100), the Hegemony's last warships after Khar'shan fell, joined via Citadel: Batarian Codes; if Balak escaped X57: Bring Down the Sky, the asset instead represented Balak himself at reduced value (15). Governor Grothan Pazness (20) and the Void Devils vorcha fighter wing (30), Vorcha Labor Team (15) and Liquid Assets (40, Illium's "Gang of Five" financiers) came from Leviathan planet scans. The Citadel Defense Force (10), drawn from every Council race, was created after the attempted coup and fluctuated based on numerous Citadel side-quests and Spectre Terminal authorizations (ranging from -4 to +40). The Spectre Unit (40), led by spokesman Jondum Bau, and Hanar and Drell Forces (50) came from Citadel: Hanar Diplomat. The Leviathan Enthrallment Team (400) delivered Leviathan artifacts behind enemy lines to turn Reaper creatures into thralls. From the Omega DLC came the Omega Eezo Hoard (300), the Omega Raiding "RUM" Fleet (75) under Captain Jarral, and General Oleg Petrovsky (30) as an Alliance prisoner if spared.
