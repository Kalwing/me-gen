The Talons were a mercenary organization based on Omega, distinguished from gangs like the Blood Pack by their high level of organization but equally dishonorable. Members wore prominent red facepaint and were predominantly turian, controlling a largely turian district of the station. Their criminal services included drug running, weapons smuggling, extortion, contract killing, slave trading, and letting ships dock at Talon-controlled ports to avoid dealing with Aria T'Loak's organization, for a substantial fee.

In Mass Effect: Ascension (2183) the Talons were the best option for freelancers seeking a foothold on Omega, charging high rates but leaving customers alone and asking few questions; the Cerberus operative Pel ran his base from a Talon warehouse. By early 2186, in Mass Effect: Retribution, they were a small but growing turian gang running a red sand smuggling operation. After they stopped paying Aria her cut, she sent her daughter Liselle with Paul Grayson and Sanak to raid a Talons warehouse, killing all the mercenaries present; a subsequent parley left Aria with an even larger cut, which the Talons accepted knowing they could not win.

Prior to the 2185 plague the Talons were an obscure group. The plague and Commander Shepard's actions against Omega's mercenary bands created a power vacuum the Talons rapidly filled. After Omega fell to Cerberus and Aria was exiled, the Talons ran a resistance effort, freeing detained civilians and staging hit-and-run attacks. Under Nyreen Kandros, who succeeded the profit-minded Derius in 2186 unnoticed even by Aria, the Talons became an organized force fighting to protect Omega's people, swelling with other resistance fighters and gangs. During Aria's offensive to retake the station, Nyreen committed the Talons as reinforcements; she died in the final battle and was replaced by an unnamed successor. After liberation the Talons became Omega's new security force, and some were later hired by the Alliance as engineers armed with omni-bows.
