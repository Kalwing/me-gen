The M-37 Falcon was an assault rifle in Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Andromeda. An Alliance weapon, it launched 25mm mini-grenades and was lighter and more accurate than most grenade launchers. It burned through specialized ammunition generated by a field fabrication kit as well as standard thermal clips, so the clips were its only real limitation.

In Mass Effect 3 it was found during Priority: Cerberus Headquarters and could be bought from Kassa Fabrication via the Alliance Requisitions terminal; multiplayer rated it rare. Its grenades could not penetrate objects or score headshots but took no penalty against armor, traveled fast, bounced off terrain, and detonated on contact or after a short delay. Like the Krysae, they had a proximity fuse producing airburst explosions that hit enemies behind cover and knocked them out of it. The Falcon was uniquely effective at staggering even large foes such as Banshees, Brutes, and Geth Primes over a wide area, though it could not interrupt sync-kill animations. It had the second-highest damage shot of any assault rifle behind the M-99 Saber, offset by low semi-automatic fire rate, small magazine, and long reload. It reliably primed Incendiary, Disruptor, and Cryo effects on multiple enemies per shot, making it an excellent support weapon for setting up Power Combos, and worked well for squadmates like Ashley Williams and James Vega. A later update cut its multiplayer fire rate from 125 to 50 then raised its damage to compensate.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda the Initiative's Falcon worked similarly, could not use an Assault Rifle Receiver Mod, had no Concussive multiplayer variant, and received post-launch buffs including a grenade explosion radius increase from 2 to 3 meters. Modding it with a Beam Emitter produced a continuous stream of grenades.
