Medi-gel was an all-purpose medicinal salve combining an anaesthetic and a clotting agent, used by paramedics, EMTs and military personnel and produced by the Sirta Foundation, a human genetics and bio-engineering corporation. It sealed wounds instantly against infection, contaminants, gases and liquids, gripping flesh until subjected to a specific ultrasound frequency to allow rapid healing. Because it used biological nanides to repair tissue, it technically violated Citadel genetic modification law, but the restriction was ignored due to its benefits.

Despite its biogenetic nature, medi-gel could repair fully synthetic platforms as well as organics; Lieutenant Girard spoke of using it on injured geth in 2186, and Shepard routinely used it to repair geth and mech squadmates. It had strong sedative properties, with large doses inducing a protective comatose state that slowed the effects of critical injuries. It was dispensed from squeezable tubes or injected intravenously, and by 2183 a minor field dose could be applied via omni-tool, remotely boosting an entire squad's health. Around 2185 combat hardsuits gained automatic self-healing systems that used small quantities of medi-gel to regenerate health during combat, aided by cybernetic augmentations to skin, muscle and bone. By 2186 formulas could be tailored to species biochemistry, including a Cerberus enhancement effective only on hanar; contrary to some Alliance soldiers' belief, it worked on batarians. Maya Brooks claimed it could mimic skin pigmentation and that overdose altered perception and mood.

Its limits included an inability to compensate for massive blood loss (as with a dying quarian civilian on Rannoch), an inability to halt infections already set in, and an inability to fully repair serious injury, where it served only to stabilize patients until surgery; amputation of necrotic limbs remained sometimes necessary. Across the trilogy medi-gel was a collectible consumable tied to the First Aid, Unity and First Aid abilities, with carrying capacity upgrades: up to 10 units in Mass Effect (refilled free between missions), 3 to 9 in Mass Effect 2 (not refilled, surplus salvaged for 100 credits), and up to a campaign-upgradable amount in Mass Effect 3 (auto-refilled, surplus salvaged for +150 experience).
