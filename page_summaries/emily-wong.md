Emily Wong was an investigative journalist working on the Citadel who repeatedly asked Commander Shepard for help accessing areas she lacked authorization to enter. In the first Mass Effect, while Shepard hunted evidence against Saren Arterius, Emily was investigating corruption and organized crime near the stairs to Flux in the Upper Wards. She cited Shepard's psychological profile to plead her case and offered compensation. Shepard could give her an OSD recovered from Fist's office containing his files. Her next assignment concerned the working conditions of Citadel traffic controllers; if not previously rebuffed, she asked Shepard to plant a bug in the traffic control tower above C-Sec, which Shepard could do or refuse. News reports on the Citadel elevators covered her "crime syndicate" exposé compiled with Shepard's help, and either the traffic controller story or an interview announcement depending on Shepard's choice.

By 2185 Emily worked as a newscaster for the Future Content Corporation news service, seen via holographic terminals on the Citadel. If Shepard had assisted her, she emailed an interview request expressing surprise that Shepard was alive after two years.

In 2186 Emily reported for FCC News and the Alliance News Network from Earth on the Sol system's comm buoy outage, maintaining limited off-world communication through short text messages sent via a Quantum Entanglement Communicator. She witnessed a Reaper descending over Los Angeles and traveled to El Monte Airport, where National Guard troops attempted an ineffective counterattack against the Reapers. Realizing her broadcast signal was drawing Reaper forces, she tried to flee in a sky van, was shot and badly wounded, and steered the van at ramming speed into an approaching Reaper. Her signal was lost and she was presumed dead.
