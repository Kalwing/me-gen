The Voyager Cluster lay among the outer edges of the Milky Way but was still considered part of Earth Systems Alliance space. It connected via mass relay to the Hades Gamma cluster and contained three systems: Amazon, Columbia, and Yangtze, all named for rivers. Its planets and bodies included Agebinium, Derneuca, Sonedma, Sybin, and Tremar in Amazon; Clojiia, Nepheron, Ontaheter, Gromar, and two asteroid belts in Columbia; and Renshato, Binthu, Dregir, Alrumter, and Patajiri in Yangtze.

As of 2183, Cerberus maintained a substantial presence in the cluster, operating from planets that were otherwise deserted and therefore went unnoticed. At least four bases, complete with troops and defenses, had been established in the region. Commander Shepard could undertake numerous assignments there, including UNC: Cerberus, UNC: Espionage Probe, UNC: Hades' Dogs, UNC: Locate Signs of Battle, UNC: Prothean Data Discs, UNC: Turian Insignias, and UNC: Valuable Minerals.

The cluster's name may derive from the real-world Voyager space probe program, or from the French term voyageur, a licensed fur trader who used rivers for travel in pre-road North America, fitting the river-themed system names. During development the cluster was called "Voyager Xi," and unused data pointed to two additional systems that were cut.
