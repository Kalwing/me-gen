The page explained the morality system in the mobile game Mass Effect: Infiltrator and listed the Paragon and Renegade actions it triggered for protagonist Randall Ezno. These functioned like the interrupt system of Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, letting Randall take direct physical action at set moments, but the choice was mandatory and untimed. A Renegade interrupt played a metal "gong" sound, a Paragon interrupt an uplifting hum. Randall did not accumulate explicit Paragon or Renegade points, but the game tracked his actions, and his facial scars and biotic aura changed accordingly: Renegade choices turned them red, Paragon choices left them blue.

Documented decision points included capturing the target on the Ice Giant (Checkpoint 4), where Randall either nudged the fallen turian and said "No hard feelings, pal," or kicked and pistol-whipped him as "Scum." In the Barn's Medical Bay he could spare an unarmed female Cerberus employee ("Leave") or kill her ("Everyone dies"); could kill a Cerberus scientist holding an asari hostage and free her ("Go!") or kill both hostage and captor ("You're done"); and in the X1 Wing could spare a scared Cerberus Engineer ("You're lucky") or kill him ("Everyone dies"). On the Desert Planet (LV426) after the final fight, the badly wounded Inali Renata, Randall's modified former friend and handler, was either spared or killed; this outcome was not chosen by the player but determined by Randall's cumulative morality at the game's end.
