Incendiary Ammo was an ammo power available in the single-player modes of Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. It caused weapon shots to burn through armor, set enemies on fire, damage health, and make unshielded organics panic, but by default dealt no bonus damage to shields or barriers. Synthetic enemies were resistant to its fire damage in Mass Effect 2 but not in Mass Effect 3, though it always did full damage to armor regardless of enemy type.

In Mass Effect 2 it scaled from 20% to 40% of weapon damage over 3 seconds across three ranks, with a 1.5-second recharge and a duration lasting until overridden by another ammo power. Rank 4 evolved it into Inferno Ammo (an explosive charge spreading the payload in a 3-meter radius, up to 60% weapon damage) or Squad Incendiary Ammo (granting the effect to all squadmates). It stopped health regeneration of enemies like vorcha and krogan once their health bar was damaged, dealt instant damage to armor, and was among the best ammo powers for maximizing a single shot against armored targets. Non-sentient enemies such as Husks, klixen, and Abominations did not panic. It was available to the Soldier, Vanguard, Jacob Taylor, and Grunt.

In Mass Effect 3 it boosted health and armor damage (from +10% up to +30%, or +24% with the squad bonus), with rank options for panic chance, squad sharing, ammo capacity, headshot damage, and an Explosive Burst evolution igniting enemies with intermittent 2.5-meter, 100-damage explosions with a 50% trigger chance per shot. It primed targets for Fire Explosions after 30 points of damage, but only on armored or unprotected targets, and could be pre-stacked on shielded or barriered enemies. Explosive Burst was counted as power damage, was highly effective with rapid-fire and multi-pellet weapons, and did not harm shielded or barriered enemies directly though its explosions still hit nearby unprotected foes. The burn effect revealed cloaked enemies such as Geth Hunters and delayed shield recharge. It was available to the Soldier, Vanguard, James Vega, Grunt, and Jacob Taylor. Several bugs let its burn damage stack with itself for up to triple damage and let it inherit damage modifiers from Incinerate, Inferno Grenade, and Warp.
