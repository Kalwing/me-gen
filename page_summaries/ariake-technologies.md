Ariake Technologies, also called Ariake Tech, was an Earth-based electronics concern best known for its high-grade omni-tools. The company later expanded into armor, producing a line with an enhanced ablative weave for extra protection, and manufactured Mercenary Armor for humans, krogan, and turians. In Mass Effect, an armory license for Ariake Technologies could be purchased from the C-Sec Requisitions Officer or from Commander Rentola aboard the SSV Normandy after his rescue from Virmire, for 6,250 credits. Ariake Technology also developed telemetry data-cleaning algorithms, for which Samantha Traynor and EDI purchased licenses during a conversation with Shepard after Priority: Sur'Kesh.

In Mass Effect, all Ariake weapons incorporated radiation synch technology for better all-around performance, and the company produced the Tsunami assault rifles, Katana shotguns, Raikou pistols, and Naginata sniper rifles. Its armor used an ablative weave that boosted shields at the cost of tech and biotic protection. In Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3, Ariake Technologies manufactured the M-23 Katana Shotgun, a common mercenary and colony weapon, and the M-27 Scimitar Assault Shotgun, an elite rapid-fire model. In Mass Effect 2 it produced the Kuwashii Visor for N7 Armor, trading protective coverage for visibility and accuracy, and in Mass Effect 3 it made customizable armor components favoring melee and headshot damage. It also produced Logic Arrest omni-tools with an adaptive overlay boosting medical recharge and shields at the cost of longer cooldown. The name Ariake is of Japanese origin, poetically translating as "Dawn, But Still the Moon Remains in the Sky."
