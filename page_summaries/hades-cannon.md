The Hades Cannon was an anti-aircraft weapon of Reaper design: a massive directed-energy cannon mountable on a four-legged chassis resembling a smaller Destroyer-class Reaper. Highly accurate and powerful, it could shoot down craft as small and nimble as a UT-47A Kodiak Drop Shuttle. The Reapers deployed several Hades Cannons during their assault and occupation of Earth. During the final battle for Earth, Commander Shepard destroyed one of them with an M-920 Cain to clear a landing path for the galactic fleet's ground forces, codenamed "Hammer."
