The Adhi were four-legged, dog-like lifeforms native to Andromeda, sighted on multiple worlds across the Heleus Cluster, where they often foraged together in packs. They were extremely intelligent and could understand complex commands, and wandering explorers and mercenaries were often accompanied by one or two trained adhi for defense. Their jaw strength was measured in excess of 1900 Newtons, dangerous even to a target in a hardsuit, and they showed no fear of scout rovers. The adhi genome showed signs of extensive genetic engineering resembling the "uplifting" of some domestic animals on Earth before gene modification laws, which allowed them to survive hazardous environments and may have accounted for their near-tactical intelligence even in the wild.

As an enemy unit, the Adhi appeared as an Outlaw type: Wild Adhi were undomesticated, while Outlaw Adhi were fitted with cybernetics to enhance their abilities or force obedience. Like Wraiths and Breachers, they were used to draw fire and flush players out of cover. Their most dangerous attack clamped their jaws around a player's right arm and dragged them into the open, forcing a button-mashing escape sequence and leaving them exposed to enemy fire; they could also swipe with their front claws for light damage. They had no shields, armor, or barriers.

Adhi typically attacked in pairs and often gave away their presence with a harsh, throaty roar, even when already close. They could not use their biting attack on characters perched atop crates or similar elevated objects, instead circling and growling. Skills covering blind spots were effective countermeasures: Singularity or Annihilation with Rank 6 Vortex could keep multiple adhi floating and primed for biotic detonations, Trip Mines helped against swarming, and Remnant VI or Assault Turret helpers could draw their attention. Adhi had some ability to sniff through Tactical Cloak.
