Illo Nazario was a turian arms dealer known for business dealings with batarians, encountered in Mass Effect Galaxy. Jacob Taylor learned of Nazario through a salarian contact named Ish, a friend of Miranda Lawson, on Tortuga. Ish reported that Nazario had not cut any deals in weeks, prompting rumors that he was "planning something," had taken a new job, or was dead.

Jacob fought through Nazario's bodyguards to reach him. Miranda warned that batarian terrorists had infiltrated the compound and were pursuing Jacob. Jacob met the batarian leader, who explained they had come to meet Nazario and "remind him who he's dealing with"; the batarian eventually agreed to turn back, assuming Jacob would kill Nazario himself.

Jacob found Nazario ailing. Nazario explained he had recently worked with a batarian cell in the Fringe, learned the details of their plans, and ended the relationship, after which the batarians infected him with a biological weapon in retaliation. He offered to tell Jacob everything he knew in exchange for a cure. If Jacob carried unrefined element zero and had rescued Batha, he could provide the cure immediately; otherwise Nazario was given medical quarters aboard the ship. Jacob could extort the information by threatening to withhold the cure or offer it freely to earn Nazario's trust. Nazario ultimately revealed that Jath'Amon was behind the planned attack on the Citadel, then asked Jacob for protection, fearing for his life. Like Saren Arterius and Warden Kuril, Nazario was "barefaced."
