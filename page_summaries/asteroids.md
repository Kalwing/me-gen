This page is largely an index/list article with only a short passage of real prose. It explains that asteroids in Mass Effect are found in belts orbiting star systems and can be plotted on the Galaxy Map, and that asteroids have been used as weapons, particularly by krogan and turians, by dropping them from orbit for cheap, devastating effect. The krogan used such tactics during the Krogan Rebellions, rendering at least three turian planets uninhabitable. Asteroid drops on garden worlds such as Palaven, Thessia, or Earth are illegal under Tier 1 of the Citadel Conventions, though terrorist groups have still attempted them (as in Bring Down the Sky). The remainder of the page is a categorized list of notable asteroids (silicaceous, carbonaceous, metallic, rocky, and unknown types) and the resources or collectibles found on them.
