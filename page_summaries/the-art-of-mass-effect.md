The Art of Mass Effect was a 175-page concept art book published by Prima in November 2007, written by Casey Hudson and released alongside the first Mass Effect; it was also bundled with special editions of Prima's Mass Effect strategy guide. It collected concept art for the original game ranging from felt sketches to 3D renders, covering characters, scenery and technology. The book opens with a page-long introduction by game director Casey Hudson and is divided into five parts: Heroes and Villains, Life-Forms, Transport, A Galaxy of Details, and Alien Worlds (with acknowledgements). Art director Derek Watts noted in the Beyond the Game documentary that Hudson wrote all of the book's commentary "in like a week." Its ISBN was 978-0-7615-5851-4. Transcriptions of each part's commentary are held on separate wiki pages.
