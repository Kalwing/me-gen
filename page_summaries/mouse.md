Mouse was a human living in the Citadel's Zakera Ward. Formerly a duct rat, he had been one of the children who fed target information to the assassin Thane Krios, who rewarded him with treats such as real chocolate. When Thane left the Citadel, Mouse begged him for a holophoto so someone would remember him. Years later Thane's son Kolyat used that holo to identify himself to Mouse and seek work on the Citadel; Mouse spread word through Thane's old contacts, and the criminal Elias Kelham hired Kolyat.

Commander Shepard met Mouse mid comm-transaction while searching for Kolyat, and Mouse was rattled to see the apparently retired Thane and the presumed-dead Shepard. He resisted giving up Kolyat's employer but was eventually coaxed or coerced into naming Kelham. In some outcomes of Kelham's interrogation, Shepard could reveal Mouse as the informant, leaving his fate unknown but likely grim. Captain Bailey noted Mouse illegally sold Shepard VIs; Shepard could pressure him into handing one over or demanding a cut. In Mass Effect 3, Mouse held scores at Castle Arcade on the Silversun Strip.
