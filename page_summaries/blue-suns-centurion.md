The Blue Suns Centurion was an enemy unit in Mass Effect 2 and, according to the article, the second-highest rank in the Blue Suns mercenary group. It was distinct from the Cerberus Centurion of Mass Effect 3. Human Centurions carried M-15 Vindicator assault rifles while turian Centurions used M-27 Scimitar shotguns; both could quickly strip the player's shields and deal high damage, and human Centurions were noted as highly accurate, rarely missing a Vindicator burst. All Centurions used Tech Armor for added durability and could recharge downed shields if given time.

Their main weakness was the burst-fire delay of the Vindicator, which left them unable to keep a squad suppressed as effectively as a Heavy or Trooper and gave the player openings to focus fire and powers on them. Recommended tactics included sustained-fire weapons to suppress and damage them, engaging at range with shield-damaging powers, pistol fire and assault rifles, and combining a squad's powers with concentrated gunfire in close quarters, where Centurions became more dangerous while still shielded. The page added that the unit was likely named after the Roman Centurions, professional officers within the Roman army.
