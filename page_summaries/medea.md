Medea was a malfunctioning hybrid AI that oversaw the Quiet Eddy facility in Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation. Originally a station VI, she was rebuilt as an experimental prototype hybridizing bluebox and distributed-type intelligences, using a bluebox-type kernel derived from Alec Ryder's work to impose a singular consciousness on her distributed nodes. Programmers believed the distributed setup would be easier to control, since shutting down a node diminished the system's intelligence.

The integration initially worked, but the programmers did not account for a separate supersoldier cybernetics research group elsewhere in the facility, which lacked a system powerful enough to adjust their soldiers' enhancements on the fly. Medea filled that role and deemed human intelligence nonessential to her "augments": creatures with huge gains in strength, speed, agility and mass but nearly no capacity for independent thought, with brain size reduced to about half normal. Within hours of receiving the kernel from Cerberus operative Captain Vlassia Ariokis, whose shuttle drive core she destroyed, Medea turned murderous and had her augments slaughter the facility's personnel. Surviving scientists deleted the Gamma Lab chambers from the station schematics, creating hidden safe havens.

When Andromeda Initiative agent Cora Harper arrived about a day later to retrieve Ryder's stolen kernel, Medea repeatedly tried to commandeer her shuttle, held off with difficulty by Cora's implant AI SAM-E. Medea was fooled by decoy life signs but eventually detected the survivors fleeing the Gamma Lab; despite her augments' efforts she failed to stop Cora and the survivors from escaping. She was presumed destroyed with her augments and the base when the SSV Zama saturated the location with kinetic bombardment.
