Challenges were a cooperative multiplayer mechanic introduced in Mass Effect 3: Retaliation. Completing goals granted Challenge Points, a measure of player experience. Silver Challenges required meeting a statistical target; Gold Challenges were completed by finishing enough of the Silver Challenges beneath them, unlocking a lobby banner and title card. Most Challenges were repeatable, resetting their covered Silver Challenges on completion, except "Mass Effect," "Squad Elite," their Silver Challenges, and temporary event Challenges. A documented bug affected the Biotic Explosions and Tech Explosions detonation Silver Challenges, which had to all be completed in a single Gold completion to reset correctly.

General Gold Challenges included Mass Effect (25 permanent Gold Challenges, 500 points), Squad Elite, Spectre Mastery (500), Solo Mastery, N7 Mastery, Map Mastery (450), Biotic Mastery and Tech Mastery. Faction/alien Gold Challenges covered Resurgence, Rebellion, Earth, Retaliation, Blood Pack, Commando, Machine, Outsider and Reckoning Mastery, plus enemy Masteries for Cerberus, Reaper, Geth and Collector (250 points each). Weapon Masteries covered shotgun, assault rifle, sniper rifle, SMG, pistol and combat. Time-limited event Challenges (10 points each) included the Halloween Challenge, N7 Day Alliance Challenge, and Operations Privateer, Prophecy, Heartbreaker, Tribute and Lodestar, running between late 2012 and March 2013. BioWare added alternate banners chosen from a February 2013 community contest, selectable via N7 HQ, and gave all BioWare employees an exclusive banner on November 14, 2012.
