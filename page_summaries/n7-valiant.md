The N7 Valiant was a sniper rifle in Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Andromeda (distinct from The Valiant, a kett commander in Andromeda). It was tested by Alliance soldiers during harsh survival exercises on the planet Kruljaven. The streamlined weapon used a sophisticated fire-control system that stabilized the barrel during targeting to improve accuracy, at the cost of reduced capacity and rate of fire, a trade most soldiers found worthwhile for the precision and quick reload.

In Mass Effect 3 it was a multi-shot sniper rifle with damage per shot greater than the M-97 Viper but less than the M-92 Mantis, a far higher effective rate of fire than either, a three-round default magazine, and very low recoil (much less than the Black Widow). At maximum rate of fire it could exceed the Black Widow's DPS against soft targets but lacked one-shot-kill potential and native penetration. A level 1 Valiant was heavier than the M-99 Saber but became much lighter at high levels, giving it one of the best power-to-weight ratios in the game. It was included in the N7 Collector's Edition Pack for Collector's or Digital Deluxe buyers, delivered with an e-mail from Lt. D. Eugene of the Alliance Engineering Corp, who had led the Normandy's Alliance retrofit; in the Legendary Edition it was the most expensive weapon at 250,000 credits from Spectre Requisitions. It was an ultra-rare multiplayer weapon requiring no expansion.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda it was a semi-automatic sniper rifle with the same fire-control accuracy-for-capacity trade-off; post-launch patches improved damage, clip size, max ammo, and rate of fire and reduced its tight-aim recoil penalty. Shepard is shown holding the Valiant on the back of the Mass Effect 3 case.
