Index page aggregating Mass Effect and Mass Effect 2/3 Codex entries on galactic organizations: the pro-human group Cerberus and its reclusive leader the Illusive Man, Cerberus combat units (Atlas, Centurion, Engineer, Guardian, Nemesis, Phantom, Trooper), the mercenary companies Blue Suns (founded by batarian slaver Solem Dal'serah, tutored by con artist Bernard "Legits" Ledger), Eclipse (founded by asari commando Jona Sederis, based on Omega), and the Blood Pack (built from a vorcha gang into a legion by krogan battlemaster Ganar Wrang), plus the megacorporation Eldfell-Ashland Energy and the mercenary trade in general.
