Docks: Holding Area was a location on the Citadel Presidium, directly below Normandy Dock: Bay D24. It had formerly been reserved for Alliance embassy deliveries, but during the Reaper invasion Councilor Udina issued a unanimously approved Council motion to repurpose the docks into emergency civilian housing, funded by all the major races. Adjacent sections E24, E26, and E28 were converted into a refugee camp, and the refugee population grew over time.

Points of interest included a security checkpoint where a refugee pleaded with a dock officer to admit his family (Shepard could side with either, affecting War Assets), and where Solik Vass appeared after the attack on the Citadel. Bay E24 held Avina and, during the medi-gel sabotage investigation, Conrad Verner. Bay E28 was where James Vega often played poker. Cargo Hold A held Garrus Vakarian before the Citadel attack, a Shepard VI store model, and Darner Vosque or Sayn during the recruitment of the Blue Suns and Eclipse for Aria. Cargo Hold B held Kelly Chambers (if she survived the Collectors and was invited to dinner), a doctor working on medi-gel sabotage, and the refugee leader Tactus. Cargo Hold C held the Batarian State Arms terminal, a batarian preacher, Zaeed Massani (after the volus ambassador mission), and the injured batarian terrorist Ghorek. The Memorial Wall was where Steve Cortez mourned his late husband and Ashley Williams comforted her sister.

Missions tied to the area included Aria: Blue Suns, Aria: Eclipse, Citadel: Batarian Codes, Citadel: Hanar Diplomat, Citadel: Inspirational Stories, Citadel: Medical Supplies, Citadel: Medi-Gel Sabotage, Citadel: Wounded Batarian, Hades Nexus: Prothean Sphere, and Kite's Nest: Pillars of Strength.
