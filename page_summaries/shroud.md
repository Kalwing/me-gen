The Shroud was a salarian construct on the surface of Tuchanka, built roughly 2,000 years ago to stabilize the planet's atmosphere after the krogan's self-induced nuclear winter. It was originally deployed by a series of ground-based mass-driver towers that launched its components into orbit; all but one of those towers were later destroyed, and that surviving tower came to be called "the Shroud," though the name was technically inaccurate. The tower was repurposed to disperse particles and chemicals into Tuchanka's atmosphere for climate control.

The Shroud was part of a salarian scheme to uplift the krogan during the Rachni Wars, when a losing Council traded technology to the krogan in exchange for soldiers. During the subsequent Krogan Rebellions, after the genophage was released, a salarian STG team sabotaged the Shroud to ensure that any genophage cure could not be easily disseminated through it. The structure stood in an old arena built as a shrine to Kalros, the mother of all thresher maws, placed there in the hope that the threat would deter anyone from reaching the Shroud and discovering the sabotage.

In Mass Effect 3, Commander Shepard and Mordin Solus (or Padok Wiks) needed the Shroud to disperse a genophage cure across Tuchanka as part of securing a turian-krogan alliance. A Destroyer-class Reaper had seized the tower to poison the atmosphere. Shepard's squad dodged its beam to reach two maw hammers that summoned Kalros, who attacked the Destroyer; the Reaper smashed her into the Shroud, damaging it, but Kalros dragged the Reaper underground. Shepard then reached the control room, where a temperature malfunction and the STG countermeasures made remote bypass impossible, forcing the salarian scientist to access the controls manually. Whether or not the cure was distributed, the Shroud was ultimately destroyed in an explosion.
