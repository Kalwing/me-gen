Mass Effect: Genesis was a downloadable "interactive backstory comic" produced by Dark Horse Comics, released for the PlayStation 3 version of Mass Effect 2 on January 18, 2011 and for Xbox 360 and PC on May 17, 2011. It served as a substitute for playing the original Mass Effect, summarizing that game's story for new players and letting them make six of its major plot decisions, which then carried into Mass Effect 2. When downloaded, it played between the opening level and character customization, with separate male and female Shepard versions. It was free on PlayStation 3, $3.99 on Xbox 360, and 320 BioWare Points on PC. An updated version, Mass Effect: Genesis 2, shipped in the Mass Effect 3: Special Edition.

The six choices were: Shepard's love interest; whether the Rachni Queen on Noveria was freed or destroyed; the fate of Urdnot Wrex on Virmire; who was sacrificed on Virmire, Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko; the fate of the Citadel Council during the Battle of the Citadel; and whether Shepard endorsed David Anderson or Donnel Udina for the Council. The comic omitted several elements of Mass Effect, including the Feros mission, the geth's overall role, and all assignments. Imported playthroughs still triggered Genesis, replaying the player's choices; it always depicted Benezia dying in Liara's arms and assumed both Wrex and Garrus joined the team. In Legendary Edition, playing through Genesis is optional.
