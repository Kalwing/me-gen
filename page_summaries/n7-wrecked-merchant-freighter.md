"N7: Wrecked Merchant Freighter" was an optional Mass Effect 2 assignment on the planet Neith in the Amun system of the Eagle Nebula, acquired by scanning the planet, which detected wreckage and surface movement but no organic life. The wreck was the merchant freighter MSV Corsica. Recovered datapads and logs (authorized by Captain Kratt) established that the ship's VI had suffered a spreading malfunction; Kratt locked the VI out of critical systems with executive authorization, but short-range sensors failed, the ship went onto a collision course, the crew was ordered to the escape pods, and a security report noted the mechs in the cargo containers were activating and self-destructing. The shipping manifest listed a massive cargo of mechs bound for an ERCS facility: 180 LOKI Mechs and a YMIR Mech from Hahne-Kedar's Experimental Mechanics Division, plus reflective mech armor from the MSV Corsica's other listings.

Shepard and squad landed away from the crash site, collected platinum, credits from salvageable damaged mech parts, and datapads, then deactivated the distress transmitter on the beacon plateau. Doing so activated LOKI Mechs, and the squad had to fight or slip back to the shuttle through waves of mechs and a worsening sandstorm, with the YMIR Mech appearing partway along either path. Advancing past a spawn point permanently stopped its mechs; Infiltrators could reach the shuttle cloaked without firing, and destroying all 180-plus mechs also ended the mission. The mission found no survivors and deactivated the beacon. Rewards were 7,500 credits and 2,000 platinum.
