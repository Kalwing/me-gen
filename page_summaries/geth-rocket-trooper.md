Geth Rocket Troopers were geth support units that wielded rocket launchers, accompanying other geth troops and usually attacking from range. In the original Mass Effect they carried a pulse rifle modified to fire various rockets, had two to three levels of regenerating shielding, and wore dark red armor distinguishing them from standard Geth Troopers; the Geth Juggernaut was described as a deadlier variant. They used Distortion Rockets with heavy splash damage under 100m and fast Scram Rockets from 100 to 400m, switching to pulse rifle mode at close quarters. A single rocket could kill or badly wound infantry. Sabotage left them defenseless, shield-bypassing powers like Warp, Damping and Overload were effective, and even basic AI Hacking could turn them, though their value as allies depended on distance from other geth.

In Mass Effect 2 they stayed back and fired barrages from ML-77 Missile Launchers, had low health with light shielding at higher difficulties, and often used cover, including balconies from which Pull or Throw could dislodge them. Combat Drone was highly effective, distracting them and often causing them to kill themselves firing at point-blank.

In Mass Effect 3 they returned with a black-and-white color scheme and ML-77 Missile Launchers whose rockets now homed on targets and flew faster, damaging any friend or foe in the blast radius; at close range they could also melee. They had shielding on all difficulties, lower base health than a Geth Trooper, dodged by hopping aside, and took 40% bonus headshot damage. Rockets could be dodged with a timed combat roll or shot out of the air by an accurate or area-damage weapon. Overload could instantly kill them, Disruptor Ammo damaged shields and health, and Sabotage turned them against other geth; they were the priority target in any mixed geth group.
