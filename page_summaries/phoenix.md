Phoenix was a star system in the Argos Rho cluster containing two asteroid belts and at least four planets. Activity in the system dated back to the Krogan Rebellions, but it remained a frontier system into the present age because of its inhospitable worlds. A carbonaceous asteroid in the field emitted a weak, intermittent 1540 MHz signal; scanning it in Mass Effect 3, Shepard's crew detected a quarian energy reading that Tali identified, leading a recon team to an abandoned freighter where they recovered a Prothean data disc.

The system's planets were Patashi (first from the star), Sylsalto (second), Intai'sei (third), Tuntau (fourth), and Vebinok (fifth). Intai'sei hosted Shepard's Apartment and was tied to the assignment Pinnacle Station: Convoy and later the mission Citadel: Kakliosaur Fossil; Tuntau was linked to Wrex: Family Armor. An asteroid belt in Phoenix held Pinnacle Station, the Council special-operations training facility. In Mass Effect the system was drawn with Intai'sei as the outermost planet despite its ingame numbers placing it third, an error corrected in Mass Effect 3. Because the Pinnacle Station DLC was cut from Mass Effect Legendary Edition, Intai'sei and the Pinnacle Station asteroid were removed from the system in the remaster.
