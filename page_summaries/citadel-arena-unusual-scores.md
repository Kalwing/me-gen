Citadel Arena: Unusual Scores was a side mission in the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC, available after Citadel Docks: Retake the Normandy, once the Armax Arsenal Arena opened with the rest of Silversun Strip. After Shepard won several matches and purchased all available prizes except the ally licenses, the Armax Arsenal Arena IT department emailed the Combat Simulator Control terminal through Principal Systems Administrator Virginia Ruojian. IT was trying to isolate a bug, believed to be a memory leak, that caused unusual scores to appear, and hired Shepard as a paid consultant and "baseline" to reliably reproduce high scores.

The mission ran in stages, each set up by an email from Ruojian. First Shepard fought Super-Elite enemies, after which IT concluded memory-intensive creatures were not the cause and suspected the bonus-generator code; reading that email awarded a Sniper Rifle High-Velocity Barrel. Shepard then completed a match with every positive bonus modifier enabled. IT next reported the leak was not caused by scoring but by a background program siphoning resources, joked that the arena had been built on an old Prothean burial ground, and asked Shepard to fight geth elites on the Spin Zone map with enhanced enemy shields, attaching a next-of-kin liability waiver; this stage awarded the Pistol Power Magnifier mod.

That final test malfunctioned badly: the map filled with digital artifacts and "Fatal Error" text, the announcer distorted, and instead of geth the arena spawned waves of super-elite Collectors, then geth, then Cerberus, then Reapers, culminating in a single wave combining Banshees, Praetorians, Geth Primes, and Atlases. Unlike normal simulations, losing all health and shields caused a Critical Mission Failure. After Shepard survived, Ruojian sent a final apologetic email: the tests had stabilized the level, eliminated the memory leak, and corrected the scores to place Shepard at the top of the leaderboard, while the fans had enjoyed the unscripted brush with death. Completion awarded the Assault Rifle Thermal Scope mod and unlocked the corrected "Fatal Error" map for future arena matches.
