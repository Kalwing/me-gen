Barla Von was a volus financial adviser who ran an office in the Financial District of the Presidium. Captain Anderson called him a "financial genius," noting he was skilled at moving large sums of money without leaving a paper trail and that, while some questioned the legality of his services, he did nothing illegal and merely knew all the loopholes. Von sold his services to the Citadel elite, including diplomats, ambassadors, and Spectres. His real area of expertise, however, was the information trade; he served as an agent for the Shadow Broker, occasionally selling information to the Broker, though like all the Broker's agents he knew little about his employer.

In 2183, at Anderson's suggestion, Commander Shepard met Barla Von while seeking proof of Saren Arterius' rogue status. Von provided the information for free because the Shadow Broker had been betrayed by Saren. He told Shepard that the Shadow Broker had hired a krogan mercenary to deal with Saren and directed Shepard to the C-Sec Academy, where the freelancer was visiting.

In 2186, Barla Von was at the Presidium Commons next to the Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies shop, concluding business arrangements in light of the war. Shepard met him at Liara's suggestion; Von referenced their earlier meeting if it had occurred, or claimed no prior acquaintance if not, and expressed surprised admiration that Anderson had stayed on Earth. Von offered Shepard the location of a sizable mercenary group trapped in Reaper-controlled territory, promising that if Shepard evacuated them they would join the war effort. He explained that the group's leader would pay a high commission for the rescue, and that he too would be well paid. After Cerberus' attempt to seize the Citadel, Barla Von left his post and later took part in the Armax Arsenal Arena challenges at Silversun Strip, scoring very high. An audio log aboard the Hyperion in Mass Effect: Andromeda revealed that Barla Von had helped Alec Ryder contact the Shadow Broker directly for aid with his AI research. Cut content from Mass Effect 2 would have had Von found dead amid the collapse of the Shadow Broker's empire, but this was removed and he reappeared alive in Mass Effect 3.
