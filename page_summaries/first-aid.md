First Aid was a healing ability appearing as a tech talent in Mass Effect and as a power in Mass Effect 3. It was distinct from Unity, the squad revival ability of Mass Effect and Mass Effect 2.

In Mass Effect, First Aid was available to Engineers, Sentinels, the Turian Agent, and the Asari Scientist, and unlockable for Soldiers, Infiltrators, and the Quarian Machinist. It was activated through an independent hotkey and consumed one unit of medi-gel per use, restoring health to the entire squad based on the squad's combined skill in the talent. It could be used with zero points invested, restoring 40 health. Ranks 6 and 12 were labelled Advanced First Aid and Master First Aid but conferred no unique benefit beyond larger healing increments. The base cooldown was 60 seconds (reduced to 20 in Legendary Edition) and could be shortened by talents such as Medicine, many omni-tools, and the Asari Scientist class talent, or lengthened by Damping. Using First Aid 150 times unlocked an achievement and made the talent available as a bonus talent for Adepts and Vanguards. Sentinels and Engineers trained as medics could use it to revive downed squadmates in active combat and ignore toxic damage, otherwise possible only through Unity.

In Mass Effect 3, First Aid superseded Unity and was available to all single-player classes. Shepard could use it to heal NPC squadmates, revive fallen squadmates to full health and shields, and restore all of Shepard's health bars except on Insanity difficulty, where restoration was limited if Shepard was taking health damage. It affected only living squadmates' health, not shields, and squadmates could also be revived manually at no medi-gel cost, though First Aid was the only way to revive squadmates killed by instant-kill attacks such as a Banshee's impale. Each use expended no more than one unit of medi-gel, with a 50 percent chance of costing none if the Efficiency Increase upgrade was obtained. It had no recharge time and did not share the universal power cooldown, allowing near-constant use.
