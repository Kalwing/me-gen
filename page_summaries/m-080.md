The M-080 was a large six-wheeled infantry fighting vehicle (IFV) armed with a turret carrying two coaxial barrels, a machine gun and a main cannon. Three variants appeared: the base IFV, an APC that removed the turret for an armored passenger top, and a missile artillery version mounting two powerful Thanix missiles. Like the M29 Grizzly and M35 Mako it was non-drivable scenery, and according to The Art of Mass Effect it was actually an early design of the M35 Mako, built on a near-identical chassis.

By 2185 the M-080 was in use across many factions. In Mass Effect 2, IFV variants appeared at an Eclipse base on Daratar (painted black) and a Blue Suns base on Joab (metallic). An Eclipse M-080 sat in Donovan Hock's garage during Kasumi Goto's loyalty mission, its main gun used to blast open a wall, and an APC variant was also parked there. Another M-080 was seen crashed through a wall of the hospital on Tuchanka bearing krogan clan markings, and overturned examples littered Kopis during the Project Firewalker: Prothean Site mission.

In Mass Effect 3, Cerberus fielded APC variants at its Mars base and the Alliance used the same APCs and missile-truck variants during the final battle for Earth. Shepard used one missile truck to destroy the Reaper Destroyer guarding the London beam, firing two Thanix missiles into its "eye" after fighting off Marauders, Banshees, and Brutes. The APC also served as multiplayer map scenery on Firebase Condor and Firebase London.
