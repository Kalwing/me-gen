Defense Matrix was a tech power in Mass Effect 3's single-player mode. When active it reinforced the user's armor with protective Foucault currents, providing passive damage reduction, and it could be purged at any time to restore a percentage of shields. At rank 1 it gave 15% damage reduction, restored 50% of shields on purge, had a 10-second recharge, and slowed power use by 60%. Higher ranks improved recharge speed, raised shield restoration (up to 100%), increased damage protection (up to 30%), and offered evolutions including Shield Recharge (reduced shield-recharge delay), Power Synergy (25% more tech power damage while active), and Power Recharge (reduced the power-use penalty from 60% to 30%).

The damage protection applied to both health and shields, persisted after shields broke, stacked with Tech Armor and other powers, and ended when the matrix was purged, returning only on reactivation. The rank 1 power-use penalty was not an overall recharge reduction but a flat 60-point reduction of the Power Recharge Speed bonus shown on the loadout screen, functioning like a weapon-weight penalty. The matrix could be activated and purged without interrupting running or firing, and merely activating it did not put powers on cooldown, allowing a rapid "double-tap" activate-then-purge for a large emergency shield restore. The purge was one of very few single-player methods to restore shields without waiting for normal recharge (Energy Drain being another), and was useful against damage-over-time attacks such as those of Banshees and Swarmers. Combined with the shield gate mechanic, restoring any amount of shielding could restore invulnerability frames and let Shepard survive an additional large burst of damage, which mattered most on Insanity. An Infiltrator could purge to restore shields without breaking Tactical Cloak upgraded with the rank 6 Bonus Power evolution.

In single-player it was available to EDI and to Commander Shepard as a bonus power. It was not available in multiplayer. It was distinct from Mass Effect 2's shield-restoring Geth Shield Boost.
