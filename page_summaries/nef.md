Nef was a human teenager living on Omega who was killed by the Ardat-Yakshi Morinth. Morinth seduced Nef and then murdered her by melding minds with her. In Mass Effect 2, while hunting Morinth, Shepard and the justicar Samara could help Nef's mother Diana investigate the death, as no one on Omega seemed to care that her daughter had been murdered.

Searching Nef's room turned up evidence that she was an artistically talented, lonely, reserved and shy girl whose sculptures had drawn purchase offers from several art galleries. Diana related that Nef's personality had abruptly changed after she became infatuated with an asari (Morinth); Nef began spending entire nights at the Afterlife Club, gaining VIP access by dropping Jaruut's name, and sometimes vanished for days. Morinth enthralled Nef by appealing to her artistic and musical interests and may have plied her with the drug Hallex to lower her inhibitions. Nef's diary recorded early doubts that someone so exotic could be interested in her, and when she was at her most vulnerable Morinth joined minds with her and killed her.

If Shepard helped Diana and later aided Samara in killing Morinth, Diana sent a message saying that although her daughter was gone forever, she was glad to see Nef avenged. If Shepard instead helped Morinth kill Samara, Diana still sent the message, since everyone believed Morinth had died.
