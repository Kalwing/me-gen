Jaroth was the leader of the Eclipse mercenary company on Omega who, like Captain Wasea, did not wear the standard Eclipse uniform. He shipped tainted element zero across Citadel space and held a personal grudge against the vigilante Archangel, who in the previous month had killed one of Jaroth's top lieutenants, his own brother, during a raid on an Eclipse transport. Because Archangel had made enemies of all the major Omega gangs, Jaroth allied with Tarak of the Blue Suns and Garm of the Blood Pack to destroy him. The three conspirators further planned to move against Aria T'Loak afterward; Jaroth secured Garm's support for that scheme and composed a message relaying the news to Tarak.

Jaroth disseminated the battle plan against Archangel to Eclipse. He accepted Tarak's proposal to send expendable freelancers as a distraction ahead of the infiltration team, intending to follow the freelancers' deaths with LOKI Mechs. He disdained freelancers and could be interrupted by a trio of heavily armed freelancers led by a human, whose questions he might still answer. When these "freelancers" (actually Commander Shepard's team) crossed the bridge to Archangel's base and revealed their true allegiance, Jaroth committed his entire faction, including LOKI Mechs and a YMIR Mech, against them. All were defeated. Jaroth took the field last, reasoning that if he wanted the job done right he had to do it himself, and was killed as well. In gameplay he was an Engineer armed with an M-6 Carnifex pistol, able to use Incinerate and Combat Drone, with strong shields and armor and moderate health.
