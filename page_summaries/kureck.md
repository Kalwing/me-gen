Kureck was a krogan Battlemaster who led a group of Blood Pack mercenaries searching for salvage in the Teltin Facility on Pragia during Mass Effect 2. He had been hired by the human Aresh Aghdashloo, who was trying to rebuild the abandoned facility. When Commander Shepard's squad arrived, Kureck opened fire on them and was killed in the resulting firefight.

In combat Kureck carried an M-300 Claymore Heavy Shotgun and could use Warp, resorting to Berserker Charge at close range. He had standard krogan regeneration and charge in addition to armor and a biotic barrier, which he could instantly restore once depleted, though he would only do so after his armor had been completely destroyed. Unlike most krogan he initially hung back at a distance, taunting his own team, and would only advance to close range if the two accompanying Blood Pack Warriors were killed first.
