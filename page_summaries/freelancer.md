Freelancers were mercenaries not affiliated with any established band or group. They operated alone and worked for anyone who paid. During the Archangel recruitment mission in Mass Effect 2, the Blue Suns, Eclipse, and Blood Pack hired freelancers to serve as distractions for Archangel while Tarak, the local Blue Suns leader, carried out his plan to bring down the vigilante. Shepard and squad had to sign up as freelancers themselves to gain access to Archangel's hideout.

Freelancers were armed with M-8 Avenger assault rifles, M-3 Predator pistols, or M-9 Tempest submachine guns. They had only health on lower difficulties and light shielding on higher ones. Because Shepard's team had signed on among them, they could be caught by surprise and were very easy to eliminate with short controlled bursts. Squadmates with crowd-control powers could tear through their ranks; combining Jack's Shockwave with Jacob's Pull, or using Singularity as an Adept, Combat Drone as an Engineer, or Adrenaline Rush with the M-6 Carnifex as a Soldier, all proved effective. Saving this mission for last was recommended so that these powers would be available.
