The kirik were a species of biotic-capable, beetle-like insectoids native to the arid world of Ekram. They drew scientific excitement as the first known insects able to control biotics, since arthropod-like creatures had previously been thought to lack the complex neural pathways needed for biotic powers. Further study found the kirik also showed signs of rudimentary intelligence. The lead biologist of the team studying them argued they were ideal candidates for "uplifting," which sparked a contentious galaxy-wide debate about the dangers and ethics of such an action. In December 2185, the Citadel Council dispatched representatives to evaluate the kiriks' intelligence against standard galactic sapience tests. The species featured in the Cerberus Daily News "Biotic Insects Found on Ekram" storyline.
