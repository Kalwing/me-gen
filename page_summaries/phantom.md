The Phantom was a Cerberus combat unit encountered in Mass Effect 3, described as the result of reverse-engineered Reaper technology: agile biotic cyborgs specializing in evasive maneuvers and firing from cover. Their implants remained a mystery to the Alliance, but they were known to project personal biotic barriers. They fought at close quarters with monomolecular blades, a single stab from which could kill instantly, and carried a semi-automatic kinetic projectile weapon built into the right gauntlet. They could briefly cloak to regenerate health and recharge barriers, so the Alliance recommended personal confirmation of a Phantom's death.

Defensively, Phantoms relied on agility, sidestepping, rolling and leaping away from fire, and used suit-mounted stabilizing thrusters to recover quickly from knockdown. They could raise a localized biotic barrier making them briefly immune to most powers, and were always immune to long-duration shock-stun effects (Disruptor Ammo, Overload, Energy Drain, Sabotage), could not be fully frozen by cryo powers, did not panic when on fire, and were immune to grab instant-kills. A Phantom's barrier was roughly twice as strong as its health.

Notable tactically for being no larger than basic infantry despite "super elite" status, Phantoms were best engaged from a distance. Their gauntlet blaster could strip 875 shield points in a single shot on Gold. Their sword could be broken by accurate hitscan or explosive-round fire, after which the Phantom would hang back in cover and fire its palm weapon instead. Their self-healing could be blocked by Reave and permanently prevented by Incendiary Ammo damage. Stasis or Submission Net left them helpless for headshots, and they often paired with a Nemesis, which acted as bait. An advancing Phantom emitted a low electronic muttering, but a stationary one was silent and could ambush the unwary.
