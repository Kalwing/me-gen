Prologue: Save Joker was the first mission of Mass Effect 2, acquired immediately after importing or creating a character, before any character customization was possible. It involved no combat, awarded no morality points, and did not allow saving. Its purpose was to present the events leading to Commander Shepard's disappearance, roughly two years before the main events of Mass Effect 2.

The game opened with a conversation between the Illusive Man and Miranda Lawson discussing the outcome of the Battle of the Citadel; imported saves reflected whether the Citadel Council had been saved or lost, while new characters defaulted to the Council being lost. The scene then cut to the SSV Normandy, dispatched by the Council after Sovereign and Saren Arterius's attack to clean up remaining geth. The Normandy emerged in the Amada System of the Omega Nebula and detected an approaching unknown ship.

The unknown vessel attacked with a beam weapon that cut straight through the Normandy's kinetic barriers and armor, crippling the ship and breaching the hull. Shepard ordered an evacuation. A squadmate (Ashley Williams, Kaidan Alenko or Liara T'Soni, depending on prior romance and the Virmire decision) reported that Joker, the helmsman, refused to leave. After sending the squadmate to the escape pods, Shepard moved forward through burning, hull-breached corridors to the cockpit and, politely or forcefully, convinced Joker to abandon ship.

Shepard carried Joker to an escape pod and secured him inside. Before Shepard could climb in, the enemy cruiser attacked again; the blast threw Shepard clear of the pod, forcing Shepard to eject it with only Joker aboard, saving his life. A final blast destroyed the Normandy and sent Shepard tumbling into space, where the hardsuit was punctured and leaking air. Shepard struggled briefly, lost consciousness, and was last seen drifting toward a nearby planet. In the PlayStation 3 version, Liara T'Soni always appeared as the squadmate regardless of imported choices.
