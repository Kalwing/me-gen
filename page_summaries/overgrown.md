Overgrown was a Mass Effect: Andromeda mission on Havarl, available only after the mission Cross-Cultural Alliances and the activation of the planet's Remnant vault ushered in Initiative-angaran cooperation. Nexus scientists Dr. Tassana Rilar and Dr. Erich Baumgarten at Pelaav Research Station asked Ryder to analyze mutated wildlife to determine the cause of Havarl's accelerated growth rate.

Ryder cross-referenced genetic markers against a Genetic Archive Database, then tracked and scanned three mutated creatures altered by the malfunctioning vault: a challyrion of abnormal size and aggression north of Pelaav, a rylkor made more territorial and adaptable east of the Central Wilds Forward Station, and an eiroch in the Ancient Courtyard, which Ryder found under attack by a group of Roekaar Raiders and Sharpshooters who had to be defeated first.

Searching the Roekaar hunting party's supplies yielded a datapad translated from Shelesh, revealing that the Roekaar, following a researcher named Vaaren's theory, had found a single mutation mechanism affecting both animal and plant cells. They estimated an 82% chance the mutant strains could be replicated on other worlds, intending to render planets poisonous to outsiders and drive off the kett and the Initiative permanently. The datapad pointed to a Roekaar base in the north of the Ancient Courtyard. Clearing it, Ryder accessed the Roekaar Genetic Research Log; SAM offered to delete or copy the files, though neither choice had known consequences. Nearby communications terminals held personal messages between the defector Anivaal and his family and former comrade Nihsa. Returning to Pelaav, Ryder delivered the data to the grateful scientists. Rewards included 530 XP, 73 AVP, and a 5% increase to Havarl viability.
