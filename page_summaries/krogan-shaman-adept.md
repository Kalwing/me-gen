The Krogan Shaman Adept was a playable class in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer, with 1000 barrier and 1000 health, and the powers Barrier, Shockwave, Warp and Rage. Its melee set was the Krogan Headbutt, the Krogan Charge heavy melee, and a physical-punch over-cover grab; it had no dodge and could not roll between cover.

Access to both Shockwave and Warp made it the only krogan class able to kill enemies with powers alone, while its ability to carry heavier weapons, fight in melee and absorb more damage than other Adept classes made it very well-rounded. Its core tactic was combining Warp and Shockwave for powerful Biotic Explosions, favoring low cooldowns and lighter weapons, and often skipping Barrier points to keep cooldowns short since Rage could offset the fragility. Player notes flagged the Shockwave rank 4 Radius evolution as bugged, and covered faction use: Shockwave to expose Guardians and Warp as anti-armor for Cerberus, strong all-round performance against the Collectors (every enemy vulnerable to biotics) and Reapers, and caution against geth stagger attacks and Bombers.
