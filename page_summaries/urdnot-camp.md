The Urdnot Camp was a small, rubble-strewn settlement on the krogan homeworld of Tuchanka, controlled by Clan Urdnot. A shuttle landing pad connected to the camp proper by a derelict passageway. The camp contained the throne dais of Clan Urdnot's leader, a building housing the Urdnot shaman where representatives of other clans gathered, a varren-fighting pit, the clan's food stores (frequently raided by invasive pyjak), a merchant's kiosk run by Ratch, and the laboratory of the clan's Lord High Researcher, Fortack.

In 2185 Commander Shepard traveled to the Urdnot Camp to aid Mordin Solus in searching for a missing fellow Special Tasks Group member during the mission "Mordin: Old Blood," and to help Grunt complete his Rite of Passage in "Grunt: Rite of Passage." Shepard could also speak with the leader of Clan Urdnot, who was either Urdnot Wrex (if he survived Virmire and joined Shepard) or Urdnot Wreav. Additional activities included helping a krogan mechanic find a combustion manifold to repair a vehicle, addressing the clan's chronic pyjak infestation, and betting on a varren fight. If Urdnot Wrex was alive, a Clan Nakmor representative was present at the camp to negotiate an alliance with Clan Urdnot, and Shepard could interact with the negotiation. The traditionalist Gatatog Uvenk was also present, opposing the current clan leadership. Missions and assignments tied to the location included "Tuchanka: Urdnot Wrex," "Tuchanka: Combustion Manifold," "Tuchanka: Killing Pyjaks," and "Tuchanka: Old Blood: Missing Scout."
