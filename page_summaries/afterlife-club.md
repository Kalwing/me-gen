The Afterlife Club was a nightclub on the mercenary-controlled space station Omega. It had three floors: a ground floor with a large circular bar and asari dancers resembling Chora's Den on the Citadel, a second floor with dancers on a suspended ring around a purple holographic cylinder and booths along an elevated outer section, and a third-floor balcony ringing the club with flaming torches. A well-guarded private lounge at the back was used by Aria T'Loak, the asari ruler of Omega, to observe the floor. A separate VIP section had a back-alley, bouncer-protected entrance accessed with a namedrop passphrase.

In Mass Effect: Redemption, Liara met her drell informant at the bar while searching for the missing Commander Shepard, then met Aria to learn where the Blue Suns had taken Shepard's body. In Mass Effect 2, Shepard picked up several missions and assignments there; during Samara's recruitment, entry to the VIP section required claiming that "Jaruut" had sent them, and Shepard helped track Morinth there. In Mass Effect: Retribution, Paul Grayson reported to Aria in the club after leading an assault on the Talons, danced with Liselle in the VIP section, and Kai Leng later came to negotiate with Aria on the Illusive Man's behalf.

In Mass Effect 3: Omega, after Cerberus conquered Omega in 2186, General Oleg Petrovsky converted Afterlife into his command center. When Aria returned with Shepard to liberate the station, the final assault took place in Afterlife. The battle killed Talon leader Nyreen Kandros, and an enraged Aria fell into a trap that incapacitated her. Shepard freed Aria and defeated the Cerberus soldiers and Adjutants; Petrovsky surrendered, Aria announced she had regained control of Omega, and she vowed to rebuild.
