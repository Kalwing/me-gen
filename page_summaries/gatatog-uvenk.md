Gatatog Uvenk was a krogan battlemaster and leader of Clan Gatatog on Tuchanka in 2185. A traditionalist who championed the warrior culture that drove the Krogan Rebellions, he believed Clan Gatatog stood at a turning point between renewed power and glory or fading into history, and so allied his people with the dominant Clan Urdnot to curry favor with its leader. He resided at the Urdnot border camp. If Urdnot Wrex led the clan, Uvenk openly opposed his reforms as weakening the krogan; if Urdnot Wreav led, Uvenk was deferential and supportive.

When Commander Shepard accompanied Grunt to complete his Rite of Passage, Uvenk insulted Grunt as a tank-bred krogan and opposed his joining Urdnot, appealing directly to the Urdnot Shaman, who was irritated by his protests. Shepard made Uvenk back down by proving the team would serve as Grunt's krantt, either humbling him with words or answering with a headbutt; Uvenk withdrew his objection but vowed to settle the matter elsewhere. After the final battle of the Rite, Uvenk arrived by shuttle with several Gatatog Warriors. If Grunt had killed the thresher maw, Uvenk acknowledged his strength and offered him in-name-only membership in Clan Gatatog as a trophy to boost Uvenk's own stature; if the maw was merely survived, Uvenk dismissed Grunt as impure. Grunt violently rejected the offer, and a battle ensued that ended in the deaths of Uvenk and his warriors. Grunt afterward thanked Shepard and expressed satisfaction at Uvenk's death. Uvenk said: "Aliens don't know strength. My followers are true krogan. Everything about Grunt is a lie." In combat he hung back using Warp while his warriors charged, wielding an M-300 Claymore heavy shotgun, a biotic Barrier, and armor. He wore modified Battlemaster Armor from the Geth Armory and was voiced by Michael Dorn.
