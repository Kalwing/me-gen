The batarians were a race of four-eyed bipeds native to Khar'shan, governed by the isolationist Batarian Hegemony. The Terminus Systems were infested with batarian pirate gangs and slaver rings, though the page noted these criminals did not represent average citizens, most of whom were forbidden to leave batarian space by their paranoid government. Batarians had faces covered in fine hairs, a ridge of cartilage along the skull and neck, needle-like teeth, red blood, and two pairs of dark eyes. When batarians achieved spaceflight they found Prothean ruins on Bira, a moon of Verush, which let them develop FTL travel; the Citadel Council later granted them an embassy about a century after first contact.

Batarian aggression caused repeated crises: a fleet bombarded the salarian colony Mannovai around 1785 CE, the Hegemony annexed the asari colony Esan in 1913, and Citadel forces clashed with batarians on Enael in 2115. After humans began colonizing the Skyllian Verge in the mid-2160s, the batarians asked the Council in 2171 to declare the Verge an area of batarian interest; when refused, they closed their embassy and became a rogue state. Government-funded pirates raided colonies such as Mindoir, culminating in the Skyllian Blitz of 2176 against Elysium, after which the Alliance retaliated at Torfan in 2178. In 2183, batarian extremists led by Balak hijacked Asteroid X57 to drop it on Terra Nova, a plot thwarted by Commander Shepard. In Mass Effect Galaxy, ambassador Jath'Amon secretly led batarian terrorists trying to unleash a bioweapon at a peace meeting, defeated by Jacob Taylor. The Arrival events destroyed the Bahak system's relay and over 300,000 batarians, raising fears of war with the Alliance.

Batarian culture centered on a purchasable social caste system and institutionalized slavery, with abducted colonists such as Talitha implanted with control devices without anesthetic; batarians believed species with fewer than four eyes were less intelligent, and their body language conveyed social rank. Mordin Solus noted batarians believed the soul left the body through the eyes at death. Their military hardware came from the nationalized Batarian State Arms, and their forces included the Special Intervention Unit and Batarian External Forces. In 2163 the Hegemony had recovered the Leviathan of Dis, a billion-year-old Reaper corpse, from Jartar; its secret study indoctrinated the science team and officials, who sabotaged Hegemony defenses when the Reapers invaded through the Vular system in 2186 and crushed the batarian navy. Survivors fled to Alliance space as refugees, and captured batarians were converted into Cannibals. If Balak had escaped X57, he became the highest-ranking surviving Hegemony officer and could aid Shepard's war effort from the Citadel. A few hundred batarians joined the Andromeda Initiative aboard the arks Paarchero and Keelah Si'yah.
