Brief gameplay page with minimal lore prose: Biotic Hammer was a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode, used by the Krogan Warlord Sentinel, that charged the user's hammer with biotic energy to drastically increase melee direct damage and force against armor and barriers, expending charges on melee attacks and able to detonate all four types of power combos.
