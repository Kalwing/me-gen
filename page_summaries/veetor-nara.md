Veetor'Nara was a shy, neurotic quarian with a history of mental instability. Though never directly dangerous to himself or others, he could not handle stressful situations such as large crowds or violence without suffering a mental disconnect. Because of his fear of crowds, he chose to spend his Pilgrimage helping the people of Freedom's Progress, a small human colony.

When contact with Freedom's Progress went offline, Tali, Prazza, and a squad of quarians traveled there to find him and encountered Commander Shepard. After the colony was attacked by the Collectors, Veetor fell into a state of panic; the Collectors did not find him, presumably because his environment suit hid him from their sensors or because their technology targeted humans specifically. He seized control of the colony's security mechs to protect himself, not realizing his "attackers" were fellow quarians. After Shepard calmed him, Veetor shared security footage he had pieced together of the Collectors and omni-tool data on the Seeker Swarms, which proved invaluable to Shepard's mission.

Veetor could then be released to Tali's custody or taken to Cerberus for questioning. If released to Tali, he later appeared during her trial on the Migrant Fleet, recovering under the care of Dr. Elan'Shiya; if Kal'Reegar was present and Shepard rallied the crowd, Veetor testified in Tali's defense, offering to be exiled alongside Kal'Reegar. If handed to Cerberus, he appeared unstable on the Fleet, complaining of torture and mistaking Shepard for a husk, screaming at the Commander to stay away; Dr. Elan'Shiya believed he had suffered "brute-force" stabilizing medicine rather than physical torture. His Freedom's Progress footage of the Collector abductions could later be seen at the Shadow Broker's Base.
