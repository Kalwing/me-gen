Mass Effect: Deception is the fourth Mass Effect tie-in novel, released January 31, 2012. Unlike the previous three novels, which were written by Drew Karpyshyn, Deception was written by William C. Dietz.

The story follows Navy admiral David Anderson and his partner Kahlee Sanders as they search for a way to stop the cycle of the Reapers, sentient machines that invade the galaxy every fifty thousand years to harvest all organic life. They uncover evidence that the Reaper threat is real, and in doing so expose the machinations of Cerberus and its leader, the Illusive Man, putting them in mortal danger. They find an unlikely ally in Gillian Grayson, a young woman with extraordinary biotic powers who was once the subject of horrifying experiments and is now free and mastering her abilities. After learning that Cerberus caused her father's death, Gillian swears vengeance against the group and the Illusive Man, threatening to unravel Anderson and Kahlee's efforts.

The novel drew fan criticism for numerous continuity and factual errors. BioWare and Del Rey Books apologized and announced that changes would be made to future editions; on December 14, 2012, BioWare said they were checking with the publisher about updates, but no revised edition has been released.
