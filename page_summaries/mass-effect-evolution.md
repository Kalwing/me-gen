Short reference page. Mass Effect: Evolution is a four-issue comic mini-series that delves into the origins of the Illusive Man and the human-survivalist organization Cerberus, with the story opening during the First Contact War between humans and turians. Published by Dark Horse Comics, it was written by BioWare lead writer Mac Walters, scripted by John Jackson Miller, and drawn by Omar Francia, with cover design by Massimo Carnevale and alternate covers by Joe Quinones. Announced July 15, 2010, the first issue was released January 19, 2011. On September 21, 2011, Dark Horse released a trade paperback collecting all four issues along with Mass Effect: Incursion and Mass Effect: Inquisition.
