N7: Archeological Dig Site was a Mass Effect 2 assignment on planet Joab in the Enoch system, unlocked after acquiring Jacob's loyalty mission opened the Rosetta Nebula. Scans showed Blue Suns mercenaries had seized an illegal archeological dig where a Prothean signature had been detected. Commander Shepard's shuttle landed and the squad fought successive waves of Blue Suns Legionnaires, Troopers, a Commander, and Lieutenant Locke, all of whom had to be killed to enter the site. Terminals and a PDA revealed the operation was run for ExoGeni Special Projects (chief researcher Dr. Farrin) under a transport-and-escort deal with Blue Suns co-executive officer Vido Santiago; Locke's orders were to rendezvous with Captain Vorhess aboard the MSV Strontium Mule in the Arinlarkan system once the artifact was secured.

The final chamber held a Prothean pyramid and a video log. Scanning it triggered a vision like the one Shepard received from the Eden Prime beacon, but ending with Collectors, which completed the assignment. Reading the PDA (or simply finishing the mission) unlocked the N7: MSV Strontium Mule assignment. Rewards included 125 experience, 7,500 credits and 500 element zero. A follow-up news report erroneously placed the missing ExoGeni team in the "Aeia Cluster."
