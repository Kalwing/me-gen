Sol is the home star system of humanity and the Systems Alliance. In Mass Effect it is accessible after reaching level 20. In Mass Effect 2 most of its planets can be mined by the Normandy SR-2, the exceptions being Earth and Mars, though none of the bodies rate better than "Good" for minerals.

The system was previously cut off from the galactic mass relay network because the Charon Relay orbiting Pluto had been encased in ice; its discovery and activation allowed humanity's expansion into the galaxy. During their height 50,000 years ago, the Prothean Empire maintained an outpost on Mars and studied the primitive inhabitants of Earth.

Sol contains nine planets recognized for game purposes: Mercury (first), Venus (second), Earth (third), Mars (fourth), Jupiter (fifth), Saturn (sixth), Uranus (seventh), Neptune (eighth), and Pluto (ninth), along with Luna, a moon of Earth. Associated missions and assignments include Prologue: Earth and Priority: Earth, UNC: Rogue VI on Luna, and Priority: Mars. In Mass Effect and Mass Effect 2, Sol is one of the largest systems in the game, with nine planets where most systems have four or five. Only the Asteroid Belt is depicted in-game, not the Kuiper Belt. The in-game positions of the planets in Mass Effect 2 correspond to a date of 26 January 2185, 175 years after the game's North American release date.
