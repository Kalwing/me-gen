Citadel: Krogan Sushi was a Mass Effect 2 assignment available after Freedom's Progress, once Commander Shepard had access to the Citadel. Walking past two krogan, Kargesh and Rukar, on Level 27 of the Zakera Ward market and overhearing their conversation activated the assignment: Kargesh wanted to taste fish from the lakes of the Presidium. Shepard had to ask around before answering the pair and was directed upstairs to the Dark Star Lounge on Level 28, where a turian Presidium Groundskeeper explained that the Presidium lakes were reservoirs supplying water and held no fish, and that the only fish on the Citadel were sold at the Citadel Souvenirs store.

Shepard could return to Kargesh and Rukar and tell the truth that there were no fish, gaining 5 Paragon points, or buy a fish from Citadel Souvenirs and sell it to Kargesh as Presidium fish, gaining 5 Renegade points for the lie. Kargesh only offered payment in exchange for a fish, but both routes awarded 1,000 credits, and any fish purchased still appeared in the Normandy's aquarium. The assignment gave 40 experience, or 50 on the harder difficulty. Trivia noted that Wrex, if spoken to on the Presidium in the first game, might muse about wanting a snack of fish from the lake; that the two krogan could mention an Urdnot once being admitted to the Council Chambers, likely a reference to Wrex; and that if the Citadel Council had been left to die during the Battle of the Citadel, Kargesh's companion might comment about humans having assassinated the Council.
