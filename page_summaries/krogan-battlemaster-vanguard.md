The Krogan Battlemaster Vanguard was a playable class in Mass Effect 3 multiplayer. Its powers were Biotic Charge, Carnage, Barrier and Rage, with 1000 barrier and 1000 health. Its melee set was the Krogan Headbutt (regular), Krogan Charge (heavy) and a physical-punch over-cover grab; it had no dodge and could not roll between cover.

Its Biotic Charge was distinct from other Vanguards': slower to reach the target and longer to cool down, but higher damage, and counted as a melee attack for triggering Blood Rage. With 250 more health than the Krogan Soldier or Sentinel, up to 1800 shields, instant shield restoration on Charge, and Barrier damage-reduction evolutions, it was among the most durable characters in the game, behind only the Geth Juggernaut and Krogan Warlord. Recommended builds emphasized survivability over Carnage, using the Fitness tree's "Martial Artist" evolution for melee or the rank 5 "Weapon Synergy" Charge evolution with a strong close-range weapon such as the Reegar Carbine, N7 Piranha or M-300 Claymore. Player notes covered faction-specific threats: Phantoms and Guardians for Cerberus, Praetorians and Swarms for Collectors, Pyros and Primes for the geth (where it excelled, being immune to most stagger), and Banshees and Brutes for the Reapers, all of which could bypass its tankiness with instant-kill attacks.
