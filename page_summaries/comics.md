The page catalogued the eleven comics set in the Mass Effect universe as of publication. Because the games were treated as the player's story, almost no comic featured a player character as protagonist; the exceptions were the interactive comics Mass Effect: Genesis and Mass Effect: Genesis 2, which let players make key decisions from earlier games, and two issues of Mass Effect: Foundation that told a story about Mass Effect Galaxy protagonist Jacob Taylor. All non-interactive comics released to that point were colored by Michael Atiyeh despite varying authors and artists.

The multi-issue series were: Mass Effect: Redemption, a four-issue prequel to Mass Effect 2 involving Liara T'Soni, Cerberus, and an urgent mission, first issue released January 26, 2010, written by Mac Walters and scripted by John Jackson Miller; Mass Effect: Evolution, four issues set in 2157 during the First Contact War covering the origins of the Illusive Man and Cerberus, first issue January 19, 2011; Mass Effect: Invasion, four issues focused on Aria T'Loak's fight against Cerberus at Omega, first issue October 19, 2011; Mass Effect: Homeworlds, four issues each spotlighting a Mass Effect 3 squad member, first issue April 25, 2012; Mass Effect: Foundation, thirteen issues of original stories by Mac Walters, first issue July 24, 2013; and Mass Effect: Discovery, four issues expanding on Mass Effect: Andromeda, first issue May 24, 2017.

Single-issue comics included Mass Effect: Incursion (2010, Aria T'Loak, set one week before Mass Effect 2), Mass Effect: Inquisition (2010, Captain Bailey investigating Executor Pallin for corruption), Mass Effect: Conviction (2011, David Anderson recruiting James Vega on Omega to guard a prisoner after Arrival), Mass Effect: Blasto: Eternity is Forever (2012, the hanar Spectre Blasto), and Mass Effect: He Who Laughs Best (2013, Jeff "Joker" Moreau). Three hardcover and paperback anthologies collected these works: Mass Effect Library Edition Volume 1 (2013), Volume 2 (2015), and Mass Effect: The Complete Comics (2020).
