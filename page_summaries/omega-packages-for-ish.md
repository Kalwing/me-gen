"Omega: Packages for Ish" was an assignment in Mass Effect 2, available after completing Horizon. On Omega, near the entrance to the Market/Apartment, Shepard met a salarian businessman named Ish who asked the Commander to retrieve two packages: one near Marab's shop, Saronis Applications, on the Citadel (Zakera Level 26), and one in the bar Eternity on Illium.

The packages appeared as interactable datapads. If Jacob or Miranda were in the party, additional backstory dialogue was revealed; the two had worked with Ish before and considered him a coward and a liar. Only one package was needed to complete the assignment. After finding a package, Shepard learned that one of Aria T'Loak's men, the batarian Anto Bek'tall, was doing clandestine outside work arranging safe passage through the Terminus Systems for smugglers.

Shepard then had three main ways to finish: deliver the packages to Ish as agreed (up to +4500 credits and +5 Renegade); convince or threaten Ish that Aria was too dangerous and he should flee Omega (+3375 credits, no morality); or inform Anto about Ish's scheme, optionally extorting Anto for silence (+2250 credits) or giving the information freely, after which Anto left to deal with Ish and Ish was never heard from again. Choosing to make Ish flee could leave the assignment permanently open in the journal. The reward was 40 (50) experience plus the credits and morality above.
