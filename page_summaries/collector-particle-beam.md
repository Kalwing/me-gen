The Collector Particle Beam was a heavy weapon in Mass Effect 2, distinct from the Mass Effect 3 Particle Rifle. It was a focused-radiation beam weapon of Collector origin, precise and lethal, and very effective against shields, barriers, and armor. Producing its destructive beam required energy several orders of magnitude greater than launching a mass-accelerated projectile. The weapon Shepard retrieved could not be understood or replicated, though the Normandy's engineers might eventually produce beam weapons on a larger scale with enough research.

Shepard could find it during the mission on Horizon: on a stasis pod near the stairs where the frozen colonists were first found, near the locked door leading to Delan after the Collector waves were beaten, or just past the Garage exit. If missed at all three points, it was awarded at the mission's end. Picking it up also fully replenished Shepard's heavy weapon ammo.

Mechanically it fired fully automatically but consumed five units of ammunition at a time. It and the M-451 Firestorm were the only heavy weapons affected by the range-scaling multiplier (less damage at extreme range, over double at point-blank) and the ragdoll double-damage bonus, and both were boosted by Adrenaline Rush and Tactical Cloak. It dealt no bonus damage to heads or weak points. It had strong range, ammo efficiency, and precision, stripping the barriers or shields of tough enemies such as YMIR Mechs and Tela Vasir, and its damage rate against armor outstripped the Firestorm's, rendering that weapon largely obsolete. It caused no friendly-fire damage, allowing safe point-blank use, could quickly destroy Collector Guardian shields even on Insanity, could set off the fuel tanks of unprotected pyro-type enemies and Geth Destroyers, and often melted victims into a yellow puddle without leaving a corpse. It was riskier on higher difficulties because it required constant line of sight, and it was especially useful for classes lacking anti-barrier damage or long-range precision. Collector Assassins wielded it, and game assets for it existed in Mass Effect 3 but were normally inaccessible.
