Siano was the first planet of the Dirada System in the Pylos Nebula of the Milky Way. It was named for an ancient asari philosopher known for being a contrarian, and it orbited Dirada in a retrograde direction. Siano was believed to be an object that fell into the system millions of years ago from parts unknown; the outermost of Dirada's inner asteroid belts was thought to have been a small planetoid broken up by Siano's passage into the system.

Siano was formed of low-density rock and was tidally locked to Dirada, so the same hemisphere always faced the star. There was evidence that a complex of artificial structures once existed in the north of the sunward-facing hemisphere, badly degraded by millennia of heat and radiation, and several bunkers of radioactive waste, apparently byproducts of primitive fission plants, were found on the far side. By Mass Effect 3 the Reapers had ignored Siano, concentrating on more populous systems. The planet's initial scan result was "Rich," yielding palladium, platinum, iridium, and element zero. Its description was noted as a possible reference to the Gerry Anderson series Space: 1999. Access required the Star Chart from the Baria Frontiers store in Mass Effect 2, and in Mass Effect 3 progress in Leviathan: Find Ann Bryson.
