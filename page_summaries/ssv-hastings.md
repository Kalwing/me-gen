The SSV Hastings was a Systems Alliance frigate commanded by Captain Belliard. Like all human frigates, she was named for an important battle in human history, in this case the Battle of Hastings of 1066, which was pivotal to the formation of modern England. In 2165 Lieutenant David Anderson served aboard the Hastings as executive officer. The ship was one of many Alliance vessels patrolling the Skyllian Verge, protecting human colonies and research outposts on the far fringes of Alliance space.

When the research base on Sidon was attacked, the Hastings happened to be the closest vessel and responded to the distress signal. Lieutenant Anderson led the ground team of five, which included Gunnery Chief Jill Dah, tech specialist Corporal Ahmed O'Reilly, Private First Class Dan Shay and Private Second Class Indigo Lee. All of them survived the destruction of the station, though Gunnery Chief Dah was wounded. Afterward the Hastings traveled to the Citadel. These events are detailed in the novel Mass Effect: Revelation.
