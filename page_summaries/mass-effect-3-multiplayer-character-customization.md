In Mass Effect 3 multiplayer, players created characters by choosing class, race, weapon loadout, and equipment. Six classes were available (Adept, Engineer, Infiltrator, Sentinel, Soldier, Vanguard), each with multiple races. Common human male and female kits were available from the start; other kits were unlocked by finding class cards in Store item packs, provided the relevant DLC was installed. Kits were rated Common, Uncommon, Rare and Ultra-Rare, with new races added by the Battlefield 3 Pack, Resurgence, Rebellion, Earth, Retaliation and Reckoning packs, including Volus, Awakened Collector, Geth Juggernaut, Krogan Warlord, Cabal, Talon Mercenary and Alliance Infiltration Unit variants. Players named each character and customized armor primary and highlight colors, unlocking more appearance options with each duplicate class card; the Battlefield 3 Soldier's armor could not be recolored.

Two weapons could be equipped at once, unlocked through the Store across five types (assault rifles, heavy pistols, shotguns, sniper rifles, submachine guns) at four rarities, with many gated behind specific DLC packs. Each weapon took two mods, some mutually exclusive. Equipment split into single-use items (varying by pack tier: Recruit, Veteran, Spectre and Jumbo packs), persistent Gear (requires the Rebellion Pack; upgradable to Level V), and Supplies/consumables: Cobra Missile Launcher, Thermal Clip Pack, Ops Survival Pack, and Medi-Gel, each stockpiled up to 255 with capacity upgrades raised by Operations Privateer and Alloy. Reaching level 20 allowed promoting a class into a 75-Military-Strength War Asset, resetting it to level 1 while keeping name, appearance and loadout. A Battlefield 3 Infiltrator kit existed in the files but was cut.
