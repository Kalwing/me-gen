Eva Coré was a human mercenary featured in the comic Mass Effect: Evolution who worked with Jack Harper and Ben Hislop and fought in the resistance against turian forces on occupied Shanxi during the First Contact War. On Shanxi, Eva, Hislop, and Harper ambushed a turian patrol and captured the turian general Desolas Arterius. Seeking the artifact Desolas was after, the group subdued his remaining unit outside a cave; Eva guarded the prisoners while Jack and Ben investigated the artifact, but when it seemingly attacked the two men she was distracted long enough for the turians to overpower her, and Desolas took the humans prisoner.

After the war, having been turned over to General Williams by their captors, Eva reunited with Jack and told him Ben had died from artifact exposure. She chose to accompany Jack in his pursuit of revenge against Desolas. On Illium they tracked Desolas to a warehouse, where they were ambushed by Desolas and his brother Saren's forces and found a cybernetically enhanced Ben Hislop under the artifact's control. Taken prisoner again, Eva and Jack were brought to Palaven to witness Desolas's plan to use the artifact, the Arca Monolith, to transform the turians into super-soldiers. At Temple Palaven they subdued their guards and tried to stop Desolas. Eva attempted to pull Ben free, hoping his condition could be reversed, and when Jack doubted it she fired on the Arca Monolith; the artifact used Ben to slam her head against a wall. Jack carried the dying Eva from the Temple, and with her last words she told him to leave her and save Ben. Jack later included both Ben's and Eva's names in his manifesto as he became the Illusive Man. Decades later a Cerberus mech unit deployed on Mars may have been named after her.
