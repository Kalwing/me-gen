Centurions were Cerberus officers encountered in Mass Effect 3 and Mass Effect: Infiltrator who led squads of Cerberus soldiers into battle. In Mass Effect 3 they were described as Cerberus's front-line tacticians, meant to carry out the Illusive Man's strategic goals but with leeway to adapt as a fight developed. The only intelligence the Alliance had gathered on them concerned their armament.

Each Centurion carried an M-96 Mattock heavy rifle modified to launch smoke grenades, and also used frag grenades, which had a small blast radius but could instantly kill a low-level or unlucky player on harder difficulties, and electrified melee batons stronger than those of Assault Troopers. They had moderate health and regenerating shields, took cover frequently, and could combat-roll to dodge gunfire and projectile powers such as Warp or Incinerate. Their smoke grenades were their greatest threat: they caused no damage but obscured vision so completely that they broke the player's targeting reticule, while enemies continued to fire through the smoke accurately and could reposition unseen. Weapon mods and scopes that saw through smoke, such as the Assault Rifle Thermal Scope, Sniper Rifle Enhanced Scope and Sniper Rifle Thermal Scope, or the Javelin's built-in ability, helped counter them, and squadmates were unaffected by smoke. Rank 4-plus Overload or Energy Drain instantly stripped a Centurion's shields and briefly stunned it, and in multiplayer an enraged krogan could kill a fully shielded Centurion with a single heavy melee.

In Mass Effect: Infiltrator, Centurions were an elite Cerberus infantry unit that Randall Ezno faced during his escape from the Barn, armed with the M-96 Mattock Auto and leading troopers into battle. They had considerable health over a strong regenerating shield, tended to stay in cover unless the player was in their line of fire, could be flushed out with Pull, and took bonus damage from headshots. They were named after Roman centurions, who commanded a "century" of about 80 legionaries, though Cerberus Centurions led only small squads; the Infiltrator version lacked the smoke grenades and wore Trooper armor with a red touch.
