This archive collected Cerberus Daily News reports from January 2186. An out-of-game notice announced that the Cerberus Daily News would begin a hiatus on 24 January after a full year of daily postings, returning only for three weeks in 2011 tied to a downloadable content release and the launch of Mass Effect 3. The month's largest running story was the discovery of a lost human colony in the Alpha Centauri system: asari explorers were ambushed by unknown humans who took a scientist prisoner, and the Alliance, unable to find any records, linked the settlers to the Manswell Expedition of 2070, a private venture funded by billionaire Victor Manswell that launched roughly 300 colonists in cryogenic freeze in 2075 and was never heard from again. A first-contact mission under Commander Jon Hayes met armed resistance with casualties on both sides before the asari prisoner was released; the colonists ultimately split, with about half choosing to stay and half returning to Earth. A separate thread followed the drug "Creeper," a hallucinogenic mold on Sathur's ossilbir vines that triggered product recalls across 60-plus Citadel worlds, fetched record black-market prices, and led Governor Vien Suchart to freeze immigration. Scientists on Mars assessed a newly opened chamber in the Prothean ruins amid accusations of secrecy between Dr. Unira T'Lam and Dr. Ilsa Warren, and Dr. Amanda Kenson of the University of Arcturus published a paper arguing the mass relays predate the Protheans, met with skepticism from Dr. Aurana T'Meles. Late in the month, a batarian frigate from Aratoht confronted the Alliance cruiser SSV Budapest in the Bahak system; the Alliance claimed pirate pursuit, the Batarian Hegemony demanded withdrawal, and the Budapest retreated, defusing the crisis into an embarrassment. Other reports covered violent water-rights riots on Olor, joint human-turian military exercises on Kruljaven amid Khar'shan tensions, a controversial krogan art showcase on Illium curated by Dr. Alissa Banser, an asari prisoner-of-war memoir by Jiana Kintani and Bellah Ans, an eezo processing-plant explosion on Urksone, and the colony of Drasta adopting a ten-year barter economy under leader Loran Evanko.
