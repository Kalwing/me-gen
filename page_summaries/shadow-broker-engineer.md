Shadow Broker Engineers were the tech specialists of the Shadow Broker's private army, tasked with defending the Broker's interests. They were encountered by Shepard, Liara T'Soni and the squad during the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, appearing on Illium and at the Broker's base on Hagalaz.

Offensively they carried the M-6 Carnifex and used the Combat Drone and Incinerate abilities. Defensively they had moderate health and shielding plus access to Tech Armor. Recommended tactics were to stay in cover against Incinerate, shoot down their Combat Drones, and use Energy Drain to quickly strip their shields while restoring the player's own.
