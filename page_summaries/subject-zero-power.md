Subject Zero was a passive class power used exclusively by the squad member Jack in the single-player modes of Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. The page described it as a game mechanic representing Jack's self-experimentation with her unusual biotic abilities.

In Mass Effect 2 the power enhanced Jack's health and weapon damage while reducing the recharge time of her powers, scaling upward across its first three ranks. At Rank 4 it evolved into one of two specializations: Primal Adept, which pushed her biotics to their limit for the greatest reduction in power recharge time, or Primal Vanguard, which focused her energy into combat for the highest weapon damage bonus.

In Mass Effect 3 the power was rebuilt with six ranks. It boosted health and shield bonuses alongside power recharge speed, with later ranks offering choices between recharge speed and durability, between weapon damage and increased power force and duration, and between a squad-wide biotic recharge bonus and a further personal recharge boost. In both games the power was available only to Jack and was not offered in multiplayer.
