Thin page (under ~150 words of real prose). Captain Vorhess was a batarian Blue Suns mercenary who, along with Sergeant Boortis, hijacked the MSV Strontium Mule. He was killed during a firefight against Commander Shepard and the squad on the ship's bridge. In combat he carried an M-15 Vindicator, was very accurate, and had high health, strong shielding, and Tech Armor, but no armor layer.
