"N7: Imminent Ship Crash" was an optional Mass Effect 2 assignment reached by scanning the Nariph system of the Pylos Nebula after buying the Star Chart from Baria Frontiers on Illium. The freighter MSV Broken Arrow, an Alliance-aligned merchant ship arming fringe colonies near geth space, had been attacked and boarded by geth. Its captain, Thomas Jonner, evacuated the crew to the nearby Fargone Colony on Jonus and stayed aboard alone, using his authorization codes to disable the engines so the decaying orbit would bring the ship down and its self-destruct would deny the geth roughly 100 crates of military-grade weapons in the cargo hold.

Commander Shepard and squad boarded to reactivate power before the Broken Arrow crashed, working against a six-minute countdown transmitted by EDI. They fought through Geth Troopers and Geth Rocket Troopers, bypassed locked doors, and reached the engine room, where two timed power-coupling switches on opposite sides of the chamber had to be activated between endless waves of geth, followed by a console bypass behind the engine to restart the drives. Captain's logs and a ship status report recovered along the way documented the yellow and red alerts, the evacuation, and Jonner's scuttling plan. Infiltrators could trivialize the mission by cloaking before each switch.

Completing the assignment stabilized the Broken Arrow, caused the remaining geth to disengage, and saved Fargone Colony, which lay in the ship's projected impact path. Governor Rafael Cologne of Fargone Colony e-mailed the Normandy thanks after identifying the crew from the ship's docking data, noting that the roughly 100 munitions crates were also preserved so the Broken Arrow's work arming colonies on the edge of geth space could continue. Rewards were 7,500 credits and 2,000 iridium.
