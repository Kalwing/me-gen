This page covered gameplay information and tactics for fighting Saren Arterius in Mass Effect; the general biography is on the Saren Arterius page. The former Spectre searched the galaxy for the Conduit, a means of bringing back the Reapers, and Commander Shepard confronted him face to face on two occasions.

The first fight took place on Virmire, where Saren fought from a special hovercraft, using powerful tech attacks alongside a rocket launcher similar to those of Geth Rocket Troopers. He often hovered over fuel tanks, which could be shot, Sabotaged, or Overloaded to take him out quickly. He opened with tech attacks including a high-level Damping that could put a squad's abilities on cooldown, and was immune to biotics except Warp and Stasis. In the Legendary Edition he summoned geth troops. The final confrontation occurred on the Citadel during the battle for the station; Saren again rode his hovercraft, but Shepard could attempt to convince him he was indoctrinated. If not convinced, he fought once more.

After Saren's death in the Council Chambers, by suicide or in combat, Sovereign reanimated his body into a Husk-like state, controlling the corpse and speaking in his voice for one last battle. This second avatar retained tech powers and was more powerful and dangerous than the original, behaving like a Geth Hopper: it jumped around frequently, was highly resistant to low-level biotics but vulnerable to Master-rank Lift and Stasis, hit Shepard with an immediate Sabotage, and fired a version of Carnage as rapid energy balls that largely ignored shields. A cutscene triggered when its health dropped a little above 50 percent, after which it regained its shields. Destroying it corrupted Sovereign's control signal, causing the Reaper to lose its shields and allowing the Alliance fleet to destroy it.
