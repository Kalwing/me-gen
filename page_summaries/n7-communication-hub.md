An optional N7 assignment in Mass Effect 3, mentioned by Specialist Traynor after Priority: Thessia. Cerberus had attacked a communications facility on Ontarom, in the Kepler Verge, and Shepard landed to keep Cerberus from seizing its critical access points. The mission uses the multiplayer map Firebase Dagger, a largely open-air combat zone beneath a satellite dish.

Shepard's squad faced most Cerberus infantry types, Assault Troopers, Centurions, Combat Engineers, several Nemesis snipers, a Phantom and Turrets, across three successive navpoints. After clearing the initial drop zone and activating the first console, more Cerberus attacked, including Nemeses. Disabling the second terminal triggered a cutscene and dialogue with Ms. Grace Sato, who provided the location of the third navpoint (the choice carried no morality reward). The final wave added multiple Nemeses firing from the central tower, plus Centurions and a Phantom. Deactivating the third station ended the mission shortly after.

On the map Shepard could recover the Cerberus Codes for Citadel: Cerberus Ciphers and a set of Ariake Technologies Greaves. Completion rewarded the Communications Array war asset, 20,000 credits and +5 Reputation.
