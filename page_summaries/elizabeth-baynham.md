Dr. Elizabeth "Lizbeth" Baynham was an ExoGeni Corporation scientist working in the Bio-Medical Division on Feros, holding a Security Level 4 exemption. She was the daughter of Juliana Baynham. Lizbeth was assigned to ExoGeni's study of the Thorian, the creature that infected the Zhu's Hope colonists with mind-controlling spores. She protested ExoGeni's use of the colonists as test subjects and was placed on probation as punishment, afterward fearing the corporation would make her a subject too.

When the geth attacked Feros and the ExoGeni building was evacuated, Lizbeth stayed behind to send a message about the Thorian to Colonial Affairs on Earth, but the geth cut the power before she could transmit it. Not knowing whether her mother was alive, she hid in the building's basement and fought off nearby varren. When Commander Shepard's team arrived, the jumpy Lizbeth briefly fired at the Commander before providing information on geth activity and handing over her ID card for access to restricted areas. She was anxious for news of her mother and relieved to hear Juliana had survived if Shepard had met her. After the energy fields were lowered, Lizbeth confessed she had been too afraid to stop the experiments and wanted to help.

Riding back toward Zhu's Hope in the Mako, Lizbeth heard Juliana's distress call and made Shepard stop at the Skyway Weigh Station to rescue her mother. She confronted Ethan Jeong with ExoGeni's culpability and, learning ExoGeni planned to purge the colony, asked Shepard to use anti-Thorian gas grenades to save the colonists. After the Thorian's destruction, Lizbeth moved to Zhu's Hope with the survivors. If the colony survived she stayed on as an ExoGeni attaché and reported rebuilding efforts; if it became nonviable she wanted only to leave Feros behind.

In 2185 she sent Shepard a message thanking the Commander for help on Illium and again apologizing for shooting at them, expressing regret if the colonists had been killed rather than disabled. By 2186 she had become a spokesperson for Zhu's Hope, describing the colony's defense of other worlds against the Reapers to Alliance News Network.
