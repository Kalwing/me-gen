A **Shepard VI** is a virtual intelligence based on Commander Shepard. There may be more than one version of such a VI in existence.

|  | ***“The chances of surviving are… slim.”*** — Spoilers for **Mass Effect 2** follow. |  |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Mass Effect 2

If Commander Shepard helps Thane Krios track down his son, they start their search by speaking to Captain Bailey at the C\-Sec Office on Zakera Ward. Bailey points them to Mouse, a former "duct rat", who, among other illegal activities, is selling a VI personality he made based on Shepard. The VI can make snarky comments based on its owner's electronic activities like deleting files; however, it is apparently so buggy that it crashes every thirty minutes.

Bailey recommends that Shepard pick up a copy of the VI from Mouse, which the Commander may do while interrogating Mouse outside Dark Star Lounge with Thane. Alternatively, the Commander can ask for a cut of the profits from selling the VIs, or demand that Mouse stop producing them altogether. Mouse will agree to whatever Shepard wants concerning the VI, but he insists that making it was legal as the Commander was dead at the time. If Shepard obtains a copy, Mouse claims that three volus are regularly patching the software.

## Mass Effect 3

In 2186, after completing a mission to Palaven to rescue the turian primarch, Commander Shepard may encounter a holographic image of themselves on display at the growing refugee camp on the Citadel. When interacted with, a prerecorded male voice states that the projection is a VI, "model 1\.7 AGB, Commander Shepard", and to speak with a store clerk to unlock a demo. The nearby female sales clerk will oblige, apparently unaware she is speaking with the "war hero" her merchandise is based on. She explains that only the demo version is currently working because a kid spilled their soda on the VI's hardware.

After it is unlocked, the Shepard VI demo will speak a number of lines in a synthesized voice similar to the real Shepard's voice. These lines vary depending on the Commander's personality and reputation. After an initial greeting that only plays once, there is a set number of lines that are always spoken by the VI in the same order, ending with a goodbye, after which the real Shepard wonders aloud if they really sound like the VI. The VI can be used any number of additional times, but the same lines repeat.

## Trivia

- In Mass Effect 2, when Bailey first mentions the Shepard VI sold by Mouse at the start of Thane: Sins of the Father, he may quote voice lines the VI supposedly uses. Both lines are obvious parodies of things the Commander may have actually said in Mass Effect, including one line they may have uttered about killing people on their way to "real problems" if they confronted Lord Darius on Nonuel.
	- If they are in the party, Tali or Garrus may rib Shepard about the VI lines, calling them "pretty extreme," a phrase that is itself likely a callback to a common reaction from squadmates to many of the Commander's more Renegade actions in the first game.
- In Mass Effect 3, the Renegade Shepard VI's line about "bootleg" versions of the VI may be a reference to the version sold by Mouse in Mass Effect 2; it may also be referencing the fact that technically, any versions of a Shepard VI would be bootlegs, as they would all presumably have been made without the real Commander Shepard's express authorization.
- In Mass Effect 3, the Renegade Shepard VI's line about wrestling a varren for money may be a reference to the American sport of alligator wrestling.
	- This line is very similar to something that Tela Vasir may say if Shepard helps Liara hunt down the Shadow Broker in Mass Effect 2\.
