**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Govorkam System /  **Fourth planet**

## Description

Disputes among exiles break out frequently over **Bakker**; the gas giant is useful for ships looking to replenish their helium\-3 fuel, and thus a resource to be claimed.
