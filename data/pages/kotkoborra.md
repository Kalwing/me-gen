**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Skeldah System /  **Second planet**

## Description

Unlike the nearby world of Bleeding Ruby, **Kotkoborra** appears to have been in a state of ongoing volcanism for millennia. Gasses released by this activity pervade the atmosphere.
