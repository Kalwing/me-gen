**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Shojaon System /  **Second planet**

## Description

**Pas\-24**'s hot surface is composed primarily of magnesia with notable sodium deposits. Geographic features suggest the planet once possessed oceans. How this is possible is unknown.
