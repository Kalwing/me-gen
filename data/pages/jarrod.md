**Jarrod** was a krogan warlord who lived in the time during and after the Krogan Rebellions as well as the father of Urdnot Wrex.

## Background

One of the few warlords that came out alive from the Rebellions and the subsequent turian counterattack, Jarrod carried a serious grudge against the Citadel races after the war and the devastating effects that the genophage had on his species. This anger blinded him to the ramifications of the krogan's drastic reduction in numbers and the genophage's effects preventing them from repopulating like they used to. Instead, Jarrod and other tribal leaders who followed him intended to continue fighting regardless.

However, there were other krogan leaders, including his own son Wrex, who refused to follow Jarrod's call to war and instead sought to focus on breeding to recoup the losses suffered in the Rebellions. Insulted by this, Jarrod arranged for a Crush (a meeting on neutral ground) with Wrex and the other tribal leaders calling for peace, placing it at the ancestral burial grounds of the Hollows where they could discuss what should be done.

At the Crush, Jarrod initially tried to sway his son to his ideas, but Wrex insisted that the survival of their species depended on forgetting about revenge and focusing on repopulation, at least for a single generation. When it became clear that Wrex and his supporters would not be persuaded, Jarrod gave a signal. His men, who had been lying in wait in the Hollows' many graves, launched their ambush and attacked Wrex and his warriors. While most of Wrex's allies were slaughtered quickly, Wrex himself was attacked by Jarrod. The two krogan battled, culminating in Wrex killing Jarrod by driving a dagger into his father's chest before escaping.

Wrex's betrayal at the hands of his own father, and seeing Jarrod's willingness to commit a forbidden act of violence at the Hollows merely to continue his vendetta against the Citadel races, opened Wrex's eyes to the fatalistic mindset that had infected his species in the wake of the genophage. This prompted his departure from his home system where he became another of countless krogan who eked out a living drifting around the galaxy as mercenaries and bounty hunters.
