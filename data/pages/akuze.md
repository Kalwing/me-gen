**Akuze** is a human colony on the outskirts of Systems Alliance space. In 2177, Akuze was in the early stages of colonization when all contact was lost with the pioneer team. An Alliance Marine unit was then sent in to investigate, but found only the intact settlement and no survivors. When the unit camped for the night, they were set upon by thresher maws. Not knowing what they faced, the unit was slaughtered almost to the last man; fifty marines were lost, and only one made it back to the landing zone alive.

## Mass Effect

If Commander Shepard was the only known Marine to make it out of Akuze alive and they meet C\-Sec officer Eddie Lang on the Citadel, Lang will comment on a monument at Akuze with an entire section dedicated to the Commander.

Shepard can later discover that there was another survivor, Corporal Toombs, who claims the slaughter at Akuze was not an accident. According to Toombs, the renegade black ops group Cerberus deliberately set the thresher maws on the marines, to study the creatures and see how the unit reacted.

## Mass Effect 2

If Commander Shepard is a survivor of Akuze, there is a news announcement about a Shepard Memorial being erected on Akuze, and that once the site is cleared of thresher maws, a commemoration ceremony hosted by Admiral Steven Hackett will be held in 2186\.

If Shepard rescued Toombs in 2183, the Commander will receive a threatening message from him about Shepard's new alliance with Cerberus.

#### Lair of the Shadow Broker

After Shepard and Liara defeat Tela Vasir while trying to recover data on the location of the Shadow Broker, the asari Spectre may mention the massacre of the Commander's former squad on Akuze as evidence of Shepard's hypocrisy in accusing her of being a traitor while being allied with Cerberus, the very group responsible for those deaths.

## Mass Effect 3

If Shepard survived the Akuze incident, a video recording can be viewed on Cronos Station of a conversation between a Cerberus scientist and the Illusive Man which confirms that Cerberus was behind the attack.
