The **batarians** are a trio of belligerent denizens in Omega's slums district.

|  | ***“The chances of surviving are… slim.”*** — Spoilers for **Mass Effect 2** follow. |  |
| --- | --- | --- |

After Mordin Solus asks Shepard to look for his missing assistant, Daniel, Shepard finds him being threatened by three batarians who are convinced that Daniel is spreading the plague. Once Shepard runs across Daniel, the situation can be resolved peacefully or with gunfire.

## Capabilities

Offensive
All three batarians are armed with pistols.

Defensive
The batarians have low shielding and only 1 point of health, making them very easy to take down.

## Tactics

- The batarians are armed with basic M\-3 Predator pistols and have negligible defenses. A few shots should be enough to take each of them down.
