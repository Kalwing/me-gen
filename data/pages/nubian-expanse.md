**Prerequisite:** Jack: Subject Zero (Mass Effect 2\)

**Prerequisite:** Priority: Thessia (Mass Effect 3\)

The **Nubian Expanse** is an Attican Traverse cluster located on the eastern edge of the Milky Way. No single dominant race has laid any claim to its meager resources; it is mostly a haven for outlaws.
