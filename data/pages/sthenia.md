**Location:** Milky Way / Nimbus Cluster / Pelion System /  **Second planet**
**Prerequisite:** Priority: The Citadel II (Mass Effect 3\)

## Description

The most temperate planet in the solar system, **Sthenia** is a terrestrial world with a light atmosphere of nitrogen and argon. Asari from neighboring Trategos built mining operations around the southern pole after discovering mountains with veins of iridium and osmium.
