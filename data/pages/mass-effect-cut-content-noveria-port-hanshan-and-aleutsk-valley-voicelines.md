The following dialogues, related to the mission Noveria: Geth Interest and Noveria: Matriarch Benezia on Noveria and the various side\-quests in Port Hanshan, were cut from Mass Effect. These voicelines concern the first part of the mission, up until the point when the player enters Peak 15's garage. They are presented in an approximate chronological order.

**Note:**  this page makes use of formats that may not display text as intended if viewing on mobile. It is recommended to view this page on desktop to ensure accuracy and full interactivity.
