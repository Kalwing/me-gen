**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Valay System /  **Second planet**

## Description

**Phostaan** is largely barren, but its atmosphere is theoretically capable of producing rainfall. Powerful localized storms could appear as often as three or four times a year.

## Anomaly

### River

DATA:

- Source: Rainfall
- Composition: Liquid water
- Length: 2285 km

ANALYSIS:

A violent and recent rainstorm must have occurred to create this river. The water will be absorbed or evaporated soon.

Awards \+270 XP when scanned.
