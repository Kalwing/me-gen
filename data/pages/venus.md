**Location:** Milky Way / Local Cluster / Sol System /  **Second planet**
**Prerequisite:** Level 20 (Mass Effect)

## Description

With its molten temperatures, sulphuric acid clouds, and crushing carbon dioxide atmosphere, **Venus** has only a handful of aerostat research outposts.

## Trivia

- Aerostat habitats are colonies that float in the atmosphere of a planet, usually one with a high density atmosphere. They have been proposed for the colonisation of Venus, as breathable air is less dense than the atmosphere of Venus, and would thus act as a lifting gas.
