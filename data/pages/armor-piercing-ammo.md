**Armor\-Piercing Ammo** is a power in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3's single\-player mode.

## Mass Effect 2

Increases damage to armor and health.

### Power Ranks

- **Rank 1** \- Your ammo is shaped and hardened to pierce targets, inflicting extra damage to armor and health.
	- **Recharge Time**: 1\.50 seconds
	- **Duration**: lasts until overridden by another ammo power.
	- **Armor and Health Damage**: \+30\.00% weapon damage

- **Rank 2** \- Your ammo is shaped and hardened to pierce targets, inflicting extra damage to armor and health.
	- **Recharge Time**: 1\.50 seconds
	- **Duration**: lasts until overridden by another ammo power.
	- **Armor and Health Damage**: \+40\.00% weapon damage

- **Rank 3** \- Your ammo is shaped and hardened to pierce targets, inflicting extra damage to armor and health.
	- **Recharge Time**: 1\.50 seconds
	- **Duration**: lasts until overridden by another ammo power.
	- **Armor and Health Damage**: \+50\.00% weapon damage

**Rank 4**

Evolves Armor\-Piercing Ammo into one of the following options:

#### *Tungsten Ammo*

- Advanced tungsten carbide rounds in discarding sabots increase the damage done by Armor\-Piercing Ammo.
	- **Recharge Time**: 1\.50 seconds
	- **Duration**: lasts until overridden by another ammo power.
	- **Armor and Health Damage**: \+70\.00% weapon damage

#### *Squad Armor Piercing Ammo*

- Time spent custom gunsmithing allows the entire squad to gain the effects of Armor\-Piercing Ammo.
	- **Recharge Time**: 1\.50 seconds
	- **Duration**: lasts until overridden by another ammo power.
	- **Armor and Health Damage**: \+50\.00% weapon damage

### Player Notes

- Armor\-piercing Ammo is one of only three ammo powers available as a Bonus Power, along with Warp Ammo and Shredder Ammo. It can provide the highest raw damage bonus against enemy armor (yellow bar) of any ammo power, and the second highest possible damage bonus against health (red bar) aside from Shredder Ammo. This makes it an excellent choice for any player class that lacks Incendiary Ammo, especially if Tungsten Ammo is chosen at rank 4\.
- Due to how damage is applied in Mass Effect 2, Armor\-Piercing Ammo is the best ammo power to have active on weapons when going for one\-shot kills against any armored foe, followed by Incendiary Ammo and Warp Ammo.

### Availability

- Shepard via Advanced Training
- Garrus Vakarian (Loyalty Power)

### Player Notes

Mechanics
- Armor\-Piercing Ammo is an ammo power that by default can confer up to three separate benefits: (1\) it boosts damage against enemy health (red bar) and armor (yellow bar) by a percentage; (2\) it enables shots to physically penetrate or pierce through objects; and (3\) it reduces the effectiveness of enemy armor.

**(1\) Damage Bonuses:**
- Upgrades to power damage will increase Armor\-Piercing Ammo's listed damage bonuses against health and armor.
- While the Krysae Sniper Rifle, N7 Typhoon, and Collector SMG deal 50% more damage vs. armored foes, this modifier is not factored in to the bonus damage of any ammo powers present on those weapons.
	- In the same vein, the Reegar Carbine deals 50% base damage vs. armor, but ammo power damage ignores this negative modifier.

**(2\) Penetration of Terrain and Other Solid Objects:**
- This enables most weapons to fire through objects, including terrain, enemy armor plating, other enemies, or even your own squadmates (which normally obstruct your shots).
- Though not listed in the in\-game description text, there is a 50% damage penalty for piercing any object, same as the penalty applied by certain weapon mods. When combining Armor\-Piercing Ammo with piercing mods, the least of all penalties is applied. In multiplayer, Armor\-Piercing Rounds and Drill Rounds do **not** carry this penalty.
- The following weapons have innate penetration that can be further increased by Armor\-Piercing Ammo: Black Widow, Executioner Pistol, Javelin, M\-98 Widow, N7 Crusader, N7 Typhoon. These weapons also have the unique trait of being **totally exempt from damage penalties** when penetrating objects, even when using piercing ammo augmentations or weapon mods that otherwise usually carry a penalty.
- The Particle Rifle and the Reegar Carbine will benefit from the cover penetration conferred by Armor\-Piercing Ammo.
- Armor\-Piercing Ammo does not confer cover penetration to weapons that are unable to pierce cover: Acolyte, Adas Anti\-Synthetic Rifle, Chakram Launcher, Geth Plasma Shotgun, Graal Spike Thrower, Kishock Harpoon Gun, Krysae Sniper Rifle, M\-37 Falcon, Scorpion, Striker Assault Rifle, Venom Shotgun.

**(3\) Reduced Armor Effectiveness:**
- Armor\-Piercing Ammo reduces the flat damage reduction provided by armor of targets such as Banshees or Geth Pyros by the percentage listed. The total effectiveness of enemy armor can be reduced by a full 100%.
- This works like the "defenses ignored" effect provided by weapon mods like the Assault Rifle Piercing Mod, and these effects stack cumulatively.
- This does **not** work as a party\-friendly "debuff" that weakens armor for the entire party's weapons like Warp Ammo and Cryo Ammo (or the powers Warp and Cryo Blast). Like with a piercing weapon mod, only the user benefits.

- Care should be used when choosing the rank 4 Squad Bonus if other squad members are using ammo powers, as then activating an ammo power with the Squad Bonus will override other squad members' ammo powers. You can usually tell which ammo power is actually active based on the icon appearing over your gun. Also note that the reduced effectiveness of the Squad Bonus version of the ammo power applies to penetration depth, though not to the armor effectiveness reduction.
- The ammunition capacity boost, if chosen, only applies on the active ammo power. Switching to another ammo power will remove the extra ammunition even if both have the capacity boost.

Strategies
- Object penetration isn't a property of other armor\-weakening ammunition types, such as Cryo Ammo and Warp Ammo, though it can be conferred by certain weapons and/or weapon mods. This makes Armor\-Piercing Ammo uniquely useful against armor\-plated opponents such as Brutes, Cannibals after they devour a corpse, or Cannibals and Husks after they are given plating by a Marauder. It also enables piercing of the handheld shields carried by Guardians and CAT6 Heavies.
- Certain enemies such as Atlases, Scions, Ravagers, and Adjutants have weak areas that can be exploited especially well by weapons that fire piercing shots; see their pages for more information.
- Armor\-Piercing Ammo is excellent for NPC squadmates, as they have a tendency to try and shoot directly at enemies even when they are in cover or obscured by terrain. It is best used on the weapons that fire penetrating shots by default (see above), as these never suffer damage penalties for shooting through objects, and the piercing amounts (depth) from the two separate effects will stack.
