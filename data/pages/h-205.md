**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Anasa System /  **Fifth planet**

## Description

A diminutive sphere of rock and ice, **H\-205** is entirely unmapped and is not universally shown on Heleus Cluster charts. It could one day prove an excellent hiding place.
