**Location:** Milky Way / Kite's Nest / Indris System /  **Fourth planet**
**Prerequisite:** Leviathan: Find Garneau (Mass Effect 3\)

## Description

The hydrogen\-helium gas giant **Hiba** has an unstable orbit, which over time has led to it clearing the space around it of asteroids and protoplanetary material. As a result, its rings are thick and quite visible.
