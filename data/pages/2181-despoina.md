**Location:** Milky Way / Sigurd's Cradle / Psi Tophet System /  **First planet**
**Prerequisite:** Citadel: Leviathan III (Mass Effect 3)

## Description

This planet was only spotted recently by human space probes, and no records indicate it has been explored since. Scans indicate it is covered in an ocean of liquid water and has a nitrogen\-oxygen atmosphere. It is highly likely to have at least some photosynthetic life.

## Missions

- *Mission:* Despoina: Leviathan

## Trivia

- In Greek mythology, Despoina is a goddess of the Eleusinian mysteries, initiation rites held under the cult of Demeter. It's also used as an alternative to Mary, mother of Jesus, as a honorific for women adding or denoting status. Its etymology means host/master/landlord for women. It has since become a common Orthodox Greek name for women.
