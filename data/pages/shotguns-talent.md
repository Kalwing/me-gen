**Shotguns** is a combat talent inherently available to Soldiers, Vanguards, the Krogan Battlemaster, and the Quarian Machinist; however, it can be trained as a bonus talent, providing any player class more damage with shotguns.

Possessing the talent allows the player to aim down sights when holding a shotgun, zooming their view, though unlike with the other weapon types, this does not improve shot accuracy. In fact, even though the talent provides bonuses to accuracy, there is little, if any, noticeable effect on the spread of shotgun pellets from accuracy bonuses of any kind, either from this talent or from any other source such as weapon upgrades. Thus, unlike other weapons, the passive benefits from increased ranks in the Shotguns talent are effectively confined to the damage bonus.

Training higher levels of the Shotguns talent unlocks the Carnage active ability, enabling the periodic firing of a specialized explosive round.

**Legendary Edition:**  Any player class can zoom or scope with any weapon without requiring the corresponding weapon talent; however, this will slow the player's movement. The Carnage ability has been improved, greatly increasing its damage and enabling it to purge weapon heat on activation.

**Level 2**: Increases damage by 8%. Increases accuracy by 14%.
**Level 3**: Increases damage by 10%. Increases accuracy by 17%.

## Carnage

*For the power in Mass Effect 3, see Carnage.*
Carnage is an unlockable talent used with shotguns to fire a concentrated fireball\-like blast from the shotgun, causing it to deal massive amounts of damage to anything it strikes. This does not deal friendly fire damage to Shepard or allies.

The shot fired with Carnage has perfect accuracy, and can be used to hit targets over long distances for which shotguns are normally not ideal; however, the shot has a travel time that can make it easy to miss moving targets. Carnage is also one of the few ways weapons in Mass Effect can deal area\-of\-effect damage.

Counterintuitively, Carnage does not actually shoot a shotgun round in a conventional sense, though a shotgun must be selected to use it, and the projectile appears to come from the weapon. While it does use weapon damage in its calculations, the generated projectile is a standalone ability in most regards: no heat is produced; any force component and on\-hit properties of ammunition upgrades are lost; Spectre Training and 75% Renegade bonuses are applied twice (once to weapon base damage and again to the Carnage itself). All in all, Carnage is fairly underwhelming compared to the active abilities of the other weapon talents.

Several enemies, notably Geth Shock Troopers and Geth Destroyers, use Carnage at the beginning of a battle. The projectile is usually slow enough to dodge and takes a while to recharge.

**Note:** Switching weapons, changing the equipped shotgun to a different model, or modifying any upgrades currently installed on the shotgun will immediately cancel Carnage if it was active but not yet fired. This applies to all similar weapon talents in Mass Effect, for both Shepard and squadmates.

### Carnage

Fires a blast of particles inflicting weapon damage \+50% to any enemy within 2 m of its impact point.
**Recharge Time**: 45s
**Accuracy Cost**: 40%
### Advanced Carnage

Inflicts weapon damage \+100% within 2 m of its impact point.
**Recharge Time**: 45s
**Accuracy Cost**: 40%
### Master Carnage

Inflicts weapon damage \+150% within 3 m of its impact point.
**Recharge Time**: 45s
**Accuracy Cost**: 40%
## Carnage (Legendary Edition)

Carnage in Legendary Edition works much as it did in the original game, except that it purges all residual heat on use, has a much shorter 6 second duration during which the enhanced shot can be fired, and deals greatly increased damage by comparison, especially at base level (though it is still fairly underwhelming). Note that the heat purging feature will **not** enable the firing of a shotgun that has been Sabotaged by an enemy, and Sabotage also disables **all** special weapon abilities until it wears off, even on weapons that weren't Sabotaged.

### Carnage

Only available when using shotguns. Purges heat on use.

- Fires a projectile inflicting damage equal to 200% of weapon DPS to any enemy within 2m of its impact point
- **Duration**: 6s
- **Recharge time**: 45s

### Advanced Carnage

Only available when using shotguns. Purges heat on use.

- Fires a projectile inflicting damage equal to 225% of weapon DPS to any enemy within 2\.5m of its impact point
- **Duration**: 6s
- **Recharge time**: 45s

### Master Carnage

Only available when using shotguns. Purges heat on use.

- Fires a projectile inflicting damage equal to 250% of weapon DPS to any enemy within 3m of its impact point
- **Duration**: 6s
- **Recharge time**: 45s
