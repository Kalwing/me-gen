**Location:** Milky Way / Kepler Verge / Newton System /  **Third planet**
**Prerequisite:** Priority: Thessia (Mass Effect 3\)

## Description

**Juncro** is a hydrogen\-helium gas giant with traces of chlorine and sodium in its upper atmosphere. Like Uranus is \[sic] the Sol system, it is "tipped on its side," its north pole facing the star Newton.
