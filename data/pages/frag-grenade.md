**Frag Grenade** is a grenade power in Mass Effect 3's single\-player and multiplayer modes.

## Power Ranks

### Rank 1: Frag Grenade

Rip your enemies apart with this shrapnel\-packed grenade.

- **Damage:** 600 (900 multiplayer)
- **Radius:** 6\.50 m

### Rank 2: Max Grenades

Increase grenade capacity by 1\.

### Rank 3: Damage

Increase damage by 20%.

- **Damage:** 720 (1080 multiplayer)
- **Radius:** 6\.50 m

### Rank 4: Damage/Radius

| *Damage* Increase damage by 30%. - **Damage:** 900 (1350 multiplayer) - **Radius:** 6\.50 m | *Radius* Increase impact radius by 30%. - **Damage:** 720 (1080 multiplayer) - **Radius:** 8\.45 m |
| --- | --- |

### Rank 5: Max Grenades/Bleed Damage

| *Max Grenades* Increase grenade capacity by 2\. | *Bleed Damage* Increase damage to organics by 40% (50% multiplayer) over 10 seconds (5 seconds multiplayer). |
| --- | --- |

### Rank 6: Armor\-Piercing/Shield Overload

| *Armor\-Piercing* Increase damage to armor by 50% (75% multiplayer). | *Shield Overload* Increase damage to shields by 50% (75% multiplayer). |
| --- | --- |

## Player Notes

- Grenade powers do not have a recharge time and can be used immediately at any time provided grenades are available. Using the power consumes one grenade each time.
- If the player has multiple grenade powers, the grenade pool is shared between them.
- As with all grenades, Frag Grenades are effective at taking out groups of weaker enemies. The detonation will also stagger most lighter enemies in the blast zone that aren't killed outright. They are also useful for bouncing or arcing around terrain to hit enemies hiding behind cover.
- Frag Grenades thrown by players usually detonate 1 second after they come into contact with a horizontal surface, and they detonate instantly on hitting an enemy directly. When squadmates are ordered to use them in single\-player (they cannot use grenades of their own accord), they not only detonate instantly but travel instantly as well.
- Frag Grenades can detonate all non\-biotic Power Combos (tech, fire, and cryo) but they can't set up, or "prime," combos of any type.
- The rank 6 Shield Overload evolution only increases damage to shields (blue), *not* barriers (violet), unlike with most powers with similar evolutions.
- Though this power is available to Admiral Anderson during the prologue, he will have no grenades, rendering it useless.
- The Bleed Damage evolution, as well as both the rank 6 evolutions, are multiplicative, increasing the damage by a percent of total (instead of base) damage.

## Availability

- **Single\-Player:** Soldier, David Anderson, James Vega, Grunt
- **Multiplayer:** Battlefield 3 Soldier, Human Soldier
