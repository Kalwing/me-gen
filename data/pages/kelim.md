**Prerequisite:** Priority: Sur'Kesh (Mass Effect 3\)

## Description

**Kelim** is a tectonically inert rock with an atmosphere of krypton, xenon, and argon. There are a few valuable lodes of light metals scattered across its surface, but these are difficult to find; most were mined out in the years leading up to the Krogan Rebellions.
