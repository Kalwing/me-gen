**Prerequisite:** Meridian: The Way Home

The Jardaan sent the artificial world Meridian into **Saajor** after separating it from the Khi Tasira space station centuries prior to the Andromeda Initiative's arrival into Andromeda. According to Dr. Suvi Anwar, Saajor's gas clouds allowed Meridian to stay hidden for so long.
