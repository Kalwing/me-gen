**Biotic Protector** is a biotic power in Mass Effect 3's single\-player mode.

## Power Ranks

### Rank 1: Biotic Protector

Deploy a shield that protects against all damage at the expense of moving, shooting, or using powers while it is enabled.

- **Recharge Speed:** 10 sec
- **Duration:** 4 sec

### Rank 2: Recharge Speed

Increase recharge speed by 25%.

- **Recharge Speed:** 8 sec
- **Duration:** 4 sec

### Rank 3: Duration

Increase duration by 25%.

- **Recharge Speed:** 8 sec
- **Duration:** 5 sec

### Rank 4: Blast/Bonus Power

| *Blast* Trigger a 3 meter radius blast when deploying the shield, dealing 300 points of damage. - **Recharge Speed:** 8 sec - **Duration:** 5 sec | *Bonus Power* Give Biotic Protector a 50% chance of not triggering a cooldown. - **Recharge Speed:** 8 sec - **Duration:** 5 sec |
| --- | --- |

### Rank 5: Recharge Speed/Duration

| *Recharge Speed* Increase recharge speed by 30%. - **Recharge Speed:** 6\.45 sec - **Duration:** 5 sec | *Duration* Increase duration by 30%. - **Recharge Speed:** 8 sec - **Duration:** 6\.20 sec |
| --- | --- |

### Rank 6: Weapon Damage/Damage Reduction

| *Weapon Damage* Deal 50% more weapon damage for 10 seconds after the shield expires. - **Recharge Speed:** 6\.45 sec (Recharge Speed) 8 sec (Duration) - **Duration:** 5 sec (Recharge Speed) 6\.20 sec (Duration) | *Damage Reduction* Reduce incoming damage by 50% for 10 seconds after the shield expires. - **Recharge Speed:** 6\.45 sec (Recharge Speed) 8 sec (Duration) - **Duration:** 5 sec (Recharge Speed) 6\.20 sec (Duration) |

## Player Notes

- The Blast evolution of Biotic Protector is a useful fallback for Nyreen if she is overwhelmed by enemies. The blast is able to detonate all four types of Power Combos.
- Biotic Protector can allow Nyreen to be used to distract enemies, particularly those such as Phantoms, Adjutants and Rampart Mechs that close in to their targets.
- Biotic Protector does not start recharging until the invulnerability bubble ends. The Bonus Power evolution is therefore quite useful if you want Nyreen to benefit from Biotic Protector and maintain its powerful rank 6 buffs as often as possible while still being able to call on her Incinerate or Overload.
- Nyreen can't activate Biotic Protector if she's stunned, so don't wait until enemies like Rampart Mechs close to melee range to order her to activate it.
