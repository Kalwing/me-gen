**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Faroang System /  **Third planet**

## Description

**Farrum**'s carbon dioxide and argon atmosphere is inhospitable to most life, as are ocean temperatures which (in sunlight during the proper season) can reach the boiling point.
