**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Nol System /  **Second planet**

## Description

**Ditivios** is largely covered in oceans of nitrogen churned by powerful winds. The storms make detailed surface scans
difficult.

## Anomaly

### Storm

DATA:

- Radius: 137 km
- Wind speed: 157 km/h
- Rotation: Counterclockwise

ANALYSIS:

This massive hurricane is carrying a sort of nitrogen\-adapted plankton over the ocean. Ditivios appears to be home to simple lifeforms.

Awards \+270 XP when scanned.
