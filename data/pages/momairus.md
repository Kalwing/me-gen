**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Rohvir System /  **Fourth planet**

## Description

**Momairus** appears to have been stripped of a breathable atmosphere by Scourge phenomena over the past century. What remains is a barren sphere of boron and carbon.

## Anomaly

### Aluminum Deposit

DATA:

- Aluminum

ANALYSIS:

A valuable light metal. This deposit is close to the surface and relatively easy to mine.

Awards a random small amount of Aluminum when scanned.
