**Prerequisite:** Feros: Geth Attack (Mass Effect)

As of 2183, the **Maroon Sea** is the subject of an Alliance\-sponsored effort to colonize the Matano system, which is in the early stages of development. ExoGeni operates a secret research facility in a remote part of the cluster.

Several worlds have resources easily accessible from orbit.
