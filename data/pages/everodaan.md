**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Layan System /  **Third planet**

## Description

Images from an unmanned flyover of **Everodaan** brought its gemstone mountains to angaran attention centuries ago. The planet was an icon of alien beauty, now generally forgotten.
