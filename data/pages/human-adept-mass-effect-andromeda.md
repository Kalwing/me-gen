*For the Human Adept in Mass Effect 3 Multiplayer, see Human Adept.*
*For the Ex\-Cerberus Human Male Adept, see Project Phoenix Adept.*

A powerful biotic with light armor who uses powers to control crowds and cause widespread damage.
**Male:** The **Human Adept** doesn't really talk about why he joined the Initiative; in fact, he doesn't talk much at all. Given the quiet intensity with which he rips through enemies, none of his teammates are rushing to ask about his past.

**Female:** The Human Adept doesn't much care about the romance of settling a new galaxy. What she sees here is a power vacuum—and she's damn well going to fill it.

## Player Notes

### General Notes

- The Human Adept depends more on their powers than their guns. As such, they can only carry up to 30 weight units' worth of guns without slowing their recharge speed.
- The Human Adept somewhat lacks direct countermeasures against shields and armor, and can only affect significant damage through exposure to biotic explosions or continuous gunfire.
	- Pull or Singularity can prime biotic explosions while Shockwave can set them off. Shockwave can also prime explosions at rank 6\. Pull and Singularity can be configured to be anti\-shield, while Shockwave can be configured to deal more damage to armor at rank 6 instead of being another primer.
