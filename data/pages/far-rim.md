**Prerequisite:** Horizon (mission) (Mass Effect 2\)

**Prerequisite:** Priority: The Citadel II (Mass Effect 3\)

The **Far Rim** is a Terminus Systems territory right at the edge of the galaxy. It is generally considered the beginning of "geth space". The exiled quarians also have a presence in this area in the Migrant Fleet.
