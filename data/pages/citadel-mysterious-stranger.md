**Citadel: Mysterious Stranger** is a cut assignment which was planned during development for Mass Effect 2. Set on the Citadel, it involved a romantic relationship between an asari dancer and a Terra Firma politician.

## Plotline

In the Dark Star Lounge, Shepard would have overheard the concerns of an asari dancer called Nara Selephais: she went on a date with a human man and would wish to see him again, but she's been unable to find him. Offering to help, Shepard is given a few "hints" to track him on the Citadel. It turns out the man, Akio, is a politician (or diplomat) for the Terra Firma party, and asks Shepard to keep his identity secret from Nara. Returning to her, the player must choose between lying to honor Akio's request, or telling her the truth. Either decision leads to the conclusion of the assignment.

## Trivia

- The book Mass Effect: The Official Cocktail Book opens the chapter on recipes inspired by the Dark Star Lounge by alluding to a hypothetical situation where you'd "wake up next to an attractive stranger the next morning whose name you can't quite recall", which is strangely reminiscent of this cut quest, especially given that it's the same location.
