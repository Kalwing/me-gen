**Pistols** are one of the usable weapon classes in Mass Effect and Mass Effect: Andromeda.

## Mass Effect

Despite their reputation as a backup weapon or sidearm, pistols are perfectly serviceable as a primary weapon. In fact, with their relatively high accuracy and low heat generation, they are more versatile than other weapons, though they lack the rate of fire of assault rifles, the range of sniper rifles, and the stopping power of shotguns. All pistols are capable of fully automatic fire, and they are more stable than assault rifles, losing less accuracy over time from continuous fire.

Zooming, also referred to as "tight\-aiming" or "aiming\-down\-sights", will increase weapon accuracy and magnify your view. It is only possible to zoom if you are trained in the use of the selected weapon type, i.e. you possess a talent that improves proficiency with that weapon. All of the player classes possess some form of pistol training: all classes except the Sentinel have the Pistols talent, while the Sentinel gains pistol training through its class talent.

**Legendary Edition:** Any player class can zoom or scope with any weapon without requiring any weapon training from a talent, though at the cost of reduced movement speed. The addition of bonus headshot damage has improved the damage potential of all weapon types. Pistols in particular gain a lot of benefit from headshots, not only because of their high accuracy, but because of the additional headshot damage bonus provided by the unlockable Marksman ability. Most pistol models in Legendary Edition are not capable of fully automatic fire, with only the HMWP Master line of pistols having this firing mode.
Pistols have a rate of fire of 4 shots per second.

### Legendary Edition Firing Patterns

In Legendary Edition, the various pistols possess unique attributes that determine how they fire, further distinguishing each model.

## Mass Effect: Andromeda

A mixture of "hand cannons" and submachine guns (SMGs), pistols are generally the lightest weapons on the battlefield. This light weight allows more frequent use of powers. Single\-shot pistols tend to be more effective against armor than SMGs but require careful aim.

In Mass Effect: Andromeda, Pistols and Submachine Guns (SMGs) were combined into a single **Pistols** category.

- Even if the in\-game names and descriptions of some weapons specifically call out the weapon as an SMG (e.g. Charger), the weapon is classified as a **"Pistol"**.
- The **"Heavy"** designation seen in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3 that was used for pistols was dropped in Mass Effect: Andromeda.

### Pistols Customization

Pistols can be customized by two different methods in Mass Effect: Andromeda.

#### Weapon Modifications

*Main article: Mods*
Mods are customizations that are exclusive to weapons only in Mass Effect: Andromeda. Mods use round\-shaped sockets on a weapon and can only be changed at a Loadout terminal.

#### Weapon Augmentations

*Main article: Augmentations*
Augmentations are semi\-permanent customizations to crafted weapons or armor in Mass Effect: Andromeda. When a weapon is created during Development (crafting), hexagonal\-shaped sockets are available to add augmentations to the weapon. These augmentations cannot be changed like weapon Mods and stay part of that weapon until it is deconstructed.
