**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Skeldah System /  **Fifth planet**

## Description

The Scourge has stripped away much of **H\-329**'s atmosphere, leaving it unable to retain heat. Its liquid nitrogen oceans are now entirely frozen.
