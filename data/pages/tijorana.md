**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Nalesh System /  **First planet**

## Description

An angaran poet described **Tijorana** as the "gemstone eye of Nalesh." Its limestone surface glimmers with copper deposits, though few explorers have ever made planetfall.
