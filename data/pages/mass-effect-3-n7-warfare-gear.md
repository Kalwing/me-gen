**Mass Effect 3: N7 Warfare Gear** is a DLC pack for Mass Effect 3. It was available as a retail bonus item to players who pre\-ordered the game from certain retailers. It is also included in the Wii U version of Mass Effect 3\. It is currently available on PC as part of the Mass Effect 3 DLC Bundle.

## Content

- Two assault rifles:
	- N7 Valkyrie
	- M\-55 Argus

- One armor set:
	- N7 Defender Armor
