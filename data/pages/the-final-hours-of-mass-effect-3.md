**The Final Hours of Mass Effect 3** is a documentary work published by journalist Geoff Keighley in March, 2012, around the time of the release of Mass Effect 3. Through exclusive anecdotes and interviews with BioWare, it covers the origins of the studio and the inception of the first Mass Effect game all the way to the end of development for Mass Effect 3\.

The Final Hours of Mass Effect 3 was originally released in an interactive document format allowing for link embeds, as well as music and videos, which became deprecated in the following years. In consequence, the "original" version was removed from sale, though a truncated Kindle version remains available. Following the release of Mass Effect Legendary Edition, Electronic Arts uploaded the original version on their website as a freely downloadable bonus requiring Adobe Air to open, though a number of functionalities remain broken.
