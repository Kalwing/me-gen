**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Shojaon System /  **Fourth planet**

## Description

Spectacular auroral effects flash through **Pas\-48**'s magnetosphere over a temperate, mineral\-rich surface. Only the lack of liquid water makes it inappropriate for colonization.
