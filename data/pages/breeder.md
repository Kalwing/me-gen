“I am not queen. Not proud rachni. Alone.”
The **Breeder** is an artificial rachni queen, genetically engineered by the Reapers to create Ravagers for their armies. Commander Shepard encounters her in captivity while searching for a krogan scout team that disappeared investigating a possible rachni presence on Utukku. The Breeder only appears if the rachni queen was killed by Shepard on Noveria in 2183. The Breeder is entrenched deep within the tunnels on Utukku, guarded by Reaper technology and barriers controlled by local nodes. She speaks through the mouths of the dead krogan scouts, many of whom are suspended on the walls by the same webbing encountered on the way into the central chamber.

The Breeder claims she was created from "pieces" of real rachni and at least appears more or less physically identical to a true rachni queen; however, her mind has been twisted as a result of her unnatural creation, causing her to refer to herself as a "monster" and to speak in the first\-person singular rather than the plural. The Breeder is also much more insistent that she be spared, offering to spawn new children loyal to Shepard's cause in exchange for freedom. Unlike the Queen, who will calmly accept her fate if left behind, the Breeder will become enraged and scream for her minions to kill Shepard should the Commander choose not to spare her.

Also, unlike the Queen, the Breeder will prove untrustworthy if freed by Shepard, resulting in heavy losses to the war effort.

## Trivia

- According to Mass Effect Legendary Edition gameplay statistics as of July 27, 2021, of the 7% who killed the original queen on Noveria, 67% killed the Breeder in Mass Effect 3 while 33% saved the Breeder.
