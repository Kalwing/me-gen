**Unity** is a default starting power in Mass Effect 2 available only to Commander Shepard. It utilizes medi\-gel from Shepard's limited reserve to revive fallen companions and, if upgraded, to heal them and also Shepard.

Revives any wounded, fallen squad member.

- **Recharge Time**: 3\.00 seconds

## Player Notes

- Unity has no ranks and is available by default from Prologue: Awakening onwards.
- Shepard starts with the capacity to carry three units of medi\-gel, which can be increased up to nine with maximum upgrades.
- By default, Unity will revive any fallen squadmates to full health and shields, but it will have no effect on Shepard. Researching the Emergency Shielding and Trauma Module upgrades gives Unity the additional ability to restore both Shepard and damaged but conscious squadmates to full shields and health, respectively.
- Unity shares the same cooldown timer as all other powers. Attempting to cast Unity when no party member (including Shepard) is damaged, or they are not damaged in a way that medi\-gel is currently upgraded to heal will result in the power simply not casting. If the ability icon is highlighted or moused over in the HUD, a red tooltip will explain why Unity is not currently applicable.
- Medi\-gel can be administered while Shepard is under fire, and (depending on upgrades) it is capable of restoring Shepard and squadmate health and shields even while damage is being taken during the restore effect.
	- Unity can act to block more than just 100% shields' worth of damage, thanks to the brief immunity to health damage gained whenever shields are downed. Because the shield restore is not instant but gradual, this allows for multiple potential damage immunity periods following the first one that triggers from shields going down initially.
- Like most other powers, Unity is unusable while storming—the restoration only begins after a short omni\-tool activation animation. If Shepard is hit during the casting delay/animation period by certain enemy attacks or powers that cause staggers or stuns, the medi\-gel will be interrupted before it goes into effect. Once the healing effect successfully begins it can't be interrupted.
- At least one medi\-gel is always expended if Shepard or a squadmate is damaged when casting Unity, assuming medi\-gel can repair the type of damage incurred.
	- No more than two medi\-gel can be used per cast of Unity, and the only condition where two medi\-gel is expended is if there are two squadmates and *both* are down.
	- The exception to the above is when Shepard has only one medi\-gel left, in which case casting Unity will restore Shepard (if applicable) and revive and/or fully restore both squadmates even if both were downed and/or damaged.
- Squadmates that are revived will not remember any previous orders to hold a specific position and will choose their own cover or movements unless given new orders.

## Availability

- Shepard (default starting power)
