**Location:** Milky Way / Hourglass Nebula / Osun System /  **Fourth planet**
**Prerequisite:** Priority: Thessia (Mass Effect 3\)

## Description

**Olokun** ('sky harvest') is a standard gas giant composed of hydrogen and helium. The spacefarers from Erinle gather helium\-3 from here rather than Orunmila, as its atmosphere is much more predictable.
