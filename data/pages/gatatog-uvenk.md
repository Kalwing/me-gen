“Aliens don't know strength. My followers are true krogan. Everything about Grunt is a lie.”

**Gatatog Uvenk** is a krogan battlemaster and the leader of Clan Gatatog on Tuchanka in 2185\.

## Background

Uvenk is among the demographic of traditionalists who champion the krogan's warrior culture that singlehandedly threatened the races of the Citadel long ago during the Krogan Rebellions. Despite Clan Gatatog's history among the krogan, including having one of the longest standing settlements, he admits that his clan has been approaching a turning point by 2185, either to achieve power and glory or be reduced to a piece of history with a pile of bodies buried in Tuchanka's soil. Fear of the latter eventually compelled him to ally his people with the dominant Clan Urdnot and try to curry favor with its leader.

Uvenk resides in the Urdnot border camp, sticking close by the reiging clan leader. His traditionalist leanings have him voicing his opposition to the reformations initiated by Urdnot Wrex if he is the clan leader, claiming that they will only serve to weaken the krogan as a whole. On the other hand, if Urdnot Wreav is the clan leader, Uvenk is much more deferential and supportive of his chief's conservative stance.

When Commander Shepard accompanies Grunt to complete his Rite of Passage, Uvenk insults Grunt for his status as a tank\-bred krogan and vehemently opposes allowing him to join the ranks of Urdnot. When the Urdnot leader indicates preference for sending Grunt to the Rite, Uvenk tries to go around them and speak directly to the Urdnot Shaman, though his protests against allowing Grunt to undertake the Rite are met with irritation by the venerable Shaman. Shepard is able to make Uvenk back down after proving to the Shaman that they will support Grunt as his krantt, either by humbling Uvenk with words or answering his insults with a headbutt in the typical krogan fashion. Uvenk resentfully withdraws his denial for Grunt's Rite for the moment but threatens to settle the matter elsewhere.

Shortly after the third and final battle of the Rite, a shuttle carrying Uvenk and several Gatatog Warriors arrives. If Shepard and Grunt completed the Rite of Passage by killing the Thresher Maw, Uvenk acknowledges the unquestionable proof of Grunt's strength and reveals his plan to convince Grunt to join Clan Gatatog, albeit as a trophy and in\-name\-only member, solely in order to increase Uvenk's own stature. Conversely, if the Rite was finished by surviving the Thresher Maw's attack without killing it, Uvenk merely tells Grunt that he is not worthy of joining a clan because he is impure.

If Uvenk tries to recruit Grunt, his cynical overtures are violently rebuffed by Grunt, who has no interest in being used as a tool while having his identity, his krantt, and his lineage disrespected. Either way, a battle with the Gatatog warriors and Uvenk ensues which ends in their deaths. On the Normandy afterward, Grunt expresses his thanks to Shepard and his satisfaction at Uvenk's demise.

## Capabilities

**Offensive**

Uvenk fights with a M\-300 Claymore Heavy Shotgun at short range. He also uses biotic powers such as Warp.

**Defensive**

Uvenk has standard krogan health regeneration and charge, in addition to a biotic Barrier and armor. He can regenerate his Barrier if left alone long enough, and if his layer of armor has been stripped.

## Tactics

- Uvenk will not immediately charge Shepard as most krogan would. Instead he will hold back and use Warp while his Gatatog Warriors charge Shepard's position.
- Once his subordinates have been dispatched he will charge on his own at which point he can be rather easily overwhelmed.
