**Location:** Milky Way / Minos Wasteland / Arrae System /  **Fourth planet**
**Prerequisite:** Priority: The Citadel II (Mass Effect 3\)

## Description

**Nirrus**, a moderate\-sized hydrogen\-helium gas giant, is of mild interest to turian scientists because of its abnormal atmospheric composition. The planet has only a fraction of the expected methane, with high levels of carbon monoxide and ethylene instead.
