**Location:** Milky Way / Styx Theta / Acheron System /  **Third planet**
**Prerequisite:** Noveria: Geth Interest (Mass Effect)

## Description

An enigmatic rock and ice planet, **Grosalgen** has a thin atmosphere of carbon dioxide and ethane. The surface is composed of frozen water and chlorine.
