**Location:** Milky Way / Local Cluster / Sol System /  **Eighth planet**
**Prerequisite:** Level 20 (Mass Effect)

## Description

Though **Neptune**, like Uranus, has plentiful helium, its remoteness made it an unpromising target for mining before the development of mass effect drive. With Uranus cheaper to exploit, it has never seen extensive development. The only permanent human presence is a small research facility on Triton.
