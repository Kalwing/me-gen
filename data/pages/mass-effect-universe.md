**Mass Effect: Universe** was BioWare's planned fifth main game in the Mass Effect franchise. It was being developed up until BioWare's official shutdown in 2026\.

## Development

After post\-release development ended on Mass Effect 3, BioWare producer Fabrice Condominas stated that the "trilogy was over" and that Commander Shepard would not be returning. This led to Mass Effect: Andromeda's development. However, in August 2018, trilogy director Casey Hudson, who has since left BioWare, indicated that teams were already working on future Mass Effect and/or Dragon Age content.

In February 2019, Hudson stated "in my mind, it's very much alive. I'm thinking all the time about things that I think will be great. It's just a matter of getting back to it as soon as we can." In the same month, Mark Darrah reiterated this statement, further stating that future games could take plot threads from either Andromeda or Mass Effect 3\.

In October 2020, BioWare confirmed that they were working on the next installment of the series, and that it was in the early stages. In December 2020, a trailer was released for the next Mass Effect game, indicating a return to the Milky Way. By this stage, the game was in very early development, but had entered the storyboard phase.

In 2021, BioWare ceased updates for Anthem, and pivoted back to the Dragon Age and Mass Effect IPs.

On N7 Day 2022, BioWare confirmed that the new game's director since March 2019 was Mike Gamble, who had worked on the series since Mass Effect 2. Also working on the project are Parrish Ley, creative director since the first Mass Effect game, as well as Mary DeMarle (senior narrative director) and Danielle Enns (senior development director), who had both joined BioWare recently. Derek Watts, the trilogy's art director, was also said to return.

On November 7 2023, BioWare's new blog for N7 Day was noted to contain various ones and zeroes which translate from binary as "EPSILON." On the same day, a page under that name was discovered on EA's website, as well as several 5\-second teasers showing a figure walking through an apparent storage room. The teasers referenced an "Andromeda distress signal." After the Epsilon teaser, a link was discovered to another countdown under the name "Oculon\-2819\-Defiance" on EA's official site. Oculon\-2819\-Defiance was listed as an access code here, the same as Epsilon.

In the same month, it was reported that BioWare was having "canon debates" as to the events of the prior games, such as who lived and died in the original trilogy, along with details on Sara and Scott Ryder.

In February 2024, it was confirmed that the game was still in pre\-production, while BioWare's main focus remained on Dragon Age: The Veilguard.

After the release of Dragon Age: The Veilguard in October 2024, BioWare shifted its development focus to the new Mass Effect entry. At some point during development, the game shifted from Frostbite to the Unreal engine.

On N7 Day 2025, it was reiterated that the next Mass Effect game was still in production, but it was not ready to be revealed. Concept art for a krogan civil war was found through viral marketing for the event.

Prior to the game's cancellation, BioWare was aiming to release the game in late 2028\. According to reports, development was progressing well despite some missed deadlines.

On October 21, 2026, BioWare announced that it was closing down, and canceling development on the game. The next day, a teaser trailer was leaked over the web, revealing the game's title to have been Mass Effect: Universe. Journalist Jason Schreier confirmed the trailer's authenticity.

A change.org petition was launched in light of the news. As of early November, 2026, it had gathered over 320,000 verified signatures.

It was later reported that part of the reason for the game's cancellation (and BioWare's disbandment) was that EA decided to put resources in Star Wars: Battlefront III, in that while both Universe and Battlefront III would cost hundreds of millions to develop, a Star Wars game was deemed the safer bet, along with how EA had traditionally favored shooters. The poor performance of Dragon Age: The Veilguard and Anthem also contributed to EA's decision to cancel both the project and disband BioWare. Furthermore, Star Wars was a larger IP than Mass Effect.

## Plot

The game's story took place in the Milky Way, 600 years after the Reaper War, and involved Liara T'Soni. Character deaths and relationships from prior games were to be canonized. The game would have had the same tone as the original trilogy.

The setting of Universe would have involved a galactic cold war that combined all three of the previous endings of Mass Effect 3\. By the timeframe of the game, the mass relays (which were destroyed at the end of Mass Effect 3\) would have been rebuilt. The cold war in question would have been a three way one, between an alliance of humans, krogans, and batarians, a separate coalition of Council species, and the geth as the third galactic superpower, having constructed megastructures. A mysterious threat would lurk in the background.

Similar to Commander Shepard, players would have roleplayed as an N7 human protagonist. The protagonist would have been implanted with experimental technology. Shepard's legacy would be preserved within the game's lore, regardless of Shepard's decision made at the end of Mass Effect 3\.

Story connections would exist between the Milky Way and Andromeda galaxies.

## Gameplay

Universe would have featured a system where players could explore alternate universes through their saves (hence the name). The main character, who has been likened to James Bond and John Wick, would have been customizable, and could alternate between gunplay and hand to hand combat. Missions would be handcrafted but have freedom within them, in a vein similar to the Hitman series. Players would have been able to hack and manipulate the environment such as letting you "see through walls, identify systems, and remotely trigger hazards.

Romance would feature in the game, and squadmates would clash with each other. It has been stated that the romance would not have been "sanitized."

## Trivia

- The "Epsilon" teaser was a possible reference to the game's status as the fifth mainline entry, as Epsilon is the fifth letter of the Greek alphabet.
- The "Oculon\-2819\-Defiance" teaser syncs with the timeframe of Mass Effect: Andromeda, as the events of the game occur in 2819\.
- The game was planned to use a photorealistic art style.
- After the game's cancellation, some reports spread that a Pride flag had been removed from the game. Kotaku claimed it was removed because "it could cause friction" with executive management. According to Destructoid however, the flag wasn't a Pride flag and was removed by the developers' own volition because it looked too similar to the real\-world Pride flag. No official statement was given on what had actually happened.
