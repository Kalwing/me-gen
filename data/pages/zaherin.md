**Prerequisite:** Leviathan: Find Ann Bryson (Mass Effect 3\)

The asari (and their domesticated vorcha) have staked claims in **Zaherin**, although its worlds turned out to have little resources worth studying or exploiting. The vorcha have been left to their own devices in Namakli after an economic downturn.
