**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Vaar System /  **Fourth planet**

## Description

Great oceans of ethane and thin sheets of water ice cover the surface of **H\-173**. Ice mining might be possible here, but there are surely richer sources on more agreeable worlds.
