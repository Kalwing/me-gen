**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Pytheas System /  **Third planet**

## Description

Tidal heating and bombardment from Pytheas' asteroid belt has aggravated **Pele**'s volcanic activity. Methane in its upper atmosphere contributes to the planet's scorching conditions.

## Trivia

- This planet is named after a Hawaiian fire goddess who's responsible for the creation of the Hawaiian islands.
