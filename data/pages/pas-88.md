**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Inalaara System /  **Second planet**

## Description

The dominant lifeform on **Pas\-88** appears to be a finger\-length worm that thrives on the thin nitrogen and xenon atmosphere.

## Anomaly

### River

DATA:

- Source: Glacial meltwater
- Composition: Liquid ammonia
- Length: 143 km

ANALYSIS:

A kett scout vessel crashed into an ammonia glacier on Pas\-88\. Though the crew died, the ship's reactor continues to emit heat, melting the glacier and creating this great river.
