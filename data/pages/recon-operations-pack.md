The **Recon Operations Pack** is a DLC pack for Mass Effect 2 released on January 18, 2011, and is only available on the PlayStation Network.

## Content

- Collector Armor
- Collector Assault Rifle
- Recon Hood
- Sentry Interface
- Umbra Visor

## Emails

**Umbra Visor**

---

From: Illusive Man

Shepard,

I've had a night\-vision device delivered to the Normandy. The techs say it helps put biotic and omni\-tool attacks on\-target, and rather than listen to their explanation of how, I just sent it your way. It will be in your quarters.

**Sentry Interface Visor**

---

From: Illusive Man

Shepard,

Our software experts looked over your loadout telemetry, and they had a recommendation: a visor with onboard processors that manages your armor's shield strength. I've sent the visor to the Normandy, it will be in your quarters.

## Pricing

The pack is available on the PlayStation Network for $1\.99 USD. It is also included in the N7 Complete Arsenal Bundle.
