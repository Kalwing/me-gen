**Omega: Beggar Bowl** is a cut assignment which was planned during development for Mass Effect 2. Set on Omega, it involved a conflict between several beggars and the merchant Marsh.

## Plotline

The batarian merchant Marsh would task Shepard with this quest after he's seen talking with a beggar. Finding the same beggar, the player would have the choice between punching him or setting him free. Shepard could also talk to several more beggars, and could as a result confront Marsh and Intimidate him. Either fulfilling Marsh's orders or confronting him would complete the quest.
