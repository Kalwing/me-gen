The former Spectre **Saren Arterius** searches for clues across the galaxy in order to find the Conduit, a means of bringing back the Reapers. Commander Shepard follows up on Saren's trail, eventually confronting him face to face on two different occasions.

## Saren on Hovercraft

The first fight with Saren Arterius takes place on Virmire. In this encounter Saren fights while standing on a special hovercraft. He uses powerful tech attacks in addition to his weapons, which include a rocket launcher similar to the one used by Geth Rocket Troopers.

Shepard encounters Saren for the last time on the Citadel during the final battle for control of the station. Initially Saren rides his hovercraft once again, but Shepard has the option to convince him he's indoctrinated. If Saren is not convinced, he fights Shepard once more in his hovercraft.

**Legendary Edition:**  Saren will summon geth troops of various types to assist him.

### Tactics

- Order your squadmates to attack Saren aggressively because Saren usually focuses on Shepard and leaves the remaining squad members alone. However, avoid bunching up with your team, because his rocket attack can hit everyone at once otherwise. Keep your damage protection stats high to reduce the damage from this attack, and keep moving or stay behind large cover.
- On Virmire, Saren will often choose to hover over one of the many fuel tanks in the battle area. Shoot the fuel tanks (or better yet, Sabotage or Overload them) to take him out quickly.
- Saren will open the battle with his tech attacks, which includes a high level Damping that can put most of your squad's abilities on cooldown. It's possible to counter this by using Damping on him first. Adrenaline Burst can also come in handy for characters who possess it.
- Saren is immune to biotic attacks except for Warp and Stasis.
- Saren can use his platform to distance himself from Shepard.
- In the first battle on Virmire, Saren only uses an occasional Carnage blast and is seen using Throw in the first confrontation cut\-scene, though Barrier is the only biotic ability he uses in the actual fights. In the Council chambers, however, he uses Damping and Sabotage quite a bit, especially at the beginning of the fight.
- Master\-level Warp plus weapons armed with Proton Rounds or similar ammo can quickly take Saren's health bar out without even needing to fully strip his shields. Be sure to land Warp on him before using Overload, which shouldn't be needed for this strategy at all.

## Saren Husk

After Saren's death in the Council Chambers either through suicide or conventional fight, Sovereign reanimates his body into a Husk\-like state. By controlling Saren's corpse and speaking in his voice, Sovereign has the turian fight one last battle. This second avatar of Saren/Sovereign still uses tech powers turns out to be quite a bit more powerful and dangerous than the original.

**Legendary Edition:**  Saren will summon geth troops of various types to assist him.

### Tactics

General
- Stay as mobile as possible because this battle is more about mobility than cover. Keeping moving is your best defense—you will take very little damage if you keep moving. There is cover available behind the big rocks at the front if needed, but if you depend heavily on it the battle will take quite a while. Generally speaking, chasing him around while firing as much as possible tends to work. This is complicated a bit by the geth minions Saren will summon in Legendary Edition; in these situations it can be best to stay behind the cover of the two pillars at either end of the area, at least until the geth have been neutralized somewhat.
- Almost immediately after the battle begins, Saren always hits Shepard with a Sabotage attack. Switch to a different weapon, or use your abilities while waiting for the effect to wear off.
- This reincarnation of Saren is much like a Geth Hopper and jumps around frequently, with similar disabling abilities and battlefield role to the Geth Stalker. While he is highly resistant against low\-level biotics, Master rank Lift will suspend him and render him an easy target.
- Because he has no other weapons to speak of, Saren relies a lot on his disabling abilities and will unleash them often, usually when he comes to a stop and targets someone with his laser.
- Saren's version of Carnage fires numerous powerful energy balls in quick succession when he comes to a stop on a wall or ceiling. This damage will mostly ignore shields and also splashes in an area. Keep moving to avoid taking as much damage. Occasionally he will fire off single blasts while crawling around on the ground.
- After the various cutscenes, note which weapon types your squadmates have out and switch them as needed, as cutscenes habitually end with squadmates not using the weapons you want them to be using. If you are playing Legendary Edition, also don't forget you can order them to focus fire on Saren rather than any of his geth, or vice versa as needed.
- After you reduce Saren's health to a little higher than 50%, the cutscene in the middle of the battle triggers, after which he regains his shields.
- Master\-level Warp plus weapons armed with Proton Rounds can quickly take Saren's health bar out without even needing to fully strip his shields, which are twice as strong as his health bar. Be sure to land Warp on him before using Overload, which shouldn't be needed for this strategy at all.

Abilities and Weapons
- Saren is totally immune to Neural Shock and Singularity. Throw will only affect him if he is already suspended by Master\-level Lift, which is of questionable usefulness in any case.
- Advanced Stasis or higher will lock him in place for a decent period. Having Stasis Specialization courtesy of being a Bastion ensures a shorter fight since you won't have to wait for the stasis effect to wear off before you start shooting.
- Once Saren's shields are down, Warp can greatly increase the damage your weapons do (or even before his shields are down, if shield piercing ammunition is used). Combining this with Master Lift can defeat him in short order.
- Assault rifles with Overkill and pistols with Marksman will be of great use here due to the sheer amount of fire they put out. Shotguns can be riskier as it can be difficult or dangerous to get into effective range. Sniper rifles should be avoided; they fire too slowly and it's extremely hard to hit Saren while he's flying around, though it can be possible to line up shots when he comes to a stop to fire his own attacks or if he's immobilized with Stasis or Lift. Squadmates are also fairly accurate with sniper rifles even against fast\-moving targets.
- Tech talents are of limited usefulness in this fight, but they still can help. While Sabotage of any rank will never disable any of Saren's attacks, and Damping of any rank will not stun him or prevent him from using any abilities, if you hit Saren when he comes to a stop on a wall or ceiling to use one of his ranged attacks (either his laser or to fire the energy blasts), his attack will be interrupted and he will jump away. Overload also causes this, and is useful for the damage to his shield and making his health more vulnerable after his shield is down.
- Sabotage and Hacking are both highly useful against Saren's geth allies if you are playing Legendary Edition.
