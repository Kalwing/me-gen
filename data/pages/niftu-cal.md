**Niftu Cal** is a volus in Nos Astra on Illium.

|  | ***“The chances of surviving are… slim.”*** — Spoilers for **Mass Effect 2** follow. |  |
| --- | --- | --- |

Commander Shepard can find him in the Eclipse base as part of Samara's recruitment mission, standing in front of a vending machine. When he notices that there is someone behind him, he slowly turns around and waddles into Shepard, nearly causing the smaller volus to fall over. After regaining his balance, he starts to pace back and forth while prattling on about his newfound, godlike biotic powers and boasting of immortality. By speaking with him, the Commander learns that the mercenaries pumped the volus with biotic\-enhancing drugs (probably Minagen X3 and/or red sand) and that Niftu Cal is a member of Pitne For's trade group.

The Commander can convince the "biotic god" to give up on fighting the mercenaries and find a place to rest. He refuses, and Shepard can tap him on the head, sending him off balance and down into drowsiness. If so, Niftu later appears at the spaceport with his wits restored and thanks Shepard for stopping him earlier.

Otherwise, Shepard can tell him to go right on ahead and charge the mercs. Niftu Cal attempts to fight Captain Wasea by sending a feeble Warp field at her that dissipates just in front of her nose; she responds in kind with a biotic blast of her own, sending the volus flying into some boxes at the other end of the room, never to be seen again.

If Niftu Cal was ignored entirely, he does not make an appearance at the spaceport.

## Trivia

- Niftu Cal's quote, "I am a biotic god!" is referenced twice in Mass Effect 3 Multiplayer: in the description of the Volus Adept class and the "Biotic God" banner (which includes an image of the Volus Adept).
