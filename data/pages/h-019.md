**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Jirayder System /  **Third planet**

## Description

This cold, heavy\-gravity world was once inhabited by a primitive but intelligent species. Based on preliminary scans of their villages, they may have gone extinct shortly after the Scourge appeared in their sky.
