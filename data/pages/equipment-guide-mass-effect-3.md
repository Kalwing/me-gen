This **Equipment Guide** details how important collectibles such as weapons, weapon mods, armor, intel, and Citadel mission items can be acquired during gameplay throughout Mass Effect 3. Many items can be found during missions while others can be purchased from the various merchants on the Citadel and a few other select locations.

**Legendary Edition:**  All content for the Mass Effect trilogy that was previously available only from Downloadable Content (DLC) is now included by default. Legendary Edition features many differences from the original trilogy games, including multiple changes to how certain equipment that is DLC\-exclusive in the original games is obtained, especially in the latter two games. Differences between versions should be marked with orange notation.

## Upgrades by Location/Mission

### Main Missions

*See also: Missions\#Plot Missions 2*
Priority: Mars

1. M\-92 Mantis: Picked up in front of a dead soldier soon after leaving the shuttle. Available to Infiltrators at the start of the mission. If it is not picked up it can be obtained later on from a dead soldier right before entering the tram car.
2. Ariake Technologies Gauntlets: Picked up from a shelf in the room with the security console that opens the first door back outside.
3. M\-4 Shuriken: Same room as previous. Picked up past the console that opens the first door back outside, is located near a med\-kit. Available to Adepts and Engineers at the start of the mission.
4. Kassa Fabrication Chestplate: Picked up outside next to a ladder back up.
5. SMG Scope: Located to the right of the bottom of the stairs in the dark cafeteria before reaching the shootable glass windows.
6. SMG Ultralight Materials: In the room with the Weapon Bench behind some boxes.
7. Sniper Rifle Extended Barrel: Found in the sterilization room on a table; angle the sterilizing lasers so that you can reach this item.
8. Shotgun High Caliber Barrel: Found in the sterilization room.
9. M\-23 Katana: Located near a dead Cerberus Assault Trooper after the sterilization room. Available to Sentinels, Soldiers, and Vanguards at the start of the mission.
10. Sniper Rifle Concentration Mod: In the tram station control room to the left of the "Auto turret controls" button.
11. M\-15 Vindicator: Located at the other end of the tram line at the arrival station, in a closet at the back\-left.
12. Shotgun Shredder Mod: Located near the Vindicator, in a closet at the back\-right of the other end of the tram station.

Priority: Palaven

1. M\-97 Viper: In the middle of the first base on some crates, near a datapad.
2. Pistol Piercing Mod: In a tent neighboring General Corinthus in the same base, in the vicinity of a weapon bench and next to Armax Arsenal shoulder armor.
3. Armax Arsenal Shoulder Guard: Next to the Pistol Piercing Mod above.
4. Assault Rifle Piercing Mod: Past General Corinthus' tent to the other end of where you enter the first base, to the left near the gate behind a barrier next to an M\-27 Scimitar.
5. M\-27 Scimitar: Next to the Assault Rifle Piercing Mod above.
6. Hahne\-Kedar Greaves: In a tent from the opposite end of where you enter the first base.
7. Pistol Magazine Upgrade: Between General Corinthus' tent and a tent with a workbench, Pistol Piercing Mod, Armax Arsenal Shoulder Armor, and a workbench, this mod will be atop of a crate next to a datapad.
8. Assault Rifle Magazine Upgrade: In the airfield after leaving General Corinthus' base for the second time, this mod is located near the center alongside some thermal clips.
9. Sniper Rifle Spare Thermal Clip: After defeating the Brute and wandering down a winding pathway, you will find two living soldiers that a party member will address directly. This mod will be next to one of them.
10. Sniper Rifle Enhanced Scope: In the second camp, head all the way to the left along the wall. Once you reach the other end, look to your right and, in front of a tent next to a medkit, this mod can be found.
**Note:** Make sure to get this item before the end of the fight in the second camp, as the mission will then end automatically and you won't be able to get this item.

Priority: Sur'Kesh

1. Assault Rifle Extended Barrel: After exiting the first cutscene with the female krogan Eve, turn around and head up the stairs. This will be lying on a shelf between the two doors.
2. Shotgun Blade Attachment: After going back outside, you will encounter a group of enemies. In an alcove to the right after encountering these enemies, this mod will be waiting on a shelf on the wall.
3. Shotgun Spare Thermal Clip: After fighting two groups of enemies, you will enter a room where the passageway is blocked by two plumes of fire. Vaulting the barrier to the left and then jumping over the pit to your right, you can see this mod in a corner to your right.
4. Pistol High\-Caliber Barrel: After being shot at by the two enemies standing in an open shuttle, head to your left past the overhang. When you encounter the next group of enemies, hug the left wall, and this mod will be on a shelf.
5. Armax Arsenal Greaves: After standing to be identified by a scanner, you will enter a large room with two floors. Walk straight ahead on the floor you entered from, and this armor will be waiting on top of the first table in front of you.
6. Sentry Interface: In the same room as the Armax Arsenal Greaves, on the same floor, head to the opposite end of the ground floor from where you entered, past the ladder, and head to your left. This armor is lying atop of the bench to your right.
7. M\-13 Raptor: After entering the vicinity of the second quarantine checkpoint, head straight onwards from the entrance until you hit the wall. To your right on a bench, near a corpse, is this sniper rifle.
8. Pistol Scope: After entering the vicinity of the second quarantine checkpoint, hug the wall to the left of where you initially enter. This mod is atop of a desk perpendicular to the left wall.
9. Scorpion: After reaching the second quarantine checkpoint, you will need to find a power terminal. In the same red room as the power terminal, on the opposite wall from the terminal on the ground, you can find this pistol.

Priority: Tuchanka

1. Graal Spike Thrower: As you first exit the crawlers, the weapon is leaning against a railing next to a Krogan Scout.
2. Pistol Magazine Upgrade: To the right of the Graal Spike Thrower, next to the first crawler.

This will put you in a triangular area which your party will recognize as some sort of memorial. Once you've defeated the enemies that attack you, look around the rubble on the left side (as you enter from the covered structure). The mod is on the ground by the rubble.
7. Assault Rifle Extended Barrel: After picking up the scope above, there will be a flight of stairs. At the landing, turn to your left; this mod is on the floor.
8. M\-5 Phalanx: Immediately after debarking the convoy to activate the maw hammers, head up the flight of stairs. This weapon will be to your left on the landing.

1. Assault Rifle Piercing Mod: In the room after meeting Bailey, up the stairs, on the counter to the right next to a med\-kit.

7. M\-358 Talon: Behind the locked door on the left from the entrance to this hallway, unlocked by a terminal in the second room on the right. Under the shelves on the wall.
8. Securitel Helmet: Still in the same hallway area, second room on the left, next to the one that is locked, on a shelf.
9. Pistol High\-Caliber Barrel: On the shelf above the Securitel Helmet.
10. Sniper Rifle Piercing Mod: In the large, open room which exits to the Executor's office (there is a sign above the exit which says 'Executor'), turn to your right at the exit. On the corner of the long desk, you can pick up this mod.

Priority: Geth Dreadnought

1. Shotgun Smart Choke: Once you are inside the dreadnought, in a little section left of the fork at the first door.
2. Arc Pistol: After using the docking controls, Tali'Zorah nar Rayya puts this weapon right in front of you during a cutscene.
3. Sniper Rifle Enhanced Scope: Through first door after picking up the Arc Pistol, next to GARDIAN Defense Data.
4. Archon Visor: Room after corridor with proximity mines and geth, next to Signal Console.
5. Geth Pulse Rifle: On a crate to the right in the room just beyond the room with the Archon Visor, just before the door to the Operations Center.
6. SMG Scope: In Operations Center, next to Operations Center Console (very hard to miss).
7. Assault Rifle Precision Scope: In the Drive Core, where you find Legion (or a Geth VI) it is on your right after the cutscene near a monitor on the lower level.

Priority: Rannoch

1. SMG High Caliber Barrel: At the bottom of the hill at the start of the Geth Base mission, in the area the geth were coming out of.
2. Kassa Fabrication Gauntlets: While climbing the geth base, along the back wall by a ladder.
3. Assault Rifle Piercing Mod: After finding the gauntlets, turn to your right and jump across the gap. You can pick up the mod here.
4. SMG Ultralight Materials: After you go through the door and they talk about a blast shield, down the ramp to the left.
5. Assault Rifle Stability Damper: After the SMG Ultralight Materials, in a cul\-de\-sac to the right at the beginning of the next fight.
6. Geth Plasma Shotgun: After the Assault Rifle Stability Damper, on top of crates at the end of corridor next to an ammo crate.

Priority: Thessia

1. Umbra Visor: In the first courtyard behind Lieutenant Kurin, leaning against a barrier.
2. Shotgun High Caliber Barrel: On balcony above broken shotgun (3000 credits), opposite M\-98 Widow (the ramp leading up to this mod is located in right corner before going up the ramp to the Asari sniper and M\-98 Widow.)
3. M\-98 Widow: About half way through the mission, on the perch the Asari sniper stands on.
4. Sniper Rifle Concentration Mod: Near the M\-98 Widow.
5. Assault Rifle Extended Barrel: After your first fight with the Ravagers, at the top and tucked between the rubble to the right.
6. SMG Heat Sink: On the ground outside the Athame Temple in front of the force field.

Priority: Horizon

1. Pistol Scope: After you enter the first building, go downstairs, and fight a group of Cerberus Troopers, pick up this mod next to a Cerberus Trooper corpse lying next to a desk on top of a short set of stairs.
2. Rosenkov Materials Shoulders: To the left of exit door in the second chamber after draining the pool; it is on top of a bulkhead.
3. M\-12 Locust: Located in the room where you encounter Cannibals and Marauders supported by a barrier engine, after repairing a console, on a table in the raised right side of the lab.
4. Shotgun Blade Attachment: Just past the M\-12 Locust on another table.
5. M\-99 Saber: After the fight with the Brutes and a Banshee, in one of the closed Husk pods. Opening the pods requires overriding the controls at a terminal at the opposite end of the same hall. The terminal is a hologram of the husk pods in a small alcove just before the small set of stairs.

Priority: Cerberus Headquarters

1. M\-37 Falcon: After launching the fighter into the wall, the weapon can be found between the debris.
2. Serrice Council Greaves: Behind you after viewing the first set of three logs (Lazarus Project).
3. Delumcore Overlay: After a conversation with Admiral Hackett about attacking the station and EDI bypassing the second of two doors, turn right and proceed to the end of the corridor. Enter the door on the right, walk around the console and crates in the center to find this pickup.

### Optional Missions

*See also: Missions\#Galaxy at War Missions*
N7: Cerberus Lab

1. Sniper Rifle Enhanced Scope: In the room that the ramp to the left leads to, you can find this mod tucked into the corner on your left after you have entered the room.
2. Alien Medi\-Gel Formula: Located in the middle of the area in an alcove, it can be retrieved from a console.

N7: Cerberus Attack

**Note:** Completing the mission objective will end the mission instantaneously. Make sure to collect all items beforehand.

1. Old Grid Schematics: On the other desk apart from the cannon controls in the control room, you can pick up this item by clicking on a hologram.
2. Armax Arsenal Gauntlets: Near the control room, there are two ramps downwards, one closer to the desk which holds the Old Grid Schematics listed above and which leads outside, and one closer to the actual cannon controls that leads to another room within the same building. Take the ramp to the other room inside the building, and against the wall at the opposite end from the ramp you can pick up this armor.

Grissom Academy: Emergency Evacuation

1. Assault Rifle Stability Damper: After you begin to head towards Orion Hall, take the door to the right at the sign that reads 'Ascension Hall.' In this next room, climb over the barrier to your right and head up the stairs leading to a door. This mod will be near the top.
2. SMG Heat Sink: After you encounter the biotic being confronted by two Cerberus Troopers, jump over the barrier into the next room. This item is located directly across the room from the barrier.
3. M\-22 Eviscerator: After you watch the student being gunned down by Cerberus troops, head into the hallway from which he came (it is on the left from where you entered.) This gun is lying on a couch to your left from entering this hall.
4. M\-96 Mattock: After you watch the student being gunned down, open the door to the right from where you enter. There is a couch in front of you as you enter this new room; this gun will be lying on its right side from where you enter.
5. Mnemonic Visor: After you grab the Mattock, head in a straight line away from where you entered this room until you reach the wall. To your left is a couch, and atop of it is this armor.
6. Assault Rifle Precision Scope: This is lying atop one of the desks upstairs, where the students are, after fighting an Atlas for the first time.
7. M\-96 Mattock (or M\-22 Eviscerator): This and the Sniper Rifle Piercing Mod are contingent on having made a Paragon decision at the end of the Mass Effect 2 DLC, Project Overlord (included in **LE**). After meeting Octavia, a room will be unlocked for you at the end of the hall. This gun will be inside. Might be replaced by an M\-22 Eviscerator on some playthroughs.
8. Sniper Rifle Piercing Mod: This is in the same unlocked room as the Mattock or Eviscerator above, also dependent on the way Project Overload was finished.
9. Biotic Amp Interfaces: After meeting Octavia, the tech student shielding herself and other students from the Cerberus troopers, continue towards the next objective. In the next room, you will find a console on the right near the door. You can download the Biotic Amp Interfaces here.
10. Serrice Council Chestplate: After the room in which you download the Biotic Amp Interfaces, open the door. In the wrecked room you find, head to the left from where you entered. This armor is lying on the couch on the left wall.
11. SMG Magazine Upgrade: This item is located in the room after encountering the unmanned Atlas. Directly to the left of the entrance to this room, this mod is lying on a couch.

Arrae: Ex\-Cerberus Scientists

**Note:** Repairing the last AA gun will immediately take you back to the scientists. Make sure to collect all items beforehand.

1. Assault Rifle Magazine Upgrade: To the right of the work bench found after meeting with the scientists the first time.
2. Shotgun High Caliber Barrel: After a cutscene at the top of the stairs, go forward and to the right up to a door. This mod is behind a crate.
3. Cerberus Turian Poison: From the same stairs, to the left in a corner on top of a box. It looks like a microscope.
4. Kassa Fabrication Greaves: On the right as you enter the med bay.
5. M\-6 Carnifex: After climbing the ladder in the gas\-lit maintenance area after leaving the scientists, this will be on top of a pipe to your left.
6. SMG High Caliber Barrel: Within a crate alcove amongst some thermal clip boxes on the left after the first encounter with Cerberus back outside, directly before the Telemetry Override.
7. Sniper Rifle Piercing Mod: Before climbing either tower against the back wall between the towers.
8. Shotgun Spare Thermal Clip: Climb down the ladder next to the AA gun that is to your right as you approach. On the landing between the first and second ladders, you can pick up this mod.
**Note:** The lower ladder on the right is down\-only; you will be able to see this mod from the ground but you must go up the left ladder, then over the connecting platform and back down.

Attican Traverse: Krogan Team

1. M\-300 Claymore: In the container, in building on left, at the start of the mission.
2. Pistol High\-Caliber Barrel: In the container to the left of the Claymore.
3. SMG High Caliber Barrel: To the right of the weapon bench.
4. Shotgun Shredder Mod: After encountering the first Ravager, to the left next to a dead krogan.
5. Dying Krogan Message: After the roof collapses, hug the left wall. This item can be found behind one of the sections of webbing that can be burnt through.
**Note:** When you pick up this item, it will not register on your screen immediately; however, it will still be included in your journal.
6. Shotgun Spare Thermal Clip: After the roof collapses and you emerge from the narrow cavern into the more brightly\-lit, wider area, you will encounter two groups of enemies. After finishing off the second, head to the left wall. This item will be against the wall.
7. Ariake Technologies Shoulders: After picking up the Shotgun Spare Thermal Clip, you will drop down and need to follow a winding path. Eventually, you will need to jump over a gap in order to continue. Up the path in front of you, you will see what looks like two blue lights. This is a dead krogan; this armor is lying next to him.

7. Serrice Council Shoulder Guard: Near the Disciple.
8. Assault Rifle Precision Scope: After bypassing the door leading to the room with an asari commando corpse, this mod can be found by some containers on the left\-hand side of the room.

N7: Cerberus Abductions

### Medi\-gel Upgrades

#### Capacity

All Medi\-Gel Capacity upgrades are available from the Sirta Supplies terminal at Huerta Memorial Hospital or through the Procurement terminal aboard the Normandy (for a 10% price markup) after certain Priority missions. There are six in total:

- **Medi\-Gel Capacity** \- Purchasable after Priority: Mars.
- **Medi\-Gel Capacity I** \- Purchasable after Priority: Palaven.
- **Medi\-Gel Capacity II** \- Purchasable after Priority: Sur'Kesh.
- **Medi\-Gel Capacity III** \- Purchasable after Priority: Tuchanka.
- **Medi\-Gel Capacity IV** \- Purchasable after Priority: The Citadel II.
- **Medi\-Gel Capacity V** \- Granted if Dr. Chloe Michel is recruited and Citadel: Medical Supplies is completed with Charm or Intimidate. Can carry over to subsequent playthroughs with the same character.

#### Efficiency

- **Efficiency Increase** \- Granted if Dr. Karin Chakwas is recruited and Citadel: Medical Supplies is completed with Charm or Intimidate. This upgrade doesn't carry over to subsequent playthroughs with the same character.
