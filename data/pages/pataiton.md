**Location:** Milky Way / Maroon Sea / Vostok System /  **Fourth planet**
**Prerequisite:** Feros: Geth Attack (Mass Effect)

## Description

A rather small hydrogen\-helium gas giant, **Pataiton**'s atmosphere contains large quantities of chlorine.
