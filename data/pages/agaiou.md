**Prerequisite:** Priority: The Citadel II (Mass Effect 3\)

**Agaiou** is a binary star system. Its worlds are mere scientific curiosities: one used to have a civilization 2\.7 million years ago, and the other failed to keep a stable orbit and is now being ejected from the system.
