**Prerequisite:** Priority: Thessia (Mass Effect 3\)

The **Kepler Verge** lies in the Attican Traverse. Humans have staked some colonial, mining, and terraforming claims, although some of those ventures prove too cost\-ineffective for the time being.

## Trivia

- This cluster is probably named for the German astronomer and mathematician Johannes Kepler. The systems within are named after other great human astronomers.
