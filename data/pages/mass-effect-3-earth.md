**Mass Effect 3: Earth** is a DLC pack for Mass Effect 3. It was released on July 17, 2012 on Xbox 360 and PC worldwide. For Playstation 3, it was released on July 17, 2012 in North America, and on July 18, 2012 in Europe. The Earth pack is available at no additional cost. It is included with copies of Mass Effect 3: Special Edition for Wii U.

## Content

- Three maps:
	- Firebase Rio
	- Firebase Vancouver
	- Firebase London
- Six multiplayer characters:
	- N7 Fury Adept
	- N7 Destroyer Soldier
	- N7 Demolisher Engineer
	- N7 Paladin Sentinel
	- N7 Shadow Infiltrator
	- N7 Slayer Vanguard
- Three weapons:
	- Acolyte Pistol
	- N7 Piranha Shotgun
	- N7 Typhoon Assault Rifle
- Five1 Weapon Mods:
	- Assault Rifle Omni\-Blade
	- Pistol Cranial Trauma System
	- Pistol Ultralight Materials
	- SMG High\-Velocity Barrel
	- SMG Recoil System
- Eleven gear upgrades
- One match objective:
	- Teams must protect and escort a Combat Drone to a designated extraction zone. This objective randomly occurs during waves 3, 6, and 10\.
- One new difficulty setting:
	- On Platinum difficulty, the selected enemy faction will appear at full force immediately. As the match progresses, enemies from other factions will begin appearing.

1 It was initially reported that twelve weapon mods would be included in Earth, however, seven of them were removed due to balance issues. BioWare released the missing mods in a patch released alongside Mass Effect 3: Retaliation.
