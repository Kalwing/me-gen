**Location:** Milky Way / Omega Nebula / Sahrabarik System /  **Third planet**
## Description

If it were closer to Sahrabarik, **Bindur** would have an atmosphere of carbon dioxide and ethane. In the deep cold of the outer solar system, however, both elements have long since frozen to the ground.
