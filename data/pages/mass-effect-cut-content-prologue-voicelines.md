## nor10\_ambient\_03

**Note:**  this dialogue was retranscribed based on the English audio bank Mass Effect Legendary Edition\\Game\\ME1\\BioGame\\Content\\Packages\\ISACT\\nor10\_ambient\_03\.isb. The reconstructed order of the voicelines may not be fully accurate.

**Context:** Prologue: Find the Beacon: this scene would occur on the SSV Normandy's crew deck, in the cantina, before the mission on Eden Prime begins. Animation files show this scene has been fully choreographed, and depicted Kaidan biotically throwing a cup at Jenkins. Commentary was added here based on those animations.

**Speakers**:

- Kaidan Alenko
- Dr. Karin Chakwas
- Richard L. Jenkins

---

**Jenkins**: Come on, Lieutenant! I missed your demonstration, let me see.

**Doctor Chakwas**: Leave him alone, Jenkins.

**Jenkins**: The lieutenant doesn't mind. Right, LT?

**Kaidan**: I'm a little tired, Jenkins.

**Jenkins**: Ah come on, man! The whole crew's talking about it, let me see! Do something easy, one of the chairs or something.

**Kaidan**: *\[sigh]* Alright.

*(Kaidan biotically hurls his drinking cup at Jenkins, who recoils)*

**Jenkins**: Oof!

**Kaidan**: How was that, Jenkins? You want to see it again?

**Jenkins**: Erm, no... No, I'm good, Lieutenant, I... better head down below.

*(Jenkins leaves the cantina)*

**Doctor Chakwas**: That was a little extreme. Are you feeling alright, Lieutenant?

**Kaidan**: Just a slight headache. Between Jenkins and Joker, there's always someone yapping in my ear.

**Doctor Chakwas**: It's not their fault and you know it. Those damned biotic implants...

**Kaidan**: Don't worry about it, I'm okay. Really.

Data

The above would trigger *LE1\.CH0\_Eden\_Prime.Normandy\_Conversation.**Set\_Jenkins\_Tossed\_True**.*
