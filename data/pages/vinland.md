**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Eriksson System /  **First planet**

## Description

**Vinland** is a small rocky world with a "pressure\-cooker" atmosphere composed of argon and carbon dioxide. Its valuable cobalt deposits would require robotic mining due to volatile surface gases.

## Anomaly

### Crater

DATA:

Hypervelocity impact crater

Rim diameter: 496 km

Depth: Approx. 3\.7 km

Ejecta: Silicates, mineral deposits, trace amounts of element zero

ANALYSIS:

A crater caused by the impact of heavy debris, possibly from the Scourge. The meteorite still contains valuable resources.

Awards \+270 XP when scanned.

## Trivia

The planet is named after the North American region of the same name, which was visited by Lief Eriksson, the system's namesake.
