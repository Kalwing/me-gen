**Location:** Milky Way / Armstrong Nebula / Hong System /  **Fifth planet**

## Description

**Treagir** is a tiny "ice dwarf", with a trace atmosphere of xenon and krypton. The frozen surface is mainly composed of water ice and ammonia. Cryovolcanic processes are gradually repaving the surface with sheets of fresh ice.
