**Mass Effect: Foundation** is a comic series written by Mac Walters. It is the fifth Mass Effect comic series produced, with 13 issues released as opposed to the usual 4\. The first issue was released on July 24, 2013\. The series has been stated to have a connection to the Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC pack for Mass Effect 3, and issue 5 ties directly into Mass Effect 2.

Thus far three paperback volumes have been released. Volume 1 collects issues 1\-4, volume 2 collects issues 5\-8, and volume 3 collects issues 9\-13\. Volume 1 was released on February 5, 2014, Volume 2 was released on June 4, 2014, and Volume 3 was released on November 5, 2014\.
