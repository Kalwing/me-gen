## Description

**Nodacrux** is a verdant world with abundant water, temperate climate, a thick oxygen\-nitrogen atmosphere and a rich ecosystem. It would seem to be perfect for life. The relatively high percentage of oxygen makes humans feel energized and alive, though it has also allowed insect analogues to grow to frightful sizes.

Unfortunately, Nodacrux is a case of "almost but not quite." Thunderstorms are as common as on Earth, but in Nodacrux's thicker, oxygen\-rich atmosphere, they are deafening and spark constant wildfires. More damning, however, are the large and ubiquitous tufts of pollen that float in the high\-pressure air. In humans and other oxygen\-breathing species, they cause severe or lethal allergic reactions.

## Properties

The planet has a very rugged mountain complex that is difficult to navigate even with the Mako's climbing abilities. Minerals and other points of interest are usually nestled either on top of sheer cliffs or surrounded by them with only one accessible path that isn't always obvious from looking at the map.

Points of Interest 3, 4, and 5 are easily reachable from the landing zone. The western mineral deposit can be reached only after dropping off steep cliffs which cannot then easily be re\-crossed, so returning to orbit after conducting the survey is recommended. The Exogeni facility is fairly easy to reach via a gently sloping, winding path from the south. The best path to Points of Interest 2 and 6 is via a pass north of the facility.

The southern mineral deposit cannot be reached from the north; instead, approach from the southeast.

## Trivia

- The name Nodacrux may be derived from two Latin roots: *crux*, meaning "cross" as well as "pain", "torture", "misery"; and *nodus*, meaning "knot" or "knob".
