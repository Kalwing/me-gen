**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Vaar System /  **Second planet**

## Description

**Margoth**'s extraordinarily dangerous surface is of less interest than its ring system. Its inner ring is primarily dust and rock; the outer ring is composed of large chunks of water ice.
