**Location:** Milky Way / Athena Nebula / Tomaros System /  **Fourth planet**
**Prerequisite:** Priority: The Citadel II (Mass Effect 3\)

## Description

The ice giant **Beness** is the outermost gas giant of the Tomaros system. It likely formed much closer to the system's star and migrated outward over time. A debris field of scrap metal near the planet suggests Reapers have been here.
