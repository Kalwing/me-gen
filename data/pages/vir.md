**Location:** Milky Way / Minos Wasteland / Fortis System /  **First planet**
**Prerequisite:** Star Chart from Baria Frontiers store (Mass Effect 2\)

**Prerequisite:** Priority: Sur'Kesh (Mass Effect 3\)

## Description

A pressure\-cooker planet with a thick, nitrogen\-heavy atmosphere, **Vir** is largely ignored by the galactic community. Probes have revealed a crust of nickel and scorched carbon, both of which can be found in abundance elsewhere, at far lesser temperatures.

## Trivia

- Vir in Latin simply means "man", but in certain contexts it can be defined as "hero" or "soldier". The word virtue is also derived from vir.
