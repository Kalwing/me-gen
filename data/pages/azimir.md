**Location:** Milky Way / Kite's Nest / Vular System /  **Second planet**
**Prerequisite:** Aria: Blue Suns (Mass Effect 3\)

## Description

**Azimir** is a hydrogen\-helium gas giant whose gravitational pull perturbs the orbit of two nearby asteroid fields, adding momentum so that asteroids shatter during collisions rather than accrete into planets.

Debris from a great battle can be seen here. Batarian naval ships, merchant ships, and a few Reaper destroyers have been pounded into wreckage. It appears that the batarian navy tried to defend the helium\-3 refueling stations, giving civilian ships time to escape the system.
