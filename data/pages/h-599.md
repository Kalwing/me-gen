**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Zheng He System /  **Second planet**

## Description

With its distinctive ring, **H\-599** was used as a rendezvous point during the Nexus' early attempts at exploration. Pilots still use the term "a 99 run" for a short journey with numerous hazards.
