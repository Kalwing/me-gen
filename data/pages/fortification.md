**Fortification** is a power in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3's single\-player and multiplayer modes.

## Mass Effect 2

 A brief but massive boost to your armor.

### Power Ranks

- **Rank 1** \- Reinforces armor with a non\-Newtonian fluid that hardens when struck, providing a brief, huge bonus.
	- **Recharge Time**: 12\.00 seconds
	- **Duration**: 60\.00 seconds
	- **Armor Bonus**: \+25\.00% of maximum shields

- **Rank 2** \- Reinforces armor with a non\-Newtonian fluid that hardens when struck, providing a brief, huge bonus.
	- **Recharge Time**: 12\.00 seconds
	- **Duration**: 60\.00 seconds
	- **Armor Bonus**: \+50\.00% of maximum shields

- **Rank 3** \- Reinforces armor with a non\-Newtonian fluid that hardens when struck, providing a brief, huge bonus.
	- **Recharge Time**: 12\.00 seconds
	- **Duration**: 60\.00 seconds
	- **Armor Bonus**: \+75\.00% of maximum shields

**Rank 4**
Evolves Fortification into one of the following options:

#### *Improved Fortification*

- The armor is more resilient and reacts better to multiple hits, increasing its duration.
	- **Recharge Time**: 12\.00 seconds
	- **Duration**: 180\.00 seconds
	- **Armor Bonus**: \+75\.00% of maximum shields

#### *Heavy Fortification*

- By altering the chemistry of the prime ingredients of the armor, it now absorbs even more damage.
	- **Recharge Time**: 12\.00 seconds
	- **Duration**: 60\.00 seconds
	- **Armor Bonus**: \+100\.00% of maximum shields

### Player Notes

- Despite the in\-game wording, when used by Shepard as a bonus power, Fortification boosts maximum shield values and also restores shields to their new maximum upon activation. It has no effect on health.
- Fortification is functionally the same as Barrier and Geth Shield Boost, and differs only slightly from the *Improved* evolution of the latter. Like the other two powers, Fortification can be activated without interrupting Shepard when storming.
- Unlike Barrier (a biotic power) or Geth Shield Boost (a tech power), Fortification is a "combat" power (like Concussive Shot) that doesn't fall into either category, and therefore does not receive any enhancement from biotic or tech upgrades. As it possesses no known unique properties or mechanics when compared with Barrier, this makes Fortification clearly an inferior choice for Shepard if both powers are available options.
- Unlocking Grunt's Fortification (on completion of Grunt: Rite of Passage) can create the conundrum that he will constantly reactivate it during battles even if Squad Power Use is disabled, making him less likely to use his other powers or be able to use them on demand. The only way to have a loyal Grunt but avoid the automatic use of Fortification is to reset his powers using the Advanced Training terminal at the Shadow Broker's Base (requires either Lair of the Shadow Broker DLC or Legendary Edition) and invest no points in Fortification.

### Availability

- Shepard via Advanced Training
- Grunt (Loyalty Power)

## Mass Effect 3

### Power Ranks

#### Rank 1: Fortification

Reinforce armor using protective Foucault currents. Purge the current and send its charge to your gauntlets for increased melee damage.

Slows power use by 50%.

### Player Notes

Mechanics
- As with Tech Armor, the damage protection from Fortification applies to both health and shields, and remains in effect even when shields break. The damage protection can stack with Tech Armor or other powers like Reave. The damage protection ends when Fortification is purged, and only returns when it is reactivated.
- The power use penalty at rank 1 Fortification is **not** 50% overall reduction to power recharge speed, but a reduction of the Power Recharge Speed bonus visible in the weapon loadout screen. In other words, it functions like a weapon weight penalty.
	- If you have \+200% Power Recharge Speed when looking at the weapons screen, activating rank 1 Fortification reduces that Power Recharge Speed to \+150%.
	- The power speed penalty reduction of 30% at rank 6 Fortification reduces the penalty down to 20%. E.g. if you have a \+200% Power Recharge Speed bonus in the weapon screen, activating rank 6 Fortification only reduces the bonus to \+180%.
- Fortification can be activated instantly while moving, and will not cause the character to stop and perform any activation animations. Similarly, de\-activating Fortification is also instant.
- The rank 5 Power Synergy evolution improves the damage of *all* powers, not just tech powers like the similar evolution of Defense Matrix. However, this does *not* include ammo power damage, with the exception of the Explosive Burst evolution of Incendiary Ammo (single\-player only).
	- Fortification's Power Synergy evolution provides a greater boost to powers compared to the similar evolutions of Defense Matrix and Barrier. Also, it is the only one of the three that improves the damage of Explosive Burst Incendiary Ammo.

Strategies
- In order to gain/maintain the extra melee damage, one must purge their Fortification every 20 seconds. For the Krogan Soldier and to some degree the Geth Soldier, this has little effect if done when your shields are at max, as you can still withstand a fair amount of abuse even without the extra protection. For the Geth Soldier this provides a much more effective area melee, and for Krogan Soldiers it can boost their melee damage to an even higher level.
	- Since Fortification's cooldown is fairly quick even with a lot of extra weight, one can also take a heavy loadout and still be fairly effective, or use light weapons for near\-instantaneous cooldowns. A lower cooldown means you can regain your protection faster, and for Krogan Soldiers going for a melee build, this can be a life saver.
- Fortification synergizes well with Tech Armor, as when combined they can provide up to 90% increase in damage resistance, and up to 60% increase in power damage. While equipping both simultaneously sounds hard on power recharge times, they can still be kept low (around 2\-4 seconds, depending on the power) by wearing specific armors and by carrying only light weapons. Intel Terminal upgrades and individual recharge speed upgrades for each power can also be used to mitigate the issue.
	- Terminus Armor can further boost survivability with its massive \+30% shield increase, and Cerberus Spirit Armor can be used to further boost both power damage and recharge speeds (and comes with a minor shield boost as well).
