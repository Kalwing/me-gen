**Location:** Milky Way / Ismar Frontier / Faia System /  **First planet**
**Prerequisite:** Dossier: The Veteran (Mass Effect 2\)

## Description

**Imaen** is a small, cratered rock. Its crust contains various light metals, though none in any concentration worth the trouble of mining. While the rest of the Faia system has seen extensive industrialization, Imaen lies fallow.
