**Mass Effect: Discovery** is a comic series set around the events of Mass Effect: Andromeda and following the adventures of turian Tiran Kandros during his infiltration of the Andromeda Initiative. Four issues long, the first installment was released on May 24, 2017 and the last on October 25, 2017\. All issues have been collected into trade paperback, released on January 17, 2018\.

Four variant covers were made for the issues, done in classic sci\-fi pulp style by artist Kate Niemczyk and resident colorist Michael Atiyeh.
