**Armiger Legion** is a passive class power in Mass Effect 3's multiplayer mode.

## Power Ranks

### Rank 1: Armiger Legion

The turian's \[sic] lethal 26th Armiger Legion is a respected and feared frontline assault squad.

More weapon damage.
Greater stability and weapon control.
More strength.

- **Weapon Stability Bonus:** 20%
- **Weight Capacity Bonus:** 15
