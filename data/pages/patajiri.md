**Location:** Milky Way / Voyager Cluster / Yangtze System /  **Fifth planet**
**Prerequisite:** UNC: Missing Marines (Mass Effect)

## Description

**Patajiri** has a thin atmosphere composed of neon and chlorine. Its frozen surface is mainly composed of silica with deposits of copper. During the long, cold night, the chlorine in the atmosphere falls to the ground in the form of frost.
