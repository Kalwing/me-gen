**Location:** Milky Way / Caleston Rift / Talava System /  **First planet**
**Prerequisite:** Collector Ship (mission) or Project Firewalker: Rosalie Lost and Horizon (mission) (Mass Effect 2\)

## Description

A large rock planet orbiting an F\-class star, **Aitarus** is pummeled by radiation, heavy gravity, and tectonic activity. Its crust is mostly silicates and of little value. Travel is not advised.
