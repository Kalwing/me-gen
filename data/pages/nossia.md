**Location:** Milky Way / Athena Nebula / Ialessa System /  **Fifth planet**
**Prerequisite:** Priority: The Citadel II (Mass Effect 3\)

## Description

Scans show little of value on **Nossia**, aside from sizable copper deposits near the equator. The only landmark of note is the wreck of a krogan freighter, a remnant of attacks on the system during the latter half of the Krogan Rebellions.
