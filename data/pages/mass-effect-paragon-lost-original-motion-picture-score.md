The **Mass Effect: Paragon Lost Original Motion Picture Score** was made available to the public on November 21, 2012\. 26 of its 27 tracks were composed by Joshua Mosley and David Kates, and the 27th is a song by The Anix. The soundtrack is currently available on iTunes. and Amazon.
