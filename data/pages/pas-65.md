**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Tafeno System /  **First planet**

## Description

**Pas\-65** is a scorching desert that is nonetheless surprisingly cool given the output and nearness of the Tafeno sun. The temperature is a product of Pas\-65's highly reflective metal deposits.
