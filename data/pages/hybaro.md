**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Ratul System /  **Third planet**

## Description

**Hybaro** has adapted bizarrely to the Scourge. For about a third of its orbital period, the Scourge's effects permit plant life to flourish; at all other times, Hybaro boils in the Ratul sun.
