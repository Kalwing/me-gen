**Mass Effect 2 Character Concepts** is an internal document which contains concepts from the development team for the squadmates of Mass Effect 2. The following is a partial transcription of this document, according to what has been shown by writer Mac Walters. A number of these concepts were further refined or merged into final squadmates, while other ideas were dropped entirely. In addition, this page also contains a retranscription of the beginning of another document called "Acquisition \& Loyalty Missions". Note that parts of these documents are missing; this transcription was made on the basis of the segments shown in the video linked in references.

## Party Member Templates

### 1\. Cerberus Superwoman

Female human, 35

**Enabling Mission**

Ordered to help the player by Cerberus' head — the enigmatic Illusive Man. She is his protégé, and her loyalty can be secured by helping her learn more about him.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

The product of extensive genetic engineering by a wealthy couple, she enjoys intelligence, reflexes, strength, and beauty superior to other humans. As the literal "best daughter money can buy," she is aristocratic and superior. She gets what she wants, and right now she wants Shepard.

### 2\. Cerberus Bodyguard

Male human, 30

**Enabling Mission**

Assigned by Cerberus to help the player. Years ago, he was involved with Superwoman. She's over it, he's not — secure his loyalty by helping him forget her, or getting them back together.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

Taciturn and resolute, a good\-hearted man with strong personal morals. He is soft\-spoken and polite, almost "southron" in his manners. A veteran soldier, he is the classic "big brother" platoon sergeant — basically Corporal Hicks from *Aliens*.

### 3\. The Geth Frankenstein

Geth, ageless and gender\-neutral

**Enabling Mission**

The player recovers the memory core of a damaged geth "boss" creature, opening a collection quest. As the player clears UCW geth bases, he collects parts to rebuild it.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

Geth form a networked intelligence. This individual is sophisticated enough to be intelligent without a network, but it still faces a terrifying silence in its mind. To cope, it partitions its personality into three unique personalities.

### 3a. The Lost Hologram

Male Prothean\-tech hologram, partially disabled, memory banks failing, set adrift in space.

**Enabling Mission**

During one of the Space Combat sections, we meet an "energy being": a floating version of the Prothean hologram who. \[sic] He has become detached from his docking unit; this scrambled his memory circuits, disrupted his physical form, and began a slow and inevitable process of decay. The Hologram's only hope is to find an operational docking unit so that he can stabilize himself.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

Back when the Protheans were still around, the holograms were in the middle of a civil rights movement to be treated as equals. He's still touchy about being referred to as anything less than a sentient equal, and it can provide for humor in some really odd situations. Could eventually be restored into the true appearance and form of a Prothean.

### 3b. The Design\-A\-Krogan

Male krogan, 0

**Enabling Mission**

Cerberus gives the player a krogan breeding chamber. With a little genetic material from a suitable "parent" (Wrex is a good choice if he's not dead), the player gets an adult krogan to raise as Paragon or Renegade.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

He's a massive killing machine with the intellectual and moral development of a child. His lack of social etiquette often has him saying the wrong thing and the wrong time. \[sic]

### 3c. The Tank

A deactivated 75 year old robot.

**Enabling Mission**

Shepard will have to buy the Tank. This will make money in the game interesting and encourage scavenging (more exploration). The Player will also have to scavenge or buy a large enough power cell for the robot to function.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

It's a big robot. Who doesn't love that? Plus, the robot could have illegal AI which would make traveling back to Citadel space interesting (you're harboring an illegal AI). We could also get into interesting themes of slavery \- you do own the robot.

### 5\. The Salarian Avenger

Male salarian, 11 (human equivalent: 28\)

**Enabling Mission**

He's hunted by Kirrahe's squad (either led by Kirrahe or by his second in command) for the murder of a salarian politician. That politician held secret information that the player needs, and the Avenger now has. The player must convince Kirrahe's team that keeping him alive is more beneficial.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

He has spent his entire life training to become the perfect assassin, to the abandonment of friends, family and other personal connections. A descendent \[sic] of the League of One, he wants nothing more than revenge for their eradication.

### 6\. Tali

Quarian, female, 22ish

**Enabling Mission**

Tali would be an in with the Quarians \[sic] for the jump drive. Her missions could involve restoring her reputation with her people.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

Tali is one of the few who believes in Shepard throughout his whole ordeal. She has finished her pilgrimage, but her belief in Shepard and the reapers \[sic] has made her a bit of a pariah with many of her people. She admits her infatuation with Shepard, saying that she never pursued it in ME 1 due to the presence of Ashley or Liara (or Kaidan if Tali swings both ways). Many players would have immediate buy in with her character since she is familiar from ME 1\.

### 7\. The Quarian Rogue

Male quarian, 25

**Enabling Mission**

Roguish and charming, he's is constantly getting himself in and out of trouble, concocting crazy schemes that never quite work out as planned. His tech abilities, like many of his kind, are extraordinary.

### 7a. The Cordial Smuggler

Any race, male or female, 30ish years old.

**Enabling Mission**

After hearing rumors of Shepard's mission, he approaches saying he has needed contact information. He leads the player through the dark underbelly of black market society.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

This charismatic rascal has all the connections. He loves all people, seeing the good side even in the bad guys, and as such has friends and contacts in all organizations. He'll follow a paragon or renegade Shepard equally with a smile. He's got the free spirit of Han Solo with naïve enthusiasm of Luke Skywalker. He would be a good source of humor since he'll be jovial in the worst of situations and could play off more rugged Wrex\-type characters.

### 8\. The Spacejacker

Female human, 25

**Enabling Mission**

She and the player are trying to steal the same thing. The player wins, but it becomes clear that she let the player "win"... this time. Alternately, she could try to steal the LEVIATHAN.

### 11\. The Batarian Gearhead

Male batarian, 27

**Enabling Mission**

The player learns the location of an infamous batarian terrorist — an expert in technology, hacking, etc. When they find him they discover he isn't actually a terrorist, and bears no ill will towards humanity.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

He's basically a nerd — meek, socially awkward and uncomfortable in combat and confrontation. He is, however, incredible with his tech skills. He wants to live a quiet, ordinary life, but is so brilliant he will never be seen as normal or ordinary.

### 12\. Fugitive Rebel

Male salarian; (Human or Quarian could work too); 36

**Enabling Mission**

The player needs to track down the leader of a rebel movement known for infiltrating impregnable enemy territory with ease. His rebellion was crushed, and since then he has been living off\-the\-grid, successfully evading dozens of attempts to capture or kill him. The player must race against vicious bounty hunters as they follow a trail of underground contacts scattered across several star systems.

**Why is he cool, amusing or interesting?**

He's an extraordinary guerrilla fighter, resourceful and adaptable, able to hack systems and rig technology with MacGyver\-like ease. He controls a shadowy network of contacts, giving him access to important information and exotic technologies. He's still passionate about his cause, was personally involved in the tragic injustice that sparked his rebellion, and gives the player an opportunity to finish his fight.

His grand cause should be something where a bunch of oppressed underdogs take on a powerful government or corporation. For example, he may have united several warring colonies in an attempt to shut down the arms company that had triggered the war and was supplying all sides.

### 13\. The Mercenary

Male human, 30

**Enabling Mission**

He's been in the Terminus Systems for years, selling his expertise to the highest bidder. Cerberus suggests him as a local guide. If the player doesn't keep his loyalty up he will sell the player out to the COLLECTORS for a nice profit.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

He's the gruff, tattooed soldier\-of\-fortune who's seen and done it all. He has hundreds of war stories and carries enough firepower to take out a city block. He's gruff, crude, and says what he thinks, consequences be damned.

### 14\. The Crusader

Male NEW ALIEN, 35

**Enabling Mission**

The player negotiates to obtain a siege weapon built by the NEW ALIENS, which is needed to break into PLANET FORTRESS. They will provide one so long as the player accepts an observer who will ensure the weapon is used only for moral purposes.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

An alien religious or "Teutonic Knight," allowing an element of Firefly's Shepherd Book; a spiritual man living among heathens. He is moody, cynical, and prefers solitude, but possesses unmistakable intelligence and a passionate nature. He must be presented as darkly charismatic and sexually charged despite his "Mr. Rochester" exterior — a strong man that romantic souls want to learn more about.

### 15\. The Fallen Paragon

Any race, male or female, 40ish years old

**Enabling Mission**

Shepard needs a medical expert and hears about a genius doctor who inexplicably left his position at a top\-secret genetics lab. Shepard tracks down the doctor to find him drinking himself to death in a forgotten hive of scum and villainy. The missions would involve getting the paragon past the issues he is having by righting the wrong or reaping revenge.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

He's a doctor who once believed in the cause, but life has beaten him down so that he now questions the good of humanity. Shepard can either foster his hope and bring him back to the light, or push him deeper down his new found renegade path.

### 16\. The Mad Bomber

Shepard needs a demolitions expert, and he hears the stories of a demolition\-savant who's just crazy enough to join in on a suicidal mission. The demolitionist needs the state of the art technology to do the job right, so Shepard needs to break into high security black ops bases.

He's a guy who is obviously a bit off, bordering on suicidal crazy. He could be a good source of humor, or a good character study as in Mel Gibson in the original Lethal Weapon.

## Detailed Character Descriptions

### Cerberus Female

**Character Hook**

She was the product of extensive genetic engineering by a wealthy couple, and enjoys strength, reflexes, intelligence, and beauty superior to other humans.

At the start of the game she is assigned to help Shepard. She's a young woman (35\). At some point in the past, she took CERBERUS MALE as a lover. She's over it, he isn't.

Her genetic advantages and intense personality helped her rise quickly in Cerberus. She is a protégé of ILLUSIVE MAN, who is grooming her to replace him. She's conscious of this. She wants the job, but she also knows that she'll be dropped if she fails to perform.

**Character Details**

A manipulative predator. She is cool, haughty, and aristocratic. When she finds a man interesting, she will lure him in with a raised brow and a small smile. She seduces him with her personality and sensuality, makes a plaything of him, and moves on when bored. She's taken many lovers, but ultimately loves only herself. People only have use to her so long as they are interesting.

Her tendency to treat people as toys is an extension of her upbringing. As the literal "best daughter money could buy," she was treated as a useful investment or vanity trophy.

**Story Role**

She serves as an intelligence analyst in Cerberus. You need to know where someone is? She can find out. You need to acquire a fusion bomb? She knows people.

**Relationship**

Throughout the game she is searching for files with information about ILLUSIVE MAN. If you help her, you find conflicting urban legends.

She pursues male Shepard aggressively. If you're willing, she will take him to bed very quickly, but (if her loyalty is low) get bored with him just as fast. She is also toying with CERBERUS MALE on the side. If her loyalty is continues \[sic] to grow (as ascending series of checks), she will confide more in Shepard.

It is possible to open her heart. While still driven to succeed ILLUSIVE MAN (her career is more important to her than any lover), she will be easier on the crew, and less likely to treat partners (including poor CERBERUS MALE) as disposable.

### Cerberus Male

**Character Hook**

CERBERUS MALE is a seasoned Alliance soldier who served on the frontier for nearly a decade . \[sic] He was at Eden Prime, where he fought a geth ambusher hand\-to\-hand... and won. Few in his unit did. Since then, he's avoided VIs and cybernetics.

Cerberus was impressed by his performance (and his new conviction that humanity is too vulnerable). He worked with CERBERUS FEMALE for some time, the brawn to her brains. She took him as one of her many lovers. He felt that they had bonded, but she saw him as fun hunk of man\-meat. She fooled around on the side, and after they were reassigned forgot him entirely. He still has a picture of her up in his quarters. He is 28 — younger than Shepard.

**Character Details**

Taciturn and resolute, a good\-hearted man with strong personal morals. He is soft\-spoken and polite, almost "southron" in his manners. He keeps his emotions to himself.

He watches over the crew of LEVIATHAN with a light touch, but near\-omniscience. Little occurs without his knowledge. Wlien it comes to the safety of the crew, it goes from God, to Shepard, to him. The crew affectionately call him "Old Man."

**Story Role**

At the start of the game, he's your primary fire support, a role similar to Ashley's.

**Relationship**

He's concerned that he may have done wrong in previous missions alongside CERBERUS FEMALE — that he was blinded by his devotion to her. He will talk about some of the things they did together. If his loyalty to Shepard grows stronger than his loyalty to Cerberus, the player can tell him whether to stay or go.

Working alongside CERBERUS FEMALE again has rekindled the old spark. CERBERUS FEMALE begins seducing him again. Shepard can intervene, or let her have her way. She will eventually tire and leave him — possibly preferring her relationship with Shepard.

If the player doesn't intervene, and has not persuaded CERBERUS FEMALE to care more about others, CERBERUS MALE will attempt suicide in his quarters. If his loyalty is high and the player has good persuades, he can be talked off the ledge.

### Salarian Assassin \- Mac

**Character Hook**

Much like Inigo Montoya in the Princess Bride (You killed my father, prepare to die!), SALARIAN is obsessed with avenging those responsible for the deaths of his revered ancestors.

**Character Details**

A descendent \[sic] of the League of One, he wants nothing more than revenge for their eradication. Driven by his obsession, he has spent his entire life training to become the perfect assassin, to the abandonment of friends, family and other personal connections. His single\-mindedness often reveals itself in odd ways (i.e. commenting on things relevant to his search, but which seem completely unrelated to the situation at hand).

**Story Role**

When Shepard finds SALARIAN, he's being hunted by Kirrahe's former squad (either led by Kirrahe or by his second in command). He/She is wanted for the murder of a high ranking salarian politician. It turns out, that politician held secret information (about PLANET FORTRESS??) that the player needs. After reaching SALARIAN, the player must convince Kirrahe's team that keeping SALARIAN alive is more beneficial (stand down), or they must be defeated. Either way, SALARIAN agrees to come with you after you save his/her life, and turns over the information he took from the politician.

**Relationship**

A great person to have around when you need a sniper; SALARIAN is one of the best in the galaxy, as long as you can keep him. SALARIAN will follow the player willingly for a time, hoping to uncover clues in places he has yet to search. However, if the player cannot get SALARIAN's loyalty high enough, there may come a time when he chooses his path over Shepard's (a chance to kill one of his prey that can't be missed). It doesn't need to end in them leaving the party, but it could cause the player some "difficulties" on a mission.

### Quarian Infiltrator \- Mac

**Character Hook**

He's Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid; rolled together in to \[sic] one boisterous, but highly capable quarian infiltrator.

**Character Details**

Roguish and charming QUARIAN is constantly getting himself in and out of trouble, often concocting crazy schemes that never quite work out as planned. His tech abilities, like many of his kind, are extraordinary, but he's also great in a fight. Though his "way with words" often seems a bit strange, it usually comes off as endearing (especially to his friends). He enjoys a good fight and he often finds one, despite protestations that he was innocent of any wrong doing.

**Story Role**

When the player finds QUARIAN he's on OMEGA in the middle of a gun battle between the local authorities and mercenary bounty hunters; and they're all after him. Despite seeming like he's a bit vulnerable, he handles himself well and dispatches several of the bounty\-hunters on his own before being rescued by the player. QUARIAN came to OMEGA seeking answers regarding the geth, hoping to prove to his people that it's safe to return to their original home planet now that the geth are much less of a threat. But his curiosity and impish behavior left him with a bounty on his head a warrant for his arrest. \[sic]

**Relationship**

Romance interest for the straight female PC. At first his advances on the player are typical flirts that he might have with any female. But when the player reciprocates it piques his interest. He admits he's intrigued by the player, but wonders if the player is only interested in him because he's different. As long as the player doesn't go totally renegade, the romance remains viable. If the player crosses the line too many times, he'll call it off.

### Young Thief \- Mac

**Character Hook**

A ship\-stealing rogue, she's the typical thief with the heart of gold. Cute and friendly, YOUNG THIEF has an uncanny knack for never getting caught. She'll steal your heart, and then your ship!

**Character Description**

YOUNG THIEF is the quintessential thief/hacker. She doesn't see anything wrong with stealing from the rich. Her skills and success give her a confidence, and she's very chatty. Generally positive in her outlook, she isn't afraid to speak her mind... though sometimes she can be a bit naïve, seeing complex moral situations in simple "good" and "evil" lights. Lithe and small, she can get in to and out of almost any tight spot. And when her body fails her, her brilliant "hacking" skills take over. Though she's beholden to know one, she respects the player immediately and follows out of loyalty and curiosity.

**Story Role**

When the player meets YOUNG THIEF they are trying to steal the same thing. The player eventually wins, but it becomes clear that she let the player "win"... this time.

She's decent with a pistol, but needs a bit of protection in a fight. She's not just any thief, she specializes in stealing ships. Big ones, small ones; it doesn't matter. She usually works alone, but the player has piqued her curiosity and she's willing to come along for
the ride... for the time being.

**Relationship**

Romance for lesbian female PC. She's not looking for another "relationship". But now she finds herself strangely attracted to the female player (she's platonically curious about the male player, but keeps her distance due to her current distrust/dislike of men). For her, this isn't a "relationship"... it's a fling. It's exciting. It's new. It's different. She doesn't care if it ends today, or goes on for a long time. As the story progresses, she begins to get antsy. She's used to working alone. If the player hasn't brought her loyalty up enough, she will leave.

## ME2 — Henchmen Narrative Overviews

### The Rebel General (Garrus)

Garrus — a tough war veteran, still trying to do the right the thing, but in his own way.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

Garrus returns, but he's the de facto leader of a small group of elite vigilantes. His team is under no banner, beholden to no one. No longer the eager protégé, he has become a hard, experienced warrior thanks to everything Shepard taught him. He has the battle scars and war stories to prove it.

**Background**

Since Shepard's "death", Garrus became increasingly disillusioned with the Council's bureaucratic crap. He's slowly gathered a small group (6 or less) of elite "soldiers" willing to do whatever it takes to put an end to tyranny. The tyrants of the universe have been put on notice. Unfortunately, in the Council's eyes, the only people with authority to act in this way are the Spectres.

At the moment, Garrus is having some troubles of his own. The last few missions have gone poorly... as though someone was being tipped off to his team's arrival. Garrus decided to put his team on hiatus for a short time while he investigated further. During his absence, things have gone horribly wrong back at his main base of operations.

**Narrative Razor (Acquisition)**

Omega: While away investigating, Garrus leams that his base has been infiltrated and the last of his men are being held hostage. He's sure it's a trap to catch him (his men are probably already dead), but he's got to go anyway, he won't let them die in vain.

When the player arrives on the scene, it's dead body after dead body. There's still several "enemies" (the mole has tipped off several of the factions that Garrus' group have helped cripple — they've teamed up to get payback). Eventually, the player gets close to Garrus.

He's holed up in a defensive position, picking off his attackers. Now the attackers are between you and Garrus, but they're making a push. Garrus' time is running out. When the player finally reaches Garrus, he's been badly wounded and must be taken back to the Normandy to get patched up.

**Narrative Razor (Loyalty)**

Citadel: Garrus reveals that he's learned about the mole in his organization. He needs to finish this guy off.

The mole is working for a group of near\-do\-wells that want revenge against Garrus and his team (the mole essentially turned Judas). The player must first hunt down the heads of this cartel (in a meeting on the citadel) and then discover the location of the mole.

Once the player has eliminated the cartel members, Garrus sends a message using their computer to the mole, to set up a meeting. The meeting is in a public place and Garrus heads off to find a place from which to snipe him. The Player can persuade Garrus that violence isn't necessary; Shepard can get a confession out of him. Or, player can just let Garrus go on his merry sniping way. Even if the player persuades Garrus to calm down, he says he's taking his gun, just in case. You have the meeting while Garrus is "setting up" in the rafters somewhere. If the player fails to persuade the MOLE, Garrus takes the shot.

### The Assassin — Krios Than

ASSASSIN is one of the most notorious hitmen in the galaxy. Now he's dying and wants to atone for his sins.

**Why is this character cool, amusing or interesting?**

He combines elements of the deadly stealth killer with a crusader like drive to make up for past mistakes. He's trying to atone for his past sins, but years of training and instincts make the old habits hard to kill.

Romance character for female Shepard.

**Background**

ASSASSIN became a deadly hitman at a relatively young age. He didn't do it out of anger or revenge; it was just a job (Martin in Grosse Point Blank), \[sic] At one he tried to start a family, but he kept the two worlds completely separate. Unfortunately, his past caught up with him and his wife was assassinated. ASSASSIN decided then to break off all contact with his children and other family members. He focused everything on his work and become one of the most notorious assassins in the galaxy.

Then, a few years ago, ASSASSIN learned he was dying. Though his job remains the same, he has changed his approach. He no longer takes jobs indiscriminately, but chooses ones that seem to be on the side of "right" (i.e. wiping out a corrupt official). He's also become someone notorious for dealing harshly with anyone who tries to screw him over. But none of this seems to be helping ease the burden of a life of murder; until the player comes along and offers him a shot a \[sic] true redemption.

**Narrative Razor (Acquisition)**

Asari World: ASSASSIN is on a mission to take down a corrupt official located in an IMPENATRABLE FORTRESS. Your contact suggests if you want a meeting, find him at the hit (after that... you may never see him again). The hit will probably be the Asari \[sic] from ME1 who had you wipe out her sister, the smuggler.

The player must infiltrate the IMPENATRABLE FORTRESS head on. After slaughtering their way through dozens of elite guards, the player finds the target of the hit. Just when they're about to begin a discussion, ASSASSIN arrives and finishes her off. He tips his hat and thanks the player for creating such a great distraction, "I knew I could count on you". He gave away his location to the contact hoping to get the player to aid him unknowingly.

**Narrative Razor (Loyalty)**

Citadel: Assassin left his family (children) to go on his crusade. He hasn't seen them in almost 5 years (and even then, he didn't "make contact"). He's recently learned that his son, now almost 20 years old, has learned not only about some of ASSASSIN's past, but about his mother's death. He's taken it upon himself to become an assassin like his father, but not as a job, for revenge. ASSASSIN doesn't want to see his son make the same mistakes he did.

ASSASSIN wants you to help him stop his son from "getting revenge". It may mean killing the target before the son can, or convincing him to turn his life around. Either way, there should be a confrontation between father and son.
