**Location:** Milky Way / Gemini Sigma / Ming System /  **Third planet**
**Prerequisite:** Priority: Sur'Kesh (Mass Effect 3\)

## Description

**Altanorch** is a small but typical hydrogen\-helium gas giant with no outstanding features. Its combination of strong magnetic field and relatively shallow gravity well make it a popular "stopover" world for discharging FTL drive cores.

## Properties

Altanorch is too small to be a "typical hydrogen\-helium gas giant" (William Hubbard, *The New Solar System* 4th ed, 1999; p. 194\). At 60 solar masses the star is not more than 360000 years old. Altanorch is a superterrestrial protoplanet.
