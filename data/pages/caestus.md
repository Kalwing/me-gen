**Prerequisite:** Star Chart from Baria Frontiers store (Mass Effect 2\)

**Caestus** only has two planets but both are rich in resources. Turians have settled here, although the main world Invictus is used as a hideout by criminals from the Terminus Systems.

## Invictus

**Invictus** is the *first* planet orbiting the star Caestus.

*Main article: Invictus*
## Temerarus

**Temerarus** is the *second* planet orbiting the star Caestus.

*Main article: Temerarus*
## Trivia

- Caestus may be named after a battle glove used by the athletes of the classical world in the sports of boxing and pankration.
