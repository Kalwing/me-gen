**Mass Effect: Homeworlds** is a comic series written by Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3 lead writer, Mac Walters in cooperation with character writers for each respective character. The first issue was released in April 2012\. Each installment focuses on a squad member from Mass Effect 3\.

A trade paperback collecting all four issues was released November 21, 2012\.
