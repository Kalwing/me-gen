**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Onaon System /  **Fourth planet**
## Description

**Obayro**'s surface is primarily composed of iron, but mountains rich with less common metals dot the landscape. Scans indicate that the angara have often sent mining expeditions here.
