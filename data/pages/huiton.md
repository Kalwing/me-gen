**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Meos System /  **Fifth planet**

## Description

**Huiton**'s hydrogen and nitrogen atmosphere is notable for its unique weather patterns \- storms begin and end here with almost mechanized consistency.

## Anomaly

### Storm

DATA:

- Radius: 8847 km
- Wind speed: 486 km/h
- Rotation: Counterclockwise

ANALYSIS:

This storm does not fit Huiton's usual patterns; it appears to be the result of a comet impact that occurred within the past decade.
