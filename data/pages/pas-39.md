**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Hefena System /  **First planet**

## Description

**Pas\-39** appears to be the dying remains of Jaali No, a massive gas giant named for a mythical angaran hero. It has been largely stripped of its atmosphere.

## Additional Information

Kamiiro No apparently once orbited this world before it was reduced to its current state.
