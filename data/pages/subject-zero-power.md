**Subject Zero** is a passive class power in Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3's single\-player mode.

## Mass Effect 2

*See also: Squad Members Guide (Mass Effect 2\)\#Jack*

Enhances Jack's weapon damage and health; improves her power recharge time.

### Power Ranks

- **Rank 1** \- Jack's experimentation with her unique abilities increases her health and weapon damage, and reduces the recharge time of her powers.
	- **Health**: \+5\.00%
	- **Weapon Damage**: \+6\.00%
	- **Power Recharge Time**: \-6\.00%

- **Rank 2** \- Jack's experimentation with her unique abilities increases her health and weapon damage, and reduces the recharge time of her powers.
	- **Health**: \+10\.00%
	- **Weapon Damage**: \+12\.00%
	- **Power Recharge Time**: \-12\.00%

- **Rank 3** \- Jack's experimentation with her unique abilities increases her health and weapon damage, and reduces the recharge time of her powers.
	- **Health**: \+15\.00%
	- **Weapon Damage**: \+18\.00%
	- **Power Recharge Time**: \-18\.00%

**Rank 4**

Evolves Subject Zero into one of the following options:

#### *Primal Adept*

- Jack can push her biotic abilities to the limit, greatly reducing recharge times of her powers.
	- **Health**: \+20\.00%
	- **Weapon Damage**: \+18\.00%
	- **Power Recharge Time**: \-25\.00%

#### *Primal Vanguard*

- Jack focuses her ruthless energy into combat, heightening her weapon damage.
	- **Health**: \+20\.00%
	- **Weapon Damage**: \+25\.00%
	- **Power Recharge Time**: \-18\.00%

## Mass Effect 3

*See also: Squad Members Guide (Mass Effect 3\)\#Jack*

### Power Ranks

#### Rank 1: Subject Zero

Boost biotic and defensive abilities.

- **Health \& Shield Bonus:** 10%
- **Power Recharge Speed Bonus:** 20%

#### Rank 2: Durability \& Recharge

Increase health and shield bonuses by 10%.
Increase recharge speed bonus by 20%.

- **Health \& Shield Bonus:** 20%
- **Power Recharge Speed Bonus:** 40%

#### Rank 3: Durability \& Recharge

Increase health and shield bonuses by 10%.
Increase recharge speed bonus by 20%.

- **Health \& Shield Bonus:** 30%
- **Power Recharge Speed Bonus:** 60%

#### Rank 4: Recharge Speed/Durability

| *Recharge Speed* Increase recharge speed bonus of all powers by 40%. - **Health \& Shield Bonus:** 30% - **Power Recharge Speed Bonus:** 100% | *Durability* Increase health and shield bonuses by 20%. - **Health \& Shield Bonus:** 50% - **Power Recharge Speed Bonus:** 60% |
| --- | --- |

#### Rank 5: Weapon Damage/Duration \& Force

| *Weapon Damage* Increase weapon damage by 20%. | *Duration \& Force* Increase power force and duration by 30%. |
| --- | --- |

#### Rank 6: Squad Bonus/Recharge Speed

| *Squad Bonus* Increase recharge speed of all squadmate biotic powers by 10%. | *Recharge Speed* Increase recharge speed bonus of all powers by 60%. - **Health \& Shield Bonus:** 30% (Recharge Speed) 50% (Durability) - **Power Recharge Speed Bonus:** 160% (Recharge Speed) 120% (Durability) |
