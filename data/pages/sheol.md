**Prerequisite:** Star Chart from Baria Frontiers store or Project Firewalker: Volcano Station and Horizon (mission) (Mass Effect 2\)

**Prerequisite:** Priority: Rannoch (Mass Effect 3\)

**Sheol** is a red dwarf. Its sole planet is noteworthy for having a wealth of material and cultural resources: a Prothean burial ground, attendant graverobbing, and mining operations forwarded to Pamyat.

## Gei Hinnom

**Gei Hinnom** is the *first* planet orbiting the star Sheol.
