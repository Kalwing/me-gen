**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Eriksson System /  **Third planet**
## Description

Named for the first landing of the human explorer Leif Eriksson, **Helluland**'s crust is largely composed of aluminum with sulfur deposits, brought to the surface via volcanic activity.

## Anomaly

### Aluminum

DATA:

Aluminum

ANALYSIS:

A valuable light metal. This deposit is close to the surface and relatively easy to mine.

Awards a random small amount of Aluminum when scanned.
