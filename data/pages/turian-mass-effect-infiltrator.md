An **unnamed turian** is the main enemy of Randall Ezno during a capture mission on an ice planet.

|  | ***“I like the power.”*** — Spoilers for **Mass Effect: Infiltrator** follow. |  |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Capture

After a fight on an ice planet against Randall Ezno, a top Cerberus operative, the unnamed turian is beaten. Randall taunts him by saying that he is not Archangel. The turian is then captured and taken into a secret facility, known as the Barn, where he becomes a test subject for Cerberus scientists.

## Tactics

- The unnamed turian possesses considerable health and a high layer of shielding. Like with other enemies in the game, his shielding will regenerate over time.
- The turian will often take cover during the firefight, and tends not to come out of cover unless you're in his line of sight. This allows you to wait behind cover for your powers to recharge without being disturbed. If necessary, using Pull will flush him out of cover.
- Like other humanoid enemies in the game, he is vulnerable to headshots, receiving bonus damage when shot there.

## Escape

After finishing his mission, Randall and his Volus ally, protesting the brutal treatment of his handler Inali Renata, released all of the test subjects. As a result, the facility falls into chaos and an alarm sounds in the turian's cell. The prisoner's restraints fall away and he feels his strength returning. Grabbing a weapon, his new objective is to escape from the Cerberus base.

He fights his way past Cerberus troops and other escaped test subjects, culminating in a confrontation with an Ogre Mech and an endless supply of Orcus Mechs.

Eventually the turian reaches a large, empty room. Upon moving to the far wall, the turian sees the Director of the facility and several Cerberus personnel on the other side of a window. The Director laughs at the turian, as a turret deploys in the room. With no available cover, the view fades to black.
