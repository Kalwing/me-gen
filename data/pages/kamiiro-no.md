**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Hefena System /  **Second planet**

## Description

**Kamiiro** **No** was named for the little brother of mythical angaran hero Jaali No. Before the Scourge, Kamiiro No existed in the shadow of a much larger gas giant.

## Anomaly

### Asteroid

DATA:

- M\-type asteroid
- Diameter: 244 km
- Surface gravity: 0\.01 G

ANALYSIS:

Before the Scourge, the asteroids ringing Kamiiro No were a moon housing a small angaran colony. The moon and the colony are now long gone.
