**Location:** Milky Way / Styx Theta / Acheron System /  **Fourth planet**
**Prerequisite:** Noveria: Geth Interest (Mass Effect)

## Description

Though exceptionally large for a terrestrial world, **Imaneya** is composed of water ice with a core of various carbonaceous compounds. It has a density only half that of Earth, and an atmosphere of nitrogen and carbon monoxide. The surface is composed of surprisingly pure water ice, with a high albedo.

## Trivia

- "Surprisingly pure water ice with a high albedo" is a surface characteristic shared by Enceladus, a moon of Saturn known for being one of the most reflective bodies in the Solar System.
- In Mass Effect 3: Citadel, one of the advertisements in the Cision Motors skycar lot claims that the Cision Stalwart model skycar is manufactured with the aid of "filtered spring water shipped from the pure ice mountains of Imaneya".
