**Prerequisite:** Horizon (mission) (Mass Effect 2\)

**Prerequisite:** Priority: The Citadel II (Mass Effect 3\)

**Ma\-at**'s sole gas giant is believed to have captured all other planets in the vicinity. There is nothing else of note in the system except moderate resources in Ammut and the geth that mine it for hydrogen.

## Ammut

**Ammut** is the *first* planet orbiting the star Ma\-at.

*Main article: Ammut*
## Trivia

- Ma\-at is likely named for the goddess of truth and justice in the ancient Egyptian pantheon.
