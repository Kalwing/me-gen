The **Pathfinder Observer** is a sniper rifle in Mass Effect: Andromeda.

## Description

This semi\-automatic sniper rifle feeds data directly to a Pathfinder's SAM, allowing adjustments to the weapon's balance, recoil, and heat buildup. The Observer records all scope data, even beyond the visual spectrum. It is a scientific tool as much as a deadly weapon.

### Features

- Like all weapons in the Pathfinder Elite Weapons Set, the Observer is not available above Rank V.
- Despite being classified as a Rare weapon, the Observer only has 2 augmentation slots.
