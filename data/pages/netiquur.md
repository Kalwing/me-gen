**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Vaotessa System /  **Second planet**

## Description

Though **Netiquur** is arid and cold, liquid water can be found in its underground seas and during rare rains. Multiple angaran colonies were planned; none were ever founded.

## Anomaly

### Biomatter Growth

DATA:

- Classification: Plant
- Origin: Netiquur (or possibly offworld)
- Quarantine recommendation: Level 3

ANALYSIS:

This vast forest appears to be sustained by an enormous underground reservoir. The individual plants seem to be linked, almost acting as a single organism.
