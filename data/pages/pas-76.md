**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Saajor System /  **First planet**

## Description

The nickel and cobalt surface of **Pas\-76** is hot enough to melt many other metals. A few deposits turn molten and resolidify with the change in seasons.

## Anomaly

### Aluminum

DATA:

- Aluminum

ANALYSIS:

A valuable light metal. This deposit is close to the surface and relatively easy to mine.

Awards a random small amount of Aluminum when scanned.
