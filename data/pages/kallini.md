**Prerequisite:** Priority: The Citadel II (Mass Effect 3\)

**Kallini** is a small system with asari mining interests. Its three worlds are inhospitable, but rich in resources and in the process of being exploited up until the Reaper invasion.

**Ithrone** is the *first* planet orbiting the star Kallini.
