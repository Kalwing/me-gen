During the corporate skirmishes on Garvug, comm buoys in **Paz** have been temporarily destroyed, cutting it off from all outside communication. Sonax Industries have since taken control of all comm buoys in the system.

## Garvug

**Garvug** is the *first* planet orbiting the star Paz.
