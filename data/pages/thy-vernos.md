**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Govorkam System /  **planet**

## Description

A massive ball of rock and frozen methane, Thy'vernos has little apparent material or scientific value. Would\-be miners from Kadara keep scanning it, just in case.

## Anomaly

DATA:

Beryllium

ANALYSIS:

A metal ideal for lightweight construction in a variety of applications. This deposit is readily accessible.

Awards a random small amount of Beryllium when scanned.
