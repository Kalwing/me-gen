**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Shojaon System /  **Fifth planet**

## Description

A tiny gas planet at the edge of the Shojaon system, **Mocalaam** is a perfect location for exploring ships to restock helium\-3 and discharge the static buildup in their drives.
