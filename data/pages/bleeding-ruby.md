**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Skeldah System /  **First planet**
## Description

Named by an exile with a penchant for poetry, **Bleeding Ruby**'s rings and tectonic instability are the result of a single asteroid impact that occurred less than a century ago.
