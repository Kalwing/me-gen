**Prerequisite:** N7: MSV Strontium Mule (Mass Effect 2\)

**Prerequisite:** Priority: The Citadel I or Citadel: Leviathan III (Mass Effect 3\)

**Sigurd's Cradle** is situated at roughly the center of the Terminus Systems. It has known turian, human, and asari territories. Unbeknownst to many, a surviving group of Leviathans lurk within barely\-explored recesses of this cluster.
