## sta20\_avina\_stores

**Note:**  these voicelines were retranscribed directly from the dialogue file Mass Effect\\BioGame\\CookedPC\\Packages\\Dialog\\STA20\\sta20\_avina\_stores\_D.upk or Mass Effect Legendary Edition\\Game\\ME1\\BioGame\\CookedPCConsole\\BIOA\_STA20\_02Statue\_DSG\_LOC\_INT.pcc, where they were made non\-functional, despite audio being present. All text was copied accurately, and any typos or errors are verbatim.

**Context:** at the Avina terminal near the Krogan Monument, there are alternate lines for an alternate reality where the krogan statue was taken down centuries before. There are no conditionals associated with these lines, and it is thus unknown how they could have been used.

**Speaker**:

- Avina

---

**Avina**: Welcome to Presidium Tourism Terminal III. Here in the financial district, a number of businesses offer various goods and services to their exclusive clientele. This site was once the location of a memorial statue commemorating the krogan soldiers who gave their lives to protect Citadel Space during the Rachni Wars. In the wake of the Krogan Rebellions, several embassies petitioned to have the memorial removed. Initially the Council resisted, and the petition was denied. But hatred of the krogan was strong, and as the Krogan Rebellions intensified, the Council finally relented. The memorial was removed despite their reservations.

*(The conversation proceeds to the regular dialogue wheel and continues as normal)*
