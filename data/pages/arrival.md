**Arrival** is a DLC pack for Mass Effect 2. It was accidentally revealed on February 21, 2011 by the addition of three new trophies in a patch for the PlayStation 3 version of Mass Effect 2\. While the addition of the trophies was intentional, BioWare assumed they would be invisible. BioWare released the first screenshot of Arrival on March 14, 2011, officially confirming the existence of the DLC pack. Arrival was released on March 29, 2011\. Like most of the DLC for the original Mass Effect trilogy games, it is included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition. Admiral Hackett returns, playing "a prominent role" in the pack.

## Description

A deep cover agent is missing in Batarian space, after reporting evidence of an imminent Reaper invasion. Shepard must travel to the edge of the galaxy, rescue the agent, and discover the truth behind the Arrival. Uncover new research and 3 new achievements in this spectacular new adventure for Mass Effect 2\.

## Content

- One assignment:
	- Arrival
- One location:
	- The Bahak system in the Viper Nebula.
- Three upgrades:
	- Heavy Weapon Ammo
	- Medi\-Gel Capacity
	- Heavy Skin Weave

## Pricing

Arrival is available for 560 BioWare Points on PC, and $6\.99 on PlayStation 3 and on the Xbox 360\.

## Trivia

- On the occasion of the DLC's release, Bioware published a promotional broadcast on Cerberus Network describing the discovery of Dr. Kenson on the origin of Mass Relays.

## Cut Content

- Pre\-development notes for this DLC include plans for upgrades to biotic and tech cooldowns, an extra health upgrade for the player, and two tech upgrades for a "popular henchman". It also mentions the DLC was planned to be "2\.50 hour" long.
