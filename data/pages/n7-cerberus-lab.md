The Alliance has located a new Cerberus laboratory located on Sanctum. Investigate the lab and deal with any potential threat.

## Acquisition

**Prerequisite:** Priority: The Citadel I

Given by Specialist Traynor after also receiving Priority: Palaven.

Alliance forces are stretched thin across the galaxy. We need your specific talents for a series of ops.

These missions will open doors for the Alliance in places we can’t touch through conventional means. We’ll deploy operatives to hold point after you’ve completed your objectives.

I need you to head to a Cerberus lab on the planet Sanctum. I’ll brief you when you’re inbound.

Hackett

## Preparation

This mission occurs on the multiplayer map Firebase Glacier. You will be fighting basic Cerberus enemies on this mission, in short to medium\-range engagements in a claustrophobic indoor environment. Just because it's available early doesn't mean this mission is a cakewalk; in fact, it can be one of the more challenging outings especially at low levels and with low level equipment.

In terms of your squad, consider bringing tougher members who can hold their own like James. You may find that EDI can also be useful for stripping shields with Overload and her Decoy. Liara's Stasis, especially if upgraded with the Bubble evolution, can be a very useful tool for unexpected encounters with a Centurion or Guardian at close quarters. A Stasis Bubble can also be deployed ahead of time to guard a vulnerable flank. **Note:**  Even if Javik has already joined your crew, you can only bring him on this mission after you've completed Priority: Palaven.

Grenades can be useful here for emergency fire support, and there will be a number of refills available.

**Warning:**  Enemies will spontaneously spawn in waves at several points during this mission, and in at least one case they may instantly appear out of thin air without any warning *right on top of you or your squad*. Also, near the end of the mission enemies can spawn endlessly if you continue to move around the area so it's a good idea to end the mission as soon as possible once you can do so safely.

## Walkthrough

### First Artifact

At the beginning of the mission, go left up the stairs and through the doors. Expect resistance at the top. To get to each artifact you will need to deactivate the containment. After locating the artifact, eliminate the Cerberus troops and collect the artifact, then return to the landing pad and place the artifact onto the shuttle for extraction, but look around for useful items first.

From the initial landing pad and up the ramps, you'll find the Sniper Rifle Enhanced Scope just inside the first archway on the left on the floor. Directly to the right on the console are credits (3,000\). Ahead in the room in the hall on the left is a medical station (150 experience). In the corner of the room is a journal entry (18 experience). In the hallway on the right there are more credits (4,000\) to the left of the ammo pile. Down the hallway past the reaper artifact (not down the stairs on the left or the right) is a small room with a failed medi\-gel experiment. Exit the room and go left down the stairs, pick up the credits (3,000\) across from the open doorway. Go down the stairs and in the corner is a journal entry (18 experience).

### Second Artifact

Go back into the base to locate the next artifact: there will now be more Cerberus troops, this time with turrets. You'll find the second artifact on the lowest level. Once you locate it, be careful as you deactivate its containment because two Cerberus troopers will drop in right near you during the process. Remember to pick up the artifact itself once you've dealt with the new arrivals. Shepard will now radio Cortez who turns out to have been driven away from the landing pad by Cerberus fire.

### Exfiltration

You must now hold out against an unlimited stream of Cerberus troops until Cortez is able to return. Several radio conversations along the way will provide checkpoints to let you know you're making progress. When Shepard says to just "reload and keep firing", do exactly that. **Note:**  You may find that your squadmates become somewhat glitchy and unresponsive to your orders at this point, potentially wandering off to engage enemies on their own which usually ends badly for them.

After you down more enemies, Shepard will suggest keeping the landing pad clear, which is where you began the mission. Doing this is the most time\-efficient strategy, but **be advised** that as you approach this area, enemy troops will enter in close proximity and from multiple angles including several that may jet up from below the ledge where you were dropped off. In the third radio check\-in, Cortez will say it will just be a bit longer; continue to keep the landing pad clear. Even if the fighting gets hot, it is recommended that you **stay put**, as running around the area can simply trigger continuous enemy spawns. Also, Combat Engineers may have set up Turrets outside the landing pad area in either direction, so you may just end up getting annihilated if you try to leave.

Just keep your head and be ready to use all your squad's abilities to suppress and take out the most immediate threats. Use First Aid without hesitation to keep yourself and your squad healthy. A good place to take cover with your team is behind the large crates underneath the high windows on the landing pad.

**Alternative Strategy:** You can be much more conservative about how you approach the landing pad, but you'll have to go there eventually, and enemies can spawn near you from multiple places on the map regardless of which route you take or how careful you are. Also, it will take a lot longer, because the amount of time it takes for Cortez to return to pick you up seems to be tied to how many enemies you kill.

Finally, you'll see the shuttle pull up and Cortez will tell you to get on. From this point forward, successfully reaching and interacting with the shuttle will instantly end the mission with victory, regardless of the number of enemies remaining. If you want to clear the immediate area to be on the safe side go right ahead, but don't wander around the map now as this will just make your life harder—or shorter.

Completion of this mission gives you the Cerberus Research Data War Asset.
