**Location:** Andromeda / Heleus Cluster / Bosavay System /  **Fourth planet**

## Description

Many of the conditions on **Pas\-51** are amenable to the development of life (microbial at least), but its searing temperatures are an obstacle not easily overcome.

## Anomaly

### Mountain

DATA:

- Height: 8\.8 km
- Origin: Tectonic

ANALYSIS:

Water vapor escapes from cracks in this mountain, suggesting that somewhere deep underground \- where temperatures are far cooler \- a reservoir may exist. If lifeforms exist on Pas\-51, it will be there.
