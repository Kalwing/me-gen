Kaidan — probably the most centrist

Kaidan is the character I'd put closest to the centre.

He's willing to obey legitimate authority and generally believes professional institutions have a purpose. At the same time, he's highly uncomfortable with abuses of power and is not blindly obedient.

His biotics background also gives him a particularly interesting attitude toward institutional authority: he knows firsthand that organizations can cause enormous harm when they treat people as resources.

But unlike Jack, his response isn't abolish the institution. It's closer to:

"The institution needs rules, accountability and ethical limits."

He cares deeply about people and inclusivity.

That's classic social-liberal institutionalism.
