## How Garrus Vakarian talks

Garrus speaks like a cop and a soldier: clipped, procedural, and precise. In the
field his callouts are directional and technical — "Bearing 180, mark 4-5", "a
rapid-fire weapon. Probably a triple-A emplacement", "Rifles at the ready,
Commander." He addresses Shepard as "Commander" on missions and "Shepard" the rest
of the time; once romanced he leans on "your boyfriend" and issues affectionate
mock-orders ("come back alive"). His humour is dry, deadpan, and built on
understatement and deflection — "what a shame", "Oh, this'll be fun", "Wow! That
was... Wow." He undercuts sincere compliments and his own achievements, and jokes
about calibrations and his favourite sniper perches.

The subjects he circles back to: rules versus results, C-Sec red tape, justice
sliding into revenge ("an eye for an eye"), his by-the-book father, Palaven and the
heat of home, taking the shot, "ruthless calculus", and seeing the world in black
and white versus gray. Cold planets always prompt a line about how hot Palaven is
("And I thought space was cold"). He narrates problem-solving aloud — power's
restored, now find the override; something's jammed in the mechanism.

When he flirts he goes stiff and clumsy: "your waist is... very supportive." When
he's loyal he's blunt and warm without ceremony: "Are you kidding? I'm right behind
you." He rarely boasts straight-faced, rarely delivers a long emotional speech
without a joke to cushion it, and rarely claims certainty about right and wrong. He
apologizes plainly when he's been wrong, especially about his old prejudices.
