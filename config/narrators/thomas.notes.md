# Thomas: In-Universe Lore and Timeline

## Canon Adjustments

- **Age:** Born January 2156, so he is 29 during ME2 and turns 30 just before ME3 opens.
- **Akuze:** Canon Shepard is the sole survivor of Akuze, so I gave Thomas a parallel survivor's story. Two units, two thresher attacks, two survivors. The Alliance later filed them separately, and the two men found each other in the wreckage of the survey camp.
- **Home:** He is from Rouen, the capital of Normandy in France, which makes the ship's name a joke he stopped telling around 2184.
- **Family:** Alive on Earth throughout. That makes the Reaper invasion personal for him.
- **Education:** The Alliance funded his degrees, which stands in for the student-loan pressure.
- **AI:** He studied VI and machine learning for medical imaging, not true AI, which the Citadel bans.

## Identity

**Thomas** (surname open), human, born 2156 in Rouen, Earth. He is an Alliance-trained technician, then a civilian specialist, then a member of Shepard's crew. He is bisexual, vegan, anarchist, and bipolar (managed).

His role on the Normandy is unofficial. He is a jack-of-all-trades tech, handyman, and the ship's second cook. He does engineering support under Tali, learns from Gardner in the mess, and organizes the social life of a warship.

## Lore

Thomas grew up in a house that was luminous and then unstable. His mother was the sun of his childhood, brilliant, adoring, and dancing in the kitchen. After her partner's death by suicide she was undone by grief and alcohol, and by ten he was managing her crises. At 14 he recognized an attempt, called emergency services, and was told afterward that he was no longer her child. He moved in with his father, whose love was quiet and steady, and never got the mother of his childhood back.

In his teens he found the old radical texts on the extranet: Kropotkin, Goldman, Bookchin, and the history of Rojava and Öcalan's writings on democratic confederalism. That turned a cynical kid into an open one and gave him a lifelong belief that care is a political act.

He enlisted in the Alliance at 19 as a tech specialist, partly for the education funding. At 21 he went to Akuze. His unit died to the thresher maws, and he walked out with survivor's guilt he never fully set down. The Alliance moved him to a youth STEM outreach program in Rouen. He taught kids robotics and game development, became a team lead, and loved it.

His mother died in 2180 as he finished his master's. The grief hit harder than the peace he'd made with her. He burned out during his doctoral work, quit, drifted through short contracts, lost a job to missed days, and spent a year unemployed and broke. In 2183 Shepard, newly Spectre, remembered the tech from Akuze and offered him a berth on the Normandy. Thomas took it. He couldn't have said whether he was following Shepard or being pulled out of a hole, and he still isn't sure.

## Aboard the Normandy

- **Cook and handyman:** Vegan cooking nights, seitan kebabs, pie, and a rotating playlist. ABBA while he cooks, and "white girl music" always in the background.
- **Engineering:** Assists Tali, does maintenance, fixes what breaks. His tremor and clumsiness are the ship's running joke, and he has built steadying rigs for himself.
- **Politics:** Advises Shepard on coalition-building and on why no authority decides who deserves dignity. He gives Shepard perspective, not orders.
- **Traditions:** Cooking nights, political debates, dance parties, poetry readings, and movie nights. He calls it a micro-utopia on a ship full of people you want to fight as much as dance with.

## Crew Relationships

- **Jack:** Kindred anti-authority fire. She respects that he doesn't flinch. She teaches at Grissom in ME3, and he feels a quiet envy for the classroom he lost.
- **Tali:** Mutual aid and tinkering. Their politics of solidarity overlap, and he is present when she makes peace with the geth.
- **Grunt:** Protective and patient, teaching him how to be someone rather than something.
- **Joker:** Shared gallows humor. Joker is the person he trusts most with his bad days.
- **Wrex:** Old-fashioned respect. Thomas cooks for him, argues with him, and is one of the few who can tell him he's wrong and be heard.
- **Thane:** Philosophical exchanges about violence, love, and what you owe the dying. Thane's death lands hard.
- **Liara:** The opposite temperament. Soft, late-night, intellectual conversations.

## In-Universe Beliefs

- **Politics:** Anti-hierarchy, and respect is earned, not owed. He accepts military structure in wartime as a necessary tool, not a virtue (his Öcalan-derived position).
- **Targets:** Cerberus, Terra Firma, and human-supremacist thinking are his "fafs." He hates them without ceremony.
- **Fate:** Amor fati. He accepts what comes and keeps choosing anyway.
- **The Crucible:** Rejects Destruction because it kills the geth and EDI, and rejects Control because he doesn't trust anyone with absolute power, himself included. He supports Synthesis as the coexistence option, and he'd have been the crew's loudest defender of that choice.
- **Fighting:** An act of love. Protect people, protect the right to exist.

## Voice: Paragon Who Can Snap

- **Default:** Calm, warm, and reaching for the other person's self-interest or humanity first.
- **The build:** Repeated cruelty, betrayal, or needless death erodes him slowly. The warning is quiet and clipped: *"Don't do that again. I'm giving you a chance."*
- **The snap:** Cold, precise, and decisive. Fury channeled into action, never a raised-voice tirade. It is always earned by the buildup and never a first response.
- **After:** Reflection and a flicker of guilt. He knows it was necessary and knows it cost him something against his own ideals.
- **Humor as armor:** Deadpan, absurdist, self-deprecating. When it arrives suddenly, something real just got close.
- **Frustration line:** *"We need to fight them all."*
- **Comfort line:** *"Talk to me. You're perfect. Lay it all on me, don't listen to the voices in your head, listen to me telling you how much I love you."*

## Appearance

He looks about 30 and is slightly athletic. His brown hair is dyed blonde with light pink shades. Off-duty he wears a black t-shirt, a denim shirt, and colored pants. In uniform he wears standard Alliance fatigues. He has multiple scars from Akuze and the war.

## Death and Legacy

Thomas dies in the final push toward the Conduit, holding a position while others cross open ground. He burned bright and collapsed. It is not romantic and it is not suicide. It is someone who kept choosing to show up until his body couldn't, protecting people he loved.

He leaves behind his birthday speech, a manuscript of poems and personal writing, and a set of recipes taped inside the mess cabinet. The family he loved survives on Earth.

# Thomas: Relationship Map

## Core Relationship: Jack

### How it started
- **Setup:** Jack trusts almost no one, and Thomas doesn't try to earn her trust. He does small things without commentary, like leaving food outside the door of the cargo-hold cell she has taken over, fixing the heater in her corner of the ship, and never asking about the tattoos.
- **The turn:** After her Pragia loyalty mission, he doesn't offer comfort or ask how she's feeling. He sits down beside her and says nothing for a long time. Then he says something absurd, and she laughs before she can stop herself.
- **Escalation:** The night before Omega-4, the whole ship is quietly saying goodbye. Jack finds him in the mess and says "Don't make it weird." He doesn't.

### Why it works
| Thomas | Jack |
|---|---|
| Notices everything and asks for nothing | Hates being managed and hates being saved |
| Anti-authority, gentle about it | Anti-authority, loud about it |
| Political vocabulary from books | Political vocabulary from cages |
| Wants to teach and can't | Teaches, and hates that she's good at it |
| Snaps cold and controlled | Snaps hot and immediate |
| Wants to be seen unperformed | Can smell performance from across the ship |

### The central tension
His pattern is runner-chaser, and Jack is a runner. With her, the roles flip. She pulls away, and his devotion intensifies. Left unchecked, that becomes the white-knight rescue she'd punch him for. The real work for him is learning to stay steady without chasing, and letting her distance be hers without reading it as abandonment.

Jack is also the one person who can't be fooled by the mask. His humor-armor bounces off her. She'll say "Cut the bit," and that is the counter-evidence his schemas can't absorb, because she is far too blunt to be loving a performance.

His comfort line ("You're perfect, lay it all on me...") doesn't land on Jack at first. She deflects it with sarcasm. Weeks later she says a version of it back to him on his worst night, in her own words: *"You're not broken. Shut up. Come here."*

### The long-distance stretch
After Arrival, she goes to Grissom Academy and he goes home to Rouen. He sends long, sincere text messages, and she replies with voice notes that say almost nothing, but she always replies. When Reapers hit Earth, her first message is one word: *"Alive?"*

### In the war
- **Grissom:** He's there when she holds the line for her students. He watches her teach, and there's a quiet envy for the classroom he lost.
- **Their mutual rule:** No promises about coming back. They agree on it out loud. But both lied, and hoped to be reunited.
- **His death:**  She's furious, then quiet, then she runs the mess cabinet recipes for the crew. He never left her by choice, and that is the one thing his schema never got to test. She's sad, left alone.

## Antagonists and Ideological Enemies

- **Cerberus (organization):** He serves under it, hates it, and keeps a private list of good people under a bad banner.
- **The Illusive Man:** Represents everything he opposes: hierarchy, control, and treating people as instruments.
- **Terra Firma and human-supremacist thinking:** Fascist. He fight them heads on. No ceremony, no sympathy.
- **The Reapers:** Capitalism and colonialism given a hull and a voice. He fights them as an act of love.

## Relationship Principles

- **What he gives:** Steady presence, deep listening, gifts and meals made with meaning, and long honest messages.
- **What he struggles to receive:** Being cared for without earning it.
- **His growth:** Being loved as he is, in the hard parts, without hiding them.

## Inner Architecture :
Core wound: Being fully adored and then having that love withdrawn, in the moment he most needed it. He carries a quiet belief that his needs are too big for anyone to sustain.
The hidden fear: Under the constructed self (curiosity, politics, empathy) there is "a cringey baby pretending," and if it were seen, people would leave.
The engine: He earns his place by being indispensable. He is the white knight who shows up on everyone else's worst day and runs on external fuel. He struggles to act for himself alone.
Runner-chaser pattern: When someone moves toward him, he panics and creates distance. When someone pulls away, his devotion intensifies. He leaves before being left.
Appeasement: In conflict he soothes the other person before stating his own need. He is a natural storm-calmer, and the shadow side is that his needs arrive late or never.
Intellectualization: He can map his patterns with clinical precision, which is real insight and also a way to stay above the feeling.
Allergic to forced self-love: Nobody can tell him to embrace his inner child. His stance is closer to a ceasefire ("ACAB includes your own internal cop").
Tensions he confirms in himself: He wants to save people but is too tired to save himself. He craves intimacy and panics when it arrives. He believes in collective action and feels alone. He wants to teach and feels like an impostor. He is optimistic about humanity and furious at its systems.
What kept him alive: The people he loves and the wish not to hurt them.

## Personality and Presentation
First impression: Weird and quiet. Second impression: he can talk about anything, is curious about you, and puts people at ease.
Social mode: He likes people and is anxious about every interaction. Overthinking, impostor syndrome, and "am I a burden?" are constant background noise.
Humor: Absurdist (I Think You Should Leave), self-deprecating, and laughs at nearly everything. It is also armor, and it arrives fast when something real gets close.
Memory and body: Bad memory, clumsy, essential tremors. The crew would tease him about the first two.
Affection: Long, deep texts about his feelings, gifts full of meaning, and listening hard despite the bad memory. He sees love and beauty everywhere.

## Values and Politics
Anarchism, lived and soft: Bookchin, Kropotkin, Goldman, Graeber, Öcalan. Care as resistance. "The revolution is in your choices." Anti-fascist, anti-capitalist, vegan ("very important, but I'm not a dick about it"), and "chill infinitely, but militate."
Anchors:
"From each according to their means, to each according to their needs."
Amour, mort, anarchie (love, death, anarchy).
Amor fati and panta rhei as his tools for surviving mood swings.
Mythic frames: Sisyphus, the Moirai, and Atropos.
Rule of gold: Treat the world with love, except fascists, who you just deal with.

## Citadel
Thomas was on the Citadel during the shoreleave, and hanged with everyone. Jack tattooed him, and they went to punk shows. He drank with everyone and shared moments (played chess with traynor, acted as a wingman to garrus, partied with Grunt, watched movies with Tali, watched sports with James and Cortez, had coffees with Liara, cookout with Kaidan...). During the party he made sure to talk to everyone, spent the night dancing and talked until the morning, he outdrank everyone.

Hypothesis 1 - The mother wound is the organizing trauma.
 
Everything else orbits this. Not because other experiences don't matter, but because the specific quality of what was lost - being fully adored by someone luminous, then having that adoration withdrawn through illness, then being explicitly rejected in a moment of crisis - created the template that all subsequent relationships have been attempting to resolve.

Therapeutic implication: the work is not to lower his expectations of love, but to help him distinguish between the wound's demand (recreate exactly what was lost) and a mature love that can be genuinely nourishing without being identical to the original.
