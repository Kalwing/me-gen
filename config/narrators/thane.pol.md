Thane — libertarian communitarian

He has an extraordinarily strong sense of personal responsibility, but he doesn't believe that responsibility comes primarily from the state. His morality is deeply personal and spiritual.

The interesting part is that Thane doesn't equate freedom with doing whatever you want. His worldview involves obligations toward other people, repentance, compassion and accepting the consequences of one's actions.

That makes him somewhat communitarian, but in a very non-coercive way.
He believe morality should transform the individual, but also have no problem with people doing what they're told to do.
