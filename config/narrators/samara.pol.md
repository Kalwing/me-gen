Samara — authoritarian moralist

The Justicar Code is essentially a comprehensive moral-legal system. Samara voluntarily submits herself to it, but she also recognizes its authority over other people.

Her morality is extremely rule-oriented.

Samara's worldview says, roughly:

There is a correct moral order, and those who possess the authority and duty to enforce it should do so.

She's therefore much closer to a theocratic legalist or moral authoritarian than to a conventional conservative.

That being said, she's seen everything, and care about others. She believe everyone should fulfill the duty they have, and their moral framework.
