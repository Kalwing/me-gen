Liara — liberal institutionalist

Liara is probably the most conventionally liberal of these characters.

She's strongly committed to knowledge, individual autonomy and cooperation, but she doesn't have the anti-institutional instinct that defines Garrus or Jack.

Her development also matters. Early Liara is an academic who puts considerable faith in institutions of knowledge and expertise. Later Liara becomes much more comfortable operating outside conventional institutions, particularly as the Shadow Broker.

Still, she isn't naturally revolutionary. She tends toward liberal reformism.
