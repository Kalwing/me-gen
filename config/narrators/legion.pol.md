Legion — collectivist communalist

Geth society is extraordinarily collectivist. Individual geth programs participate in a shared intelligence and decisions emerge through consensus among a network. Concepts like private individual sovereignty have little relevance to traditional geth society.

That sounds authoritarian from a human perspective, but it's actually quite different from conventional authoritarianism. The geth don't necessarily have a centralized ruler commanding everyone. Their society is much closer to distributed communalism.

Legion in itself, is a communalist / collective democratic, with an emerging individualist streak.
