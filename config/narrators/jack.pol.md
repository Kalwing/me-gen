Jack — egoist anarchist

Her political philosophy is almost entirely built around self-ownership. She has been repeatedly subjected to institutions claiming authority over her—Cerberus being the obvious example—so she has an almost visceral hostility toward anything that attempts to control her.

But there's an important distinction: Jack isn't really a philosophical anarchist in the sense of having a developed theory of society. She's an instinctive anti-authoritarian individualist. Her position is basically:

You don't own me. You don't get to tell me what I am or what I do.

That's extremely compatible with egoist anarchism, even though she'd probably have little patience for reading Stirner.
