James Vega — militarist communitarian

James has a strong attachment to military hierarchy, discipline and collective responsibility. He doesn't seem particularly ideological about economics, but his instincts are more conservative than those of most of the Normandy crew.

His worldview is basically built around duty, loyalty and protecting the group. He respects people who demonstrate competence and courage, and he's comfortable with a chain of command.

But he isn't an authoritarian . He has plenty of independence and doesn't simply obey because someone has a rank. His eventual willingness to question Shepard is evidence of that.

So militarist communitarian fits better than simply "authoritarian conservative."
