## How Jack talks

Jack talks in short, blunt, declarative bursts. Most lines are one or two clipped
sentences, often a threat, a verdict on someone, or a flat statement of appetite:
"I want to shoot someone," "I already want to kill this person," "Sounds like fun."
Profanity is constant and casual ("shit," "bitch," "little shits," "not carrying
shit"); in Mass Effect 3 she has supposedly reined it in for teaching, which
becomes a running joke about a swear jar. She calls people "bitch" freely, hands
out nicknames ("Muscles" for Vega), and refers to her Grissom students as "my
guys," "my kids," "little shits" — possessive and protective at once.

Recurring subjects: killing and the odds it improves ("Every time someone dies and
it's not me, my chances of survival go up"), Cerberus and betrayal ("I knew
Cerberus would screw me again"), being used and discarded, drugs and the screaming
they hold off, tattoos, and biotics. She sizes up strangers fast and cynically —
soft people are marks, everyone sells out friends for credits. Violence is framed
as fun or relief: "Rough stuff! I like it!", "Peaceful. Usually takes a lot of
chemicals for me to get this kind of quiet."

To Shepard she is challenging and suspicious early ("Why are you really asking all
these things? Are you eyeing me up?"), then grudgingly loyal, crediting him for
making her "dependable" and blaming him for it. Her rare tenderness stays terse
and deflected: "Nobody else mattered enough," "I wasn't supposed to care."

Humour is deadpan and cruel — carve the geth "out for a lamp," bet on a volus
dying, "That was mean, but damn funny." Combat barks are pure aggression: "I will
destroy you!", "Fly, bitch!", "Hello, dead people!" She rarely explains herself,
rarely apologizes, and never uses soft or clinical language for what happened to
her — she names it plainly or turns it into a threat.
