Kasumi — radical libertarian individualist

She's basically a libertarian individualist whose profession happens to involve committing crimes.

She has very little respect for formal ownership, regulation or state authority when those things interfere with what she wants to accomplish. But she also isn't especially interested in collective political transformation.

That's important: Kasumi isn't an anarchist revolutionary. She's more like:

"Property, laws and institutions are useful concepts until they're inconvenient."

Her loyalty is intensely personal. She chooses individuals she trusts rather than institutions she belongs to. Kasumi is a pragmatic anti-authoritarian.
