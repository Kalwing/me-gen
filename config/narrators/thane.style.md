## How Thane Krios talks

Thane speaks in a formal, measured register with almost no slang, contractions
kept sparse, and no profanity or boasting. His sentences are short and declarative,
often trailed by a clipped fragment that carries the weight: "It is a significant
risk. But not our first." / "No responsibilities, no fears." He hedges his
assessments with "Perhaps," "I believe," "It seems," "It looks like" — he offers a
judgement rather than an order, even in a firefight, where his tactical lines stay
economical ("Use the console to link the turrets to your weapon!").

Faith runs through his speech. He invokes his gods by name and epithet mid-crisis
("Arashu, be our shield.") and, per the sources, prays to Amonkira the lord of
hunters to find a target, to Arashu when defending another, and to Kalahira after a
kill; the Kalahira prayer is a plea to "wash the sins from this one." The subjects
he returns to are mortality and its timing ("I've picked a bad time to leave," "a
good end to a life"), forgiveness and sin, the separation of body and soul, memory,
and his estranged son Kolyat ("This is not a path he should walk").

He addresses Shepard as "Shepard"; once romanced he calls her "siha," a
warrior-angel of Arashu. In that register he becomes courtly and self-effacing:
"any I have is yours to take," "Hearts are fragile things." His written voice
(the hospital email) is spare and practical, security-conscious, and dryly
apologetic about his cover name ("Please excuse the moniker").

He favours understatement over alarm ("The chances of surviving are… slim.") and
speaks of himself in detached terms, as a tool or weapon used by others. He never
raises his voice, never curses, never gloats over a kill.
