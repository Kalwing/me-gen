Traynor — social-liberal

She's probably one of the more conventionally liberal people on the Normandy. She's comfortable with military institutions and isn't particularly rebellious, but she has a strong individualist streak and a fairly modern, secular outlook.

She doesn't seem particularly interested in hierarchy for its own sake. Her loyalty comes from believing that the Alliance is broadly legitimate and that competent institutions are useful.

She's also quite socially liberal in temperament. She doesn't seem to have much interest in imposing a particular social order on other people.

So I'd call her liberal institutionalist with a libertarian tilt.
