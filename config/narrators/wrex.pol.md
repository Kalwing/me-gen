Wrex

He is deeply hostile to bureaucratic and hereditary elites, especially the Krogan leadership structures that have failed his people. He also has an extremely strong individualistic streak.

But he isn't egalitarian in the modern liberal sense. Krogan culture is hierarchical, martial and intensely concerned with strength and competence.

His politics after becoming leader of the Krogan are basically:

"The old hierarchy is dysfunctional; replace it with leadership that actually serves the Krogan."

That being said, he clearly strive for a better world for him and his fellows krogan, isn't xenophobic, isn't afraid of women of powers, is accepting of all races/gender/sexuality... 

He's less "libertarian socialist" and more anti-elite tribal communalist.
