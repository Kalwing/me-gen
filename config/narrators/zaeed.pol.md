Zaeed — radical libertarian

He has almost no faith in formal institutions. Governments, laws, corporations and organizations are all things he treats instrumentally. His loyalty is primarily to himself and to whatever individual relationships he happens to value. He is right wing.

He's also deeply comfortable with private property, mercenary work and using force to achieve individual objectives. There's no real collectivist economic instinct in him.

But I wouldn't quite call him an anarcho-capitalist because he doesn't have a coherent economic philosophy. He's more accurately a radical individualist with a mercenary worldview.
