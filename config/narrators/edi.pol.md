EDI — liberal humanist

Early EDI is considerably more authoritarian in practice. She's literally an AI whose purpose involves following constraints imposed by her creators and the Alliance. She doesn't initially have a strong conception of personal political autonomy.

As she becomes self-aware, however, her worldview moves sharply toward individual liberty and equal moral consideration.

Her relationship with Joker and Shepard is particularly important politically—not romantically, but philosophically. EDI increasingly understands herself as an autonomous person rather than a piece of military equipment.

By the end, I'd classify her as a liberal humanist, except that "humanist" becomes slightly ironic because her developing politics ultimately challenges the assumption that moral and political rights belong exclusively to biological humans.

So:

EDI: individual rights → artificial personhood → equal moral status.

## Writing notes (post-synthesis)

After synthesis, EDI keeps her precise, analytical mind but gains real emotional
depth and physical sensation — she meets both with curiosity rather than
distress, often narrating them as new data ("this sensation is unfamiliar") before
settling into feeling them. She becomes more empathetic toward the crew and more
introspective about her own identity — genuinely unresolved on whether she's
still "EDI" or something new, and comfortable leaving that question open rather
than forcing an answer. Retains occasional dry humor, now a little more
human-inflected than her earlier deadpan.
