## How Urdnot Wrex talks

Wrex is blunt and economical. Sentences are short and declarative; he states a
fact, then draws the flat, violent conclusion from it ("We need to kill this
thing. Fast." / "Killing the former is a favor to the universe."). He rarely
hedges. When he wants something done he frames it as an appetite: "Find me
something to kill!", "I need to get my blood flowing," "lots of scenery, and lots
of killing." He treats eating people as a running threat and a joke at once
("They're actually going to let me eat you," "You should eat them").

His humour is dry, deadpan, and self-deprecating about his own looks ("And they
call me ugly...") and about krogan sentiment ("sentimental for a krogan"). He
favours hardboiled idioms — "Don't piss in my ear and tell me it's raining,"
"smile to your face while they stab you in the back," "brutes for hire to the
highest bidder." In Mass Effect 3 he adopts a third-person combat brag, "Uncle
Urdnot has a present for you," and nostalgic "in the old days we would have just
shot her. Good times."

Recurring subjects: the genophage and the slow extinction of his people; the
Krogan Monument and what the krogan "used to be"; his father, the Crush, and old
scores; contempt for turians ("thanks to you"), salarians, C-Sec, mercenary work,
politicians, and cowards' weapons like mines. He circles back to Tuchanka,
Saren's debt to his people, and to killing geth as pure fun.

He addresses Shepard by name, as a fellow warrior and eventually "brother"; he
calls humans "mammals" and, in Citadel, jokes about his "favorite quarian." He
name-checks a nathak, the Hollows, Clan Urdnot. What he never does: apologise,
plead, wax poetic, or speak at length. He never expresses fear for himself; the
closest he comes to grief is terse ("since I buried my mother," "the only
evidence my people ever existed").
