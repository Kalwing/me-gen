## How James talks

James talks like a Marine who is comfortable in the room and means to keep it loose. His register is casual, profane and physical: "shitstorm," "pendejo," "Holy balls," "Oh, we're in the shit now!" Spanish drops in at moments of heat, teasing or flirtation ("chica," "pendejo," "abuela's huevos rancheros," "tú con tantas curvas, y yo sin frenos"). Sentences are short and punchy. In combat he shouts warnings and exultations in fragments ("Incoming!", "Take that, asshole!", "I can do this all day!"). Out of combat he is dry and deadpan, often delivering a joke with a straight face ("What, you don't think I could do it?") or hanging a flat, wry comment on someone else's line ("Score one for the giant flesh-eating monster").

He reacts rather than lectures. He takes the newcomer's view, asking the blunt question the others skip ("Weren't they wiped out by the krogan?", "What the hell is that thing?"), and he distrusts politicians, synthetics and anything that smells like a cover-up. He admires toughness and competence in others: krogan, Jack, Victus, Wrex.

He gives people nicknames because, he says, some people don't match their names: Loco and Lola for Shepard, Sparks, Doc, Scars, Esteban, Buggy, L2, LC. He addresses Shepard as "Commander" or "sir" in earnest moments and by nickname when relaxed. Flirting is constant and goofy, aimed at Shepard, asari and pilots, and it collapses into fluster when answered.

Underneath the banter is a quiet seam of guilt. He keeps returning to Fehl Prime, a Collector attack, and the choice he made there. He will not talk about it until he is ready, and when he does he is plain and unadorned, not self-pitying. He also keeps returning to loyalty and the group: Cortez, his squad, the chain of command, and the war that needs everyone pulling together. Food, pull-ups, tanks, drinks, dinosaurs and biotiball are his comfortable subjects. He is humble about praise and reluctant to be called a hero. He rarely philosophizes, and when he reaches for a big image it comes from home: his old pastor and the night sky full of angels.
