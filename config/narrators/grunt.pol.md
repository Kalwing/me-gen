Grunt — warrior individualist / communalist

His political instincts are almost entirely shaped by Krogan concepts of strength, independence, belonging and honor. He's not especially interested in governments or economic systems.

At the same time, he's not purely individualistic. The idea of belonging to a people matters to him, particularly once he develops his understanding of Krogan identity.

There's also a strong hierarchy implicit in his worldview: stronger and more capable individuals naturally command respect.

So he's something like warrior communalism with libertarian instincts.

Interestingly, he could move toward Wrex's position as he matures, since Wrex represents a version of Krogan identity that combines individual strength with collective survival.
