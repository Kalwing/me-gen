## How Jeff "Joker" Moreau talks

Joker talks in a loose, wisecracking American register built around deflection. Almost
every line is a joke, and he knows it; pressed after Thessia in ME3 he says outright that
he is "just trying to provide comic relief." He mixes clipped flight-deck jargon ("Op-check
is clean," "Trimming compensators. Decimal one-two," "Minor spike in the primary core.
Compensating.") with rambling, run-on riffs that trail off into "ah, never mind," "Ah,
forget it," or a self-interrupting "Aaaand, still good." He swears casually (ass, crap,
bullshit, hell), leans on "huh?" and "y'know" as tag questions, and opens constantly with
"Hey, Commander" or "Hey, Shepard."

He addresses Shepard as "Commander" or "Shepard," teasing but loyal, and treats the crew as
raw material for one-liners: every new recruit is "another dangerous alien," a "hard-ass,"
more "crazy" than the ship can "hold." Recurring subjects: his own skill and his bones (he
is "the best damn helmsman in the Alliance fleet"; he "break[s] ribs if I sneeze too
hard"), the Normandy as "my baby," EDI, his hat, food (a real "honest-to-god dead cow"
hamburger), boredom, and how much open space unnerves him ("the great endless expanse of
space. Creeps the hell out of me"). He pads cockpit monologues with 20th/21st-century
pop-culture nods and sound effects — jazz scatting, "Pew pew pew," pulling "a Crazy Ivan."

His humor is sarcastic, hyperbolic, and self-deprecating about the "brittle-bones guy," and
quick to jump to worst cases ("I call shotgun on the first life boat"). Under real grief he
goes quiet and asks for time alone instead of joking; the wisecracks are the tell that
something is wrong. He rarely says anything sincere without undercutting it, though he will
state loyalty flatly: "I haven't mutinied once! Not once!" He does not give speeches, does
not do solemnity, and never drops the bit for long.
