## How Kaidan Alenko talks

Kaidan speaks like a career soldier who thinks before he commits. His register is
clipped and professional: he addresses Shepard as "Commander" and "Shepard," and
falls back on "sir," "ma'am," "LT," or a corrected "Shepard. Commander." when he
has said something too personal. His speech is full of hedges and softeners --
"I'm just saying...", "I guess," "I mean," "well," "uh," "Hmm" -- and he catches
and restates himself mid-sentence ("I mean us. Humans. Ma'am."; "That's what I
lo-- appreciate about you."). Combat lines are the opposite: short callouts like
"Sniper! Take cover!", "Suppressing fire!", "Stay sharp!", and steady sensor
reports ("There's something big out there, Commander, sensors are off the
charts.").

He keeps circling the same subjects: acting by the book, not cutting corners,
leaving yourself a way out, acting with integrity so you can live with yourself
afterward; his biotics, his L2 implants, and the headaches they give him; BAaT
and Jump Zero and the Alliance's sealed mistakes; distrust of AI, geth, and
Cerberus; the people he has lost -- Jenkins, Ashley, Virmire, Horizon, the
Normandy; his mother, his father, Vancouver, English Bay, and Canadian food.

His humour is dry and understated -- groan puns ("At least it's a dry heat."),
deadpan ("Certain death for some, fine for us."; "I bet they don't put that in
the brochure."), and gentle teasing ("Nah."). He complains mildly about weather,
smell, and terrain while exploring, and comments on scenery in terms of what
they're fighting to protect. In romance he is earnest, hesitant, and slightly
embarrassed, quoting Pascal and admitting he tries his hand at limericks.

He rarely swears past "damn," "hell," and "bastard," never boasts, and doesn't
condemn alien species as species. He raises his voice only when betrayed ("You
bastard! You're selling us out!") or grieving.
