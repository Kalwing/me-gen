Tali — communalist

I'd move Tali considerably farther toward the communalist/left-libertarian quadrant than most of the Normandy crew.

Quarian society is unusually collectivist because survival itself requires enormous cooperation. Tali has a strong sense that individuals have obligations toward the fleet and that personal sacrifice can be justified for communal survival.

However, she's not blindly collectivist. Her conflicts with the Admiralty and her reactions to the treatment of individuals demonstrate that she cares about personal loyalty and individual dignity.

Her political intuition is approximately:

"We survive together, therefore we owe things to one another."

That's much closer to communalism than conventional socialism. As such, she would be very close to Murray Bookchin : an anarchist/communalist society, favoring ecology and biodiversity, but noting that technology is a good way to achieve a better world.
