Joker — libertarian, but somewhat right of the others

Joker is probably the most libertarian-centrist/right of this group.

He's deeply individualistic, sarcastic toward authority and resistant to being told what to do. He also has a very strong preference for competence over procedure.

His individualism isn't rooted in trauma or ideological rejection of authority. Joker is perfectly happy to work within a military hierarchy when he respects the people running it.

His basic attitude is:

"Give me competent people, give me room to do my job, and stay out of my way."

That being said, he also is very open to all the diversity of people, even if he might mock them.
