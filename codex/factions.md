# Factions

- After Commander Shepard's team killed Chief Weyrloc Guld and stopped Maelon's research, Clan Weyrloc was absorbed by Clan Urdnot under Wrex or largely wiped out under Wreav, surviving only as a diminished remnant that put a bounty on Maelon. (source: Clan Weyrloc)
- After Saren's 2183 attack C-Sec was heavily restructured with human intake; in 2186 Councilor Udina sent Captain Armando-Owen Bailey to investigate Executor Pallin on a manufactured case that Pallin had turned traitor, and Bailey killed him when he snapped and attacked — only for Udina himself to be exposed as Cerberus's inside man during its coup on the Citadel, which Bailey and Shepard broke by retaking C-Sec headquarters. (source: Citadel Security Services)
- Before first contact with the Citadel, the turians already had a single unified global culture built on hierarchical meritocracy, tempered by civic duty instilled from childhood, with military service treated as the highest form of merit (source: The Turian Hierarchy Before the Citadel — Early History).
- By 500 BCE the turians had mastered spaceflight, discovered several mass relays and founded colonies; expansion outran the Hierarchy's reach and colonial chieftains called Primarchs sought autonomy (source: The Turian Hierarchy Before the Citadel — Early History).
- C-Sec was proposed originally by the turians; recruits needed sponsorship by a Councilor or associate-race ambassador, and C-Sec frequently clashed with the Council's Spectres over the Spectres' freedom from due process. (source: Citadel Security Services)
- CAT6: private military corporation named for "Category 6", the term for soldiers dishonorably discharged from the Systems Alliance; its mercenaries had extensive criminal records and steroid-abuse histories and named squads after sharp objects (Rapier, Hatchet, Dagger, and the elite Razor squad). In 2186 CAT6 fought Shepard across the Citadel — an ambush at Ryuusei's Sushi Bar, the Archives, and the seizure of the Normandy SR-2 — as muscle for a dormant Lazarus Project Shepard clone and its true architect, the ex-Cerberus agent Maya Brooks; nearly all the mercenaries who fought Shepard were killed. Fielded Atlas mechs, CAT6 Heavies, Snipers, Specialists and Disruption Drones. (source: CAT6)
- Cerberus also grew a secret clone of Shepard as an organ source during the Lazarus Project; it was never used but was later awakened by Maya Brooks in 2186 to impersonate Shepard, and was stopped aboard the Citadel (source: Lazarus Project).
- Citadel Council: governing body of the Citadel and ultimate authority in Citadel space, an executive committee of one representative per member species, founded by the asari and salarians in 500 BCE (beginning the Galactic Standard calendar), joined by the turians with full membership by 900 CE, with humanity granted an embassy in 2165 CE; it judges Council law, settles disputes between governments, and acts through the Spectres, the Citadel Fleet (flagship Destiny Ascension), the Council Demilitarization Enforcement Mission, and the salarian Special Tasks Group, while holding no formal power over independent governments. Long dismissed the Reaper threat until the 2186 invasion; its members either died at the Battle of the Citadel (succeeded by Irissa, Esheel, and Quentius) or survived (Tevos, Valern, and Sparatus), with Anderson or Udina becoming the first human Councilor. (source: Citadel Council)
- Citadel Security Services (C-Sec) was the Citadel's volunteer police service, answering to the Citadel Council and headed by a turian Executor; it fielded roughly 200,000 mostly-turian constables (humans the largest minority, then salarians and asari) across Enforcement, Investigation, Customs, Network/E-Crimes, Special Response, and Patrol divisions, with the C-Sec Academy in the Lower Wards. (source: Citadel Security Services)
- Clan Urdnot is a krogan tribe on Tuchanka that by 2186 became the uncontested representative of krogan power to the galaxy, having absorbed or wiped out Clans Nakmor, Jurdon, Quash, and Raik; it is led by Urdnot Wrex or his brother Urdnot Wreav depending on Shepard's actions in Mass Effect. (source: Clan Urdnot)
- Clan Weyrloc is a large krogan tribe on Tuchanka tied to the Blood Pack, whose founder was a Weyrloc chief; in 2185 it was based in a hospital sponsoring Maelon's genophage-cure research, intending to outbreed Clan Urdnot and then wage war on the galaxy. (source: Clan Weyrloc)
- Dah'tan Manufacturing was a batarian computer-component corporation based on Camala; Dr. Shu Qian sourced components from it to avoid suspicion, and Edan Had'dah had the krogan Skarr burn the facility down to erase links to Sidon, with the badly burned batarian Jella the only one to survive the fire — though Saren Arterius later let her die during interrogation to cover his tracks. By 2185 it was expanding toward the Nemean Abyss. (source: Dah'tan Manufacturing)
- During the Reaper War, Aria T'Loak worked to unite Eclipse, Blood Pack and Blue Suns under her command, with Shepard able to help by freeing Jona Sederis from prison or elevating Sayn to lead Eclipse. (source: Eclipse)
- Eclipse favored stealth, biotics and cutting-edge equipment over discipline, was composed mainly of asari, salarians and humans, and fielded YMIR, FENRIS and LOKI Mechs. (source: Eclipse)
- Eclipse ran extensive smuggling operations, controlling nearly 20% of all smuggling on Omega; recruits on Illium were rumored to be required to commit a murder before receiving a uniform. (source: Eclipse)
- Eclipse was a mercenary corporation founded by asari commando Jona Sederis, hiring out to any employer regardless of scruples, with Sayn as her second-in-command. (source: Eclipse)
- Grim Skulls: small Skyllian Verge mercenary band of a few dozen trained thugs; wiped out by Saren Arterius on Juxhi while trying to buy weapons stolen from a turian freighter, a deal the Blue Suns had originally arranged before backing out to avoid Spectre attention. (source: Grim Skulls)
- HOME Group: incorporated collective holding assets around Earth, owner of the Home Away luxury station; linked to UNIN via board member Alis Silhaus, it funded the Alliance black site Quiet Eddy and orchestrated the initial theft of Alec Ryder's AI kernel, implanted in the rogue AI Medea; Cora Harper was sent to recover it but retrieval proved impossible in the wrecked base, and the kernel and Medea were destroyed with Quiet Eddy under orbital bombardment after Cora led the facility's survivors out. (source: HOME Group)
- Homeward Sol: human-centric grassroots movement opposing human expansion beyond the Sol system, made up of xenophobes and Earth-centrists, funded partly by lawsuits against provoked celebrities but capable of espionage and terrorism; in Initiation it harassed Cora Harper and bombed the Hyperion in an attempt to destroy Alec Ryder's SAM project. (source: Homeward Sol)
- In 2173 a Nashan Stellar Dynamics kickback scandal forced out party leader Inez Simmons; front-runner Claude Menneau then vanished en route to Shanxi, leaving Charles Saracino to run unopposed and win leadership (source: Terra Firma).
- In 2183, Administrator Anoleis temporarily shut down Synthetic Insights' Noveria offices over Qui'in's knowledge of the administrator's corruption. (source: Synthetic Insights, Ltd.)
- In 2185 Eclipse, Blood Pack and Blue Suns briefly allied under Eclipse's Jaroth to kill the vigilante Archangel on Omega, and separately plotted to overthrow Aria T'Loak. (source: Eclipse)
- In Mass Effect the STG 3rd Infiltration Regiment under Captain Kirrahe scouted Saren's base on Virmire; in Mass Effect 3 an STG facility on Sur'Kesh researched the genophage and held the surviving krogan females, and STG had earlier sabotaged the Shroud on Tuchanka to block cure dissemination. (source: Special Tasks Group)
- In Mass Effect, Saracino led a Terra Firma protest on the Citadel during Citadel: Our Own Worst Enemy and asked Shepard to endorse his bid for a political seat (source: Terra Firma).
- In Mass Effect: Retribution the Talons stopped paying Aria T'Loak her cut of their red sand operation; she raided their warehouse and negotiated an even larger share, which they accepted knowing they could not win (source: Talons).
- Known Shadow Broker associates included agents Barla Von, Brooks, Nyxeris, Tela Vasir, and Tazzik, former agents Feron and Fist, and hirelings such as the Blue Suns, Urdnot Wrex, and Zaeed Massani (source: Shadow Broker).
- Known Spectres include Beelo Gurji (the first, 693 CE), Nihlus Kryik (killed on Eden Prime), Jondum Bau, Tela Vasir (secretly a Shadow Broker agent), and Avitus Rix; only two people, both asari matriarchs, ever declined the offer. (source: Spectres)
- Liara T'Soni took over the Shadow Broker organization after killing the yahg, keeping the leadership change secret even from long-time underlings like Barla Von; in 2186 she crashed the Broker's ship into a Cerberus cruiser to thwart an Illusive Man takeover and ran operations from the Normandy SR-2 (source: Shadow Broker).
- Mercenaries operated wherever there was profit, hired for muscle, protection or assassination and often calling themselves "private security organisations"; they raided outposts and ships when unemployed and were most often turian, human, batarian or krogan. (source: Mercenaries)
- More than 50,000 years ago the early turians were known to the Protheans but dismissed as too primitive to matter, which spared them the Reaper harvest of that cycle (source: The Turian Hierarchy Before the Citadel — Early History).
- Most of the Lazarus Cell was killed when its medical officer Wilson turned the station's mechs on the staff; survivors Lawson, Jacob Taylor and the revived Shepard regrouped at Minuteman Station, then operated from the Normandy SR-2 with 24 new members to stop the Collectors (source: Lazarus Cell).
- Notable Alliance dissenters included the isolationist party Terra Firma and the Illusive Man's Cerberus; some L2 biotics opposed the Alliance over health damage from early Alliance-sponsored biotic research. (source: Systems Alliance)
- Notable Eclipse figures included Jaroth (Omega), Captain Enyala, Captain Wasea, Chief Roe (Bekenstein), Captain Vorleon (Helyme) and Morl (Lorek). (source: Eclipse)
- Notable merc organizations included the Blood Pack, Blue Suns, CAT6, Eclipse, Grim Angels, Grim Skulls (mostly wiped out by Saren Arterius in 2165), Talons, Three Sabers and Tiburón Rojo. (source: Mercenaries)
- Obscure before the 2185 Omega plague, the Talons filled the power vacuum left by the plague and Shepard's actions against the station's mercenary bands (source: Talons).
- Project Phoenix was one of the Illusive Man's early Cerberus experiments in enhanced human physiology, producing highly skilled combat and biotic specialists trained in Cerberus lash and stasis technology (source: Project Phoenix Adept).
- Reunited after the Unification Wars, the turian navy expanded to at least 32 fleets despite the Hierarchy having no enemies; around 704 CE it made first contact with the asari- and salarian-led Citadel Council, which offered immediate membership (source: The Turian Hierarchy Before the Citadel — Early History).
- Saren Arterius sabotaged David Anderson's Spectre evaluation with a falsified report; Commander Shepard became the first human Spectre in 2183 after exposing Saren, and if they survived Virmire, Kaidan Alenko or Ashley Williams becomes the second human Spectre by 2186. (source: Spectres)
- Shadow Broker Agents were the organization's rank-and-file soldiers, armed with M-8 Avengers and Flashbang Grenades, deployed on Illium and at the Hagalaz base (source: Shadow Broker Agent).
- Sisterhood Initiates were exclusively asari Eclipse mercenaries encountered only on Illium during Samara's recruitment mission, using biotic barriers instead of shields on higher difficulties (source: Sisterhood Initiate)
- Spectres (Special Tactics and Reconnaissance) are Citadel Council agents with supralegal authority and power of life and death, founded in 693 CE from salarian STG operatives and asari huntresses to counter krogan expansion; fewer than a hundred are believed to exist and all their records are sealed. (source: Spectres)
- Spectres answer only to the Council, have no command structure, and are hand-picked after proving exceptional ability; rogue Spectres are hunted by other Spectres, and status is lost when a Spectre is declared legally dead. (source: Spectres)
- Synthetic Insights products included the graybox (released 2160) and the SleepEasy Series security VIs; after the geth attack on the Citadel, falling trust in AI research damaged its reputation and it barely hired anyone by 2185. (source: Synthetic Insights, Ltd.)
- Synthetic Insights, Ltd. was one of only four corporations licensed by the Citadel Council to develop artificial intelligence, with operations on Illium and extensive, heavily secured holdings on Noveria managed at Port Hanshan by Lorik Qui'in. (source: Synthetic Insights, Ltd.)
- Talein's Daughters fought on Omega and Nos Astra and defended asari colonies from geth; in 2180 it accepted the biotic human Cora Harper via the Alliance's Valkyrie Program, under commander Nisira T'Kosh. (source: Talein's Daughters)
- Talein's Daughters was an asari commando unit based on Thessia that ran operations throughout asari space, with two-year tours; its motto was "I am a daughter of Talein. Against chaos—we stand. Against death—we refuse." (source: Talein's Daughters)
- Task Force Aurora was a Systems Alliance research group created by Admiral Hackett and headed by Dr. Garret Bryson to investigate pre-revelation legends and rumors about the Reapers (source: Task Force Aurora).
- Task Force Aurora's research into Leviathan, continued by Shepard after Bryson's murder, led through Mahavid and Namakli to first contact with the Leviathans on 2181 Despoina, who then aided the galaxy against the Reapers (source: Task Force Aurora).
- Terra Firma cited Shanxi and the First Contact War as proof aliens could not be trusted, marked Armistice Day with annual protests, opposed teaching alien languages in schools, and attracted xenophobes it declined to rein in on free-speech grounds (source: Terra Firma).
- Terra Firma was a human political party that opposed humanity's integration into the galactic community, founded after first contact with the turians out of concern that human individuality would be diluted (source: Terra Firma).
- The Alliance formed after the 2148 Mars Prothean ruins discovery: within a year Earth's eighteen largest nations ratified its charter and pooled their militaries; in 2149 it discovered the Charon Relay and began rapid interstellar expansion. (source: Systems Alliance)
- The Alliance gained a Citadel embassy in 2165 (first ambassador Anita Goyle) and, after Shepard exposed Saren, its first human Spectre in 2183; whether the Council was saved at the Battle of the Citadel determined if humanity gained a Council seat or unexpected military superiority. (source: Systems Alliance)
- The Alliance was a Treaty of Farixen signatory, restricted in dreadnoughts and compensating with treaty-exempt carriers; only about 3% of humans volunteered for service, earning it a "sleeping giant" reputation. (source: Systems Alliance)
- The Cerberus dossier's satirical "Illusive Man Profile" logged his cigarettes, drinks, perfect skyball games, tailored armored suit, celebrity liaisons, and messages sent to worlds across the galaxy (source: Shadow Broker Dossiers/Cerberus).
- The Lazarus Cell was a compartmentalized Cerberus cell led by Miranda Lawson under the Illusive Man, tasked with recovering and reviving the dead Commander Shepard aboard the Lazarus Research Station (source: Lazarus Cell).
- The Lazarus Project was the Cerberus effort that spent two years and over 4 billion credits rebuilding Shepard's corpse with cybernetic implants; the Illusive Man forbade a brain control chip, and Cronos Station records later confirmed no genetic or neural tampering (source: Lazarus Project).
- The League of One was an elite salarian espionage and assassination service of no more than a dozen agents, identified only by an induction medallion; after the Salarian Union declassified its files to reassure the asari, the exposed League murdered the salarian inner cabinet before being hunted down by the STG (source: League of One).
- The Migrant Fleet Marines were the quarian Migrant Fleet's military body for naval-infantry and police operations, led in the field by figures such as Kal'Reegar. (source: Migrant Fleet Marines)
- The Nemesis was a Cerberus sniper-specialist unit in Mass Effect 3 with implants to absorb M-98 Widow recoil for instant-kill shots; self-destruct mechanisms on death and Cerberus troops' preference for suicide over capture kept the Alliance from ever recovering an example of nemesis augmentation technology (source: Nemesis (enemy)).
- The Shadow Broker dossier on Cerberus described it as a rogue human-supremacist agency founded in 2157, active in Citadel and Terminus space and classed as a terrorist threat, and logged decades of covert operations: the Pragia facility (2160), media infiltration, Project DOORWAY forgery of Council ID, the 2168 Trident salarian-syndicate massacre, Project TRAPDOOR (omega-enkaphalin biotic suppression tests on asari), the assassination of Pope Clement XVI, and the FTL sabotage of the MSV Anixara (source: Shadow Broker Dossiers/Cerberus).
- The Shadow Broker maintained a private army whose Engineers (M-6 Carnifex, Combat Drone, Incinerate, Tech Armor), Heavies (ML-77 Missile Launcher) and Vanguards (M-27 Scimitar, Warp, Barrier) defended his interests on Illium and at his Hagalaz base, fought during the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission (source: Shadow Broker Engineer).
- The Shadow Broker organization maintained a private army whose troops wore black-and-white armor bearing a triangular red emblem resembling a yahg's maw, with Agents, Engineers, Heavies, Vanguards, and Rocket Drones (source: Shadow Broker).
- The Sisterhood Initiate's asari-only, Illium-only nature suggests Eclipse recruits from asari-dominated worlds join an Eclipse "sisterhood" rather than the mainstream organization (source: Sisterhood Initiate)
- The Sisterhood was an elite Eclipse cell recruiting young asari who had to perform one murder as initiation. (source: Eclipse)
- The Special Tasks Group (STG) is a salarian espionage organization of small independent cells performing counterterrorism, infiltration, reconnaissance, assassination, and sabotage, deployed by the Salarian Union or Citadel Council; seconded units are called Infiltration Regiments, fully government-funded unlike Spectres. (source: Special Tasks Group)
- The STG hunted down the League of One after the Council formed in 500 BCE; top agents are known only by codenames such as the Ever Alert and the Silent Step, and the Spectres were modeled on the STG. (source: Special Tasks Group)
- The Systems Alliance Charter was drafted and ratified in early 2149 by Earth's eighteen largest nations plus representatives from Mars and Luna; the founding nations ranged from the United North American States and the United Kingdom to China, India, Brazil, Nigeria and Russia, with Ireland joining a year later. (source: The Founding of the Systems Alliance)
- The Systems Alliance was the supranational government representing Earth and all human colonies in Citadel space, governed by a parliament at its capital Arcturus Station and headed by a Prime Minister (Amul Shastri as of 2186). (source: Systems Alliance)
- The Talons were a highly organized, red-facepainted mercenary organization based on a mostly turian district of Omega, dealing in drugs, weapons smuggling, extortion, contract killing, slave trading, and charging ships to dock away from Aria T'Loak's reach (source: Talons).
- The UNAS was invaded by the Reapers in 2186 CE; its cities, including Fort Worth and the Texan Megapolis, were bombarded, and a communications blackout left the state of the occupation largely unknown (source: United North American States)
- The UNAS was one of eighteen nations that ratified the Systems Alliance charter in 2149 CE after the Mars Prothean technology discovery, and contributed military assets to the Alliance military (source: United North American States)
- The United North American States (UNAS) was an Earth coalition formed around 2096 CE from the merger of Canada, Mexico, and the United States; secessionist opposition, and the destruction of the Statue of Liberty by the extremist group Freedoms First on November 1, 2096, triggered the Second American Civil War (source: United North American States)
- UNAS President Enrique Aguilar was assassinated in 2176 CE alongside Chinese People's Federation premier Ying Xiong by dissident Michael Moser Lang, who used an M-12 Locust hidden in a video camera; the 2185 case Ford v. Huerta, over President Christopher Huerta's legitimacy after a stroke, was decided 5-4 in Huerta's favor by the Systems Alliance (source: United North American States)
- Under Nyreen Kandros, whose leadership of the Talons was concealed behind the invented figurehead "Derius" until she revealed herself as their real leader in 2186, the Talons became an organized resistance force protecting Omega's people; after the station's liberation they became its new security force (source: Talons).
- Under Urdnot Wrex, Clan Urdnot became a reformist faction holding neutral ground open to all krogan and redirecting research from weapons to crop genetics and medicine; under Urdnot Wreav it remained an aggressive traditional clan that stockpiled weapons of mass destruction. (source: Clan Urdnot)
- Unlike militaries, mercenaries sourced weapons and armor from many manufacturers rather than a single company, and tended to use superior equipment. (source: Mercenaries)
- When the Illusive Man turned to indoctrination to ensure his troops' loyalty, many Project Phoenix operatives defected and joined the Systems Alliance to fight the Reapers (source: Project Phoenix Vanguard).
