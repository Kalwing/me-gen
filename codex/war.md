# War and Warfare

- Admiral Kastanie Drescher's Second Fleet counterattacked and drove the turians off Shanxi; the turians mobilized for full-scale war, but the Citadel Council, alarmed at Alliance military capability, intervened and brokered a truce (source: Shanxi).
- After batarian-backed pirates attacked Elysium in the Skyllian Blitz, the Alliance ran suppression campaigns in the 2170s, destroying pirate bases on Torfan and later setting listening posts in the Styx Theta cluster; the quarian Migrant Fleet answered privateer raids mercilessly. (source: Pirates)
- After the war the Citadel Council prohibited activating any dormant mass relay leading to an uncharted system. (source: Rachni Wars)
- Against the Reapers the Protheans' unified command and single military doctrine became fatal weaknesses; they fought world to world, sacrificing whole colonies to buy regroup time, while indoctrinated traitors betrayed their hiding places. (source: Prothean)
- Alliance military doctrine bypasses enemy strongpoints to strike rear areas; its elite are drawn through Interplanetary Combatives Training, whose graduates earn the N7 designation, and organized under the Military Vocational Code and the N-designation system. (source: Codex/Humanity and the Systems Alliance)
- Alliance military doctrine emphasised fire support, flexibility, speed and individual initiative, bypassing enemy strongpoints to strike supply lines and headquarters while leaving only token scouting garrisons on colonies and massing powerful fleets at mass relay nexuses. (source: Systems Alliance)
- An Eclipse smuggling depot on Daratar used a "Farewell Protocol" that scuttled the operation when detected, its mercenaries fleeing in Mantis Gunships while three YMIR Mechs were left to destroy the cargo before it could be captured. (source: N7: Eclipse Smuggling Depot)
- Asari field no conventional army: huntresses fight as small special-operations and commando units skilled in ambush and assassination, backed by a powerful navy including the dreadnought Destiny Ascension. The Justicar Order acts as extra-judicial executioners bound by the Oaths of Subsumation. (source: Codex/Aliens: Council Races; Asari)
- At Cronos Station Shepard recovered the Prothean VI Vendetta, learned the Catalyst was the Citadel, and killed Kai Leng, but the Illusive Man had already fled to the Citadel and alerted the Reapers, who seized it and moved it to Earth. (source: Priority: Cerberus Headquarters)
- At least once a year a Terminus Systems fleet raided the Attican Traverse with small strikes on poorly defended colonies; the Citadel Council rarely retaliated, fearing that patrols into the Terminus would unite its species and trigger a long, costly war (source: Terminus Systems).
- At the 2183 Battle of the Citadel the rogue Spectre Saren Arterius attacked through a hidden relay with a geth fleet, krogan, and his flagship Sovereign; the Destiny Ascension evacuated the Council while the Wards sealed, and Commander Shepard followed from Ilos and reopened the arms. In the Paragon account Admiral Hackett's Fifth Fleet saved the Destiny Ascension and destroyed Sovereign at heavy cost; in the Renegade account the fleet let the Council die and humanity filled the vacuum. (source: Codex/Citadel and Galactic Government)
- At the First Contact War the Alliance navy numbered over 200 vessels; by 2183 it was one of the greater military forces in Citadel space, with Treaty of Farixen dreadnought limits offset by treaty-exempt carriers. (source: Systems Alliance)
- At Virmire, Saren was found to be breeding a genophage-cured krogan army meant to be destroyed with the base; Captain Kirrahe refused to launch the assault until Shepard personally talked down (or killed) a furious Wrex alone on the beach, with Ashley and Kaidan standing by — a confrontation that could end in Wrex's death or his survival as leader of Clan Urdnot (source: Virmire: Wrex and the Genophage).
- Batarian radicals seized an Alliance colony's Javelin Mk.II missile base on the moon Franklin (Skepsis system) and launched missiles at the colony on Watson; Shepard deactivated one missile and had to choose which district the last kill-switch code would spare, the spaceport or the residential core (source: N7: Javelin Missiles Launched).
- Because faster-than-light attackers arrive before luminal sensors can detect them, fleets defend by surrounding themselves with spheres of scouting frigates that relay warnings; reconnaissance is the frigate's most important role (source: Starships)
- Before the Reaper invasion the quarian Admiralty Board voted to retake Rannoch, opening with precision strikes on four geth systems; the geth then accepted Reaper upgrades, which neutralized quarian countermeasures and turned the war. (source: Quarian)
- Biotic salarians were considered too valuable for combat and were assigned to the intelligence services rather than the front line. (source: Salarian)
- By 2186 the quarian Migrant Fleet armed every Civilian Fleet ship, including liveships, with Thanix cannons, effectively making them dreadnoughts; Admiral Han'Gerrel vas Neema argued the quarians were no longer bound since their embassy was closed, while Shala'Raan vas Tonbay claimed liveships were built for food cultivation, not combat (source: Treaty of Farixen).
- By the 2186 Reaper invasion the Thanix and its variants were in widespread use across Alliance fleets, with an up-scaled version built for dreadnoughts and a ground-based Thanix missile deployed during the final battle for Earth (source: Thanix).
- Captain Kirrahe's survival on Virmire depended entirely on Shepard destroying the geth flyers guarding the refueling platform; ignoring them killed Kirrahe and his fate reverberated through Mass Effect 2 and 3 (source: Virmire: Assisting Kirrahe's Team).
- Carriers stay clear of gun range and fight through fighters carrying disruptor torpedoes; a single torpedo down the flight deck can gut a carrier, so most seal the deck during battle (source: Starships)
- Cerberus recruited on Omega by offering combat training and gene therapy packages, and framed its security crackdowns as defense against Reaper incursions (source: Mass Effect 3: Omega/Fight for Omega).
- Citadel space combat rests on mass accelerators, whose recoil (equal to impact energy) is the prime limiting factor; kinetic barriers deflect fast-moving objects but not heat, toxins or radiation; GARDIAN lasers handle point defense; and disruptor torpedoes and the hull-mounted Javelin torpedo rack close the range. Thermal clips, a geth innovation adopted galaxy-wide, followed the geth's conclusion that firefights are won by whoever puts the most rounds downrange fastest. (source: Codex/Weapons, Armor and Equipment; Codex/Ships and Vehicles)
- Dreadnoughts are a hoarded strategic resource committed only to critical battles; the Treaty of Farixen stipulates how many each navy may field, with the turian peacekeeping fleet permitted the most (source: Starships)
- During the Battle of the Citadel in 2183 the Destiny Ascension, carrying the evacuated Council, had its main drives disabled by geth cyber warfare and massed force and had to be rescued (or was left to be destroyed) by the Alliance Fifth Fleet at Shepard's choice. (source: The Destiny Ascension — Flagship of the Citadel Fleet)
- During the Krogan Rebellions, after the genophage was released, a salarian STG team sabotaged the Shroud so that any genophage cure could not be easily disseminated through Tuchanka's atmosphere (source: Shroud)
- During the quarian campaign to retake Rannoch, the Reaper-upgraded geth trapped the quarian Migration Fleet; Shepard boarded a geth dreadnought (Priority: Geth Dreadnought) and shut down its Reaper control signal by freeing Legion from the drive core, where it had been wired in as the signal's distribution hardware. (source: Priority: Geth Dreadnought)
- During the Reaper War, Admiral Hackett assigned Commander Shepard a series of Alliance special ops against Cerberus: destroying a fighter base on Noveria, neutralizing a cannon installation and cruiser on Tuchanka, raiding a Cerberus laboratory on Sanctum, defending abducted civilians on Benning, and holding a communications hub on Ontarom, each yielding an Alliance war asset. (source: N7: Cerberus Attack)
- During the Virmire assault Shepard activated a Prothean beacon in Saren's lab and learned from a hologram that Sovereign was not a Reaper ship but an actual Reaper (source: Virmire: Assault).
- Eclipse mercenaries captured an Eldfell-Ashland mining facility on Helyme to hunt the cargo ship Ter Alan, which was later found wrecked with no survivors on a planet in the Dranek system. (source: N7: Captured Mining Facility)
- Elysium was held by Alliance Marines on leave and armed civilians on the ground while the Alliance Navy, including the SSV Agincourt, destroyed the pirate fleet overhead; the defenders held for hours until reinforcements arrived and the enemy fled. (source: Skyllian Blitz)
- Garrus's Archangel squad was destroyed when Sidonis, captured by the Blue Suns, lured him away to the Kenzo District so the gangs could bomb the hideout and gun down the rest of the team (source: Mass Effect: Homeworlds 3).
- Geth military doctrine emphasizes ambush and surprise; the geth expend mobile platforms freely because a destroyed program is archived and re-downloaded. (source: Geth)
- Humanity joined the treaty in 2183 and gained the Council 3:5 ratio after its Council seat; if the Council died aboard the Destiny Ascension, the turians declared themselves no longer bound and expanded shipbuilding (source: Treaty of Farixen).
- If the Destiny Ascension survived the Battle of the Citadel it was upgraded and finally used for large-scale ship-to-ship combat in the battle to retake Earth; if destroyed, the asari ceded their share of Citadel defense duty to the turians. (source: The Destiny Ascension — Flagship of the Citadel Fleet)
- In 2185 Shepard and Cerberus ran N7 assignments against Terminus mercenary bands: disrupting a Blood Pack communications relay and mining operation on Tarith that was feeding a planned large-scale invasion staged from Zada Ban, and shutting down a Blue Suns false-distress-beacon base on Sanctum that lured and raided merchant freighters. (source: N7: Blood Pack Communications Relay)
- In 2186 Cerberus, under General Oleg Petrovsky, invaded and occupied the space station Omega after luring Aria T'Loak away, imposing martial law, curfews and district lockdowns; a rebellion led by Nyreen Kandros's Talon mercenary gang fought back, and Aria allied with Commander Shepard to retake the station (source: Mass Effect 3: Omega).
- In 2186 the Alliance fought a losing battle at Arcturus Station against about a dozen Reaper capital ships while the main Reaper fleet bypassed it toward Sol; Admiral Hackett abandoned the station and sacrificed the Second Fleet so the Third and Fifth Fleets could escape. (source: Arcturus Station)
- In every war it fought, the salarian military struck first without warning, executing preemptive strikes hours ahead of an enemy attack and never issuing an official declaration of war. (source: Salarian)
- In fleet combat, frigate "wolf packs" of four to six speed through enemy formations to hunt ships whose kinetic barriers have been dropped by fighter-launched disruptor torpedoes, while cruiser squadrons screen the dreadnought battle line's flanks (source: Starships)
- In Mass Effect 3, if the quarians survived their war with the geth, Kal'Reegar and his Migrant Fleet Marine squad were reported killed on Palaven defending a turian communications relay tower. (source: Migrant Fleet Marines)
- Krogan forces drove the rachni to their homeworld Suen, bombarded its cities, and detonated bombs in the underground nests, creating massive sinkholes; the rachni were declared extinct in 300 CE, with a Council listening post left in the home system. (source: Rachni Wars)
- Military planners in the Reaper War rejected rupturing mass relays (each detonation devastates its own solar system) and using starships as FTL projectiles (mass effect drives carry hardwired anti-collision safeties). (source: Codex/The Reaper War)
- N7 designation marked the Alliance's special forces, trained at the Interplanetary Combatives Academy in Rio de Janeiro; candidates had to survive combat "in an admirable and effective fashion" to earn the rank, and during the Reaper War many N7s trained other species in anti-Reaper asymmetric warfare. (source: N7 Fury Adept)
- N7 Fury operatives used implants to fuel biotics and extreme movement speed, moving in and out of combat before unleashing sweeping biotic attacks, including short-range combat teleports. (source: N7 Fury Adept)
- N7 soldiers were the Alliance's go-anywhere special forces, trained at the Interplanetary Combatives Academy in Rio de Janeiro and required to survive combat "in an admirable and effective fashion"; during the Reaper War many trained other species in anti-Reaper asymmetric warfare, including implant-enhanced sword specialists like the N7 Slayer (source: N7 Slayer Vanguard).
- Negotiation with the rachni was impossible because the hive queens dwelt in deep nests on worlds no outsider could survive, so the Citadel races concluded the species could only be stopped by war. (source: Rachni Wars)
- Notable salarian units included the 3rd Infiltration Regiment, the First Fleet, and the Third Fleet. (source: Salarian)
- Occupying turians on Shanxi faced their own logistical crisis because their biology required all food to be imported, and they wrongly believed they had defeated the bulk of humanity's navy (source: Shanxi).
- On Eden Prime (Priority: Eden Prime) Cerberus attacked and occupied the colony to seize a Prothean artifact, which proved to be the living Prothean Javik in a damaged stasis pod, recovered by Shepard after fighting through Cerberus infantry and an Atlas mech. (source: Priority: Eden Prime)
- On Haestrom in 2185, a company of geth wiped out Kal'Reegar's Migrant Fleet Marine escort protecting Tali'Zorah's science team, leaving Reegar its sole survivor and himself in danger fighting a self-repairing Geth Colossus until Shepard ordered him to stand down; a separate marine squad died trying to retake the geth-overrun ship Alarei. (source: Migrant Fleet Marines)
- On Omega in 2185, Garrus Vakarian operated as the vigilante "Archangel," building a twelve-member squad that declared war on the station's criminal gangs while avoiding civilian casualties, and could not be stopped even after the gangs united against him (source: Mass Effect: Homeworlds 3).
- One week before the SSV Normandy's destruction, Aria T'Loak and her batarian bodyguards ambushed and wiped out a Blue Suns–Collector meeting on Omega where humans were being sold to the Collectors, losing one bodyguard (source: Mass Effect: Incursion).
- Phantoms projected localized biotic barriers granting brief power immunity, were immune to shock-stun effects, cryo-freezing and grab kills, and used suit thrusters to recover instantly from knockdown; the Alliance advised confirming each Phantom's death personally. (source: Phantom)
- Pirates operated across the lawless Terminus Systems, scavenging shipping lanes, raiding outposts, and running slaving and gun-running, hiding on uncharted worlds and following only leaders who proved their ruthlessness. (source: Pirates)
- Priority: Earth was the final battle of the Reaper War: allied fleets engaged the Reapers above Earth while the ground force "Hammer" tried to land troops at the Citadel transport beam, first requiring destruction of a Reaper "Hades Cannon" anti-aircraft gun with M-920 Cain shots. (source: Priority: Earth)
- Priority: Mars was the opening engagement of the Reaper War for the Normandy: Shepard recovered the Prothean Crucible data from the Mars archives and clashed with Cerberus for the first time, learning Cerberus troops were implanted with Reaper technology (source: Priority: Mars).
- Priority: Palaven saw Shepard fight Reaper ground forces on Palaven's moon Menae, discover Primarch Fedorian was dead, and secure General Adrien Victus as the new turian Primarch and Hierarchy representative for the war summit (source: Priority: Palaven).
- Priority: Perseus Veil: the quarian Migrant Fleet admirals revealed they had launched an open war on the geth and pushed them back toward Rannoch, after which the Reapers upgraded the geth; they asked the Normandy to disable a geth dreadnought broadcasting the Reaper signal (source: Priority: Perseus Veil).
- Priority: Rannoch: Shepard used a prototype targeting laser to guide Normandy and quarian fleet fire that destroyed a Reaper base controlling the geth and killed a Reaper Destroyer on the ground, then mediated the final quarian-geth confrontation, which could end in the extinction of either race or, with sufficient reputation and trust, a lasting peace with both fleets joining the war (source: Priority: Rannoch).
- Priority: Sur'Kesh was a Cerberus assault on the salarian STG base where Shepard extracted the last surviving fertile krogan female ("Eve"), the key to a genophage cure demanded as the price of krogan aid against the Reapers (source: Priority: Sur'Kesh).
- Priority: The Citadel I: the Citadel Council, with their own worlds under Reaper attack, refused to commit military aid to Earth or to help build the Prothean device, and Shepard's Spectre status was reinstated under Alliance Emergency War Powers (source: Priority: The Citadel I).
- Priority: Thessia: a Reaper assault on the asari homeworld during which Shepard learned the asari had secretly based their advanced development on a hidden Prothean Beacon in the Temple of Athame; Cerberus assassin Kai Leng killed the science team, fought Shepard, and stole the Prothean VI Vendetta (source: Priority: Thessia).
- Priority: Tuchanka: the krogan attacked a Reaper Destroyer occupying the Shroud tower with turian air support; Shepard activated the ancient Maw Hammers to summon Kalros, the mother of all thresher maws, which dragged the Reaper beneath Tuchanka, allowing the genophage cure (or a sabotaged fake) to be dispersed from the Shroud (source: Priority: Tuchanka).
- Quarian law parcels out marine squads as police aboard the Fleet, and exiles or executes those who endanger the whole Flotilla; treason trials use the Admiralty Board as judges. (source: Quarian)
- Reaper ground forces include husks (impaled humans fitted with cybernetics), banshees (from Ardat-Yakshi asari), brutes (turian-krogan fusions), cannibals (batarians that heal by eating the dead), marauders (turians that armor and command lesser troops), ravagers (rachni turned into walking artillery), and harvesters (winged flyers whose appearance signals an invasion). Their capital-ship barriers typically fail under four attacking dreadnoughts, and heat-focused weapons such as the Thanix are disproportionately effective. (source: Codex/The Reapers)
- Salarian warfare runs through the intelligence services: the Special Tasks Group operates covert cells, and the earlier assassination team the League of One vanished after being declassified, reportedly killing the government's inner cabinet on its way out. (source: Codex/Aliens: Council Races)
- Shepard's team destroyed Saren's Virmire base with the Normandy's drive core converted into a nuclear bomb, placed by the base's geothermal taps; Kirrahe's diversion split the squad between the bomb site and the AA tower, and when both came under overwhelming attack one of Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko had to be left behind — Ashley stayed with the bomb and died in its detonation while Shepard reached Kaidan at the AA tower in time (source: Virmire: Assault).
- Signing the Treaty of Farixen was a requirement for any race seeking a Citadel embassy and associate Council membership; carriers, though as large as dreadnoughts, were not covered by it (source: Treaty of Farixen).
- STG Project Firebreak, detailed in Mordin Solus's Shadow Broker dossier, was a covert drop on Tuchanka that dispersed a genophage-modification payload into a Weyrloc agricenter's soil, water and food supplies (source: Shadow Broker Dossiers/Dr. Mordin Solus).
- The 2170 batarian slaver raid on the human colony Mindoir killed most colonists and left survivors fitted with cranial control implants; an Alliance Marine relief force was pinned down by the raiders' defenses and could not reach the victims. (source: Mindoir)
- The Alliance assault on Cerberus Headquarters (Priority: Cerberus Headquarters) targeted Cronos Station, orbiting Anadius in the Horse Head Nebula; Hackett treated it as the opening phase of the assault on the Reapers because it exposed the Crucible, and it was the point of no return in Mass Effect 3. (source: Priority: Cerberus Headquarters)
- The Arrival: an Alliance deep-cover team on asteroid 157-Golgotha, indoctrinated by the Reaper artifact Object Rho, was stopped by Shepard, who launched the asteroid into the Alpha Relay; the resulting explosion destroyed the relay and the entire Bahak system, killing its batarian population, to delay the Reaper invasion (source: Project Base).
- The assault on Saren's base on Virmire (2183) paired a diversionary frontal strike by Captain Kirrahe's three salarian STG fireteams (Aegohr, Mannovai, and Jaëto, named for the salarians' first colony worlds) with Commander Shepard's covert infiltration from the rear (source: Virmire: Assault).
- The Battle of Palaven opened with Fleet Admiral Irix Coronati's "Fifteen-Minute Plan," using the carriers Undaunted and Resolute for drone reconnaissance before jumping dreadnoughts into the Reaper fleet; Reaper destroyers then jumped to Palaven and forced a costly ground war and turian retreat. The later Miracle at Palaven leaked a false battle plan, used the dreadnought Indomitable as bait, and had landed krogan commandos smuggle warp bombs and fission weapons into Reaper ships opened to indoctrinate turian leaders, destroying many at the cost of the captives inside. (source: Codex/The Reaper War)
- The Battle of Shanxi bred lasting human-turian mistrust but also impressed the galaxy, since apart from the krogan the turians had previously found no equals in war (source: Shanxi).
- The Benning abductions and continued civilian attacks were attributed by Hackett to a rogue Cerberus group; Shepard's intervention let the Alliance retake the colony and gain the Arcturus First Division. (source: N7: Cerberus Abductions)
- The Blitz's failure led two years later to the Alliance's retaliatory strike on Torfan against batarian pirate bases; a War Hero Shepard earned the Star of Terra defending Elysium, while a Ruthless Shepard fought at Torfan. (source: Skyllian Blitz)
- The Blue Suns base on Sanctum was defended by two YMIR Mechs and Captain Narom; interrogation logs there showed the Suns had tortured a client's agent to locate a Prothean dig site and then seized it for themselves. (source: N7: Blue Suns Base)
- The Cerberus occupation of Omega featured a communication war: Petrovsky's official Cerberus citizen feed issued directives and propaganda while the Talons broadcast on low-tech microwave emissions and recruited civilian "Codebreakers" to crack Cerberus's substitution-cipher orders; fighting centered on the Fumi and Zeta Districts with Kandros acting as a decoy to cover civilian evacuations (source: Mass Effect 3: Omega/Fight for Omega).
- The Citadel Conventions, adopted after the Krogan Rebellions, regulate weapons of mass destruction in four tiers, and the Treaty of Farixen fixes a 5:3:1 dreadnought ratio among turians, other Council races and associate members. Relay activation was likewise restricted after the Rachni Wars. (source: Codex/Citadel and Galactic Government; First Contact War)
- The Earth assault's second phase used Thanix missiles coordinated through EDI to destroy a Reaper Destroyer guarding the conduit; Harbinger then descended and routed Hammer, leaving a wounded Shepard to reach the beam alone. (source: Priority: Earth)
- The First Contact War (2157) began when a human scout fleet activating a dormant mass relay met a turian patrol; the turians invaded Shanxi, the Alliance took control of the human response, and the roughly three-month conflict cost 623 human lives before the Citadel Council brokered peace. (source: Systems Alliance)
- The First Contact War (2157) began when a turian patrol fleet destroyed an Alliance expedition at the Shanxi-Theta relay; the turians then captured Shanxi after a guerrilla siege in which they dropped orbital debris to level city blocks and starve out marine fire teams, forcing the garrison to surrender (source: Shanxi).
- The First Contact War, called the Relay 314 Incident by the turians, was a three-month 2157 conflict that began when turian patrols fired on humans reactivating the dormant Relay 314, an act forbidden since the Rachni Wars. The turians besieged and took the colony Shanxi until General Williams surrendered it; a month later Admiral Kastanie Drescher's Second Fleet retook the world and the Council brokered peace. Only 623 humans died, but the war fixed humanity's reputation as aggressors, fed Alliance xenophobia and the rise of Terra Firma and Cerberus, and left Saren Arterius with a lasting hatred of humanity after his brother's death. (source: First Contact War)
- The Genophage was a biological weapon that reduced the krogan not in fertility but in viable pregnancies, leaving most fetuses without nervous systems; every cell was infected to defeat gene therapy. The salarians built it as an undeployed deterrent; the turians deployed it immediately during the Krogan Rebellions. Centuries of decline bred a dangerous fatalism among the krogan. In Mass Effect 2 Mordin Solus engineered an updated variant; in Mass Effect 3 a cure derived from Maelon's research could be dispersed through the Shroud on Tuchanka, over salarian sabotage. (source: Genophage)
- The Morning War, called the Geth War by others, broke out when the quarians, recognizing that their networked geth had become sentient, ordered every geth program terminated around 1895 CE; the geth, joined by no one, won within a year, overran Rannoch and the quarian colonies, and killed all but a few million quarians. The survivors fled aboard the Migrant Fleet and were stripped of their Citadel embassy; the geth did not pursue, retreating behind the Perseus Veil and destroying any vessel that entered for three centuries. (source: Geth War)
- The Phantom was a Cerberus combat unit of the Reaper War, an agile biotic cyborg built from reverse-engineered Reaper tech, fighting with monomolecular blades and a gauntlet-mounted kinetic weapon and able to cloak to regenerate health and barriers. (source: Phantom)
- The Protheans united all organic life under one empire to fight a hostile machine intelligence, holding it off in the long conflict known as the Metacon War. (source: Prothean)
- The quarian-geth war (Morning War) killed billions of quarians and drove the survivors from Rannoch; the geth ceased pursuit once the quarians fled far enough to no longer register as a threat. (source: Quarian)
- The Rachni Wars began around 1 CE after salarian explorers opened a relay into rachni space; the rachni reverse-engineered FTL and expanded rapidly, overwhelming Citadel defenses with sheer numbers. (source: Rachni)
- The Rachni Wars pitted the Citadel races against the hive-minded rachni, and led the salarians to uplift the krogan as shock troops; the war's end brought the Citadel Conventions restricting relay activation and the public founding of the Spectres. The subsequent Krogan Rebellions, driven by krogan overpopulation and expansion, ran roughly a century until the turians deployed the salarian-built genophage. (source: Codex/Aliens: Non-Council Races; Codex/Citadel and Galactic Government)
- The Rachni Wars prompted the Citadel races to curb their rapid expansion for fear of triggering another galactic war. (source: Rachni)
- The Rachni Wars were a series of conflicts beginning around 1 CE after a Citadel Council expedition activated a dormant mass relay into rachni space; the rachni reverse-engineered captured FTL drives, built fleets, and overwhelmed Council defenses with sheer numbers for nearly a century. (source: Rachni Wars)
- The Reaper War began in 2186 when the Reapers arrived through batarian space and took Earth within hours, jumping relay to relay to bypass the Sixth and Seventh Fleets while capital ships destroyed the First and Fourth Fleets and severed Earth's communications. They struck the batarian homeworld Khar'shan, hit turian space at Taetrus (the Vallum Blast), and ground through the Battles of Palaven, Rannoch and Thessia and the Cerberus Coup on the Citadel. Intelligence estimated more than 400 processor ships on Earth killing about 1.86 million humans a day, projecting the planet's depopulation within a decade. (source: Codex/The Reaper War; Codex/The Reapers)
- The salarian military was a small volunteer force focused on maneuver warfare and the doctrine that a war should be won before it begins, relying on unmatched intelligence rather than numbers. (source: Salarian)
- The salarian Special Tasks Group (STG) was the field arm of salarian intelligence, and its effectiveness during the Krogan Rebellion provided the template for the Council's Spectre program. (source: Salarian)
- The salarians cultivated a strong alliance with the turians so that any threat to salarian space risked turian intervention. (source: Salarian)
- The salarians ended the stalemate by culturally uplifting the krogan, relocating them from ruined Tuchanka and arming them as reinforcements whose rapid breeding and toughness let them strike the queens in their lairs. (source: Rachni Wars)
- The salarians uplifted the krogan in 80 CE to fight the rachni; the krogan struck the queens in their lairs, bombarded Suen, and detonated bombs in the nests, with the rachni declared extinct in 300 CE. (source: Rachni)
- The Shroud on Tuchanka was built as part of a salarian scheme to uplift the krogan during the Rachni Wars, when a losing Council traded technology for krogan foot soldiers (source: Shroud)
- The Skyllian Blitz (2176 CE) was a large pirate, slaver, and batarian-warlord assault on the human colony Elysium in the Skyllian Verge, funded partly by batarian financiers as reprisal for human expansion and instigated by Elanos Haliat to make himself leader of the Terminus pirate bands. (source: Skyllian Blitz)
- The Skyllian Blitz of 2176 was a pirate and slaver assault on the human colony Elysium, funded by the batarian Hegemony after the Council refused in 2171 to declare the Skyllian Verge a batarian interest zone; the Alliance answered in 2178 with its costly assault on Torfan. (source: Codex/Aliens: Non-Council Races)
- The Treaty of Farixen is modeled on the 1922 Washington Naval Treaty, and the unrestricted growth of carriers parallels that treaty's unintended boost to aircraft carrier development (source: Treaty of Farixen).
- The Treaty of Farixen was an arms-control agreement among the major Citadel races, set at the Farixen Naval Conference, limiting dreadnought construction to a 5:3:1 ratio of turian to Council (asari/salarian) to associate-race ships (source: Treaty of Farixen).
- The turian military was the center of turian society and an all-encompassing public works organization, providing police, fire, engineering and merchant-marine functions as well as armed force (source: Turian).
- The turian navy was divided into at least 32 fleets, was allotted more dreadnoughts than any race by the Treaty of Farixen (37 in 2183, 39 by 2185), contributed the largest portion of the Citadel Fleet, and fielded weapons such as the Phaeston, Krysae and ML-77 (source: Turian).
- The turian Unification Wars began around 300 BCE, sparked by colonial nationalism but fought over disputed territory; the Hierarchy stayed officially neutral, keeping to strict diplomacy, until mounting turian casualties forced it to intervene and compel the weakened Primarch chieftains to surrender (source: The Turian Hierarchy Before the Citadel — Early History).
- The Unification War was a turian civil war contemporaneous with the founding of the Citadel Council, ending with the Hierarchy reasserting control; a lasting cultural bias against biotic soldiers dates to it, and the Hierarchy still keeps its elite biotic Cabals (10-15 soldiers under a kabalim) isolated from regular units. (source: Codex/Aliens: Council Races; Codex/Omega: Reclaimed)
- The Vanguard was a front-line combat class combining Soldier weapons training with Adept biotics, using biotic charge (via L5n implants in later games) to close distance and destroy enemies at short range; all Krogan Battlemasters, including Urdnot Wrex, belonged to this class (source: Vanguard).
- The Virmire mission chain began after two of the plot worlds Therum, Noveria, or Feros were completed, when a salarian STG recon team under Captain Kirrahe sent only static from Virmire; Kirrahe had already lost half his men (source: Virmire (mission)).
- The War on Taetrus (2185) followed the Vallum Blast, in which the turian separatist group Facinus rammed a starship into the colony's capital; the Turian Hierarchy and Taetrian forces invaded separatist strongholds and eliminated Facinus in a short, decisive campaign, aided by fortifications from an earlier civil war. (source: Taetrus)
- Theories on the war's cause include Reaper indoctrination (the Rachni Queen described being forced to "resonate with its own sour, yellow note") and Dr. Garret Bryson's unconfirmed idea that the Leviathan drove the rachni to build an anti-Reaper army. (source: Rachni Wars)
