# Social

- Aboard the Normandy SR-2, Crewman Vadim Rolston confided his fears about his family's safety to his friend Crewman Sarah Patel, who promised that if he died she would make sure his daughter knew how amazing her father was; nearby, Crewmen Jenny Goldstein and Thomas Hawthorne shared meals while loudly grousing to Mess Sergeant Gardner about the food, only warming to it once the Special Ingredients mission improved the menu. (source: Normandy SR-2)
- A cut Citadel side quest, "Mysterious Stranger," had an asari named Nara Selephais confide in Shepard about a secret human date; the man turned out to be a Terra Firma Party politician who insisted on anonymity, leaving Shepard to choose whether to protect or expose the interspecies relationship. (source: Mass Effect 2 Cut Content/Miscellaneous Texts)
- Admiral Han'Gerrel openly disliked fellow admiral Zaal'Koris vas Qwib Qwib, dismissing him as a "whining suit-wetter," while asari-tech admiral Daro'Xen vas Moreh returned the sentiment, calling Gerrel himself an "aging warship"; despite his hawkish push for war with the geth, learning the truth of Rael'Zorah's illegal experiments on live geth shocked and horrified Gerrel, and he promised Tali'Zorah he would keep the secret to protect her. (source: Han'Gerrel vas Neema)
- Admiral Han'Gerrel vas Neema and Tali'Zorah's father Rael'Zorah were old friends from serving together on the gunship Yaska before their Pilgrimages; during a batarian raid that killed six of ten crewmen, the two teenage crewmen defied orders to hold position and recaptured a freighter, earning medals and early Pilgrimages rather than the court-martial "the brass" wanted. (source: Han'Gerrel vas Neema)
- Admiral Shala'Raan was willing to sacrifice her own political power and call in multiple favors to help Tali, telling her "Do you know how many favors I had to call in… I wore holes in my suit to get you that opportunity," representing the family side of quarian society. (source: Tali'Zorah vas Normandy — Character Analysis (Part 2) — Mass Effect 2: Leadership and Family)
- A drunk Tali toasted Miranda in Mass Effect 3, saying "I didn't like her… but I respected her. She did whatever it took to stop her father. She never gave in to him, never changed herself to please him," recognizing a parallel to her own struggle with her father's legacy. (source: Tali'Zorah vas Normandy — Character Analysis (Part 5) — The Romance Arc and Conclusion)
- After Aria T'Loak's retaking of Omega, her bodyguard Bray emailed Shepard that the Talons had become the station's new security force in honor of Nyreen Kandros, signing off with a wry note of batarian-human goodwill: "there's at least one batarian here that doesn't want you dead." (source: Bray)
- After completing their loyalty missions, a charisma check allowed Shepard to defuse a standoff between Tali and Legion over the geth having scanned classified quarian data; Tali agreed to let Legion send non-classified information and said she understood why Legion would protect his people "just as Tali would do for hers." (source: Tali'Zorah vas Normandy — Character Analysis (Part 3) — The Treason Trial and Her Fatal Flaw)
- After Forvan's poisoning scheme was exposed, a salarian bartender replaced him and afterward served Shepard complimentary drinks (source: Omega: Batarian Bartender)
- After Javik quoted "fear not the weapon but the one who wields it," James Vega replied, "That's what I love about you, buggy. You're like Sun Tzu with more eyes." (source: James Vega/Unique dialogue)
- After Lair of the Shadow Broker, Liara T'Soni told Shepard that Jacob Taylor was "solid and stable," and that Shepard "may need that more than she thinks in the time to come," before wishing them both happiness. (source: Jacob Taylor)
- After learning of her father's presumed death on the Alarei, Tali shut down her emotions and refused to recover his body, instead focusing on the mission with the words "Leave him. The mission comes first. It's what he would have wanted," which her father's teachings about duty had overridden her natural compassion. (source: Tali'Zorah vas Normandy — Character Analysis (Part 3) — The Treason Trial and Her Fatal Flaw)
- After Liara T'Soni took over as Shadow Broker, she voiced concern that her rescued informant Feron's ordeal under torture had left him mentally scarred; asked by Shepard if she was romantically involved with him, she denied it, calling it "irresponsible" to pursue anything with him while he was still so emotionally unstable. Feron, for his part, told Shepard that Liara was a far better friend than he deserved. (source: Feron)
- After Shepard exposed the krogan enforcer blackmailing Dr. Chloe Michel through him, the salarian shopkeeper Morlan's stock greeting to Shepard shifted from an eager sales pitch to a nervier, more deferential "Yes, human. You need something, yes?" (source: Citadel: Doctor Michel)
- After the Arrae mission, Garrus Vakarian and James Vega held an impromptu war-stories competition in the Normandy's kitchen, while Kaidan Alenko or Ashley Williams (whichever was aboard) separately shared their thoughts on the rescued Cerberus scientists elsewhere on the ship. (source: Arrae: Ex-Cerberus Scientists)
- After the Attican Traverse rachni mission, Garrus Vakarian regaled young Ensign Copeland with rachni war stories in the Port Observation Lounge, while war correspondent Diana Allers, if aboard, discussed the scoop's potential with Specialist Traynor. (source: Attican Traverse: Krogan Team)
- After the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, Liara T'Soni asked Shepard whether the fight was partly to give Jack "faith in something more than anger"; when Shepard agreed Jack was improving, Liara found the reassurance thin but conceded Jack was still important to him. (source: Jack)
- After the Shepard clone escaped taunting "I should go," Shepard, sealed in the Citadel Archives vault, fixated not on the danger but on whether that catchphrase really sounded like them, prompting a squadmate to confirm it did before another asked why Shepard wasn't more worried about being trapped. (source: Citadel Archives: Escape)
- A garbled account of David Anderson's disastrous first mission with Saren Arterius on Camala — in which Saren covered his own actions by shifting blame onto Anderson — hardened into a persistent Citadel rumor that Anderson had briefly been the first human Spectre and washed out so badly he was kicked out; the C-Sec fixer Harkin was still repeating the story almost two decades later. (source: Milque)

(source: Ashley Williams/Unique dialogue)
