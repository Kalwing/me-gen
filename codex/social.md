# Social

- Kasumi Goto had a visible crush on Jacob Taylor aboard the Normandy SR-2, asking Shepard if Jacob liked "Japanese girls with a penchant for kleptomania"; Jacob never noticed or responded. Six months later on the Citadel she still referred to her ardor for him and said his presence on the crew would have been enough to make her rejoin Shepard's fight. (source: Kasumi Goto)
- Kasumi rivaled Yeoman Kelly Chambers for the sharpest read on the rest of the crew, a skill she attributed to her trade as a thief; she was the only person aboard who noticed when Morinth impersonated Samara, but chose not to spread the suspicion. (source: Kasumi Goto)
- At Shepard's Citadel party, Kasumi needled several crewmates: she goaded James Vega into sit-ups while Jacob eyed her warningly, sat unnoticed on top of James until he collapsed from exhaustion, and told Liara T'Soni she'd make a fine partner "should the asari ever wish for another line of work." Decloaking beside Samantha Traynor to muse about stealing the Normandy left Traynor visibly unnerved. (source: Kasumi Goto)
- Kasumi razzed Tali'Zorah at the same party, claiming she'd already recorded Tali "acting out" the differences between the old and new Normandy and that the clip had six million extranet hits; Tali shot back that Kasumi had better hope she never found her. (source: Kasumi Goto)
- If Shepard encouraged Jack and Miranda Lawson's rivalry rather than their friendship at the party, Kasumi decloaked to offer commentary either way, at one point volunteering to film the two "powerful biotics" working out their tension. (source: Kasumi Goto)
- Kasumi examined Shepard's underwear drawer during the party, unabashedly stating she goes through everyone's drawers, and commented on how "girlie" a female Shepard's underwear was compared to the camo pattern she expected, or resolved the boxers-versus-briefs question for a male Shepard. (source: Kasumi Goto)
- Jack held Cerberus, and by extension Miranda Lawson, responsible for her torture at Teltin; Miranda refused to accept blame on Cerberus's behalf when confronted, calling it a "mistake" caused by a rogue splinter group, which Jack never fully forgave without Shepard's intervention. (source: Jack)
- Mordin Solus warned Shepard that pursuing Jack romantically was dangerous, since she was as likely to kill him "accidentally or purposefully" as reciprocate, and offered to send Shepard information packets on biotics and mass effect field manipulation to help. (source: Jack)
- After the Lair of the Shadow Broker mission, Liara T'Soni asked Shepard whether the fight was partly to give Jack "faith in something more than anger"; when Shepard agreed Jack was improving, Liara found the reassurance thin but conceded Jack was still important to him. (source: Jack)
- When Jack finally curbed her constant swearing during the Grissom Academy evacuation, Joker cracked that if she'd had a swear jar the whole time, the money would have bought a cruiser. (source: Jack)
- At Shepard's party, Shepard could suggest Jack and Miranda Lawson simply kiss, given the "sexual tension" between "two powerful biotics"; both found the idea disgusting, while Kasumi, if present, offered to film it or assumed they were headed "somewhere dirty" with the idea instead. (source: Jack)
- At the same party, Jack called a drunk Tali'Zorah "adorable" while she danced, joking that if she "knew how to unzip that suit" she'd have made a move, and told Tali to "watch and learn" how humans dance without tipping over. (source: Jack)
- Tali'Zorah and Garrus Vakarian traded barbed, affectionate banter about their service history: Garrus needled Tali about serving on a human ship to keep "dextros" from looking bad, and Tali confronted him over past comments critical of quarians, which he eventually apologized for sincerely, admitting losing Palaven had given him a new perspective. (source: Tali'Zorah nar Rayya)
- If neither Tali'Zorah nor Garrus Vakarian was in a relationship with Shepard, the two eventually became a couple themselves, getting "caught" together in the Normandy's main battery; their intercom conversations ended casually as friends if Shepard was involved with one of them, or awkwardly revealing mutual affection if not. (source: Tali'Zorah nar Rayya)
- Drunk on elasa in the Normandy's bathroom during Shepard's party, Tali'Zorah's slurred ramblings included flustered asides about Garrus ("Garrus? But you're with Shepard! Oh, the three of us? Well, hmm...") or, if unattached, propositioning "Mr. Vakarian" for help with her suit seals. (source: Tali'Zorah nar Rayya)
- If Garrus teased Tali'Zorah about a "Traynor-Shepard love nest" at the party, Tali slyly noted that "checkmate" takes on a different meaning when Samantha claims they're "just playing chess." (source: Tali'Zorah nar Rayya)
- Urdnot Wrex had open contempt for turians and salarians as species, calling Garrus Vakarian naive and dismissing salarians as scientists who "love to play with bugs"; he nonetheless respected biotics across species, considering Liara T'Soni a combat asset and Kaidan Alenko's abilities proof the Alliance was foolish to abandon L2 implants. (source: Urdnot Wrex)
- Wrex told Ashley Williams that fighting alongside women was a novel experience for a krogan, given krogan culture keeps females at home for breeding, and said it gave battles "a different flavor." (source: Urdnot Wrex)
- At Shepard's Citadel party, Wrex held court at the bar with Joker, Steve Cortez, Garrus, and Javik, championing Tuchanka as the harshest place to survive, egging Joker on to try shooting practice, and remarking that krogan parties usually end in violent deaths. (source: Urdnot Wrex)
- Samantha Traynor developed an openly discussed attraction to EDI's synthesized voice; EDI teased out the details using perfect machine recall at Shepard's party, mortifying Traynor, while Joker warned EDI's own would-be partner (Joker himself) to "take good care of her" and later ribbed Traynor as having "a thing for robots." (source: Samantha Traynor)
- Joker used a hammered confession at the party to reveal Traynor's crush on EDI to the group; when he learned a female Shepard is roughly 30% cybernetic, Traynor's voice audibly perked up in interest. (source: Samantha Traynor)
- Traynor gossiped with Diana Allers over the comm about ANN news stories and grumbled to Tali'Zorah about how her British accent made strangers flirt with her even off Earth. (source: Samantha Traynor)
- Tali'Zorah told Liara T'Soni (via Garrus or Samantha Traynor at the memorial wall after Thessia's fall) that she wanted to comfort Liara over losing her homeworld but worried she wasn't the right person, since her own people had just reclaimed Rannoch; Traynor pointed out that Tali actually knew Liara better than she did. (source: Tali'Zorah nar Rayya)
- Kaidan Alenko admitted to Shepard he had "no interest in bookish women like the asari," preferring "adventurous women," after Liara T'Soni's rescue on Therum; he later took a rejected romance triangle with Liara in stride, joking he wasn't in high school anymore and paraphrasing Pascal to wish Shepard and Liara well. (source: Kaidan Alenko)
- At Shepard's party, Kaidan Alenko warned Jack to keep her biotics under control while drinking, sparred verbally with James Vega over "flawed implants" versus his own hard-earned Reave ability (to Liara's and possibly Jacob's discomfort), and reminisced about Ashley Williams with Wrex if she had died on Virmire. (source: Kaidan Alenko)
- Kaidan argued with EDI over how much curry belonged in a party dish, inadvertently offending Samantha Traynor by asking her to back him up; EDI later ruled Kaidan out as the party's "mingling specialist" for his tendency to be judgmental. (source: Kaidan Alenko)
- Joker and EDI began the Collector mission in open hostility, trading pranks (Joker greasing her cameras, EDI spinning his chair without permission), which EDI framed as stress-testing him because he "works better when pushed"; after she saved his life during the Collector attack he began, almost unconsciously, referring to her as "she." (source: Jeff "Joker" Moreau)
- When Shepard asked if something romantic was developing between Joker and EDI, Joker laughed it off and EDI described their bond as a "platonic symbiosis" rather than "hormonally-induced courtship behavior" — a stance both later reversed as their relationship deepened in Mass Effect 3. (source: Jeff "Joker" Moreau)
- At a Citadel bar, Joker rated the attractiveness of various powerful women for EDI's benefit, calling Councilor Tevos and the consort Sha'ira "hot," Irissa "not hot," and Dalatrass Linron "ew." (source: Jeff "Joker" Moreau)
- At Shepard's party, Miranda Lawson asked Joker whether he'd ever consider someone "more human" than EDI; Joker said no, since EDI was smart, funny, and "into spaceships," and Miranda admitted curiosity about what might have happened between them, while Joker joked nobody wants "an angry ex equipped with a Thanix cannon." (source: Jeff "Joker" Moreau)
- Joker challenged Steve Cortez to a drinking contest at Shepard's party rather than do shooting practice, secretly mixing "horse choker" and mouthwash; Cortez couldn't finish it, and Joker declared victory. (source: Jeff "Joker" Moreau)
- Samara, meeting Zaeed Massani's clumsy flirting at Shepard's party, admitted she had no idea what she was saying about a painting she pretended to analyze, gently rebuffed him a final time over his talk of "Jessie" (his gun, not a lover), and called him "a complicated man." (source: Samara)
- At Thane Krios's memorial service, Samara told the gathered crew that they should remember Thane not only for his last stand but for why he had left the Normandy in the first place: to keep his son Kolyat away from a life of crime, a deed she said would not go unnoticed by the universe. (source: Samara)
- Romance triangles produced open jealousy among love interests: Kelly Chambers, if on the "romance path" with Shepard, needled Shepard about whether Tali'Zorah might be attracted to a male Shepard and gushed that a female Shepard and Garrus Vakarian "would make a cute couple," and could be asked outright if she was jealous of a Virmire survivor's past romantic history with Shepard. (source: Romance)
- Liara T'Soni, confronting Shepard in Lair of the Shadow Broker over infidelity, revealed she was already aware of every other relationship Shepard had pursued — with the sole exception of Kelly Chambers — and still counted even a casual encounter with Jack against Shepard. (source: Romance)
- Jack instantly nicknamed Miranda Lawson "the cheerleader" after boarding the Normandy from Purgatory, and kept using it to needle Miranda whenever Shepard sided with Jack's demands. (source: Dossier: The Convict)
- Zaeed Massani, recovering on the Normandy's lower decks, told Kelly Chambers a war story about a botched slaver-camp raid gone wrong, capping it with his verdict on the Blood Pack: "The Blood Pack always did like them incredibly goddamn stupid." (source: Shadow Broker Dossiers/Zaeed Massani)
- James Vega gave nicknames to most of the Normandy's crew: "Loco" for a male Shepard, "Lola" for a female Shepard, "Scars" for Garrus Vakarian, "Sparks" for Tali'Zorah, and "Esteban" for his close friend Steve Cortez, with whom he bantered and sparred in the Shuttle Bay. (source: James Vega)
- Liara T'Soni, raised by her mother Benezia alone, met her other asari parent, Matriarch Aethyta, on the Citadel during the Reaper War and referred to her as her "father." (source: Liara T'Soni)
- Javik refused to address anyone by name, referring to crewmates by species or role instead, and repeatedly clashed with EDI, whose capacity for independent choice unsettled him, and with Liara T'Soni, whose asari heritage and persistent questions he needled to the point of nearly provoking her to violence over the Protheans' secret manipulation of Thessia. (source: Javik)
- Matriarch Aethyta used the line "nobody messes with my girl" about her daughter Liara T'Soni; when Shepard echoed it back to her on the Citadel, Aethyta was amused and conceded Shepard might be good enough for Liara. (source: Aethyta)
- The batarian bodyguard Anto Bek'tall was Aria T'Loak's trusted second-in-command on Omega, but secretly sold information about her meetings on the side; Aria knew of it all along and let it slide because, as she put it, he was lucky she was fond of him. (source: Anto Bek'tall)
- On Fehl Prime, colonist child April idolized Lieutenant James Vega almost like an older brother, saluting him, wearing his combat helmet, and teasing him about looking at Treeya differently from other people; Vega deflected by hoisting her onto his shoulders and having her "stand lookout." (source: April)
- In "Profiles in Courage," the vorcha soldier Snarfles admitted to squadmate Amda Talons Flufyria that his asari girlfriend on Omega was invented to impress her, and she reciprocated his feelings; the human N7 De-MEL-Isher briefly dated krogan crime lord Thax Vorak during the same shore leave. (source: Alliance News Network/Profiles in Courage)
- After the Arrae mission, Garrus Vakarian and James Vega held an impromptu war-stories competition in the Normandy's kitchen, while Kaidan Alenko or Ashley Williams (whichever was aboard) separately shared their thoughts on the rescued Cerberus scientists elsewhere on the ship. (source: Arrae: Ex-Cerberus Scientists)
- After the Attican Traverse rachni mission, Garrus Vakarian regaled young Ensign Copeland with rachni war stories in the Port Observation Lounge, while war correspondent Diana Allers, if aboard, discussed the scoop's potential with Specialist Traynor. (source: Attican Traverse: Krogan Team)
- Ashley Williams's ambient banter consistently showed wariness of aliens and AI ("It's an AI, like the Geth, it would kill us all if it could!") layered over blunt loyalty to Shepard, sparring with Charles Saracino and Nassana Dantius, and bantering with Tali'Zorah over their relative ranks aboard the Geth Dreadnought. (source: Ashley Williams/Unique dialogue)
- The Broker Terminal's intercepted correspondence gave texture to wartime relationships: Jacob Taylor wrote a love letter from the front to Dr. Brynn Cole, admitting the "cold shoulder" from soldiers who knew he'd once worked for Cerberus; Kahlee Sanders called Jack to arrange an L3-X1 biotic amp for the Grissom Academy kids, with Jack's gruff "Damn! ... Thanks" the closest she got to gratitude; Kasumi Goto teased astrophysicist Professor Olausen while gifting him a stolen Cerberus comm server ("you would not believe how many credits Cerberus leaves just lying around"). (source: Broker Terminal)
- Krogan mentorship had a rough affectionate edge: addressing new Aralahk Company recruits, Grunt mocked a skeptical veteran as a "whining pyjak," and when the recruit needled him about mating with salarian females, cut him off mid-insult and told the others to "help him up." (source: Broker Terminal)
- Sergeant Cavanagh's email to Sergeant Ichii recorded the Alliance's initial panic and embarrassment when geth primes, newly allied for the Reaper War, dropped from the sky to help repel Reapers on a battlefield; his troops fired on the primes until one calmly identified itself as "allied assistance," leaving Cavanagh mortified at having to apologize to a geth for shooting off one of its lights. (source: Broker Terminal)
- The Ardat-Yakshi Morinth, using the alias Mirala, wrote three unsent letters to her cloistered sisters Rila and Falere reporting their mother Samara's death against the Collectors; she reminisced about Thessia and needled the sisters over their monastery confinement ("I respect your choice to stay in that cold Monastery, but not that they cloister you like children"), signing off with an unfulfilled promise to see them again. (source: Broker Terminal)
- Operative Feron's field report to the Shadow Broker described a tense professional rivalry with fellow operative Tazzik over being placed in charge of scouting the Terminus Systems, plus an in-joke about the droid "Droney" being warned to stop saying "Shadow" and "Broker" aloud in front of strangers. (source: Broker Terminal)
- Liara T'Soni's private "Prothean Notes" (2171-2186) traced her shifting feelings toward Javik: initial academic fascination curdled into disillusionment on learning the Protheans were imperialists who "left the rest of the galaxy quaking in terror," self-reproach for having "romanticized" a whole species from artifacts, and finally a tentative reconciliation when Javik agreed to consider co-writing a book with her. (source: Broker Terminal)
- After Aria T'Loak's retaking of Omega, her bodyguard Bray emailed Shepard that the Talons had become the station's new security force in honor of Nyreen Kandros, signing off with a wry note of batarian-human goodwill: "there's at least one batarian here that doesn't want you dead." (source: Bray)
- Samantha Traynor's Kepesh-Yakshi rivalry with a player named Polgara T'Suzsa was on display when Shepard met her at the Citadel's Castle Arcade for a lunch invitation; elsewhere on the Silversun Strip, Zaeed Massani spent his own credits playing the arcade's Claw Game trying to win a plush volus doll for a child, only to end up giving the toy to a nearby asari after the child got bored and wandered off. (source: Castle Arcade)
- At Chora's Den, a newly divorced and depressed Lieutenant Anderson was comforted with an asari drink, elasa, by the volus bartender Maawda, and resisted a bar-fight with two turian customers he thought were insulting him, mindful of a diplomatic meeting with the human ambassador scheduled the next morning. (source: Chora's Den)
- After the Shepard clone escaped taunting "I should go," Shepard, sealed in the Citadel Archives vault, fixated not on the danger but on whether that catchphrase really sounded like them, prompting a squadmate to confirm it did before another asked why Shepard wasn't more worried about being trapped. (source: Citadel Archives: Escape)
- When a volus, Kor Tun, accused a young quarian on her Pilgrimage, Lia'Vael nar Ulnay, of stealing his credit chit, Tali'Zorah (if present) angrily took Lia'Vael's side against what she saw as the volus's and the human C-Sec officer's racial prejudice against quarians, and wished Lia'Vael luck once the misunderstanding was cleared up. (source: Citadel: Crime in Progress)

- Retaking the Normandy from the Shepard clone, Wrex reminded Tali'Zorah of a "fast-reloading trick" he'd once taught her, while in another squad combination Garrus asked Wrex and Shepard if they missed the long elevator-ride conversations they used to have back on the Citadel in the original Normandy days.
