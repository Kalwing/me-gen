# Places

- "Tikkun" is Hebrew for "a fix" or "correction," and several worlds in the system follow a Jewish-culture naming scheme (source: Tikkun).
- A carbonaceous asteroid in Tereshkova's asteroid field yielded a recovered League of One Medallion after Joker maneuvered a team to salvage debris from it (source: Tereshkova).
- A salvage team that ventured into the Perseus Veil was drawn in by geth technomental domination and sent back as husks against the society that produced them; a leaked Spectre report said Sovereign hid near the Veil before the 2183 Citadel attack. (source: Perseus Veil)
- A spot on Trebin was one of several locations where the "song" associated with the rachni could be heard (source: Trebin).
- After the Morning War the geth slowly built a Dyson bubble around Tikkun as part of their plan for a megastructure to house every geth program; much of it was ruined when every world in the system became a battlefield in the geth-quarian war of 2186 (source: Tikkun).
- Arcturus Station is a five-kilometer Stanford Torus at the trailing L5 point of the gas giant Themis in the Arcturus system, built 2151-2162 from local asteroids and inaugurated 2156; since the First Contact War it served as the Systems Alliance's military and political headquarters, seating the Alliance Parliament, holding ~45,000 people, guarding the mass relay to Earth and functioning as a shipyard. In the 2186 Reaper War Admiral Hackett fought a delaying action there with the Second, Third and Fifth Fleets, then abandoned it and sacrificed the Second Fleet to let the others escape; the Reapers destroyed the station with the entire Parliament and Prime Minister Amul Shastri aboard. (source: Arcturus Station)
- Arcturus Station was chosen because the Charon Relay led there and the local relay was a nexus linking countless systems, making the otherwise worthless system a defensible hub; it also served as a shipyard (SSV Aconcagua) and was where Joker grew up. (source: Arcturus Station)
- Arcturus Station was the de facto capital of the Systems Alliance, a Stanford torus habitat built 2151-2162 in the Arcturus system at the Themis L5 point, housing the Alliance Parliament and Fleet Command and guarding the mass relay to Earth; it had no habitable planet so a station was built instead. (source: Arcturus Station)
- Aria T'Loak became Omega's de facto ruler around the 1980s CE by defeating in single combat the krogan crime lord she had served as a lieutenant. (source: Omega)
- Around the 1980s-2025 CE the hanar evacuated roughly 375,000 drell from Rakhana to Kahje while billions left behind died in resource wars; present-day Rakhana is a cemetery world of a few thousand drell in small clusters. (source: Rakhana)
- As of 2183 Cerberus ran at least four hidden bases with troops and defenses on deserted worlds of the Voyager Cluster, the target of numerous UNC assignments including UNC: Cerberus and UNC: Hades' Dogs (source: Voyager Cluster).
- Ashley Williams's grandfather, General Williams, commanded Shanxi's garrison when it surrendered to the turians, an act that quietly ended his career though no charges were filed (source: Shanxi).
- Before the geth assault, Feros's main colony held about a thousand people and the Zhu's Hope outpost about 200; ExoGeni secretly used Zhu's Hope as a control group after finding the Thorian beneath it, with 85% of colonists infected by mind-controlling spores. (source: Zhu's Hope)
- By Mass Effect 3 batarian raids had driven many colonists off Terra Nova, and during the Reaper invasion the Reapers landed there after the Alliance Sixth Fleet withdrew rather than be destroyed; its capital was Scott, and the asteroid X57 was planned as an artificial moon (source: Terra Nova).
- By the Reaper War, Noveria drew attention as a magnet for Cerberus interest in its advanced research and because its mass relay was the transit point between the humans' Exodus Cluster and the start of salarian space (source: Noveria).
- Cerberus occupied Omega after Mass Effect: Invasion, gaining a major strategic advantage in the Reaper war before Aria, Shepard, and Nyreen Kandros liberated it in the Omega DLC. (source: Omega)
- Cerberus was secretly involved in Sanctum's corporate finances, and embedded Systems Alliance agents urged the companies to confront the Illusive Man, but financial bickering stalled any action. (source: Sanctum)
- Citadel races (humans, turians, salarians, elcor, asari, volus) colonized the Terminus extensively, forming colonies typically independent of any interplanetary government; the turians had earlier mapped the region using an interferometric array reaching to the planet Gromar (source: Terminus Systems).
- Commander Shepard traveled to Nos Astra in 2185 to recruit Thane Krios and the justicar Samara, and could there meet Liara T'Soni, Conrad Verner, and Gianna Parasini (source: Nos Astra).
- Despite in-game dialogue placing the Purgatory Bar on the Wards, its exterior view showed it was actually on the Presidium above the Presidium Commons. (source: Purgatory Bar)
- During the 2186 Reaper invasion, Taetrus was one of the first worlds attacked after Khar'shan and Earth; two Hierarchy attempts to relay in and liberate it failed, and the Reapers broadcast their victory there across the turian comm buoy network. (source: Taetrus)
- During the 2186 Reaper invasion, turian fleets and Menae's anti-aerospace defenses forced the Reapers to take its bases one at a time by ground assault, each capture denying the turian fleet a place to repair and refuel. (source: Menae)
- During the Krogan Rebellions the genophage was transmitted into over 90 percent of the sealed underground bunkers where krogan had isolated themselves; a later shroud at the L1 Lagrange point, maintained by the CDEM from orbiting battlestations, kept surface temperatures livable (source: Tuchanka).
- During the identity-theft investigation, Shepard's team infiltrated a charity gala at the Silver Coast Casino and found Elijah Khan murdered, with a signal-distorted Mysterious Figure claiming responsibility (source: Silver Coast Casino)
- Early salarian industry gave Sur'Kesh serious pollution and waste problems, solved rapidly through social measures including complex breeding rules that kept the population crowded but sustainable; Burngrass, a native soil-enriching weed, became a major terraforming export. (source: Sur'Kesh)
- Earth is the third planet of the Sol system and humanity's homeworld and capital; its orbit is cluttered with "bootstrap"-era debris, making kinetic barriers advisable above 85 km. Before the Reaper War it was in a colonial-wealth golden age of arcology skyscrapers and rising art and industry, still divided into nation-states (United North American States, European Union, Chinese People's Federation, Union of Incorporated Nations) affiliated under the Systems Alliance, with a widening rich-poor gap; Collector data put its 2183 population at 11,490,225,106. In 2186 the Reapers overran it within hours, treating its cities as stores of human DNA. (source: Earth)
- Evacuation ships carrying mostly children fled Tiptree before the attack and reached salarian colonies; Joker's father and sister lived on Tiptree when the Reapers invaded (source: Tiptree).
- ExoGeni Corp fired a water-ice comet into Trebin's surface as the first step of a long-term plan to thicken its atmosphere and introduce rain; a survey team monitoring the impact suffered GPS failures caused by a tightbeam transmitter tower, the subject of the assignment UNC: Missing Survey Team (source: Trebin).
- Feros is the second planet of the Theseus system in Attican Beta; two-thirds of its habitable surface is buried under the ruins of a crumbling Prothean megatropolis, leaving little open ground, poor water and dust-fouled air. In 2178 the human ExoGeni Corporation founded the pilot colony Zhu's Hope atop intact skyscrapers, supported by surviving Prothean aqueducts and rooftop hydroponics; the colony was later dominated by the Thorian. (source: Feros)
- Horizon is the third planet of the Iera system, a temperate human Terminus colony whose soil proved unusually compatible with Earth crops; it drew dissidents and fringe-dwellers seeking refuge from Citadel restrictions and supplied both humans and the Turian Hierarchy. The Collectors abducted roughly a third of its population in 2185; in 2186 the Cerberus front "Sanctuary" advertised it galaxy-wide as a war refuge while secretly converting refugees into husks. (source: Horizon)
- Humans built many space stations as military installations during their expansion, prizing proximity to mass relays; most include drive discharge points and, being expensive, are frequently repurposed. (source: Space stations)
- Illium is a hot, massive garden world in the Tasale system of the Crescent Nebula, colonized and run by asari corporate interests as a trade entrepot between the Terminus Systems and the Asari Republics, with Noveria-like relaxed Council customs law; its capital is Nos Astra, equatorial populations live in arcology skyscrapers, and it legalizes nearly everything except murder, including indentured servitude and drugs banned elsewhere. Near-total surveillance keeps it glamorous and safe; it bars the Migrant Fleet from its system. In the Reaper War its paranoid, wealthy leaders fielded a fission-armed defense fleet that badly slowed the ground invasion. (source: Illium)
- Ilos is the second planet of the Refuge system in the Pangaea Expanse, a former Prothean garden world of spires and arches whose surface was later devastated by unknown means; it lies in a remote pocket of the Terminus Systems reachable only through the Mu Relay, knocked out of position by a supernova ~4,000 years earlier. It held the Conduit, a miniature mass relay to the Citadel used by the last Protheans, and was originally inhabited by the extinct inusannon before the Protheans colonized it. (source: Ilos)
- In 2183 Virmire became the site of Saren Arterius's hidden krogan-breeding and indoctrination research base, destroyed by a nuclear device at the end of Commander Shepard's mission there; its native lifeforms included Pod Crabs (source: Virmire).
- In 2185 the separatist group Facinus rammed a starship at near-FTL speed into Vallum (the Vallum Blast), killing tens of thousands; the Turian Hierarchy and Taetrian forces crushed the separatists in the short War on Taetrus, then rebuilt the colony. (source: Taetrus)
- In 2186 the Reapers destroyed Arcturus Station after bypassing its defending Second, Third, and Fifth Fleets; the entire Alliance Parliament and Prime Minister Amul Shastri died aboard, leaving a grave of over 45,000 people. (source: Arcturus Station)
- In Mass Effect 3 a Destroyer-class Reaper seized the Shroud to poison Tuchanka's air; Shepard summoned Kalros to drag the Reaper underground, and the Shroud was destroyed in an explosion whether or not the genophage cure was dispersed (source: Shroud)
- In Mass Effect Andromeda: Initiation, Cora Harper used Tamayo Point twice as a waystation returning to Sol, drawing Homeward Sol xenophobe protesters after a hostile interview by Khalisah al-Jilani (source: Tamayo Point).
- In Mass Effect, Rayingri hosted the assignment UNC: Geth Incursions around an overrun research outpost, the corpses of a salvage team killed by geth particle beams, and areas where the "song" associated with the rachni could be heard. (source: Rayingri)
- In Mass Effect: Evolution, Jack Harper and Eva Coré tracked the Arca Monolith to Nos Astra shortly after the First Contact War and were captured there in a trap set by turian general Desolas Arterius, then taken to Palaven (source: Nos Astra).
- Liara T'Soni and Feron evacuated the Shadow Broker Base's information drives by shuttle and remotely crashed the ship into a Cerberus cruiser in 2186, destroying both; salvaged tech later aided the Crucible's construction (source: Shadow Broker Base).
- Maji was the first planet of the Vamshi System in the Armstrong Nebula, a thin-atmosphere world of methane and carbon monoxide with constant dust-laden wind, used by Terminus criminals for lethal survival "sport"; it hosted a geth outpost in the assignment UNC: Geth Incursions. (source: Maji)
- Matano was a five-world system (four terrestrial, one gas giant) with planets named for Inca deities; the Systems Alliance claimed Chasca, charged ships drawing water from Supay, and patrolled against pirates, with Illapa slated to become a helium-3 hub. (source: Matano)
- Matano's outer belt held a derelict ship where a recon team found no survivors but recovered a Prothean data disc. (source: Matano)
- Mavigon held the remains of Captain Stranaka, a salarian soldier who had hunted the League of One. (source: Mavigon)
- Mavigon was the third planet of the Han System, a tin-and-potassium ice world with a Level 2 Cold Hazard, ammonia-based life around underground geothermal vents, and constant blizzards; Shepard raided a Syndicate hideout there during UNC: Hostile Takeover. (source: Mavigon)
- Menae was a military-controlled moon of Palaven (sister moon Nanus), its geological data classified since the Krogan Rebellions for fear the krogan would smash it into Palaven; subterranean mass effect field generators let personnel walk its surface without enviro-suits. (source: Menae)
- Mindoir survivor Talitha, left with Stockholm Syndrome after Marines killed her batarian captors, can be met by Shepard; the carrier SSV Einstein responded to the raid, leaving Lieutenant Ernesto Zabaleta with PTSD. (source: Mindoir)
- Mindoir was a human farming colony in the Attican Traverse, raided by batarian slavers in 2170 and later rebuilt; it is the homeworld of a Colonist-background Commander Shepard, whose family was killed there when Shepard was sixteen. (source: Mindoir)
- Mu Relay: a mass relay lost roughly 4,000 years ago, the only relay linking to the Pangaea Expanse and the Prothean colony of Ilos; a supernova pushed it from orbit and hid it in gas and dust, making it undetectable. (source: Mu Relay)
- Native Rannoch lifeforms include the qorach (a carnivorous bighorn-sheep analogue, its blind newborn a ke'sed), the regenerating lemek worm, and the edible fa'yin nut. (source: Rannoch)
- Nearly a hundred human and alien corporations built tele-operated mining and manufacturing facilities across Tyr, feeding a hydrogen fuel cell industry that made the Asgard system supply nearly 4% of the galactic market; the Reapers targeted these facilities during their invasion. Its capital was Kaldidalur (source: Tyr).
- Nepmos held an Alliance listening post and a rachni-infested mine abandoned by the Tenkarra Mining Consortium, plus two rachni colonies; Commander Shepard cleared it during UNC: Listening Post Alpha. (source: Nepmos)
- Nepmos was the first planet of the Erebus System in Styx Theta, a resource-rich terrestrial world larger than Earth with a sulphur dioxide and argon atmosphere and a crust under 8 km thick, prone to magma flows and quakes that deterred mining. (source: Nepmos)
- Nodacrux hosted an ExoGeni facility staffed by Dr. Ross and defended by mercenaries, an ancient escape pod with Thracia Colony data, Thorian Creepers and a Space Cow herd. (source: Nodacrux)
- Nodacrux was the second planet of the Vostok System in the Maroon Sea, a verdant, water-rich world rendered nearly uninhabitable by deafening firestorms and floating pollen tufts that cause lethal allergic reactions in oxygen-breathers. (source: Nodacrux)
- Nonuel held Lord Darius's warlord outpost, the target of UNC: The Negotiation; after his death the Alliance secured his eezo mining facility there over rival factions. (source: Nonuel)
- Nonuel was the fourth planet of the Plutus System in Hades Gamma, the largest body in a blue star's asteroid belt, intensely volcanic and massive because it formed around a supernova-ejected chunk of element zero, making it a secondary eezo source. (source: Nonuel)
- Nos Astra was the capital city of the asari colony world Illium in the Crescent Nebula, an outwardly gleaming port and commercial city said to be as dangerous as Omega, with shuttle routes to Thessia, the Citadel, Palaven, Irune, Kahje, and Amaterasu (source: Nos Astra).
- Noveria featured the Skadi Mountains, site of the isolated Peak 15 lab, and the Aleutsk Valley pass between Port Hanshan and Peak 15; its name derived from "Nova Siberia" (source: Noveria).
- Noveria is a corporate research enclave granted the same relaxed Council law as Illium, where safety, proscribed-materials and trafficking regulations are set aside for the companies operating there. (source: Illium)
- Noveria lay outside Citadel space and so was exempt from Council law, letting its investors run remote "hot labs" for research too dangerous or controversial to perform elsewhere; Citadel Spectres held extraterritorial privileges there by special arrangement (source: Noveria).
- Noveria was a small, frozen, barely habitable world in the Pax System of the Horse Head Nebula, a privately chartered colony owned by the Noveria Development Corporation and funded by two dozen high-technology firms (source: Noveria).
- Omega is a lawless space station built in a hollowed metal asteroid deep in the Terminus Systems, long ruled solely by the asari Aria T'Loak through combat skill and manipulation; it controls close to 20% of all galactic smuggling. In 2186 Cerberus used escaped Reaper-tech "adjutants" as a pretext to seize the station and the Omega-4 Relay beyond it, driving Aria into exile; she built a resistance around the reformed Talons and retook it. (source: Codex/Planets and Locations; Codex/Omega: Reclaimed)
- Omega was a lawless mining station in the Sahrabarik system, built in a mined-out metallic asteroid split open by a collision that exposed its element zero; it had ~7.8 million residents, no central government, and eezo as its main export. (source: Omega)
- Omega was divided into districts including Gozu (site of the Omega Clinic, quarantined during the 2185 plague), Kima (where Archangel was cornered), Carrd (elcor-run), Doru, Fumi, Kenzo, Tuhi, and Zeta. (source: Omega)
- On approach to Notanban the Normandy intercepted an undecryptable coded transmission from Solcrum's surface matching no known Citadel software language; the moon also held an asari capsule containing one of Matriarch Dilinaga's writings. (source: Solcrum)
- Ontarom, first planet of the Newton System in the Kepler Verge, was a hot, ocean-covered world habitable to most species but doomed by the decaying orbit of its moon Thonal, which caused tidal disruption and powerful electrical storms. (source: Ontarom)
- Palaven is the turian homeworld; its weak magnetic field lets solar radiation through, giving native life a metallic carapace, and turians are dextro-amino-acid based. It was the site of the Battle of Palaven, where Fleet Admiral Irix Coronati's "Fifteen-Minute Plan" downed several capital ships before the turians were forced into a costly ground war, and later of the Miracle at Palaven, a turian-krogan counterattack that used the dreadnought Indomitable as bait and smuggled krogan warp and fission bombs into indoctrination-opened Reaper ships. (source: Codex/Aliens: Council Races; Codex/The Reaper War)
- Palaven was the turian homeworld, third planet of the Trebia System in the Apien Crest; its weak magnetic field forced most native animal life to evolve metallic carapaces against solar radiation, and human visitors needed radiation protection (source: Palaven).
- Palaven's martial culture pervaded its architecture, art, and politics, and its cities were visibly fortified; it was never occupied by an invader until the Reapers bombarded every resisting city, leaving pulverized cities' dust as a planet-wide breathing hazard (source: Palaven).
- Pamyat was a Milky Way system first colonized by humans, formerly plagued by small-scale pirates who were cleared out by 2185; it held noteworthy iridium and unlivable worlds Komarov, Dobrovolski, Patsayev, and Volkov, the planets likely named for Soviet cosmonauts killed in spaceflight (source: Pamyat).
- Patavig was the third planet of the Macedon System, its surface mostly a sea of liquid ammonia hosting an ammonia-based aquatic biosphere with organisms often larger than a human; inhospitable to humans but suitable for volus colonization, it was granted to the Vol Protectorate in 2183 if the asari-led Council died (source: Patavig).
- Peak 15 comprised a main garage with inward-facing turrets, Central Station holding Mira's core and a helium-3 reactor, Rift Station (the main science area), and glacier-built Hot Labs able to sink into the ice or flood themselves with lethal neutron radiation. (source: Peak 15)
- Peak 15 was one of seventeen remote research facilities on Noveria, in the Skadi Mountains and reached via the Aleutsk Valley, with labs rented by Binary Helix and a bad reputation. (source: Peak 15)
- Peak 15 was overrun by rachni after Binary Helix tried to breed and weaponize them; Commander Shepard resolved the crisis and survivors were reassigned offworld. (source: Peak 15)
- Peak 15's Code Omega safety protocol cut all power on a containment breach so the cold would kill escaped experiments, purged the VI Mira, and let the Port Hanshan board vote to destroy the facility from orbit with an antimatter warhead. (source: Peak 15)
- Per Captain Bailey, Zakera Ward's population was dominated by volus, elcor, and hanar, with humans concentrated in the Shin Akiba commercial zone and transients sheltering in HabCapsules in the 633 Block. (source: Zakera Ward)
- Phoenix was a frontier star system in the Argos Rho cluster with two asteroid belts and planets Patashi, Sylsalto, Intai'sei, Tuntau and Vebinok, with activity dating back to the Krogan Rebellions; an asteroid belt there held Pinnacle Station. (source: Phoenix)
- Pinnacle Station had a command centre (with turian-style raised CO placement), an observation deck run by simulator designer Ochren, and a simulator offering volcanic, subterranean, tropical or warehouse settings; it was commanded by Admiral Tadius Ahern and became a Council command center during the Reaper War. (source: Pinnacle Station (location))
- Pinnacle Station was an asteroid facility in the Phoenix System, population 1,200, built as a hidden command center during the Krogan Rebellions and retrofitted as a Council special-operations training facility with a combat simulator using holograms, kinetic barriers and painful kinetic slugs. (source: Pinnacle Station (location))
- Presrop was the frigid, barren moon of Klendagon in the Century System (Hawking Eta), with a thin carbon-dioxide-and-ethane atmosphere and rich heavy-metal deposits; Alliance mineral-rights bidding was hampered by the system's proximity to the dangerous "Five Kiloparsec Ring" of star formation near the galactic core. (source: Presrop)
- Presrop's surface was a maze of jagged ridges and geological shock zones from an ancient disaster; illegal "wildcat" miners repeatedly died trying to exploit it, and it hosted Major Kyle's biotic commune compound, a thresher maw, and roaming mercenaries in the original Mass Effect. (source: Presrop)
- Project Base was a Systems Alliance deep-cover research installation on the asteroid 157-Golgotha in the Bahak system (Viper Nebula), built by Dr. Amanda Kenson's team around the embedded Reaper artifact "Object Rho" (source: Project Base).
- Purgatory was a Blue Suns maximum-security prison starship in the Hourglass Nebula, administered by Warden Kuril, where Shepard recruited the biotic convict Jack. (source: Purgatory)
- Purgatory's crew profited by threatening to dump prisoners on ports unless paid off in fuel and supplies; Kuril betrayed Shepard, and Jack's rampage after release left the ship falling apart before Shepard killed Kuril. (source: Purgatory)
- Quiet Eddy was a dwarf-planet asteroid in the Pamyat system, named after a hanar freighter's 1879 CE repair stop, that by 2184 CE hid a stealthed Systems Alliance black-ops facility. (source: Quiet Eddy)
- Rakhana was the drell homeworld, a once-lush arid world ruined by early industrialization beginning around the 1380s CE; depleted topsoil and acidified oceans set the drell, some 11 billion at their height, toward a population crash. (source: Rakhana)
- Rannoch has no insect life, so its plants evolved to rely on animals for pollination; this symbiosis is blamed for the quarians' weak immune systems, making reclamation a physiological as well as cultural necessity. (source: Rannoch)
- Rannoch is the quarian homeworld, overrun by the geth in the Morning War and left behind the Perseus Veil for three centuries while the geth tended its abandoned buildings like a cemetery. At the 2186 Battle of Rannoch the quarians massed behind the star Tikkun and sent strike teams against the Reaper transmitting upgrades to the geth; a Shepard-brokered ceasefire could let quarians and geth survive and settle Rannoch together, or the fighting could end in the near-annihilation of the quarians or the destruction of the geth. (source: Geth War; Codex/The Reaper War)
- Rannoch was the former quarian homeworld, first planet of the Tikkun system in the Perseus Veil; its name translates as "walled garden," reflecting the cultural importance of plant life and shade. (source: Rannoch)
- Rayingri, third planet of the Gagarin system in the Armstrong Nebula, is a barren world shunned by miners; a rogue planetoid dubbed Vahtz was captured in a decaying orbit around it ~10,000 years ago and is expected to tear the two bodies apart within a few hundred years. (source: Rayingri)
- Ringed with mass-effect field generators to deflect debris, Omega grew a jellyfish-like silhouette from processing facilities built vertically off the asteroid. (source: Omega)
- Sanctum was the second planet of the Decoris System in Sigurd's Cradle, a nominal garden world whose freezing ice storms left only a thin habitable strip along the equator. (source: Sanctum)
- Sanctum's capital was named Vulpes, and carbon dioxide levels could reach 5,000 parts per million during thermal inversions. (source: Sanctum)
- Sanctum's economy rested on mining, called "ice cracking" away from the equator, for platinum, palladium, and boron used in semiconductor doping, drawing miners, mercenaries, and company men. (source: Sanctum)
- Saren Arterius planned to use the Conduit to bypass the Citadel's defenses for Sovereign; Shepard drove the Mako through it and emerged from the Relay Monument on the Presidium, and by Mass Effect 2 the statue was itself called the Conduit (source: Relay Monument)
- Shanxi was a Systems Alliance colony near the Shanxi-Theta mass relay and, before 2186, the only human colony ever occupied by an alien race, taken and later retaken during the First Contact War of 2157 (source: Shanxi).
- Sharjila hosted the UNC: Asari Diplomacy assignment against pirates and held one of Matriarch Dilinaga's writings in an abandoned camp, plus a spot where the rachni "song" could be heard (source: Sharjila).
- Sharjila was the first planet of the Macedon System in Artemis Tau, a Level 1 pressure hazard with a dense ammonia/oxygen atmosphere and a silicon-based, oxygen-breathing ecology that coated the surface in abrasive silica dust (source: Sharjila).
- Shepard visited the Urdnot Camp in 2185 to aid Mordin's search for a missing STG member and Grunt's Rite of Passage, and could meet the Clan Urdnot leader (Wrex or Wreav), a Clan Nakmor alliance envoy (only if Wrex lived), and the traditionalist rival Gatatog Uvenk (source: Urdnot Camp).
- Shifting borders placed the Mu Relay in the Terminus Systems, so the Council refused to send the Citadel Fleet through it to Ilos, leaving only the stealth-equipped Normandy able to reach it. (source: Mu Relay)
- Siano held degraded artificial structures in its northern hemisphere and bunkers of radioactive fission waste on its far side; the Reapers ignored it in favor of more populous systems (source: Siano)
- Siano was the first planet of the Dirada System in the Pylos Nebula, named for a contrarian ancient asari philosopher, a low-density tidally locked body believed to have fallen into the system millions of years ago and broken up one of Dirada's asteroid belts (source: Siano)
- Sirta Supplies was a gift-shop terminal inside Huerta Memorial Hospital on the Citadel selling flowers, candy, poetry volumes, and TM88 Peruvian Whiskey as get-well gifts for the hospitalized Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko (source: Sirta Supplies)
- Sixteen years before the Reaper War the Systems Alliance built its first dish field on Ontarom despite the storms; it grew into a crucial communications hub of quantum communicators and a symbol of civilian-military cooperation, though the asari had mocked the human settlement as foolish. (source: Ontarom)
- Sol is the home system of humanity and the Systems Alliance, with nine game-recognized planets plus Luna; it was long cut off from the relay network because the Charon Relay orbiting Pluto was encased in ice. (source: Sol)
- Solcrum is the largest moon of the gas giant Notanban in the Grissom System of the Armstrong Nebula, a Level 1 heat hazard with a trace krypton-xenon atmosphere, scorching irradiated surface, and a geth headquarters assaulted during UNC: Geth Incursions. (source: Solcrum)
- Space stations in the Milky Way fall into trade/gateway hubs near relay nexuses, orbital defence platforms (e.g. Noveria's, which can destroy ships and hit surface targets with antimatter warheads), and isolated research stations for dangerous or classified work that can be destroyed to contain a breach. (source: Space stations)
- Species names for Omega reflected their attitudes: asari "heart of evil," salarian "place of secrets," turian "world without law," krogan "land of opportunity." (source: Omega)
- Spectre Requisitions is a store in the Spectre office in the Citadel Embassies in Mass Effect 3, selling high-end weapons, upgrades, and mission items framed as politically sensitive acquisitions needing cover stories; it becomes inaccessible after Priority: Cerberus Headquarters. (source: Spectre Requisitions)
- Sur'Kesh lay far from dark space and had not been invaded by the Reapers during Mass Effect 3, though its rulers knew they were in the path of attack; it was the setting of Priority: Sur'Kesh. (source: Sur'Kesh)
- Sur'Kesh was the salarian homeworld, the second planet of the Pranas System in the Annos Basin, a humid, jungle-covered world with rich biodiversity, wetter than Earth and comparable to its former rainforests. (source: Sur'Kesh)
- Surviving commando Aeian T'Goni developed PTSD after Neaira, an asari commando turned banshee, slaughtered human farmers; T'Goni was forced to kill indoctrinated colonists and the girl Hilary, later confirmed to be Joker's younger sister (source: Tiptree).
- Svarog was the first planet of the Pax System in the Horse Head Nebula and the system's only gas giant, a small hydrogen-helium (likely ice) giant orbiting very close to its star. (source: Svarog)
- Svarog's heat, powerful winds and high orbital velocity made it a poor helium-3 mining candidate, which is why nearby Noveria imported all its helium-3 from out-system despite having plentiful deuterium. (source: Svarog)
- Taetrus was a turian colony in the Mactare system; its capital was Vallum, with other cities (Iratiana, Spaedar, Madra) in the Diluvian Wildlands on the continent Eluria, many fortified to military spec during an earlier civil war. (source: Taetrus)
- Tamayo Point was a Sol-system space station serving as a tourist jump-off point to the wider galaxy, with extensive commercial amenities and medical facilities for treating metabolic-overdrive damage, possibly named for cosmonaut Arnaldo Tamayo Méndez (source: Tamayo Point).
- Tasale was a star system acting as a gateway between the Asari Republics and the Terminus Systems, home to the asari trade world Illium (its second planet), plus Beregale, Ponolus, Teukria, Thail, and Naxell (source: Tasale).
- Tasale's worlds rated moderate to rich in resources, especially palladium, and helium-3 mining infrastructure was being built on Naxell to service Illium (source: Tasale).
- Tereshkova was a low-energy binary star system in the first Mass Effect, named after Valentina Tereshkova, with seven orbiting bodies including Antibaar, Patamalrus (projected to lose its atmosphere), Hunsalra, Thegeuse, Solmarlon and Mawinor (projected to be ejected) (source: Tereshkova).
- Terra Nova, in the Asgard System of the Exodus Cluster, was the second human extrasolar colony and the first beyond the Charon Mass Relay, discovered by the 2150 Alliance surveys and later booming from a 2170s platinum rush to the highest population of any Alliance colony (source: Terra Nova).
- The Citadel is a colossal pentagram-shaped deep-space station in the Widow system of the Serpent Nebula: a ~7.2 km central Presidium ring with five ~43.6 km Ward arms (44.7 km open), rotational gravity, ~13.2 million residents plus the keepers, able to seal into an armored ~45 km cylinder. The asari discovered it in 580 BCE; it is the political, cultural and financial capital of Citadel space, with the Council Chambers and traffic control in the Citadel Tower, government and embassies on the Presidium under the VI Avina, and the densely populated Wards policed by ~200,000 C-Sec officers, the slums known as the Foundations. It is in fact an inactive mass relay to dark space built by the Reapers, its keeper caretakers placed to hide that function so the Reapers could decapitate galactic leadership in one strike. (source: Citadel; Codex/Citadel and Galactic Government)
- The Citadel repeatedly tried and failed to negotiate settlement of Virmire with Terminus gangs and dictatorships, and eventually wrote off its colonization as impossible without political change (source: Virmire).
- The Krogan Monument is a large statue of a krogan warrior in the lake near the Citadel Tower on the Presidium, commissioned by the Council to commemorate krogan service against the rachni; a post-Rebellions movement to remove it failed. (source: Krogan Monument)
- The Migrant Fleet ("the Flotilla") was roughly 50,000 ships housing about 17 million quarians, the largest array of spacefaring vessels in the galaxy, taking days to pass a single mass relay. (source: Migrant Fleet)
- The Migrant Fleet is the roughly 50,000-ship flotilla of the exiled quarians, home to about 17 million people; youths are sent on the Pilgrimage to find a gift worthy of earning a berth, and its liveships grow the fleet's food. (source: Codex/Aliens: Non-Council Races)
- The Milky Way, a barred spiral galaxy of two to four hundred billion stars, is the setting of the original Mass Effect trilogy, explored via the Galaxy Map and the mass relay network from the SSV Normandy and Normandy SR-2. (source: Milky Way)
- The Omega 4 Relay led to the Collector Base at the galactic core on the edge of a black hole; surviving the transit required a Reaper IFF to trigger advanced safety protocols. (source: Omega 4 Relay)
- The Omega 4 Relay was the only known mass relay to glow red rather than blue. (source: Omega 4 Relay)
- The Omega 4 Relay, in the Omega Nebula, was ringed with hazard beacons; for a thousand years ships that passed never returned, only the Collectors travelling it freely. (source: Omega 4 Relay)
- The Omega Nebula corresponded to the real Messier 17 (NGC 6618), also called the Swan, Checkmark, Lobster, or Horseshoe Nebula, roughly 6000 light years from Earth. (source: Omega Nebula)
- The Omega Nebula was a nebula in the Terminus Systems containing the Omega space station and the unmapped Omega 4 Relay; its worlds were dominated by batarian resourcing interests, pirates, and mercenaries. (source: Omega Nebula)
- The Perseus Veil was a purple-and-gold opaque nebula at the galactic rim forming the border between geth space and the Terminus Systems, near-impossible to scan or navigate, behind which the geth isolated themselves after the Geth War. (source: Perseus Veil)
- The Personal Apartment in Tiberius Towers on the Citadel's Silversun Strip belonged to Admiral David Anderson, who meant to retire there with Kahlee Sanders and instead lent it to Commander Shepard for shore leave; it held audio recordings of Anderson's biography. (source: Personal Apartment)
- The Project Base team fitted the asteroid with scrap engines and navigation tech acquired via Omega so it could be launched into the Alpha Relay; the whole team was indoctrinated by Object Rho during their study of it (source: Project Base).
- The Prothean Empire kept an outpost on Mars and studied Earth's primitive humans 50,000 years ago; in Mass Effect 2 all Sol planets except Earth and Mars can be mined by the Normandy SR-2. (source: Sol)
- The Purgatory Bar was a dance club on the Citadel owned by Aria T'Loak, who used it as her base of operations after Cerberus took Omega and derisively called it "hell." (source: Purgatory Bar)
- The Quiet Eddy facility, a military-corporate partnership researching illegal AI, was destroyed by the SSV Zama after its VI Medea received Alec Ryder's stolen SAM kernel and turned berserk cybernetic augments on the staff. (source: Quiet Eddy)
- The Relay Monument on the Citadel Presidium, long believed to be Prothean art, was in fact an inactive small-scale mass relay paired with the Conduit on Ilos, forming a Prothean "back door" onto the Citadel that bypassed the Reapers' control signal (source: Relay Monument)
- The Serpent Nebula contained dozens of mass relays and linked the Citadel in a single jump to the home clusters of the salarians (Annos Basin), turians (Apien Crest), asari (Athena Nebula), krogan (Krogan DMZ), and elcor (Silean Nebula); its systems were Boltzmann, Wheeler, and Widow, which held the Citadel (source: Serpent Nebula).
- The Serpent Nebula is a light-reflecting, hard-to-navigate nebula that serves as the Citadel's outer screen. (source: Codex/Citadel and Galactic Government)
- The Serpent Nebula was a dense nebula surrounding the Citadel whose thick gas and dust prevented organized attacks or locating the station without coordinates, making it one of the Citadel's most effective defenses; some believed the Citadel itself created it through millennia of self-repair ejecta (source: Serpent Nebula).
- The Shadow Broker Base held central operations, a prison block and torture facilities, and the Broker's office, Intel Center, and private quarters; after Liara T'Soni's takeover its Intel Center gave Shepard terminals for squad respec, resource deliveries, dossiers, investment, research, planet surveys, and a video surveillance archive (source: Shadow Broker Base).
- The Shadow Broker Base was a massive unnamed ship hidden in the electrical storms of Hagalaz's atmosphere, kept aloft by constantly running engines and protected by lightning-conducting rods, hull capacitors, and motion dampeners (source: Shadow Broker Base).
- The Shroud stood in an old arena built as a shrine to Kalros, the mother of all thresher maws, placed there to deter anyone from reaching it and discovering the STG sabotage (source: Shroud)
- The Shroud was a salarian atmospheric-control tower on Tuchanka, built about 2,000 years ago to stabilize the planet's climate after the krogan nuclear winter, originally deployed by ground-based mass-driver towers (source: Shroud)
- The Silver Coast Casino was a three-floor casino on the Citadel's Silversun Strip owned by weapons dealer Elijah Khan, offering Quasar, roulette, and Tuchanka-broadcast varren racing (source: Silver Coast Casino)
- The Silversun Strip was a Citadel Wards hub added in Mass Effect 3's Citadel DLC, containing the Armax Arsenal Arena, Shepard's Personal Apartment, the Silver Coast Casino, Castle Arcade, and a bar, and serving as a social hub for meeting current and former squadmates (source: Silversun Strip)
- The Terminus Systems lay beyond the Attican Traverse, outside Citadel Council and Systems Alliance space, home to minor species united only by their refusal to recognize Council authority or the Citadel Conventions (source: Terminus Systems).
- The Terminus had no government but was a haven for piracy and the slave trade, with Omega station serving as its trade capital, and included the quarian homeworld Rannoch and the vorcha homeworld Heshtok (source: Terminus Systems).
- The Urdnot Camp was a small rubble-strewn settlement on Tuchanka controlled by Clan Urdnot, containing the clan leader's throne dais, the shaman's building where other clans gathered, a varren-fighting pit, food stores raided by pyjak, a merchant kiosk, and the Lord High Researcher's lab (source: Urdnot Camp).
- The Voyager Cluster lay at the outer edge of the Milky Way within Systems Alliance space, linked by mass relay to Hades Gamma and containing the river-named systems Amazon, Columbia, and Yangtze (source: Voyager Cluster).
- The first three humans on Palaven were the mercenaries Jack Harper, Eva Coré, and Ben Hislop, paraded by Desolas Arterius after the First Contact War; Garrus Vakarian grew up there (source: Palaven).
- The long-term fate of Zhu's Hope depended on how many colonists Shepard saved from the Thorian; if the colony and Shiala survived and their medical problems were treated, the lingering Thorian hive-mind let colonists coordinate and learn combat, helping them evacuate ahead of a Reaper attack in Mass Effect 3. (source: Zhu's Hope)
- The nuclear winter collapsed krogan society into warring clans; salarian first contact in 80 CE enabled a resurgence, and the salarians built the Shroud facility on Tuchanka to block harmful solar radiation (source: Tuchanka).
- The quarians were driven from Rannoch by the geth almost three centuries ago; the geth then acted as caretakers, repairing the ecology and restoring structures, while thousands of geth stations and the geth armada watched over the planet. (source: Rannoch)
- The rachni located the Mu Relay over centuries; after the krogan exterminated them the coordinates survived only in the resurrected Rachni Queen's genetic memory, passed by Matriarch Benezia to Shepard (and already to Saren Arterius). (source: Mu Relay)
- Therum was a heavy-metal-rich industrial world in the Knossos System (Artemis Tau) claimed by the Systems Alliance, its capital Nova Yekaterinburg; fossils of silicon-based life show it was once more habitable, and its Prothean ruins were mostly looted by mining firms (source: Therum)
- Therum was the Mass Effect mission location where Shepard rescued Liara T'Soni from a Prothean ruin during a geth attack; it evolved from cut demo content set on a planet called Caleston (source: Therum)
- Thessia is the asari homeworld, long organized as republican city-states run by an inclusive "electronic democracy" with no politicians that defers to Matriarchs in a crisis; the Temple of Athame on Thessia held a Prothean beacon containing the VI Vendetta, kept a state secret until 2186. In the Reaper War asari hit-and-run tactics failed against superior Reaper numbers and orbital bombardment, and the lightly defended homeworld fell. (source: Codex/Aliens: Council Races; Codex/The Reaper War)
- Thessia sat on the Illium–Thessia shipping lane; travel there was strictly controlled but smuggling persisted (source: Thessia)
- Thessia was governed as many separate republics rather than one state, had long been free of war, and its e-democracy communications backbone made it vulnerable when the Reapers invaded in 2186 (source: Thessia)
- Thessia, in the Parnitha System of the Athena Nebula, was the asari homeworld and the core of the Milky Way's largest economy, its vast element zero reserves setting the galactic price of eezo (source: Thessia)
- Tikkun was the quarian home star system, containing Rannoch (the homeworld), Adas, Kaddi, Haza, and the asteroid Uriyah, plus a mass relay and the anchored Migrant Fleet (source: Tikkun).
- Tiptree was a small human colony attacked by a small Reaper force in 2186; an asari commando unit sent to evacuate it was mostly destroyed as husks and marauders landed (source: Tiptree).
- Trebin was the fourth planet of the Antaeus System (Hades Gamma), a lifeless nickel-and-silver world with a nitrogen-argon atmosphere (source: Trebin).
- Tuchanka is the lethal, war-ravaged krogan homeworld, organized into clan territories with neutral ground held by the dominant clan; its landmarks include the ancestral burial caverns called the Hollows and the Shroud, a planet-wide atmospheric-processing tower (secretly sabotaged by the salarian STG) through which a genophage cure could be dispersed. Krogan youths undergo the Rite of Passage on its traditional Rite grounds. (source: Clan Urdnot; Genophage; Grunt: Rite of Passage)
- Tuchanka was the first alien homeworld Shepard could visit (source: Tuchanka).
- Tuchanka, the krogan homeworld, was the fourth planet of the Aralakh System in the Krogan DMZ, once lush under the star Aralakh ("Eye of Wrath") but reduced to radioactive wasteland by nuclear war around 1900 BCE (source: Tuchanka).
- Tyr was the third planet of the Asgard System in the Exodus Cluster, Earth-like in composition but lying over 4 AU from its star with a nitrogen-ethane atmosphere; overshadowed by habitable Terra Nova, it served a support role (source: Tyr).
- Virmire was a lush tropical frontier world in the Hoc System of the Sentry Omega cluster, on the edge of the Attican Traverse, ideal for carbon-based colonization but left unsettled because Terminus Systems instability made raids by pirates and slavers too likely (source: Virmire).
- Warfare and civilization depend on the mass relay network, paired mass-effect devices that fling ships across the galaxy; even so less than one percent of stars have been explored, and "Citadel space" informally means any region held by a Council-acknowledging species. The relays, like the Citadel, were built by the Reapers. (source: Codex/Citadel and Galactic Government; Citadel)
- Wrex saw the Monument as embodying lost krogan values of honor, loyalty, courage and fortitude; Tali resented that the krogan got a Presidium statue while quarians lived in exile, and in the Citadel DLC a drunken Grunt and Aralakh Company climbed it in 2186. (source: Krogan Monument)
- Xenophobic groups like Terra Firma cited Shanxi against alien contact; a 2173 Terra Firma rally there was to feature candidate Claude Menneau, who vanished en route (source: Shanxi).
- Zakera Ward ran from Presidium Junction (nearest the Presidium) to Zakera Point, with a multi-level Mid-Ward District building that Shepard used as a mission hub in 2185, holding the Dark Star Lounge, a C-Sec office, Rodam Expeditions, the Sirta Foundation, and the Fishdog Food Factory. (source: Zakera Ward)
- Zakera Ward was one of the Citadel's five wards, a commercial hub with shops, a club, and a C-Sec office, and the start and end point of many Mass Effect 2 missions including Garrus's and Thane's loyalty missions. (source: Zakera Ward)
- Zhu's Hope was the port settlement on Feros where Shepard first landed, built into an ancient skyscraper using Prothean water conduits and centered on the crashed freighter Borealis, brought by the salarian merchant Ledra. (source: Zhu's Hope)
