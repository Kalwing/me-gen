# Ships and Vehicles

- About a month after the Battle of the Citadel an unidentified starship intercepted the Normandy SR-1 despite active stealth; its beam weapons knocked out kinetic barriers and weapons in a single hit, breaching the hull and killing over 20 crew including Navigator Pressly. Shepard died separately and afterward, going back alone through the wrecked ship for Joker and getting blown clear of the last escape pod into vacuum near Alchera (source: The SSV Normandy SR-2 (Part 1) — Origin and EDI).
- Admiral Zaal'Koris crashed his personal flagship into a Rannoch antiship cannon that was devastating the Migrant Fleet and survived pinned down on the surface; read `scenes/rannoch-admiral-koris.yaml` rather than a bullet for the rescue mission, since whether he lives or dies fighting there swings Civilian Fleet strength and possibly quarian-geth peace. (source: Migrant Fleet)
- After covertly researching the stealth technology of the Normandy and Normandy SR-2, the salarians produced stealth-capable dreadnoughts, a previously unthinkable feat. (source: Salarian)
- After the Rachni Wars, Council Space species refused to open a primary mass relay without knowing its destination, fearing another hostile species; human pioneers opening relays at random helped trigger the First Contact War with the turians (source: Mass Relay).
- After the Reapers destroyed the Empire's warships, the Prothean resistance built the Particle Rifle, a powerful assault rifle that fired without thermal clips or specialized ammunition, since supply lines could no longer be maintained. (source: Prothean)
- After the SSV Normandy SR-1 was destroyed over Alchera by the Collectors, the Alliance located the wreckage in the Amada System and asked Shepard to place a memorial monument on the site and recover dog tags from the 20 crew still unaccounted for; the lost crew's names later appeared on the Normandy SR-2's memorial wall in Mass Effect 3 (source: Normandy Crash Site (assignment)).
- An 800-meter dreadnought mass accelerator could fire a 20-kilogram slug at 4025 km/s (1.3% light speed) every two seconds, each slug carrying about 38 kilotons of energy; the Treaty of Farixen capped dreadnought numbers per navy (source: Starships)
- Arcturus Station functioned as the headquarters and shipyard of the Alliance Navy, with Fleet Command providing its defense; developer intent placed the First Fleet there and Hackett's Fifth Fleet at the Citadel, though the games muddled this. (source: Arcturus Station)
- Before the Reaper invasion the quarians armed their Civilian Fleet and destroyed a geth Dyson sphere meant to unite all geth into one optimized network. (source: Quarian)
- By 2186, after Shepard defected from Cerberus, the Systems Alliance reclaimed the Normandy SR-2 as based on its own SR-1 design, refitted it in Alliance colors, adding a war room, meeting room and QEC to Deck 2 and moving the armory to Deck 5; the refit was left partly finished by the Reaper invasion (source: The SSV Normandy SR-2 (Part 4) — The Alliance Refit).
- Collector-operated Oculi were piloted by a single Collector drone stripped to its nervous system and hardened for vacuum; each carried a powerful beam weapon and could breach a ship's hull after shedding armor. (source: Oculus)
- Destroyers were an obsolete Alliance light-escort class (in service as of 2157, gone by the 2180s as frigates absorbed the role); shuttles such as the FTL-capable UT-47 Kodiak transferred personnel, and ten-man corvettes ran combat and survey missions (source: Starships)
- Destroyers were ~160 m four-legged Reapers, the most common true Reapers, firing a ship-killing beam from opened front plates and acting as walking tanks on the ground (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 2) — Constructs and Capital Classes)
- During the 2186 Reaper War the Collectors returned from dark space aboard low-velocity transports called Black Arks to act as reserves mopping up pockets of resistance. (source: Collector)
- In 2186 the quarians armed their entire Civilian Fleet, fitted dreadnought-scale and Thanix weapons, and used Daro'Xen's ladar-jamming countermeasure to cripple geth ships before the geth's Reaper upgrades neutralized it. (source: Migrant Fleet)
- In the 2186 Reaper invasion, Oculi were deployed en masse as atmospheric and exoatmospheric fighters over Earth and Palaven. (source: Oculus)
- In the original Mass Effect, kills made inside the Mako granted reduced XP; Mass Effect Legendary Edition removed that penalty and added rear thrusters, stronger weapons, faster shield regen, no fall damage, and camera-relative steering. (source: Mako Guide)
- Intruders were mid-tier Reaper ships between Destroyers and capital ships, with stronger beam weapons, shields, and missile/fighter point defense (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 2) — Constructs and Capital Classes)
- Jeff "Joker" Moreau became pilot of the SSV Normandy by stealing the ship during its Arcturus Station shakedown, evading every security patrol shot; turian General Invectus was so impressed he urged the Alliance to keep Joker as pilot (source: Mass Effect: He Who Laughs Best).
- Mercenary Anti-Tank troops in the first Mass Effect carried assault rifles able to switch to rocket fire and focused on the Mako. (source: Mercenaries)
- Messner, who supplied Delta Squad a M35 Mako on Fehl Prime and proposed using the colony defense cannons against the Collector ship, was a Cerberus double agent who had deliberately lured the Collectors to Fehl Prime; read `scenes/fehl-prime-brood-alliance.yaml` rather than a bullet — the causality (the jamming device, sabotaging Brood's attack run, forcing Treeya into a Prothean archive, his death at Vega's hands) does not survive compression. (source: Messner)
- Milky Way military warships were classed by four weights: frigates (planet-capable light scouts fighting in "wolf pack" flotillas of four to six), cruisers (middleweight patrol units leading frigate flotillas, ~300 crew), carriers (dreadnought-sized fighter platforms), and kilometer-long dreadnoughts whose power lay in main-gun length (source: Starships)
- MSV Hugo Gernsback (registry BW46-10034-087): privately held frigate under Captain Harris Fairchild and first officer Ronald Taylor, not rated for suborbital descent; struck by an impactor and crash-landed on 2175 Aeia around 2175, killing part of the crew. (source: MSV Hugo Gernsback)
- MSV Strontium Mule: three-deck freighter under Captain Jarrett Barnes, lured into a Blue Suns trap and later captured, its crew killed; Captain Vorhess sought to decrypt authorization codes to its valuable cargo from the black box. (source: MSV Strontium Mule)
- Non-sentient Reaper Troop Transports (200 m to 1 km) carried husks to unconquered worlds, and Processors served as mobile DNA-harvesting centers; both were controlled remotely by actual Reapers. (source: Reaper)
- Orbital defence stations provide early warning and control of the ground situation for colonies; Noveria's battle stations can target and destroy approaching vessels and strike surface targets with antimatter warheads. (source: Space stations)
- Processors (~600 m) and troop transports (200 m to 1 km) were remotely controlled, non-sentient Reaper machines that processed organic material and moved husks and captured populations (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 2) — Constructs and Capital Classes)
- Purgatory was a cruiser-weight former "ark ship" for agricultural animals, seized by the Blue Suns in the Skyllian Verge and rebuilt into a prison ship of about 30 cell blocks, minimally armed with GARDIAN defenses and reliant on Blue Suns fighters. (source: Purgatory)
- Reaper Capital Ships (Sovereign-class) were two kilometers long, armed with a spinal magnetohydrodynamic cannon yielding 132 to 450 kilotons that no known ship could survive, plus tendril cannons, GARDIAN-style point defense, and Oculus drones; they could take sustained fire from four dreadnoughts before losing their kinetic barriers. (source: Reaper)
- Reaper capital ships were the largest class, ~2 km long and squid-shaped, leading the fleet and attacking whole planets with a hull-length cannon no dreadnought could survive, plus arm weapons, point defense, and Oculus drone swarms (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 2) — Constructs and Capital Classes)
- Reaper Destroyers were only 160 meters but could destroy cruisers in seconds with a folding beam weapon and could unfold their legs to walk as near-invulnerable ground walkers supporting husks. (source: Reaper)
- Reaper warships fall into four types: two-kilometer Sovereign-class capital ships, each harvested from a single species per extinction cycle; 160-meter destroyers, the bulk of the fleet, made from unharvested species; remotely operated troop transports carrying husks; and remotely operated processors, or "slaughter ships," for mass DNA harvesting. Their FTL is more than twice Citadel speed, their power sources need no captured fuel, and their capital-ship main guns are rated between 132 and 454 kilotons, which no dreadnought has survived. (source: Codex/The Reapers)
- Reaper-derived reverse-engineered technology included the Thanix cannon, the Thanix missile, the M-597 Ladon, and parts of EDI's AI. (source: Reaper)
- Reapers could reach FTL speeds far beyond contemporary ships, generate immense mass effect fields allowing planetary landings and impossible maneuvers, and recognized each other via an Identify Friend/Foe system that sought a friendly intelligence and carried a system-disabling virus if stolen. (source: Reaper)
- Reapers were vulnerable during hibernation between cycles and could be destroyed at full power by a concentrated effort of combined organic fleets, as Sovereign was by the Alliance Fleet in 2183 after Shepard destroyed the reanimated Saren avatar that was keeping its shields up. (source: Reaper)
- Rear Admiral John Grissom and volunteers crossed the reactivated Charon Relay in an experimental jump ship in mid-2149, the first crewed mass relay transit, reaching the Arcturus system 36 light-years away. (source: The Founding of the Systems Alliance)
- Relay transit requires the ship's pilot to supply the transit mass before the vessel is moved into the approach corridor; the relay then aligns with its partner and propels the ship across space (source: Mass Relay).
- Ronald Taylor became acting captain of the wrecked Gernsback and ruled it alone for seven years by hoarding food, enslaving survivors and murdering the officers who objected; a decade later his son Jacob Taylor obtained the crash location and could bring Shepard to investigate. Read `scenes/aeia-jacob-and-his-father.yaml` rather than a bullet for what "acting captain" concealed and how Shepard disposes of him. (source: MSV Hugo Gernsback)
- Salarian GARDIAN starship defenses emphasized high performance over reliability, accepting that a malfunction could cost lives. (source: Salarian)
- Salarian warships used costly ultraviolet antiship lasers, the latest GARDIAN defense systems, and hull-mounted Thanix cannons even on scouting flotillas. (source: Salarian)
- Several Oculi ambushed the Normandy SR-2 approaching the Collector Base; one breached the hangar and was destroyed by Shepard on foot, and its beam could kill Jack if the ship lacked Heavy Ship Armor. (source: Oculus)
- Shepard boarded the derelict Strontium Mule in the Arinlarkan system through a cargo-hold hatch and killed Vorhess and the mutinous Sergeant Boortis, claiming the ship's assets. (source: MSV Strontium Mule)
- Sovereign (Nazara) was a two-kilometer Reaper dreadnought, the first Reaper met by the modern Citadel races, which passed itself off as a geth or Prothean flagship commanded by Saren Arterius while in fact controlling him. Its attacks on Eden Prime and the Citadel proved Reaper superiority, but when Saren died, Sovereign reanimated his corpse into a metal-skeleton avatar Shepard had to fight and destroy separately in the Citadel Tower's garden; only then did its shields fail, and the Normandy and the Fifth Fleet destroyed it. Later Reapers no longer shared that flaw. (source: Codex/The Reapers)
- Sovereign could generate mass effect fields strong enough to land its immense bulk on a planetary surface, as demonstrated at Eden Prime, implying a massive element zero core; its underside extruded tubes to interface with and override the Citadel's controls. (source: Sovereign)
- Sovereign was a unique, fully alive capital-class Reaper ~2 km long that served as leader and scout, waking periodically over millennia to gauge galactic advancement and tasked with starting the next invasion by activating the Citadel (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 2) — Constructs and Capital Classes)
- Sovereign was an enormous Reaper dreadnought about two kilometers long, crewed by geth and krogan, serving as Saren Arterius's flagship; its spinal main gun fired a stream of molten metal via an electromagnetic field at a fraction of light speed and was later reverse-engineered by turians into the Thanix Cannon. (source: Sovereign)
- Space-combat systems across the trilogy include FTL drives, mass accelerators as main guns (recoil the prime limiting factor), kinetic barriers that deflect fast objects but not heat, toxins or radiation, GARDIAN anti-fighter lasers, disruptor torpedoes, ablative armor, heat sinks and hull radiators for heat management, and combat drones. (source: Codex/Ships and Vehicles)
- Starships/List catalogs every identified vessel in the setting by class, affiliation and source, spanning Reaper capital ships (Sovereign, Harbinger, the Derelict Reaper, Leviathan of Dis), Council-race dreadnoughts, Alliance Everest- and Kilimanjaro-class dreadnoughts and Fifth Fleet cruisers, Migrant Fleet liveships and Admiralty ships, independent MSV freighters, and the Andromeda Initiative arks and the Pathfinder ship Tempest (source: Starships/List)
- The A-61 Mantis Gunship is a vectored-thrust military aircraft first built in 2170, modular enough to be configured as a low-altitude gunship, a fighter, a high-altitude bomber or a single-stage-to-orbit spaceplane, though it has no FTL drive; an element zero core lightens its engines for vertical takeoff and low-fuel hovering. In gunship configuration it carries Inferno PKR missile bays and belly-mounted M350 mass-accelerator autocannons and can deploy troops or FENRIS Mechs. Mercenary groups often strip out its kinetic barriers. (source: A-61 Mantis Gunship)
- The Alliance and turians jointly developed the stealth frigate SSV Normandy, commissioned in 2183 under Captain David Anderson. (source: Systems Alliance)
- The Alliance Navy was headquartered at Arcturus Station, in a system containing several primary mass relays linking major human colonies plus the secondary relay to Sol; it offset Treaty of Farixen dreadnought limits by fielding carriers, as large as dreadnoughts but armed primarily with fighters rather than ship-length mass accelerator cannons. (source: Systems Alliance)
- The asari-built Destiny Ascension was among the largest ships designed for the first Mass Effect, though the Citadel and mass relays dwarfed even ships of that size; species fleets were each given distinct silhouettes (organic human hulls, hornet-like geth ships, plated turian ships, ocean-vessel salarian ships) (source: The Art of Mass Effect/Transport).
- The Ascension's operating cost usually exceeded its long-range value, so it served mainly as a deterrent; its class dated to at least 580 BCE, matching the asari ship that first discovered the Citadel, and it needed a small support fleet because its point defenses could be overwhelmed. (source: The Destiny Ascension — Flagship of the Citadel Fleet)
- The Citadel Fleet, led by the asari dreadnought Destiny Ascension, defends the Citadel alongside the hard-to-navigate Serpent Nebula, rapid mass relay reinforcement, the station's closing hull and anti-fighter turrets. At the 2183 Battle of the Citadel the Council's survival was not guaranteed: whether the stricken Destiny Ascension escaped with the Council aboard, or was left undefended and destroyed by the geth with the Council still aboard, depended on Shepard's choice to divert the Alliance fleet to defend it while the Wards were sealed (scenes/citadel-tower-saren-showdown.yaml). (source: Citadel; Codex/Citadel and Galactic Government)
- The Collector Cruiser was the only known Collector ship type, a hive vessel combining organic protrusions with a metallic superstructure, large enough to carry Collectors, husks, Scions and Praetorians plus stasis pods for whole colonies, yet able to land and take off vertically. Its main weapon was a multi-directional particle beam that ignored kinetic barriers and could cripple an Alliance frigate in a few shots; its sensors detected stealthed ships like the Normandy, and an IFF system let it cross the Omega-4 Relay. One such cruiser destroyed the Normandy SR-1, killing Commander Shepard and twenty crew. (source: Collector Cruiser)
- The Derelict Reaper was a two-kilometer dead Reaper orbiting the failed star Mnemosyne, disabled about 37 million years earlier by a mass accelerator round that also carved Klendagon's Great Rift Valley; its still-active indoctrination field turned a Cerberus research team into husks. (source: Derelict Reaper)
- The Destiny Ascension was the flagship of the Citadel Fleet and pride of asari engineering: an asari-designed dreadnought four times the size of the largest human ship, crewed by up to 10,000 asari under Matriarch Lidanya, effectively a mobile city. (source: The Destiny Ascension — Flagship of the Citadel Fleet)
- The Devastator was a Reaper flying carrier that launched large numbers of Oculus drones and Destroyers, trading armor for internal drone storage (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 2) — Constructs and Capital Classes)
- The flagship of the Prothean navy, Penumbra Apex, carried a cannon comparable in power to a Geth Dreadnought; Prothean fighter pilots alone maintained their craft and considered it an honor to be buried with their ship. (source: Prothean)
- The Geth Dropship is an approximately frigate-sized geth vessel shaped like an oversized wingless hornet, built to deploy large numbers of geth troops from overhead, drop Armatures and Colossi in mid-flight, and provide air support with a heavy projectile attack; it can also anchor to structures with three near-invulnerable claws to power blocking energy fields. Like other geth vehicles it is itself a sentient mobile platform. It was central to the geth attack on Feros and fought over the Geth Dreadnought and Rannoch in Mass Effect 3. (source: Geth Dropship)
- The Hades Cannon was a four-legged Reaper anti-air/anti-spacecraft weapon whose accurate energy beam could down drop shuttles; during the 2186 invasion of Earth these made troop landings nearly impossible until Shepard destroyed one with the M-920 Cain (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 2) — Constructs and Capital Classes)
- The Idenna was a decommissioned batarian Hensa-class cruiser in the quarian Migrant Fleet with a crew of 693; after a Cerberus attack it became one of the first ships permitted to leave the Flotilla, refitted with its crew cut to about 50, on a five-year mission to find a new homeworld. (source: Idenna)
- The Javelin is an experimental close-assault rack of disposable disruptor-torpedo tubes slung on a warship's hull, firing on converging trajectories so the warheads' dark energy resonates; it is fitted mainly to frigates but also to dreadnoughts, which cannot aim their main guns at close range. (source: Codex/Ships and Vehicles)
- The Kwunu was the volus' only dreadnought, named for the diplomat who negotiated their turian client-race status; funded by the Elkoss Combine and armed entirely with Thanix Magnetic-Hydrodynamic Weapons on its broadsides and main gun, it was jointly gifted to the Vol Protectorate and Turian Hierarchy (source: Volus).
- The lab ship Alarei was overrun by geth that Rael'Zorah had secretly rebuilt, killing him and his team; read `scenes/alarei-rael-zorah-body.yaml` rather than a bullet for the trial context and Tali's discovery of the body. The decommissioned batarian Hensa-class cruiser Idenna was boarded in Mass Effect: Ascension by Cerberus operatives under Paul Grayson pursuing his daughter Gillian, armed and directed by the quarian traitor Golo; read `scenes/idenna-cerberus-assault-grayson-kills-golo.yaml` for how Grayson defected mid-fight and killed Golo to save the ship. (source: Migrant Fleet)
- The M-44 Hammerhead is a highly maneuverable mass-effect-assisted armored infantry fighting vehicle that replaces wheels with three solid-fuel rocket thrusters to hover about two meters up at speeds to 120 km/h, skim calm water and leap obstacles; backup microboosters preserve full mobility after the loss of two main thrusters. It keeps an airtight interior, 360-degree kinetic barriers and a guided missile system, and its controls emulate a tank's so tank crews need no retraining. (source: Codex/Special Vehicles: The Hammerhead)
- The M35 Mako is an environmentally sealed infantry fighting vehicle carried by Alliance frigates, armed with a turreted 155mm mass accelerator and a coaxial machine gun and powered by a hydrogen-oxygen fuel cell with a small element zero core that allows air-drop deployment and thruster jumps. (source: Codex/Ships and Vehicles)
- The M35 Mako was the six-wheeled, environmentally sealed infantry fighting vehicle used to explore uncharted worlds in the first Mass Effect, mounting a shield-piercing mass accelerator cannon and a coaxial machine gun, with propulsion jets for jumping obstacles and projectiles; it could only be destroyed while occupied. (source: Mako Guide)
- The Migrant Fleet (Flotilla) carried roughly 17 million quarians across salvaged and secondhand ships, sustained by strip-mining systems it passed through and by "gifts" from local industries wanting it to move on. (source: Quarian)
- The Migrant Fleet was organized into four groups: the Patrol Fleet (light frigates and fighters, internal security), the Civilian Fleet (majority of ships plus the three Liveships), the Heavy Fleet (main combat force), and Special Projects research vessels, each under a different Admiralty Board member. (source: Migrant Fleet)
- The MSV Broken Arrow was an Alliance-aligned merchant freighter that ran military-grade weapons to colonies on the fringe of geth space; boarded by geth, its captain Thomas Jonner evacuated the crew and disabled the engines to crash and scuttle the ship, until Shepard restarted the drives and saved the endangered Fargone Colony (source: N7: Imminent Ship Crash).
- The MSV Corsica was a merchant freighter carrying 180 LOKI Mechs and a YMIR Mech from Hahne-Kedar's Experimental Mechanics Division to an ERCS facility; a spreading VI malfunction crashed it on Neith, and its cargo mechs activated and began self-destructing (source: N7: Wrecked Merchant Freighter).
- The MSV Estevanico was an Alliance merchant freighter lost for about a year and found wrecked on Zanethu; recovered data showed it had been attacked and overrun by Blood Pack mercenaries and vorcha, making its crew the first Alliance crew on record to encounter the vorcha (source: N7: MSV Estevanico).
- The MSV Strontium Mule was a derelict freighter captured by Blue Suns mercs under Captain Vorhess and Sergeant Boortis in the Omega Nebula; its crew, led by Captain Jarrett Barnes, had been lured into a Blue Suns trap and killed, and its FTL drive had been repaired with substandard parts (source: N7: MSV Strontium Mule).
- The Normandy SR-1 cost an estimated 120 billion credits, roughly the drive-core cost of thousands of fighters or one heavy cruiser; Rear Admiral Mikhailovich called it an extreme allocation of resources for a single prototype (source: The SSV Normandy SR-1 (Part 2) — Drive Core, Stealth, and Service History).
- The Normandy SR-1 distributed core systems across Deck 1 rather than centralizing them; the CO's station sat at the rear of the CIC, attributed to Turian command doctrine, and the prototype successfully tested this hybrid command structure under human-led operations (source: The SSV Normandy SR-1 (Part 1) — Purpose and Interior).
- The Normandy SR-1 led the assault that destroyed Sovereign at the Battle of the Citadel, and was later destroyed by a Collector cruiser near Alchera in 2183, killing over twenty crew including Navigator Charles Pressly; Cerberus built the larger Normandy SR-2 from her design (source: SSV Normandy)
- The Normandy SR-1 was an Alliance-Council stealth prototype frigate built jointly by humans and turians; the SR-2 was a larger Cerberus-built successor later appropriated by the Alliance. Both use internal heat sinks, hull refrigeration and an oversized Tantalus mass effect core to move undetected without heat-emitting thrusters, and both received Silaris armor, Cyclonic Barrier Technology and Thanix cannon upgrades. (source: Codex/Ships and Vehicles)
- The Normandy SR-1 was named for the 1944 Allied invasion of Normandy, launched on its first mission to Eden Prime in 2183, played a critical supporting role at the Battle of the Citadel against Sovereign, and was destroyed above Alchera later that year by the Collectors, whose firepower far exceeded its design limits (source: The SSV Normandy SR-1 (Part 2) — Drive Core, Stealth, and Service History).
- The Normandy SR-1's IES (internal emission sink) stealth system stored waste heat in lithium heat sinks for hours of silent running but did not function at FTL; it was powered by the roughly-120-billion-credit Tantalus Drive Core, about twice the size of a standard core (source: SSV Normandy)
- The Normandy SR-1's Internal Emission Sink stored waste heat in internal lithium heat sinks, allowing several hours of silent running; it could not be used in FTL, and stored heat eventually had to be vented or the ship would be lost (source: The SSV Normandy SR-1 (Part 2) — Drive Core, Stealth, and Service History).
- The Normandy SR-1's Tantalus drive core, powered by a high-output antimatter reactor, was proportionally twice the size of any other ship's drive core and moved the ship by generating mass distortions it "fell into," reducing reliance on heat-emitting thrusters (source: The SSV Normandy SR-1 (Part 2) — Drive Core, Stealth, and Service History).
- The Normandy SR-2 entered service in 2185 and later, after Shepard's defection from Cerberus, was recommissioned as the SSV Normandy SR-2 by the Alliance; in Mass Effect 3 it rescued Shepard from the Reaper invasion of Earth and served as the mobile base for the Crucible mission before crash-landing on an unknown world after being caught in the Crucible's energy wave (source: Normandy SR-2).
- The Normandy SR-2 entered service in 2185, built by Cerberus at secret expense in the Voyager Cluster for the revived Shepard's team; based on the SR-1's layout but almost twice the mass, with civilian-grade comforts, and unable to land on every planet, requiring shuttle deployment (source: The SSV Normandy SR-2 (Part 1) — Origin and EDI).
- The Normandy SR-2 had five decks: captain's cabin, Combat Information Center, crew's quarters, engineering with the oversized Tantalus core, and a hangar for the Kodiak shuttle and M-44 Hammerhead; weapon, armor and shield upgrades determined crew survival in the suicide mission (source: Normandy SR-2).
- The Normandy SR-2 led the charge of the united Citadel races in the battle for Earth at the close of the Reaper War; its whereabouts afterward remain unknown (source: The SSV Normandy SR-2 (Part 4) — The Alliance Refit).
- The Normandy SR-2 relied mostly on defensive systems but carried powerful armament: paired Javelin disruptor torpedoes whose resonating dark-energy warheads shredded targets, and the Turian-developed Thanix magnetohydrodynamic cannon, a miniature of Sovereign's main weapon that fired superheated molten metal and could destroy a Collector cruiser in two shots (source: The SSV Normandy SR-2 (Part 2) — Armament and Defenses).
- The Normandy SR-2 was driven by four antiproton thrusters plus the Heed Industries Helios metallic-hydrogen thruster module; its Tantalus drive core was oriented vertically and three times the size of the SR-1's, and the Internal Emission Sink allowed roughly two to three hours of undetected travel but did not fool Collector scanners (source: The SSV Normandy SR-2 (Part 3) — Propulsion, Stealth, and Sensors).
- The Normandy SR-2's AI, EDI (Enhanced Defense Intelligence), served as electronic-warfare defense with behavioral blocks preventing direct system interface; those blocks were released during the Collector boarding, giving EDI full ship control (source: The SSV Normandy SR-2 (Part 1) — Origin and EDI).
- The Normandy SR-2's defenses included kinetic barriers, cyclonic barrier technology multi-core shielding that slapped aside incoming force, asari-designed Silaris ablative armor, and computer-controlled GARDIAN point-defense lasers that were near-100% accurate early in an engagement before heat buildup degraded them (source: The SSV Normandy SR-2 (Part 2) — Armament and Defenses).
- The Normandy SR-2's Thanix Cannon, a turian magnetic-hydrodynamic weapon researched from Garrus's design, was powerful enough to destroy a Collector Ship with repeated hits (source: Research)
- The Normandy was conceived as a hovering military prototype with no visible landing gear, its exterior referencing the F-14 Tomcat, delta-wing fighters and the Concorde, with vectored engine nozzles added so it could stop at spaceports (source: The Art of the Mass Effect Trilogy/Mass Effect).
- The Normandy's shakedown was delayed a week while the Alliance waited for a single turian overseer, General Invectus, whom David Anderson described as as close to Hierarchy trust as they would get (source: Mass Effect: He Who Laughs Best).
- The Oculus design was based on a stereo speaker, with HAL 9000 as the reference for its red "eye"; the term is Latin for "eye." (source: Oculus)
- The Oculus was a fast Reaper cybernetic craft used for space and atmospheric superiority, first fielded by the Collectors to guard the Collector Base. (source: Oculus)
- The quarian ark Keelah Si'yah launched for Andromeda in 2186 carrying about 4,000 quarians plus batarians, drell, elcor and hanar, and suffered the Fortinbras Plague in transit. (source: Quarian)
- The quarian navy fielded only a few hundred dedicated warships for tens of thousands of civilian ships, most of which carried armament; it shot to kill any vessel whose intent could not be verified, so returning Pilgrims used code phrases. (source: Quarian)
- The Reaper IFF was a Reaper-designed Identify Friend/Foe device required for the Normandy SR-2 to pass safely through the Omega 4 relay, recovered from a derelict Reaper orbiting the brown dwarf Mnemosyne (source: Reaper IFF)
- The Reaper IFF, recovered from the derelict Reaper orbiting Mnemosyne, let the Normandy SR-2 safely transit the Omega 4 mass relay but transmitted the ship's location and locked its systems, enabling the Collector ambush and abduction of the crew (source: The SSV Normandy SR-2 (Part 3) — Propulsion, Stealth, and Sensors).
- The salarian navy fielded sixteen dreadnoughts, fewer than the Treaty of Farixen allowed, because their ship designs grew increasingly complex. (source: Salarian)
- The Shadow Broker Base was a massive unnamed ship that continuously flew through Hagalaz's volatile atmosphere for concealment; it was not suited to space travel, and its exterior was patrolled by the Broker's army and maintained by drones amid constant lightning storms (source: Shadow Broker Base).
- The SSV Hastings was a Systems Alliance frigate commanded by Captain Belliard, named for the Battle of Hastings (1066); Lieutenant David Anderson served aboard her as executive officer in 2165 while she patrolled the Skyllian Verge, and she responded to the attack on the Sidon research base (source: SSV Hastings)
- The SSV Iwo Jima was a Systems Alliance frigate named for the WWII battle; sent to Camala in 2165 to evacuate scientist Kahlee Sanders, she was struck at point-blank range by explosive rounds from the krogan Skarr's portable mass accelerator cannon while her shields were down to land, and crashed and was destroyed (source: SSV Iwo Jima)
- The SSV Normandy SR-1 was a prototype Normandy-class stealth "deep scout" frigate, co-developed by the Alliance and Turian Hierarchy with Council sponsorship and commissioned in 2183, first commanded by Elli Zander, then David Anderson, then Commander Shepard (source: SSV Normandy)
- The SSV Normandy SR-1 was the first ship of its class, a three-deck stealth frigate co-developed by the Systems Alliance and Turian Hierarchy under Citadel Council sponsorship less than three decades after the First Contact War, built for long-range reconnaissance and covert strike-team deployment in unstable regions like the Attican Traverse and Terminus systems (source: The SSV Normandy SR-1 (Part 1) — Purpose and Interior).
- The Thanix fires a liquid iron-uranium-tungsten alloy suspended in an element-zero electromagnetic field, accelerated to near light speed and solidifying into a projectile that pierces any known shield or armor; small enough to mount on frigates and fighters, it gave the Normandy SR-2 cruiser-level firepower (source: Thanix).
- The Thanix was a turian-designed ship-to-ship weapon, a scaled-down copy of Sovereign's main gun that the turians secretly reverse-engineered from salvage and element zero recovered while clearing debris after the 2183 Battle of the Citadel, introduced about a year later (source: Thanix).
- The tomkah began as a rejected M35 Mako design from the first Mass Effect, reused in Mass Effect 2 (source: Tomkah).
- The tomkah depended on a part called the combustion manifold; it could run without one but mechanics were reluctant to field an affected vehicle, and a replacement took about three days to build (source: Tomkah).
- The tomkah was a large krogan infantry fighting vehicle used on Tuchanka, resembling the Alliance M35 Mako and M29 Grizzly but nearly twice as tall to fit krogan passengers, with a dorsal double-barreled turret (likely a mass accelerator cannon) and armor able to withstand a ten-ton bomb (source: Tomkah).
- The UT-47 Kodiak drop-shuttle ("combat cockroach") is a contragravitic troop transport carrying 12 soldiers plus crew, environmentally sealed for hard vacuum, high pressure and temperatures from near absolute zero to over 900 degrees Celsius; the Mass Effect 3 UT-47A variant adds a front-mounted mass-accelerator cannon. (source: Codex/Ships and Vehicles)
- The volus preferred airpower in conflicts and maintained several flotillas of heavy-bomber frigates capable of operating in any atmosphere, relying on the turians for ground support (source: Volus).
- Three central, heavily defended Liveships grew most of the Fleet's food, including the bioengineered celery keleven; the loss of one would starve millions. (source: Migrant Fleet)
- Warships are classed by weight: frigates, fast scouts that operate in wolf-pack flotillas and can land on planets; cruisers, standard patrol combatants; kilometer-long dreadnoughts, capital ships with long spinal main guns held as strategic reserves; and dreadnought-sized carriers that fight mainly through launched fighters. The Treaty of Farixen fixes a 5:3:1 dreadnought ratio among turians, other Council races and associate members. (source: Codex/Ships and Vehicles; Codex/Citadel and Galactic Government)
- With the Thanix Cannon upgrade the Normandy SR-2 could destroy the Collector Cruiser with minimal damage during the Suicide Mission (source: Thanix).
