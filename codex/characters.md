# Characters

- A Shepard VI was a virtual intelligence based on Commander Shepard, first sold illegally by Mouse on the Citadel in Mass Effect 2 and later marketed as "model 1.7 AGB" at the Citadel refugee camp in 2186, speaking synthesized Paragon or Renegade one-liners (source: Shepard VI).
- A spared Petrovsky cooperated with Alliance interrogators and gave up a Cerberus base location, becoming a War Asset. (source: Oleg Petrovsky)
- A surveillance transcript from Mordin's Omega clinic recorded his triage creed, "save who you can, move on," and his reaction to his assistant Daniel Abrams leaving to help the sick in the slums: "His decision. Hope he lives. Can't help him now" (source: Shadow Broker Dossiers/Dr. Mordin Solus).
- A surviving Morinth could later appear during the assault on Earth as a Reaper-converted Banshee. (source: Morinth)
- A video-essay identified Tali'Zorah's fatal flaw as an irrational, instilled drive to be the sacrificial lamb for the greater good; she wanted the evidence of her father Rael'Zorah's illegal geth weapons tests suppressed even at the cost of her own exile (source: Tali'Zorah vas Normandy — Character Analysis (Part 3) — The Treason Trial and Her Fatal Flaw).
- Miranda tracked her sister Oriana to the fallen Cerberus refuge Sanctuary and confronted their father Henry Lawson there; the squad recovered data pointing to Cerberus Headquarters at Cronos Station. (source: Miranda Lawson)
- Abrudas: turian lieutenant who served in the First Contact War in 2157 under General Desolas Arterius; her soldiers found a downed turian research vessel crashed on Shanxi, and acting on a false message from Desolas she led a squad into a trap and unwittingly gave human mercenaries the vessel's location. The first female turian visually depicted in the franchise. (source: Abrudas)
- Across the trilogy Tali's ship-name changed from "nar Rayya" (Pilgrimage-age) to "vas Neema" (adult, ME2) to "vas Normandy" (imposed by the Admiralty Board at her treason trial and kept thereafter) (source: Tali'Zorah nar Rayya).
- Admiral Daro'Xen vas Moreh was a quarian Admiralty Board member with clinical intellect and a scientific obsession with the geth; she judged Tali'Zorah's trial, developed geth ladar jamming that triggered the quarian war, and created the Sync Laser used in the Rannoch Reaper-base assault. She was voiced by Claudia Black. (source: Daro'Xen vas Moreh)
- Admiral Han'Gerrel vas Neema was a quarian Admiralty Board member commanding the Heavy Fleet, a longtime troublemaker on the turian border and a leading advocate of retaking the quarian homeworld from the geth (source: Han'Gerrel vas Neema).
- Admiral Rael'Zorah was Tali'Zorah's distant, demanding father and a member of the quarian Admiralty Board, obsessed with reclaiming Rannoch; as a trainee he and Han'Gerrel earned early Pilgrimages for defending the gunship Yaska against batarians. (source: Rael'Zorah)
- Admiral Ravis Kandros was a Turian Hierarchy official of the prominent Kandros family and mother of Lieutenant Tiran Kandros; she convened the meeting that authorized Tiran's covert infiltration of the Andromeda Initiative over forbidden geth technology. (source: Ravis Kandros)
- Admiral Shala'Raan vas Tonbay, called "Auntie Raan" by Tali, sat on the quarian Admiralty Board and moderated Tali's treason trial after recusing herself; in Mass Effect 3 she took Tali's role if Tali was dead, opposed Han'Gerrel's firing on the geth dreadnought, and afterward renamed herself Shala'Raan vas Rannoch (source: Shala'Raan vas Tonbay).
- Admiral Steven Hackett, born in Buenos Aires in 2134, commanded the Alliance Fifth Fleet from Arcturus Station, rose from enlisted man to admiral, fought in the First Contact War, and was one of three officers who recommended Shepard as the first human Spectre (source: Steven Hackett)
- Admiral Tadius Ahern was a Systems Alliance officer commanding Pinnacle Station, a former turian command center turned Citadel Council training facility; a First Contact War veteran, he trained turian officers despite a grudge against turians. (source: Tadius Ahern)
- Adrien Victus: turian general and First Contact War veteran known for clever, unorthodox tactics; became Primarch of Palaven in 2186 after Primarch Fedorian was killed on Menae. Led the war summit, backed curing the genophage in exchange for krogan aid, and lost his son Lieutenant Tarquin Victus, who died defusing a hidden turian bomb in Tuchanka's Kelphic Valley. (source: Adrien Victus)
- Aeian T'Goni: asari huntress ("PTSD Soldier") treated for PTSD at Huerta Memorial Hospital in 2186 after the Reaper attack on the colony Tiptree, where she was forced to kill a human girl, Hilary (revealed to be Joker's sister), and saw her friend Neaira turned into a banshee. Commits suicide if Shepard grants her a firearm. (source: Aeian T'Goni)
- After Shepard's death, Garrus became the Omega vigilante "Archangel," leading a twelve-member squad against the Blue Suns, Blood Pack, and Eclipse until his lieutenant Sidonis betrayed them; the three merc groups allied to corner him, and a Blue Suns gunship scarred the right side of his face. (source: Garrus Vakarian)
- After learning of the Reapers and the Collectors' true nature, the yahg Broker dealt with the Collectors to secure his own survival; Commander Shepard and Liara T'Soni killed him aboard his Hagalaz ship, after which Liara became the new Shadow Broker (source: Shadow Broker).
- After the Battle of the Citadel Hackett became head of the Alliance military, and by Mass Effect 3 was Fleet Admiral; he sacrificed the Second Fleet to save the Third and Fifth, oversaw construction of Project Crucible, and commanded Shield Fleet escorting the Crucible to the Citadel during the battle for Earth (source: Steven Hackett)
- Aleena: asari commando and mercenary, a close friend of Urdnot Wrex; when Wrex was contracted to kill her they dueled through a doomed salarian space station for days, and she apparently survived its core going critical, later taunting Wrex with "Better luck next time..." Producer Mike Gamble confirmed she is not Aria T'Loak. (source: Aleena)
- All three of Samara's daughters were Ardat-Yakshi; she recruited to Shepard's crew on Illium in 2185 by swearing the Third Oath of Subsumation. (source: Samara)
- Amanda Kenson: human scientist from the University of Arcturus who published findings that most mass relays predate the Protheans; led an expedition that found the Reaper artifact Object Rho near the Bahak system's Alpha Relay. Captured by batarians on Aratoht and rescued by Shepard at Admiral Steven Hackett's request, she was revealed to have been indoctrinated along with her "Project" team, turned on Shepard to stop the relay's destruction, and killed herself with a detonator at Project Base in 2185. (source: Amanda Kenson)
- Amul Shastri was Prime Minister of the Systems Alliance from at least 2185 until 2186, publicly downplaying the Reaper threat, and was killed with the entire Alliance Parliament when Reaper forces destroyed Arcturus Station, leaving Donnel Udina as the Alliance's de facto political leader (source: Amul Shastri).
- An unnamed turian in Mass Effect: Infiltrator was defeated by Cerberus operative Randall Ezno on an ice planet, captured, and made a test subject at the Cerberus facility known as the Barn (source: Turian (Mass Effect: Infiltrator)).
- An unnamed volus hacker, himself a prisoner held in a Medical Bay datacore at the Barn facility, served as Cerberus operative Randall Ezno's mission support in Mass Effect: Infiltrator, outmatching the facility's UDI AI and arranging his shuttle escape to LV426 (source: Volus (Mass Effect: Infiltrator)).
- Anderson stepped down as Normandy captain to make Shepard a Spectre, later served as Councilor or Admiral, led the human resistance on Earth during the Reaper War, and died in the Citadel Control chamber after the indoctrinated Illusive Man forced Shepard to shoot him. (source: David Anderson)
- Anita Goyle rejected Alec Ryder's proposals for AI research because AIs were illegal in Council space, and her corroboration of reports about his work contributed to his dismissal from the Alliance (source: Anita Goyle).
- Anita Goyle was the first human ambassador to the Citadel, later succeeded by Donnel Udina; in Mass Effect: Revelation she negotiated down Council sanctions after the Sidon AI research leak and had Anderson sent as a Spectre candidate (source: Anita Goyle).
- Anto Bek'tall was a batarian bodyguard and second-in-command to Aria T'Loak on Omega who secretly sold information and sought outside work; during the Cerberus occupation he was killed, almost incidentally, when Colonel Raymond Ashe remotely released a smuggled Adjutant inside Afterlife (source: Anto Bek'tall).
- April was a young colonist on Fehl Prime in 2185, daughter of Christine, who idolized Lieutenant James Vega; she was abducted by the Collectors and died with the other colonists when the Collector ship crashed before evacuation (source: April).
- Archuk was the ruthless krogan leader of a Blood Pack squad that besieged the human colony of Fehl Prime in 2183, breaking its kinetic barriers with a bomb; he defeated James Vega in a duel but was killed by Milque's sniper rifle (source: Archuk).
- Aresh Aghdashloo: a human biotic and survivor of the child experiments at the Cerberus Teltin Facility on Pragia; in Mass Effect 2 he hired Blood Pack mercenaries and tried to rebuild the facility, and Shepard could spare or kill him; if spared, he died in 2186 saving evacuating children on Reaper-occupied Elysium (source: Aresh Aghdashloo)
- Aria T'Loak's daughter Liselle was killed by Kai Leng during a Cerberus operation; the Illusive Man exploited Aria's misplaced suspicion of Paul Grayson to draw her into working with Cerberus (source: Aria T'Loak)
- Aria T'Loak: asari "Pirate Queen" and de facto ruler of Omega, a powerful biotic with commando training and over a thousand years of experience; deposed Omega's krogan ruler (the Patriarch), lost Omega to Cerberus under General Petrovsky, and in Mass Effect 3 allied with Shepard to unite the mercenary bands and retake the station (source: Aria T'Loak)
- Ariokis killed the mercenary Ygara Menoris on Illium to steal an AI kernel, then survived the chaos at Quiet Eddy and ultimately helped Cora Harper complete her SAM-E overclocking procedure and rescued her from augments (source: Vlassia Ariokis).
- Armando-Owen Bailey: human C-Sec officer in the Citadel's Zakera Ward, promoted from the ranks after the geth assault; a results-focused, unorthodox officer who aided Shepard across Mass Effect 2 and 3, was promoted to Commander after killing Executor Pallin, and helped expose Udina as the Cerberus coup's traitor (source: Armando-Owen Bailey)
- Armistan Banes: a researcher hired by the Systems Alliance who disappeared; his corpse was found on a derelict ship in the Sparta system, and the marines who recovered it (under Rear Admiral Kahoku) went missing. (source: Armistan Banes)
- Ashley Madeline Williams: human Systems Alliance soldier, Gunnery Chief in the 2nd Frontier Division, born April 14, 2158 on the colony Sirona; sole survivor of the 212 after the geth attack on Eden Prime and reassigned to the SSV Normandy under Commander Shepard. (source: Ashley Williams)
- Ashley Williams could die at Virmire if Shepard chose to save Kaidan Alenko instead; if she survived she returned in Mass Effect 2 confronting Shepard on Horizon, and in Mass Effect 3 as a Lieutenant Commander, was offered Spectre status by Udina, and could be killed or talked down during the Cerberus coup on the Citadel. (source: Ashley Williams)
- Ashley Williams was a potential romance partner for a male Shepard, spending the night with him before Ilos and able to rekindle the relationship in Mass Effect 3; she was religious and fond of Tennyson's poetry, especially "Ulysses." (source: Ashley Williams)
- Ashley Williams was critically injured on Mars by Dr. Eva on the Illusive Man's order and recovered at Huerta Memorial Hospital on the Citadel. (source: Ashley Williams)
- Ashley Williams was granddaughter of General Williams, who surrendered the garrison at Shanxi to the turians in the First Contact War, becoming the first human to surrender to an alien force; the resulting "Williams curse" stalled her Alliance career. (source: Ashley Williams)
- At Sanctuary, Oriana was held as a human shield by Henry Lawson until Miranda threw him from the tower; if Miranda died earlier, Oriana herself broadcast the warnings exposing Sanctuary and took the surname Lawson. (source: Oriana Lawson)
- Bates was a Systems Alliance soldier of Unit 212's Dog Squad on Eden Prime who fled the geth attack after refusing Ashley Williams' calls for support, was captured and interrogated by Kai Leng, and was then killed by Leng to make it look as though he died with his squad (Mass Effect: Foundation). (source: Bates)
- Batha was an asari bioscience and vaccination specialist from the Nemean Abyss, kidnapped with Doctor Hendricks to the Ahn'Kedar Orbital Platform and tricked into developing a vaccine for a batarian "blood plague" that was actually a bioweapon meant for the Citadel; rescued by Nax and Jacob Taylor, she completed the vaccine, cured Illo Nazario, and inoculated the crew and Citadel victims (Mass Effect Galaxy). (source: Batha)
- Bel Anoleis (full name Rannadril Ghan Swa Fulsoom Karaten Narr Eadi Bel Anoleis) was the corrupt salarian administrator of Noveria's Port Hanshan who stonewalled Shepard's investigation into Matriarch Benezia; depending on Shepard's choices he resigned under Board pressure, was arrested by undercover agent Gianna Parasini, or died in a shootout with her. (source: Bel Anoleis)
- Believing that because the Protheans became the Collectors who serve the Reapers the hanar should serve the Reapers too, Zymandis tried to upload a virus to disable the planetary defenses of Kahje; Spectre Jondum Bau, aided by Shepard (and Kasumi Goto), stopped him and Bau shot him dead. (source: Zymandis)
- Ben Hislop was a human munitions-expert mercenary and Jack Harper's right-hand man during and after the First Contact War, mutated by the Arca Monolith into a synthetic meta-turian servant of Desolas and Saren Arterius; he killed Eva Coré under the artifact's control, then rammed Desolas into it and died, and Harper later memorialized him in the Illusive Man's manifesto (Mass Effect: Evolution). (source: Ben Hislop)
- Boris Mikhailovich: Systems Alliance officer of the Fifth Fleet commanding the 63rd Scout Flotilla; as a Rear Admiral he gave the Normandy a hostile surprise inspection on the Citadel, objecting to its cost, stealth system, turian design and multi-species crew, with Shepard able to sway, rebuff, or refuse him. Led the 63rd against Sovereign at the Battle of the Citadel with the fewest losses of any Alliance flotilla, was promoted to Admiral, and aids the anti-Reaper war in Mass Effect 3 only if the Council was not saved. (source: Boris Mikhailovich)
- Blasto was a fictional hanar action-movie hero, billed as the Milky Way's first hanar Spectre; his films (Blasto: The Jellyfish Stings, Blasto 6: Partners in Crime, Blasto 7: Blasto Goes to War!) and a Dark Horse comic (Blasto: Eternity is Forever) ran from 2185 into the Andromeda galaxy centuries later, with Javik drafted into a satirical 2186 sequel alongside Shepard during the Citadel DLC. (source: Blasto)
- Brooks (Mass Effect: Foundation): human agent of the Shadow Broker whose real name is unknown; in 2161 CE she was sent to a mining facility orbiting Themis to kill the Cerberus financier Roth, using a child mine laborer to infiltrate the site and forcing a decoy to wire money to the banker Barla Von. After learning the real Roth had fled to Earth, she refused to take the child off-station and was fatally shot in the back by the child, who revealed herself to be a girl and stole Brooks' ship. Distinct from Maya Brooks of Mass Effect 3: Citadel. (source: Brooks (Mass Effect: Foundation))
- By 2185 Harkin operated on the Citadel as the racketeer "Fade," having helped Sidonis vanish into the Terminus Systems; Shepard and Garrus tracked him down during Garrus's loyalty mission and C-Sec later jailed him (source: Harkin).
- By 2186 Dr. Chloe Michel was the human medical lead at Huerta Memorial Hospital and could be recruited as the Normandy SR-2's doctor (requiring Shepard to first decline Dr. Chakwas if she survived the suicide mission); aboard ship she arranged a medical-supply trade with the refugee leader Tactus, befriended Engineer Greg Adams, brought Garrus turian chocolates for once saving her life in 2183, and argued that synthetics were not truly alive. (source: Chloe Michel)
- Captain David Edward Anderson (born June 8, 2137, London) was a First Contact War hero and first N7 graduate, original captain of the SSV Normandy and Shepard's mentor; he was kept out of the Spectres after Saren blamed him for a refinery disaster during the Kahlee Sanders rescue. (source: David Anderson)
- Captain Dillard was a Systems Alliance official overseeing the SSV Normandy's test flight at Arcturus Station who threatened Joker with court-martial for "stealing" the ship before General Invectus insisted Joker be made its pilot. (source: Dillard)
- Captain Preitor Gavorn was a turian hired by Aria T'Loak to keep the vorcha out of Omega's Afterlife Club, armed with an M-97 Viper; he dismissed the vorcha as a "pestilence" incapable of planning and expected steady work given how fast they bred. (source: Preitor Gavorn)
- Captain Toni was the strict but respected leader of the Alliance special forces unit Delta Squad, deployed to the pharmaceutical colony Fehl Prime, whose troops were untested rookies (source: Toni).
- Captain Vlassia Ariokis (service number 6732-IC-1917) was an Alliance Intelligence operative at the Quiet Eddy black ops facility who viewed assassinations as judgment calls (source: Vlassia Ariokis).
- Captain Wasea: asari commando leading an Eclipse cadre on Illium; Shepard fought and killed her while helping the justicar Samara identify the ship Eclipse used to smuggle Samara's target off world. Her Eclipse cell had drugged the volus Niftu Cal into displaying biotic powers, and in battle she wielded an M-300 Claymore, used Warp, and threw canisters of the biotic-boosting toxin Minagen. Voiced by Gwendoline Yeo. (source: Captain Wasea)
- Castis Vakarian: retired C-Sec officer and father of Garrus and Solana Vakarian; taught Garrus to shoot on Palaven, repeatedly clashed with his son over Garrus's rule-breaking methods, and talked him out of becoming a Spectre. Befriended human military attache Alec Ryder and kept up the friendship after Alec's discharge; in Mass Effect 3 he believed Garrus's account of the Reapers and pushed Primarch Fedorian into forming a turian task force for Palaven. Stranded on Palaven with Solana during the Reaper attack, both later escaped off-world with their ultimate fate unknown. (source: Castis Vakarian)
- Catalyst: ancient artificial intelligence (also called the Intelligence) residing in the Citadel, embodying the collective consciousness and memories of the Reapers; created by the Leviathans to oversee organic-synthetic relations, it instead built the Reapers and the cycle of harvests, making the first Reaper, Harbinger, from the Leviathans themselves, and commanded the building of the mass relays. It appeared to Commander Shepard as a translucent boy killed on Earth and offered the endgame choices of Destroy, Control and Synthesis. (source: Catalyst)
- Cathka: male batarian Blue Suns mechanic and sergeant on Omega during the operation to kill Archangel; nominally part of the infiltration team, he stayed at the gunship hangar to run repairs and organize the hired freelancers. He briefed Commander Shepard on the mercenary plan during the recruitment mission, and Shepard could pick up a sparking tool to incapacitate him and stop him repairing Tarak's gunship. (source: Cathka)
- Cerberus and the Illusive Man contracted Zaeed to join Shepard's mission against the Collectors, recruiting him on Omega with his fee paid personally by the Illusive Man. (source: Zaeed Massani)
- Charles Saracino was the head of the pro-human Terra Firma party, met by Commander Shepard at an Armistice Day protest on the Citadel while campaigning for a "spacer" seat in the Alliance parliament at Arcturus Station; the Illusive Man had his rival Claude Menneau assassinated because Saracino was seen as easier to influence, and depending on whether Shepard endorsed him he either won further parliamentary seats or was convicted of tax evasion. (source: Charles Saracino)
- Charn: batarian second-in-command to Balak during the batarian attack on Asteroid X57 (Mass Effect: Bring Down the Sky); resentful that Balak turned a slave raid into a plot to crash the asteroid into the colony world, he stayed only out of fear of Balak and guarded the final fusion torch. Commander Shepard could talk him into leaving peacefully in exchange for a facility pass and a promise to kill Balak, or kill him and take the pass from his body. Voiced by Jason Singer. (source: Charn)
- Charr: gentle, poetry-reciting krogan transport technician on Illium, unusually sensitive for his species, in a rocky relationship with the asari merchant Ereba; Shepard could help reconcile them, after which he brought her to Tuchanka. By 2186 he had joined krogan scouts sent to Utukku to check for rachni; his unit was wiped out and his body, holding a final recorded message for Ereba, could be found in the caves and delivered to her on the Citadel. (source: Charr)
- Chellick (first name Decian per cut content; spelled "Chellik" in Mass Effect: Homeworlds): C-Sec detective on the Citadel and former colleague of Garrus Vakarian. In Homeworlds he dismissively processed the quarian stowaway Tali'Zorah nar Rayya; in Mass Effect he ran an anti-arms-smuggling operation using the informant Jenna at Chora's Den and asked Shepard to meet his krogan contact Jax posing as a buyer, rewarding Shepard with weapon mods if the cover held. A later Mass Effect 2 news vid mislabels him "Executor Chellick." (source: Chellick)
- Chief Weyrloc Guld: krogan leader of Clan Weyrloc on Tuchanka, bent on curing the genophage at any cost, including experiments on humans and willing clan members. During Mordin Solus's 2185 loyalty mission he offered to let Shepard leave to spread word of Weyrloc dominance, but Shepard killed him and his soldiers while reaching Mordin's student Maelon. Reputed among his followers to have a "destiny" for fathering two living children, one of them female. (source: Chief Weyrloc Guld)
- Chorban: self-proclaimed salarian scientist who, with his volus partner Jahleed, stole and reconfigured a medical scanner to study the Citadel's keepers; first met by Shepard in the Citadel Tower. Shepard could take over the keeper scans, mediate his falling-out with Jahleed, or kill him. If he survived, a Mass Effect 2 email reported the keepers were bio-engineered millions of years ago by Sovereign's makers and set to react to a signal roughly every 50,000 years, timed to recur around 2185, implying the Citadel was not built by the Protheans. (source: Chorban)
- Christine: determined human colonist on Fehl Prime and mother of April, from Mass Effect: Paragon Lost; she rallied the colonists during the 2183 Blood Pack siege, buying time for Alliance marines. In 2185 she and April were abducted by the Collectors; revived aboard the Collector ship, they died with all the other colonists when James Vega's choice led to the ship crashing. (source: Christine)
- Claude Menneau was a candidate for leadership of the Terra Firma party in 2173 after Inez Simmons resigned over the Nashan Stellar Dynamics kickback scandal; leading Charles Saracino in the polls, he disappeared with his vessel and bodyguards while en route to Shanxi. (source: Claude Menneau)
- Claude Menneau was assassinated by the Cerberus operative Pel, who with pilot Paul Grayson had been planted to shadow him, so that the Illusive Man's preferred candidate Charles Saracino could take the Terra Firma leadership unopposed. (source: Claude Menneau)
- Cohen asked Shepard to help produce the Thoros-B cure, and when the asari commando Alestia Iallis (transferred to Peak 15 by Saren Arterius) ambushed the squad he claimed to be genuinely surprised; he could lend Shepard his project-lead pass to the maintenance area between the quarantine and secure labs. (source: Zev Cohen)
- Collector General: an insectoid, centauroid entity with eight bladed forelimbs and retractable wings that oversaw all Collector operations from beyond the Omega 4 Relay and could possess any Collector drone at any distance, unlocking its biotic potential; it served as the intermediary between the Collectors and the Reaper Harbinger. (source: Collector General)
- Colonel Raymond Ashe was a ruthless, alien-hating Cerberus operative who, during the Adjutant crisis on Omega, orchestrated his own imprisonment, killed his guards, seized Omega Control, and broadcast a Cerberus coup declaration. (source: Raymond Ashe)
- Commander Rentola was a salarian officer, quartermaster and Kirrahe's second-in-command in the 3rd STG on Virmire; he always survived the assault on Saren's base and later helped deploy the modified genophage on Tuchanka with Mordin Solus (source: Rentola)
- Commander Shepard was named after astronaut Alan Bartlett Shepard Jr. and voiced by Mark Meer (male) and Jennifer Hale (female). (source: Commander Shepard)
- Commander Shepard was the customizable Systems Alliance N7 protagonist of the original trilogy (born April 11, 2154; service no. 5923-AC-2826), a Skyllian Blitz veteran and biotic, whose class, pre-service history (Spacer, Earthborn, Colonist), and psychological profile (Sole Survivor of Akuze, War Hero of Elysium, or Ruthless at Torfan) were set at character creation. (source: Commander Shepard)
- Conrad Verner had a sister, Cassandra Verner, who joined the Andromeda Initiative and thought little of him. (source: Conrad Verner)
- Conrad Verner was an obsessive fan of Commander Shepard who recurred only in imported save files, aspiring to be a Spectre, later impersonating an Alliance officer on Illium and founding a refugee charity called "Shepards." (source: Conrad Verner)
- Corporal Richard L. Jenkins was an Alliance Marine from Eden Prime aboard the SSV Normandy who was killed by geth drones shortly after landing on Eden Prime; his name was later added to the Normandy SR-2 memorial wall (source: Richard L. Jenkins)
- Corporal Toombs was an Alliance marine and survivor of the Akuze thresher maw attack, which Cerberus had engineered; Cerberus captured him afterward and experimented on him for years, including injecting him with thresher maw acid (source: Toombs).
- Cortez previously served in the First Fleet aboard the SSV Hawking flying F-61 Tridents, and during the final assault on Earth his shuttle was shot down by a Harvester; he survived only if he had been encouraged to visit the refugees' Memorial Wall (source: Steve Cortez)
- Crooks was a Systems Alliance pilot originally assigned to the SSV Normandy; Joker Moreau locked him in a shuttle at Arcturus Station during the hijacking test flight and replaced him as pilot at General Invectus's insistence. (source: Crooks)
- Dahlia Dantius was the sister of asari diplomat Nassana Dantius, a biotic slaver and pirate leader in the Artemis Tau cluster based on Sharjila; she blackmailed Nassana, who sent Shepard to kill her under a false kidnapping story. (source: Dahlia Dantius)
- David Archer was the autistic mathematical savant brother of Cerberus scientist Gavin Archer; forcibly merged into Project Overlord's control VI on Aite, he drove it berserk, was freed by Shepard, and could later be sent to Grissom Academy and then help build the Crucible. (source: David Archer)
- Delan (Delanynder) was a polite hanar merchant who ran the Emporium on the Citadel Presidium in the first Mass Effect, selling luxury goods, corporate licenses, and a Medi-gel upgrade. (source: Delan)
- Delan was a human mechanic on the colony Horizon in Mass Effect 2 who distrusted the Systems Alliance, hid during the Collector attack, refused to help Shepard, and walked away disillusioned afterward. (source: Delan (Mass Effect 2))
- Desolas Arterius was a turian general in the First Contact War and Saren Arterius' older brother who, in Mass Effect: Evolution, sought to use the Arca Monolith to turn the turians into super-soldiers; his brother had Temple Palaven destroyed by orbital strike, killing him. (source: Desolas Arterius)
- Detective Anaya was an asari officer of Illium Law Enforcement in Nos Astra who investigated the murder of Dakni Kur and was ordered to detain the justicar Samara, later thanking Commander Shepard for resolving the situation (source: Anaya).
- Diana Allers was a human Alliance News Network war correspondent from Milgrom, Bekenstein, host of "Battlespace," who could embed aboard the Normandy SR-2 during the Reaper War, report on Earth or Cerberus to aid the war effort, and be romanced by Shepard in a unique no-commitment arc; if refused she died aboard the SSV Shasta. (source: Diana Allers)
- Din Korlack was the volus ambassador to the Citadel, sharing an office with the elcor ambassador Calyn; openly hostile to humans in Mass Effect, later revealed to have had Cerberus ties that he severed, becoming a turian bounty target rescued by Shepard and Zaeed in Mass Effect 3. (source: Din Korlack)
- Doctor Droyas was a krogan researcher at Saren's Virmire base working to counter the genophage, killed alongside husk test subjects when Shepard's squad stormed his lab. (source: Doctor Droyas)
- Doctor Saleon was a salarian geneticist who used his employees as living incubators for cloned organs he sold on the black market; he fled the Citadel as "Dr. R. Heart" aboard the MSV Fedele and was killed when Shepard and Garrus tracked him down. (source: Doctor Saleon)
- Donnel Udina was humanity's Citadel ambassador (successor to Anita Goyle), later the first human Councilor, who distrusted Shepard, played it safe politically, and in Mass Effect 3 became the Alliance's top political figure after Arcturus Station's destruction. (source: Donnel Udina)
- Donovan Hock was an Afrikaans-accented weapons dealer, smuggler, and art patron on Bekenstein who murdered Keiji Okuda and stole his graybox; during Kasumi Goto's loyalty mission he confronted Shepard and Kasumi and was killed when his A-61 Mantis Gunship was shot down. (source: Donovan Hock)
- Dr. Alestia Iallis: asari molecular geneticist stationed at Peak 15 on Noveria, transferred there at the behest of Saren Arterius. Secretly an asari commando under Matriarch Benezia's orders to kill Shepard, she ambushed the squad with geth at Rift Station and was killed. (source: Alestia Iallis)
- Dr. Anneliese "Ann" Bryson, born 2158, was a Systems Alliance scientist and daughter of Dr. Garret Bryson, a member of Task Force Aurora who led Project Scarab to Namakli, was briefly possessed by Leviathan, and could later aid the Crucible project with her paleotechnology research (source: Ann Bryson).
- Dr. Brynn Cole: ex-Cerberus scientist who researched Collector technology and Reaper-to-Collector communication; after realizing the Illusive Man was disappearing project members, she led a group of Cerberus defectors and fled, later sheltering in a base on Gellix. Partner of Jacob Taylor if he survived the suicide mission, with whom she smuggled out dozens of scientists; if rescued by Shepard she joins the Crucible Project under Admiral Hackett, and is later revealed to be pregnant with Jacob's child. (source: Brynn Cole)
- Dr. Chloe Michel: civilian human doctor, daughter of Alliance medics, who ran the Med Clinic in the Citadel's Upper Wards after being fired from a previous practice for giving away supplies.
