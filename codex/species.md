# Species

- A thresher maw grew over 30 meters above ground with a larger body beneath; it fed by a photosynthesis using solar radiation plus consumed minerals, and attacked with acid spit, burrowing ambushes and infrasound claw strikes that shields could not block (source: Thresher Maw)
- An alien flu outbreak during the raloi welcome ceremonies forced the raloi to wear environmental suits around other species; the elcor had known of the raloi for generations, preserved in the mnemonic "Happy Turians Treat Raloi and Rachni to Dinner." (source: Raloi)
- An individual geth program was roughly VI-level; geth reasoned only in groups, with a mobile platform housing around a hundred programs and Legion housing 1,183. They had no government, rank, individuality, or capacity for deceit, and decided matters by consensus. (source: Geth)
- Ardat-Yakshi: asari with a rare genetic defect that destroys a partner's nervous system during melding, occurring almost exclusively among "purebloods" born of two asari parents. (source: Asari)
- Asari government is the Asari Republics, an inclusive electronic democracy deferring to Matriarchs; the military resembles tribal warrior bands of professional commando huntresses skilled in ambush and assassination, backed by a powerful navy including the dreadnought Destiny Ascension. (source: Asari)
- Asari pass through three life stages: Maiden (exploration, from puberty), Matron (settling and child-rearing, around age 350), and Matriarch (community sage, around age 700). (source: Asari)
- Asari reproduce with a partner of any gender or species via parthenogenesis through a nervous-system "meld"; offspring are always asari, and melding can also be used to share thoughts and memories. (source: Asari)
- Asari: mono-gendered species native to Thessia, roughly thousand-year lifespan, natural biotics; the most influential and respected species in the galaxy and co-founders of the Citadel Council with the salarians in 500 BCE. (source: Asari)
- Because vorcha adapt as individuals, the species no longer evolves and its DNA-equivalent has been unchanged for millions of years; the vorcha never invented space travel but spread through the Terminus Systems as Blood Pack mercenary fodder after being discovered by another species (source: Vorcha).
- Before the Reaper invasion the quarians moved to retake Rannoch; depending on Shepard's actions the geth were destroyed and Rannoch reclaimed, the quarians were wiped out, or the two races made peace. (source: Quarian)
- Clan Nakmor joined the Andromeda Initiative, underwent cryosleep gene therapy that slightly improved birth viability, and later founded New Tuchanka on Elaaden under Overlord Nakmor Morda after a broken Nexus leadership deal. (source: Krogan)
- Collector drones are periodically possessed by the Collector General, an overseer itself controlled by the Reaper Harbinger; a possessed Collector's skin cracks with red light, its eyes glow orange, and it gains resilience and powerful biotic attacks. (source: Collector)
- Collector military doctrine relied on hit-and-run abductions: a single Collector Cruiser carrying troops and Seeker Swarms would paralyze a colony's population, seal them in stasis pods, and flee if resistance threatened the ship, sometimes obliterating harvested settlements from orbit to remove evidence. (source: Collector)
- Collectors: an enigmatic insectoid race from beyond the Omega 4 Relay in the Terminus Systems, long dismissed as myth, revealed by their quad-strand DNA to be Protheans captured during their cycle's harvest and transformed by the Reapers through extensive genetic rewrites (three fewer chromosomes) and cybernetics into mindless drone clones lacking self-awareness, glands, or a digestive system. (source: Collector)
- Drell had an average lifespan of 85 galactic standard years, denser muscle tissue giving wiry strength, fused ring and middle fingers, two sets of eyelids, and skin infused with a mild hallucinogenic venom. (source: Drell)
- Drell possessed eidetic memory, allowing vivid, sometimes involuntary total recall triggered by external stimuli; Thane Krios could describe every assassination he performed in perfect detail. (source: Drell)
- Drell were largely deeply religious and polytheistic, worshipping gods including Amonkira (Lord of Hunters), Arashu (Goddess of Motherhood and Protection) and Kalahira (Goddess of Oceans and Afterlife), and believing body and soul form a separable Whole. (source: Drell)
- During the 2186 Reaper invasion Reaper husks were deployed on Irune, whose cities sat on trade routes rather than defensible ground; volus refugees fled to turian colonies and a joint turian-krogan force later landed to liberate the planet (source: Volus).
- During the 2186 Reaper invasion the asari initially withheld support, revealed the Prothean beacon to Commander Shepard after Thessia was attacked, lost Thessia, and then committed forces to the Allied war effort. (source: Asari)
- During the 2186 Reaper invasion the Collectors returned from dark space aboard low-velocity transports called Black Arks to act as reserves wiping out pockets of resistance; the Leviathans could sever Harbinger's control over Collectors, and though most so freed died, a few "awakened" Collectors fought alongside the galaxy against the Reapers. (source: Collector)
- During the 2186 Reaper invasion the Reapers could not cow the vorcha or starve them of resources, so they focused on immobilizing Heshtok's population; alien-officered vorcha units had mixed results, with the Void Devils fighting well at Heshtok and a vorcha fleet annihilated at Lihrat (source: Vorcha).
- During the 2186 Reaper War drell served as Adepts, Vanguards and Infiltrators; over 4,500 drell also joined the Andromeda Initiative aboard the ark Keelah Si'yah. (source: Drell)
- Elcor: massive, slow, immensely strong quadrupedal Citadel species from the high-gravity world Dekuuna; communicate through scent, infrasound and micro-movements, prefixing speech to other species with an emotive statement; governed by the precedent-bound Courts of Dekuuna and reliant on VI combat systems, with soldiers nicknamed "living tanks"; navy decimated defending Dekuuna in the 2186 Reaper invasion (source: Elcor).
- Freed by Shepard, the Rachni Queen rebuilt her species and later aided the war effort; the Reapers on Utukku converted indoctrinated rachni into Ravagers that spawn Swarmers. (source: Rachni)
- Geth subtypes included Hoppers, Troopers, Destroyers, Hunters, Pyros, Snipers, Armatures, Colossi, Primes, Juggernauts, and Dreadnoughts; their doctrine emphasized ambush and surprise, and they expended platforms freely since destroyed programs were archived and re-downloaded. (source: Geth)
- Harbinger, the first Reaper, remained in dark space and directed the Collectors, able to take direct control of individual Collectors in battle, and devised the plan to build the human-Reaper (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 2) — Constructs and Capital Classes)
- Human biotics arose only from pre-natal element zero exposure, which carried high medical risk; humans also had unusually high genetic diversity, useful as an experimental control group (source: Human)
- Humanity gained a Citadel embassy in 2165; most humans were governed by the Systems Alliance from Arcturus Station, with independent fringe colonies, Earth nation-states and the corporate-linked Union of Incorporated Nations also holding power (source: Human)
- Humans were physically comparable to turians in unarmed combat, resembled but were less agile than asari, and were stronger but slower than salarians; they lived to about 150 years and had eradicated nearly all disease (source: Human)
- Humans, from Earth, were the newest major sentient species on the galactic stage and the fastest-expanding, having discovered a Prothean data cache on Mars in 2148 and the mass relay network soon after (source: Human)
- In 2185 Mordin Solus revealed krogan were slowly evolving against the genophage, and his STG team applied a modified version; in the 2186 Reaper War Shepard could cure it at the Shroud in exchange for krogan aid, or let salarian sabotage stand. (source: Krogan)
- In 2185 the virtual aliens' "ghost ship" entered the salarian Antilin system seeking a new power source; contact was made by human exobiologist Dr. Jordan Detweiler, and Ambassador Sygan used his body to petition the Citadel Council, after which some 400 individuals of various races volunteered to swap places with virtual aliens (source: Virtual Alien).
- In 2185 the virtual aliens' ship sought salarian help as its power failed; via Dr. Jordan Deitweiler and Ambassador Sygan they revealed they could inhabit the bodies of people who entered their virtual world, and around 400 individuals later swapped places with them (source: Two Races We Never See — the Reloi and the "Virtual Aliens").
- In 2186 the Reapers invaded through batarian space, overran Earth and began a harvest that killed millions before Commander Shepard united the galaxy and the Crucible retook Earth (source: Human)
- In 2186 the Reloi withdrew entirely from the Citadel and isolated themselves on Tervest, unable and unwilling to fight the Reapers, planning to destroy their orbital equipment so the Reapers would mistake them for a pre-spaceflight species (source: Two Races We Never See — the Reloi and the "Virtual Aliens").
- In Mass Effect 3 the quarians attacked Rannoch and destroyed the geth megastructure, prompting the geth to accept Reaper control code to survive; Shepard's choices let the geth achieve true individual consciousness, be wiped out, or make peace and co-exist with the quarians on Rannoch. (source: Geth)
- Indoctrination, a permanent mental erosion inflicted by proximity to Reapers or their artifacts, could turn influential individuals or whole civilizations into willing Reaper slaves. (source: Reaper)
- Kalros, "the mother from which all other thresher maws spawn" and the largest ever seen, roamed Tuchanka near the Shroud and in 2186 was summoned by maw hammers to drag a Reaper Destroyer underground (source: Thresher Maw)
- Killing a thresher maw on foot was a rare krogan feat; Urdnot Wrex was the last to do so before Grunt faced one during Clan Urdnot's Rite of Passage (source: Thresher Maw)
- Krogan are large reptilian bipeds from Tuchanka, a harsh predator-filled world they rendered a radioactive wasteland in a nuclear war roughly 4,000 years ago before being uplifted by the salarians. (source: Krogan)
- Krogan Berserkers were tank-bred krogan created by the warlord Okeer to be "perfect krogan" free of genophage markers and "weak genes"; most were failures given to Blue Suns commander Jedore, while the one specimen Okeer judged perfect became Grunt. (source: Krogan Berserker)
- Krogan biology features redundant organs (two hearts, four lungs, four testicles, hence "quad"), a neuroconductive backup circulatory system that makes them near-impossible to paralyze, hazard-resistant hides, fluid-storing shoulder humps, 240-degree vision, lifespans over a thousand years, and a berserk "blood rage." (source: Krogan)
- Marauders could reinforce husks and cannibals by wrapping them in an energy ribbon that hardened into scab-like armor plating, immobilizing both for several seconds; Alliance standing orders were to kill the Marauder first (source: Marauder).
- Marauders were seen interfacing with a Leviathan orb artifact near the end of the Namakli Leviathan mission (source: Marauder).
- Marauders were synthetic-organic Reaper troops created from harvested turians, serving as support units, front-line fighters, and squad leaders in the Reaper army in Mass Effect 3 (source: Marauder).
- Marauders were the only Reaper units protected by shields rather than armor or biotic barriers, and the only ones to use conventional-style firearms (Phaeston rifles); per Aeian T'Goni, those weapons could not be used by anyone but Marauders (source: Marauder).
- Mordin Solus assessed that the Collectors have no culture, art, or self-preservation instinct, fighting to the death even when pointless, and that their bases and technology are built purely for utility with no ergonomics. (source: Collector)
- On humid Kahje, drell required climate-controlled dome cities, and the leading cause of drell death became Kepral's Syndrome, an incurable, noncommunicable degradation of the lungs caused by long-term humidity exposure. (source: Drell)
- Prothean intervention secretly accelerated asari civilization: Protheans posed as the goddess Athame, enhanced asari biotics, and left a beacon containing the VI Vendetta in the Temple of Athame on Thessia, kept a state secret until 2186. (source: Asari)
- Quarians created the geth around the 1850s CE as VI-grade labor; when the geth became sentient the panicked government ordered them destroyed, the geth won, and billions of quarians died and the survivors fled to the Migrant Fleet. (source: Quarian)
- Quarians were a nomadic humanoid species with weak immune systems who must live permanently in sealed enviro-suits; they are a dextro-protein species like turians, with three-fingered hands and UV-capable vision. (source: Quarian)
- Reaper ground forces included husks (converted corpses), cannibals (mutated batarians), marauders (turians), brutes (turian-krogan), ravagers (rachni artillery), banshees (Ardat-Yakshi asari), harvesters (flying gunship-transports), the Oculus drone, and swarmers (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 1) — Origin, Purpose, and Ground Forces)
- Reaper harvesting rounded victims into husk-run camps, sorted them by genetic fitness, and dissolved the fit into genetic paste (minds preserved as a Reaper's gestalt consciousness) while the unfit became husks; unsuitable populations were also converted via dragon's teeth. (source: Reaper)
- Reaper subtypes included the first Reaper Harbinger, two-kilometer Sovereign-class Capital Ships, non-sentient remotely controlled Troop Transports and Processors, and 160-meter Destroyers that could walk as heavy ground units. (source: Reaper)
- Reapers were gigantic biomechanical machines built partly from harvested organic beings, resembling space squids and ranging from building- to mountain-sized, each housing the fused minds of billions of one species (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 1) — Origin, Purpose, and Ground Forces)
- Roughly three centuries after the Morning War, the Reaper Sovereign (Nazara) offered the geth technology to build their Dyson-sphere-like "megastructure" in exchange for aid; the majority refused and called the Reapers "Old Machines," while a heretic minority (about five percent) accepted and served Saren Arterius. (source: Geth)
- Salarian young psychologically imprinted on the clan Dalatrass who isolated herself with their clutch, and salarians tended lifelong to defer to those they knew in youth. (source: Salarian)
- Salarians had no concept of romantic love or sexual attraction, and Mordin Solus stated their reproduction was not hormone-based. (source: Salarian)
- Salarians uplifted the krogan to throw their numbers against the rachni; after the rachni were eradicated the krogan overpopulated, seized inhabited worlds, and were struck with the turian-deployed genophage that cut viable births to about one in a thousand. (source: Krogan)
- Salarians were haplo-diploid egg-layers: unfertilized eggs produced males and fertilized eggs females, and social codes kept about 90 percent of the species male. (source: Salarian)
- Swarmers had almost no health, died on their own after a limited lifespan, and were unaffected by non-damaging freezing powers; their design deliberately echoed the "facehuggers" of the Alien films, matching the rachni's overall Alien-inspired origin. (source: Swarmer)
- Swarmers were small synthetic-organic creatures in Mass Effect 3, presumably derived from Rachni Workers and mutated by Reaper technology. (source: Swarmer)
- Swarmers were spawned in groups by puncturing the orange sacs on Ravagers or destroying Gestation Pods; they attacked with a suicide leap causing explosive and acid damage, and a Swarmer that died on contact left a damaging acid puddle. (source: Swarmer)
- The Alliance first met thresher maws on Akuze in 2177, where they wiped out a marine force; Cerberus was said to have engineered the massacre, and a later one on Edolus, to study the creatures (source: Thresher Maw)
- The Citadel Council formally welcomed the raloi in 2185, ejecting krogan delegates for introducing the lethal sport Kowla; in 2186 the raloi withdrew from the Citadel, isolated themselves on Turvess, and destroyed their satellites hoping the Reapers would spare a seemingly pre-spaceflight world. (source: Raloi)
- The drell repay the hanar through the Compact, an obligation fulfilled by performing difficult tasks such as combat; being asked to serve is considered a great honor. (source: Drell)
- The drell were a reptile-like race rescued from their dying homeworld Rakhana by the hanar around the 1980s CE; only about 375,000 were saved and relocated to the hanar homeworld Kahje, while billions died in resource wars. (source: Drell)
- The First Contact War (2157) began when turians attacked a human fleet activating a dormant relay and occupied Shanxi; Admiral Kastanie Drescher's Second Fleet retook it and the Council brokered peace, making the Systems Alliance humanity's governing body (source: Human)
- The geth ("Servant of the People" in Khelish) were networked artificial intelligences created by the quarians as labor and war machines; they gained emergent sentience through their network and won the Morning War, exiling the quarians as the Migrant Fleet while deliberately allowing them to flee. (source: Geth)
- The harvest: every 50,000 years the Reapers let civilizations rise using the Citadel and mass relays they secretly built, then returned to destroy advanced species and preserve their minds inside new Reapers (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 1) — Origin, Purpose, and Ground Forces)
- The human-Reaper larva was an unfinished Reaper grown from abducted humans reduced to genetic material; it had a humanoid upper body, was ~200 m tall even as a larva, and was destroyed by Shepard's team (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 2) — Constructs and Capital Classes)
- The Leviathans were ~450 m ocean-dwelling apex creatures who ruled the galaxy billions of years ago, controlled other species through mind control, and hid on ocean worlds after their AI became the Reapers (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 2) — Constructs and Capital Classes)
- The Lystheni were a salarian offshoot, currently unwelcome in Council space and found among galactic outcasts on Omega. (source: Salarian)
- The Protheans arose on a lost homeworld, learned mass effect physics from inusannon ruins, and built a galaxy-wide empire that assimilated all resisting organic species under one rule and one military doctrine. (source: Prothean)
- The Protheans bred rachni as war weapons until they rebelled; the krogan later exterminated the rachni by 300 CE, but a cryo-suspended queen egg survived and was hatched by Binary Helix on Noveria. (source: Rachni)
- The Protheans were an anthropoidal race with two pairs of dual-pupiled eyes and a head carapace who could absorb memories, skills and language by touch; they had quad-strand DNA and some wielded potent biotics. (source: Prothean)
- The rachni were an insect-like hive-minded species from tidally locked Suen, with Workers, Soldiers, biotic Brood Warriors and giant Queens who guide the hive by telepathic "song." (source: Rachni)
- The raloi were a feathered avian species from the planet Turvess who made first contact with the asari in 2184 after their first space telescope detected the asari cruiser Azedes. (source: Raloi)
- The Reaper War (2186) destroyed the Batarian Hegemony within days and overran Earth, Palaven and Thessia; it ended with the Crucible and its Catalyst (the Citadel) offering Shepard the choice of Destroy, Control, or Synthesis. (source: Reaper)
- The Reapers arrived around 48,000 BCE through the Citadel, used Prothean census data to hunt every Prothean, and over centuries killed or enslaved them all; survivors like Javik and the Ilos team persisted in stasis. (source: Prothean)
- The Reapers originated about a billion years ago when an AI built by the Leviathans to end organic-synthetic conflict turned on its makers, wiped out most of them, and used their bodies and minds to build the first Reaper, Harbinger (source: Reaper Classes and Sizes (Part 1) — Origin, Purpose, and Ground Forces)
- The Reapers repurposed captured Protheans into the Collectors, and the Protheans had earlier cultivated the asari, humans and hanar; the hanar worship them as the "Enkindlers." (source: Prothean)
- The Reapers were a machine race of synthetic-organic starships that hibernated in dark space for 50,000-year cycles; each was "billions of organic minds, uploaded and conjoined within immortal machine bodies," their standardized exterior derived from the ancient Leviathans. (source: Reaper)
- The Reapers were created by the Leviathans' "Intelligence" to solve organic-synthetic conflict; it processed the Leviathans into Harbinger and ran cyclical galactic purges for millions of years, wiping out the Protheans 50,000 years ago. (source: Reaper)
- The Reloi were an avian, feathered, spacefaring race from the planet Tervest, known to the elcor, discovered by the asari in 2184 and welcomed to the galactic community in 2185; contact ceremonies gave them an alien flu, prompting environmental suits (source: Two Races We Never See — the Reloi and the "Virtual Aliens").
- The salarians uplifted the krogan with technology and a new homeworld to defeat the rachni, then developed the genophage that the turians deployed to end the Krogan Rebellions. (source: Salarian)
- The salarians were warm-blooded amphibians from Sur'Kesh, the second species to join the Citadel after the asari; their hyperactive metabolism let them function on one hour of sleep, granted photographic memory, and limited their lifespan to roughly 40 human years. (source: Salarian)
- The turians were a militaristic, disciplined species from Palaven in the Trebia system, the third race to join the Citadel Council; their civilization spanned fifteen thousand years and was known to the Protheans, who deemed them primitive (source: Turian).
- The unnamed "virtual aliens" escaped their homeworld's impending supernova ~8,000 years before the trilogy by uploading roughly one billion consciousnesses, plus an entire virtual world, into an AI-piloted starship that drifted for millennia (source: Two Races We Never See — the Reloi and the "Virtual Aliens").
- The virtual aliens were a race of roughly one billion who, about 8,000 years ago, downloaded their minds into a supercomputer-hosted virtual world aboard a starship to escape their homeworld's imminent supernova, piloted by a purpose-built AI on a millennia-long galactic journey (source: Virtual Alien).
- The volus hailed from Irune, a high-pressure world with ammonia-based biochemistry and 1.5 g gravity; they wore pressurized suits and breathers because normal air was poisonous and low pressure would split their flesh, and all volus contracted the disease Yoqtan in childhood as a rite of passage (source: Volus).
- The volus were the third race to post a Citadel embassy, authored the Unified Banking Act that made the credit the standard interstellar currency, and became a client state of the Turian Hierarchy after first contact during the Krogan Rebellions, yet were never offered a Council seat (source: Volus).
- The vorcha of Heshtok were the shortest-lived known sapient species, averaging a 20-year lifespan, and possessed clusters of non-differentiated cells that let individuals rapidly adapt to a single environment (thicker skin, stronger muscles, better lungs), regenerate, regrow limbs, and resist nearly all disease (source: Vorcha).
- Thorian Creepers were corpse-like humanoid creatures grown by the Thorian, hibernating (and invulnerable) until roused to claw or vomit shield-ignoring green acid; they were shipped to Nodacrux and Binthu for study (source: Thorian Creeper)
- Thresher maws were giant burrowing carnivores that spent their lives hunting food, reproducing via spores hardy enough to survive deep space and re-entry, which spread them across many worlds (source: Thresher Maw)
- Thriving on Sur'Kesh 50,000 years before the modern age, salarians were kept by the Protheans as "lizard people" and eaten as a delicacy, according to Javik. (source: Salarian)
- Turian history included the Unification War among distant colonies, the Krogan Rebellions (in which they deployed the salarian genophage and won a Council seat by 900 CE), the 2157 Relay 314 Incident against humanity, and the 2186 Reaper War, which saw the Reapers destroy Taetrus and assault Palaven and Menae (source: Turian).
- Turians stood over six feet tall with avian, raptor-like features, taloned two-fingered hands, mandibles, flanging voices, dark blue blood, and a thulium-bearing metallic carapace evolved against Palaven's high solar radiation; they were viviparous, with females lacking the males' cranial crests (source: Turian).
- Turians were built on dextro-amino acids, sharing that trait only with quarians; levo food was non-nutritious at best and fatally allergenic at worst (source: Turian).
- Varren meat is a common food source (Illium's Fishdog Food Shack, Citadel margarine ads), salarians studied them as bioweapons ("species 408"), and Thessian varren can develop element-zero-fueled biotics; a tame retired pit champion named Urz lived beside the trader Ratch at the Urdnot Camp (source: Varren).
- Varren were vicious, rapidly breeding omnivores native to Tuchanka that hunt in packs and scavenge when outmatched; they followed krogan expansion everywhere as ecological pests, and krogan and batarians raise them as beasts of war and companions, with a silver-scaled subgenus nicknamed "fishdogs" (source: Varren).
