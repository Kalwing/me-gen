# Generation example — target density and flavour

An illustration of what a generated section should feel like: a narrator who reaches
for lore, history and worldbuilding, has opinions about them, and lets that material
carry real weight instead of sitting as garnish on a plot recap.

This is **not a template**. Bracketed items (`[Description]`,
`[Citadel's party dance scene description]`, `[if romanced: ...]`) are shorthand for
"the real, grounded lore goes here" — never literal output syntax. Actual sections are
continuous prose with no brackets, no labels, and no stage directions.
