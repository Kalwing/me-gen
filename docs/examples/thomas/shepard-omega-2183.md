## Setting

*Omega, 2183. Shepard has just been made the first human Spectre and handed the Normandy to chase Saren. Thomas has been on Omega for two years: short contracts, then let go for missed days, then a year with no work and no money. Lately he has kept the coolers running at a batarian noodle stall in the lower markets in exchange for meals. He and Shepard found each other at Akuze six years ago and have drifted since. The stall owner told Shepard where to look, and Shepard finds him sitting on a crate behind the stall. Roughly thirteen minutes. The tags follow the closed performance vocabulary, and there are no stage directions inside the speech.*
