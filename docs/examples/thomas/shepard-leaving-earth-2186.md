# Shepard: Leaving Earth, 2186

Nov 9, 2026 · @Thomas Dargent

## Setting

*March 2186, aboard the Normandy, the night after Mars. After his thirtieth, Thomas re-enlisted in the Alliance to work on the Reaper threat and was posted to Vancouver, two floors below the room where Shepard was being held; he was with Shepard when the Reapers hit and Anderson sent him to the ship. His family is still in Rouen, and nobody has heard anything. On Mars they recovered the Prothean plans for the Crucible, brought Liara aboard, and nearly lost Kaidan, who is in the med bay. Eva's body is under a tarp in the shuttle bay. Shepard doesn't need to convince Thomas to stay; he comes to tell him what they're facing and ask whether he's sure. He finds him late, in engineering, with his arms inside a panel. Roughly twenty minutes, spoken low. The tags follow the closed performance vocabulary, and there are no stage directions inside the speech.*
