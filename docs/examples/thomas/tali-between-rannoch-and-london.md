# Tali: Between Rannoch and London

Nov 3, 2026 · @Thomas Dargent

## Setting

*Engineering, late. Somewhere between Rannoch and London, a couple of weeks before the push. The ship is quiet except for the drive core. Thomas is sitting on the deck plating with his back against a console, breathing too fast; before he sat down he said he'd done everything wrong. Tali kneels in front of him. Roughly ten minutes, spoken slowly, with room for the breathing.*
