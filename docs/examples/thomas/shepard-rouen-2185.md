# Shepard: Rouen, 2185

Nov 4, 2026 · @Thomas Dargent

## Setting

*A hospital in Rouen, 2185. To Thomas, and to the rest of the galaxy, Shepard has been dead for two years. After the SR-1 went down, Thomas lost Shepard, then watched the crew scatter across the galaxy; on top of the illness he has carried for years, he came apart, and he has been in hospital for months, since an attempt on his life in the middle of 2184. Shepard, rebuilt by Cerberus, has a new Normandy and a crew he doesn't trust. He has spoken to Thomas's doctor and met his twin sister in the corridor. He comes into the room alone and takes the chair. Roughly thirteen minutes, spoken low. The tags follow the closed performance vocabulary, and there are no stage directions inside the speech.*
