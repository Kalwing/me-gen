**Summary:** A timeline of human spaceflight from 2069 to 2148 — before the Charon
Relay was found: the Armstrong outpost on Luna, the failed Manswell Expedition, the
first Mars settlements, the formation of the United North American States and the
Second American Civil War, Lowell City on Mars, Gagarin Station ("Jump Zero"), and the
2147–2148 discovery of element zero and the Prothean ruins on Mars that led to mass
effect technology and, soon after, the Systems Alliance.

## First expansion, 2069–2080s

Humanity's first era of expansion into space began in 2069 CE with the establishment of
Armstrong Outpost on Luna, on the 100th anniversary of the Apollo 11 landing. Earth at
the time still struggled with climate change, overpopulation, and rising populism;
inequality widened even as scientific advancement continued.

Frustrated with the slow pace of progress, a billionaire named Victor Manswell funded a
private expedition in 2075, before mass relay travel. About 300 colonists were placed
in cryogenic freeze for the journey. The launch succeeded, but the colonists were never
heard from again.

As Luna's population grew, deeper missions into the solar system followed. In the 2080s
NASA and the Chinese space program ran crewed missions to Mars and built several
research stations; the European Space Agency established the first permanent Mars
settlement twenty years later.

## The United North American States and the Second American Civil War

Under population pressure, around 2096 CE Canada, Mexico, and the United States united
to form the United North American States. Secessionist factions opposed this, including
the extremist group Freedom's First. On the morning of 1 November 2096, the New York
chapter of Freedom's First attacked Liberty Island and destroyed the Statue of Liberty.
The outrage triggered the Second American Civil War between secessionist and UNAS
forces — a six-year conflict ending in an overwhelming UNAS victory.

While the Americas fought, the European Space Agency established Lowell City in Eos
Chasma on Mars, then humanity's most distant colony and a bastion for the hope of
terraforming. With colonies on Luna and Mars, Earth's overpopulation began to ease.

## Toward FTL

The era of prosperity continued into the 2130s, when two notable figures were born:
Steven Hackett in 2134 and David Anderson in 2137. Humanity colonized the outer solar
system and began researching faster-than-light travel. In 2143 construction began on
Gagarin Station — named for Yuri Gagarin — beyond the orbit of Pluto, colloquially
"Jump Zero" (a reference to failed FTL attempts). It was then humanity's largest and
most distant station, used for telemetry, exploration, and experiments too dangerous to
run near populated worlds.

## Element zero and the Prothean ruins, 2147–2148

In 2147, traces of element zero were discovered for the first time on Mars. Aggressive
research over the following year led to the discovery of ancient alien ruins — of a
species that would be identified as the Protheans. Among the finds was a small cache of
advanced Prothean technology hidden deep beneath the south polar region, Promethei
Planum, including a large supply of already-refined element zero.

Building on these remnants, humans quickly explored the applications of element zero and
worked out the science of mass effect fields, leading to faster-than-light travel. The
discoveries of the next year would begin the founding of the Systems Alliance.
