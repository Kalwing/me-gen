**Summary:** Arcturus Station was the capital of the Systems Alliance — a Stanford
torus space habitat built in the strategically vital Arcturus system, the first
interstellar system humanity reached through the Charon Relay. Construction ran from
2151 to 2162; the station housed the Alliance Parliament and Fleet Command, staged the
task force of the First Contact War, and was destroyed by the Reapers in 2186 with the
entire Parliament aboard.

## Why Arcturus

When humanity first activated the Charon Relay, there was no way to know what lay on
the other side. It connected to the neighboring Arcturus system, which turned out to be
unclaimed — and the relay there was a nexus linking countless other systems and
clusters, like an interstellar highway junction. That made the single system a natural,
defensible hub for the fledgling Alliance's exploration efforts.

Arcturus lies 36 light-years from Sol. It is a type-K main-sequence star, older than
Sol, orbited only by lifeless gas giants and the remnants of an asteroid belt — no
worlds suitable for colonization. Its strategic value was enormous because of the
relay's reach, so the Alliance needed an alternative way to establish a permanent
presence: a station.

## Construction

As the Alliance Navy's military and exploratory fleets grew, so did the need for a
proper headquarters located on the resources needed for expansion and in the most
strategically vital territory in Alliance space. Plans were drawn for Arcturus Station,
a Stanford torus–style habitat designed to house both the civilian and military
branches of Alliance leadership.

Construction began in 2151. The toroidal structure at the core — holding habitats and
common areas — was likely finished first; once this self-sustaining spin station was
complete, executive officers and construction crews could take up residence. Codex
references to the structure's size are debated: artwork often shows a 5 km full length,
but other references suggest 5 km is the diameter of the toroidal structure, making the
overall length roughly twice that. The habitat is centered on a central superstructure
well suited to station operations, much like the Citadel Tower.

Expansion of the outer arms, main docks, and shipyard facilities went on gradually over
the next 11 years, using the system's mineral-rich asteroids for material. Population
climbed to about 45,000 over the following decade. The station was officially
inaugurated in 2156, though construction did not finish until around 2162 — by which
time the Alliance had already fought the First Contact War.

## Role in the First Contact War

The station's tactical importance during the Relay 314 Incident cannot be overstated:
without Arcturus Station, the Alliance could not have fielded the task force that
liberated Shanxi and forced the Turians to the negotiating table. Afterward the station
became central to Alliance diplomacy. General Desolas Arterius came there to exchange
prisoners with General Williams; among those exchanged were Jack Harper and Eva Coré
(events told in *Mass Effect: Evolution* #2).

## Capital of the Alliance

Over the next two decades Arcturus Station grew into a symbol of Alliance power in
Council space and became the de facto capital, home to the Systems Alliance Parliament.

In 2183 it was the site of the first test flight of the newly completed SSV Normandy, a
stealth frigate designed jointly with the Turian Hierarchy. Captain Anderson and
Captain Dillard attended alongside Hierarchy official General Invectus. The flight took
an unexpected turn when pilot Jeff "Joker" Moreau — born on Arcturus — effectively
hijacked the Normandy to prove his skill, trapping the ship's original pilot on the
station, locking himself in the cockpit, evading a scramble of patrol fighters, and
returning the ship safely to dock. Impressed, General Invectus pressured the Alliance
to assign Joker as the Normandy's pilot, which likely spared him a court martial.

Station defense was provided primarily by Fleet Command, for a time headed by Admiral
Steven Hackett before his reassignment to the Citadel with the Fifth Fleet (some lore
has this originally intended as Admiral Mikhailovich and the First Fleet). The station
almost certainly had its own mounted defenses — at least Guardian and anti-ship
lasers — but little detail exists.

## Destruction, 2186

In 2186 Arcturus Station was the site of a decisive battle between the Alliance's
Second, Third, and Fifth Fleets and Reaper forces bound for Sol. The Alliance defended
the station against about a dozen Reaper capital ships, but the bulk of the Reaper
fleet bypassed it entirely and surged through the mass relay toward Earth. Admiral
Hackett, unable to win, abandoned the station and sacrificed the Second Fleet to let
the Third and Fifth escape. Arcturus Station was then utterly destroyed. The entire
Alliance Parliament and Prime Minister Amul Shastri were aboard and were killed —
leaving a grave of more than 45,000 people.
