**Summary:** Part 1 of a character analysis of Jack: her abduction as an infant by
Cerberus, the biotic-enhancement experiments and kill-conditioning at the Teltin
facility on Pragia, the subject riot and her escape, the violent years that followed,
her attempt to save biotic children from a second Cerberus facility, her capture and
abuse aboard the Blue Suns prison ship Purgatory, and her recruitment by Shepard in
2185. Part 2 is a psychological analysis of her arc through Mass Effect 3.

Names attached to her: Jack; Subject Zero; Jacqueline Nought; Jennifer. She first
appears in Mass Effect 2 and immediately became a divisive character — some players put
off by her abrasiveness, others intrigued by it. Born a biotic and modified into a
weapon, she is one of the most powerful and notorious human criminals in the setting.
(The video's author frames Jack as among the best-written characters in the series and a
frequent romance choice; parts of this analysis are interpretation, not hard canon.)

## Abduction

In 2165 Cerberus — a human-supremacist paramilitary group — abducted the infant later
called Jennifer, seeing biotic potential from accidental element-zero exposure in the
womb. Cerberus told her mother the child had died from that exposure.

(Biotics: the ability of some individuals to manipulate dark energy to raise or lower an
object's mass, biologically generating a mass effect field — for offense such as
throwing or lifting enemies and creating vortices, and for defense such as barriers.)

## The Teltin facility, Pragia

Cerberus sent Jennifer to the Teltin facility on Pragia, whose goal was to enhance
biotic potential in humans "no matter the risk." Given the designation Subject Zero and
eventually the name Jack, she was the focal point of all the facility's research. Other
children there were treated as expendable: enhancements that proved lethal were tested
on them first and only applied to Jack if found beneficial.

Cerberus scientists tortured her physically and mentally to study how pain and distress
affected her biotics. She was usually kept apart from the other children, in a room
behind soundproof one-way glass — she could see and hear them, but they could not see or
hear her. She spent hours screaming and beating on the glass to get their attention.
Because Cerberus regularly killed the other children in experiments meant to advance her
abilities, they came to hate her as the source of their suffering. The only time they
interacted was in forced gladiatorial combat, staged to monitor her biotics and
condition her to enjoy killing: if she hesitated she was shocked; if she killed without
hesitation she was injected with narcotics, hardwiring a link between euphoria and
killing. The abuse "worked" — she became one of the strongest human biotics alive at a
very young age.

## Riot and escape

Eventually the subjects rioted and attacked the guards, damaging the facility and
freeing Jack. Her pent-up rage and sudden release sent her into a blind fury; she nearly
killed everyone in the facility — guards, scientists, and other subjects alike — then
stole a shuttle and escaped Pragia. A passing freighter picked her up, used her, and
sold her into slavery.

## The violent years

In the years after, Jack lived a violent, crime-filled life: murder, assault,
kidnapping, drugs, theft, arson, piracy, vandalism, and a stint in a cult.

At some point she found another Cerberus facility using biotic children and attacked it
to free them. Covering their escape, she fought off Cerberus agents but was overrun by Blue Suns mercenaries — to Cerberus's frustration — who captured
her and took her to their prison ship, Purgatory.

## Purgatory

Her captivity there brought more violence: a group of guards and prisoners beat and
raped her. After she healed, she brutally murdered every one of her attackers. This led
Warden Kuril to decide she was not worth the Blue Suns' effort, and he offered to sell
her back to Cerberus.

## Recruitment, 2185

Commander Shepard — revived and working with Cerberus — arrives at Purgatory to complete
the purchase and recruit Jack for the mission against the Collectors. The deal nearly
goes through, but when Kuril tries to capture Shepard a fight breaks out, Jack escapes,
and goes on a rampage. Talked down from killing Shepard over the Cerberus connection,
she agrees to join the team.
