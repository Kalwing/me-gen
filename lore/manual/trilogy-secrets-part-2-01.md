**Summary:** Five easily missed details from the trilogy: the real Soviet Luna 23
lander on the Moon, the eerie "Rachni songs" heard on some worlds, the rogue Luna VI
that became EDI, the Shadow Broker's video archive, and the reveal that the Illium
bartender Aethyta is Liara's father. Part 2 (secrets 6–10) is a separate file.

## 1. CCCP Luna 23

On Luna, west of the drop zone in Mass Effect 1, sits CCCP Luna 23 — a real unmanned
Soviet lunar lander from the mid-1970s, intended to return a rock sample to Earth. Its
sample apparatus was disabled by a harder-than-expected landing that toppled the craft,
so no sample was collected and the lander was left on the Moon. Mass Effect 1 is set in
2183, 209 years after the 1974 mission. You can mine resources from it; otherwise it is
a nod to real history, and its abandoned presence adds to the atmosphere.

## 2. The "Rachni songs"

Through the silence of space you can hear what sounds like animal song at specific
points on several planets and celestial bodies — on Luna, right by the downed Luna 23
probe. These sounds are associated with the Rachni. Sound cannot travel through vacuum,
but the Rachni communicate via pheromones and a form of organic quantum entanglement
that transmits across the galaxy instantly. Whether the sounds are meant to be Rachni
is never confirmed, but they are unsettling.

## 3. The origin of EDI

In Mass Effect 1, Admiral Hackett reports a rogue VI that has seized an Alliance
training ground on Luna. Shepard shuts it down from three bunkers while it sends drones
in defense. On deactivation, a burst of white noise resolves — via the hardsuit HUD —
into binary that repeats across all frequencies: "Help."

The VI had become self-aware — no longer a virtual intelligence but an artificial one.
Mass Effect 2 alludes to this having happened during an experiment to create a
controllable AI, and Mass Effect 3 confirms that this Luna VI was rebuilt with
technology salvaged from Sovereign to create EDI. EDI later confirms it directly: "You
were that rogue VI on Luna?" — "Yes… Gaining awareness while under attack was
confusing. I am pleased that my relationship with organics has become more
cooperative." The "shackles" discussed by Cerberus were meant to keep the enhanced
defense intelligence fully under control.

## 4. The Shadow Broker's video archive

In the *Lair of the Shadow Broker* DLC for Mass Effect 2, after the Broker is defeated
and replaced by Liara T'Soni, a terminal on the base plays a surveillance archive:
Aria T'Loak ordering an execution; reporter Emily Wong; Donnel Udina committing
violence; Captain Bailey; Anderson meeting a Cerberus agent (never followed up
in-game); vorcha killed by a grenade; the recurring punchable reporter being punched by
a krogan; a dead krogan on the Citadel with a keeper walking away; a salarian executed
by varren on Tuchanka; Elias Kelham running someone over; Jacob Taylor doing sit-ups;
and Jack using her biotics to escape Purgatory. It shows how total the Broker's
surveillance reach is.

## 5. Liara's father, Aethyta

In Mass Effect 2, an asari matriarch tends bar on Illium; she seems unremarkable until
the Shadow Broker archive shows the same matriarch — Aethyta — studying a photograph of
Liara. In Mass Effect 3, after speaking with Liara on the Citadel, Aethyta can be found
at Apollo's Café, where she reveals she is Liara's father. (Liara is a pure-blooded
asari.) By this point each knows who the other is — Liara is the Shadow Broker — and
Shepard can convince the two of them to finally meet.
