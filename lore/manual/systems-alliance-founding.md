**Summary:** How the Systems Alliance formed after the discovery of the Prothean ruins
on Mars: the cultural and religious upheaval that followed, the unifying "us versus
them" effect on global politics, the drafting of the Systems Alliance Charter by
Earth's eighteen largest nations in 2149, the creation of the Alliance military, the
discovery that Charon (Pluto's moon) was a dormant mass relay, and Rear Admiral John
Grissom's first crewed transit of the Charon Relay to Arcturus.

## The impact of the Mars discovery

The Prothean ruins on Mars, together with the newly usable element colloquially known
as eezo, had lifted humanity to mastery of its own solar system. Technological
advancement skyrocketed, and this rippled through every part of human society — a
higher (if imperfect) standard of living became attainable to most citizens, and
technology, infrastructure, art, and especially religion changed rapidly, as if guided
by an outside influence.

New beliefs took hold almost overnight. Groups and cults such as the interventionary
evolutionists proclaimed the ruins as proof that all human history had been directed by
alien forces. Established religions were shaken: the major Abrahamic faiths struggled
to fit extraterrestrial life into their frameworks; some traditions tried to absorb the
new knowledge, some stayed silent, and a stubborn few denied the Mars evidence and
cried conspiracy. The discovery dominated the media and raised the obvious questions:
who were the aliens, were they friendly, and are they still out there.

## A unifying effect on politics

While the discovery fractured most of the world's religions, it had the opposite effect
on politics. Instead of nations fighting over their differences, it was now "us versus
them," and the foundations of a united human front were laid.

On the frontier, efforts to push human advancement further into the stars were being
held back by this same lack of unity. In early 2149, even as humanity's best minds
worked on ways around known physics, political leaders across the system agreed their
differences had to be set aside. Along with representatives from Mars and Luna, Earth's
eighteen largest nations drafted and ratified the Systems Alliance Charter,
establishing a representative political body to expand and defend human territory in
space.

## The founding nations

The Argentine Republic; the Chinese People's Federation; the Commonwealth of
Australia; the Federal Republic of Germany; the Federal Republic of Nigeria; the
Federative Republic of Brazil; the French Republic; the Indian Union; Japan; the
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia; the Republic of Korea; the Republic of Turkey; the Republic
of Singapore; the Republic of South Africa; the Russian Federation; the United Arab
Emirates; the United Kingdom of Great Britain; and the United North American States.
The Republic of Ireland joined a year later.

## The Alliance military and the FTL problem

No longer divided by internal strife, these nations pooled their scientific and
technological might to form the Systems Alliance military, with resources dispatched to
the outer solar system. Relativity was about to become a serious obstacle to spreading
humanity's influence: primitive FTL drives could not run for long without time
dilation, and even the most advanced drives could not run indefinitely because of a
side effect of using eezo as fuel. Humanity would have to find a way around this or
accept that expansion would be slow and disconnected.

## Charon: a mass relay encased in ice

The answer came from the Prothean archives. In 2148, the translated Mars data cache
revealed that Charon — the largest moon of the planetoid Pluto — was actually a
colossal piece of Prothean technology encased in ice, dormant for 50,000 years. Backed
by the growing Alliance, a human science team near Pluto confirmed the finding and,
under orders from Earth, began excavation to reactivate the structure.

Despite only a limited understanding of mass effect technology, the team soon
determined the device's purpose: to allow instantaneous relativistic-free travel
between two points in space. The theory was beyond reach, but activation turned out to
be surprisingly simple — almost as though the relay had been waiting to be discovered.
It was not clear what dangers transit might pose. Exploratory probes sent through the
Charon Relay instantly dropped out of contact, and it became clear the only way to
learn what lay on the other side was to send people.

## John Grissom and the first transit

Although the Alliance was new, many of its high-ranking officers had come from Earth's
various military organizations, so it was agreed that one of the most decorated,
Rear Admiral John Grissom, should lead the first team of explorers through the relay.
Around the middle of 2149, Grissom and several volunteers boarded an experimental
jump ship and became the first humans to transit the Charon Relay, arriving instantly
at a second mass relay in the Arcturus system, 36 light-years away. There Grissom and
his crew learned that the relays form a vast galaxy-spanning network.

On their return, Grissom and his fellow explorers were hailed as heroes, with Grissom
himself idolized as one of the greatest pioneers of human exploration. A sullen,
retiring man, he nonetheless became the face of the Systems Alliance as humanity
entered a new age of expansion.
