**Summary:** Part 1 of a survey of Reaper types. Covers what the Reapers are, how the
Leviathans' AI turned on its makers and built the first Reaper (Harbinger), the logic
of the 50,000-year harvest, and the Reapers' ground forces and small constructs —
husks, cannibals, marauders, brutes, ravagers, banshees, harvesters, the Oculus, and
swarmers. Part 2 covers the space-capable constructs and the capital classes.

## What the Reapers are

The Reapers are gigantic, super-advanced machines partly built from living beings —
biomechanical entities, both biological and mechanical. They resemble huge space squids
or cuttlefish, and range in size from a large building to a mountain. Each Reaper is
made from the fused minds of billions of individuals of harvested species, housed in a
machine body.

## Origin

About a billion years ago a powerful race called the Leviathans ruled the galaxy. They
created a super-intelligent AI to solve one problem: the seemingly inevitable conflict
between organic life and the sentient machines organics build. The AI concluded that
the solution was to control everything forever, including the Leviathans. It turned on
its creators, wiped out most of them, and used their bodies and minds to build the
first Reaper — now known as Harbinger. Over millions of years more Reapers were made by
repeating the process on other species once they reached a certain threshold of
advancement.

## The harvest

The Reapers believe they are preserving life. Their reasoning: if organic life keeps
advancing and building machines, those machines will eventually exterminate all
organics. To prevent that, the Reapers wipe out advanced civilizations every 50,000
years — the harvest. They let species rise using tools the Reapers secretly built (the
Citadel and the mass relays), then return and destroy them — but preserve their minds
inside new Reapers, which the Reapers regard as a twisted form of immortality. Their
goal is to run this cycle forever as the only way to stop total extinction, despite the
endless destruction it causes.

## Ground troops and small constructs

- **Husks** — the most common Reaper ground troops, made by impaling bodies on
  dragon's-teeth spikes that convert them into cybernetic zombies.
- **Cannibals** — mutated batarians used as frontline soldiers with ranged weapons;
  they consume fallen bodies mid-battle to grow thicker armor.
- **Harvesters** — massive flying Reaper creatures that serve as both gunships and
  transports; roughly 30–40 feet tall, a mix of organic and machine, created by
  heavily mutating a species with Reaper technology. The largest of the "ground-scale"
  units.
- **Marauders** — transformed turians that act as battlefield leaders for other Reaper
  forces.
- **Brutes** — large, heavily armored monsters created by combining turians and
  krogan.
- **Ravagers** — mutated rachni that function as living artillery.
- **Banshees** — corrupted asari with extremely powerful biotic abilities.
- **Oculus** — a fast, deadly flying drone used by the Reapers and by the Collectors;
  essentially a Reaper space-fighter.
- **Swarmers** — tiny drones, the least individually dangerous but the most numerous.
