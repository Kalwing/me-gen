**Summary:** A collection of short Jack dialogue and party-banter clips spanning
Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3: a cut conversation about red sand, the Mass Effect 3
scene introducing her varren Eezo, Normandy confrontations with Miranda, and her later
life as an instructor at Grissom Academy leading a biotic student squad. Useful as
voice reference and for Jack's personal continuity.

## Cut dialogue: red sand (Mass Effect 2)

Investigating a data pad with Shepard, Jack reads off a shipping manifest: Pinnacle
Station / Pitne IV sold two thousand units of Minagen X3 to the Eclipse mercenary
company, along with six hundred units of red sand. It is not the ship name Samara is
after, but Jack figures selling the manifest to the volus will tie the Eclipse up for
a long time.

The two of them talk about the drug trade. Jack says they keep running into red sand
addicts — "idiots who would do that to themselves." Shepard pushes back: does using
drugs really make someone an idiot? Jack allows that addicts are "looking for
something they won't find," which makes them easy victims. Shepard points out that
Jack takes drugs all the time. Jack: "If I don't, I wake up screaming, with furniture
slamming into the wall."

On red sand itself: a small snort gives a non-biotic "enough biotics to move a pencil
around with your mind for a little while." Shepard asks why an asari — who already has
biotic ability — would use it. Jack: "You get a great high, too." Then they keep
moving.

## Eezo the varren (Mass Effect 3)

Jack introduces her pet varren, named Eezo. He is from Thessia, "naturally biotic from
all the element zero." She explains that constant biotic exposure makes such varren
short-tempered, so many of them get abused or abandoned — "Can you imagine someone
doing that?" She found him at a rescue shelter and has been trying to teach him that
it is okay to trust people. He started out very angry, but with "lots of love" he has
become "a big old softy." She coos over him: "Who's my badass biotic? Who's my badass
biotic?"

## Normandy confrontation with Miranda (Mass Effect 2)

Jack and Miranda nearly come to blows in the sub-deck. Jack insists that what Cerberus
did to her "was wrong"; Miranda answers that "it wasn't Cerberus, not really" and that
Jack "was clearly a mistake." Shepard steps in — the mission against the Collectors is
too important to let personal feelings get in the way. Jack agrees to put the fight on
hold: "Sure, I'll do my part. I'd hate to see her die before I get a chance to fillet
her myself." Afterward Miranda tells Shepard that as long as Jack does her job, they
will be fine.

## Flirtation / interrogation (Mass Effect 2)

When Shepard keeps visiting to ask questions, Jack gets suspicious: "People usually
walk by now. Why are you really asking all these things? Are you eyeing me up? Because
if this is just about sex, maybe you should just say so." Shepard says there is no
hurry — he wants to know what makes her tick first. Jack: "You don't need to know
someone to sleep with them. You just have to know where to put it."

## Grissom Academy and the student squad (Mass Effect 3)

Shepard finds Jack leading a squad of biotic students at Grissom Academy during the
Reaper War. She had "some free time" after Mass Effect 2; the Alliance brass knew she
had helped Shepard and offered her the teaching post, and the students "responded well
to her teaching style." She calls herself "The Psychotic Biotic." Her students include
Rodriguez, whom she rides hard: "I will destroy you." — "You couldn't destroy wet
tissue paper. Drink your juice, Rodriguez."

Getting the students out alive, Jack promises them tattoos — "my treat" — an Ascension
Project logo, a glowing fist, "maybe a unicorn for Rodriguez."

Afterward there is an argument about how the students should be deployed. Jack and the
students trained for barrier work to protect frontline squads; the Alliance wants to
use them as another artillery unit. Shepard say they will be deployed as supports, as per Jack's recommendation to protect them.

Once the students are formally military, the crew needle her: will she wear a uniform,
or "just get the officer bars tattooed on"? Joker jokes about a swear jar — "I bet if
we empty that thing we can afford another cruiser" — because Jack "promised to watch
her language to maintain the necessary professionalism."

Jack tells Shepard that the "little shits are useless without me, so I've got to stick
with them — I'm dependable now, apparently. I blame you for this." She credits
Shepard's "little suicide squad" with teaching her something about teamwork.

## Reunion with Garrus (Mass Effect 3)

"As charming as ever. Good to see you again, Garrus." — "You still look like [expletive]."
Right then all Jack cares about is getting "my guys" out. Garrus notes he cannot think
of anybody who could care about the students more than she does.

## Mass Effect 3: Citadel DLC — reconciliation with Miranda

At the Citadel bar, Shepard can steer Jack and Miranda through a long round of insults
that ends in a grudging peace. Miranda is still weak from fighting Kai Leng; Jack has
been "fighting [expletive] and marauders on the front lines of this war." Shepard
suggests, deadpan, that "it might help if you two just kissed and got it over with —
this is all just sexual tension, right?" ("You are a terrible human being, Shepard. I
need another drink.")

Eventually Miranda concedes: "I hate to say it, but Cerberus was wrong about you.
You're still violent and maladjusted, but the Illusive Man never would have predicted
you'd bond with the Grissom Academy kids. You are growing despite everything Cerberus
did to you. I find that extremely admirable." Jack: "Wow. Thanks. I still really hate
you, but you have fantastic tits." Miranda: "All right, I can live with that."

## Mass Effect 3: Citadel shore leave

At the party, Shepard tries to talk duty rosters. Jack: "The duty rosters can wait.
Right now you're on shore leave, Shepard." And: "Everybody knows you can't dance."
