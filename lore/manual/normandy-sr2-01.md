## The end of the SR-1

The SSV Normandy SR-1 was a prototype deep-scout stealth frigate co-developed by the
Alliance and the Turian Hierarchy under Citadel Council sponsorship, commissioned in
2183. It was first commanded by an Alliance officer, Eli Xander; after Xander clashed
with Turian chief engineer Octavio Tatum over the limits of the drive core, Xander was
removed and David Anderson took command. The ship then passed to Commander Shepard when
Shepard became the first human Spectre.

About a month after the Battle of the Citadel, an unidentified starship intercepted the
Normandy despite its active stealth systems. Helmsman Jeff "Joker" Moreau began evasive
maneuvers, but the attacker's beam weapons cut straight through the Normandy's defenses.
Kinetic barriers and weapons were knocked out in a single hit; within seconds the ship
was crippled, the upper decks were breached, a starboard drive cell was destroyed, and
over 20 crew were killed — including Commander Shepard. The SR-1 was completely
destroyed above Alchera.

## Cerberus builds the SR-2

The Normandy SR-2 entered service in 2185 as the successor to the SR-1. The Illusive
Man had Cerberus build it — at great and deliberately secret expense, in the Voyager
Cluster — for the newly revived Shepard's team, to aid their mission against the
Collectors' galaxy-wide human abductions. It served as the headquarters of the Lazarus
Cell.

The SR-2 is closely based on the SR-1's design layout but has key differences: it is
almost twice the mass of the original, and Cerberus built in more crew-oriented
comforts — civilian-grade accommodations, leather chairs, a kitchen. It keeps the SR-1's
stealth characteristics, but its greater mass means it cannot land on every planet, so
shore parties use a shuttle to reach the surface. It carries over the SR-1's advanced
systems: the lithium-heat-sink stealth array and the oversized Tantalus drive core
(covered in Part 3).

## EDI

The SR-2's most significant upgrade over the SR-1 is EDI — the Enhanced Defense
Intelligence — an AI represented by a floating blue holographic sphere at terminals
throughout the ship. EDI provides basic information about the Normandy and Cerberus, and
functions as the ship's electronic-warfare defense.

Because of the danger of a rogue AI, EDI was given behavioral blocks and could not
interface with ship systems directly. When the Collectors boarded and abducted most of
the crew, an emergency response — EDI working with Joker — released those blocks,
granting EDI full control of the Normandy.

Before Shepard's crew defected from Cerberus back to the Systems Alliance, EDI also
served as the Illusive Man's eyes and ears aboard, monitoring the ship's many listening
devices and sending him regular reports. During this time the Illusive Man restricted
Shepard's access to some of EDI's files and capabilities — EDI surmised he did not want
Shepard to know everything about Cerberus.

EDI gained access to anti-Reaper algorithms and devoted significant processing power to
analyzing them. Asked how she could hope to fight beings millions of years more
advanced, she revealed that she was designed in part with technology from Sovereign's
remains, and is therefore herself partly based on Reaper technology.

After the defection, EDI — a fully sentient AI with free will — followed Shepard's lead
and severed her ties to Cerberus, though she still downplays her level of sentience to
Alliance personnel she does not know, since concern about AI technology remains.
