**Summary:** Part 4 of a breakdown of the Normandy SR-2: how, after Shepard defected
from Cerberus in 2186, the Systems Alliance reclaimed the ship (it was based on their
SR-1 design), studied it, corrected Cerberus's construction faults, and recommissioned
it in Alliance colors — a refit left only partly finished when the Reaper invasion
began — followed by a deck-by-deck tour of the refitted ship and a closing note on its
role in the Reaper War. Parts 1–3 cover origin/EDI, armament, and propulsion/stealth.

## The refit

By 2186 Shepard had defected from Cerberus and turned the SR-2 over to the Systems
Alliance, which considered the ship its property since it was based on the SSV Normandy
SR-1 design. The Alliance studied and modified her — adding new parts, fixing faults in
Cerberus's construction, redesigning areas, and swapping Cerberus equipment for Alliance
equipment — then recommissioned her as an Alliance vessel in Alliance colors. The refit
was only partially complete when the Reaper invasion interrupted it, which makes the
contrast with the Cerberus version even starker. Most notably, Deck 2 was rearranged to
add a war room, meeting room, and QEC, and Deck 5 became the new armory.

## Deck 1 — Captain's cabin

Contains an aquarium, armor locker, private terminal, a display case for model ships,
and a bathroom. The private terminal is used to check email, view combat demonstration
videos, and view or change squad members' outfits.

## Deck 2 — Combat Information Center

Contains the bridge, the galaxy map, and a conference room, with a private terminal near
the galaxy map for the captain. The armory was moved down to the shuttle bay on Deck 5
per Alliance regulations, and the laboratory was converted into the conference room to
make space for the war room — a new addition where war assets and galactic readiness can
be examined, and which contains a quantum entanglement communications room the captain
uses to contact individuals aiding the war effort.

## Deck 3 — Crew deck

Contains the medical bay, crew sleeping quarters, kitchen, observation deck, AI Core,
life support, bathrooms, Liara T'Soni's office, and the main battery. Liara's office is
in the same space that held Miranda's office and quarters before the refit, and contains
the Shadow Broker terminal, granting access to highly sensitive information on
individuals relevant to the mission. EDI's mobile platform can sometimes be found in the
AI Core, still accessible from inside the med bay. Just outside the elevator is a
memorial wall recording crew casualties — including those lost on the derelict Reaper
mission, the Suicide Mission, and the 20 crew lost when the SR-1 was destroyed.

## Deck 4 — Engineering

Contains the drive core. The starboard cargo room doubles as Diana Allers's quarters and
remote broadcast studio; the port cargo room serves as Javik's quarters. Engineer Adams
works here; if military pardons are issued through the Spectre office at the Citadel
embassies, he is joined by Kenneth Donnelly and Gabriella Daniels.

## Deck 5 — Shuttle bay

Contains the armory, cargo hold, and two Kodiak shuttles. James Vega and Lieutenant
Steve Cortez spend their time here. A procurement terminal sells equipment from various
retailers at a slight premium over Citadel prices; a weapon modification bench, an armor
locker, and a small gym are also here.

## Legacy

Built by Cerberus with great labor and secrecy to be larger and more powerful than the
original, the SR-2 ended up nearly double the SR-1's size and mass, more heavily armed
and armored, and needing an even larger Tantalus core — housed in a sphere holding
element zero and technologically different from the original, generating power in a way
meant to evoke a supercollider. With her speed, maneuverability, Javelin torpedoes,
GARDIAN lasers, the Thanix cannon, kinetic barriers, deflective armor, stronger
shielding, and EDI coordinating combat functions, the Normandy is still touted as the
fastest, most advanced, and most iconic vessel of the Alliance fleet. She led the
charge of the united Citadel races in the battle for Earth at the close of the Reaper
War; her whereabouts afterward remain unknown.
