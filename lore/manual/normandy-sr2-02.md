**Summary:** Part 2 of a breakdown of the Normandy SR-2: its offensive weapons — paired
Javelin disruptor torpedoes and the Turian-developed Thanix magnetohydrodynamic cannon
(a miniature of Sovereign's main weapon) — and its defensive suite: kinetic barriers,
cyclonic barrier technology (CBT) multi-core shielding, ablative Silaris armor, and
GARDIAN point-defense lasers, along with the physics and limits of each. Part 1 covers
origin and EDI; Parts 3–4 cover propulsion/stealth and the Alliance refit.

## Doctrine

As a stealth reconnaissance frigate, the SR-2 is not heavily armed and relies mostly on
passive and defensive systems. What armament it carries, however, is extraordinarily
powerful.

## Javelin disruptor torpedoes

Initially the Normandy carried two Javelin launchers mounted on joints between the
engine mounts and primary hull. The Javelin is an experimental close-assault system:
paired disruptor torpedo tubes slung on the exterior hull. The torpedoes fire on
converging trajectories and detonate in a precisely timed sequence so that the dark
energy of their warheads resonates, magnifying the space-time warp and shredding the
target. Several volleys can destroy a large Collector vessel.

Like fighter torpedoes, Javelins are cold-launched for safety — compressed inert gas in
each sealed tube pushes the torpedo into vacuum (with a visible puff of crystals at the
tube mouth) before it ignites its thrusters. Javelin mounts are usually fitted on swift
frigates that close to knife-fight range as a matter of course, but can also be fitted
on heavier ships for short-range work such as trans-relay assaults — useful for
dreadnoughts, which cannot bring their main guns to bear on close targets. As missile
weapons, Javelins are subject to highly accurate GARDIAN defensive fire, so they must
be launched in large numbers at short range to have any chance of hitting.

## Thanix cannon

The Normandy is also equipped with the Thanix magnetohydrodynamic weapon — a
Turian-developed, miniaturized version of the weapon Sovereign used against the Citadel.
It uses an electromagnetic field to shape and accelerate a stream of superheated molten
metal to relativistic velocity, then impacts the target for horrendous damage. Its core
is a liquid alloy of iron, uranium, and tungsten suspended in an eezo-powered
electromagnetic field; the molten metal solidifies into a projectile as it is fired,
hitting with enough force to pierce any known shield or armor. It fires reliably every
five seconds and appears as a bright blue beam, similar to the red trails from
Sovereign's arms at the Battle of the Citadel. In combat the Thanix can destroy a
Collector cruiser in two shots.

## Defenses

The SR-2's true tactical value is defensive.

**Kinetic barriers** — mass effect fields that halt incoming projectiles; ineffective
against heat or radiation (lasers, particle weapons).

**Cyclonic barrier technology (CBT)** — multi-core shielding refit to the SR-2. Instead
of static barriers, rotationally firing the mass effect field projectors creates
rapidly oscillating barriers that violently slap aside incoming linear force rather than
stopping it — "like trying to shoot a target inside a spinning ball." If an emitter is
damaged, the CBT reverts to a traditional shield array, making it most effective during
opening volleys. Drawbacks limit it to frigates and fighters: its many high-frequency
sensors and emitters need frequent maintenance, and a partially damaged CBT can
endanger its operator with mass effect fields skewing in unpredictable directions.

**Ablative armor / Silaris armor** — plating meant primarily to defeat directed-energy
weapons that kinetic barriers cannot stop. The armor boils away when heated, and the
vaporized material scatters energy-weapon beams. Asari-designed Silaris heavy ship
armor was retrofitted to the hull, resisting even the heat and kinetic energy of
starship weapons. Its central material — carbon nanotube sheets woven with chemical
vapor deposition diamond, crushed by mass effect fields into superdense layers — is
nearly unsurpassed in strength, and the process compensates for diamond's brittleness.
Limits: it is prohibitively expensive for anything larger than a frigate, and it must
be attached to the ship's superstructure, so shockwaves from heavy firepower can still
destroy the metal beneath. (A popular misconception is that the diamond makes it
sparkle; atmospheric nitrogen impurities during forging actually give it a metallic
gray or yellow sheen.)

**GARDIAN point defense** — anti-missile and anti-fighter laser turrets on the exterior
hull, under computer control; the gunnery officer need only switch the system on and
designate targets. Since lasers move at light speed they cannot be dodged at
non-relativistic speeds, so early in a battle GARDIAN fire is 100% accurate (though not
100% lethal — damaged fighters must break off). Limits: diffraction spreads the beam
and drops energy density with range, making high-powered lasers short-ranged; and
weapons-grade lasers need cooldown time, so sustained fire builds heat that reduces
damage, range, and accuracy, letting a fighter swarm press closer as a battle drags on.
GARDIAN lasers typically operate in the infrared; salarians use near-ultraviolet lasers
with six times the range, valuing extra time to shoot down incoming missiles over
mechanical reliability. Lasers are not blocked by capital-ship kinetic barriers, but
their range confines them to rare knife-fight engagements.
