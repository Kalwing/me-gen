**Summary:** Five more easily missed details: Garrus and Tali quietly romancing each
other if Shepard romances neither, Kelly Chambers reappearing in the Citadel refugee
camp, the long-running Conrad Verner arc and its fourth-wall jokes, the explanation for
pigeons on the Citadel, and the "refund guy" who recurs across all three games. Part 1
(secrets 1–5) is a separate file.

## 6. Garrus and Tali

If Shepard romances neither Garrus nor Tali, then near the end of Mass Effect 3 the two
of them can be caught together, flustered and improvising excuses ("I think one of my
mandibles got hooked on her helmet… infection risks…"). Tali plays it down —
"This is just a fling, Vakarian. I'm using you for your body." — "You're so mean." —
"And I'm okay with that." Garrus notes it helps to have something to come back to.

## 7. Kelly Chambers

If Shepard romances Kelly Chambers in Mass Effect 2, she can be met again in Mass
Effect 3 in the Citadel's refugee camp, where she explains she cannot rejoin the
Normandy after the trauma the Collectors inflicted. Shepard can convince her to change
her identity so Cerberus cannot find her. If Shepard does, she survives the Cerberus
attack on the Citadel and is grateful; if not, her death is reported afterward.

## 8. Conrad Verner

In Mass Effect 1, Conrad Verner is a harmless fan who wants Shepard's validation. In
Mass Effect 2 he turns up on Illium in fake N7 armor causing trouble at a bar. In Mass
Effect 3 he is as dim as ever. Engaging with him consistently across all three games
pays off in the end; miss a single encounter and he never returns. This is deliberate:
nearly every Conrad line contains a fourth-wall break poking fun at Mass Effect's
mechanics or at RPG conventions in general — he is Mass Effect's equivalent of
M'aiq the Liar. (Asked whether he has combat training: "I'm saving the galaxy,
Shepard. I don't have time for training." He funds his gear with his wife's support,
"talks to people" to find problems only he can solve, and pokes through crates "for
extra credit.")

## 9. Pigeons on the Citadel

There are pigeons on the Citadel, a space station far from Earth. The VI Avina explains
that the current decoration budget was approved by the human ambassador for Earth, and
that each species with ambassadorial rights can apply for a slot on the Citadel's
decoration schedule to change the promenade and parks below — the only condition being
that the introduced environmental features not be toxic to species living on the
Citadel. So represented species can bring native flora and fauna from their homeworlds
to the station — which is how a pest species like the pigeon ended up on the Citadel.

## 10. The refund guy

In Mass Effect 1 a man is seen doggedly trying to get a refund. He is at it again in
Mass Effect 2. In Mass Effect 3 Shepard can finally intervene, either helping him get
his refund or talking him out of it — a total of 15 credits, fought over for three
years.
