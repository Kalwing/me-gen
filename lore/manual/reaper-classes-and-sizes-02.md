**Summary:** Part 2 of a survey of Reaper types, ordered by increasing size: the Hades
Cannon, Destroyers, the human-Reaper larva, the Leviathans (Reaper creators, included
for context), Processors, Intruders, troop transports, the Devastator carrier, Reaper
capital ships, Sovereign, and Harbinger. Part 1 covers Reaper origin and ground forces.

## Hades Cannon

A huge anti-air / anti-spacecraft weapon the Reapers use to control the skies. It looks
like a smaller Reaper walking on four legs, and fires a highly accurate energy beam
able to shoot down even small, fast drop shuttles. During the invasion of Earth in
2186 these cannons made troop landings nearly impossible until Commander Shepard
destroyed one with the M-920 Cain, clearing the way for allied forces to land.

## Destroyers

Smaller but very dangerous Reapers, about 160 m tall — small next to capital ships but
massive next to vehicles. They are the most common true Reapers, usually built from
species not used for bigger ones. Destroyers have four legs and several arms around the
head, and open their front plates to fire a beam that can destroy ships in seconds.
Faster and more agile than capital Reapers, they can chase or board smaller ships; on
the ground they act as gigantic walking tanks and are very hard to kill without heavy
firepower.

## Human-Reaper larva

An unfinished Reaper being grown from human bodies on Harbinger's orders. The
Collectors abducted large numbers of humans and reduced them to raw genetic material to
build it. Unlike the usual squid shape, it had a humanoid upper body — evidence that a
Reaper's form partly reflects the species used to create it. Shepard found it in a
larval stage, hooked to feeding tubes; even unfinished it was ~200 m tall and could
move, attack, and fire a beam from its mouth. Shepard's team destroyed it before it
could fully develop.

## Leviathans (for context)

Not Reapers, but their creators. An ancient race that ruled the galaxy billions of
years ago — huge ocean-dwelling creatures ~450 m long, resembling giant squid or
cuttlefish with multiple eyes and long tentacles. They viewed themselves as the apex
life form and treated other species as tools, using a powerful form of mind control to
turn beings into obedient servants. They built the intelligence that became the
Reapers, which then turned on them. Afterward the Leviathans hid deep in ocean worlds,
watching the cycle continue for millions of years while erasing evidence of their
existence.

## Processors

Creepy moving machines ~600 m long, used to collect DNA from living beings. Not alive
or self-aware — tools controlled remotely by the Reapers. They gather and process
organic material from captured populations into the raw material for new Reapers, like
mobile factories following the invasion fleet.

## Intruders

A mid-level Reaper ship between the Destroyers and the capital ships. Stronger and
tougher than smaller Reapers, with better beam weapons for long-range attacks, stronger
shields, and point-defense systems that shoot down incoming missiles and fighters.

## Troop transports

Large Reaper machines between 200 m and a full kilometer long, used to move husks and
other troops to new planets and to bring captured populations back for processing. Like
the Processors, they are remotely controlled rather than alive — essentially gigantic
delivery ships.

## Devastator

A Reaper that works like a flying carrier: its main role is to launch large numbers of
Oculus drones and supporting Destroyer units rather than rely on its own guns. Useful
for opening attacks on planets and weakening defenses before the main force arrives. It
can still fight with weapons comparable to a capital ship, but is less durable because
much of its interior is given over to drone storage instead of armor.

## Reaper capital ships

The biggest and most powerful class — usually ~2 km long, shaped like a gigantic space
squid. These lead the Reaper fleet and attack whole planets. Their main weapon is a
massive cannon running the length of the body, firing a blast no normal ship — not even
a dreadnought — can survive. They also have smaller weapons on their arms, point
defenses, and swarms of Oculus drones, and can take heavy fire from multiple warships
before weakening.

## Sovereign

One of the most important Reapers — leader and scout for the rest. Initially assumed to
be just one of many capital ships, Sovereign is in fact unique: a fully alive Reaper
with its own mind, about 2 km long, with a main gun that can destroy the biggest ships
in one shot. For thousands of years it stayed hidden, waking periodically to gauge how
advanced galactic species had become. Its job was to start the next invasion by
controlling others through indoctrination and activating the Citadel so the rest of the
Reapers could return. The geth worship it as a god, but Sovereign regards all other
life, geth included, as weak and temporary — tools, not equals.

## Harbinger

The first Reaper ever made, and effectively the leader and mastermind behind many of
their plans. It remains in dark space beyond the galaxy with the rest of the Reapers
but directs events through the Collectors as its servants, and can take direct control
of individual Collectors in battle — speaking through them and making them stronger, so
that it feels as though Harbinger is personally fighting Shepard. It devised the plan to
harvest humans and build the human-Reaper, and shows a particular interest in humanity.
Confident and intimidating, it insists resistance is pointless; even when its plans
fail it does not panic, saying only that the Reapers will find another way.
